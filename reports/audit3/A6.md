## 49: sitemap lastmod

Live, before (`curl -s https://marlbridge.com/sitemap-index.xml`, excerpt):
```
<sitemap><loc>https://marlbridge.com/sitemap-resources-0.xml</loc><lastmod>2026-11-24T00:00:00.000Z</lastmod></sitemap>
<sitemap><loc>https://marlbridge.com/sitemap-practice-0.xml</loc></sitemap>
<sitemap><loc>https://marlbridge.com/sitemap-flashcards-0.xml</loc><lastmod>2026-11-20T00:00:00.000Z</lastmod></sitemap>
...
<sitemap><loc>https://marlbridge.com/sitemap-localized-0.xml</loc></sitemap>
```

Existing pattern (astro.config.mjs): `buildLastmodMap()` takes resource and article `updatedDate ?? publishedDate`, AP `updatedDate` and flashcard deck
`updated`. `buildIndexLastmods()` gives a listing page the newest date among what it lists, and a teacher profile its `verifiedOn`. No git dates and no build date
are used anywhere, and the new code follows the same rule. New rules (`src/utils/seo/sitemap-lastmod.ts`):
- `/practice/{code}/`: the newest real date among the practice-questions resources its questions are parsed from (`src/utils/practice/bank.ts`).
  These are the question-bank data's own dates.
- `/practice/{code}/diagnostic/{set}/`: the newest date among the resources of the set's question ids, or `setReview.reviewedOn` if later.
  The page shows that review date.
- `/practice/`: the newest of the above.
- Translated legal pages (15): the date the page itself displays, `LEGAL_COPY[key][locale].lastUpdated` (for example '১৭ নভেম্বর ২০২৬').
  This is parsed, not translated. The test requires all 15 to parse and the three languages to agree per page.
- A translated page whose English counterpart has a lastmod takes it. Today that means `/{ar,ur,bn}/articles/` (they list every article) and
  `/{ar,ur,bn}/resources/` (per-type, per-subject counts). The home page is excluded: the English home is dated from the resources it lists, and the
  translated homes do not list them.
- All other translated pages (about, pricing, contact, trial, boards, ...) have no real date, the same as their English pages, so they get none.

Result from source (no build): all 56 practice URLs dated. Examples: `/practice/` 2026-11-24, `/practice/0625/` 2026-11-17,
`/practice/0620/diagnostic/core/` 2026-11-16 (the review date). 21 of the 57 localized URLs are dated: resources 2026-11-24,
articles 2026-10-10, accessibility 2026-08-17, cookies 2026-11-17, editorial-policy 2026-11-16, privacy 2026-09-23, terms 2026-08-26.
After a build, the index should show practice 2026-11-24 and localized 2026-11-24 (the newest URL in each).
