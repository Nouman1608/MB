# Overview vs subtopic contradictions: resolved against the official sources (D-543j)

Input: `reports/owner-decisions/overview-subtopic-contradictions.md` (12 overview-vs-subtopic findings plus 5 "overview pages disagree" cases = 17 rows). Each row was checked against the board's own specification, syllabus, question paper or grade-threshold table. "L" numbers are line numbers in the markdown files under `src/content/resources/`. PDF page or section is given where the text had one.

## Result

**None of the 17 rows is a real contradiction, and no page or data record was edited.** Each row is one of three things: (1) the two numbers describe different things (a section vs the whole paper, an AS paper vs an A Level paper, one exercise's pacing vs the paper, a total across papers vs one paper); (2) two different specifications (9625 vs 9725); (3) the audit script read a "first teaching" year as a "first exam" year, or picked up an unrelated number. In every row the site pages agree with the official source. `src/data/academic/assessments.ts` was not the wrong party in any row, so it is unchanged.

Verdict key: **both right** = the two numbers are different valid things; **pages agree** = the script flagged a non-conflict; **A right / B right** = one page wrong (none occurred).

## Needs owner

1. Row 5: the AQA 7702 **Paper 2** duration (2 hours 30 minutes) was not read from an AQA page. The scheme-of-assessment page gives marks and weighting but no duration; the Paper 1 paper cover confirms 2 hours 30 for Paper 1. All Marlbridge pages already say 2 hours 30 for Paper 2 and none disagree, so this is a source gap, not a contradiction. Opening the 7702/2 question paper cover would close it.
2. Row 11: "about 30 minutes" for Exercise 6 (0539) is Marlbridge pacing advice; no official source states per-exercise time. Leave it, or label it "suggested" if the owner wants it kept apart from official durations.
3. Not a page fault: rows 2-5, 12-17 (10 of the 17) are artefacts of `scripts/audit-overview-subtopic-consistency.mjs` (first-teaching year read as first-exam year; module-to-paper sentences read as paper counts; totals read as per-paper marks; section marks read as paper marks). The script could skip "Section"/"Exercise" marks, sums with "+" or "in total", and "first taught/teaching" years. Not changed in this task.
