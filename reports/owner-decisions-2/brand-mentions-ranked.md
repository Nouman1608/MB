## Note on Google guidance

- Google treats links created to manipulate ranking as link spam: buying or selling links, excessive link exchanges, automated link building, low-quality directory links, and guest posts or press releases carrying keyword-heavy anchor text.
- Where money, goods or a free product change hands for a link (including a paid "featured" or "sponsored" directory listing), the link should carry rel="sponsored" (rel="nofollow" is also accepted). If a directory marks a paid listing that way, that is correct: do not ask the publisher to remove it.
- Practical approach: earn mentions by being useful (original data, a quotable teacher, a free resource). Write guest articles for readers, with one author-bio link, a brand name anchor, no keyword anchors. Never pay for a followed link. For a listing, check the live page and record whether the link is followed, nofollow or sponsored; do not edit anchors to chase it.
- Gulf point: some countries require a licence or approval to advertise or provide paid tuition (Qatar Living guidance mentions approvals). The owner should confirm each country's rule before listing in a local directory. I did not verify the law.
