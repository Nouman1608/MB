# Syllabus changes 2028–2030: Cambridge IGCSE Mathematics 0580 and AS & A Level Physics 9702

**Written 27 Sep 2026 (follow-up M7 to resource-library-audit-2026-09-27.md, section 13).**

Why this matters: students who start a two-year course in September 2026 sit their exams in 2028, so they will be examined on the 2028–2030 syllabuses, not the 2025–2027 ones that the site's 0580 and 9702 pages cite.

**Short answer:** for both codes, the subject content is unchanged. Every topic, subtopic and learning outcome has the same number, name and wording (apart from typography). The papers, marks, durations and weightings are also unchanged. The differences are small wording changes in the assessment description, the command-word table and administrative sections. Cambridge's own "Changes to this syllabus" page says, for both codes, "There are no significant changes which affect teaching."

**Method.**
1. I extracted each PDF with `pdftotext -layout`. I then normalised whitespace, page headers and footers, "(continued)" markers and the learning-outcome numbering style ("1" became "1.") and ran a line diff and a word diff of the whole document.
2. I checked the topic and subtopic headings against `src/data/academic/syllabus-topics.ts` (72 subtopics for 0580, 76 for 9702).
3. For 0580, I checked the Core-column "Extended content only." cells both in the text and visually on the rendered PDF pages (pp. 16–31).
4. For the 9702 data sheets, the `-layout` extraction of the old PDF is garbled on page 58, so I compared them in raw (non-layout) extraction.

## 4. Recommended page updates (prioritised)

None of these is urgent for accuracy: the pages' content is correct for 2028–2030 as well. The issue is that the cited series ends in 2027, while this autumn's starters sit exams in 2028.

1. **Add a one-line edition note to the 0580 and 9702 hubs, and then to the pages**, when the owner is ready. For example: "Cambridge's 2028–2030 syllabus has no changes to this content; these notes also apply to exams in 2028." This should be a template-level note on the hub, or the house edition-note pattern used for 0452 and 5014. It should not be a mass edit to 106 files. It needs an owner decision because it changes live text. The hub text currently comes from `syllabusTopics.syllabusSeries` ("for the 2025-2027 examination series") and from the `syllabuses.ts` notes (0580: "Syllabus for examination series 2025, 2026 and 2027").
2. **When 2028–2030 becomes current (from the March 2028 series in India, June 2028 elsewhere):**
   - Switch the `status` values in `syllabus-topics.ts`: 2025–2027 to 'superseded' and 2028–2030 to 'current'. Keep both records if the November 2027 series still needs the old one.
   - Update the `assessments.ts` source URLs (the paper data is unchanged).
   - Update the `syllabusSeries` frontmatter and the 10 + 3 in-body PDF citations.
   - Refresh the coverage matrices' "Applicable exam years" and "Official source" columns.
3. **0580 "Sketch" (low):** where a practice answer or study guide explains what "sketch" means, add "with sensible proportions". This applies to the algebra-and-graphs guide (2.11) first.
4. **9702 exam-technique wording (low):** if a future page describes Paper 5, do not say "two compulsory questions" for 2028 onwards. Also, "Suggest" now includes "put forward considerations".
5. **Cosmetic (lowest):** "Wave–particle" with an en dash in the 22.3 headings, and lower-case "universe" in the cosmology guide. Optional.

## 5. Data model (`src/data/academic/`)

- **`syllabus-topics.ts`: changed.** The model already supports a series with `status: 'future'` (`SyllabusStatus = 'current' | 'future' | 'superseded'`). Every public reader filters to 'current': `topicsFor()` (hubs, checklists, practice, diagnostics, the tuition panel, the sitemap), both checklist index pages and `scripts/academic-coverage-report-v2.mjs`. Readers that do not filter are:
  - `validate-academic-content.mjs`: takes the union of slugs across series for the same board, subject and qualification.
  - `validate-cross-board-integrity.mjs`: checks that the code matches `syllabuses.ts`, that the combination is ACTIVE and that the source host is the board's domain.
  - `academic-coverage-dashboard.mjs`: prints the status.

  So I added complete 2028–2030 entries for 0580 (72 subtopics, 19 'supplement' and 53 'both', `tierVerified: true` after the visual check) and 9702 (76 subtopics, AS/A stages), both with `status: 'future'`. They reuse the current slugs, because the subtopics are identical, so the validator's slug union is unchanged and no page or URL changes. The only name difference is 22.3 "Wave–particle duality" (with the en dash, as printed in 2028–2030). The `KNOWN_OTHER_SERIES` doc comment is updated, because it said topics are extracted only when a series becomes current.
- **`syllabuses.ts`: not changed.** It holds one record per board, qualification and subject. It has no series or status field, and its `officialUrl` is the series-independent qualification page. A second record would be a model change. Its `notes` render on the live hub, so editing them would change live pages (see recommendation 1).
- **`assessments.ts` (not in scope, not changed):** it supports `specStatus: 'future'`. A future record for 0580 or 9702 would be identical to the current one except for the source URL, so it adds nothing until the switch-over.

## 6. What I could not verify

- **Version 1 of the 0580 2028–2030 syllabus.** Only Version 2 is published, so I cannot say what the page 9 change in Version 2 corrected.
- **Specimen papers for 2028–2030.** Cambridge says syllabuses and specimen materials are the final authority. I did not check whether new specimen papers exist or differ, for example in Paper 5 question count.
- **Formula typesetting.** Mathematical notation was compared from text extraction. The 9702 data sheets and the 0580 List of formulas matched in raw extraction, but I checked only the 0580 "Extended content only" pages visually. Superscripts and fractions in formulas were not checked visually page by page.
- **Carry-forward rules.** These are in the Cambridge Handbook and a separate supplement, which I did not read.
