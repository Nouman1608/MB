# Flashcards: review status and what a teacher should check (D-438)

86 decks, 18,099 cards (Mathematics 3,387 · Chemistry 3,131 · Physics 2,892 · Biology 2,494 ·
Economics 2,448 · Business 2,336 · Accounting 1,411). 1,278 cards are upper tier; 2,808 restate a
named revision-notes page. **All 86 decks and the 24 revision-notes pages written for them were
reviewed by the subject teachers on 5 Nov 2026** (owner, 5 Nov 2026); each deck records its
reviewer in `deckReview` and each page in `reviewer`/`reviewedDate`. See "Teacher review" below.

The first version of this list (5 Nov 2026) raised four groups of problems. All four were worked
through the same day against the official specifications, data booklets and mark schemes
(Cambridge, AQA, OCR, Pearson and OxfordAQA documents downloaded and read; IB guides are licensed
and could not be fetched, so IB items rest on the course's own pages). What was done is below,
followed by what is still open.

  (revision notes, practice, study guide).

  for approach.

- **Cambridge 9701** — [Co(NH₃)₆]²⁺ colour now the same on all three pages; the AS nitrogen page's

- **Cambridge 9708** — pollution-permit worked example: the 100-tonne limit restored.
- **Edexcel 4EC1** — YED labels clarified (0–1 normal good, a necessity; above 1 luxury);
  current account note added.

  who is counted.

  no "added value", motivation methods).

  the June 2026 mark scheme does; worked figures recalculated.

  v1.1 (they had followed the old 7131/7132 text).

## Done since (5 Nov 2026, last round)

- **YEC11 unit codes** — the three WEC11/01 "Markets in action" pages are retagged to YEC11 (as the
  WEC12/01 growth pages were), `updatedDate` 2026-11-05.
- **J249 P2.2l** — the specification never states F = Δp/Δt. The worked calculation (0.30 kg ball
  stopped in 0.020 s) is F = m × a with a = Δv/t, which is P2.2l (separate Physics, not bold), so it
  is no longer upper tier; the qualitative longer-collision-time card stays Higher (momentum is
  bold). Deck header updated.
- **J249 free body diagrams** — P2.2g and P2.2h are bold, so practice Q4 (lamp at rest) is now
  marked Higher; Q5 (adding collinear forces for a resultant) stays both tiers (P2.2i).
- **9215 ROCE** — OxfordAQA's 9215 vocabulary list defines "profit from operations" as the profit
  for the year before finance charges and taxation, and no 9215 document gives a sole-trader
  version of ROCE (the A-level sheet's sole-trader formula belongs to 9615). The pages' single
  formula is right; the source is now cited on the ratios page.
- **Large subtopics** — 74 of about 2,640 subtopics have 13 or more cards, mostly broad units
  (a whole Edexcel A Level Biology topic, for example). Reviewed cards were kept; the study panel
  now has a "Session length" choice (all, 10 or 20 cards) on decks of more than 10 cards, and
  "Only cards I have not got yet" carries on through the rest.
