# Flashcards: review status and what a teacher should check (D-438)

86 decks, 18,038 cards (Mathematics 3,387 · Chemistry 3,131 · Physics 2,892 · Biology 2,460 ·
Economics 2,422 · Business 2,335 · Accounting 1,411). 1,279 cards are upper tier; 2,699 restate a
named revision-notes page. **No deck has been reviewed as a deck by a subject teacher**; each
flashcard page says so until `deckReview` is filled in.

The first version of this list (5 Nov 2026) raised four groups of problems. All four were worked
through the same day against the official specifications, data booklets and mark schemes
(Cambridge, AQA, OCR, Pearson and OxfordAQA documents downloaded and read; IB guides are licensed
and could not be fetched, so IB items rest on the course's own pages). What was done is below,
followed by what is still open.

## 1. Errors on existing Marlbridge pages — done

Corrected (review fields unchanged, `updatedDate` 2026-11-05):

- **Cambridge 9702** — absolute zero is the minimum internal energy, not zero kinetic energy
  (revision notes, practice, study guide).
- **AQA 7408** — red shift made consistent with the specification: z = Δλ/λ = −v/c, v positive
  for approach.
- **OxfordAQA 9630** — Hooke's law holds up to the limit of proportionality.
- **Cambridge 9701** — [Co(NH₃)₆]²⁺ colour now the same on all three pages; the AS nitrogen page's
  "around 945" corrected to the data-booklet 944 kJ mol⁻¹.
- **IB DP Economics** — the missing sixth fiscal-policy goal restored to the table.
- **Cambridge 9708** — pollution-permit worked example: the 100-tonne limit restored.
- **Edexcel 4EC1** — YED labels clarified (0–1 normal good, a necessity; above 1 luxury);
  current account note added.
- **OxfordAQA 9625/9725** — employee retention rate: OxfordAQA's own formula kept, with a note on
  who is counted.
- **OCR J204** — component-level notes aligned with the J204 specification (segmentation bases,
  no "added value", motivation methods).
- **OxfordAQA 9215** — ROCE and profit in relation to revenue now use profit from operations, as
  the June 2026 mark scheme does; worked figures recalculated.
- **AQA AS 7137** — finance and operations pages rebuilt to the current 7137/7138 specification
  v1.1 (they had followed the old 7131/7132 text).
- **OCR GCSE Physics J249** (found during the tier check) — 48 pages had labelled
  separate-Physics-only content "Higher Tier only". Labels now follow the specification's bold
  statements.

Checked and correct as printed (the board's own wording): 9610 "pluripotent … cells are found in
mature mammals"; H431 Lewin under contingency theory; 9620 cisplatin "a nitrogen atom on a
protein".
