## AP Chemistry

- **Exam:** 2027-05-06 (May 2027). Hybrid digital: multiple-choice answered in the Bluebook app; free-response viewed in Bluebook and handwritten in a paper booklet.

## AP Statistics

- **Exam:** 2027-05-11 (May 2027). Fully digital: both sections answered in the Bluebook app (new for May 2027).

- **Clarifications:** No separate clarifications document is published; the revised CED is effective Fall 2026.
