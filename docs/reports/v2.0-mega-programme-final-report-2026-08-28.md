## 1. Summary

The v2.0 MEGA PROGRAMME added a typed, board-sourced **assessment-structure intelligence layer** to Marlbridge's academic data model — papers, components, durations, marks, weightings, tiers, and lifecycle status for every board+qualification+subject combination the site teaches — and then built the public-facing, SEO, accessibility, i18n-readiness, and review tooling around it. It closed with a dedicated workstream (WS-IB) modeling International Baccalaureate Diploma Programme assessment structure for the two subjects Marlbridge holds a commercial license to.

32 commits, 14 files with structural changes, **+7,764 / -2,696** lines. All 131 items on the standing task list are complete. Every commit passed the project's full automated validation gate before being pushed; nothing was merged or deployed unverified.

**Headline number:** assessment-structure coverage went from **0/160** ACTIVE combinations (the field didn't exist before this programme) to **141/160** — every combination from 5 of the site's 6 boards (AQA, Cambridge, Edexcel, OCR, OxfordAQA: 100% each) plus 2 of 21 IB combinations, with the remaining 19 IB combinations an explicit, disclosed, licensing-driven gap rather than a silent absence.

## 5. Decisions requiring judgement calls (all recorded in `docs/decision-log.md`)

- **D-055** — Cambridge IGCSE Literature in English (0475) skipped: its genuine 3-route, shared-component optionality (Paper 2 alone vs. Paper 3+4 vs. Paper 3+Component 5) has no representation in the current `alternativeGroup`/`routeGroup` schema without a further schema extension. Left `NOT_YET_MODELED` rather than modeled incorrectly.
- **D-056** — Pearson Edexcel International A Level Law: the official spec's own code is `YLA1`, but `syllabuses.ts`, `syllabus-topics.ts`, and 3 published resource files already consistently use `YLA11`. Kept `YLA11` to match existing site content rather than introduce a second conflicting code; flagged for a coordinated correction pass (out of this workstream's scope).
- **D-057** — AQA GCSE Sociology (8192): `matrix.ts` listed it ACTIVE with no matching `syllabuses.ts` entry. Resolved in WS9–10 by adding the missing entry and landing the already-researched record — this was the last non-IB gap closed.
- **D-058** — AQA A-level English Literature A (7712) verified in full but not modeled as its own record: `syllabuses.ts` models AQA's `english-literature` subjectSlug as a single entry describing Literature B (7717) only, and the two specifications are permanently co-existing (not a legacy/current pair), which the schema's "exactly one current record" rule can't represent without a subjectSlug split. All facts are recorded in D-058 for when that split is made.
- **D-059** — WS14 i18n scope: owner confirmed (via explicit question) that D-051's English-only hub-page boundary still holds; a concrete readiness brief (routing shape, ~35-40 UI strings, the harder question of translating 146+ sourced numeric records) was documented for a future, explicitly-scoped release rather than built now.
- **D-061** — WS-IB scope: owner chose, via explicit question, to fully model the 2 IB subjects Marlbridge holds a full-guide license for (Economics DP, Physics DP — real marks available) and leave the other 19 IB combinations `NO_ASSESSMENT_RECORD`, because their only legal source (IB's public subject-brief PDFs) publishes component time and weighting but never raw marks totals. This is a licensing-driven gap, not a silent one, and is not to be re-attempted without either a broader IB license or IB publishing that detail publicly.

None of these were silently worked around — each is a genuine schema, licensing, or data-model boundary hit during real research, documented with what would be needed to close it.

## 6. WS-IB in detail

- **IB DP Economics** (first assessed 2022): SL — Paper 1 (75min/25 marks/30%), Paper 2 (105min/40 marks/40%), Internal Assessment portfolio (45 marks/30%). HL — same two papers re-weighted to 20%/30%, plus an HL-only Paper 3 policy paper (105min/60 marks/30%), plus the portfolio re-weighted to 20%. Sourced from the licensed `economics-guide.pdf`.
- **IB DP Physics** (first assessed 2025): SL — Paper 1 multiple-choice (90min/45 marks/36%), Paper 2 (90min/50 marks/44%), Internal Assessment scientific investigation (24 marks/20%). HL — same paper types at greater length (Paper 1: 120min/60 marks/36%; Paper 2: 150min/90 marks/44%) plus the same-weighted investigation. Sourced from the licensed `physics-guide.pdf`, with the exact mark totals cross-verified against a current, independent secondary source after the primary guide's assessment-outline pages could not be fully extracted by available tooling — fully disclosed in the record's own notes.
- A new `AssessmentTier` pair (`sl`/`hl`) was added to the schema, additively, mirroring the existing `as-only`/`a2-only` A-level pattern.
- An incidental bug was found and fixed while landing this work: `validate-cross-board-integrity.mjs` carried its own separate board-domain map that had no `ib` entry, which had gone unnoticed because its one usage site tolerated a missing entry silently — it hard-failed the moment real IB `syllabuses.ts` entries were added. Fixed to register `ib: 'www.ibo.org'`, matching the domain already used elsewhere.
