# Trustpilot review requests (October 2026)

Trustpilot page: https://www.trustpilot.com/review/marlbridge.com
(already linked from the site's Organization `sameAs`, D-403).

Real reviews from parents and students are the strongest trust signal a
first-time family sees, and the Trustpilot page currently has few. This file
is the message to send and the rules to follow. Nothing here is automated;
the owner sends the messages.

## Trustpilot's rules (follow these or reviews can be removed)

- **Ask everyone, not only happy families.** Send the message to every
  current family (or every family in a class, a term, a whole group), never
  only to the ones you expect to be positive.
- **No rewards.** No discount, free class, gift or fee change in return for a
  review, and nothing that sounds like one.
- **Never write or edit a review for someone**, and do not suggest wording
  or a star rating.
- **Staff, teachers and their relatives must not review.**
- **Reply to every review**, good or bad, politely and within a few days. A
  calm reply to a critical review often reassures new families more than a
  five-star one.

## When to send

- To current families now, in one batch.
- After that, to each family around the end of their first full month, and
  again after exam results.
- One reminder at most, about a week later, to anyone who has not replied.
  Do not chase further.
