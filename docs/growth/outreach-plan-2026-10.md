# Link-building outreach plan — October 2026

Builds on `linkable-assets-outreach.md` (the framework). This file is the
first populated version: real, publicly listed targets found on 9 Oct 2026,
what to offer each, and ready-to-send messages. No personal emails are
listed; use each site's own public contact route. Fill in the Status
column as you go.

**Rules (unchanged from the framework):** offer something useful to their
audience, never "please link to us"; never pay for links; disclose that
you run Marlbridge; follow each community's rules (r/igcse, for example,
bans unapproved advertising, so always ask the moderators first).

## Timing hooks

- **Cambridge results days** (January for the November series, August for the
  June series): pitch the grade threshold explorer to journalists and
  communities a week before.
- **Entry deadlines** (on the exam calendar): pitch the calendar to exams
  officers and parent guides about a month before each deadline.
- **Start of term** (late August to September): pitch checklists and the
  revision planner to schools.

## Message templates

Replace the bracketed parts. Keep each message short and specific.

### A. Community moderators (Reddit or Discord)

> Subject: Free syllabus checklists for the resources list?
>
> Hi, I run Marlbridge, an online tutoring academy in Lahore. We've put
> together free printable topic checklists for 160 Cambridge, Edexcel, AQA,
> OCR and IB courses (every topic and subtopic on one page, from the official
> syllabus), plus a grade-threshold explorer with the official Cambridge
> tables. No sign-up, no paywall. Would they be useful for the [subreddit /
> server]'s resource list? Happy to follow whatever rules you have for this,
> and fine if not.
>
> Checklists: https://marlbridge.com/checklists/
> Grade thresholds: https://marlbridge.com/grade-thresholds/

## Tracking

Record each contact in the tables above (date, channel, reply). Links
gained show up in Search Console under Links > Top linking sites; the
27 October results check covers search changes, not links, so review the
Links report separately about a month after the first replies.
