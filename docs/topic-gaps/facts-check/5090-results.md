## Not checked online (search budget ran out)

None of these claims looked wrong on reading. They are standard textbook statements. They were not verified online and should be checked in a follow-up pass.

- coordination-and-response-in-plants: how a clinostat works (slow rotation, so seedlings keep growing in their starting direction). Roots respond weakly or not at all to one-sided light, and any curve is away from the light. Tip experiments on oat seedlings: tip removed means little growth and no bending; a light-proof cap means the shoot grows straight; a sleeve below the tip still allows bending.
- transport-in-humans: red cells pass through capillaries in single file. Capillary walls are one cell thick. Standard ECG paper speed is 25 mm per second. The first heart sound is the AV valves closing and the second is the semilunar valves closing. Men are affected by CHD at younger ages than women. Coronary arteries branch from the aorta.
- human-gas-exchange: atmospheric air is about 78% N₂, 21% O₂, 0.9% Ar and 0.04% CO₂. Expired air is about 16% O₂ and 4% CO₂. Hydrogencarbonate indicator is yellow, red or magenta/purple. The larynx contains the vocal cords. The brain detects a rise in blood CO₂.
- plant-nutrition: starch-test steps and colours. Soda lime or sodium hydroxide absorbs CO₂.
- human-nutrition: vitamin C and collagen. Symptoms of scurvy, rickets and anaemia. Enamel is the hardest substance in the body. Cement covers the root. Pepsin works best at acidic pH and trypsin at slightly alkaline pH. Lacteals absorb fatty acids and glycerol.
- biotechnology: lactase splits lactose into glucose and galactose. Pectinase gives more juice and clearer juice. Biological washing powders contain proteases, lipases and amylase.
- disease-and-immunity: only female mosquitoes feed on blood. *Plasmodium* passes to the liver, then to red blood cells. Larvae and pupae breathe at the water surface, so an oil film works. Larva-eating fish and larva-killing bacteria are used. There is no HIV vaccine. Nicotine, tar and carbon monoxide effects. Passive immunity lasts a few months.
- transport-in-flowering-plants: most stomata are on the lower epidermis. Almost all water taken up is lost by transpiration. A carrot root stores sugar in its first year.
- classification: the binomial names (*Panthera leo*, *P. tigris*, *Felis catus*, *Oryza sativa*, *O. glaberrima*, *Zea mays*, *Musca domestica*, *M. autumnalis*, *Brassica oleracea*, *B. rapa*, *Allium cepa*). Mules are almost always infertile. Arthropod counts for crab, woodlouse and mite. Monocot flower parts come in threes. Fungal cell walls are not cellulose.
- development: insect-pollinated pollen is larger than wind-pollinated pollen. Asexual examples (runners, tubers, bulbs, budding).
