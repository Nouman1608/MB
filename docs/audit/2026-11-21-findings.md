## E943 - IB DP Physics topic list leaves E.2 Quantum physics unmarked as HL only [Confirmed error, Low]

- **Files:** named in the observation below (not in the frozen baseline snapshot)
- **Where:** src/data/academic/syllabus-topics.ts DP Physics record (rendered on the IB DP Physics hub and checklist; main at aaf03ed)

**What is wrong.** The record marks A.4, A.5, B.4 and D.4 '(HL only)' and its note says HL-only sub-topics are 'marked exactly as the guide marks them'. The IB Physics guide (first assessment 2025), Table 4 'Physics syllabus content overview', marks E.2 Quantum physics '•••', which its key defines as 'Topics with content that should only be taught to HL students', exactly as it marks A.4, A.5, B.4 and D.4; the Physics subject brief (January 2022) shows the same. The record lists 'E.2 Quantum physics' with no HL flag, so an SL reader is shown it as common content.

**Fix.** Mark E.2 Quantum physics (HL only), and check the resources that cover it for the same label.

## I398 - Minor differences between IB topic records and the IB guides now public [Inconsistency, Low]

- **Files:** named in the observation below (not in the frozen baseline snapshot)
- **Where:** src/data/academic/syllabus-topics.ts DP Biology, DP Business Management, DP Geography and DP Economics records (main at aaf03ed)

**What is wrong.** (1) DP Biology gives 'A2.1 Origins of cells' and 'D4.2 Sustainability and change' from the January 2022 subject brief; the Biology guide (first assessment 2025), the syllabus document, prints 'A2.1 Origin of cells' and 'D4.2 Stability and change'. The record's note chose the brief's wording over its secondary source, which matched the guide. (2) DP Business Management's note says every numbered sub-topic was 'transcribed directly from the subject brief'; the brief prints '3.6 Debt/equity ratio analysis (HL only)', while the record has '3.6 Efficiency ratio analysis (HL only)', which is what the Business management guide (first assessment 2024) prints. The record is right; its stated source is not. (3) DP Geography's note says its 46 sub-topics came from a UWC East Africa copy of the guide; the IB Geography guide at ibo.org contains all 46 names, so the note can cite the official guide. The DP Economics note says the content came from a guide 'originally accessed under a 2026-08-22 owner licensing confirmation'; the Economics guide (first assessment 2022) is now public at ibo.org and matches the record (for example 2.4 'Critique of the maximizing behaviour of consumers and producers (HL only)', although the SL subject brief lists 2.4 without the qualifier).

**Fix.** Use the guides' names for A2.1 and D4.2 (or note the brief's variants), and cite the ibo.org guides as the sources in the Business Management, Geography and Economics notes.

## U78 - IB DP ESS subtopics rest on two teacher websites [Unverified item, Low]

- **Files:** named in the observation below (not in the frozen baseline snapshot)
- **Where:** src/data/academic/syllabus-topics.ts DP Environmental Systems and Societies record (rendered on the IB ESS hub; main at aaf03ed)

**What is wrong.** The record's own note says that subtopics 2.1-8.3 'are not in the subject brief' and were taken from mrkremerscience.com and sciencesauceonline.com, and that 'the official syllabus guide itself ... was not consulted'. The ESS subject brief (first assessment 2026) confirms the eight topic names, Topic 1's 1.1-1.3 and the three HL lenses, but none of the other 26 subtopic names. The audit could not find the ESS guide among the IB subject guides served at ibo.org. The hub presents these names as official subject content.

**Fix.** Confirm the subtopics from the IB ESS guide, or show only the topics the brief confirms, or say on the page that the subtopic names are unconfirmed.
