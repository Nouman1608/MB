## I387 - Three leads confirmed against the published mark schemes and the IB Chemistry guide [Inconsistency, Low]

- **Files:** named in the observation below (not in the frozen baseline snapshot)
- **Where:** igcse-mathematics-transformations-and-vectors; cambridge-a-level-accounting-exam-preparation; ib-dp-chemistry-structure-1-practice (main at 668ee60)

**What is wrong.** (1) igcse-mathematics-transformations-and-vectors L83-86: 'a transformation named without its full details earns no marks even if the diagram is correct'. Cambridge 0580/31 June 2024 mark scheme Q6(c)(i) 'Rotation / [centre] (0,0) / ... 3 B1 for each' and Q6(c)(ii) 'Translation ... 2 B1 for each', and the 2025 specimen schemes, award the name of the transformation a mark of its own. (2) cambridge-a-level-accounting-exam-preparation L64-67: 'an unclear or missing working line forfeits credit even when the final answer given is right'. The Generic Marking Principles in the 9706 June 2024 mark schemes (Papers 21, 31) and the 2023 specimen schemes: 'If working/explanation is considered essential for full credit, this will be indicated in the question paper and in the mark scheme. In all other instances, the correct answer to a calculation should be given full credit, even if no supporting working is shown.' (3) ib-dp-chemistry-structure-1-practice L102 says the questions cover the five sub-topics 'in the same proportion the syllabus guide gives them, with the mole (1.4) weighted most heavily'. The Chemistry guide (first assessment 2025) syllabus outline, p. 24, gives teaching hours for Structure 1 as a whole (17 SL, 21 HL) and no weighting by sub-topic.

**Fix.** (1) Say the transformation's name and each property earn marks separately. (2) Say workings protect method marks when the answer is wrong, and that a correct answer earns full credit unless the question asks for working. (3) Remove the proportion claim.
