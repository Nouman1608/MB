## E931 - Three repairs introduced a false statement [Confirmed error, Medium]

- **Files:** named in the observation below (not in the frozen baseline snapshot)
- **Where:** the six Cambridge 0450 business files; aqa-gcse-business-business-in-the-real-world; edexcel-a-level-business-managing-activities-practice (main at e614762)

**What is wrong.** (1) E379's successor note, added to igcse-business-understanding-business-activity L43, its notes L28 and practice L32, igcse-business-people-in-business L47, igcse-business-marketing L42 and its notes L28, says 0264 'renumbers every sub-topic'. Cambridge IGCSE Business 0264 (2027-2029) keeps 1.1 Business activity, 1.2 Economic sectors, 1.3 Enterprise, business growth and size, 1.4 Types of business organisation, 1.5 Business objectives and stakeholder objectives, 3.2 Market research and 3.3 Marketing mix, among others. (2) E374's repair made the notes rely on the guide's worked example, aqa-gcse-business-business-in-the-real-world L169-172: 'if expansion lowers total cost to £135,000 for 20,000 units, the average unit cost falls to £6.75'. Total cost rises from £80,000 to £135,000; only the average falls. The notes list 'Saying economies of scale reduce total costs' as a trap. (3) E388's replacement scheme, edexcel-a-level-business-managing-activities-practice L120: 'As the source describes, customers paying late while suppliers require quick payment [1]'. The Bright Leaf source (L43-51) says the owner 'has struggled to pay suppliers on time despite the year-end accounts showing a profit' and says nothing about customers paying late.

**Fix.** (1) Say that 0264 restructures the content and changes some numbering, or list what changes. (2) Say total cost rises to £135,000 while average unit cost falls. (3) Credit what the source says, or add the late-paying customers to the source.
