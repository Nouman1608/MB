# Marlbridge audit - absence re-verification, round three

*Follows rounds one and two. No resources were read in this round either.*

## Two documents, one clean and one that would not open all the way

The Pearson Edexcel International Advanced Subsidiary/Advanced Level Business
specification (XBS11/YBS11) was read end to end and confirmed complete by reaching the
calculator appendix and the registered-office block. **All four absence findings resting
on it are upheld, and every item they enumerate was confirmed verbatim** — including a
twenty-two item list of omitted content that turned out to be exactly right.

The IB Diploma Programme Business management guide **could not be read complete.** The
extraction runs to 135,110 characters and stops mid-word inside a mark descriptor on page
56 of a 70-page document.

## U70 is the entry that matters

This is the first failure of the closing-matter test in the re-verification, and it settles
the argument round two started.

| document | characters extracted | complete? |
|---|---|---|
| OCR A Level Business H431 | 106,651 | **yes** — reached the summary of updates and colophon |
| IB Business management guide | 135,110 | **no** — stopped mid-word on p56 of 70 |

**A longer extraction was incomplete and a shorter one was complete.** Length carries no
information about whether a document was fully read. Only the closing matter does.

What is unread in the IB guide is exactly the region that matters most for absence
findings: the remaining internal-assessment criteria and **all five appendices** — the
presentation of financial statements, the formulae, the discount tables, the glossary of
command terms and the glossary of subject-specific terms.

So one of the four IB findings is narrowed rather than settled. It asserts that a sampling
method "occurs nowhere in the guide". The sub-topic's own closed list of three methods was
confirmed, so the substantive half stands — the resource teaches four where the guide names
three. The stronger claim is withdrawn until the whole guide can be read, because a glossary
of subject-specific terms is precisely where a fourth method could be defined.

## The corrections that would go wrong

**Edexcel: the command-word tariff is not a single number.** A finding correctly says the
specification publishes a mark against each command word. What it does not say is that
**assess is 10 marks for the two IAS units and 12 for the two IA2 units.** An instruction to
"set each question at the published figure for its command word" is therefore wrong for any
Unit 3 or Unit 4 resource. This is the U69 cross-scope hazard again, this time inside a
single specification.

**Edexcel: the finding's own fallback is unsound.** A second-year metric is set in a
first-year resource. The finding offers, as an alternative to moving it, "reframe around
retention without the calculation". But the specification's bullet reads **"labour turnover
and retention"** as one item in the IA2 unit — so the fallback still lands on second-year
content. The only clean first-year reframing avoids both terms.

**IB: don't over-correct paper 1.** The finding that paper 1 is wrongly described as a
pre-seen case study is upheld: the case study, 800–1,000 words, is expressly not seen before
the examination. But a pre-released *statement* and approximately the first 200 words of the
case study genuinely **are** released, three months ahead. The error is calling the case
study pre-seen, not claiming that anything is released. A corrector who deleted all mention
of advance material would introduce a new error.

**IB: keep the weightings apart.** The internal assessment is one task for both levels — but
it is weighted 30% at SL and 20% at HL. A correction saying "both levels do the same IA"
must not flatten that.

**IB: the ratio sub-topic is HL only.** The finding is right that a sub-topic is misnamed and
three of its four ratios are missing. Adding stock turnover, debtor days and creditor days to
an SL-labelled resource would push HL-only content into SL — the tier hazard from U65 in a
different form.

## Q344 - All four Edexcel international business findings hold, and two of their corrections are unsafe as written [Questionable claim, Medium]

- **Files:** `a-level-edexcel-business-managing-business-activities`, `a-level-edexcel-business-marketing-and-people`, `a-business-marketing-people-practice`, `edexcel-a-level-business-managing-activities-practice`
- **Where:** Correction constraints, Edexcel XBS11/YBS11
- **Locate by:** `title: "Edexcel A Level Business: Managing Business Activities (YBS11)"`

**What is wrong.** The specification was read end to end and confirmed complete by reaching the calculator appendix and the registered-office block. All four findings are upheld and every item they list was confirmed verbatim. The unit said to have five content areas has five, and the two said to be missing entirely, resource management and external influences, contain between them every one of the twenty-two items the finding enumerates. The entrepreneurs-and-leaders area contains all eight items the second finding names. The command-word table publishes exactly the eight words and tariffs quoted, above the specification's own claim to have applied a consistent mark tariff, and none of the three words the practice papers use appears in it. The metric said to be second-year content is in the second-year unit, in the human-resources sub-topic, and the first-year people area has no turnover metric. Two corrections are nevertheless unsafe. The command-word tariff is not single-valued: assess is ten marks for the two first-year units and twelve for the two second-year units, so an instruction to set each question at the published figure for its command word will be wrong for any second-year resource. And the second-year bullet reads labour turnover and retention as one item, so the finding's own fallback of reframing around retention without the calculation still lands on second-year content; the only clean first-year reframing avoids both terms.

**Fix.** Scope the assess tariff by unit before applying it, and drop the retention fallback.

**Evidence.** Pearson Edexcel International Advanced Subsidiary in Business (XBS11) and International Advanced Level in Business (YBS11) specification, Issue 1, September 2017, 61 numbered pages plus ten appendices - https://qualifications.pearson.com/content/dam/pdf/International%20Advanced%20Level/Business/2018/Specification-and-Sample-Assessment/International-A-Level-Business-Spec.pdf

## Q345 - Three IB findings are upheld within the pages that were read, and each correction needs a bound the finding does not give [Questionable claim, Medium]

- **Files:** `ib-dp-business-management-subject-guide`, `ib-dp-business-management-assessment-revision-notes`, `ib-dp-business-management-syllabus-guide`, `ib-dp-business-management-finance-accounts-revision-notes`
- **Where:** Correction constraints, IB Business management
- **Locate by:** `title: "IB Diploma Programme Business Management: Syllabus Guide"`

**What is wrong.** The internal assessment is a single section headed for both levels, describing one business research project of at most eighteen hundred words, so the finding that a separate standard-level task is described is upheld. The first paper is built on a statement pre-released three months ahead plus approximately the first two hundred words of the case study, while the case study itself, of eight hundred to a thousand words, is expressly not seen before the examination, so the finding that the paper is wrongly described as a pre-seen case study is upheld. The sub-topic the third finding names is titled Efficiency ratio analysis and is marked higher level only, and it requires stock turnover, debtor days, creditor days and the gearing ratio, strategies to improve them, and insolvency versus bankruptcy, exactly as the finding states. Each correction needs a bound. The internal-assessment correction must keep the weightings apart, thirty per cent at standard level and twenty at higher, because the task is the same but the weighting is not. The paper-one correction must not delete the advance material, because a pre-released statement and a two-hundred-word extract genuinely are released; the error is calling the case study pre-seen, not claiming that anything is released. And the ratio correction must carry the higher-level-only marking, or it will push higher-level content into a standard-level resource.

**Fix.** Apply all three corrections with the bounds stated, and mark the ratio sub-topic higher level only.

**Evidence.** International Baccalaureate Diploma Programme Business management guide, first assessment 2024, published February 2022, updated May 2022, 70 pages - read only to page 56, see U70 - https://ibo.org/globalassets/new-structure/university-admission/pdfs/subject-guides/business-management-guide.pdf

## U70 - A document in this round could not be read complete, and it failed the test at a higher character count than one that passed [Unverified item, High]

- **Files:** `ib-dp-business-management-subject-guide`, `ib-dp-business-management-marketing-revision-notes`
- **Where:** Incomplete source read
- **Locate by:** `title: "IB Diploma Programme Business Management: Subject Overview"`

**What is wrong.** The IB Business management guide could not be read end to end. The extraction runs to one hundred and thirty-five thousand one hundred and ten characters and stops mid-word inside a mark descriptor on page fifty-six of a seventy-page document, so the remaining internal-assessment criteria and all five appendices are unread: the presentation of financial statements, the formulae, the discount tables, the glossary of command terms and the glossary of subject-specific terms. This is the first failure of the terminal-marker test in this re-verification and it settles the point the previous round argued: an extraction that stopped at one hundred and thirty-five thousand characters is incomplete while one that reached one hundred and six thousand six hundred and fifty-one characters was complete, so the character count carries no information about completeness and only the closing matter does. The consequence for this document is specific. Three of its four findings concern the assessment outline, the internal assessment and a syllabus sub-topic, all of which sit inside the pages that were read, and they are settled in the companion entry Q345. The fourth asserts that a sampling method occurs nowhere in the guide; the sub-topic's own closed list of three was confirmed, but the nowhere-in-the-guide half cannot be established while a glossary of subject-specific terms remains unread. Note for correction: that finding should be narrowed to what was verified - the sub-topic names three methods and the resource gives four - and the stronger claim withdrawn until the whole guide is read.

**Fix.** Obtain the complete guide by another route, narrow the sampling finding to the sub-topic list in the meantime, and record the closing matter reached for every source in this audit.

**Evidence.** International Baccalaureate Diploma Programme Business management guide, first assessment 2024, published February 2022, updated May 2022, 70 pages - read only to page 56, see U70 - https://ibo.org/globalassets/new-structure/university-admission/pdfs/subject-guides/business-management-guide.pdf
