## E920 - Five statements in four Cambridge resources give syllabus content the syllabus contradicts [Confirmed error, High]

- **Files:** named in the observation below (not in the frozen baseline snapshot)
- **Where:** cambridge-igcse-economics-microeconomic-decision-makers L48, L83, L126; a-level-business-marketing-revision-notes L171, L176; igcse-biology-human-nutrition L128, L147; cambridge-o-level-environmental-management-agriculture L53 (main at 182db89)

**What is wrong.** (1) cambridge-igcse-economics-microeconomic-decision-makers lists marginal cost as 0455 Topic 3 content (L48), defines it (L83) and tells students to learn it (L126). The 0455 2026 syllabus, 3.7 guidance, says: 'Note: marginal cost is not required.' (2) a-level-business-marketing-revision-notes L171 and L176 call A Level Topic 9 Strategic marketing. In 9609 (2026-2028) Topic 8 is Marketing (8.1 Marketing analysis, 8.2 Marketing strategy) and Topic 9 is Operations management. (3) igcse-biology-human-nutrition L147 presents 'Describe the structure of a villus, limited to a wall one cell thick and a good blood supply' as a Supplement statement. 0610 (2026-2028, version 3) 7.5.4 reads only 'Describe the structure of a villus'; the limitation is invented. (4) The same file, L128, calls the substrate/product enzyme statements '7.1's' table. They are 7.4.3, Chemical digestion; 7.1 is Diet and has no enzyme content. (5) cambridge-o-level-environmental-management-agriculture L53 still lists 'soil type (loam, sandy and clayey soils)' as coverage. 5014 (2025-2026) 3.2 compares sandy and clay soils only and never names loam; D-241 corrected the rest of this file's soil coverage.

**Fix.** Remove marginal cost from the 0455 coverage and learning list, or label it background beyond 3.7; renumber Strategic marketing to 8.2 (Topic 8); quote 7.5.4 without the added limitation; cite the enzyme table as 7.4; drop loam from the 5014 coverage bullet.

## E921 - Seven assessment statements in thirteen Cambridge resources are contradicted by the syllabus [Confirmed error, Medium]

- **Files:** named in the observation below (not in the frozen baseline snapshot)
- **Where:** igcse-mathematics-algebra-and-graphs L27, -coordinate-geometry L26, -geometry L26, -probability L25, -statistics L26, -transformations-and-vectors L26, -trigonometry L26; cambridge-a-level-law-exam-preparation L43; a-level-world-history-usa-practice L64, L68; igcse-environmental-management-land-practice L119; o-level-urdu-first-language-paper-2-texts-revision-notes L144-145; igcse-global-perspectives-individual-report-practice L39, L103; cambridge-igcse-economics-microeconomic-decision-makers L30 (main at 182db89)

**What is wrong.** (1) Seven 0580 topic guides say Extended content is 'needed for grades A*-C'. 0580 (2025-2027, version 3): Core candidates are eligible for grades C to G, Extended for A* to E, so Extended is needed for A* to B, not C. (2) cambridge-a-level-law-exam-preparation L43 says Papers 3 and 4 are both problem-based rather than abstract. 9084 (2026-2028): each has Section A, one scenario-based problem question from two, and Section B, two essays from a choice of three. (3) a-level-world-history-usa-practice L64 says each Section B question is worth 20 marks, within a 45-minute answer (L68). 9489 (2027-2029) Paper 2: 1 hour 45 minutes, 60 marks, two questions of 30 marks each, part (a) 10 and part (b) 20. (4) igcse-environmental-management-land-practice L119 sets Paper 1's 'shorter questions' against Paper 2's 'longer, scenario-based case study questions'. 0680 (2027-2029): both papers consist of short-answer and extended response questions based on source material, Paper 2 adding data processing and fieldwork, and recommended case studies have been removed. (5) o-level-urdu-first-language-paper-2-texts-revision-notes L144-145 speaks of the Part 2 choice 'between a ghazal-based and nazm-based question'. 3247 (2027) Part 2 is 'Two essay questions based on the prescribed set texts', with no such split. (6) igcse-global-perspectives-individual-report-practice Q3 (L39) asks for 'the five skills assessed throughout Global Perspectives' and the answer (L103) gives five without collaboration. 0457 (2025-2027) names the skills as research, analysis and evaluation, reflection, communication and collaboration, and the Written Exam assesses only research, analysis and evaluation. (7) cambridge-igcse-economics-microeconomic-decision-makers L30 calls Topic 3 the largest content strand. In 0455 (2026) Topic 3 has eight sub-topics and Topic 2 has eleven.

**Fix.** Say Extended is needed for grades A* and B; describe Papers 3 and 4 as a problem question plus two essays; give 30-mark questions with a 10/20 split and a timing that fits 1 hour 45 minutes for two; describe both 0680 papers from the syllabus; drop the ghazal/nazm split; ask for the syllabus's skills (or restate the question) and include collaboration; remove 'largest'.

## I369 - Seven Cambridge resources describe their syllabus loosely: an AO label, topic lists, attributed wording, unit numbers, paper roles and metadata [Inconsistency, Low]

**What is wrong.** (1) igcse-commerce-commercial-operations-practice L122 calls justification 'the AO3 application skill'; in 0715 (2028) AO2 is Application and AO3 Analysis and Evaluation. (2) cambridge-o-level-business-exam-preparation L57-58 lists the topics both papers examine as business activity, finance, marketing and people; 7115 (2026) has six topic areas including operations management and external influences, and Paper 1 draws on the entire syllabus. (3) a-level-law-criminal-law-practice answer 5 (L62) attributes a five-item list of sentencing aims to 'the syllabus'; 9084 2.3.3 reads 'Aims of sentencing: what sentences are trying to achieve' and names none. (4) a-level-cambridge-sociology-globalisation-media-religion-revision-notes L27-29 builds Paper 4's sections from 'Units 7-8, 9-10, 11-12' of 'twelve content units'; 9699 (2024-2026, version 3) has no numbered units. (5) a-level-biology-enzymes-revision-notes L89 says Paper 5 draws directly on this topic's practicals and does not mention Paper 3; in 9700 (2025-2027) AS Paper 3 is the laboratory practical and Paper 5 is a written planning paper whose context may lie outside the syllabus. (7) o-level-economics-basic-economic-problem-revision-notes teaches production possibility curves, which 2281 (2026) makes sub-topic 1.4, but its description and syllabusTopics list only 1.1-1.3.

**Fix.** Correct each description to the syllabus wording cited, and add 1.4 to the 2281 file's description and syllabusTopics.
