## E925 - Three 9UR0 Paper 3 resources say the Section A listening audio cannot be stopped or replayed; the specification gives students individual control of the recording [Confirmed error, High]

- **Files:** named in the observation below (not in the frozen baseline snapshot)
- **Where:** a-level-edexcel-urdu-listening-reading-writing; edexcel-a-level-urdu-listening-reading-writing-practice; edexcel-a-level-urdu-listening-reading-writing-revision-notes (main at 1755e30)

**What is wrong.** Pearson 9UR0 specification p. 29 (PDF p. 31), Section A: 'students will have individual control of the recording and may stop, revisit and replay sections of the recording as they wish'. a-level-edexcel-urdu-listening-reading-writing L93-97 advises staying with the current question because 'a listening exam continues regardless of whether you are ready for the next section, unlike a reading passage you can return to'. edexcel-a-level-urdu-listening-reading-writing-practice Q6 (L53) is built on that premise, and its answers say 'The listening audio continues regardless of whether the candidate is ready' (6(a), L82) and 'a listening exam does not pause or wait' (6(b), L83). edexcel-a-level-urdu-listening-reading-writing-revision-notes L49 ('the listening audio continues regardless'), L54 ('hope the passage replays, which is not always possible depending on the exam format') and the exam-trap line L69 ('the audio does not wait') repeat it. The strategy these files teach (never go back for a missed detail) is the opposite of what the paper allows. First raised as a D-247 observation.

**Fix.** State that in Section A students control the recording and may stop, revisit and replay it; rewrite the advice around managing the 45 recommended minutes when replaying, and rebuild practice Q6 and its answers.
