# Marlbridge audit — the sibling sweep, and the gap it found instead

*Repair verification, round two. The question was whether the worst failure this audit has
measured had recurred. It had not — and looking for it surfaced something else.*

---

## Q366: the failure did not recur

**E208** measured it directly, once: **ten of 78 siblings** still carried a corrected defect
*after* the finding was signed off, because remediation was scoped to the files the finding
happened to list rather than to the defect. Whether the current repair rounds repeated that
across 130 closures had never been tested.

**The exposure.** Of the 130 confirmed errors the decision log declares closed, **116 named
fewer files than their own family**. Those families hold **208 sibling files (109 distinct)**
that no finding named.

That number measures *room* for the failure. It is not the failure, and only reading
distinguishes the two.

**The reading.** Fourteen unnamed siblings, across four defects, three boards, and both the
practice and revision-notes resource types. **Not one still carries the defect.**

| defect | siblings read | result |
|---|---|---|
| invented topic numbering 1.1–1.4 (psychology, 2 boards) | 4 | never reached them — none uses sub-topic numbering at all |
| three civil tracks where there are four (law) | 3 | 2 don't discuss tracks; the third names all four, intermediate included |
| "five factors" where the syllabus names six (commerce) | 3 | none states a count; one asks for a stated two |
| nine-topic vs seven-topic structure (Cambridge 5014) | 4 | reached all of them — **and all were handled** |

The fourth is the interesting one. The defect *did* reach every sibling, and the repair was
scoped to the defect rather than the file: all six resources of that code, **including the
four no finding named**, now carry an explicit note that they follow the outgoing edition,
name the newer edition and its different structure, and tell the reader to check which
edition their course uses. That is the second of the two options the finding's own correction
offered, applied uniformly.

**Two cautions against my own screen**, both recorded in the finding:

- It grouped three commerce files into one family **across two different syllabus codes**
  (0715 and 7100). The family rule — same subject, same code, same topic — over-groups where
  a file serves more than one code.
- **116 is a count of opportunity that a hasty reader would take for a count of damage**, and
  it is the opposite of what reading found. The exposure list is a reading queue.

Fourteen of 109 is a sample, not a clearance. The finding says so.

## I355: the gap a reader would actually meet

This is what the sweep found while looking for something else, and it is the more consequential
of the two.

**All six resources this corpus holds for Cambridge O Level Environmental Management 5014
declare the superseded 2025–2026 series. Not one resource of that code targets the live
cycle.**

Everything about how this was handled is honest:

- Every file says it follows the outgoing edition, names the newer one, describes how the
  topic structure differs, records that the outgoing series is available for its final sitting
  in a single administrative territory, and tells the reader to confirm which edition applies.
- The repair round **disclosed the omission in its own commit message**, listing the full
  retarget as deferred. Nothing was concealed.

**The consequence stands anyway.** A candidate sitting the live cycle finds six resources for
the subject and every one of them is for a syllabus that does not apply to them, with no
companion to move to.

The sibling International GCSE code (0680) *does* have current-cycle coverage, which makes the
gap look narrower than it is. They are different qualifications; a 5014 candidate cannot
simply use 0680.

The same commit deferred a second omission of the same shape and larger: **a Global
Perspectives A-Level Component 4 resource missing entirely, covering half that qualification's
marks.**

**This is not a defect in any file.** Every statement in all six is true. It is an *absence* —
and absences do not surface in a findings record organised by file, which is why it took a
sweep aimed at something else to find it.

## What this changes about where the risk sits

Two rounds of repair verification now say the same thing from opposite directions. The
repair work is scoping correctly — E208's failure mode did not recur in this sample, and the
one regression found (E900) came from a reasoning error about evidence, not from sloppy scope.

What is left is a category this audit was not built to see. A findings record indexed by file
answers "what is wrong with this page?" It cannot answer "what is missing from the site?" Both
gaps here were found by accident, and both were disclosed by the repair instance rather than
discovered by the audit.

## I355 - Every resource for one qualification sits on a superseded cycle, correctly labelled, and no companion for the live cycle was ever published [Inconsistency, High]

- **Files:** `o-level-cambridge-environmental-management-rocks-and-minerals`, `o-level-environmental-management-energy-practice`
- **Where:** Syllabus series across all six resources of the code
- **Locate by:** `title: "Cambridge O-Level Environmental Management: Rocks and Minerals and Their Exploitation (5014)"`

**What is wrong.** Checking the sibling sweep turned up a gap of a different kind, and it is the one a reader of the site would actually meet. All six resources this corpus holds for one O Level code declare the superseded two-year series, and the ledger confirms that not one resource of that code targets the cycle now live. The labelling is honest: every file says it follows the outgoing edition, names the newer edition, describes how the topic structure differs, records that the outgoing series is available for its final sitting in a single administrative territory, and tells the reader to confirm which edition their course uses. The repair round that did this also recorded the omission in its own commit message rather than concealing it, listing the full retarget of these files as deferred, so nothing here was hidden. The consequence stands regardless. A candidate sitting the live cycle of this code finds six resources for the subject and every one of them is for a syllabus that does not apply to them, with no companion to move to. The sibling international GCSE code of the same subject does have resources on the current cycle, which makes the gap look narrower than it is, because the two codes are different qualifications and a candidate cannot simply use the other. The same commit deferred a second and larger omission of the same shape, a component resource for another qualification that is missing entirely and covers half that qualification marks. Note for correction: this is not a defect in any file. Every statement in all six is true. It is an absence, and absences do not surface in a findings record that is organised by file, which is why it took a sweep aimed at something else to find it.

**Fix.** Either publish companions on the live cycle for this code, or state on each resource and on the subject listing that the site does not currently cover the live cycle, so that a candidate can tell before reading rather than after. Then audit the corpus for other qualifications whose only coverage is a superseded series, since a file-organised findings record cannot surface them.

## Q366 - The sibling-scoping failure did not recur: fourteen files no finding named were read and not one still carried the defect [Questionable claim, Medium]

- **Files:** `o-level-environmental-management-energy-practice`, `igcse-oxfordaqa-psychology-cognition-and-behaviour`
- **Where:** Unnamed sibling files of the declared-closed findings
- **Locate by:** `title: "Cambridge O-Level Environmental Management: Energy and the Environment — Practice Questions (5014)"`

**What is wrong.** The most expensive failure this audit has measured is remediation scoped to the files a finding happened to name rather than to the defect itself, which was found once at a rate of ten in seventy-eight and left corrected errors standing in resources nobody had been pointed at. Whether the current repair rounds repeated it had not been tested, so it was tested. Of the hundred and thirty confirmed errors the decision log declares closed, a hundred and sixteen named fewer files than their own family, and those families contain two hundred and eight sibling files, a hundred and nine of them distinct, that no finding named. That number measures room for the failure and not the failure, and reading is the only thing that distinguishes the two. Fourteen of those unnamed siblings were read, chosen across four different defects, three boards and both the practice and the notes resource types. Not one of them still carries the defect. Three of the four defects turn out never to have reached the siblings at all: four psychology files use no sub-topic numbering of any kind, so the invented numbering scheme could not have travelled to them; two law files do not discuss the civil tracks and the third names all four correctly including the intermediate track created in October twenty twenty-three; and three commerce files give the six factors without a count or ask for a stated two. The fourth defect did reach every sibling and was handled at the level of the defect rather than the file: all six resources of that code carry the superseded topic structure and every one of them, including the four no finding named, now carries an explicit note that it follows the outgoing edition, names the newer edition and its different structure, and tells the reader to check which edition their course uses. That is the second of the two options the finding own correction offered, applied uniformly. Note for correction: two cautions about the screen that produced the exposure figure. It grouped three commerce files into one family across two different syllabus codes, so the family rule of same subject, same code and same topic over-groups where a file serves more than one code; and the figure of a hundred and sixteen is a count of opportunity that a hasty reader would take for a count of damage, which is the opposite of what reading found.

**Fix.** Treat the sibling-exposure list as a reading queue rather than a defect count, correct the family rule to split files that serve more than one syllabus code, and read a further sample from the remaining ninety-five unnamed siblings before concluding that the failure mode is gone rather than absent from this sample.
