## E922 - Seven resource families state specification content, tiering or grade eligibility that the AQA or Cambridge document contradicts [Confirmed error, Medium]

- **Files:** named in the observation below (not in the frozen baseline snapshot)
- **Where:** aqa-as-level-business-what-is-business-revision-notes, -practice; aqa-a-level-economics-aggregate-demand-supply; aqa-a-level-chemistry-bonding; aqa-gcse-chemistry-quantitative-chemistry; aqa-gcse-physics-exam-preparation; igcse-physics-motion-forces-and-energy, -thermal-physics, -waves; cambridge-igcse-economics-microeconomic-decision-makers (main at a23c2e9)

**What is wrong.** (1) AQA 7131 3.1.3 is 'How the external environment can affect costs and demand', with factors 'competition, market conditions, incomes, interest rates, demographic factors, environmental issues and fair trade'. aqa-as-level-business-what-is-business-revision-notes L28, L51, L88-89, self-test Q4 (L104) and its answer (L109), and aqa-as-level-business-what-is-business-practice Q3 (L37) with answer 'Economic conditions; costs and demand' (L74) give 'economic conditions' and 'costs and demand' as the topic's two named factors. The study guide (L100-103) is right. (2) AQA 7136 3.2.2 is 'How the macroeconomy works: the circular flow of income, aggregate demand/aggregate supply analysis and related concepts', with 3.2.2.1 circular flow of income, 3.2.2.3 determinants of AD (including the accelerator and savings), 3.2.2.4 AD and the level of economic activity (the multiplier and MPC) and 3.2.2.6 LRAS (including institutional structure and the Keynesian AS curve). aqa-a-level-economics-aggregate-demand-supply L15, L21, L25 and L38 retitle the section 'Aggregate demand and aggregate supply', and the coverage list (L40-49) omits the circular flow, the multiplier and MPC, the accelerator, savings and the Keynesian AS curve while describing 3.2.2.4 as AD shifts on output. (3) AQA 7405 3.1.3.1-3.1.3.7: aqa-a-level-chemistry-bonding's coverage list (L38-54) adds 'the electronegativity trend across a period and down a group' (3.1.3.6 defines electronegativity and polar bonds only) and solubility as a named property, and omits 3.1.3.4's four crystal structure types and six named crystals (diamond, graphite, ice, iodine, magnesium, sodium chloride), compound-ion formulas (3.1.3.1), molecules with polar bonds but no dipole (3.1.3.6) and hydrogen bonding in the density of ice (3.1.3.7). (4) AQA 8462: 4.3.2.4 'Limiting reactants (HT only)' is unlabelled in aqa-gcse-chemistry-quantitative-chemistry L56 and L131, while its neighbours carry Higher Tier labels; 4.3.3 Yield and atom economy is common to both tiers (only the theoretical-mass calculation and the choice of reaction pathway are HT only), but L15, L60, L62 and L132 label percentage yield and atom economy Higher Tier; and L15 calls the guide 'the full content of Topic 4.3' though 4.3.5 (volumes of gases, 24 dm3, chemistry only, HT only) is not covered (Q400.6). (5) AQA 8463 4.1.1.4: 'Students should be able to recall and apply both equations' (P = E/t, P = W/t), and Appendix A lists them among the recall equations, not the equation-sheet set; aqa-gcse-physics-exam-preparation L66-67 tells students power = energy transferred / time 'is on AQA's Physics equation sheet'. (6) Cambridge 0625 (2026-2028, p. 9): Core candidates are 'eligible for grades C to G' and Extended candidates 'for grades A* to G'. igcse-physics-motion-forces-and-energy L22-24, igcse-physics-thermal-physics L23-25 and igcse-physics-waves L22-24 say Supplement content is 'needed for grades A*-C'; a Core candidate can reach C, so Supplement is needed for A* to B. (7) Cambridge 0455 3.8.1 competitive markets: 'The effect of having a high number of firms on price, quality, choice, profit. Note: the theory of perfect and imperfect competition and diagrams are not required.' cambridge-igcse-economics-microeconomic-decision-makers lists 'the features of perfect competition and monopoly' as coverage (L51), teaches a perfect competition versus monopoly table (L99-115) and sets it in the checklist (L133).

**Fix.** Correct each statement to the specification wording cited, in the named file and every sibling that repeats it: (1) the 3.1.3 factor list; (2) the 3.2.2 title and its six sub-sections, or narrow the guide's stated scope to 3.2.2.2-3.2.2.6 without claiming the section; (3) the 3.1.3 coverage list; (4) HT label on limiting reactants, both-tier status for percentage yield and atom economy with the two HT-only parts marked, and 4.3.5 added or the 'full content' claim removed; (5) power as a recall equation; (6) Supplement needed for grades A* to B, Core eligible for C to G; (7) 3.8.1 as the effect of many firms on price, quality, choice and profit, with the perfect competition theory removed or flagged as beyond the syllabus.

## I371 - Six AQA resources misstate a weighting, a paper title or what the specification says [Inconsistency, Low]

- **Files:** named in the observation below (not in the frozen baseline snapshot)
- **Where:** aqa-a-level-mathematics-exam-preparation; aqa-gcse-business-exam-preparation; gcse-history-historic-environment, -revision-notes; aqa-gcse-mathematics-ratio-proportion-rates-of-change; aqa-a-level-english-literature-b-theory-and-independence-practice; a-level-aqa-sociology-education-practice (main at a23c2e9)

**What is wrong.** (1) AQA 7357 gives each paper '33⅓ %' (specification at a glance and the 4.2 table); aqa-a-level-mathematics-exam-preparation L18-20 gives Paper 3 as 33.34%. (2) AQA 8132 Paper 1 is 'Influences of operations and HRM on business activity'; aqa-gcse-business-exam-preparation L18-19 has 'Influences on operations and HRM' (the sibling business-operations guide is right). (3) AQA 8145 Part four (all four British depth studies): the historic environment question 'draw[s] on second order concepts of change, continuity, causation and/or consequence'. gcse-history-historic-environment L46-51 and gcse-history-historic-environment-revision-notes L56 list significance among the concepts the element assesses, and the revision notes' answer 1 (L130) adds 'with significance also commonly tested'; the guide's own L55-58 gives the four. (4) AQA 8300: 'All content can be assessed on any of the three question papers.' aqa-gcse-mathematics-ratio-proportion-rates-of-change L29-31 says ratio questions 'are not confined to a single paper the way some topics are'. (5) AQA 7717 3.3 requires texts that 'allow access to a range of critical views' and 'maximise opportunities for writing with reference to the AQA Critical anthology', each linked to a different anthology section; it does not say the lens must be chosen before the text. aqa-a-level-english-literature-b-theory-and-independence-practice Q7 (L62) and its answer (L85) attribute that ordering to the specification (the guide's own L141 gives it as advice, which is fine). (6) AQA 7192: the web specification contains no levels descriptors; a-level-aqa-sociology-education-practice L148-149 and L173-174 tell markers to use 'the levels descriptors in the specification' (Q400.2); L202 correctly calls them the published descriptors.

**Fix.** Use the specification's own figure or wording: 33⅓% for all three 7357 papers; 'Influences of'; the four concepts for the historic environment question; any 8300 content on any paper; present the lens-first order as advice; refer to AQA's published mark scheme levels.
