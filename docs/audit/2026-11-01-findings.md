## E924 - Three board-structure errors: 9UR0 Paper 3 mark split, YLA1 paper content and 9202 required practicals [Confirmed error, High]

- **Files:** named in the observation below (not in the frozen baseline snapshot)
- **Where:** a-level-edexcel-urdu-listening-reading-writing; edexcel-a-level-urdu-listening-reading-writing-practice; edexcel-ial-law-exam-preparation; oxfordaqa-igcse-chemistry-exam-preparation (main at 556975e)

**What is wrong.** (1) Pearson 9UR0 specification pp. 6 and 29: Paper 3 Section A (listening comprehension) is 30 marks and Section B (listening, reading and writing) is 30 marks, recommended 45 minutes and 1 hour 30 minutes. a-level-edexcel-urdu-listening-reading-writing L83-86 says Section B 'carries the majority of the paper's marks and time' and builds its revision advice on 'the paper's actual weighting'; edexcel-a-level-urdu-listening-reading-writing-practice Q5(a)-(b) answers (L79-80) say Section B carries 'the larger share of the paper's time and marks' and 'of both time and marks'. The time split is right; the marks are equal. (2) Pearson IAL Law specification Issue 4 pp. 6-7 and 22-29: Paper 1 (Underlying Principles of Law and the English Legal System) covers 1.1 the nature, purpose of and liability in law and 1.2 the sources of English law, its enforcement and administration; Paper 2 (The Law in Action) covers 2.1 The market (the law of contract, with remedies and defences), 2.2 The criminal offender and 2.3 The individual. edexcel-ial-law-exam-preparation L20-22 says Paper 1 covers 'the basics of the law of contract, and criminal law' and Paper 2 'the law of tort and applied, synoptic content'. The structure line (3 hours, 100 marks, 50%, June only) is right. (3) OxfordAQA 9202 specification: the required practicals in the content are electrolysis of copper sulfate solution (p. 20), flame tests for a metal ion (p. 22), titration of a strong acid with a strong base (p. 25), factors affecting rate (p. 27) and the test for a C=C double bond (p. 33). oxfordaqa-igcse-chemistry-exam-preparation L50-52 names the required practical work as 'filtration, crystallisation, chromatography, titration, gas collection'; only titration is a required practical.

**Fix.** (1) Give Paper 3 as 30 + 30 marks and base the practice-time advice on Section B's longer time and combined skills, not a larger mark share (both files, including the Q5 answers). (2) Rewrite the YLA1 paper-content sentence from the specification's section headings. (3) Replace the list with the five 9202 required practicals.

## I374 - Seven items: a core statement labelled Physics only, H431 synoptic components and theme labels, an H556 paper characterisation, a 9615 paper title, a 9725 misquotation and a 9690 statement overtaken by specimen papers [Inconsistency, Low]

- **Files:** named in the observation below (not in the frozen baseline snapshot)
- **Where:** edexcel-igcse-physics-waves-revision-notes; edexcel-igcse-physics-waves; ocr-a-level-business-exam-preparation; ocr-a-level-business-marketing-and-marketing-strategies; ocr-a-level-physics-exam-preparation; oxfordaqa-a-level-accounting-exam-preparation; oxfordaqa-a-level-business-exam-preparation; oxfordaqa-a-level-sociology-exam-preparation (main at 556975e)

**What is wrong.** (1) Pearson 4PH1 p. 24: 3.23 (sound waves are longitudinal and can be reflected and refracted) has no P reference, so it is also in the Science (Double Award); 3.24P-3.29P are Physics only. edexcel-igcse-physics-waves-revision-notes L93-95 and edexcel-igcse-physics-waves L163-166 open their 'Sound (Physics only)' sections with the 3.23 content. (2) OCR H431 specification p. 10 marks all three components '*' ('Indicates synoptic assessment'), p. 4 says 'Each component includes synoptic assessment' and the glance page says 'All components include synoptic assessment'. ocr-a-level-business-exam-preparation L21, L47, L101 and the self-test answer at L118 say Components 01 and 03 are the synoptic ones. (3) OCR H431 lists its content areas without numbers or 'Theme' labels (specification p. 10; glance page); ocr-a-level-business-marketing-and-marketing-strategies (description L15, L21, L25, L30-31, L134-135) calls them Theme 1, Theme 2 and Theme 3. (4) OCR's Physics A question-paper guide p. 7 describes Paper 1 (Modelling physics) as focusing on modules 3 and 5 and Paper 2 (Exploring physics) on modules 4 and 6; the H556 specification gives both components the same AO weightings (AO1 13-14%, AO2 15-16%, AO3 8-9%). ocr-a-level-physics-exam-preparation L30-34 presents the two papers as mathematical modelling versus experimental and investigative physics, and L47 and the self-test answer at L118 say practical skills are examined 'particularly on Exploring physics'. (5) OxfordAQA 9615 specification p. 8: AS Paper 2 is 'Financial & Management Accounting'; oxfordaqa-a-level-accounting-exam-preparation L20 calls it 'Financial Management and Accounting'. (6) The 9725 qualification page reads 'each A2 paper has separate topics and a simplified structure, with all questions based on case studies', and the summary of changes (p. 2) says all four sections of BSS3 and BSS4 are case-study based. oxfordaqa-a-level-business-exam-preparation L51 puts 'separate topics and a simplified, fully case-study-based structure' in quotation marks as the board's words (repeated unquoted at L37 and L110) and L53 tells candidates to expect 'a single extended case study'. (7) The 9690 qualification page (read 2026-09-16) links a specimen question paper and mark scheme for 9690/01. oxfordaqa-a-level-sociology-exam-preparation (published 2026-09-09; description L12, L20, L30-35, L84, L90, self-test Q1 at L98, L115) says no specimen assessment materials exist; the site's own oxfordaqa-a-level-sociology-exploring-sociology-revision-notes L78 and oxfordaqa-a-level-sociology-practice L25 refer to that specimen paper.

**Fix.** (1) Move 3.23 out of the Physics-only label in both files. (2) Say all three components include synoptic assessment. (3) Use OCR's content-area names without Theme numbers. (4) Describe the papers by their modules and drop 'particularly on Exploring physics'. (5) Use the specification's title. (6) Quote the page exactly and say the A2 papers are built on case studies throughout, not a single one. (7) Point to the Unit 1 specimen question paper and mark scheme and rewrite the sections built on their absence.
