## E923 - Seven IB resource families state content, criteria, marks or tier labels that the IB guide or brief contradicts [Confirmed error, Medium]

- **Files:** named in the observation below (not in the frozen baseline snapshot)
- **Where:** ib-dp-psychology-biological-approach (+ revision notes, practice); ib-dp-biology-theme-b-form-function (+ revision notes, practice); ib-dp-physics-exam-preparation; ib-dp-geography-global-climate (+ revision notes, practice); ib-dp-history-exam-preparation; ib-dp-computer-science-theme-a (+ revision notes); ib-dp-chemistry-reactivity-3-mechanisms (main at 3c6d6ed)

**What is wrong.** (1) Psychology guide (first assessment 2019, p. 23): the biological approach topics are 'the relationship between the brain and behaviour', 'hormones and pheromones and their effects on behaviour', 'the relationship between genetics and behaviour' (SL and HL) and 'the role of animal research in understanding human behaviour' (HL only), and AO3 questions are set from these headings. ib-dp-psychology-biological-approach L15 and L53-66 give three strands, physiology, neurochemistry and genetics, with no pheromones and no HL animal-research topic; the revision notes (L38-39, L45, L62, answer L129) and practice (answer 1, L66) repeat them. (2) Biology guide (2025) B3.1.2: 'Properties of gas-exchange surfaces: Include permeability, thin tissue layer, moisture and large surface area'; blood supply and ventilation belong to B3.1.3 (maintaining concentration gradients in animals). ib-dp-biology-theme-b-form-function L89-93, the revision notes (L78, L144) and practice 5(a) (L91) give large surface area, thin barrier, good blood supply and ventilation as the four shared features (Q400.15, Q400.16). (3) Physics guide (2025) p. 68: SL Paper 2 is 55 marks, not 50 (ib-dp-physics-exam-preparation L23); p. 76-77: the IA criteria are research design, data analysis, conclusion and evaluation (6 marks each), but L59-62 list theoretical framing, experimental design, data recording and processing, and evaluation, with no conclusion. (4) Geography guide (2019) p. 47-48: Global climate - vulnerability and resilience has three sub-topics, causes (energy balance, greenhouse effect, feedback loops), consequences, and responding (disparities in exposure and vulnerability; adaptation and mitigation). ib-dp-geography-global-climate's coverage (L38-52) lists only vulnerability, resilience, adaptation and mitigation, and presents exposure, sensitivity and adaptive capacity as the specification's named components of vulnerability (L45, L64-69; revision notes L37, L69, L100, L127; practice answer 2, L57), which the guide does not name. The guide (p. 73-74) also lists this unit among topics 'unlikely to be suitable for fieldwork investigation', but the guide L107-113 and revision notes L106-111 call it a viable fieldwork choice. (5) DP History subject brief (2028 course): Paper 1 sets three static questions, on the content of two sources against an inquiry question, on how a source's context shapes its use, and on synthesising perspectives. ib-dp-history-exam-preparation L26-28 and checklist L121-122 give the outgoing course's 'compare and contrast' and 'using the sources and your own knowledge' types; the sibling assessment revision notes (L63) already say those belong to the previous course. (6) Computer science guide (2027) A3.3 Database programming (SL and HL): A3.3.1 SQL data definition and manipulation language, A3.3.2 'Construct queries between two tables in SQL' with joins, filtering, pattern matching and ordering. ib-dp-computer-science-theme-a L59-61 and revision notes L57 say querying is covered 'conceptually, independent of any one query language'. (7) Chemistry guide (2025): standard electrode potentials and cell potential for spontaneity (Reactivity 3.2.12 onward), Lewis acid-base theory (Reactivity 3.4.6 onward) and coordination bonds in transition element complexes are additional higher level; ib-dp-chemistry-reactivity-3-mechanisms L50-53, L59-61 and L118-123 give them with no HL marker, although the file marks HL extensions elsewhere (Q400.18, Q400.19).

**Fix.** Correct each family to the document: (1) the three SL/HL topic headings including pheromones, and the HL animal-research topic; (2) the four B3.1.2 properties, with blood supply and ventilation placed under concentration gradients; (3) SL Paper 2 55 marks and the four IA criteria; (4) all three Unit 2 sub-topics in the coverage, the vulnerability framework given as the resource's own, and the fieldwork advice reversed or qualified by the guide's list; (5) the three 2028 Paper 1 question types; (6) SQL queries as required content; (7) HL markers on the AHL electrochemistry and Lewis acid-base content.

## I372 - Eight IB resources misdescribe a sub-topic, a form count, a tariff or a paper [Inconsistency, Low]

- **Files:** named in the observation below (not in the frozen baseline snapshot)
- **Where:** ib-dp-biology-theme-a-unity-diversity; ib-dp-psychology-biological-approach-practice; ib-dp-language-a-literature-readers-writers-texts (+ practice, revision notes); ib-dp-mathematics-ai-geometry-trigonometry (+ revision notes); ib-myp-individuals-societies-disciplines (+ revision notes); ib-dp-economics-exam-preparation; ib-dp-chemistry-exam-preparation; ib-dp-chemistry-structure-1 (+ revision notes) (main at 3c6d6ed)

**What is wrong.** (1) Biology guide A3.1 Diversity of organisms covers variation, species concepts, binomial naming, chromosome numbers, karyotypes and genome diversity; classification is A3.2 (HL). ib-dp-biology-theme-a-unity-diversity L95-101 describes A3.1 as 'the logic behind classification'. (2) Psychology guide p. 44: Paper 1 short-answer questions are 9 marks each and the essay is 22 marks; ib-dp-psychology-biological-approach-practice gives Q7 [9] and says (L82-86) it is 'written at Paper 1 essay length and mark value'. (3) Language A: literature guide p. 27: the prescribed reading list has four literary forms (SL three, HL all four), named on p. 31 as fiction, non-fiction, poetry and drama; ib-dp-language-a-literature-readers-writers-texts L117-118 speaks of 'the three forms this component covers', practice Q2 (L34) and answer 'Prose, poetry, and drama' (L63), and revision notes self-test 5 (L120). (4) Mathematics AI additional higher level Topic 3 is 3.7 radians, 3.8 circular functions, 3.9 matrix transformations, 3.10-3.13 vectors and 3.14-3.16 graph theory; ib-dp-mathematics-ai-geometry-trigonometry L26-27 and revision notes L24 say the HL increase is 'almost entirely HL-only vector work'. (5) The MYP individuals and societies brief's topic list ends 'measurements and trends, industrialization and technological developments'; ib-myp-individuals-societies-disciplines L93-99 and revision notes L102-107 present the list as verbatim but add 'ecological relationships'. (6) Economics guide p. 66-67: Paper 1 is one extended-response question in two parts (10 and 15 marks); Paper 2 is data response in seven parts. ib-dp-economics-exam-preparation L25-26 says both papers test definitions, diagram construction, short-answer application and extended evaluation (Q400.21). (7) Chemistry guide p. 73: Paper 1 is multiple-choice (1A) and data-based questions (1B); ib-dp-chemistry-exam-preparation L35, L41 and L113 put short-answer questions on Paper 1 (Q400.17). (8) Chemistry guide Structure 1.5.3: 'The molar volume of an ideal gas'; ib-dp-chemistry-structure-1 L65 and revision notes L91 list molar volume under 1.4 (Q400.20).

**Fix.** Reword each to the document: A3.1's actual content; Q7's mark value or the claim about essay length; four literary forms; the full AHL Topic 3 list; the brief's topic list without the added item; the two Economics papers described separately; Paper 1 without short-answer questions; molar volume under 1.5.
