## E928 - Three repairs introduced a wrong statement of the board document [Confirmed error, Medium]

- **Files:** named in the observation below (not in the frozen baseline snapshot)
- **Where:** a-level-mathematics-pure-mathematics-2; a-level-edexcel-economics-markets-in-action; ocr-a-level-economics-microeconomics (main at f378821)

**What is wrong.** (1) E263's repair, a-level-mathematics-pure-mathematics-2 L40-42: 'a candidate takes Paper 1 plus Paper 2 for the AS Level, or Paper 1 plus Paper 3 for the A Level'. Cambridge 9709 (2026-2027) p. 10: 'Candidates take two components for AS Level Mathematics. Candidates take four components for A Level Mathematics'; the AS routes also include Paper 1 with Paper 4 or Paper 5. (2) E315's repair, a-level-edexcel-economics-markets-in-action L58: cross-drawing 'applies only to Unit 4, whose questions may draw on any content covered in Units 1 to 3'. The Pearson International A Level Economics specification says of Unit 3 'Questions may draw on any content covered in Units 1 and 2' and of Unit 4 'Units 1, 2 and 3'. (3) E337's repair, ocr-a-level-economics-microeconomics L44-50, numbers the eleven sub-topics as 2.4 price determination and the interaction of markets, 2.5 the price mechanism, 2.6 elasticity of demand, 2.7 elasticity of supply, 2.8 the concept of the margin, 2.9 consumer and producer surplus, 2.10 market failure, externalities, information failure and public goods. OCR H460 (Version 3.0) has 2.4 Consumer and producer surplus, 2.5 The interaction of markets, 2.6 Elasticity (PED, YED, XED and PES), 2.7 The concept of the margin, 2.8 Market failure and externalities, 2.9 Information failure, 2.10 Public goods; there is no 'price mechanism' sub-topic.

**Fix.** (1) State the routes as the syllabus does: two components for AS Level, four for A Level. (2) Say Units 3 and 4 both draw on earlier units. (3) Copy the H460 numbering and titles.
