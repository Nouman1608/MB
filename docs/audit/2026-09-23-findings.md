# Marlbridge audit - correction audit, round one

*A new workstream. No resources were read, and no specification was opened.*

## Why this exists

Three rounds of absence re-verification re-checked 42 findings. **One was overturned. Twenty-four
carried a correction that would have introduced a new error if applied as written.** None of
those correction failures had anything to do with the truncation defect that prompted the
re-verification — they were corrections written wider than the evidence behind them.

That suggested the wrong thing was being audited. So this round takes a different population:
open confirmed errors, screened by signature rather than by source document.

**835 open findings carry a correction. 89 carry any warning about how to apply it.**

## What the first pass found

Twenty-seven open findings concern practice papers whose section structure doesn't match the
real paper. Their corrections divide into two remedies — *drop the lettered headings*, or
*rebuild to the real structure*. **These are opposites**, and which one is right depends on
whether the real paper has lettered sections.

Every one of those findings already establishes that fact in its own observation. The audit
offers the two remedies interchangeably anyway, several times as a free choice joined by "or".

**This has already caused harm, twice, and the audit recorded the damage without withdrawing
the cause:**

- **E335** records that an earlier remediation round removed the section headings from three
  papers "on the ground that they implied a structure the board does not set" — and states
  plainly that "the headings were right and the content under them was not, so that fix
  addressed the wrong half of the defect."
- **E494** records that an earlier round "deleted the real lettered sections from two business
  practice papers" on the same mistaken belief.

**Five resources were made worse by a correction of this form.** Both episodes were written up
as new confirmed errors rather than as reasons to stop prescribing it.

## The remedy is still live in six open findings that refute it themselves

| finding | what its own observation establishes | what its correction still offers |
|---|---|---|
| E335 | Sections A and B "are real"; Section A is 15 marks of multiple choice | "delete the two headings" |
| E391 | "Section A is fifteen marks of multiple choice in both components" | "or drop the lettered headings" |
| E625 | "Both papers of this specification have two compulsory sections" | "or drop the section headings" |
| E631 | "The specification states for both units that the paper is split into two sections" | "or drop the section labels" |
| E642 | "the syllabus states expressly that the paper contains two sections" | "or drop the section labels" |
| E367 | components 01 and 02 each define a Section A and a Section B | "or drop the section labels" |

In every case the fact that settles the choice is already in the finding. **No source document
is needed to fix this — what's needed is that the choice stop being offered.**

## Where removal *is* right

The same family contains the opposite defect, and those findings are equally explicit:

- **E357** — "The string Section A does not occur anywhere in the official guide… The lettered-section architecture belongs to the United Kingdom awarding bodies." Removal is correct.
- **E519** — the paper "has no sections: it prints six questions under content headings". Removal is correct.
- **E524** — the mirror case: "Every live paper of this specification is divided into two lettered sections… Neither practice paper has any sections at all." Rebuilding is correct.

E524 had already noticed the meta-pattern in passing — *"The section errors in this corpus are
therefore two opposite mistakes rather than one systematic misunderstanding"* — but that
observation was never carried back into the corrections.

**Nine of the twenty-seven have now been read closely enough to assign a remedy with
confidence. The other eighteen need the same one-line check**, which is a read of their own
observation, not of a specification.

## What this says about the other 826 corrections

The screen that surfaced this family flags **255 open confirmed errors** on at least one of
seven signatures generalised from the confirmed cases in rounds one to three: an off-syllabus
claim with no code attached, a tier established in the observation and dropped from the fix, an
enumeration the source doesn't itself make, deletion of material the source requires in another
form, an imported term, a single figure assumed where the source publishes two, and an
unchecked fallback option.

One caution on that number. **My first run of the screen reported 430, and 301 of those were a
defect in my own script** — a case-insensitive match on the abbreviation "AS" was firing on the
ordinary English word "as". That is fixed, and the 255 figure is post-fix, but it is a screen
and not a verdict: each flag still has to be checked individually, as these two were.

## Conventions

These two findings are about the audit, not about the resources. The located file in each is a
resource the finding concerns; the defect is in the instruction attached to it.

## Q346 - A correction this audit still prescribes has already been applied and made five resources worse [Questionable claim, High]

- **Files:** `ocr-gcse-biology-scaling-up-practice`, `ocr-gcse-business-activity-practice`, `igcse-oxfordaqa-accounting-recording-practice`, `edexcel-a-level-accounting-principles-practice`, `psychology-approaches-and-debates-practice`, `a-business-strategy-practice`
- **Where:** Recommended fixes across the section-structure family
- **Locate by:** `title: "OCR GCSE Biology: Scaling Up — Practice Questions"`

**What is wrong.** Twenty-seven open findings concern practice papers whose section structure does not match the real paper, and their corrections divide into two remedies: drop the lettered headings, or rebuild the paper to the real structure. The two are opposites, and which one is right depends on whether the real paper has lettered sections - a fact each finding's own observation already states. The audit nevertheless offers them interchangeably, several times as a free choice joined by the word or. This has already caused harm twice. One finding records that an earlier remediation round removed the headings from three papers of a board on the ground that the board does not set lettered sections, and states that the headings were right and the content beneath them was not, so the fix addressed the wrong half of the defect. A second finding records that an earlier round deleted the real lettered headings from two further papers on the same mistaken belief. Five resources were therefore made worse by a correction of this form, and both episodes were written up as new confirmed errors rather than as reasons to withdraw the remedy. The remedy is still live in at least six open findings whose own text refutes it: one offers deleting two headings that its own observation has just established are real; one offers dropping the lettered headings of a paper whose opening section it has just described as fifteen marks of multiple choice; three offer dropping the section labels of papers their observations describe as having two sections, one of them quoting the syllabus as stating expressly that the paper contains two; and one offers dropping the labels of a component whose two lettered sections the specification defines. Note for correction: the distinguishing fact is in every case already in the finding, so no source document is needed to choose; what is needed is that the choice stop being offered.

**Fix.** Withdraw the drop-the-labels option from every finding whose observation establishes that the real paper has lettered sections, and repair the five resources the earlier rounds damaged.

## Q347 - The section-structure findings are two opposite defects, and a corrector cannot tell them apart from the corrections alone [Questionable claim, Medium]

- **Files:** `a-level-sociology-methods-in-context-practice`, `ib-dp-economics-demand-supply-equilibrium-practice`, `aqa-gcse-sociology-families-education-practice`
- **Where:** Section-structure family, remedy selection
- **Locate by:** `title: "Methods in Context: Practice Questions"`

**What is wrong.** One finding in the corpus already names the pattern, observing that the section errors are two opposite mistakes rather than one systematic misunderstanding: some resources invent sections on papers that have none, and others omit sections on papers that do. That observation was made in passing and never applied to the corrections. Sorted by the fact each observation establishes, the family splits cleanly. Removal is the right remedy where the real paper genuinely has no lettered sections, and the findings that establish this say so plainly - one records that the string Section A occurs nowhere in the official guide and that the lettered architecture belongs to the United Kingdom awarding bodies, another that the paper prints six questions under content headings with no sections at all. Rebuilding is the right remedy where the real paper has sections and the resource has the wrong ones or none, and those findings likewise say so, one recording that every live paper of its specification is divided into two lettered sections each opening with two multiple-choice questions. A corrector working from the corrections rather than the observations has no way to tell which case they are in, because both remedies appear in both groups. Note for correction: this is a presentation defect in the audit, not a new defect in the resources; the findings are sound and their evidence is sufficient, and what is missing is that the remedy was never derived from the evidence the finding had already gathered.

**Fix.** Label each section-structure finding with which of the two remedies applies, taken from its own observation, before any of them is worked.

**Evidence.** Marlbridge audit evidence record, findings E287, E310, E335, E357, E367, E381, E390, E391, E424, E434, E494, E511, E519, E524, E618, E625, E631, E642 - https://github.com/Nouman1608/MB/tree/main/docs/audit
