## E946 - Cookie and Privacy Policy short versions still say, without qualification, that page views are recorded only after Accept (English, Arabic, Urdu, Bengali) [Confirmed error, Medium]

- **Files:** named in the observation below (not in the frozen baseline snapshot)
- **Where:** /legal/cookies/, /legal/privacy/, /ar/legal/cookies/, /ar/legal/privacy/, /ur/legal/cookies/, /ur/legal/privacy/, /bn/legal/cookies/, /bn/legal/privacy/

**What is wrong.** D-280 made analytics run by default, with no banner, for visitors outside the UK, EEA and Switzerland, and D-281 added that rule to the translations, but both left the opening sentences of each short version unchanged. The Cookie Policy still says 'Before you choose on the cookie banner, and if you click Reject, Google Analytics sets no cookies' and 'Page views are recorded only after you click Accept'; the Privacy Policy still says 'Page views are recorded only if you accept'. The same unqualified sentences stand in the ar, ur and bn versions (e.g. ur cookies: 'پیج ویوز صرف "قبول" پر کلک کرنے کے بعد ریکارڈ ہوتے ہیں'; ar privacy: 'لا تُسجَّل مشاهدات الصفحات إلا إذا وافقت'; bn privacy: 'পেজ ভিউ শুধুমাত্র আপনি গ্রহণ করলে রেকর্ড হয়'). For a visitor outside the UK and Europe, which is most of the site's traffic, both statements are false: headless Chromium with the Worker's attribute shows no banner, consent 'granted' and a page_view sent on load with nothing clicked. Each page then contradicts itself a few lines later, where the new regional paragraph says analytics 'runs as if you had clicked Accept'. The GA4 section headings were correctly qualified ('In the UK and Europe before you choose, or anywhere after you click Reject'); the short versions were missed. Eight pages, one repair.

**Fix.** Qualify the short-version sentences to match the GA4 sections, e.g. 'In the UK and Europe, before you choose on the cookie banner, and anywhere if you click Reject, Google Analytics sets no cookies...' and 'Page views are recorded after you click Accept, or outside the UK and Europe unless you click Reject.' Same change in the ar, ur and bn copy in src/i18n/pages/legal.ts.
