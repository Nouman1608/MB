# Marlbridge audit - reading instead of screening

*Correction audit, round five. No resources were read, and no specification was opened.
Both findings come from reading the audit's own findings one at a time.*

## Q357: the held set goes from nine to six to one

Three rounds ago this audit published a category - corrections unsafe in two independent
ways at once, because the tier they depend on may have been read backwards AND the
correction carries no tier forward. It held nine findings in that category. A measurement
of lexical collisions reduced it to six. Reading the six individually leaves **one**.

The other five carried no tier at all:

| finding | flagged as a tier | what it actually said |
|---|---|---|
| E168 | `core` | "the **ferrous core** in a solenoid" |
| E502 | `extended` | "character sets including **extended forms**" |
| E537 | `core` | "**core content** of the two lower qualifications" |
| E539 | `core` | "**core-content** questions in the section reserved for depth studies" |
| E891 | `core` | "a **core programme** requirement" |

Only the chemistry finding carries a genuine tier, and it states it as a difference between
two codes: **Supplement in 0620 and required in 5070**. That distinction makes the correction
**safe rather than unsafe** - the outcome is required of every candidate for the second code,
so adding the explanatory paragraph is right whatever the column check says about the first.
Only the tier *label* awaits verification.

**The category has no verified members.**

The detector that produced the six was written from the collisions already observed - argon
core, extended response - rather than from the vocabulary of the subjects in the corpus.
That is the same inductive error as the screen it was built to correct, one level up.

## Q358: the section family, and a correction to Q347

Q347 said the section-structure findings split into two opposite remedies and that a
corrector working from the corrections alone had no way to tell which case applied. Reading
all twenty-seven shows that **overstated it**.

- **Removal (6)** - the real paper has no lettered sections, and each finding says so
  plainly in its own observation.
- **Rebuild (17)** - the real paper has sections and the file invents an extra or has none.
  The dominant shape is an invented Section C above two real ones; delete the third, keep
  the two. Most corrections already say exactly that.
- **Split (2)** - the finding spans qualifications with opposite answers. One has the
  International GCSE paper with no sections and the A Level with four; the other has the
  A Level with none and the two lower-level papers with two.

Two corrections carry a weak option that is wrong but not damaging: renaming section
headings that already carry the board's own names.

**The genuinely unsafe corrections remain the six named earlier**, which offer dropping the
lettered headings of papers their own observations establish as having them. Nothing in this
reading adds to that list.

## The pattern across five rounds

| round | screened group | published size | size after reading |
|---|---|---|---|
| one | section-structure family | 27 | 27, of which 6 unsafe |
| two | UNSCOPED | 5 | 4 real, 1 model |
| three | TIER_BLIND | 86 | 68, then ~51 |
| four | unsafe twice over | 9 | 6 |
| five | unsafe twice over | 6 | **1, and it is safe** |

Every published group size has been wrong, and every correction has come from reading
individual findings rather than from a better screen. The screens are a reading list. They
are not a measurement, and the counts drawn from them should not be quoted as one.

## Q357 - Read individually, five of the six held findings are not tier findings and the sixth is safe to apply [Questionable claim, High]

- **Files:** `metal-properties-and-reactivity`
- **Where:** Correction to Q354 and Q356
- **Locate by:** `title: "Metal Properties and Reactivity"`

**What is wrong.** Q354 held nine corrections as unsafe twice over, on the grounds that each concerned a two-column tier and added content. Q356 reduced that to six after measuring lexical collisions in the tier vocabulary and said the remainder needed an individual read. Reading them leaves one. Five of the six carry no tier at all, in collisions the detector built for Q356 did not know: a ferrous core in a solenoid, character sets including extended forms, core content of two lower qualifications, core-content questions placed in a section reserved for depth studies, and a core programme requirement alongside extending understanding. Only the chemistry finding carries a genuine tier, and it states it carefully as a difference between two codes, Supplement in one syllabus and required in the other. That distinction makes its correction safe rather than unsafe: the outcome is required of every candidate for the second code, so adding the explanatory paragraph is right for that code whatever the first code turns out to say, and the only thing awaiting column verification is which tier label the paragraph should carry for the first. The category this audit published, corrections unsafe in two independent ways at once, therefore has no verified members. Note for correction: the detector that produced the six was written from the collisions already observed rather than from the vocabulary of the subjects in the corpus, which is the same inductive error as the screen it was built to correct, one level up.

**Fix.** Record the unsafe-twice-over category as empty, apply the chemistry correction with a tier label pending column verification, and return the other five to the ordinary working set.

## Q358 - Reading the section family through assigns a remedy to every one of the twenty-seven, and shows the difficulty was narrower than stated [Questionable claim, Medium]

- **Files:** `the-basic-economic-problem-practice`, `a-level-sociology-methods-in-context-practice`, `aqa-gcse-sociology-families-education-practice`
- **Where:** Remedy assignment across the section-structure family
- **Locate by:** `title: "The Basic Economic Problem: Practice Questions"`

**What is wrong.** An earlier finding of this audit said that the section-structure corrections divide into two opposite remedies and that a corrector working from the corrections alone could not tell which case applied. Reading all twenty-seven shows that overstated it. Six take removal because the real paper has no lettered sections at all, and each of those six says so in its own observation: one records that the string Section A occurs nowhere in the official guide, another that the paper prints its questions under content headings with no sections, another that no paper of either syllabus is divided that way. Seventeen take rebuilding, because the real paper does have sections and the file either invents an extra one or has none; the commonest shape by far is a file carrying an invented Section C above two real ones, and for those the remedy is to delete the third heading while keeping the two, which most of the corrections already say. Two take a split remedy because the finding covers files of different qualifications at once, one where the International GCSE paper has no sections while the A Level paper has four, and one where the A Level paper has none while the two lower-level papers have two. Two further corrections offer a weak alternative that is not dangerous but is wrong: renaming section headings that already carry the board's own names. Note for correction: the genuinely unsafe corrections remain the six recorded earlier, which offer dropping the lettered headings of papers their own observations establish as having them, and nothing found in this reading adds to that list.

**Fix.** Work the family from the assignment in this finding, treat the six named earlier as the only unsafe corrections, and do not rename headings that already carry the board's names.

**Evidence.** Marlbridge audit findings record, the twenty-seven section-structure findings read individually from outputs/marlbridge-audit.md - https://github.com/Nouman1608/MB/blob/main/docs/audit/2026-09-23-findings.md
