## E937 - Site pages state facts that the boards' own documents contradict [Confirmed error, Medium]

- **Files:** named in the observation below (not in the frozen baseline snapshot)
- **Where:** urdu-language.md; boards.ts; qualifications.ts; myp-sciences.md; understanding-mark-schemes-and-examiner-reports.md; grade-thresholds, exam-calendar and syllabus-updates pages (main at 00598db)

**What is wrong.** (1) src/content/subjects/urdu-language.md L12 lists 'Pearson Edexcel International Advanced Level Urdu'. Pearson's International A Level subject availability table has no Urdu, and the site's own matrix.ts L308-309 records the Pearson route as the UK GCE A Level Urdu 9UR0, 'not IAL'. (2) boards.ts L61 (rendered on /boards/oxfordaqa/) calls OxfordAQA 'the international-qualifications arm of AQA' that is 'run jointly with Oxford International Education Group'; L52 repeats 'its international-qualifications arm'. oxfordaqa.com About us: 'a partnership between Oxford University Press ... and AQA'. (3) qualifications.ts L71 (/levels/as-level/) says AS is 'distinct from the first year of a full A Level', that AQA is 'the only board recorded here with a genuinely separate AS route', and that Cambridge and OxfordAQA 'teach towards a combined AS & A Level qualification instead'; L61 (/levels/a-level/) says both examine a combined route 'rather than separately taught AS'. The 9709 syllabus: AS makes up the first half of the A Level and 'can also be delivered as a standalone qualification'. The OxfordAQA International AS and A-level Psychology specification: the International AS 'can be taken as a stand-alone qualification or it can count towards the International A-level'. L61 and L71 also disagree about Pearson Edexcel. (4) qualifications.ts L90 (/levels/ib-myp/) says the MYP has 'a single integrated "Sciences" subject rather than separate Biology/Chemistry/Physics'; myp-sciences.md L7 calls MYP Sciences 'an integrated science course' and L12 says it 'typically' is integrated. ibo.org, Science in the MYP: courses 'usually include biology, chemistry and physics'; integrated courses are one option. The site's own MYP Sciences topic record says discrete, modular or integrated. (5) understanding-mark-schemes-and-examiner-reports.md L29-31: alternatives in brackets 'are the only paraphrases that score. Elsewhere, the specific term is mandatory.' Cambridge's generic marking principles (9609 June 2024 mark schemes) say slashes or 'or' separate alternatives, and content in brackets 'is not required to earn the mark'. (6) grade-thresholds/index.astro L28: 'All marks are raw marks ... not weighted'. Cambridge's June 2026 tables head the syllabus column 'Maximum weighted mark' and derive syllabus thresholds from weighted component thresholds; the 0620 route 11, 31, 51 has component maxima 40 + 80 + 40 and a maximum weighted mark of 200. The threshold figures themselves match the tables. (7) exam-calendar/index.astro L107-108: entry deadlines are 'the same for every student in a given series'. Cambridge's November 2026 key dates give the final entries deadline as 16 August (International) and 21 September (UK), and the International document adds 21 September for retakes from June 2026. The page gives the International dates without saying so. (8) syllabus-updates/index.astro L137, over the Upcoming cards: 'students should keep following the existing specification until the dates below'. AQA 7137/7138 and OxfordAQA 9725 are for first teaching from September 2026, so learners starting now follow them; OxfordAQA 9292, 9690 and 9245 have no predecessor (assessments.ts notes say so); the IB Psychology course for first assessment 2027 is already taught. (9) The same cards give first assessment 2028 for OxfordAQA 9725 and 9690. Both specifications: 'For International AS exams May/June 2027 onwards', which the 9725 record's own component titles also give.

**Fix.** (1) Pearson Edexcel A Level Urdu (9UR0). (2) A partnership between Oxford University Press and AQA; drop Oxford International Education Group and 'arm' (L52 and L61). (3) Say AS is certificated on its own at Cambridge, OxfordAQA and AQA and, at Cambridge, OxfordAQA and Pearson, also counts towards the A Level; make L61 and L71 agree. (4) Say MYP Sciences is usually taught as biology, chemistry and physics, with integrated courses as an option. (5) State the Cambridge convention: slashes or 'or' separate alternatives; bracketed text is not required. (6) Say syllabus figures are weighted marks as Cambridge publishes them. (7) Say the entry deadlines are Cambridge's International ones and UK centres differ. (8) Replace with advice by date: new learners from September 2026 follow the new specification. (9) First AS assessment 2027 (A2 2028).

## E938 - Site pages tell readers things the site's own code or data contradicts [Confirmed error, Medium]

- **Files:** named in the observation below (not in the frozen baseline snapshot)
- **Where:** legal/cookies, legal/privacy, practice, subject hub and programme templates; syllabus-updates; gulf; uk; pakistan; about; legal/editorial-policy; contact; pricing; computer-science.md; geography.md; ar/ur/bn copy (main at 00598db)

**What is wrong.** (1) Analytics. cookies.astro L25-28 and L35-36 ('no analytics request is sent until you click Accept'), privacy.astro L56-57 ('no analytics data is collected before you accept'), practice/index.astro L95 ('nothing is sent anywhere') and practice/[code]/index.astro L236-237 ('Nothing is sent to Marlbridge or anyone else'). ConsentAnalytics.astro loads gtag.js from googletagmanager.com on every page and runs gtag('config') with consent defaulted to denied (L97-105, L134). Google's consent mode overview calls this advanced consent mode: 'While consent is denied, the Google tags send measurements without cookies.' Events are passed to gtag whatever the choice: WhatsAppButton, EnquiryForm, CorrectionForm and TrialFunnelEvents call gtag('event') directly, and practice pages send question_answered (with is_correct) and quiz_complete (with score_percent) through mbTrack. (2) The subject hub template [subject].astro L199-206 prints 'Topics 1-22' (AS) and 'Topics 23-37' (A Level), written for 9701, on every hub with staged topics. 34 syllabus records have stages and only 9701 has those ranges: 9702 is AS 1-11, A 12-25; 9700 1-11, 12-19; 9618 1-12, 13-20. The note 'assessed on the AS papers' also shows on linear AQA A-level hubs such as 7402 and 7405. (3) syllabus-updates L242-248 adds SL and HL components for DP Psychology: '7 components, 232 marks, 8h' (2019) and '218 marks, 7.8h' (2027). The site's records give 2019 SL 3 components, 93 marks, and HL 4 components, 139 marks; the IB guide for first assessment 2027 gives SL Papers 1 and 2 (35 marks each) and the internal assessment (24), with HL adding Paper 3 (30). The component lists repeat 'Paper 1' without SL or HL. L207-210 says the figures are 'computed directly from each board's own published component tables' and L281-282 that every entry is 'sourced directly' from the board, but the 2019 record (assessments.ts L3403-3404) takes Paper 1 and Paper 3 marks from papacambridge.com mirrors and the internal assessment breakdown from third-party resources. The IB's 2019 subject brief gives durations and weightings (they match) but no marks. (4) The programme template [slug].astro (L125, L95, L183) shows 'Groups capped at 15 - one-to-one also available', the group and one-to-one fee panel and the group tuition FAQs on every teaching programme. That includes IB (ib.md L20 and pricing.ts L78: one-to-one only), and Academic Support and IELTS (pricing.ts L25-27: no fixed per-subject fee, route to an enquiry; academic-support.md is headed 'One-to-One Academic Support'). (5) gulf/index.astro L111-113, and uk/index.astro L59 and L159-161, say the regional rates are currency conversions of the Pakistan rate. The group rates shown come from REGION_PRICING, which pricing.ts L88-100 says is set independently; only the one-to-one rows are conversions. The UK group fees of 60 and 75 GBP compare with a conversion of about 50 and 63 GBP. (6) computer-science.md L12 says Cambridge's codes are 'still being confirmed' and L16 that 'Study material has not yet been published', but matrix.ts has 0478, 2210 and 9618 and 59 Computer Science resources are published, 27 of them Cambridge. geography.md L16 and L18 answer 'Cambridge' only, while the page renders OxfordAQA 9230, 9635 and IB DP Geography, with 24 resources. (7) syllabus-updates L126 promises 'every specification currently mid-transition' ('8 are in transition today'), but leaves out records marked current that are mid-transition. These are AQA 7132 (final 2027, replaced by 7138) and 7131 (final AS 2026); OxfordAQA 9625, whose record says to reclassify it once 9725 teaching starts in September 2026; OCR H436, first assessment 2028, while AQA 7138 with the same dates is listed as upcoming; and DP Psychology (2019). (8) Fee basis. contact/index.astro L22 and pricing/index.astro L19 and L67 ('Every fee below is per subject, per month') leave out that one-to-one and IB are charged per class, which the same pricing page shows at L102-104; the ar, ur and bn contact FAQs and pricing leads say the same. The ar, ur and bn tutoring pages (marketing.ts L150, L154, L158) give per subject per month with the 20% and 10% discounts, without saying the discounts apply to group classes only (tuition.ts L291, L300). The ar, ur and bn landing pages (copy.ts L93, L117, L141) promise an email reply within one working day, where pricing.ts L192-196 says two. (9) pakistan/index.astro L41: Learners Academy 'delivers every board and qualification Marlbridge publishes here, in group and one-to-one formats'. boards.ts L69 says OCR is 'Not offered by Learners Academy directly', IB is one-to-one only, and the contact page says SAT is not yet offered. (10) Authorship. about/index.astro L206 and editorial-policy.astro L33-36 and L119-123 say most resources carry a named author and a minority carry the Academic Team byline; in fact 1,250 of 1,645 (76%) carry marlbridge-academic-team. Editorial policy L43-45 says each resource is checked 'by its named author'. L111-112 says practice questions are written by the Academic Team, yet 138 practice-question resources credit named teachers. L50-52 says the second-reviewer credit is complete 'for a small number of resources'; no resource names a reviewer, and the only reviewed item is one article, as the ar, ur and bn policies say.

**Fix.** (1) Either load gtag.js only after Accept (basic consent mode) and route every event through a consent check, or rewrite the cookie, privacy and practice statements to describe cookieless measurement before consent. (2) Build the AS and A Level notes from each record's own topic numbers; do not show the AS note for linear specifications. (3) Show SL and HL totals separately and label components by tier; describe the 2019 marks' sourcing as it is, or remove the 2019 mark totals. (4) Show the group panel and group FAQs only for programmes with group tuition, and the enquiry route for IELTS and Academic Support. (5) Say group rates are set for each region and one-to-one rates are conversions. (6) Remove the stale FAQ answers and list the boards the hub renders. (7) Reclassify 7131, 7132, 9625, H436 and the IB pair, or reword the lead. (8) State both bases (group per subject per month; one-to-one and IB per class) and that the discounts are group-only; give the two-working-day email window in all languages. (9) Name the exceptions (OCR, IB one-to-one only, SAT). (10) Make the authorship and review statements match the bylines and review records.
