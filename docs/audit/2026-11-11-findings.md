## I386 - Four leads confirmed against the board documents [Inconsistency, Low]

- **Files:** named in the observation below (not in the frozen baseline snapshot)
- **Where:** aqa-a-level-physics-exam-preparation; aqa-as-level-business-what-is-business-revision-notes and practice; ocr-a-level-physics-exam-preparation; ib-dp-physics-exam-preparation; oxfordaqa-igcse-pakistan-studies-exam-preparation (main at d04ba3c)

**What is wrong.** (1) aqa-a-level-physics-exam-preparation L62-68 takes a micrometer's absolute uncertainty as 'half the smallest division: 0.01 / 2 = 0.005 mm'. AQA's Required practical handbook (Version 2.2, January 2026) lists the micrometer under 'Measurement (two judgements required)' and states 'The uncertainty of a measurement (two judgements) is at least ±1 of the smallest scale reading', that is ±0.01 mm. (2) aqa-as-level-business-what-is-business-revision-notes L55-57 teaches SMART objectives and says '"Grow" = a purpose, not an objective' (also L98, L107 and the practice file). The AQA 7131/7132 specification (Version 2.0) does not name SMART, and 3.1.1 lists 'Business objectives such as profit, growth, survival'. (3) ocr-a-level-physics-exam-preparation L40-43 contrasts Unified physics, 'a synoptic paper', with 'a module-scoped one'. H556 (Version 3.0): 'All components include synoptic assessment.' (4) ib-dp-physics-exam-preparation L31-33 says Paper 1's 'question types are mixed together'; the Physics guide (first assessment 2025) p. 68 sets Paper 1 as 'Paper 1A-Multiple-choice questions' and 'Paper 1B-Data-based questions'. Also oxfordaqa-igcse-pakistan-studies-exam-preparation L123 links the 2024/03 specification, which is labelled Version 1.2, beside a Version 1.9 citation.

**Fix.** (1) Use ±0.01 mm, as the handbook does. (2) Label SMART as a framework beyond the specification and drop the claim that growth is not an objective. (3) Say all H556 components are synoptic and Paper 3 draws on all modules. (4) Describe Papers 1A and 1B as separate sections. Update the 9236 link.
