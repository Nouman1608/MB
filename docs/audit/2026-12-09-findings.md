**E949 — closed.** `editor` is gone from the Article schema and from both templates. Built from `ffac190`, **0** of 3,082 pages carry a JSON-LD `editor` property, against 1,988 at `e20e5cc`. Nothing was substituted: `reviewedBy` is also 0, and `article.ts` now carries the instruction not to add `editor`, `reviewedBy`, `contributor` or any other person property, which is the right place for it — the next person to want a "reviewed by" signal in structured data will read that comment before writing the code.

The guard is real. **[4]** in `audit-review-coverage.mjs`, inside `audit:all`, rejects any JSON-LD carrying `editor` or `reviewedBy`. I did not rely on the negative suite: injecting `"editor":{"@type":"Person","name":"Test Person"}` into one built page's JSON-LD made the audit exit 1 with the specific `[4]` diagnostic naming that file, and restoring the file returned it to 0.

All four comment blocks were rewritten, and accurately. `reviewedDate` now reads "The date the `reviewer` credit was applied to this page (for most pages, 28 or 29 Sep 2026). Not a record that a review took place on that date" — which is exactly what the field holds. `reviewStatus` and `reviewedByTeachers` now point at D-379 and D-380 rather than at the superseded system, and the provenance comment in `resources/[slug].astro` no longer calls the byline a "genuine named-reviewer" credit.

**I415 — closed, and the "nothing else changed" claim is verified rather than accepted.** Both matchers now import one rule from `src/utils/content/subject-match.mjs`: split the profile entry on "/", then compare word sets ignoring case, punctuation and "Studies".

Two independent checks:

- **Exhaustive matcher comparison.** I ran the old `subject-teachers.ts` rule and the new shared rule over every pair of the 58 subject display titles and the 23 teacher profiles — 1,334 pairs. Exactly **3** outcomes change: Ameer Hamza, Iftikhar Azeemi and Nouman Ahmed now match "Sciences (MYP)". Nothing else moves in either direction.
- **Before-and-after builds.** Comparing the teacher block on every built page at `e20e5cc` and at `ffac190`: those same three are added on the 34 MYP Sciences resource pages each, and all three now appear on the MYP Sciences hub. No teacher is added or removed anywhere else, and no other page gains or loses a block. Pages carrying a teacher block go from 1,959 to 1,993, the 34 MYP Sciences pages being the difference.

So D-380 does what D-379 recorded but did not achieve, and the owner's answer of 1 October ("Show the three") now supersedes D-340's "MYP Sciences: none" in fact as well as in the log.

**One improvement worth recording, because it was not asked for.** The shared rule drops only "Studies" as a filler word. Rule [10] previously also dropped "and", "the" and "of", which made "Language A: Literature" and "Language A: Language and Literature" indistinguishable — two different IB subjects that a reviewer credit must not confuse. The new rule separates them: `subjectCovered(['Language A: Literature'], 'language-a-language-and-literature')` is false. Nine edge cases were run against the shared rule and all nine behave correctly, including the "Business Studies" covers `business` and "English Literature" does not cover `english` pair that the original finding turned on. No credit in the current data depended on the looser reading — `validate-review-integrity` passes across 2,311 resources and 4 articles under the tighter rule — so this closed a latent hole rather than fixing a live error.
