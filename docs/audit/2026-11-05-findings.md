## I378 - IB Computer science Theme A now labels the TCP/IP layers HL only, but A2.1.3 (SL and HL) requires mapping network devices to those layers [Inconsistency, Low]

- **Files:** named in the observation below (not in the frozen baseline snapshot)
- **Where:** ib-dp-computer-science-theme-a (+ revision notes) (main at 6a9c629)

**What is wrong.** IB Computer science guide (first assessment 2027): A2.1.3 'Describe the function of network devices', for both levels, lists 'Gateways, hardware firewalls, modems, network interface cards, routers, switches, wireless access points' and 'How devices map to the layers of the TCP/IP model'; A2.1.5 (HL only) is the function of the model. ib-dp-computer-science-theme-a L50-56 and its revision notes L46-49 now present the TCP/IP model's four layers only under 'HL only (A2.1.5)', and neither file mentions A2.1.3's network devices. An SL student would conclude that the layers are not required. Round 27's I376 did not cite A2.1.3; this corrects it.

**Fix.** Add A2.1.3 (network devices and how they map to the TCP/IP layers) for both levels, and keep only the function of the model and the interaction between layers as HL only.

## I379 - Eleven leads from Q399 and Q400 confirmed against the board documents or by the files' own arithmetic [Inconsistency, Low]

- **Files:** named in the observation below (not in the frozen baseline snapshot)
- **Where:** cambridge-o-level-islamiyat-major-themes-of-the-quran; aqa-a-level-accounting-double-entry-model; aqa-a-level-mathematics-exam-preparation; igcse-sociology-family-education-crime-practice; igcse-mathematics-statistics; igcse-biology-human-nutrition; igcse-biology-organisation-revision-notes; ib-dp-language-a-literature-exam-preparation; ib-myp-design-criteria-in-practice; ocr-gcse-biology-exam-preparation; ocr-gcse-chemistry-exam-preparation (main at 6a9c629)

**What is wrong.** (1) Cambridge 2058: the 2024-2025 and 2026-2027 syllabuses set the same fifteen passages and the same Question 1; cambridge-o-level-islamiyat-major-themes-of-the-quran L10 ('For examination in 2024-2025'), L26-27 ('follows the 2024-2025 syllabus, which has since closed') and L67 label the file with the closed edition. (2) aqa-a-level-accounting-double-entry-model L104-117: with cost £20,000, residual £2,000 and a 4-year life, a 25% reducing-balance rate leaves £6,328 after year 4 (the rate that reaches the residual is about 43.8%), so the conclusion that reducing balance 'leaves a higher closing net book value in later years' follows from the chosen rate, not the method, which front-loads the charge. (3) aqa-a-level-mathematics-exam-preparation L56 and self-test 4 with its answer (L111-112, L117-120): (1 - cos 2x)/sin 2x = tan x needs sin 2x ≠ 0, that is sin x ≠ 0 and cos x ≠ 0; the question and answer give sin x ≠ 0 as the full condition. (4) Cambridge 0495 4.3: ageing population is a demographic factor (4.3.1), while boomerang children are listed under changing roles of children (4.3.5). igcse-sociology-family-education-crime-practice Q4 (L40, 'three ways an ageing population is changing family life') credits 'falling birth rates combined with rising life expectancy' (the cause) and 'boomerang children' (L66) as ways. (5) Cambridge 0580 gives weightings for assessment objectives and papers only; igcse-mathematics-statistics L34-36 says Statistics carries 'equal weight in the exam to any other topic'. (6) Cambridge 0610 p. 9: Core candidates take Papers 1 and 3, 'based on the Core subject content only'; villi structure, microvilli and lacteals are Supplement (7.x, p. 21). igcse-biology-human-nutrition L183-188 says villus labelling questions 'appear on both Core and Extended papers'. (7) igcse-biology-organisation-revision-notes L69-70 defines an organism as 'built from one or more organ systems'; 0610 classifies prokaryotes and protoctists as organisms (1.2), which have no organ systems. (8) IB Language A: literature guide pp. 40-42: the individual oral is 'a prepared response of 10 minutes, followed by 5 minutes of questions'; ib-dp-language-a-literature-exam-preparation L68-87 contrasts a 10-minute weak plan with a stronger plan of 5 + 5 minutes plus a closing comparison. (9) MYP design brief: on-screen examinations are taken in language and literature, individuals and societies, sciences, mathematics and interdisciplinary learning, and an ePortfolio in language acquisition and one of design, arts or physical and health education; ib-myp-design-criteria-in-practice L32-37 says 'MYP subjects are graded against four equally weighted criteria ... assessed through an ePortfolio rather than a timed written paper'. (10) OCR J247 specification: no digestion content (the only digestive enzyme is amylase in a B1.2 practical), yet ocr-gcse-biology-exam-preparation L29-33 says cell structure, transport and enzymes underpin 'the digestion, ecology and inheritance content of the later topics'. (11) OCR J248 specification p. 14: C3.2 Energetics is in Topic C3 (Paper 1/3); ocr-gcse-chemistry-exam-preparation L35-40 lists energetics with rates and organic chemistry as topics 4-6 content on the second paper.

**Fix.** Relabel (1) to 2026-2027; qualify (2)'s conclusion or use a rate that reaches the residual; add cos x ≠ 0 in (3); rebuild (4)'s answer from 4.3.1 effects; remove (5)'s weighting claim; limit (6) to Extended papers; define (7) to include unicellular organisms; fit (8)'s stronger plan to 10 minutes; confine (9) to design and the other ePortfolio subjects; remove digestion from (10); move energetics to the first paper in (11).
