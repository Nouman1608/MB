## Q390 - D-234 moves the nine Cambridge IGCSE Accounting resources to the 2027 to 2029 syllabus, and every syllabus claim it makes is correct [Questionable claim, Medium]

- **Files:** `igcse-accounting-sources-and-recording-of-data`
- **Where:** The nine 0452 resources and the three data-layer files D-234 changes, read on main at 406ff00 (D-234 is a461135)
- **Locate by:** `title: "IGCSE Accounting: Sources and Recording of Data (Cambridge 0452)"`

**What is wrong.** D-234 is an owner item, not a repair: it closes no finding. It was verified because it changes nine resources and the data layer. Both official documents were read in Chrome from the 0452 qualification page by same-origin fetch and pdf.js, no challenge appeared, and each was read to its closing contact block: the syllabus for 2027, 2028 and 2029 (718141, Version 1, 30 pages, September 2024) and the syllabus for 2026 (697149, Version 2, 28 pages). Every checked claim holds. The 2027 to 2029 content overview gives Topic 7 as Accounting concepts and modern practice with 7.2 Ethical considerations and 7.3 Technology and sustainability, 4.4 as Irrecoverable debts and allowance for irrecoverable debts, 5.4 Manufacturing accounts and 5.5 Clubs and societies, and 6.3 Inter-business comparison, and its changes page names 7.2, 7.3 and the Topic 7 title as new. The assessment overview gives Paper 1 as 1 hour 30 minutes and 40 marks and Paper 2 as 1 hour 45 minutes, 100 marks and five compulsory questions. The data layer's 3.2 "Corrections of errors" follows the subject-content heading; the content overview itself says "Correction of errors", so either wording is the document's own. The Topic 2 and 3 additions match the 2027 subject content: ledger accounts presented in three-column running balance format and kept digitally; invoice, debit note, credit note, cheque counterfoil, paying-in slip, receipt, bank statement and statement of account, produced manually or digitally; the seven books of prime entry; receipts and payments by cash, cheque, debit and credit card, online and bank transfer; the purpose of the imprest system; the benefits and limitations of keeping cash at the business property and of manual and digital original entry; the six error types under 3.1; cash book updates without dividends; uncredited deposits and bank errors; the impact of digital transactions on bank reconciliation and on control accounts; and the note that control account balances need not be reconciled with the ledgers. The 2026 differences the edition notes state are also in the 2026 document: Paper 1 at 1 hour 15 minutes and 35 marks, Topic 7 as Accounting principles and policies, the provision for doubtful debts wording, 5.4 Clubs and societies and 5.5 Manufacturing accounts, Inter-firm comparison, income statements, the express statement that three-column running-balance accounts are not required, completing pro-forma documents, the advantage of books of prime entry, and dividends among the cash book updates. The resource changes are internally sound. The imprest correction is right: the amount to restore a float is the total of the vouchers, so a $200 float with $148 of vouchers needs $148, not the $52 the earlier answer gave. The running balance example runs 300, 750, 250 and 200, all Dr. The new questions (sources practice Q10 [4] and Q11 [2+2], verification practice Q11 [2+2]) have schemes that sum to their tariffs. No finding in either array is open against these files; E619, which recorded the pinning to the single-year 2026 syllabus, was already declared closed. Topics 4 to 7 have no 0452 resources, which D-234 states as the owner's scope choice.

**Fix.** None for D-234. The 110 confirmed errors open after round 12 are unaffected; none is in these files.
