# Marlbridge audit - the unscoped corrections, and a drift guard

*Correction audit, round two. No resources were read.*

## Q352: four corrections would delete content another board requires

U69 recorded the hazard: a finding that says a term is off-syllabus, rather than off-syllabus
**for a named code**, produces a correction that can cross a board boundary and delete
required content. The screen flags five open confirmed errors on that signature. **Four are
live, and two can be proved outright** — because this audit has already read the other board's
specification end to end and found the term required there.

| the finding says | the correction says | but this audit has read |
|---|---|---|
| OCR J205 GCSE Economics contains no occurrence of public good, merit good or demerit good | "Remove the market failure block" | **Cambridge 2281** lists *public good, merit good, demerit good* by name as the key terms of its own market-failure sub-topic |
| AQA 8132 GCSE Business never uses "added value" | "Remove the added value material" | **OCR H431** requires learners to *explain what is meant by added value, explain how it can be calculated, calculate it,* and *evaluate its usefulness* |

**This corpus holds resources for both of those syllabuses.** A corrector sweeping on either
instruction would strip required content out of them.

The other two are the same pattern without a read to prove them:

- Remove quadratic inequalities from an O Level mathematics family — where quadratic
  inequalities are required at AS and A Level.
- Remove perfect competition, public goods, merit and demerit goods, consumer surplus and
  income elasticity from an International GCSE economics family — where **the finding itself
  records** that the same board teaches all of it at AS and A Level, and that the site's own
  AS resources place it correctly.

In every case the finding's observation names the code and the correction does not. **The
repair is to carry the code down into the fix, not to reinvestigate the finding.**

## The fifth is a false positive, and it's the model

`o-level-environmental-management-rocks-practice` was flagged by the screen but is the
best-written correction in the set. Its observation distinguishes the outgoing series from the
current cycle, records that the wording in one is *illustrative rather than restrictive*, and
states in terms that calling the material off-syllabus **overstates what the wording
establishes**. It then narrows its own correction accordingly.

That is what a scoped correction looks like. Worth using as the pattern.

## Q353: two provenance defects, one historical and one live

**The stale snapshot behind Q350 was identifiable all along.** `repository-live` is a git
checkout holding a readable commit — `07287def`, 7 September — three days older than the
repair it missed. One line would have shown that before anything was claimed from it. A
provenance script now reports the commit, or the absence of one, for every snapshot the audit
holds:

| snapshot | md files | newest file | git HEAD |
|---|---|---|---|
| `repository` (frozen baseline) | 1,353 | 2026-09-05 | not a checkout — commit of record `ee1b341f` |
| `repository-current-files` | 1,360 | 2026-09-05 | `6f6bd5e6` |
| `repository-live` | 1,685 | 2026-09-07 | `07287def` |

**The second defect is live.** The triage that screens open findings for unsafe corrections is
computed from a fixed evidence file, and repair work is proceeding in parallel. Of its 255
flagged findings, **five were closed by D-197/198/199 within half an hour of its generation**,
and a sixth was deferred — all in one subject cluster. Two percent now, and it will grow. A
corrector working an old triage would be auditing corrections to findings that no longer
exist.

Worth noting in the other direction too: D-197's own commit message records finding a
"previously unannounced 2026-09-23 correction-audit commit" via drift-check. The coordination
gap runs both ways.

**Reconcile the triage against the decision log before working it.**

## Q352 - Four corrections tell a corrector to delete content that another board in this same corpus requires [Questionable claim, High]

- **Files:** `ocr-gcse-economics-introduction-revision-notes`, `gcse-business-real-world-revision-notes`, `inequalities-revision-notes`, `igcse-oxfordaqa-economics-markets-revision-notes`
- **Where:** Corrections that name no code
- **Locate by:** `title: "OCR GCSE Economics: Introduction to Economics — Revision Notes"`

**What is wrong.** Five open confirmed errors carry a correction that instructs removal of material as off-syllabus without naming the code it is off-syllabus for. Four are live hazards, and for two the contradiction is already proved inside this audit. One finding establishes that a GCSE economics specification contains no occurrence of public good, merit good or demerit good and tells a corrector to remove the market-failure block; the O Level economics syllabus read end to end in an earlier round lists public good, merit good and demerit good by name as the key terms of its own market-failure sub-topic, and this corpus holds resources for that syllabus. Another finding establishes that a GCSE business specification never uses added value and tells a corrector to remove the added-value material; the A Level business specification read end to end in the same round requires learners to explain what is meant by added value, explain how it can be calculated, calculate it and evaluate its usefulness, and this corpus holds resources for that specification too. The other two are hazards on the same pattern without a read to prove them: a correction to remove quadratic inequalities from an O Level mathematics family, where quadratic inequalities are required at AS and A Level, and a correction to remove perfect competition, public goods, merit and demerit goods, consumer surplus and income elasticity from an International GCSE economics family, where the finding itself records that the same board teaches all of it at AS and A Level and that the site's own AS resources place it correctly. Note for correction: in every case the finding's observation names the code and the correction does not, so the repair is to carry the code down into the fix, not to reinvestigate the finding.

**Fix.** Rewrite each of the four corrections to name the code the material is off-syllabus for, and check the code of any file before applying an absence correction to it.

**Evidence.** Cambridge O Level Economics 2281 syllabus for 2026, version 2, published December 2025, read end to end in the second re-verification round, sub-topic 2.10 Market failure - https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/697295-2026-syllabus.pdf
**Evidence.** Cambridge OCR Level 3 Advanced GCE in Business H431 specification, version 2.1, January 2026, read end to end in the second re-verification round, Added value topic of the operations-management area - https://www.ocr.org.uk/Images/170837-specification-accredited-a-level-gce-business-h431.pdf

## Q353 - The correction-audit triage is a snapshot of the open set and has already drifted, and the snapshot that produced an earlier error was identifiable all along [Questionable claim, Medium]

- **Files:** `o-level-environmental-management-rocks-practice`
- **Where:** Triage provenance and drift
- **Locate by:** `title: "O Level Environmental Management: Rocks and Minerals — Practice Questions"`

**What is wrong.** Two provenance defects sit behind this round. The first is that the local snapshot whose staleness produced an earlier bad finding was a git checkout the whole time, holding a readable commit of 7 September, three days older than the repair it missed. One line would have shown that before anything was claimed from it, and a provenance script now reports the commit or the absence of one for every snapshot the audit holds. The second is live rather than historical. The triage that screens open findings for unsafe corrections is itself computed from a fixed evidence file, and repair work is proceeding in parallel: of the two hundred and fifty-five findings it flags, five were closed within half an hour of its being generated and a sixth was deferred, all in one subject cluster. That is a small proportion now and it will grow, and a corrector working an old triage would be auditing corrections to findings that no longer exist. Note for correction: one of the five findings flagged on the unscoped signature is a false positive worth keeping as a model, since its observation distinguishes the outgoing series from the current cycle, records that the wording in one is illustrative rather than restrictive, and states in terms that calling the material off-syllabus overstates what the wording establishes.

**Fix.** Reconcile the triage against the decision log before working it, record the commit of every snapshot used, and treat the environmental-management finding as the pattern for how a scoped correction should read.

**Evidence.** Marlbridge correction-audit triage, outputs/correction-audit-triage.txt, 255 open confirmed errors flagged on seven signatures - https://github.com/Nouman1608/MB/blob/main/docs/audit/2026-09-23-findings.md
