## I388 - Fourteen claims stated more firmly than the board documents support: reword conservatively [Inconsistency, Low]

- **Files:** named in the observation below (not in the frozen baseline snapshot)
- **Where:** o-level-geography-natural-environment-practice; a-level-business-marketing-revision-notes; a-level-computer-science-processor-fundamentals-practice; edexcel-igcse-biology-reproduction-inheritance-practice; ib-dp-ess-topic-1-foundations-practice; ib-dp-global-politics-peace-conflict (guide, notes, practice); ib-dp-history-investigation-ia-practice; igcse-oxfordaqa-urdu-study-employment-practice; oxfordaqa-a-level-psychology-biopsychology-development-practice; oxfordaqa-igcse-english-literature-exam-preparation; aqa-a-level-economics-aggregate-demand-supply; aqa-a-level-english-literature-b-theory-and-independence (guide, notes); ib-dp-chemistry-exam-preparation and ib-dp-chemistry-assessment-revision-notes (main at d59813a)

**What is wrong.** (1) Q399.10, o-level-geography-natural-environment-practice answer 1, L103-106: plate movement '(diverging, converging, or sliding past one another) builds up stress in the crust and allows magma to reach the surface'. The 2217 syllabus asks for the processes at each type of boundary without saying magma reaches the surface at all three. (2) Q399.11, a-level-business-marketing-revision-notes L75-77 and self-test answer 5 L197-201: a decline-stage product 'is the same signal' as a Boston Matrix dog, 'signalling the same underlying situation'. 9609 3.3.3 names both tools and does not equate them. (3) Q399.12, a-level-computer-science-processor-fundamentals-practice Q1 L34 and answer L78-79: 'Name the two registers that together allow the CPU to fetch an instruction', crediting only PC and MAR, while the same file's fetch answer also uses the MDR and CIR; 9618 4.1 names no pair. (4) Q399.14, edexcel-igcse-biology-reproduction-inheritance-practice answer 5(b) L78 lists 'the halving of chromosome number' as genetic mixing; 4BI1 3.30 says meiosis produces 'genetically different haploid gametes' without naming halving as the cause. (5) Q399.18, ib-dp-ess-topic-1-foundations-practice Q6 L54 asks for 'the type of feedback loop implied by the worsening flooding', while the scheme L74 describes the wetland as a storage regulating flow; the question presupposes a feedback loop the scenario does not show. (6) Q399.19 and .20, ib-dp-global-politics-peace-conflict L72, its revision notes L67 and L120, and the practice answer L62 give 'UN peace enforcement or NATO involvement' as examples of peacekeeping; the Global politics guide lists peacemaking, peacekeeping and peacebuilding and does not use 'peace enforcement'. (7) Q399.21, ib-dp-history-investigation-ia-practice answer 8 L84 presents weighing which contributing factor 'mattered most' as the use of significance; weighing causes is cause and consequence among the guide's four concepts. (8) Q399.24, igcse-oxfordaqa-urdu-study-employment-practice answer 1 L75 credits 'مقصد/عزم' for 'ambition'; مقصد is goal or purpose (the file itself translates it 'goal') and عزم is resolve. (9) Q399.25, oxfordaqa-a-level-psychology-biopsychology-development-practice answer 4 L65: 'A perceived threat is detected and processed by the hypothalamus [1]'. 9685 3.2.1 names 'the fight or flight response, including the role of adrenaline' and no detecting structure. (10) Q406, oxfordaqa-igcse-english-literature-exam-preparation L24-26: 'the board states the paper structure, timings, marks and weightings above are unchanged'. The figures match both 9275 specifications, but no OxfordAQA document makes that statement. (11) D-244, aqa-a-level-economics-aggregate-demand-supply L189: 'Step 4 is the distinction examiners reward most'. The 7136 Paper 2 reports say separating short-run and long-run effects marks out 'better students' (June 2023) and 'stronger responses' (June 2025). (12) D-244, aqa-a-level-english-literature-b-theory-and-independence L53-54 and its revision notes L37-38: 'a strict word count band'. The 7717 specification: 'two essays of 1250-1500 words'; the June 2025 NEA report: 'The suggested 1500-word limit is a maximum, and not a requirement'; the teaching guide: 'a concerted effort to work within the stipulated word limits'. (13) D-245, ib-dp-chemistry-exam-preparation L48-51 says Paper 2 'is where evaluate, discuss and justify command terms appear most', and ib-dp-chemistry-assessment-revision-notes L47-49 heads a section 'how each paper uses' command terms; the Chemistry guide's command terms are not assigned to papers.

**Fix.** (1) Say earthquakes occur at all three boundary types, and volcanic activity at constructive and destructive boundaries, where magma reaches the surface. (2) Define a dog as a low market share in a low-growth market, and say it may, but need not, coincide with the decline stage. (3) Ask for 'the register that holds the address of the next instruction and the register that address is copied to' (PC, MAR), or credit any correct pair used in the fetch (PC and MAR; MAR and MDR). (4) Credit independent assortment (and crossing over) for the genetic differences, and give halving as producing haploid gametes. (5) Ask what happens to flood peaks when the storage is removed, or accept an answer that says no feedback loop is involved. (6) Give UN peacekeeping missions as the example, and remove 'peace enforcement or NATO involvement'. (7) Use significance for how important an event or development was (for example its scale, duration or consequences), and move weighing causes to cause and consequence. (8) Credit a clear rendering of 'ambition' (for example عزائم or خواہش) or accept a range; keep مقصد as 'goal'. (9) Say the amygdala detects the threat and signals the hypothalamus, which activates the sympathetic nervous system, or credit either structure. (10) Drop the attribution: say the revised specification keeps the same papers, timings, marks and weightings. (11) Say separating short-run from long-run effects is what distinguishes stronger answers. (12) Give the range 1,250-1,500 words and say 1,500 is the maximum; remove 'strict band'. (13) Remove the per-paper claim, or say the command terms are the same for both papers and are set by assessment objective.
