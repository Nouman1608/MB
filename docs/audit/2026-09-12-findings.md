# Part 1 — Retractions and corrections to the 11 September findings

## R1 — Q205 is RETRACTED. Its prescribed fix would delete required syllabus content. [Urgent]

- **Applies to:** `igcse-oxfordaqa-economics-markets-revision-notes`, `igcse-oxfordaqa-economics-how-markets-work`
- **Original finding:** Q205 suggested that a third elasticity measure might be off-syllabus
  and should be removed if not listed.

**Why it is wrong.** Cross elasticity of demand is required content. OxfordAQA
International GCSE Economics (9214), Version 1.0, for teaching from September 2023 and
exams from May/June 2025, section 3.1.3.5 lists under Content, verbatim:

> Cross elasticity of demand

and under Additional information, verbatim:

> that cross elasticity of demand is measured as the percentage change in quantity demanded
> of one good, divided by the percentage change in price of a second good and be able to
> perform calculations from given data.

Section 3.1.3.6 Price elasticity of supply is likewise a full sub-topic. All three measures
are required.

**Action.** The remediation correctly declined this finding and made no edit. That was the
right call. But Q205's "What is wrong" and "Fix" still stand unamended in
`docs/audit/2026-09-11-findings.md`, so a later pass reading that file alone would act on
an instruction that removes an on-syllabus bullet from two resources. Amend the entry in
place to record the retraction. Declining in a commit message is not sufficient protection.

**Source.** OxfordAQA International GCSE Economics (9214) specification, Version 1.0 —
https://www.oxfordaqa.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/oxfordaqa-international-gcse-economics-specification.pdf
