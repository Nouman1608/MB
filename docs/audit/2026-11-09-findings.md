## E932 - Two repairs introduced a false statement about the assessment or the law [Confirmed error, Medium]

- **Files:** named in the observation below (not in the frozen baseline snapshot)
- **Where:** igcse-oxfordaqa-world-history-international-relations-depth-studies; law-english-legal-system-practice (main at d393064)

**What is wrong.** (1) E556's repair, igcse-oxfordaqa-world-history-international-relations-depth-studies L128-132: 'Section A is entirely source-based, so its 12-mark item is always a source-usefulness question'. OxfordAQA 9245 specification: 'Question 3 tests AO3 and is worth 8 marks ... evaluate how a source is useful to a historian studying a given issue'; 'Question 4 tests AO3 and is worth 12 marks. It requires students to analyse and evaluate a given issue or development' using the sources and own knowledge. (2) E671's repair, law-english-legal-system-practice answer 3 L55, gives the intermediate track 'no more than two claimants and two defendants' and says 'several classes of claim are assigned to it, or to the multi-track, whatever their value'. CPR r.26.9(7)(d): 'the claim is brought by one claimant against either one or two defendants, or is brought by two claimants against one defendant'; the classes assigned regardless of value go to the multi-track.

**Fix.** (1) Give Question 3 (8 marks) as the usefulness question and Question 4 (12 marks) as the evaluation of an issue from the sources. (2) State the party condition as in r.26.9(7)(d) and assign the listed classes to the multi-track.
