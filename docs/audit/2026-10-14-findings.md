## E902 - A repair round answered a four-term question by adding a statutory term the file own specification does not name, so a correction pushed the paper off its specification [Confirmed error, High]

- **Files:** `a-law-the-law-in-action-practice`
- **Where:** Question 2 and its answer
- **Locate by:** `State the four implied terms the Consumer Rights Act 2015 places into a contract to supply goods`

**What is wrong.** E680 recorded that this question asks for the four terms the Consumer Rights Act implies into a contract to supply goods, while its scheme names three and fills the fourth credit with the short-term right to reject, which is a remedy. That part was right. Its correction offered two routes, name a fourth implied term that the file own specification carries, or reduce the stem to three and retariff, and its note warned that one board in the batch names exactly three, so a fourth would push a resource off-specification on that board. The file is tagged to the Pearson International A-level, YLA1. Read complete, that specification names exactly three implied terms for goods, sections 9, 10 and 11, and places section 20 among remedies; the domestic AQA A-level names the same three. No board in the batch names a fourth. The repair round D-192 took the first route and added section 13, goods sold by sample matching the sample, as the fourth credited term, and kept the stem at four terms for four marks. Section 13 is real law, but it is not specification content for this qualification, so the question now requires a candidate to supply a term the specification does not teach in order to reach full marks. E680 own note is where this went wrong: it placed the three-term list on the other board, when it is the list on this one. Note for correction: do not delete section 13 from the answer as if it were false law; it is correct law and may stay as clearly labelled extension material carrying no mark.

**Fix.** Reduce the stem to the three implied terms with their section numbers, retariff at three marks, and either remove section 13 from the scheme or label it as extension beyond the specification with no credit.

**Evidence.** Pearson Edexcel International Advanced Level Law YLA1 specification, Issue 4, read complete in a browser session and parsed with pdf.js: 48 pages, glossary present, ending in the registered-office block. Content area 2.1.3 names terms implied into a contract to supply goods as section 9 satisfactory quality, section 10 fitness for particular purpose and section 11 description, and lists section 20, the short-term right to reject, under remedies. - https://qualifications.pearson.com/content/dam/pdf/International%20Advanced%20Level/Law/2015/specification-and-sample-assessments/Pearson-Edexcel-IAL-Law-Specification.pdf
**Evidence.** AQA A-level Law 7162 specification, version 1.0, read complete with pdf.js: 32 pages, ending in the registered-address block. It names the same three implied terms, s9, s10 and s11, and the same remedies, s20, s23 and s24. - https://filestore.aqa.org.uk/resources/law/specifications/AQA-7162-SP-2017.PDF

## Q379 - The remaining non-Cambridge absence documents are read complete: of fifty-two findings none is overturned, four corrections need a bound, three claims cannot be settled from these documents, and one applied correction is damaged [Questionable claim, High]

**What is wrong.** Thirty-one of the thirty-two remaining documents were read complete. Upheld with a bound: E874, whose second-year list is right for GDP, inflation and unemployment but not for incomes, interest rates and exchange rates, which the first-year section 3.1.1.3 requires; E347, whose absent terms are absent but whose competitive-markets sub-topic requires the characteristics of a competitive market, so the perfect-competition question should be reframed rather than deleted; E482, whose nine place angles are introduced by the specification as ideas that can usefully be explored in a list it calls not exhaustive; and E331, whose externality policies are introduced with including, an open list, not four closed policies. Not settled from these documents: the A-level half of E473, since the specification gives objective weightings by unit and by section only as the objectives assessed, not the split within a section; E339, whose three-section structure comes from live question papers the specification does not describe; and E238, whose only cited source is a school copy of the IB chemistry guide, which is not an authoritative copy and was not used. A term split by a line break inside its hyphen, extra- state, returned no match on a first search in a complete text; the phrase is in the document. And a three-column syllabus table had to be resolved by the horizontal position of each text item before the causes and types of conflict could be placed in the prescribed-content column rather than the column of possible examples.

**Fix.** Record the four bounds and the three unsettled items against their findings. Treat E238 as unverified until an authoritative copy of the guide is read. Apply E902.
