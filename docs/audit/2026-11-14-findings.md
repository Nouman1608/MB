## E934 - A repair misstates the Civil Procedure Rules exception for clinical negligence claims [Confirmed error, Medium]

- **Files:** named in the observation below (not in the frozen baseline snapshot)
- **Where:** law-english-legal-system-practice (main at c763a79)

**What is wrong.** E932's repair, law-english-legal-system-practice answer 3 L55, now says clinical negligence claims go to the multi-track 'unless liability has been admitted in full in the pre-action letter of response (CPR r.26.9(10))'. CPR r.26.9(10)(b) allows the exception only where all three conditions hold: '(i) the claim is one which would normally be allocated to the intermediate track; (ii) there has been an admission of liability in full ... and (iii) the admission ... is made in the defendant's letter of response ..., provided that the defendant has not raised a defence to the claim under the Limitation Act 1980'. The answer omits (i) and the Limitation Act proviso.

**Fix.** State the exception with all its conditions, or say that clinical negligence claims generally go to the multi-track, with a narrow exception for intermediate-track-value claims where liability is admitted in full in the letter of response.
