## I408 - Two taught-out IB courses record no last assessment, though the IB's own pages give 2026 [Inconsistency, Low]

- **Files:** named in the observation below (not in the frozen baseline snapshot)
- **Where:** assessments.ts DP Computer Science (2014) and DP Psychology (2019) records (main at 5ffa29f)

**What is wrong.** Both records say the IB gives no final session and so record none: Computer Science (2014) 'the IB page gives no final session for it, so none is recorded'; Psychology (2019) 'the IB psychology updates page gives no final exam session for it, so none is recorded'. The IB's own course pages do give the year: the DP computer science page 'summarizes the Diploma Programme (DP) computer science course for last assessment in 2026' and offers 'the subject brief for the last assessment in 2026', and the DP psychology page (last updated 19 February 2026) 'summarizes the Diploma Programme (DP) psychology course for the last assessment in 2026'. With no final year recorded, the data layer's own rule treats a record as covering every year from its first assessment onward, so both taught-out courses read as open-ended. The ten IB Psychology resources already say the course's last examination session is November 2026, which fits the IB's year.

**Fix.** Record 2026 as the last assessment year of both courses, citing the IB course pages, and drop the 'gives no final session' sentences.
