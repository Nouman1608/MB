## READ THIS FIRST - a method defect that affects earlier rounds

**U59 is the most important entry in this file, and it is about the audit rather than the
resources.**

A reading pass in this round reported that one paper has no lettered sections, and recorded
that absence as established by reading rather than by searching - which is this audit's
standing rule. The adversarial pass found the terms on three separate pages of the guide.
The cause is that extracting a long PDF as linear text can **truncate silently** part of
the way through: the extraction ended around page 55, and the assessment chapter begins at
page 59. A complete search of the extracted text therefore returned nothing while the
document itself contained the terms throughout. The verifier reproduced the failure, then
loaded all 85 pages and found them.

This matters well beyond the one finding, which has been withdrawn. **Absence findings have
been made throughout this audit** - "X is not in the specification", "this term appears
nowhere" - and the rule requiring reading rather than searching does not protect against a
document that ends early without announcing it. Those findings have not all been
re-established against confirmed full-document reads. Until they are, treat any absence
finding in this audit as provisional, and re-check it by loading the complete document and
confirming the page count reached before acting on it.

**Four further corrections to the audit's own claims, made in place:**

- The ESS teaching-hours defect is a **column** error, not a row shift. The erroneous
  figure is a standard-level value appearing in a higher-level position, so a corrector
  told to look for a one-row displacement will look for the wrong thing.
- The Global Politics weightings are wrong, but the "HL Paper 3 at 20%" figure is **not**
  the retired guide's - that guide had no Paper 3 at all, and its 20% was an oral extension.
- The Mathematics Paper 3 correction reverses for the successor course, and the current
  course runs to **November 2028**, not May.
- The MYP achievement-scale correction must be **additive**. Several of the target strings
  are direct quotations of official IB subject briefs, and editing a figure inside a

**U60 records nine places where correcting one course, level or code by another's rule
would introduce an error.** The sharpest: the two business codes are opposite on forms of

## E849 - All five resources of one subject describe a course that has been replaced [Confirmed error, High]

- **Files:** `ib-dp-psychology-subject-guide`, `ib-dp-psychology-syllabus-guide`
- **Where:** Course version
- **Locate by:** `syllabusSeries: "First assessment 2019"`

**What is wrong.** Every one of this subject's five resources describes the syllabus for first assessment 2019. That course was replaced: the new one launched in February 2025, was first taught in August 2025 and is first assessed in May 2027, and its last predecessor session is November 2026. The replacement is not a revision but a rebuild. The four options are gone, replaced by four contexts all studied at both levels; one of them absorbs what was the abnormal psychology option, the board itself using the word renamed; six named concepts are added; the internal assessment changes from an experimental study to a research proposal marked out of twenty-four; and every paper changes duration, mark total and weighting, to thirty-five, thirty-five and thirty at standard level and twenty-five, twenty-five, thirty and twenty at higher. The higher-level internal assessment stays at twenty per cent, so a blanket statement that every weighting changed is wrong. And the two approach-based revision files cannot be ported by find and replace: in the new course the approaches are content areas applied across four contexts and integrated with the six concepts, so they need rescoping.

**Fix.** Rewrite all five to the current course, or label the family as legacy with a final-session date and publish current companions.
