## I357 - The aligned-to line on two physics resources lists the Combined Science code under GCSE Physics [Inconsistency, Low]

- **Files:** `aqa-gcse-physics-national-and-global-energy-resources`
- **Where:** aqa-gcse-physics-national-and-global-energy-resources and its practice file, the rendered "Aligned to" line
- **Locate by:** `title: "National and Global Energy Resources"`

**What is wrong.** D-236 records that the resource line now renders as "Aligned to AQA GCSE Physics (8463, 8464)". The line is built from the board, qualification and subject names followed by the declared codes, so it presents 8464 as a GCSE Physics code. 8464 is AQA GCSE Combined Science: Trilogy, a separate double-award qualification, which is how the data layer D-236 adds names it. Both files' descriptions state the Combined Science route correctly, so a reader of the body is not misled; the label line is.

**Fix.** Render a declared code under its own qualification title where it differs from the subject, or name Combined Science in the line for 8464.

**Evidence.** Marlbridge decision log entries D-235 and D-236 and the resource files they change, read on main at 058ec4e. - https://github.com/Nouman1608/MB/commit/3265e3a
