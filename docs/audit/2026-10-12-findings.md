**Read the caveat with the number.** 150 = not declared closed by any round. 693 = declarations,

## Q374 - The published open-error count never subtracted the repair rounds before D-192, so five hundred and eight confirmed errors declared closed were still counted open and the figure of 658 was really 150 [Questionable claim, High]

- **Files:** `igcse-oxfordaqa-biology-organisation`
- **Where:** The open confirmed-error figure published in every artefact, the index and the repair work order
- **Locate by:** `title: "OxfordAQA IGCSE Biology: Organisation (9201)"`

**What is wrong.** The reconciliation module that decides which findings a repair round has declared closed began at D-192. Every repair round before it was worked directly from this audit own findings under the same identifiers, and every one of them postdates the frozen baseline the findings were raised against, so nothing those rounds closed was ever subtracted. The earlier correction to this count read D-192 to D-210 and stopped there, and nothing in it explains why the window began where it did: the earlier rounds were simply never read. The seed case is the organisation finding cleared by the preceding round. The repair round D-141 names it and its file, commit c9bc195 on 6 September removed the wrong phrase, and the file on main now lists the five numbered sub-topics and places the digestive enzymes in the bioenergetics topic, yet the finding was still in the open set when the preceding round was written. The index had meanwhile recorded that same clearance as closed under a decision-log entry numbered D-226, which does not exist: the decision log ends at D-225, and the number labelled this audit own clearance with the repair instance namespace. Every entry from D-138 to D-191 has now been read in full and transcribed from its batch lists and finding tables, never from its heading, applying the same three rules as before: deferred is not closed, a qualifier is not a closure, and a declared closure this audit has seen overturned is not a closure. Excluded and kept open: the eleven confirmed errors D-138 names only in its heading range and in no batch list, which the next finding records; one D-138 skipped as a file-mapping error; one D-138 and D-144 dispute as a false positive; one D-144 lists as fixed while attaching a partial-scope note to it; and the two findings of the original baseline, which the build accounts for separately. Every finding this audit reopened in its re-verification rounds of 12 and 13 September was re-closed by D-188 or D-191, and those later entries are the ones counted. The reconciled figures: open confirmed errors 658 to 150, declared closed 185 to 693, still open despite a closing round 47 to 61. The second route, which does not read the decision log at all, tested every marker of the 508 moved findings against main: in 331 every marker is gone, in 143 every marker is still present, 19 are mixed, and 15 cannot be tested because a marker is missing or does not resolve in the baseline. A marker surviving is not a defect surviving, and it was read rather than assumed: of twelve surviving-marker findings drawn at random, three show the correction at the marker line itself, the other nine anchor on text a correct fix leaves in place, a question stem or a coverage sentence, and none shows the defect demonstrably still there. Note for correction: 150 is a count of findings no repair round has declared closed, not a count of errors verified to remain, and 693 is a count of declarations, not of verified fixes. The original baseline pair is worth a separate read: the first finding of the baseline now reads as fixed on main, the file explicitly naming the Combined Science code and sub-topic, and the second has had its worked-example input corrected so the surviving radius value is right for the new luminosity, but the distance half was not checked in all three files and neither status has been changed here.

**Fix.** Adopt the reconciled figures and publish the caveat beside them. Correct the index row that cites a nonexistent decision-log entry. Correct the repair work order, whose headline objective and whose instruction to apply the organisation correction were both written against the stale figure and a correction already applied. Re-read the two baseline findings end to end and update their status on that read, not on this one.
