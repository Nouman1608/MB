# Marlbridge audit - absence re-verification, round two

*Follows the absence re-verification round. No resources were read in this round either.*

## What changed, in one paragraph

Four more specifications were read end to end - Cambridge O Level Physics 5054, Pearson
Edexcel International GCSE Physics 4PH1, Cambridge O Level Economics 2281 and Cambridge OCR
A Level Business H431 - and the sixteen absence findings resting on them were re-checked one
by one. **All sixteen are upheld and none was overturned.** Twelve of them carry a constraint
on the correction rather than a doubt about the finding. Two entries are added about the
audit's own method, U68 and U69, and five recording the constraints, Q339 to Q343.

## U68 corrects the previous round's headline claim

The last round said the truncation defect threatens absence findings made against documents
longer than about fifty pages, and put the threshold at a hundred thousand characters of
extracted text. **That figure was wrong as a general rule, and this round proves it.** The
business specification re-read here extracted to 106,651 characters across 2,572 lines and
was complete - it reaches the summary-of-updates table, the contact block and the copyright
colophon that close the printed document.

A character count is a property of the extraction path in use on the day, not of the
document. An audit that trusts a remembered threshold will clear documents that were
truncated and re-read documents that were not. **The reliable test costs nothing: an
extraction is complete when it reaches the document's own closing matter** - for a Cambridge
syllabus the changes-to-this-syllabus page and the Cambridge Assessment address block, for a
Pearson specification the glossary and the registered-office block, for an OCR specification
the summary of updates and the registered-company block. All four documents in this round
were confirmed that way before any finding was judged.

The earlier finding should not be deleted - the truncation it describes did happen. It should
be narrowed to the extraction path it was observed on.

## U69 is the one that could do real damage

Two findings in this round concern the same physics at the same level on two different
boards, and **their corrections point in opposite directions.**

The Cambridge O Level syllabus names seven energy stores, and magnetic is not among them; a
finding of this audit is upheld that a resource wrongly adds it as an eighth. The Edexcel
International GCSE specification names eight stores in a single outcome, and **magnetic is
expressly one of them.** A corrector who took the Cambridge finding and swept the corpus for
the word magnetic in an energy-store list would delete required content from the Edexcel
resources.

The hazard exists wherever a finding says a term is off-syllabus rather than off-syllabus
*for a named code*, and this audit's findings are written in the first form more often than
the second. The fix is not to rewrite them but to check the code of the file being edited
before carrying any absence claim across a board boundary.

## The two samples disagree, and that matters

| | upheld cleanly | correction narrowed | overturned | left open |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Round one - 2 chemistry syllabuses, 18 findings | 2 | 5 | 1 | 3 |
| Round two - 4 specifications, 16 findings | 4 | 12 | 0 | 0 |

The pattern that holds across both is the important one: **corrections need narrowing far
more often than findings need withdrawing.** But the rate of outright error in the findings
themselves is much lower in this sample than the chemistry sample suggested. Six documents
of eighty-one have now been cleared. Neither rate should be projected onto the rest.

## What the constraints look like in practice

The five Q entries give them in full. A representative few:

- **Physics 5054.** The correction pointing at the area under a speed-time graph is right,
  but that outcome is limited by its own words to constant speed or constant acceleration -
  so it is not a route for a question involving changing acceleration. And the lever
  correction must not strip every lever context, because the turning-effect outcome expressly
  requires everyday examples of a moment.
- **Physics 4PH1.** Neutron radiation is named in exactly one outcome. The two outcomes on
  the nature and penetrating power of radiation name only alpha, beta and gamma - so adding a
  fourth row to a penetration table adds content the specification does not have.
- **Astrophysics 4PH1.** The outcome on massive stars is a single line naming no stage at
  all, unlike its neighbour for Sun-like stars which enumerates four. The stages a corrector
  adds are correct physics but must not be attributed to the specification.
- **Economics 2281.** The market-failure sub-topic carries an express note that demand and
  supply diagrams are not required - so a corrector illustrating externalities adds excluded
  material.
- **Business H431.** A resource booklet is provided for **every** one of the three
  examinations, not only the two with a lettered Section B, so supplying stimulus material
  only where a Section B appears leaves the third paper wrong.

## Conventions

Each finding gives its files, the section concerned, and a single-line verbatim string to
locate it by. For these seven the located file is a resource the amended finding concerns;
the finding itself is about the audit's method or about how its corrections should be
applied, not about that file's content.

## Q340 - Two of the four physics findings against the O Level syllabus need their corrections bounded [Questionable claim, Medium]

- **Files:** `kinematics-practice`, `moments-and-stability-practice`, `energy-work-and-power-revision-notes`, `energy-work-and-power-practice`
- **Where:** Correction constraints, Cambridge 5054
- **Locate by:** `title: "Kinematics and Motion Graphs: Practice Questions"`

**What is wrong.** All four findings are upheld against a complete read of all fifty-three pages. The equations of uniformly accelerated motion appear nowhere in the document, and this syllabus has no data or formulae sheet of any kind, so the finding that a practice paper sets and marks them is confirmed twice over. No form of the word lever, and no mention of mechanical advantage or force multiplication, occurs anywhere; the apparatus list names pivots, not levers. The energy-store outcome names exactly seven stores and magnetic is not among them. The term centre of mass occurs nowhere, in the content, the symbols table or the conventions appendix, while centre of gravity is the heading and the wording of all three outcomes. Two corrections nevertheless need bounding. The kinematics correction points at the area under a speed-time graph, which is right, but that outcome is limited by its own words to motion with constant speed or constant acceleration, so it is not a route for any question involving changing acceleration. The lever correction must not strip every lever context, because the turning-effect outcome expressly requires everyday examples of a moment, and a lever is one; what is off-syllabus is the taxonomy of lever classes and the force-multiplier treatment, not the context.

**Fix.** Bound the kinematics correction to constant acceleration and the lever correction to the taxonomy, and apply the other two as written.

**Evidence.** Cambridge O Level Physics 5054 syllabus for 2026, 2027 and 2028, version 2, published December 2025, 53 pages - https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/697324-2026-2028-syllabus.pdf

## Q341 - Three of the four Edexcel physics findings would introduce new errors if their corrections were applied in full [Questionable claim, Medium]

- **Files:** `edexcel-igcse-physics-radioactivity-and-particles`, `edexcel-igcse-physics-magnetism-revision-notes`, `edexcel-igcse-physics-solids-liquids-gases-revision-notes`, `edexcel-igcse-physics-astrophysics`
- **Where:** Correction constraints, Edexcel 4PH1
- **Locate by:** `title: "Radioactivity and Particles"`

**What is wrong.** All four findings are upheld against a complete read of all fifty-five pages including the nine appendices. The outcome on radiation does name four types including neutron radiation, verbatim. Neither the tesla, nor magnetic flux density, nor the equation for the force on a current-carrying conductor occurs anywhere, and the formulae appendix lists eighteen relationships that students must recall, none of them that equation, above a sentence stating that the listed relationships will not be provided - so there is no data sheet either. Latent heat occurs nowhere and the change-of-state section adds only the specific-heat-capacity unit. The sentence that all bullet points in the content must be taught as a minimum is present verbatim, and all six outcomes cited by the coverage finding exist as quoted. Three corrections need bounding. Neutron radiation is named in that one outcome and nowhere else: the two outcomes on the nature and penetrating power of radiation name only alpha, beta and gamma, so neutron belongs in the decay and nuclear-equation treatment and adding a fourth row to a penetration table would add content the specification does not have. The magnetism correction must not remove the magnetic-force material wholesale, because two outcomes require the force on a moving charged particle and an understanding of why a force acts on a current-carrying wire; only the quantitative treatment is off-specification. And the astrophysics outcome on massive stars is a single line that names no stage at all, unlike its neighbour for Sun-like stars which enumerates four: the stages a corrector adds are correct physics but must not be attributed to the specification.

**Fix.** Bound all three corrections to the outcomes named, and apply the coverage correction as written.

**Evidence.** Pearson Edexcel International GCSE in Physics (4PH1) specification, Issue 4, September 2024, 55 pages including nine appendices - https://qualifications.pearson.com/content/dam/pdf/International%20GCSE/Physics/2017/specification-and-sample-assessments/international-gcse-physics-2017-specification.pdf

## Q342 - The economics corrections must not import enumerations and diagrams the syllabus does not have [Questionable claim, Medium]

- **Files:** `the-basic-economic-problem`, `o-level-economics-the-allocation-of-resources-revision-notes`, `economics-microeconomic-decision-makers-revision-notes`
- **Where:** Correction constraints, Cambridge 2281
- **Locate by:** `title: "The Basic Economic Problem"`

**What is wrong.** All four findings are upheld against a complete read of all thirty-one pages. Topic 1 has four sub-topics and the fourth is production possibility curve diagrams, as quoted. The market-failure guidance names its areas and closes the list with the words that examples are required with respect to these areas only, and information failure occurs nowhere in the document. The content overview names five microeconomic decision makers and government is not among them; government is the subject of topic 4. The three blocks said to be undelivered are all required: the forms, functions and characteristics of money, the whole of the trade-unions sub-topic, and the firms-and-production sub-topic. Three constraints follow. The production-possibility correction must add a drawn diagram, not a description, because the sub-topic requires definition, drawing and interpretation. The market-failure correction lists seven causes where the syllabus phrases six, pairing merit with demerit goods and external costs with external benefits, so a seven-item list must not be attributed to the syllabus; and the same sub-topic carries an express note that demand and supply diagrams relating to market failure are not required, so a corrector illustrating externalities would add excluded material. The money correction should not promise four functions, because the syllabus enumerates none.

**Fix.** Add a drawn curve, keep the syllabus phrasing of the market-failure list, add no externality diagram, and enumerate no fixed number of functions of money.

**Evidence.** Cambridge O Level Economics 2281 syllabus for 2026, version 2, published December 2025, 31 pages - https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/697295-2026-syllabus.pdf

## Q343 - The business corrections turn on names and numbers the specification does not use [Questionable claim, Medium]

- **Files:** `ocr-a-level-business-objectives-and-strategic-decisions`, `ocr-a-level-business-external-influences`, `ocr-a-level-business-external-influences-revision-notes`, `a-business-strategy-practice`
- **Where:** Correction constraints, OCR H431
- **Locate by:** `title: "OCR A-Level Business: Objectives and Strategic Decisions (H431)"`

**What is wrong.** All four findings are upheld against a complete read of the whole specification. The three components are structured exactly as described, the string Section C occurs nowhere, the area said to hold sixteen topics holds exactly sixteen, the three market structures named are the only three and perfect competition occurs nowhere, and the live document is version 2.1 of January 2026 carrying both the withdrawal notice and the rebrand. Four constraints follow. A resource booklet is provided for every one of the three examinations, not only for the two that have a lettered Section B, so a correction that supplies stimulus material only where a Section B appears leaves the third paper wrong. The specification calls the area Business objectives and strategy; the phrase business objectives and strategic decisions is the wording of a component content-overview bullet, so a correction must not present the resource's own title as the specification's name for the area. The matrix the finding calls the growth matrix is named Ansoff's matrix throughout, and the forecasting topic restricts moving averages to an odd number of years, so an even-period example would be off-specification. And monopoly is treated in two distinct topics, the market-structures topic and market dominance, which a tidying correction must not merge.

**Fix.** Use the specification's own names, supply stimulus material for all three components, and keep the two monopoly topics separate.

**Evidence.** Cambridge OCR Level 3 Advanced GCE in Business H431 specification, version 2.1, January 2026, 52 numbered pages plus summary of updates - https://www.ocr.org.uk/Images/170837-specification-accredited-a-level-gce-business-h431.pdf

## U68 - The truncation threshold belongs to the extraction path, not to the document, and the audit was testing for it the wrong way [Unverified item, High]

- **Files:** `ocr-a-level-business-external-influences`
- **Where:** Absence-finding method
- **Locate by:** `title: "OCR A Level Business: External Influences Facing Businesses (H431)"`

**What is wrong.** An earlier finding of this audit states that extraction passes one hundred thousand characters at a certain page of a long syllabus and that absence findings against documents longer than about fifty pages are therefore at risk. This round refutes the figure as a general rule. The business specification re-read here extracted to one hundred and six thousand six hundred and fifty-one characters across two thousand five hundred and seventy-two lines and was complete: it reaches the summary-of-updates table, the contact block and the copyright colophon that close the printed document. A character count is therefore a property of the extraction path in use on the day, not a property of the document, and an audit that trusts a remembered threshold will clear documents that were truncated and re-read documents that were not. The reliable test is different and costs nothing: an extraction is complete when it reaches the document's own closing matter - for a Cambridge syllabus the changes-to-this-syllabus page and the Cambridge Assessment address block, for a Pearson specification the glossary and the registered-office block, for an OCR specification the summary of updates and the registered-company block. All four documents in this round were confirmed that way before any finding was judged. Note for correction: the earlier finding should not be deleted, because the truncation it describes did occur; it should be narrowed to the extraction path it was observed on, and the terminal-marker test recorded as the standing check.

**Fix.** Replace the character-count threshold with the terminal-marker test, and state which closing matter was reached for every absence finding.
