## I377 - Two residues from round-12 closures: an IB Psychology opening taken from the 2027 guide in a file sourced to the 2019 brief, and a 9489 Paper 1 practice with two sources [Inconsistency, Low]

- **Files:** named in the observation below (not in the frozen baseline snapshot)
- **Where:** ib-dp-psychology-subject-guide; a-world-history-modern-europe-practice (main at 7b8cde8)

**What is wrong.** (1) ib-dp-psychology-subject-guide L29-37 opens 'IB Diploma Programme Psychology aims to develop students' knowledge and understanding of psychological concepts, content and contexts ... As the IB itself puts it, the goal is not to create psychologists, but to promote psychological literacy.' That wording is in the Psychology guide for first assessment 2027 (p. 12: 'The goal of the DP psychology course is not ...'); the 2019 subject brief opens 'At the core of the DP psychology course is an introduction to three different approaches to understanding behaviour', and neither the 2019 brief nor the 2019 guide contains 'psychological literacy'. The source section (L163-170) says 'This resource is sourced from the 2019 brief throughout, not a mixture of the two', and the description (L10) repeats the framing. (2) Cambridge 9489 syllabus for 2026, p. 42: Paper 1 Document question, 'Part (b) Candidates will be expected to use all the sources' and 'there will be at least three sources on a specific issue'. a-world-history-modern-europe-practice L45-60 sets its Paper 1 question on two sources (A and B), and part (b) asks 'How far do Sources A and B support this view?'. The same file calls the paper 'Historical Sources'; the syllabus names it 'Document question'.

**Fix.** (1) Replace the opening with the 2019 brief's course description, or attribute it to the 2027 guide and drop 'sourced from the 2019 brief throughout'. (2) Add at least a third source, make part (b) use all the sources, and use the syllabus's paper name.
