# Marlbridge audit - correction to the correction audit

*Correcting a figure published in the previous file. No resources were read for this.*

## What was wrong

Yesterday's Q346 said that **five resources** had been made worse by a correction that
deleted lettered section headings. That number came from adding the narrative counts in two
findings — "three papers" in one, "two papers" in the other — and **not from looking at the
files.**

That is the same defect this workstream was created to catch: a claim written wider than the
evidence behind it. So it is recorded here rather than quietly amended.

## What the files say

Diffing the frozen baseline (`ee1b341f`) against the live snapshot: **eleven files carried
section headings at baseline and carry none now.** The count was wrong in both directions —
too low overall, and wrong about which files.

**Six are damage** (the real paper has lettered sections, on evidence already held):

| file | code | baseline headings |
|---|---|---|
| `ocr-a-level-business-external-influences-practice` | OCR H431 | A, B — both real |
| `a-business-strategy-practice` | OCR H431 | A, B real; C invented |
| `edexcel-a-level-business-managing-activities-practice` | Edexcel WBS12 | A, B — real paper has A, B **and C** |
| `o-level-economics-the-allocation-of-resources-practice` | Cambridge 2281 | A, B — both real |
| `ocr-gcse-business-activity-practice` | OCR J204 | A, B real; C invented |
| `ocr-gcse-business-operations-finance-influences-practice` | OCR J204 | A, B real; C invented |

**One removal was correct** and must be left alone: `ib-dp-economics-demand-supply-equilibrium-practice`
— no paper in that course has lettered sections.

**Four are unverified** and must not be touched until the specification has been read:
`business-activity-and-classification-practice` (Cambridge 7115),
`igcse-business-understanding-activity-practice` (Cambridge 0450),
`edexcel-igcse-economics-business-economics-practice` and
`igcse-edexcel-economics-market-system-practice` (both Edexcel 4EC1).

Note the trap in three of the six: the invented Section C must **not** come back with the
real Section A and B.

## The science papers were not damaged — they were half repaired

The three papers described in E335's narrative are not in the list above. All five papers of
that family still carry lettered headings; they were **relabelled, not removed**. I had
suspected the new labels made a false claim. They don't:

| | baseline Section A | live Section A |
|---|---|---|
| OCR GCSE Biology, Scaling Up | 5 marks, 2 written items | **5 marks, 5 genuine multiple-choice items** |
| OCR GCSE Chemistry, Purity | 7 marks, 2 written items | 5 marks, 5 multiple-choice items |
| GCSE Chemistry, Atomic Structure | 7 marks, 3 written items | 5 marks, 5 multiple-choice items |
| OCR GCSE Physics, Forces | 6 marks, 2 written items | 5 marks, 5 multiple-choice items |
| OCR A Level Chemistry, Amount of Substance | 5 marks, 2 written items | 5 marks, 5 multiple-choice items |

Each now opens with real four-option multiple-choice items. **The question type is fixed.**

**The weight is not.** Section A is 5 marks in all five, against the 15 the specification sets
— a third of its true value. Paper totals run 76–90 where the real papers are 90. So E335 is
genuinely still open, its status of "no remediation verified" is out of date, and the
remaining work on those five is arithmetic rather than structural.

## What this changes for anyone repairing files

Work from the seven-file list above, not from the count in Q346. Verify the four unverified
files first. Restore only the sections the real paper has. Leave the Diploma Programme
economics file alone. The science papers are not part of this repair at all.

## Q348 - The count of damaged resources in Q346 is wrong in both directions, and the file evidence gives a different list [Questionable claim, High]

- **Files:** `o-level-economics-the-allocation-of-resources-practice`, `ocr-gcse-business-operations-finance-influences-practice`, `edexcel-a-level-business-managing-activities-practice`, `business-activity-and-classification-practice`, `igcse-business-understanding-activity-practice`, `edexcel-igcse-economics-business-economics-practice`, `igcse-edexcel-economics-market-system-practice`
- **Where:** Correction to Q346
- **Locate by:** `title: "O Level Economics: The Allocation of Resources — Practice Questions"`

**What is wrong.** Q346 stated that five resources had been made worse by a correction that deleted lettered section headings. That figure was taken from two findings' own narratives, three papers in one and two in the other, and not from the files. Comparing the frozen baseline against the live snapshot shows eleven files that carried section headings at baseline and carry none now, and the three science papers described in one of those narratives are not among them: all five science papers of that family still carry lettered headings, relabelled rather than removed. Of the eleven, six are damage on evidence already held in this audit, because the real paper has lettered sections in each case: two practice papers of one A Level business specification whose two components each define a Section A and a Section B; one practice paper of an international A Level business unit whose paper has three sections, of which the baseline file already carried only two; one practice paper of an O Level economics syllabus whose structured paper has a compulsory thirty-mark Section A and a twenty-mark-per-question Section B; and two practice papers of a GCSE business specification whose Section A an audit finding records as fifteen marks of multiple choice in both components. One removal was correct and must be left alone, on a Diploma Programme economics file whose course has no lettered sections on any paper. Four remain unverified and must not be touched until the relevant specification has been read: two Cambridge business files and two Edexcel international GCSE economics files. Note for correction: the invented third section carried by three of the six must not be restored along with the real two.

**Fix.** Use the seven-file list in this finding rather than the count in Q346, verify the four unverified files before acting on them, and restore only the sections the real paper has.

## Q349 - The five science practice papers were half repaired: the question type is now right and the mark weight is not [Questionable claim, Medium]

- **Files:** `ocr-gcse-biology-scaling-up-practice`
- **Where:** Section A of the five OCR science practice papers
- **Locate by:** `title: "OCR GCSE Biology: Scaling Up — Practice Questions"`

**What is wrong.** An open finding records that these five papers fill a real fifteen-mark multiple-choice Section A with two or three written short-answer questions and contain no multiple-choice item at all. That is no longer true of the live files, and the finding's status of no remediation verified is out of date. Each of the five now opens with five genuine multiple-choice items carrying four lettered options apiece, on subject matter appropriate to the topic, and the heading has been relabelled to name the section as multiple choice. The question type is therefore corrected. The weight is not: Section A totals five marks in every one of the five files against the fifteen the specification sets, so the section that opens every real paper is worth a third of its true value, and a candidate rehearsing on these files still meets it at the wrong weight. The totals do not match either, running between seventy-six and ninety marks across the five where the real papers are ninety. Note for correction: the outstanding work on these files is arithmetic rather than structural, and the earlier description of them as damaged by heading removal does not apply to them at all.

**Fix.** Raise Section A to fifteen marks of multiple choice in each of the five files and reconcile each paper to its real total, then close the original finding on evidence rather than on the relabelling alone.
