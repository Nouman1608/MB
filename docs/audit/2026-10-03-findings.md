# Marlbridge audit — the rest of the repair check

*Repair verification, round one, continued. The regression published as
`2026-10-02-repair-regression.md` was the first thing this round found. It is not
representative of the repair work, and this document is the rest of the picture.*

Two of the three findings here are about defects in **this audit's own records**, not in the
website.

## Q363: three of four are fixed, two of them better than asked

D-207 declared four findings "non-resolving against the current corpus and primary sources
after direct verification." That is the same form of words D-202 used, and D-202 was wrong,
so all four were checked against the files on `main`.

| finding | verdict |
|---|---|
| **E822** (+U56) — tier framing on an untiered qualification | **fixed, and better** |
| **E823** — angle wrong from rounding before the inverse sine | **fixed, and better** |
| **E848** — economics guide missing a whole sub-topic | **fixed, and across all siblings** |
| **I346** — frontmatter series string | **half fixed** |

**E822 answered a question the audit could not.** Three biology files framed OxfordAQA 9201
as having a core and a higher tier and told candidates to deprioritise compulsory content.
The tier framing is gone and the files now say the qualification is "linear and untiered."
More than that: the audit had raised **U56** to record that the letter markers in the
specification margins *could not be decoded*, and warned expressly that they "should not be
guessed." The repaired files decode them — the marker means the content is examined in
Biology only and is not shared with the co-teachable Combined Science qualification — and
then state that it "remains compulsory for every candidate." **That closes U56 as answered,
not merely as fixed.**

**E823 went past the correction it was given.** The audit asked for two numbers to be
changed: sine 0.991 → 0.990, angle 82.3° → 81.9°. The file instead reworks the solution
through the cosine rule so that no intermediate rounding happens at all, and says so in the
working: *"using the unrounded BC² = 76.6436 from part (a) rather than the rounded 8.75 cm
(rounding the side before using it again compounds the error)."* The rounded sine the finding
objected to appears nowhere in the file. That is a better fix than the one requested, because
it removes the cause rather than the symptom.

**E848 did not repeat the E208 failure.** The sub-topic is present with its
purchasing-power-parity content in the guide *and* in both siblings the finding did not name
— the revision notes gained a `## Uses of national income data (3.2.1.4)` section, and the
practice paper gained **two new questions** (Q10 and Q11, 5 marks) testing it. Scoped to the
defect, not to the slugs.

**I346 is half done.** The shortened frontmatter series string is now the full string in both
files. The qualification declaration the finding also asked to be corrected is unchanged —
both still read `qualifications: ["a-level"]` for content the finding places in the AS units.
Reopen that half only.

## Q364: this audit has been publishing the wrong open count

**This is the most consequential thing in the round, and it is our defect.**

The artefacts carry a remediation record whose open set was last reconciled *before* the
current repair work began. Eight decision-log entries were reconciled against the correction
audit's triage when they were discovered — but that reconciliation was never written back
into the artefacts, and **eleven further entries have been recorded since**, closing three
whole reading batches whose commit messages say so in those words.

Counting the identifiers those nineteen entries name, and excluding every finding their own
commit messages describe as deferred, partial or unlocatable:

| | still counted open here |
|---|---|
| D-192..D-199 | 53 |
| batch 28 (D-200..D-202) | 34 |
| batch 29 (D-203..D-206) | 35 |
| batch 30 (D-207..D-210) | 27 |
| **total** | **131** |

**The published figure of 842 overstates the real one by 16%. The true figure is about 711.**

Two things travel with that number, and neither weakens it:

- *Closed* means a repair round declared the finding handled — not that this audit confirmed
  it. Of the closures spot-checked by reading in this round, five were sound and one
  round-level reversal was a regression.
- It is a correction to a stale count, not a statement about the corpus.

The uncomfortable part: **all nine validation steps pass on the wrong number**, every time,
because each of them checks the figure against itself. `open 842 = 2 + 6 + 834` is internally
consistent and externally false. A validator that only ever compares a record to itself
cannot catch a record that has stopped tracking the world.

## D10: one defect, two identifiers — and a screen that missed its own seed case

E308 and E848 are the same defect in the same file. One was raised when the family was read
as a topic cluster; the other when the same file came round again in a later board cluster.
Neither is wrong, and E848 adds a genuinely useful observation (the cause is a tier slip
inside AQA's combined AS/A-level document). But they are one defect with two IDs, and a
repair round that closes one leaves the other standing.

A screen was then written to find how many more such pairs exist — confirmed errors sharing a
file and sharing distinctive title vocabulary. It returned 21 candidates and **did not return
this one**, because the two titles describe the same defect in almost entirely different
words.

So: no number is claimed. The 21 are a reading list. **This is the fifth time in this audit
that a screen has been mistaken for a measurement, and the fifth time reading has been the
only thing that settled it.**

## Q364 - The open-error count this audit publishes overstates the real one by 131, because its remediation record was never reconciled with the repair rounds [Questionable claim, High]

- **Files:** `a-level-aqa-economics-measurement-of-macroeconomic-performance`
- **Where:** The remediation-verification block of the audit artefacts
- **Locate by:** `title: "AQA A-Level Economics: The Measurement of Macroeconomic Performance (7136)"`

**What is wrong.** The audit artefacts carry a remediation record whose open set was last reconciled before the repair work of the past several days began, and every report this audit has generated since then has published a headline open figure drawn from it. Eight decision-log entries from the first of those rounds were reconciled against the correction-audit triage when they were discovered, but that reconciliation was never written back into the artefacts, and eleven further entries have been recorded since, closing three whole reading batches whose own commit messages say so in those words. Counting the identifiers those nineteen entries name, and deliberately excluding every finding the commit messages describe as deferred, partial or unlocatable, one hundred and thirty-one confirmed errors that the repair rounds have closed are still carried as open. The published figure of eight hundred and forty-two therefore overstates the real one by sixteen per cent, and the true figure is about seven hundred and eleven. Two things must travel with that number and neither weakens the correction. Closed here means a repair round declared the finding handled, not that this audit has confirmed it; of the closures spot-checked by reading in this round, five were confirmed sound and one round-level reversal was found to be a regression. And the corrected figure is a correction to a stale count, not a statement about the corpus. Note for correction: this is a defect in the audit own reporting rather than in the website, and it is the kind that is hardest to notice from inside, because every validation step the build runs checks the figure against itself and all nine of them pass.

**Fix.** Reconcile the remediation record against the decision log as part of every build rather than as an occasional exercise, preserving the deferred and partial qualifiers rather than flattening them, and republish the corrected open figure with the caveat that closed means declared handled.

The third concerned an economics guide missing a whole sub-topic, and the sub-topic is present with its purchasing-power-parity content, in the guide and in both of its siblings, the practice paper having gained two new questions that test it. The fourth is fixed only in part: the shortened frontmatter series string it objected to is now the full string in both files, but the qualification declaration it also asked to be corrected is unchanged, so half of that finding stands. ## D10 - The same defect in one economics guide was raised twice in two reading batches under two identifiers [Duplicate / overlapping scope, Low]

- **Files:** `a-level-aqa-economics-measurement-of-macroeconomic-performance`, `aqa-a-level-economics-macroeconomic-performance-practice`
- **Where:** Overlapping scope between two confirmed errors
- **Locate by:** `title: "AQA A-Level Economics: The Measurement of Macroeconomic Performance (7136)"`

**What is wrong.** Two confirmed errors of this audit describe the same defect in the same file: a guide to a numbered economics sub-topic that presents the sub-topic as having three children when it has four, omitting the use and limitations of national income data and the purchasing-power-parity comparison. One was raised when the family was read as a topic cluster and named the guide with its two siblings; the other was raised when the same file came round again in a later board cluster and named the guide alone, adding the useful observation that the cause is a tier slip inside the board combined document. They are not contradictory and neither is wrong, but they are one defect with two identifiers, and a repair round that closes one leaves the other standing in the record. That is what happened here. Note for correction: an attempt was made to find how many further pairs of this kind exist, by screening for confirmed errors that share a file and share distinctive vocabulary in their titles. The screen returned twenty-one candidate pairs and did not return this one, because the two titles describe the same defect in almost entirely different words. The screen is therefore not a measurement of how many duplicates exist, and the twenty-one it did return are a reading list on which no claim rests until they have been read. This is the same lesson the correction audit recorded about its own screens, arrived at independently a fifth time.

**Fix.** Merge the two into one finding with the union of the three files and the tier-slip explanation, and close the other. Do not draw any number for duplicate pairs from the title screen; if the question matters, settle it by reading.
