## Q381 - The correction for E640 would strip, as another board's content, four issues and debates that the labelled syllabus itself names at A Level [Questionable claim, High]

- **Files:** `a-psychology-approaches-revision-notes`
- **Where:** Free will and determinism, nature and nurture, reductionism and holism, idiographic and nomothetic sections
- **Locate by:** `title: "A Level Psychology: Approaches, Issues and Debates — Revision Notes"`

**What is wrong.** E640 is open and the file on main is unchanged. Its core claim holds: the 9990 syllabus names four AS approaches, and the file teaches five, with a psychodynamic and a humanistic approach that the syllabus does not name as approaches; humanistic and Rogers return no match anywhere in it, psychodynamic appears only as an explanation inside specialist-option topics, and Maslow only under need theories in organisational psychology. But the finding goes further and says the block of issues and debates is content of a different board and appears nowhere in the syllabus. That is wrong for four of its sections. The syllabus A Level list names nature versus nurture, reductionism versus holism, determinism versus free-will and idiographic versus nomothetic, which are exactly the debates the file teaches, and it adds cultural differences. What is wrong is their placement: the file is labelled to Paper 1, and the Paper 1 issues and debates are the five AS ones, of which only nature versus nurture is taught. The first correction option, relabel the resource to the board whose content it actually is, would therefore misdescribe this syllabus's own A Level content as another board's, and a rewrite that deletes the debates would remove required A Level material. Note for correction: the gender and culture bias and the socially sensitive research sections are not in either list, and cultural differences is the nearest named debate.

**Fix.** Rewrite the approaches to the four the syllabus names. Keep the four debate sections, labelled as A Level issues and debates applied to the specialist options, or move them to an A Level resource; add the five AS issues and debates for Paper 1; and drop the humanistic strand. Do not relabel the file to another board.

**Evidence.** Cambridge International AS and A Level Psychology 9990 syllabus for 2024 to 2026, read complete in a browser session and parsed with pdf.js: 62 pages, ending in the address block. At AS Level it names four approaches, biological, cognitive, learning and social, and five issues and debates for the core studies: the application of psychology to everyday life, individual and situational explanations, nature versus nurture, the use of children and the use of animals in psychological research. At A Level it names eight issues and debates for the specialist options, adding cultural differences, reductionism versus holism, determinism versus free-will and idiographic versus nomothetic. Psychodynamic explanations appear only inside specialist-option topics and Maslow only under need theories in organisational psychology; humanistic, Rogers and attachment return no match. - https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/634461-2024-2026-syllabus.pdf
**Evidence.** The named resource files read on main at 2126b0b from a depth-one clone. - https://github.com/Nouman1608/MB/tree/main/src/content/resources

## Q382 - E175 was declared closed with its correction only partly applied: the syllabus's gas example is still not named [Questionable claim, Medium]

- **Files:** `a-physics-temperature`
- **Where:** Temperature scales paragraph and types-of-thermometer table
- **Locate by:** `title: "Temperature"`

**What is wrong.** Two bounds on E175 first. The 9702 outcome introduces its four example properties with including, which the syllabus does not define, so the list is not closed and the baseline file's volume of a liquid was an additional example rather than a substitution. The omission of named examples was the real defect. On main the coverage block is corrected, with the cooling-curve outcome gone and the fusion and vaporisation distinction present, and the scales paragraph now names the resistance of a metal, the e.m.f. of a thermocouple and the density of a liquid. For the gas it names the pressure of a gas at constant volume, and the gas row added to the thermometer table is a constant-volume gas thermometer. The syllabus names the volume of a gas at constant pressure, and that example appears nowhere in the file. The constant-volume thermometer is sound physics and a legitimate additional example, so the correction did no damage, but it did not supply what the finding asked for. E175 is therefore recorded as not closed.

**Fix.** Name the volume of a gas at constant pressure among the example properties. The constant-volume row may stay as an additional example.

**Evidence.** Cambridge International AS and A Level Physics 9702 syllabus for 2025 to 2027, read complete with pdf.js: 68 pages, ending in the address block. Outcome 14.2.1 asks candidates to state examples of a physical property that varies with temperature, including the density of a liquid, volume of a gas at constant pressure, resistance of a metal and e.m.f. of a thermocouple. The syllabus does not define the word including. - https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/664565-2025-2027-syllabus.pdf
