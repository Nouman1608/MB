## Why the flag was still right to raise

This is the part worth keeping, and it is not a concession.

**A finding that hands over a number its own evidence does not show is a drafting fault
whether or not the number happens to be right.** A corrector cannot tell an unsupported number
from a wrong one without redoing the work — and redoing the work is precisely how the two
flagged corrections of this group were separated. One was genuinely unscoped and has been
closed; this one was merely unshown, and is sound.

The rule stands unchanged: **a correction stating a count must show where the count comes
from.** "Unsupported" and "wrong" look identical from inside the findings record, which is why
both stop the work.
