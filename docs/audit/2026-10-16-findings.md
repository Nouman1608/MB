## Q384 - E48 is withdrawn: the specification it cites places Ijma and Qiyas exactly where the resource does [Questionable claim, High]

- **Files:** `igcse-oxfordaqa-islamiyat-sources-of-authority`
- **Where:** The E48 practice file's description and scope paragraph
- **Locate by:** `title: "OxfordAQA IGCSE Islamiyat: Sources of Authority in Islam (9237)"`

**What is wrong.** E48 said that the official 9237 specification places Ijma and Qiyas at 3.2.2.6, and that the practice file claims 3.2.2.5 without covering schools of thought. The document it cites, version 1.1, was read complete. Sub-topic 3.2.2.5 is Ijma and Qiyas as a source of Shariah, and its whole content is the meaning, role and importance of Ijma and Qiyas in forming religious rulings. Sub-topic 3.2.2.6 is the use of Shariah in everyday life. Schools of thought are named nowhere in the subject content. Both halves of the finding are therefore wrong against its own source, and the file's mapping to 3.2.2.1 to 3.2.2.5 is correct. D-138 and D-144 disputed E48 as a false positive and D-227 deferred it on the same reading, which was right. D-227 deferred it because it could find no text of E48 to quote. The text exists: it is in the evidence file's post_baseline_findings array, not its findings array. That is the same gap that left the four findings in the next finding unworked.

**Fix.** No change to the file. Record E48 as withdrawn by the audit.

**Evidence.** OxfordAQA International GCSE Islamiat 9237 specification, version 1.1, for exams May/June 2026 onwards, read complete in a browser session and parsed with pdf.js: 36 pages, ending in the contact block. 3.2.2.5 is Ijma and Qiyas as a source of Shariah: the meaning, role and importance of Ijma (consensus) and Qiyas (analogy) in forming religious rulings. 3.2.2.6 is the use of Shariah in everyday life. The word school occurs nowhere in the subject content. - https://www.oxfordaqa.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/oxfordaqa-international-gcse-islamiat-specification.pdf
