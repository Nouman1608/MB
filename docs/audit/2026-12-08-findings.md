## E949 - the structured data still says a named person edited the page, which the policy now says is not so [Confirmed error, High]

- **Files:** `src/utils/schema/article.ts`; `src/pages/resources/[slug].astro`; `src/pages/articles/[slug].astro`; `src/content.config.ts` (comments)
- **Where:** the JSON-LD `Article` block on every page with a "Reviewed by" line — 1,987 resources and 1 article

**What is wrong.** D-379 changed what the page says to a reader and left what it says to a machine. Every page with a reviewer still emits:

```json
"editor": { "@type": "Person", "name": "Hina Mogul" }
```

schema.org defines `editor` as the person who edited the work. The site's own position, as of D-379, is that the named teacher did not edit the page — the Academic Team wrote it — and that no dated check is claimed. The structured data therefore asserts to Google, to AI crawlers and to anyone parsing the page exactly the claim the visible text was changed to stop making. This is the E946 shape: a correction applied to one surface and not to its parallel.

The emitting code states the reasoning that has now lapsed: "`editor` is the correct standard CreativeWork property for a named person who checked/edited the content before publication." That was defensible under the old policy. Under the new one the premise is gone, and the property should go with it — schema.org has no "accountable person" property, and substituting `reviewedBy` would re-assert the review claim rather than retire it.

Three comment blocks also still describe the superseded system and will mislead the next person who reads them before the policy:
- `src/pages/resources/[slug].astro:316` — "the genuine named-reviewer byline above, which is untouched".
- `src/content.config.ts` — `reviewStatus` says 'reviewed' means "a human with subject expertise has actually checked the content"; `reviewedByTeachers` describes "the more specific 'Reviewed by [Name]' byline and schema.org `editor` claim it gates ... completely unchanged and untouched"; `reviewedDate` says it records "when a genuine human review actually took place".
- `reviewedDate` itself is kept in the data, which is reasonable — it records when the credit was applied — but its comment should say that, not that a review happened on that date.

**Fix.** Drop `editor` from the Article schema and the two templates that pass it, and rewrite the four comment blocks to match the policy. The visible byline, the profiles and rules [9] and [10] all stay as they are. One rendered check afterwards: no page should emit `"editor"`.

**Note for correction:** do not replace `editor` with `reviewedBy`, `contributor` or any other person property. The policy's position is that the site makes no claim about who checked the page, so the right structured-data representation is no property at all.

**Evidence.** Round 62. Built from `fa3f1a4` (3,082 pages); `grep` for `"editor"` across the built output returns 1,987 resources and 1 article; the JSON-LD block of `a-level-biology-cell-membranes-practice` read in full; `src/utils/schema/article.ts` lines 18–42 and the four comment blocks read; the editorial policy read as built.

## I415 - two matchers answer the same question differently, so one recorded side effect did not happen [Inconsistency, Medium]

- **Files:** `scripts/validate-review-integrity.mjs` (rule [10]); `src/utils/content/subject-teachers.ts`; `src/content/authors/{nouman-ahmed,ameer-hamza,iftikhar-azeemi}.md`
- **Where:** subject matching, and D-379's "side effects" note

**What is wrong.** D-379 records: "Nouman Ahmed, Ameer Hamza and Iftikhar Azeemi now appear as teachers on the MYP Sciences pages. This replaces your September answer of 'no teacher for MYP Sciences'." They do not appear. I built the site before and after D-379 and compared the teacher block on every resource page: those three gained **zero** listings.

The cause is two matchers for the same relationship, which now disagree:

| | compares | "MYP Sciences" vs MYP Sciences pages |
|---|---|---|
| `validate-review-integrity.mjs` rule [10] | profile entry against the subject **slug** `myp-sciences`, as an unordered word set | matches |
| `subject-teachers.ts` | profile entry against the subject **display title** `Sciences (MYP)`, as an ordered normalised string | does not match |

So the three profiles satisfy the validator while changing nothing a reader sees. D-340's "MYP Sciences: none" therefore still stands on the site — by accident, not by decision. Two things follow. The owner should say which they want, because right now the data says one thing and the site shows another. And the divergence is latent: a future profile edit can change the public teacher lists without the validator noticing, or satisfy the validator without changing the lists, in either direction.

**What did change, for the record.** Across the 1,959 resource pages that carry a teacher block, D-379 added **394 new public teacher listings**: Sajawal Zahid on 154 pages, Hina Mogul on 84, Ammar Bilal on 61, Asif Iqbal on 59, Javaid Iqbal Sabri on 22, Farhat ul Ain Sehgal on 14. No teacher was removed from any page and no page gained or lost its block. Each listing follows from the owner's own answer that the person teaches that subject and board, so none is unsupported — but 394 pages now name a teacher who was not named there before, which is a larger public change than the entry's "side effects to know about" conveys.

**Fix.** Decide the MYP Sciences question first, since it is the owner's to make. Then make the two matchers agree — the cleanest way is for `subject-teachers.ts` to use the same word-set comparison as rule [10], which would also make "Sciences (MYP)" and "MYP Sciences" interchangeable everywhere and remove the trap for the next profile edit.

**Evidence.** Round 62. Full builds at `8f5b44f` and `fa3f1a4`; the `subject-teachers` block extracted from all 1,959 resource pages in each and compared by author slug. Both matchers re-implemented and run against the real strings: `normalise("MYP Sciences") === normalise("Sciences (MYP)")` is false; rule [10]'s word-set comparison of `"MYP Sciences"` against `myp-sciences` is true. `matrix.ts:109` gives the display title.
