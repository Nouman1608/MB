## E939 - Syllabus topic lists shown as the official subject content leave out or replace whole topics, sections or options [Confirmed error, Medium]

- **Files:** named in the observation below (not in the frozen baseline snapshot)
- **Where:** src/data/academic/syllabus-topics.ts (rendered on each subject hub as "the official subject content ... reproduced from the syllabus document", and on checklists)

**What is wrong.** The hub template ([subject].astro L647-651) says 'The N topics below are the official subject content of ... reproduced from the syllabus document', and the checklists say they are built from the official topic list. These records do not match their documents at topic level. Cambridge: (1) 9696 uses the four papers as topics 1-4, folds syllabus topics 1-6 under them and leaves out all 8 A Level option topics (7 Tropical environments ... 14 Economic transition) and the 56 numbered subtopics. (2) 9990 uses papers as topics and leaves out the 20 numbered specialist-option topics (1.1 Schizophrenia ... 4.5 Satisfaction at work); its numbers 1.1-1.4, 3.1-3.4, 4.1-4.4 mean different things in the syllabus. (3) 0457 replaces the syllabus's 22-topic list with the three components. (4) 0470 and 2147 give Core option B as 'The Twentieth Century, 1919-1989' and option A without 'the development of modern nation states'; the syllabuses' titles differ (option B covers international relations since 1919). (5) 0680 topic 3 lists 3.1-3.4 and its note says the structure is complete; the syllabus also has 3.5 Oil pollution and 3.6 Plastic pollution. (6) 0417 invents subtopic numbers 3.1 and 3.2 for unnumbered row headings. AQA: (7) 7405 goes from Alkenes to Organic analysis; 3.3.5 Alcohols is missing (33 topics against 34). (8) 7408 leaves out the five optional topics 3.9 Astrophysics to 3.13 Electronics (20 subtopics). (9) 7162 leaves out the Paper 3 options 3.4 Law of contract and 3.5 Human rights, and invents 3.1.1-3.2.5 numbering for unnumbered strands. (10) 8700, 8702, 7702 and 7717 follow the paper and section layout with invented numbers instead of the specifications' subject content (8702 also lacks 3.3 Skills; 7717 lacks 4.3.1 Text selection). (11) 7192, 7182 and 8192 regroup content by paper: 7192 lacks 3.1.3 Theory and Methods and 3.3.1-3.3.2 and folds eight options into two entries; 7182 invents numbers and folds nine options into three; 8192 reorders and drops sub-sections. OCR and Pearson: (12) 9UR0 lists the three papers, not the specification's themes and works. (13) YPH11 leaves out Units 3 and 6, which have their own content sections. (14) 4EA1, 4ET1 and YET01 list no sections for components and units the specifications divide into named sections. OxfordAQA: (15) 9202 has 9 topics; 3.10 Organic chemistry is missing (the syllabuses.ts notes for 9202 say ten). (16) 9630 has 9 topics; the A-level topics 3.10 Magnetic fields, 3.11 Thermal physics, 3.12 Nuclear energy and 3.13 Energy sources (19 subtopics) are missing. (17) 9640 lacks 3.5 Quantitative skills. (18) 9270 lacks 3.3 Non-exam assessment and 3.4 Speaking and listening; 9275 lacks 3.3 Route B (poetry and non-exam assessment), which its own assessment record models. (19) 9635 replaces Unit 1's three sections with two invented 'Unit 1a/1b' entries. (20) 9625/9725 merges sections 3.3.4-3.3.6 into one topic under a name the specification does not use, and holds only the withdrawn 9625 content under a label naming both codes. (21) 9210 stops Programming at 3.2.8 (3.2.9-3.2.13 missing) and 9610 stops Unit 2 at 3.2.6 (3.2.7-3.2.11 missing), while their notes call those the specification's structure. Numbering not as printed in records whose names match (9260, 9237, 9245, 9675, J560, H240, J247, YEC11, YAC11, 4EC1) is listed in I396.

**Fix.** Rebuild each record from the document's subject-content section: every topic and option, the document's own numbers and names, and nothing that is a paper or component rather than content. Where a record cannot be completed, do not describe it on the page as the official subject content.

**Evidence.** Round 43 (2026-09-17). ## E940 - Assessment figures, dates and notes contradict the board documents [Confirmed error, Medium]

- **Files:** named in the observation below (not in the frozen baseline snapshot)
- **Where:** src/data/academic/assessments.ts; syllabuses.ts; syllabus-topics.ts (rendered on the subject hubs, including notes)

**What is wrong.** (1) IB DP Mathematics: analysis and approaches and applications and interpretation, HL Paper 3 is recorded as 75 minutes. Its board summary (L639) says the course covers 'astrophysics and particle physics'; neither term is in the specification. (7) AQA 7182 notes say every paper has four 24-mark sections; Paper 2 has three sections (24, 24, 48). (8) AQA 7137 board summary and notes (syllabuses.ts L856, L859) say AS covers only Unit 3.1; AS Paper 2 assesses Unit 3.2 Managing people and operations, as assessments.ts records. (9) OCR J249 notes say Paper 1/3 covers topics 1-3 plus 7; the specification: P1-P4 and P9, with P5-P8 on Paper 2/4. (10) OCR H431 notes say only Papers 01 and 03 are synoptic, and H436 notes that only Components 01 and 03 assess quality of extended response; OCR marks all three in each. (11) Pearson YLA1 notes put contract and criminal law in Paper 1 (both are Paper 2), and its summary calls it an International AS/Advanced Level, where the specification offers only the full International A Level. (12) Pearson YPH11 notes say IAL Biology needs at least 20% mathematics marks; the Biology specification says 10%. (13) Pearson YEC11 notes call the qualification linear; it is modular, as the same notes later say. (14) Cambridge 2147 notes say Paper 2 is based on a Depth study; the syllabus sets it on a prescribed Core topic. (15) Cambridge 2059 notes say the structure 'genuinely differs from IGCSE 0448'; the 0448 and 2059 syllabuses have the same structure in 2026 and 2028-2030. (16) Cambridge 0680 and 0479 notes say the edition recorded is the only one published; Cambridge publishes a 2025-2026 0680 syllabus (664643) and a 2028-2030 0479 syllabus (744788). (17) Cambridge 0470 notes give 721455 as its 2027-2028 syllabus; 721455 is 2147's, and 0470's is 721327.

**Fix.** Correct each figure, date and note to the document named; for the IB papers use the guides.

## Q424 - Syllabus data layer read record by record (434 records for five boards and 22 IB assessment records): E939, E940, E941, I394, I395, I396, U77 [Questionable claim, Low]

- **Files:** named in the observation below (not in the frozen baseline snapshot)
- **Where:** src/data/academic/syllabuses.ts, assessments.ts, syllabus-topics.ts (main at 5657fdf)
