## Understanding

- I can explain what the amplitude, angular frequency and phase constant in x = A cos(ωt + φ₀) each tell me, and which ones the system fixes. *(Guide: "The solution: x = A cos(ωt + φ₀)")*
- I can state where in the cycle displacement, velocity and acceleration are zero and where each is largest. *(Guide: "Zeros and extremes" and Figure 1; Practice Q1)*
- I can explain why the period of SHM does not depend on the amplitude, and say what does change when A changes. *(Guide: "Amplitude does not change the period"; Practice Q5(d))*
- I can describe the natural frequency of a system and explain why a sinusoidal driving force at that frequency makes the amplitude grow. *(Guide: "Resonance"; Practice Q3, Q7)*

## Calculation

- I can apply Newton's second law to a system and reach d²x/dt² = −ω²x, then read off ω. *(Guide: "From Newton's second law to the SHM equation"; Practice Q5(a)–(b))*
- I can find A and φ₀ from x₀ and v_x0, choosing the correct quadrant. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q4)*
- I can differentiate x(t) to find v_x(t) and a_x(t), and use v_max = Aω and a_max = Aω². *(Guide: "Velocity and acceleration by differentiation"; Practice Q2, Q4)*
- I can find the speed at any position from |v_x| = ω√(A² − x²). *(Practice Q4(b))*
- I can find a spring constant from measured period data using k = mω². *(Practice Q6(d))*

## Reasoning with representations

- I can read A, T, φ₀ and v_max from x–t and v_x–t graphs, and integrate or differentiate to get the other graphs. *(Guide: Worked example 2 and Figure 3; Practice Q6)*
- I can use an a_x–x graph to test for SHM and find ω² from its slope. *(Guide: "Reading SHM from graphs"; Practice Q2)*
- I can sketch lined-up x, v_x and a_x graphs for a given starting condition. *(Practice Q6(c))*
- I can explain why a secant estimate of velocity from data differs from the instantaneous value. *(Practice Q6(e))*
