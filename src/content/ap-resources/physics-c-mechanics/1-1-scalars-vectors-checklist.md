Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/physics-c-mechanics/1-1-scalars-vectors-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/physics-c-mechanics/1-1-scalars-vectors-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can say whether a quantity is a scalar or a vector, and explain my choice by asking whether it has a direction. *(Guide: "Scalars and vectors"; Practice Q1)*
- I can explain the difference between distance and displacement, and between speed and velocity. *(Guide: "Scalars and vectors"; Practice Q7)*
- I can explain why a unit vector has magnitude 1 and no unit. *(Guide: "Position vectors and their unit vectors"; Practice Q5(b))*

## Skills

- I can draw a vector as an arrow to a stated scale and add vectors tip to tail. *(Guide: Figure 1; Practice Q7(a))*
- I can state a positive direction and use + and − signs for vectors along one axis. *(Guide: "One dimension: the sign is the direction"; Practice Q6(a))*
- I can resolve a vector given as a magnitude and direction into components, with correct signs, even when the angle is measured from the y-axis. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q2)*
- I can find the magnitude and direction of a vector from its components, checking the quadrant with a sketch. *(Guide: "Unit vectors and components"; Practice Q4)*
- I can write a position vector r in î, ĵ, k̂ notation, find |r| and the unit vector r̂. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q3)*
- I can find a resultant by adding components, and a displacement by subtracting position vectors. *(Guide: Worked examples 1 and 2; Practice Q4)*

## Reasoning

- I can state the largest and smallest possible resultant of two vectors and use them to check an answer. *(Guide: "Adding vectors by components: the resultant"; Practice Q6)*
- I can derive the magnitude of a symbolic vector and its unit vector. *(Practice Q5)*
- I can explain why average speed is usually larger than the magnitude of average velocity. *(Practice Q7(d))*
