## Recap

- The **net force** is the vector sum of all forces exerted **on** the system. Partners on other objects stay out.
- **Translational equilibrium:** ΣF = 0. Apply it as separate component equations.
- **First law:** if ΣF = 0, the system’s velocity stays constant in size **and** direction. Rest is one case.
- Constant velocity means a = dv/dt = 0 and a straight-line position graph.
- Forces can balance along one axis and not another. Only the velocity component along the unbalanced direction changes.
- An **inertial frame** is one in which the first law is observed to hold. Frames moving at constant velocity relative to it are inertial too; accelerating frames are not.

## Key relationships

| Idea | Statement | Use it to |
|---|---|---|
| Net force | ΣF = F₁ + F₂ + … | combine forces by components |
| Equilibrium | ΣF_x = 0, ΣF_y = 0 (ΣF_z = 0) | find up to two unknowns in 2D |
| First law | ΣF = 0 ⇔ v constant | link forces to motion graphs |
| One axis balanced | ΣF_x = 0 ⇒ v_x constant | treat each axis separately |
| Inertial frame | first law holds | choose where to analyse from |

## Assumptions behind the numbers

- g = 9.8 m/s² near Earth’s surface.
- The ground is treated as an inertial frame unless told otherwise.
- "At rest" or "constant velocity" in a question is your signal that ΣF = 0.

## Mistakes to avoid

1. **Motion needs a force.** Only a change in velocity needs a net force.
2. **Equilibrium = rest.** Constant velocity is equilibrium too.
3. **v = 0 for an instant = equilibrium.** Check the forces, not the velocity.
4. **Constant speed while turning = equilibrium.** Direction is changing, so ΣF ≠ 0.
5. **Mixing components.** Keep x and y equations separate, with signs.
6. **Including third-law partners in ΣF.** They act on other objects.
7. **Reasoning from inside an accelerating vehicle.** Switch to the ground frame.

## Quick self-check

1. Two forces on a puck are (3.0, 0) N and (0, 4.0) N. What third force gives equilibrium, and how big is it? *((−3.0, −4.0) N, size 5.0 N.)*
2. A crate slides across a floor at constant velocity while a worker pushes it horizontally with 60 N. What is the friction force? *(60 N, opposite to the push, because ΣF_x = 0.)*
3. x(t) = 4.0t and y(t) = 2.0 + 1.5t² (m). In which direction are the forces balanced? *(Along x only: v_x is constant, v_y changes.)*
