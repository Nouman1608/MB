Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/physics-c-mechanics/2-9-resistive-forces-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/physics-c-mechanics/2-9-resistive-forces-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can explain what makes a force resistive, give the direction of F_r = −kv for any velocity, and state the unit of k. *(Guide: "What makes a force resistive")*
- I can explain why the constant-acceleration equations do not apply when a resistive force acts. *(Guide: Worked example 1)*
- I can explain terminal velocity as zero net force, not zero force, and say why an object thrown faster than v_T slows down. *(Guide: "Terminal velocity"; Practice Q3)*

## Calculation

- I can find the terminal velocity and the time constant τ = m/k from the forces. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q1)*
- I can write Newton's second law as a differential equation, with signs that match my axis, for coasting, falling and rising objects. *(Guide: "Newton's second law becomes a differential equation"; Practice Q4(a))*
- I can separate variables and integrate between matching limits to find v(t). *(Guide: "Separation of variables"; Practice Q4(b), Q5(b))*
- I can find a(t) by differentiating v(t), and x(t) by integrating with the initial position, including the finite coasting distance mv₀/k. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q5(c))*
- I can find the time to reach a given speed using logarithms, such as the halving time τ ln 2. *(Guide: Worked example 1(c); Practice Q4(c))*

## Reasoning

- I can sketch v–t and a–t graphs, showing the initial slope, the asymptote and the time constant. *(Guide: Figure 1; Practice Q2, Q5(d))*
- I can linearise velocity data with ln v against t to test the model and extract k. *(Practice Q6)*
- I can compare two objects with the same k but different masses at release and at later times. *(Practice Q7)*
