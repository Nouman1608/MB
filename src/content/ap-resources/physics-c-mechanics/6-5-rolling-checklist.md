Work through the list without notes. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/physics-c-mechanics/6-5-rolling-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/physics-c-mechanics/6-5-rolling-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can write the total kinetic energy of a body that moves and spins as translational plus rotational, using I about the centre of mass. *(Guide: "Kinetic energy of a body that moves and spins"; Practice Q2)*
- I can explain why the contact point of a rolling wheel is momentarily at rest and find the velocity of any rim point. *(Guide: Figure 1; Practice Q1)*
- I can explain why static friction dissipates no energy from an ideal rolling body, using the power F · v. *(Guide: "Why static friction does no work here"; Practice Q3)*
- I can explain why kinetic friction on a slipping body dissipates energy, and why v_cm and ω are not linked while it slips. *(Guide: "Rolling while slipping")*

## Calculation

- I can derive Δx_cm = rΔθ, v_cm = rω and a_cm = rα, and write rolling kinetic energy as ½M(1 + β)v_cm². *(Guide: "Rolling without slipping: deriving the links")*
- I can combine F = Ma_cm, τ = I_cm α and a_cm = rα to find the acceleration of a rolling body and the friction on it, with its direction. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q4)*
- I can find the smallest coefficient of static friction that allows rolling without slipping. *(Guide: Worked example 1(c); Practice Q4(b))*
- I can find v_cm(t) and ω(t) for a slipping body, when rolling starts, and the energy dissipated. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q6)*

## Reasoning and skills

- I can plot v² against h for ramp data, find β from the slope and plan a check for slipping. *(Practice Q5)*
- I can explain, with a factor of change, why a body with smaller β rolls down a slope faster. *(Practice Q7)*
- I can use angular momentum about a point on the floor to check the final speed of a slipping ball. *(Practice Q6(d))*
