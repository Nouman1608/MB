Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/physics-c-mechanics/4-1-linear-momentum-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/physics-c-mechanics/4-1-linear-momentum-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can state the definition p = mv, give its unit two ways (kg·m/s and N·s) and explain why momentum points the same way as velocity. *(Guide: "The definition: p = mv")*
- I can explain why the momenta of a system can cancel while the kinetic energies cannot. *(Guide: "Momentum of a system")*
- I can state the condition for the collision model and explain why only the states just before and just after are needed. *(Guide: "Collisions and explosions as models"; Practice Q3)*
- I can explain what makes an interaction an explosion, without needing fire or noise. *(Guide: "Collisions and explosions as models"; Practice Q7(c))*

## Calculation

- I can calculate a momentum with the correct sign in one dimension from a position function. *(Practice Q1)*
- I can find the momentum of a two-object system in two dimensions by components, and give its size and direction with the correct quadrant. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q4)*
- I can differentiate x(t) or r(t) to find momentum as a function of time, and find when it is zero or greatest. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q5)*
- I can use K = p²/(2m) to move between kinetic energy and momentum and to predict factors of change. *(Guide: "Momentum and kinetic energy"; Practice Q2, Q7)*

## Reasoning

- I can sketch a momentum–time graph from a position function and label zeros, turning points and end values. *(Guide: Figure 2; Practice Q5(b))*
- I can justify the collision model with numbers by comparing the velocity changes caused by internal and external forces during contact. *(Guide: the bat-and-ball estimate; Practice Q6)*
- I can explain why an external force that is negligible during an impact may still matter before or after it. *(Practice Q6(d))*
