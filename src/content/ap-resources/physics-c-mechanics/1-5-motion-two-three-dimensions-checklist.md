Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/physics-c-mechanics/1-5-motion-two-three-dimensions-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/physics-c-mechanics/1-5-motion-two-three-dimensions-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can explain how motion in a plane splits into two one-dimensional motions that share only the time. *(Guide: "One motion, two one-dimensional problems")*
- I can explain why a push along one axis does not change the motion along the perpendicular axis. *(Guide: "One motion, two one-dimensional problems"; Practice Q1)*
- I can describe a projectile as having zero horizontal acceleration and constant downward acceleration g, and say what the speed and acceleration are at the top. *(Guide: Figure 1)*
- I can state that this course analyses motion in two dimensions only, and that a z-component would extend the same method (background, not assessed here). *(Guide: "Position, velocity and acceleration as vectors")*

## Calculation

- I can differentiate r(t) component by component to find v(t) and a(t), and give a speed and direction. *(Practice Q2, Q4)*
- I can integrate a component that changes with time, using its own initial condition, while the other component stays uniform. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q4)*
- I can solve a projectile problem where launch and landing heights differ, using the same t in both components. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q5)*
- I can derive the trajectory equation and the level-ground range from a = −g j. *(Guide: "Projectile motion: a special case"; Practice Q6)*

## Reasoning

- I can predict how flight time and landing distance change when the launch height or speed changes. *(Practice Q3, Q6(d))*
- I can say when the range formula R = v₀² sin 2θ / g must not be used. *(Guide: Worked example 1)*
- I can linearise video data, for example y/t against t, to find g and a launch velocity. *(Guide: "Investigating motion in a plane"; Practice Q7)*
- I can design a video experiment that tests whether vertical motion depends on horizontal speed. *(Practice Q7(d))*
