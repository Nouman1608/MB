Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/physics-c-mechanics/5-1-rotational-kinematics-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/physics-c-mechanics/5-1-rotational-kinematics-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can explain why a rotating body must be treated as a rigid system, and when its rotation can be ignored so it can be treated as an object. *(Guide: "Rigid systems and the choice of model"; Practice Q5(d))*
- I can state a positive sense (clockwise or counterclockwise, and from where I am looking) before using any sign. *(Guide: "Angular position and angular displacement")*
- I can explain why every point of a rigid system has the same θ, ω and α. *(Guide: Figure 1)*
- I can explain why the constant-α equations fail when α depends on time. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q4)*

## Calculation

- I can convert between rev, degrees, rpm and radian units before I calculate. *(Guide: "Angular velocity and angular acceleration as derivatives"; Practice Q2)*
- I can differentiate θ(t) to find ω(t) and α(t), with correct units. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q1)*
- I can integrate α(t) to find ω(t) and θ(t), including ω₀ and θ₀. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q4)*
- I can derive the three constant-α equations by integration, including the chain-rule step. *(Guide: "Deriving the constant-α equations")*
- I can find angular displacement and total angle turned from an ω–t graph or from ω(t). *(Guide: Figure 2; Practice Q5(b))*

## Reasoning

- I can decide whether a system is turning clockwise or counterclockwise, and spinning faster or slower, from the signs of ω and α. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q3)*
- I can sketch θ–t, ω–t and α–t graphs for a described rotation and link their slopes and areas. *(Practice Q5)*
- I can predict how the time and angle change when α or the final ω is changed, and spot faulty scaling arguments. *(Guide: Worked example 3; Practice Q7)*
- I can linearise rotation-sensor data to test a model and extract a constant from the slope. *(Practice Q6)*
