Work through the list without notes. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/english-literature-and-composition/4-4-plot-order-and-contrasts-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/english-literature-and-composition/4-4-plot-order-and-contrasts-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can explain the difference between the order of events in a story world and the order in which the text presents them. *(Guide: "Plot as a sequence of choices"; Practice question 1)*
- I can explain why the order in which information is revealed shapes interpretation. *(Guide: "Plot as a sequence of choices"; Practice question 3)*
- I can explain that a contrast emphasises particular traits or characteristics. *(Guide: "Contrasts: what they emphasise"; Practice question 5)*
- I can explain how a contrast can represent a conflict in values. *(Guide: "Contrasts: what they emphasise"; Practice question 6)*

## Skills

- I can list the events of a short text in the order given and mark where the order turns. *(Guide: "A method"; Practice questions 4 and 7)*
- I can identify a flashback, a delay or a repetition and say what it does. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice questions 1, 3 and 7)*
- I can find a contrast between outward behaviour and inner state. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice questions 2 and 6)*
- I can name the trait or value on each side of a contrast. *(Practice questions 5 and 6)*

## Reasoning

- I can say how an ending returns to, reverses or answers the opening. *(Guide: Worked example 2, step 4; Practice question 7)*
- I can explain why a plot-ordering choice produces suspense, irony or sympathy. *(Guide: Worked examples 1 and 2; Practice questions 1 and 8)*
- I can describe order without labelling archetypes or plot diagrams. *(Guide: "Common misconceptions")*
- I can write a paragraph that links order, contrast and effect with an accurate quotation. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice question 8)*
