Work through the list without notes. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/english-literature-and-composition/9-1-characters-who-change-or-remain-unchanged-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/english-literature-and-composition/9-1-characters-who-change-or-remain-unchanged-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can explain the difference between an event that happens to a character and a change in that character. *(Guide: "What does it mean for a character to change?"; Practice Question 1)*
- I can explain why staying the same can be as meaningful as changing. *(Guide: "What does it mean for a character to change?"; Practice Question 3)*
- I can explain why minor characters often remain unchanged and what they do for the work. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Question 3)*
- I can explain how a character's change, or lack of it, affects the interpretation of the whole work. *(Guide: Worked examples 1 and 2; Practice Question 6)*

## Skills

- I can find a baseline for a character using early evidence. *(Guide: "A method"; Practice Question 4)*
- I can identify the pressure that tests the baseline. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Question 4)*
- I can compare a response with the baseline and say whether it is a change, a partial change or no change. *(Guide: "A method"; Practice Question 2)*
- I can quote accurately to show a before and an after. *(Practice Questions 7 and 8)*

## Reasoning

- I can say what a change reveals about a character's values. *(Guide: Worked examples 1 and 2; Practice Question 5)*
- I can explain how two characters' changes work together to support an interpretation. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Question 6)*
- I can compare a weak and a strong sentence about change and say why the strong one scores. *(Guide: "Weak and strong versions side by side")*
- I can write a paragraph on change with a claim, evidence and commentary linked to the whole work. *(Practice Questions 7 and 8)*
