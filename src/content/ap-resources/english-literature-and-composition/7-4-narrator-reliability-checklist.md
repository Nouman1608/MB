Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/english-literature-and-composition/7-4-narrator-reliability-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/english-literature-and-composition/7-4-narrator-reliability-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can explain how a narrator's reliability affects a narrative and a reader's understanding of events and characters. *(Guide: "Why reliability matters"; Practice Question 9)*
- I can name sources of unreliability: self-interest, bias, limited knowledge, emotional state, naivety. *(Guide: "Sources of unreliability"; Practice Questions 3 and 5)*
- I can explain that some narrators provide details that others cannot, and that multiple narrators may contradict each other. *(Guide: "What narrators can and cannot know"; Practice Questions 4 and 8)*
- I can tell a limited narrator from an unreliable one. *(Guide: FAQs and "Sources of unreliability"; Practice Question 5)*

## Skills

- I can list signs of unreliability: self-contradiction, over-insistence, defensive address, mismatch of telling and showing, contradiction by others, gaps. *(Guide: "Signs to look for"; Practice Questions 1, 2 and 3)*
- I can use specific words or phrases as evidence of unreliability. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Questions 1 and 7)*
- I can analyse two contradictory accounts by weighing vantage point, motive and other evidence. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Questions 4 and 8)*
- I can write a paragraph that argues for a narrator's unreliability with evidence. *(Guide: Worked example 1 model sentence; Practice Question 7)*

## Reasoning

- I can explain why an unreliable narrator makes a story richer rather than empty. *(Guide: "Common misconceptions")*
- I can argue that a contradiction is itself the point, when the text supports that reading. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Question 8)*
- I can use a public-domain story, such as The Tell-Tale Heart or The Cask of Amontillado, to illustrate unreliability. *(Guide: "Public-domain examples to learn from"; Practice Questions 1, 2, 6 and 9)*
