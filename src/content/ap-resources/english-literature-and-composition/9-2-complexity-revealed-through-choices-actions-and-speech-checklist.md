Work through the list without notes. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/english-literature-and-composition/9-2-complexity-revealed-through-choices-actions-and-speech-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/english-literature-and-composition/9-2-complexity-revealed-through-choices-actions-and-speech-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can explain what makes a character complex, as opposed to merely difficult or likeable. *(Guide: "Complexity: when a character pulls in more than one direction"; Practice Question 5)*
- I can explain why choices that cost something reveal more than free choices. *(Guide: "Complexity"; Practice Question 1)*
- I can explain why a character's response to the resolution is revealing. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Question 3)*
- I can explain why inconsistency is evidence and not an error. *(Guide: "Complexity"; Practice Question 5)*

## Skills

- I can locate the tension between two values or motives in a character. *(Guide: "A method"; Practice Question 4)*
- I can gather evidence from choice, action and speech. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Questions 4 and 8)*
- I can quote accurately to show a contradiction between word and deed. *(Practice Questions 7 and 8)*
- I can say what a character's complexity does in the work (irony, sympathy, critique). *(Guide: Worked examples 1 and 2; Practice Question 2)*

## Reasoning

- I can explain how a character's complexity affects my reading of the narrator. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Question 6)*
- I can explain how a response to the resolution changes my reading of the ending. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Question 3)*
- I can compare a weak and a strong sentence about complexity and explain the difference. *(Guide: "Weak and strong versions side by side")*
- I can write a paragraph on complexity with a claim, evidence and commentary. *(Practice Questions 7 and 8)*

When every box is ticked, move on to the [9.3 study guide](/advanced-course-resources/english-literature-and-composition/9-3-significant-events-study-guide/).
