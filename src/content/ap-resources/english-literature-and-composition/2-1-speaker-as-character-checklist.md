Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/english-literature-and-composition/2-1-speaker-as-character-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/english-literature-and-composition/2-1-speaker-as-character-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can explain that the speaker of a poem is a created voice, not necessarily the poet. *(Guide: "A poem has a voice"; Practice question 6)*
- I can name five ways a speaker reveals perspective: words, details, organisation of thought, decisions and actions. *(Guide: "A poem has a voice, and the voice is a character")*
- I can explain what an addressee is and why it matters. *(Guide: "Four questions for any speaker"; Practice question 1)*
- I can explain what tone is and how it is shown. *(Guide: "Evidence of perspective in a speaker's language"; Practice question 5)*

## Skills

- I can identify speaker, addressee and occasion in a short poem. *(Guide: Worked example 2, Step 1; Practice question 1)*
- I can find a self-correction or omission and say what it reveals. *(Guide: Worked example 2, Step 3; Practice question 2)*
- I can explain what a repeated word shows about a speaker's mind. *(Guide: Worked example 1, Step 3)*
- I can write a short claim about a speaker with two quoted lines. *(Practice question 7)*

## Reasoning

- I can explain irony in a speaker's reply to another person's word. *(Practice question 3)*
- I can say what a single object, such as an unopened tin, shows about a speaker's wish. *(Practice question 4)*
- I can explain how word choice and the order of thoughts together reveal an attitude. *(Practice question 8)*
