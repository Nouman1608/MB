Work through the list without notes. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/english-literature-and-composition/8-5-allusion-in-poetry-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/english-literature-and-composition/8-5-allusion-in-poetry-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can define an allusion and explain how it differs from a quotation. *(Guide: "What an allusion is"; Practice Question 1)*
- I can explain why allusions create emotional or intellectual associations. *(Guide: "What an allusion is"; Practice Question 5)*
- I can name the three stances a poem can take toward its source: honour, undercut, complicate. *(Guide: "What an allusion is")*
- I can explain why an allusion may depend on where it falls in the poem. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Question 3)*

## Skills

- I can state the source of an allusion in one sentence. *(Guide: "A method for allusion"; Practice Questions 2 and 5)*
- I can choose which associations of the source a poem uses. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Question 2)*
- I can find words near the allusion that show the poem's stance. *(Guide: Worked examples 1 and 2; Practice Question 4)*
- I can reason about an unfamiliar reference from tone and context. *(Guide: "A method for allusion"; Practice Question 6)*

## Reasoning

- I can explain how an allusion changes my reading of the whole poem. *(Guide: Worked examples 1 and 2; Practice Questions 7 and 8)*
- I can explain how a poem can both use and question the source it alludes to. *(Guide: Worked example 2)*
- I can compare a weak and a strong sentence about an allusion and say why the strong one scores. *(Guide: "Weak and strong versions side by side")*
- I can write a paragraph on an allusion with a claim, evidence and commentary. *(Practice Questions 7 and 8)*

When every box is ticked, move on to the [8.6 study guide](/advanced-course-resources/english-literature-and-composition/8-6-thesis-and-commentary-in-a-poetry-essay-study-guide/).
