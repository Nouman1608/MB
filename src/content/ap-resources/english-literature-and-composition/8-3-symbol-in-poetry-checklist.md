Work through the list without notes. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/english-literature-and-composition/8-3-symbol-in-poetry-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/english-literature-and-composition/8-3-symbol-in-poetry-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can explain the difference between a symbol and an image. *(Guide: "What makes something a symbol?"; Practice Question 1)*
- I can explain why a symbol is built by the poem and is not a fixed code. *(Guide: "What makes something a symbol?"; Practice Question 5)*
- I can explain the difference between a symbol and a metaphor. *(Guide: "What makes something a symbol?")*
- I can explain how a symbol can imply a speaker's attitude. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Question 6)*

## Skills

- I can spot a possible symbol by its weight (repetition, position, emotion). *(Guide: "Five questions"; Practice Question 4)*
- I can track how a poem uses a symbol across its lines. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Question 2)*
- I can state what a symbol suggests as a sentence about this poem. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Question 7)*
- I can explain a double meaning in a word that carries a symbol, such as "blackening". *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Question 3)*

## Reasoning

- I can avoid one-to-one decoding and give evidence for what the poem makes the symbol suggest. *(Guide: "Weak and strong versions"; Practice Question 5)*
- I can link a symbol to the speaker's attitude and to the poem's whole meaning. *(Guide: Worked examples 1 and 2; Practice Questions 6 and 8)*
- I can explain how two symbols in one poem work together. *(Guide: Worked example 2)*
- I can write a paragraph on a symbol with a claim, evidence and commentary. *(Practice Questions 7 and 8)*

When every box is ticked, move on to the [8.4 study guide](/advanced-course-resources/english-literature-and-composition/8-4-extended-and-complex-metaphor-study-guide/).
