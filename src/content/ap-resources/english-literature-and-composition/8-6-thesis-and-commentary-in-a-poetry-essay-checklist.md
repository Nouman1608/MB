Work through the list without notes. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/english-literature-and-composition/8-6-thesis-and-commentary-in-a-poetry-essay-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/english-literature-and-composition/8-6-thesis-and-commentary-in-a-poetry-essay-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can explain what a thesis is and why restating the prompt or summarising does not count. *(Guide: "What a defensible thesis is, and is not"; Practice Question 1)*
- I can explain what a line of reasoning is. *(Guide: "From thesis to line of reasoning to paragraphs"; Practice Question 3)*
- I can explain the difference between summary, device-naming and commentary. *(Guide: "The paragraph engine"; Practice Question 4)*
- I can explain what the sophistication point rewards. *(Guide: "A self-assessment rubric"; Practice Question 6)*

## Skills

- I can write a defensible thesis for an unseen poem. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Question 7)*
- I can split a thesis into claims that form a line of reasoning. *(Guide: "From thesis to line of reasoning to paragraphs"; Practice Question 7)*
- I can write a paragraph in the order claim, evidence, commentary, link. *(Guide: "The paragraph engine"; Practice Question 8)*
- I can use the commentary chain: device, effect, meaning, thesis. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Question 4)*

## Reasoning

- I can test my own thesis by asking whether a reasonable reader could disagree. *(Guide: "What a defensible thesis is, and is not")*
- I can end a paragraph with a link that ties the evidence back to the thesis. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Question 5)*
- I can revise a weak paragraph into a strong one and say what changed. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Question 8)*
- I can hold two readings in tension to develop a more complex interpretation. *(Practice Question 6)*

When every box is ticked, move on to the [8.7 study guide](/advanced-course-resources/english-literature-and-composition/8-7-evidence-and-composition-in-a-poetry-essay-study-guide/).
