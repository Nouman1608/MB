Work through the list without notes. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/english-literature-and-composition/9-6-thesis-and-commentary-in-a-literary-argument-essay-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/english-literature-and-composition/9-6-thesis-and-commentary-in-a-literary-argument-essay-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can explain what the literary argument prompt asks, and why the claim must be about the whole work. *(Guide: "What the literary argument essay asks of you")*
- I can explain what a defensible thesis is for a whole-work argument and why a restatement or a plot summary does not count. *(Guide: "A defensible thesis about a whole work"; Practice Question 1)*
- I can explain what a line of reasoning is. *(Guide: "From thesis to line of reasoning"; Practice Question 2)*
- I can explain what the sophistication point rewards. *(Guide: "Sophistication: what it is and is not"; Practice Question 6)*

## Skills

- I can choose a work that offers rich, varied evidence for a given prompt. *(Guide: "What the literary argument essay asks of you"; Practice Question 5)*
- I can write a thesis and three claims for a prompt. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Question 7)*
- I can use precise description as evidence when I cannot recall exact words, and never invent a quotation. *(Guide: "Commentary on evidence from memory"; Practice Question 3)*
- I can use the commentary chain: evidence, what it shows, how it supports the claim, how that supports the thesis. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Question 4)*

## Reasoning

- I can choose a pattern for a line of reasoning (development, layers, cause and consequence) to suit the work. *(Guide: "From thesis to line of reasoning")*
- I can develop a tension or alternative reading without losing my thesis. *(Guide: "Sophistication: what it is and is not"; Practice Question 6)*
- I can revise a weak paragraph into a strong one and say what changed. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Question 8)*
- I can write a body paragraph with a claim, specific evidence, commentary and a link to the thesis. *(Practice Question 8)*

When every box is ticked, move on to the [9.7 study guide](/advanced-course-resources/english-literature-and-composition/9-7-selecting-evidence-for-a-literary-argument-study-guide/).
