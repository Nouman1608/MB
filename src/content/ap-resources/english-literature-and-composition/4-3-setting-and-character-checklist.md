Work through the list without notes. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/english-literature-and-composition/4-3-setting-and-character-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/english-literature-and-composition/4-3-setting-and-character-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can say what setting includes: time, place, weather, season, era, social world and objects. *(Guide: "What setting is, and what it does"; Practice question 1)*
- I can explain how setting helps establish mood and atmosphere. *(Guide: "What setting is, and what it does"; Practice question 2)*
- I can explain how the environment a character inhabits gives information about that character. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice question 4)*
- I can explain why a setting that contrasts with a character's feelings is still meaningful. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice questions 3 and 5)*

## Skills

- I can work through detail, mood, character and meaning for a setting. *(Guide: "A method"; Practice questions 6 and 7)*
- I can link a mood to specific words, not just to a general impression. *(Guide: Worked example 1, step 2; Practice questions 1 and 2)*
- I can describe the relationship between a character and a place using two or more details. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice questions 6 and 8)*
- I can distinguish describing a setting from explaining its function. *(Guide: "What setting is, and what it does"; Practice question 8)*

## Reasoning

- I can say when setting matches a character's state and when it contrasts with it. *(Guide: Worked examples 1 and 2; Practice questions 3 and 5)*
- I can tell when a detail is merely scenic and when the text invites a bigger claim. *(Guide: "Common misconceptions"; Practice question 4)*
- I can choose the two or three details that carry a setting's meaning. *(Guide: "Common misconceptions"; Practice question 8)*
- I can write a paragraph that links setting details, mood and a character's situation. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice question 8)*
