Work through the list without notes. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/english-literature-and-composition/8-4-extended-and-complex-metaphor-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/english-literature-and-composition/8-4-extended-and-complex-metaphor-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can explain the difference between a simile, a metaphor, an extended metaphor and a conceit. *(Guide: "One comparison, developed"; Practice Question 1)*
- I can name the tenor and vehicle of a metaphor. *(Guide: "One comparison, developed"; Practice Questions 1 and 4)*
- I can explain why strain or surprise in a conceit is part of its effect. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Question 6)*
- I can explain why a conceit's elements must be read together and not as a list. *(Guide: "The two-step skill")*

## Skills

- I can unpack an individual element of a metaphor and say what it suggests. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Questions 2 and 4)*
- I can quote the words that show each element. *(Practice Questions 7 and 8)*
- I can explain how two elements of a metaphor combine to make a larger meaning. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Questions 3 and 5)*
- I can explain how two separate comparisons in one poem support each other. *(Guide: Worked example 1, "How the elements combine")*

## Reasoning

- I can link an extended metaphor to the speaker's argument or attitude. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Question 7)*
- I can link an extended metaphor to the whole poem, including its ending. *(Guide: Worked examples 1 and 2; Practice Question 8)*
- I can compare a weak and a strong sentence about a metaphor and explain why the strong one scores. *(Guide: "Weak and strong versions side by side")*
- I can write a paragraph on an extended metaphor with a claim, evidence and commentary. *(Practice Questions 7 and 8)*

When every box is ticked, move on to the [8.5 study guide](/advanced-course-resources/english-literature-and-composition/8-5-allusion-in-poetry-study-guide/).
