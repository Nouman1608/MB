Work through the list without notes. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/english-literature-and-composition/4-2-character-relationships-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/english-literature-and-composition/4-2-character-relationships-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can explain what "nuance" and "complexity" mean when I write about a relationship. *(Guide: "What nuance and complexity mean"; Practice question 2)*
- I can explain how conflict between characters can come from different value systems even when they care about each other. *(Guide: "What nuance and complexity mean"; Practice question 3)*
- I can explain why politeness, silence and avoidance count as evidence of tension. *(Guide: "What nuance and complexity mean"; Practice question 1)*
- I can explain why a kind character can still belong to a complicated relationship. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice question 5)*

## Skills

- I can work through surface, undercurrent, evidence and meaning for a relationship. *(Guide: "A method"; Practice question 6)*
- I can list the places where evidence of a relationship appears: speech, action, avoidance, gestures, narration, change. *(Guide: "What nuance and complexity mean"; Practice question 4)*
- I can compare what a character says with what the character does. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice questions 4 and 6)*
- I can use "and yet", "but" or "while" to hold both layers of a relationship in one sentence. *(Guide: "A method"; Practice question 8)*

## Reasoning

- I can explain what a nuanced relationship adds to a story's meaning. *(Guide: Worked example 1, step 4; Practice question 8)*
- I can tell when a detail shows affection inside a disagreement. *(Practice question 7)*
- I can check the narrator's summary against what the scene shows. *(Guide: "Common misconceptions"; Practice questions 2 and 5)*
- I can write a paragraph on a relationship with two accurate quotations and commentary. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice question 8)*
