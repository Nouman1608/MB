Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/english-literature-and-composition/7-7-thesis-commentary-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/english-literature-and-composition/7-7-thesis-commentary-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can explain that a thesis expresses an interpretation that needs a defence through evidence and a line of reasoning. *(Guide: "From claim to argument"; Practice Question 1)*
- I can explain that commentary shows the logical relationship between a thesis and the claims and evidence. *(Guide: "The commentary chain"; Practice Question 5)*
- I can name three sophistication moves: broader context, alternative interpretation and analogy. *(Guide: "Sophistication moves at a glance"; Practice Questions 2, 3 and 4)*
- I can explain why a sophistication move must be integrated and supported by evidence. *(Guide: "Common misconceptions"; Practice Question 7)*

## Skills

- I can build a thesis from a weak draft step by step. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Questions 1 and 7)*
- I can write commentary that discusses an alternative interpretation and answers it. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Questions 2 and 8)*
- I can link an interpretation to a broader context using evidence from the text. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Question 3)*
- I can choose an analogy that clarifies a point, and reject one that does not fit. *(Guide: "Sophistication moves at a glance"; Practice Question 4)*

## Reasoning

- I can revise a thesis when my evidence contradicts it. *(Guide: FAQs; Practice Question 6)*
- I can use the self-assessment table to rate a draft. *(Guide: "Self-assessment table")*
- I can plan a thesis, a line of reasoning and a sophistication move for a short story. *(Practice Question 9)*
