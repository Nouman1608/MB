Work through the list without notes. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/english-literature-and-composition/3-5-conflict-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/english-literature-and-composition/3-5-conflict-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can define conflict as tension between competing values. *(Guide: "Conflict is competing values under pressure")*
- I can tell internal conflict from external conflict. *(Guide: "Conflict is competing values under pressure"; Practice questions 1 and 2)*
- I can explain how two conflicts can intersect and heighten one another. *(Guide: "Several conflicts at once"; Practice question 3)*
- I can explain how an inconsistency can represent a conflict of values. *(Guide: "Inconsistency as evidence of conflict")*

## Skills

- I can name the values on each side of a conflict. *(Guide: "Naming the values"; Practice question 8)*
- I can use stage directions as evidence of an unspoken internal conflict. *(Practice question 2)*
- I can find a contradiction between words and action and say what it shows. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice question 4)*
- I can write a short explanation of intersecting conflicts using two details. *(Practice question 7)*

## Reasoning

- I can explain how two characters using the same word reveal a conflict of values. *(Guide: Worked example 1, Step 3)*
- I can explain how a conflict makes a character's decision more or less likely. *(Guide: Worked example 1, Step 5)*
- I can explain why a belief that serves a purpose resists correction. *(Practice questions 5 and 6)*
