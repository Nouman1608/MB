Work through the list without notes. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/english-literature-and-composition/3-7-commentary-and-line-of-reasoning-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/english-literature-and-composition/3-7-commentary-and-line-of-reasoning-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can explain that commentary shows the relationships among evidence, line of reasoning and thesis. *(Guide: "What commentary is")*
- I can tell evidence, paraphrase and commentary apart. *(Guide: "Evidence, paraphrase and commentary"; Practice questions 1 and 3)*
- I can describe four levels of commentary and aim for the top two. *(Guide: "Levels of commentary")*
- I can define a line of reasoning as a logical sequence of claims that defends a thesis. *(Guide: "The line of reasoning"; Practice question 4)*

## Skills

- I can write commentary that says what the evidence shows, how it works and why it matters. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice question 6)*
- I can name the writer's choice that my commentary explains. *(Guide: Worked example 1, Step 2; Practice question 2)*
- I can rewrite thin commentary as stronger commentary. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice question 7)*
- I can order three claims into a line of reasoning. *(Guide: "A line of reasoning in outline"; Practice question 8)*

## Reasoning

- I can use a transition to show how one claim builds on another. *(Practice question 5)*
- I can check that claims do not repeat each other. *(Guide: "The line of reasoning")*
- I can explain how a line of reasoning may qualify an earlier claim. *(Guide: "The line of reasoning")*
