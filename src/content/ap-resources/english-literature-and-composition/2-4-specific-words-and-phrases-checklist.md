Work through the list without notes. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/english-literature-and-composition/2-4-specific-words-and-phrases-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/english-literature-and-composition/2-4-specific-words-and-phrases-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can define antecedent and referent and give an example. *(Guide: "Antecedents and referents")*
- I can explain what makes a referent ambiguous. *(Guide: "Ambiguous referents"; Practice question 6)*
- I can explain what repetition does in a poem. *(Guide: "Repetition"; Practice question 3)*
- I can define alliteration as a repeated sound, not a repeated letter. *(Guide: "Alliteration"; Practice question 1)*

## Skills

- I can find the antecedent of a pronoun, including when it is in the title. *(Guide: Worked example 1, Step 1)*
- I can name two readings of an ambiguous pronoun. *(Guide: Worked example 2, Steps 1 and 2; Practice question 7)*
- I can notice a small change in a repeated word and say what it adds. *(Practice question 5)*
- I can tie a sound pattern to the meaning of the words around it. *(Guide: Worked example 1, Step 2; Practice question 2)*

## Reasoning

- I can explain what an ambiguity does to the reader's interpretation. *(Guide: Worked example 2, Step 3; Practice question 9)*
- I can explain how repetition builds an image of absence or loneliness. *(Practice questions 4 and 8)*
- I can replace "the poet uses alliteration" with a sentence on what the sound reinforces. *(Guide: "Strong and weak statements about words and phrases")*
