Work through the list without notes. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/english-literature-and-composition/4-1-contrasting-characters-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/english-literature-and-composition/4-1-contrasting-characters-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can define protagonist, antagonist and contrasting characters in my own words. *(Guide: "Why writers contrast characters"; Practice question 2)*
- I can explain why a contrast of values matters more than a contrast of appearance or habit. *(Guide: "Why writers contrast characters"; Practice question 1)*
- I can explain why an antagonist may be a society, nature or an inner conflict and need not be a villain. *(Guide: "Why writers contrast characters" and Worked example 2; Practice questions 2 and 7)*
- I can say the difference between describing a contrast and explaining its function. *(Guide: "A method"; Practice question 5)*

## Skills

- I can work through detail, difference, value and effect for a pair of characters. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice question 6)*
- I can name the value each character stands for from specific textual details. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice questions 1 and 6)*
- I can find the antagonist in a short summary or passage and say what kind it is. *(Practice questions 2 and 7)*
- I can choose short, accurate quotations that show a value, not just a trait. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice question 8)*

## Reasoning

- I can state a defensible claim about what a contrast does for the conflict or theme. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice questions 3 and 8)*
- I can show how a contrast can change or soften as a story goes on. *(Guide: "Common misconceptions"; Practice question 3)*
- I can show how two readings of the same moment (such as grief and joy) expose a contrast between characters. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice question 4)*
- I can write a paragraph that places evidence from both characters and connects it with commentary. *(Practice question 8)*
