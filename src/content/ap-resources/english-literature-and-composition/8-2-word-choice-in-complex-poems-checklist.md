Work through the list without notes. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/english-literature-and-composition/8-2-word-choice-in-complex-poems-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/english-literature-and-composition/8-2-word-choice-in-complex-poems-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can explain the difference between denotation and connotation. *(Guide: "Why this word and not another?"; Practice Question 3)*
- I can explain what ambiguity is and why it is different from vagueness. *(Guide: "Why this word and not another?"; Practice Question 4)*
- I can explain how coined or fused words (compounds, inventions) contribute to meaning. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Question 1)*
- I can explain what a word choice reveals about the speaker's attitude. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Question 7)*

## Skills

- I can compare a poet's word with a plainer alternative and say what changes. *(Guide: Figure 1; Practice Question 3)*
- I can set out two readings of an ambiguous word, each supported by context. *(Guide: "When evidence is ambiguous"; Practice Question 4)*
- I can use the Reading A, Reading B, tension, revised claim template. *(Guide: "When evidence is ambiguous"; Practice Questions 6 and 8)*
- I can quote a word or phrase accurately and embed it in my own sentence. *(Practice Questions 7 and 8)*

## Reasoning

- I can show how a connotation links one word to the poem's whole meaning. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Question 5)*
- I can revise my claim in response to ambiguous evidence, not only acknowledge an alternative. *(Guide: "When evidence is ambiguous"; Practice Question 6)*
- I can explain why a polite or calm tone can be unsettling when the diction has a second reading. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Question 2)*
- I can write a paragraph on diction with a claim, quoted evidence and commentary. *(Practice Questions 7 and 8)*

When every box is ticked, move on to the [8.3 study guide](/advanced-course-resources/english-literature-and-composition/8-3-symbol-in-poetry-study-guide/).
