Work through the list without notes. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/english-literature-and-composition/8-1-structure-and-contrasts-in-poetry-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/english-literature-and-composition/8-1-structure-and-contrasts-in-poetry-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can explain what "structure" means in a poem beyond naming a rhyme scheme. *(Guide: "Structure and contrast")*
- I can explain how enjambment and end-stopping change the pace and feeling of a passage. *(Guide: "Structure and contrast"; Practice Question 3)*
- I can explain why an interruption of an established pattern creates a point of emphasis. *(Guide: Figure 1; Practice Question 3)*
- I can distinguish juxtaposition, situational irony, verbal irony and paradox. *(Guide: "Three kinds of contrast"; Practice Questions 2, 4, 6)*

## Skills

- I can find the volta of a poem from its thought and explain what it turns from and to. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Questions 1 and 8)*
- I can explain what a punctuation mark such as a semicolon, dash or colon does in a specific line. *(Guide: "Structure and contrast"; Practice Questions 5 and 7)*
- I can follow the five-step method (paraphrase, mark turns, name the contrast, explain the effect, link to the whole). *(Guide: "A method for structure and contrast")*
- I can quote a short phrase accurately as evidence for a structural claim. *(Practice Questions 7 and 8)*

## Reasoning

- I can explain how a juxtaposition produces irony, using evidence from the poem. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Question 4)*
- I can turn a structural observation into a claim about the poem's overall meaning. *(Guide: Worked examples 1 and 2; Practice Question 8)*
- I can compare a weak and a strong sentence about structure and say why the strong one earns more credit. *(Guide: "Weak and strong versions side by side")*
- I can write a paragraph on structure that has a claim, evidence and commentary. *(Practice Questions 7 and 8)*

When every box is ticked, move on to the [8.2 study guide](/advanced-course-resources/english-literature-and-composition/8-2-word-choice-in-complex-poems-study-guide/).
