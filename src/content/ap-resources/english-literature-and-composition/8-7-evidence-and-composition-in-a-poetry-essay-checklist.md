Work through the list without notes. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/english-literature-and-composition/8-7-evidence-and-composition-in-a-poetry-essay-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/english-literature-and-composition/8-7-evidence-and-composition-in-a-poetry-essay-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can name the six jobs evidence can do: illustrate, clarify, exemplify, associate, amplify, qualify. *(Guide: "What evidence can do"; Practice Questions 5 and 7)*
- I can explain what makes evidence effective and what makes it sufficient. *(Guide: "Evidence and composition"; Practice Question 4)*
- I can explain why interpretation is a recursive process. *(Guide: "Evidence and composition")*
- I can explain why composition errors that interfere with meaning limit the top score. *(Guide: "Composition under time pressure"; Practice Question 6)*

## Skills

- I can choose the smallest quotation that carries a claim. *(Guide: "Choosing evidence"; Practice Question 1)*
- I can embed a quotation grammatically and keep the poet's wording. *(Guide: "Integrating quotations"; Practice Question 2)*
- I can show a line break with a slash and use the present tense. *(Guide: "Integrating quotations"; Practice Question 3)*
- I can build an evidence ledger from an unseen poem. *(Guide: "Choosing evidence"; Practice Question 7)*

## Reasoning

- I can audit a weak paragraph for relevance, quantity and commentary. *(Guide: Worked example 1)*
- I can judge whether evidence is spread widely enough for a claim about the whole poem. *(Guide: "Choosing evidence"; Practice Question 4)*
- I can plan a 40-minute essay with realistic time allocations. *(Guide: "Composition under time pressure")*
- I can write a body paragraph with embedded quotations, commentary and a link to the thesis. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Question 8)*

When every box is ticked, try the [Unit 8 diagnostic](/advanced-course-resources/english-literature-and-composition/unit-8-diagnostic/).
