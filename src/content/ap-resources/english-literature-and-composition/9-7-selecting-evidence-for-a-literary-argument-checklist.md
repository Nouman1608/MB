Work through the list without notes. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/english-literature-and-composition/9-7-selecting-evidence-for-a-literary-argument-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/english-literature-and-composition/9-7-selecting-evidence-for-a-literary-argument-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can explain what makes evidence relevant, specific, accurate, analysable, placed and purposeful. *(Guide: "What makes a piece of evidence good"; Practice Question 1)*
- I can explain why evidence for a whole-work claim must come from more than one part of the work. *(Guide: "Evidence for an argument about a whole work"; Practice Questions 2 and 5)*
- I can explain the three forms of evidence (quotation, close paraphrase, precise reference) and when to use each. *(Guide: "Three forms of evidence"; Practice Question 3)*
- I can explain why I must never invent a quotation. *(Guide: "Three forms of evidence"; Practice Question 3)*

## Skills

- I can build an evidence ledger for a work I know well. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Question 7)*
- I can choose three or four scenes from different parts of a work for a given prompt. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Question 2)*
- I can assign a job (illustrate, exemplify, amplify, qualify, and so on) to each piece of evidence. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Question 7)*
- I can write a ledger entry that gives location, evidence, what it shows and the prompts it serves. *(Guide: "Building a ledger"; Practice Question 6)*

## Reasoning

- I can reject vivid evidence that is irrelevant to the claim. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Question 7)*
- I can use qualifying evidence to complicate and strengthen my thesis. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Question 4)*
- I can diagnose a weak paragraph as insufficient, irrelevant or unexplained. *(Guide: "Weak and strong versions side by side"; Practice Question 5)*
- I can write a body paragraph with embedded evidence, commentary and a link to the thesis. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Question 8)*

When every box is ticked, try the [Unit 9 diagnostic](/advanced-course-resources/english-literature-and-composition/unit-9-diagnostic/).
