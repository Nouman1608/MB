Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/english-literature-and-composition/5-2-literal-and-figurative-meaning-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/english-literature-and-composition/5-2-literal-and-figurative-meaning-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can define literal and figurative meaning and give a quick test for telling them apart. *(Guide: "Literal and figurative meaning"; Practice question 1)*
- I can explain denotation and connotation. *(Guide: "Specific words and phrases"; Practice question 4)*
- I can explain how words with multiple meanings add nuance. *(Guide: "Specific words and phrases"; Practice question 3)*
- I can explain what hyperbole and understatement each do. *(Guide: "Hyperbole and understatement"; Practice questions 1 and 5)*

## Skills

- I can work through literal, figurative, word and effect. *(Guide: "A method"; Practice question 6)*
- I can test a word against an alternative to show its function. *(Guide: "Testing a word against its alternatives"; Practice question 4)*
- I can describe how adjectives and adverbs shape a reader's response. *(Guide: "Specific words and phrases"; Practice question 4)*
- I can identify understatement and the gap it creates. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice questions 5 and 6)*

## Reasoning

- I can say what hyperbole conveys about a speaker's perspective. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice question 7)*
- I can explain how plain speech can earn trust for a final claim. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice question 8)*
- I can say how a poem moves between literal and figurative layers. *(Guide: Worked example 1, step 4; Practice question 7)*
- I can write a paragraph on word choice with accurate quotations and commentary. *(Practice question 8)*
