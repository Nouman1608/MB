Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/english-literature-and-composition/3-2-changing-and-static-characters-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/english-literature-and-composition/3-2-changing-and-static-characters-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can define dynamic and static characters. *(Guide: "Change is shown by comparison")*
- I can explain the difference between external and internal change. *(Guide: "Change is shown by comparison"; Practice question 4)*
- I can explain why I need two points in the text to show change. *(Guide: "How to show that a character has changed")*
- I can explain how a dynamic character's choices affect the climax or resolution. *(Guide: "Why change matters to the plot"; Practice question 7)*

## Skills

- I can compare an early and a late detail and say what has changed. *(Guide: Worked examples 1 and 2; Practice questions 1 to 3)*
- I can name a turning point. *(Guide: Worked example 1, Step 2)*
- I can notice continuity beneath a change. *(Practice question 5)*
- I can write a short explanation of change using two pairs of details. *(Practice question 8)*

## Reasoning

- I can explain why a writer repeats details to make change visible. *(Guide: Worked example 3; Practice question 6)*
- I can explain what a static character does in a work. *(Guide: "Why a character may stay the same"; Practice question 9)*
- I can describe a change for the worse and explain what it shows. *(Guide: Worked example 2)*
