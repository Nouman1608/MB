Work through the list without notes. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/computer-science-a/2-5-compound-boolean-expressions-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/computer-science-a/2-5-compound-boolean-expressions-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can state when `!a`, `a && b` and `a || b` are true. *(Guide: "Combining true and false"; Practice question 1)*
- I can state the precedence of `!`, `&&` and `||`. *(Guide: "Precedence"; Practice question 2)*
- I can explain short-circuit evaluation for `&&` and for `||`. *(Guide: "Short-circuit evaluation"; Practice questions 4 and 5)*
- I can explain why `x == 1 || 2` does not compile and why `x > 3 || x < 8` is always true. *(Guide: "Writing ranges"; Practice question 6)*

## Skills

- I can evaluate a compound Boolean expression for given values, showing each part. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice questions 2 and 7)*
- I can write a range test and an outside-the-range test. *(Guide: "Writing ranges"; Practice questions 3 and 8)*
- I can complete a truth table for an expression with three variables. *(Practice question 9)*
- I can translate rules with "and", "or", "unless" and "not" into one Boolean expression. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice questions 8 and 10)*

## Reasoning

- I can use a guard on the left of `&&` to make the right side safe. *(Guide: "Short-circuit evaluation"; Practice question 5)*
- I can say which method calls in a compound condition are actually executed. *(Practice question 4)*
- I can explain how adding brackets changes the result of a condition. *(Guide: Worked example 1, items 4 and 5; Practice question 10)*
