Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/computer-science-a/2-6-comparing-boolean-expressions-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/computer-science-a/2-6-comparing-boolean-expressions-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can state what it means for two Boolean expressions to be equivalent. *(Guide: "When are two conditions the same?"; Practice question 7)*
- I can state both De Morgan's laws. *(Guide: "De Morgan's laws"; Practice question 1)*
- I can explain what `==` compares for objects, and what `equals` compares. *(Guide: "Comparing object references"; Practice questions 3 and 6)*
- I can explain what `null` means and why a method must not be called on it. *(Guide: "null references"; Practice question 4)*

## Skills

- I can build a truth table for expressions of two or three variables. *(Guide: "Proving equivalence with a truth table"; Practice question 7)*
- I can rewrite a negated compound condition using De Morgan's law and relational complements. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice questions 1, 5 and 8)*
- I can trace references through assignments, including aliasing and reassignment. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice questions 6 and 9)*
- I can write a null-safe condition by putting the null test first with `&&` or `||`. *(Guide: "null references"; Practice question 10)*

## Reasoning

- I can show that two expressions are not equivalent by finding one row that differs. *(Guide: Worked example 1(c); Practice questions 2 and 7)*
- I can say why the complement of `<` is `>=`, using a boundary value. *(Guide: "De Morgan's laws"; Practice question 5)*
- I can predict which calls are never made because of short-circuit evaluation. *(Practice questions 4, 9 and 10)*
