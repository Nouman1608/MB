Short on time? This page is the recap. For the proofs, a step-by-step diagram and worked examples, use the [full study guide](/advanced-course-resources/chemistry/7-6-properties-equilibrium-constant-study-guide/).

## Recap

- A value of K belongs to **one equation written one way**. Rewrite the equation and K changes.
- Coefficients become **powers** in the expression; that is why the rules work the way they do.
- To find K for an **overall multistep process**, add the steps and multiply their K values. Intermediates cancel.
- Q has the same mathematical form as K, so every rule also applies to Q.

## Key relationships

| Change to the equation | New K | Example (K = 100) |
|---|---|---|
| Reverse | 1/K | 0.010 |
| Multiply all coefficients by 2 | K² | 1.0 × 10⁴ |
| Multiply all coefficients by ½ | √K | 10 |
| Multiply by c (general) | Kᶜ | 100ᶜ |
| Add equation 1 and equation 2 | K₁ × K₂ | 100 × K₂ |
| Reverse and multiply by c | K⁻ᶜ = 1/Kᶜ | for c = ½: 0.10 |

Parallel with Hess's law (Topic 6.9): ΔH changes sign, is multiplied by c, and adds. K inverts, is raised to the power c, and multiplies.

## Assumptions behind the rules

- Every K is at the **same temperature**.
- Every K is the **same type** (all Kc or all Kp). Do not convert between them.
- Pure solids and liquids are left out of every expression, so they never affect the result.

## Mistakes to avoid

1. **Doubling K** when the equation is doubled. Square it.
2. **Adding K values** when equations are added. Multiply them.
3. **Negative K** after reversing. Take the reciprocal; K is always positive.
4. **Halving K** when the equation is halved. Take the square root.
5. **Forgetting the power** on a step you multiplied before adding.
6. **Leaving an intermediate uncancelled.** Check that the summed equation matches the target exactly.

## Quick self-check

An equation has K = 2.0 × 10⁻³.

1. What is K for the reverse equation? *(5.0 × 10²)*
2. What is K if all coefficients are tripled? *(8.0 × 10⁻⁹)*
3. What is K if all coefficients are halved? *(0.045)*
4. Two steps with K = 4.0 and K = 0.50 add to give an overall equation. What is the overall K? *(2.0)*
