## Understanding

- I can explain what a resonance hybrid is, and why the molecule does not switch between its resonance structures. *(Guide: "The resonance hybrid")*
- I can explain why resonance is needed to predict bond lengths, bond energies and where the charge is. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q5)*
- I can explain what formal charge assumes, and why it is not the real charge on an atom. *(Guide: "Formal charge")*
- I can explain why species with an odd number of valence electrons show a limitation of the Lewis model. *(Guide: Worked example 3; Practice Q7)*

## Skills

- I can draw all the equivalent resonance structures of a molecule or ion. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q4, Q5)*
- I can calculate the average bond order from resonance structures, including cases such as nitrate where it is not 1.5. *(Practice Q2)*
- I can calculate the formal charge on every atom and check that the sum equals the overall charge. *(Practice Q1, Q6)*
- I can rank bond lengths using bond orders from Lewis diagrams. *(Practice Q3, Q5)*

## Reasoning

- I can use formal charge to choose between two possible skeletons. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q6)*
- I can rank nonequivalent resonance structures using the octet rule, the size of the formal charges and electronegativity. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q4)*
- I can explain what to do when the octet rule and formal charge favour different diagrams. *(Guide: "When the octet rule and formal charge disagree")*
- I can evaluate a claim about a molecule's structure using resonance or formal charge as evidence. *(Practice Q5, Q7)*
