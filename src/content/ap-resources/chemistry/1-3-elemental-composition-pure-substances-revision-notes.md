## Recap

- Some pure substances are made of **separate molecules** (H₂O, CO₂). Others are atoms or ions held together in a fixed ratio, described by a **formula unit** (NaCl, SiO₂).
- **Law of definite proportions:** every pure sample of a compound has the same mass ratio of its elements. Pure water is always 11.19% H and 88.81% O by mass.
- Different compounds of the same elements have different ratios (H₂O₂ is 5.93% H).
- The **empirical formula** is the lowest whole-number ratio of atoms. C₆H₁₂O₆ → CH₂O.
- Mass composition fixes the empirical formula only. Compounds that share an empirical formula (C₂H₂ and C₆H₆) have the same percentages.

## Key relationships

| Relationship | Use it to |
|---|---|
| % element = (atoms in formula × atomic mass) ÷ M × 100 | find percentage composition from a formula |
| mass of element = mass of sample × (% element ÷ 100) | predict an element's mass in any pure sample |
| n = m ÷ atomic mass, for each element | turn element masses into moles |
| mole ratio = each n ÷ smallest n | find the atom ratio |

**Whole-number fixes:** .50 → × 2; .33 or .67 → × 3; .25 or .75 → × 4; .20, .40, .60, .80 → × 5.

## Assumptions behind the numbers

- The sample is **pure**. A mixture can have any composition.
- In a "metal + oxygen" experiment, the reaction is complete and the mass gain is all oxygen.
- Masses are accurate enough that each ratio lands within a few hundredths of a simple fraction.

## Mistakes to avoid

1. **Comparing percentages as if they were atom ratios.** Convert to moles first.
2. **Rounding 1.5 to 2 or 1.33 to 1.** Multiply the whole ratio instead.
3. **Dividing by atomic number.** Use atomic mass.
4. **Using the product's mass as the oxygen mass.** Oxygen mass = product mass − metal mass.
5. **Calling NaCl a molecule.** It is a formula unit in a lattice.
6. **Rounding mole values early.** Keep four significant figures until the ratio step.

## Quick self-check

1. What is the empirical formula of N₂O₄? *(NO₂)*
2. What percentage of CO₂ is carbon by mass? *(27.29%)*
3. A compound is 30.45% N and 69.55% O by mass. What is its empirical formula? *(NO₂)*
4. A mole ratio comes out as P 1.000 : O 2.500. What is the empirical formula? *(P₂O₅)*
