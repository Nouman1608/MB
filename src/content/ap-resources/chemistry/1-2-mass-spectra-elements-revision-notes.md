## Recap

- **Isotopes** are atoms of the same element (same number of protons) with different numbers of neutrons, so they have different masses.
- A mass spectrometer turns atoms into **+1 ions**, separates them by **mass-to-charge ratio (m/z)** and counts them.
- For a sample of **one element**: number of peaks = number of isotopes; m/z of each peak = isotope mass (amu); peak height = relative abundance.
- The **average atomic mass** on the periodic table is a weighted average of the isotope masses. It is also the molar mass in g mol⁻¹.

## Key relationships

| Relationship | Meaning | Use it to |
|---|---|---|
| fraction = % ÷ 100 | all fractions add up to 1 | prepare abundances for the weighted average |
| fraction = height ÷ (sum of all heights) | heights scaled with the tallest = 100 | convert relative heights into fractions |
| average = Σ (mass × fraction) | weighted average | find the average atomic mass from a spectrum |
| m₁(1 − x) + m₂x = average | two isotopes, x = fraction of isotope 2 | find abundances from an average atomic mass |

## Assumptions

- Each peak comes from a **singly charged ion of one atom**, so m/z equals the isotope's mass. (Spectra with molecules, 2+ ions or several elements are outside this topic's assessment.)
- The sample is **one pure element**.
- The periodic-table value assumes the **natural mix of isotopes**; a sample with a different mix has a different average.

## Mistakes to avoid

1. **Simple mean instead of weighted average.** Weight each mass by its abundance.
2. **Relative heights used as percentages.** If the tallest peak is 100, divide each height by the total of all heights.
3. **Forgetting ÷ 100.** 75.76% is 0.7576.
4. **Mixing up height and position.** Height = how many; position = how heavy.
5. **Thinking an atom has the average mass.** Each atom is one isotope; the average is for the whole sample.
6. **Skipping the check.** An answer outside the range of isotope masses is always wrong.

## Quick self-check

1. Chlorine is 75.76% ³⁵Cl (34.969 amu) and 24.24% ³⁷Cl (36.966 amu). What is its average atomic mass? *(35.45 amu)*
2. An element has peaks at m/z 20.0 (90.0%) and 22.0 (10.0%). What is its average atomic mass? *(20.2 amu)*
3. Two peaks have relative heights 100 and 50. What are the percentage abundances? *(66.7% and 33.3%)*
4. In question 1, how tall is the ³⁷Cl peak if the ³⁵Cl peak is drawn at 100? *(32.0)*
