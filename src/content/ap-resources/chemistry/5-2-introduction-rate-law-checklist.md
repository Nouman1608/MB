Work through the list without notes. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/chemistry/5-2-introduction-rate-law-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/chemistry/5-2-introduction-rate-law-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can describe how spectrophotometry, gas measurements, conductivity or sampling can follow a reaction over time. *(Guide: "Watching a reaction as it happens")*
- I can explain why absorbance data can stand in for concentration data. *(Practice Q6)*
- I can write a rate law and name the order for each reactant and the overall order. *(Guide: "The rate law"; Practice Q4)*
- I can explain why orders must come from experiment rather than from coefficients. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q7)*
- I can explain why k depends on temperature but not on concentration. *(Guide: "The rate constant, k"; Practice Q3)*

## Calculation

- I can predict the change in rate when one or more concentrations are multiplied by a factor. *(Guide: "What each order means"; Practice Q1, Q5)*
- I can work out the units of k from the overall order. *(Practice Q2)*
- I can find each order from a pair of experiments in which only one concentration changes. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q4)*
- I can find an order when two concentrations change at once, by removing the known effect first. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q7)*
- I can calculate k with units, and use a rate law to find a rate or a concentration. *(Practice Q4, Q5, Q6)*

## Reasoning

- I can use data to show that a rate law based on coefficients is wrong. *(Practice Q7)*
- I can evaluate a claim that k changed, using the data and the temperature. *(Practice Q6)*
