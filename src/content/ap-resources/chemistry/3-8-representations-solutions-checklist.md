Work through the list without notes. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/chemistry/3-8-representations-solutions-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/chemistry/3-8-representations-solutions-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can say what a particle diagram of a solution must show: the relative numbers of particles and the interactions between them. *(Guide: "What a particle diagram of a solution must show")*
- I can explain why water points its oxygen towards cations and a hydrogen towards anions. *(Guide: Figure 1; Practice Q2)*
- I can explain why polyatomic ions and molecular solutes stay whole when they dissolve. *(Practice Q5(b), Q6)*

## Skills (drawing and reading diagrams)

- I can draw an ionic solution with separate ions in the formula ratio and balanced charges. *(Guide: "Drawing ionic solutes"; Practice Q1)*
- I can draw water molecules with the correct orientation around both a cation and an anion. *(Guide: Figure 1; Practice Q6(b))*
- I can draw a molecular solute such as methanol with hydrogen bonds to water placed correctly. *(Guide: "Drawing molecular solutes"; Practice Q5(a))*
- I can compare the concentrations shown by two diagrams that represent different volumes. *(Guide: Worked example 3; Practice Q3)*
- I can calculate ion concentrations from a diagram when I know how many moles each drawn particle represents. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q4(b))*
- I can show a dilution or a mixing step in a diagram by keeping or adding up particle counts and changing the volume. *(Guide: Figure 2; Practice Q4(c), Q7)*

## Reasoning

- I can find and explain the errors in someone else's diagram of a solution. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q6)*
- I can contrast the interactions in an ionic solution with those in a molecular solution. *(Practice Q5(c))*
- I know that colligative properties and molality or percent-by-mass calculations are not assessed in this course. *(Guide: "Common misconceptions")*
