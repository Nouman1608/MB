Work through the list without notes. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/chemistry/6-9-hesss-law-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/chemistry/6-9-hesss-law-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can explain that a process can be split into a series of steps, real or imagined, and that each step has its own ΔH. *(Guide: "One change, more than one route"; Practice Q5)*
- I can explain, using conservation of energy, why the ΔH values of the steps add up to the ΔH of the whole process. *(Guide: "Why the steps must add up"; Practice Q6)*
- I can explain why two different routes between the same start and end must give the same ΔH. *(Guide: "Why the steps must add up"; Practice Q6)*
- I can explain why a liquid and a gas of the same compound do not cancel in a Hess's law sum. *(Guide: Figure 1; Practice Q7)*

## Calculation

- I can give ΔH for a reversed equation by changing its sign. *(Practice Q1)*
- I can give ΔH for an equation multiplied by a whole number or a fraction. *(Practice Q1, Q3)*
- I can choose which given equations to reverse or scale so they add to a target equation. *(Guide: Worked examples 1 and 2; Practice Q2)*
- I can cancel substances correctly when the amounts on each side are different, leaving the remainder. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q4)*
- I can add a physical step, such as vaporization, to a chemical step to reach a target. *(Guide: Figure 1; Practice Q7)*
- I can show that ΣΔHf°(products) − ΣΔHf°(reactants) is a route through the elements. *(Guide: Worked example 3)*

## Reasoning

- I can draw or read a Hess cycle or a step energy diagram and use it to find an unknown ΔH. *(Guide: Figures 1 and 2; Practice Q5)*
- I can judge whether a difference between two measured routes is experimental error, giving a specific source of error. *(Practice Q6)*
- I can explain why the overall ΔH is negative or positive by comparing energy absorbed in some steps with energy released in others. *(Practice Q5)*
