Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/chemistry/3-9-separation-solutions-mixtures-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/chemistry/3-9-separation-solutions-mixtures-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can explain why filtration removes an undissolved solid but cannot separate the parts of a solution. *(Guide: "Why a filter cannot separate a solution"; Practice Q1, Q6)*
- I can name the stationary phase and the mobile phase in paper, thin-layer and column chromatography. *(Guide: "Chromatography: a tug-of-war")*
- I can explain why paper and silica gel attract polar components more strongly than nonpolar ones. *(Guide; Practice Q3)*
- I can explain how distillation uses differences in vapor pressure that come from differences in intermolecular forces. *(Guide: "Distillation"; Practice Q4)*

## Skills

- I can calculate an Rf value, measuring both distances from the origin to the centre of the spot. *(Guide: Figure 1; Practice Q2, Q7)*
- I can rank the components of a mixture by polarity from a chromatogram on a polar stationary phase. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q3)*
- I can predict the order in which components leave a column. *(Guide: "Column chromatography"; Practice Q5)*
- I can sketch or describe a distillation setup, with the thermometer bulb level with the side arm and water entering the condenser at the lower end. *(Guide: Figure 2)*
- I can read a distillation curve and say which liquid is collecting at each stage. *(Guide: Worked example 2)*
- I can choose a separation method that fits a mixture and justify the choice. *(Guide: Worked example 3; Practice Q6)*

## Reasoning

- I can explain why every Rf value rises when a more polar solvent is used, while the order of the spots stays the same. *(Practice Q5, Q7)*
- I can explain which of two liquids distils first by comparing their intermolecular forces, not their molar masses. *(Practice Q4)*
- I can evaluate a claim about a separation, using evidence from a chromatogram or from distillation data. *(Practice Q6, Q7)*
