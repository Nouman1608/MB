Work through the list without notes. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/chemistry/5-11-catalysis-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/chemistry/5-11-catalysis-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can explain the two ways a catalyst raises the rate: more favourable orientation and a new path with a lower activation energy. *(Guide: "What a catalyst does"; Practice Q1, Q7)*
- I can explain why a catalyst is not used up overall even though it takes part in the mechanism. *(Guide: "Catalysts in a mechanism"; Practice Q5(d))*
- I can state what a catalyst does not change: ΔH and the energies of the reactants and products. *(Guide: Figure 1; Practice Q1)*
- I can describe how enzyme, acid–base and surface catalysts each create new intermediates and new elementary steps. *(Guide: "Three ways catalysts work"; Practice Q5, Q6, Q7)*

## Skills

- I can identify the catalyst and the intermediates in a proposed mechanism and check that the steps add up. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q2, Q5(a))*
- I can use initial-rate data to show that a catalyst appears in the rate law, and calculate k with units. *(Practice Q4)*
- I can write a mechanism in which the catalyst is consumed in the slow step and regenerated later. *(Practice Q4(c))*
- I can calculate forward and reverse barriers for catalysed and uncatalysed paths from an energy profile. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q3)*
- I can sketch or describe a catalysed energy profile with extra humps and a dip for the new intermediate. *(Guide: Figure 1; Practice Q7(c))*

## Reasoning

- I can use a collision-energy distribution to explain why a lower barrier gives a faster rate at the same temperature. *(Guide: Figure 2; Practice Q7(a))*
- I can explain why a powdered surface catalyst works faster than a lump of the same mass. *(Practice Q6(c))*
- I can evaluate a claim about a catalyst using particle-level evidence, not just the overall equation. *(Practice Q5(d), Q6(d), Q7(a))*
