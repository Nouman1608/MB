## Understanding

- I can explain VSEPR as Coulombic repulsion between electron domains that spread as far apart as possible. *(Guide: "Why electron pairs spread out")*
- I can explain why a lone pair, or a multiple bond, squeezes the neighbouring bond angles. *(Guide: "Lone pairs push harder"; Practice Q4)*
- I can explain why a π bond is weaker than a σ bond, and why a π bond stops rotation and allows *cis* and *trans* isomers. *(Guide: "Sigma and pi bonds"; Practice Q5)*

## Skills

- I can count the electron domains on any atom, treating a double or triple bond as one domain. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q1)*
- I can name the electron-domain geometry and the molecular geometry for 2 to 6 domains, using all eleven shape names. *(Guide: shapes table; Practice Q1, Q6)*
- I can give ideal bond angles (180°, 120°, 109.5°, 90°) and say when the real angle is slightly smaller. *(Guide: Figure 1; Practice Q4, Q5)*
- I can place lone pairs correctly in trigonal bipyramidal (equatorial) and octahedral (opposite) arrangements. *(Guide: "Five and six domains", Worked example 2; Practice Q6, Q7)*
- I can assign sp, sp² or sp³ to any atom with 2 to 4 domains, lone pairs included, and I know that atoms with 5 or 6 domains need only a shape. *(Guide: Worked example 3; Practice Q2, Q4)*
- I can count the σ and π bonds in a molecule. *(Guide: Worked example 3; Practice Q4)*

## Reasoning

- I can compare bond lengths and bond energies using bond order and atomic radius. *(Guide: "Bond order, bond length and bond energy"; Practice Q4(e))*
- I can decide whether a molecule has a dipole moment by combining bond polarity with shape. *(Guide: "Dipole moments", Worked example 1; Practice Q3, Q6(c))*
- I can evaluate a claim about shape or polarity, such as "every AX₂ molecule is bent", with evidence from a Lewis diagram and VSEPR. *(Practice Q7)*
