Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/chemistry/9-8-galvanic-voltaic-electrolytic-cells-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/chemistry/9-8-galvanic-voltaic-electrolytic-cells-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can explain the job of the electrodes, the half-cell solutions, the salt bridge and the voltmeter or power supply. *(Guide: "What each part of a galvanic cell does"; Practice Q1)*
- I can tell a galvanic cell from an electrolytic cell using thermodynamic favourability and the direction of the energy change. *(Guide: "Two kinds of electrochemical cell"; Practice Q6(d))*
- I can state that oxidation happens at the anode and reduction at the cathode in every cell. *(Practice Q3, Q7)*
- I can describe what happens to atoms and ions at each electrode surface at the particle level. *(Guide: "The view at the particle level"; Practice Q7(b))*

## Skills

- I can identify the anode and cathode from an observation such as a mass change or a gas forming. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q2, Q4)*
- I can write the half-reactions and the electron-balanced overall equation for a cell. *(Practice Q4(a), Q6)*
- I can trace electron flow in the wire and ion flow in the salt bridge or molten electrolyte on a diagram. *(Guide: Figures 1 and 2; Practice Q2)*
- I can link the mass changes at the two electrodes through moles of electrons. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q4(b), Q6(c))*
- I can predict the products of electrolysing a molten salt and say where any gas forms. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q3)*

## Reasoning

- I can predict and explain how removing or changing the salt bridge, or changing an electrode, alters the cell's behaviour. *(Guide: "Changing the set-up: what happens?"; Practice Q5)*
- I can explain why reversing a galvanic cell's reaction needs a power supply. *(Practice Q6(d))*
- I can find and correct errors in someone else's description of a cell. *(Practice Q7(a))*
