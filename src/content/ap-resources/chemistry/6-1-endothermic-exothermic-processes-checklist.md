Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/chemistry/6-1-endothermic-exothermic-processes-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/chemistry/6-1-endothermic-exothermic-processes-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can define the system and the surroundings for a reaction in a beaker, and say which one the thermometer measures. *(Guide: "System, surroundings and the direction of energy"; Practice Q5)*
- I can state that exothermic means the system's energy decreases and endothermic means it increases, with energy conserved overall. *(Guide; Practice Q2)*
- I can explain that energy crosses a system's boundary as heat or as work, and give an example of work done by a gas. *(Guide: "Heat and work"; Practice Q5(c), Q6)*
- I can classify all six phase changes as endothermic or exothermic and explain why using attractions between particles. *(Guide: "Three kinds of process"; Practice Q1)*
- I can explain that breaking bonds needs energy and forming bonds releases it, so a reaction's sign depends on the balance. *(Guide: "Chemical reactions")*

## Skills

- I can calculate ΔT from thermometer readings and use its sign to classify a process. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q2, Q5)*
- I can decide when a temperature change is too small, compared with the thermometer's precision, to support a claim. *(Guide: Worked example 1)*
- I can draw an energy-transfer diagram with arrows pointing between the system and the surroundings in the correct direction. *(Guide: Figure 1)*

## Reasoning

- I can classify the same event from two different systems, such as a flame and the water it heats. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q4)*
- I can explain why dissolving may be exothermic or endothermic by comparing the attractions formed with the attractions broken. *(Guide: "Dissolving"; Practice Q3, Q7)*
- I can evaluate a claim that a temperature change proves (or rules out) an energy change. *(Guide: Worked example 3; Practice Q6)*
- I can explain why an endothermic pack makes a hand feel cold without saying that "cold" moves. *(Practice Q7(b))*
