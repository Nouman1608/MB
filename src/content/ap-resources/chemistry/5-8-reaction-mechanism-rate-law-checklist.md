Work through the list without notes. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/chemistry/5-8-reaction-mechanism-rate-law-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/chemistry/5-8-reaction-mechanism-rate-law-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can explain why the slowest elementary step limits the rate of the whole reaction. *(Guide: "The slowest step sets the pace")*
- I can explain why the rate law comes from the slow step and not from the overall equation. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q6(b))*
- I can explain why an intermediate cannot appear in an experimental rate law, while a catalyst can. *(Guide: "Writing the rate law from a mechanism"; Practice Q4(d))*

## Calculation

- I can write the rate law predicted by a mechanism whose first step is slow. *(Practice Q1, Q4(b))*
- I can give the units of k from the overall order of the rate law. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q4, Q5)*
- I can calculate a rate from a rate law, k and concentrations. *(Practice Q4(c), Q5(c), Q6(c))*
- I can predict the factor by which the rate changes when one or more concentrations change, including zero-order reactants. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q2)*
- I can find the rate law and k from initial-rate data. *(Practice Q5(a))*

## Reasoning

- I can decide whether a mechanism is consistent with both the overall equation and an experimental rate law. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q3, Q5(b))*
- I can evaluate a claim that uses overall coefficients as reaction orders. *(Practice Q6)*
- I can explain why two mechanisms can predict the same rate law, and what extra evidence could separate them. *(Guide: "Testing a mechanism against experiment"; Practice Q7)*
