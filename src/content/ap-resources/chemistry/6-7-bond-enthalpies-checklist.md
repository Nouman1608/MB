Work through the list without notes. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/chemistry/6-7-bond-enthalpies-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/chemistry/6-7-bond-enthalpies-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can explain why breaking a bond needs energy and forming the same bond releases the same amount. *(Guide: "Where the energy of a reaction comes from"; Practice Q1)*
- I can define bond enthalpy and say why it is always a positive number. *(Guide: "Bond enthalpy: an average value")*
- I can explain why a table value is an average, and why that makes the answer an estimate. *(Guide; Practice Q4)*
- I can explain why a fuel releases energy without saying that breaking bonds releases energy. *(Practice Q7)*

## Calculation

- I can draw Lewis structures and list every bond broken and formed, multiplied by the coefficients. *(Guide: "Counting bonds without mistakes"; Practice Q3, Q5)*
- I can estimate ΔH as Σ(bonds broken) − Σ(bonds formed), with the correct sign. *(Guide: Worked examples 1 and 2; Practice Q2)*
- I can use the correct values for double and triple bonds, including O=O, N≡N and the C=O bonds in CO₂. *(Practice Q3, Q5)*
- I can cancel bonds that do not change and get the same answer. *(Guide: Worked example 2)*
- I can scale ΔH to a given mass of reactant. *(Practice Q5)*
- I can work backwards from a known ΔH to estimate one unknown bond enthalpy. *(Guide: Worked example 3; Practice Q6)*

## Reasoning

- I can decide whether a reaction is exothermic or endothermic by comparing the energy needed to break bonds with the energy released forming bonds. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q1, Q7)*
- I can represent the method as a step diagram: up to separate atoms, then down to products. *(Guide: Figure 1; Practice Q7)*
- I can give two reasons a bond-enthalpy estimate differs from a measured value: averaging and non-gaseous substances. *(Guide: "Why the answer is only an estimate"; Practice Q4, Q7)*
