## Recap

- **Economic growth** is a long-run rise in an economy's capacity to produce. It is measured by the **growth rate of real GDP per capita**.
- The **aggregate production function** links output to labour, physical capital, human capital and technology.
- Other things equal, **more employment means more output**: firms need more workers to produce more.
- **Labour productivity** is output per employed worker. It rises with **physical capital per worker**, **human capital per worker** and **better technology**.
- Output per capita rises with physical and human capital per capita.
- Growth is an **outward shift of the PPC** and a **rightward shift of LRAS**: the same rise in full-employment output, drawn two ways.

## Key relationships

| Measure | Formula | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Real GDP | nominal GDP ÷ GDP deflator × 100 | removes inflation |
| Real GDP per capita | real GDP ÷ population | average output per person |
| Growth rate | (new − old) ÷ old × 100 | use per capita values for growth in living standards |
| Shortcut | growth of real GDP − growth of population | approximate only |
| Labour productivity | real GDP ÷ employed workers | output per worker, not per person |
| Real GDP | employment × productivity | growth can come from either |

## Assumptions behind the models

- The PPC and LRAS show **full-employment** output with given resources and technology.
- "Other things equal" when linking employment to output: capital and technology are held constant.
- Recovery from a recession (inside the PPC to on it) is **not** long-run growth.

## Mistakes to avoid

1. Using **nominal** GDP to measure growth.
2. Forgetting to divide by the **population**.
3. Dividing by the population instead of **employment** when asked for productivity.
4. Calling a rightward **AD** shift economic growth.
5. Saying a higher price level shifts LRAS.
6. Forgetting that a bigger population with the same capital means **less capital per worker**.

## Quick self-check

VD is the valda, the currency of the fictional country Valdoria used in the study guide.

1. Real GDP is VD 720 billion and the population is 36 million. What is real GDP per capita? *(VD 20,000)*
2. Next year real GDP is VD 756 billion and the population is 36.9 million. What is the growth rate of real GDP per capita? *(VD 20,487.80; growth 2.44%)*
3. Real GDP is VD 450 billion and 18 million people are employed. What is labour productivity? *(VD 25,000 per worker)*
4. Name the three determinants of labour productivity. *(Physical capital per worker, human capital per worker, technology)*
