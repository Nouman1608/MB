Work through the list without notes. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/macroeconomics/4-5-money-market-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/macroeconomics/4-5-money-market-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can define the money market, money demand and money supply, and say why the nominal interest rate is the "price" of holding money. *(Guide: "What is the money market?"; Practice Q1)*
- I can explain why the quantity of money demanded falls when the nominal interest rate rises. *(Guide: "Money demand: why the curve slopes down"; Practice Q1)*
- I can explain why the money supply curve is vertical. *(Guide: "Money supply: why the curve is vertical"; Practice Q6(e))*
- I can name the factors that shift money demand and money supply, and tell a shift from a movement along a curve. *(Guide: "What shifts the curves"; Practice Q3, Q4)*

## Skills

- I can draw a money market graph with the nominal interest rate and quantity of money on the axes, a vertical MS and a downward-sloping MD. *(Guide: Figure 1; Practice Q6(a))*
- I can find the equilibrium nominal interest rate from a table or an equation. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q5(a), Q7(a))*
- I can calculate the size of a surplus or shortage of money at a given interest rate. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q5(b))*
- I can show a shift in MD or MS on a graph and label the new equilibrium rate. *(Guide: Figure 2; Practice Q6(b))*

## Reasoning

- I can explain how buying or selling bonds moves the interest rate back to equilibrium. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q2, Q5(c))*
- I can explain how a change in the price level or real GDP changes the equilibrium nominal interest rate, and what happens to bond prices. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q3, Q6(c)–(d))*
- I can work out the change in money supply needed to hold the interest rate steady, and explain why two opposite shifts can give an indeterminate result. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q7)*
