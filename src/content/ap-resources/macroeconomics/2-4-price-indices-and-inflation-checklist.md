Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/macroeconomics/2-4-price-indices-and-inflation-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/macroeconomics/2-4-price-indices-and-inflation-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can define the CPI and explain what it measures about a household's standard of living. *(Guide: "The consumer price index (CPI)")*
- I can explain why the market basket must stay fixed and why the base year has an index of 100. *(Guide: "The consumer price index (CPI)"; Practice Q1)*
- I can define inflation, deflation and disinflation and tell them apart from data. *(Guide: "Why we need a price index"; Practice Q3, Q6(d))*
- I can explain the difference between a nominal and a real variable. *(Guide: "Using an index to compare values across years")*

## Calculation

- I can calculate the cost of a market basket and turn it into a CPI. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q1, Q6)*
- I can calculate an inflation rate from two index numbers, dividing by the earlier one. *(Practice Q2, Q6(c))*
- I can convert a nominal wage into a real wage and find the percentage change in the real wage. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q5)*
- I can express a price from one year in another year's money using the ratio of the indices. *(Practice Q7(a))*

## Reasoning

- I can decide whether a worker's purchasing power rose or fell, and support it with a calculation. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q5, Q7(b))*
- I can explain substitution bias, and use quality changes or new goods as further reasons the CPI may overstate inflation. *(Guide: "Why the CPI may overstate inflation"; Practice Q4, Q6(e), Q7(c))*
