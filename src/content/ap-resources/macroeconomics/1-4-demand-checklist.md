Work through the list without notes. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/macroeconomics/1-4-demand-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/macroeconomics/1-4-demand-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can define demand and quantity demanded, and explain why "willing and able" both matter. *(Guide: "What demand means")*
- I can state the law of demand and explain why the demand curve slopes downward. *(Guide: "The law of demand")*
- I can explain the difference between a change in quantity demanded and a change in demand. *(Guide: "Change in quantity demanded v. change in demand"; Practice Q1, Q6(c))*
- I can tell normal goods from inferior goods, and substitutes from complements, using how buyers respond. *(Guide: "The determinants of demand"; Practice Q2, Q3)*

## Skills

- I can build a market demand schedule by adding the quantities all buyers demand at each price. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q5(a)–(b))*
- I can draw a correctly labelled demand graph with price on the vertical axis and quantity on the horizontal axis. *(Guide: Figure 1; Practice Q6(a))*
- I can show an increase or a decrease in demand as a labelled shift with an arrow. *(Guide: Worked example 2, Figure 2; Practice Q6(b))*
- I can show a change in the good's own price as a movement between two points on the same curve. *(Guide: Figure 1; Practice Q6(d))*
- I can read a before-and-after schedule and say whether demand increased or decreased. *(Practice Q4, Q5(c))*

## Reasoning

- I can predict the direction of the shift caused by a change in income, the price of a related good, tastes, expectations or the number of buyers, and give the reason. *(Guide: "The determinants of demand"; Practice Q2–Q4, Q6(e))*
- I can explain why the overall effect of two opposite shifts is uncertain without more information. *(Practice Q7(b)–(c))*
- I can spot the error when a fall in price is described as an increase in demand. *(Guide: "Common misconceptions"; Practice Q7(d))*
