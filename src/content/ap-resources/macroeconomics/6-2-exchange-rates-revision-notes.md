## Recap

- In the **foreign exchange market**, one currency is swapped for another. The **exchange rate** is the price of one currency in terms of the other.
- The same rate can be quoted both ways; the two quotes are **reciprocals**.
- **Appreciation:** a currency buys **more** of the other currency. **Depreciation:** it buys **less**.
- Against each other, if one currency appreciates, the other **must** depreciate.
- In a **flexible** system, the rate is set by the people and firms buying and selling the currencies, for trade in goods and services and for buying assets.

## Key relationships

Examples use the fictional valda (VD) of Valdoria and kesta (KS) of Kestria.

| Relationship | Rule | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Reciprocal | VD per KS = 1 ÷ (KS per VD) | 1 VD = 4 KS → 1 KS = VD 0.25 |
| Home price → foreign price | price in VD × (KS per VD) | VD 30 × 4 = KS 120 |
| Foreign price → home price | price in KS ÷ (KS per VD) | KS 2,000 ÷ 4 = VD 500 |
| % change in a currency's value | (new price of that currency − old) ÷ old × 100 | 4 → 5 KS per VD: valda +25% |
| The other currency's % change | use its own price (the reciprocal) | 0.25 → 0.20 VD per KS: kesta −20% |

## Assumptions behind the numbers

- Rates are quoted without buy–sell spreads or fees.
- Goods keep the same price in their home currency unless told otherwise.
- Drawing the market graph (Topic 6.3) and the causes of shifts (Topic 6.4) are separate topics.

## Mistakes to avoid

1. **Ignoring the "per".** A higher number can mean appreciation or depreciation depending on the quote.
2. **Flipping the sign of a percentage.** +25% for one currency is −20% for the other.
3. **Multiplying instead of dividing.** Check the units cancel.
4. **Rounding reciprocals too early.** Keep four decimal places.
5. **Saying both currencies appreciated** against each other.

## Quick self-check

1. 1 VD = 2.5 KS. What is the price of 1 KS in valdas? *(VD 0.40)*
2. The rate moves from 1 VD = 4.0 KS to 1 VD = 3.2 KS. Which currency appreciated, and by what percentage? *(The kesta, by 25%; the valda depreciated by 20%.)*
3. A Kestrian book costs KS 600 when 1 VD = 4 KS. What does it cost in valdas? *(VD 150)*
