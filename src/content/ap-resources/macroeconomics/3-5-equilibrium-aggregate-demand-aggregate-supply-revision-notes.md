## Recap

- **Short-run equilibrium:** aggregate quantity demanded = aggregate quantity supplied, where **AD crosses SRAS**. It gives the equilibrium price level (PL₁) and real output (Y₁).
- **Above** the equilibrium price level there is a surplus: inventories rise unexpectedly, so the price level and output fall. **Below** it there is a shortage: inventories fall, so the price level and output rise.
- **Long-run equilibrium:** AD and SRAS cross **on LRAS**, so Y₁ = Yf and unemployment equals the natural rate.
- Short-run equilibrium can be **below, at or above** Yf.
- **Output gap = actual real GDP − Yf.** Negative = recessionary. Positive = inflationary.

## Key relationships

| Short-run equilibrium | Output gap | Unemployment vs natural rate | Cyclical unemployment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Left of LRAS | Negative (recessionary) | Above | Positive |
| On LRAS | Zero | Equal | Zero |
| Right of LRAS | Positive (inflationary) | Below | Negative |

| Quantity | Formula |
|---|---|
| Output gap | actual real GDP − Yf |
| Gap as % of Yf | (actual − Yf) ÷ Yf × 100 |
| Surplus at a price level | real GDP supplied − real GDP demanded |

## Assumptions

- Nominal wages and some other input prices are sticky in the short run, so SRAS slopes upward.
- Yf and the natural rate are fixed unless the question says otherwise.
- Shifts of AD and SRAS are Topic 3.6; how a gap closes in the long run is Topic 3.7.

## Mistakes to avoid

1. Saying the economy is at **full employment** just because AD = SRAS.
2. Finding short-run equilibrium where SRAS meets **LRAS**.
3. Getting the **sign** of the gap wrong: below Yf is negative.
4. Linking a recessionary gap with unemployment **below** the natural rate.
5. Shifting a curve to remove a surplus. The economy moves **along** AD and SRAS.
6. Drawing long-run equilibrium without all three curves crossing at **one point**.
7. Labelling axes "price" and "quantity" instead of **price level** and **real GDP**.

## Quick self-check

VD is the valda, the currency of the fictional country Valdoria used in the study guide.

1. At a price level of 115, real GDP demanded is VD 440 billion and real GDP supplied is VD 470 billion. Surplus or shortage? Which way does the price level move? *(Surplus of VD 30 billion; it falls)*
2. Equilibrium real GDP is VD 470 billion and Yf is VD 500 billion. Size and type of gap? *(−VD 30 billion, −6%; negative, recessionary)*
3. Unemployment is 3.8% and the natural rate is 4.5%. Where is short-run equilibrium? *(Right of LRAS; positive, inflationary gap)*
