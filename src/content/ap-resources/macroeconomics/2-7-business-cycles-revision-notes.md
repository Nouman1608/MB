## Recap

- A **business cycle** is the short-run rise and fall of **real** output and employment, caused by changes in aggregate demand and/or aggregate supply.
- **Expansion:** real GDP rises. **Recession:** real GDP falls.
- **Peak:** the turning point where expansion ends. **Trough:** the turning point where recession ends.
- **Potential output** = full-employment output: unemployment equals the **natural rate** (frictional + structural).
- **Output gap** = actual − potential. Negative = **recessionary**; positive = **inflationary**.
- "Two quarters of falling real GDP" is only a rule of thumb; official dating bodies look at many indicators.

## Key relationships

| Relationship | Formula or rule | Use it to |
|---|---|---|
| Output gap | actual real GDP − potential real GDP | size the gap in money |
| Output gap (%) | (actual − potential) ÷ potential × 100 | compare gaps across years |
| Cyclical unemployment | actual unemployment rate − natural rate | link the gap to jobs |
| Phase | sign of the change in real GDP | recession (−) or expansion (+) |
| Peak-to-trough change | (trough − peak) ÷ peak × 100 | size a recession |

## Reading a business cycle graph

1. Straight upward line = potential output (trend).
2. High points of the actual curve = peaks; low points = troughs.
3. Curve falling = recession; rising = expansion.
4. Curve above the line = positive gap, unemployment below the natural rate; below the line = negative gap, unemployment above it.

## Assumptions behind the numbers

- Real GDP (not nominal) measures output (Topic 2.6).
- Potential output and the natural rate are estimates and can change over time.
- Explaining **why** AD or AS changes is Unit 3; here you describe and measure the cycle.

## Mistakes to avoid

1. **Calling slower growth a recession.** Real GDP must fall.
2. **Assuming expansion means a positive gap.** Early expansions often have negative gaps.
3. **Calling peaks and troughs phases.**
4. **Reversing the gap sign:** actual − potential.
5. **Thinking full employment means 0% unemployment.**
6. **Dividing the gap by actual output** instead of potential.

## Quick self-check

VD is the valda, the currency of the fictional country Valdoria used in the study guide.

1. Actual real GDP is VD 570 billion and potential is VD 600 billion. What is the output gap? *(−VD 30 billion, −5% of potential; recessionary)*
2. Unemployment is 6.5% and the natural rate is 4.5%. What is cyclical unemployment? *(2 percentage points)*
3. Real GDP fell for two quarters and has just risen by 0.5%. Which phase is the economy in? *(Expansion; the trough has passed.)*
