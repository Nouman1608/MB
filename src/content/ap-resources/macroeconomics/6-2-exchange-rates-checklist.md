## Understanding

- I can define the exchange rate as the price of one currency in terms of another. *(Guide: "What an exchange rate is")*
- I can define appreciation and depreciation, and explain why one currency's appreciation is the other's depreciation. *(Guide: "Appreciation and depreciation"; Practice Q2)*
- I can explain that, in a flexible system, a currency's value is set by people buying and selling it for trade and for assets. *(Guide: "How currencies are valued relative to one another"; Practice Q6(e))*

## Calculation

- I can turn a rate quoted as KS per VD into VD per KS using the reciprocal. *(Guide: "Two ways to quote the same rate"; Practice Q1, Q6(a))*
- I can convert a price from one currency into another, using units to decide whether to multiply or divide. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q3, Q5(a), Q6(d))*
- I can calculate the percentage change in each currency's value, using each currency's own price. *(Guide: Worked examples 1 and 3; Practice Q4, Q5(c), Q6(c))*
- I can find the rate between two currencies from their rates against a third. *(Practice Q7(a)–(b))*

## Reasoning

- I can decide which currency appreciated from a change in the rate, however the rate is quoted. *(Guide: Figure 1; Practice Q2, Q6(b))*
- I can explain why a 25% appreciation of one currency is a 20% depreciation of the other. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q4)*
- I can explain how a change in the exchange rate changes what a good costs a foreign buyer when its home price stays the same. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q5, Q7(c))*
