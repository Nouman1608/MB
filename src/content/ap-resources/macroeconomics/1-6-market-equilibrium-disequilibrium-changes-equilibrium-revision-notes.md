## Recap

- **Market equilibrium** is the price at which quantity demanded equals quantity supplied. On a graph it is where D and S cross.
- At any other price the market is in **disequilibrium**.
- **Surplus:** price above equilibrium, Qs > Qd. Sellers cut the price.
- **Shortage:** price below equilibrium, Qd > Qs. The price is bid up.
- While the price adjusts, buyers and sellers move **along** the curves. Neither curve shifts.
- Equilibrium changes only when a **determinant** of demand or supply shifts a curve. At the old price there is then a shortage or surplus, and the price moves to a new equilibrium.

## Key relationships

| Situation | Rule | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Surplus | Qs − Qd at the given price | price falls |
| Shortage | Qd − Qs at the given price | price rises |
| Demand increases | D shifts right | P rises, Q rises |
| Demand decreases | D shifts left | P falls, Q falls |
| Supply increases | S shifts right | P falls, Q rises |
| Supply decreases | S shifts left | P rises, Q falls |
| D up and S up | effects on P conflict | P indeterminate, Q rises |
| D down and S down | effects on P conflict | P indeterminate, Q falls |
| D up and S down | effects on Q conflict | P rises, Q indeterminate |
| D down and S up | effects on Q conflict | P falls, Q indeterminate |

## Assumptions behind the model

- The market is **competitive**: many buyers and sellers, and none can set the price alone.
- Prices are free to move up or down.
- When one determinant changes, everything else that affects demand and supply stays the same.
- Out of scope here: consumer and producer surplus, price ceilings and floors, and elasticity (Microeconomics course).

## Mistakes to avoid

1. **Calling a surplus a sign of a low price.** It means the price is too **high**.
2. **Measuring the gap from Qₑ.** Use Qs and Qd at the **same** price.
3. **Shifting supply when demand changes.** The quantity supplied changes along the same curve.
4. **Shifting both curves for one event.** Ask: does it affect buyers or sellers?
5. **Giving a definite answer for a conflicting double shift.** Say "indeterminate".
6. **Unlabelled graphs.** Label axes, D₁, D₂ (or S₁, S₂), P₁, P₂, Q₁, Q₂.

## Quick self-check

VD is the valda, the currency of the fictional country Valdoria used in the study guide.

1. At VD 5, Qd = 90 and Qs = 130 units. Surplus or shortage, and how big? *(Surplus of 40)*
2. At VD 3, Qd = 50 and Qs = 35 units. What happens to the price? *(Shortage of 15, so the price rises)*
3. A new technology cuts production costs. Effect on P and Q? *(Supply shifts right: P falls, Q rises)*
4. Demand falls and supply rises at the same time. What is certain? *(The price falls; the quantity is indeterminate)*
