## Recap

- The **CPI** compares the cost of a **fixed market basket** of consumer goods and services in a given year with its cost in the **base year**.
- It shows how much more income a household needs to keep the **same** standard of living.
- **Inflation:** the price level rises. **Deflation:** it falls. **Disinflation:** inflation is still positive but lower than before.
- **Real** variables are nominal variables adjusted (deflated) by a price index.
- The CPI tends to **overstate** inflation: substitution bias, quality changes, new goods.

## Key relationships

| Relationship | Formula | Use it to |
|---|---|---|
| CPI | (cost of basket in year t ÷ cost in base year) × 100 | build the index |
| Inflation rate | (new index − old index) ÷ old index × 100 | find the percentage change in prices |
| Real value | nominal value ÷ index × 100 | express money in base-year purchasing power |
| Value in another year's money | value × (index in target year ÷ index in original year) | compare money across years |
| Real % change (approx.) | nominal % change − inflation rate | quick check on real changes |

## Assumptions behind the numbers

- The basket represents what a **typical** consumer buys; any one household may differ.
- Quantities in the basket stay **fixed** between years.
- The same goods are priced each year, with **unchanged quality**.
- Out of scope: the producer price index (PPI). Calculating the GDP deflator is in Topic 2.6.

## Mistakes to avoid

1. **Index points ≠ percentages** unless the earlier index is 100.
2. **Wrong denominator:** divide by the *earlier* index.
3. **Inverting the ratio:** given-year cost goes on top.
4. **Calling disinflation "deflation".**
5. **Changing basket quantities** between years.
6. **Saying substitution makes the CPI understate inflation.** It overstates it.

## Quick self-check

VD is the valda, the currency of the fictional country Valdoria used in the study guide.

1. A basket costs VD 80 in the base year and VD 92 now. What is the CPI? *(115)*
2. The CPI rises from 150 to 156. What is the inflation rate? *(4%)*
3. A nominal wage of VD 840 when the CPI is 120 is worth how much in base-year valdas? *(VD 700)*
