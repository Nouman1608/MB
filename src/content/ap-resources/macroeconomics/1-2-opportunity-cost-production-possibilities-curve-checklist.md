Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/macroeconomics/1-2-opportunity-cost-production-possibilities-curve-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/macroeconomics/1-2-opportunity-cost-production-possibilities-curve-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can define opportunity cost as the next best alternative given up, and explain how scarcity creates tradeoffs. *(Guide: "Opportunity cost: every choice gives something up")*
- I can define a PPC and state the assumptions behind it. *(Guide: "What the PPC model assumes")*
- I can explain what points on, inside and outside a PPC mean for efficiency and unemployed resources. *(Guide: Worked example 1(d); Practice Q1, Q7(c))*
- I can tell a movement along the PPC apart from a shift of the curve. *(Guide: "Our fictional economy: Valdoria"; Practice Q3)*

## Calculation

- I can calculate total and per-unit opportunity cost from a PPC table, with units. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q2, Q7(a))*
- I can find opportunity costs from a straight-line PPC and swap them to the other good. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q5(a))*
- I can test whether a combination is attainable by comparing it with the maximum on the PPC. *(Practice Q5(b), Q7(c))*
- I can explain why moving from a point inside the PPC to the curve has no opportunity cost. *(Guide: Worked example 1 "Check"; Practice Q5(c))*

## Graphing and reasoning

- I can draw a correctly labelled PPC with the right shape for constant, increasing or decreasing opportunity cost, and explain each shape. *(Guide: "Why the shape depends on opportunity cost"; Practice Q4, Q6(a), Q7(b), Q7(d))*
- I can show and explain an outward shift (growth) and an inward shift (contraction), naming changes in resources or technology that cause them. *(Guide: "Shifts of the PPC: growth and contraction"; Practice Q3, Q6(d))*
- I can show a pivot when technology improves only one good, and say how the opportunity costs change. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q6(e))*
- I can explain how producing more capital goods today affects the PPC in the future. *(Guide: "Shifts of the PPC: growth and contraction"; Practice Q6(c)–(d))*
