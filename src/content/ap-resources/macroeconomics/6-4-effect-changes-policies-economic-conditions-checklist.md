Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/macroeconomics/6-4-effect-changes-policies-economic-conditions-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/macroeconomics/6-4-effect-changes-policies-economic-conditions-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can list the factors that shift the demand for a currency: foreign tastes, foreign incomes, relative price levels, relative interest rates and expectations. *(Guide: "What shifts the demand for a currency"; Practice Q1)*
- I can list the factors that shift the supply of a currency, including tariffs and quotas on the other country's goods. *(Guide: "What shifts the supply of a currency"; Practice Q2)*
- I can explain why higher domestic incomes increase the supply of, not the demand for, the domestic currency. *(Guide: Worked example 2(c); Practice Q1)*
- I can tell a shift of a curve from a movement along it. *(Guide: "From movements to shifts")*

## Graphing and calculation

- I can show a shift in currency demand or supply on a correctly labelled graph and mark the new equilibrium exchange rate. *(Guide: Figures 1 and 2; Practice Q6(b))*
- I can calculate a new equilibrium exchange rate and quantity after a shift, and the shortage or surplus at the old rate. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q5)*
- I can calculate the percentage changes in the values of both currencies. *(Guide: Worked example 1(d); Practice Q5(d))*

## Reasoning

- I can trace deficit-financed fiscal policy through the real interest rate to the exchange rate. *(Guide: "Fiscal policy and the exchange rate"; Practice Q6(a)–(c))*
- I can trace expansionary and contractionary monetary policy through interest rates to the exchange rate. *(Guide: "Monetary policy and the exchange rate"; Practice Q3, Q6(d))*
- I can explain why higher relative inflation makes a currency depreciate, using both curves. *(Practice Q4)*
- I can decide whether the rate change and the quantity change are certain when two shifts happen together. *(Guide: "When two things change at once"; Practice Q7)*
