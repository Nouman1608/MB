## Recap

- **Fiscal policy:** the government's changes to **spending** and **taxes/transfers** to reach goals such as full employment and stable prices.
- **Government spending** is a component of AD, so it changes AD **directly**.
- **Taxes and transfers** change disposable income, then consumption (or investment), so they change AD **indirectly**.
- **Expansionary** policy (G ↑, taxes ↓, transfers ↑) closes a **recessionary** gap. **Contractionary** policy (G ↓, taxes ↑, transfers ↓) closes an **inflationary** gap.
- In the short run, fiscal policy changes **AD, real output and the price level** (and unemployment in the opposite direction to output).
- **Lags:** it takes time to recognise a gap, decide on a policy, implement it, and for it to take effect.

## Key relationships

| Quantity | Formula |
|---|---|
| Spending multiplier | 1 ÷ MPS = 1 ÷ (1 − MPC) |
| Tax multiplier | −MPC ÷ MPS |
| Change in G needed | output gap ÷ spending multiplier |
| Change in taxes or transfers needed | output gap ÷ size of tax multiplier |

| MPC | Spending multiplier | Tax multiplier | G needed for a 20 gap | Tax change needed for a 20 gap |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.75 | 4 | −3 | 5 | 6.67 |
| 0.8 | 5 | −4 | 4 | 5 |
| 0.9 | 10 | −9 | 2 | 2.22 |

## Assumptions

- The price level is treated as constant in the calculations, so the AD shift equals the change in real GDP. With an upward-sloping SRAS, real GDP actually rises by a little less.
- The MPC is constant and saving is the only leakage.
- Monetary policy (central bank, interest rates) is a separate tool, studied in Unit 4.

## Mistakes to avoid

1. **Multiplying** the gap by the multiplier instead of **dividing**.
2. **Using the spending multiplier for taxes or transfers.**
3. **Counting transfers as G.**
4. **Wrong direction:** a recessionary gap needs expansionary policy.
5. **Saying expansionary policy lowers the price level.** It raises it in the short run.
6. **Forgetting lags** when asked why fiscal policy may not stabilise the economy.

## Quick self-check

1. MPC 0.8, recessionary gap of VD 20 billion. Minimum rise in G? *(VD 4 billion)*
2. Same economy. Tax cut needed instead? *(VD 5 billion)*
3. Real GDP is above Yf. Name one fiscal action. *(Cut G, raise taxes or cut transfers.)*
