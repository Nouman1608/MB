Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/macroeconomics/4-2-nominal-v-real-interest-rates-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/macroeconomics/4-2-nominal-v-real-interest-rates-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can define the nominal interest rate and the real interest rate, and say which one measures purchasing power. *(Guide: "What an interest rate measures"; Practice Q1, Q6(a))*
- I can explain why lenders and borrowers build expected inflation into the nominal rate. *(Guide: "How lenders and borrowers set a nominal rate")*
- I can explain the difference between the real rate expected when a loan is agreed and the real rate calculated in hindsight. *(Guide: "Calculating a real rate in hindsight"; Worked example 2(d))*

## Calculation

- I can calculate a nominal rate from an expected real rate and expected inflation. *(Guide: Worked example 1(a); Practice Q2, Q6(b))*
- I can calculate a real rate in hindsight from a nominal rate and actual inflation, including a negative real rate. *(Guide: Worked example 1(b); Practice Q3, Q6(c))*
- I can calculate inflation from CPI data and then the real interest rate for each year, including years with deflation. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q5)*
- I can find the new nominal rate when expected inflation changes and the expected real rate stays the same. *(Practice Q4, Q7(d))*

## Reasoning

- I can explain who gains and who loses when actual inflation is higher or lower than expected. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q6(d))*
- I can explain why a higher nominal rate is not always a better deal for a saver. *(Guide: Worked example 2(c); Practice Q7(b))*
- I can explain why deflation makes the real rate higher than the nominal rate. *(Guide: Worked example 2, "Check")*
