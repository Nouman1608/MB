## Recap

- Every adult is **employed**, **unemployed** or **not in the labor force**.
- **Unemployed** means without a job, available, and actively looking. Retirees, students not seeking work and **discouraged workers** are outside the labor force.
- The measured rate can **understate** joblessness: discouraged workers are left out, and part-time workers who want full-time work count as fully employed.
- **Frictional:** searching between jobs or entering work. **Structural:** skills or location mismatch, often after technological change. **Cyclical:** caused by a downturn.
- **Full employment** = no cyclical unemployment, not zero unemployment.
- The natural rate can change slowly, for example when the labor force's age mix, search methods or skills change.

## Key relationships

| Relationship | Formula | Use it to |
|---|---|---|
| Labor force | employed + unemployed | find the denominator of the unemployment rate |
| Unemployment rate | unemployed ÷ labor force × 100 | measure the share of the labor force out of work |
| Participation rate | labor force ÷ adult population × 100 | measure how many adults work or seek work |
| Natural rate | frictional rate + structural rate | find the rate at full-employment output |
| Cyclical rate | actual rate − natural rate | measure unemployment caused by the business cycle |

## How moves between groups change the rates

| Move | Unemployment rate | Participation rate |
|---|---|---|
| Unemployed → employed | falls | unchanged |
| Employed → unemployed | rises | unchanged |
| Not in labor force → unemployed (starts looking) | rises | rises |
| Unemployed → discouraged | falls | falls |

## Mistakes to avoid

1. Dividing the unemployed by the **adult population** instead of the labor force.
2. Counting **discouraged workers** as unemployed.
3. Treating a part-time worker as unemployed.
4. Assuming a falling unemployment rate always means more jobs.
5. Including cyclical unemployment in the natural rate.
6. Calling a lasting skills mismatch "cyclical".

## Quick self-check

1. Employed 9.0 million, unemployed 1.0 million. Unemployment rate? *(10%)*
2. Labor force 6.3 million, adult population 9.0 million. Participation rate? *(70%)*
3. Natural rate 4.0%, actual rate 6.5%. Cyclical rate? *(2.5%)*
