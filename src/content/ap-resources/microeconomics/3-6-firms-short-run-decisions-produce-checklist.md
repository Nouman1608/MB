Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/microeconomics/3-6-firms-short-run-decisions-produce-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/microeconomics/3-6-firms-short-run-decisions-produce-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can explain the difference between shutting down (short run) and exiting (long run). *(Guide: "Two different time periods, two different decisions"; Practice Q7(d))*
- I can explain why fixed cost does not affect the short-run shutdown decision. *(Guide: "The short-run decision: produce or shut down"; Practice Q7(a))*
- I can explain why a firm may keep producing at an economic loss. *(Guide: "Why producing at a loss can make sense"; Practice Q2, Q5(c))*
- I can explain why firms enter a market with economic profit and exit one with expected economic losses, and why barriers matter. *(Guide: "The long-run decision: enter or exit"; Practice Q3, Q6(d))*

## Calculation and graphing

- I can decide between producing and shutting down by comparing TR with TVC, or P with AVC. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q1, Q4)*
- I can calculate a firm's result when it produces and when it shuts down (−TFC). *(Guide: Worked examples 1 and 2; Practice Q2, Q6(b))*
- I can find the shutdown price (minimum AVC) and the break-even price (minimum ATC) from a table. *(Guide: Worked example 1(a); Practice Q4(b))*
- I can draw MC, ATC and AVC with MC through both minimums, and show the price ranges for profit, loss and shutdown. *(Guide: Figure 1; Practice Q5)*
- I can shade the loss of a firm that is producing at a price between minimum AVC and minimum ATC. *(Practice Q5(b))*

## Reasoning

- I can use economic profit, not accounting profit, to predict entry or exit. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q3, Q6)*
- I can explain how a change in fixed cost changes the break-even price but not the shutdown price. *(Practice Q7(b))*
- I can judge a firm's short-run and long-run decisions from the same set of figures. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q6)*
