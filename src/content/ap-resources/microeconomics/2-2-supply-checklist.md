Work through the list without notes. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/microeconomics/2-2-supply-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/microeconomics/2-2-supply-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can define supply and quantity supplied, and explain "willing and able" and "other things equal". *(Guide: "What supply means")*
- I can state the law of supply and explain the upward slope using rising marginal cost and the profit incentive. *(Guide: "Why the supply curve slopes upward"; Practice Q3)*
- I can explain why a high-cost seller may supply nothing at a low price. *(Guide: individual supply schedules, Riverside)*

## Calculation and graphing

- I can build an individual supply schedule from marginal cost, producing each unit while price is at least MC. *(Guide: "Why the supply curve slopes upward"; Practice Q4)*
- I can add individual quantities at each price to find market supply. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q1, Q7)*
- I can draw a supply curve with both axes labelled (price with units; quantity with units and period) and the curve labelled. *(Guide: Figure 1; Practice Q5)*
- I can show a change in quantity supplied as a movement along the curve, and a change in supply as a shift right or left. *(Guide: Figures 1 and 2; Practice Q5)*
- I can read a shift both ways: more at each price, or a lower price for each quantity. *(Guide: Worked example 2(c))*

## Reasoning

- I can name the determinants of supply and give the direction of the shift for each: input prices, technology, prices of other goods the seller could make, taxes and subsidies, expectations, number of sellers and natural conditions. *(Guide: "The determinants of supply"; Practice Q2, Q6)*
- I can explain how a per-unit tax or subsidy changes supply. *(Practice Q2, Q5)*
- I can explain how sellers respond to a change in technology and to a change in incentives, and say when the combined effect is uncertain. *(Practice Q6)*
- I can tell a change in quantity supplied from a change in supply, and correct a statement that confuses them. *(Guide: "Change in quantity supplied versus change in supply"; Practice Q7)*
