Work through the list without notes. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/microeconomics/6-4-effects-government-intervention-different-market-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/microeconomics/6-4-effects-government-intervention-different-market-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can say whether a policy changes marginal cost, marginal revenue or only fixed cost, and predict whether price and output change. *(Guide: "One question for every policy"; Practice Q1)*
- I can explain who bears a per-unit tax in a competitive market, using elasticity. *(Guide: "Per-unit taxes and subsidies"; Practice Q2)*
- I can describe the three pricing options for a natural monopoly and the profit and deadweight loss at each. *(Guide: "Regulating a natural monopoly"; Practice Q6)*
- I can state the aim of antitrust policy and when it is a poor choice. *(Guide: "Antitrust policy"; Practice Q4, Q6(e))*

## Calculation and graphing

- I can calculate a monopoly's price, output, tax revenue and deadweight loss with a per-unit tax, and compare a lump-sum tax that raises the same revenue. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q3)*
- I can find a monopoly's output and shortage under a price ceiling and calculate the deadweight loss. *(Guide: Worked example 3; Practice Q7)*
- I can calculate the unregulated, fair-return and socially optimal outcomes for a natural monopoly, including the lump-sum subsidy needed. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q6)*
- I can find employment and the wage at a monopsony with and without a minimum wage. *(Guide: "Price controls in different market structures"; Practice Q5)*
- I can draw a monopoly graph with a price ceiling and a natural monopoly graph with U, F and S labelled. *(Guide: Figures 1 and 2; Practice Q6(a))*

## Reasoning

- I can explain why a price ceiling can raise a monopoly's output but always cuts output in a competitive market. *(Guide: "Price controls in different market structures"; Practice Q7(e))*
- I can explain why a minimum wage can raise employment at a monopsony but not in a competitive labour market. *(Practice Q5(d))*
- I can explain why the natural monopoly subsidy must be lump-sum, not per-unit. *(Guide: "Regulating a natural monopoly"; Practice Q6(d))*
- I can judge whether a policy raises efficiency by asking whether it corrects the incentive behind the market failure. *(Guide: "When intervention increases efficiency")*
