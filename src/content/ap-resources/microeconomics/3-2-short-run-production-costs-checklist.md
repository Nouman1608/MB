Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/microeconomics/3-2-short-run-production-costs-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/microeconomics/3-2-short-run-production-costs-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can tell a fixed cost from a variable cost with an example of each, and explain why TFC is paid even at zero output. *(Guide: "Fixed and variable costs"; Practice Q4(a))*
- I can explain why AFC falls at every output. *(Guide: "Why the cost curves have their shapes"; Practice Q3)*
- I can explain why MC falls at first (specialisation and the division of labour) and then rises (diminishing marginal returns). *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q6)*
- I can explain why MC cuts AVC and ATC at their minimum points. *(Guide: "Why the cost curves have their shapes"; Practice Q3, Q4(d))*

## Calculation and graphing

- I can find TFC from a total cost table and calculate TVC, MC, AFC, AVC and ATC at each output. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q1, Q4)*
- I can calculate marginal cost from production data using MC = wage ÷ MP. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q6)*
- I can use ATC = AFC + AVC to find a missing cost or TFC. *(Practice Q7)*
- I can draw TFC, TVC and TC, with TC parallel to TVC and starting at TFC. *(Guide: Figure 1)*
- I can draw labelled MC, ATC, AVC and AFC curves with the minimum of ATC to the right of the minimum of AVC. *(Guide: Figure 2; Practice Q5)*

## Reasoning

- I can say which cost curves shift when a fixed cost, a variable input price or productivity changes. *(Guide: "What shifts the cost curves"; Practice Q2, Q5, Q6(d))*
- I can explain why a change in fixed cost never changes marginal cost. *(Guide: Worked example 1(c); Practice Q2)*
- I can evaluate the claim that producing more always lowers average total cost. *(Guide: "Common misconceptions"; Practice Q7)*
