Work through the list without notes. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/microeconomics/2-8-effects-government-intervention-markets-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/microeconomics/2-8-effects-government-intervention-markets-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can define a price ceiling, a price floor, a quantity control, a per-unit tax and a per-unit subsidy. *(Guide: "Summary of the policies")*
- I can say when a price ceiling or floor is binding and whether it causes a shortage or a surplus. *(Guide: "Price ceilings", "Price floors"; Practice Q1)*
- I can explain why any of these policies lowers total surplus in an efficient competitive market, and what deadweight loss measures. *(Guide: "The benchmark: an efficient market")*
- I can describe how goods are rationed under a binding ceiling and why the shortage can grow over time. *(Guide: "Price ceilings"; Practice Q6)*

## Calculation and graphing

- I can find the price buyers pay, the price sellers keep and the new quantity after a per-unit tax. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q4)*
- I can do the same for a per-unit subsidy, and calculate its cost to the government. *(Guide: "Per-unit subsidies"; Practice Q7)*
- I can calculate consumer surplus, producer surplus, tax revenue and deadweight loss after an intervention, and check they add up to the original total. *(Guide: Worked examples 1 and 2; Practice Q4, Q5)*
- I can find the quantity traded and the deadweight loss under a binding price control or quota. *(Guide: Worked example 1, "Quantity controls"; Practice Q5)*
- I can draw a labelled graph of a ceiling, a floor or a tax, showing both prices, the quantity traded, any revenue rectangle and the deadweight loss triangle. *(Guide: Figures 1 and 2; Practice Q5)*

## Reasoning

- I can use the elasticities of demand and supply to say who bears more of a tax or gains more from a subsidy. *(Guide: "Who bears a tax? Elasticity decides"; Practice Q2, Q4(b), Q7(d))*
- I can explain why a subsidy creates deadweight loss even though buyers and sellers both gain. *(Practice Q7(c))*
- I can explain why a quota raises the price buyers pay and who receives the gap. *(Guide: "Quantity controls"; Practice Q3)*
