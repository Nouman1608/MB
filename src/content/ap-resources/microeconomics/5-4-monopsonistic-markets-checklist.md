Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/microeconomics/5-4-monopsonistic-markets-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/microeconomics/5-4-monopsonistic-markets-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can describe a monopsonistic labour market and explain why the firm is a wage setter facing the upward-sloping market supply of labour. *(Guide: "What a monopsony is")*
- I can explain why hiring one more worker costs more than that worker's wage, so MRC is above the wage. *(Guide: "Why MRC is above the wage"; Practice Q1, Q6(d))*
- I can tell a monopsony (one buyer) from a monopoly (one seller). *(Guide: Common misconceptions)*
- I can state the hiring rule MRC = MRP and explain why the wage comes from the supply curve, not from the MRC = MRP point. *(Guide: "The hiring rule"; Practice Q4)*

## Calculation and graphing

- I can calculate total labour cost and MRC from a supply schedule, and check MRC as the new wage plus the raises for existing workers. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q3, Q5)*
- I can find the monopsony's employment and wage from a table. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q5)*
- I can write the MRC equation for a straight-line supply curve and solve for the monopsony and competitive outcomes. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q7)*
- I can draw a correctly labelled monopsony graph with S, MRC, MRP, Lm, Wm, Lc and Wc, and shade the deadweight loss. *(Guide: Figure 1; Practice Q6)*
- I can calculate the deadweight loss of a monopsony from straight-line curves. *(Guide: Worked example 2(d); Practice Q7)*

## Reasoning

- I can compare a monopsony with a perfectly competitive labour market: employment, wage, MRC and the gap between wage and MRP. *(Guide: comparison table; Practice Q2, Q5(d))*
- I can explain why a monopsony does not hire workers whose MRP is above the wage they would accept. *(Guide: Worked example 1(d); Practice Q5(c))*
- I can explain why the monopsony outcome is inefficient. *(Guide: "Monopsony compared with a competitive labour market")*
