Work through the list without notes. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/microeconomics/1-2-resource-allocation-economic-systems-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/microeconomics/1-2-resource-allocation-economic-systems-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can explain why scarcity forces every society to allocate resources. *(Guide: "The problem every economy must solve")*
- I can state the three basic questions and say which one a given decision answers. *(Guide: "The three basic questions"; Practice Q1, Q5(a))*
- I can define an economic system as institutional arrangements plus a coordinating mechanism. *(Guide: "What an economic system is")*
- I can describe how a command, a market and a mixed economy each answer what, how and for whom. *(Guide: comparison table; Practice Q3)*
- I can name the coordinating mechanism of each system: a central plan, prices, or prices plus government decisions. *(Guide: Figure 2; Practice Q2)*

## Skills

- I can classify a described economy using evidence about both ownership and coordination. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q3, Q6)*
- I can draw a labelled spectrum of economic systems and place economies on it with reasons. *(Guide: Figure 1; Practice Q6)*
- I can calculate the total cost of two production methods and find which one a market firm chooses. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q4)*

## Reasoning

- I can explain how input prices signal scarcity and guide the "how" decision in a market. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q4(d))*
- I can explain, using information and incentives, why central planning can lead to shortages and surpluses. *(Guide: "Command economy"; Practice Q5)*
- I can give a strength and a limitation of each system, including who may go without under a market answer to "for whom". *(Guide: comparison table; Practice Q5(d))*
- I can state similarities and differences between the systems and explain the limitations of the models. *(Guide: "Similarities, differences and limitations of the models"; Practice Q7)*
