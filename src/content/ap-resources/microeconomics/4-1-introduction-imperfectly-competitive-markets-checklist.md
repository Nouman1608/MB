Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/microeconomics/4-1-introduction-imperfectly-competitive-markets-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/microeconomics/4-1-introduction-imperfectly-competitive-markets-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can name the three imperfectly competitive product-market structures and the factor-market one (monopsony). *(Guide: "The four market structures"; Practice Q1)*
- I can compare the four market structures by number of sellers, type of product, barriers to entry, demand facing the firm and long-run profit. *(Guide: table in "The four market structures")*
- I can name and give an example of three barriers to entry: high start-up costs, legal barriers and exclusive ownership of a key resource. *(Guide: "Barriers to entry"; Worked example 3; Practice Q5)*
- I can state one similarity between all four models and one limitation of using them for real markets. *(Guide: "Similarities, differences and limits of the models"; Practice Q7)*

## Calculation and graphing

- I can calculate total revenue and marginal revenue from a demand schedule. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q4)*
- I can split marginal revenue into a quantity effect and a price effect. *(Guide: Figure 2; Practice Q4)*
- I can find where total revenue is largest and say whether demand is elastic or inelastic on each side of it. *(Guide: Worked example 1(c); Practice Q4)*
- I can draw demand and MR for a price taker (one horizontal line) and for an imperfectly competitive firm (MR below demand, same price intercept). *(Guide: Figure 1; Practice Q6)*

## Reasoning

- I can explain why a firm with a downward-sloping demand curve has MR below price. *(Guide: "Why a firm must cut its price to sell more"; Practice Q2)*
- I can explain why P > MC at the chosen output means too little is produced, and why the firm still will not produce more. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q3, Q7)*
- I can tell P > MC (an efficiency idea) apart from P > ATC (a profit idea). *(Guide: "Common misconceptions"; Practice Q3)*
- I can explain how barriers to entry let economic profit last in the long run. *(Practice Q5)*
