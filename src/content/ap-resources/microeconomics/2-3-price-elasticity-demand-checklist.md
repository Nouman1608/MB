Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/microeconomics/2-3-price-elasticity-demand-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/microeconomics/2-3-price-elasticity-demand-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can define price elasticity of demand and explain why it uses percentage changes rather than raw changes. *(Guide: "What elasticity measures")*
- I can classify demand as elastic, inelastic or unit elastic from the size of |PED|, and explain that the label applies to a price range. *(Guide: "Elastic, inelastic and unit elastic"; Practice Q4)*
- I can describe and draw perfectly elastic and perfectly inelastic demand curves. *(Guide: Figure 1; Practice Q7)*
- I can explain how substitutes, necessity, share of income, time and market definition affect PED. *(Guide: "What makes demand more or less elastic"; Practice Q2, Q6)*

## Calculation and graphing

- I can calculate PED with the midpoint method from a table, showing both percentage changes. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q1, Q4, Q6)*
- I can calculate PED from given percentage changes, and use a known PED to predict a percentage change in quantity. *(Guide: Worked example 2)*
- I can calculate total revenue before and after a price change. *(Practice Q4, Q6)*
- I can draw a labelled straight-line demand curve and mark its unit-elastic midpoint and its elastic and inelastic sections. *(Guide: Figure 2; Practice Q5)*
- I can show total revenue as a rectangle under the demand curve. *(Practice Q5)*

## Reasoning

- I can explain why slope is not elasticity, using numbers from a straight-line demand curve. *(Guide: "Slope is not elasticity"; Practice Q5, Q7)*
- I can use the total revenue test in both directions: predict TR from elasticity, and infer elasticity from what happened to TR. *(Guide: "Elasticity and total revenue"; Practice Q3)*
- I can advise a seller whether to raise or cut price to increase revenue. *(Guide: Figure 3; Practice Q7)*
- I can explain why demand usually becomes more elastic over time. *(Practice Q6)*
