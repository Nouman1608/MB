Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/microeconomics/4-5-oligopoly-game-theory-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/microeconomics/4-5-oligopoly-game-theory-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can describe an oligopoly: a few firms, high barriers to entry and interdependent decisions. *(Guide: "What makes a market an oligopoly"; Practice Q1)*
- I can explain why oligopolists have an incentive to collude and form a cartel. *(Guide: "Collusion and cartels")*
- I can define a game, a strategy, a payoff matrix, a dominant strategy and a Nash equilibrium. *(Guide: "The language of game theory")*
- I can explain why oligopoly is inefficient and why its price is usually higher, and its output lower, than in perfect competition. *(Guide: Figure 1; Practice Q4, Q7(d))*

## Calculation and skills

- I can read a payoff matrix, knowing which payoff belongs to which player. *(Guide: Worked example 1(a))*
- I can find a player's dominant strategy, or show it has none, by comparing its own payoffs within each column or row. *(Guide: "A method that always works"; Practice Q2, Q5(a))*
- I can find every Nash equilibrium of a two-player, two-action game, including when only one player has a dominant strategy. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q3)*
- I can calculate the payment or penalty that must be exceeded to change a player's dominant strategy, using the largest payoff gap. *(Guide: Worked example 1(c); Practice Q5(c), Q6(c))*
- I can build a cartel payoff matrix from a demand equation and a constant marginal cost. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q7)*

## Reasoning

- I can explain the prisoner's dilemma and why it makes cartels hard to sustain. *(Guide: "Why oligopolists rarely reach the monopoly outcome"; Practice Q5(b))*
- I can apply game theory to a non-market situation, such as a shared resource. *(Guide: "Games outside markets"; Practice Q6)*
- I can explain how changing one player's payoffs changes the equilibrium, and why it may still not reach the cooperative outcome. *(Practice Q5(d))*
