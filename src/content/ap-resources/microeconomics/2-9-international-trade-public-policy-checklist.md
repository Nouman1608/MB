Work through the list without notes. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/microeconomics/2-9-international-trade-public-policy-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/microeconomics/2-9-international-trade-public-policy-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can define a tariff and an import quota in one sentence each. *(Guide: "Tariffs and quotas: why and how")*
- I can explain why a small open economy's domestic price becomes the world price. *(Guide: "From a closed market to an open one")*
- I can say whether a country imports or exports by comparing the world price with the autarky price. *(Guide: "The exporting case"; Practice Q2, Q7)*
- I can name who gains and who loses surplus when a country imports and when it exports. *(Guide: summary table; Practice Q2)*

## Calculation and graphing

- I can calculate imports or exports as a gap between quantity demanded and quantity supplied. *(Guide: Worked examples 1 and 3; Practice Q1, Q7)*
- I can calculate consumer, producer and total surplus before and after a market opens to trade. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q4)*
- I can draw a labelled tariff graph with the world price, the price with the tariff, and the new imports. *(Guide: Figure 2; Practice Q5)*
- I can calculate tariff revenue, the deadweight loss and the changes in consumer and producer surplus from a tariff. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q5)*
- I can find the smallest tariff that stops all imports. *(Guide: Worked example 2(d); Practice Q5(d))*
- I can find the domestic price under a binding import quota, using a schedule or the demand and supply equations. *(Guide: "Quotas: effects without a graph"; Practice Q6)*

## Reasoning

- I can explain why most of the producers' loss from imports is a transfer, not a loss to the country. *(Guide: "The importing case on a graph")*
- I can explain why a tariff moves producers along their supply curve rather than shifting it. *(Guide: "Common misconceptions"; Practice Q3)*
- I can explain how a quota raises domestic production and lowers consumer and total surplus, and why it earns no tariff revenue. *(Practice Q6)*
- I can evaluate a claim that trade "harms" a country by weighing the losing group against the net gain. *(Practice Q7)*
