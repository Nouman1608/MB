Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/statistics/4-10-carrying-out-test-difference-between-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/statistics/4-10-carrying-out-test-difference-between-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can explain what the two-sample t-statistic measures, in standard errors from 0. *(Guide: "The test statistic")*
- I can explain why the p-value is calculated assuming the two population means are equal. *(Guide: "Interpreting the p-value"; Practice Q2)*
- I can explain why failing to reject H₀ does not show the means are equal. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q3, Q7)*
- I can explain why conservative degrees of freedom give a slightly larger p-value. *(Guide: "Degrees of freedom and the p-value"; Practice Q4)*

## Calculation

- I can calculate the standard error and t-statistic from summary statistics or raw data. *(Guide: Worked examples 1 and 2; Practice Q1, Q5, Q6)*
- I can find the p-value with technology, using the tail or tails set by Hₐ. *(Guide: Figure 1; Practice Q5, Q6)*
- I can bound a p-value with a t-table and the conservative df. *(Guide: Worked example 1, Check; Practice Q5)*
- I can state the range in which the degrees of freedom must lie. *(Practice Q4)*

## Reasoning

- I can interpret a p-value in context, including the observed difference and the direction of Hₐ. *(Guide: Worked examples 1 and 2; Practice Q2, Q5)*
- I can compare the p-value with α and write a conclusion about Hₐ, in context, with non-definitive language. *(Practice Q3, Q5, Q6)*
- I can say whether a conclusion can be about cause and effect, and to whom it applies. *(Guide: Worked example 1, Answer the question; Practice Q6)*
- I can explain how a two-sided test and a confidence interval for μ₁ − μ₂ agree. *(Guide: Worked example 2, Check; Practice Q7)*
