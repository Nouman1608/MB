Work through the list without notes. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/statistics/3-7-carrying-out-test-population-proportion-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/statistics/3-7-carrying-out-test-population-proportion-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can explain why the standard error in a test uses p₀ and not p̂. *(Guide: "The test statistic"; Practice Q1)*
- I can explain what the null distribution is and why the standard normal curve can model it when the conditions are met. *(Guide: "The test statistic")*
- I can explain the significance level α as the probability, chosen in advance, of rejecting H₀ when H₀ is true. *(Guide: "Significance level and the decision"; Practice Q7)*
- I can explain why failing to reject H₀ is not evidence that H₀ is true. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q3)*

## Calculation

- I can calculate p̂ and the z test statistic, with the formula and numbers shown. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q1, Q4)*
- I can find the p-value for a right-tailed, left-tailed or two-sided alternative, with a table or technology. *(Guide: "Finding the p-value"; Practice Q2)*
- I can find the correct p-value even when p̂ is on the opposite side of p₀ from Hₐ. *(Practice Q6)*

## Reasoning

- I can make a decision by comparing the p-value with α, quoting both numbers. *(Guide: "Writing the conclusion"; Practice Q4)*
- I can write a conclusion in context, about the true population proportion, in terms of Hₐ, with non-definitive language. *(Guide: both worked examples; Practice Q3, Q5)*
- I can use the result of a test to answer the investigative question that started the study. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q5, Q7)*
- I can explain why α and Hₐ must be chosen before looking at the data. *(Practice Q4(d), Q6(c))*
- I can carry out all four parts of a test (hypotheses, method and conditions, calculations, conclusion) without prompts. *(Practice Q5)*
