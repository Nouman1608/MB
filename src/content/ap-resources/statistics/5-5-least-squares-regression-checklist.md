Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/statistics/5-5-least-squares-regression-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/statistics/5-5-least-squares-regression-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can explain that the least-squares line is the line with the smallest possible sum of squared residuals, and why the residuals are squared. *(Guide: "Which line is the best line?"; Practice Q6)*
- I can explain why a line whose residuals add to 0 is not necessarily the least-squares line. *(Guide: "Facts about the least-squares line"; Practice Q6)*
- I can explain that the slope and intercept are statistics from one sample and would change with a different sample. *(Guide: "Facts about the least-squares line")*
- I can explain why b and r always have the same sign. *(Guide: "Background: how the slope is linked to r"; Practice Q2)*

## Skills

- I can use technology to find a, b, r and r², with the explanatory variable entered as x. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q4)*
- I can read the slope, intercept and r² from technology output and write the equation with the variables defined. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q5)*
- I can use the fact that the line passes through (x̄, ȳ) to find ȳ or the intercept. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q3, Q5)*
- I can find r from r², choosing the sign from the slope. *(Guide: "The coefficient of determination, r²"; Practice Q2, Q7)*
- I can make a prediction with the line and say whether it is interpolation or extrapolation. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q4)*

## Reasoning

- I can interpret the slope in context as a predicted change in y per one-unit increase in x, with units. *(Guide: "Interpreting the slope and the y-intercept"; Practice Q1, Q4)*
- I can interpret the y-intercept in context, or explain why it has no sensible meaning. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q5)*
- I can interpret r² as the proportion of the variation in y explained by the linear relationship with x. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q5, Q7)*
- I can spot wrong claims about r², extrapolation and causation made from a regression line. *(Practice Q7)*
