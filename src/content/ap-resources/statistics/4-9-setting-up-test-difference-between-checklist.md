Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/statistics/4-9-setting-up-test-difference-between-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/statistics/4-9-setting-up-test-difference-between-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can explain the difference between asking "how big is the difference?" (an interval) and "is there convincing evidence of a difference?" (a test). *(Guide: "From an interval to a test")*
- I can explain why the test uses a t-distribution and not the standard normal. *(Guide: "Choosing the procedure")*
- I can explain what makes two samples independent, and recognise paired data that need a different test. *(Guide: Figure 1 and Worked example 3; Practice Q2)*
- I can explain what each of the three conditions protects against. *(Guide: "The three conditions")*

## Skills

- I can name the two-sample t-test for a difference between two population means in full. *(Practice Q2, Q5)*
- I can define μ₁ and μ₂ in context, with the response variable, units and each population or treatment. *(Guide: "Defining the parameters"; Practice Q5)*
- I can write H₀ and a one-sided or two-sided Hₐ in either equivalent form, keeping one order of subtraction. *(Guide: Worked examples 1 and 2; Practice Q1)*
- I can check the 10% condition for each sample against its own population, and say when it is not needed. *(Guide: Worked example 3(d); Practice Q3)*
- I can check the sample data condition for small samples with the 1.5 × IQR rule or a graph. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q4, Q6)*

## Reasoning

- I can decide whether a two-sample t-test is appropriate and explain why not when a condition fails. *(Guide: Worked example 3; Practice Q6)*
- I can find and correct errors in someone else's set-up. *(Practice Q7)*
- I can say, before carrying out a test, whether its conclusion could be about cause and effect and to whom it could apply. *(Guide: Worked example 2, Check)*
