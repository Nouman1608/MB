Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/statistics/2-1-tabular-graphical-representations-distributions-two-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/statistics/2-1-tabular-graphical-representations-distributions-two-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can explain what a cell, a row total, a column total and the grand total of a two-way table each count. *(Guide: "Two-way tables")*
- I can explain why counts can mislead when groups have different sizes, and why proportions within each group are fair. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q6)*
- I can say what the width, the height and the area of a rectangle in a mosaic plot each show. *(Guide: "Mosaic plots"; Practice Q3, Q7)*
- I can explain what it means for two categorical variables to be associated, and what no association looks like on a graph. *(Guide: "Association between two categorical variables"; Practice Q2)*

## Skills

- I can complete a two-way table with missing cells and totals, and check it against the grand total. *(Practice Q4, Q6)*
- I can find the distribution of one variable within each category of the other. *(Guide: "Two-way tables"; Practice Q5)*
- I can draw a segmented bar chart with bars of height 1, segments in the same order and a key that does not rely on colour. *(Guide: "Segmented bar charts"; Practice Q5)*
- I can choose the right graph for a comparison and describe a fair side-by-side bar chart. *(Practice Q1, Q6)*
- I can recover counts from a mosaic plot using width × height × total. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q3, Q7)*

## Reasoning

- I can decide whether two categorical variables appear to be associated and justify it with within-group proportions. *(Practice Q4, Q7)*
- I can support or reject a claim in context, quoting the proportions it depends on. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q5, Q6)*
- I can explain why an association in observational data does not show cause and effect. *(Guide: "Association between two categorical variables"; Practice Q6)*
