Work through the list without notes. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/statistics/1-7-summary-statistics-one-quantitative-variable-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/statistics/1-7-summary-statistics-one-quantitative-variable-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can explain the difference between a measure of centre and a measure of variability. *(Guide: "Why summarise a distribution with numbers?")*
- I can explain what a standard deviation tells me as a typical distance from the mean, and why it is not a maximum distance. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q7)*
- I can say which statistics are resistant (median, IQR) and which are not (mean, range, s), and explain why. *(Guide: "Resistant and non-resistant statistics"; Practice Q2)*
- I can predict how multiplying the data by a constant, or adding a constant, changes each statistic. *(Guide: "Changing the units"; Practice Q3)*

## Skills

- I can find the median and quartiles of a data set with an odd or even number of values. *(Guide: "Measures of position"; Practice Q1)*
- I can find the percentile of a given value. *(Guide: "Measures of position"; Practice Q7)*
- I can calculate the range and IQR and interpret the IQR as the spread of the middle half. *(Practice Q1, Q5)*
- I can calculate the sample standard deviation by hand, dividing by n − 1, and check it with Sx on a calculator. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q4)*
- I can apply the 1.5 × IQR rule and the 2-standard-deviation rule to flag potential outliers. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q5, Q6)*

## Reasoning

- I can justify whether the mean or the median better describes a typical value, using the shape and any outliers. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q5)*
- I can compare two distributions using summary statistics, with comparative words, values and units. *(Practice Q6)*
- I can write every interpretation in context, naming the variable, the individuals and the units. *(All worked examples)*
