Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/statistics/2-3-estimating-probabilities-simulation-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/statistics/2-3-estimating-probabilities-simulation-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can explain what a random process, a trial, an outcome and an event are, with an example of each. *(Guide: "Random processes, outcomes and events"; Practice Q1)*
- I can explain probability as the relative frequency of an event over a very large number of trials. *(Guide: "Probability as long-run relative frequency")*
- I can explain why a relative frequency from data or a simulation is only an estimate of the true probability. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q7)*
- I can state the law of large numbers and explain why it says nothing about the next trial. *(Guide: "The law of large numbers"; Practice Q3, Q7)*

## Skills

- I can assign single digits, pairs of digits or random integers to outcomes so that the probabilities match the real situation. *(Guide: "Designing a simulation"; Practice Q2, Q6)*
- I can describe one trial of a simulation and say what to record. *(Practice Q5, Q6)*
- I can carry out trials from a line of random digits, allowing repeats when the parts of a trial are independent. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q4)*
- I can carry out trials without replacement, skipping a value that repeats within a trial. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q5)*
- I can estimate a probability as count ÷ total from a table of simulation results, and use it to estimate an expected number. *(Practice Q5, Q6)*

## Reasoning

- I can judge which of two simulation estimates is more reliable, using the number of trials. *(Practice Q7)*
- I can explain why the "law of averages" is wrong for independent trials. *(Guide: "Common misconceptions"; Practice Q3, Q7)*
- I can write a conclusion to a simulation in context, with the count, the total and the estimate. *(All worked examples)*
