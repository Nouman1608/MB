Work through the list without notes. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/statistics/1-11-random-sampling-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/statistics/1-11-random-sampling-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can explain why a sample chosen by chance lets me generalise to the population, and why a haphazard choice is not random. *(Guide: "Why choose a sample at random?")*
- I can explain the difference between sampling with replacement and sampling without replacement. *(Guide: "Sampling without replacement and with replacement"; Practice Q4)*
- I can state what makes a sample a simple random sample: every possible sample of size n is equally likely. *(Guide: "Simple random sample (SRS)"; Practice Q2)*
- I can explain how strata differ from clusters, both in how the groups are formed and in who is sampled. *(Guide: "Strata versus clusters"; Practice Q1)*

## Skills

- I can identify a simple random, stratified, cluster or systematic random sample from a description of a study. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q1, Q2)*
- I can describe how to select an SRS with a random number generator or numbered slips, including labels, repeats and a stopping rule. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q5)*
- I can use a line of random digits to choose an SRS, skipping numbers that are not labels and repeats. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q5)*
- I can describe how to select a stratified sample, including a sample size in proportion to each stratum. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q7)*
- I can describe how to select a cluster sample and a systematic sample, including the interval k and the random start. *(Guide: "Systematic random sample"; Practice Q6, Q7)*

## Reasoning

- I can choose a useful characteristic for strata and explain why it helps. *(Guide: "Stratified random sample"; Practice Q3)*
- I can explain why a systematic sample can fail when the list has a repeating pattern. *(Practice Q6)*
- I can justify which sampling method suits a given question and population, using the context. *(Guide: "Choosing a method"; Practice Q7)*
