Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/statistics/4-5-carrying-out-test-population-mean-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/statistics/4-5-carrying-out-test-population-mean-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can explain why a test for a mean uses a t-distribution with n − 1 degrees of freedom and not the standard normal curve. *(Guide: "The test statistic"; Practice Q2, Q7)*
- I can explain that a matched-pairs test is a one-sample t-test on the differences, with n equal to the number of pairs. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q6)*
- I can explain why failing to reject H₀ does not show that H₀ is true. *(Guide: "Decision and conclusion"; Practice Q4, Q7)*

## Calculation

- I can calculate the t test statistic from summary statistics or raw data, using the standard error s / √n. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q1, Q5)*
- I can find the p-value with technology for a left-tailed, right-tailed or two-sided alternative. *(Guide: "Finding the p-value"; Practice Q2)*
- I can use a t-table to find a range for a p-value, using the next smaller df when my df is missing. *(Guide: Worked example 3)*
- I can find the differences, x̄d and sd for matched pairs and check the conditions on the differences. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q6)*

## Reasoning

- I can interpret a p-value in context, starting from the assumption that the true mean equals μ₀. *(Guide: "Interpreting the p-value"; Practice Q3, Q5)*
- I can make a decision by comparing the p-value with the α chosen in advance, using both numbers. *(Guide: "Decision and conclusion"; Practice Q4)*
- I can write a non-definitive conclusion about the true mean or mean difference of the population, in terms of Hₐ, and use it to answer the investigative question. *(Guide: Worked examples 1 and 2; Practice Q5, Q6)*
- I can find and correct errors in another student's test. *(Practice Q7)*
