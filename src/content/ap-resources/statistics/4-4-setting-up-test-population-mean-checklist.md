Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/statistics/4-4-setting-up-test-population-mean-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/statistics/4-4-setting-up-test-population-mean-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can explain why a test for a mean uses s and a t-distribution rather than σ and a normal model. *(Guide: "From proportions to means")*
- I can explain what makes data matched pairs, and why paired data are analysed as one sample of differences. *(Guide: "Recognising matched pairs"; Practice Q2)*
- I can explain why "n < 30" alone does not make a t-test inappropriate. *(Guide: "The three conditions"; Practice Q4)*

## Skills

- I can choose between a one-sample t-test for μ, a one-sample t-test for μd and a two-sample procedure, and name the procedure in full. *(Guide: Figure 1, Worked example 3; Practice Q2, Q5)*
- I can define μ in context, naming the variable, units and population. *(Guide: "Defining the parameter"; Practice Q1)*
- I can define μd with the order of subtraction stated. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q6)*
- I can write H₀ and the correct one-sided or two-sided Hₐ for μ or μd, including for "at least" or "at most" claims. *(Guide: "Writing the hypotheses"; Practice Q3)*
- I can check the random and 10% conditions in context, and say when the 10% condition does not apply. *(Guide: Worked examples 1 and 2; Practice Q5)*
- I can check the sample data condition for a small sample, or for the differences, using the 1.5 × IQR rule. *(Guide: Worked examples 1 and 2; Practice Q6)*

## Reasoning

- I can make the direction of Hₐ match the order of subtraction and the wording of the question. *(Guide: "Writing the hypotheses"; Practice Q6(b))*
- I can decide that a t-test is not appropriate when a condition fails, and suggest what to do. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q4)*
- I can find and correct errors in someone else's set-up. *(Practice Q7)*
