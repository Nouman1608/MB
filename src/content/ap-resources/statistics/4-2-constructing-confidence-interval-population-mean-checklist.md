Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/statistics/4-2-constructing-confidence-interval-population-mean-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/statistics/4-2-constructing-confidence-interval-population-mean-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can explain why using s in place of σ leads to a t-distribution instead of the standard normal. *(Guide: "Why a new distribution?"; Practice Q7(a))*
- I can describe t-distributions: symmetric, bell-shaped, heavier tails than the standard normal, and closer to it as df increases. *(Guide: "t-distributions"; Practice Q1)*
- I can explain why a matched-pairs design is analysed as one sample of differences. *(Guide: "Choosing the procedure and defining the parameter"; Practice Q6(a))*

## Skills

- I can choose a one-sample t-interval for μ or for μd, and tell it apart from a two-sample or proportion procedure. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q3)*
- I can define the parameter in context, with the variable, units, population and, for pairs, the order of subtraction. *(Practice Q5(a), Q6(b))*
- I can find t* for a given confidence level using df = n − 1. *(Guide: "The calculation"; Practice Q2)*
- I can calculate the standard error s/√n and the margin of error t* × s/√n. *(Guide: Worked example 3; Practice Q4)*
- I can construct a t-interval from raw data or from summary statistics, keeping unrounded values until the end. *(Guide: Worked examples 1 and 3; Practice Q4, Q5)*

## Reasoning

- I can check the randomization, 10% and sample data conditions with numbers and context, looking at the data when n < 30. *(Guide: "The three conditions"; Practice Q5(b))*
- I can check the sample data condition on the differences for paired data. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q6(c))*
- I can explain what goes wrong if z* is used with s, or if a sample with an outlier is used when n < 30. *(Practice Q7)*
