Work through the list without notes or a calculator. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-ab/7-4-reasoning-slope-fields-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-ab/7-4-reasoning-slope-fields-practice/). This topic is shared by Calculus AB and Calculus BC.

## Understanding

- I can explain why a differential equation usually has a family of solutions, and how one point selects a particular solution. *(Guide: "Solutions are functions, and there are many")*
- I can say how the members of a family are related when dy/dx depends on x only or on y only. *(Guide: shifts table; Practice Q4)*
- I can explain why a horizontal line of flat segments is a solution but a sloping line of flat segments is not. *(Guide: "Constant solutions"; Practice Q1)*

## Skills

- I can find the constant solutions of a differential equation and check them by substitution. *(Practice Q1, Q5(c), Q6(a))*
- I can sketch the particular solution through a given point, on both sides of the point. *(Guide: "How to sketch a particular solution"; Practice Q5(a))*
- I can find d²y/dx² by implicit differentiation and write it in terms of x and y. *(Guide: Worked examples 1 and 2; Practice Q3, Q5(d), Q7(b))*
- I can write a tangent line to a particular solution and use it to estimate a value. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q6(c))*

## Reasoning

- I can describe the long-run behaviour of a solution from the field and justify it with the slopes. *(Guide: Worked example 1(c); Practice Q2, Q5(b), Q6(d))*
- I can decide whether a tangent-line estimate is an over- or underestimate using concavity. *(Guide: Worked example 2(b); Practice Q6(c))*
- I can justify a relative maximum or minimum of a particular solution with dy/dx and d²y/dx². *(Practice Q5(d))*
- I can show that a straight line is a solution and reason about whether nearby solutions approach it or move away. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q7)*
