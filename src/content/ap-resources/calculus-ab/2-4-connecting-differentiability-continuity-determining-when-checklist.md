Work through the list without notes or a calculator. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-ab/2-4-connecting-differentiability-continuity-determining-when-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-ab/2-4-connecting-differentiability-continuity-determining-when-practice/). This topic is shared by Calculus AB and Calculus BC.

## Understanding

- I can state that a function differentiable at a point is continuous there, and explain why. *(Guide: "Differentiable means continuous"; Practice Q1)*
- I can use the contrapositive: a discontinuity at a means f′(a) does not exist. *(Guide: "The contrapositive: a fast test"; Practice Q5(b), Q6(c))*
- I can explain why a point missing from the domain of f is missing from the domain of f′. *(Guide: the r(x) example; Practice Q3)*
- I can explain, with an example, why continuity does not guarantee differentiability. *(Guide: "The converse is false"; Practice Q7(d))*

## Skills

- I can recognise a corner, a cusp, a vertical tangent and a discontinuity on a graph. *(Guide: Figure 1; Practice Q2, Q5)*
- I can find both one-sided limits of the difference quotient, using the correct value of f(a). *(Guide: Worked examples 1 and 2; Practice Q6(b), Q7(b))*
- I can rewrite an absolute-value function piece by piece near the point where its inside changes sign. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q7(b))*
- I can find constants that make a piecewise function continuous and differentiable at a join, or show none exist. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q4)*

## Reasoning

- I can write a justification that names the one-sided limits or quotes the theorem, not just "it's sharp". *(Guide: "Writing a justification"; Practice Q5, Q6)*
- I can check continuity before comparing slopes at the join of a piecewise function. *(Guide: "The two-step method"; Practice Q6)*
- I can explain why a calculator's numerical derivative may report a number where no derivative exists. *(Guide: "A warning about calculators"; Practice Q7(c))*
