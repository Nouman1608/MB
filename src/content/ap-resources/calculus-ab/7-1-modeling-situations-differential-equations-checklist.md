Work through the list without notes or a calculator. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-ab/7-1-modeling-situations-differential-equations-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-ab/7-1-modeling-situations-differential-equations-practice/). This topic is shared by Calculus AB and Calculus BC.

## Understanding

- I can say what a differential equation is and give an example that links a function, its input and its derivative. *(Guide: "The big idea")*
- I can explain why the solution of a differential equation is a function, not a number. *(Guide: "The big idea")*
- I can tell a first-order equation from a second-order one. *(Guide: "The big idea"; Practice Q7(c))*
- I can explain, using a graph, how a rate that depends on the quantity changes the steepness of the curve. *(Guide: Figure 1)*

## Skills

- I can translate "proportional to", "inversely proportional to", "jointly proportional to" and "the difference between" into symbols. *(Guide: "From words to symbols"; Practice Q1, Q2, Q5)*
- I can build a "rate in minus rate out" model. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q6)*
- I can choose the sign of the rate from the wording and say which sign k has. *(Guide: "Getting the sign right"; Practice Q2, Q7(a))*
- I can find k from one given rate, and give its units. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q3, Q5(b), Q7(b))*
- I can use a differential equation to find the rate at a new value and interpret it with units. *(Guide: Worked examples 1 and 2; Practice Q3, Q6(c))*

## Reasoning

- I can put a given differential equation into words. *(Guide: Worked example 3; Practice Q4)*
- I can find the value of the quantity at which the rate is zero, and say what that means in context. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q6(d))*
- I can explain in context why a rate is large or small at a given value. *(Practice Q5(c), Q7(d))*
