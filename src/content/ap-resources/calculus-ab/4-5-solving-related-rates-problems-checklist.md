Work through the list without notes or a calculator. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-ab/4-5-solving-related-rates-problems-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-ab/4-5-solving-related-rates-problems-practice/). This topic is shared by Calculus AB and Calculus BC.

## Understanding

- I can explain what a related rates problem asks: one rate found from other known rates at the same instant. *(Guide: "From technique to problem solving")*
- I can decide which lengths in a situation are constants and which are variables, and label a diagram that way. *(Guide: Figure 1; Practice Q6(a))*
- I can explain why a related rate usually changes from moment to moment, using the shape of the container or the geometry. *(Guide: Worked example 2(b); Practice Q4, Q7(c))*

## Skills

- I can use Pythagoras to link distances and find a missing side at the instant before substituting. *(Guide: Worked example 1(a); Practice Q1, Q6)*
- I can use tan θ to find the rate of change of an angle, including the sec²θ factor. *(Guide: Worked example 1(b); Practice Q3)*
- I can use similar triangles to remove a variable whose rate is unknown, in a cone, a trough or a shadow problem. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q2, Q5, Q7)*
- I can combine an inflow and an outflow into a net rate of change of volume. *(Practice Q7(b))*
- I can give each rate with correct units and the correct sign for an increasing or decreasing quantity. *(Guide: "A six-step method"; Practice Q6(b))*

## Reasoning

- I can write a full interpretation: which quantity, direction, size with units, and the instant. *(Guide: "Interpreting the answer"; Practice Q6(c))*
- I can tell the rate of change of a length apart from the speed of a moving point, such as a shadow's length versus its tip. *(Practice Q5(d))*
- I can judge whether an answer is reasonable, and say when a model stops being realistic. *(Guide: Worked example 1 check; Practice Q6(d))*
