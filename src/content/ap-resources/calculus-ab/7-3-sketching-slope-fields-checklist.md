Work through the list without notes or a calculator. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-ab/7-3-sketching-slope-fields-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-ab/7-3-sketching-slope-fields-practice/). This topic is shared by Calculus AB and Calculus BC.

## Understanding

- I can explain what one segment of a slope field represents, and why it is a piece of a tangent line and not the solution itself. *(Guide: "What a slope field shows")*
- I can explain why a slope field can be drawn without solving the differential equation. *(Guide: "What a slope field shows"; Revision notes)*
- I can say what identical columns, identical rows and horizontal segments tell me about dy/dx. *(Guide: "Patterns that let you read a field quickly"; Practice Q2)*

## Skills

- I can find the slope of the segment at a given point, substituting x and y in the right places. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q1)*
- I can sketch a slope field at a given grid of points from a table, with short, centred segments and consistent steepness. *(Guide: "How to sketch a slope field by hand"; Practice Q5(a))*
- I can sketch a slope field for an equation written with other variables, such as dH/dt, and label the axes correctly. *(Practice Q6(a))*
- I can find every line or curve where the segments are horizontal by solving dy/dx = 0. *(Guide: "Patterns"; Practice Q4, Q5(c))*

## Reasoning

- I can match an equation to a slope field by checking structure, then zero slopes, then the sign at a test point. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q3)*
- I can use the signs of factors to say where segments rise and where they fall. *(Guide: Worked example 1 "Check by signs"; Practice Q5(d), Q7)*
- I can interpret horizontal and steep segments in a real context, with units. *(Practice Q6(c), Q6(d))*
- I can trace a rough solution curve through a point by following the segments. *(Guide: "A first look at solution curves")*
