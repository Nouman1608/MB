Work through the list without notes or a calculator. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-ab/2-5-applying-power-rule-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-ab/2-5-applying-power-rule-practice/). This topic is shared by Calculus AB and Calculus BC.

## Understanding

- I can find the derivative of x³ from the limit definition and explain how the pattern n·xⁿ⁻¹ arises. *(Guide: "Building the pattern from the definition")*
- I can use the limit definition to confirm the power rule for a negative power such as 1/x². *(Guide: "Testing the pattern on 1/x and √x"; Practice Q5)*
- I can state the power rule, including the condition that the exponent is a constant. *(Guide: "The power rule"; Revision notes)*
- I can explain why the power rule does not apply to 2ˣ or to a constant such as π³. *(Guide: "Common misconceptions")*

## Skills

- I can rewrite reciprocals and roots as powers of x, such as 1/x⁵ = x⁻⁵ and ∜(x³) = x^(3/4). *(Guide: "Rewrite before you differentiate"; Practice Q1, Q2)*
- I can lower a negative or fractional exponent by 1 without sign errors. *(Guide; Practice Q1, Q6(b))*
- I can combine products and quotients of powers into a single power before differentiating. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q6)*
- I can work backwards from a derivative to the power function it came from. *(Practice Q3)*
- I can find the slope and equation of a tangent line to y = xʳ at a given point. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q4, Q7(b))*
- I can find the x-values where a tangent to a power function has a given slope, including negative solutions. *(Practice Q4, Q7(c))*

## Reasoning

- I can explain why the derivative of a product of powers is not the product of their derivatives, using a numerical counterexample. *(Practice Q6(d))*
- I can identify where the power rule gives an undefined derivative and use the definition to decide between a vertical tangent and a cusp. *(Guide: "When the power rule gives 'undefined'"; Practice Q7(d))*
- I can explain why the domain of f′ can be smaller than the domain of f. *(Guide: "When the power rule gives 'undefined'")*
