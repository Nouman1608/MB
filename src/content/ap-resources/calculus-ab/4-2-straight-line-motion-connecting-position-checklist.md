Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-ab/4-2-straight-line-motion-connecting-position-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-ab/4-2-straight-line-motion-connecting-position-practice/). This topic is shared by Calculus AB and Calculus BC.

## Understanding

- I can explain velocity as the rate of change of position and acceleration as the rate of change of velocity, with units. *(Guide: "Position, velocity and acceleration"; Practice Q6(a))*
- I can explain the difference between velocity and speed. *(Guide: "Direction of motion"; Practice Q6(c))*
- I can explain why v = 0 does not always mean a change of direction. *(Guide: "Direction of motion"; Practice Q7(b))*
- I can explain the difference between displacement and total distance travelled. *(Guide: "Displacement and total distance"; Practice Q4)*

## Skills

- I can find v(t) and a(t) from x(t), including with the product rule. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q1, Q7(a))*
- I can use a sign analysis of v(t) to find when an object moves in each direction. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q7(b))*
- I can read acceleration and speed from a velocity graph. *(Guide: Worked example 2 and Figure 2)*
- I can estimate acceleration from a velocity table, and find an average acceleration. *(Practice Q5(a), Q5(d))*
- I can find total distance by splitting time at the turning points. *(Guide: Worked example 1 and Figure 1; Practice Q4, Q7(d))*
- I can use a graphing calculator to find when an object reaches a position, and its speed then. *(Practice Q6(c))*

## Reasoning

- I can decide whether an object is speeding up or slowing down by comparing the signs of v and a, and say so in a sentence. *(Guide: "Speeding up and slowing down"; Practice Q2, Q3, Q6(d), Q7(c))*
- I can use the Intermediate Value Theorem to show an object must be at rest at some time. *(Practice Q5(b))*
- I can justify a greatest height using the sign change of velocity. *(Practice Q6(b))*
