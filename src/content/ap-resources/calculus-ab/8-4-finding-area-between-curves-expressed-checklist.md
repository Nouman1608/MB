Work through the list without notes. Use a calculator only where the linked question allows one. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-ab/8-4-finding-area-between-curves-expressed-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-ab/8-4-finding-area-between-curves-expressed-practice/). This topic is shared by Calculus AB and Calculus BC.

## Understanding

- I can explain how thin vertical strips of height top − bottom and width Δx lead to the area integral. *(Guide: "From area under a curve to area between curves", Figure 1)*
- I can explain why top − bottom gives the area even when the region lies partly or wholly below the x-axis. *(Guide: "Why the x-axis does not matter"; Practice Q5(d))*
- I can explain why a negative result means the subtraction was the wrong way round. *(Guide: Worked example 1 check; Practice Q2)*
- I can explain when an area must be split into two integrals. *(Guide: Worked example 3)*

## Skills

- I can find the limits of integration by solving f(x) = g(x) algebraically. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q1, Q5(a))*
- I can find intersection points with a calculator, store them and use them as limits. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q4, Q6(a))*
- I can decide which curve is on top using a test value between the limits. *(Guide: "The method, step by step"; Practice Q5(b))*
- I can write an area integral with correct limits, brackets and dx before evaluating it. *(Guide: Worked example 2 notation; Practice Q1, Q6(b))*
- I can evaluate an area exactly, including with trigonometric functions. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q2, Q3)*

## Reasoning

- I can find the area between a curve and the x-axis, treating the axis as y = 0. *(Guide: "A curve and the x-axis"; Revision notes self-check 2)*
- I can interpret the height of a strip, f(x) − g(x), in a real context. *(Practice Q6(d))*
- I can set up an area that depends on a constant and solve for the constant. *(Practice Q7)*
