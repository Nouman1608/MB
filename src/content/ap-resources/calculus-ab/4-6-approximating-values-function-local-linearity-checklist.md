Work through the list without notes or a calculator. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-ab/4-6-approximating-values-function-local-linearity-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-ab/4-6-approximating-values-function-local-linearity-practice/). This topic is shared by Calculus AB and Calculus BC.

## Understanding

- I can explain local linearity: near a point where f is differentiable, the graph looks almost like its tangent line. *(Guide: "Zoom in and the curve looks straight")*
- I can explain why a linearization does not exist at a corner. *(Guide: "The linearization formula")*
- I can explain, with a sketch, why a graph that bends upward gives underestimates and one that bends downward gives overestimates. *(Guide: Figure 1 and "Overestimate or underestimate?"; Practice Q3)*

## Skills

- I can write L(x) = f(a) + f′(a)(x − a) for a given function, including one that needs the chain rule. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q2, Q5(a))*
- I can choose a sensible point a and estimate a value such as ∛8.3 or 1/4.9 by hand. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q5(b))*
- I can use a value and a derivative from a context or a table to estimate a nearby value, with a positive or negative step. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q1, Q4)*
- I can estimate f(x) when I am given f(a) and a formula for f′ only. *(Guide: Worked example 3; Practice Q6(b))*

## Reasoning

- I can use the sign of f″ between a and x to justify whether an estimate is too high or too low. *(Guide: Worked example 3; Practice Q5(c), Q6(c))*
- I can handle a point of tangency where f″ changes sign, treating each side separately. *(Practice Q7(b))*
- I can explain in context why an estimate gets less reliable further from a. *(Guide: error table and Worked example 2(d); Practice Q6(d))*
- I can state an estimate with "≈" and units, and say how it relates to the actual value. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q5(d), Q6(b), Q7(c))*
