Work through the list without notes. Use a calculator only where a statement needs decimal values. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-ab/7-8-exponential-models-differential-equations-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-ab/7-8-exponential-models-differential-equations-practice/). This topic is shared by Calculus AB and Calculus BC.

## Understanding

- I can turn "the rate of change of a quantity is proportional to the quantity" into dy/dt = ky, and tell it apart from "proportional to time". *(Guide: "From a sentence to a differential equation"; Practice Q1)*
- I can say what y, t, dy/dt and k mean in a given context, including the units of k. *(Guide: "From a sentence to a differential equation"; Practice Q6(b))*
- I can explain why k is a relative rate, so the actual rate dy/dt changes as y changes. *(Guide: "What y₀ and k mean"; Practice Q4)*
- I can explain why the doubling time or half-life does not depend on the starting amount. *(Guide: Figure 1; Practice Q4)*

## Skills

- I can derive y = y₀e^(kt) from dy/dt = ky by separating variables, keeping the constant as a factor. *(Guide: "Solving dy/dt = ky"; Practice Q2, Q5(c))*
- I can find k from two data values, a doubling time or a half-life. *(Guide: Worked examples 1 and 2; Practice Q3, Q6(b), Q7(a))*
- I can use the model to find a value, an instantaneous rate with units, and the time to reach a given amount. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q6)*
- I can use a decay model for velocity to find acceleration, position and the total distance travelled. *(Guide: Worked example 3; Practice Q7)*

## Reasoning

- I can confirm a proposed solution by differentiating it and checking the initial value, and find the error in a wrong one. *(Guide: "Confirm the solution before you use it"; Practice Q5)*
- I can judge whether an answer is reasonable from the sign of k, the units and the long-run behaviour. *(Guide: "Confirm the solution"; Practice Q5(d), Q7(d))*
- I can explain why rounding k early changes later answers. *(Guide: Worked example 1 tip; Practice Q6(c))*
