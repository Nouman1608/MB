Work through the list without notes or a calculator. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-ab/5-8-sketching-graphs-functions-their-derivatives-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-ab/5-8-sketching-graphs-functions-their-derivatives-practice/). This topic is shared by Calculus AB and Calculus BC.

## Understanding

- I can say which derivative reveals each key feature of a graph: increase, extrema, concavity and inflection. *(Guide: "What a sketch has to show")*
- I can match each sign pair of f′ and f″ to one of the four basic shapes. *(Guide: shapes table; Practice Q2)*
- I can explain why a zero of f′ or f″ is not enough on its own, and what extra evidence is needed. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q1)*
- I can explain why a peak of the f′ graph is a point of inflection of f, not a maximum. *(Guide: Figure 2; Practice Q6)*

## Skills

- I can build a combined sign chart for f′ and f″ from a formula and calculate f at every key point. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q7)*
- I can read where f increases, has extrema and changes concavity from a graph of f′. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q6)*
- I can draw conclusions about f from a table of f′ values plus information about f′ between them. *(Guide: Worked example 3; Practice Q4)*
- I can describe the graph of f′ from the features of f. *(Guide: "Going the other way"; Practice Q3)*
- I can sketch f with every extremum and inflection point labelled, using the correct bend in each interval. *(Practice Q5(d), Q6(d), Q7(d))*

## Reasoning

- I can justify each feature by naming the sign change or behaviour of f′ or f″ that causes it. *(Practice Q5(a), Q5(b), Q6)*
- I can handle a critical point where f′ does not exist, such as a cusp, and say which test still works there. *(Practice Q7(b))*
