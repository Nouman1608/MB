Work through the list without notes. Use a calculator only where the linked question allows one. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-ab/8-3-accumulation-functions-definite-integrals-applied-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-ab/8-3-accumulation-functions-definite-integrals-applied-practice/). This topic is shared by Calculus AB and Calculus BC.

## Understanding

- I can explain, using "rate × short time", why the integral of a rate over an interval is the net change in the amount. *(Guide: "The big idea")*
- I can explain the difference between net change and total change, and write an integral for each. *(Guide: Worked example 1 extension; Practice Q4)*
- I can explain why the derivative of an amount function Q(x) = Q(a) + ∫ (a to x) Q′(t) dt is the rate Q′(x). *(Guide: "From net change to an amount function"; Practice Q7(a))*
- I can give the units of an integral from the units of the rate and of the input. *(Guide: "Units"; Practice Q1)*

## Calculation

- I can find an amount at a later time from a starting value and a rate given as a formula, a graph or a table. *(Guide: Worked examples 1 and 2; Practice Q2, Q5)*
- I can write the amount when there is a rate in and a rate out, with the subtraction the right way round. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q3, Q6(c))*
- I can use a calculator to evaluate an applied integral and to solve rate in = rate out, keeping stored values. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q6)*

## Reasoning

- I can decide whether an amount is increasing or decreasing at a given time by comparing the rate in with the rate out. *(Guide: Worked example 2(c); Practice Q6(b))*
- I can find and justify the greatest or least amount with the candidates test, including the endpoints. *(Guide: "Greatest and least amounts"; Practice Q6(d), Q7(c))*
- I can write a complete interpretation of a definite integral: the quantity, the interval and the units. *(Guide: Worked example 3; Practice Q1, Q5(a), Q7(b))*
- I can tell ∫ (a to b) P(t) dt apart from ∫ (a to b) P′(t) dt = P(b) − P(a) in context. *(Guide: Worked example 3; Practice Q5(b))*
