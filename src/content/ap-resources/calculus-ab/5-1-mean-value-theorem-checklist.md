Work through the list without notes or a calculator. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-ab/5-1-mean-value-theorem-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-ab/5-1-mean-value-theorem-practice/). This topic is shared by Calculus AB and Calculus BC.

## Understanding

- I can state the Mean Value Theorem, naming both conditions and the conclusion. *(Guide: "The theorem, part by part")*
- I can explain the conclusion as a tangent line parallel to a secant line, and as "instantaneous rate equals average rate". *(Guide: Figure 1; Practice Q7(a))*
- I can explain why continuity is needed on the closed interval but differentiability only on the open interval. *(Guide: "The theorem, part by part")*
- I can explain why a failed condition removes the guarantee but does not prove that no c exists. *(Guide: Worked example 3; Practice Q4, Q5(b))*

## Skills

- I can find every value of c the theorem guarantees, rejecting solutions outside (a, b). *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q1, Q5(a))*
- I can decide whether a function meets both conditions, spotting corners, cusps, vertical tangents and breaks. *(Guide: Worked example 3; Practice Q2, Q4)*
- I can choose constants that make a piecewise function differentiable, then apply the theorem. *(Practice Q6)*
- I can use a table of a differentiable function to show that the derivative takes a given value on a sub-interval. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q3, Q7(a))*

## Reasoning

- I can write a three-part justification: conditions with a reason, the average rate with numbers, and the theorem named. *(Guide: "Writing a justification"; Practice Q7)*
- I can interpret the conclusion in context, with units. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q7(a))*
- I can combine the Mean Value Theorem with the Intermediate Value Theorem applied to a continuous derivative. *(Practice Q7(b))*
- I can explain why an average rate is not an upper or lower bound on the instantaneous rate. *(Practice Q7(c))*
