Work through the list without notes or a calculator. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-ab/2-7-derivatives-cos-x-sin-x-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-ab/2-7-derivatives-cos-x-sin-x-practice/). This topic is shared by Calculus AB and Calculus BC.

## Understanding

- I can state the derivatives of sin x, cos x, eˣ and ln x, with the correct signs and the condition x > 0 for ln x. *(Guide: "The four new rules"; Practice Q1, Q3)*
- I can explain, using the limit definition and the Topic 1.8 trig limits, why d/dx sin x = cos x. *(Guide: "Why the trig rules are true")*
- I can use a graph to show that the slopes of sin x match the heights of cos x. *(Guide: Figure 1)*
- I can explain why eˣ is its own derivative and why the slope of ln x is 1/x. *(Guide: "Why eˣ is its own derivative", Figure 2; Practice Q7(d))*
- I can explain why the trig rules need radians. *(Guide: "Why radians matter")*

## Skills

- I can differentiate a sum of constant multiples of sin x, cos x, eˣ, ln x and powers of x, giving constants a derivative of 0. *(Guide: "Rewrite before you differentiate"; Practice Q1, Q3)*
- I can find the equation of a tangent line to a graph built from these functions. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q5(b), Q7)*
- I can find a point where the slope has a given value, or show that no such point exists. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q4)*
- I can evaluate a limit by recognising it as f′(a), naming f and a. *(Guide: Worked example 3; Practice Q2, Q6)*

## Reasoning

- I can tell which expressions this topic covers and which need the chain rule, such as sin(3x). *(Guide: "Rewrite before you differentiate")*
- I can use the intermediate value theorem on f′ to justify that a horizontal tangent exists, stating continuity. *(Practice Q5(c))*
- I can catch the common slips: the sign of d/dx cos x, the power rule on eˣ, and differentiating constants such as e². *(Guide: "Common misconceptions"; Revision notes)*
