Work through the list without notes or a calculator. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-ab/1-6-limits-by-algebraic-manipulation-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-ab/1-6-limits-by-algebraic-manipulation-practice/). This topic is shared by Calculus AB and Calculus BC.

## Understanding

- I can explain why 0/0 is an indeterminate form and give two 0/0 expressions with different limits. *(Guide: "Why direct substitution sometimes fails"; Practice Q1)*
- I can explain why cancelling a factor (x − a) does not change the limit as x → a. *(Guide: "The key fact"; Practice Q6(b))*
- I can explain the difference between lim (x → a) f(x) and f(a), using a graph with a hole. *(Guide: Figure 1; Practice Q6(c), Q6(d))*
- I can tell 0/0 apart from a nonzero number over 0, and say what each means. *(Guide; Practice Q5)*

## Skills

- I can factor a quadratic, a difference of squares or a difference of cubes to cancel (x − a). *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q1, Q6)*
- I can multiply by a conjugate, expanding only the side with the root, to find a limit. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q2, Q7)*
- I can combine fractions inside a fraction and cancel to find a limit, handling a − x = −(x − a). *(Guide: "Technique 3 in action"; Practice Q3)*
- I can use sin²x = (1 − cos x)(1 + cos x) or sin 2x = 2 sin x cos x to find a trig limit. *(Guide: "Technique 4 in action"; Practice Q4)*
- I can keep "lim" on every line until the substitution step. *(Guide: "A note on notation")*

## Reasoning

- I can state the condition that makes each technique appropriate before I use it. *(Guide: technique table; Revision notes)*
- I can check an exact limit with one nearby value, and explain why the check is not a proof. *(Guide: Worked examples 1 and 2; Practice Q7(c))*
- I can explain why the limit as x → 0 of (sin x)/x cannot be found by cancelling, and name the topic that handles it. *(Guide: "Pointer to Topic 1.8")*
