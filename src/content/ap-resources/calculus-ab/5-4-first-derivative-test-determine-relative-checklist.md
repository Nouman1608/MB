Work through the list without notes or a calculator. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-ab/5-4-first-derivative-test-determine-relative-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-ab/5-4-first-derivative-test-determine-relative-practice/). This topic is shared by Calculus AB and Calculus BC.

## Understanding

- I can state the First Derivative Test, including the condition that f is continuous at the critical point. *(Guide: "The First Derivative Test")*
- I can explain why a change from increasing to decreasing gives a relative maximum, using the link to Topic 5.3. *(Guide: "Why it works", Figure 1)*
- I can explain why f′(c) = 0 alone does not prove an extremum, and give an example of a critical point that is neither. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q1, Q4)*
- I can explain why a sign change of f′ at a jump or a gap in the domain proves nothing. *(Guide: "When the test does not apply"; Practice Q6)*

## Skills

- I can find all critical points, including those where f′ does not exist, and build a sign chart for f′. *(Guide: Worked examples 1 and 2; Practice Q3, Q7)*
- I can classify each critical point as a relative maximum, a relative minimum or neither. *(Practice Q1, Q7(b))*
- I can find the value of a relative extremum by substituting into f, and keep it separate from its x-coordinate. *(Practice Q2, Q7(c))*
- I can locate relative extrema from a graph of f′, and ignore peaks and valleys of f′ that are not on the x-axis. *(Guide: "Reading extrema from a graph of f′"; Practice Q5)*

## Reasoning

- I can write a justification that names the function, the x-value and the change of sign of f′. *(Guide: "Writing a justification"; Practice Q5(a), Q5(b))*
- I can explain why an argument based only on f′(c) = 0 is incomplete. *(Practice Q7(d))*
- I can decide whether a point is a relative extremum directly from nearby function values when the test does not apply. *(Practice Q6(c))*
