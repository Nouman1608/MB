Work through the list without notes or a calculator. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-ab/6-8-finding-antiderivatives-indefinite-integrals-basic-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-ab/6-8-finding-antiderivatives-indefinite-integrals-basic-practice/). This topic is shared by Calculus AB and Calculus BC.

## Understanding

- I can explain what ∫ f(x) dx = F(x) + C means and why the + C is needed. *(Guide: "From one antiderivative to a family"; Practice Q6(b))*
- I can describe the graphs of a family of antiderivatives as vertical shifts with equal slopes at each x. *(Guide: Figure 1; Practice Q6(c))*
- I can explain the difference between an indefinite integral and a definite integral. *(Guide: comparison table; Practice Q6(d))*
- I can explain why some functions, such as e^(x²), have no formula antiderivative, yet still have antiderivatives. *(Guide: Worked example 3(c); Practice Q7(d))*

## Skills

- I can integrate powers, including negative and fractional powers, and use ln|x| for 1/x. *(Guide: basic rules table; Practice Q1, Q2)*
- I can integrate eˣ, aˣ, sin x, cos x, sec²x, csc²x, sec x tan x and csc x cot x with the correct signs. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q3, Q7(c))*
- I can recognise 1/√(1 − x²) and 1/(1 + x²) as derivatives of arcsin x and arctan x. *(Guide: Worked example 2(b); Practice Q3)*
- I can expand, split or use an identity to rewrite an integrand before integrating. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q5, Q7(a), Q7(b))*

## Reasoning

- I can check any claimed antiderivative by differentiating it, and explain what is wrong when the check fails. *(Guide: Worked example 3; Practice Q4, Q7(d))*
- I can write integral notation correctly, with dx and + C, and without false equal signs. *(Guide: "A note on notation" and "Common misconceptions")*
