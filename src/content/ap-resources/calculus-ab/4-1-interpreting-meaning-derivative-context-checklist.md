Work through the list without notes or a calculator. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-ab/4-1-interpreting-meaning-derivative-context-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-ab/4-1-interpreting-meaning-derivative-context-practice/). This topic is shared by Calculus AB and Calculus BC.

## Understanding

- I can explain f′(a) as the rate at which f changes per unit of input, at the single input value a. *(Guide: "What a derivative measures"; Practice Q2)*
- I can say what the sign of a derivative tells me about the quantity in context. *(Guide: "What a derivative measures"; Practice Q5(b))*
- I can tell apart f(a), an average rate over an interval and f′(a), and give the units of each. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q6(c), Q7(b))*
- I can explain why f′(a) only approximates the change in f over the next unit of input. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q5(c))*

## Skills

- I can find the units of a first or second derivative from the units of the function and of its input. *(Guide: "Units of a derivative"; Practice Q1, Q4)*
- I can convert a rate to different units, such as per minute to per hour or metres to centimetres. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q6(b))*
- I can estimate a derivative from a table using the closest data on either side. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q3, Q6(a))*
- I can find a derivative from a formula and state it with units. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q5(a))*
- I can estimate a small change in a quantity as rate × step. *(Guide: Worked example 3; Practice Q7(c))*

## Reasoning

- I can write a four-part interpretation sentence: quantity, input value, direction, rate with units. *(Guide: "Writing an interpretation sentence"; Practice Q5, Q6(b), Q7(a))*
- I can interpret a second derivative in context, including when the first derivative is negative. *(Guide: Worked example 3; Practice Q4, Q6(d))*
- I can use units to check that a combined rate makes sense. *(Practice Q7(d))*
