Work through the list without notes or a calculator. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-ab/2-6-derivative-rules-constant-sum-difference-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-ab/2-6-derivative-rules-constant-sum-difference-practice/). This topic is shared by Calculus AB and Calculus BC.

## Understanding

- I can use the limit definition to show that the derivative of a constant is 0. *(Guide: "1. Constant rule")*
- I can explain how the constant multiple and sum rules follow from properties of limits. *(Guide: "The four rules")*
- I can explain with a graph why adding a constant does not change the derivative. *(Guide: Figure 1; Practice Q5(c))*
- I can explain why the rules do not let me split a product or a quotient. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q3, Q6(b))*

## Skills

- I can differentiate a polynomial term by term, keeping coefficients and dropping constant terms. *(Guide: "Differentiating polynomials term by term"; Practice Q1, Q5(a))*
- I can treat numbers such as π² as constants. *(Practice Q1)*
- I can expand brackets or split a fraction with a one-term denominator before differentiating. *(Guide: "Rewrite first"; Practice Q3, Q6)*
- I can combine derivative values from a table using the sum, difference and constant multiple rules. *(Guide: "Unknown functions"; Practice Q2)*
- I can find the equation of a tangent line to a polynomial at a given point. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q7(c))*
- I can find the points where a graph has a horizontal tangent. *(Guide: Worked example 3; Practice Q4)*

## Reasoning

- I can interpret a derivative value as a rate of change, with units, in context. *(Practice Q5(b))*
- I can use a derivative to show that a rate is always positive. *(Practice Q5(d))*
- I can set up and solve equations for unknown coefficients from a point and a slope. *(Practice Q7(a))*
- I can explain why a given slope is impossible for a curve. *(Practice Q7(d))*
