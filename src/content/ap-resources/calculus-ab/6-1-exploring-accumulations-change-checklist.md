Work through the list without notes or a calculator. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-ab/6-1-exploring-accumulations-change-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-ab/6-1-exploring-accumulations-change-practice/). This topic is shared by Calculus AB and Calculus BC.

## Understanding

- I can explain why the area under a constant-rate graph equals the change in the quantity, using rate × time. *(Guide: "Starting simple"; Revision notes self-check 1)*
- I can explain why the area under any rate graph gives the accumulated change over an interval. *(Guide: "Rates that change smoothly")*
- I can explain why an area below the axis counts as a decrease. *(Guide: "The sign of an accumulated change"; Practice Q3)*
- I can tell apart the height of a rate graph, an area under it and the amount of the quantity. *(Guide: Worked example 3; Practice Q4)*

## Skills

- I can state the units of an area under a rate graph as rate units × input units. *(Guide: units table; Practice Q1)*
- I can split a region into rectangles, triangles, trapezoids and parts of circles to find its area. *(Guide: Worked examples 1 and 2; Practice Q2, Q5, Q7(a))*
- I can find a net change as area above minus area below, and a total change (such as distance) as area above plus area below. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q6, Q7(b))*
- I can find the amount at the end of an interval from a starting amount and the accumulated change. *(Guide: Worked example 1(d); Practice Q3, Q5(c), Q6(d))*

## Reasoning

- I can write an interpretation that names what accumulated, the interval, the size and direction, and the units. *(Guide: Worked example 3; Practice Q5(b), Q6(d))*
- I can explain where a quantity is greatest from where its rate changes sign. *(Guide: Worked example 1 check; Practice Q5(d), Q7(d))*
- I can set up an equation using areas to find when an accumulated change reaches a given value. *(Practice Q7(c))*
