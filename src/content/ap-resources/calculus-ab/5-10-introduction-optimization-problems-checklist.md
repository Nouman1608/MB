Work through the list without notes or a calculator. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-ab/5-10-introduction-optimization-problems-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-ab/5-10-introduction-optimization-problems-practice/). This topic is shared by Calculus AB and Calculus BC.

## Understanding

- I can explain why the search for a maximum or minimum inside an interval starts with critical points. *(Guide: "What an optimization problem is")*
- I can name the quantity to optimise, the constraint and the interval in a problem, whatever its setting. *(Guide: "The common structure"; Practice Q2)*
- I can tell the maximum value apart from the location of the maximum, and answer the one that is asked. *(Guide: "A note on notation and words"; Practice Q1, Q3)*

## Skills

- I can use a constraint to write a quantity as a function of one variable. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q3, Q6(a))*
- I can state the interval of allowed values from the situation, such as positive lengths. *(Guide: "Where the interval comes from"; Practice Q2, Q6(a))*
- I can find the absolute maximum and minimum on a closed interval with the candidates test, including endpoints. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q1, Q5)*
- I can find and test the critical point of a function I have built, then give the value and the dimensions that produce it. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q6(b), Q6(c))*
- I can minimise a distance by working with its square. *(Practice Q7(a)–(c))*

## Reasoning

- I can justify an absolute extremum on an open interval with the single-critical-point argument and a first or second derivative test. *(Guide: "The method", step 7; Practice Q4, Q6(b))*
- I can explain why the second derivative test alone does not prove an absolute extremum. *(Guide: "Common misconceptions"; Practice Q5 note)*
- I can explain why a critical point that seems natural may be a maximum when a minimum is wanted. *(Practice Q7(d))*
