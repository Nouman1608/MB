Work through the list without notes or a calculator. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-ab/6-10-integrating-functions-long-division-completing-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-ab/6-10-integrating-functions-long-division-completing-practice/). This topic is shared by Calculus AB and Calculus BC.

## Understanding

- I can explain why some integrands must be rewritten in an equivalent form before any rule applies. *(Guide: "Why rewrite before integrating?")*
- I can decide from the degrees of top and bottom whether long division comes first. *(Guide: decision table; Practice Q1, Q7(a))*
- I can use the discriminant to decide whether a quadratic leads to an arctan form, and explain why x² − 4 does not. *(Guide: "When does arctan apply?"; Practice Q2, Q7(d))*
- I can explain where the factor 1/a in the arctan form comes from. *(Guide: "Where the 1/a comes from"; Practice Q3)*

## Skills

- I can divide one polynomial by another, write quotient + remainder/divisor and check by multiplying back. *(Guide: "Tool 1: long division"; Practice Q1, Q5(a))*
- I can integrate the result of a division, including a remainder term that gives ln. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q5(b), Q7(b))*
- I can complete the square, including when the x² term is negative. *(Guide: "Tool 2: completing the square"; Practice Q2, Q4, Q6(b))*
- I can use the arctan and arcsin forms with the correct a, and change limits in a definite integral. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q3, Q4)*
- I can split a linear numerator into k × (derivative of the bottom) + constant and integrate both parts. *(Guide: Worked example 3; Practice Q6)*

## Reasoning

- I can check an antiderivative by differentiating it. *(Guide: Worked example 3)*
- I can check a definite integral with a quick size estimate or a bounding function. *(Guide: Worked examples 1 and 2; Practice Q7(c))*
- I can explain why ∫ p(x)/q(x) dx is not (∫ p dx)/(∫ q dx). *(Guide: "Common misconceptions"; Practice Q1)*
