Work through the list without notes or a calculator. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-ab/5-6-determining-concavity-functions-over-their-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-ab/5-6-determining-concavity-functions-over-their-practice/). This topic is shared by Calculus AB and Calculus BC.

## Understanding

- I can describe concave up and concave down in terms of how the slope changes, using tangent lines. *(Guide: Figure 1)*
- I can explain why concavity is different from increasing or decreasing. *(Guide: "Which way does the graph bend?"; Practice Q1)*
- I can explain why f″ > 0 means concave up. *(Guide: "The second derivative decides concavity")*
- I can state the two conditions for a point of inflection. *(Guide: "Points of inflection"; Practice Q6(c))*

## Skills

- I can find f″, locate where it is zero or undefined, and build a sign chart. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q1, Q6)*
- I can find f″ from a given f′ and use it to locate inflection points. *(Practice Q4)*
- I can read concavity and inflection points of f from a graph of f′. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q5(a), Q5(b))*
- I can find f″ at a point from the slope of a graph of f′. *(Practice Q5(d))*
- I can give a point of inflection as a coordinate pair. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q6(c))*

## Reasoning

- I can explain why f″(c) = 0 alone does not prove an inflection point, using x⁴ as an example. *(Guide: table in "Points of inflection"; Practice Q2, Q7(c))*
- I can tell the difference between where f′ crosses zero (extrema of f) and where f′ turns (inflection points of f). *(Guide: Worked example 2(c); Practice Q5(c))*
- I can explain why a gap in the domain, such as x = 0 for 1/x, is not a point of inflection. *(Guide: "Points of inflection"; Practice Q6(c))*
- I can interpret the signs of f′ and f″ in context with the words "increasing at a decreasing rate". *(Guide: "Concavity in context"; Practice Q3)*
