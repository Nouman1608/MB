Work through the list without notes or a calculator. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-ab/1-8-determining-limits-squeeze-theorem-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-ab/1-8-determining-limits-squeeze-theorem-practice/). This topic is shared by Calculus AB and Calculus BC.

## Understanding

- I can state the squeeze theorem, including where the inequality must hold. *(Guide: "The squeeze theorem"; Practice Q1)*
- I can explain why the product law cannot find lim (x → 0) x² sin(1/x). *(Guide: "When rewriting is not enough"; Practice Q6(a), Q6(d))*
- I can explain why the squeeze theorem gives the limit at a but not the value f(a). *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q5(b))*
- I can say what follows when the two outer limits are different: no conclusion. *(Guide: Worked example 2(c); Practice Q3, Q5(c))*

## Skills

- I can build bounds from −1 ≤ sin u ≤ 1 by multiplying by x² or |x| and adding a constant. *(Guide: "How to build the bounds" and Worked example 1)*
- I can use absolute values when a factor such as x or x − 3 can be negative. *(Guide: "How to build the bounds"; Practice Q3, Q6(b))*
- I can find a limit with the squeeze theorem and name all three conditions. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q5(a), Q6(c))*
- I can use lim (x → 0) (sin x)/x = 1 and lim (x → 0) (1 − cos x)/x = 0 to find related limits, matching the angle and the denominator. *(Guide: Worked example 3; Practice Q2, Q4, Q7(c))*

## Reasoning

- I can explain, with the unit-circle areas, why cos x ≤ (sin x)/x ≤ 1 near 0 and why that gives the limit 1. *(Guide: "Result 1" and Figure 2)*
- I can turn a given inequality into a new one, such as the bounds for (tan x)/x, and keep the signs right. *(Practice Q7(a), Q7(b))*
- I can check a squeeze answer with one nearby value and explain why the check is not a proof. *(Guide: Worked examples 1 and 3; Practice Q7(d))*
- I can explain why the trig limits need radians. *(Guide: "Degrees warning")*
