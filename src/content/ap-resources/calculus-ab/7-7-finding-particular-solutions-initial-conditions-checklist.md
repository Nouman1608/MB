Work through the list without notes or a calculator. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-ab/7-7-finding-particular-solutions-initial-conditions-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-ab/7-7-finding-particular-solutions-initial-conditions-practice/). This topic is shared by Calculus AB and Calculus BC.

## Understanding

- I can explain the difference between a general solution and a particular solution. *(Guide: "From a family to one curve"; Practice Q6)*
- I can explain why only one particular solution passes through a given point. *(Guide: "From a family to one curve"; Practice Q6(c))*
- I can explain why y₀ + ∫ (a to x) f(t) dt passes through (a, y₀) and solves dy/dx = f(x). *(Guide: "The integral form"; Practice Q2)*
- I can explain why a particular solution must live on one interval containing the initial point. *(Guide: "Domain restrictions"; Figure 1)*

## Skills

- I can substitute an initial condition into the integrated equation to find the constant. *(Guide: Worked examples 1 to 3; Practice Q4, Q5(a))*
- I can choose the correct sign after a square root, using the initial value. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q4)*
- I can remove ln|y| and keep the sign of the initial value. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q1, Q6(b))*
- I can state the largest open interval on which a particular solution is valid. *(Guide: Worked example 3; Practice Q3, Q7(b))*
- I can write the integral form of a particular solution with the correct limit and starting value. *(Guide: "The integral form"; Practice Q2)*
- I can find a value of a particular solution, such as f(4) or g(1), exactly. *(Practice Q4, Q6(d), Q7(c))*

## Reasoning

- I can check a particular solution by testing the initial point and differentiating. *(Guide: Worked examples 1 to 3)*
- I can explain why y = e^(G(x)) + C can pass through the right point and still be wrong. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q1)*
- I can interpret a particular solution and its domain in a context, with units. *(Practice Q5)*
- I can describe the behaviour of a solution near the edge of its domain. *(Practice Q7(d); Figure 1)*
