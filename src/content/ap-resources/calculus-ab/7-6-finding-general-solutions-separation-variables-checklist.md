Work through the list without notes or a calculator. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-ab/7-6-finding-general-solutions-separation-variables-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-ab/7-6-finding-general-solutions-separation-variables-practice/). This topic is shared by Calculus AB and Calculus BC.

## Understanding

- I can explain what a general solution is and why it contains one arbitrary constant. *(Guide: "From checking solutions to finding them"; Figure 1)*
- I can explain why one constant is enough when I integrate both sides. *(Guide: "One constant is enough")*
- I can explain, using the chain rule, why separating and integrating both sides gives a correct solution. *(Guide: "Why this is valid")*
- I can decide whether a differential equation is separable, factoring or using exponent laws first where needed. *(Guide: separable table; Practice Q1, Q6(c))*

## Skills

- I can find the general solution of dy/dx = f(x) by antidifferentiating. *(Guide: "The simplest case")*
- I can separate the variables and antidifferentiate both sides with the correct antiderivatives. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q2, Q5)*
- I can tell when a fraction gives a logarithm and when it does not, for example ∫ (1/y²) dy = −1/y. *(Guide: "Not every fraction gives a logarithm"; Practice Q2, Q3)*
- I can turn ln|y + b| = G(x) + C into y = A e^(G(x)) − b, with the constant as a multiplier. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q4, Q6(c))*
- I can split e^(x + y) or e^(x − y) into a product and solve. *(Guide: Worked example 3)*
- I can leave a solution in implicit form when y cannot be isolated. *(Guide: "When you cannot solve for y"; Practice Q7)*

## Reasoning

- I can check a general solution by differentiating, explicitly or implicitly. *(Guide: Worked examples 1 to 3; Practice Q5(c), Q7(b))*
- I can explain why y = e^(G(x)) + C and y = tan(x²) + C are wrong forms. *(Guide: "Watch the constant"; Practice Q5(c))*
- I can find and explain the error when someone integrates y with respect to x without separating. *(Practice Q6(a), Q6(b))*
- I can say which constant solutions are lost when I divide by h(y), and how A = 0 can bring them back. *(Guide: Worked example 2, step 5)*
