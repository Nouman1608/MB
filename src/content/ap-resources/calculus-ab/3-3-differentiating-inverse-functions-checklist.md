Work through the list without notes or a calculator. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-ab/3-3-differentiating-inverse-functions-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-ab/3-3-differentiating-inverse-functions-practice/). This topic is shared by Calculus AB and Calculus BC.

## Understanding

- I can explain why reflecting a graph in y = x turns a slope m into 1/m at the matching point. *(Guide: "The idea", Figure 1; Practice Q3)*
- I can explain the difference between f⁻¹(x) and 1/f(x). *(Guide: "A note on notation"; Practice Q2)*
- I can explain why a horizontal tangent on f gives a vertical tangent on its inverse. *(Guide: "When the inverse is not differentiable"; Practice Q4, Q7(d))*

## Skills

- I can derive g′(x) = 1/f′(g(x)) by differentiating f(g(x)) = x with the chain rule, or by implicit differentiation. *(Guide: "Deriving the rule with the chain rule")*
- I can find the matching input a with f(a) = b before using f′(a). *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q1, Q7(b))*
- I can read an inverse's values and slopes from a table of f and f′. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q2)*
- I can write the tangent line to the graph of an inverse at a given point. *(Guide: Worked examples 1 and 2; Practice Q6(c))*
- I can combine the inverse rule with the chain rule or product rule. *(Guide: Worked example 2(d); Practice Q6(d))*
- I can give units for the derivative of an inverse in a context and interpret it. *(Practice Q5)*

## Reasoning

- I can show that a function is one-to-one by showing its derivative keeps one sign. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q5(a), Q6(a))*
- I can confirm an answer by checking the rule on a known inverse pair, such as eˣ and ln x, or with a nearby value. *(Guide: "Checking the rule"; Practice Q7(c))*
- I can spot the "1/f′(b)" error in someone else's working and correct it. *(Practice Q7(a))*
