Work through the list without notes or a calculator. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-ab/1-9-connecting-multiple-representations-limits-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-ab/1-9-connecting-multiple-representations-limits-practice/). This topic is shared by Calculus AB and Calculus BC.

## Understanding

- I can explain why a filled point at x = c gives f(c) but does not decide the limit as x → c. *(Guide: Worked example 1(b); Practice Q1)*
- I can name three ways a limit can fail to exist and recognise each on a graph and in a table. *(Guide: "One limit, four representations"; Practice Q4)*
- I can explain why a table or a graph screen only suggests a limit, and give an example where it misleads. *(Guide: "How a table can mislead"; Practice Q4, Q7(d))*

## Skills

- I can read left-hand, right-hand and two-sided limits from a graph with holes, jumps and isolated points. *(Guide: Figure 1, Worked example 1; Practice Q1)*
- I can estimate a limit from a table by following the trend from each side. *(Guide: "Reading a table"; Practice Q2, Q6(a))*
- I can find one-sided limits of a piecewise formula and choose a constant that makes the limit exist. *(Guide: Worked example 3; Practice Q5, Q7)*
- I can translate between a sentence and limit notation, in both directions. *(Guide: "Reading a formula and reading words"; Practice Q7(c))*
- I can find limits of sums, differences, products and quotients when one function is a graph and the other is a table or a formula, and I check that a bottom limit is not 0 before dividing. *(Guide: Worked example 1(c), Worked example 2(c); Practice Q6(b), Q6(c))*

## Reasoning

- I can find a composite limit f(g(x)) by tracking the side from which g(x) approaches its limit. *(Guide: Worked example 2(a); Practice Q3, Q6(d))*
- I can split a limit into one-sided limits when a two-sided limit does not exist, and still reach a conclusion. *(Guide: Worked example 1(d); Practice Q6(b))*
- I can build a formula or describe a graph that matches a given list of limit statements. *(Practice Q5)*
