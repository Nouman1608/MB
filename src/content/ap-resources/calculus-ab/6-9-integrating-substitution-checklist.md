Work through the list without notes or a calculator. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-ab/6-9-integrating-substitution-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-ab/6-9-integrating-substitution-practice/). This topic is shared by Calculus AB and Calculus BC.

## Understanding

- I can explain substitution as the chain rule run backwards, using an example such as 3x² cos(x³). *(Guide: "The idea: the chain rule run backwards")*
- I can spot a suitable inner function u because its derivative appears as a factor, up to a constant. *(Guide: "How to spot a substitution"; Practice Q1, Q2)*
- I can explain why a constant factor can be adjusted but a variable factor cannot. *(Guide: "You cannot adjust a variable"; Practice Q5(c))*
- I can explain why the limits change in a definite integral, using the two equal areas in Figure 1. *(Guide: Figure 1; Practice Q6(d))*

## Skills

- I can find du, adjust for a constant and rewrite an integral entirely in u. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q1, Q5(a))*
- I can integrate a function of a linear expression ax + b by dividing by a. *(Guide: "Linear inner functions")*
- I can replace a leftover x by solving u = g(x) for x. *(Guide: Worked example 3; Practice Q4, Q7)*
- I can evaluate a definite integral by changing the limits to u-values, including limits that come out in decreasing order. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q3, Q6)*
- I can write + C on every indefinite answer and check it by differentiating. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q5(b))*

## Reasoning

- I can explain the error when x-limits are used with an antiderivative in u, and say what the right limits are. *(Guide: "Common misconceptions"; Practice Q3, Q6(d))*
- I can judge whether my answer is reasonable from the sign or size of the integrand. *(Guide: Worked examples 2 and 3; Practice Q7(c))*
- I can choose between changing the limits and returning to x, and use either route correctly. *(Guide: "Two correct routes"; Practice Q6)*
