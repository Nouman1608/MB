Work through the list without notes or a calculator. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-ab/2-8-product-rule-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-ab/2-8-product-rule-practice/). This topic is shared by Calculus AB and Calculus BC.

## Understanding

- I can state the product rule in symbols and in words. *(Guide: "The rule"; Practice Q1)*
- I can show with an example that (f · g)′ is not f′ · g′. *(Guide: "Why the derivative of a product is not f′ · g′"; Practice Q6(d))*
- I can explain the rule with the growing-rectangle picture, saying why the corner piece does not count. *(Guide: Figure 1)*
- I can explain the subtract-and-add step in the limit proof and why continuity of f is needed. *(Guide: "The limit proof")*

## Skills

- I can differentiate a product of a power of x with sin x, cos x, eˣ or ln x, keeping every sign. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q1, Q3)*
- I can use the product rule with values from a table, keeping the value f(a)g(a) separate from the slope. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q2, Q6)*
- I can factor a derivative such as (2x + 3)eˣ and find where the tangent line is horizontal. *(Guide: Worked examples 1 and 3; Practice Q4, Q5)*
- I can find the equation of a tangent line to the graph of a product. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q5(c), Q7(c))*
- I can differentiate a product of three factors. *(Guide: "Three factors"; Practice Q7)*

## Reasoning

- I can decide whether a product needs the product rule, the constant multiple rule or expanding first. *(Guide: "When to use it, and when not to"; Revision notes)*
- I can tell a product from a composition such as sin(x²), which needs the chain rule. *(Guide: "When to use it, and when not to")*
- I can justify that a horizontal tangent occurs only where a factor such as 2x + 3 is zero, because eˣ is never 0. *(Practice Q5(b))*
