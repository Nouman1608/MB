Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-ab/6-5-interpreting-behavior-accumulation-functions-involving-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-ab/6-5-interpreting-behavior-accumulation-functions-involving-practice/). This topic is shared by Calculus AB and Calculus BC.

## Understanding

- I can explain why a graph of f is really a graph of g′ when g(x) = ∫ (a to x) f(t) dt. *(Guide: "The big idea")*
- I can say which feature of f gives each feature of g: sign for increasing, slope for concavity, area for values. *(Guide: "How features of f show up in g"; Practice Q1)*
- I can explain why a zero of f without a sign change gives no extremum of g. *(Guide: feature table; Practice Q3, Q7(b))*
- I can explain why a corner in f can still give a point of inflection of g. *(Guide: Worked example 1(c))*

## Skills

- I can find the intervals where g increases or decreases from a graph, a formula or a sign description of f. *(Guide: Worked examples 1 and 2; Practice Q2, Q5(b))*
- I can find the points of inflection of g from where f changes between increasing and decreasing. *(Guide: Worked examples 1(c) and 2(b); Practice Q5(c), Q7(c))*
- I can find values of g with signed areas, including values to the left of the starting point. *(Guide: Worked example 1(d); Practice Q5(a))*
- I can use the candidates test to find the absolute extrema of g on a closed interval. *(Guide: Worked example 1(d); Practice Q4, Q5(d))*
- I can estimate a value of an accumulation function from a table with a trapezoidal sum. *(Guide: Worked example 3(d); Practice Q6(d))*

## Reasoning

- I can justify every claim about g with a reason about g′ = f or g″ = f′, not about g itself. *(Guide: "How to justify a claim about g")*
- I can use the Intermediate Value Theorem, with stated facts about f, to locate and count the critical points of g from a table. *(Guide: Worked example 3(b); Practice Q6(b))*
- I can interpret the rate and the accumulated amount in context, with units. *(Practice Q6(a))*
