Work through the list without notes or a calculator. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-ab/4-4-introduction-related-rates-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-ab/4-4-introduction-related-rates-practice/). This topic is shared by Calculus AB and Calculus BC.

## Understanding

- I can explain what "related rates" means: quantities linked by an equation have linked rates of change. *(Guide: "Quantities that change together")*
- I can explain why differentiating s³ with respect to t gives 3s² · ds/dt, and why the factor ds/dt is there. *(Guide: "The chain rule does the linking"; Practice Q1)*
- I can sort the information in a problem into facts true at all times and values true only at one instant. *(Guide: "Always true, or only true now?"; Practice Q5(a))*
- I can explain why substituting instant values before differentiating gives a wrong answer. *(Guide: "Always true, or only true now?"; Practice Q5(c))*

## Skills

- I can differentiate an equation term by term with respect to t, attaching a rate to every changing quantity. *(Guide: reference table; Practice Q6(a))*
- I can use the product rule when two changing quantities are multiplied. *(Guide: Worked example 2(a); Practice Q2, Q3, Q7)*
- I can use the quotient rule when one changing quantity is divided by another. *(Guide: Worked example 2(b); Practice Q4)*
- I can give a decreasing quantity a negative rate and report the answer with units. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q5(b))*

## Reasoning

- I can use units and signs to check whether a related rate answer is sensible. *(Guide: Worked example 1 "Check")*
- I can explain why the same equation gives different rates at different instants. *(Practice Q6(d))*
- I can explain which terms of a product-rule derivative make a quantity grow or shrink overall. *(Guide: Worked example 2(a); Practice Q7(c))*
