Work through the list without notes. Use a calculator only where a statement says so. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-ab/8-7-volumes-cross-sections-squares-rectangles-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-ab/8-7-volumes-cross-sections-squares-rectangles-practice/). This topic is shared by Calculus AB and Calculus BC.

## Understanding

- I can explain why the volume of a solid is the integral of its cross-sectional area, using thin slabs of volume A(x) Δx. *(Guide: "The idea: a solid is a stack of thin slabs")*
- I can check the formula on a prism: equal slices give area × length. *(Guide: "A quick check"; Practice Q7(c))*
- I can describe where a cross section sits: standing at right angles to the base, on a segment across it. *(Guide: Figures 1 and 2)*

## Calculation

- I can find the side length s as top − bottom for slices perpendicular to the x-axis. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q1, Q2, Q5(a))*
- I can find the side length s as right − left, in terms of y, for slices perpendicular to the y-axis, and integrate with dy. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q3)*
- I can write A = s² for squares and A = s × height for rectangles, including heights given in words. *(Guide: area table; Practice Q4, Q6)*
- I can evaluate a volume integral exactly, expanding (top − bottom)² first. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q5(b))*
- I can find a volume with a calculator, storing intersection values and giving three decimal places. *(Guide: Worked example 3; Practice Q4, Q6)*

## Reasoning

- I can explain why (f − g)² and f² − g² give different volumes, and which one is right here. *(Guide: "Common misconceptions"; Practice Q2)*
- I can show that rectangles of constant height h give a volume of h × (area of the base). *(Practice Q5(c))*
- I can interpret a cross-sectional area and a volume in context, with square and cubic units. *(Practice Q6)*
- I can find an unknown constant from a given volume. *(Practice Q7(b))*
