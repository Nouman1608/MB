Work through the list without notes or a calculator. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-ab/6-7-fundamental-theorem-calculus-definite-integrals-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-ab/6-7-fundamental-theorem-calculus-definite-integrals-practice/). This topic is shared by Calculus AB and Calculus BC.

## Understanding

- I can say what an antiderivative is and check a proposed one by differentiating it. *(Guide: "Antiderivatives"; Practice Q1, Q2)*
- I can explain why ∫ (a to x) f(t) dt is an antiderivative of a continuous f, and why two antiderivatives of f differ by a constant. *(Guide: "Where the theorem comes from"; Practice Q3)*
- I can state the evaluation form of the Fundamental Theorem of Calculus, including the continuity condition. *(Guide: "Where the theorem comes from"; Practice Q5(b))*
- I can explain why no + C is needed in F(b) − F(a). *(Guide: FAQ and "Where the theorem comes from")*

## Skills

- I can evaluate a definite integral of powers and roots, rewriting them as powers first. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q1, Q6(a))*
- I can evaluate definite integrals of sin x, cos x, sec²x, eˣ and 1/x, getting the trig signs right. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q2, Q5(c))*
- I can write [F(x)] (a to b) and subtract F(a) in brackets. *(Guide: "How to evaluate a definite integral"; Practice Q2)*
- I can turn a limit of Riemann sums into a definite integral and evaluate it. *(Guide: "Reading a limit of Riemann sums"; Practice Q4)*

## Reasoning

- I can spot when the integrand is not continuous on [a, b], and explain why the theorem then gives a wrong answer. *(Guide: "When the theorem does not apply"; Practice Q5)*
- I can explain a zero or negative definite integral using signed areas. *(Guide: Worked example 2(a); Practice Q6)*
- I can use ∫ (a to b) F′(x) dx = F(b) − F(a) to find a net change and a final amount, with units. *(Guide: Worked example 3; Practice Q7)*
