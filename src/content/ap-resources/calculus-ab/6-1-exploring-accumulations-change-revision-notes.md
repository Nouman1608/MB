## Recap

- A **rate of change** graph shows how fast a quantity changes. The **area** between that graph and the horizontal axis over an interval is how much the quantity **changed** over the interval.
- Constant rate: change = rate × time, which is a rectangle's area.
- If the graph is built from straight segments or circular arcs, find the area with **geometry**: rectangles, triangles, trapezoids, semicircles and quarter circles.
- Rate **positive** (graph above the axis): the quantity increases. Rate **negative** (graph below): it decreases.
- The area is a **change**, not an amount. Amount at the end = amount at the start + net change.

## Key relationships

| Idea | Rule | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Units of the area | rate units × input units | (L/min) × min = L |
| Net change | area above − area below | 13.5 − 6 = 7.5 kWh |
| Total change (e.g. distance) | area above + area below | 2π + 18 ≈ 24.283 m |
| Amount at the end | start + net change | 4 + 7.5 = 11.5 kWh |
| Average rate over [a, b] | net change ÷ (b − a) | 2,450 ÷ 20 = 122.5 passengers/min |

Useful areas: triangle ½bh; trapezoid ½(a + b)h; semicircle ½πr²; quarter circle ¼πr².

## Assumptions

- The graph shows a **rate**, not the quantity itself. Read the axis label before you start.
- Geometry gives an exact answer only when the pieces really are lines or circular arcs. Use the description and labelled points, not your ruler.
- "Area" in this topic always means area between the graph and the horizontal axis, over the stated interval.

## Mistakes to avoid

1. **Giving the change as the final amount.** Add the starting amount.
2. **Adding an area below the axis** when the question asks for net change.
3. **Rate units on the answer**, such as "litres per minute" for a volume.
4. **Counting grid squares** without checking the scale on each axis.
5. **Interpretations without an interval or units.**

## Quick self-check

1. Oil leaks from a machine at a constant 0.6 litres per hour for 5 hours. How much leaks out? *(3 litres: the rectangle is 5 wide and 0.6 tall)*
2. A rate graph has area 7 above the axis and area 10 below it on [0, 9]. The quantity starts at 25. What is it at t = 9, and what is the total amount of change? *(25 + 7 − 10 = 22; total change 7 + 10 = 17)*
3. A rate is measured in grams per minute and time in minutes. What unit does the area have? *(grams)*
