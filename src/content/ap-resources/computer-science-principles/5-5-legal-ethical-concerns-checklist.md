Work through the list without notes. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/computer-science-principles/5-5-legal-ethical-concerns-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/computer-science-principles/5-5-legal-ethical-concerns-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can explain what intellectual property is and why digital copying raises concerns. *(Guide: "Who owns what is made on a computer?"; Practice Q4)*
- I can define plagiarism and explain how it differs from using permitted material with credit. *(Guide: "Plagiarism and citation"; Practice Q1, Q8)*
- I can describe Creative Commons, open source and open access. *(Guide: "Legal ways to use other people's work"; Practice Q2, Q9)*
- I can explain the difference between legal and ethical concerns. *(Guide: "Legal and ethical concerns of computing innovations"; Practice Q7)*

## Skills

- I can decide whether I may use a piece of material and say what I must do. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q3, Q5)*
- I can write an acknowledgement or citation for borrowed material. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q8)*
- I can name legal and ethical concerns raised by a computing innovation. *(Guide: "Legal and ethical concerns of computing innovations"; Practice Q6)*

## Reasoning

- I can analyse a scenario by stakeholders, benefits, harms and safeguards. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice question 10)*
- I can give a balanced judgement that does not take a side. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q7)*
- I can suggest a specific safeguard that keeps a benefit and reduces a harm. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice question 10)*

All ticked? Move on to the [Topic 5.6 study guide](/advanced-course-resources/computer-science-principles/5-6-safe-computing-study-guide/), Safe Computing.
