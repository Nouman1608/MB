Work through the list without notes. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/computer-science-principles/5-3-computing-bias-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/computer-science-principles/5-3-computing-bias-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can explain how bias in data and in algorithms leads to biased results. *(Guide: "How biased data cause biased results" and "How algorithms can introduce bias"; Practice Q1, Q2)*
- I can explain that bias can be embedded at all levels of development. *(Guide: "Computers are not automatically neutral"; Practice question 10)*
- I can explain what a proxy is and why a rule that names no group can still disadvantage one. *(Guide: "How algorithms can introduce bias"; Practice Q4, Q6, Q9)*
- I can explain why more data does not remove bias. *(Guide: "How biased data cause biased results"; Practice Q7 and question 10)*

## Skills

- I can calculate accuracy for each group and overall. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q3, Q8)*
- I can apply a rule to a table of cases and list who is selected. *(Guide: Worked example 2)*
- I can state specific actions that reduce bias. *(Guide: "Reducing bias"; Practice Q5, Q8)*

## Reasoning

- I can explain why an overall figure can hide a gap between groups. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q8)*
- I can evaluate a rule for proxy effects and suggest a fairer check. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q9)*
- I can analyse a data source and say whose experience it may leave out. *(Guide: "How biased data cause biased results"; Practice Q7)*
