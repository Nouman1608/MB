Work through the list without notes. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/computer-science-principles/5-2-digital-divide-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/computer-science-principles/5-2-digital-divide-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can define the digital divide and name the characteristics it depends on. *(Guide: "What is the digital divide?"; Practice Q1, Q2)*
- I can describe the parts of access: device, connection, cost, skills and suitability. *(Guide: "Parts of access"; Practice Q7)*
- I can explain how the divide raises issues of equity, access and influence. *(Guide: "Equity, access and influence"; Practice Q3, Q6, question 10)*
- I can explain that the divide affects groups and individuals, within and between countries. *(Guide: "What is the digital divide?")*

## Skills

- I can calculate the percentage of a group with and without access. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q4, Q8)*
- I can match an action to the barrier it addresses. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q5, Q9)*
- I can say which kind of actor (individual, organisation, government) could take an action. *(Guide: "Who affects the divide?"; Practice Q9)*

## Reasoning

- I can evaluate a set of options and recommend a combination. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice question 10)*
- I can say who is still left out after a proposed solution. *(Guide: Worked example 2 step 4; Practice question 10)*
- I can give a balanced, non-political analysis. *(Guide: "Common misconceptions"; Practice Q8, Q9)*

All ticked? Move on to the [Topic 5.3 study guide](/advanced-course-resources/computer-science-principles/5-3-computing-bias-study-guide/), Computing Bias.
