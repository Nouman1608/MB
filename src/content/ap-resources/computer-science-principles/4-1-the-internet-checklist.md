Work through the list without notes. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/computer-science-principles/4-1-the-internet-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/computer-science-principles/4-1-the-internet-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can define computing device, computing system, computer network, path, routing and bandwidth. *(Guide: "From one device to a network"; Practice Q1)*
- I can explain how the Internet works as interconnected networks with open protocols. *(Guide: "What makes the Internet the Internet"; Practice Q5, question 10)*
- I can explain what a packet contains and why packets may arrive out of order or not at all. *(Guide: "Packets"; Practice Q4, Q9)*
- I can explain the difference between the Internet and the World Wide Web. *(Guide: "The Internet is not the Web"; Practice Q2, question 10)*

## Skills

- I can list the paths between two devices in a network diagram. *(Guide: Worked example 1 part (a); Practice Q6, Q8)*
- I can calculate transfer time from file size and bandwidth, converting bytes to bits. *(Guide: Worked example 1 part (b); Practice Q3, Q8)*
- I can reassemble a message from numbered packets and spot a missing one. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q9)*

## Reasoning

- I can explain why dynamic routing lets data keep flowing when part of a network fails. *(Guide: Worked example 1 part (c); Practice Q8)*
- I can explain why an open, scalable design lets the Internet grow. *(Guide: "What makes the Internet the Internet"; Practice Q5)*
- I can justify why a live call might use a protocol that does not resend packets. *(Guide: Worked example 2 part (b); Practice Q7)*
