Work through the list without notes. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/computer-science-principles/5-1-beneficial-harmful-effects-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/computer-science-principles/5-1-beneficial-harmful-effects-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can explain that not every effect of an innovation is anticipated. *(Guide: "One effect, two views"; Practice Q2)*
- I can explain that one effect can be beneficial and harmful depending on who is affected. *(Guide: "One effect, two views"; Practice Q3, Q9)*
- I can give examples of an innovation used beyond its intended purpose. *(Guide: "Use beyond the intended purpose"; Practice Q1, Q7)*
- I can explain why scale and rapid sharing can magnify an effect. *(Guide: "Use beyond the intended purpose"; Practice Q4)*

## Skills

- I can analyse an innovation by purpose, users, effects, data and safeguards. *(Guide: "A method for analysing an innovation"; Practice Q6)*
- I can name a beneficial effect and a harmful effect for named groups. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q8, Q9)*
- I can propose a specific safeguard linked to a harm. *(Guide: Worked examples 1 and 2; Practice question 10)*

## Reasoning

- I can explain why developers cannot foresee every use, and what responsible developers do. *(Guide: "What responsible developers do"; Practice Q5)*
- I can tell an unintended benefit from an unintended harm in a scenario. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q7)*
- I can give a balanced answer without taking a side. *(Guide: "Common misconceptions"; Practice question 10)*
