Work through the list without notes. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/computer-science-principles/2-2-data-compression-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/computer-science-principles/2-2-data-compression-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can explain what data compression is and why fewer bits does not necessarily mean less information. *(Guide: "Why compress data?"; Practice Q2)*
- I can explain what lossless compression guarantees. *(Guide: "Lossless compression"; Practice Q1)*
- I can explain what lossy compression allows and why it can usually reduce size more. *(Guide: "Lossy compression"; Practice Q5)*
- I can say what the saving depends on (redundancy and algorithm). *(Guide: "Why compress data?"; Practice Q7)*

## Skills

- I can run-length encode a string and decode a run-length code. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q3, Q4)*
- I can calculate the percentage saved from the original and compressed sizes. *(Guide: Worked examples 1 and 2; Practice Q8)*
- I can show that a scheme is lossless by decoding it. *(Guide: Worked example 1 part (c); Practice question 10)*

## Reasoning

- I can choose lossless or lossy for a situation and justify it with the priority. *(Guide: "Choosing between them"; Practice Q6, Q9)*
- I can name the trade-off of a compression choice. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q9)*
- I can give an example where a compression method fails to save space and explain why. *(Guide: "Lossless compression"; Practice question 10)*
