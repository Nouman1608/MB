Work through the list without notes. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/computer-science-principles/2-4-using-programs-data-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/computer-science-principles/2-4-using-programs-data-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can explain what iterative and interactive use of a program with data means. *(Guide: "Why use a program at all?"; Practice Q5)*
- I can state the difference between transforming, filtering, combining and visualising. *(Guide: "Four processes on data"; Practice Q1, Q3, Q4)*
- I can explain how combining data sources, clustering and classifying help gain insight. *(Guide: "Four processes on data"; Practice Q6, question 10)*
- I can explain why results depend on the quality and size of the group of data. *(Guide: Worked example 2 interpretation; Practice question 9)*

## Skills

- I can trace a loop that filters a list and give its output. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q2, Q8)*
- I can trace a loop that transforms every element or keeps a running total. *(Guide: "Tracing list-processing code"; Practice Q3, Q7)*
- I can modify a condition to change what a filter keeps. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q8)*

## Reasoning

- I can plan a sequence of processing steps for a question and say what each shows. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice question 9)*
- I can explain why a first result may lead to a second question. *(Guide: Worked example 2 passes 2 and 3; Practice question 9)*
- I can say what to check before trusting a result from a program. *(Guide: "Common misconceptions"; Practice questions 9 and 10)*
