Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/physics-1/7-3-representing-analyzing-shm-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/physics-1/7-3-representing-analyzing-shm-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can explain why displacement in SHM is measured from the equilibrium position, not from the end of the spring. *(Guide: "Displacement as a function of time")*
- I can say where the velocity is zero and where the speed is largest, and explain why. *(Guide: "Where the zeros and extremes are"; Practice Q1)*
- I can explain why the acceleration is largest at the turning points and zero at equilibrium. *(Guide: Figure 1; Practice Q1)*
- I can explain why changing the amplitude does not change the period. *(Guide: "Amplitude does not change the period"; Practice Q3, Q6)*

## Calculation

- I can choose between x = A cos(2πft) and x = A sin(2πft) from how the motion starts. *(Guide: "Cosine or sine?"; Practice Q4)*
- I can find the displacement at a given time with my calculator in radians. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q2)*
- I can find the time when an oscillator first reaches a given displacement. *(Guide: Worked example 1)*
- I can calculate the acceleration of a spring–object system at any displacement, with the correct sign. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q5)*

## Reasoning and graphs

- I can read amplitude, period and frequency from a position–time graph or table. *(Guide: Worked example 3; Practice Q4)*
- I can sketch velocity–time and acceleration–time graphs that match a position–time graph. *(Guide: Figure 1; Practice Q4)*
- I can use an acceleration–displacement graph to show that motion is SHM and to find k/m and the period. *(Guide: Figure 2; Practice Q7)*
- I can use timing data to support or refute a claim about amplitude and period. *(Practice Q6)*
