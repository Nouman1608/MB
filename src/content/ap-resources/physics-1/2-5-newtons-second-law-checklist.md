Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/physics-1/2-5-newtons-second-law-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/physics-1/2-5-newtons-second-law-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can explain what "unbalanced forces" means and why they always change a system’s velocity. *(Guide: "From balanced to unbalanced forces")*
- I can state Newton’s second law in words, including that the acceleration points along the net force, not necessarily along the velocity. *(Guide: "Newton’s second law"; Practice Q3)*
- I can explain why internal forces cannot change the velocity of a system’s centre of mass, using the two skaters as an example. *(Guide: "Choosing the system")*
- I can explain why ma never appears as an arrow on a free-body diagram. *(Guide: Figure 2; "Common misconceptions")*

## Skills

- I can draw a free-body diagram with separate, labelled arrows and use it to write ΣF = ma along each axis. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q4)*
- I can find the net force from several forces with signs before dividing by the mass. *(Practice Q1, Q4)*
- I can choose a whole system to find an acceleration, then a single object to find a tension or contact force. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q5)*
- I can predict the factor by which the acceleration changes when the net force and the mass both change. *(Guide: "Functional dependence"; Practice Q2)*
- I can find a mass from the slope of an acceleration–force graph. *(Guide: Figure 1; Practice Q6)*
- I can derive symbolic expressions for the acceleration and tension of a connected system and test them with limiting cases. *(Guide: Worked example 3; Practice Q7)*

## Reasoning

- I can use a force–acceleration graph to support or refute a claim about friction. *(Practice Q6)*
- I can justify why a contact force between two pushed blocks depends on which block is pushed. *(Practice Q5)*
- I can explain why the forces on an object can be balanced along one axis but unbalanced along another, and what that does to its motion. *(Practice Q7)*
