Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/physics-1/4-3-conservation-linear-momentum-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/physics-1/4-3-conservation-linear-momentum-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can explain, using Newton's third law, why forces between parts of a system cannot change its total momentum. *(Guide: "Why forces inside a system cannot change its momentum"; Practice Q2)*
- I can explain that momentum is conserved in every interaction, but a chosen system's momentum is constant only when the net external force on it is zero. *(Guide: "Choosing the system decides whether momentum changes"; Practice Q5)*
- I can explain why external forces such as friction can be ignored for the short time of a collision, but not afterwards. *(Guide: "Collisions and explosions"; Practice Q4(a))*
- I can describe a whole system as one object moving with its centre-of-mass velocity, and say when that velocity stays constant. *(Guide: "The centre-of-mass velocity"; Practice Q7(c))*

## Skills

- I can add signed momenta to find a system's total momentum, and divide by the total mass to find v_cm. *(Guide: "One system, one total momentum"; Practice Q1)*
- I can draw a momentum bar chart for a collision and check that the system bars match. *(Guide: Figure 1)*
- I can use conservation of momentum to find an unknown velocity just after a collision or an explosion, including one that starts in motion. *(Guide: Worked examples 1 and 2; Practice Q4)*
- I can find the change in a system's momentum from the external impulse, Δp_sys = F_net,ext Δt. *(Guide: Worked example 3; Practice Q5)*
- I can set up momentum conservation separately for x and y components in a two-dimensional collision or explosion. *(Guide: Figure 2; Practice Q3)*
- I can design a cart experiment to test momentum conservation and use a graph of p_after against p_before. *(Practice Q6)*

## Reasoning

- I can choose a system so that its momentum is constant, and say where momentum goes when it is not. *(Guide: Worked example 3; Practice Q5(b))*
- I can derive a symbolic expression for a velocity after an explosion and use it to judge a claim. *(Practice Q7)*
- I can predict how a speed must change when a mass or angle changes in a two-dimensional set-up, without solving simultaneous equations. *(Guide: "Reasoning without solving")*
