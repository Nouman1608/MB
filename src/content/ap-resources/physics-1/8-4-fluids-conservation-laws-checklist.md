Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/physics-1/8-4-fluids-conservation-laws-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/physics-1/8-4-fluids-conservation-laws-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can explain why a pressure difference between two places makes a fluid flow. *(Guide: "Why fluids flow")*
- I can explain why an incompressible fluid must leave a full pipe at the same rate it enters. *(Guide: "The continuity equation"; Figure 1)*
- I can explain Bernoulli's equation as conservation of energy, and say why each term is an energy per unit volume. *(Guide: "Conservation of energy: Bernoulli's equation")*
- I can state the assumptions behind continuity, Bernoulli and Torricelli: ideal fluid, full pipes, steady flow, wide tank. *(Guide; Revision notes)*

## Skills

- I can derive V/t = Av and use it to find flow rates and filling times. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q4)*
- I can use A₁v₁ = A₂v₂, squaring a diameter ratio where needed. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q1, Q4)*
- I can use Bernoulli's equation to find a pressure, including a change in height. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q5, Q7)*
- I can derive v = √(2gh) and combine it with projectile motion to find where a jet lands. *(Guide: Worked example 3; Practice Q6)*
- I can draw Bernoulli bar charts with equal totals at every point. *(Guide: Figure 2; Practice Q7)*
- I can plan an experiment to test Torricelli's result and choose what to plot for a straight line. *(Guide: "Designing an experiment"; Practice Q6)*

## Reasoning

- I can rank the pressures in a level pipe of changing width, and justify the ranking with Newton's second law and with energy. *(Practice Q2, Q7)*
- I can predict how an exit speed changes when the water level changes. *(Practice Q3)*
- I can compare a measured slope with a prediction and give a physical reason for the difference. *(Practice Q6)*
- I can check Bernoulli's equation against the depth rule P = P₀ + ρgh for a fluid at rest. *(Guide: "Two checks that it makes sense")*
