Short on time? This page is the recap. For explanations, diagrams and worked examples, use the [full study guide](/advanced-course-resources/physics-1/5-5-rotational-equilibrium-newtons-first-law-study-guide/). This is the algebra-based course: no calculus is needed.

## Recap

- **Rotational equilibrium:** the torques on a system add to zero, Στ = 0.
- **Rotational first law:** a rigid system keeps a constant angular velocity only if the net torque on it is zero. A steadily spinning fan and a still see-saw both qualify.
- **Corollary:** if the torques are unbalanced, the angular velocity must be changing.
- A **force diagram** shows each force at the point where it acts. You need this, not a single-dot free-body diagram, to find torques.
- **Independence:** a couple gives ΣF = 0 but Στ ≠ 0. A spinning ball in free fall has Στ = 0 about its centre of mass but ΣF ≠ 0.
- Only rotation in **one plane** is analysed in this course.

## Key relationships

| Relationship | Symbols and units | Use it to |
|---|---|---|
| τ = r⊥F = rF sin θ | N·m; θ between r and F | find each torque |
| Στ = 0 | sum with signs, e.g. ccw + | test or impose rotational equilibrium |
| ΣF_x = 0, ΣF_y = 0 | N | test or impose translational equilibrium |
| flat ω–t graph | rad/s against s | show Στ = 0 |
| sloping ω–t graph | slope ≠ 0 | show unbalanced torques |

## Assumptions behind the numbers

- The system is **rigid**: it does not bend or change shape.
- The weight of a uniform object acts at its **centre**.
- When an object is in both kinds of equilibrium, the net torque is zero about **any** point, so you may choose the pivot.
- g = 9.8 m/s².

## Mistakes to avoid

1. **No sign convention.** State "ccw positive" before writing Στ = 0.
2. **Full force instead of its perpendicular part.** Use T sin θ when the force is at an angle θ to the rod.
3. **Pivot at a known force.** Put it where an unknown acts so that force drops out.
4. **Equilibrium means at rest.** It means constant ω.
5. **ΣF = 0, so no rotation.** A couple can still start it turning.
6. **Forgetting the object's own weight.** A uniform beam's weight acts at its middle.

## Quick self-check

1. A 2.0 kg mass hangs 0.60 m left of a see-saw's pivot. What mass, hung 0.40 m right of the pivot, balances it (light see-saw)? *(3.0 kg)*
2. Two 4.0 N forces act perpendicular to a 0.50 m bar, one at each end, in opposite directions. Find ΣF and the net torque about the centre. *(0 N; 2.0 N·m)*
3. A uniform 6.0 kg plank rests on supports at its two ends. What force does each support exert? *(29.4 N ≈ 29 N each)*
4. A wheel spins at a steady 8 rad/s. Is the net torque zero? *(Yes: constant ω.)*
