Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/physics-1/2-1-systems-center-mass-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/physics-1/2-1-systems-center-mass-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can define a system and its environment, and draw a boundary to show my choice. *(Guide: "What is a system?", Figure 1)*
- I can sort interactions into internal and external for a given boundary, and show how a different boundary changes the sorting. *(Guide: Figure 1; Practice Q6)*
- I can explain how a system's properties come from its parts, and why its internal structure affects the analysis. *(Guide: "The properties of a system come from its parts")*
- I can explain how energy or mass can cross a boundary, and move the boundary to make a system closed. *(Guide: "Open and closed systems"; Practice Q3)*

## Skills

- I can calculate the center of mass of two particles on a line and check it with the inverse mass ratio. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q1)*
- I can calculate x_cm and y_cm for up to five particles in two dimensions. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q4)*
- I can replace a uniform symmetric part with a particle at its centre and include it in a calculation. *(Guide: Worked example 3; Practice Q5)*
- I can place a center of mass on a line of symmetry without calculating, even when no material is there. *(Guide: "Use symmetry first"; Practice Q2)*
- I can plot center-of-mass positions against time with labelled axes and a suitable scale, and find a velocity from the slope. *(Practice Q6)*

## Reasoning

- I can justify when a system can be modelled as one object at its center of mass, and when that model fails. *(Guide: "When can a system be treated as one object?"; Practice Q7)*
- I can explain how parts of a system can move differently from each other and from the system as a whole. *(Guide: "Parts can behave differently from the whole"; Practice Q6)*
- I can explain how changing an outside condition can change a system's structure, such as a stack that starts to slide apart. *(Guide: "Changing the conditions can change the structure"; Practice Q7)*
