Work through the list without notes. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/physics-1/2-4-newtons-first-law-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/physics-1/2-4-newtons-first-law-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can define net force as the vector sum of the forces on a system, and explain why third-law partners are left out. *(Guide: "Net force is a vector sum")*
- I can state Newton's first law and explain that constant velocity means constant speed **and** direction. *(Guide: "Newton's first law"; Practice Q1)*
- I can explain why equilibrium includes motion at constant velocity, and why a ball at the top of its flight is not in equilibrium. *(Guide: "Translational equilibrium"; Practice Q2)*
- I can describe an inertial frame as one where the first law holds, and give an example of a frame that is not inertial. *(Guide: "Inertial reference frames"; Practice Q6)*

## Skills

- I can add two perpendicular forces to find a net force, and find the third force needed for equilibrium. *(Guide: sled example; Practice Q4)*
- I can write ΣF_x = 0 and ΣF_y = 0 from a free-body diagram and derive expressions for unknown forces. *(Guide: Worked examples 1 and 2; Practice Q5)*
- I can find the tension in two symmetric cords holding up a weight, and say what happens as the cords become flatter. *(Guide: Worked example 2, Figure 2)*
- I can sketch velocity–time graphs before, during and after a force acts. *(Guide: Figure 1; Practice Q3, Q7)*

## Reasoning

- I can predict which velocity component changes when forces balance along one axis but not the other. *(Guide: "Balanced in one direction, unbalanced in another"; Practice Q7)*
- I can use limiting cases to check an expression for a force. *(Guide: Worked examples 1 and 2)*
- I can explain the motion of a ball in an accelerating vehicle from both the ground frame and the vehicle frame. *(Guide: "Inertial reference frames"; Practice Q6)*
