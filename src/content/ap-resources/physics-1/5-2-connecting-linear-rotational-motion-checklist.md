Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/physics-1/5-2-connecting-linear-rotational-motion-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/physics-1/5-2-connecting-linear-rotational-motion-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can explain why every point on a rigid body has the same ω and α but a different linear speed. *(Guide: "One body, many paths"; Practice Q1)*
- I can explain why Δs = rΔθ only works with the angle in radians. *(Guide: "Arc length and the radian")*
- I can explain why pulleys or sprockets linked by a belt or chain share linear speed, not angular velocity. *(Guide: "Things that share a linear speed"; Practice Q2)*
- I can describe the direction of a point's velocity on a rotating body as tangent to its circle, clockwise or counterclockwise. *(Guide: Figure 1)*

## Skills

- I can derive v = rω and a_T = rα from Δs = rΔθ. *(Guide: "Linear speed and tangential acceleration")*
- I can convert an angle turned into an arc length or a string length, and back again. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q4)*
- I can find a string's or load's speed and acceleration from a drum's ω and α, and check the result with linear kinematics. *(Guide: Worked example 1)*
- I can use r₁ω₁ = r₂ω₂ for belt drives and chains. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q2, Q7)*
- I can calculate v, a_T, a_c and the total acceleration of a point on a rotating body. *(Guide: Worked example 3; Practice Q6)*
- I can sketch v–t, ω–t and v–r graphs for points at different distances from the axis. *(Guide: Figure 1; Practice Q5)*

## Reasoning

- I can compare linear and angular quantities for two points on one body using ratios. *(Guide: Worked example 3; Practice Q1, Q3)*
- I can explain why a_c = ω²r is larger farther from the axis, even though a_c = v²/r has r on the bottom. *(Guide: "Same ω, different v"; Practice Q3)*
- I can derive a symbolic expression and use it to support or reject a claim. *(Practice Q6, Q7)*
