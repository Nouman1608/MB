**BC-only material.** Work through the list without notes. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-bc/9-4-defining-differentiating-vector-valued-functions-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-bc/9-4-defining-differentiating-vector-valued-functions-practice/). Prerequisites (Topics 9.1, 9.2, 2.8, 3.1) are on the [Calculus BC roadmap](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-bc/#roadmap).

## Understanding

- I can explain what a vector-valued function is and how its position vector traces a curve. *(Guide: "What is a vector-valued function?")*
- I can explain, from the limit definition, why r′(t) is found one component at a time. *(Guide: "The derivative: one component at a time")*
- I can explain why r′(t) is tangent to the curve and points in the direction of increasing t. *(Guide: "What r′(t) tells you about the curve"; Figure 1)*

## Skills

- I can find the domain of a vector-valued function from both of its components. *(Guide: "Domain"; Practice Q2)*
- I can find r′(t) and r″(t), using the product and chain rules inside components. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q1, Q5(a), Q6(a))*
- I can find the slope of the tangent line and write its equation at a given t. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q6(c))*
- I can find where a curve has horizontal or vertical tangents, checking that the other component is not zero. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q5(b))*
- I can estimate r′(t) from a table using difference quotients, with units. *(Practice Q7(a))*

## Reasoning

- I can describe the direction of motion from the signs of the components of r′(t). *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q3, Q6(d), Q7(d))*
- I can explain the difference between the vector r″(t) and the scalar d²y/dx², and compute both. *(Guide: "r″(t) is not d²y/dx²"; Practice Q4)*
- I can interpret a component of r′(t) or r″(t) in context, with units. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q7(b), Q7(c))*
