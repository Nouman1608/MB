**BC-only material.** Work through the list without notes. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-bc/7-9-logistic-models-differential-equations-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-bc/7-9-logistic-models-differential-equations-practice/). Prerequisites (Topics 7.1, 7.4 and 7.8) are on the [Calculus BC roadmap](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-bc/#roadmap).

## Understanding

- I can explain how a logistic model differs from an exponential model, and why logistic growth levels off. *(Guide: "From exponential to logistic growth")*
- I can explain what "jointly proportional" means and turn such a sentence into dy/dt = ky(a − y). *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q4)*
- I can say what the carrying capacity means in context. *(Guide: Worked example 1(a); Practice Q6(b))*

## Skills

- I can rewrite a logistic equation given in another form (such as by − cy² or ry(1 − y/a)) as ky(a − y) and read off k and a. *(Guide: "Other forms you will meet"; Practice Q2, Q6(a))*
- I can find the constant solutions and the long-run limit, using the initial condition. *(Guide: "Reading the equation without solving it"; Practice Q1, Q5(a))*
- I can find the value of y when it is changing fastest, and the greatest rate, with units. *(Guide: Worked example 1(b); Practice Q2, Q5(b), Q7(d))*
- I can find d²y/dt² for a logistic equation and use its sign to describe concavity. *(Guide: "Concavity"; Practice Q5(c))*
- I can find a constant of proportionality from one known rate. *(Guide: Worked example 2(a))*

## Reasoning

- I can describe how a solution behaves when it starts above the carrying capacity, at it, or at zero. *(Guide: Figure 2 and Worked example 1(e); Practice Q3, Q5(d))*
- I can explain why the relative growth rate in a logistic model is not constant. *(Guide: Worked example 1(d); Practice Q6(d))*
- I can use concavity to explain why an Euler estimate for a logistic model is too low or too high. *(Practice Q7(b))*
