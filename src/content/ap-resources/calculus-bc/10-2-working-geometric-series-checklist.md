**BC-only material.** Work through the list without notes. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-bc/10-2-working-geometric-series-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-bc/10-2-working-geometric-series-practice/). Prerequisites (Topics 1.15 and 10.1, and exponent and log laws) are on the [Calculus BC roadmap](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-bc/#roadmap).

## Understanding

- I can decide whether a series is geometric by checking for a constant ratio between successive terms. *(Guide: "What makes a series geometric")*
- I can derive Sₙ = a(1 − rⁿ)/(1 − r) by subtracting r·Sₙ from Sₙ. *(Guide: "The partial sum formula")*
- I can explain, using lim rⁿ and the definition of convergence, why a geometric series converges when |r| < 1 and diverges when |r| ≥ 1. *(Guide: "When does a geometric series converge?"; Practice Q4)*

## Skills

- I can find the first term and common ratio of a geometric series whatever its starting index, and find its sum. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q1, Q2)*
- I can rewrite terms such as 3ⁿ⁺¹/5ⁿ or 2²ⁿ/5ⁿ in the form (constant)·(ratio)ⁿ. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q4)*
- I can find the values of x for which a geometric series with a ratio containing x converges, excluding the endpoints. *(Guide: Worked example 3; Practice Q3)*
- I can write a repeating decimal as a geometric series and as a fraction. *(Guide: "Repeating decimals are geometric series"; Practice Q5)*
- I can use the partial-sum formula, and solve for the number of terms needed to reach a target. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q6(b)–(c), Q7(b))*
- I can find a missing ratio from a given first term and sum. *(Practice Q6(a), Q7(d))*

## Reasoning

- I can state the condition |r| < 1 before using a/(1 − r), and explain why the formula fails otherwise. *(Guide: Worked example 1(b); Practice Q6(d))*
- I can tell "the first n terms" (Sₙ) from "the total" (the full sum) in a context. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q7(a)–(b))*
- I can justify a claim about a total by comparing it with the sum of a geometric series. *(Practice Q7(c))*
