**BC-only material.** Work through the list without notes. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-bc/6-11-integration-by-parts-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-bc/6-11-integration-by-parts-practice/). Prerequisites (Topics 2.8, 6.7, 6.8, 6.9) are on the [Calculus BC roadmap](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-bc/#roadmap).

## Understanding

- I can derive ∫ u dv = uv − ∫ v du from the product rule, step by step. *(Guide: "Deriving the formula from the product rule")*
- I can explain why integration by parts trades one integral for another, and when the trade is worth it. *(Guide: "Choosing u and dv"; Practice Q3)*
- I can recognise when substitution, not parts, is the right method. *(Guide: "Why we need a new technique"; Common misconceptions)*

## Skills

- I can find an indefinite integral such as ∫ x e^(2x) dx by parts, including the chain-rule factor in v. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q1)*
- I can integrate ln x by taking dv = dx. *(Guide: "When dv = dx"; Practice Q2)*
- I can apply parts twice to integrals such as ∫ x² sin x dx. *(Guide: "Repeated parts"; Practice Q5)*
- I can solve for an integral that returns to itself, such as ∫ eˣ cos x dx. *(Guide: "Integrals that come back"; Practice Q4)*
- I can evaluate a definite integral by parts, applying the limits to both uv and the new integral. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q2, Q6)*
- I can check any antiderivative by differentiating it with the product rule. *(Practice Q5, Q6(d))*

## Reasoning

- I can justify a choice of u and dv using the two conditions (dv integrable, du simpler), and explain a case where LIATE fails. *(Guide: "Choosing u and dv"; Practice Q3)*
- I can set up and evaluate an accumulation problem with a product integrand, and state the answer with units. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q7)*
- I can use a definite integral of a rate to justify a claim about a total amount, such as energy delivered. *(Practice Q7(c))*
