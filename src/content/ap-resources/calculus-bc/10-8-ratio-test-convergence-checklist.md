**BC-only material.** Work through the list without notes. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-bc/10-8-ratio-test-convergence-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-bc/10-8-ratio-test-convergence-practice/). Prerequisites (Topics 1.15, 10.2, 10.3, 10.5, 10.6) are on the [Calculus BC roadmap](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-bc/#roadmap).

## Understanding

- I can state the ratio test, including all three cases L < 1, L > 1 and L = 1. *(Guide: "The ratio test"; Practice Q1)*
- I can explain the link between the ratio test and geometric series. *(Guide: "The idea" and "Why each case holds")*
- I can explain why L = 1 gives no conclusion, using Σ 1/n and Σ 1/n² as examples. *(Guide: "When the ratio test fails"; Practice Q7(d))*
- I can explain why only the limit of the ratios matters, not the first few ratios. *(Guide: Worked example 1, Figure 1)*

## Skills

- I can form aₙ₊₁/aₙ the right way up and simplify powers such as 7ⁿ⁺¹/7ⁿ. *(Guide: "Algebra you will need"; Practice Q1, Q6(a))*
- I can cancel factorials, including (n + 1)!/n! and (2n + 2)!/(2n)!. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q3)*
- I can keep absolute value bars when the terms change sign. *(Guide: Common misconceptions; Practice Q5)*
- I can find the values of a constant for which the ratio test shows convergence. *(Practice Q4)*
- I can use the ratio test when the terms are given by a recursive rule. *(Practice Q7(b), (c))*

## Reasoning

- I can write a conclusion that states L, compares it with 1 and names the ratio test. *(Guide: Worked examples 1 and 2; Practice Q5)*
- I can recognise series where the ratio test will give L = 1 and choose a better test. *(Guide: "Choosing the ratio test"; Practice Q2, Q6(b), (c))*
- I can explain why L > 1 means the terms cannot tend to 0. *(Guide: "Why each case holds"; Practice Q7(c))*
