**BC-only material.** Work through the list without notes. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-bc/9-5-integrating-vector-valued-functions-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-bc/9-5-integrating-vector-valued-functions-practice/). Prerequisites (Topics 6.7, 6.8, 6.9, 7.7 and 9.4) are on the [Calculus BC roadmap](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-bc/#roadmap).

## Understanding

- I can explain why a vector-valued function is integrated one component at a time. *(Guide: "Integrate one component at a time")*
- I can explain why the constant of integration for a vector is a vector ⟨C₁, C₂⟩ with two separate parts. *(Guide: "Two constants, not one"; Practice Q2)*
- I can explain why ∫ from a to b of r′(t) dt equals the net change r(b) − r(a), and why it is not the position. *(Guide: "Net change and the position formula"; Practice Q2)*
- I can recognise that a pair of rates dx/dt and dy/dt is the same problem as a rate vector. *(Guide: "Parametric form is the same problem"; Practice Q7)*

## Skills

- I can evaluate a definite integral of a vector-valued function and give the answer as a vector. *(Practice Q1)*
- I can find a particular solution r(t) from a rate vector and an initial condition given at any time, not only t = 0. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q2, Q5)*
- I can use substitution inside a component when it is needed. *(Practice Q5)*
- I can work from an acceleration vector to a position vector, using the starting velocity and then the starting position. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q3, Q6)*
- I can use a graphing calculator and the formula r(b) = r(a) + ∫ from a to b of r′(t) dt when a component has no elementary antiderivative. *(Guide: Worked example 3; Practice Q4, Q7)*
- I can find a position at an earlier time by subtracting the accumulated change. *(Practice Q4, Q7(b))*

## Reasoning

- I can check a particular solution by differentiating it and substituting the given time. *(Guide: "Checking your answer"; Practice Q5(c))*
- I can explain an error in a student's choice of constant. *(Practice Q6(d))*
- I can use the sign of a rate on an interval to justify whether a coordinate increases or decreases. *(Practice Q7(b), Q7(c))*
