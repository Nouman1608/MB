## Understanding

- I can state Gauss's law, Φ_E = ∮E·dA = q_enc/ε₀, and explain each symbol, including the outward area vector. *(Guide: "From flux to Gauss's law")*
- I can explain why the flux through a closed surface does not depend on its size when q_enc is fixed. *(Guide: "From flux to Gauss's law"; Practice Q1)*
- I can explain why zero net flux does not mean zero field on the surface. *(Guide: "Common misconceptions")*
- I can say when Gauss's law is useful for finding E and when it is true but not helpful. *(Guide: "Choosing a Gaussian surface")*

## Calculation and skills

- I can choose a spherical, cylindrical or pillbox Gaussian surface and justify the choice from the symmetry. *(Practice Q5, Q6)*
- I can derive E inside and outside a uniformly charged sphere and evaluate it with numbers. *(Guide: Worked example 1)*
- I can derive the field of an infinite line and of an infinite sheet. *(Guide: "Standard results"; Practice Q2, Q5)*
- I can find the net enclosed charge when several charges lie at different radii. *(Practice Q4)*
- I can set up and evaluate q_enc = ∫ρ(r′)4πr′² dr′ for a non-uniform density. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q7)*
- I can sketch E(r) for a sphere or cylinder and label the value at the surface. *(Guide: Figure 1; Practice Q6)*

## Reasoning

- I can check a result for continuity at r = R and for point-charge behaviour at large r. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q6, Q7)*
- I can predict a factor of change in E, such as E(R/2)/E(R), from the functional dependence. *(Practice Q3)*
- I can find where the field is greatest by setting dE/dr = 0. *(Practice Q7)*
