## Understanding

- I can explain what magnetic flux measures, say that it is a scalar and give its unit, 1 Wb = 1 T·m². *(Guide: "What magnetic flux measures")*
- I can define the area vector of a surface, including the outward rule for closed surfaces. *(Guide: "The area vector and the sign of flux")*
- I can decide whether a flux is positive, negative or zero from the directions of B and A. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q4)*
- I can explain why the net magnetic flux through any closed surface is zero, and link this to the absence of magnetic monopoles. *(Guide: "Closed surfaces"; Practice Q3, Q6)*

## Calculation and skills

- I can calculate Φ_B = BA cos θ, taking θ from the normal even when the angle to the plane is given. *(Practice Q1, Q4)*
- I can find the flux from vector components of B and A. *(Guide: "Flat surfaces in a uniform field"; Practice Q2)*
- I can find the flux through loops inside and around a long solenoid. *(Guide: "Flux through a solenoid"; Practice Q5)*
- I can set up ∫B·dA with strips (dA = ℓ dx) or rings (dA = 2πr dr) and evaluate it. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q7)*
- I can draw a diagram showing a loop, its area vector and the field, and label the angle θ. *(Guide: Figure 1)*

## Reasoning

- I can compare the flux through the same loop at different orientations, including the change after a half turn. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q4)*
- I can explain why "field at the middle × area" fails when B varies non-linearly. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q7)*
- I can use ∮B·dA = 0 to find the flux through one part of a closed surface from the others. *(Practice Q5, Q6)*
