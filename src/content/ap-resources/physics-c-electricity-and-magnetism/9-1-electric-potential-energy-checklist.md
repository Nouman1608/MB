## Understanding

- I can define the electric potential energy of two charges as the external work needed to assemble them from infinite separation. *(Guide: "Energy stored in an arrangement of charges"; Practice Q1)*
- I can explain why potential energy belongs to the system and not to one charge. *(Guide: "Common misconceptions")*
- I can explain what a negative U means physically. *(Guide: "Signs, sizes and the U(r) graph"; Practice Q1)*
- I can explain why the work done depends only on the start and end separations, not on the path. *(Guide: Worked example 2)*

## Calculation and skills

- I can derive U = kq₁q₂/r by integrating the external force from infinity. *(Guide: "Deriving U from the work done" and Worked example 2)*
- I can calculate U for a pair of charges, keeping the signs, including charges given as multiples of e. *(Practice Q4)*
- I can find the total U of three or more charges by adding each pair once. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q3, Q5)*
- I can find the work done by an external agent and by the electric forces when a system is rearranged. *(Practice Q5, Q7)*
- I can find a force from U(r) or U(y) using F = −dU/dr. *(Practice Q6, Q7)*

## Reasoning

- I can predict factors of change in U and in F when charges or separations change. *(Practice Q2, Q6)*
- I can sketch U against separation for like and unlike charges and read the force direction from the slope. *(Guide: Figure 1; Practice Q6)*
- I can use the shape of a U graph to decide whether a position is stable or unstable. *(Practice Q7)*
