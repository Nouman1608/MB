## Understanding

- I can explain in words what electric flux measures and give its unit, N·m²/C. *(Guide: "What flux measures")*
- I can define the area vector of a surface and state which way it points on a closed surface. *(Guide: "The area vector"; Practice Q2)*
- I can explain why flux is a scalar and what a negative flux means. *(Guide: "Flux through a flat surface in a uniform field"; Practice Q4)*
- I can explain why the net flux through a closed surface in a uniform field is zero. *(Guide: "Closed surfaces", Figure 2; Practice Q5)*

## Calculation and skills

- I can calculate Φ_E = EA cos θ when the angle is given from the surface or from its normal. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q1)*
- I can calculate flux from field components using the dot product. *(Guide: Worked example 1(b); Practice Q4)*
- I can draw a surface edge-on with its area vector and mark the correct angle. *(Guide: Figure 1)*
- I can find the flux through each face of a box, with signs, and the net flux. *(Practice Q5)*
- I can set up ∫E·dA with strips on which E is constant, including strips whose length changes. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q7)*

## Reasoning

- I can find the flux through a curved surface in a uniform field by closing it with a flat surface. *(Guide: Worked example 3; Practice Q6)*
- I can predict the factor by which flux changes when E, A or the angle changes. *(Practice Q3)*
- I can judge whether a quick estimate of flux is too large or too small for a non-uniform field. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q7)*
