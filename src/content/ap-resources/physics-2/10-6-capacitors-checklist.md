Work through the list without notes. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/physics-2/10-6-capacitors-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/physics-2/10-6-capacitors-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can describe a parallel-plate capacitor as two conducting plates holding equal and opposite charges, and say what Q means. *(Guide: "Two plates, equal and opposite charge"; Practice Q1)*
- I can explain why capacitance depends only on A, d and κ, and not on Q or ΔV. *(Guide: "What sets the capacitance")*
- I can explain why the field between the plates is uniform and which way it points. *(Guide: "The field between the plates"; Figure 1)*
- I can explain the stored energy as the work done to separate the charge, and show it as an area on a Q–ΔV graph. *(Guide: Figure 2; Practice Q4)*
- I can explain how a dielectric reduces the net field and so increases the capacitance. *(Guide: "Dielectrics"; Practice Q3)*

## Calculation and skills

- I can use C = Q/ΔV and C = κε₀A/d, converting mm and pF correctly. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q1, Q5)*
- I can find the field from E = ΔV/d or E = Q/(ε₀A) and the energy from U_C = ½QΔV. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q4, Q5c)*
- I can find the deflection of a charged particle between the plates using projectile-style equations. *(Guide: Worked example 3; Practice Q6)*
- I can plan an experiment to test C ∝ 1/d and find ε₀ from the gradient of a linear graph. *(Practice Q7)*

## Reasoning

- I can decide whether Q or ΔV is fixed and predict how C, Q, ΔV, E and U_C change. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q2, Q3)*
- I can use a proportionality argument to predict whether a deflected particle hits a plate. *(Practice Q6d)*
- I can suggest why real capacitance data do not pass exactly through the origin. *(Practice Q7d)*
