Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/physics-2/15-8-types-radioactive-decay-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/physics-2/15-8-types-radioactive-decay-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can describe an alpha particle and explain why it is not a helium atom. *(Guide: "The particles involved"; Practice Q1)*
- I can describe a positron, a neutrino and an antineutrino, giving the charge and mass of each. *(Guide: "The particles involved")*
- I can explain why neutrinos pass through ordinary matter almost without interacting. *(Guide: "The particles involved"; Practice Q3)*
- I can explain that the type of decay depends on the isotope, not just the element. *(Guide: "Why a nucleus decays, and what decides how")*
- I can explain when gamma decay happens and why it changes neither A nor Z. *(Guide: "The four types of decay"; Practice Q4)*

## Calculation and skills

- I can write the general equation for alpha, beta-minus, beta-plus and gamma decay, and say what happens to a nucleon in each beta decay. *(Guide: "The four types of decay"; Practice Q5)*
- I can check any decay equation for nucleon number, charge and lepton number. *(Guide: "Conservation rules for every decay"; Practice Q2, Q5)*
- I can find an unknown particle or product from the conservation rules. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q1)*
- I can count the alpha and beta decays in a chain from the start and end nuclei. *(Guide: Worked example 2)*
- I can draw each decay as a move on a chart of proton number against neutron number. *(Guide: Figure 1; Practice Q6)*

## Reasoning

- I can tell whether two nuclei are the same nucleus, isotopes of one element, or different elements. *(Practice Q6c)*
- I can use momentum and energy conservation to find how the energy of an alpha decay is shared. *(Guide: Worked example 3; Practice Q7a)*
- I can explain why alpha particles from one decay have a single energy but beta electrons have a range, and what that shows about neutrinos. *(Guide: Worked example 3; Practice Q7b–c)*
