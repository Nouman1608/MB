## Understanding

- I can describe an electromagnetic wave as electric and magnetic fields oscillating in step, at right angles to each other. *(Guide: "What is waving?"; Practice Q3)*
- I can explain why EM waves are transverse and say which field gives the polarization direction. *(Guide: "Transverse, so it can be polarized"; Practice Q6c)*
- I can describe a plane wave and the orientation of its wavefronts. *(Guide: "Plane waves and wavefronts"; Practice Q6b)*
- I can explain why EM waves cross a vacuum but sound cannot. *(Guide: "No medium needed"; Practice Q4)*
- I can state that all EM waves travel at c in a vacuum, whatever their frequency. *(Guide; Practice Q5c)*
- I can explain what "light" means in its narrow and its wide sense. *(Guide: "The electromagnetic spectrum")*

## Calculation and skills

- I can use c = fλ with MHz, GHz and nm prefixes. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q2, Q5)*
- I can use d = ct for travel times and echoes. *(Guide: Worked example 3; Practice Q5c, Q7c)*
- I can sketch E and B for a wave travelling along a given axis, and mark one wavelength. *(Guide: Figure 1, Worked example 2; Practice Q6a)*
- I can find the frequency and wavelength of light inside a material from its speed there. *(Practice Q7a, Q7b)*

## Reasoning

- I can list the spectrum categories in order of wavelength and of frequency. *(Guide: Figure 2; Practice Q1)*
- I can list the visible colours in order and rank them by frequency. *(Practice Q5d)*
- I can explain why light entering glass does not change colour. *(Practice Q7d)*
