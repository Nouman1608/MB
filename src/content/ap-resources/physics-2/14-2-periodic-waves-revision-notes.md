## Recap

- A **periodic wave** repeats in time at each place and in space at each instant.
- **Period T:** time for one full oscillation. **Frequency f:** oscillations per second (Hz).
- **Wavelength λ:** distance between successive matching points (crest to crest).
- **Amplitude A:** maximum displacement from equilibrium. It is independent of T and f.
- **Displacement–position graph** (snapshot): repeat distance = λ. **Displacement–time graph** (one point): repeat time = T.
- **Pitch** goes with frequency; **loudness** goes with amplitude.
- The **energy** a wave carries increases with amplitude and with frequency.

## Key relationships

| Relationship | Symbols and units | Use it to |
|---|---|---|
| T = 1/f | T in s, f in Hz | switch between period and frequency |
| v = fλ = λ/T | v in m/s, λ in m | link speed, frequency and wavelength |
| x(t) = A cos(ωt) = A cos(2πft) | at one location; x(t) is the displacement, not a position; radians | find displacement at a given time |
| y(x) = A cos(2πx/λ) | at one instant; x is position along the wave; radians | find displacement at a given position |
| ω = 2πf | rad/s | switch between A cos(ωt) and A cos(2πft) |

| Change (same medium unless stated) | f | λ | v |
|---|---|---|---|
| Source frequency doubled | × 2 | × ½ | same |
| Faster medium, same source | same | longer | faster |
| Amplitude doubled | same | same | same |

## Assumptions behind the numbers

- The wave is sinusoidal, with constant amplitude.
- In x(t) = A cos(2πft), x stands for displacement; in y(x) = A cos(2πx/λ), x stands for position.
- Cosine is used when y = +A at t = 0 (or x = 0); sine when y = 0 and increasing.
- The speed is set by the medium only, so it is the same for every frequency.

## Mistakes to avoid

1. **Reading λ from a time axis** (or T from a position axis).
2. **Crest to trough as λ.** That is λ/2.
3. **Peak-to-peak height as A.** That is 2A.
4. **"Higher f means faster."** Same medium, same v; λ shrinks.
5. **Degrees mode** for cos(2πft) or cos(2πx/λ).
6. **Linking pitch to amplitude.** Pitch follows frequency.

## Quick self-check

1. What is the period of a 200 Hz wave? *(5.0 × 10⁻³ s)*
2. Sound at 550 Hz travels at 330 m/s. What is its wavelength? *(0.60 m)*
3. A wave on a string has λ = 2.0 m and v = 12 m/s. What is its frequency? *(6.0 Hz)*
4. The frequency of a source is tripled in the same medium. What happens to λ? *(It becomes one third.)*
