Short on time? This page is the recap. For the ray diagrams, the sᵢ–sₒ sketch and the worked examples, use the [full study guide](/advanced-course-resources/physics-2/13-4-images-formed-lenses-study-guide/).

## Recap

- **Converging lens** (thicker in the middle): parallel rays meet at F on the transmitted side.
- **Diverging lens** (thinner in the middle): parallel rays spread as if from F on the incident side.
- A thin lens has a focal point on **each side**, both a distance f from the lens when the same medium surrounds it.
- **Real image**: refracted rays actually meet; can be shown on a screen. **Virtual image**: rays only appear to come from a point.
- **Principal rays**: (1) parallel, then through or away from F; (2) straight through the centre; (3) through or toward F, then parallel.

## Key relationships

| Relationship | Symbols and units | Use it to |
|---|---|---|
| 1/sᵢ + 1/sₒ = 1/f | all distances in the same unit, measured from the lens midline | find the image position, the object position or f |
| \|M\| = \|hᵢ/hₒ\| = \|sᵢ/sₒ\| | size ratio: no units, no sign | find image height; decide enlarged or reduced |
| single lens: real image ⇒ inverted, virtual image ⇒ upright | sᵢ > 0 real, sᵢ < 0 virtual | state the orientation in words |
| 1/sᵢ = −1/sₒ + 1/f | graph 1/sᵢ against 1/sₒ: slope −1, intercepts 1/f | find f from data |

| Lens and object | Image |
|---|---|
| Converging, sₒ > 2f | real, inverted, reduced |
| Converging, sₒ = 2f | real, inverted, same size |
| Converging, f < sₒ < 2f | real, inverted, enlarged |
| Converging, sₒ = f | no image (rays leave parallel) |
| Converging, sₒ < f | virtual, upright, enlarged |
| Diverging, any sₒ | virtual, upright, reduced |

## Assumptions behind the numbers

- The lens is **thin**: rays bend once, at the midline.
- Rays stay close to the principal axis, so all parallel rays meet at one focal point.
- The object is real (sₒ > 0) and the same medium surrounds the lens.

## Mistakes to avoid

1. **Positive f for a diverging lens.** It must be negative.
2. **1/sᵢ = 1/f + 1/sₒ.** Subtract: 1/sᵢ = 1/f − 1/sₒ.
3. **Forgetting to take the reciprocal** at the end.
4. **Calling a virtual image "imaginary" or "not there".** You can see it through the lens.
5. **Half a lens, half an image.** The full image forms, dimmer.
6. **Giving |M| a sign.** Quote the size ratio and say "upright" or "inverted" in words.

## Quick self-check

1. f = 20 cm, sₒ = 25 cm. Where is the image, and what is |M|? *(sᵢ = +100 cm; |M| = 4.0, real and inverted)*
2. A diverging lens has f = −10 cm and the object is 10 cm away. Find sᵢ and |M|. *(sᵢ = −5.0 cm; |M| = 0.50, virtual and upright)*
3. Where must an object be for a converging lens to form a same-size image? *(At 2f)*
