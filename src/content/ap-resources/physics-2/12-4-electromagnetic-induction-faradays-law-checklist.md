## Understanding

- I can define magnetic flux and explain why only the part of B perpendicular to a surface counts. *(Guide: "Magnetic flux", Figure 1; Practice Q1)*
- I can describe the area vector and say what a positive or negative flux means. *(Guide: "Magnetic flux")*
- I can name the three ways to change the flux through a loop. *(Guide: "Three ways to change the flux")*
- I can explain why an emf needs a changing flux, and why an open loop has an emf but no current. *(Guide: "Faraday's law"; Practice Q4)*
- I can explain Lenz's law as a consequence of energy conservation. *(Guide: "Lenz's law"; Practice Q7c)*

## Calculation and skills

- I can calculate Φ = BA cos θ, taking θ from the area vector. *(Practice Q1)*
- I can calculate the average emf and current for a coil of N turns. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q3, Q5)*
- I can find the direction of an induced current with the four Lenz steps and the right-hand rule. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q2, Q5c)*
- I can derive ε = Bℓv for a rod on rails and use it with I = ε/R and F = IℓB. *(Guide: "The rod on rails", Worked example 2; Practice Q7)*
- I can sketch an emf–time graph from a flux–time graph, and sketch both for a loop moving through a field. *(Guide: Figure 3; Practice Q4, Q6)*

## Reasoning

- I can predict how the emf changes when N, the area, the field change or the time changes. *(Practice Q3)*
- I can explain why a rod falling on rails reaches a terminal speed and predict how it depends on R. *(Practice Q7)*
- I can justify a direction with a full chain of reasoning, not just "by Lenz's law". *(Practice Q6c)*
