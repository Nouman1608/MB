## Understanding

- I can explain that overlapping pulses pass through each other and leave unchanged. *(Guide: "Superposition"; Practice Q1)*
- I can define constructive and destructive interference in terms of the directions of the displacements. *(Guide: "Superposition", Figure 1)*
- I can explain beats as two waves drifting in and out of phase. *(Guide: "Beats")*
- I can describe how a standing wave forms and define a node and an antinode. *(Guide: "Standing waves")*
- I can state whether each type of string end or pipe end is a node or an antinode. *(Guide: boundary-conditions table; Practice Q6)*

## Calculation and skills

- I can find the net displacement of overlapping pulses by adding signed displacements. *(Practice Q1)*
- I can calculate a beat frequency and the time between loud moments. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q2)*
- I can draw the first three standing-wave patterns for a string fixed at both ends and for a pipe closed at one end. *(Guide: Figure 2)*
- I can use λ = 2L/n or λ = 4L/n with v = fλ and v = √(F_T/μ) to find frequencies, wave speed or tension. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q5)*
- I can linearise f = v/(4L), plot f against 1/L and find v from the gradient. *(Practice Q7)*

## Reasoning

- I can explain why only odd harmonics exist when one end is a node and the other an antinode. *(Guide: "Harmonics"; Practice Q3, Q6)*
- I can decide from a list of resonant frequencies whether a pipe is open at both ends or closed at one end. *(Guide: Worked example 3)*
- I can use a change in beat frequency to decide whether a frequency is above or below a reference. *(Guide: Worked example 1)*
