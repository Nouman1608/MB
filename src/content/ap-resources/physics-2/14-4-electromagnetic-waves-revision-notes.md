## Recap

- An **EM wave** is an electric field and a magnetic field oscillating together. E and B are perpendicular to each other and in step.
- Both fields are perpendicular to the direction of travel, so EM waves are **transverse** and can be **polarized**. The polarization direction is the direction of **E**.
- EM waves are usually modelled as **plane waves**: flat wavefronts perpendicular to the direction of travel.
- EM waves need **no medium**. In a vacuum every EM wave travels at **c**, whatever its frequency. In air the speed is very close to c.
- The spectrum runs from kilometre wavelengths (radio) to picometre wavelengths (gamma). You need the **order**, not the exact ranges.
- Visible EM waves are called **light**; the word is sometimes used for the whole spectrum.

## Key relationships

| Relationship | Symbols and units | Use it to |
|---|---|---|
| c = fλ | c = 3.00 × 10⁸ m/s, f in Hz, λ in m | move between frequency and wavelength |
| T = 1/f | T in s | find the period of the oscillating fields |
| d = ct | d in m, t in s | find travel times and distances (halve for an echo) |

| Order (decreasing wavelength, increasing frequency) |
|---|
| radio → microwave → infrared → **visible** → ultraviolet → X-ray → gamma |
| visible: red → orange → yellow → green → blue → violet |

## Assumptions behind the numbers

- Speed in air is taken as c.
- Unit prefixes: nm = 10⁻⁹ m, MHz = 10⁶ Hz, GHz = 10⁹ Hz.

## Mistakes to avoid

1. **Making high-frequency waves faster.** All EM waves share c in a vacuum.
2. **Putting B parallel to E, or along the direction of travel.** All three directions are mutually perpendicular.
3. **Using B for the polarization direction.** Use E.
4. **Reversing the spectrum** or swapping infrared and microwave, or ultraviolet and X-ray.
5. **Forgetting unit prefixes** (MHz, GHz, nm) in c = fλ.
6. **Not halving** the distance for an echo.

## Quick self-check

1. A wave travels along +x and its E field oscillates along z. Along which axis does B oscillate? *(y)*
2. What is the frequency of an EM wave with a wavelength of 0.20 m? *(1.5 × 10⁹ Hz)*
3. How long does light take to travel 6.0 × 10³ m? *(2.0 × 10⁻⁵ s)*
4. Which has the higher frequency, green light or red light? *(Green)*
