## Understanding

- I can describe internal energy as particle kinetic energy plus configuration potential energy, and explain why an ideal gas has no internal potential energy. *(Guide: "Energy inside a gas")*
- I can explain why U of a fixed amount of ideal gas depends only on its temperature. *(Guide: "Energy inside a gas"; Practice Q4)*
- I can explain the difference between heating, work and internal energy, and why a gas does not "contain heat". *(Guide: "Two ways to change internal energy"; Practice Q2)*
- I can state the first law as ΔU = Q + W and say that W is the work done on the gas. *(Guide; Practice Q1)*

## Calculation and skills

- I can derive W = −PΔV for a piston and give the correct sign for expansion and compression. *(Guide: "Work done by a piston"; Practice Q5)*
- I can find the work done on a gas from the area under a PV graph that is not a horizontal line. *(Practice Q3)*
- I can calculate W, ΔU and Q for an isobaric or isochoric process using U = (3/2)nRT. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q4, Q5)*
- I can sketch an isotherm and an adiabatic curve from the same starting point and say which is steeper. *(Guide: "Four special processes"; Practice Q6)*
- I can find W, ΔU and Q for every step of a cycle and check that ΔU_cycle = 0. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q7)*

## Reasoning

- I can compare two processes between the same volumes and justify which has the larger |W| using areas. *(Practice Q6)*
- I can decide the sign of the net work from the direction of a cycle on a PV diagram. *(Guide: "Cycles"; Practice Q7)*
- I can explain why equal temperatures at two points do not make the path between them isothermal. *(Practice Q7c)*
