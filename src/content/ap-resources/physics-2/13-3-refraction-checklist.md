Work through the list without notes. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/physics-2/13-3-refraction-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/physics-2/13-3-refraction-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can define refraction and explain it as a result of the speed of light changing at a boundary. *(Guide: "Why light bends")*
- I can explain why a ray that meets a boundary along the normal does not change direction. *(Guide: "Why light bends")*
- I can state that a larger index of refraction means a lower speed of light, and that the frequency does not change. *(Guide: "The index of refraction"; Practice Q1)*
- I can state the two conditions needed for total internal reflection. *(Guide: "Total internal reflection"; Practice Q4)*

## Calculation and skills

- I can use n = c/v to find a speed or an index of refraction. *(Practice Q1, Q5c)*
- I can apply Snell's law with both angles measured from the normal, pairing each n with its own angle. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q3)*
- I can follow a ray through two or more parallel boundaries. *(Guide: Figure 1; Practice Q5)*
- I can calculate a critical angle from sin θc = n₂/n₁ and use it to decide whether a ray escapes. *(Guide: Worked examples 1 and 3; Practice Q4, Q7)*
- I can choose sin θ₁ and sin θ₂ as graph axes, plot data and find n from the slope of a best-fit line. *(Guide: Worked example 2, Figure 3; Practice Q6c)*
- I can describe a procedure for measuring angles of incidence and refraction, including a step that improves reliability. *(Practice Q6a)*

## Reasoning

- I can predict whether a ray bends toward or away from the normal, without calculating, from the two indices. *(Guide: "Snell's law"; Practice Q2)*
- I can explain why total internal reflection is impossible when light goes into a higher n. *(Guide: "Total internal reflection"; Practice Q4)*
- I can evaluate a claim about how a change of surrounding medium changes the critical angle and what is seen. *(Guide: Worked example 3; Practice Q7c)*
