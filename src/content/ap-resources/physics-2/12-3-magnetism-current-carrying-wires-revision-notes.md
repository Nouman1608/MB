## Recap

- A current makes a magnetic field. Around a long straight wire the field lines are **circles centred on the wire**; B is tangent to them, with no part towards, away from or along the wire.
- **Right-hand rule (field):** thumb along the current, fingers curl the way B goes. Current into the page gives clockwise circles.
- **Loop:** at the centre, B points along the axis. Curl fingers with the current; the thumb gives B.
- **Superposition:** add the fields of several wires as vectors. They can cancel.
- **Right-hand rule (force):** fingers along the current, curl towards B, thumb gives F.

## Key relationships

| Relationship | Symbols and units | Use it to |
|---|---|---|
| B = μ₀I / (2πr) | μ₀/(2π) = 2 × 10⁻⁷ T·m/A; r in m from the wire's centre | find the field of a long straight wire |
| B ∝ I, B ∝ 1/r | ratios | predict factors of change |
| F = IℓB sin θ | ℓ = length in the field; θ between current and B | find the force on a wire |
| F/ℓ = μ₀I₁I₂ / (2πd) | d = separation | find the force between parallel wires |

| Situation | Result |
|---|---|
| Wire at right angles to B | F = IℓB (largest) |
| Wire parallel to B | F = 0 |
| Parallel wires, same direction | attract |
| Parallel wires, opposite directions | repel |

## Assumptions behind the numbers

- Wires are **long and straight** compared with the distance r.
- Current is **conventional current** (the direction positive charge would move).
- The field B in F = IℓB sin θ is **uniform** over the length ℓ.

## Mistakes to avoid

1. **Radial field lines.** That is an electric-field picture.
2. **Using 1/r².** The wire field goes as 1/r.
3. **Adding magnitudes.** Add vectors: opposite fields subtract, perpendicular ones combine by Pythagoras.
4. **Force along B.** The force is at right angles to B.
5. **Unequal forces between wires.** They form a third-law pair.
6. **Whole length for ℓ.** Use only the part in the field.

## Quick self-check

1. Find B at 2.0 cm from a long wire carrying 3.0 A. *(3.0 × 10⁻⁵ T)*
2. A 0.10 m wire carries 2.0 A at right angles to a 0.50 T field. Find the force. *(0.10 N)*
3. The current is tripled and the distance is tripled. What happens to B? *(Unchanged)*
4. Two parallel wires carry currents in the same direction. Attract or repel? *(Attract)*
