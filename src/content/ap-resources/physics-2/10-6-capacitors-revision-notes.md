## Recap

- A **parallel-plate capacitor** is two conducting plates holding **+Q and −Q**. Q means the charge on one plate; the total is zero.
- **Capacitance** C = Q/ΔV is the charge stored per volt. Unit: farad (F = C/V).
- C depends **only** on the capacitor's geometry and the material between the plates, not on Q or ΔV.
- Between the plates the field is **uniform**, from + to −, except near the edges (ignored).
- A charge between the plates has **constant acceleration** qE/m: it moves like a projectile, along a parabola.
- The stored energy equals the work needed to separate the charge; it is the area under a Q–ΔV graph.
- A **dielectric** sets up an induced field opposite to the plates' field. The net field drops and C rises by κ.

## Key relationships

| Relationship | Symbols and units | Use it to |
|---|---|---|
| C = Q / ΔV | C in F, Q in C, ΔV in V | define or measure capacitance |
| C = κε₀A / d | ε₀ = 8.85 × 10⁻¹² C²/(N·m²); A of one plate in m²; d in m | find C from the geometry |
| E = ΔV / d = Q / (ε₀A) | V/m = N/C (second form: vacuum or air) | find the uniform field between the plates |
| U_C = ½QΔV = ½C(ΔV)² = Q²/(2C) | J | find the energy stored |
| a = qE / m | m/s² | treat a particle between the plates as a projectile |

| Situation | Fixed | Example: dielectric κ added |
|---|---|---|
| Isolated (disconnected) | Q | ΔV, E and U_C fall by κ |
| Connected to a battery | ΔV | Q and U_C rise by κ; E between the plates is the same |

## Assumptions behind the numbers

- Plates are flat and parallel, with d much smaller than the plate size, so **edge effects are ignored**.
- A dielectric, when present, **fills** the gap.
- Gravity is negligible for electrons and protons between the plates.

## Mistakes to avoid

1. **Saying C changes because Q or ΔV changed.** Only A, d and κ set C.
2. **Using the charge on both plates.** Q is on one plate.
3. **Dropping the ½** in U_C.
4. **Not checking what is fixed** before a "what happens if" question.
5. **Deflecting a negative charge toward the negative plate.** It goes toward the positive plate.
6. **Forgetting unit prefixes.** pF = 10⁻¹² F, nF = 10⁻⁹ F, µF = 10⁻⁶ F; mm must become m.

## Quick self-check

1. Plates of area 0.025 m² are 1.0 mm apart in air. What is C? *(2.21 × 10⁻¹⁰ F, 221 pF)*
2. An isolated charged capacitor has a dielectric with κ = 2 slid between its plates. What happens to ΔV? *(It halves)*
3. How much energy does a 10 µF capacitor store at 20 V? *(2.0 × 10⁻³ J)*
