Work through the list without notes. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/physics-2/13-1-reflection-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/physics-2/13-1-reflection-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can explain what a light ray represents and draw rays perpendicular to plane and circular wavefronts. *(Guide: "Light as a ray", Figure 1; Practice Q3)*
- I can say when the ray model is good enough and name effects, such as diffraction, that need the wave model. *(Guide: "When the ray model works, and when it fails"; Practice Q2)*
- I can explain why a laser beam can be treated as a single ray in optics experiments. *(Guide: "When the ray model works, and when it fails")*
- I can state the law of reflection, with both angles measured from the normal, and say that the rays and the normal lie in one plane. *(Guide: "Reflection and the law of reflection"; Practice Q1)*
- I can explain specular and diffuse reflection in terms of the direction of the normal over the lit area. *(Guide: "Specular and diffuse reflection", Figure 3; Practice Q6)*

## Calculation and skills

- I can draw a ray diagram with a ruler, arrows and a dashed normal at each point of reflection. *(Guide: "Ray diagrams"; Practice Q5a, Q6a)*
- I can convert an angle measured from a surface into an angle of incidence. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q1)*
- I can find how far a reflected beam turns when a mirror turns, and how far a spot moves on a distant wall. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q4)*
- I can use equal angles and similar triangles to decide where to place a mirror so that a beam hits a target. *(Practice Q5)*
- I can trace a ray through two mirrors using the angle sum of a triangle. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q7a)*

## Reasoning

- I can compare what a driver sees on a dry and a wet road and justify the difference with ray diagrams. *(Practice Q6)*
- I can show that two mirrors at 90° send a ray back antiparallel to its original direction, and explain why the paths are offset. *(Practice Q7b, Q7c)*
