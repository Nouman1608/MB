Work through the list without notes. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/english-language-and-composition/3-4-commentary-whole-argument-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/english-language-and-composition/3-4-commentary-whole-argument-practice/); also see the [revision notes](/advanced-course-resources/english-language-and-composition/3-4-commentary-whole-argument-revision-notes/).

## Understanding

- ☐ I can explain the difference between relevance and significance. *(Guide: "Relevance and significance"; practice question 1)*
- ☐ I can name four levels of commentary and say where each appears. *(Guide: "Levels of commentary within an argument"; practice question 2)*
- ☐ I can list four weak commentary habits and their fixes. *(Guide: "Avoiding commentary that does not help"; practice question 3)*

## Skills

- ☐ I can write evidence-level commentary that explains what a figure or quotation shows. *(Guide: Worked example 1; practice question 6)*
- ☐ I can write a bridge sentence linking two paragraphs. *(Practice questions 2 and 6)*
- ☐ I can use key terms and thesis echoes to keep an argument connected. *(Guide: "Techniques for keeping an argument connected"; practice question 5)*
- ☐ I can rewrite vague commentary so that it links to the thesis. *(Practice question 7)*

## Reasoning

- ☐ I can explain the significance of a sentence for a speech's larger argument. *(Guide: Worked example 2; practice question 4)*
- ☐ I can distinguish relevance from significance in a particular sentence. *(Practice question 8)*
- ☐ I can judge whether commentary overreaches its evidence. *(Guide: "Avoiding commentary that does not help")*
