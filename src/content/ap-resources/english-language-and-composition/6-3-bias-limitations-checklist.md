Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/english-language-and-composition/6-3-bias-limitations-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/english-language-and-composition/6-3-bias-limitations-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can explain what bias is and how it differs from having a position. *(Guide: "Bias"; Practice question 5)*
- I can explain how the degree to which a source considers other positions shows its degree of bias. *(Guide: "Bias"; Practice question 3)*
- I can list common limitations of evidence. *(Guide: "Limitations"; Practice question 2)*
- I can explain why bias and limitations are different. *(Guide: "Limitations"; Practice question 2)*

## Skills

- I can identify signs of bias in a source (interest, loaded language, omission). *(Guide: Worked example 1, Step 2; Practice question 1)*
- I can identify limitations of a source's evidence. *(Guide: Worked example 1, Step 3; Practice question 2)*
- I can compare sources by degree of bias. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice questions 3 and 7)*
- I can write a sentence that uses a biased or limited source responsibly. *(Guide: "Acknowledging and accounting for limitations"; Practice questions 4 and 8)*

## Reasoning

- I can say what a biased source can still be used for. *(Guide: "Common misconceptions"; Practice question 6)*
- I can adjust my claim to match what limited evidence can show. *(Guide: "Acknowledging and accounting for limitations"; Practice question 8)*
- I can use independent evidence to cover a source's gap. *(Guide: Worked example 1, Step 5; Practice question 8)*
- I can write a one-sentence evaluation of a source's reliability. *(Guide: Worked example 2, Step 5; Practice questions 6 and 7)*
