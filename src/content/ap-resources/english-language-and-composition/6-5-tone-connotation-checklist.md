Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/english-language-and-composition/6-5-tone-connotation-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/english-language-and-composition/6-5-tone-connotation-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can define tone and say how it differs from mood. *(Guide: "What tone is"; Practice question 5)*
- I can explain how readers infer tone from connotations. *(Guide: "From connotation to tone"; Practice question 2)*
- I can name tone precisely rather than as "positive" or "negative". *(Guide: "Naming tone precisely"; Practice question 1)*
- I can explain how tone serves purpose. *(Guide: "Tone in published prose"; Practice question 3)*

## Skills

- I can identify the tone of a passage and support it with specific words. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice questions 1 and 6)*
- I can explain the connotations of the words that carry tone. *(Guide: Worked example 1, Step 3; Practice question 2)*
- I can analyse tone in a published text. *(Guide: "Tone in published prose"; Practice question 3)*
- I can rewrite the same facts in a different tone. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice questions 4 and 7)*

## Reasoning

- I can explain why two writers address the same subject in different tones. *(Guide: "Tone in published prose"; Practice question 8)*
- I can choose words to create a tone suited to an audience. *(Guide: "Creating tone in your own writing"; Practice question 4)*
- I can judge whether a tone label is precise enough. *(Guide: "Naming tone precisely"; Practice question 1)*
- I can write a one-sentence analysis linking words, tone and purpose. *(Guide: Worked example 1, Step 6; Practice questions 6 and 8)*
