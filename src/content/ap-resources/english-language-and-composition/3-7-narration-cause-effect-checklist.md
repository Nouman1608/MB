## Understanding

- ☐ I can state what narration needs to be effective in an argument. *(Guide: "Narration in an argument"; practice question 1)*
- ☐ I can describe three shapes of cause-effect reasoning. *(Guide: "Cause-effect in an argument"; practice question 2)*
- ☐ I can explain why reflection and evidence for links matter. *(Guide: "Two methods, two promises")*

## Skills

- ☐ I can write a narrative paragraph with specific details and reflection. *(Guide: Worked example 1; practice question 6)*
- ☐ I can identify the links in a cause-effect chain. *(Guide: Worked example 2; practice question 7)*
- ☐ I can choose causal language that matches the evidence. *(Guide: "Making cause-effect convincing")*
- ☐ I can add a sentence of reflection to a narrative. *(Practice question 3)*

## Reasoning

- ☐ I can judge which links in a causal chain are weak and why. *(Guide: Worked example 2; practice questions 5 and 7)*
- ☐ I can explain how a writer combines narration and reflection to develop an argument. *(Guide: Thoreau example; practice question 8)*
- ☐ I can explain how a cause-effect sentence structures a writer's reasoning. *(Guide: Wollstonecraft example; practice question 4)*
