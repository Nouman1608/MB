## Understanding

- I can explain how evidence can support, complement or contradict a thesis. *(Guide: "Three relationships between evidence and a thesis"; Practice question 1)*
- I can explain how complementary evidence differs from supporting evidence. *(Guide: "Seeing the relationships at work"; Practice question 2)*
- I can explain why contradictory evidence should be addressed rather than hidden. *(Guide: "Using each type well"; Practice question 3)*

## Skills

- I can classify a source as supporting, complementing or contradicting a thesis, with a reason. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice questions 4 and 6)*
- I can name the four responses to contradictory evidence: qualify, concede, rebut and refute. *(Guide: "Using each type well"; Practice question 5)*
- I can plan how to use all three kinds of evidence in a body paragraph. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice question 7)*

## Reasoning

- I can explain how a writer uses support, complement and contradiction in a paragraph. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice question 7)*
- I can judge a source by its claim, its purpose and its relevance to the thesis. *(Guide: "Choosing and judging sources"; Practice question 6)*
- I can explain what kind of evidence a public-domain writer offers, such as Thoreau's first-hand experiment, and its limits. *(Guide: "A public-domain example"; Practice question 8)*
