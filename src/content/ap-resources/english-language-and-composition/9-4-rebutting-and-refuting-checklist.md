Work through the list without notes. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/english-language-and-composition/9-4-rebutting-and-refuting-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/english-language-and-composition/9-4-rebutting-and-refuting-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can define rebutting and refuting and explain how they differ. *(Guide: "Two ways to answer an opposing claim"; Practice question 1)*
- I can explain why a strawman damages credibility. *(Guide: "Fairness: avoid the strawman"; Practice question 2)*
- I can explain how conceding, rebutting and refuting each enhance credibility. *(Guide: "Two ways to answer an opposing claim"; Practice question 3)*

## Skills

- I can identify counterexamples, challenged assumptions, challenged evidence and alternative explanations. *(Guide: "Tools for answering a claim"; Practice question 4)*
- I can tell whether a response is a rebuttal or a refutation. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice question 5)*
- I can write both a rebuttal and a refutation of one opposing claim. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice question 6)*

## Reasoning

- I can explain how a writer refutes a claim and judge how convincing the evidence is. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice question 7)*
- I can choose when to concede, rebut or refute according to the evidence I have. *(Guide: "Two ways to answer an opposing claim"; Practice question 6)*
- I can analyse a public-domain refutation, such as Mill's on the claim that truth always prevails, for its method. *(Guide: "A public-domain example"; Practice question 8)*
