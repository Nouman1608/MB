Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/english-language-and-composition/6-2-position-perspective-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/english-language-and-composition/6-2-position-perspective-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can define position and perspective and tell them apart. *(Guide: "Two questions to ask of every source"; Practice question 3)*
- I can explain how sources can share a position but differ in perspective. *(Guide: "Same position, different perspectives"; Practice question 2)*
- I can list clues to a source's perspective. *(Guide: "Clues to perspective"; Practice question 1)*
- I can explain why having a perspective is not the same as being unreliable. *(Guide: "Common misconceptions"; Practice question 5)*

## Skills

- I can state the position and perspective of a source in a short table. *(Guide: "Same position, different perspectives"; Practice question 6)*
- I can explain how a perspective shapes the evidence a source chooses. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice question 4)*
- I can write a sentence that combines sources with the same position and different perspectives. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice question 7)*
- I can describe the perspective in a published text. *(Guide: "Perspective in a published text"; Practice question 1)*

## Reasoning

- I can say what a perspective leaves out and why it matters. *(Guide: Worked example 2, Step 4; Practice question 8)*
- I can use differences in perspective to strengthen my own argument. *(Guide: "Using position and perspective in writing"; Practice question 7)*
- I can judge how far a source's evidence travels given its perspective. *(Guide: "Clues to perspective"; Practice question 8)*
- I can write a one-sentence analysis linking perspective to evidence. *(Guide: Worked example 2, Step 5; Practice question 4)*
