## Understanding

- ☐ I can explain the difference between an audience's beliefs, values, needs and background. *(Guide: "Four lenses for analysing an audience"; practice question 2)*
- ☐ I can explain what common ground and concession are and why writers use them. *(Guide: "Common ground and concession"; practice questions 1 and 3)*
- ☐ I can explain why a writer's perception of an audience guides choices. *(Guide: "From who is the audience to what does this audience need")*

## Skills

- ☐ I can write an audience analysis with one belief, value and need and a choice for each. *(Guide: Worked example 1; practice question 7)*
- ☐ I can choose evidence that suits an audience's beliefs and needs. *(Practice question 4)*
- ☐ I can find a detail in a passage that answers an audience's concern. *(Guide: Worked example 2; practice question 6)*
- ☐ I can use common ground in an opening sentence. *(Practice question 1)*

## Reasoning

- ☐ I can explain how a writer's opening answers an anticipated fear. *(Guide: Lincoln example; practice question 8)*
- ☐ I can explain why a concession can strengthen an argument. *(Practice question 3)*
- ☐ I can link each choice to its lens, not just to a label like 'adults'. *(Guide: Worked example 1)*
