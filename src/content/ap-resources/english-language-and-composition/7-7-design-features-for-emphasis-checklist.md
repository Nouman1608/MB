Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/english-language-and-composition/7-7-design-features-for-emphasis-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/english-language-and-composition/7-7-design-features-for-emphasis-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can explain how design features such as italics and boldface create emphasis. *(Guide: "Design as part of the message"; Practice question 1)*
- I can explain why emphasis works by contrast and why overuse cancels it. *(Guide: "Emphasis works by contrast"; Practice question 2)*
- I can explain why design choices depend on audience and medium. *(Guide: "Design depends on audience and medium"; Practice question 3)*

## Skills

- I can identify headings, lists, capitals, white space and other design features in a text. *(Guide: "A catalogue of design features"; Practice questions 4 and 5)*
- I can analyse the hierarchy a design creates: what is prominent, what is quiet and what path the eye follows. *(Guide: "Design in an argument you are analysing"; Practice question 6)*
- I can redesign an over-emphasised text so that only the essential points stand out. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice question 7)*

## Reasoning

- I can explain the effect of a design choice on an audience and link it to a writer's purpose. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice question 6)*
- I can notice a gap between what a design emphasises and what it plays down, and say what that suggests. *(Guide: "Design in an argument you are analysing"; Practice question 8)*
- I can judge when design emphasis helps an argument and when it undermines it. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice question 8)*
