Work through the list without notes. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/english-language-and-composition/4-3-thesis-previewing-reasoning-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/english-language-and-composition/4-3-thesis-previewing-reasoning-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can explain how a thesis may preview the line of reasoning of an argument. *(Guide: "What a thesis already tells the reader"; Practice question 2)*
- I can explain why a preview does not have to list points, aspects or evidence. *(Guide: "What a thesis already tells the reader"; Practice question 5)*
- I can tell a preview from an announcement and from a bare list. *(Guide: "Preview, announcement and list"; Practice question 3)*
- I can list the ways a thesis can signal structure (reasons, progression, concession). *(Guide: "How a preview signals structure"; Practice question 4)*

## Skills

- I can identify the thesis in a text and say what it indicates about the structure. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice question 1)*
- I can build a previewing thesis from a line-of-reasoning plan. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice question 6)*
- I can write a thesis for a rhetorical analysis that previews strategy and effect. *(Guide: "Weak, better and strong theses for an analysis"; Practice question 7)*
- I can revise a weak thesis into one that previews reasoning. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice question 4)*

## Reasoning

- I can check whether the body of an argument delivers what the thesis previews. *(Guide: Worked example 2, Step 3; Practice question 1)*
- I can decide how much preview a given essay needs and justify it. *(Guide: "What a thesis already tells the reader"; Practice question 8)*
- I can explain why a list of devices is not a thesis. *(Guide: "Weak, better and strong theses for an analysis"; Practice question 7)*
- I can revise the thesis or the body when they disagree. *(Guide: "Common misconceptions"; Practice question 8)*
