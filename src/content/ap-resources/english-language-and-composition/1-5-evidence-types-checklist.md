Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/english-language-and-composition/1-5-evidence-types-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/english-language-and-composition/1-5-evidence-types-practice/); also see the [revision notes](/advanced-course-resources/english-language-and-composition/1-5-evidence-types-revision-notes/).

## Understanding

- ☐ I can define evidence and explain how it differs from a claim and from reasoning. *(Guide: "Evidence is what stands behind a claim"; "Evidence is not the same as the claim")*
- ☐ I can describe the strength and weakness of at least five types of evidence. *(Guide: "The types of evidence")*
- ☐ I can explain the four questions for judging evidence. *(Guide: "What makes evidence useful")*

## Skills

- ☐ I can label the type of evidence in a sentence. *(Practice questions 1, 2 and 4)*
- ☐ I can identify a claim and three types of evidence in a paragraph. *(Guide: Worked example 1; practice question 6)*
- ☐ I can choose the best evidence for a given claim. *(Guide: Worked example 2; practice questions 3 and 7)*
- ☐ I can say what a piece of evidence adds to a claim. *(Practice questions 4 and 5)*

## Reasoning

- ☐ I can explain why a piece of evidence is weaker than another. *(Practice question 7)*
- ☐ I can explain how a list of examples can build force through accumulation. *(Guide: Stanton example; practice question 8)*
- ☐ I can explain why evidence without commentary may not persuade. *(Guide: "Common misconceptions")*
