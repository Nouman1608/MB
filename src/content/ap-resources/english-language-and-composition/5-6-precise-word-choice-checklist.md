Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/english-language-and-composition/5-6-precise-word-choice-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/english-language-and-composition/5-6-precise-word-choice-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can explain that adjectives and adverbs both modify and convey a perspective. *(Guide: "Two jobs for descriptive words"; Practice question 1)*
- I can explain how precise word choice reduces confusion. *(Guide: "Precision"; Practice question 4)*
- I can explain why vague words weaken an argument. *(Guide: "Precision"; Practice question 2)*
- I can explain why a strong verb is often better than a verb plus an adverb. *(Guide: "Precision"; Practice question 5)*

## Skills

- I can identify modifiers that convey attitude in a passage. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice questions 1 and 3)*
- I can replace vague words with precise ones. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice questions 2 and 6)*
- I can analyse the effect of modifiers in a published text. *(Guide: "Modifiers in published prose"; Practice question 3)*
- I can write a sentence that uses a modifier to convey perspective and exact details to support it. *(Guide: Worked example 1, Step 4; Practice question 8)*

## Reasoning

- I can explain how exact details give a writer's perspective a basis. *(Guide: Worked example 2, Step 3; Practice question 7)*
- I can judge whether a modifier adds meaning or is filler. *(Guide: "Common misconceptions"; Practice question 6)*
- I can decide between a modifier and a stronger noun or verb and justify the choice. *(Guide: "Precision"; Practice question 5)*
- I can write a one-sentence analysis of how descriptive and precise words advance an argument. *(Guide: Worked example 2, Step 5; Practice question 7)*
