Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/english-language-and-composition/2-7-writing-defensible-thesis-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/english-language-and-composition/2-7-writing-defensible-thesis-practice/); also see the [revision notes](/advanced-course-resources/english-language-and-composition/2-7-writing-defensible-thesis-revision-notes/).

## Understanding

- ☐ I can explain what makes a thesis defensible: needs a defence and can be defended. *(Guide: "What makes a thesis defensible")*
- ☐ I can list six shapes of weak thesis. *(Guide: "Shapes of thesis to avoid"; practice question 5)*
- ☐ I can explain the thesis demands of the three exam essays. *(Guide: "Thesis for the three exam essays")*

## Skills

- ☐ I can turn a topic into a thesis with a position and a reason. *(Guide: Worked example 1; practice questions 1 and 4)*
- ☐ I can write a rhetorical analysis thesis that says what choices accomplish. *(Guide: Worked example 2; practice questions 2, 7 and 8)*
- ☐ I can revise a weak thesis using four questions. *(Guide: "Revising a weak thesis: four questions"; practice question 6)*
- ☐ I can tell a thesis from a restatement of a prompt. *(Practice question 3)*

## Reasoning

- ☐ I can explain why a thesis that lists devices is weak. *(Practice questions 2 and 8)*
- ☐ I can choose whether to preview the structure and justify the choice. *(Guide: "Does the thesis need to preview the structure?")*
- ☐ I can judge whether a thesis can be defended in the time and space available. *(Guide: "What makes a thesis defensible")*
