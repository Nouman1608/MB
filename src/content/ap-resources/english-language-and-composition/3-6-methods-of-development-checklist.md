Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/english-language-and-composition/3-6-methods-of-development-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/english-language-and-composition/3-6-methods-of-development-practice/); also see the [revision notes](/advanced-course-resources/english-language-and-composition/3-6-methods-of-development-revision-notes/).

## Understanding

- ☐ I can define a method of development and say what it gives an audience. *(Guide: "Why methods of development exist"; practice question 1)*
- ☐ I can name the five typical methods and their signals. *(Guide: "The five methods at a glance")*
- ☐ I can explain why methods often combine. *(Guide: "Methods work together")*

## Skills

- ☐ I can identify the dominant method of a passage and cite a signal. *(Guide: Worked example 1; practice questions 2, 3, 4 and 6)*
- ☐ I can choose a method by audience, purpose and need. *(Guide: "Choosing a method"; Worked example 2; practice question 7)*
- ☐ I can write the same idea using two different methods. *(Guide: "The same topic developed five ways")*
- ☐ I can explain what a method contributes to an argument. *(Practice questions 5 and 8)*

## Reasoning

- ☐ I can explain how Washington's parable works as narration and analogy. *(Guide: Washington example; practice questions 5 and 8)*
- ☐ I can justify an order of methods for a sceptical audience. *(Guide: Worked example 2; practice question 7)*
- ☐ I can explain why a method is a way of developing an idea and not a type of whole essay. *(Guide: "Common misconceptions")*
