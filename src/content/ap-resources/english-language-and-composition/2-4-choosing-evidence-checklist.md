## Understanding

- ☐ I can list the six jobs evidence can do and give an example of each. *(Guide: "Six jobs evidence can do")*
- ☐ I can explain the four things a writer weighs when choosing evidence. *(Guide: "Strategy: match the evidence...")*
- ☐ I can explain what makes a source credible. *(Guide: "Credibility of the source"; practice question 5)*

## Skills

- ☐ I can name the job a piece of evidence does in a passage. *(Practice questions 1 and 3)*
- ☐ I can choose the most strategic evidence for a named audience. *(Guide: Worked example 1; practice questions 2 and 6)*
- ☐ I can explain what a sentence of evidence does for the argument, citing details. *(Guide: Worked example 2; practice question 7)*
- ☐ I can explain why a writer uses a familiar saying. *(Practice question 4)*

## Reasoning

- ☐ I can explain how Lincoln's analogy clarifies, associates and amplifies. *(Guide: Lincoln example; practice question 8)*
- ☐ I can judge whether a writer's selection of evidence is fair or one-sided. *(Guide: "Strategic selection versus cherry-picking"; practice question 5)*
- ☐ I can explain why true evidence can still be a poor choice for an audience. *(Guide: "Common misconceptions")*
