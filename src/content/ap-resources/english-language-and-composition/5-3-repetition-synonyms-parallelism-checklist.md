## Understanding

- I can explain how repetition, synonyms, pronoun references and parallel structure show or develop relationships in a text. *(Guide: "Four tools that link"; Practice question 5)*
- I can explain what makes a pronoun reference clear or vague. *(Guide: "Pronoun references"; Practice question 2)*
- I can define parallel structure and give an example of faulty parallelism. *(Guide: "Parallel structure"; Practice question 3)*
- I can explain why a synonym can change meaning or connotation. *(Guide: "Synonyms"; Practice question 4)*

## Skills

- I can identify repetition, synonyms, pronouns and parallelism in a passage. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice questions 1 and 7)*
- I can repair vague pronoun references. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice questions 2 and 6)*
- I can repair faulty parallelism in a list or comparison. *(Guide: Worked example 1, Step 3; Practice questions 3 and 6)*
- I can write sentences that use parallel structure to link ideas of equal weight. *(Guide: "Parallel structure"; Practice question 8)*

## Reasoning

- I can explain what a device does for the argument, not just name it. *(Guide: Worked example 2, Step 5; Practice questions 1 and 7)*
- I can judge when repetition helps a reader and when it becomes needless. *(Guide: "Repetition"; Practice question 1)*
- I can decide whether to repeat a word, use a synonym or use a pronoun, and justify the choice. *(Guide: "Using the devices together"; Practice question 6)*
- I can write a one-sentence analysis linking a device to a relationship between ideas. *(Guide: Worked example 2, Step 6; Practice question 7)*
