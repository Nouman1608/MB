Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/english-language-and-composition/3-1-commentary-evidence-claim-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/english-language-and-composition/3-1-commentary-evidence-claim-practice/); also see the [revision notes](/advanced-course-resources/english-language-and-composition/3-1-commentary-evidence-claim-revision-notes/).

## Understanding

- ☐ I can explain what commentary is and why evidence needs it. *(Guide: "The missing middle of most weak paragraphs")*
- ☐ I can describe five jobs commentary can do. *(Guide: "What commentary does")*
- ☐ I can describe the commentary ladder from repeat to extend. *(Guide: "The commentary ladder"; practice question 2)*

## Skills

- ☐ I can write a lead-in that names the source and the point. *(Guide: "Introducing source material with commentary"; practice question 4)*
- ☐ I can explain a word or detail in a quotation and say what it does. *(Guide: Worked example 2; practice questions 1 and 8)*
- ☐ I can connect evidence to a claim in a sentence of commentary. *(Practice questions 3 and 7)*
- ☐ I can distinguish commentary from summary. *(Practice question 5)*

## Reasoning

- ☐ I can repair a paragraph whose commentary only repeats the claim. *(Guide: Worked example 1; practice question 6)*
- ☐ I can extend commentary to the larger significance or the audience. *(Guide: Worked example 2; practice question 8)*
- ☐ I can refine a claim in response to a precise word in the evidence. *(Guide: Emerson example)*
