## Recap

- To build new molecules, organisms must take in **atoms and molecules from their environment**.
- **Carbon, hydrogen and oxygen** are the most common elements in all four classes. Carbon forms four bonds, so it makes chains and rings.
- **Sulfur** is in proteins, in the side chains of cysteine and methionine.
- **Phosphorus** is in phosphate groups: in the head of every phospholipid and in every nucleotide of DNA and RNA.
- **Nitrogen** is in the bases of nucleic acids and in the amino group of every amino acid.
- Plants take in CO₂ from the air and nitrate, phosphate and sulfate ions from soil water. Animals get all of these from food.

## Key relationships

| Class | Elements | Where the "extra" element sits |
|---|---|---|
| Carbohydrates | C, H, O | none |
| Fats and steroids | C, H, O | none |
| Phospholipids | C, H, O, P (often N) | P in the phosphate head |
| Proteins | C, H, O, N, S | N in amino groups; S in cysteine and methionine side chains |
| Nucleic acids | C, H, O, N, P | N in the bases; P in the sugar–phosphate backbone |

| Tool | How to use it |
|---|---|
| **% by mass** | (atomic mass × number of atoms) ÷ molar mass × 100 |
| **Tracers** | ³⁵S labels proteins; ³²P labels nucleic acids and phospholipids |

## Assumptions behind the rules

- "Carbohydrates are C, H, O" covers almost all of them; a few modified sugars (as in chitin) carry nitrogen.
- Tracer experiments assume a radioactive isotope is used by cells exactly like the ordinary atom.
- Subtract the background count before comparing tracer readings.

## Mistakes to avoid

1. Saying proteins contain no sulfur, or that DNA does.
2. Saying phosphorus is only in DNA (forgetting phospholipids, RNA and ATP).
3. Saying all lipids contain phosphorus.
4. Saying plants build their mass mainly from soil minerals.
5. Using the number of atoms instead of their mass in a percentage calculation.

## Quick self-check

1. Which element would you use to label proteins but not DNA? *(Sulfur, as ³⁵S.)*
2. Percentage of carbon in glucose, C₆H₁₂O₆? *(6 × 12.01 = 72.06; 72.06 ÷ 180.18 × 100 = 40.0%)*
3. Why does a plant short of phosphate grow slowly? *(It cannot make enough nucleotides for new DNA and RNA, or phospholipids for new membranes.)*
