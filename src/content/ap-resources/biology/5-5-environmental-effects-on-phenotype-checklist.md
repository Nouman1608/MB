## Understanding

- I can explain why a phenotype depends on both the genotype and the environment, and define phenotypic plasticity. *(Guide: "Genotype is not the whole story"; Practice Q1)*
- I can explain the two main ways the environment acts: by changing gene expression, or by changing how well a gene product works, without changing the DNA sequence. *(Guide: "How the environment reaches the genes"; Practice Q2)*
- I can explain temperature-dependent sex determination in reptiles, including the role of aromatase. *(Guide: Worked example 2)*
- I can explain seasonal coat colour, hydrangea flower colour, tanning, human height and weight, and yeast mating signals as examples of one genotype giving several phenotypes. *(Guide: examples table; Practice Q2, Q5, Q6, Q7)*
- I can explain why a phenotype caused by the environment is not passed on through the gametes. *(Guide: "Common misconceptions"; Practice Q7)*

## Calculation and skills

- I can calculate means and percentage changes, dividing by the starting value. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q4, Q7)*
- I can estimate a pivotal temperature by interpolating to 50%. *(Guide: Worked example 2, Figure 2)*
- I can read a reaction norm and say which genotype is most plastic and whether genotype and environment interact. *(Guide: Figure 1; Practice Q4)*
- I can calculate a standard error and use ±2 SE to judge whether a change is likely to be real. *(Practice Q7)*

## Reasoning

- I can design an experiment that separates genetic and environmental effects using clones, a common garden, or each individual as its own control. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q3, Q5, Q7)*
- I can identify confounded variables, such as day length and temperature in the wild, and design a test that separates them. *(Practice Q5)*
- I can use evidence to argue whether a signal acts by contact or by a released chemical. *(Practice Q6)*
- I can explain why a plastic response is not always helpful, for example when a cue no longer matches the conditions. *(Guide: "Common misconceptions"; Practice Q5)*
