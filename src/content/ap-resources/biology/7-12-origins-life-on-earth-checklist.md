Work through the list without notes. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/biology/7-12-origins-life-on-earth-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/biology/7-12-origins-life-on-earth-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can state the three course dates (4.6, 3.9 and 3.5 bya) and say what each one tells me about the origin of life. *(Guide: "Geological evidence"; Practice Q1)*
- I can explain why fossils give a minimum age for life and why the hostile period limits the earliest plausible date. *(Guide: Figure 1; Practice Q1, Q4)*
- I can describe what a stromatolite is and why it counts as evidence of early life. *(Guide: "Geological evidence"; Practice Q5)*
- I can describe the RNA world hypothesis and state its three assumptions in my own words. *(Guide: "The three assumptions"; Practice Q3)*
- I can explain why RNA, unlike DNA or protein alone, could be both genetic material and catalyst. *(Guide: Figure 2; Practice Q7)*

## Skills

- I can calculate time spans from the course dates and scale them onto a model timeline. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q4)*
- I can state a null hypothesis for an experiment on RNA catalysis. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q6)*
- I can predict the results of control tubes (RNase, protease, no template, mismatch) and match each to an assumption. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q6)*

## Reasoning

- I can identify which observations support the RNA world hypothesis and which do not. *(Guide: "Evidence that fits the hypothesis"; Practice Q2)*
- I can evaluate a claim about very old fossils and suggest what further evidence would strengthen it. *(Practice Q5)*
- I can explain why DNA and proteins could later have replaced RNA in most roles. *(Guide: "Why DNA and proteins later took over"; Practice Q7)*
- I can explain the limits of laboratory experiments as evidence for events on early Earth. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q6)*
