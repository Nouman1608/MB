## Recap

- **Fossils** show extinct species and changes in lineages over time.
- **Relative dating:** in undisturbed sedimentary rock, lower layers are older.
- **Radiometric dating:** isotopes decay at a constant rate. Carbon-14 (half-life about 5,700 years) dates once-living remains up to about 50,000 years. Older fossils are bracketed by dating volcanic layers above and below them with longer-lived isotopes.
- **Geographical data:** matching layers and fossils between sites gives ages; the same fossil species on now-separate continents shows they were once joined.
- **Homologous structures:** same basic parts, different functions (mammal forelimbs) → common ancestry.
- **Vestigial structures:** reduced remnants (whale pelvis, flightless-bird wings, cave-fish eyes) → descent from ancestors that used them.
- **Molecular evidence:** compare DNA or amino acid sequences; differences build up after lineages split.
- **Biogeography:** island species resemble the nearest mainland species.

## Key relationships

| Idea | Rule |
|---|---|
| fraction left | (1/2)ⁿ after n half-lives |
| age | number of half-lives × half-life |
| 50%, 25%, 12.5%, 6.25% | 1, 2, 3, 4 half-lives |
| sequence differences | fewer differences → more recent common ancestor |
| percent identity | (matching positions ÷ total positions) × 100 |

| Structure type | Same build? | Same function? | Evidence of common ancestry? |
|---|---|---|---|
| homologous | yes | often no | yes |
| vestigial | reduced version | lost main function | yes |
| analogous | no | yes | no |

## Assumptions behind the numbers

- Layers have not been overturned or disturbed.
- Decay rate is constant; nothing added or removed from the sample after death.
- The starting amount of carbon-14 matched the atmosphere at the time.
- Sequence comparisons use the same gene, aligned position by position.

## Mistakes to avoid

1. Using carbon-14 for fossils millions of years old.
2. Saying nothing is left after two half-lives.
3. Calling analogous structures evidence of common ancestry.
4. Saying vestigial means "useless".
5. Describing data without quoting values.
6. Saying one living species evolved from another living species.

## Quick self-check

1. A bone has 25% of its original carbon-14. Age, with half-life 5,700 years? *(2 half-lives: 11,400 years)*
2. Why do a bat wing and a whale flipper count as evidence of common ancestry? *(Same set of bones, different functions: inherited from a shared ancestor.)*
3. Species A and B differ at 3 amino acids; A and C at 12. Which pair shares the more recent common ancestor? *(A and B.)*
