Work through the list without notes. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/biology/8-5-community-ecology-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/biology/8-5-community-ecology-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can define a community and explain how it differs from a population and from an ecosystem. *(Guide: "From populations to communities")*
- I can explain the difference between species composition, species richness and species evenness. *(Guide: "Describing a community"; Practice Q4)*
- I can classify competition, predation, herbivory, parasitism, mutualism and commensalism by their effect on each species (+, − or 0). *(Guide: "Interactions between populations"; Practice Q1)*
- I can explain how interactions decide which populations get energy and matter in a community. *(Guide: "Interactions between populations"; Practice Q7)*

## Calculation and skills

- I can calculate Simpson's diversity index from species counts, squaring each proportion before adding. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q2, Q4)*
- I can calculate a mean, standard deviation and SE, and draw or read error bars of mean ± 2 SE. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q5)*
- I can use overlapping or non-overlapping error bars to decide whether two means are likely to differ. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q5, Q6)*
- I can read a predator–prey graph with two vertical axes and describe the lag between the peaks. *(Guide: Figure 1; Practice Q3)*

## Reasoning

- I can explain why predator and prey numbers cycle and why the predator peak comes later. *(Guide: "Predator and prey populations"; Practice Q3)*
- I can explain competitive exclusion and how niche partitioning lets similar species coexist. *(Guide: "Competition and niche partitioning"; Practice Q5, Q7)*
- I can follow a trophic cascade step by step and predict the effect of adding or removing a top level. *(Guide: "Trophic cascades"; Practice Q6)*
- I can use data to name an interaction and justify its sign pattern. *(Practice Q1, Q7)*
