Work through the list without notes. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/biology/2-2-cell-size-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/biology/2-2-cell-size-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can explain why a cell's supply depends on its surface area but its demand depends on its volume. *(Guide: "Why can't a cell just keep growing?")*
- I can explain why SA/V falls as a cell grows, using how area and volume scale with length. *(Guide: "How SA/V changes with size"; Practice Q1)*
- I can explain how small size, flat or long shapes and membrane folds (microvilli, root hairs, cristae) keep exchange fast enough. *(Guide: "Ways to keep SA/V high"; Practice Q5)*
- I can explain why larger organisms exchange heat more slowly per unit mass and have a lower metabolic rate per unit body mass. *(Guide: "From cells to whole organisms"; Practice Q3)*

## Calculation and skills

- I can calculate SA, V and SA/V for cubes and spheres, and use the shortcuts 6/s and 3/r, with units. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q4)*
- I can calculate SA and V for cylinders and rectangular solids, including both ends of a cylinder. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q2, Q7)*
- I can use a supply-and-demand model to find the largest size a cell can reach. *(Guide: Worked example 1 (c); Practice Q7)*
- I can calculate metabolic rate per unit mass from whole-body data. *(Practice Q6)*

## Reasoning

- I can use a diffusion model to explain why a small cube is supplied more completely, and state how the model differs from a real cell. *(Practice Q4)*
- I can design a controlled experiment on how a surface feature affects uptake, standardising the measurement fairly. *(Practice Q5)*
- I can distinguish total rate from rate per unit mass when evaluating a claim. *(Practice Q6)*
