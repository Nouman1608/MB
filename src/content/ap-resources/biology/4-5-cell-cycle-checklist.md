Work through the list without notes. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/biology/4-5-cell-cycle-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/biology/4-5-cell-cycle-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can put the stages of the cell cycle in order (G1, S, G2, mitosis, cytokinesis) and say which ones make up interphase. *(Guide: Figure 1)*
- I can describe what the cell does in G1, S and G2, and explain why interphase is not a resting stage. *(Guide: "Interphase: G1, S and G2"; Practice Q1)*
- I can explain what G0 is, and that many cells in G0 can re-enter the cycle when they receive the right cues. *(Guide: "G0: leaving the cycle"; Practice Q7)*
- I can describe the main events of prophase, metaphase, anaphase and telophase. *(Guide: "Mitosis: sharing the copies out")*
- I can compare cytokinesis in animal cells (cleavage furrow) and plant cells (cell plate). *(Guide: "Cytokinesis: two new cells"; Practice Q3)*
- I can explain why mitosis gives two genetically identical daughter cells, and name its roles in growth, repair and asexual reproduction. *(Guide: "What mitosis is for"; Practice Q6)*

## Calculation and skills

- I can give the number of chromosomes, chromatids and the DNA content of a cell at any stage of the cycle. *(Guide: Worked example 2, Figure 2; Practice Q2, Q5)*
- I can calculate a mitotic index from cell counts, including interphase cells in the total. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q4)*
- I can estimate the time spent in a stage from the fraction of cells in it, and state the assumption this needs. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q4)*
- I can identify G1, S and G2 on a DNA-content graph or table and calculate the rate of DNA synthesis. *(Guide: Figure 3; Practice Q5)*

## Reasoning

- I can describe a trend in cell-count data with numbers, and suggest a biological reason for it. *(Practice Q4)*
- I can predict what happens to the daughter cells if sister chromatids fail to separate, or if cytokinesis fails. *(Practice Q3, Q6)*
- I can explain why a population with some cells in G0 grows more slowly than one where every cell divides. *(Practice Q7)*
