Work through the list without notes. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/biology/8-3-population-ecology-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/biology/8-3-population-ecology-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can define a population and explain why a group of several species, or one species spread across separate places, is not one population. *(Guide: "What a population is"; Practice Q1)*
- I can describe ways members of a population interact with each other and with their environment. *(Guide: "What a population is")*
- I can explain how birth rate, death rate and population size together set how fast a population grows. *(Guide: "What changes population size"; Figure 1)*
- I can explain how adaptations for obtaining and using energy and matter affect births and deaths. *(Guide: "Energy, matter and births and deaths"; Practice Q6)*
- I can describe the conditions that allow exponential growth and explain why it cannot continue. *(Guide: "Exponential growth"; Practice Q7)*

## Calculation and skills

- I can calculate dN/dt from births and deaths, with units. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q2, Q5)*
- I can calculate per capita birth, death and growth rates and use them to compare populations of different sizes. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q5)*
- I can use dN/dt = rₘₐₓN and project a population one time step at a time. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q7)*
- I can calculate means of replicate counts and the ratio over each time interval to test for exponential growth. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q4)*
- I can construct a line graph of population data with labelled axes, units, a suitable scale (including a log scale) and error bars where needed. *(Guide: "Representing population data on graphs"; Figure 2; Practice Q4)*

## Reasoning

- I can explain why the per capita rate stays constant while dN/dt rises in exponential growth. *(Guide: "Exponential growth"; Practice Q3)*
- I can identify the variables and control group in a population experiment and predict the effect of food supply on dN/dt. *(Practice Q6)*
