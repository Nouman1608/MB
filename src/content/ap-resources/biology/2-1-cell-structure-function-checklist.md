Work through the list without notes. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/biology/2-1-cell-structure-function-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/biology/2-1-cell-structure-function-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can state what ribosomes are made of, what they do and why finding them in every cell supports common ancestry. *(Guide: "Ribosomes"; Practice Q1)*
- I can list the parts of the endomembrane system and say what the system does as a whole. *(Guide: "The endomembrane system"; Figure 1)*
- I can contrast rough and smooth ER by structure and by function, including the ER's roles in support and transport. *(Guide: "Endoplasmic reticulum")*
- I can describe how the Golgi complex modifies and packages products, using glycosylation as an example. *(Guide: "Golgi complex"; Practice Q2)*
- I can explain how lysosomes digest material and how they take part in apoptosis. *(Guide: "Lysosomes"; Practice Q6)*
- I can compare plant and animal vacuoles and explain how a central vacuole keeps turgor pressure. *(Guide: "Vacuoles"; Practice Q4)*
- I can explain why mitochondria and chloroplasts have double membranes and why the inner mitochondrial membrane is folded. *(Guide: Figure 2; Practice Q3)*

## Calculation and skills

- I can read pulse-chase data to find the order of compartments and calculate a rate of secretion. *(Guide: Worked example 1)*
- I can calculate means, percentage changes and ratios from organelle data, dividing by the correct starting value. *(Practice Q4, Q7)*

## Reasoning

- I can predict what happens when one step of the secretory route is blocked. *(Guide: Worked example 1 (d); Practice Q2)*
- I can design a controlled experiment on secretion, with a suitable control group. *(Practice Q5)*
- I can make a claim about a cell's function, or a defective structure, and support it with evidence and reasoning. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q6, Q7)*
