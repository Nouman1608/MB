Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/biology/6-5-regulation-gene-expression-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/biology/6-5-regulation-gene-expression-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can explain how a regulatory protein and a regulatory DNA sequence work together to control transcription. *(Guide: "Why cells regulate their genes")*
- I can define constitutive, inducible and repressible genes and give an example of each. *(Guide: "Three patterns of expression"; Practice Q1)*
- I can draw and label the lac operon with and without lactose, and say where the repressor gene is. *(Guide: Figure 1; Practice Q4)*
- I can explain how tryptophan switches the trp operon off, and contrast it with the lac operon. *(Guide: "The trp operon"; Practice Q2)*
- I can explain how DNA methylation and histone acetylation change gene expression without changing the DNA sequence. *(Guide: "Epigenetic regulation"; Practice Q3)*

## Skills

- I can calculate fold change and percentages from expression data and say which value is the reference. *(Guide: Worked examples 1 and 2; Practice Q4, Q5)*
- I can predict the effect of a mutation in a repressor gene, an operator or a promoter. *(Guide: Worked example 1)*
- I can use deletion data to show that a control sequence acts only on the gene it sits next to. *(Practice Q5)*

## Reasoning

- I can explain how the position of the operator lets one repressor control several genes, and how a shared control sequence does the same job in eukaryotes. *(Guide: "Operons" and "Coordinate regulation in eukaryotes"; Practice Q5)*
- I can make a claim that a gene is controlled epigenetically and support it with evidence of reversibility. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q6)*
- I can explain how a chain of transcription factors produces sequential gene expression in development, and predict the effect of removing one link. *(Guide: "From expression to phenotype"; Practice Q7)*
- I can explain why the amount of a gene product, not only its presence, affects the phenotype. *(Practice Q7)*
