Work through the list without notes. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/biology/1-5-lipids-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/biology/1-5-lipids-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can explain why most lipids are hydrophobic, using the polarity of C–H bonds, and say why lipids are not counted as polymers. *(Guide: "What makes a molecule a lipid?")*
- I can tell a saturated fatty acid from an unsaturated one, and explain what "more unsaturated" means. *(Guide: Figure 1; Practice Q1)*
- I can describe how a fat is built from glycerol and three fatty acids, and say how many water molecules are released. *(Guide: "Fats (triglycerides)"; Figure 2)*
- I can describe a phospholipid's hydrophilic head and hydrophobic tails, and explain why phospholipids form a bilayer in water. *(Guide: Figure 3; Practice Q2, Q5)*
- I can state the jobs of fats, steroid hormones, cholesterol and phospholipids, and recognise a steroid by its four fused rings. *(Guide: "Structure decides function")*

## Calculation and skills

- I can calculate the mass of fat or carbohydrate needed to store a given amount of energy, with units on every line. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q3, Q7)*
- I can describe a trend in a table or graph using quoted values, and estimate a value between two data points. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q4)*
- I can calculate means from repeated trials and say whether the ranges overlap. *(Practice Q6)*

## Reasoning

- I can explain why more double bonds make a lipid more liquid at room temperature, using kinks and packing, not bond strength. *(Guide: "Why kinks change whether a fat is solid or liquid"; Practice Q4)*
- I can predict how a change in the lipids a cell makes (more or fewer unsaturated tails, no cholesterol, no phospholipids) would affect its membranes, and justify the prediction. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q5, Q6)*
- I can explain why fat is a better energy store than carbohydrate for an animal that must carry its store. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q7)*
- I can identify the independent variable and the purpose of a control in an experiment on membrane lipids. *(Practice Q6)*
