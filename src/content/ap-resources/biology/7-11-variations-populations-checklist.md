Work through the list without notes. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/biology/7-11-variations-populations-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/biology/7-11-variations-populations-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can define genetic diversity and describe three ways to compare it between populations. *(Guide: "What genetic diversity means")*
- I can explain why a species or population with little genetic diversity is at greater risk of decline or extinction, using one named example. *(Guide: "Low diversity: a population at risk"; Practice Q1)*
- I can explain why a genetically diverse population is more likely to contain individuals that survive a new pressure, such as an antibiotic or a disease. *(Guide: "High diversity: a resilient population"; Practice Q5)*
- I can explain how gene flow from another population can rescue a small, inbred population. *(Guide: "Low diversity"; Practice Q4)*

## Skills

- I can use allele frequencies and the Hardy–Weinberg equations to predict how many individuals survive a pressure. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q2)*
- I can read a distribution of a trait and explain why its spread, not just its mean, decides who survives an extreme. *(Guide: Figure 1)*
- I can calculate percentage changes and growth over several doublings from population data. *(Practice Q4, Q6)*
- I can state a null hypothesis for an experiment that compares populations of different diversity. *(Practice Q5)*

## Reasoning

- I can explain, with data, why an allele that is adaptive in one environment can be harmful in another. *(Guide: "The same allele can help or harm"; Practice Q3, Q6)*
- I can build a claim–evidence–reasoning argument that connects population data to natural selection. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q4)*
- I can suggest an alternative explanation for a trend and a way to test it. *(Practice Q4)*
- I can evaluate a conservation plan in terms of the genetic diversity it keeps or loses. *(Practice Q7)*
