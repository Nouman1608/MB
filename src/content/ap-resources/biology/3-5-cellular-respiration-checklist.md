Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/biology/3-5-cellular-respiration-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/biology/3-5-cellular-respiration-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can explain why respiration and fermentation are found in all forms of life, and what respiration is for. *(Guide: "Why cells respire")*
- I can define oxidation and reduction and explain the role of NAD⁺/NADH and FAD/FADH₂ as electron carriers. *(Guide: "Oxidation, reduction and electron carriers")*
- I can state where glycolysis, pyruvate oxidation, the Krebs cycle and the ETC happen, with the main inputs and outputs of each. *(Guide: Figure 1; Practice Q1)*
- I can explain how electron transport builds a proton gradient across the inner mitochondrial membrane, and say which side has the higher pH. *(Guide: Figure 2; Practice Q3)*
- I can explain how ATP synthase uses the proton gradient to make ATP, and why cristae increase ATP output. *(Guide: "Stage 4"; Practice Q3)*
- I can describe how prokaryotes carry out chemiosmosis across the plasma membrane, and how anaerobic prokaryotes use acceptors other than oxygen. *(Guide: "Stage 4"; Practice Q5)*
- I can explain how fermentation regenerates NAD⁺ so glycolysis continues without oxygen, and name its products. *(Guide: "Fermentation"; Practice Q2)*

## Calculation and skills

- I can construct a correctly labelled graph of respiration data, choosing the graph type and showing variability. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q4)*
- I can calculate a rate of respiration per gram per hour from a graph or table. *(Guide: Worked example 1)*
- I can convert a pH difference into a ratio of H⁺ concentrations. *(Practice Q5)*
- I can compare the energy yield of aerobic respiration and fermentation using given model values. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q7)*

## Reasoning

- I can predict, link by link, the effects of blocking the ETC, blocking ATP synthase or letting protons leak across the membrane. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q3, Q6)*
- I can explain how uncoupling in brown fat produces heat for an endotherm. *(Guide: "Uncoupling"; Practice Q6)*
- I can compare the ETC and chemiosmosis in respiration and photosynthesis. *(Guide: "Respiration and photosynthesis compared")*
