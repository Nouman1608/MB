Work through the list without notes. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/biology/8-2-energy-flow-through-ecosystems-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/biology/8-2-energy-flow-through-ecosystems-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can explain how endotherms and ectotherms control body temperature, including behavioural regulation. *(Guide: "Endotherms and ectotherms"; Practice Q4)*
- I can describe what happens to an organism with a net energy gain and with a net energy loss. *(Guide: "Energy budgets"; Practice Q5)*
- I can explain why some organisms switch between asexual and sexual reproduction as energy supply changes. *(Guide: "Reproductive strategies and energy"; Practice Q7)*
- I can put population, community, ecosystem and biome in order and define each. *(Guide: "Levels of organisation")*
- I can name the reservoirs and processes of the water, carbon, nitrogen and phosphorus cycles, and what each of the five nitrogen-cycle steps converts. *(Guide: Figure 2; Practice Q1, Q6)*
- I can explain how photosynthetic and chemosynthetic autotrophs capture energy and how heterotrophs obtain it. *(Guide: "Autotrophs" and "Heterotrophs"; Practice Q3)*

## Calculation and skills

- I can calculate net primary production and transfer efficiencies between trophic levels. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q2, Q7)*
- I can calculate an energy budget: assimilated energy, growth and net gain or loss. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q5)*
- I can read changes and trends from a data table, with units. *(Practice Q4, Q6)*

## Reasoning

- I can explain why energy flows but matter cycles, and why food chains are short. *(Guide: "Why so little energy reaches the top"; Practice Q5)*
- I can predict how less sunlight or fewer producers changes populations and the number of trophic levels. *(Guide: "Changes in energy availability"; Practice Q7)*
- I can explain why the carbon cycle depends on the nitrogen and phosphorus cycles. *(Practice Q6)*
