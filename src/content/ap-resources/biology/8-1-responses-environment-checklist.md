Work through the list without notes. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/biology/8-1-responses-environment-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/biology/8-1-responses-environment-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can tell a behavioural response from a physiological one and give an example of each, linked to an internal or external change. *(Guide: "Responses to the external and internal environment"; Practice Q1)*
- I can explain the difference between taxis and kinesis and say what evidence would distinguish them. *(Guide: Figure 1; Practice Q1)*
- I can explain the difference between innate and learned behaviour, and why selection can favour both. *(Guide: "Innate and learned behaviour")*
- I can name the five kinds of signal and give an example of each, with what the signal achieves. *(Guide: "Communication"; Practice Q6, Q7)*
- I can explain how cooperation, including kin selection, can raise individual fitness and population survival. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q5)*

## Calculation and skills

- I can identify the independent variable, dependent variable and controlled variables in a behaviour or plant-response experiment. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q4)*
- I can choose a control that differs only in the factor being tested, and justify it. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q2, Q4, Q7)*
- I can calculate χ² for choice-chamber counts, use the right degrees of freedom and state "reject" or "fail to reject". *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q3)*
- I can calculate percentages and per-individual values from behaviour data. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q5, Q6)*

## Reasoning

- I can explain how a signal leads to differential reproductive success and, if heritable, to evolution. *(Practice Q6)*
- I can explain how a plant's response to herbivores can raise its fitness. *(Practice Q7)*
- I can explain why correlational field data support but do not prove a cause, and design an experiment to test it. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q6)*
