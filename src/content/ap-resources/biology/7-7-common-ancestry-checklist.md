Work through the list without notes. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/biology/7-7-common-ancestry-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/biology/7-7-common-ancestry-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can explain what common ancestry means, and why it does not mean one living species evolved from another. *(Guide: "What common ancestry means"; Common misconceptions)*
- I can name features shared by all life and say why they do not single out eukaryotes. *(Guide: "Background: unity across all of life"; Practice Q1)*
- I can describe the membrane-bound organelles shared by eukaryotes and explain why shared mitochondria point to one endosymbiosis. *(Guide: "Membrane-bound organelles"; Practice Q2, Q5)*
- I can describe linear chromosomes with telomeres and contrast them with a circular prokaryotic chromosome. *(Guide: "Linear chromosomes", Figure 1; Practice Q3)*
- I can describe split genes and the spliceosome, and say why prokaryotes lack them. *(Guide: "Genes that contain introns"; Practice Q1)*

## Skills

- I can read a gene-structure diagram and identify which intron positions are shared. *(Guide: Figure 2, Worked example 1)*
- I can calculate growth as a percentage of a control and use it to compare groups. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q4)*
- I can analyse intron data from several organisms and describe the pattern. *(Practice Q4)*

## Reasoning

- I can tell homologous features from features that only share a function. *(Guide: "Why shared features point to one ancestor"; Practice Q7)*
- I can argue that several shared, complex features are best explained by one ancestor, and evaluate a claim of separate origins. *(Practice Q5, Q7)*
- I can explain why intron loss or a missing organelle does not rule out common ancestry. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q2, Q4)*
- I can predict the effects of disrupting splicing or telomere maintenance on eukaryotes and on prokaryotes. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q3, Q6)*
