Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/biology/5-2-meiosis-genetic-diversity-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/biology/5-2-meiosis-genetic-diversity-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can explain how correct separation in meiosis I and II gives every gamete one haploid set that mixes maternal and paternal chromosomes. *(Guide: "Correct separation")*
- I can describe crossing over: when it happens, which chromatids take part and what a chiasma is. *(Guide: "Crossing over", Figure 1; Practice Q1)*
- I can explain why crossing over makes new combinations of alleles but not new alleles, and why it makes sister chromatids differ. *(Guide: "Crossing over"; Practice Q4)*
- I can explain independent assortment in terms of how homologous pairs line up at metaphase I. *(Guide: Figure 2)*
- I can explain how random fertilization adds to variation as well as restoring the diploid number. *(Guide: "Random fertilization")*

## Calculation and skills

- I can use 2ⁿ to find the number of chromosome combinations in gametes, and multiply for offspring of two parents. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q2, Q7)*
- I can calculate the probability that a gamete carries only maternal (or only paternal) chromosomes. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q7)*
- I can predict the chromosome numbers of gametes and zygotes after nondisjunction in meiosis I or meiosis II. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q3, Q6)*
- I can list the chromatids produced by a crossover at a given point and identify the recombinants. *(Practice Q4)*

## Reasoning

- I can pose a testable question from an observation or data set about meiosis or variation, and name the variables to test it. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q5)*
- I can explain why genetically varied offspring may survive changing conditions better than clones, and evaluate a claim with data. *(Guide: "Why the four cells are not the same"; Practice Q5)*
- I can explain how DNA markers could show whether an extra chromosome came from an error in meiosis I or meiosis II. *(Practice Q6)*
