Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/biology/7-5-hardy-weinberg-equilibrium-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/biology/7-5-hardy-weinberg-equilibrium-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can explain what Hardy–Weinberg equilibrium describes and why it represents a population that is not evolving. *(Guide: "A model of a population that is not changing")*
- I can list the five conditions and name the evolutionary force each one rules out. *(Guide: "The five conditions"; Practice Q2)*
- I can explain why the conditions are never all met, and why the model is still useful as a null hypothesis. *(Guide: "The five conditions")*
- I can explain where p², 2pq and q² come from using random union of gametes. *(Guide: Figure 1)*
- I can explain why inbreeding or self-fertilisation changes genotype frequencies but not, by itself, allele frequencies. *(Guide: "The five conditions"; Practice Q6)*

## Calculation and skills

- I can calculate p and q by counting alleles from genotype numbers. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q3, Q6)*
- I can find q from the frequency of a recessive phenotype, and then p, the carrier frequency and expected numbers. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q1, Q7)*
- I can calculate expected genotype numbers and run a chi-square test with the correct degrees of freedom. *(Guide: Worked example 3; Practice Q5)*
- I can describe a graph of allele frequency over generations using specific values. *(Guide: "Graphs of allele frequency over time"; Practice Q4)*

## Reasoning

- I can decide which condition is most likely broken from a description or a graph, and justify the choice. *(Practice Q2, Q4)*
- I can state a correct conclusion after a chi-square test, saying "suggests" rather than "proves". *(Guide: Worked example 3; Practice Q5)*
- I can explain why selection removes a rare recessive allele slowly. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q7)*
- I can explain when √(q²) gives a misleading answer. *(Guide: "Two routes to allele frequencies"; Practice Q7)*
