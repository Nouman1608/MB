Work through the list without notes. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/biology/2-3-plasma-membrane-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/biology/2-3-plasma-membrane-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can explain why phospholipids form a bilayer with polar heads facing the fluid on both sides and nonpolar tails facing each other. *(Guide: "Phospholipids: heads out, tails in"; Practice Q1)*
- I can explain why the bilayer forms and stays together without the cell spending energy. *(Guide: "Phospholipids: heads out, tails in"; Practice Q7)*
- I can predict which regions of a membrane protein are hydrophobic and which are hydrophilic from where they sit, including the lining of a pore. *(Guide: "Membrane proteins"; Practice Q2)*
- I can tell integral proteins from peripheral proteins and explain how each is held in the membrane. *(Guide: "Membrane proteins"; Practice Q5)*
- I can name the components of the fluid mosaic model and describe the role of each in keeping the internal environment of the cell steady. *(Guide: "What each component does for the cell"; Practice Q4)*
- I can explain what "fluid" and "mosaic" each mean in the fluid mosaic model. *(Guide: "The fluid mosaic model"; Practice Q7)*

## Calculation and skills

- I can label a membrane diagram, decide which side is the outside of the cell and justify it. *(Guide: "Reading membrane diagrams"; Practice Q4)*
- I can calculate the length of an α-helix from its number of amino acids and judge whether it can span the membrane. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q3)*
- I can calculate the percentage of fluorescence recovered after photobleaching, and a mean and percentage change from repeats. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q6)*

## Reasoning

- I can use photobleaching data to argue that membrane proteins move sideways, and explain why some recover less. *(Guide: Worked example 2)*
- I can explain the purpose of a control in a membrane experiment and propose a better one. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q6)*
- I can describe what a membrane diagram or model leaves out, and correct errors in a student's model. *(Guide: "Reading membrane diagrams"; Practice Q4, Q7)*
