Work through the list without notes. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/biology/3-1-enzymes-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/biology/3-1-enzymes-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can state that enzymes are proteins that act as biological catalysts and are not used up. *(Guide: "Why cells need catalysts"; Practice Q1)*
- I can explain how an enzyme speeds up a reaction by lowering the activation energy. *(Guide: "Activation energy"; Practice Q1)*
- I can draw and label an energy profile with and without an enzyme, showing that ΔG does not change. *(Guide: Figure 1; Practice Q2)*
- I can describe the active site and explain why the substrate must match it in shape **and** in charge. *(Guide: "Enzyme structure: the active site"; Practice Q3)*
- I can describe the steps of the enzyme–substrate complex model, E + S → ES → E + P. *(Guide: Figure 2)*
- I can explain how controlling enzymes lets a cell regulate its reactions. *(Guide: "Enzymes regulate what happens in a cell")*

## Calculation and skills

- I can calculate an initial rate and a later rate from product–time data, with units. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q4)*
- I can use 1 ÷ time as a relative rate when an end point is timed. *(Guide: Worked example 2)*
- I can convert between mmol, nmol and mol to compare catalysed and uncatalysed rates. *(Practice Q7)*

## Reasoning

- I can explain why the rate falls during a reaction (substrate used up, not enzyme). *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q4)*
- I can identify the independent and dependent variables in an enzyme experiment and justify negative and positive controls. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q5)*
- I can predict how changing a charged R group in the active site affects binding and activity. *(Practice Q6)*
