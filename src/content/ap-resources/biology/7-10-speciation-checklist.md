Work through the list without notes. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/biology/7-10-speciation-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/biology/7-10-speciation-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can state the biological species concept, explain why reproductive isolation is the key to speciation, and give two limits of the concept. *(Guide: "What is a species?")*
- I can name the five pre-zygotic and three post-zygotic barriers and give an example of each. *(Guide: "Reproductive barriers"; Practice Q1, Q5)*
- I can explain why a mule is sterile using chromosome numbers. *(Guide: "Why is a mule sterile?")*
- I can compare allopatric and sympatric speciation and describe three ways gene flow can be cut without a geographic barrier. *(Guide: Figure 1; Practice Q2)*
- I can contrast gradualism with punctuated equilibrium and recognise each in fossil data. *(Guide: Figure 2; Practice Q3)*
- I can explain divergent evolution and why speciation is fast during adaptive radiation, and contrast it with convergent evolution. *(Guide: "How fast does speciation happen?"; Practice Q6)*

## Calculation and skills

- I can use chromosome counts to explain why a polyploid plant is isolated from its parents. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q4)*
- I can calculate percentages from mating, fertilization or hybrid data and compare them with random mating. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q5, Q7)*
- I can calculate and compare rates from species-count data. *(Practice Q6)*

## Reasoning

- I can decide from data whether two populations are fully separate species or still exchanging some genes. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q7)*
- I can predict how a change in the environment that removes a barrier affects gene flow and the species boundary. *(Guide: Worked example 2 (d); Practice Q5, Q7)*
- I can explain why similar traits in unrelated lineages are evidence of similar selection pressures, not of close relationship. *(Guide: "Convergent evolution"; Practice Q6)*
