Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/biology/7-3-artificial-selection-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/biology/7-3-artificial-selection-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can define artificial selection and state the three conditions it needs: variation, heritability and differential reproduction. *(Guide: "Humans as the selecting force")*
- I can explain how artificial selection differs from natural selection, genetic engineering and non-heritable change. *(Guide: comparison table; Practice Q1)*
- I can explain why breeders can only select from heritable variation that already exists, and name the sources of new variation. *(Guide: "Humans as the selecting force"; Practice Q2)*
- I can explain why selection shifts the mean of a trait but reduces variation within the selected population, while increasing differences between breeds. *(Guide: "What selection does to a population"; Figure 2)*
- I can give examples of species changed by artificial selection, such as the vegetables bred from wild cabbage. *(Guide: "One wild plant, many vegetables")*

## Skills

- I can describe selection data from a table or graph, quoting specific values and describing trends. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q3, Q4)*
- I can calculate a change in mean, a percentage change and a change per generation. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q4)*
- I can read a frequency distribution and say how both its position and its spread changed. *(Guide: Figure 2)*

## Reasoning

- I can explain why an unselected control line is needed before a change is put down to selection. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q4)*
- I can explain why genetically uniform crops or herds are vulnerable to disease, and how breeders can restore variation. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q5)*
- I can explain why a selection response slows to a plateau, and why crossing with another population can restart it. *(Practice Q2, Q7)*
- I can evaluate whether data show a genetic response to human-caused selection or a non-heritable effect of the environment. *(Practice Q6)*
