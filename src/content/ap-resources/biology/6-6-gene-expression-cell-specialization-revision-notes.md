Short on time? This page is the recap. For explanations, Figures 1 and 2 and worked examples, use the [full study guide](/advanced-course-resources/biology/6-6-gene-expression-cell-specialization-study-guide/).

## Recap

- Nearly all cells carry the same genome. **Differential gene expression** gives each cell type its own proteins, structure and function.
- **General transcription factors** and **RNA polymerase** assemble at the **promoter** (often with a TATA box). Alone, they give a low, basal rate.
- **Activators** bind **enhancers**. Enhancers can be far **upstream or downstream** of the start site, even in introns. DNA loops so activators reach the promoter complex through mediator proteins.
- **Repressors** (negative regulators) bind DNA, for example at **silencers**, and block transcription.
- Each gene needs a **combination** of activators. Each cell type has its own set of activators, so it expresses its own set of genes.
- **miRNAs** (from hairpin RNA) and **siRNAs** (from double-stranded RNA), about 21–23 nucleotides long, base-pair with mRNA. Result: less translation, mRNA breakdown, or both. DNA unchanged.

## Key relationships

| DNA sequence | What binds | Effect on transcription |
|---|---|---|
| promoter | general transcription factors, RNA polymerase | needed to start at all |
| enhancer | activators | raises the rate |
| silencer | repressors | lowers the rate or blocks it |

| Experiment result | Conclusion |
|---|---|
| deleting a region **lowers** reporter output | region is (or contains) an enhancer |
| deleting a region **raises** reporter output | region is (or contains) a silencer |
| blocking a miRNA raises protein **more** than mRNA | the miRNA also blocks translation |

## Assumptions

- Reporter genes in cultured cells show what a control region can do, not always what happens on the chromosome.
- Data in % are relative to a stated reference, such as the full-length construct.

## Mistakes to avoid

1. Saying different cell types have different genes.
2. Saying activators bind the promoter, or that enhancers must be upstream.
3. Saying miRNAs change DNA or stop transcription.
4. Assuming unchanged mRNA means unchanged protein.

## Quick self-check

1. A full-length construct gives 900 units; with region Q deleted it gives 45. What percentage remains, and what is Q? *(45 ÷ 900 × 100 = 5.0%; Q is an enhancer, or contains one.)*
2. Why can an enhancer 10 000 base pairs away still affect its gene? *(The DNA bends into a loop, bringing bound activators into contact with the promoter complex.)*
3. Cell type 1 has activators A and B; cell type 2 has only A. A gene needs A and B. Which cell expresses it? *(Cell type 1 only.)*
