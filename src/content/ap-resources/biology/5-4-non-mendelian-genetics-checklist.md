Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/biology/5-4-non-mendelian-genetics-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/biology/5-4-non-mendelian-genetics-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can distinguish incomplete dominance from codominance, with an example of each. *(Guide: "Incomplete dominance and codominance"; Practice Q7)*
- I can explain why linked genes do not assort independently, and how crossing over produces recombinants. *(Guide: "Linked genes and gene mapping"; Practice Q5)*
- I can explain why X-linked recessive traits are more common in XY individuals, and why an affected father cannot pass one to his son. *(Guide: "Sex-linked traits"; Practice Q2)*
- I can describe ZW sex determination in birds and haplodiploidy in bees, and predict which sex shows a Z-linked recessive trait more often. *(Guide: "Other sex-determination systems"; Practice Q6)*
- I can explain pleiotropy and tell it apart from linkage using cross data. *(Guide: "Pleiotropy"; Practice Q1, Q7)*
- I can explain why mitochondrial and chloroplast traits are typically inherited from the mother and do not follow Mendel's ratios. *(Guide: "Non-nuclear inheritance"; Practice Q3)*

## Calculation and skills

- I can calculate recombination frequency from test-cross data and convert it to map units. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q5)*
- I can order three genes from their pairwise map distances. *(Guide: Figure 1; Practice Q4)*
- I can use a Punnett square to predict sons and daughters in an X-linked or Z-linked cross. *(Guide: Figure 2, Worked example 3; Practice Q2, Q6)*
- I can use chi-square to test a 1 : 2 : 1 or 1 : 1 : 1 : 1 hypothesis and state the conclusion correctly. *(Guide: Worked examples 1 and 2; Practice Q5, Q7)*

## Reasoning

- I can name the non-Mendelian pattern that best explains a data set, and support it with a specific clue. *(Guide: "When the ratios do not fit"; Practice Q1, Q3)*
- I can use reciprocal crosses to tell organelle inheritance from nuclear inheritance. *(Guide: "Non-nuclear inheritance"; Practice Q3)*
- I can predict how changing the arrangement of alleles in a parent changes which classes are parental, and explain why map distance stays the same. *(Practice Q5)*
