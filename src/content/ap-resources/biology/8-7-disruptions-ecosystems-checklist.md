Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/biology/8-7-disruptions-ecosystems-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/biology/8-7-disruptions-ecosystems-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can define an adaptation and explain why it gives an advantage only in a particular environment. *(Guide: "Adaptations come from variation that already exists"; Practice Q1)*
- I can explain why mutations are not caused by the environment, and describe how a new selective pressure changes allele frequencies. *(Guide: "Variation meets a changing environment"; Practice Q1)*
- I can define heterozygote advantage and identify it from relative fitness values. *(Guide: "Heterozygote advantage"; Practice Q2)*
- I can explain how an invasive species spreads and harms native species, using one real example. *(Guide: "Invasive species"; Practice Q3)*
- I can describe how logging, urbanisation, monocropping, introduced diseases and climate change disrupt ecosystems and can cause extinctions. *(Guide: "Human activities speed up change")*
- I can explain how a geological or meteorological event, such as El Niño, continental drift or a meteor impact, changes habitats and species distributions. *(Guide: "Geological and meteorological events"; Practice Q7)*

## Calculation and skills

- I can calculate concentration factors along a food chain and explain biomagnification. *(Guide: Worked example 1, Figure 1; Practice Q4)*
- I can read a logarithmic axis, where each grid line is ten times the one below. *(Guide: Figure 1)*
- I can calculate a percentage change and a mean from ecological data. *(Practice Q5, Q7)*
- I can carry out a chi-square test: null hypothesis, expected counts, χ², degrees of freedom and critical value. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q6)*

## Reasoning

- I can explain the full chain of eutrophication, including why oxygen falls after the algal bloom peaks. *(Guide: Figure 2; Practice Q5)*
- I can state a conclusion as "reject" or "fail to reject" the null hypothesis, and say what the test does and does not show. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q6)*
- I can predict the effect of a disruption on a population and explain why low genetic diversity raises the risk of extinction. *(Practice Q7)*
