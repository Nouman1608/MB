Work through the list without notes. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/biology/7-2-natural-selection-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/biology/7-2-natural-selection-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can explain why natural selection acts on phenotypes, and how genotypes are affected only through the phenotypes they produce. *(Guide: "Selection acts on phenotypes")*
- I can tell heritable from environmental variation, and explain why only heritable variation changes the next generation. *(Guide: "Heritable and non-heritable variation"; Practice Q1)*
- I can define a selective pressure and give biotic and abiotic examples from a changing environment. *(Guide: "Environments change and apply selective pressures"; Practice Q2, Q5)*
- I can explain why sickle-cell trait raises fitness where malaria is common but not where it is absent. *(Guide: "The same trait can help or harm", Worked example 1)*
- I can explain how insecticide or herbicide resistance spreads, and why the chemical does not cause the resistance. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q2)*
- I can explain how differences in the types or numbers of molecules in cells, such as enzyme variants, membrane lipids or extra gene copies, affect fitness in a given environment. *(Guide: "Variation in molecules affects fitness"; Practice Q3, Q4, Q5)*

## Calculation and skills

- I can calculate relative fitness for each phenotype in each of two environments. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q6)*
- I can calculate the percentage of a phenotype after a round of selection. *(Guide: Worked example 2)*
- I can read optima and ratios from a graph of enzyme activity against temperature. *(Practice Q4)*
- I can calculate a seed-weighted (or offspring-weighted) mean to show how selection shifts a trait. *(Practice Q7)*

## Reasoning

- I can state the full chain from molecule to cell function to phenotype to survival and reproduction. *(Guide: "Variation in molecules affects fitness"; Practice Q5)*
- I can evaluate a claim that a trait is "an advantage" by asking in which environment, using data. *(Practice Q6)*
- I can explain why a costly resistance trait may decline when the selective pressure is removed. *(Guide: Worked example 2, Figure 2; Practice Q5)*
