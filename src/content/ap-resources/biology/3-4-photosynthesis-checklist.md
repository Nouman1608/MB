## Understanding

- I can write the overall equation for photosynthesis and say what happens to the sugar made. *(Guide: "Capturing light energy")*
- I can label the stroma, thylakoids, thylakoid space and grana, and say which stage happens where. *(Guide: Figure 1; Practice Q1)*
- I can trace electrons from water through photosystem II, the electron transport chain and photosystem I to NADPH. *(Guide: "The light reactions", Figure 2)*
- I can explain where the O₂ released comes from. *(Guide: step 2; Practice Q2)*
- I can explain how redox reactions in the ETC build a proton gradient, and on which side of the thylakoid membrane H⁺ is high. *(Guide: steps 3 and 6; Practice Q1)*
- I can explain how ATP synthase uses the gradient to make ATP (photophosphorylation). *(Guide: step 6; Practice Q3)*
- I can explain how ATP and NADPH power the Calvin cycle, and why the two stages depend on each other. *(Guide: "The Calvin cycle")*
- I can state that ETCs also occur in mitochondria and prokaryotic plasma membranes. *(Guide: "The light reactions")*

## Calculation and skills

- I can calculate masses of CO₂, water, glucose and O₂ from the equation. *(Guide: Worked example 1; Practice Q4)*
- I can turn a pH difference into a ratio of H⁺ concentrations. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q7)*
- I can read a light-response graph and explain its shape. *(Practice Q5)*

## Reasoning

- I can predict the effects of an inhibitor or mutation on O₂, ATP, NADPH and sugar production. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q3, Q7)*
- I can use evidence to argue that photosynthesis began in prokaryotes, that cyanobacteria oxygenated the atmosphere and that chloroplasts came from cyanobacteria. *(Guide: "Where photosynthesis came from"; Practice Q6)*
