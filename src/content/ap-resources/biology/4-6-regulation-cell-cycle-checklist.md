Work through the list without notes. If you cannot do a statement, follow its link, then come back. Guide = [study guide](/advanced-course-resources/biology/4-6-regulation-cell-cycle-study-guide/); Practice = [practice questions](/advanced-course-resources/biology/4-6-regulation-cell-cycle-practice/).

## Understanding

- I can name the G1, G2 and M checkpoints, say where each sits in the cycle and what each one checks. *(Guide: Figure 1 and the checkpoint table; Practice Q1)*
- I can list what may happen to a cell that does not pass a checkpoint: pause for repair, enter G0, or undergo apoptosis. *(Guide: "Checkpoints: stop or go"; Practice Q3)*
- I can explain how a cyclin switches on a CDK, and what the active complex does to target proteins. *(Guide: "Two proteins, two jobs"; Practice Q2)*
- I can describe apoptosis and give two of its roles in the body. *(Guide: "Apoptosis: programmed cell death")*
- I can explain how mutations that overactivate "drive" proteins or disable "stop" proteins can lead to cancer. *(Guide: "When control fails: cancer"; Practice Q6)*

## Calculation and skills

- I can read a graph of cyclin concentration, CDK amount and CDK activity, and calculate the rate at which cyclin builds up. *(Guide: Figure 2, Worked example 1)*
- I can calculate percentages and percentage changes from cell-cycle data and quote them as evidence. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q4, Q6)*
- I can use a simple model to show how the balance between division and apoptosis controls cell numbers. *(Practice Q7)*

## Reasoning

- I can predict, link by link, the effect of a cyclin that is never made or never broken down. *(Guide: Worked example 1)*
- I can predict the effect of a broken M checkpoint on the chromosome numbers of daughter cells. *(Practice Q5)*
- I can explain why losing a DNA-damage checkpoint raises the risk of cancer, using experimental data. *(Guide: Worked example 2; Practice Q3)*
- I can evaluate a claim that a single mutation "causes" cancer, and explain why several failures usually act together. *(Practice Q6)*
