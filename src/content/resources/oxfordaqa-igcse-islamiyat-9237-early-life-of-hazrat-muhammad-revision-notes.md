**These notes condense content area 3.2.1.2 of 9237: "The birth and upbringing of Hazrat Muhammad (pbuh), his parents and the role of Abdul Mutalib, Abu Talib and Bahira", "Hazrat Muhammad's (pbuh) marriage to Hazrat Khadija (RA), and his business dealings" and "The key qualities of Hazrat Muhammad's (pbuh) character including courage, bravery, treatment of the poor, earning an honest living and fair dealings."** The full account is in the [early life study guide](/resources/oxfordaqa-igcse-islamiyat-9237-early-life-of-hazrat-muhammad/).

## Timeline

| Age | Event |
|---|---|
| Before birth | Father Abdullah dies |
| Birth, about 570 CE | Year of the Elephant; Makkah; clan of Banu Hashim |
| Infancy | With the wet-nurse Halimah al-Sa'diyyah in the desert |
| About 6 | Mother Aminah dies at Abwa |
| About 6 to 8 | In the care of grandfather Abdul Mutalib |
| About 8 | Abdul Mutalib dies; uncle Abu Talib becomes guardian |
| About 12 | Caravan to Syria; meets the monk Bahira |
| Youth | Shepherd; joins the Hilf al-Fudul |
| About 25 | Trades for Khadija (RA); marries her (about 595 CE) |
| About 35 | Settles the Black Stone dispute (about 605 CE) |
| About 40 | Call to prophethood (610 CE) |

## Key people

| Person | Who | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Abdullah | Father | Died before the Prophet (pbuh) was born |
| Aminah | Mother | Raised him until her death at Abwa |
| Halimah | Wet-nurse | Cared for him in the desert; Banu Sa'd |
| Abdul Mutalib | Grandfather | Chief of Makkah; kept the Zamzam well; gave him honour; died when he was about eight |
| Abu Talib | Uncle | Guardian; took him to Syria; protected him; never accepted Islam |
| Bahira | Monk of Busra | Recognised signs of prophethood; warned Abu Talib to protect the boy |
| Khadija (RA) | Wife | Wealthy trader; hired him; proposed; first believer |
| Maysarah | Khadija's servant | Travelled with him and reported on his honesty |

## What each guardian gave him

- **Halimah:** health, clear Arabic, desert strength.
- **Abdul Mutalib:** status, closeness to the Ka'aba, an example of leadership.
- **Abu Talib:** a home, protection, early experience of trade.
- **Bahira:** an outside sign that he was special (a tradition, not stated in the Qur'an).

## Orphanhood

He lost both parents and then his grandfather. Muslims link this with his later care for orphans, widows and the weak.

## Work and business dealings

- Shepherd in youth: patience and care for the weak.
- Merchant: honest, fair weights and prices, kept promises.
- Known as **al-Amin** (the trustworthy) and **as-Sadiq** (the truthful).
- Khadija's trip to Syria: a good profit by honest means; Maysarah praised his manners.

## Marriage to Khadija (RA)

- Khadija: wealthy widow, about forty; he was about twenty-five.
- She proposed after hearing of his honesty.
- Married about 595 CE; lasted twenty-five years.
- Children: Qasim and Abdullah (died young), Zainab, Ruqayyah, Umm Kulthum, Fatimah.
- She was his first and strongest supporter.

## The Black Stone

The clans rebuilt the Ka'aba and argued over who should lift the Black Stone. The first man to enter, Muhammad (pbuh), put the stone on a cloak, and a leader from each clan held a corner. The dispute ended peacefully. It shows **wisdom, fairness and trust**.

## Character: qualities and evidence

| Quality | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Courage and bravery | Joined the Hilf al-Fudul; steady under threat |
| Treatment of the poor | Helped orphans and widows; generous; lived simply |
| Earning an honest living | Shepherd and merchant; never begged |
| Fair dealings | Al-Amin; honest weights; kept promises |
| Truthfulness | As-Sadiq; people left valuables with him |
| Wisdom | Black Stone |

## Using the prescribed passage, Qur'an 68:4

| Words | Use for |
|---|---|
| "you [O Muhammad]" | Allah speaks directly to him; a personal honour |
| "are of" | A settled trait, not a passing mood |
| "a great moral character" | His character is the model for believers |

**Linked verse:** 10:16, "I have remained among you a lifetime before it": his people knew his honesty for forty years.

## Lessons from his early life

- **Honesty in work:** earning an honest living is honourable.
- **Care for orphans:** he knew the pain of losing parents.
- **Peace-making:** the Black Stone dispute was settled without bloodshed.
- **Loyalty to the weak:** the Hilf al-Fudul pact defended wronged people.
- **Preparation:** forty years of trusted character made his later claim believable (10:16).

## How to build a 4-mark "explain" answer

1. Name the person or event.
2. Say what happened.
3. Explain the effect on his life or character.

**Mini example (Khadija, RA):** "Khadija hired him to trade because she had heard of his honesty [1]. His success and his honest dealing led to their marriage [1]."

## Exam traps

- Guardians in order: **Aminah, Abdul Mutalib, Abu Talib**.
- Abu Talib protected the Prophet (pbuh) but **did not accept Islam**.
- Bahira's story is a **tradition**, not a Qur'an verse.
- The marriage came **before** the call to prophethood by about fifteen years.
- Give a **reason or example** for each quality.
- Do not confuse Abdullah (father) with Abdullah (son who died young).

## Self-test

1. In which year was the Prophet (pbuh) born, and what is it called?
2. Which clan did he belong to?
3. Who was his wet-nurse?
4. Who looked after him after his mother died?
5. What was Abu Talib's relationship to him?
6. Who was Bahira?
7. Why was Khadija (RA) impressed by Muhammad (pbuh)?
8. What two titles did the Makkans give him?
9. How did he end the dispute about the Black Stone?
10. Which verse is the prescribed passage for his character?

**Answers:** 1. About 570 CE; the Year of the Elephant. 2. Banu Hashim of the Quraysh. 3. Halimah al-Sa'diyyah. 4. His grandfather Abdul Mutalib. 5. His uncle and later guardian. 6. A Christian monk of Busra who recognised signs of prophethood. 7. Because of his honesty and fair dealings in trade. 8. Al-Amin and as-Sadiq. 9. He placed the stone on a cloak and let the leaders of each clan lift it together. 10. Qur'an 68:4.
