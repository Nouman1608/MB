## Ulbricht and Honecker compared

| | Ulbricht (to 1971) | Honecker (1971–89) |
|---|---|---|
| Economy | NES and ESS: incentives and planning reform | Consumer goods, housing, subsidies; borrowing from the West |
| Relations with FRG | Hostile; built the Wall | Ostpolitik agreements; 1987 visit to Bonn |
| Identity | One Germany under socialism | Separate GDR identity (1974 constitution) |
| Weakness | Tension with Moscow over reforms | Rising debt; refused Gorbachev's reforms |

## Methods of control

| Method | Examples |
|---|---|
| Stasi | Full-time officers and informers; surveillance, mail, phone taps |
| Youth | Young Pioneers, FDJ, political education |
| Church | Pressure, then 1978 agreement; churches later sheltered opposition |
| Media | Censored press, radio and TV; Western TV still widely watched |
| Welfare | Jobs, cheap housing and subsidies to win acceptance |

## Worked example: describe two features

*Describe two features of security at the Berlin Wall.* (6 marks)

1. A **death strip** between inner and outer walls, with raked sand, lights and alarms, made escape
   attempts easy to spot.
2. **Armed border guards** in watchtowers had orders to stop escapers, and many people were shot dead
   trying to cross, such as Peter Fechter in 1962.

## Why did the GDR collapse? Factors to weigh

| Factor | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Gorbachev | Reforms encouraged protest; no Soviet intervention; October 1989 visit |
| Hungary and the exodus | Border opened to Austria in September 1989; embassy refugees |
| Mass peaceful protest | Leipzig Monday demonstrations; "We are the people" |
| Economic failure | Debt, subsidies, shortages, poor-quality goods |
| SED weakness | Honecker's removal; Krenz's confusion; accidental opening of the Wall |
| West Germany | Attraction of the deutschmark; Kohl's push for unity in 1990 |

## Common mistakes and exam traps

- **Dating the Wall wrongly.** It went up in August 1961, not 1958 (the ultimatum).
- **Treating Ostpolitik as reunification policy.** It accepted two states and improved contact.
- **Ignoring support for the regime.** Welfare and security did win some acceptance.
- **Forgetting Hungary.** The opening of the Hungarian–Austrian border triggered the 1989 exodus.
- **Using Western or Stasi sources without provenance.** Each has a clear purpose; use it in
  cross-referencing.

## Frequently asked questions

**Why did the GDR's economy struggle in the 1980s?**
Subsidies and welfare cost more than the economy
produced, debt to the West grew and goods were scarce and poor.

**Why did the Wall open on 9 November 1989?**
A hastily announced change to travel rules led crowds to the
crossings, and guards, without clear orders, let them through.

**Why did reunification happen so quickly?**
East Germans voted for parties promising unity, the
economy collapsed, and the four wartime Allies agreed in the Two Plus Four Treaty.
