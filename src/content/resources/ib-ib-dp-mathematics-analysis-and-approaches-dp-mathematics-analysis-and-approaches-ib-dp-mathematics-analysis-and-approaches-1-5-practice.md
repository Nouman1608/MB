This set covers **syllabus section 1.5** of IB DP Mathematics: Analysis and Approaches (first assessment 2021): laws of exponents with integer exponents, the meaning of log and ln, and evaluating logarithms with technology. It is common content for SL and HL. Questions are labelled "(calculator-free)" for Paper 1 style or "(calculator allowed)" for Paper 2 style. Give answers exactly or to 3 significant figures unless told otherwise.

## Section A

**1.** (calculator-free) Simplify each expression, giving answers with positive exponents.

**(a)** x⁷ × x⁻³ **[1]**
**(b)** (2y⁻²)³ **[1]**
**(c)** 12p⁵q⁻² ÷ (3p⁻¹q³) **[2]**

**2.** (calculator-free) Evaluate, giving exact answers.

**(a)** 4⁻² **[1]**
**(b)** (3/5)⁻² **[1]**
**(c)** (−2)⁻³ **[1]**

**3.** (calculator-free) Write each expression in the form axⁿ, or as a sum of such terms.

**(a)** 7/(2x⁴) **[1]**
**(b)** 1/(5x)² **[1]**
**(c)** (9x⁶ + 6x²)/(3x³) **[2]**

**4.** (calculator-free) Write down the value of each of the following.

**(a)** log 100 000 **[1]**
**(b)** ln 1 **[1]**
**(c)** log 0.0001 **[1]**
**(d)** e^(ln 9) **[1]**

**5.** (calculator-free)

**(a)** Write the equation e^(2y) = x in logarithmic form, and hence express y in terms of x. **[2]**
**(b)** Write the equation log a = 3b in exponential form. **[1]**

**6.** (calculator-free) Solve 3^(x + 2) = 1/27. **[2]**

## Section B

**7.** (calculator-free) Show that (2x⁻¹)³ × (x²/4)² ≡ x/2, for x ≠ 0. **[3]**

**8.** (calculator-free) Simplify (x⁻¹ + y⁻¹) ÷ (xy)⁻¹, where x, y ≠ 0. **[3]**

**9.** (calculator-free) Show that (5^(n + 2) − 5ⁿ)/(4 × 5ⁿ) = 6 for every integer n. **[3]**

**10.** (calculator allowed)

**(a)** Solve 10^x = 0.35. **[2]**
**(b)** Solve 2e^(3x) − 5 = 9. **[3]**

**11.** (calculator allowed) The temperature T °C of a cup of tea t minutes after it is poured is modelled by T = 20 + 70e^(−0.05t).

**(a)** Write down the temperature of the tea when it is poured. **[1]**
**(b)** Find the time taken for the tea to cool to 50 °C. **[3]**
**(c)** Explain why, according to the model, the temperature never falls to 20 °C. **[2]**

**12.** The pH of a solution is given by pH = −log h, where h is the hydrogen ion concentration in mol dm⁻³.

**(a)** (calculator-free) A solution has h = 3.2 × 10⁻⁴. Without a calculator, explain why its pH lies between 3 and 4. **[2]**
**(b)** (calculator allowed) Find the pH of this solution. **[2]**
**(c)** (calculator allowed) Find h for a solution with pH 8.1. **[2]**

**13.** (calculator allowed) An investment of 2000 dollars has value V dollars after t years, where V = 2000e^(0.035t).

**(a)** Find the value after 10 years, to the nearest dollar. **[2]**
**(b)** Find the time it takes for the value to reach 5000 dollars. **[3]**
**(c)** Show that the time taken for the value to double is (ln 2)/0.035 years, and evaluate it. **[2]**

## Answers

**1. (a)** x^(7 + (−3)) = **x⁴** [1]
**(b)** 2³y⁻⁶ = **8/y⁶** [1]
**(c)** (12/3)p^(5 − (−1))q^(−2 − 3) = 4p⁶q⁻⁵ [1] = **4p⁶/q⁵** [1]

**2. (a)** 1/4² = **1/16** [1]
**(b)** (5/3)² = **25/9** [1]
**(c)** 1/(−2)³ = **−1/8** [1]

**3. (a)** **(7/2)x⁻⁴** [1]
**(b)** 1/(25x²) = **(1/25)x⁻²** [1]
**(c)** 9x⁶/(3x³) + 6x²/(3x³) [1] = **3x³ + 2x⁻¹** [1]

**4. (a)** **5** [1]
**(b)** **0** [1]
**(c)** 0.0001 = 10⁻⁴, so **−4** [1]
**(d)** **9**, since e^x and ln x are inverse functions [1]

**5. (a)** 2y = ln x [1], so **y = (ln x)/2** [1]
**(b)** **a = 10^(3b)** [1]

**6.** 1/27 = 3⁻³, so x + 2 = −3 [1], giving **x = −5** [1]

**7.** (2x⁻¹)³ = 8x⁻³ [1]. (x²/4)² = x⁴/16 [1]. Product = (8/16)x^(−3 + 4) = x/2 = RHS [1]
*Examiner insight:* Start from the left-hand side and finish at the right-hand side; do not write the identity at the top and manipulate both sides.

**8.** (xy)⁻¹ = 1/(xy), so dividing by it means multiplying by xy [1]. (1/x + 1/y) × xy = y + x [2], so the answer is **x + y**.
*Examiner insight:* x⁻¹ + y⁻¹ is not (x + y)⁻¹. The exponent laws never combine a sum.

**9.** 5^(n + 2) = 25 × 5ⁿ [1]. So the numerator is 5ⁿ(25 − 1) = 24 × 5ⁿ [1]. Then 24 × 5ⁿ/(4 × 5ⁿ) = 6 [1], which does not depend on n.
*Examiner insight:* Factorising out the common power 5ⁿ is the key step. Subtracting exponents in 5^(n + 2) − 5ⁿ is not a valid move.

**10. (a)** x = log 0.35 [1] = **−0.456** (3 s.f.) [1]
**(b)** 2e^(3x) = 14, so e^(3x) = 7 [1]. 3x = ln 7 [1], so x = (ln 7)/3 = **0.649** (3 s.f.) [1]

**11. (a)** t = 0: T = 20 + 70 = **90 °C** [1]
**(b)** 70e^(−0.05t) = 30, so e^(−0.05t) = 3/7 [1]. −0.05t = ln(3/7) [1], so t = −ln(3/7)/0.05 = **16.9 minutes** (3 s.f.) [1]
**(c)** e^(−0.05t) > 0 for all t, so 70e^(−0.05t) > 0 [1]. Hence T > 20 for all t; the temperature approaches 20 °C but never reaches it [1]

**12. (a)** 10⁻⁴ < 3.2 × 10⁻⁴ < 10⁻³, so −4 < log h < −3 [1]. Multiplying by −1 reverses the inequalities: 3 < pH < 4 [1]
**(b)** pH = −log(3.2 × 10⁻⁴) [1] = **3.49** (3 s.f.) [1]
**(c)** log h = −8.1 [1], so h = 10⁻⁸·¹ = **7.94 × 10⁻⁹ mol dm⁻³** (3 s.f.) [1]

**13. (a)** V = 2000e^(0.35) [1] = **2838 dollars** [1]
**(b)** 2000e^(0.035t) = 5000, so e^(0.035t) = 2.5 [1]. 0.035t = ln 2.5 [1], so t = **26.2 years** (3 s.f.) [1]
**(c)** Doubling: 2000e^(0.035t) = 4000, so e^(0.035t) = 2 and t = (ln 2)/0.035 [1] = **19.8 years** (3 s.f.) [1]
*Examiner insight:* The doubling time does not depend on the starting amount: the 2000 cancels. That is a property of every model of the form Ae^(kt).

## Where marks are usually lost

- Raising only the letter, not the coefficient, to a power: (2y⁻²)³ is 8y⁻⁶, not 2y⁻⁶.
- Answers left with a negative exponent when the question asks for positive exponents.
- Taking the logarithm before isolating the exponential term.
- Using the LN key where the question uses log (base 10), or the reverse.
- Reversing an inequality incorrectly, or not at all, after multiplying by −1.
