## Questions

### Section A: short questions

**1.** Which molecular formula belongs to an alkene?

A: C₄H₁₀

B: C₅H₁₀

C: C₃H₇OH

D: C₂H₅COOH **[1]**

**2.** Which compound is saturated?

A: ethene

B: propene

C: ethanol

D: but-2-ene **[1]**

**3.** Use the general formula to write the molecular formula of the alcohol with five carbon atoms. **[1]**

**4.** State the meaning of the term *functional group*. **[2]**

**5.** A student writes the displayed formula of ethane with one carbon atom bonded to four hydrogen atoms and also to the other carbon atom. Explain what is wrong. **[2]**

**6.** Draw the displayed formula of ethene. **[2]**

**7.** State how many carbon atoms are present in a molecule of the carboxylic acid C₂H₅COOH, and explain your answer using the general formula. **[2]**

### Section B: structured questions

**8.** The formulae of five compounds are listed: C₆H₁₄, C₄H₈, C₂H₅OH, HCOOH, C₇H₁₄.

**(a)** Identify the **two** compounds in the same homologous series. Give a reason. **[2]**

**(b)** Identify the compound that is a carboxylic acid and state the value of *n* in its general formula. **[2]**

**(c)** Identify the saturated hydrocarbon. **[1]**

**9.** The boiling points of some straight-chain alcohols are given.

| Alcohol | Number of carbon atoms | Boiling point / °C |
|---|---|---|
| methanol | 1 | 65 |
| ethanol | 2 | 78 |
| propan-1-ol | 3 | 97 |
| butan-1-ol | 4 | 118 |

**(a)** Describe the trend shown by the data. **[1]**

**(b)** *(Extended)* State which general characteristic of a homologous series this trend illustrates. **[1]**

**(c)** *(Extended)* State the formula of the unit by which each alcohol differs from the next. **[1]**

**(d)** *(Extended)* Explain why all four alcohols react in a similar way with sodium. **[1]**

**10.** *(Extended)* Compounds P and Q both have the molecular formula C₄H₁₀.

P: CH₃CH₂CH₂CH₃

Q: CH₃CH(CH₃)CH₃

**(a)** State the term used to describe P and Q. **[1]**

**(b)** Explain why P and Q are described in this way. **[2]**

**(c)** Explain why the structural formula of each compound is more useful than the molecular formula C₄H₁₀. **[1]**

**11.** *(Extended)* A student draws two structures for C₄H₈:

Structure 1: CH₂=CHCH₂CH₃

Structure 2: CH₃CH₂CH=CH₂

She says they are isomers. Explain whether she is correct. **[2]**

**12.** Draw the displayed formula of ethanoic acid, and identify its functional group. **[3]**

**13.** *(Extended)* Define the term *homologous series* and describe **four** of its general characteristics. **[5]**

## Answers

**1.** **B** [1]. C₅H₁₀ fits CₙH₂ₙ.

**2.** **C** [1]. Ethanol's only carbon–carbon bond is single.

**3.** C₅H₁₁OH [1].

**4.** An atom or group of atoms [1] that determines the chemical properties of a homologous series [1].

**5.** Each carbon can form only four bonds [1]; a carbon bonded to four hydrogens and another carbon has five bonds, so each carbon should have three hydrogens [1].

**6.** Correct C=C double bond [1]; two hydrogens on each carbon, each with a single bond [1]:

```
H       H
 \     /
  C = C
 /     \
H       H
```

**7.** Three carbon atoms [1]: in CₙH₂ₙ₊₁COOH, n = 2 counts the carbons outside the COOH group, and the COOH group contains one more carbon [1].

**8. (a)** C₄H₈ and C₇H₁₄ [1]: both fit the general formula CₙH₂ₙ, so both are alkenes [1].

**(b)** HCOOH [1]; n = 0 [1].

**(c)** C₆H₁₄ [1].

**9. (a)** The boiling point increases as the number of carbon atoms increases [1].

**(b)** A trend in physical properties [1].

**(c)** –CH₂– [1].

**(d)** They have the same functional group, –OH, so they have similar chemical properties [1].

**10. (a)** Structural isomers [1].

**(b)** They have the same molecular formula [1] but different structural formulae / different arrangements of atoms [1].

**(c)** It shows unambiguously how the atoms are arranged, so it distinguishes P from Q [1].

**11.** She is not correct [1]. Both have the double bond between an end carbon and the next carbon in an unbranched chain; Structure 1 is Structure 2 written from the other end, so they are the same compound (but-1-ene) [1].

**12.** Displayed formula with all bonds, including C=O and O–H [2] (one mark if one bond is missing):

```
    H   O
    |   ‖
H — C — C — O — H
    |
    H
```

Functional group: –COOH (carboxylic acid group) [1].

**13.** Definition: a family of similar compounds with similar chemical properties due to the same functional group [1]. Any four characteristics [4]: same functional group [1]; same general formula [1]; differ from one member to the next by a –CH₂– unit [1]; display a trend in physical properties [1]; share similar chemical properties [1].

## Where marks are usually lost

- Leaving out the O–H bond, or a hydrogen, in a displayed formula.
- Giving a carbon atom three or five bonds.
- Counting only *n* carbons in a carboxylic acid.
- Saying a C=O bond makes a compound unsaturated.
- Writing "same formula" instead of "same molecular formula, different structural formula" for isomers.
- Counting the same chain written from the other end as an isomer.
- Saying members of a series have "similar physical properties" instead of a trend.
