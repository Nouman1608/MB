## Questions

**1.** The exchange rate is £1 = US$1.30.

**(a)** Convert US$650 to pounds. **[2]**
**(b)** Convert £240 to US dollars. **[2]**

**2.** y is directly proportional to x. When x = 6.5, y = 26.

**(a)** Find an equation connecting y and x. **[2]**
**(b)** Find y when x = 9.25. **[1]**
**(c)** Find x when y = 15. **[1]**

**3.** A coach driver covers a route in 2 hours 30 minutes at an average speed of 84 km/h.

**(a)** Find the time taken at an average speed of 105 km/h. **[2]**
**(b)** Find the average speed needed to take 1 hour 45 minutes. **[2]**

**4.** y is inversely proportional to x. When x = 8, y = 7.5.

**(a)** Find an equation connecting y and x. **[2]**
**(b)** Find y when x = 25. **[1]**
**(c)** Find x when y = 0.6. **[1]**

**5.** Sana invests £4,200 at 3.2% per year.

**(a)** Find the simple interest earned in 5 years. **[2]**
**(b)** Find how much more interest she would earn in 5 years if the 3.2% were compound interest instead. **[3]**

**6.** A boat costs £34,000 new. Its value falls by 18% in the first year, 12% in the second year and 9% in the third year, each as a percentage of the value at the start of that year.

**(a)** Find its value after three years. **[3]**
**(b)** Find the overall percentage decrease, correct to 3 significant figures. **[2]**

**7.** Compound interest of 4% per year is added to a savings account. After 2 years the account holds £2,704. How much was originally paid in? **[3]**

**8.** (Higher tier only) The power P kW produced by a wind turbine is proportional to the cube of the wind speed v m/s. P = 27 when v = 6.

**(a)** Find a formula for P in terms of v. **[2]**
**(b)** Find P when v = 10. **[1]**
**(c)** Find v when P = 64. **[2]**

**9.** (Higher tier only) The brightness B of a lamp is inversely proportional to the square of the distance d metres from it. B = 50 when d = 6.

**(a)** Find a formula for B in terms of d. **[2]**
**(b)** Find B when d = 15. **[1]**
**(c)** Find d when B = 2. **[2]**

**10.** (Higher tier only) y is directly proportional to the square root of x. y = 10.5 when x = 49. The quantity z is inversely proportional to the square root of x. z = 2 when x = 36.

**(a)** Write y in terms of x. **[2]**
**(b)** Find y when x = 64. **[1]**
**(c)** Find the value of x for which y = z. **[3]**

**11.** (Higher tier only) The value V pounds of a machine n years after it was bought is V = 45,000 × 0.85ⁿ.

**(a)** State the starting value and the percentage the value falls by each year. **[2]**
**(b)** Find the value after 4 years, to the nearest pound. **[2]**
**(c)** Find the number of whole years after which the value is first less than half of its starting value. Show your trials. **[3]**

## Answers

**1. (a)** Dividing to get pounds: 650 ÷ 1.30 [1] = **£500** [1]
**(b)** Multiplying to get dollars: 240 × 1.30 [1] = **US$312** [1] **[4]**
*Examiner insight:* Answers tend to gain credit when you show the operation and the rate. A quick size check helps: dollars should be the larger number here, so (b) must exceed 240.

**2. (a)** 26 = k × 6.5 [1], so k = 4 and **y = 4x** [1]
**(b)** y = 4 × 9.25 = **37** [1]
**(c)** 15 = 4x, so x = **3.75** [1] **[4]**
*Examiner insight:* An equation with the letter k left in usually does not answer "find an equation". Write k as a number in the final line.

**3. (a)** Distance = 84 × 2.5 = 210 km [1]. Time = 210 ÷ 105 = **2 hours** [1]
**(b)** 1 hour 45 minutes = 1.75 hours [1]. Speed = 210 ÷ 1.75 = **120 km/h** [1] **[4]**
*Examiner insight:* Converting minutes to a decimal is a frequent slip. 45 minutes is 0.75 hours, not 0.45. Say the units in each answer.

**4. (a)** k = xy = 8 × 7.5 = 60 [1], so **y = 60/x** [1]
**(b)** y = 60/25 = **2.4** [1]
**(c)** 0.6 = 60/x, so x = 60 ÷ 0.6 = **100** [1] **[4]**
*Examiner insight:* Check direction: y falls as x rises, so 2.4 is smaller than 7.5 and 100 is bigger than 8. Wrong-direction answers suggest the direct method was used.

**5. (a)** Interest = 4200 × 3.2 × 5 / 100 [1] = **£672** [1]
**(b)** Compound: 4200 × 1.032⁵ [1] = £4,916.41, so interest = £716.41 [1]. Difference = 716.41 − 672 = **£44.41** [1] **[5]**
*Examiner insight:* Keep unrounded values until the end. Rounding 1.032⁵ early can change the penny figure in a "how much more" question.

**6. (a)** Multipliers 0.82, 0.88 and 0.91 [1]. Value = 34000 × 0.82 × 0.88 × 0.91 [1] = **£22,326.30** [1]
**(b)** Overall multiplier = 0.82 × 0.88 × 0.91 = 0.656656 [1]. Decrease = 1 − 0.656656 = 0.343344, which is **34.3%** (3 s.f.) [1] **[5]**
*Examiner insight:* Adding 18 + 12 + 9 = 39% is the common wrong route. A single overall multiplier is the clearest way to show the method.

**7.** After 2 years the account is multiplied by 1.04² [1] = 1.0816. Original = 2704 ÷ 1.0816 [1] = **£2,500** [1] **[3]**
*Examiner insight:* Working backwards means dividing by the multiplier. Taking 4% off £2,704 twice is a different calculation and gives a different value.

**8. (a)** P = kv³ [1]. 27 = k × 216, so k = 0.125 and **P = 0.125v³** [1]
**(b)** P = 0.125 × 1000 = **125** [1]
**(c)** 64 = 0.125v³ [1], so v³ = 512 and **v = 8** [1] **[5]**
*Examiner insight:* Start with P = kv³ so the cube is visible in your working. Writing P = kv gives a different k and a wrong later answer.

**9. (a)** B = k/d² [1]. 50 = k/36, so k = 1800 and **B = 1800/d²** [1]
**(b)** B = 1800 ÷ 225 = **8** [1]
**(c)** 2 = 1800/d², so d² = 900 [1] and **d = 30** [1] **[5]**
*Examiner insight:* After finding d² you still need the square root. Stopping at 900 is an easy way to lose credit. A distance is positive, so reject −30.

**10. (a)** y = k√x [1]. 10.5 = 7k, so **y = 1.5√x** [1]
**(b)** y = 1.5 × 8 = **12** [1]
**(c)** z = k/√x with 2 = k/6, so k = 12 [1]. Set 1.5√x = 12/√x, so 1.5x = 12 [1], giving **x = 8** [1] **[6]**
*Examiner insight:* Find both constants before comparing. Multiplying both sides by √x removes the fractions and keeps the equation simple.

**11. (a)** The starting value is **£45,000** [1] and 0.85 = 1 − 0.15, so the value falls by **15%** each year [1]
**(b)** V = 45000 × 0.85⁴ [1] = 23,490.28, which is **£23,490** to the nearest pound [1]
**(c)** Half the starting value is 22,500 [1]. n = 4 gives £23,490, which is above 22,500. n = 5 gives £19,967, which is below [1]. So it is first less than half after **5 years** [1] **[7]**
*Examiner insight:* A table of trial values makes the stopping point clear. Check the value just before as well as the one that passes the target. State the answer in whole years.

## Where marks are usually lost

- Using a direct method for an inverse problem. Check which way y moves when x increases.
- Leaving k unknown in the final equation, or finding k and not using it.
- Not converting minutes to decimal hours in speed and time questions.
- Multiplying by an exchange rate when the question needs division, and not checking the size of the answer.
- Adding percentages across years instead of multiplying multipliers.
- Rounding an interim multiplier or value, which shifts the last digits.
- Forgetting to take the final square root, or to reject a negative length.
- Giving a decay multiplier as the percentage itself, for example 0.15 instead of 0.85.
- Not interpreting a formula or a trial result in the words of the question.
