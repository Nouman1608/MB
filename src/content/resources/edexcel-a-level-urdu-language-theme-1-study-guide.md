This guide covers **Theme 1, Evolving Pakistani society (پاکستانی معاشرے کا ارتقا)**, in Pearson Edexcel GCE A-Level Urdu (9UR0), for teaching from 2018. The theme has three sub-themes: family (خاندان), work (کام) and equal access to education in Pakistan (تعلیم), the last of which is the sub-theme used as a research subject. The aim is to give you the ideas, the Urdu vocabulary and a model of the kind of accurate, organised Urdu writing the qualification rewards.

## Where this fits in 9UR0

Theme 1 is one of the content themes that run across the qualification. As the [Paper 1 study guide](/resources/a-level-edexcel-urdu-translation-and-reading/) explains, the independent research task is based on one research subject, and for Themes 1, 3 and 4 your research must concern Pakistan. Theme 1 material also feeds reading, listening and speaking-style practice, and the [Paper 3 study guide](/resources/a-level-edexcel-urdu-listening-reading-writing/) shows how sources on a sub-theme are summarised and evaluated. Learning the theme in Urdu, not through English notes alone, is what makes the vocabulary available when you need it.

## Sub-theme 1.1: family (خاندان)

Key ideas to be able to discuss in Urdu:

- Traditional and changing family structures: **مشترکہ خاندانی نظام** (joint family system) and **الگ گھرانا / چھوٹا خاندان** (separate or smaller household).
- The role of elders and respect for them, and who looks after older relatives.
- Changing roles of parents and children, and marriage customs and expectations.
- The effects of migration from villages to cities, and of work abroad, on family life.

Useful vocabulary:

| Urdu | Meaning |
|---|---|
| خاندان | family |
| بزرگ | elder |
| رشتے دار | relative |
| شادی | marriage |
| ذمہ داری | responsibility |
| روایت | tradition |
| نسل | generation |
| دیکھ بھال | care |

A balanced position is usually the strongest: joint families offer support and shared costs, while smaller households offer privacy and independence. You can argue that society is changing without presenting either form as simply right or wrong.

## Sub-theme 1.2: work (کام)

Ideas to cover:

- Types of work: agriculture, industry, services, informal and self-employment, and work abroad.
- **خواتین کی ملازمت** (women's employment) and the barriers and opportunities involved.
- Unemployment (**بے روزگاری**), the pressure on young people, and the role of skills and training.
- Technology, freelancing and changes in how and where people work.

|---|---|
| روزگار | livelihood, employment |
| ملازمت | job, service |

| ہنر | skill |

| کاروبار | business |

## Sub-theme 1.3: equal access to education (تعلیم)

This sub-theme is the research subject. Your research must cover the aspects set by the specification, be based on Pakistan, and be written up in Urdu. Points to investigate and compare:

- Access for girls and boys, and for urban and rural children.
- Public (**سرکاری**) and private (**نجی**) schools, and differences in resources and quality.
- Language of instruction and its effect on opportunity.
- Constitutional commitments: Article 25A, added in 2010, provides for free and compulsory education for children in a stated age range. Check the wording and age range before you quote it.
- Poverty, distance, child labour and the cost of schooling as barriers.

Keep a research file with named sources, dates and figures you have checked for yourself. Do not quote a statistic you cannot trace to a source.

## Worked example: a model paragraph, with annotation

Task: write a short opinion on how family life in Pakistan is changing.

پاکستان میں خاندان کا ڈھانچہ بدل رہا ہے۔ پہلے زیادہ تر لوگ مشترکہ خاندان میں رہتے تھے، لیکن اب شہروں میں روزگار کی وجہ سے بہت سے جوڑوں نے الگ گھر بنا لیے ہیں۔ اس تبدیلی کا ایک فائدہ یہ ہے کہ میاں بیوی اپنے فیصلے خود کر سکتے ہیں، مگر بزرگوں کی دیکھ بھال مشکل ہو گئی ہے۔ میری رائے میں سب سے بہتر راستہ یہ ہے کہ نئی نسل الگ رہ کر بھی اپنے بزرگوں سے رابطہ اور ان کی خدمت جاری رکھے۔

What makes it work:

- **Structure**: a statement of the change, a contrast (پہلے ... لیکن اب), a benefit and a cost, and a reasoned opinion.
- **Ergative construction**: جوڑوں نے الگ گھر بنا لیے ہیں uses نے with a transitive perfective verb, and the verb agrees with the object (گھر, masculine plural here), not with the subject.
- **Connectives**: لیکن, مگر, اس تبدیلی کا, میری رائے میں give the paragraph a clear line of argument.
- **Agreement**: مشکل ہو گئی ہے agrees with the feminine noun دیکھ بھال.

## Useful connectives and phrases

Learn these as ready-made building blocks and practise using each in a sentence of your own.

| Urdu | Use |
|---|---|
| اس کے برعکس | in contrast |
| اس کے علاوہ | in addition |
| نتیجتاً | as a result |
| میرے خیال میں | in my opinion |
| ایک طرف ... دوسری طرف | on one hand ... on the other |
| آخر میں | finally |

Build each paragraph around one of these links so that your argument can be followed from the first sentence to the last.

## Common mistakes

- Dropping نے, or making the verb agree with the wrong noun, in perfective sentences.
- Using English-style word order instead of verb-final Urdu.
- Misplaced dots or a missing diacritic that changes a word.
- One-sided arguments with no evidence, or research that is not about Pakistan.
- Learning isolated words without sentences to use them in.

## Exam technique

Plan before you write. Use one main idea per paragraph, link ideas with connectives, and keep the opinion consistent. Prefer simple structures you can control to ambitious ones you cannot. Practise writing within the word range set for the task and leave time to check gender agreement, tense and spelling.

## Check your understanding

1. What does مشترکہ خاندانی نظام mean?
   *Answer: The joint family system, in which several generations or related households live and share responsibilities together.*
2. Give the Urdu for "unemployment" and for "livelihood".
   *Answer: بے روزگاری and روزگار.*
3. Which country must your research cover for Theme 1?
   *Answer: Pakistan.*
4. In پاکستان میں ... جوڑوں نے الگ گھر بنا لیے ہیں, why is the verb plural?
   *Answer: With نے, the verb agrees with the object گھر, which is masculine plural in this sentence.*
5. Name two barriers to equal access to education in Pakistan.
   *Answer: For example, poverty and distance, or unequal access for girls.*
6. Translate into Urdu: "education is a right".
   *Answer: تعلیم ایک حق ہے۔*
