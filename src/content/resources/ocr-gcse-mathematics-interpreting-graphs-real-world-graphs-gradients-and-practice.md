## Questions

**1.** A graph converts pounds (horizontal axis) to Danish kroner (vertical axis). It is a straight line through (0, 0) and (50, 430).

**(a)** Write down the gradient and say what it means. **[1]**
**(b)** Use the gradient to change 645 kroner into pounds. **[2]**

**2.** Temperature in kelvin is given by K = C + 273, where C is the temperature in degrees Celsius. The graph of K against C is a straight line.

**(a)** Find K when C = 36. **[1]**
**(b)** Write down the gradient and the intercept of the graph. **[2]**
**(c)** Explain why K is not directly proportional to C. **[1]**

**3.** Priya walks along a path. Her distance-time graph (time in hours, distance from home in km) joins (0, 0), (1, 5), (1.5, 5), (2.5, 11).

**(a)** Find her speed in the first hour. **[1]**
**(b)** For how many minutes is she stationary? **[1]**
**(c)** Find her speed in the final section. **[1]**
**(d)** Find her average speed for the whole walk. **[2]**

**4.** The cost, C pounds, of m metres of fabric is shown by a straight line through (0, 0) and (6, 39).

**(a)** Find the price per metre. **[1]**
**(b)** Write an equation linking C and m. **[1]**
**(c)** Find the cost of 9.5 metres. **[1]**
**(d)** Another graph is a straight line through (0, 5) and (4, 13). Explain why it does not show direct proportion. **[1]**

**5.** Fifteen volunteers take 8 hours to pack parcels. The number of volunteers n and time t hours are inversely proportional.

**(a)** Find the time taken by 24 volunteers. **[2]**
**(b)** How many volunteers would finish in 3 hours? **[2]**
**(c)** Describe the shape of the graph of t against n. **[1]**

**6.** A ramp rises 0.9 m over a horizontal distance of 7.2 m.

**(a)** Write the gradient as a ratio vertical : horizontal in the form 1 : n. **[1]**
**(b)** A ramp with the same gradient covers a horizontal distance of 20 m. How far does it rise? **[2]**

**7.** (Higher tier only) A lift-off test records velocity against time. The graph joins (0, 0), (6, 18), (20, 18), (26, 0), with time in seconds and velocity in m/s.

**(a)** Find the acceleration in the first 6 seconds. **[1]**
**(b)** Find the total distance travelled. **[3]**
**(c)** Find the average speed for the 26 seconds, to 3 significant figures. **[1]**

**8.** (Higher tier only) A particle's distance s metres from a point after t seconds is s = t² + 2t.

**(a)** Complete the values of s for t = 0, 1, 2, 3, 4. **[1]**
**(b)** Find the average speed between t = 1 and t = 4. **[2]**
**(c)** Find the gradient of the chord from t = 2 to t = 3. **[1]**
**(d)** A tangent to the curve at t = 2 passes through (0, -4) and (4, 20). Find its gradient and say what it represents. **[2]**

**9.** (Higher tier only) A shop's takings rate (pounds per hour) is plotted against hours after opening. The graph joins (0, 10), (4, 50), (6, 50).

**(a)** Find the takings in the first 4 hours. **[2]**
**(b)** Find the takings from 4 to 6 hours. **[1]**
**(c)** Find the mean takings per hour over the 6 hours, to the nearest penny. **[2]**
**(d)** State the unit of the area under this graph. **[1]**

**10.** (Higher tier only) A vehicle's speed v m/s is given by v = 60t ÷ (t + 5) for t seconds. Values are in the table.

| t | 0 | 5 | 10 | 15 | 20 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| v | 0 | 30 | 40 | 45 | 48 |

**(a)** Use four trapezia of equal width to estimate the distance travelled in 20 seconds. **[3]**
**(b)** State whether your answer is an over-estimate or an under-estimate. Give a reason. **[1]**
**(c)** A tangent at t = 10 passes through (4, 32) and (16, 48). Find the acceleration at t = 10. **[2]**
**(d)** Find the average acceleration from t = 0 to t = 20. **[2]**

**11.** A mobile plan's monthly cost C pounds is a straight line against data used g gigabytes, through (0, 12) and (10, 17).

**(a)** Find the gradient and say what it means. **[2]**
**(b)** What does the intercept represent? **[1]**
**(c)** Find the cost when 14 gigabytes are used. **[1]**

**12.** (Higher tier only) A cyclist's velocity-time graph joins (0, 0), (8, 10), (28, 10), (36, 4), (40, 0), with time in seconds and velocity in m/s.

**(a)** Find the acceleration in the first 8 seconds. **[1]**
**(b)** Find the deceleration between 28 s and 36 s. **[2]**
**(c)** Find the total distance travelled. **[4]**
**(d)** Find the average speed over the 40 seconds. **[2]**

## Answers

**1. (a)** Gradient = 430 ÷ 50 = **8.6**, meaning 8.6 kroner per pound. [1]
**(b)** £ = 645 ÷ 8.6 [1] = **£75** [1]
*Examiner insight:* Dividing by the gradient to go from kroner back to pounds is the step that is often reversed, and a quick check that 75 × 8.6 gives 645 protects you.

**2. (a)** K = 36 + 273 = **309** [1]
**(b)** Gradient = **1** [1], intercept = **273** [1]
**(c)** The line misses the origin (K = 273 when C = 0), so doubling C does not double K. [1]
*Examiner insight:* Base your explanation on the line missing the origin. Saying "it is not a straight line" would be wrong because the line is straight.

**3. (a)** 5 ÷ 1 = **5 km/h** [1]
**(b)** 1 to 1.5 hours is **30 minutes** [1]
**(c)** (11 - 5) ÷ (2.5 - 1.5) = **6 km/h** [1]
**(d)** Total distance = 11 km in 2.5 hours [1]; 11 ÷ 2.5 = **4.4 km/h** [1]
*Examiner insight:* Average speed uses the whole time including the rest, so do not average 5 and 6, and show the total distance and time to protect your method.

**4. (a)** 39 ÷ 6 = **£6.50 per metre** [1]
**(b)** **C = 6.5m** [1]
**(c)** 6.5 × 9.5 = **£61.75** [1]
**(d)** The line misses the origin: a length of 0 gives a cost of 5. [1]
*Examiner insight:* A money answer should show pence to two decimal places, so write £61.75 and not £61.7.

**5. (a)** tn = 15 × 8 = 120 [1]; t = 120 ÷ 24 = **5 hours** [1]
**(b)** 120 ÷ 3 [1] = **40 volunteers** [1]
**(c)** A smooth curve that falls as n increases and does not touch either axis. [1]
*Examiner insight:* Finding the constant product first shows your method clearly, and a bare wrong answer leaves nothing to show what you intended.

**6. (a)** 0.9 ÷ 7.2 = 0.125 = 1/8, so **1 : 8** [1]
**(b)** 20 ÷ 8 [1] = **2.5 m** [1]
*Examiner insight:* Say which quantity is the vertical one, because writing 8 : 1 reverses the ratio.

**7. (a)** 18 ÷ 6 = **3 m/s²** [1]
**(b)** Trapezium: parallel sides 14 s and 26 s, height 18 [1]; ½ × (14 + 26) × 18 [1] = **360 m** [1]
**(c)** 360 ÷ 26 = **13.8 m/s** [1]
*Examiner insight:* Show how you split the shape. Using only the 18 and the 26 as sides ignores the 14 s top edge and gives a wrong area.

**8. (a)** **0, 3, 8, 15, 24** [1]
**(b)** (24 - 3) ÷ (4 - 1) [1] = **7 m/s** [1]
**(c)** (15 - 8) ÷ 1 = **7** [1]
**(d)** Gradient = 24 ÷ 4 = **6** [1], the speed at t = 2 s (instantaneous velocity, 6 m/s). [1]
*Examiner insight:* A tangent gradient needs units and an interpretation in context, so write "6 m/s at 2 seconds" rather than just "6".

**9. (a)** Trapezium: ½ × (10 + 50) × 4 [1] = **£120** [1]
**(b)** 50 × 2 = **£100** [1]
**(c)** Total = 220 [1]; 220 ÷ 6 = **£36.67** [1]
**(d)** Pounds per hour multiplied by hours gives **pounds** [1]
*Examiner insight:* A mean per hour needs the total over the whole six hours, so divide by 6 rather than by the number of segments.

**10. (a)** Strip width 5. Area ≈ 5 × (0/2 + 30 + 40 + 45 + 48/2) [1]; = 5 × 139 [1] = **695 m** [1]
**(b)** **Under-estimate**, because the curve is concave, so the trapezia fall short of it. [1]
**(c)** (48 - 32) ÷ (16 - 4) [1] = **4/3 m/s²** (1.33) [1]
**(d)** (48 - 0) ÷ 20 [1] = **2.4 m/s²** [1]
*Examiner insight:* A reason must refer to the shape of the curve relative to the trapezia, not just state under- or over-estimate.

**11. (a)** (17 - 12) ÷ 10 = **0.5** [1]; £0.50 per gigabyte. [1]
**(b)** The fixed monthly charge of **£12** when no data is used. [1]
**(c)** 12 + 0.5 × 14 = **£19** [1]
*Examiner insight:* An interpretation needs both a number and units, so "50p for each extra gigabyte" says more than "0.5".

**12. (a)** 10 ÷ 8 = **1.25 m/s²** [1]
**(b)** (4 - 10) ÷ 8 = -0.75 [1]; deceleration **0.75 m/s²** [1]
**(c)** 0 to 8 s: ½ × 8 × 10 = 40 [1]; 8 to 28 s: 20 × 10 = 200 [1]; 28 to 36 s: ½ × (10 + 4) × 8 = 56 [1]; 36 to 40 s: ½ × 4 × 4 = 8, total **304 m** [1]
**(d)** 304 ÷ 40 [1] = **7.6 m/s** [1]
*Examiner insight:* Label each piece of the area with its time interval, since one slip in a multi-part total is easier to find and fix than in a single sum.

## Where marks are usually lost

- Giving gradients or areas with no units, or the wrong units.
- Turning a decimal-hour time into minutes incorrectly, for example treating 0.5 hours as 5 minutes.
- Finding the average speed from the final position instead of the total distance travelled.
- Reversing a ratio or an exchange rate when converting back.
- Using a tangent with two points too close together, which magnifies reading errors.
- Forgetting the ½ in the area of a triangle or trapezium.
- Describing the area under a velocity-time graph as speed instead of distance.
- Calling any straight line direct proportion, without checking it passes through the origin.
- Not saying whether an area estimate is an over- or under-estimate when asked.
