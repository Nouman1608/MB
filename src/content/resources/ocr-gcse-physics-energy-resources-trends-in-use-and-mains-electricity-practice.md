## Questions

**1.** The list shows six energy resources: coal, tides, uranium, biofuel, natural gas, hydroelectricity. State the three that are renewable. **[3]**

**2.** Explain why uranium is classed as a non-renewable energy resource. **[2]**

**3.** Describe how the energy of water stored behind a dam is used to generate electricity, and state one advantage of this over a coal-fired power station. **[4]**

**4.** The table shows the electricity generated, in TWh, from five sources in a country in two years.

| Source | 2000 | 2024 |
|---|---|---|
| Oil | 40 | 12 |
| Coal | 95 | 30 |
| Gas | 110 | 130 |
| Wind | 6 | 70 |
| Solar | 0 | 28 |

**(a)** Calculate the percentage change in the electricity generated from gas between 2000 and 2024. **[2]**
**(b)** Calculate the share of the total that came from wind in 2000 and in 2024. Give each answer as a percentage to 1 decimal place. **[2]**
**(c)** Suggest one reason for the fall in the use of coal. **[1]**

**5.** Compare solar energy and tidal energy as resources for generating electricity. **[4]**

**6.** A farm grows crops to make biofuel.
**(a)** Explain why biofuel is a renewable resource. **[2]**
**(b)** State one disadvantage of growing crops for biofuel. **[1]**

**7.** **(a)** State the difference between a direct potential difference and an alternating potential difference. **[2]**
**(b)** Give one source of each. **[1]**

**8.** A data logger records the UK mains supply. The time base is 2.5 ms per division. One complete wave on the trace takes up 8 divisions.
**(a)** Calculate the period of the wave. **[2]**
**(b)** Calculate the frequency of the wave. **[2]**
**(c)** Calculate the number of complete cycles in one minute. **[1]**

**9.** **(a)** State the function of each of the live, neutral and earth wires in a mains cable. **[3]**
**(b)** State the approximate potential difference between the live wire and the neutral wire in the UK. **[1]**

**10.** A toaster is wired so that its switch is in the neutral wire instead of the live wire. Explain why the toaster may still be dangerous when the switch is open. **[4]**

**11.** A kettle has a metal case. Its live wire becomes loose inside and touches the case.
**(a)** Explain the danger if the case is not connected to the earth wire. **[2]**
**(b)** Explain how a connection between the case and the earth wire changes the situation. **[2]**
**(c)** State the purpose of the insulation around the wires. **[1]**

**12.** A coal-fired power station has an output of 900 MW. A wind farm has an average output of 9 MW.
**(a)** Calculate the energy transferred by the power station in 6 hours, in megawatt-hours. **[2]**
**(b)** Calculate how many wind farms of this average output would be needed to match the power station. **[2]**
**(c)** Explain two reasons why a country could not simply replace the power station with wind farms alone. **[2]**
**(d)** State the frequency of the mains supply in the UK, and calculate the number of cycles in 0.5 s. **[2]**

## Answers

**1.** Tides [1], biofuel [1], hydroelectricity [1]. **[3]**
*Examiner insight:* Name exactly three. Adding extra resources to the list risks including a wrong one.

**2.** It is used faster than it forms [1], so the supply is limited and will run out [1]. **[2]**
*Examiner insight:* "It runs out" alone is thin. Say why: it takes far longer to form than the rate at which we use it.

**3.** Water stored at a height flows downhill [1]. It passes through turbines and turns them [1]. The turbines turn generators that produce electricity [1]. Advantage: no carbon dioxide is released when generating, or output can be adjusted quickly [1]. **[4]**
*Examiner insight:* A describe question rewards the chain of events in order. State the advantage as a comparison with coal, not just a feature.

**4. (a)** Change = 130 − 110 = 20 TWh [1]. Percentage change = 20 ÷ 110 × 100 = **18.2% increase** [1].
**(b)** Totals: 2000 is 251 TWh and 2024 is 270 TWh. Share in 2000 = 6 ÷ 251 × 100 = **2.4%** [1]. Share in 2024 = 70 ÷ 270 × 100 = **25.9%** [1].
**(c)** Coal is a non-renewable fossil fuel that releases carbon dioxide when burned, so it has been replaced by renewable sources such as wind and solar [1]. **[5]**
*Examiner insight:* Divide by the starting value for percentage change and by the total for a share. Show the total used so your method is visible.

**5.** Both are renewable and need no fuel [1]. Solar output depends on daylight and cloud, so it varies [1]. Tidal output varies but is predictable because tides can be forecast [1]. Tidal schemes need suitable coastal sites, while solar panels can be fitted in many places [1]. **[4]**
*Examiner insight:* A compare question needs both resources in each point. Listing facts about one resource and then the other with no link gains less.

**6. (a)** The crops can be regrown [1], so the fuel is replaced about as fast as it is used [1].
**(b)** Land is used for fuel instead of for food, or growing takes time and land [1]. **[3]**
*Examiner insight:* Link the reason for "renewable" to replacement. "It is natural" is not enough.

**7. (a)** In a direct p.d. the direction stays the same [1]. In an alternating p.d. the direction reverses repeatedly [1].
**(b)** Direct: a cell or battery. Alternating: the mains supply [1]. **[3]**
*Examiner insight:* Give both parts of the contrast. Describing only a.c. leaves the d.c. half unstated.

**8. (a)** Period = 8 × 2.5 ms [1] = **20 ms (0.020 s)** [1].
**(b)** Frequency = 1 ÷ 0.020 [1] = **50 Hz** [1].
**(c)** 50 × 60 = **3000 cycles** [1]. **[5]**
*Examiner insight:* Convert milliseconds to seconds before using f = 1 ÷ T, and include the unit with each answer.

**9. (a)** Live: carries the alternating p.d. from the supply to the appliance [1]. Neutral: completes the circuit [1]. Earth: a safety wire connected to the metal case that carries no current in normal use [1].
**(b)** About **230 V** [1]. **[4]**
*Examiner insight:* Give a function for each wire. Describing the earth wire as "the wire that carries current back" confuses it with the neutral.

**10.** The switch only breaks the neutral wire [1]. The appliance remains connected to the live wire at about 230 V [1]. A person who touches a conducting part provides a path to earth [1]. A current passes through the person, causing a shock [1]. **[4]**
*Examiner insight:* Say that the live wire is still connected. Saying only that the toaster "is still on" does not explain the danger.

**11. (a)** The metal case becomes live at about 230 V [1]. A person who touches it provides a path to earth and gets a shock [1].
**(b)** The case is at 0 V through the earth wire, with a large p.d. across the loose live wire [1]. A large current flows through the earth wire instead of through the user [1].
**(c)** To stop a person touching the live conductor and to stop contact with other conductors [1]. **[5]**
*Examiner insight:* For an explain question, connect each effect to the one before it: touching the case, large p.d., current through the body.

**12. (a)** Energy = power × time = 900 MW × 6 h [1] = **5400 MWh** [1]. (This is 1.9 × 10^13 J to 2 significant figures.)
**(b)** 900 ÷ 9 [1] = **100 wind farms** [1].
**(c)** Wind output depends on the weather, so it cannot be relied on [1]. Fossil stations can run whenever demand needs them, but wind farms cannot [1]. (Other valid reasons: land and set-up, effect on the landscape.)
**(d)** **50 Hz** [1]. Cycles in 0.5 s = 50 × 0.5 = **25 cycles** [1]. **[8]**
*Examiner insight:* In an extended question every part still needs its own working. A correct power-times-time answer needs the unit.

## Where marks are usually lost

- Calling a resource renewable because it is cheap or clean, instead of because it is replaced.
- Dividing by the wrong number: the starting value for percentage change, the total for a share.
- Leaving off units such as TWh, MWh, Hz and ms, or leaving off the percentage sign.
- Using the time base in milliseconds without converting before f = 1 ÷ T.
- Mixing up the functions of the neutral wire and the earth wire.
- Writing "current" where "potential difference" is meant when describing the live wire at 230 V.
- Saying a switch in the neutral wire is safe because the appliance stops working.
- Giving a feature of one resource and not comparing it with the other in a compare question.
- Writing "trend" answers without figures from the data.
