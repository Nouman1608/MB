## Section A

**1.** Which of these is an associated organ and **not** part of the alimentary canal?

A: colon

B: duodenum

C: oesophagus

D: pancreas **[1]**

**2.** State the syllabus meaning of absorption. **[1]**

**3.** Put these regions in the order food passes through them: ileum, stomach, rectum, oesophagus, duodenum, colon. **[2]**

**4.** State the organ that makes bile **and** the organ that stores bile. **[2]**

**5.** Which process is the uptake and use of nutrients by cells?

A: absorption

B: assimilation

C: digestion

D: ingestion **[1]**

**6.** Name the region of the alimentary canal where most water is absorbed, and one other region where some water is absorbed. **[2]**

## Answers

**1.** **D** (pancreas) [1]. Total: 1 mark.

**2.** The movement of nutrients from the intestines into the blood [1]. Total: 1 mark.

*Examiner insight:* "Taking food into the body" is ingestion and scores zero here.

**3.** Oesophagus, stomach, duodenum, ileum, colon, rectum. All six in the correct order [2]; one pair of adjacent regions swapped [1 only]. Total: 2 marks.

**4.** Makes bile: liver [1]. Stores bile: gall bladder [1]. Total: 2 marks.

**5.** **B** (assimilation) [1]. Total: 1 mark.

**6.** Most water: small intestine [1]. Some water: colon [1]. Total: 2 marks.

**7. (a)** J stomach; K liver; L gall bladder; M pancreas; N duodenum; P colon. One mark for each [6].

**(b)** Stomach and duodenum (J and N) [1]. Total: 7 marks.

*Examiner insight:* In (b), the liver and pancreas help digestion but food does not pass through them, so they are not part of the canal.

**8.** Egestion [1]; assimilation [1]; ingestion [1]; absorption [1]; digestion [1]. Total: 5 marks.

**9. (a)** The removal of undigested food from the body as faeces [1].

**(b)** Faeces are mostly undigested food that was never absorbed into the body's cells [1]; excretion removes waste produced by metabolism inside cells, such as carbon dioxide or urea [1]. Total: 3 marks.

**10.** Salivary glands secrete saliva containing amylase [1], which starts the digestion of starch in the mouth [1]. The stomach churns food with its muscular wall [1] and secretes gastric juice containing protease and hydrochloric acid [1]. The pancreas secretes pancreatic juice containing amylase, protease and lipase into the duodenum [1]. Total: 5 marks.

*Examiner insight:* Each organ needs what it secretes or does; "the stomach digests food" is too vague to score.

**11.** Food is ingested into the mouth, where teeth break it up and salivary amylase starts digesting starch in the rice [1]. It passes down the oesophagus to the stomach, where it is churned and protein in the fish starts to be digested [1]. In the duodenum, bile and pancreatic juice are added and digestion continues [1]. Digestion is completed in the ileum and the soluble products are absorbed into the blood [1]. Undigested material passes to the colon, where some water is absorbed [1]. Faeces are stored in the rectum and egested through the anus [1]. Total: 6 marks.

*Examiner insight:* A mark needs both the organ and what happens there, in the right order. Missing the duodenum is the most common gap.

**12. (a)** Absorption of (some) water [1].

**(b)** The faeces would be more watery or runnier [1], because less water is absorbed from them before they reach the rectum [1].

**(c)** Most nutrients are absorbed in the small intestine [1], which has not been removed [1]. Total: 5 marks.

## Where marks are usually lost

- Calling the liver or pancreas part of the alimentary canal.
- Saying the gall bladder makes bile.
- Mixing up absorption (into the blood) and assimilation (used by cells).
- Giving "excretion" for the removal of faeces.
- Putting the ileum before the duodenum, or the rectum before the colon.
- Describing organs with no secretion or process named, such as "the stomach digests food".
- Forgetting that most water is absorbed in the small intestine, not the colon.
