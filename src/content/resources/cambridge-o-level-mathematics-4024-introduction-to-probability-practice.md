These questions cover section 8.1, Introduction to probability, of the Cambridge O Level Mathematics (Syllabus D) 4024 syllabus for 2025, 2026 and 2027. The method is explained in the [introduction to probability study guide](/resources/cambridge-o-level-mathematics-4024-introduction-to-probability/), and the [introduction to probability revision notes](/resources/cambridge-o-level-mathematics-4024-introduction-to-probability-revision-notes/) summarise it. Give probabilities as fractions in their simplest form, as decimals or as percentages, as the question asks.

## Section A

**1.** A fair six-sided dice is rolled. Find the probability of

**(a)** a 5,

**(b)** an even number,

**(c)** a number greater than 4. **[3]**

**2.** A bag contains 4 yellow, 7 green and 9 blue beads. One is chosen at random. Find the probability that it is

**(a)** green,

**(b)** not yellow. **[3]**

**3.** The probability that a team wins is 0.35. Find the probability that it does not win. **[1]**

**4.** The probability of an event is 2/5. Write this as a decimal and as a percentage. **[2]**

**5.** The probabilities for the four sections of a spinner are x, x + 0.1, 0.3 and 0.2. Find x. **[3]**

**6.** A letter is chosen at random from the word STATISTICS. Find the probability that the letter is

**(a)** S,

**(b)** T or I,

**(c)** a vowel. **[3]**

## Section B

**7.** In a class of 40 students, 22 study French (F), 15 study Spanish (S) and 6 study both. A student is chosen at random. Find

**(a)** P(French only),

**(b)** P(neither language),

**(c)** P(F or S). **[4]**

**8.** A pie chart shows the vehicles passing a school: car 180°, bus 90°, bicycle 60° and lorry 30°. One vehicle is chosen at random from those counted. Find the probability that it is

**(a)** a bicycle,

**(b)** not a car. **[3]**

**9.** A card is chosen at random from a standard pack of 52 cards. Find the probability of

**(a)** a red king,

**(b)** not a heart,

**(c)** a picture card (king, queen or jack) or an ace. **[3]**

**10.** There are 80 people on a staff. 45 are women. 30 people own a car, and 12 of them are women. A person is chosen at random. Find the probability that the person is a man who does not own a car. **[4]**

**11.** A bag contains x red counters and 12 blue counters. The probability of choosing a red counter is 1/4. Find x. **[3]**

**12.** Explain why a probability cannot be −0.2 or 4/3. **[2]**

**13.** The probability that it rains on a given day is 3/8. Write this as a decimal and as a percentage, and find the probability that it does not rain, as a fraction. **[3]**

**14.** A spinner was spun 50 times. The table shows how often each score came up.

| Score | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Frequency | 8 | 12 | 15 | 10 | 5 |

One of the 50 spins is chosen at random. Find the probability that its score was

**(a)** 3,

**(b)** at least 4,

**(c)** even. **[4]**

**15.** Cards numbered 1 to 20 are placed in a bag and one is chosen at random. Find the probability that the number is

**(a)** a multiple of 4,

**(b)** a square number,

**(c)** a prime number. **[3]**

**16.** Choose one of the words impossible, unlikely, even chance, likely or certain for each event.

**(a)** A fair coin lands heads.

**(b)** A fair dice shows 7.

**(c)** A fair dice shows a number less than 7. **[3]**

## Answers

**1. (a)** **1/6** [1]. **(b)** 3 even numbers in 6, so **1/2** [1]. **(c)** 5 and 6, so 2/6 = **1/3** [1].

**2.** Total = 4 + 7 + 9 = 20 [1]. **(a)** P(green) = **7/20** [1]. **(b)** P(not yellow) = 16/20 = **4/5** [1].

**3.** 1 − 0.35 = **0.65** [1].

**4.** 2 ÷ 5 = **0.4** [1] and **40%** [1].

**5.** x + (x + 0.1) + 0.3 + 0.2 = 1 [1]. 2x + 0.6 = 1 [1]. **x = 0.2** [1].

**6.** STATISTICS has 10 letters: S ×3, T ×3, A, I ×2, C [1]. **(a)** 3/10 [1]. **(b)** T or I: 3 + 2 = 5 letters, so **1/2** [1]. **(c)** Vowels A, I, I: **3/10** [1].

*Examiner insight:* Part (c) needs the A and both I letters. Count the total number of letters first.

**7.** French only = 22 − 6 = 16, Spanish only = 15 − 6 = 9 [1]. **(a)** 16/40 = **2/5** [1]. **(b)** Neither = 40 − (16 + 6 + 9) = 9, so **9/40** [1]. **(c)** (16 + 6 + 9)/40 = **31/40** [1].

**8.** The angles total 360° [1]. **(a)** 60/360 = **1/6** [1]. **(b)** 1 − 180/360 = **1/2** [1].

**9. (a)** 2/52 = **1/26** [1]. **(b)** 39/52 = **3/4** [1]. **(c)** 12 picture cards + 4 aces = 16, so 16/52 = **4/13** [1].

**10.** Men = 80 − 45 = 35 [1]. Men with a car = 30 − 12 = 18 [1]. Men without a car = 35 − 18 = 17 [1]. P = **17/80** [1].

**11.** x/(x + 12) = 1/4 [1]. 4x = x + 12 [1]. **x = 4** [1].

**12.** A probability must lie between 0 and 1 inclusive [1]. −0.2 is below 0, and 4/3 = 1.33 is above 1 [1].

**13.** 3 ÷ 8 = **0.375** and **37.5%** [1][1]. P(no rain) = 1 − 3/8 = **5/8** [1].

**14.** Total = 50 [1]. **(a)** 15/50 = **3/10** [1]. **(b)** (10 + 5)/50 = 15/50 = **3/10** [1]. **(c)** Scores 2 and 4: (12 + 10)/50 = 22/50 = **11/25** [1].

**15. (a)** 4, 8, 12, 16, 20 so 5/20 = **1/4** [1]. **(b)** 1, 4, 9, 16 so 4/20 = **1/5** [1]. **(c)** 2, 3, 5, 7, 11, 13, 17, 19 so 8/20 = **2/5** [1].

**16. (a)** **Even chance** [1]. **(b)** **Impossible** [1]. **(c)** **Certain** [1].

## Where marks are usually lost

- Dividing by the wrong total, or forgetting some outcomes when letters repeat.
- Counting 1 as a prime number.
- Treating a Venn diagram circle as the whole group.
- Not simplifying fractions.
- Giving a probability greater than 1, or odds instead of a fraction.
