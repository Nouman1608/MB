AQA A-Level Psychology (7182) is linear and not tiered, with three written papers of 2 hours each, 96 marks each and one third of the A-level, 288 marks in total. Every paper mixes multiple-choice, short-answer and extended-writing questions. Use this page to plan around that structure, together with our topic pages on [Approaches in Psychology](/resources/a-level-aqa-psychology-approaches-in-psychology/), [Biopsychology](/resources/aqa-a-level-psychology-7182-biopsychology/) and [the multi-store and working memory models](/resources/a-level-aqa-psychology-multi-store-and-working-memory-models/).

## Paper 1: Introductory Topics in Psychology

Paper 1 is 2 hours, 96 marks and one third of the A-level. It is split into four sections of 24 marks each, and every section mixes multiple choice, short answer and extended writing. The compulsory content is Social influence, Memory, Attachment, and Clinical Psychology and Mental Health. **How to spend the time:** four equal sections of 24 marks suggest about 30 minutes per section, which you can treat as a fixed budget and check on the clock as you move from section to section.

## Paper 2: Psychology in Context

Paper 2 is also 2 hours, 96 marks and one third of the A-level, but with three sections of 24, 24 and 48 marks. The compulsory content is Approaches in Psychology, Biopsychology and Research methods. Again, each section mixes multiple choice, short answer and extended writing. **How to spend the time:** the 48-mark section carries half the paper, so give it half the time, about 60 minutes, and spend about 30 minutes on each of the two 24-mark sections.

## Paper 3: Issues and Options in Psychology

Paper 3 is 2 hours and 96 marks, recorded as 33.34% so that the three weightings sum to exactly 100. It has four sections of 24 marks each. The compulsory content is Issues and debates in Psychology, plus one topic from each of three option groups: Relationships, Gender or Cognition and development; Schizophrenia, Eating behaviour or Stress; and Aggression, Forensic Psychology or Addiction. **How to spend the time:** the option choices are made in advance by your centre or by you, so revise the options you will answer, and give the Issues and debates section as much practice as any option, since it is compulsory.

## Timing and marks-per-minute

- Each paper: 120 minutes / 96 marks = 1.25 minutes per mark.
- A 24-mark section: 24 x 1.25 = 30 minutes.
- Paper 2's 48-mark section: 48 x 1.25 = 60 minutes.
- Paper 1 check: 4 x 30 = 120 minutes. Paper 2 check: 30 + 30 + 60 = 120 minutes. Paper 3 check: 4 x 30 = 120 minutes.
- Total across the qualification: 3 x 96 = 288 marks.

Because a section holds multiple-choice, short-answer and extended-writing items together, a section budget is more useful than a question budget: do the quick items fast, then spend the saved time on the extended answer.

## Command words and question styles

Short-answer items usually begin "Outline", "Identify" or "Explain" and need accurate terminology in a sentence or two. Extended answers asking you to "Discuss" or "Evaluate" want description of theory or research followed by commentary, such as strengths, limitations, applications and comparison. Research methods items often ask you to apply knowledge to a short scenario, so refer to the participants, variables and procedure given rather than answering in general terms.

## Worked practice scenario: summarising a small data set

**Question:** A psychologist tests recall of a word list after a 30-second distraction task. Eight participants recall the following numbers of words: 9, 7, 12, 8, 10, 7, 11, 8. (a) Calculate the mean. (b) Calculate the median. (c) State the range. (d) Explain one reason the mean may be a misleading summary for a small sample.

**Model answer:**

```
(a) Sum = 9 + 7 + 12 + 8 + 10 + 7 + 11 + 8 = 72
    Mean = 72 / 8 = 9.0 words

(b) Ordered: 7, 7, 8, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12
    Median = (8 + 9) / 2 = 8.5 words

(c) Range = highest - lowest = 12 - 7 = 5 words
```

(d) With only eight participants, a single extreme score would shift the mean noticeably, because every value is included in the calculation; the median would change far less. The mean of 9.0 therefore may not represent a typical participant if one person scored unusually high or low, and a measure of spread, such as the range of 5, should be reported alongside it.

## Before/during exam checklist

- Know which three papers cover which compulsory content, and which options you are answering on Paper 3.
- Practise one timed paper per week in the final month.
- Write down the section marks at the start of each paper and note the finish time for each section.
- For every extended answer, plan description first, then at least two pieces of evaluation.
- Learn research methods vocabulary precisely, because it is compulsory on Paper 2.
- Show working for any calculation, including units where relevant.
- Use the context of any scenario in your answer.
- Leave two minutes at the end for the multiple-choice items you skipped.

## Frequently asked questions

**How many papers are there in AQA A-Level Psychology 7182?**
There are three written papers, each 2 hours and 96 marks, each worth one third of the A-level, 288 marks in total. The qualification is not tiered.

**How are the marks divided within each paper?**
Papers 1 and 3 are each split into four sections of 24 marks. Paper 2 has three sections of 24, 24 and 48 marks. Every section mixes multiple choice, short answer and extended writing.

**What is on Paper 2?**
The compulsory content is Approaches in Psychology, Biopsychology and Research methods.

**Do I choose options on Paper 3?**
Yes. Alongside the compulsory Issues and debates in Psychology, you answer on one topic from each of three option groups: Relationships, Gender or Cognition and development; Schizophrenia, Eating behaviour or Stress; and Aggression, Forensic Psychology or Addiction.
