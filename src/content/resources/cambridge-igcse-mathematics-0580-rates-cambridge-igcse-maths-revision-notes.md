These notes condense subtopic **1.12 Rates** of the Cambridge IGCSE Mathematics 0580 syllabus for 2025, 2026 and 2027. Core and Extended candidates learn the same three statements. Worked examples are in the [rates study guide](/resources/cambridge-igcse-mathematics-0580-rates-cambridge-igcse-maths/), and exam-style questions with mark schemes are in the [rates practice questions](/resources/cambridge-igcse-mathematics-0580-rates-cambridge-igcse-maths-practice/).

## The three statements

1. Use common measures of rate: hourly pay, exchange rates, flow rates, fuel consumption.
2. Apply other measures of rate: pressure, density, population density. **The formula is given in the question.**
3. Solve problems involving average speed. **You must know speed = distance ÷ time.**

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Rate | Amount of one quantity per one unit of another |
| Compound unit | A unit made of two units, such as km/h, g/cm³, N/m² |
| Flow rate | Volume per unit time, such as litres per minute |
| Fuel consumption | Distance per unit of fuel (km per litre) or fuel per distance (litres per 100 km) |
| Density | Mass per unit volume |
| Pressure | Force per unit area |
| Population density | Number of people per unit area |
| Average speed | Total distance ÷ total time, including stops |

## One relationship, three forms

```
rate = A ÷ B          A = rate × B          B = A ÷ rate

speed = distance ÷ time      distance = speed × time      time = distance ÷ speed
density = mass ÷ volume      mass = density × volume      volume = mass ÷ density
pressure = force ÷ area      force = pressure × area      area = force ÷ pressure
```

The unit shows the order: N/m² is newtons ÷ square metres.

## Common rates: quick methods

- **Pay:** pay = rate × hours. Hours = pay ÷ rate.
- **Flow:** time = volume ÷ flow rate. Two flows at once (filling and leaking) combine first: net rate = in − out.
- **Fuel:** km per litre = distance ÷ litres. Litres per 100 km = litres ÷ (distance ÷ 100). The two forms are reciprocals of each other, scaled by 100.
- **Exchange rate:** a rate in one currency per unit of another; see the money notes for conversions.

## Unit conversions for compound units

| Conversion | Rule | Example |
|---|---|---|
| km/h → m/s | ÷ 3.6 | 54 km/h = 15 m/s |
| m/s → km/h | × 3.6 | 25 m/s = 90 km/h |
| g/cm³ → kg/m³ | × 1000 | 0.9 g/cm³ = 900 kg/m³ |
| litres → cm³ | × 1000 | 2.5 litres = 2500 cm³ |
| m³ → litres | × 1000 | 1.8 m³ = 1800 litres |
| m² → cm² | × 10 000 | 0.04 m² = 400 cm² |

Method: convert the top unit, then the bottom unit, one at a time. A bigger unit on the bottom gives a bigger number: 1 g/cm³ is 1 000 000 g/m³, because a cubic metre holds a million cubic centimetres.

## Time in rate questions

- Minutes to hours: divide by 60. 2 h 24 min = 2.4 h.
- Decimal hours to minutes: multiply the decimal part by 60. 2.5 h = 2 h 30 min.
- Never type 2 h 24 min as 2.24.

## Average speed checks

- Include stopped time in the total time.
- The mean of two speeds is **not** the average speed unless equal **times** were spent at each speed.
- Average speed lies between the slowest and fastest speeds. If yours does not, recheck.

## Exam traps

- Calculating mass ÷ density when density × volume was needed: check that the units cancel to the unit you want.
- Using 1 m³ = 100 cm³ or 1 m² = 100 cm².
- Leaving a flow volume in m³ while the rate is in litres per minute.
- Giving a fuel answer in the wrong form (km per litre when litres per 100 km was asked).
- Rounding an intermediate time, such as 6.67 minutes, and then converting it.
- Writing an answer without its unit when the question does not provide one.

## Self-test

1. Leo earns $217 for 14 hours of work. Find his hourly rate of pay.
2. A tap fills an 80-litre bath at 12 litres per minute. How long does it take, in minutes and seconds?
3. A car travels 540 km on 36 litres of fuel. Find the fuel consumption in (a) km per litre, (b) litres per 100 km.
4. A metal has density 2.7 g/cm³. Find the mass of 250 cm³ of it.
5. A block of mass 1.2 kg has volume 1500 cm³. Find its density in g/cm³.
6. Pressure = force ÷ area. Find the force that produces a pressure of 1500 N/m² on an area of 0.04 m².
7. A region has a population of 2.4 million and an area of 8000 km². Find its population density.
8. Write 54 km/h in m/s.
9. Write 25 m/s in km/h.
10. A bus travels for 2 hours 24 minutes at an average speed of 65 km/h. How far does it travel?
11. A train travels 210 km at an average speed of 84 km/h. How long does the journey take?
12. Write a density of 0.9 g/cm³ in kg/m³.

### Answers

1. 217 ÷ 14 = **$15.50 per hour**
2. 80 ÷ 12 = 6.666... minutes; 0.666... × 60 = 40, so **6 minutes 40 seconds**
3. (a) 540 ÷ 36 = **15 km per litre** (b) 36 ÷ 5.4 = **6.67 litres per 100 km** (3 s.f.)
4. 2.7 × 250 = **675 g**
5. 1200 ÷ 1500 = **0.8 g/cm³** (convert 1.2 kg to 1200 g first)
6. 1500 × 0.04 = **60 N**
7. 2 400 000 ÷ 8000 = **300 people per km²**
8. 54 ÷ 3.6 = **15 m/s**
9. 25 × 3.6 = **90 km/h**
10. 2.4 × 65 = **156 km**
11. 210 ÷ 84 = 2.5, so **2 hours 30 minutes**
12. 0.9 × 1000 = **900 kg/m³**
