These notes condense "How to correct errors in double entry records" from section 3.2 of the OxfordAQA International GCSE Accounting (9215) specification. Correcting errors "includes the use of the general journal and suspense account". All amounts are in dollars.

Full explanations and a complete suspense account example are in the [correcting errors study guide](/resources/oxfordaqa-igcse-accounting-9215-correcting-errors-oxfordaqa-igcse-accounting/). For marked questions, use the [correcting errors practice questions](/resources/oxfordaqa-igcse-accounting-9215-correcting-errors-oxfordaqa-igcse-accounting-practice/).

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| General journal | Book of prime entry for items not recorded in any other book, including corrections |
| Narrative | A short explanation under a journal entry, starting "Being..." |
| Suspense account | A temporary account holding a trial balance difference until the errors are found |
| Undercast / overcast | Added up too low / too high |
| One-sided (partial) entry | Only one side of the double entry was made |
| Correcting entry | The journal entry that turns what was recorded into what should have been recorded |

## Journal layout

```
Date      Account                         Debit    Credit
31 Dec    [Account debited]                 xxx
              [Account credited]                      xxx
          Being correction of ...
```

## The three-line method

```
1. WHAT WAS DONE        (accounts, sides, amounts)
2. WHAT SHOULD BE DONE  (the correct entry)
3. CORRECTION           (the difference, account by account)
Then: did the totals agree? No -> the missing side is SUSPENSE.
```

## Correction patterns

| Error | Suspense? | Pattern of the correction |
|---|---|---|
| Commission | No | Dr the right personal account, Cr the wrong one (or the reverse) |
| Principle | No | Move the amount from the wrong class of account to the right one |
| Original entry | No | Both accounts by the difference between the right and wrong amounts |
| Omission | No | The full entry that was never made |
| Complete reversal | No | Both accounts by **twice** the amount |
| Compensating | No | Each wrong account by its own error |
| Addition (one account) | Yes | That account by the error; suspense on the other side |
| Partial omission | Yes | The missing side; suspense on the other side |
| Transposition (one side) | Yes | The wrong account by the difference; suspense on the other side |
| Unequal posting | Yes | The wrong account by the difference; suspense on the other side |

## Awkward cases

| Situation | Correction |
|---|---|
| Entry on the **wrong side of one account** (other side correct) | That account by **twice** the amount; suspense for the other side |
| **Day book** undercast or overcast | Correct only the total account (sales, purchases, returns); individual personal accounts were posted from documents and are already right |
| Wrong amounts on **both** sides, but different | Each account by its own error; suspense for any net difference |
| Correct amount, wrong account **and** wrong side | Think it through with the three lines; never guess |

**Small reminder.** Carriage inwards of 160 paid by cash was credited to cash and also **credited** to carriage inwards. Done: Cr Cash 160, Cr Carriage inwards 160. Should be: Cr Cash 160, Dr Carriage inwards 160. Correction: **Dr Carriage inwards 320, Cr Suspense 320.**

## Suspense account rules

```
Debits > credits in the trial balance  -> suspense opens as a CREDIT
Credits > debits                       -> suspense opens as a DEBIT
Each journal line involving suspense is posted to the suspense account
on the same side as in the journal.
All errors found -> suspense balance = nil.
Balance left -> an error is still undiscovered.
```

**Small reminder.** Debits 58,240, credits 58,540. Credits are higher by 300, so suspense opens with a **debit** of 300.

## Exam traps

- Using suspense for commission, principle, omission, original entry, complete reversal or compensating errors.
- Opening suspense on the same side as the larger total.
- Correcting a wrong-side or reversed entry with the single amount instead of double.
- Changing customer or supplier accounts for a day book casting error.
- Omitting narratives, or writing a narrative that does not say what is being corrected.
- Debit and credit in the journal swapped: check that the correction moves each account towards its correct balance.

## Quick self-test

1. What is the general journal used for in error correction?
2. A trial balance has debits of 74,180 and credits of 73,920. On which side does the suspense account open, and with what amount?
3. A payment of 260 to K Amadi was debited to K Amedi's account. Give the correcting journal. Is suspense needed?
4. A credit sale of 1,500 to R Diaz was not recorded at all. Give the correcting journal.
5. Purchases returns of 210 were debited to purchases returns and credited to the supplier. Give the correcting journal.
6. The purchases journal was overcast by 400. Give the correcting journal.
7. Wages of 2,350 were credited correctly to bank but debited to wages as 2,530. Give the correcting journal.
8. Discount received of 75 was debited to the supplier and also debited to discount received. Give the correcting journal.
9. Motor repairs of 480 were debited to the motor vehicles account. Name the error and give the correcting journal.
10. After all corrections, the suspense account still has a debit balance of 90. What does this show?
11. Why does a complete reversal need twice the amount?
12. Write a suitable narrative for the journal in question 6.

### Answers

1. To record the **correcting entries** (with narratives) before they are posted to the ledger.
2. Debits are higher by 260, so suspense opens as a **credit of 260**.
3. **Dr K Amadi 260, Cr K Amedi 260.** No suspense: an error of commission leaves the totals equal.
4. **Dr R Diaz 1,500, Cr Sales 1,500.**
5. **Dr Supplier 420, Cr Purchases returns 420** (complete reversal: twice 210).
6. **Dr Suspense 400, Cr Purchases 400.**
7. **Dr Suspense 180, Cr Wages 180.**
8. **Dr Suspense 150, Cr Discount received 150** (twice 75).
9. **Error of principle**: **Dr Motor repairs (or motor expenses) 480, Cr Motor vehicles 480.**
10. At least one **error has not yet been found**.
11. The wrong entry must be **cancelled** and the correct entry **made**, so each account moves by the amount twice.
12. "Being correction of the purchases journal, overcast by 400."
