**These notes condense content area 3.2.2.2 of 9237: "The features of the Qur'an including the major themes of the Makki and Madni Surahs. The miraculous nature and infallibility of the Qur'an and the way in which it is used and treated."** The full explanation is in the [study guide](/resources/oxfordaqa-igcse-islamiyat-9237-features-and-use-of-the-quran/).

## Basic features

- 114 surahs, arranged roughly from longest to shortest, not by order of revelation.
- 30 equal parts (juz').
- Revealed in Arabic; translations help understanding but are not the Qur'an itself.
- Every surah except at-Tawbah (9) opens with the Bismillah.
- Mixed content: belief, stories, law and moral advice.

## Makki and Madni at a glance

| | Makki | Madni |
|---|---|---|
| When | Before the Hijrah | After the Hijrah |
| Community | Persecuted minority | Community and state |
| Themes | Tawhid; Prophethood; Day of Judgement; the Hereafter; stories of earlier prophets; moral character; against idol worship | Law and rulings; worship; family, inheritance and trade; ban on interest; war and treaties; the hypocrites; relations with People of the Book |
| Style | Short, rhythmic, powerful | Longer, detailed |
| Examples | al-Alaq, al-Kafirun, al-Kahf | al-Baqarah, Al Imran, an-Nisa, al-Ma'idah, an-Nur |

**Key point:** Makki surahs build belief; Madni surahs build a way of life on that belief. The division is by time (before or after the Hijrah), not strictly by place.

## Miracle (i'jaz): six lines of evidence

1. **Eloquence:** unlike poetry or ordinary speech to the Arabs.
2. **The challenge:** produce a surah like it (2:23; 17:88).
3. **Unlettered Prophet:** he did not read or write (29:48).
4. **Consistency:** no contradiction over 23 years (4:82).
5. **Truthful accounts and predictions:** earlier nations; the Romans' victory (30:2-4).
6. **Effect:** it changes people and unites communities.

## Infallibility

- The Qur'an is free from error and change.
- **Because it is Allah's word:** "Falsehood cannot approach it from before it or from behind it" (41:42).
- **Because it is preserved:** "We will be its guardian" (15:9).
- Translations and commentaries can contain errors; the Qur'an itself cannot.

## Use of the Qur'an

| Use | Example |
|---|---|
| Worship | Al-Fatiha in every rak'ah; Taraweeh in Ramadhan |
| Law | First source of Shariah; no ruling may contradict it |
| Guidance | Decisions about family, money and character |
| Learning | Reading, memorisation (huffaz), tafsir |
| Comfort | Recited in grief, fear and gratitude |
| Teaching | Used to call to Islam and answer questions |

## Treatment of the Qur'an

- Handled in a state of cleanliness (many scholars require wudu to touch the printed copy).
- Kept in a clean, high place; not on the floor; not taken to unclean places.
- Recited with tajwid and measured recitation (73:4).
- Listened to with attention (7:204).
- Followed in practice: the highest respect is to obey it.

## The prescribed passage: Qur'an 56:75-80

| Phrase | Point |
|---|---|
| "I swear by the setting of the stars" | A solemn oath showing great importance |
| "a noble Qur'an" | Its honour |
| "in a Register well-protected" | Protected; safeguarded record |
| "none touch it except the purified [i.e., the angels]" | Purity; basis for respect when handling |
| "a revelation from the Lord of the worlds" | Divine origin |

## Linking the passage to the topics

- **Miracle and infallibility:** the "Register well-protected" and the "revelation from the Lord of the worlds" show a divine, safeguarded origin.
- **Treatment:** "none touch it except the purified" is the verse most often used for the rule on wudu.
- **Use:** because it is "noble" and from "the Lord of the worlds", it is the first source of authority.

## Model answer: a 4-mark question

**Question:** Explain **two** differences between Makki and Madni surahs. **[4]**

> Makki surahs deal mainly with belief, such as Tawhid and the Day of Judgement [1], because they were revealed to a small persecuted community that needed firm faith [1]. Madni surahs contain more rulings, such as those about Zakah, trade and inheritance [1], because after the Hijrah the Muslims had a community to organise [1].

## Quick comparison of Makki and Madni examples

| Surah | Type | What to say about it |
|---|---|---|
| al-Alaq (96) | Makki | The first revelation; reading and creation |
| al-Kafirun (109) | Makki | Separation from idol worship |
| al-Kahf (18) | Makki | Stories of faith and trial |
| al-Baqarah (2) | Madni | The longest surah; many laws, including fasting and interest |
| an-Nisa (4) | Madni | Family, inheritance and justice |
| al-Ma'idah (5) | Madni | Contains the prescribed passage on completion of the religion (5:3) |

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Surah | A chapter of the Qur'an |
| Ayah (plural ayat) | A verse |
| Juz' | One of thirty parts |
| Makki | Revealed before the Hijrah |
| Madni | Revealed after the Hijrah |
| I'jaz | The inimitability of the Qur'an |
| Tajwid | Rules for correct recitation |
| Tafsir | Commentary on the Qur'an |
| Mushaf | A bound copy of the Qur'an |

## Exam traps

- Do not reverse Makki and Madni themes.
- Do not say the Qur'an is arranged in order of revelation.
- A "miracle" point needs a reason, not just the word.
- Treatment and use are different: write about both when asked for "use and treatment".
- Qur'an 56:79 is about the "purified"; say that scholars apply it to people as well as angels, and avoid claiming it settles the question alone.

## Self-test

1. How many surahs are there in the Qur'an?
2. Which surah is not preceded by the Bismillah?
3. Give two themes of the Makki surahs.
4. Give two themes of the Madni surahs.
5. What is meant by i'jaz?
6. Which verse invites people to produce a surah like the Qur'an?
7. Why does the Prophet's (pbuh) lack of formal learning count as evidence of the Qur'an's origin?
8. What does infallibility mean?
9. Give two ways Muslims show respect for the Qur'an.
10. Which phrase in 56:75-80 is used for the rule on touching the Qur'an in a state of purity?

**Answers:** 1. 114. 2. At-Tawbah (Surah 9). 3. Any two: Tawhid; Prophethood; Day of Judgement and the Hereafter; stories of earlier prophets; moral character. 4. Any two: law; worship; family and inheritance; trade and the ban on interest; war and treaties; the hypocrites. 5. The miraculous quality of the Qur'an: that others cannot produce anything like it. 6. Qur'an 2:23 (also 17:88). 7. He was unlettered, so a book of such depth is taken to come from Allah. 8. The Qur'an is free from error and change. 9. Any two: keep it clean; place it high; make wudu; recite with tajwid; listen quietly; act on it. 10. "None touch it except the purified."
