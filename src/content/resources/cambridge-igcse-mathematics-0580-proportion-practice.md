These questions cover subtopic **2.8 Proportion** in the Cambridge IGCSE Mathematics 0580 syllabus for examination in 2025, 2026 and 2027. The subtopic is **Extended only** (E2.8), so every question is Extended content for Paper 2 or Paper 4. Questions marked (calculator) need one; the rest are designed to be done without.

For worked examples of every form, read the [proportion study guide](/resources/igcse-mathematics-0580-proportion/); for a one-page summary, use the [proportion revision notes](/resources/cambridge-igcse-mathematics-0580-proportion-revision-notes/).

## Questions

### Section A: short questions

**1.** The time, t, for a journey is inversely proportional to the speed, v. Write an equation connecting t and v. **[1]**

**2.** y is directly proportional to x. When x = 3, y = 21. Find y when x = 8. **[2]**

**3.** y is proportional to the square of x. When x = 5, y = 50. Find y when x = 3. **[2]**

**4.** y is inversely proportional to x. When x = 4, y = 9. Find y when x = 6. **[2]**

**5.** p is proportional to the square root of q. When q = 9, p = 15. Find q when p = 35. **[3]**

**6.** y is proportional to x³. Describe the effect on y of multiplying x by 3. **[1]**

### Section B: structured questions

**7.** The distance, d metres, that an object falls from rest is proportional to the square of the time, t seconds. It falls 20 m in 2 seconds.

**(a)** Find a formula for d in terms of t. **[2]**

**(b)** Find how far it falls in 3 seconds. **[1]**

**(c)** Find the time it takes to fall 125 m. **[2]**

**8.** The intensity, I, of a light is inversely proportional to the square of the distance, d metres, from the source. When d = 2, I = 36.

**(a)** Find I in terms of d. **[2]**

**(b)** Find I when d = 3. **[1]**

**(c)** Find d when I = 4. **[2]**

**9.** (calculator) y is proportional to the cube root of x. When x = 8, y = 6.

**(a)** Find y when x = 100, giving your answer correct to 3 significant figures. **[2]**

**(b)** Find x when y = 15. **[2]**

**10.** y is inversely proportional to the square root of x. When x = 9, y = 4.

**(a)** Find y in terms of x. **[2]**

**(b)** Find y when x = 16. **[1]**

**(c)** Find x when y = 2. **[2]**

**11.** The number of days, D, needed to finish a job is inversely proportional to the number of workers, w. Six workers take 10 days.

**(a)** How many days do 4 workers take? **[2]**

**(b)** How many workers are needed to finish the job in 5 days? **[1]**

**12.** (calculator)

**(a)** y is proportional to x². x increases by 30%. Find the percentage increase in y. **[2]**

**(b)** y is inversely proportional to x³. x increases by 25%. Find the percentage decrease in y. **[2]**

**13.** The table shows values of x and y.

```
x    1    8    27
y   12    6     4
```

y is inversely proportional to a power or root of x. Find a formula for y in terms of x, showing how you decided. **[3]**

**14.** y is proportional to the square of (x − 1). When x = 5, y = 32. Find the value of x when y = 50, given that x > 1. **[3]**

## Answers

**1.** **t = k/v** [1] (any letter for the constant).

**2.** y = kx, 21 = 3k, k = 7 [1]. y = 7 × 8 = **56** [1].

**3.** y = kx², 50 = 25k, k = 2 [1]. y = 2 × 9 = **18** [1].

**4.** y = k/x, 9 = k/4, k = 36 [1]. y = 36/6 = **6** [1].

**5.** p = k√q, 15 = 3k, k = 5 [1]. 35 = 5√q, √q = 7 [1]. **q = 49** [1].
*Examiner insight:* stopping at √q = 7 and giving q = 7 loses the last mark. Square to undo the root.

**6.** y is multiplied by 3³: **y is multiplied by 27** [1].

**7. (a)** d = kt², 20 = 4k [1], **d = 5t²** [1].

**(b)** 5 × 9 = **45 m** [1].

**(c)** 125 = 5t², t² = 25 [1], **t = 5 seconds** [1].

**8. (a)** I = k/d², 36 = k/4 [1], k = 144, so **I = 144/d²** [1].

**(b)** 144/9 = **16** [1].

**(c)** 4 = 144/d², d² = 36 [1], **d = 6 m** [1].

**9. (a)** y = k∛x, 6 = 2k, k = 3 [1]. y = 3∛100 = 13.92… = **13.9** [1].

**(b)** 15 = 3∛x, ∛x = 5 [1], **x = 125** [1].

**10. (a)** y = k/√x, 4 = k/3, k = 12 [1]. **y = 12/√x** [1].

**(b)** 12/4 = **3** [1].

**(c)** 2 = 12/√x, √x = 6 [1], **x = 36** [1].

**11. (a)** D = k/w, 10 = k/6, k = 60 [1]. D = 60/4 = **15 days** [1].

**(b)** 5 = 60/w, **w = 12 workers** [1].
*Examiner insight:* for inverse linear proportion, D × w is always 60, a quick check on both answers.

**12. (a)** Multiplier 1.3² = 1.69 [1]. **69% increase** [1].

**(b)** Multiplier 1/1.25³ = 1/1.953125 = 0.512 [1]. **48.8% decrease** [1].
*Examiner insight:* in (a), 60% (doubling the 30%) and 30% (ignoring the square) are common wrong answers. Always square or cube the multiplier, not the percentage.

**13.** y falls as x rises, so test products [1]. y × x = 12, 48, 108 (not constant); y × ∛x = 12 × 1, 6 × 2, 4 × 3 = 12 each time [1]. **y = 12/∛x** [1].

**14.** y = k(x − 1)², 32 = 16k, k = 2 [1]. 50 = 2(x − 1)², (x − 1)² = 25 [1]. x − 1 = 5 (x > 1), **x = 6** [1].

## Where marks are usually lost

- Writing the answer to a "formula" question as just the value of k.
- Putting an inverse quantity in the numerator.
- Forgetting the final square or cube when the unknown is inside a root.
- Changing a percentage by the power instead of changing the multiplier.
- Rounding k on Paper 4 before the final answer.
