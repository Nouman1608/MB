## Section A

**1.** A measuring jug has labels at 0 ml and 50 ml, with 10 equal gaps between them. The liquid level is at the 7th mark above 0 ml.

**(a)** Work out the value of one gap. **[1]**

**(b)** Write down the volume of liquid. **[1]**

**2.** **(a)** Write 3:25 pm in the 24-hour clock. **[1]**

**(b)** Write 00:50 in the 12-hour clock, using am or pm. **[1]**

**3.** The bearing of Q from P is 072°. Work out the bearing of P from Q. **[1]**

**4.** An angle at a point is reflex. The angle on the other side of the same two arms measures 64°. Work out the size of the reflex angle. **[1]**

**5.** Change a speed of 108 km/h into metres per second. **[2]**

**6.** A photograph shows a tree next to a door. The tree is about 3 times the height of the door. Estimate the height of the tree in metres. State the assumption you make. **[2]**

## Section B

**7.** A train leaves at 10:48 and arrives at 14:12 on the same day.

**(a)** Work out the journey time. **[1]**

**(b)** The train travels 238 km. Work out its average speed in km/h. **[2]**

**8.** A cyclist rides 18 km at an average speed of 24 km/h. She then rests for 15 minutes. She then rides a further 12 km in 40 minutes.

Work out her average speed for the whole journey, including the rest, in km/h. **[4]**

**9.** A stone has a mass of 2.7 kg and a volume of 1000 cm³.

**(a)** Work out the density of the stone in g/cm³. **[2]**

**(b)** Another stone of the same material has a volume of 350 cm³. Work out its mass in grams. **[1]**

**10.** Pressure = force ÷ area.

**(a)** A force of 900 N acts on an area of 1.5 m². Work out the pressure in N/m². **[2]**

**(b)** The same force acts on a different area and produces a pressure of 400 N/m². Work out this area. **[2]**

**11.** An alloy is made by melting together 150 cm³ of metal X, with density 10.5 g/cm³, and 50 cm³ of metal Y, with density 7.3 g/cm³. The volume of the alloy is 200 cm³.

Work out the density of the alloy. **[3]**

**12.** A boat leaves a harbour at 09:35. It sails 12 km on a bearing of 130° at an average speed of 16 km/h.

**(a)** Work out the time when the boat reaches the end of this journey. **[2]**

**(b)** Write down the bearing of the harbour from the boat at the end of the journey. **[1]**

## Answers

**1. (a)** 50 ÷ 10 = **5 ml** [1].

**(b)** 7 × 5 = **35 ml** [1].

**2. (a)** **15:25** [1].

**(b)** **12:50 am** [1].

**3.** 72° + 180° = **252°** [1].

**4.** 360° − 64° = **296°** [1].

**5.** 108 × 1000 ÷ 3600 [1] = **30 m/s** [1]. (Dividing by 3.6 is equivalent.)

**6.** Assumption: a door is about 2 m high [1]. 3 × 2 = about **6 m** [1]. (Accept any answer from 5 m to 7 m that follows from a stated door height between about 1.8 m and 2.3 m.)

**7. (a)** 10:48 → 11:00 is 12 min, 11:00 → 14:00 is 3 h, 14:00 → 14:12 is 12 min: **3 h 24 min** [1].

**(b)** 3 h 24 min = 3 + 24/60 = 3.4 h [1]; 238 ÷ 3.4 = **70 km/h** [1].

**8.** Time for first part = 18 ÷ 24 = 0.75 h = 45 min [1].

Total time = 45 + 15 + 40 = 100 min = 100/60 h [1].

Total distance = 18 + 12 = 30 km [1].

Average speed = 30 ÷ (100/60) = **18 km/h** [1].

**9. (a)** 2.7 kg = 2700 g [1]; 2700 ÷ 1000 = **2.7 g/cm³** [1].

**(b)** 2.7 × 350 = **945 g** [1].

**10. (a)** 900 ÷ 1.5 [1] = **600 N/m²** [1].

**(b)** Area = force ÷ pressure [1] = 900 ÷ 400 = **2.25 m²** [1].

**11.** Mass of X = 10.5 × 150 = 1575 g and mass of Y = 7.3 × 50 = 365 g [1].

Total mass = 1940 g [1].

Density = 1940 ÷ 200 = **9.7 g/cm³** [1].

**12. (a)** Time = 12 ÷ 16 = 0.75 h = 45 min [1]; 09:35 + 45 min = **10:20** [1].

**(b)** 130° + 180° = **310°** [1].

## Where marks are usually lost

- Question 1: counting the 9 marks between the labels instead of the 10 gaps.
- Question 2(b): writing 00:50 as 0:50 pm or 12:50 pm. Times between midnight and 1 am are am.
- Question 5: multiplying by 3.6 instead of dividing. A speed in m/s is always smaller than the same speed in km/h.
- Question 7(b): dividing by 3.24, which treats 24 minutes as 0.24 hours.
- Question 8: leaving out the rest, or averaging the two speeds. Average speed always uses total distance over total time.
- Question 11: averaging the densities, (10.5 + 7.3) ÷ 2 = 8.9, which ignores that there is three times as much of metal X.

## Frequently asked questions

**Does the rest time count when working out average speed?**
Yes, if the question asks for the average speed for the whole journey. Average speed is total distance divided by total time, and the total time includes any stops.

**How do you find the density of a mixture?**
Work out the mass of each part (density × volume), add the masses, and divide by the total volume. Never average the two densities unless the volumes are equal.
