## Questions

**1.** A data set has mean 15 and standard deviation 3.2. Each value x is coded using y = 4x − 10.
**(a)** Calculate the mean of y. **[2]**
**(b)** Calculate the standard deviation of y. **[2]**

**2.** A data set has median 40 and interquartile range 12. Find the median and interquartile range of the new data set when each value x is changed to:
**(a)** y = 0.5x + 3 **[2]**
**(b)** y = 100 − x **[2]**

**3.** The values 12, 15, 17, 17, 20, 27 have mean 18 and median 17.
**(a)** A new value, 30, is added. State whether the mean and the median increase, decrease or stay the same, and give the new mean. **[3]**
**(b)** Instead, the value 27 is removed from the original six. State what happens to the mean, with a reason, and find the new mean. **[2]**

**4.** Group P has 15 values with mean 8.2 and standard deviation 1.5. Group Q has 10 values with mean 9.7 and standard deviation 2.1. Calculate the mean and the standard deviation of all 25 values. **[5]**

**5.** A group of 8 values has mean 15.5. It is combined with a second group of 12 values. The 20 values together have mean 18.2. Find the mean of the second group. **[4]**

**6.** The data set 1, 3, 4, 5, 7 is standardised to mean 0 and standard deviation 1. Calculate the five standardised values. **[4]**

**7.** Test marks have mean 48 and standard deviation 9. They are scaled to a new mean of 100 and a new standard deviation of 15.
**(a)** Find the scaled value of a mark of 57. **[2]**
**(b)** Find the original mark that becomes 85. **[2]**

**8.** In a school sports day, the high jump results have mean 1.60 m and standard deviation 0.08 m. The javelin results have mean 60 m and standard deviation 5 m. Hira jumps 1.72 m and throws 66 m. Use standardised values to decide which result is better relative to the other competitors. **[4]**

**9.** The waiting times, in minutes, at two bus stops are summarised below.

| | Mean | Standard deviation | Median | IQR |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Route A | 8.4 | 1.2 | 8 | 1.5 |
| Route B | 6.9 | 3.4 | 6 | 4.0 |

**(a)** Using the mean and standard deviation, compare the waiting times on the two routes. **[3]**
**(b)** A company says that route B "is quicker". Give one reason from the figures that supports this and one reason that weakens it. **[2]**

**10.** A set of 40 values x is coded using y = x − 50. The coded values have Σy = 60 and Σy² = 1 090.
**(a)** Find the mean and the standard deviation of x. **[4]**
A second set has 60 values with mean 48.5 and standard deviation 4.
**(b)** Find the mean of all 100 values. **[2]**
**(c)** Find the standard deviation of all 100 values. **[3]**

**11.** A taxi firm records the length of 30 journeys. The times have mean 20 minutes, standard deviation 4.5 minutes, median 19 minutes and interquartile range 6 minutes. The fare in dollars is F = 0.8t + 5, where t is the journey time in minutes.
**(a)** Find the mean and standard deviation of the fares. **[3]**
**(b)** Find the median and interquartile range of the fares. **[2]**
**(c)** A 31st journey takes exactly 20 minutes. State what happens to the mean journey time and show that the standard deviation decreases, giving its new value. **[3]**
**(d)** A fare of 25 dollars is charged. Show that its standardised value is the same as that of the journey time that produced it. **[2]**

## Answers

**1. (a)** Mean of y = 4 × 15 − 10 [1] = **50** [1]
**(b)** Subtracting 10 does not change the spread [1], so standard deviation = 4 × 3.2 = **12.8** [1]
*Examiner insight:* Show the rule you are using. A wrong answer with the rule written down is easier to give credit to than a bare number.

**2. (a)** Median = 0.5 × 40 + 3 = **23** [1]; IQR = 0.5 × 12 = **6** [1]
**(b)** Median = 100 − 40 = **60** [1]; the multiplier is −1, so the IQR is 1 × 12 = **12** [1]
*Examiner insight:* A spread cannot be negative. Writing an IQR of −12 for part (b) shows the sign rule has not been understood.

**3. (a)** New total = 108 + 30 = 138, so new mean = 138/7 = **19.7** (3 s.f.) [1]. The mean **increases** because 30 is above the old mean of 18 [1]. The ordered data is 12, 15, 17, 17, 20, 27, 30, so the median is the fourth value, 17: it **stays the same** [1]
**(b)** Removing 27 (above the mean 18) means the mean **decreases** [1]. New mean = 81/5 = **16.2** [1]
*Examiner insight:* Give the reason by comparing the new value with the existing mean. "It gets bigger because there is more data" does not explain anything.

**4.** Totals: 15 × 8.2 = 123 and 10 × 9.7 = 97 [1]. Combined mean = 220/25 = **8.8** [1]
Σx² for P = 15 × (1.5² + 8.2²) = 15 × 69.49 = 1 042.35 and for Q = 10 × (2.1² + 9.7²) = 985 [1]
Variance = (1 042.35 + 985)/25 − 8.8² = 81.094 − 77.44 = 3.654 [1]
Standard deviation = √3.654 = **1.91** (3 s.f.) [1]
*Examiner insight:* Averaging the two standard deviations gives 1.8 and is a common wrong route. Set your working out so each stage (totals, Σx², variance, root) can be seen.

**5.** Total of all 20 = 20 × 18.2 = 364 [1]. Total of first group = 8 × 15.5 = 124 [1]. Total of second group = 364 − 124 = 240 [1]. Mean = 240/12 = **20** [1]
*Examiner insight:* Work with totals, not means. Subtracting 15.5 from 18.2 and stopping there does not use the group sizes.

**6.** Mean = (1 + 3 + 4 + 5 + 7)/5 = 4 [1]. Squared deviations: 9, 1, 0, 1, 9, which sum to 20, so variance = 4 [1] and σ = 2 [1]. Standardised values (x − 4)/2: **−1.5, −0.5, 0, 0.5, 1.5** [1]
*Examiner insight:* Check your answer: standardised data must have mean 0, and the squares of the z-values must add up to the number of values (here 5).

**7. (a)** z = (57 − 48)/9 = 1 [1]; scaled value = 100 + 15 × 1 = **115** [1]
**(b)** z = (85 − 100)/15 = −1 [1]; original = 48 + 9 × (−1) = **39** [1]
*Examiner insight:* For the reverse direction, undo the scaling first to find z, then rebuild with the original mean and standard deviation. Do not apply the forward rule again.

**8.** High jump: z = (1.72 − 1.60)/0.08 = 1.5 [1]. Javelin: z = (66 − 60)/5 = 1.2 [1]. The high jump has the larger standardised value [1], so it is better relative to the other competitors, because it is 1.5 standard deviations above the mean compared with 1.2 [1]
*Examiner insight:* A conclusion must refer to the standardised values. Saying "66 is bigger than 1.72" compares units that cannot be compared.

**9. (a)** Route A has the longer waits generally, because its mean (8.4 minutes) is larger than route B's (6.9 minutes) [1]. Route A is more consistent, because its standard deviation (1.2) is smaller than B's (3.4) [1]. Both statements refer to the context, waiting times at the stops [1]
**(b)** Supports: B has the lower mean and median (6.9 and 6), so waits are shorter on average [1]. Weakens: B's spread is much larger (standard deviation 3.4, IQR 4.0), so some waits will be far longer than average, and waits are less predictable [1]
*Examiner insight:* Pair each centre with its spread and quote figures from both routes. A one-sided answer, or one with no numbers, leaves out half of the comparison.

**10. (a)** Mean of y = 60/40 = 1.5 [1]. Variance of y = 1 090/40 − 1.5² = 27.25 − 2.25 = 25 [1]. σ = **5** [1]. Mean of x = 50 + 1.5 = **51.5**, and σ of x is the same as that of y, **5** [1]
**(b)** Totals: 40 × 51.5 = 2 060 and 60 × 48.5 = 2 910 [1]. Combined mean = 4 970/100 = **49.7** [1]
**(c)** Σx² for first set = 40 × (5² + 51.5²) = 107 090; for second set = 60 × (4² + 48.5²) = 142 095 [1]. Variance = 249 185/100 − 49.7² = 2 491.85 − 2 470.09 = 21.76 [1]. σ = √21.76 = **4.66** (3 s.f.) [1]
*Examiner insight:* In extended questions, carry full-accuracy values from one part to the next. Rounding the mean to 49.7 is exact here, but rounding a standard deviation early can change the final digits.

**11. (a)** Mean fare = 0.8 × 20 + 5 = **21 dollars** [1]. The added 5 does not change the spread, so the standard deviation = 0.8 × 4.5 = **3.60 dollars** [2]
**(b)** Median = 0.8 × 19 + 5 = **20.20 dollars** [1]; IQR = 0.8 × 6 = **4.80 dollars** [1]
**(c)** The mean **stays at 20 minutes** because the new value equals the mean [1]. The sum of squared deviations is 30 × 4.5² = 607.5, unchanged by the new value (its deviation is 0) [1]. New variance = 607.5/31 = 19.6, so σ = **4.43 minutes**, which is less than 4.5 [1]
**(d)** A fare of 25 dollars comes from t = 25 minutes, since 0.8 × 25 + 5 = 25. For the fare: z = (25 − 21)/3.6 = **1.11** [1]. For the time: z = (25 − 20)/4.5 = **1.11**, the same, because coding by a positive linear rule does not change how many standard deviations a value lies from the mean [1]
*Examiner insight:* Part (c) asks you to show the decrease, so the working must justify it rather than just quote 4.43. Include units in your answers.

## Where marks are usually lost

- Adding the constant to the standard deviation or the IQR.
- Giving a negative standard deviation or IQR after multiplying by a negative number.
- Averaging two means or two standard deviations without using group sizes.
- Finding a combined standard deviation without using Σx² for each group.
- Stopping at the variance and forgetting the square root.
- Comparing raw values from two different distributions instead of z-scores.
- Answering a comparison with only centre or only spread, or with no figures.
- Saying the median "must" change when a value is added, when it can stay the same.
