Each question is labelled (non-calculator) or (calculator); give non-exact calculator answers correct to 3 significant figures unless told otherwise.

## Answers

**1. (a)** **11** [1]

**(b)** **5** [1]

**2. (a)** 27 + 16 = **43** [1]

**(b)** −8 × 25 = **−200** [1]

*Examiner insight:* a negative number cubed stays negative; +200 loses the mark.

**3. (a)** **0.125** [1]

**(b)** **0.12** [1]

*Examiner insight:* 144 = 12², and four decimal places halve to two; 1.2 or 0.012 are the usual slips.

**4. (a)** **3/13** [1]

**(b)** (5/2)² = 25/4 = **6¼** [1]

**5.** 100 − 36 = 64 [1]; √64 = **8** [1]

*Examiner insight:* √10² − √6² = 10 − 6 = 4 is wrong; work out the whole expression under the root first.

**6. (a)** 49 < 55 < 64, so **7 and 8** [1]

**(b)** 64 < 90 < 125, so **4 and 5** [1]

**7.** (15 − 4)² = 121 [1]; 121 ÷ 11 = **11** [1]

**8. (a)** √196 = **14 m** [1]

**(b)** Perimeter = 4 × 14 = 56 m [1]; 56 × 8.50 = **$476** [1]

**9. (a)** 64 = 4³ and three decimal places become one: ∛0.064 = **0.4 m** [1]

**(b)** 0.4 m = 40 cm [1]; 40² = **1600 cm²** [1]

*Examiner insight:* converting the edge to centimetres before squaring avoids the area-conversion error (0.16 m² is 1600 cm², not 16 cm²).

**10. (a)** **454.35424** [1]

**(b)** 17.8 + 5.29 = 23.09 [1]; ∛23.09 = 2.847 57… = **2.85** [1]

**(c)** 37.21 − 6.324 55… = 30.885 4… and 2.7³ = 19.683 [1]; 30.885 4… ÷ 19.683 = 1.569 14… = **1.57** [1]

*Examiner insight:* if you enter (c) in one line, put brackets round the numerator; without them only √40 is divided by 2.7³.

**11.** ∛50 = 3.684 03… [1]; ∛400 = 7.368 06… [1]; difference = 3.684 03… = **3.68 cm** [1]

*Examiner insight:* the larger cube has 8 times the volume, so its edge is 2 times as long; this is a useful check on the answer.

**12.** √(25 + 144) = √169 = 13 [1]; a + b = 17, and 13 ≠ 17, so Kim is wrong [1]

**13.** 2.9 × 10.4² = 313.664 [1]; fifth root = 3.157 13… = **3.16** [1]

**14. (a)** 27 = 3³ and three decimal places become one: **−0.3** [1]

**(b)** (−0.2)² = 0.04 and (−0.2)³ = −0.008 [1]; 0.04 − (−0.008) = **0.048** [1]

**(c)** Side = √75 = 8.660 25… cm [1]; perimeter = 4 × 8.660 25… = 34.641… = **34.6 cm** [1]

*Examiner insight:* in (b), subtracting a negative adds; 0.032 comes from treating (−0.2)³ as positive. In (c), keep the unrounded side length before multiplying by 4.

## Where marks are usually lost

- Slow or wrong recall of 13², 14² and 15² on the non-calculator papers.
- Decimal place errors such as 0.5³ = 0.0125 or √0.0144 = 0.012.
- Rooting each term of a sum or difference separately.
- Missing brackets on the calculator for a numerator or a negative base.
- Rounding before the last step, or rounding when all the figures were asked for.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I leave √0.0144 unsimplified on a non-calculator paper?**
No. 0.0144 = 144 ÷ 10 000, so √0.0144 = 12 ÷ 100 = 0.12. Questions in this subtopic expect the value.
