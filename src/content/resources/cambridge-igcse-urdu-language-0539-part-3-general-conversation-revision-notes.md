Condensed notes for **Part 3: General conversation** of the optional Component 5 Speaking test of Cambridge IGCSE Urdu as a Second Language (0539). For fuller explanations and a worked conversation, use the [Part 3 General conversation study guide](/resources/cambridge-igcse-urdu-language-0539-part-3-general-conversation/).

## Part 3 in a box

```
Part 3  General conversation
Time        3 to 4 minutes
Marks       20 (of 60 for Speaking)
Topics      two or three general topics, unseen
Objectives  S1, S2, S3, S4, S5
Language    Urdu throughout; recorded; no dictionaries
```

## Rules for the topics

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Number | at least two topics |
| Spread | one from Areas A and B; one from Areas C, D and E |
| Advance notice | none: you do not know them beforehand |
| Overlap | topics must not overlap with your presentation |
| Style | full responses; explain and justify opinions; natural replies |

## The five areas

| Area | Name | Topics in brief |
|---|---|---|
| A | Everyday activities | home life and school (home life, school routine); eating and drinking; health and fitness |
| B | Personal and social life | self, family and relationships; everyday life; eating out; holidays |
| C | The world around us | home town; clothes; shopping; public services and customs; environment, weather, finding the way; people, places and customs (greetings, travel and transport, famous sites and cities, animals, festivals) |
| D | The world of work | further education and training; careers and employment; future career plans |
| E | The international world | tourism at home and abroad; life in other countries (weather, places and customs, food and drink, meeting people) |

Memory aid: **A** is about me at home, **B** is about me with others, **C** is about around me, **D** is about work after school, **E** is about the wider world.

## Opinion frames

| Purpose | Frame |
|---|---|
| Opinion | میرے خیال میں ... |
| Reason | کیونکہ ... / اس لیے کہ ... |
| Compare | ... کے مقابلے میں ... |
| Partly agree | میں کچھ حد تک متفق ہوں، لیکن ... |
| Hypothesis | اگر میں ... ہوتا تو ... |
| Future | مستقبل میں میں ... کروں گا |
| Experience | پچھلے سال ... / بچپن میں ... |

## Assessment grid (same as Part 2)

| Criterion | Marks | Looks for |
|---|---|---|
| Comprehension and responsiveness | 10 | understands; replies naturally and spontaneously; keeps conversation going; needs little prompting |
| Quality of language | 10 | vocabulary; pronunciation and intonation; structures; errors |

Bands: 9 to 10 very good; 7 to 8 good; 5 to 6 satisfactory; 3 to 4 weak; 1 to 2 poor; 0 nothing to reward.

## Strategy: four moves for every question

1. **Answer** the question.
2. **Give a reason.**
3. **Add an example** from your life.
4. **Extend** with a contrast, a past or future idea, or a short question to the examiner.

## One question, four moves

Question: *آپ کے شہر میں ٹریفک کا مسئلہ کیسا ہے؟*

| Move | Sample |
|---|---|
| Answer | ہمارے شہر میں صبح اور شام کو بہت رش ہوتا ہے۔ |
| Reason | اس کی وجہ یہ ہے کہ گاڑیاں زیادہ ہیں اور سڑکیں تنگ ہیں۔ |
| Example | میں روز بس میں بیٹھ کر دیکھتا ہوں کہ ہم اکثر آدھا گھنٹہ پھنسے رہتے ہیں۔ |
| Extend | اگر لوگ بسیں زیادہ استعمال کریں تو یہ مسئلہ کم ہو سکتا ہے۔ |

## When you do not know much

| Move | Urdu |
|---|---|
| Personal link | میں نے خود اس کے بارے میں زیادہ نہیں سوچا، لیکن ... |
| Cautious opinion | مجھے پکا نہیں معلوم، لیکن میرا خیال ہے کہ ... |
| Ask to repeat | کیا آپ دوبارہ کہہ سکتے ہیں؟ |
| Buy time | اچھا سوال ہے، ذرا سوچنے دیجیے |

## Faults and fixes

| Fault | Fix |
|---|---|
| Memorised scripts | build vocabulary banks and opinion frames instead |
| Ignoring some areas | prepare five questions for each area |
| Answers with no reason | add *کیونکہ* every time |
| Staying on the presentation topic | move to the new topic |
| Only present tense | add past or future |
| Silence | use a repair phrase |

## Exam traps

- You may be asked about something you have never discussed. Stay calm and use the personal-link move.
- Do not wait for the examiner to prompt you.
- Do not rush. Clear pronunciation is part of the quality of language mark.
- A change of topic is normal: follow it.
- If a question seems to touch your presentation topic, answer it briefly and then give your view on the new topic, so that you show a wider range of vocabulary.

## Self-test

1. How long is Part 3?
2. How many marks is it worth?
3. How many topics are covered at least?
4. From which areas must the topics come?
5. Can the topics overlap with the presentation?
6. Which area covers careers and further education?
7. Name **two** Area C topics.
8. Give an Urdu frame for giving an opinion.
9. What can you do if you do not understand a question?
10. Name the two criteria.

**Answers**

1. Three to four minutes.
2. Twenty marks.
3. Two (two or three topics).
4. One from Areas A and B, and one from Areas C, D and E.
5. No.
6. Area D, the world of work.
7. Any two, for example home town, shopping, the environment, weather, travel and transport, festivals.
8. For example *میرے خیال میں ...*
9. Ask the examiner to repeat or rephrase, for example *کیا آپ دوبارہ کہہ سکتے ہیں؟*
10. Comprehension and responsiveness; quality of language.
