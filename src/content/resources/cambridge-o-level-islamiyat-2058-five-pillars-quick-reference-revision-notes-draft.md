# Topic 4 quick reference

Related: [Articles of Faith and Pillars of Islam revision notes](/resources/cambridge-o-level-islamiyat-articles-of-faith-and-pillars-of-islam-revision-notes/).

## The six Articles of Faith

| Article (syllabus wording) | Key facts | Significance |
|---|---|---|
| God, including what Muslims believe about him | Tawhid: Allah is one, unique and without partner; He alone creates, sustains, knows and judges | Gives meaning to all other Articles and to every act of worship |
| Angels, their nature and duties | Created from light; do not eat or sleep; Jibril, Mika'il, Israfil, the Angel of Death, the Kiraman Katibin, Munkar and Nakir | Never unobserved, so honesty in private |
| Books, their contents and purpose | Suhuf (Ibrahim), Tawrat (Musa), Zabur (Dawud), Injil (Isa), Qur'an (Muhammad (pbuh)) | The Qur'an is the final guide |
| Prophets, their character and function | Human, chosen, truthful and trustworthy; messengers brought a book or law; the Qur'an names twenty-five prophets; Muhammad (pbuh) is the Seal of the Prophets (33.40) | Examples to follow; no new prophet needed |
| God's predestination and decree | Al-qadr: Allah knows, records, wills and creates; humans still choose and are judged | Patience, humility and effort |
| Resurrection and the last day | Trumpet; resurrection; gathering; book of deeds; weighing; the sirat; paradise or hell | Accountability gives life purpose |

## Jihad

| Meaning | Key points |
|---|---|
| Greater jihad | Inner struggle to control desires and live rightly |
| Striving in other ways | Speech, study, teaching and wealth |
| Physical jihad | Permitted only for defence or to end oppression, under legitimate authority, never against civilians, women, children, the elderly, crops or places of worship |

## The five Pillars

| Pillar | How it is performed | Significance |
|---|---|---|
| Shahada | Declaration of faith in Allah and in Muhammad (pbuh) as His Messenger; repeated in every prayer | The door to Islam |
| Salat | Five times daily; intention, wudu, clean body, clothing and place, facing the qibla; rak'at of standing, recitation, bowing and prostration; Jumu'a for men; Eid prayers; missed prayers made up | Discipline, remembrance, equality and unity; protection from indecency and wrongdoing (29.45) |
| Zakat | Yearly 2.5 per cent on savings above the nisab, with other rates on crops and livestock; given to the eight categories in 9.60 | Purifies wealth, relieves poverty, builds solidarity |
| Sawm | Ramadan, dawn to sunset; exemptions for the sick, travellers, the elderly, children, pregnant, nursing and menstruating women | Self-control, gratitude, empathy |
| Hajj | Dhu al-Hijja; once for those able; ihram at the miqat, tawaf, sa'y, Arafat, Muzdalifa, Mina, sacrifice, hair cut, further tawaf | Equality, remembrance of Ibrahim, Hajar and Isma'il, forgiveness |

## Self-test

1. How many Articles of Faith are there? *Six.*
2. Name the Pillar that includes the nisab. *Zakat.*
3. What are the three kinds of meaning of jihad in the syllabus? *Physical, mental and spiritual.*
4. Which prophet received the Zabur? *Dawud.*
5. What is the percentage for zakat on savings? *2.5 per cent above the nisab.*
