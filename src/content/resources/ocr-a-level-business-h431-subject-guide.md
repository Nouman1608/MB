OCR A Level Business (H431) studies how businesses set objectives, make strategic
decisions and respond to the markets and wider environment around them, at local,
national and global scale. It is assessed by three written papers, each lasting
2 hours and carrying 80 marks, with each worth a third of the A Level. H431 is now in
its teach-out: OCR's final first teaching of it was in September 2025, its last
assessment opportunity is summer 2027, and resits after that move to the replacement
specification, H436. This page is written for students finishing H431, with a short
note on how H436 differs.

## How the course is organised

The H431 content is set out in seven areas.

1. **Business objectives and strategic decisions.** Stakeholder and business objectives,
   mission statements, corporate social responsibility, strategy and implementation,
   business plans, risk and uncertainty, opportunity cost, contingency planning and
   crisis management, models of strategic choice, financial and non-financial measures
   of performance, forecasting, decision making and decision trees, Ansoff's matrix, and
   conflicts in business decision making.
2. **External influences facing businesses.** Markets and market growth, demand and
   supply, market forces and competition, the global context and international trade
   including the EU and emerging markets, political, economic, social and technological
   factors, and the digital revolution with ethical, legal and environmental factors.
3. **Marketing and marketing strategies.**
4. **Operational strategy.**
5. **Human resources.**
6. **Accounting and financial considerations.**
7. **The global environment of business.**

The first two areas provide the framework of objectives, strategy and external context.
Areas 3 to 6 cover the main functions inside a business, and area 7 takes the analysis
to an international scale.

## How it's assessed

| Component | Title | Time | Marks | Weighting |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| H431/01 | Operating in a Local Business Environment | 2 hours | 80 | 33.33% |
| H431/02 | The UK Business Environment | 2 hours | 80 | 33.33% |
| H431/03 | The Global Business Environment | 2 hours | 80 | 33.34% |

OCR prints each component as 33.33% of the total; the third is shown here as 33.34% so
the figures add to exactly 100%. In effect the three papers count equally. The
qualification is linear and untiered, so every candidate sits all three papers at the
end of the course. Each component includes synoptic assessment, which means a paper can
draw on content from across the specification, not only the area its title suggests.

### H431 and its replacement, H436

| | H431 (this course) | H436 (replacement) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Status | Being withdrawn | Current |
| First teaching | September 2015 | September 2026 |
| Assessment | First 2017, final summer 2027 | First 2028 |
| Papers | Three, each 2 hours and 80 marks | Three, each 2 hours and 90 marks |

H436's three components are Business Activity, Marketing and People; Operations, Finance
and External Influences; and Strategy, Risk and Managing Change. All three include
questions assessing the quality of extended response, and Component 03 also includes
synoptic assessment. If you started A Level Business in September 2026 or later, you
are on H436 rather than H431; check with your teacher if you are unsure.

## How the parts fit together

The three H431 papers are organised by business environment rather than by content
area. The local, UK and global papers each require you to apply the whole toolkit of
the course, from objectives and decision-making models to marketing, operations, people
and finance, to a business operating in that setting. A question about a small local
firm might call on cash-flow forecasting, recruitment and pricing all at once, while a
question on a global business could combine international trade, emerging markets
and operational strategy.

That is why the skills matter as much as the content. Across every paper you need to
apply concepts to the business in front of you, use numerical data such as financial
measures and forecasts, analyse causes and effects, and reach a supported judgement.
Decision tools from area 1, such as decision trees and Ansoff's matrix, are useful in
answers on any paper.

## Planning your study

If you are now in the final year of H431, most of the content will already have been
taught, so the priority is consolidation. A sensible approach for the remaining time:

- **Now to the spring:** complete any outstanding content, then work through the seven
  areas again, linking each one to the local, UK and global contexts.
- **Throughout:** practise using calculations and financial measures in written
  answers, not in isolation.
- **Final term:** full timed papers for all three components, with attention to
  evaluation and judgement.

Because each paper is synoptic, avoid revising a content area only for the paper that
seems most closely linked. Start past paper practice well before the final term and use
the exam preparation page for component-by-component advice on the teach-out years.

## Frequently asked questions

**When is the last OCR A Level Business H431 exam?**
The final assessment opportunity for H431 is summer 2027. Resits after that move to the replacement specification, H436.

**How many papers are there in OCR A Level Business H431?**
Three: Operating in a Local Business Environment, The UK Business Environment and The Global Business Environment. Each lasts 2 hours and carries 80 marks.

**Are the H431 papers equally weighted?**
Yes, in effect. OCR prints each as 33.33% of the total, and together they make up the whole A Level. Each includes synoptic assessment.

**What replaces OCR A Level Business H431?**
H436, first taught from September 2026 and first assessed in 2028. It has three 2-hour papers of 90 marks each.

**Is H431 linear?**
Yes. All three papers are sat at the end of the course, and the qualification is not tiered.
