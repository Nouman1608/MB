## Questions

**1.** Consider the numbers 14, -6, 5/7, √45, 1.2 and √121.
**(a)** List the numbers that are integers. **[2]**
**(b)** Write down the number that is irrational. **[1]**

**2.** Write 0.4777... (the 7 recurs) as a fraction in its simplest form. **[3]**

**3.** Two bus routes leave a depot every 84 minutes and every 120 minutes. Both leave together at 06:00.
**(a)** Write 84 and 120 as products of prime factors. **[2]**
**(b)** Find the time of their next joint departure. **[2]**

**4.**
**(a)** Simplify √98 - √32. **[2]**
**(b)** (Enrichment) Rationalize the denominator of 10/√5. **[1]**
**(c)** Expand and simplify (3 + √5)(3 - √5). **[2]**

**5.**
**(a)** Write 5⁻² × 5⁵ ÷ 5² as a single number. **[2]**
**(b)** Calculate (2.4 × 10⁶) ÷ (6 × 10⁻³), giving the answer in standard form. **[2]**
**(c)** Write 0.00305 in standard form. **[1]**

**6.** By rounding each number to 1 significant figure, estimate (305.8 × 0.0489) ÷ 1.97. Then evaluate it exactly to 3 significant figures and comment on your estimate. **[4]**

**7.**
**(a)** List the integers x such that -2 < x ≤ 4. **[2]**
**(b)** A number line has a closed circle at -3 with a ray going left, and an open circle at 2 with a ray going right. Write the two inequalities this shows. **[2]**

**8.**
**(a)** Convert 72 km/h to m/s. **[1]**
**(b)** Convert a density of 0.9 g/cm³ to kg/m³. **[1]**
**(c)** A flight leaves a city on UTC+8 at 23:30 and lasts 6 h 40 min. It lands in a city on UTC+1. Find the local arrival time and day. **[3]**

**9.**
**(a)** y is directly proportional to x, and y = 28 when x = 8. Find y when x = 18. **[2]**
**(b)** Five pumps empty a tank in 9 hours. All pumps work at the same rate. Find the time taken by 4 pumps, in hours and minutes. **[3]**

**10.**
**(a)** 5000 is invested at 3.5% compound interest per year. Find its value after 4 years, to the nearest cent. **[2]**
**(b)** Find how much more interest this earns than simple interest at 3.5% over the same 4 years. **[3]**
**(c)** A jacket costs 156 including 20% tax. Find the price before tax. **[1]**

**11.** (Extended) A rectangle has sides 14.2 cm and 6.5 cm, each measured to 1 decimal place.
**(a)** Write the bounds for each side. **[2]**
**(b)** Find the lower and upper bounds of the area. **[2]**
**(c)** Find the lower and upper bounds of the perimeter. **[2]**
**(d)** Give the area to a degree of accuracy that is justified by the bounds. **[1]**

**12.** (Extended)
**(a)** Evaluate 32^(3/5). **[2]**
**(b)** Evaluate log₅ 125. **[1]**
**(c)** Write log 8 + log 125 as a single number (base 10). **[2]**
**(d)** Solve 3ˣ = 40, giving x to 3 significant figures. **[3]**

## Answers

**1. (a)** √121 = 11 [1]. The integers are **14, -6 and √121** [1].
**(b)** √45 = 3√5 does not terminate or recur, so **√45** [1]. **[3]**
*Examiner insight:* Evaluate roots before classifying. Leaving √121 unevaluated makes it easy to miss that it is an integer.

**2.** Let x = 0.4777... Then 10x = 4.777... and 100x = 47.777... [1]
Subtract: 90x = 43 [1]
x = **43/90** [1] **[3]**
*Examiner insight:* Subtract so that the recurring tails cancel, and cancel the fraction at the end. Check whether the fraction cancels; here 43 is prime, so it does not.

**3. (a)** 84 = 2² × 3 × 7 [1]; 120 = 2³ × 3 × 5 [1].
**(b)** LCM = 2³ × 3 × 5 × 7 = 840 minutes [1]. 840 minutes = 14 hours, so the next joint departure is at **20:00** [1]. **[4]**
*Examiner insight:* Finish in the form the question uses, here a clock time. The number 840 alone does not say when.

**4. (a)** √98 = 7√2 and √32 = 4√2 [1]. Difference = **3√2** [1].
**(b)** 10/√5 = 10√5/5 = **2√5** [1].
**(c)** 9 - 3√5 + 3√5 - 5 [1] = **4** [1]. **[5]**
*Examiner insight:* Show the simplification of each surd before combining. A decimal approximation for part (a) loses the exact form the question asks for.

**5. (a)** 5⁻²⁺⁵⁻² = 5¹ [1] = **5** [1].
**(b)** 2.4 ÷ 6 = 0.4 and 10⁶ ÷ 10⁻³ = 10⁹ [1]. 0.4 × 10⁹ = **4 × 10⁸** [1].
**(c)** **3.05 × 10⁻³** [1]. **[5]**
*Examiner insight:* After dividing the numbers you may get 0.4. It is not in standard form until you adjust it so that the first factor is between 1 and 10.

**6.** Estimate: 300 × 0.05 ÷ 2 [1] = **7.5** [1].
Exact: 305.8 × 0.0489 ÷ 1.97 = 7.59 [1].
The estimate is close, so the exact value is reasonable [1]. **[4]**
*Examiner insight:* Show the rounded numbers. A bare estimate with no rounded values is hard to credit, and a comment must refer to the two values.

**7. (a)** Excluding -2 and including 4: **-1, 0, 1, 2, 3, 4** [2].
**(b)** Closed circle with a left ray: **x ≤ -3** [1]. Open circle with a right ray: **x > 2** [1]. **[4]**
*Examiner insight:* The circle type decides the inequality symbol. Join the two parts with "or", not "and", since no number satisfies both.

**8. (a)** 72 000 m ÷ 3600 s = **20 m/s** [1].
**(b)** 0.9 g/cm³ = 900 kg/m³ [1], as 1 g/cm³ = 1000 kg/m³.
**(c)** Arrival in departure-city time: 23:30 + 6:40 = 06:10 next day [1]. UTC+1 is 7 hours behind UTC+8 [1]. Local arrival **23:10, the same day as departure** [1]. **[5]**
*Examiner insight:* For time zones, state the day as well as the time. The arrival here is earlier on the clock than the departure, which is a good check.

**9. (a)** k = 28 ÷ 8 = 3.5 [1]; y = 3.5 × 18 = **63** [1].
**(b)** Pumps × time is constant: 5 × 9 = 45 [1]. Time = 45 ÷ 4 = 11.25 hours [1] = **11 hours 15 minutes** [1]. **[5]**
*Examiner insight:* Name the type of proportion in words. Converting 0.25 hours to minutes is a separate step that is easy to skip.

**10. (a)** 5000 × 1.035⁴ [1] = **5737.62** [1].
**(b)** Compound interest = 737.62 [1]. Simple interest = 5000 × 0.035 × 4 = 700 [1]. Difference = **37.62** [1].
**(c)** 156 ÷ 1.2 = **130** [1]. **[6]**
*Examiner insight:* Carry full unrounded values forward and round once. Rounding the multiplier 1.035⁴ early can change the final cent. In (c), dividing by 1.2 is correct because 156 is already the increased price.

**11. (a)** 14.15 ≤ length < 14.25 [1] and 6.45 ≤ width < 6.55 [1].
**(b)** Lower area = 14.15 × 6.45 = 91.2675 [1]; upper area = 14.25 × 6.55 = 93.3375 [1].
**(c)** Lower perimeter = 2(14.15 + 6.45) = 41.2 [1]; upper perimeter = 2(14.25 + 6.55) = 41.6 [1].
**(d)** Both bounds round to 90 at 1 significant figure, so the area is **90 cm² to 1 s.f.** [1]. **[7]**
*Examiner insight:* Match each bound to the right pair of values. A claim of accuracy is supported by showing that the lower and upper bounds round to the same figure.

**12. (a)** The fifth root of 32 is 2 [1]; 2³ = **8** [1].
**(b)** 5³ = 125, so **3** [1].
**(c)** log 8 + log 125 = log 1000 [1] = **3** [1].
**(d)** Take logs: x log 3 = log 40 [1]; x = log 40 / log 3 [1] = **3.36** [1]. **[8]**
*Examiner insight:* In (d) write the exact form log 40 / log 3 before the decimal. Check by testing that 3 raised to 3.36 is close to 40.

## Where marks are usually lost

- Leaving a recurring decimal rounded instead of converting it exactly.
- Leaving an LCM answer as a bare number when the question asks for a time.
- Combining unlike surds, such as treating 3√2 + 2√5 as 5√7.
- Reading 5⁻² as -25 instead of 1/25.
- Giving standard form with a first factor of 10 or more, or below 1.
- Using the wrong open or closed circle, or "and" for a two-ray inequality.
- Taking 1.035⁴ as 4 × 1.035, or rounding the multiplier too early.
- Stating a bound without strict and non-strict inequality symbols.
- Failing to comment on whether a result is reasonable in context.
