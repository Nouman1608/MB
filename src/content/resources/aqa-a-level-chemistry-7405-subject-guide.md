AQA A-Level Chemistry (7405) builds a detailed picture of how substances are structured, how they react and why,
across the three traditional branches of the subject. It is taken by students heading towards chemistry, medicine,
pharmacy, chemical engineering, natural sciences and many other science-based degrees. The qualification is
linear and not tiered. It is assessed at the end of the course by three written papers, each lasting 2 hours:
Papers 1 and 2 are worth 105 marks and 35% each, and Paper 3 is worth 90 marks and 30%.

## How the course is organised

The specification lists 34 topics, grouped into physical, inorganic and organic chemistry. Each topic is marked as
AS-stage or A-level-stage content.

**Physical chemistry (topics 1 to 12).** The AS-stage topics are atomic structure, amount of substance, bonding,
energetics, kinetics, chemical equilibria with Le Chatelier's principle and Kc, and oxidation, reduction and redox
equations. The A-level-stage topics are thermodynamics, rate equations, the equilibrium constant Kp for
homogeneous systems, electrode potentials and electrochemical cells, and acids and bases.

**Inorganic chemistry (topics 13 to 18).** Periodicity, Group 2 and Group 7(17) are AS-stage. The properties of
Period 3 elements and their oxides, transition metals and reactions of ions in aqueous solution are A-level-stage.

**Organic chemistry (topics 19 to 34).** The AS-stage topics are the introduction to organic chemistry, alkanes,
halogenoalkanes, alkenes, alcohols and organic analysis. The A-level-stage topics are optical isomerism, aldehydes
and ketones, carboxylic acids and derivatives, aromatic chemistry, amines, polymers, amino acids, proteins and DNA,
organic synthesis, nuclear magnetic resonance spectroscopy, and chromatography.

## How it's assessed

| Component | Duration | Marks | Weighting | Content |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 7405/1 Paper 1 | 2 hours | 105 | 35% | Relevant physical chemistry, all inorganic chemistry, relevant practical skills |
| 7405/2 Paper 2 | 2 hours | 105 | 35% | Relevant physical chemistry, all organic chemistry, relevant practical skills |
| 7405/3 Paper 3 | 2 hours | 90 | 30% | Any content and any practical skills |

AQA specifies the physical chemistry for each paper by section number. Paper 1 draws on sections 3.1.1-3.1.4,
3.1.6-3.1.8 and 3.1.10-3.1.12, together with inorganic chemistry (section 3.2). Paper 2 draws on sections
3.1.2-3.1.6 and 3.1.9, together with organic chemistry (section 3.3). Both are made up of short and long answer
questions.

Paper 3 is different in character. It carries 40 marks of questions on practical techniques and data analysis,
20 marks of questions testing across the specification, and 30 marks of multiple-choice questions. The total for
the qualification is 300 marks. The specification was first taught in September 2015 and first assessed at
A-level in June 2017.

## How the parts fit together

Physical chemistry is the thread that ties the papers together. Some sections, such as amount of substance,
bonding, energetics and equilibria, are listed for both Paper 1 and Paper 2, because inorganic and organic
reactions alike are explained using those ideas. A student who is shaky on moles, bond enthalpies or equilibrium
will feel it across the whole course, not only in the physical topics.

There are strong links within each strand as well. Atomic structure and bonding explain the trends studied in
periodicity and the groups. Redox from topic 7 returns in electrode potentials and transition metals. Organic
chemistry is a network rather than a list: each functional group is linked to others by reactions, and organic
synthesis and analysis (organic analysis, NMR spectroscopy and chromatography) ask students to move around that
network confidently.

Practical skills are assessed on every paper, and Paper 3 gives them a large share of its marks alongside
questions that cut across the specification. Keeping clear notes on the methods, measurements and calculations
from practical work is therefore a long-term investment.

## Planning your study

The three strands can be taught side by side, which suits the way the papers share content. A workable route
is:

1. **Secure the physical foundations early.** Atomic structure, amount of substance and bonding are needed almost
   immediately in every strand.
2. **Complete the AS-stage topics in all three strands during the first year**, including energetics, kinetics,
   equilibria, redox, periodicity, the groups and the first organic topics.
3. **In the second year, add the A-level-stage topics**, revisiting the AS-stage content each one builds on:
   thermodynamics after energetics, rate equations after kinetics, Kp after Kc.
4. **Finish organic chemistry with synthesis and analysis**, which draw the earlier functional groups together.

Build and update a reaction map for organic chemistry as you go, and a short sheet of key calculations for
physical chemistry. Topic questions can be used from the first weeks; full papers are most useful once a paper's
content has been taught, with Paper 3 practice best left until most of the course is complete. Paper-by-paper
strategy and timing are covered on the exam preparation page.

## Frequently asked questions

**How many papers does AQA A-Level Chemistry have?**
Three written papers, each lasting 2 hours. Papers 1 and 2 are worth 105 marks and 35% each; Paper 3 is worth 90 marks and 30%.

**What is on each AQA A-Level Chemistry paper?**
Paper 1 covers relevant physical chemistry and inorganic chemistry; Paper 2 covers relevant physical chemistry and organic chemistry; Paper 3 can cover any content and any practical skills.

**Does AQA A-Level Chemistry include multiple-choice questions?**
Yes. Paper 3 includes 30 marks of multiple-choice questions, alongside 40 marks on practical techniques and data analysis and 20 marks testing across the specification.

**How many marks is the qualification out of?**
The three papers total 300 marks.

**When was the 7405 specification first examined?**
It was first taught in September 2015 and first assessed at A-level in June 2017.
