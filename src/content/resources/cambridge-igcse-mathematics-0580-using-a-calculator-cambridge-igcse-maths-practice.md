These questions cover subtopic 1.14 Using a calculator of the Cambridge IGCSE Mathematics 0580 syllabus for examination in 2025, 2026 and 2027. The content is the same for Core and Extended. A calculator is needed for every question, because the syllabus tests this subtopic only on the calculator papers (Papers 3 and 4). Give non-exact answers correct to 3 significant figures unless the question says otherwise.

## Section A

**1.** (calculator)

**(a)** Work out (7.25 − 1.8²) ÷ (0.6 + 2.15). Write down all the figures on your calculator display. **[1]**

**(b)** Write your answer to part (a) correct to 3 significant figures. **[1]**

**2.** (calculator) Work out √(36.8 ÷ 2.3) + 4.1³. Give your answer correct to 3 significant figures. **[2]**

**3.** (calculator) Work out

**(a)** (−3.2)³, **[1]**

**(b)** −3.2². **[1]**

**4.** (calculator) Work out (6.3 × 10⁻⁴) × (2.8 × 10⁷). Give your answer in standard form. **[2]**

**5.** (calculator) A car travels 247 km in 3 hours 40 minutes. Find its average speed. **[2]**

**6.** (calculator)

**(a)** In a money calculation in dollars, the display shows 13.4. Write down the amount of money. **[1]**

**(b)** In a time calculation in hours, the display shows 1.35. Write this time in hours and minutes. **[1]**

## Section B

**7.** (calculator) The formula f = uv ÷ (u + v) is used in optics. Find f when u = 12.5 and v = 30.4. **[2]**

**8.** (calculator) A cylindrical can has radius 3.8 cm and height 11.5 cm. The volume of a cylinder is V = πr²h.

**(a)** Work out the volume of the can. Write down all the figures on your calculator display. **[1]**

**(b)** A tank holds 25 litres of juice. 1 litre = 1000 cm³. How many cans can be completely filled from the tank? **[2]**

**9.** (calculator) Nina runs 18.4 km at an average speed of 11.5 km/h. She starts at 06 50.

**(a)** Find the time she takes, in hours and minutes. **[2]**

**(b)** Find the time she finishes. **[1]**

**10.** (calculator) Seven friends share a restaurant bill of $96.50 equally. Each person pays the same whole number of cents, and together they must pay at least the full bill.

**(a)** How much does each person pay? **[2]**

**(b)** How much more than the bill do they pay altogether? **[1]**

**11.** (calculator) A room measures 5.35 m by 4.18 m. Carpet costs $27.95 per m².

**(a)** Find the cost of carpeting the room. **[2]**

**(b)** Ali works out the area, writes it as 22.4 m², and then finds the cost as $626.08. Explain why his answer is wrong. **[1]**

**12.** (calculator) A satellite takes 1.5275 hours to complete one orbit.

**(a)** Write 1.5275 hours in hours, minutes and seconds. **[2]**

**(b)** Find the number of complete orbits the satellite makes in 24 hours. **[2]**

## Answers

**1. (a)** **1.458181818** [1]

**(b)** **1.46** [1] **[2]**

*Examiner insight:* in (a) the whole display must be copied. Writing 1.458 or 1.46 in part (a) loses that mark.

**2.** √16 = 4 and 4.1³ = 68.921 seen, or 72.921 [1]; **72.9** [1] **[2]**

**3. (a)** **−32.768** [1]

**(b)** **−10.24** [1] **[2]**

*Examiner insight:* in (b) only 3.2 is squared, and the result is then made negative. If the question had meant (−3.2)², it would show the brackets.

**4.** 17 640 [1]; **1.764 × 10⁴** [1] **[2]**

**5.** 3 h 40 min = 3 + 40 ÷ 60 = 3.666... h [1]; 247 ÷ 3.666... = 67.36... = **67.4 km/h** [1] **[2]**

*Examiner insight:* using 3.4 hours gives 72.6 km/h and no marks; using 3.67 gives 67.3 km/h, which is wrong in the third figure because the time was rounded early.

**6. (a)** **$13.40** [1]

**(b)** 0.35 × 60 = 21, so **1 hour 21 minutes** [1] **[2]**

**7.** 12.5 × 30.4 ÷ (12.5 + 30.4) = 380 ÷ 42.9 [1] = 8.8578... = **8.86** [1] **[2]**

*Examiner insight:* without brackets round the denominator, the calculator gives 12.5 × 30.4 ÷ 12.5 + 30.4 = 60.8.

**8. (a)** π × 3.8² × 11.5 = **521.6928761** cm³ [1]

**(b)** 25 litres = 25 000 cm³, and 25 000 ÷ 521.69... = 47.92... [1]; **47 cans** [1] **[3]**

*Examiner insight:* the question asks for completely filled cans, so round down. Rounding 47.92 to 48 suggests a 48th can is full when it is not.

**9. (a)** 18.4 ÷ 11.5 = 1.6 hours [1]; 0.6 × 60 = 36, so **1 hour 36 minutes** [1]

**(b)** 06 50 + 1 h 36 min = **08 26** [1] **[3]**

**10. (a)** 96.50 ÷ 7 = 13.7857... [1]; rounding up so the bill is covered: **$13.79** [1]

**(b)** 7 × 13.79 = 96.53, so **3 cents** more [1] **[3]**

*Examiner insight:* the usual rounding rule would also give $13.79 here, but the reason is the context: $13.78 each would collect only $96.46.

**11. (a)** Area = 5.35 × 4.18 = 22.363 m² [1]; 22.363 × 27.95 = 625.045... = **$625.05** [1]

**(b)** He rounded the area before multiplying, so his cost is not accurate; he should use the full value 22.363 and round only the final answer [1] **[3]**

**12. (a)** 0.5275 × 60 = 31.65 minutes [1]; 0.65 × 60 = 39 seconds, so **1 hour 31 minutes 39 seconds** [1]

**(b)** 24 ÷ 1.5275 = 15.71... [1]; **15 complete orbits** [1] **[4]**

## Where marks are usually lost

- Rounding in a "write down all the figures" part.
- Missing brackets round a denominator, as in Question 7.
- Typing −3.2² when (−3.2)² or (−3.2)³ was meant.
- Typing 3 h 40 min as 3.40 or 3.4.
- Giving money with one decimal place, such as $13.4.
- Rounding a decimal number of hours straight into minutes, so 1.35 hours becomes 1 hour 35 minutes.
- Rounding up when only complete items count, or down when a bill must be covered.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is "Using a calculator" tested on Papers 1 and 2?**
No. The 0580 syllabus excludes subtopic 1.14 from Paper 1 and Paper 2. It is tested on Papers 3 and 4.

**What should I write if my answer is exact, like 10.4976?**
Write it exactly. The 3 significant figure rule is for answers that are not exact.

**How do I convert a decimal number of hours to minutes and seconds?**
Multiply the decimal part by 60 to get minutes. If the minutes have a decimal part, multiply that by 60 to get seconds.
