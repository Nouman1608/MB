These notes cover **Exercise 1, Sentence transformation**, of 3248 Paper 2: "candidates read five short sentences and transform the sentences using given prompts, without changing their original meaning". For the whole paper see the [Paper 2 Grammar, Writing and Translation study guide](/resources/o-level-cambridge-urdu-second-language-paper-2-grammar-writing-translation/); for worked examples see the [Exercise 1 Sentence transformation study guide](/resources/cambridge-o-level-urdu-language-3248-exercise-1-sentence-transformation/).

## Task facts

| Fact | Detail |
|---|---|
| Marks | 5 |
| Objectives | R2, W3, W4, W5 |
| Task | transform five short sentences using given prompts |
| Rule | the original meaning must not change |
| Language | questions and answers in Urdu; dictionaries not allowed |
| Paper | 1 hour 30 minutes, 50 marks, four exercises |
| Not printed in the syllabus | the list of changes that may be asked; read each prompt on the paper |

## Routine for every sentence

1. Say the meaning of the original.
2. Underline the prompt.
3. Decide what must change and what must stay.
4. Write the whole new sentence.
5. Check meaning, agreement, tense, punctuation.

## Rules for the common changes

| Change | Rule | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Active to passive | object becomes subject; participle + *گیا / گئی / گئے / گئیں* agrees with it | استاد نے سبق پڑھایا۔ → سبق پڑھایا گیا۔ |
| Direct to indirect speech | remove quotation marks; add *کہ*; change person and time words | اس نے کہا، "میں آؤں گا۔" → اس نے کہا کہ وہ آئے گا۔ |
| Equivalent negative | *کوئی ... نہیں* + the opposite word | ہر دکان بند تھی۔ → کوئی دکان کھلی نہیں تھی۔ |
| Combining: contrast | *اگرچہ ... ، لیکن / پھر بھی* | اگرچہ موسم خراب تھا، پرواز روانہ ہوئی۔ |
| Combining: reason | *اس لیے / کیونکہ* | میں بیمار تھا، اس لیے نہیں آیا۔ |
| Combining: condition | *اگر ... تو* | اگر بارش ہوئی تو ہم نہیں جائیں گے۔ |
| Respect | *تم* becomes *آپ*; verb takes the plural form | تم کہاں جا رہے ہو؟ → آپ کہاں جا رہے ہیں؟ |
| Tense | change the verb and the time word | وہ پڑھتا ہے۔ → وہ پڑھتا تھا / پڑھے گا۔ |
| Number or gender | change subject and agreeing verb or adjective | لڑکا جاتا ہے۔ → لڑکیاں جاتی ہیں۔ |

## Agreement reminders

- In the simple past with *نے*, the verb agrees with the **object**, not the subject: *لڑکی نے خط لکھا* becomes *لڑکیوں نے خط لکھا*, and the verb stays *لکھا* because *خط* is masculine singular. With a plural object: *لڑکیوں نے خط لکھے*.
- Present and future tenses agree with the **subject**: *وہ جاتی ہے* but *وہ جاتے ہیں*.
- *آپ* takes plural verbs: *آپ آئے*، *آپ جاتے ہیں*.
- A noun in the passive keeps its gender and number and the verb *گیا* agrees with it.

## Person and time words in indirect speech

| Direct | Indirect |
|---|---|
| میں / ہم | وہ / وہ لوگ (matching the speaker) |
| تم / آپ | میں / ہم (matching who is spoken to) |
| یہاں | وہاں |
| آج | اسی دن |
| کل (future) | اگلے دن |
| ابھی | اسی وقت |

Check each time, because the right change depends on the sentence.

## Meaning-preserving checks

| Original | Valid? | Why |
|---|---|---|
| سب مہمان آئے۔ → کوئی مہمان غیر حاضر نہیں تھا۔ | yes | same meaning |
| سب مہمان آئے۔ → کچھ مہمان آئے۔ | no | weaker meaning |
| سب مہمان آئے۔ → کوئی مہمان نہیں آیا۔ | no | opposite meaning |

## Reading the prompt

| Prompt style | What to do |
|---|---|
| A starting phrase (*اس نے کہا کہ*) | begin with exactly those words |
| A word to use (*اگرچہ*، *کوئی ... نہیں*) | include it in the right place |
| An instruction (*مجہول جملہ لکھیے*) | change the voice and nothing else |
| A change of respect (*احترام کے ساتھ*) | change pronoun and verb |

Always keep every idea of the original: time, place, object and reason. If a detail has no place in the new structure, find a way to keep it.

## One sentence in slow motion

Original: *استاد نے طلبہ کو نیا سبق پڑھایا۔* Prompt: *مجہول جملہ لکھیے۔*

1. Meaning: the teacher taught the students a new lesson.
2. Prompt: passive voice, so the focus moves from the teacher to the thing done.
3. Keep: *طلبہ کو*، *نیا سبق*. Change: the verb becomes *پڑھایا گیا*, and the agent *استاد نے* can be dropped.
4. Write: *طلبہ کو نیا سبق پڑھایا گیا۔*
5. Check: the verb agrees with *سبق* (masculine singular), the meaning is the same and the sentence ends with *۔*.

## Common faults

- Meaning changed.
- Person not changed in indirect speech.
- Verb agreement wrong after the change.
- A part of the sentence dropped.
- Prompt ignored.
- Missing *۔* or quotation marks left in.

## Key terms

| Urdu | English |
|---|---|
| تبدیلی | change, transformation |
| مجہول | passive voice |
| معروف | active voice |
| راست تقریر | direct speech |
| بالواسطہ تقریر | indirect speech |
| زمانہ | tense |
| مذکر / مؤنث | masculine / feminine |
| واحد / جمع | singular / plural |
| ربط / حرفِ عطف | connective |
| احترام | respect |

## Exam traps

- A negative sentence is not always the opposite meaning, so check it.
- *تم* to *آپ* changes the verb as well.
- In indirect speech, *میں* does not stay *میں*.
- Do not write two sentences if the prompt asks for one.

## Self-test

1. How many marks is Exercise 1 worth?
2. What must not change in a transformation?
3. Put into the passive: *ڈاکٹر نے دوا دی۔*
4. Put into indirect speech: *اس نے کہا، "میں تھکا ہوا ہوں۔"*
5. Which connective expresses contrast?
6. Rewrite with *آپ*: *تم کیا کر رہے ہو؟*
7. Give a valid sentence for: *ہر کمرہ خالی تھا۔* using *کوئی ... نہیں*.
8. Why does *لڑکیوں نے خط لکھا* keep *لکھا*?
9. Name the Urdu term for "passive voice".
10. What should you do if the prompt names a word to use?

**Answers**

1. Five.
2. The original meaning.
3. *دوا دی گئی۔*
4. *اس نے کہا کہ وہ تھکا ہوا ہے۔*
5. *اگرچہ* (or *لیکن*).
6. *آپ کیا کر رہے ہیں؟*
7. *کوئی کمرہ بھرا ہوا نہیں تھا۔*
8. In the past tense with *نے*, the verb agrees with the object *خط*, which is masculine singular.
9. مجہول.
10. Use that word in your sentence and build the rest of the sentence around it.
