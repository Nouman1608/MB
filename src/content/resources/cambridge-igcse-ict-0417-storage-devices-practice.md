## Section B: structured questions

**7.** A video games company sells a game on Blu-ray disc.

**(a)** Explain why a Blu-ray disc is used rather than a CD. **[2]**

**(b)** Give **one** reason why the game is supplied on a read-only disc. **[1]**

**8.** A bank backs up all its customer account data every night and keeps the backups for seven years.

**(a)** Recommend a storage device for the backups. **[1]**

**(b)** Give **two** reasons for your recommendation. **[2]**

**(c)** Explain why this device would not be suitable for the bank's live customer database. **[2]**

**9.** A laptop manufacturer replaces hard disk drives with SSDs in all its models.

**(a)** Explain **three** benefits to laptop users. **[3]**

**(b)** Give **one** reason why the laptops may cost more. **[1]**

**10.** A photographer copies her images from her desktop computer to a portable hard drive each week.

**(a)** Explain why she keeps the portable hard drive in a different building. **[2]**

**(b)** Give **one** risk to the portable hard drive and **one** way to reduce the risk to her data. **[2]**

**11.** A server in an office stores files on fixed hard disk drives. A visitor knocks the server while it is running and the drive fails.

Explain why the drive may have been damaged and why an SSD would have been less likely to fail. **[3]**

**12.** A student carries coursework between home and school on a pen drive.

**(a)** Give **two** advantages of a pen drive for this purpose. **[2]**

**(b)** Describe **two** problems that could occur. **[2]**

## Answers

**1.** Read-write head (credit the arm carrying the heads). **[1]**

**2.** Serial (sequential) access. **[1]**

**3.** B: Blu-ray drive. **[1]**

**4.** Any two: solid-state flash memory (1); no moving parts (1); small and light (1); built-in USB connector (1); powered through the USB port (1). **[2]**

**5.** The device can go straight to any item of data without reading through the data stored before it. **[1]**

**6.** Any two: files are increasingly downloaded or stored in the cloud instead of on discs (1); removing the drive makes the laptop thinner and lighter (1); saves cost and power (1); pen drives and portable drives have replaced discs for transferring files (1). **[2]**

**7. (a)** A Blu-ray disc has a much larger capacity than a CD (1), so the large game files, including high-definition graphics, fit on one disc (1). **[2]**

**(b)** Any one: the customer cannot change or delete the game files (1); helps to prevent the disc being overwritten by accident (1). **[1]**

**8. (a)** Magnetic tape drive. **[1]**

**(b)** Any two: very large capacity for the whole database (1); very low cost per gigabyte (1); cartridges are robust and can be kept for many years (1); cartridges can be removed and stored off-site (1). **[2]**

**(c)** Tape uses serial access (1), so finding one customer's record would mean winding through the tape, which is far too slow when customers need their balance immediately (1). **[2]**

**9. (a)** Any three: faster access, so the laptop starts and opens files more quickly (1); no moving parts, so less likely to be damaged if dropped or knocked (1); uses less power, so the battery lasts longer (1); silent (1); lighter and thinner (1). **[3]**

**(b)** SSDs have a higher cost per gigabyte than hard disk drives (1). **[1]**

**10. (a)** If the building with the desktop suffers a fire, flood or theft (1), the copy on the portable drive survives, so the images can be restored (1). **[2]**

**(b)** Risk, any one: dropped and damaged because of moving parts; lost or stolen (1). Reduction, any one: encrypt the drive so data cannot be read if stolen; keep a second backup, for example in cloud storage; store it in a padded case (1). **[2]**

**11.** The read-write heads are very close to the spinning platters (1), so a knock can make a head touch the surface and damage it (a head crash) (1). An SSD has no moving parts, so a knock is much less likely to damage it (1). **[3]**

**12. (a)** Any two: small and light to carry (1); robust because it has no moving parts (1); works with almost any computer's USB port (1); fast to copy files (1). **[2]**

**(b)** Any two: the pen drive could be lost or forgotten, so the work is not available (1); it could be stolen or found by someone else (1); a virus could be carried from one computer to the other (1); the student could open an older version by mistake (1); the drive could be damaged or fail (1). **[2]**

**13.** Any four, with points about both: an SSD has faster access, so large video files open and save more quickly (1); a hard disk drive has a lower cost per gigabyte, so more video can be stored for the same price (1); a hard disk drive has moving parts, so it is more likely to fail if knocked, whereas an SSD has none (1); an SSD is silent and uses less power (1); a hard disk drive can offer larger capacity for the same cost, which matters because video files are large (1). **[4]**

## Frequently asked questions

**Is a memory card a storage device?**
No. In 0417 a memory card is a storage medium, read by a card reader or a camera. The storage media row covers SD, xD and CFast cards.

**Do I need to know USB versions or connection speeds?**
No. The syllabus asks for characteristics, uses, advantages and disadvantages, not technical standards.
