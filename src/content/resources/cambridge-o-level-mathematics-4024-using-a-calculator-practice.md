These questions cover subtopic 1.14 Using a calculator of Cambridge O Level Mathematics (Syllabus D) 4024, 2025 to 2027. This subtopic is assessed on the calculator paper, so every question needs a scientific calculator. Give answers to 3 significant figures unless the question says otherwise. Write down the unrounded value in your working.

## Section A

**1.** Work out 12.8 ÷ (3.6 - 1.15). **[2]**

**2.** Work out √(6.4² + 3.7²). **[2]**

**3.** A car travels 160 km in 2 hours 30 minutes. Work out its average speed in km/h. **[2]**

**4.** Write each calculator display in the form asked.

**(a)** The display shows 3.25 hours. Write this in hours and minutes. **[1]**

**(b)** The display shows 4.8 for an amount of money in dollars. Write the amount correctly. **[1]**

**(c)** The display shows 0.7 hours. Write this in minutes. **[1]**

**5.** Mia works out 2.34 × 5.67 × 1.5.

**(a)** Write down the exact answer correct to 3 significant figures. **[1]**

**(b)** She rounds 2.34 × 5.67 to 13.3 first and then multiplies by 1.5. Write her answer correct to 3 significant figures. **[1]**

**(c)** Explain why her method is not suitable. **[1]**

**6.** Work out (-3.2)² and -3.2². Explain why the two answers are different. **[3]**

**7.** A circle has radius 6.3 cm. Work out its circumference, correct to 3 significant figures, using the π key on your calculator. **[2]**

## Section B

**8.** Work out (4.2 × 10⁶) × (3.5 × 10⁻³). Give your answer in standard form. **[2]**

**9.** Work out 2 3/8 + 1 5/6. Give your answer as a mixed number. **[2]**

**10.** Change 37.65° to degrees and minutes. **[2]**

**11.** A sum of $1 500 is invested at 4% compound interest per year for 6 years. Work out the value of the investment at the end, to the nearest cent. **[2]**

**12.** Work out 5 + 4 × 3² ÷ 2. Say what answer a calculator would give if it ignored the usual order of operations and worked from left to right. **[3]**

**13.** A student types 12.8 ÷ 3.6 - 1.15 and gets 2.41. The correct working should be 12.8 ÷ (3.6 - 1.15). Explain the error and give the correct answer. **[3]**

**14.** A calculator shows 0.0125 as the result of dividing 1 by 80. Write this value as a percentage. **[1]**

## Where marks are usually lost

- Leaving out the brackets round a denominator in Question 1.
- Typing 2.30 for 2 hours 30 minutes in Question 3.
- Reading 3.25 hours as 3 hours 25 minutes in Question 4(a).
- Writing 4.8 for money in Question 4(b).
- Rounding part of the way through the calculation in Question 5.
- Copying the calculator display for standard form in Question 8.
- Rounding the interest each year in Question 11.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I use a graphical calculator in the exam?**
No. The syllabus requires a scientific calculator for Paper 2 and says that algebraic or graphical calculators are not permitted.

**Should I write the unrounded value in my working?**
Yes. Write the full value from the display, then the rounded answer. This protects your method marks and your accuracy mark.
