Pearson Edexcel GCSE Business (1BS0) follows the life of a business
from the first spark of an idea to growth into a larger organisation.
Students learn how entrepreneurs spot opportunities, how small firms
are set up and run, and how growing businesses make decisions about
marketing, operations, finance and people. The course is linear and
assessed by two written papers, one for each theme, each lasting
1 hour 45 minutes, marked out of 90 and worth 50%. It was first taught
in September 2017 and first assessed in 2019, and the current
specification is Issue 2 (July 2022).

## How the course is organised

The content is split into two themes of five topic areas each.

**Theme 1: Investigating small business**

- **1.1 Enterprise and entrepreneurship:** the dynamic nature of
  business, risk and reward, and the role of business enterprise.
- **1.2 Spotting a business opportunity:** customer needs, market
  research, market segmentation and the competitive environment.
- **1.3 Putting a business idea into practice:** aims and objectives;
  revenues, costs and profits; cash and cash-flow; and sources of
  finance.
- **1.4 Making the business effective:** options for start-ups and
  small businesses, location, the marketing mix and business plans.
- **1.5 Understanding external influences on business:** stakeholders,
  technology, legislation, the economy and other external influences.

**Theme 2: Building a business**

- **2.1 Growing the business:** business growth, changes in aims,
  globalisation, and ethics and the environment.
- **2.2 Making marketing decisions:** product, price, promotion, place
  and using the marketing mix together.
- **2.3 Making operational decisions:** business operations, managing
  stock and suppliers, managing quality and the sales process.
- **2.4 Making financial decisions:** business calculations and
  understanding business data.
- **2.5 Making human resource decisions:** organisational structures,
  recruitment, training and development, and motivation.

## How it's assessed

| Paper | Title | Duration | Marks | Weighting |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1BS0/01 | Theme 1: Investigating small business | 1 hour 45 minutes | 90 | 50% |
| 1BS0/02 | Theme 2: Building a business | 1 hour 45 minutes | 90 | 50% |

Both papers are externally assessed and share the same structure:

- **Section A** is worth 35 marks.
- **Section B** is worth 30 marks.
- **Section C** is worth 25 marks.

Across the sections, question types include calculations,
multiple-choice, short-answer and extended-writing questions.
Calculators may be used in both papers. All assessment takes place in
a single May/June series at the end of the course, so both papers are
sat together.

## How the parts fit together

The two themes tell one continuous story. Theme 1 is about a business
at the very start of its life: someone with an idea, assessing the
market, finding money and dealing with the outside world. Theme 2 picks
up the same business as it grows, when decisions become larger and
more structured.

Many ideas therefore appear twice, at different scales. The marketing
mix is introduced in Theme 1 as part of making a small business
effective, then explored element by element in Theme 2's marketing
decisions. Revenue, costs, profit and cash-flow in Theme 1 develop into
the business calculations and data interpretation of Theme 2. Aims and
objectives in Theme 1 become changes in aims as a business grows.
External influences such as the economy and legislation in Theme 1
widen into globalisation, ethics and the environment in Theme 2.

Two skills run through both papers. The first is calculation: working
out figures such as profit or cash-flow and then explaining what they
mean. The second is analysis and evaluation in extended writing, where
the strongest answers apply ideas to the business described in the
question and reach a justified decision. Practising both skills on
every topic is far more effective than leaving them until the end.

## Planning your study

The specification order is a natural teaching order, because Theme 2
builds on Theme 1.

- **First year:** work through Theme 1 from enterprise to external
  influences. Introduce simple calculations early, through revenue,
  costs, profit and cash-flow, and practise short written answers
  applied to small business examples.
- **Second year:** cover Theme 2, linking each topic back to its Theme 1
  starting point. Give extra time to business calculations and
  understanding business data, which support questions in many other
  topics.
- **Final months:** review both themes together, since both papers are
  sat in the same series, and move on to timed practice.

Follow current business news alongside your study. Real examples of
firms launching, growing or struggling help you apply ideas to the
cases you meet in the exam. Begin full past papers once a theme is
complete, and use the exam-preparation page for detailed advice on each
paper and its sections.

## Frequently asked questions

**How many papers are in Edexcel GCSE Business 1BS0?**
Two. Paper 1 assesses Theme 1, Investigating small business, and Paper 2 assesses Theme 2, Building a business. Each lasts 1 hour 45 minutes, is out of 90 marks and is worth 50%.

**What sections are in each GCSE Business paper?**
Each paper has three sections: Section A worth 35 marks, Section B worth 30 marks and Section C worth 25 marks.

**Can I use a calculator in Edexcel GCSE Business?**
Yes. Calculators may be used in both papers.

**Is Edexcel GCSE Business tiered?**
No. There is a single set of papers for all candidates.

**When are the GCSE Business exams taken?**
All assessment takes place in a single May/June series at the end of the course.
