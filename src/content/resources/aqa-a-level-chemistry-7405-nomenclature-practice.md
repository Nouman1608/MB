These questions cover section **3.3.1.1 Nomenclature** of the AQA AS and A-level Chemistry specification (7404/7405), for teaching from September 2015. It is AS content, examined on Paper 2. Learn the rules first in the [Nomenclature study guide](/resources/aqa-a-level-chemistry-7405-nomenclature/) and the [Nomenclature revision notes](/resources/aqa-a-level-chemistry-7405-nomenclature-revision-notes/). Structures are given as structural formulas; if a question asks you to draw, give a structural formula or describe the skeleton.

## Questions

**1.** State what is meant by each term.

**(a)** homologous series **[2]**

**(b)** functional group **[1]**

**2.** Give the empirical formula of each compound.

**(a)** C₆H₁₂O₂ **[1]**

**(b)** C₄H₈ **[1]**

**3.** Name each compound.

**(a)** CH₃CH(CH₃)CH₂CH₃ **[1]**

**(b)** CH₃CH₂CH(Cl)CH₃ **[1]**

**(c)** (CH₃)₃CCH₂CH₃ **[1]**

**4.** Name each compound.

**(a)** CH₃CH(OH)CH₂CH₃ **[1]**

**(b)** CH₃CH(CH₃)CH₂CH₂OH **[1]**

**(c)** CH₃CH(OH)CH(Cl)CH₃ **[2]**

**5.** Name each compound.

**(a)** CH₂=CHCH(CH₃)CH₃ **[1]**

**(b)** (CH₃)₂C=CHCH₃ **[1]**

**6.** Name each compound.

**(a)** CH₃CH(CH₃)CHO **[1]**

**(b)** CH₃CH₂COCH₂CH₃ **[1]**

**(c)** CH₃CH(Br)CH₂COOH **[1]**

**7.** Draw the structural formula of each compound.

**(a)** 2,3-dimethylbutane **[1]**

**(b)** pent-2-ene **[1]**

**(c)** 1,3-dibromopropane **[1]**

**8.** Give the molecular formula and the general formula of the homologous series for each compound.

**(a)** hexan-1-ol **[2]**

**(b)** hex-1-ene **[2]**

**9.** Name each ring compound.

**(a)** a six-membered ring of carbon atoms with one C=C double bond **[1]**

**(b)** a five-membered ring of saturated carbon atoms with a methyl group on one carbon **[1]**

**(c)** a six-membered saturated ring with a chlorine atom on one carbon **[1]**

**10.** A student names CH₃CH₂CH(CH₃)CH₂CH₂CH₃ as 2-ethylpentane. Identify the error and give the correct name. **[2]**

**11.** A student names CH₃CH(OH)CH₂CH₃ as 3-hydroxybutane. Identify the error and give the correct name. **[2]**

**12.** Explain why propanal and propanone have the same molecular formula but different names, and give the structural formula of each. **[3]**

**13.** Give three characteristics of members of a homologous series. **[3]**

**14.** Name each compound.

**(a)** CH₃CH₂CH(CH₃)CH(CH₃)CH₂CH₃ **[2]**

**(b)** BrCH₂CH(CH₃)CH₂CH₃ **[2]**

**(c)** CH₃CH₂CH=CHCH₂CH₃ **[1]**

**(d)** CH₃CH₂CH(OH)CH₂CH₂CH₃ **[1]**

**15.** A skeletal formula shows a zig-zag chain of five carbon atoms with an –OH group on the second carbon from one end. Name the compound and give its molecular formula. **[3]**

**16.** Explain why 2-methylpropane and butane have the same molecular formula but are different compounds, and give the molecular formula. **[2]**

**17.** Draw the structural formula of 2-chloro-2-methylbutane and give its molecular formula. **[2]**

**18.** Propanal and propanone fit the same general formula. State the general formula and name the functional group in each compound. **[3]**

## Answers

**1. (a)** A series of compounds with the same functional group [1]; each member differs from the next by CH₂ and they have the same general formula [1]

**(b)** The atom or group of atoms responsible for the characteristic reactions of a compound [1]. **[3]**

**2. (a)** C₃H₆O [1]

**(b)** CH₂ [1]. **[2]**

**3. (a)** 2-methylbutane [1]

**(b)** 2-chlorobutane [1]

**(c)** 2,2-dimethylbutane [1]. **[3]**

**4. (a)** butan-2-ol [1]

**(b)** 3-methylbutan-1-ol [1]

**(c)** 3-chlorobutan-2-ol [2: 1 for butan-2-ol, 1 for the 3-chloro prefix]. **[4]**

**5. (a)** 3-methylbut-1-ene [1]

**(b)** 2-methylbut-2-ene [1]. **[2]**

**6. (a)** 2-methylpropanal [1]

**(b)** pentan-3-one [1]

**(c)** 3-bromobutanoic acid [1]. **[3]**

**7. (a)** CH₃CH(CH₃)CH(CH₃)CH₃ [1]

**(b)** CH₃CH=CHCH₂CH₃ [1]

**(c)** BrCH₂CH₂CH₂Br [1]. **[3]**

**8. (a)** C₆H₁₄O [1]; CₙH₂ₙ₊₁OH [1]

**(b)** C₆H₁₂ [1]; CₙH₂ₙ [1]. **[4]**

**9. (a)** cyclohexene [1]

**(b)** methylcyclopentane [1]

**(c)** chlorocyclohexane [1]. **[3]**

**10.** The longest chain has six carbons, not five, so the parent name is hexane [1]; the methyl group is on carbon 3, so the correct name is 3-methylhexane [1]. **[2]**

*Examiner insight:* A chain that bends round a corner is still one chain. Count carbons along the whole length before naming.

**11.** The –OH group is the principal functional group, so the name must end in -ol, not use hydroxy- as a prefix [1]; the correct name is butan-2-ol [1]. **[2]**

**12.** Propanal and propanone both have formula C₃H₆O [1]; propanal is an aldehyde with the –CHO group at the end of the chain: CH₃CH₂CHO [1]; propanone is a ketone with C=O between two carbons: CH₃COCH₃ [1]. **[3]**

**13.** Any three from: same functional group; same general formula; successive members differ by CH₂; similar chemical properties; gradual trend in physical properties [3]. **[3]**

**14. (a)** 3,4-dimethylhexane [2: 1 for hexane with two methyls, 1 for the numbers 3,4]

**(b)** 1-bromo-2-methylbutane [2: 1 for the correct parent chain and prefixes, 1 for the numbers]

**(c)** hex-3-ene [1]

**(d)** hexan-3-ol [1]. **[6]**

**15.** Pentan-2-ol [1]; five carbons with an –OH on carbon 2 gives the formula CH₃CH(OH)CH₂CH₂CH₃ [1]; molecular formula C₅H₁₂O [1]. **[3]**

**16.** Both have the molecular formula C₄H₁₀ [1]; they differ in the arrangement of the carbon atoms (a branched chain in 2-methylpropane and a straight chain in butane), so they are structural isomers with different names [1]. **[2]**

**17.** CH₃C(CH₃)(Cl)CH₂CH₃ (a four-carbon chain with Cl and CH₃ both on carbon 2) [1]; molecular formula C₅H₁₁Cl [1]. **[2]**

**18.** The general formula is CₙH₂ₙO [1]; propanal contains the aldehyde group –CHO [1]; propanone contains the ketone group C=O between two carbon atoms [1]. **[3]**

## Where marks are usually lost

- Not choosing the longest chain.
- Numbering from the wrong end.
- Using hydroxy- as a prefix when an alcohol is the principal group.
- Missing the numbers or hyphens in a name.
- Giving molecular formulas that do not match the carbon count.
