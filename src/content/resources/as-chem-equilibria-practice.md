## Section B

**3.** For N₂(g) + 3H₂(g) ⇌ 2NH₃(g), ΔH = −92 kJ mol⁻¹:

**(a)** State and explain the effect of increasing pressure on the yield of ammonia. **[3]**
**(b)** State and explain the effect of increasing temperature on the yield. **[3]**
**(c)** Explain why 450 °C is used industrially even though it lowers the yield. **[3]**
**(d)** State and explain the effect of a catalyst on the position of equilibrium and on the time taken to reach it. **[3]**

**4.** Write the expression for K_c for the reaction above and state its units. **[3]**

**5.** For H₂(g) + I₂(g) ⇌ 2HI(g), at equilibrium a 2.0 dm³ vessel contains 0.20 mol H₂, 0.20 mol I₂ and 1.60 mol HI.

**(a)** Calculate K_c. **[3]**
**(b)** Explain why K_c has no units here. **[2]**
**(c)** State what happens to K_c if the pressure is increased, and explain. **[2]**

---

## Answers

**1.** The **forward and reverse reactions occur at the same rate** [1], and the **concentrations of reactants and products remain constant** — in a closed system [1].

**2.** If a **change is made to a system at equilibrium** [1], the position of equilibrium **shifts so as to oppose that change** [1].

**3. (a)** The **yield increases** [1]. There are **4 moles of gas on the left and 2 on the right** [1], so the equilibrium **shifts to the right, the side with fewer gas molecules**, to reduce the pressure [1].
**(b)** The **yield decreases** [1]. The forward reaction is **exothermic** [1], so raising the temperature shifts the equilibrium in the **endothermic (backward) direction** to absorb the added heat [1].
**(c)** At a lower temperature the yield would be higher but the **rate of reaction would be far too slow** [1]. 450 °C is a **compromise** giving an **acceptable yield within an economically useful time** [1]; unreacted gases are recycled, so the overall conversion remains high [1].

**(d)** A catalyst has **no effect on the position of equilibrium or on the yield** [1], because it **speeds up the forward and reverse reactions equally** [1]; it **reduces the time taken to reach equilibrium** [1].

**4.** K_c = [NH₃]² ÷ ([N₂][H₂]³) [1]. Units: mol⁻² dm⁶ [1] [1].

**5. (a)** Concentrations: H₂ = 0.10, I₂ = 0.10, HI = 0.80 mol dm⁻³ [1]. K_c = (0.80)² ÷ (0.10 × 0.10) [1] = **64** [1].
**(b)** There are the **same number of moles of gas on each side** [1], so the concentration units **cancel** in the expression [1].
**(c)** **No change** [1] — K_c depends only on **temperature** [1].

## Where marks are usually lost

- Saying a catalyst increases the yield.
- Giving the effect of a change without explaining which side has fewer gas moles.
- Forgetting to divide moles by volume before substituting into K_c.
- Claiming K_c changes with pressure or concentration.
