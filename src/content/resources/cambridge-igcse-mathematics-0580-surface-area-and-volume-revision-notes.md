These notes condense subtopic **5.4 Surface area and volume** from Topic 5 Mensuration of the Cambridge IGCSE Mathematics 0580 syllabus for 2025, 2026 and 2027. The Core (C5.4) and Extended (E5.4) statements are identical. For explanations and worked examples on every solid, read the [surface area and volume study guide](/resources/igcse-mathematics-0580-surface-area-and-volume/); to practise, use the [surface area and volume practice questions](/resources/cambridge-igcse-mathematics-0580-surface-area-and-volume-practice/).

## The statement

Carry out calculations and solve problems involving the surface area and volume of a cuboid, prism, cylinder, sphere, pyramid and cone. Answers may be asked for in terms of π. The syllabus says a **prism** is any solid with a uniform cross-section, for example a cylindrical sector.

## Formulas: given and not given

| Solid | Volume | Surface area | On the List of formulas? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cuboid l × w × h | V = lwh | 2(lw + lh + wh) | **neither** |
| Prism, cross-section A, length l | V = Al | add the areas of all faces | volume only |
| Cylinder | V = πr²h | curved 2πrh; total 2πrh + 2πr² | volume and curved area |
| Sphere | V = (4/3)πr³ | 4πr² | both |
| Pyramid, base area A, height h | V = (1/3)Ah | base + all triangular faces | volume only |
| Cone, slant edge l | V = (1/3)πr²h | curved πrl; total πrl + πr² | volume and curved area |

Anything marked "not given" must be learned or built from the faces.

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Volume | Space inside a solid; cubic units |
| Surface area | Total area of every face; square units |
| Curved surface area | Area of the curved part only (cylinder, cone) |
| Cross-section | The shape you see when a prism is cut at right angles to its length |
| Perpendicular height, h | Apex (or top) straight down to the base, at 90° |
| Slant height, l | Along the sloping surface of a cone or face of a pyramid |
| Net | A flat pattern that folds into the solid; its area is the surface area |

## Height or slant height?

```
cone:  l² = r² + h²          (right-angled triangle: radius, height, slant)
       volume uses h; curved surface area uses l
square-based pyramid, base side s:
       slant height of a face = √(h² + (s/2)²)
```

**Worked reminder.** Cone with r = 5 cm, h = 12 cm: l = √(25 + 144) = 13 cm. V = (1/3)π × 25 × 12 = 100π cm³. Curved area = π × 5 × 13 = 65π cm². Total = 65π + 25π = 90π cm².

## Working backwards

| Given | Find | Rearrangement |
|---|---|---|
| cylinder V, r | h | h = V ÷ (πr²) |
| cylinder V, h | r | r = √(V ÷ (πh)) |
| sphere V | r | r = ∛(3V ÷ 4π) |
| sphere surface area S | r | r = √(S ÷ 4π) |
| cone V, r | h | h = 3V ÷ (πr²) |
| cube V | side | side = ∛V |

Keep unrounded values between steps. Convert capacity first: 1 litre = 1000 cm³.

## Problem types

- **Melting and recasting:** volume is conserved. Number of small solids = big volume ÷ small volume; round down for complete solids.
- **Filling:** time = volume ÷ rate, with units matched.
- **Painting or wrapping:** surface area, often with a missing face (an open box has no lid; a cylinder pipe has no ends).
- **Equal volumes:** set two volume expressions equal and solve.

## Exam traps

- Using the slant height in the cone volume, or the perpendicular height in the curved area.
- Forgetting the base (and top) when the question asks for **total** surface area.
- Including a lid that the question says is missing.
- Using the diameter as r.
- Leaving out the (1/3) for pyramids and cones.
- Cubic units on surface area or square units on volume.
- Rounding the radius before the volume step in a reverse problem.
- Treating 1 litre as 100 cm³.

## Self-test

1. Find the volume and surface area of a cuboid 6 cm by 4 cm by 2.5 cm.
2. A cylinder has radius 3 cm and height 10 cm. Find its volume and total surface area in terms of π.
3. A sphere has radius 6 cm. Find its volume and surface area in terms of π.
4. A pyramid has a square base of side 6 cm and perpendicular height 10 cm. Find its volume.
5. A prism has cross-sectional area 14 cm² and length 9 cm. Find its volume.
6. A cylinder has volume 500 cm³ and radius 5 cm. Find its height to 3 s.f.
7. A sphere has surface area 100π cm². Find its radius.
8. A cube has volume 343 cm³. Find its surface area.
9. A cone has volume 12π cm³ and radius 3 cm. Find its height and slant height.
10. A cylinder has radius 10 cm and height 30 cm. Find its capacity in litres to 3 s.f.
11. A cone has radius 4 cm and slant height 5 cm. Find its perpendicular height and its volume in terms of π.
12. A solid has a uniform cross-section that is a sector of a circle. What type of solid is it?

### Answers

1. V = **60 cm³**; SA = 2(24 + 15 + 10) = **98 cm²**
2. V = **90π cm³**; total SA = 60π + 18π = **78π cm²**
3. V = (4/3)π × 216 = **288π cm³**; SA = **144π cm²**
4. (1/3) × 36 × 10 = **120 cm³**
5. 14 × 9 = **126 cm³**
6. 500 ÷ 25π = **6.37 cm**
7. 4πr² = 100π, r² = 25, **r = 5 cm**
8. Side 7 cm; SA = 6 × 49 = **294 cm²**
9. (1/3)π × 9 × h = 12π, so **h = 4 cm** and l = √(9 + 16) = **5 cm**
10. 3000π = 9424.7… cm³ = **9.42 litres**
11. h = √(25 − 16) = **3 cm**; V = (1/3)π × 16 × 3 = **16π cm³**
12. A **prism** (a cylindrical sector).
