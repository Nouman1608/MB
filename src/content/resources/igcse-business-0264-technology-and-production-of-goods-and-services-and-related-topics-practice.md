## Questions

**1.** Define the term automation. **[2]**

**2.** Identify two ways technology is changing production methods in manufacturing. **[2]**

**3.** Pennistone Bakery has 15 employees and makes 3,000 loaves a day. It buys an automated baking line. Output rises to 3,600 loaves a day with the same 15 employees.

**(a)** Calculate labour productivity before and after the change. **[2]**
**(b)** Calculate the percentage increase in labour productivity. **[2]**
**(c)** Explain one disadvantage of the change for the employees. **[2]**

**4.** Explain two advantages of 3D printing for a business that designs prototypes. **[4]**

**5.** Halsfield Pharmacy now accepts contactless payments and has added two self-service tills. Analyse one way this could raise productivity at the pharmacy. **[3]**

**6.** Tolleshunt Metalworks pays four machine operators £21,000 each a year. A robot costing £120,000 could do their work. The owner would make all four operators redundant.

**(a)** Calculate the annual wage saving. **[1]**
**(b)** Calculate the number of years the saving takes to equal the cost of the robot. Give your answer to one decimal place. **[2]**
**(c)** Evaluate whether the owner should buy the robot. **[6]**

**7.** Identify two ways a business can be more sustainable. **[2]**

**8.** Distinguish between reusing and recycling, using one example of each. **[3]**

**9.** Corlett Garden Centre buys 80,000 plastic plant pots a year at 9p each. A supplier offers pots made from recycled material at 12p each.

**(a)** Calculate the yearly cost of each type of pot. **[2]**
**(b)** Calculate the extra yearly cost of the recycled pots. **[1]**
**(c)** Explain one reason why Corlett might still choose the recycled pots. **[2]**

**10.** Explain one advantage and one disadvantage to a business of using environmentally friendly packaging. **[4]**

**11.** Mirebeck Laundry has an electricity bill of £18,000 a year. Solar panels costing £27,000 would supply 40% of the electricity it uses.

**(a)** Calculate the yearly saving on the electricity bill. **[1]**
**(b)** Calculate the number of years the saving takes to equal the cost of the panels. **[2]**
**(c)** Recommend, with justification, whether Mirebeck Laundry should fit the panels. **[6]**

## Answers

**1.** The use of machines or computer-controlled systems [1] to carry out tasks with little or no human input once set up [1]. **[2]**
*Examiner insight:* A definition earns credit for the idea of little human involvement; "using machines" alone does not separate automation from mechanisation.

**2.** One method [1] and a second, different method [1], from: automation of tasks; mechanisation, with machines replacing hand work; Computer-Aided Manufacture (CAM); 3D printing. **[2]**
*Examiner insight:* An "identify" question needs only the names; do not spend time explaining them.

**3. (a)** Before: 3,000 ÷ 15 = 200 loaves per employee [1]. After: 3,600 ÷ 15 = 240 loaves per employee [1]. **[2]**
**(b)** Increase = 240 − 200 = 40 [1]; 40 ÷ 200 × 100 = **20%** [1]. **[2]**
**(c)** Some jobs may be lost or the work may change [1], because fewer people are needed to run an automated line, so employees may need retraining or fear redundancy [1]. **[2]**
*Examiner insight:* Show the formula and the unit "per employee" in part (a); the percentage in part (b) is based on the original figure, not the new one.

**4.** Advantage one: no special mould or tool is needed for each design [1], so a single prototype is cheaper to produce [1]. Advantage two: a prototype can be made quickly from a digital file [1], so the design can be changed and tested sooner [1]. **[4]**
*Examiner insight:* Each advantage needs a reason, not just a label; "it is cheaper" alone is a statement, and "cheaper because no mould is needed" is explained.

**5.** Contactless payment shortens each transaction [1], so staff can serve more customers in the same time [1], and the extra tills mean customers can serve themselves, which lets staff spend time on other work, such as advice [1]. **[3]**
*Examiner insight:* Analysis means a chain of reasoning from the technology to the effect; and the answer should refer to the pharmacy, not to shops in general.

**6. (a)** 4 × £21,000 = **£84,000** [1]. **[1]**
**(b)** £120,000 ÷ £84,000 [1] = **1.4 years** [1]. **[2]**
**(c)** Advantage: the saving of £84,000 a year recovers the cost in about 1.4 years, which is quick [1]. The robot may also give steady output [1]. Disadvantage: £120,000 is a large amount to find at once, and the firm may need a loan [1]. Redundancy of four operators may hurt morale of other staff [1]. Judgement: buy, if the firm can afford the cost, because the quick recovery makes it low risk [1] and the saving continues each year the robot works [1]. **[6]**
*Examiner insight:* Evaluation needs a decision supported by the figures, and a conclusion that is only "it depends" with no reason earns little.

**7.** One method [1] and a second, different method [1], from: using renewable energy; using fewer resources or producing less waste; reusing; recycling; developing environmentally friendly products; using environmentally friendly packaging. **[2]**
*Examiner insight:* Two different methods are needed; "recycle paper" and "recycle metal" are the same method.

**8.** Reusing is using an item again as it is [1], for example refilling a container [1]. Recycling is breaking waste down to make new material [1], for example melting scrap metal. **[3]**
*Examiner insight:* A distinction needs both terms defined; the two examples must match the right term.

**9. (a)** Plastic: 80,000 × £0.09 = £7,200 [1]. Recycled: 80,000 × £0.12 = £9,600 [1]. **[2]**
**(b)** £9,600 − £7,200 = **£2,400** [1]. **[1]**
**(c)** Customers who care about the environment may prefer a garden centre that uses recycled pots [1], which could raise sales or improve its reputation by more than the extra £2,400 [1]. **[2]**
*Examiner insight:* Convert pence to pounds before multiplying, and tie the reason to Corlett as a garden centre.

**10.** Advantage: a business can appeal to customers who care about the environment [1], which may improve its reputation and sales [1]. Disadvantage: the packaging may cost more than standard packaging [1], so costs rise or profit falls unless the price is raised [1]. **[4]**
*Examiner insight:* Give one of each; two advantages cannot earn the disadvantage marks.

**11. (a)** 40% × £18,000 = **£7,200** [1]. **[1]**
**(b)** £27,000 ÷ £7,200 [1] = **3.75 years** [1]. **[2]**
**(c)** Advantage: saves £7,200 a year, and the saving continues after 3.75 years [1]. Customers may value a greener laundry [1]. Disadvantage: £27,000 is a large cost to pay at the start [1], and the saving takes almost four years, so cash is tied up [1]. Recommendation: fit the panels if the laundry can fund the cost [1], because the saving lasts well beyond 3.75 years and the reputation gain adds to it [1]. **[6]**
*Examiner insight:* Use both the saving and the time to recover; a recommendation is stronger when it names the condition that would change it.

## Where marks are usually lost

- Defining automation as "using machines" with no mention of limited human input.
- Calculating a percentage change on the new figure instead of the original.
- Leaving out units, such as loaves per employee or years.
- Giving advantages of technology when the question asks about a disadvantage for employees.
- Naming a benefit without explaining why it follows.
- Using the same sustainability method twice for a "two methods" question.
- Mixing up reusing and recycling.
- Forgetting to convert pence to pounds.
- Ending an evaluation with a summary of points instead of a decision.
