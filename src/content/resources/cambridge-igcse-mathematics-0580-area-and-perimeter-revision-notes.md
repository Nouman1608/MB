These notes condense subtopic **5.2 Area and perimeter** from Topic 5 Mensuration of the Cambridge IGCSE Mathematics 0580 syllabus for 2025, 2026 and 2027. The Core and Extended statements are identical. For explanations and worked examples, read the [area and perimeter study guide](/resources/cambridge-igcse-mathematics-0580-area-and-perimeter/); to practise, use the [area and perimeter practice questions](/resources/cambridge-igcse-mathematics-0580-area-and-perimeter-practice/).

## The statement

Carry out calculations involving the perimeter and area of a rectangle, triangle, parallelogram and trapezium. **Except for the area of a triangle, formulas are not given** in the exam.

## Formulas to learn

```
rectangle      A = l × w                P = 2l + 2w
triangle       A = ½ × b × h            (the only one on the List of formulas)
parallelogram  A = b × h
trapezium      A = ½ × (a + b) × h      a, b = the PARALLEL sides
```

h is always the **perpendicular** height: at right angles to the base.

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Perimeter | Total length of the boundary; linear units (cm, m) |
| Area | Amount of flat surface covered; square units (cm², m²) |
| Base | The side you choose to measure the height from |
| Perpendicular height | Distance from the base to the opposite vertex or side, measured at 90° to the base |
| Parallel sides | In a trapezium, the one pair of sides that never meet; they go into ½(a + b) |
| Slant side | A side that is not perpendicular to the base; used for perimeter, never in an area formula |

## Rearranged formulas

Working backwards from an area is common. Rearrange first, then substitute.

| Want | Formula |
|---|---|
| width of a rectangle | w = A ÷ l |
| height of a triangle | h = 2A ÷ b |
| height of a parallelogram | h = A ÷ b |
| height of a trapezium | h = 2A ÷ (a + b) |
| missing parallel side of a trapezium | b = 2A ÷ h − a |
| side of a square from its area | s = √A |
| width of a rectangle from its perimeter | w = P ÷ 2 − l |

**Worked reminder.** A trapezium has area 66 cm², height 6 cm and one parallel side 7 cm.

```
b = 2 × 66 ÷ 6 − 7 = 22 − 7 = 15 cm
```

## How to pick the right numbers

- Find the **right angle** marker: the height meets the base there.
- In an obtuse triangle, the height may lie **outside** the shape.
- In a trapezium, the two numbers added inside the bracket are the **parallel** sides, shown by arrows or stated in the question.
- On a coordinate grid, use horizontal or vertical sides as bases; heights are then differences in x or y.

## Perimeter checklist

```
1. Sketch the shape.
2. Find any missing side (from other sides, or by Pythagoras if right-angled).
3. Add every boundary edge once; tick each as you add it.
4. Leave out sides the question says are walls, open or shared.
5. Linear units.
```

**Worked reminder.** A right-angled triangle has shorter sides 9 cm and 12 cm. Hypotenuse = √(81 + 144) = 15 cm, so perimeter = 9 + 12 + 15 = **36 cm** and area = ½ × 9 × 12 = **54 cm²**.

## Exam traps

- Base × slant side for a parallelogram. The slant side is for the perimeter only.
- Missing the ½ in the triangle or trapezium formula.
- Putting the non-parallel sides of a trapezium into ½(a + b).
- A base in m and a height in cm in the same formula.
- Area answers without square units.
- Leaving out a side, or counting an internal line as part of the perimeter.
- Rounding a length before using it in the next part.

## Self-test

1. Find the area and perimeter of a rectangle 8.5 cm by 4 cm.
2. A triangle has base 11 cm and perpendicular height 6 cm. Find its area.
3. A parallelogram has base 9 cm, slant side 5 cm and perpendicular height 4 cm. Find its area and perimeter.
4. A trapezium has parallel sides 5 cm and 13 cm, 7 cm apart. Find its area.
5. A square has area 49 cm². Find its perimeter.
6. A rectangle has perimeter 30 cm and length 9 cm. Find its area.
7. A triangle has area 36 cm² and base 8 cm. Find its perpendicular height.
8. A parallelogram has area 63 cm² and perpendicular height 7 cm. Find its base.
9. A trapezium has area 50 cm², height 5 cm and one parallel side 8 cm. Find the other parallel side.
10. Find the area of the triangle with vertices (2, 0), (2, 8) and (7, 3).
11. A rectangle measures 1.2 m by 45 cm. Find its area in cm².
12. A rectangle has length (x + 3) cm and width 4 cm. Its area is 36 cm². Find x.

### Answers

1. Area **34 cm²**, perimeter **25 cm**
2. ½ × 11 × 6 = **33 cm²**
3. Area 9 × 4 = **36 cm²**; perimeter 2 × 9 + 2 × 5 = **28 cm**
4. ½ × 18 × 7 = **63 cm²**
5. Side 7 cm, perimeter **28 cm**
6. Width = 15 − 9 = 6 cm, area **54 cm²**
7. 2 × 36 ÷ 8 = **9 cm**
8. 63 ÷ 7 = **9 cm**
9. 2 × 50 ÷ 5 − 8 = **12 cm**
10. Vertical base from (2, 0) to (2, 8) has length 8; height = 7 − 2 = 5; area = **20 square units**
11. 120 × 45 = **5400 cm²**
12. 4(x + 3) = 36, x + 3 = 9, **x = 6**
