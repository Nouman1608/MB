Condensed recall for **5.2** in the prescribed literary texts and films of Pearson Edexcel A Level Urdu (9UR0), Paper 2. For the full explanation, read the [Translation into Urdu and Written Response to Works study guide](/resources/a-level-edexcel-urdu-translation-and-works/) and the [Paper 2 revision notes](/resources/edexcel-a-level-urdu-translation-works-revision-notes/).

## The work

- **Title:** پطرس کے مضامین (*Patras Ke Mazameen*)
- **Author:** احمد شاہ پطرس بخاری (Ahmad Shah Patras Bukhari)
- **Year:** 2011
- **Form:** essays; a prescribed literary text in Appendix 1.

Only **five essays** are prescribed, not the whole book:

1. سویرے جو کل آنکھ میری کھُلی
2. اُردو کی آخری کتاب
3. مُرید پُور کا پیر
4. مرحُوم کی یاد میں
5. لاہور کا جغرافیہ

## How it is examined

- Paper 2 is 2 hours 40 minutes, 110 marks, 30% of the A Level.
- Section B (literary text) is worth 45 marks and about 1 hour 5 minutes; you choose from two questions.
- You write exactly **two** extended responses: two literary texts, or one literary text and one film. Two films is never allowed.
- Marking criteria: critical and analytical response with cultural or social insight (AO4), range of grammatical structures and vocabulary (AO3), and accuracy of language (AO3). There are no AO1 or AO2 marks on Paper 2.

## Approach and skills

- Prepare against five angles: **form and technique, key themes and issues, characterisation, plot structure, social and cultural setting**.
- Structure each answer as **position, evidence, cultural connection**: state your stance, support it with two or three specific points (not a retelling), then link the writer's or film's treatment of an issue to a broader social or cultural theme.
- Write in controlled Urdu with correct agreement, tense and spelling, and plan before you write.
- Use your own prepared references from the work; this page does not replace studying it.

## Exam traps

- Retelling the story without connecting it to a theme weakens the AO4 mark.
- Attempting three responses, or choosing two films.
- Running over time on the first response and squeezing the second.

## Self-test

1. Who wrote پطرس کے مضامین, in what year, and what is its form?
2. Do you study the whole book?
3. Name two of the five prescribed essays.
4. What counts as strong evidence in an answer?
5. What section and marks apply?

**Answers:** 1. احمد شاہ پطرس بخاری, 2011, a collection of essays. 2. No; five essays are prescribed. 3. For example, اُردو کی آخری کتاب and لاہور کا جغرافیہ. 4. Specific, well-chosen references to the essays, explained in your own accurate Urdu. 5. Section B, 45 marks.
