Revision notes for **5.2** in the prescribed literary texts and films of Pearson Edexcel A Level Urdu (9UR0), Paper 2. For the full explanation of the paper, read the [Translation into Urdu and Written Response to Works study guide](/resources/a-level-edexcel-urdu-translation-and-works/) and the [Paper 2 revision notes](/resources/edexcel-a-level-urdu-translation-works-revision-notes/).

## The work

- **Title:** پطرس کے مضامین (*Patras Ke Mazameen*)
- **Author:** احمد شاہ پطرس بخاری (Ahmad Shah Patras Bukhari)
- **Year:** 2011
- **Form:** essays; a prescribed literary text in Appendix 1.

Only **five essays** are prescribed, not the whole book:

1. سویرے جو کل آنکھ میری کھُلی
2. اُردو کی آخری کتاب
3. مُرید پُور کا پیر
4. مرحُوم کی یاد میں
5. لاہور کا جغرافیہ

2011 is the year printed in the specification's list; the essays themselves were written in the first half of the twentieth century. **پطرس** was the pen name of Ahmad Shah Bukhari (1898 to 1958), who taught at Government College Lahore and later represented Pakistan at the United Nations. He wrote relatively little, but this small collection made him one of the best-loved humorists in Urdu.

## The five essays in brief

Use these as orientation only; your evidence must come from your own reading.

- **سویرے جو کل آنکھ میری کھُلی**: the narrator resolves to get up early to study and arranges to be woken, and the essay follows the comic collapse of his good intentions. Humour of laziness, excuses and self-deception.
- **اُردو کی آخری کتاب**: a parody of a children's Urdu school primer, with mock lessons that make fun of rote teaching and of textbook moralising.
- **مُرید پُور کا پیر**: the narrator returns to his home town, where people treat him as an important leader and expect a speech. Satire of political speech-making and of the public's readiness to revere anyone who seems important.
- **مرحُوم کی یاد میں**: a mock elegy for a worn-out bicycle, described in the solemn tone usually kept for mourning a person.
- **لاہور کا جغرافیہ**: a parody of a geography textbook, describing Lahore in mock-scholarly headings and poking fun at the city's dust, construction and habits.

## Key themes and techniques

- **مزاح** (humour) and **طنز** (satire): Patras's laughter is usually gentle, but it points at real weaknesses, in individuals, in education and in public life.
- **مبالغہ** (exaggeration): small events are blown up to absurd proportions.
- **تحریف** (parody): two essays imitate the form of school books; the joke lies in applying a serious format to an unworthy subject.
- **Mock-solemn tone**: in the bicycle essay, the gap between a grave style and a trivial subject creates the comedy.
- **Self-mockery**: the first-person narrator is often the main target of the joke, which makes the reader laugh with him rather than at others.
- **Social setting**: the essays give a picture of educated, middle-class life in colonial Lahore and north India: students, teachers, neighbours, public meetings.

When you analyse humour, do not only say an essay is 'funny'. Name the technique, give a short example from the essay and explain the effect on the reader.

## How it is examined

- Paper 2 is 2 hours 40 minutes, 110 marks, 30% of the A Level.
- Section B (literary text) is worth 45 marks and about 1 hour 5 minutes; you choose from two questions.
- You write exactly **two** extended responses: two literary texts, or one literary text and one film. Two films is never allowed.
- Marking criteria: critical and analytical response with cultural or social insight (AO4), range of grammatical structures and vocabulary (AO3), and accuracy of language (AO3). There are no AO1 or AO2 marks on Paper 2.

## Approach and skills

- Prepare against five angles: **form and technique, key themes and issues, characterisation, plot structure, social and cultural setting**.
- Structure each answer as **position, evidence, cultural connection**: state your stance, support it with two or three specific points (not a retelling), then link the writer's or film's treatment of an issue to a broader social or cultural theme.
- Write in controlled Urdu with correct agreement, tense and spelling, and plan before you write.
- Use your own prepared references from the work; this page does not replace studying it.

For essays rather than a single story, 'plot structure' means how each essay is built: its opening, how the comedy escalates and how it ends. Prepare at least three of the five essays in detail so you can compare them.

## Useful sentences with grammar notes

1. **پطرس اپنی کمزوریوں پر خود ہنستے ہیں۔** (Patras laughs at his own weaknesses.) Respectful plural ہنستے ہیں for the author; **اپنی** refers back to the subject.
2. **مضمون "مرحوم کی یاد میں" میں ایک پرانی سائیکل کا ذکر اس طرح کیا گیا ہے جیسے کسی عزیز کا ماتم ہو۔** (In the essay *Marhoom Ki Yaad Mein*, an old bicycle is described as if a loved one were being mourned.) Passive agreeing with ذکر (masculine); **جیسے ... ہو** (as if) takes a subjunctive.
3. **"اردو کی آخری کتاب" میں درسی کتابوں کے انداز کی نقل کی گئی ہے۔** (*Urdu Ki Aakhri Kitab* imitates the style of school textbooks.) Passive agreeing with نقل (feminine): کی گئی ہے.
4. **مصنف کا مقصد صرف ہنسانا نہیں بلکہ سوچنے پر مجبور کرنا بھی ہے۔** (The author's aim is not only to make us laugh but also to make us think.) The frame **صرف ... نہیں بلکہ ... بھی** is ideal for evaluation.

## Model paragraph

Task: how does Patras create humour?

پطرس بخاری کے مضامین میں مزاح کا سب سے بڑا ذریعہ مبالغہ اور خود پر ہنسنا ہے۔ "مرحوم کی یاد میں" میں راوی ایک پرانی سائیکل کا ذکر اس طرح کرتا ہے جیسے کسی عزیز کی موت کا غم منا رہا ہو، اور یہی سنجیدہ لہجے اور معمولی موضوع کا تضاد قاری کو ہنساتا ہے۔ اسی طرح "لاہور کا جغرافیہ" میں درسی کتابوں کے انداز کی نقل کر کے شہر کے مسائل پر ہلکا سا طنز کیا گیا ہے۔ میرے خیال میں پطرس کا مزاح محض تفریح نہیں، کیونکہ وہ ہنسی ہنسی میں انسانی کمزوریوں اور معاشرتی رویوں کی طرف بھی اشارہ کرتے ہیں۔

**Translation:** In Patras Bukhari's essays, the greatest source of humour is exaggeration and laughing at oneself. In *Marhoom Ki Yaad Mein*, the narrator speaks of an old bicycle as if he were grieving the death of a loved one, and it is this contrast between a serious tone and a trivial subject that makes the reader laugh. Similarly, *Lahore Ka Jughrafia* imitates the style of textbooks to make gentle fun of the city's problems. In my view Patras's humour is not mere entertainment, because while making us laugh he also points to human weaknesses and social attitudes.

**Why it works:** a clear position, two essays compared, named techniques (مبالغہ، تضاد، نقل، طنز) and a cultural point. In a real answer, add a short example of your own from each essay.

## Essay prompts to prepare

- «پطرس کے مضامین میں مزاح کے مختلف حربوں کا تجزیہ کیجیے۔» (Analyse the different techniques of humour in Patras's essays.)
- «پطرس کا مزاح محض ہنسانے کے لیے نہیں۔» (Patras's humour is not merely meant to amuse.) To what extent do you agree?
- What picture of education and public life emerges from the essays?
- Compare the role of the narrator in two of the prescribed essays.

## Exam traps

- Retelling the story without connecting it to a theme weakens the AO4 mark.
- Attempting three responses, or choosing two films.
- Running over time on the first response and squeezing the second.
- Writing about essays that are not among the five prescribed.
- Saying 'it is funny' without naming the technique and its effect.
- Treating the narrator's views as the author's sincere opinions; the narrator is often a comic mask.

## Self-test

1. Who wrote پطرس کے مضامین, in what year, and what is its form?
2. Do you study the whole book?
3. Name two of the five prescribed essays.
4. What counts as strong evidence in an answer?
5. What section and marks apply?
6. What was the author's real name, and where did he teach?
7. Which two essays parody school books?
8. What is the subject of مرحوم کی یاد میں, and how does the essay create humour?
9. What does مرید پور کا پیر satirise?
10. Give the Urdu for 'exaggeration', 'parody' and 'satire'.
11. In "اردو کی آخری کتاب" میں ... نقل کی گئی ہے, why is the verb کی گئی?
12. What does 'plot structure' mean for an essay collection?
