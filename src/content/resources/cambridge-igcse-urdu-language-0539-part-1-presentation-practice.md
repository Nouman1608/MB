> **These are original prompts and questions written for Marlbridge**, for practice on Part 1 of the
> optional 0539 Speaking test, a "two- to three-minute presentation by the candidate on a chosen topic".
> They are not official speaking-test material. The 20-mark tariff of Question 5 follows the
> syllabus (20 marks for Part 1); the tariffs on the other questions are a Marlbridge practice
> convention. Practise the speaking tasks aloud, with a teacher or fluent friend, and record yourself.

## Section A

**1.** The presentation topic must reflect an aspect of life in an Urdu-speaking community or culture. For each topic, say (in English) whether it is suitable as it stands and give a reason. **[5]**

**(a)** *ہمارے محلے میں عید کی رونق*\
**(b)** My favourite video game\
**(c)** *کراچی کے مشہور کھانے*\
**(d)** Climate change\
**(e)** *میری دادی کی سنائی ہوئی لوک کہانیاں*

**2.** A candidate prepared this cue card for a presentation on the old bazaar in his city.

```
1  آج میں آپ کو اپنے شہر کے پرانے بازار کے بارے میں بتاؤں گا۔
2  بازار کی تاریخ
3  دکانیں
4  مصالحے
5  کھانے
6  رش اور شور
7  میری رائے
```

**(a)** Give **two** ways in which this card breaks the rules. **[2]**

**(b)** Rewrite the card so that it is allowed. **[2]**

**3.** A candidate wants to bring these items into the room: (i) a photograph of the bazaar; (ii) a map of the old city; (iii) a printed page with the full text of the presentation; (iv) a simple chart of shop types. Which item is not allowed, and why? What else must the candidate check about the number of items? **[3]**

**4.** Upgrade each weak sentence by adding a reason, an example or a link. **[3]**

**(a)** *مجھے بازار پسند ہے۔*\
**(b)** *وہاں بہت لوگ ہوتے ہیں۔*\
**(c)** *دکاندار اچھے ہیں۔*

## Section B

**5.** Prepare and deliver a presentation of **2 to 3 minutes** on this topic. Make your own cue card of no more than five headings and use one or two illustrative items if you can.

**میرے شہر کا پرانا بازار**

Suggested headings, which you may change: تعارف، دکانیں اور سامان، میرا پسندیدہ حصہ، مسائل، میری رائے. **[20]**

**6.** Listen to a recording of your presentation. Use the grid below to give yourself a band for each criterion and write one change you would make. **[4]**

| Criterion | Band (very good, good, satisfactory, weak, poor) | One change |
|---|---|---|
| Content and presentation | ... | ... |
| Quality of language | ... | ... |

**7.** Match each description to the best band: *very good*, *satisfactory*, *weak*. **[3]**

**(a)** The presentation covers the topic well, with ideas in order, facts and opinions, and stays interesting. The Urdu is varied and mostly accurate, with clear pronunciation.\
**(b)** The speaker names a few facts but few ideas, pauses long between phrases, and uses only very simple sentences. Pronunciation is often unclear.\
**(c)** The speaker covers the topic in a satisfactory way and tries to link ideas. The talk is slow, and mistakes appear when longer sentences are tried.

## Answers

**1.** One mark each [5].
(a) Suitable: a festival in an Urdu-speaking community.
(b) Not suitable as it stands: it is not about Urdu-speaking life or culture.
(c) Suitable: food belongs to Urdu-speaking culture and gives plenty to say.
(d) Not suitable as it stands: too general and not linked to Urdu culture, although a candidate could narrow it, for example how a city in Pakistan copes with heat.
(e) Suitable: traditional stories are part of the culture.

**2.** (a) One mark each, any two [2]: it has seven headings and the maximum is five; heading 1 is a full sentence, so it works as written support, not a short reminder; the card is therefore also longer than needed.
(b) For example [2]: *1 تعارف، 2 دکانیں اور سامان، 3 میرا پسندیدہ حصہ، 4 رش اور شور، 5 میری رائے.* One mark for no more than five headings, one for short headings with no full sentences.

**3.** Item (iii) is not allowed [1], because a written copy of the presentation is not allowed, and illustrative material must not give written support [1]. The candidate must also check that there are no more than three or four items in total [1].

**4.** One mark each for a correct, natural improvement [3]. For example:
(a) *مجھے پرانا بازار اس لیے پسند ہے کہ وہاں ہر چیز رنگ برنگی اور سستی ملتی ہے۔*
(b) *وہاں خاص طور پر اتوار کو بہت لوگ ہوتے ہیں، اس لیے چلنا مشکل ہو جاتا ہے۔*
(c) *دکاندار بہت ملنسار ہیں، مثال کے طور پر ایک دکاندار ہر گاہک کو چکھنے کے لیے مصالحہ دیتا ہے۔*

**5.** Use the Part 1 grid. Award up to 10 for content and presentation and up to 10 for quality of language [20]. Content: topic covered with facts, ideas and at least one opinion with a reason; clear sequence (opening, details, opinion, close); linking words; a delivery that holds interest. Language: range of vocabulary, accurate structures, clear pronunciation and intonation. A presentation under two minutes, or one read from a script, should score lower.

*Model presentation (a structure to learn from, not to memorise):*

> السلام علیکم! آج میں آپ کو اپنے شہر کے پرانے بازار کے بارے میں بتاؤں گی۔
>
> یہ ایک تنگ گلیوں والا بازار ہے جہاں کپڑے، مصالحے، برتن اور مٹھائیاں ملتی ہیں۔ میں ہر اتوار اپنی امی کے ساتھ وہاں جاتی ہوں۔ دکاندار گاہکوں کو آواز لگا کر بلاتے ہیں، اور لوگ قیمت پر بحث بھی کرتے ہیں، جسے مول تول کہتے ہیں۔
>
> مجھے سب سے زیادہ مصالحوں کی دکان پسند ہے، کیونکہ وہاں سے بہت اچھی خوشبو آتی ہے۔ مثال کے طور پر ایک دکاندار ہر گاہک کو چکھنے کے لیے تھوڑا سا مصالحہ دیتا ہے۔
>
> میرے خیال میں پرانا بازار ہماری ثقافت کا حصہ ہے، لیکن وہاں رش اور شور بہت ہوتا ہے، اس لیے بزرگوں کے لیے وہاں چلنا مشکل ہے۔ آخر میں میں یہ کہنا چاہوں گی کہ ہمیں ان بازاروں کی حفاظت کرنی چاہیے۔ شکریہ!

**6.** One mark for each band that you can justify with a reason from your recording, and one mark for each change that is specific [4]. Good changes: add an opinion with *کیونکہ*; cut a repeated sentence; slow down at the end; practise one difficult sound.

**7.** One mark each [3]. (a) very good. (b) weak. (c) satisfactory.

## Where marks are usually lost

- **Question 2**: more than five headings, or a full sentence on the card.
- **Question 3**: bringing a printed script or notes as illustrative material.
- **Question 5**: facts only, with no opinion; speaking for under two minutes; reading from the card.
- **Question 5**: a closing that just stops, with no final sentence.
- **Question 7**: mixing up satisfactory and good. Satisfactory talk is often slow, with errors once longer sentences are tried.
