This guide covers **Exercise 1** of Cambridge O Level Second Language Urdu 3248 Paper 2 Grammar, Writing and Translation. The syllabus describes it as **Sentence transformation**: "candidates read five short sentences and transform the sentences using given prompts, without changing their original meaning". It is worth **5 marks** and tests the objectives **R2, W3, W4 and W5**. For the whole paper, see the [Paper 2 Grammar, Writing and Translation study guide](/resources/o-level-cambridge-urdu-second-language-paper-2-grammar-writing-translation/).

## Where Exercise 1 sits

Exercise 1 is the first of four exercises in a paper of 1 hour 30 minutes and 50 marks. For Exercises 1, 2 and 3, all questions and responses are in Urdu. Dictionaries may not be used. The exercise is short, but every sentence tests accuracy in the grammar of Urdu.

| Objective | In sentence transformation |
|---|---|
| R2 understand ideas, opinions and attitudes | understand exactly what the original sentence says |
| W3 manipulate the target language accurately | change the structure with correct verbs, agreement and word order |
| W4 control of punctuation and spelling | correct *،*, *۔* and quotation marks, and accurate spelling |
| W5 use appropriate register and style | keep the same level of politeness and tone |

The syllabus does not list the kinds of change that may be asked for, and the prompt may be a starting phrase, a word to use or an instruction. Always read the prompt printed on the paper. The changes below are common Urdu grammar changes that suit this kind of task, and they are a useful set to practise.

## The golden rule: keep the meaning

A transformation **rebuilds** the sentence; it does not **replace** the idea. If the original says that every student passed, a transformed sentence must still say that every student passed. Changing "every student passed" to "no student failed" keeps the meaning, but changing it to "some students passed" does not.

Before you write, ask: "Does my new sentence mean the same thing as the first one?"

## Common transformations

### 1. Active to passive

| Original | Transformed |
|---|---|
| استاد نے سبق پڑھایا۔ | سبق پڑھایا گیا۔ |
| ماں نے بچوں کو کہانی سنائی۔ | بچوں کو کہانی سنائی گئی۔ |

The object becomes the subject. The verb takes the form of the past participle with *گیا / گئی / گئے / گئیں*, agreeing with the new subject. The agent can be omitted or added with *کی طرف سے* or *کے ذریعے*.

### 2. Direct to indirect speech

| Direct | Indirect |
|---|---|
| اس نے کہا، "میں بہت تھکا ہوا ہوں۔" | اس نے کہا کہ وہ بہت تھکا ہوا ہے۔ |
| اس نے کہا، "میں کل آؤں گا۔" | اس نے کہا کہ وہ اگلے دن آئے گا۔ |

Remove the quotation marks, add *کہ*, and change the **person** (*میں* becomes *وہ*). Words of time and place may also need to change (*کل* becomes *اگلے دن* or *اس دن*, depending on the sentence).

### 3. Equivalent negatives

| Original | Transformed |
|---|---|
| ہر طالب علم امتحان میں کامیاب ہوا۔ | کوئی طالب علم امتحان میں ناکام نہیں ہوا۔ |
| سب لوگ موجود تھے۔ | کوئی شخص غیر حاضر نہیں تھا۔ |

Check carefully: *کوئی ... نہیں* with the **opposite** word gives the same meaning.

### 4. Combining two sentences

| Two sentences | Combined |
|---|---|
| موسم خراب تھا۔ پرواز وقت پر روانہ ہوئی۔ | اگرچہ موسم خراب تھا، پرواز وقت پر روانہ ہوئی۔ |
| میں بیمار تھا۔ میں اسکول نہیں گیا۔ | میں بیمار تھا، اس لیے اسکول نہیں گیا۔ |
| بارش ہو گی۔ ہم پکنک پر نہیں جائیں گے۔ | اگر بارش ہوئی تو ہم پکنک پر نہیں جائیں گے۔ |

Choose the connective that matches the relationship: contrast (*اگرچہ، لیکن*), reason (*اس لیے، کیونکہ*), condition (*اگر ... تو*).

### 5. Change of person or respect

| Original | Transformed |
|---|---|
| تم نے یہ کام کب ختم کیا؟ | آپ نے یہ کام کب ختم کیا؟ |
| تم کہاں جا رہے ہو؟ | آپ کہاں جا رہے ہیں؟ |

Moving from *تم* to *آپ* changes the verb ending as well as the pronoun. The plural form is used for respect.

### 6. Change of tense

| Original | Transformed |
|---|---|
| وہ روزانہ کتاب پڑھتا ہے۔ | وہ روزانہ کتاب پڑھتا تھا۔ |
| وہ روزانہ کتاب پڑھتا ہے۔ | وہ روزانہ کتاب پڑھے گا۔ |

Change the time word as well when the prompt asks (*آج* becomes *کل*).

### 7. Change of number or gender

| Original | Transformed |
|---|---|
| لڑکا اسکول جاتا ہے۔ | لڑکیاں اسکول جاتی ہیں۔ |
| طالب علم نے کتاب پڑھی۔ | طالبات نے کتابیں پڑھیں۔ |

The verb (and sometimes the adjective) must agree with the new subject or object.

### 8. Change of sentence start

If the prompt gives a first word or phrase, build the rest around it. The sentence must be natural Urdu and must contain every idea of the original.

## Worked example

Five original sentences, with the prompt in brackets, followed by a model transformation and the reason for each choice.

| Sentence and prompt | Model answer | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| 1. ماں نے بچوں کو کہانی سنائی۔ (مجہول جملہ لکھیے) | بچوں کو کہانی سنائی گئی۔ | passive: the verb takes *گئی* to agree with *کہانی* |
| 2. اس نے کہا، "میں بہت تھکا ہوا ہوں۔" (آغاز: اس نے کہا کہ) | اس نے کہا کہ وہ بہت تھکا ہوا ہے۔ | indirect speech: *میں* becomes *وہ* and the verb changes to *ہے* |
| 3. ہر طالب علم امتحان میں کامیاب ہوا۔ (استعمال کیجیے: کوئی ... نہیں) | کوئی طالب علم امتحان میں ناکام نہیں ہوا۔ | opposite word with *نہیں* keeps the meaning |
| 4. موسم خراب تھا۔ پرواز وقت پر روانہ ہوئی۔ (استعمال کیجیے: اگرچہ) | اگرچہ موسم خراب تھا، پرواز وقت پر روانہ ہوئی۔ | contrast connective joins both ideas |
| 5. تم نے یہ کام کب ختم کیا؟ (احترام کے ساتھ لکھیے) | آپ نے یہ کام کب ختم کیا؟ | *تم* becomes *آپ*; the past form with *نے* does not change |

### Wrong answers and why they lose marks

| Wrong answer | Problem |
|---|---|
| (3) کوئی طالب علم امتحان میں کامیاب نہیں ہوا | the meaning is reversed: it now says no one passed |
| (2) اس نے کہا کہ میں بہت تھکا ہوا ہوں | the person has not changed |
| (1) سبق کہانی سنائی گئی | wrong word: *کہانی* is the object, and *بچوں کو* has been lost |
| (4) موسم خراب تھا اگرچہ پرواز وقت پر روانہ ہوئی | *اگرچہ* is in the wrong clause |
| (5) آپ نے یہ کام کب ختم کیے | wrong verb ending; the verb agrees with *کام* (masculine singular here) |

## A method for each sentence

1. **Read the original** and say its meaning to yourself.
2. **Read the prompt** and underline what it asks you to use or change.
3. **Decide which parts must change** (verb, pronoun, order) and which must stay.
4. **Write the new sentence** in full.
5. **Check** meaning, agreement, tense and punctuation.

## Common mistakes

- **Changing the meaning.** The most serious fault.
- **Dropping part of the original**, such as the time word or the object.
- **Forgetting to change the verb** when the pronoun or tense changes.
- **Wrong agreement** after a change of gender, number or voice.
- **Ignoring the prompt**, for example not using the word that was required.
- **Careless punctuation**, such as missing *۔* or leaving quotation marks in indirect speech.
- **Over-long answers.** One neat sentence is enough.

## Exam technique

- Do the exercise quickly: five marks, with about one minute for each sentence.
- Write clearly in the space given.
- Re-read each answer against the original for meaning.
- If you are unsure, choose the simplest correct structure.
