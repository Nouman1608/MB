Condensed recall for **sub-theme 1.3** of Theme 1 in Pearson Edexcel A Level Urdu (9UR0): equal access to education in Pakistan (تعلیم), which is the research subject for this theme. For fuller explanation and a model paragraph, read the [Theme 1 study guide](/resources/edexcel-a-level-urdu-language-theme-1-study-guide/).

## Research rules

- Theme 1 research must be **based on Pakistan** and written up in Urdu.
- Keep a file of named sources, dates and figures you have checked yourself. Never quote a statistic you cannot trace.
- Compare at least two viewpoints on each point.

## Points to investigate

- Access for girls and boys, and for urban and rural children.
- Public (**سرکاری**) and private (**نجی**) schools: resources and quality.
- Language of instruction and its effect on opportunity.
- Constitutional commitment: Article 25A, added in 2010, provides for free and compulsory education for children in a stated age range. Check the wording and age range before you quote it.
- Barriers: poverty, distance, child labour and the cost of schooling.

## Using research in an answer

Section C of Paper 1 rewards knowledge and understanding of society and culture through your research (AO4, 20 marks), response to the unseen Urdu text (AO2, 10 marks) and accuracy and range of language (AO3, 10 marks). Use both your research and the source text, and make clear which is which. See the [Paper 1 study guide](/resources/a-level-edexcel-urdu-translation-and-reading/).

## Exam traps

- Research that is not about Pakistan.
- Unsupported statistics and sweeping claims.
- Quoting Article 25A without checking its wording.
- Mixing research and source text so the examiner cannot tell them apart.

## Self-test

1. Which country must Theme 1 research cover?
2. Give the Urdu for 'literacy' and 'curriculum'.
3. Name two barriers to equal access to education.
4. What did Article 25A add, and what must you do before quoting it?
5. How are the 40 marks of Paper 1 Section C split?

**Answers:** 1. Pakistan. 2. خواندگی and نصاب. 3. For example, poverty and distance, or unequal access for girls. 4. It was added in 2010 and provides for free and compulsory education for children in a stated age range; check the wording and age range first. 5. AO4 research 20, AO2 unseen text 10, AO3 language 10.
