These questions practise **5.6, بجرنگی بھائی جان** (Kabir Khan, 2015), a prescribed film for Section C of Paper 2
in Pearson Edexcel A Level Urdu (9UR0). Section A builds the Urdu you need to write about the film. Section B
practises the essay itself: in the exam you choose one of two questions, written in Urdu, and answer in Urdu, with a
recommended 320–370 words, no dictionary and no notes. The essay tariff of 45 follows the specification's three
grids: critical and analytical response (AO4, 20), range of grammatical structures and vocabulary (AO3, 15) and
accuracy of language (AO3, 10). The other tariffs are Marlbridge's own.

Prepare with the [Bajrangi Bhaijaan study guide](/resources/edexcel-a-level-urdu-language-9ur0-bajrangi-bhaijaan-kabir-khan/)

## Questions

### Section A: language for writing about the film

**1.** Translate into Urdu: "In the film, Pawan's honesty creates both humour and tension." **[5]**

**2.** Give the Urdu term for each of these: (a) close-up; (b) background music; (c) climax; (d) characterisation. **[4]**

**3.** Rewrite this sentence in the passive voice, without naming the director: «ہدایت کار نے پہاڑی مناظر بہت خوبصورتی سے فلمائے ہیں۔» **[2]**

**4.** The sentence below contains two agreement errors. Correct them and explain each correction. «یہ فلم ناظرین کو بہت متاثر کرتا ہے اور اس کی پیغام آج بھی اہم ہے۔» **[4]**

**5.** Join these two sentences using اگرچہ ... پھر بھی: «فلم کا انجام حقیقت سے کچھ دور ہے۔» «فلم کا انجام ناظرین کو گہرا تاثر دیتا ہے۔» **[2]**

**6.** Identify the tense of the verb in the sentence below and explain why using it can help an essay's mark for range of language. «جب پون شاہدہ سے ملا، تب تک وہ اپنی ماں سے بچھڑ چکی تھی۔» **[2]**

**7.** Translate into Urdu: "If Pawan had lied at the border, his journey would have become easier." **[3]**

### Section B: essay practice

**8.** Write one paragraph in Urdu, of about 80–90 words, answering: «فلم میں چاند نواب کا کردار کیوں اہم ہے؟» **[6]**

**9.** Write a plan, in Urdu or English, for this essay question: «فلم "بجرنگی بھائی جان" میں مذہبی رواداری کا موضوع کس طرح پیش کیا گیا ہے؟» Your plan should show a position, three pieces of evidence, a counter-view and a conclusion. **[6]**

**10.** «"بجرنگی بھائی جان" میں پون کا سفر صرف جغرافیائی نہیں بلکہ اخلاقی بھی ہے۔» اس بیان کا تنقیدی جائزہ لیجیے اور فلم سے مثالیں دیجیے۔ **[45]**

**11.** «فلم کی موسیقی اور مناظر اس کے پیغام کو کس حد تک مؤثر بناتے ہیں؟» اپنے جواب میں فلم کی تکنیک کا تجزیہ کیجیے۔ **[45]**

**12.** «کیا فلم کا انجام حقیقت پسندانہ ہے؟» دلائل اور مثالوں کے ساتھ اپنی رائے دیجیے۔ **[45]**

## Answers

**1.** Model: «فلم میں پون کی ایمان داری مزاح اور تناؤ دونوں پیدا کرتی ہے۔» Marking (one mark per section): فلم میں = in the film [1]; پون کی ایمان داری = Pawan's honesty [1]; مزاح اور تناؤ = humour and tension (accept مزاح اور کشمکش) [1]; دونوں = both [1]; پیدا کرتی ہے = creates, with the feminine form agreeing with ایمان داری [1]. *Examiner insight:* پیدا کرتا ہے would be a verb-ending error; as in Paper 2's translation marking, a wrong ending is a grammatical error, not a tolerable spelling slip.

**2.** (a) کلوز اپ or قریبی شاٹ [1]; (b) پس منظر موسیقی [1]; (c) نقطۂ عروج [1]; (d) کردار نگاری [1]

**3.** «پہاڑی مناظر بہت خوبصورتی سے فلمائے گئے ہیں۔» The agent and نے are removed and مناظر becomes the subject [1]; the passive فلمائے گئے ہیں agrees with مناظر (masculine plural) [1]

**4.** کرتا ہے → **کرتی ہے** [1], because فلم is feminine and the verb must agree with it [1]; اس کی پیغام → **اس کا پیغام** [1], because پیغام is masculine, so the possessive takes the masculine form [1]. Corrected: «یہ فلم ناظرین کو بہت متاثر کرتی ہے اور اس کا پیغام آج بھی اہم ہے۔»

**5.** «اگرچہ فلم کا انجام حقیقت سے کچھ دور ہے، پھر بھی وہ ناظرین کو گہرا تاثر دیتا ہے۔» The conjunction pair is placed correctly, with اگرچہ opening the first clause and پھر بھی the second [1]; the repeated subject is replaced by the pronoun وہ, and the verb دیتا ہے agrees with the masculine انجام [1]

**6.** Past perfect (pluperfect), formed with چکی تھی [1]; the specification counts conceptually challenging tenses such as the pluperfect as complex language, which the range grid rewards [1]

**7.** Model: «اگر پون سرحد پر جھوٹ بول دیتا تو اس کا سفر آسان ہو جاتا۔» (accept اگر پون نے سرحد پر جھوٹ بولا ہوتا تو...) The اگر ... تو structure [1]; a past conditional form for "had lied" [1]; ہو جاتا agreeing with the masculine سفر [1]

**8.** Award one mark for each of: a clear opening position on why Chand Nawab matters [1]; a precise reference to his role in the journey [1]; a reference to his video and its effect on ordinary people [1]; analysis of what this shows about the media [1]; a link to the wider theme of the media in the Urdu-speaking world [1]; accurate agreement throughout, including with the feminine ویڈیو [1]. Model:

> چاند نواب فلم میں ایک صحافی سے بڑھ کر ہے۔ شروع میں وہ صرف ایک اچھی خبر کی تلاش میں ہے، لیکن پون کے ساتھ سفر کرتے
> ہوئے وہ اس کی سچائی کا گواہ بن جاتا ہے۔ اس کی بنائی ہوئی ویڈیو انٹرنیٹ پر پھیل جاتی ہے اور سرحد کے دونوں طرف عام
> لوگوں کو متاثر کرتی ہے۔ یوں فلم دکھاتی ہے کہ میڈیا صرف نفرت ہی نہیں بلکہ محبت اور اعتماد بھی پھیلا سکتا ہے۔ میرے
> خیال میں اس کردار کے بغیر فلم کا انجام ممکن نہ ہوتا۔

**9.** Award one mark for each of: a position that answers the question (for example, the film presents tolerance as respect that does not require giving up one's own faith) [1]; evidence from Pawan's devotion to Hanuman and how it is presented [1]; evidence from his growing respect for Muslim places of worship, such as the mosque sequence [1]; evidence from the qawwali at the Sufi shrine as a shared space of devotion [1]; a counter-view, for example that the film's picture of tolerance is idealised or simplified [1]; a conclusion linking the theme to the film's wider message about ordinary people across borders [1]

**10.** AO4 critical and analytical response [20]; AO3 range of grammatical structures and vocabulary [15]; AO3 accuracy of language [10]. Indicative content (other valid, well-supported arguments are equally creditworthy):
- **Geographical journey:** the road structure takes Pawan across a border, through checkpoints and into unfamiliar places; each stage brings a physical obstacle.
- **Moral journey:** his honesty is tested repeatedly, most clearly when he insists on permission at the border; he learns to respect another faith while keeping his own.
- **Change in others:** the journey also changes Chand Nawab and the people who see the video, so the moral journey is shared.
- **Evaluation:** some may argue that Pawan does not change much morally, because his principles are fixed from the start; a strong answer weighs this against the way his understanding of the "other" grows.
- **Technique:** landscape shots stress distance; humour comes from his honesty in dangerous situations; music marks turning points.
- **Conclusion:** a judgement on how far the statement is true, linked to the social theme of borders and humanity.

**11.** AO4 critical and analytical response [20]; AO3 range of grammatical structures and vocabulary [15]; AO3 accuracy of language [10]. Indicative content:
- **Music:** the qawwali at the shrine expresses shared devotion and gives the theme of tolerance an emotional form; songs mark emotional turning points in the journey.
- **Landscape:** mountain scenery makes the journey visually striking and emphasises how far Shahida is from home.
- **Crowds and the ending:** the gathering at the border turns individual kindness into a collective message.
- **Evaluation:** music and spectacle can make the message powerful, but some viewers may find them sentimental, which can weaken the film's seriousness; a strong answer reaches a reasoned judgement.
- **Language:** precise cinematic terms in Urdu (پس منظر موسیقی، کلوز اپ، منظر، نقطۂ عروج) used to explain effect, not just named.

**12.** AO4 critical and analytical response [20]; AO3 range of grammatical structures and vocabulary [15]; AO3 accuracy of language [10]. Indicative content:
- **The case for "not realistic":** large crowds gathering at the border because of one video, and the stand-off there being resolved through them, can be seen as idealised.
- **The case for its truth:** the ending reflects the film's view that ordinary people on both sides share humanity; the power of shared video is a real feature of modern media.
- **Shahida finding her voice:** a symbolic climax; consider whether symbolism matters more here than realism.
- **Genre:** a popular feature film uses emotion and spectacle; judge the ending by what it aims to achieve as well as by realism.
- **Conclusion:** a clear personal judgement, justified with evidence, for example that the ending is not realistic but is truthful about the film's central hope.

*Examiner insight for 10 to 12:* the AO4 grid rewards arguments "consistently justified with appropriately selected evidence from the work". A response that retells the journey in order, however accurate, is "descriptive" and stays in the lower bands.

## Where marks are usually lost

- Answering the essay in English, or mixing English film terms into an Urdu essay.
- Agreement errors with فلم, ویڈیو and ایمان داری (feminine) and with منظر, پیغام and سفر (masculine).
- Naming a technique without explaining its effect on the viewer.
- Retelling the plot instead of arguing a position.
- Leaving no counter-view, which limits evaluation.
