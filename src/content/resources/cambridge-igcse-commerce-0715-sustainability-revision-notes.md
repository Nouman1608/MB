Condensed notes for section 6.1 of Cambridge IGCSE Commerce (0715). For fuller explanations, two worked examples and an annotated answer, use the [sustainability study guide](/resources/cambridge-igcse-commerce-0715-sustainability/).

## The syllabus in two lines

1. **6.1.1 Purpose:** making commercial processes eco-friendly and environmentally responsible for future generations.
2. **6.1.2 Actions:** nine ways to improve sustainability.

## Purpose of sustainability

- **Supply chain:** the steps from raw materials to the final customer, and disposal.
- **Aim:** reduce harm to the environment and keep resources for people in the future.
- **Key phrase:** "for future generations".
- **Extra benefits for the business:** lower costs, better image, meeting rules, secure supply of materials.

## Nine actions to recall

Mnemonic **"My Lorry Prefers Empty Roads; Really, Don't Fix Water"**:

| Letter | Action | One-line meaning |
|---|---|---|
| M | Materials, sustainable | Renewable, recycled or low-impact inputs |
| L | Logistics, sustainable | Cleaner vehicles, fuller loads, shorter routes |
| P | Packaging, reduced | Less, lighter or reusable |
| E | Eco-friendly products | Goods made to cause less harm |
| R | Recycling and reducing waste | Reprocess materials and waste less |
| R | Returns and exchanges reduced | Good information and quality so fewer goods go back |
| D | Digital resale marketplaces | Customers resell used goods through the firm |
| F | Free repair services | Repair, don't replace |
| W | Water stewardship | Careful use, reuse and protection of water |

## Benefit and limit for each action

| Action | Benefit | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| Sustainable materials | Protects forests and resources | Often costs more |
| Sustainable logistics | Less fuel and pollution | New vehicles and systems cost money |
| Reduced packaging | Less waste; lower costs | Possible damage in transit |
| Eco-friendly products | Meets customer demand | Higher price; claims must be honest |
| Recycling and reducing waste | Saves materials and disposal fees | Needs systems, space, effort |
| Fewer returns | Fewer extra deliveries | Needs investment in information and quality control |
| Digital resale | Goods stay in use longer | Checking quality; running the platform |
| Free repairs | Longer life; less waste; loyalty | Staff time and parts |
| Water stewardship | Protects scarce water | Equipment and monitoring |

## Match the action to the stage of the supply chain

| Stage | Actions that fit |
|---|---|
| Sourcing and production | Sustainable materials; water stewardship; waste reduction |
| Storage and transport | Sustainable logistics |
| Packing and selling | Reduced packaging; eco-friendly products |
| After sale | Fewer returns; digital resale; free repairs; recycling |

## Who gains?

| Party | Gain |
|---|---|
| Business | Lower costs; better image |
| Customers | Longer-lasting, safer products |
| Community | Cleaner air, water, land |
| Future generations | Resources remain |

Remember the timing: costs often come first, benefits later.

## Quick calculation pattern

*A firm uses 80 g of plastic per unit and cuts it to 60 g. It sells 40,000 units. Plastic costs $3 per kg.*

- Saving per unit: 80 - 60 = 20 g.
- Total saving: 20 x 40,000 = 800,000 g = 800 kg.
- Money saved: 800 x 3 = $2,400.

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Sustainability | Meeting present needs without harming future generations' ability to meet theirs |
| Supply chain | The linked steps from raw materials to the customer |
| Eco-friendly | Causing little harm to the environment |
| Renewable | Able to be replaced naturally or reused |
| Recycling | Reprocessing used materials into new products |
| Returns | Goods sent back by customers |
| Resale marketplace | A site where used goods are sold again |
| Water stewardship | Responsible management of water use |
| Waste reduction | Producing less waste in the first place |

## Exam traps

- Link every action to the **environment** and to the **business**.
- Recycling is not the same as **reducing waste**.
- "Eco-friendly" claims must be true; otherwise they become misleading advertising.
- Do not write "sustainability means making profit for a long time".
- Name the **stage** of the supply chain where the action applies.
- Costs often come first; savings come later.

## Self-test

1. Define sustainability.
2. What is the purpose of sustainability in the supply chain?
3. List **four** of the nine actions in the syllabus.
4. How does reducing packaging help the environment?
5. What is a digital resale marketplace?
6. Why do free repair services help sustainability?
7. How does sustainable logistics reduce harm?
8. Explain why reducing returns is more sustainable.
9. What is water stewardship?
10. State **one** limit of using sustainable materials.
11. Give **two** benefits to a business of acting sustainably.
12. A shop cuts its returns from 300 to 210 a month. Each return costs $5. Calculate the monthly saving.

**Answers**

1. Meeting today's needs without damaging resources or the environment for future generations.
2. To make commercial processes eco-friendly and environmentally responsible for future generations.
3. Any four: sustainable materials; sustainable logistics; reducing packaging; eco-friendly products; recycling and reducing waste; reducing returns and exchanges; digital resale marketplaces; free repair services; water stewardship.
4. It uses fewer materials, makes less waste and reduces the weight of deliveries.
5. A website or app where customers can sell their used goods, so items stay in use.
6. Products are repaired instead of replaced, so they last longer and less waste is produced.
7. Cleaner vehicles, fuller loads and shorter routes use less fuel and cause less pollution.
8. Fewer parcels travel back and forth, so less fuel and packaging are used and fewer goods are wasted.
9. Using water carefully, reusing it and protecting local water sources.
10. Any one: higher cost; limited supply.
11. Any two: lower costs; better image; customer loyalty; meeting rules.
12. Fewer returns: 300 - 210 = 90. Saving = 90 x 5 = $450.
