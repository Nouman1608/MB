## Section A: short answers

**1.** Which **one** of the following best describes an e-ticket? **[1]**

A: A paper ticket posted to the customer

B: An electronic ticket with a code that is scanned at the venue

C: A receipt from a bank for a payment

D: A seat map shown on a website

**2.** State what is meant by *double booking*. **[1]**

**3.** Give **two** characteristics of an online booking system. **[2]**

**4.** Name **two** items, other than flights, that can be booked online in the travel industry. **[2]**

**5.** State **one** reason why booking websites use HTTPS. **[1]**

## Section B: structured questions

**6.** A customer books two seats for a film using a cinema's website.

**(a)** Describe the steps the customer and the system go through, from searching for the film to receiving a ticket. **[5]**

**(b)** Explain how the system makes sure no one else can book the same seats. **[2]**

**7.** An airline sells most of its tickets online.

**(a)** Give **two** advantages to the airline of selling tickets online. **[2]**

**(b)** Explain **two** advantages to customers of booking flights online rather than through a travel agent's office. **[4]**

**8.** Tickets for a popular band go on sale online at 9 a.m. Thousands of fans try to book at once.

**(a)** Describe **one** problem fans may face. **[2]**

**(b)** The website limits each customer to four tickets. Suggest **one** reason for this. **[1]**

**9.** A football club sells match tickets online. Each ticket is linked to a named supporter.

**(a)** Describe how the ticket is checked when the supporter arrives at the stadium. **[2]**

**(b)** Explain **one** concern supporters may have about the club storing their details. **[2]**

**10.** An elderly man prefers to book train tickets at the station. Give **two** reasons why he may prefer this to booking online. **[2]**

## Section C: extended questions

**11.** A small theatre is deciding whether to replace its telephone booking service with an online booking system. Discuss the advantages and disadvantages for the theatre. **[6]**

**12.** Evaluate online booking for a family planning a holiday that includes flights, a hotel and car hire. **[6]**

---

## Answers

**1.** **B** [1]. **[1]**

**2.** The same seat or room being sold to two different customers [1]. **[1]**

**3.** Real-time access to a central database [1]; interactive seat maps [1] (also credit: search filters, secure payment, instant confirmation, e-tickets, customer accounts). **[2]**

**4.** Hotel rooms [1]; car hire [1] (also credit: train tickets, holiday packages, ferry crossings). **[2]**

**5.** To encrypt personal and payment details so they cannot be read if intercepted [1]. **[1]**

**6. (a)** The customer searches for the film and chooses a date and screening time [1]; the system checks the database and shows a seat map of available seats [1]; the customer selects two seats and enters personal and payment details [1]; payment is authorised and the database is updated to show the seats as booked [1]; a confirmation and e-ticket are sent by email or to the app [1].

**(b)** When the customer selects the seats they are held, so they no longer appear as available to other users [1]; after payment the database marks them as booked immediately, and if payment is not made the hold is released after a time limit [1]. **[7]**

**7. (a)** Fewer staff are needed to take bookings, reducing costs [1]; customers from any country can book at any time, increasing sales [1] (also credit: booking data helps plan flight schedules).

**(b)** Customers can compare prices and times from different airlines at once [1], so they are more likely to find the cheapest or most convenient flight [1]. They can book at any time without travelling to the agent's office [1], so they save time and can book late at night or at weekends [1]. **[6]**

**8. (a)** The website may slow down or crash because of the very large number of users [1], so fans may lose their place or find tickets have sold out before they can pay [1] (also credit: fans placed in a long online queue).

**(b)** To stop people buying large numbers of tickets to resell at higher prices, so more fans can buy tickets [1]. **[3]**

**9. (a)** The barcode or QR code on the e-ticket is scanned at the turnstile [1]; the system checks it against the database to confirm it is valid and has not been used, then marks it as used [1].

**(b)** If the club's database is hacked, supporters' personal and payment details could be stolen [1] and used for fraud or identity theft [1]. **[4]**

**10.** He can ask staff for advice on the best ticket or route [1]; he may not have a computer, smartphone or internet access, or may not trust giving card details online [1]. **[2]**

**11.** Advantages: customers can book at any time, not only when the phone line is staffed, which may increase sales [1]; customers can choose their own seats from a seat map [1]; fewer staff are needed to answer calls, reducing costs [1]. Disadvantages: the website and database are expensive to set up and maintain [1]; the theatre becomes responsible for protecting customers' payment data online [1]; some regular customers, such as older people without internet access, may stop booking [1]. **[6]**

**12.** For: the family can compare flights, hotels and car hire from many companies and find the best prices [1]; they can book everything at any time from home [1]; they receive instant confirmation and e-tickets, so documents are not lost [1]. Against: there is a risk of fake websites or stolen card details [1]; if one part, such as the flight, changes, it may be difficult to change the other bookings without fees [1]. Judgement: online booking is suitable if the family uses well-known secure websites and checks all details carefully before paying [1]. **[6]**

## Where marks are usually lost

- Giving the booking steps without the database update.
- Saying "it prevents double booking" without the hold and update.
- Giving advantages for customers when the question asks about the organisation.
- Forgetting to use details from the context, such as the stadium or the band.
