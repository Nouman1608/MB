**These notes condense A time of challenges: the USA, 1961-74, the second topic of the Paper 4 American option in Cambridge 9489.** For explanation see the [1961-74 study guide](/resources/cambridge-a-level-world-history-9489-time-of-challenges-usa/); for essays and short questions see the [1961-74 practice questions](/resources/cambridge-a-level-world-history-9489-time-of-challenges-usa-practice/).

## Syllabus coverage

- 5.1 How significant were the presidencies of Kennedy, Johnson and Nixon for the USA?
- 5.2 How well did US governments deal with Cuba and Vietnam?
- 5.3 How much progress was made in achieving civil rights for African Americans?
- 5.4 Why were there challenges to authority?

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| New Frontier | Kennedy's domestic and foreign programme, announced 1960 |
| Great Society | Johnson's programme of social reform, 1964-68 |
| War on Poverty | Johnson's anti-poverty measures under the Economic Opportunity Act (1964) |
| ExComm | Kennedy's advisory committee in the Cuban Missile Crisis |
| Quarantine | The naval blockade of Cuba, October 1962 |
| Domino theory | Belief that Vietnam's fall would lead to others falling to communism |
| Rolling Thunder | US bombing campaign against North Vietnam, 1965-68 |
| Vietnamisation | Nixon's transfer of fighting to South Vietnamese forces |
| Silent majority | Nixon's term (1969) for Americans who did not protest |
| New Federalism | Nixon's revenue sharing with the states |
| Black Power | Movement for Black self-reliance and pride, popularised by Carmichael in 1966 |
| Identity politics | Campaigns based on group identity: gay rights, Native American rights, feminism |
| NOW | National Organization for Women, founded 1966 |
| AIM | American Indian Movement, founded 1968 |

## 5.1 Presidents compared

| | Kennedy | Johnson | Nixon |
|---|---|---|---|
| Key laws | Minimum wage $1.25; Area Redevelopment; Equal Pay Act | Civil Rights Act; Voting Rights Act; Medicare and Medicaid; ESEA; Economic Opportunity Act | EPA; Clean Air Act; revenue sharing; NEP |
| Blocked or failed | Medical care; education aid | Underfunded by Vietnam; urban riots | Family Assistance Plan; trust destroyed by Watergate |
| Significance | Rhetoric and agenda-setting | Largest social reform since the New Deal | Pragmatic conservatism; presidential power checked |

## 5.2 Vietnam -- the four stages

1. **Commitment (1954-63):** Geneva, Diem, domino theory, advisers rising to about 16,000.
2. **Escalation (1964-65):** Tonkin Resolution, Rolling Thunder, combat troops.
3. **Stalemate (1965-68):** search and destroy, body counts, Ho Chi Minh trail, peak of over 500,000 troops.
4. **Exit (1968-75):** Tet, Vietnamisation, Cambodia, bombing, Paris Accords, fall of Saigon.

## 5.3 Civil rights -- the balance sheet

- **Campaigns:** Freedom Rides, Birmingham, March on Washington, Freedom Summer, Selma.
- **Opposition:** literacy tests and registrars; Connor and Wallace; filibusters; Klan violence.
- **Federal action:** marshals and troops; 1964, 1965, 1968, 1972 laws; *Loving* (1967).
- **Verdict:** legal equality largely won by 1968; economic and northern inequality persisted.

## 5.4 Challenges -- why?

- **Long-term:** affluence and education produced a confident youth generation; discrimination remained.
- **Triggers:** Vietnam and the draft; assassinations; the slow pace of change.
- **Groups:** feminists, Black Power, gay rights, Native Americans, anti-war students.
- **Authorities' reaction:** police in Chicago (1968), the National Guard at Kent State (1970), FBI surveillance.

## Exam traps

- **Tet** was a communist military setback but a US political defeat.
- **Kennedy and civil rights:** cautious before June 1963.
- **Nixon:** expanded environmental and some welfare policy -- not simply a conservative.
- **Voting Rights Act** is 1965, not 1964.
- **"How well"** questions on Cuba: weigh the Bay of Pigs against the missile crisis outcome.

## Self-test

1. What were Kennedy's margins in 1960? *303 electoral votes to 219, with a very narrow popular vote margin.*
2. What did the 1963 Equal Pay Act require? *Equal pay for men and women doing equal work.*
3. What did the Economic Opportunity Act (1964) create? *War on Poverty programmes such as the Job Corps and VISTA.*
4. What did Kennedy secretly agree in October 1962? *To remove US missiles from Turkey.*
5. Where was Vietnam divided in 1954? *At the 17th parallel.*
6. What did the Gulf of Tonkin Resolution allow? *The President to take all necessary measures to repel attacks, without a declaration of war.*
7. What was Vietnamisation? *Handing the fighting to South Vietnamese forces while withdrawing US troops.*
8. What happened at Selma on 7 March 1965? *State troopers attacked marchers on the Edmund Pettus Bridge (Bloody Sunday).*
9. Which 1967 case struck down bans on interracial marriage? *Loving v. Virginia.*
10. Who founded the Black Panthers? *Bobby Seale and Huey Newton.*
11. What happened at Kent State in May 1970? *National Guardsmen shot dead four students during an anti-war protest.*
12. Which ruling forced Nixon to release the tapes? *United States v. Nixon (1974).*
