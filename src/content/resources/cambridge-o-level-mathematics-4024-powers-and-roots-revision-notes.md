Condensed notes for subtopic **1.3 Powers and roots** of Cambridge O Level Mathematics (Syllabus D) 4024, 2025 to 2027. The [Powers and roots study guide](/resources/cambridge-o-level-mathematics-4024-powers-and-roots/) explains each idea with worked examples; the [Powers and roots practice questions](/resources/cambridge-o-level-mathematics-4024-powers-and-roots-practice/) give exam-style questions with mark schemes.

## Syllabus outcome

Calculate with squares, square roots, cubes, cube roots and other powers and roots of numbers. This includes recall of squares and their roots from 1 to 15, and recall of cubes and their roots for 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 and 10.

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Power (index) | How many times the base is multiplied | 2⁵ = 32 |
| Square | Number times itself | 9² = 81 |
| Square root | Positive number whose square is given | √81 = 9 |
| Cube | Number times itself twice | 4³ = 64 |
| Cube root | Number whose cube is given | ∛64 = 4 |
| Perfect square or cube | A square or cube of an integer | 144, 216 |

|---|---|---|---|---|

| 6³ = 216 | 7³ = 343 | 8³ = 512 | 9³ = 729 | 10³ = 1000 |

Cubes 1 to 5 and 10 are required recall. The others are worth learning.

Other useful powers: 2⁶ = 64, 2⁷ = 128, 2⁸ = 256, 2¹⁰ = 1024, 3⁴ = 81, 3⁵ = 243, 5⁴ = 625, 10⁶ = 1 000 000.

## Signs

| Expression | Value | Why |
|---|---|---|
| (-4)² | 16 | even power, positive |
| (-4)³ | -64 | odd power, negative |
| -4² | -16 | power applies to 4 only |
| √49 | 7 | the symbol means the positive root |
| ∛(-27) | -3 | (-3)³ = -27 |
| √(-9) | no real value | no real number squares to -9 |

Equation rule: x² = 49 gives x = 7 or x = -7. But x³ = 27 gives only x = 3.

## Roots of fractions and decimals

| Expression | Working | Value |
|---|---|---|
| √(49/81) | 7/9 | 7/9 |
| ∛(8/125) | 2/5 | 2/5 |
| √0.25 | 0.5 × 0.5 = 0.25 | 0.5 |
| √0.0036 | 0.06 × 0.06 | 0.06 |
| ∛0.008 | 0.2 × 0.2 × 0.2 | 0.2 |
| √1.69 | 1.3 × 1.3 | 1.3 |

Check a decimal root by multiplying it back.

## Estimating a root

Bracket the number between two perfect squares (or cubes).

| Root | Neighbours | Result |
|---|---|---|
| √50 | 49 < 50 < 64 | between 7 and 8 |
| √90 | 81 < 90 < 100 | between 9 and 10 |
| ∛100 | 64 < 100 < 125 | between 4 and 5 |
| ∛30 | 27 < 30 < 64 | between 3 and 4 |

If the number is close to the lower square, the root is just above the lower integer. For instance, √50 is a little over 7 (7.07).

## Order of operations

1. Brackets (including under a root sign).
2. Powers and roots.
3. Multiplication and division.
4. Addition and subtraction.

```
√(9 + 16) = √25 = 5            √9 + √16 = 3 + 4 = 7
3 x 2^3 = 3 x 8 = 24           (3 x 2)^3 = 6^3 = 216
5^2 - 3^3 = 25 - 27 = -2
```

A root over a sum is not the sum of roots.

## Calculator (Paper 2)

- Use the power key for x^y. Enter a negative base in brackets.
- Use the general root key for fourth roots and above, or enter the power as a fraction.
- Do not round part way through.
- Non-exact answers are given to 3 significant figures unless the question says otherwise.

Examples: 7⁵ = 16 807; the fifth root of 7776 is 6; 1.05¹⁰ = 1.628... = 1.63 (3 s.f.).

## Exam traps

- 6² means 6 × 6, not 6 × 2.
- 4³ is 64, not 12.
- -5² = -25 but (-5)² = 25.
- Do not forget -k as a second answer to x² = k².
- √(a + b) is not √a + √b.
- A cube root of a negative number is negative.
- If asked "between which two integers", give both integers.
- Check the units: area is in squared units, volume in cubed units.

## Self-test

1. Write down the value of √144.
2. Work out 12³ ÷ 12².
3. Work out 2 × ∛125 + 3².
4. Work out (-2)⁵.
5. Work out √(100 - 36).
6. Between which two consecutive integers does √60 lie?
7. Solve x² = 169.
8. Solve x³ = -64.
9. Write √(36/121) as a fraction.
10. A cube has volume 216 cm³. Find the area of one face.

**Answers**

1. **12**.
2. 1728 ÷ 144 = **12**.
3. 2 × 5 + 9 = **19**.
4. **-32**.
5. √64 = **8**.
6. 49 < 60 < 64, so between **7 and 8**.
7. **x = 13 or x = -13**.
8. **x = -4**.
9. **6/11**.
10. Edge = ∛216 = 6 cm, so one face = **36 cm²**.
