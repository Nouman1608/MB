## Section A: short answers

**1.** State what is meant by a *corporate house style*. **[1]**

**2.** Identify **two** items of an organisation's stationery that should follow its house style. **[2]**

**3.** Which **one** of the following is where a house style is stored for a presentation?

A: a stylesheet

B: a master slide

C: a query

D: a macro

**[1]**

**4.** Give **two** elements, other than fonts, that a house style normally specifies. **[2]**

**5.** A student says: "A house style and a paragraph style are the same thing." Explain why the student is not correct. **[2]**

**6.** State **one** reason why a house style may specify the format in which dates are written. **[1]**

## Section B: structured questions

**7.** A chain of dental surgeries produces letters, appointment cards, a website and leaflets.

**(a)** Explain **two** reasons why the chain should use a corporate house style for all of these. **[4]**

**(b)** Describe how the house style can be applied to the website so that every page matches. **[2]**

**8.** An invented company has the following house style for reports: headings in a sans-serif font, 16 pt, bold, dark green; body text 11 pt, left aligned, 1.15 line spacing; company logo in the header, aligned right; page number centred in the footer.

A draft report has: headings 16 pt, bold, black; body text 11 pt, fully justified, 1.15 line spacing; logo in the header, aligned left; page number centred in the footer.

**(a)** Identify **three** ways in which the draft does not follow the house style. **[3]**

**(b)** Explain why the colour of the headings should be corrected by editing the heading style rather than by selecting each heading. **[2]**

**9.** A charity creates a word-processing template that contains its house style.

**(a)** Describe **three** items the template would contain. **[3]**

**(b)** Explain **one** benefit to the charity's volunteers of starting each document from the template. **[2]**

**10.** A sports club is redesigning its logo and colour scheme.

Describe **two** drawbacks of having a corporate house style that the club will face when it makes this change. **[2]**

**11.** A school's leaflets for parents each have a different layout, font and logo size, because different teachers create them.

Discuss the advantages and disadvantages of the school introducing a corporate house style. **[6]**

**12.** A bank wants its customers to know that an email really comes from the bank.

Explain how a consistent house style across its letters, website and emails could help with this, and state **one** limitation. **[3]**

## Answers

**1.** A set of rules for the appearance (and wording) of all of an organisation's documents and media, so that they look consistent / recognisably from the same organisation. **[1]**

**2.** Any two: letterhead (1); compliment slip (1); envelope (1); business card (1); invoice or order form (1). **[2]**

**3.** B: a master slide. **[1]**

**4.** Any two: logo and its position or size (1); colour scheme (1); margins or layout (1); line or paragraph spacing (1); header and footer content (1); slogan or standard wording (1); date format (1). **[2]**

**5.** A paragraph style is one named set of formatting applied to paragraphs in the software (1); a house style is the whole set of organisation-wide rules, which may be carried out using many styles, templates and master slides (1). **[2]**

**6.** Any one: so that all documents are consistent (1); to avoid confusion between formats such as 03/04/27, which can be read as 3 April or 4 March (1); to look professional (1). **[1]**

**7. (a)** Two reasons, each with an explanation, for example:
- Patients recognise the chain (1) because the same logo and colours appear on the letters, cards, website and leaflets (1).
- The chain looks professional and trustworthy (1) because every item matches, whichever surgery produced it (1).
- Staff save time (1) because they start from templates that already contain the layout and logo (1). **[4]**

**(b)** Create an external stylesheet holding the fonts, colours and layout rules (1); attach the same stylesheet to every page, so a change to the stylesheet updates all pages (1). **[2]**

**8. (a)** 1 mark each: headings are black and not dark green (1); body text is fully justified and not left aligned (1); logo is aligned left and not right (1). **[3]**

**(b)** Editing the style changes every heading at once (1), so no heading is missed and all are guaranteed to be the same colour (1). **[2]**

**9. (a)** Any three, 1 mark each: named styles for headings and body text (1); page setup such as margins and orientation (1); logo in the correct position (1); header and footer with standard content such as the charity's address or registered number (1); the colour scheme (1). **[3]**

**(b)** For example: volunteers do not need to know the rules or design anything (1), so their documents match the house style automatically and take less time to produce (1). **[2]**

**10.** Any two, 1 mark each: every template must be updated (1); master slides and the website stylesheet must be changed (1); existing printed stationery may have to be thrown away and reprinted, which costs money (1); members and staff must be told and shown the new rules (1). **[2]**

**11.** Up to 6 marks, for both sides. Maximum 4 for one side only.

Advantages:
- All leaflets would look consistent, whichever teacher made them (1).
- Parents would recognise leaflets as coming from the school (1).
- The school would appear more professional and organised (1).
- Teachers would save time by using a template (1).
- Important details such as the school address would be correct on every leaflet (1).

Disadvantages:
- Designing the house style and templates takes time (1).
- Teachers need to be shown how to use the template (1).
- Teachers have less freedom to design a leaflet that suits a particular event (1).
- Someone must check that the rules are followed (1). **[6]**

**12.** Customers become used to the bank's logo, colours and layout on letters and the website (1), so an email in the same style is easier to recognise as genuine and one that looks different may raise suspicion (1). Limitation: a criminal can copy the logo and colours, so the appearance alone does not prove the email is genuine (1). **[3]**

## Frequently asked questions

**Do I need to name real company house styles in the exam?**
No. The syllabus asks for the purpose and uses of a house style in general. Apply your points to the organisation named in the question.

**How many points do I need for an "explain" question?**
Each mark usually needs a reason, so an answer worth 4 marks typically needs two points, each explained, rather than four bare labels.
