Condensed notes for syllabus section 6.2, Robotics: the meaning of robotics, the three characteristics of a robot, and the roles, advantages and disadvantages of robots.

## Four facts to start with

1. **Robotics** is a branch of computer science covering the design, construction and operation of robots.
2. A robot has a **mechanical structure**, **electrical components** and is **programmable**.
3. Robots are used in industry, transport, agriculture, medicine, domestic settings and entertainment.
4. Robots have strong advantages, such as accuracy and working in danger, but cost a lot and can replace workers.

## Key terms

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Robotics | Branch of computer science dealing with the design, construction and operation of robots |
| Robot | Programmable machine with a mechanical structure and electrical components that carries out physical tasks |
| Mechanical structure | The framework or body of the robot |
| Sensor | Collects data from the surroundings |
| Microprocessor | Processes data and decides what to do |
| Actuator | Produces movement or action |
| Programmable | Able to follow instructions that can be written and changed |
| Drone | Unmanned flying machine; an example of a robot |

## Three characteristics

| Characteristic | Example |
|---|---|
| Mechanical structure or framework | Arm, wheels, rotors, body |
| Electrical components (sensors, microprocessors, actuators) | Camera, processor, motors |
| Programmable | Set of instructions that can be changed |

## Roles by area

| Area | Examples |
|---|---|
| Industry | Welding, painting, assembling, lifting heavy loads |
| Transport | Driverless vehicles, delivery drones |
| Agriculture | Planting, harvesting, spraying, crop monitoring |
| Medicine | Surgery assistance, carrying medicines, rehabilitation |
| Domestic | Vacuuming, lawn mowing, security |
| Entertainment | Toys, animatronic figures, camera drones |

## Advantages and disadvantages

| Advantages | Disadvantages |
|---|---|
| Works without breaks, 24 hours a day | High cost to buy, install and maintain |
| Accurate and consistent | Job losses |
| Safe in dangerous places | Poor at unexpected situations |
| Strong and fast | Breakdowns need skilled repair |
| Lower long-term labour cost | Safety and security risks |

## Robot or only an automated system?

| Test | If yes |
|---|---|
| Does it have a mechanical body that carries out physical tasks? | Robot |
| Is it controlled by a program? | Programmable |
| Does it use sensors, a microprocessor and actuators? | Electrical components |

A greenhouse controller has the electronics and program, but no robotic body, so it is an automated system rather than a robot.

## Worked example: a delivery drone

| Characteristic | In the drone |
|---|---|
| Mechanical structure | Frame with four rotors and a holder for the parcel |
| Electrical components | GPS and camera sensors, a processor, motors that spin the rotors |
| Programmable | Flight route and landing point entered by the operator |

Advantage: reaches remote villages quickly without a driver. Disadvantage: bad weather can stop it flying, and its battery limits the distance.

## How to develop a robot point

| Weak | Developed |
|---|---|
| "Robots are safer" | "A robot can handle toxic chemicals, so workers are not harmed" |
| "Robots are cheaper" | "Over many years one robot can replace several wages, although the first cost is high" |
| "Robots lose jobs" | "Workers whose tasks are replaced may be unemployed unless they are retrained" |

## Model answer to learn: robot in a factory

A paint-spraying robot has a mechanical arm, sensors that detect the position of the car body, a microprocessor that controls the spray pattern and motors that move the arm. It is programmable, so it can be changed to paint a new model. It paints with the same thickness each time and does not breathe the fumes, but it is expensive and cannot deal with an unexpected change in the car body.

## Exam traps

- **Robot means humanoid.** Most are not.
- **Robot thinks for itself.** It follows a program.
- **Characteristic missing.** Always give all three.
- **Advantage not linked to context.**
- **All automated systems are robots.** They need a mechanical structure.
- **Forgetting the cost.** Robots are expensive to buy and maintain.

## Self-test

1. What is robotics?
2. State three characteristics of a robot.
3. Give one example of a robot in agriculture.
4. Give one example of a robot in medicine.
5. Why can a robot be used in a dangerous environment?
6. Give two advantages of using a robot in a factory.
7. Give two disadvantages of using a robot in a factory.
8. Is a greenhouse temperature controller a robot? Explain.
9. What does "programmable" mean?
10. Name an example of a domestic robot.

**Answers**

1. A branch of computer science covering the design, construction and operation of robots.
2. A mechanical structure or framework; electrical components such as sensors, microprocessors and actuators; programmable.
3. Any one: fruit-picking robot, crop-spraying drone, field-monitoring drone, planting robot.
4. Any one: surgical robot, medicine-delivery robot, rehabilitation robot.
5. Because it protects people from harm; a robot can work with heat, chemicals or explosives without risk to a person.
6. Any two: works without breaks, accurate, consistent, fast, strong, lower long-term labour costs.
7. Any two: high cost, job losses, breakdowns, cannot adapt to unexpected situations.
8. No: it has sensors, a microprocessor and actuators, but no robotic body, so it is an automated system.
9. The robot follows a set of instructions that can be written, changed and run again.
10. Any one: robot vacuum cleaner, lawn-mowing robot.

## Command words to expect

| Command word | What to write |
|---|---|
| State, Give, Identify | A short answer with no explanation |
| Define | A precise meaning |
| Describe | The main points or characteristics, or the steps in order |
| Explain | A reason or "how and why", supported with evidence from the question |
| Compare | Similarities and differences, naming both sides every time |
| Suggest | A sensible proposal that applies your knowledge to the situation given |
| Evaluate | A judgement based on advantages and disadvantages, ending with a conclusion |
