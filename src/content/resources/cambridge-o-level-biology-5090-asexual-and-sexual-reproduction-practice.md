## Section A

**1.** Which statement describes asexual reproduction?

A: two parents produce genetically different offspring

B: one parent produces genetically identical offspring

C: gametes fuse to form a zygote

D: meiosis produces haploid cells that fuse

**[1]**

**2.** Which is an example of asexual reproduction?

A: fertilisation of an egg by a sperm

B: pollination of a flower

C: formation of a bud on a yeast cell

D: production of seeds by a flower

**[1]**

**3.** State what is meant by a clone. **[1]**

**4.** State the chromosome number (haploid or diploid) of a zygote. **[1]**

**5.** Name the type of nuclear division involved in asexual reproduction. **[1]**

## Section B

**6.** Describe sexual reproduction. **[4]**

**7.** A gardener grows strawberry plants. The plants produce runners with new plants growing on them.

**(a)** State the type of reproduction shown by the runners. **[1]**

**(b)** State **two** advantages to the gardener of growing new plants from runners. **[2]**

**(c)** State **one** disadvantage of a field containing only plants grown from runners. **[1]**

**(d)** Explain why this disadvantage matters if a new disease appears. **[2]**

**8.** A single bacterial cell divides every 25 minutes.

**(a)** Calculate the number of divisions in 2 hours 5 minutes. **[1]**

**(b)** Calculate the number of bacteria present at the end, if each division doubles the number. **[2]**

**(c)** State why the bacteria are genetically identical to the original cell. **[1]**

**(d)** Suggest why this rapid reproduction can be a problem when bacteria cause an infection. **[1]**

**9.** Compare asexual and sexual reproduction in terms of the number of parents, the type of cell involved and the genetic make-up of the offspring. **[6]**

**10.** Discuss the advantages and disadvantages of sexual reproduction for a species living in an environment that is changing. **[4]**

**11.** A rabbit population reproduces sexually. A disease kills most rabbits, but a few survive. Explain how sexual reproduction increases the chance that some rabbits will survive. **[3]**

**12.** State **two** reasons why asexual reproduction is useful for plants that cannot move. **[2]**

**13.** A farmer has a field of banana plants that were all grown from cuttings taken from one plant. A fungal disease spreads through the field and kills most of the plants.

**(a)** Explain why the disease affected almost all the plants. **[2]**

**(b)** Suggest how growing plants from seed would change the outcome. **[2]**

---

## Answers

**1.** B [1]. **[1]**

**2.** C [1]. **[1]**

**3.** A genetically identical copy of an organism (an organism genetically identical to its parent) [1]. **[1]**

**4.** Diploid [1]. **[1]**

**5.** Mitosis [1]. **[1]**

**6.** Two gametes are produced (usually by two parents) [1]. The gametes are haploid [1]. Their nuclei fuse at fertilisation to form a diploid zygote [1]. The offspring are genetically different from each other and from the parents [1]. **[4]**

**7.**

**(a)** Asexual reproduction [1].

**(b)** Any two of: only one parent is needed; the new plants are identical to a parent with good features (for example good fruit); new plants grow quickly; no need for pollination [1 each].

**(c)** All the plants are genetically identical (there is no variation) [1].

**(d)** If the disease can infect one plant it can infect all of them [1], because none has a different feature that might give resistance, so the whole crop could be lost [1]. **[6]**

**8.**

**(a)** 2 hours 5 minutes = 125 minutes, so 125 divided by 25 = 5 divisions [1].

**(b)** 2 to the power 5 [1] = 32 bacteria [1].

**(c)** They form by binary fission after the DNA is copied, so each new cell receives an identical copy [1].

**(d)** The bacteria can multiply quickly, so the infection can spread rapidly [1]. **[5]**

**9.** Number of parents: asexual one, sexual usually two [1, 1]. Type of cell: asexual does not involve gametes, sexual involves haploid gametes [1, 1]. Genetic make-up: asexual offspring are genetically identical to the parent [1], sexual offspring are genetically different [1]. **[6]**

**10.** Advantages: offspring are genetically different, giving variation [1]; some individuals may be better adapted to the new conditions and survive [1]. Disadvantages: it needs two parents and a mate [1]; it is slower and uses more energy [1]. **[4]**

**11.** Sexual reproduction produces genetically different offspring, so the rabbits show variation [1]. Some may have features that make them resistant to the disease [1], so they survive and reproduce, passing on the features [1]. **[3]**

**12.** Any two of: plants do not need a mate or pollinator; they can spread without moving; they can produce new plants quickly; new plants have the features of a successful parent [1 each]. **[2]**

**13.**

**(a)** The plants are clones, so they are genetically identical [1]. If one is susceptible to the fungus, all are susceptible [1].

**(b)** Plants from seed are produced by sexual reproduction and show variation [1], so some may be resistant and survive the disease [1]. **[4]**

## Where marks are usually lost

- Writing "similar" instead of "genetically identical".
- Giving advantages only in a "discuss" question.
- Linking meiosis to asexual reproduction.
- Saying the zygote is haploid.
- Forgetting to link variation to survival when the environment changes.
