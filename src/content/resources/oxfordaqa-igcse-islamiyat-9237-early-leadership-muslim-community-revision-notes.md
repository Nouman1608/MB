**These notes condense content area 3.1.1.8 of 9237: "the leadership of Islam after Hazrat Muhammad's (pbuh) death, and the role and importance of the four rightly guided Caliphs".** The full explanation is in the [Early leadership study guide](/resources/oxfordaqa-igcse-islamiyat-9237-early-leadership-muslim-community/).

## The big picture

- The Prophet (pbuh) died in 11 AH (632 CE). No prophet could follow (33:40), but a leader was needed.
- The leader was the **Khalifah** (successor); the office was the **Caliphate**.
- The first four are the **Khulafa ar-Rashidun**, "the rightly guided".
- Together they ruled about **29 years** (632-661).

## Timeline

| Caliph | Rule | Years | Selection |
|---|---|---|---|
| Abu Bakr (RA) | 632-634 | 2 | Consultation at the Saqifa; public pledge |
| Umar (RA) | 634-644 | 10 | Nominated by Abu Bakr |
| Uthman (RA) | 644-656 | 12 | Committee of six |
| Ali (RA) | 656-661 | 5 | Pledge by the people of Madinah |

**Memory aid:** the order follows the vowels A-U-U-A: **A**bu Bakr, **U**mar, **U**thman, **A**li. Rule lengths 2, 10, 12 and 5 years add up to 29.

## What each did

| Caliph | Title | Main achievements | Ending |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Abu Bakr** | As-Siddiq (the truthful) | Held the community together; Ridda wars; had the Qur'an collected after Yamama (Zayd ibn Thabit) | Died of illness, 634 |
| **Umar** | Al-Faruq (distinguisher) | Conquests in Syria, Egypt, Iraq, Persia; Jerusalem treaty; provinces, judges, *diwan*; Hijri calendar; just and simple | Killed in prayer by Abu Lu'lu'a, 644 |
| **Uthman** | Dhun-Nurayn (owner of two lights) | Standard copies of the Qur'an sent to cities; navy; enlarged the Prophet's Mosque | Besieged and killed, 656 |
| **Ali** | Ali ibn Abi Talib; cousin and son-in-law | Knowledge and justice; capital in Kufa; Camel and Siffin | Killed by Ibn Muljam, 661 |

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Khalifah | Successor; head of the community |
| Khulafa ar-Rashidun | Rightly guided Caliphs |
| Bay'ah | Pledge of allegiance |
| Shura | Consultation |
| Ridda | Apostasy (the wars of Abu Bakr) |
| Diwan | Register of stipends |
| Imam | Leader (in Shi'a belief, the divinely guided successors) |
| Bid'ah | Innovation in religion |
| Sunnah | The Prophet's example |

## Sunni and Shi'a views (neutral)

- **Sunni:** the community chose Abu Bakr by consultation, and the first four are rightly guided.
- **Shi'a:** the Prophet (pbuh) named Ali (RA) at Ghadir Khumm, so Ali (RA) is the first Imam.
- **Common ground:** both honour Ali (RA) and the Prophet's family and accept the Qur'an and Sunnah.

Use the neutral words "Sunni Muslims believe..." and "Shi'a Muslims believe...".

## Why the Caliphs are important

1. **Companions of the Prophet (pbuh):** they learned directly from him.
2. **Continuity:** they kept the community united after his death.
3. **Qur'an:** collected under Abu Bakr and standardised under Uthman.
4. **Leadership qualities:** consultation, justice, humility, service.
5. **Expansion:** Islam spread beyond Arabia.
6. **Hadith:** the Prophet (pbuh) told Muslims to follow the Sunnah of the Rightly Guided Caliphs.

## The passage: Hadith 28 of an-Nawawi

| Phrase | Meaning |
|---|---|
| "I counsel you to have Taqwa of Allah" | Awareness of Allah is the basis |
| "listen and obey [your leader], even if a slave were to become your Ameer" | Obedience and unity; authority does not depend on rank |
| "you will see great controversy" | The Prophet (pbuh) foresaw disagreements |
| "keep to my Sunnah and to the Sunnah of the Khulafa ar-Rashideen" | Follow the Prophet and his successors |
| "Cling to it stubbornly" | Hold firmly to authentic practice |
| "Beware of newly invented matters ... every Bidah is misguidance" | Avoid adding to the religion |

## Exam traps

- Caliphs were not prophets.
- Do not confuse the Ridda wars (Abu Bakr) with the later civil conflicts (Ali).
- Do not give dates without actions.
- Describe Shi'a and Sunni views without calling either wrong.
- Spell the titles as in the specification: "Khulafa ar-Rashideen" in the Hadith, "Caliphs" in the specification.

## A model 6-mark paragraph

**Question:** Explain **two** reasons why Hazrat Uthman (RA) and Hazrat Abu Bakr (RA) are important. Apply **one** teaching. **[6]**

Abu Bakr (RA) is important because he held the community together after the Prophet's death and fought those who left Islam or refused Zakah [1], which protected the new state [1]. Uthman (RA) is important because he sent standard copies of the Qur'an to the major cities [1], so Muslims everywhere recited the same text [1]. The Hadith says to keep to "the Sunnah of the Khulafa ar-Rashideen" [1], which shows that the Prophet (pbuh) valued their leadership and told Muslims to follow it [1].

## Four ways of choosing a leader

| Caliph | Method | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| Abu Bakr | Discussion among leading Companions, then a public pledge | Consultation (*shura*) and community consent |
| Umar | Named by Abu Bakr, then accepted by the people | A leader may be nominated, with the community's agreement |
| Uthman | Chosen from six Companions named by Umar | Consultation within a small group of senior figures |
| Ali | Pledge by the people of Madinah in a time of crisis | Acceptance by the community at a difficult moment |

**Key point for an exam answer:** there was no single fixed method, but every Caliph took office with the pledge of the community, and each pledged to rule by the Qur'an and Sunnah.

## Challenges facing the early community

- **Death of the Prophet (pbuh):** shock and the need for a leader.
- **Ridda wars:** tribes leaving Islam or refusing Zakah.
- **Preserving the Qur'an:** the loss of memorisers at Yamama.
- **Rapid expansion:** administration for new lands.
- **Division:** the killing of Uthman, the Camel and Siffin, the Khawarij.

## Model answer shapes

- **2 marks "Give two ways Abu Bakr helped the community":** He fought the Ridda wars; he had the Qur'an collected.
- **4 marks "Explain why Umar is important":** He organised provinces, judges and a treasury [1 and 1], and he was just and accountable, checking on the poor at night [1 and 1].
- **12 marks:** weigh whether obedience to leaders (the Hadith) or the quality of the leader matters more; use "taqwa", "listen and obey" and "great controversy"; give a judgement.

## Self-test

1. In which year did the Prophet (pbuh) die?
2. Who was the first Caliph?
3. Which Caliph introduced the Hijri calendar?
4. Who sent standard copies of the Qur'an to the cities?
5. How many years did the four Caliphs rule altogether?
6. Which battle led to the Qur'an being collected under Abu Bakr?
7. Who was Ali (RA) to the Prophet (pbuh)?
8. What was the pledge called?
9. Which Hadith is the prescribed passage for 3.1.1.8?
10. What did the Prophet (pbuh) say about *bid'ah*?

**Answers:** 1. 11 AH (632 CE). 2. Abu Bakr (RA). 3. Umar (RA). 4. Uthman (RA). 5. About twenty-nine years (632-661). 6. Yamama. 7. Cousin and son-in-law. 8. Bay'ah. 9. Hadith 28 of the Forty Hadith of an-Nawawi. 10. Beware of newly invented matters in religion, because every innovation is misguidance.
