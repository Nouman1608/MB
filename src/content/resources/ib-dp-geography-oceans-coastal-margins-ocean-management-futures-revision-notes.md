Condensed recall for **2.4 Ocean management futures** (IB DP Geography, Option B: Oceans and coastal margins). See the [Oceans and coastal margins study guide](/resources/ib-ib-dp-geography-oceans-coastal-margins-study-guide/).

## Key ideas

Governance, sustainable use and uncertainty. Oceans lie largely beyond single national control, so international frameworks matter.

## Governance tools

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| United Nations Convention on the Law of the Sea | Sets rules for ocean use, including exclusive economic zones |
| Exclusive economic zone (EEZ) | Zone in which a coastal state has rights over resources |
| Marine protected areas | Conserve habitats and give ecosystems space to recover |
| Fish quotas, certification schemes | Limit and label sustainable catches |
| Plastic reduction agreements | Target marine plastic |
| Ecosystem-based management | Manages the whole system rather than one species or use |

## Pressures on oceans

Overfishing, marine plastic, oil and chemical pollution, shipping, offshore energy, deep-sea mining and tourism, with climate change altering currents, ecosystems and sea level.

## Evaluation criteria

- Is it **enforceable** (especially beyond national waters)?
- Is it **fair** to developing states?
- Is it **effective across borders**?
- Is it **adaptable**?

**Adaptive management** can change as evidence improves, so it is often presented as more resilient than fixed plans under climate uncertainty.

## Building an answer

1. Name the pressure and the measure.
2. Explain how the measure should work.
3. Weigh strengths against limits (enforcement, fairness, cross-border effect).
4. Conclude with a judgement.

## Exam traps

- Listing pressures without linking them to a management response.
- Assuming a treaty works because it exists: ask about enforcement.
- Forgetting developing-state fairness.
- Inventing treaty dates or fish-stock figures; state general patterns instead.

## Self-test

1. What does an exclusive economic zone give a coastal state?
2. Why are marine protected areas used?
3. Name three pressures on the oceans.
4. Give two criteria for judging a management approach.
5. Why is adaptive management attractive under climate change?

**Answers:** 1. Rights over the resources of that zone. 2. To conserve habitats and fish stocks and let ecosystems recover. 3. For example overfishing, marine plastic, shipping. 4. For example enforceability and fairness to developing states. 5. It can be revised as evidence and conditions change.
