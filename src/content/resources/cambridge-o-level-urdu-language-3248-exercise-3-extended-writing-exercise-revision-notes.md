These notes cover **Exercise 3, Extended writing exercise**, of 3248 Paper 2: "candidates write a piece of continuous prose of approximately 200 words. A stimulus of short prompts will be printed in the question paper. A purpose, format and audience for the writing will be specified." For the whole paper see the [Paper 2 Grammar, Writing and Translation study guide](/resources/o-level-cambridge-urdu-second-language-paper-2-grammar-writing-translation/); for a worked model see the [Exercise 3 Extended writing exercise study guide](/resources/cambridge-o-level-urdu-language-3248-exercise-3-extended-writing-exercise/).

## Task facts

| Fact | Detail |
|---|---|
| Marks | 20 (the largest exercise in Paper 2) |
| Objectives | W1, W2, W3, W4, W5 |
| Length | approximately 200 words, continuous prose |
| Stimulus | short prompts printed on the paper |
| Specified | purpose, format and audience |
| Language | question and response in Urdu; dictionaries not allowed |
| Paper | 1 hour 30 minutes, 50 marks, four exercises |
| Not printed in the syllabus | the split of the 20 marks: see past-paper mark schemes |

## What each objective asks

- **W1:** clear, relevant ideas and opinions that cover the prompts.
- **W2:** logical paragraphs with linking devices.
- **W3:** accurate grammar, a range of structures and suitable vocabulary.
- **W4:** correct punctuation and spelling.
- **W5:** register, style and format that suit the purpose and audience.

## PFA: purpose, format, audience

| Letter | Ask | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| P | Why am I writing? | inform, persuade, describe, advise, invite, thank |
| F | What form? | informal letter or email, article, blog, report, review, speech |
| A | Who will read it? | friend, teacher, principal, magazine readers |

## Format checklist

| Format | Must have |
|---|---|
| Informal email or letter | subject or date, greeting (*پیارے ...*), friendly body, closing (*تمھارا دوست*) |
| Formal letter | *محترم ...*، *آپ*, respectful closing (*آپ کا مخلص*) |
| Article | title, opening idea, body, closing thought |
| Blog | personal voice, address to readers |
| Report | title, headed parts, facts, recommendation |
| Review | what, good points, weak points, verdict |
| Speech | greeting, appeals, strong close |

## Five-minute plan

1. Underline P, F and A.
2. List every prompt.
3. Add an example or reason to each.
4. Put them in order.
5. Plan the opening and closing.

## Linking words

| Use | Words |
|---|---|
| First, next, last | سب سے پہلے، پھر، اس کے بعد، آخر میں |
| Add | اس کے علاوہ، نیز |
| Contrast | لیکن، البتہ، تاہم |
| Reason and result | اس لیے، چنانچہ، جس کی وجہ سے |
| Opinion | میرے خیال میں، میری رائے ہے کہ |
| Conclude | خلاصہ یہ ہے کہ |

## Register guide

| Reader | Pronoun | Opening | Closing |
|---|---|---|---|
| Friend | *تم* | پیارے دوست | تمھارا دوست |
| Teacher or principal | *آپ* | محترم / محترمہ | آپ کا مخلص / آپ کی مخلص |
| Magazine readers | *ہم / آپ* | پیارے ساتھیو | شکریہ |
| Report reader | third person | (title) | (recommendation) |

Do not switch between *تم* and *آپ* in one piece.

## Accuracy checks

- **Verb and subject agreement:** *وہ جاتی ہے*، *وہ جاتے ہیں*.
- **Past tense with *نے*:** the verb agrees with the object: *میں نے کتاب پڑھی* but *میں نے خط پڑھا*.
- **Postpositions after nouns:** *کتاب میں*, *میز پر*, *دوست کو*.
- **Punctuation:** *۔* at the end of statements, *؟* at the end of questions, *،* for commas.
- **Common spellings:** *انھوں*, *تمھارا*, *مشکلات*, *ذمہ داری*.

## Common faults

| Fault | Fix |
|---|---|
| Prompt missed | tick each prompt off the plan |
| Format ignored | add the greeting, title or closing |
| Wrong register | choose *تم* or *آپ* once and keep it |
| One block of text | split into paragraphs of 3 or 4 sentences |
| Too short | develop each prompt with a reason or example |
| Too long | cut repeated ideas |
| English word order | put the verb at the end of the Urdu sentence |

## Exam traps

- Starting to write before planning.
- Writing a perfect first paragraph and leaving no time for the last prompt.
- Using a formal letter layout for a friend.
- Using words you cannot spell.
- Forgetting to leave time to proofread.

## Self-test

1. How many marks is Exercise 3 worth?
2. How long should the piece be?
3. What does PFA stand for?
4. Which pronoun suits a message to a principal?
5. Give **two** linking words for adding an idea.
6. Where should the verb go in a basic Urdu sentence?
7. What punctuation ends a question?
8. Why should every prompt appear in the plan?
9. Give **one** feature of an article that a letter does not need.
10. What is wrong with writing *تم* in one line and *آپ* in the next?

**Answers**

1. Twenty.
2. Approximately 200 words, in continuous prose.
3. Purpose, format, audience.
4. *آپ*.
5. Any two: اس کے علاوہ، نیز، مزید برآں.
6. At the end.
7. *؟*
8. The task specifies the prompts, and covering all of them is part of communicating the required ideas (W1).
9. A title.
10. It mixes registers, which weakens the appropriate style required by W5.
