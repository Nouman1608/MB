These questions cover section 4.4, Similarity, of the Cambridge O Level Mathematics (Syllabus D) 4024 syllabus for 2025, 2026 and 2027. The method is explained in the [similarity study guide](/resources/cambridge-o-level-mathematics-4024-similarity/), and the [geometry revision notes](/resources/o-level-mathematics-geometry-revision-notes/) summarise it. Show your working. Give non-exact answers to 3 significant figures unless told otherwise.

## Section A

**1.** Triangles ABC and PQR are similar, with A matching P, B matching Q and C matching R. AB = 5 cm, PQ = 8 cm and BC = 7.5 cm. Find QR. **[2]**

**2.** Two rectangles are similar. One measures 6 cm by 4 cm. The longer side of the other is 15 cm. Find its shorter side. **[2]**

**3.** Two similar shapes have corresponding lengths in the ratio 2 : 5. Write down the ratio of their areas. **[1]**

**4.** Two similar solids have corresponding lengths in the ratio 3 : 4. Write down the ratio of their volumes. **[1]**

**5.** Two similar shapes have areas 48 cm² and 75 cm². Find the ratio of their corresponding lengths in its simplest form. **[2]**

**6.** Two similar jugs have heights 10 cm and 15 cm. The smaller jug holds 400 ml. How much does the larger jug hold? **[3]**

## Section B

**7.** In triangle ABC, D lies on AB and E lies on AC. DE is parallel to BC. AD = 3 cm, AB = 9 cm and DE = 4 cm.

**(a)** Show that triangles ADE and ABC are similar, giving reasons.

**(b)** Find the length of BC.

**(c)** The area of triangle ADE is 5 cm². Find the area of the quadrilateral DBCE. **[8]**

**8.** A model of a ship is made to a scale of 1 : 200.

**(a)** The model is 45 cm long. Find the length of the real ship in metres.

**(b)** The real ship has a deck of area 500 m². Find the area of the deck on the model, in square centimetres.

**(c)** The hull of the model has a volume of 250 cm³. Find the volume of the real hull in cubic metres. **[6]**

**9.** Two similar cylinders have surface areas in the ratio 9 : 16. The smaller cylinder has a volume of 270 cm³. Find the volume of the larger cylinder. **[3]**

**10.** A vertical post of height 1.5 m casts a shadow 2 m long. At the same time a building casts a shadow 28 m long. Find the height of the building, and state the assumption you made. **[3]**

**11.** Explain why all circles are similar, but not all rectangles are similar. **[2]**

**12.** Triangle P has sides 6 cm, 8 cm and 10 cm. Triangle Q has sides 9 cm, 12 cm and 15 cm.

**(a)** Show that the triangles are similar.

**(b)** The area of triangle P is 24 cm². Find the area of triangle Q.

**(c)** Find the ratio of the perimeters of P and Q. **[5]**

## Answers

**1.** Scale factor = 8 ÷ 5 = 1.6 [1]. QR = 1.6 × 7.5 = **12 cm** [1].

**2.** Scale factor = 15 ÷ 6 = 2.5 [1]. Shorter side = 2.5 × 4 = **10 cm** [1].

**3.** **4 : 25** [1].

**4.** **27 : 64** [1].

**5.** Area ratio 48 : 75 = 16 : 25 [1]. Length ratio = **4 : 5** [1].

**6.** Length scale factor = 15 ÷ 10 = 1.5 [1]. Volume scale factor = 1.5³ = 3.375 [1]. Capacity = 400 × 3.375 = **1350 ml** [1].

**7. (a)** Angle DAE = angle BAC because it is a common angle [1]. Angle ADE = angle ABC because they are corresponding angles with DE parallel to BC [1]. Two pairs of equal angles, so the triangles are similar [1].

**(b)** Scale factor = 9 ÷ 3 = 3 [1]. BC = 3 × 4 = **12 cm** [1].

**(c)** Area scale factor = 3² = 9, so area ABC = 5 × 9 = 45 cm² [1][1]. DBCE = 45 − 5 = **40 cm²** [1].

**8. (a)** 45 × 200 = 9000 cm [1] = **90 m** [1].

**(b)** Area scale factor = 200² = 40 000. 500 m² ÷ 40 000 = 0.0125 m² [1] = **125 cm²** [1].

**(c)** Volume scale factor = 200³ = 8 000 000, so 250 × 8 000 000 = 2 × 10⁹ cm³ [1] = **2000 m³** [1].

*Examiner insight:* Convert units only at the end. 1 m² = 10 000 cm² and 1 m³ = 1 000 000 cm³.

**9.** Length ratio = √9 : √16 = 3 : 4 [1]. Volume ratio = 27 : 64 [1]. Larger volume = 270 × 64/27 = **640 cm³** [1].

**10.** Scale factor = 28 ÷ 2 = 14 [1]. Height = 14 × 1.5 = **21 m** [1]. The assumption is that the sun's rays make the same angle with level ground, so the two triangles are similar (the post and building are both vertical) [1].

**11.** Every circle is an enlargement of any other, since all lengths scale by the ratio of the radii [1]. Rectangles can have different ratios of length to width, for example 2 : 1 and 3 : 1, so they need not be enlargements of each other [1].

**12. (a)** 9 ÷ 6 = 1.5, 12 ÷ 8 = 1.5 and 15 ÷ 10 = 1.5 [1]. All three pairs of sides are in the same ratio, so the triangles are similar [1].

**(b)** Area factor = 1.5² = 2.25 [1], so area = 24 × 2.25 = **54 cm²** [1].

**(c)** The perimeters are in the same ratio as the lengths, **2 : 3** [1].

## Where marks are usually lost

- Matching the wrong sides when the triangles are turned.
- Using the length scale factor for areas or volumes.
- Giving "they look similar" in place of reasons.
- Forgetting to take a square root or cube root when moving from an area or volume ratio to a length ratio.
- Mixing up units after a scale conversion (cm², m², cm³, m³).
