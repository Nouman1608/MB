## The exercise in one table

| Feature | Exercise 1 |
|---|---|
| Marks | 8 (of Paper 1's 60) |
| Text | one short text: advertisement, brochure, leaflet, guide, report, manual, instructions, or newspaper/magazine article |
| Skill | skimming and gist-reading |
| Answer length | a single word or short phrase |
| Objectives | R1, R2, R3, R4 |
| Language | questions and answers in Urdu, no dictionary |
| Suggested time | about 10-12 minutes |

## Question word → answer form

| Urdu | Wants | Example answer |
|---|---|---|
| کب | a time or date | 10 نومبر / شام 4 بجے |
| کتنے دن | a duration | پانچ دن |
| کہاں | a place | ہال نمبر 2 |
| کتنے / کتنی | a number or amount | 25 فیصد |
| کون / کس نے | a person | ٹیم کی رکن فاطمہ |
| کیوں | a reason | جگہ محدود ہے |
| کیسے | a method | بس سے |
| کس مقصد کے لیے | purpose (gist) | دعوت دینا / معلومات دینا |

**Rule:** predict the form before you scan. A کب question answered with a duration scores nothing.

## Qualifiers: the trap words

These words narrow, extend or reverse a rule. When the key word of a question appears near one of
them, read the whole sentence.

| Word | Meaning | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| صرف | only | narrows |
| کم از کم | at least | sets a minimum |
| زیادہ سے زیادہ | at most | sets a maximum |
| سے کم / سے زیادہ | less than / more than | sets a limit |
| کے علاوہ | apart from, as well as | adds or excludes |
| سوائے | except | excludes |
| مگر / لیکن / البتہ | but / however | introduces the exception |
| ضرور | definitely | marks a requirement |

## Text-type signals

- **Instructions:** polite imperatives کیجیے، رکھیے، بھریے; negatives with نہ (نہ کریں). Sequence
  words پہلے، پھر، اس کے بعد، آخر میں.
- **Advertisements:** prices, رعایت (discount), مفت (free), deadlines (آخری تاریخ).
- **Articles and reports:** the first sentence normally gives who, what, where and when; quoted speech
  (فاطمہ نے کہا) gives opinions and reasons.

## Worked example: a leaflet

**Text** (written for this page):

> **شہر کا کتاب میلہ**\
> تاریخ: 12 تا 14 دسمبر\
> وقت: صبح 10 بجے سے رات 8 بجے تک\
> جگہ: ٹاؤن ہال، ہال نمبر 3\
> داخلہ: بڑوں کے لیے 100 روپے، طلبہ کے لیے 50 روپے (اسکول کارڈ ضرور ساتھ لائیں)۔ بارہ سال سے کم
> عمر بچوں کا داخلہ مفت ہے۔\
> ہر روز شام 5 بجے بچوں کے لیے کہانی کا وقت ہوگا۔ ہال کے اندر کھانے پینے کی اجازت نہیں، البتہ باہر
> کینٹین موجود ہے۔

**English gloss:** "City Book Fair. Dates: 12 to 14 December. Time: 10 a.m. to 8 p.m. Place: Town Hall,
Hall 3. Entry: 100 rupees for adults, 50 rupees for students (be sure to bring your school card).
Entry is free for children under twelve. Every day at 5 p.m. there will be story time for children.
Eating and drinking are not allowed inside the hall; however, there is a canteen outside."

| Question | Form wanted | Answer | Trap |
|---|---|---|---|
| میلہ کتنے دن جاری رہے گا؟ | a duration | تین دن | "دو دن": 12, 13 and 14 are three days |
| طلبہ کو داخلے کے لیے کیا ساتھ لانا ہوگا؟ | a thing | اسکول کارڈ | ضرور marks the requirement |
| دس سال کا بچہ داخلے کے کتنے پیسے دے گا؟ | an amount | کچھ نہیں (مفت) | سے کم: under twelve is free |
| لوگ کھانا کہاں کھا سکتے ہیں؟ | a place | باہر کینٹین میں | البتہ introduces the exception |
| بچوں کے لیے کہانی کا وقت کب ہوگا؟ | a time | ہر روز شام 5 بجے | not 10 بجے, the opening time |
| یہ پرچہ کس مقصد کے لیے لکھا گیا ہے؟ | a purpose (gist) | لوگوں کو کتاب میلے کی معلومات دینا / میلے میں آنے کی دعوت دینا | not one detail such as the price |

## Step-by-step method

1. **Look at the layout** for ten seconds: title, headings, bold words, figures. These tell you the
   text type and where details sit.
2. **Read all the questions**, circling each question word and writing the answer form beside it.
3. **Scan** for the key word of the first question (داخلہ، وقت، جگہ). Questions usually follow the order
   of the text.
4. **Read the whole sentence** around the key word, and the next one, for a qualifier.
5. **Write the shortest complete answer**, with its unit.
6. **Answer the gist question last**, once you have read the whole text.

## More useful vocabulary

| Urdu | English |
|---|---|
| تا | to (between two dates or numbers) |
| داخلہ | entry, admission |
| جاری رہنا | to continue, last |
| موجود | available, present |
| شرکت | participation |
| رجسٹریشن / اندراج | registration |
| رعایتی | discounted |
| پیشگی | in advance |
| عمر | age |

## More exam traps

- Counting a date range as the difference (14 − 12 = 2) instead of the number of days (3).
- Giving the general opening time when the question asks about one event within the day.
- Answering a purpose question with a fact from one line.
- Leaving out مفت or کچھ نہیں and writing only a number that is not in the text.

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Skimming | reading quickly for the overall sense and layout |
| Scanning | searching for one specific word, number or name |
| Gist | the main point or purpose of the whole text |
| Distractor | a nearby detail that looks right but answers a different question |
| Qualifier | a word that limits or changes a rule |
| Lifting | copying words from the text; acceptable here if only the answer is copied |

## Five habits that earn the marks

1. Read the layout, then every question, before reading closely.
2. Underline the question word and predict the answer form.
3. Read the sentence after the answer for a qualifier.
4. Write one precise answer, with its unit (روپے، بجے، دن).
5. Answer every question; questions usually follow the order of the text.

## Self-test

1. How many marks is Exercise 1 worth, and what length of answer does it require?
2. Name four text types the syllabus lists for Exercise 1.
3. What form of answer does کتنے دن want, and how is it different from کب?
4. An advert says "بچوں کے لیے ٹکٹ 50 روپے، تین سال سے کم عمر بچوں کا داخلہ مفت". What does a
   two-year-old pay?
5. What does the instruction ایک لفظ یا مختصر فقرے میں جواب دیجیے mean?
6. Why is "ٹکٹ کی قیمت بتانا" a weak answer to یہ اشتہار کس مقصد کے لیے لکھا گیا ہے؟
7. Is copying words from the text acceptable in Exercise 1?
8. What does تا mean in "12 تا 14 دسمبر", and how many days is that?
9. A leaflet says "اندر کھانے کی اجازت نہیں، البتہ باہر کینٹین موجود ہے". Where can people eat?
10. Which word in "اسکول کارڈ ضرور ساتھ لائیں" marks a requirement?
11. Why answer the gist question last?
12. Give the Urdu for "in advance" and for "registration".

**Answers:** 1. 8 marks; a single word or short phrase. 2. Any four of: advertisement, brochure,
leaflet, guide, report, manual, instructions, newspaper or magazine article. 3. A duration (پانچ دن);
کب wants a point in time (15 نومبر، شام 4 بجے). 4. Nothing: entry is free (مفت), because of the
qualifier سے کم. 5. "Answer in one word or a short phrase." 6. It names one detail of the text, while
a purpose question wants the point of the whole text, such as inviting people or giving information
about an event. 7. Yes, provided only the part that answers the question is copied; own words are
required in Exercise 4, not here. 8. "To"; three days (12, 13 and 14). 9. Outside, in the canteen: البتہ
introduces the exception. 10. ضرور. 11. Because the purpose of a text is clearest once you have read all
of it, and the detail questions have already taken you through it. 12. پیشگی; رجسٹریشن or اندراج.
