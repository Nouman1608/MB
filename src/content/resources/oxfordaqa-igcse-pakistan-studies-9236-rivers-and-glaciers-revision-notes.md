These notes condense one content point from key development 3.2.1.1 of the 9236 specification:
"formation, processes and landforms of rivers and glaciers in Pakistan". The full explanation is in
the [rivers and glaciers study guide](/resources/oxfordaqa-igcse-pakistan-studies-9236-rivers-and-glaciers/);
the wider topic is in the
[Landscape revision notes](/resources/igcse-oxfordaqa-pakistan-studies-landscape-revision-notes/).

## The Indus system in order

1. Rises in **Tibet**; flows north-west through Ladakh into **Gilgit-Baltistan** (Skardu).
2. Joined by the **Gilgit River**; turns south around **Nanga Parbat** through deep gorges.
3. Dammed at **Tarbela**; joined by the **Kabul River** at **Attock**.
4. Leaves the mountains at **Kalabagh**.
5. Eastern rivers **Jhelum, Chenab, Ravi, Sutlej** (with the Beas) join as the **Panjnad**, which meets
   the Indus near **Mithankot**.
6. Crosses Sindh (Sukkur, Kotri) to the **delta** south of Thatta and the **Arabian Sea**.

**Doabs** (land between rivers): Sindh Sagar, Chaj, Rechna, Bari.

## River processes

| Process | Type | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Hydraulic action | Erosion | Force of water breaks rock |
| Abrasion | Erosion | Carried stones scrape bed and banks |
| Attrition | Erosion | Stones hit each other, becoming smaller and rounder |
| Solution | Erosion and transport | Rock minerals dissolve and are carried |
| Traction | Transport | Boulders roll along the bed |
| Saltation | Transport | Pebbles bounce along the bed |
| Suspension | Transport | Silt and clay carried in the water |
| Deposition | Deposition | Load dropped when the river slows |

## River landforms

| Course | Landform | Formed by | Pakistani example |
|---|---|---|---|
| Upper | V-shaped valley, gorge | Vertical erosion | Indus gorge around Nanga Parbat |
| Upper | Interlocking spurs | River winding round ridges | Northern mountain valleys |
| Middle and lower | Meander | Outer-bend erosion, inner-bend deposition | Rivers of the Punjab plains |
| Middle and lower | Oxbow lake | Neck cut through in flood, ends sealed by deposition | Old channels on the plains |
| Lower | Flood plain | Silt deposited in floods | Punjab and Sindh |
| Lower | Levee | Coarse sediment dropped at banks | Indus banks, strengthened by bunds |
| Lower | Braided channel | Heavy load and seasonal flow | Indus in the plains |
| Mouth | Delta | Sediment deposited at the sea | Indus delta, with mangroves |

## Glaciers

- **Formation:** snow compressed into **firn**, then **glacier ice**.
- **Budget:** accumulation zone (gain) against ablation zone (loss).
- **Major Karakoram glaciers:** Siachen, Baltoro, Biafo, Hispar, Batura.
- **Erosion:** plucking, abrasion, freeze-thaw weathering.

| Landform | Formation | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Cirque (corrie) | Hollow eroded where a glacier begins | High Karakoram |
| Arête | Ridge between two cirques | Karakoram ridges |
| Pyramidal peak | Several cirques eroding one peak | K2 often cited |
| U-shaped valley | Glacier widens and deepens a valley | Northern valleys |
| Hanging valley | Side valley left above the main trough | Northern valleys, often with waterfalls |
| Medial moraine | Debris where two glaciers join | Baltoro at Concordia |
| Terminal moraine | Debris at the glacier's snout | Ends of Karakoram glaciers |

## Links to people and hazards

- Summer **meltwater** feeds the Indus and the Tarbela and Mangla reservoirs.
- **GLOFs:** Shisper Glacier flood destroyed the Hassanabad bridge (May 2022).
- **Attabad Lake:** formed when a landslide blocked the Hunza River (January 2010).
- **Delta shrinking:** less water and silt reach the sea because of dams and barrages.

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Load | Material carried by a river or glacier |
| Firn | Compressed granular snow on its way to becoming ice |
| Ablation | Loss of ice by melting and evaporation |
| Moraine | Debris carried and deposited by a glacier |
| Doab | Land between two rivers |
| GLOF | Glacial lake outburst flood |

## Exam traps

- V-shaped (river) and U-shaped (glacial) valleys confused.
- Landforms described without the processes that form them.
- No Pakistani examples given.
- Abrasion and attrition mixed up.
- Forgetting that dams also change the lower river by trapping silt.

## Self-test

1. Where does the Indus rise?
2. At which town does the Kabul River join the Indus?
3. What is the Panjnad?
4. Define attrition.
5. Name the four processes of river transport.
6. Explain why a meander has a river cliff on one side and a slip-off slope on the other.
7. What is a levee, and how does it form?
8. Why is the Indus delta shrinking?
9. What is firn?
10. Name two large Karakoram glaciers.
11. What is a medial moraine, and where can one be seen?
12. What happened at Hassanabad in May 2022?

## Answers

1. In Tibet.
2. Attock.
3. The combined flow of the eastern rivers (Jhelum, Chenab, Ravi and Sutlej with the Beas), which
   joins the Indus near Mithankot.
4. Stones carried by the river knock against each other and become smaller and rounder.
5. Traction, saltation, suspension and solution.
6. Faster water on the outer bend erodes a steep cliff; slower water on the inner bend deposits
   sediment, forming a gentle slope.
7. A raised bank along a river, formed when floodwater spills over and drops its coarsest sediment
   first near the channel.
8. Dams and barrages hold back water and silt, so less sediment reaches the coast and seawater moves
   inland.
9. Compressed granular snow, between fresh snow and glacier ice.
10. Any two: Siachen, Baltoro, Biafo, Hispar, Batura.
11. A ridge of debris formed where two glaciers join; on the Baltoro Glacier at Concordia.
12. A flood linked to the Shisper Glacier destroyed the Hassanabad bridge on the Karakoram Highway.
