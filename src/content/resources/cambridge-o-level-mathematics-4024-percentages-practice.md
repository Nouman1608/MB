## Section A

**1.** Work out 45% of 360. **[1]**

**2.** Write 26 out of 40 as a percentage. **[2]**

**3.** **(a)** Increase 84 by 25%. **[2]**

**(b)** Decrease 90 by 12%. **[2]**

**4.** The price of a bicycle rises from $160 to $188. Find the percentage increase. **[2]**

**5.** A company buys a machine for $240 and sells it at a profit of 35%. Find the selling price. **[2]**

**6.** A buyer pays a deposit of 20% on a sofa that costs $1 450. How much is the deposit? **[1]**

**7.** Express 90 as a percentage of 60. **[1]**

**8.** After a 12% discount, a coat costs $66. Find the original price. **[3]**

## Section B

**9.** *Calculator.* Mr Khan invests $3 200 at 3.5% simple interest per year for 4 years. Calculate the total amount at the end of the 4 years. **[3]**

**10.** *Calculator.* Mrs Lee invests $3 200 at 3.5% compound interest per year for 3 years. Calculate the interest she earns. **[3]**

**11.** *Calculator.* A car costs $24 000 new. Its value falls by 18% each year. Calculate its value after 2 years. **[3]**

**12.** The price of a share is increased by 20%. A month later the new price is decreased by 20%. Show that the final price is 4% lower than the original price. **[3]**

**13.** *Calculator.* A salesperson earns $1 800 per month plus a commission of 2.5% of her sales. Her sales one month are $14 000. Calculate her total earnings that month. **[3]**

**14.** *Calculator.* After an 8% increase, a monthly rent is $91.80. Find the rent before the increase. **[2]**

**15.** A shop sells a shirt for $44, which gives a profit of 10% of the cost price. Find the cost price. **[3]**

**16.** A salary rises from $2 400 to $3 000. Find the percentage increase. **[2]**

**17.** *Calculator.* The population of a town is 50 000 and grows by 3% each year. Calculate the population after 2 years. **[3]**

## Answers

**1.** 0.45 × 360 = **162** **[1]**

**2.** 26/40 × 100 **[1]** = **65%** **[1]** **[2]**

**3. (a)** 84 × 1.25 **[1]** = **105** **[1]**

**(b)** 90 × 0.88 **[1]** = **79.2** **[1]** **[4]**

**4.** change = 28 **[1]**; 28 ÷ 160 × 100 = **17.5%** **[1]** **[2]**

*Examiner insight:* Divide by the original price, $160, not the new price.

**5.** 240 × 1.35 **[1]** = **$324** **[1]** **[2]**

**6.** 0.2 × 1450 = **$290** **[1]**

**7.** 90/60 × 100 = **150%** **[1]**

**8.** The coat is 88% of the original, so original × 0.88 = 66 **[1]**; 66 ÷ 0.88 **[1]** = **$75** **[1]** **[3]**

*Examiner insight:* Finding 12% of $66 and adding it gives $73.92, which is wrong. The discount was 12% of the original price.

**9.** I = 3200 × 3.5 × 4 ÷ 100 = 448 **[2]**; total = 3200 + 448 = **$3 648** **[1]** **[3]**

**10.** 3200 × 1.035³ **[1]** = 3547.8972 **[1]**; interest = **$347.90** **[1]** **[3]**

**11.** multiplier 0.82 **[1]**; 24 000 × 0.82² **[1]** = **$16 137.60** **[1]** **[3]**

**12.** multipliers 1.2 and 0.8 **[1]**; 1.2 × 0.8 = 0.96 **[1]**; 0.96 means a decrease of 4% **[1]** **[3]**

*Examiner insight:* An increase and decrease of the same percentage do not cancel, because the second change is taken on a different amount.

**13.** commission = 0.025 × 14 000 = 350 **[1]**; 1800 + 350 **[1]** = **$2 150** **[1]** **[3]**

**14.** original × 1.08 = 91.80 **[1]**; 91.80 ÷ 1.08 = **$85** **[1]** **[2]**

**15.** selling price = 110% of cost, so cost × 1.1 = 44 **[1]**; 44 ÷ 1.1 **[1]** = **$40** **[1]** **[3]**

*Examiner insight:* Profit is a percentage of the cost price, not of the selling price. Taking 10% off $44 gives $39.60, which is wrong.

**16.** change = 600 **[1]**; 600 ÷ 2400 × 100 = **25%** **[1]** **[2]**

**17.** multiplier 1.03 **[1]**; 50 000 × 1.03² = 50 000 × 1.0609 **[1]** = **53 045** **[1]** **[3]**

## Where marks are usually lost

- Dividing by the new value in Question 4.
- Taking 12% of the sale price instead of dividing by 0.88 in Question 8.
- Giving the interest rather than the total amount in Question 9, or the total rather than the interest in Question 10.
- Using simple interest in Question 10.
- Adding 18% twice instead of using the multiplier twice in Question 11.
- Using 1.8 as the multiplier for an 8% rise in Question 14.

## Frequently asked questions

**Are the interest formulas given in the exam?**
No. The syllabus says the formulas are not given. Learn I = PRT ÷ 100 for simple interest and the repeated multiplier for compound interest.

**Why do I divide in a reverse percentage problem?**
The new value is the original multiplied by the multiplier. Dividing by the multiplier undoes that step.
