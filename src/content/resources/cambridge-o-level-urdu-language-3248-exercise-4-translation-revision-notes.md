These notes cover **Exercise 4, Translation**, of 3248 Paper 2: "candidates translate a short passage
of approximately 150 words from English into Urdu". The paper overview is in the
[Paper 2 Grammar, Writing and Translation study guide](/resources/o-level-cambridge-urdu-second-language-paper-2-grammar-writing-translation/);
the full method and an annotated translation are in the
[Exercise 4 Translation study guide](/resources/cambridge-o-level-urdu-language-3248-exercise-4-translation/).

## Task facts

- **15 marks**, the second-largest tariff on Paper 2 (Exercise 3 extended writing has 20).
- About **150 words** of English, translated **into Urdu**.
- Question in English, answer in Urdu; no dictionaries.
- Objectives **W1–W5**.

## The objectives in one line each

- **W1**: all of the meaning, nothing added.
- **W2**: links kept (*but* → *لیکن*، *so* → *اس لیے*).
- **W3**: accurate grammar: tense, agreement, postpositions, word order.
- **W4**: Urdu punctuation and spelling.
- **W5**: the tone and register of the original.

## Word order

English **S V O** → Urdu **S O V**. Postpositions follow nouns. Time and place usually come early:
*We met him at the station yesterday* → *ہم کل اسٹیشن پر اس سے ملے۔*

## Tense map

| English | Urdu pattern |
|---|---|
| present simple (habit) | کرتا ہے |
| present continuous | کر رہا ہے |
| past simple | کیا / گیا |
| past continuous | کر رہا تھا |
| present perfect | کر چکا ہے / کر لیا ہے |
| past perfect | کر چکا تھا |
| used to | کیا کرتا تھا / کرتا تھا |
| for + period (still true) | ... سے کر رہا ہے |
| future | کرے گا |
| would (conditional) | کرتا / کرے گا (with اگر ... تو) |

## Articles

| English | Urdu |
|---|---|
| the | not translated |
| a / an (new, one) | ایک |
| some | کچھ |
| any (questions, negatives) | کوئی |

## Gender check

Pick the noun, know its gender, then make every adjective, *کا/کی/کے* and verb agree.
*پرانا مکان* / *پرانی عمارت*; *نیا قلم* / *نئی کتاب*.

## Register

| English | Friend | Teacher, elder, stranger |
|---|---|---|
| you | تم | آپ |
| come | آؤ | تشریف لائیے / آئیے |
| tell me | بتاؤ | بتائیے |
| my grandfather says | — | میرے دادا کہتے ہیں (respect plural) |

## Idioms: meaning, not words

| English | Urdu |
|---|---|
| raining cats and dogs | موسلا دھار بارش |
| over the moon | بے حد خوش |
| kept his word | وعدہ پورا کیا |
| a piece of cake | بہت آسان کام |
| at the last minute | عین وقت پر |
| to make up one's mind | فیصلہ کرنا |

## Six-step process

Read all → mark difficulties → translate sentence by sentence → no gaps (paraphrase unknown words) →
read the Urdu alone → check names, numbers, punctuation.

Ordinals agree in gender (*دوسری بار*، *پہلا دن*); days take *کو* (*پیر کو*); clock times take *بجے*
(*پانچ بجے*). Names of people and places are written in Urdu script, never translated.

## Literal versus natural

| English | Literal (avoid) | Natural |
|---|---|---|
| I am feeling cold. | میں ٹھنڈا محسوس کر رہا ہوں۔ | مجھے سردی لگ رہی ہے۔ |
| She is ten years old. | وہ دس سال پرانی ہے۔ | وہ دس سال کی ہے۔ |
| I have a headache. | میرے پاس سر درد ہے۔ | میرے سر میں درد ہے۔ |
| He made a mistake. | اس نے غلطی بنائی۔ | اس سے غلطی ہوئی۔ / اس نے غلطی کی۔ |
| Take care. | دیکھ بھال لو۔ | اپنا خیال رکھنا۔ |

Notice how often natural Urdu uses *مجھے ... ہے / لگ رہا ہے* where English uses "I have" or
"I feel".

## Structures to restructure

- **there is / are** → place + thing + *ہے / ہیں*: *میز پر دو کتابیں ہیں۔*
- **passive** → keep only if natural (*منسوخ کر دیا گیا*); otherwise make active.
- **long English sentences** → split into two Urdu sentences if the verb would come too late to be
  clear.

## Key terms

| Urdu | English |
|---|---|
| ترجمہ | translation |
| لفظی ترجمہ | literal, word-for-word translation |
| بامحاورہ ترجمہ | idiomatic translation |
| مفہوم | meaning, sense |
| محاورہ | idiom |
| ترتیبِ الفاظ | word order |

## Exam traps

- English word order kept.
- *The* translated as *وہ*.
- Gender chosen wrongly and carried through.
- *نے* missing with transitive past verbs.
- "for two years" translated as *دو سال کے لیے* when it means *دو سال سے*.
- Literal idioms; English words left in.
- Clauses omitted; ideas added that are not in the English.
- *آپ* and *تم* mixed.

## Self-test

1. How many marks is Exercise 4 worth, and about how long is the passage?
2. In which language is the question, and in which the answer?
3. What is the basic Urdu word order?
4. Translate: *I have been living here for five years.*
5. Is "the" translated?
6. Translate: *The new building is very tall.*
7. Which form of "you" for a letter to a teacher?
8. Translate the idiom: *It was a piece of cake.*
9. What should you do with an unknown English word?
10. Translate: *She drank the tea.*
11. What is a *لفظی ترجمہ*, and why is it risky?
12. Which objective covers carrying over the tone of the original?

**Answers**

1. 15 marks; about 150 words.
2. Question in English, answer in Urdu.
3. Subject–object–verb.
4. *میں پانچ سال سے یہاں رہ رہا ہوں۔*
5. No.
6. *نئی عمارت بہت اونچی ہے۔*
7. *آپ*.
8. *یہ بہت آسان کام تھا۔*
9. Express the idea with a simpler word or phrase; never leave a gap.
10. *اس نے چائے پی۔*
11. A word-for-word translation; it often produces unnatural Urdu and wrong idiom.
12. W5.
