## Section A

**1.** Clean, dry air contains about 78% nitrogen. State the approximate percentage of oxygen and name the **two** types of gas that make up the remainder. **[2]**

**2.** Which pollutant is produced by the decomposition of vegetation?

A: carbon monoxide

B: methane

C: sulfur dioxide

D: nitrogen monoxide

**[1]**

**3.** Name **two** pollutants that cause acid rain. **[2]**

**4.** State the adverse effect of carbon monoxide. **[1]**

**5.** Write the word equation for photosynthesis. **[1]**

**6.** State **two** strategies, other than planting trees, that reduce climate change. **[2]**

## Section B

**7.** Methane, CH₄, is burned in a gas boiler.

**(a)** Write a balanced equation for the complete combustion of methane. **[1]**

**(b)** When the air supply is restricted, carbon monoxide is formed instead. Balance the equation: ......CH₄ + ......O₂ → ......CO + ......H₂O **[1]**

**(c)** Name **one** other pollutant formed by incomplete combustion and state its adverse effect. **[2]**

**8.** Explain how oxides of nitrogen are formed in a car engine. **[2]**

**9.** A catalytic converter removes carbon monoxide and nitrogen monoxide from exhaust gases.

**(a)** Write the balanced equation for this reaction. **[2]**

**(b)** State which reactant is oxidised and which is reduced. **[2]**

**(c)** A student says catalytic converters help to reduce global warming. Explain why the student is wrong. **[1]**

**10.** Describe how increased levels of carbon dioxide in the atmosphere lead to global warming. **[3]**

**11.** A power station burns coal that contains sulfur compounds.

**(a)** Write an equation for the formation of sulfur dioxide from sulfur. **[1]**

**(b)** The waste gases are treated with calcium oxide. Explain why calcium oxide removes sulfur dioxide. **[2]**

**(c)** Suggest **one** other way the power station could reduce its sulfur dioxide emissions. **[1]**

**12.** State and explain how each strategy reduces climate change.

**(a)** reducing livestock farming **[2]**

**(b)** increasing the use of solar power **[2]**

**13.** A sample of 250 cm³ of clean, dry air is passed repeatedly over hot copper until there is no further change in volume.

**(a)** Calculate the final volume of gas. **[2]**

**(b)** Name the main gas left. **[1]**

**14.** Photosynthesis removes carbon dioxide from the atmosphere.

**(a)** Write the balanced symbol equation for photosynthesis. **[2]**

**(b)** State the **two** conditions needed for photosynthesis. **[2]**

## Answers

**1.** 21% [1]; noble gases and carbon dioxide [1]. **[2]**

**2.** B [1]. **[1]**

**3.** Sulfur dioxide [1]; oxides of nitrogen (nitrogen monoxide / nitrogen dioxide) [1]. **[2]**

**4.** It is a toxic gas [1]. **[1]**

**5.** carbon dioxide + water → glucose + oxygen [1]. **[1]**

**6.** Any two [1] [1]: reducing livestock farming; decreasing the use of fossil fuels; increasing the use of hydrogen; increasing the use of renewable energy such as wind or solar. **[2]**

**7. (a)** CH₄ + 2O₂ → CO₂ + 2H₂O [1]

**(b)** 2CH₄ + 3O₂ → 2CO + 4H₂O [1]

**(c)** Particulates (carbon / soot) [1]; increased risk of respiratory problems and cancer [1].

**[4]**

**8.** Nitrogen and oxygen from the air react together [1] because of the high temperature inside the engine [1]. **[2]**

*Examiner insight:* "nitrogen in the petrol burns" scores zero; the nitrogen comes from the air.

**9. (a)** 2CO + 2NO → 2CO₂ + N₂: correct formulae [1]; balanced [1]

**(b)** Carbon monoxide is oxidised [1]; nitrogen monoxide is reduced [1].

**(c)** The converter produces carbon dioxide, a greenhouse gas, so it does not reduce global warming [1].

**[5]**

**10.** Carbon dioxide absorbs thermal energy emitted by the Earth's surface [1]; it emits thermal energy in all directions, including back towards the Earth [1]; so less thermal energy is lost to space and the Earth's average temperature rises [1]. **[3]**

*Examiner insight:* "carbon dioxide traps the Sun's heat" is too vague for credit. Use the verbs the syllabus uses: the gas absorbs thermal energy, emits it, and so reduces the thermal energy lost to space.

**11. (a)** S + O₂ → SO₂ [1]

**(b)** Calcium oxide is a basic oxide and sulfur dioxide is an acidic oxide [1]; they react together (neutralisation), so the sulfur dioxide is removed from the waste gases [1].

**(c)** Use low-sulfur coal / low-sulfur fuel [1].

**[4]**

*Examiner insight:* in (b), "calcium oxide absorbs the gas" is not enough. The mark needs the reason the two substances react: a basic oxide with an acidic oxide.

**12. (a)** Livestock produce methane as a waste gas from digestion [1]; fewer animals means less methane, a greenhouse gas, released [1].

**(b)** Solar power generates electricity without burning fossil fuels [1]; so less carbon dioxide is released [1].

**[4]**

**13. (a)** Oxygen removed = 21% of 250 = 52.5 cm³ [1]; final volume = 250 − 52.5 = 197.5 cm³ (accept 198 cm³) [1]

**(b)** Nitrogen [1]

**[3]**

*Examiner insight:* the hot copper reacts with oxygen to form copper(II) oxide, so only the oxygen is removed. Do not subtract 78%: that is the nitrogen, which stays behind.

**14. (a)** 6CO₂ + 6H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂: correct formulae [1]; balanced [1]

**(b)** Light (energy) [1]; chlorophyll [1]

**[4]**

## Where marks are usually lost

- Linking carbon dioxide to acid rain.
- Saying oxides of nitrogen come from nitrogen in the fuel.
- Describing global warming as "the Sun's rays being trapped" rather than absorption and emission of thermal energy.
- Naming a strategy without saying which pollutant it reduces.
- Writing light or chlorophyll as reactants.
