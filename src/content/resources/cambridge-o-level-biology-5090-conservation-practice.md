## Section A

**1.** What is a sustainable resource?

A: one that is never used

B: one that is produced as rapidly as it is removed

C: one that takes millions of years to form

D: one that can only be used once

**[1]**

**2.** Which method of conserving fish stocks lets young fish escape from fishing nets?

A: closed seasons

B: larger mesh size

C: monitoring

D: quotas

**[1]**

**3.** State what is meant by extinction. **[1]**

**4.** State **two** methods of conserving forests. **[2]**

**5.** State **one** reason why a closed season helps to conserve fish. **[1]**

## Section B

**6.** Discuss **three** reasons for conserving a coral reef. **[3]**

**7.** A forest contains 120 000 mature trees. Every year 4 000 new trees reach maturity. A timber company cuts 6 500 mature trees a year.

**(a)** Calculate the net loss of mature trees each year. **[1]**

**(b)** Calculate the number of years before the forest is used up, to the nearest year, if the rates stay the same. **[2]**

**(c)** State the largest number of trees that could be cut each year while keeping the number of trees constant. **[1]**

**(d)** Explain why replanting is used to conserve forests. **[2]**

**8.** Explain how each of the following helps to conserve fish stocks.

**(a)** a quota **[2]**

**(b)** a protected area where fishing is banned **[2]**

**(c)** monitoring **[2]**

**(d)** education **[1]**

**9.** The table shows the mass of cod caught in one fishing ground, and the estimated number of adult cod, over several years.

| Year | Mass of cod caught (tonnes) | Estimated adult cod (thousands) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 400 | 900 |
| 3 | 520 | 720 |
| 5 | 480 | 450 |
| 7 | 300 | 270 |

**(a)** Calculate the percentage fall in the estimated number of adult cod between year 1 and year 7. **[2]**

**(b)** Suggest why the mass of cod caught fell between years 5 and 7 even though the fishing boats were still working. **[2]**

**(c)** Suggest **two** measures that would help the cod population to recover. **[2]**

**10.** Explain why a forest can be a sustainable resource but coal cannot. **[3]**

**11.** Suggest why local people living next to a protected area may not support a ban on cutting trees, and suggest how this problem could be reduced. **[3]**

## Answers

**1.** B [1]. **[1]**

**2.** B [1]. **[1]**

**3.** The permanent loss of a species (when the last individual dies) [1]. **[1]**

**4.** Any two of: education; protected areas; quotas; replanting [1 each]. **[2]**

**5.** Fish can breed in the closed season without being caught, so the population can recover [1]. **[1]**

**6.** Any three of: it has high biodiversity, which would be lost [1]; some species could become extinct [1]; reefs protect coasts from waves [1]; they support fish for food [1]; they are vulnerable environments that recover slowly [1]. **[3]**

**7.**

**(a)** 6 500 - 4 000 = 2 500 trees [1].

**(b)** 120 000 divided by 2 500 [1] = 48 years [1].

**(c)** 4 000 trees [1].

**(d)** Replanting replaces the trees that were cut [1], so the forest continues to supply timber and keeps its habitat, soil protection and carbon dioxide uptake [1]. **[6]**

**8.**

**(a)** A quota limits the amount of fish that can be caught [1], so that fish are not removed faster than they can reproduce [1].

**(b)** Fish in the area cannot be caught, so they can breed and grow [1], and the numbers can build up and fish may move out to other areas [1].

**(c)** Monitoring measures the size of the stocks and the catches [1], so the rules can be changed and illegal fishing can be detected [1].

**(d)** People understand why conservation is needed, so they follow the rules or buy sustainable fish [1]. **[7]**

**9.**

**(a)** (900 - 270) divided by 900 x 100 [1] = 70 per cent [1].

**(b)** There were far fewer cod left to catch [1], because the stock had been over-harvested [1].

**(c)** Any two of: a quota; a closed season; a larger mesh size; a protected area; monitoring [1 each]. **[6]**

**10.** A forest can be replaced by regrowth and replanting [1], so if trees are harvested no faster than they grow, the resource does not run out [1]. Coal takes millions of years to form, so it is used much faster than it is made and it will run out [1]. **[3]**

**11.** They may depend on the forest for firewood, farmland or income [1]. Education about the benefits [1], and allowing a sustainable quota or providing other work such as tourism, can reduce the problem [1]. **[3]**

## Where marks are usually lost

- Naming a method without explaining how it helps.
- Defining a sustainable resource as one that is not used.
- Treating a quota as a complete ban.
- Forgetting that mesh size lets young fish escape.
- Writing one-sided discussions with no balance.
