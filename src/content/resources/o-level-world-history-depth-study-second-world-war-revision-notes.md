**These notes condense Depth Study E of Cambridge O Level History 2147, The Second World War in Europe and the Asia-Pacific, 1939-c.1945.** They are arranged by the four key questions -- the war in Europe, the war in the Asia-Pacific, civilians, and Allied victory -- for quick recall before Paper 1 Section B (4 + 6 + 10 marks). Full explanations: [Depth Study E study guide](/resources/o-level-world-history-depth-study-second-world-war/). Exam practice: [Depth Study E practice questions](/resources/o-level-world-history-depth-study-second-world-war-practice/).

## Syllabus coverage

**CAMBRIDGE O LEVEL HISTORY (2147), 2024-2026 -- DEPTH STUDY E**

1. How did the Second World War in Europe develop?
2. How did the Second World War in the Asia-Pacific develop?
3. What was the impact of war on civilian populations in Europe and the Asia-Pacific?
4. How did the Allies achieve victory over the Axis powers?

## Twin timeline

| Year | Europe | Asia-Pacific |
|---|---|---|
| 1939 | Poland invaded (Sept); divided with USSR | War in China continues |
| 1940 | Denmark, Norway (Apr); France falls; Dunkirk; Battle of Britain; Blitz begins | Japan joins Tripartite Pact; enters northern Indochina |
| 1941 | Barbarossa (22 June); halted before Moscow (Dec) | US oil embargo; Pearl Harbor (7 Dec); Malaya invaded (8 Dec) |
| 1942 | Wannsee (Jan); Stalingrad battle begins | Singapore falls (15 Feb); Midway (June); Guadalcanal begins (Aug) |
| 1943 | Stalingrad surrender (Feb); Sicily (July); Italy surrenders (Sept); Hamburg firestorm | Guadalcanal won (Feb); island-hopping |
| 1944 | Rome taken; D-Day (6 June); Paris liberated (Aug) | US advances across the central Pacific |
| 1945 | Dresden (Feb); Hitler dies (30 Apr); surrender (May) | Okinawa (Apr-June); Hiroshima (6 Aug), Nagasaki (9 Aug); surrender (Aug-Sept) |

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Blitzkrieg | "Lightning war" -- tanks, aircraft and infantry in fast combined attacks |
| Vichy France | Collaborating French government under Pétain, 1940-44 |
| Operation Sea Lion | Planned German invasion of Britain, 1940 |
| Operation Barbarossa | German invasion of the USSR, 1941 |
| Lebensraum | "Living space" in the east for Germans |
| Island-hopping | US strategy of capturing selected Pacific islands and bypassing others |
| Area bombing | Bombing whole urban areas rather than specific targets |
| Final Solution | Nazi plan to murder all European Jews |
| Kempeitai | Japanese military police |
| MPAJA | Malayan People's Anti-Japanese Army |

## Key question 1: four things to explain

1. **German control by 1940:** Blitzkrieg; fall of France; Vichy -- but Britain undefeated after Dunkirk.
2. **Britain survives:** radar, fighter command, Spitfires and Hurricanes; Luftwaffe switch to cities; Royal Navy; Channel.
3. **Barbarossa motives:** Lebensraum, anti-communism, resources, belief the Red Army was weak.
4. **Stalingrad turning point:** Sixth Army destroyed; initiative passes to USSR; German morale hit.

## Key question 2: four things to explain

1. **US-Japan breakdown:** expansion in China; Tripartite Pact; Indochina; oil embargo.
2. **Pearl Harbor:** short-term success; carriers and fuel missed; USA enters war.
3. **Malaya and Singapore:** speed, jungle tactics, air superiority, sinking of two battleships, British complacency -- Singapore surrenders.
4. **Midway turning point:** codebreaking; four carriers sunk; Japan on the defensive.

## Key question 3: case-study grid

| Case | Impact on civilians |
|---|---|
| Blitz | Deaths, homelessness, evacuation, shelters; morale held |
| Bombing of Germany | Firestorms (Hamburg, Dresden); heavy casualties; disputed effect |
| Malaya and Singapore under Japan | Sook Ching massacres; Kempeitai terror; food shortages; forced labour |
| Poland under Nazi rule | Annexation and General Government; elites killed; ghettos; Holocaust in death camps |
| Resistance in France | Sabotage, intelligence, Maquis; SOE help; reprisals |
| Resistance in Malaya | MPAJA guerrillas, mainly Chinese, in the jungle |

## Key question 4: how victory was won

- **Italy:** Sicily (1943), Mussolini falls, slow advance -- ties down German troops.
- **Germany:** D-Day and the western advance; the much larger Soviet advance from the east; bombing of oil and transport.
- **Japan:** Guadalcanal; island-hopping; Okinawa; fire-raids; atomic bombs; Soviet entry.
- **Consolidation:** four-zone occupation of Germany; US occupation of Japan; Nuremberg and Tokyo trials.

## The atomic bomb debate -- two columns

| For using it | Against |
|---|---|
| Avoided a costly invasion (Okinawa's losses) | Huge civilian deaths and radiation sickness |
| Ended the war quickly | Japan may have been close to surrender |
| Japan had refused to surrender | Partly a warning to the USSR |

## Worked example: (c) structure on Stalingrad

*"Stalingrad was the most important turning point of the war in Europe." How far do you agree?* **[10]**

Paragraph 1: Stalingrad's effects. Paragraph 2: alternatives -- the Battle of Britain (Germany could not defeat Britain), the failure before Moscow in 1941, D-Day (second front). Paragraph 3: judgement -- for example, that Stalingrad was decisive because the Eastern Front consumed most of Germany's army.

## Exam traps

- Calling Dunkirk a victory without explaining what was lost.
- Writing about the causes of the war (Core Content key question 2.3).
- Forgetting the named case studies.
- Giving the Asia-Pacific war only a sentence.
- Saying the war ended in Europe and Asia at the same time.

## Frequently asked questions

**When did the war end?**
In Europe, with Germany's surrender in May 1945; in the Asia-Pacific, with Japan's surrender announced on 15 August and signed on 2 September 1945.

**What was the Sook Ching?**
The Japanese "purge" of Chinese men in Singapore and Malaya in 1942, in which many thousands were killed.

**What were the war crimes trials?**
International trials of German leaders at Nuremberg (1945-46) and Japanese leaders in Tokyo (1946-48).

## Self-test

1. What was Operation Sea Lion? *The planned German invasion of Britain.*
2. When did Singapore surrender? *15 February 1942.*
3. How many Japanese carriers were sunk at Midway? *Four.*
4. Which island battle in 1945 showed the cost of invading Japan? *Okinawa.*
5. Where were Japan's leaders tried? *Tokyo.*
