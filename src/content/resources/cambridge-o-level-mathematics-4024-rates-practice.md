These questions cover subtopic 1.12 Rates of Cambridge O Level Mathematics (Syllabus D) 4024, 2025 to 2027. Question 9 is for Paper 2 and needs a calculator. The others are non-calculator, as on Paper 1. Where a formula is needed for density, pressure or population density it is stated in the question. Show your working and include units.

## Section A

**1.** Maya is paid $8.40 per hour.

**(a)** Work out her pay for 37.5 hours. **[2]**

**(b)** She then works 6 more hours at 1.5 times her normal rate. Work out her total pay for all the hours. **[2]**

**2.** The exchange rate is $1 = 4.5 francs.

**(a)** Change $80 into francs. **[1]**

**(b)** Change 1 125 francs into dollars. **[2]**

**3.** A tap delivers water at 12 litres per minute. How long does it take to fill a tank of 540 litres? **[2]**

**4.** A car uses 6.4 litres of fuel for each 100 km.

**(a)** How much fuel does it use on a journey of 350 km? **[2]**

**(b)** How far can it travel on 56 litres? **[2]**

**5.** Density = mass ÷ volume. A metal block has mass 540 g and volume 200 cm³. Work out its density, in g/cm³. **[2]**

**6.** A material has density 8.0 g/cm³. Work out the mass of 125 cm³ of the material. **[2]**

**7.** Pressure = force ÷ area. A force of 600 N acts on an area of 0.25 m². Work out the pressure, in N/m². **[2]**

## Section B

**8.** Population density = population ÷ area. A region has a population of 1 200 000 and an area of 480 km². Work out the population density, in people per km². **[2]**

**9.** *Calculator.* A car travels 78 km in 1 hour 45 minutes. Calculate its average speed in km/h, correct to 3 significant figures. **[3]**

**10.** Change 72 km/h into metres per second. **[2]**

**11.** A train travels 120 km at 60 km/h and then 90 km at 45 km/h. Work out the average speed for the whole journey. **[4]**

**12.** A journey of 150 km is made at an average speed of 80 km/h. Write down the time taken in hours and minutes. **[3]**

**13.** A runner covers 400 m in 50 seconds. Work out her speed in km/h. **[3]**

**14.** A pool is filled by a pipe at 15 litres per minute. A leak loses water at 6 litres per minute. How long does it take for the volume in the pool to increase by 450 litres? **[3]**

## Answers

**1. (a)** 8.40 × 37.5 **[1]** = **$315** **[1]**

**(b)** overtime rate 8.40 × 1.5 = $12.60 **[1]**; 12.60 × 6 = 75.60, total **$390.60** **[1]** **[4]**

**2. (a)** 80 × 4.5 = **360 francs** **[1]**

**(b)** 1 125 ÷ 4.5 **[1]** = **$250** **[1]** **[3]**

*Examiner insight:* A franc is worth less than a dollar, so the number of francs should be larger. In (b) the answer in dollars must be smaller than 1 125.

**3.** 540 ÷ 12 **[1]** = **45 minutes** **[1]** **[2]**

**4. (a)** 350 km = 3.5 lots of 100 km **[1]**; 6.4 × 3.5 = **22.4 litres** **[1]**

**(b)** 56 ÷ 6.4 = 8.75 **[1]**; 8.75 × 100 = **875 km** **[1]** **[4]**

**5.** 540 ÷ 200 **[1]** = **2.7 g/cm³** **[1]** **[2]**

**6.** mass = density × volume = 8.0 × 125 **[1]** = **1000 g** **[1]** **[2]**

*Examiner insight:* Rearrange the formula first. A common mistake is to divide 125 by 8.0.

**7.** 600 ÷ 0.25 **[1]** = **2400 N/m²** **[1]** **[2]**

**8.** 1 200 000 ÷ 480 **[1]** = **2500 people per km²** **[1]** **[2]**

**9.** 1 h 45 min = 1.75 h **[1]**; 78 ÷ 1.75 = 44.571... **[1]**; **44.6 km/h** **[1]** **[3]**

*Examiner insight:* Convert to hours with a decimal. Writing 1.45 for 1 hour 45 minutes gives the wrong answer.

**10.** 72 ÷ 3.6 **[1]** = **20 m/s** **[1]** **[2]**

**11.** First part 120 ÷ 60 = 2 hours **[1]**; second part 90 ÷ 45 = 2 hours **[1]**; total distance 210 km and total time 4 hours **[1]**; 210 ÷ 4 = **52.5 km/h** **[1]** **[4]**

**12.** time = 150 ÷ 80 = 1.875 hours **[1]**; 0.875 × 60 = 52.5 minutes **[1]**; **1 hour 52.5 minutes** (1 hour 52 minutes 30 seconds) **[1]** **[3]**

**13.** speed = 400 ÷ 50 = 8 m/s **[1]**; 8 × 3.6 **[1]** = **28.8 km/h** **[1]** **[3]**

**14.** net rate 15 - 6 = 9 litres per minute **[1]**; 450 ÷ 9 **[1]** = **50 minutes** **[1]** **[3]**

*Examiner insight:* Use the net rate. Using 15 litres per minute alone ignores the leak.

## Where marks are usually lost

- Multiplying by 1.5 without including the 37.5 normal hours in total pay in Question 1(b).
- Converting in the wrong direction in Question 2.
- Dividing the wrong way round in Question 6.
- Using 1.45 hours for 1 hour 45 minutes in Question 9.
- Adding speeds or averaging them in Question 11.
- Leaving the answer as 1.875 hours in Question 12.
- Forgetting the leak in Question 14.

## Frequently asked questions

**Do I need to remember the density and pressure formulas?**
The syllabus says the required formulas will be given in the question. You do need to remember speed, distance and time.

**How do I average two speeds?**
Divide the total distance by the total time. Do not average the speeds unless the times are equal.
