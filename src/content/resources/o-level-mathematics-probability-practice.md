## Questions

**1.** *(no calculator)* A bag contains 12 balls: 5 red, 4 blue and the rest green. One ball is taken at random. Find the probability that it is **(a)** blue **(b)** not red. **[2]**

**2.** In a survey, the relative frequencies of students choosing football, tennis and other sports are 0.35, 0.25 and 0.1. Every other student chose cricket. **(a)** Find the relative frequency of cricket. **(b)** 400 students took part. How many chose tennis? **[2]**

**3.** *(no calculator)* A bag contains 7 red counters and 3 green counters. Two counters are taken at random without replacement. Find the probability that **(a)** both are red **(b)** one of each colour is taken. **[4]**

**4.** In a class of 30 students, 17 play football, 12 play basketball and 7 play neither. **(a)** How many play both? **(b)** A student is chosen at random. Find the probability that they play exactly one of the two sports. **[4]**

**5.** A fair six-sided dice is rolled 240 times. How many sixes would you expect? **[1]**

---

## Answers

**1.** **(a)** 4/12 = **1/3** [1]. **(b)** 7/12 [1].

*Tip:* "not red" is 1 − P(red), or count the balls that are not red.

*Try the real question next:* Cambridge O Level Mathematics 4024, June 2025, Paper 12, Question 2.

**2.** **(a)** 1 − (0.35 + 0.25 + 0.1) = **0.3** [1]. **(b)** 0.25 × 400 = **100** [1].

*Tip:* relative frequencies of all outcomes add up to 1.

*Try the real question next:* Cambridge O Level Mathematics 4024, June 2025, Paper 22, Question 6.

**3.** **(a)** 7/10 × 6/9 [1] = **7/15** [1]. **(b)** 7/10 × 3/9 + 3/10 × 7/9 [1] = 42/90 = **7/15** [1].

*Tip:* without replacement, both the number of that colour and the total go down by one for the second counter.

*Try the real question next:* Cambridge O Level Mathematics 4024, June 2025, Paper 22, Question 13.

**4.** **(a)** 17 + 12 + 7 − 30 [1] = **6** [1]. **(b)** Football only = 11, basketball only = 6 [1], so P = **17/30** [1].

*Tip:* a Venn diagram with four regions helps; fill in the overlap first.

*Try the real question next:* Cambridge O Level Mathematics 4024, June 2025, Paper 22, Question 9.

**5.** 240 × 1/6 = **40** [1].

*Tip:* expected frequency = probability × number of trials.

*Try the real question next:* Cambridge O Level Mathematics 4024, June 2025, Paper 22, Question 6.

## Where marks are usually lost

- Not reducing the total for the second pick without replacement.
- Counting the overlap twice in a Venn diagram.
- Relative frequencies that do not add up to 1.
