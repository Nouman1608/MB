## Questions

### Section A: short questions

**1.** Which pair of gases both contribute to acid rain?

A: carbon dioxide and methane

B: carbon monoxide and nitrogen

C: sulfur dioxide and oxides of nitrogen

D: oxygen and sulfur dioxide **[1]**

**2.** Which gas makes up about 21 % of clean, dry air?

A: argon

B: carbon dioxide

C: nitrogen

D: oxygen **[1]**

**3.** Name the pollutant described in each case.

**(a)** A toxic gas formed when a gas heater burns fuel in a room with poor ventilation. **[1]**

**(b)** A gas released by cattle as a waste product of digestion. **[1]**

**(c)** Tiny solid particles in diesel exhaust that increase the risk of cancer. **[1]**

**4.** State **two** conditions needed for photosynthesis, other than the reactants. **[2]**

**5.** Explain why reducing livestock farming helps to reduce climate change. **[2]**

**6.** Hydrogen can be used as a fuel instead of petrol. Write a balanced equation for the combustion of hydrogen, and explain why it reduces climate change. **[3]**

### Section B: structured questions

**7.** A student passes 80.0 cm³ of clean, dry air backwards and forwards over heated copper until the volume stops changing. After cooling, the volume of gas is 63.2 cm³.

**(a)** Calculate the percentage of oxygen in the sample. **[2]**

**(b)** State the colour change of the copper and name the product. **[2]**

**(c)** Explain why the gas must be cooled before the final volume is read. **[1]**

**(d)** Name the main gas left at the end. **[1]**

**8.** A coal-fired power station burns coal that contains sulfur compounds.

**(a)** Name the gas produced from the sulfur compounds and state its effect on the environment. **[2]**

**(b)** The waste gases are passed through calcium oxide. Write an equation for the reaction and explain why it works. **[3]**

**(c)** Suggest **one** other way of reducing the emission of this gas. **[1]**

**9.** Methane, CH₄, burns in a gas cooker.

**(a)** Write a balanced equation for the complete combustion of methane. **[2]**

**(b)** Write a balanced equation for the incomplete combustion of methane to form carbon monoxide and water. **[2]**

**(c)** Explain why a faulty gas cooker that burns with a yellow, smoky flame is dangerous. **[2]**

**10.** *(Extended)* Car engines release oxides of nitrogen.

**(a)** Explain how nitrogen monoxide forms in a car engine, including an equation. **[3]**

**(b)** In a catalytic converter: 2CO + 2NO → 2CO₂ + N₂. Identify the substance that is oxidised and the substance that is reduced, giving reasons. **[4]**

**11.** *(Extended)* Describe how carbon dioxide in the atmosphere causes global warming. **[4]**

**12.** *(Extended)* Write the balanced symbol equation for photosynthesis, and calculate how many molecules of carbon dioxide are needed to make three molecules of glucose. **[3]**

**13.** State and explain **three** strategies for reducing climate change. **[6]**

## Answers

**1.** **C** [1].

**2.** **D** [1].

**3. (a)** Carbon monoxide [1].

**(b)** Methane [1].

**(c)** Particulates [1].

**4.** Chlorophyll [1]; (energy from) light [1].

**5.** Livestock produce methane as a waste gas from digestion [1]; methane is a greenhouse gas, so less methane means less global warming / climate change [1].

**6.** 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O: correct formulae [1]; balanced [1]. The only product is water, so no carbon dioxide (a greenhouse gas) is released [1].

**7. (a)** Oxygen removed = 80.0 − 63.2 = 16.8 cm³ [1]; percentage = 16.8 ÷ 80.0 × 100 = **21.0 %** [1].

**(b)** Pink-brown (orange) to black [1]; copper(II) oxide [1].

**(c)** Gases expand when hot, so a hot reading would be too large / the volume must be measured at the same temperature as at the start [1].

**(d)** Nitrogen [1].

**8. (a)** Sulfur dioxide [1]; acid rain [1].

**(b)** CaO + SO₂ → CaSO₃ [1]. Calcium oxide is a basic oxide [1] and sulfur dioxide is an acidic oxide, so they react / neutralise, removing the sulfur dioxide from the waste gases [1].

**(c)** Use low-sulfur fuel (or burn less coal / use renewable energy) [1].

**9. (a)** CH₄ + 2O₂ → CO₂ + 2H₂O: correct formulae [1]; balanced [1].

**(b)** 2CH₄ + 3O₂ → 2CO + 4H₂O: correct formulae [1]; balanced [1].

**(c)** The fuel is burning incompletely because there is not enough oxygen [1], so carbon monoxide, a toxic gas, is produced [1].

**10. (a)** The temperature in the engine is very high [1]; nitrogen and oxygen from the air react [1]: N₂ + O₂ → 2NO [1].

**(b)** Carbon monoxide is oxidised [1] because it gains oxygen to form carbon dioxide [1]. Nitrogen monoxide is reduced [1] because it loses oxygen to form nitrogen [1].

**11.** Any four [4]:

- energy from the Sun is absorbed by the Earth's surface (some is reflected) [1];
- the surface emits thermal energy [1];
- carbon dioxide absorbs some of this thermal energy [1];
- it re-emits the thermal energy in all directions, including back towards the Earth [1];
- so thermal energy loss to space is reduced and the Earth warms [1].

**12.** 6CO₂ + 6H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂: correct formulae [1]; balanced [1]. Three glucose molecules need 3 × 6 = **18** molecules of carbon dioxide [1].

**13.** One mark for each strategy and one for its explanation, any three pairs [6]:

- planting trees [1]: they absorb carbon dioxide for photosynthesis [1];
- reducing livestock farming [1]: less methane from digestion [1];
- decreasing use of fossil fuels [1]: less carbon dioxide from combustion [1];
- increasing use of hydrogen [1]: it burns to form only water [1];
- increasing use of renewable energy such as wind or solar [1]: electricity without burning fuel, so no carbon dioxide [1].

## Where marks are usually lost

- Saying carbon dioxide causes acid rain.
- Giving methane's source as cars or factories.
- Stating a strategy without explaining how it works.
- Forgetting to cool the gas before reading the final volume.
- Writing that catalytic converters remove carbon dioxide.
- Describing the greenhouse effect as "trapping heat" without absorption and re-emission.
- Writing chlorophyll or light as reactants in photosynthesis.
