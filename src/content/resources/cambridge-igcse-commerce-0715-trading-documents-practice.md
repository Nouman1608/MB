## Section A - short questions

**1.** Which document does a seller send in reply to an enquiry? **[1]**
A order   B quotation   C invoice   D receipt

**2.** A buyer returns three faulty printers. Which document will the seller send to reduce the amount owed? **[1]**
A delivery note   B statement of account   C credit note   D bill of lading

**3.** Which document is a document of title for goods sent by sea? **[1]**
A air waybill   B certificate of origin   C bill of lading   D eticket

**4.** State the purpose of a statement of account. **[2]**

**5.** Distinguish between an invoice and a receipt. **[2]**

**6.** Identify **two** examples of digital technology used in trading documents. **[2]**

## Section B - structured questions

**7.** *Kofi owns a stationery shop. He wants to buy 100 boxes of printer paper and contacts three wholesalers.*
Describe the purpose of the **first three** documents Kofi and the chosen wholesaler would use, stating who sends each one. **[6]**

**8.** *A wholesaler supplies 60 school bags at a list price of $20 each. It gives a 25% trade discount and a 4% cash discount for payment within 14 days.*
(a) Calculate the invoice total. (2 marks)
(b) Calculate the amount the buyer pays if it pays within 14 days. (2 marks)
(c) Five bags are returned damaged before payment. Calculate the value of the credit note. (2 marks) **[6]**

**9.** Explain why a buyer should check and sign the delivery note when goods arrive. **[3]**

**10.** *SunOil exports olive oil by sea to a new customer in another country.*
(a) Explain the purpose of a certificate of origin. (2 marks)
(b) Explain **two** functions of the bill of lading. (4 marks)
(c) Explain how documentary credit would reduce SunOil's risk. (3 marks) **[9]**

**11.** Explain **one** difference between a bill of lading and an air waybill. **[2]**

**12.** *GadgetHub sells headphones online.*
(a) Outline **four** stages of the online ordering process, from browsing to receiving the order. (4 marks)
(b) Explain **two** benefits to GadgetHub of using electronic receipts and cloud-based storage for its documents. (4 marks) **[8]**

## Answers

**1.** B. The quotation is the seller's offer of prices and terms. **[1]**

**2.** C. A credit note reduces the amount the buyer owes. **[1]**

**3.** C. The holder of the bill of lading can claim the goods. **[1]**

**4.** It summarises the invoices, credit notes and payments for a period, usually a month [1], and shows the balance the buyer still owes [1]. **[2]**

**5.** An invoice is the seller's bill, showing the amount owed for goods supplied [1]; a receipt is proof that payment has been made [1]. **[2]**

**6.** Any two: digital signatures [1]; secure access such as passwords or encryption [1]; cloud-based storage [1]; electronic receipts [1]; etickets [1]. **[max 2]**

**7.** 2 marks each (sender plus purpose):
Enquiry: sent by Kofi to the three wholesalers [1], asking for prices, discounts, delivery times and payment terms for 100 boxes of paper [1].
Quotation: sent by each wholesaler to Kofi [1], giving its price per box, trade discount, delivery date and payment terms so he can compare offers [1].
Order: sent by Kofi to the chosen wholesaler [1], stating exactly what he wants: 100 boxes, the agreed price and delivery address [1]. **[6]**

**8.** (a) List price: 60 x 20 = $1,200 [1]. Less 25% trade discount ($300): invoice total = **$900** [1]. (2 marks)
(b) Cash discount: 4% of 900 = $36 [1]. Amount paid = 900 - 36 = **$864** [1]. (2 marks)
(c) 5 x 20 = $100 [1], less 25% trade discount ($25) = **$75** credit note [1]. (2 marks) **[6]**

**9.** To confirm that the goods received match the order in type and quantity [1]; to record any shortage or damage at once, so the seller can issue a credit note or replacement [1]; the signed copy is proof for the seller that the goods were delivered, settling disputes later [1]. **[3]**

**10.** (a) It states the country where the olive oil was produced [1], so customs in the importing country can decide what duty applies and whether the goods may enter [1]. (2 marks)
(b) Any two, 2 marks each: it is a receipt from the shipping company showing the oil was received on board [2]; it is evidence of the contract of carriage, setting out the terms of shipment [2]; it is a document of title, so whoever holds it can claim the oil at the destination port, allowing it to be released only when payment is arranged [2]. (max 4 marks)
(c) The customer's bank promises to pay SunOil [1] once SunOil presents the agreed shipping documents, such as the bill of lading [1], so SunOil relies on a bank, not on a new and unknown customer abroad, and is protected against non-payment [1]. (3 marks) **[9]**

**11.** A bill of lading is a document of title, so the holder can claim the goods, while an air waybill is not; air cargo is released to the named consignee [2]. Or: a bill of lading is used for goods sent by sea, an air waybill for goods sent by air [2]. **[max 2]**

**12.** (a) Any four, 1 mark each: browse the website and search [1]; add headphones to the basket [1]; log in or create an account [1]; enter delivery details and choose delivery [1]; pay securely online [1]; receive an order confirmation [1]; receive dispatch notice and tracking [1]; receive the order [1]. (max 4 marks)
(b) Any two, 2 marks each: electronic receipts are sent instantly by email, saving printing and postage costs [2]; customers cannot lose an electronic receipt, so returns and warranty claims are easier to check [2]; cloud storage lets staff find any invoice or order from any location quickly [2]; cloud documents are backed up, so they are not lost if a computer fails [2]. (max 4 marks) **[8]**

## Where marks are usually lost

- Saying the buyer sends the quotation or the invoice.
- Taking cash discount from the list price instead of from the invoice total.
- Forgetting to apply the trade discount to the credit note.
- Describing a bill of lading only as "a document for shipping" without its functions.
- Listing online stages out of order.

## Frequently asked questions

**Which comes first, the invoice or the delivery note?** It varies: the delivery note travels with the goods, and the invoice may be sent with them or shortly afterwards. Both come after the order.

**Why is the trade discount taken off a credit note?** Because the buyer was charged the price after trade discount, so the credit must match what was actually charged.
