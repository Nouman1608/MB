## Section A: short answers

**1.** Which of these is the best reason for placing a company logo on the master slide?

A: it makes the file size smaller

B: it appears in the same position on every slide

C: it can be animated more easily

D: it is printed only on handouts **[1]**

**2.** Name **three** objects, other than a logo, that the syllabus says can be inserted and edited consistently on a master slide. **[3]**

**3.** State what is meant by an *automated* slide number. **[1]**

**4.** A logo 800 pixels wide and 200 pixels high is resized to 8 cm wide with the aspect ratio locked. Calculate its new height. **[1]**

**5.** State **two** properties of bullets that can be formatted on a master slide. **[2]**

**6.** State why a task might require the title placeholder on the master to be moved. **[1]**

## Section B: structured questions

**7.** A travel company's presentation has 15 slides. A designer typed the numbers 1 to 15 into a text box on each slide.

**(a)** Explain **two** problems with this method.

**(b)** Describe how the slide numbers should have been added. **[4]**

**8.** A student places the school crest on the main master. It appears on every slide except slide 1, which uses the title slide layout.

**(a)** Suggest **two** reasons why the crest is missing from slide 1.

**(b)** Describe how to check that the crest now appears on every slide. **[3]**

**9.** A task says: "On the master slide, make all slide titles 36 point, bold and centred, and make first-level bullets red diamonds."

**(a)** Describe how the student should apply the title formatting.

**(b)** The student changes the bullet character for every text level. Explain why this does not meet the instruction.

**(c)** After the changes, slide 6 still has a 28 point title in a different font. Explain the cause and how to fix it. **[6]**

**10.** A museum wants its name at the top of every slide and the text *Visitor Guide* with the date at the bottom of every slide.

**(a)** Describe how to create the name at the top of every slide.

**(b)** Describe how to add the footer text and the date so that they appear on every slide. **[4]**

**11.** A designer draws a navy rectangle across the bottom of the master slide for the footer area. Afterwards the footer text and slide number cannot be seen.

**(a)** Explain why.

**(b)** Describe **two** actions that would make the footer readable. **[3]**

**12.** A college asks a student to give every slide a consistent house style. Explain **four** benefits of using the master slide to do this rather than formatting individual slides. **[4]**

## Answers

**1.** B: it appears in the same position on every slide. **[1]**

**2.** Any three, 1 mark each: images (1); text (1); shapes (1); slide headers and footers (1); placeholder position (1); automated slide numbering (1). **[3]**

**3.** A slide number field inserted by the software that shows each slide's correct number and updates automatically when slides are added, moved or deleted (1). **[1]**

**4.** 8 × 200 ÷ 800 = 2 cm (1). **[1]**

**5.** Any two, 1 mark each: bullet character or symbol (1); bullet colour (1); bullet size (1); indent or spacing (1). **[2]**

**6.** Any one: to leave space for a logo or other object (1); to place titles in a specified position on every slide (1). **[1]**

**7. (a)** Any two, 1 mark each: if slides are added, moved or deleted the numbers become wrong and must be retyped (1); typing each number is slow (1); the text boxes may be in slightly different positions or formats on different slides, so the numbering is not consistent (1).

**(b)** Use the slide number field/placeholder on the master slide (1) and switch slide numbers on for all slides in the header and footer settings (1). **[4]**

**8. (a)** Any two, 1 mark each: the title slide layout is set to hide background graphics (1); the crest was placed on a layout master rather than the main master (1); a shape or picture on slide 1 is covering it (1).

**(b)** View every slide, for example in slide sorter view, or check each layout master in master view, to confirm the crest is shown (1). **[3]**

**9. (a)** Open master view, select the main master's title placeholder (1), and set the font size to 36 point, bold and centre alignment (1).

**(b)** The instruction applied only to first-level bullets (1); sub-bullets should have kept their original bullet character (1).

**(c)** Slide 6 has local formatting on its title that overrides the master (1); reset the slide (or remove the local formatting) so that it takes the master's title style (1). **[6]**

**10. (a)** Insert a text box containing the museum name at the top of the main master (1) so it appears in the same place on every slide (1).

**(b)** Type *Visitor Guide* into the footer placeholder on the master and position the date placeholder (1), then switch on the footer and date in the header and footer settings and apply to all slides (1). **[4]**

**11. (a)** The rectangle is in front of the footer and slide number placeholders, so it covers them (1).

**(b)** Any two, 1 mark each: send the rectangle to the back (1); change the footer and slide number text to a colour that contrasts with navy, such as white (1); move the placeholders so they are not behind the shape (1). **[3]**

**12.** Any four, 1 mark each:
- Consistency: every slide has the same fonts, colours, background and logo position, giving a professional house style (1).
- Efficiency: each setting is made once, not on every slide (1).
- Easy to change: one edit to the master updates every slide (1).
- Fewer errors: no slide is missed or formatted slightly differently (1).
- Objects on the master cannot be moved accidentally when editing slides (1).
- New slides automatically take the house style (1). **[4]**

## Frequently asked questions

**Does the examiner check the master view itself?**
The evidence is usually the slides: each named item, such as the logo position or slide numbers, is checked on more than one slide. That is why setting it once on the master, and checking every layout, matters.

**Do hidden slides keep their slide numbers?**
In most packages a hidden slide still counts in the numbering, so the next slide shown keeps its original number.
