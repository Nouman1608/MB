**Syllabus:** Cambridge IGCSE Chemistry 0620, 2026–2028, subtopic 11.2 Naming organic compounds.

## Stems and endings

| Stem | C atoms | | Ending | Series |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| meth- | 1 | | -ane | alkane |
| eth- | 2 | | -ene | alkene |
| prop- | 3 | | -ol | alcohol |
| but- | 4 | | -oic acid | carboxylic acid |

- The stem counts **all** carbons in the chain, including the –COOH carbon in acids.
- Esters: alcohol part **-yl** + acid part **-oate**.

## Core names and formulae

| Name | Molecular formula | Type |
|---|---|---|
| methane | CH₄ | alkane |
| ethane | C₂H₆ | alkane |
| ethene | C₂H₄ | alkene |
| ethanol | C₂H₅OH | alcohol |
| ethanoic acid | CH₃COOH | carboxylic acid |

**Identifying the type:**

- from a name → the ending;
- from a molecular formula → the general formula or the OH / COOH at the end;
- from a displayed formula → C=C (alkene), O–H on a carbon without C=O (alcohol), C=O and O–H on the same carbon (carboxylic acid).

## Reaction products to name (11.4–11.7)

| Reaction | Product |
|---|---|
| methane + chlorine (UV) | chloromethane |
| ethene + bromine | 1,2-dibromoethane |
| ethene + hydrogen | ethane |
| ethene + steam | ethanol |
| glucose fermentation | ethanol |
| ethanol oxidation | ethanoic acid |
| ethanoic acid + sodium hydroxide | sodium ethanoate |
| ethanoic acid + ethanol | ethyl ethanoate |

Each product is examined at the same tier as its reaction.

## Unbranched compounds up to four carbons *(Extended)*

| C | Alkene | Alcohol | Acid |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | – | methanol | methanoic acid |
| 2 | ethene | ethanol | ethanoic acid |
| 3 | propene | propan-1-ol, propan-2-ol | propanoic acid |
| 4 | but-1-ene, but-2-ene | butan-1-ol, butan-2-ol | butanoic acid |

Alkanes: methane, ethane, propane, butane.

**Locant rules:**

- Number from the end that gives the **lowest** number.
- No locant for methanol, ethanol, ethene, propene or any carboxylic acid.
- Never "butan-3-ol" or "but-3-ene".

**Structural formulae to know:**

- propan-2-ol CH₃CH(OH)CH₃
- butan-2-ol CH₃CH(OH)CH₂CH₃
- but-1-ene CH₂=CHCH₂CH₃
- but-2-ene CH₃CH=CHCH₃

## Esters *(Extended)*

**Name = alcohol part (-yl) + acid part (-oate).**

| Alcohol | Acid | Ester | Structural formula |
|---|---|---|---|
| methanol | methanoic acid | methyl methanoate | HCOOCH₃ |
| ethanol | propanoic acid | ethyl propanoate | CH₃CH₂COOCH₂CH₃ |
| butan-1-ol | ethanoic acid | butyl ethanoate | CH₃COOCH₂CH₂CH₂CH₃ |
| propan-1-ol | butanoic acid | propyl butanoate | CH₃CH₂CH₂COOCH₂CH₂CH₃ |

**Splitting a formula:** acid part = chain on the C of –COO– (count that C); alcohol part = chain on the single-bonded O. The formula usually shows the acid part first; the name puts the alcohol part first.

## Naming in four steps

1. **Find the functional group** (C=C, –OH, –COOH or –COO–) to choose the ending.
2. **Count the carbons** in the chain that contains it to choose the stem (include the –COOH carbon).
3. **Add a locant** if the group could be in more than one place, numbering from the end nearest the group.
4. **Check against the formula:** draw the name back out and count atoms; they must match the molecular formula.

## Answer frames

**"State the type of compound" (1 mark):** series name, plus evidence if asked: "alcohol, because the name ends in -ol".

**"Name this compound" (1 mark):** spelling and locant must both be right: "butan-2-ol" is correct; "butanol-2" and "butan-3-ol" are not.

**"Name this ester" (Extended, 2 marks):** one mark for each part, correctly identified and in the right order.

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Stem | the start of a name, giving the number of carbon atoms |
| Ending (suffix) | the end of a name, giving the homologous series |
| Locant | number showing the position of a double bond or –OH group |
| Unbranched | all carbon atoms in one continuous chain |
| Ester | compound made from a carboxylic acid and an alcohol, with the –COO– linkage |
| Ethanoate | salt or ester derived from ethanoic acid |

## Exam traps

- **CH₃COOH is ethanoic acid,** not methanoic acid.
- **Lowest locant wins.**
- **Ethanoic acid contains O–H but is not an alcohol.**
- *(Extended)* **Ester names are reversed** relative to most formulae: alcohol part first.
- *(Extended)* **Spelling:** ethanoate, not "ethanate"; ethyl, not "ethanyl".
- *(Extended)* **But-1-ene and but-2-ene** are different compounds (isomers), not two names for one.

## Self-test

1. State the number of carbon atoms shown by the stem "prop-".
2. Name the compound CH₃CH₃.
3. State the type of compound called butanoic acid.
4. Name the organic product of ethene and steam.
5. Name the salt formed from ethanoic acid and sodium hydroxide.
6. Name the product of methane reacting with chlorine in ultraviolet light (monosubstitution).
7. *(Extended)* Name CH₃CH(OH)CH₃.
8. *(Extended)* Name CH₃CH₂CH=CH₂.
9. *(Extended)* Give the structural formula of butanoic acid.
10. *(Extended)* Name the ester made from methanol and propanoic acid.
11. *(Extended)* Name HCOOCH₂CH₃.
12. *(Extended)* Name the alcohol and acid used to make butyl methanoate.

**Answers:**

1. Three.
2. Ethane.
3. Carboxylic acid.
4. Ethanol.
5. Sodium ethanoate.
6. Chloromethane.
7. Propan-2-ol.
8. But-1-ene.
9. CH₃CH₂CH₂COOH.
10. Methyl propanoate.
11. Ethyl methanoate.
12. Butan-1-ol and methanoic acid.
