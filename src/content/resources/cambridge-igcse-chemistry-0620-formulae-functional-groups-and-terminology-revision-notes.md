## Types of formula

| Type | Shows | Example (ethene) |
|---|---|---|
| molecular | numbers of each atom | C₂H₄ |
| general | the whole series in terms of *n* | CₙH₂ₙ |
| structural *(Extended)* | arrangement of atoms, unambiguously | CH₂=CH₂ |
| displayed | every atom and every bond | H₂C=CH₂ with all four C–H bonds drawn |

**Valency check for displayed formulae:** C = 4 bonds, O = 2, H = 1. A double bond counts as 2.

## General formulae

| Series | General formula | Check with n = 2 |
|---|---|---|
| alkanes | CₙH₂ₙ₊₂ | C₂H₆ ethane |
| alkenes | CₙH₂ₙ | C₂H₄ ethene |
| alcohols | CₙH₂ₙ₊₁OH | C₂H₅OH ethanol |
| carboxylic acids | CₙH₂ₙ₊₁COOH | C₂H₅COOH **propanoic** acid (3 carbons) |

**Carboxylic acid trap:** total carbons = n + 1. Methanoic acid is HCOOH (n = 0).

**Identifying a series from a molecular formula:**

- H = 2C + 2 → alkane (e.g. C₅H₁₂).
- H = 2C → alkene (e.g. C₅H₁₀).
- ends in OH → alcohol; ends in COOH → carboxylic acid.

## Functional groups

| Series | Functional group |
|---|---|
| alkenes | C=C |
| alcohols | –OH |
| carboxylic acids | –COOH |

**Functional group:** an atom or group of atoms that determines the chemical properties of a homologous series.

## Homologous series

**Core definition:** a family of similar compounds with similar chemical properties due to the presence of the same functional group.

*(Extended)* **General characteristics:**

- same functional group;
- same general formula;
- differ by a –CH₂– unit;
- trend in physical properties (e.g. boiling point rises with chain length);
- similar chemical properties.

Alkane boiling points: CH₄ about −162 °C; C₂H₆ about −89 °C; C₃H₈ about −42 °C; C₄H₁₀ about −1 °C.

## Saturated and unsaturated

- **Saturated:** all carbon–carbon bonds are single (alkanes).
- **Unsaturated:** one or more carbon–carbon bonds are not single (alkenes).
- Only C–C bonds count: a C=O bond does not make a compound unsaturated in this definition.

## Structural isomers *(Extended)*

**Definition:** same molecular formula, different structural formulae.

| Molecular formula | Isomers |
|---|---|
| C₄H₁₀ | CH₃CH₂CH₂CH₃ (butane); CH₃CH(CH₃)CH₃ (methylpropane) |
| C₄H₈ | CH₃CH₂CH=CH₂ (but-1-ene); CH₃CH=CHCH₃ (but-2-ene) |

**Not an isomer:** the same chain drawn bent or turned round. Isomers have atoms joined in a different order.

**Structural formulae to recognise:** CH₂=CH₂, CH₃CH₂OH, CH₃COOCH₃.

## Drawing a displayed formula in four steps

1. Write the carbon chain, joined by single bonds (or a double bond where the name or formula shows one).
2. Add the functional group atoms in the right place, drawing every bond, including O–H.
3. Fill the remaining carbon bonds with hydrogens so every carbon has four bonds.
4. Count: atoms must match the molecular formula, and no carbon may have more or fewer than four bonds.

## Answer frames

**"Identify the homologous series" (1–2 marks):** name the series and quote the evidence: "alkene, because it fits CₙH₂ₙ" or "alcohol, because it contains –OH".

**"Explain why X and Y are isomers" (Extended, 2 marks):** same molecular formula (state it) + different structural formula (say what differs: branch, position of the double bond or of –OH).

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Displayed formula | shows all the atoms and all the bonds |
| General formula | represents every member of a homologous series in terms of *n* |
| Functional group | atom or group of atoms that determines the chemical properties of a series |
| Homologous series | family of similar compounds with similar chemical properties due to the same functional group |
| Saturated | all carbon–carbon bonds single |
| Unsaturated | one or more carbon–carbon bonds not single |
| Structural formula *(Extended)* | unambiguous description of how the atoms are arranged |
| Structural isomers *(Extended)* | same molecular formula, different structural formulae |

## Exam traps

- **Show the O–H bond** in a displayed formula.
- **Every carbon needs four bonds.**
- **n in CₙH₂ₙ₊₁COOH excludes the COOH carbon.**
- **Homologous series:** "same functional group", not just "similar compounds".
- *(Extended)* **Physical properties show a trend;** chemical properties are similar.
- *(Extended)* **Isomer definitions need both halves:** same molecular formula **and** different structural formula.
- *(Extended)* **A bent chain is not a branch.**

## Self-test

1. State what a displayed formula shows.
2. Give the general formula of the alkenes.
3. Write the molecular formula of the alkane with seven carbon atoms.
4. Identify the homologous series of C₉H₁₈.
5. How many carbon atoms are in C₃H₇COOH?
6. Name the functional group in alcohols.
7. Define *unsaturated*.
8. Is ethanoic acid saturated or unsaturated? Explain.
9. *(Extended)* State **three** general characteristics of a homologous series.
10. *(Extended)* Define structural isomers.
11. *(Extended)* Give the structural formulae of the two isomers of C₄H₈ named in the syllabus.
12. *(Extended)* Write the structural formula of methyl ethanoate as given in the syllabus.

**Answers:**

1. All the atoms and all the bonds in a molecule.
2. CₙH₂ₙ.
3. C₇H₁₆.
4. Alkenes (H = 2 × C).
5. Four.
6. –OH (hydroxyl group).
7. A compound with one or more carbon–carbon bonds that are not single bonds.
8. Saturated: its only carbon–carbon bond is single; the C=O bond does not count.
9. Any three: same functional group; same general formula; differ by –CH₂–; trend in physical properties; similar chemical properties.
10. Compounds with the same molecular formula but different structural formulae.
11. CH₃CH₂CH=CH₂ and CH₃CH=CHCH₃.
12. CH₃COOCH₃.
