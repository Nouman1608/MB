**These notes condense content area 3.1.2.7 of 9237: "The different understandings of Jihad fee sabilillah" and "The meaning and importance of the 4 types of Jihad: with one's heart (Bil-qalb); with one's tongue (Bil-lisan), with one's hand (Bil-yad); and with a sword (Bil-sayf). The rules for conducting armed warfare (Kitaal)."** The fuller account is in the [Jihad study guide](/resources/oxfordaqa-igcse-islamiyat-9237-jihad/).

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Jihad | Striving or struggle |
| Fee sabilillah | "In the path of Allah" |
| Kitaal | Armed fighting (one narrow part of Jihad) |
| Bil-qalb | With the heart |
| Bil-lisan | With the tongue |
| Bil-yad | With the hand |
| Bil-sayf | With the sword |
| Nafs | The self, with its desires |
| Ikhlas | Sincerity |
| Nahi annil munkar | Forbidding evil |

## The big idea

Jihad is **effort for the sake of Allah**. It is not a synonym for war. Most of Jihad is peaceful and happens in daily life.

## Different understandings

- **Inner struggle:** controlling anger, pride, greed and laziness (22:78 "strive for Allah with the striving due to Him"). A saying about a "greater" Jihad is widely taught, though some scholars question its chain.
- **Social struggle:** truth, justice, education, charity and service.
- **Defence:** armed struggle under strict rules, as a last resort.
- **Misuse:** violence against civilians or by self-appointed groups is rejected by Islam.

## The four types at a glance

| Type | Meaning | Examples | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Heart (Bil-qalb) | Inner struggle; sincere intention; resisting temptation | Controlling anger; avoiding envy; staying patient | 12:53 (the self commanding evil) |
| Tongue (Bil-lisan) | Speaking truth; teaching; advising; speaking against injustice | Explaining Islam politely; defending someone; refusing to backbite | 16:125 "invite ... with wisdom and good instruction" |
| Hand (Bil-yad) | Action and effort | Earning honestly; caring for parents; charity; helping neighbours | Hadith: change evil by the hand |
| Sword (Bil-sayf) | Armed defence within the rules | Defence of a community under attack | 2:190; 22:39 |

**Hadith link:** "Whoever sees an evil, let him change it with his hand; if he cannot, with his tongue; if he cannot, with his heart, and that is the weakest of faith." Order of effort: hand, tongue, heart.

**Importance:** the heart is the base because sincerity gives value to every other effort.

## Rules of Kitaal

| Rule | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Just cause | Self-defence against those who fight you; never to force religion | 2:190; 22:39; 2:256 |
| Proper authority | A lawful leader or state decides; not individuals or groups | Practice of the Prophet (pbuh) and caliphs |
| No transgression | Use no more force than needed | 2:190 |
| Protect non-combatants | Women, children, the old, the sick, monks, worshippers | Instructions of Abu Bakr (RA) |
| Protect property | Do not destroy trees, crops, buildings, or kill animals without need | Instructions of Abu Bakr (RA) |
| Accept peace | If the enemy leans to peace, accept | 8:61 |
| Treat prisoners humanely | Feed and care for them | 76:8 |
| Value of life | Killing one innocent is like killing all humanity | 5:32 |

## Using the prescribed passage, Qur'an 2:190

| Words | Use for |
|---|---|
| "Fight in the way of Allah" | Purpose: the cause is Allah's, not personal gain |
| "those who fight against you" | Defence only; no aggression |
| "but do not transgress" | Limits: no excess, no harm to the innocent |
| "Allah does not like transgressors" | Accountability: breaking the rules displeases Allah |

## Link to Amr bil maroof and Nahi annil munkar

Content area 3.1.2.1 asks for "commanding good" and "forbidding evil". The four types of Jihad are the practical ways of doing both.

| Idea | Link to Jihad |
|---|---|
| Commanding good (Amr bil maroof) | Teaching, encouraging and setting an example; Jihad with the tongue and the hand |
| Forbidding evil (Nahi annil munkar) | Stopping or speaking against wrongdoing, in the order given by the hadith: hand, tongue, heart |
| Intention | Heart: without sincerity neither is valued |

## How to build a 6-mark answer

1. Name the first type or reason in one short sentence [AO1].
2. Add an example or explanation that shows you understand it [AO1].
3. Repeat for the second type or reason [AO1 x2].
4. Add one quotation and say how it supports **your** point [AO2].

**Mini example (Jihad with the hand):** "A Muslim who works honestly to support their family is striving in the path of Allah, because effort with a good intention is worship. This is Jihad with the hand because it is done by action, not words."

## Peaceful and armed Jihad compared

| | Peaceful Jihad (heart, tongue, hand) | Armed Jihad (sword) |
|---|---|---|
| How often | Part of daily life | Rare; a last resort |
| Who decides | Every Muslim | Lawful authority |
| Limits | Wisdom, kindness, lawful means | Defence, no transgression, protect the innocent, accept peace |
| Main aim | Improve self and society | Protect life, faith and freedom |

## Exam traps

- Jihad is **not** the same as war. Kitaal is armed fighting.
- All **four** types must be known by Arabic name and meaning.
- Armed Jihad has conditions: just cause, lawful authority, limits.
- Do not write that Islam forbids all fighting, nor that it allows any fighting.
- For AO2, **apply** the quotation to the question.

## Self-test

1. What does Jihad mean in Arabic?
2. What does "fee sabilillah" mean?
3. What is Kitaal?
4. Name the four types of Jihad in Arabic.
5. Give an example of Jihad with the tongue.
6. Give an example of Jihad with the hand that does not involve fighting.
7. Which Qur'an verse is the prescribed passage for Jihad?
8. Give two rules for conducting armed warfare.
9. Who may decide that a community goes to armed defence?
10. Why do many Muslims say Jihad of the heart is the foundation of the others?

**Answers:** 1. To strive or struggle. 2. In the path of Allah. 3. Armed fighting. 4. Bil-qalb, Bil-lisan, Bil-yad, Bil-sayf. 5. Any one: teaching or explaining Islam; speaking up against injustice; advising a friend. 6. Any one: earning an honest living; charity; caring for parents; helping neighbours. 7. Qur'an 2:190. 8. Any two: only in defence; do not transgress; protect non-combatants; protect property; accept peace; treat prisoners humanely. 9. A lawful leader or state, not individuals or private groups. 10. Because sincere intention and self-control give value to the other efforts.
