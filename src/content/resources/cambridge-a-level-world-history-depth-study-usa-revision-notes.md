The American option of Cambridge 9489 Paper 4 is a depth study of the USA from 1945 to 1993, in three topics: Truman, Eisenhower and post-war USA (1945-61); a time of challenges (1961-74); and the USA, 1974-93. Each topic has four key questions covering domestic politics, social change, civil rights and protest, and foreign policy, and you write two 30-mark essays that **assess** or **evaluate** an issue with balanced, criteria-led argument.

## Worked example: setting a criterion

**Question:** "Johnson's Great Society was a failure." Assess this view.

- **Criterion:** judge against Johnson's own aims -- ending poverty, extending health care and education, securing civil rights.
- **Successes:** the official poverty rate fell during the 1960s; Medicare and Medicaid became permanent; the Civil Rights and Voting Rights Acts transformed Southern politics.
- **Failures:** Vietnam spending drained resources; urban riots from 1965 showed problems untouched; conservative backlash helped Nixon in 1968.
- **Judgement:** a partial success whose limits came largely from Vietnam rather than from the programmes themselves.

## Common mistakes / exam traps

- **Describing presidencies in sequence.** Essays need analysis against criteria, as the AO2 grid requires.
- **Confusing dates across topics.** The fall of Saigon (1975) belongs in 5.2 as the ending of the war, even though it falls after 1974.
- **Treating civil rights as one movement.** Distinguish King and the SCLC from SNCC, Black Power and the Black Panthers.
- **One-sided foreign policy essays.** "How different?" questions (4.4) need continuity as well as change.
- **Unsupported claims about Reaganomics.** Use specific measures and outcomes, not slogans.

## Frequently asked questions

**How are Paper 4 essays marked?**
Out of 30: 15 for AO1 (selected, precise knowledge) and 15 for AO2 (criteria, analysis, balance and judgement), each on five levels in the published mark schemes.

**Do I need to revise all three topics?**
The syllabus sets no rotation for Paper 4, so revising all three keeps your choice of two questions open.

**Is Watergate part of 5.1 or 6.1?**
Both: Nixon's fall is in 5.1, and the post-Watergate loss of trust is a problem Ford faced in 6.1.

## Self-test

1. What did Executive Order 9981 do? *Ordered equal treatment in, and ended segregation of, the armed forces (1948).*
2. Which Supreme Court case ended "separate but equal" in schools? *Brown v. Board of Education (1954).*
3. What was the Gulf of Tonkin Resolution? *Congress's 1964 authorisation for Johnson to use force in Vietnam.*
4. Name two measures of the Great Society. *Medicare and Medicaid; Elementary and Secondary Education Act.*
5. Which treaty did Reagan and Gorbachev sign in 1987? *The INF Treaty.*
