Condensed notes for section 5.1 of Cambridge IGCSE Commerce (0715). For fuller explanations, two worked examples and an annotated answer, use the [advertising study guide](/resources/cambridge-igcse-commerce-0715-advertising/).

## The syllabus in four parts

1. **5.1.1 Types of advertising:** purposes, four types, advantages and disadvantages.
2. **5.1.2 Traditional and digital media:** the methods, their pros and cons, and the factors that affect choice.
3. **5.1.3 Methods of appeal:** the ten appeals and when each suits.
4. **5.1.4 Sales promotion:** meaning, ten methods and the factors affecting choice.

## Purposes of advertising

Inform; persuade; remind; increase sales; build or protect the brand image.

## Four types of advertising

| Type | Key idea | Pro | Con |
|---|---|---|---|
| Informative | Gives facts | Helps informed choice | May not persuade |
| Persuasive | Changes opinions | Builds loyalty and image | Costly; can exaggerate |
| Collective (generic) | Group of producers promotes a product type | Shared cost; grows whole market | Rivals benefit too |
| Competitive | Challenges rivals | Wins customers from rivals | May provoke response; legal risk if untrue |

## Media to recall

**Traditional:** television, radio, cinema, newspapers (local, national), magazines and trade journals, leaflets and flyers, packaging, direct mail, sponsorship, catalogues and brochures, posters, transport vehicles, window displays, point of sale displays, signboards, word of mouth.

**Digital:** social media advertising, other internet advertising (websites, search engines), display advertisements, video advertisements, email marketing, digital billboards, social media influencers.

| Medium | One advantage | One disadvantage |
|---|---|---|
| Television | Huge audience, sight and sound | Very costly |
| Radio | Cheaper; local or national | Sound only |
| Local newspaper | Local, low cost, quick to change | Short life |
| Direct mail | Targets chosen people | Often discarded |
| Posters | Seen repeatedly | Little space for words |
| Social media | Targeted, low cost | Easily ignored |
| Search engine adverts | Reaches people already searching | Cost per click can rise |
| Email marketing | Cheap, trackable | Seen as spam |
| Influencers | Trusted by followers | Reputation risk |

## Eight factors affecting choice of media

Mnemonic **"A Cat Can Fly Light, Multi-Tasking Tigers"**:

| Letter | Factor | Think about |
|---|---|---|
| A | Audience | Who they are and which media they use |
| C | Cost | Budget and cost per person reached |
| C | Cultural and legal | Beliefs, language, advertising rules |
| F | Flexibility to change | How quickly the advert can be altered |
| L | Local and international coverage | Size of the market |
| M | Multi-channel | Combining media for stronger effect |
| T | Timing | Sales, festivals, launches |
| T | Type of product | Needs a demonstration, or simple local service |

## Ten methods of appeal

| Appeal | Core idea | Suits |
|---|---|---|
| Emotion | Stir feelings | Charities, family products |
| Fear | Show the risk without the product | Insurance, safety goods |
| Humour | Make it memorable | Snacks, everyday goods |
| Rational | Facts, price, savings | Cars, appliances, banking |
| Ambition | Product helps success | Watches, education |
| Social acceptance | Fit in with others | Fashion, drinks |
| Romantic | Love and relationships | Perfume, jewellery |
| Adventure | Excitement | Vehicles, sports gear |
| Music | Tune or jingle | Mobile services, fast food |
| Endorsement | Celebrity or influencer approval | Sportswear, beauty |

Check the match: serious products (medicine, finance) suit rational or fear appeals; leisure products suit humour, music, adventure.

## Sales promotion

**Meaning:** a short-term activity that encourages customers to buy now.

**Methods:** point of sale, special offers, sponsorship, displays, competitions, coupons, sales periods, loss leaders, free gifts, free samples.

**Factors affecting choice:** business type, competitors, cost, nature of product, new or old product, timing.

| Situation | Likely method |
|---|---|
| Launch of a new snack | Free samples |
| Clearing last season's stock | Sales period |
| Supermarket wants shoppers to fill trolleys | Loss leader |
| Brand wants to be seen at a sports event | Sponsorship |
| Encourage repeat purchase | Coupons |

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Advertising | Paid communication that informs or persuades people to buy |
| Informative advertising | Gives facts about a product |
| Persuasive advertising | Tries to change opinions |
| Collective (generic) advertising | A group promotes a type of product, not a brand |
| Competitive advertising | Aims to win customers from rivals |
| Digital advertising | Advertising through internet-based media |
| Method of appeal | The emotional or rational reason an advert gives to buy |
| Sales promotion | Short-term incentive to encourage purchase |
| Loss leader | Product sold at or below cost to attract buyers |
| Endorsement | Public approval of a product by a well-known person |

## Exam traps

- **Advertising** and **sales promotion** are not the same thing.
- **Collective** advertising is paid for by a group, not by one firm.
- Always link a medium to the **audience, cost or product**.
- Name the **exact** appeal; do not write "emotional" for fear, humour or romance.
- A loss leader is sold **at or below cost** and aims to bring in customers who also buy other goods.
- Cultural and legal factors include misleading claims and rules on advertising to children.

## Self-test

1. State **two** purposes of advertising.
2. Name the four types of advertising in the syllabus.
3. What is collective advertising?
4. Give **one** advantage and **one** disadvantage of television advertising.
5. Why can social media adverts be better targeted than a poster?
6. Name **three** digital advertising media.
7. State **three** factors that affect the choice of advertising medium.
8. Which appeal does an advert use when it shows a house fire without a smoke alarm?
9. Why might a business use a celebrity endorsement?
10. Define sales promotion.
11. What is a loss leader?
12. Which sales promotion method suits the launch of a new snack?
13. A bag costs $5. A coupon gives 20% off. Find the price with the coupon.

**Answers**

1. Any two: inform; persuade; remind; increase sales; build the brand image.
2. Informative, persuasive, collective (generic), competitive.
3. Advertising paid for by a group of producers to promote a whole type of product rather than one brand.
4. Advantage: large audience with sight and sound. Disadvantage: very high cost.
5. Social media can select users by age, location and interests; a poster is seen by everyone who passes.
6. Any three: social media advertising, websites, search engines, display advertisements, video advertisements, email marketing, digital billboards, influencers.
7. Any three: audience, cost, cultural and legal considerations, flexibility to change, coverage, multi-channel advertising, timing, type of product.
8. Fear.
9. Fans trust and want to copy the celebrity, so the product gains attention and credibility.
10. A short-term activity that encourages customers to buy now.
11. A product sold at or below cost to attract customers who then buy other goods.
12. Free samples (or a special offer).
13. 5.00 x 0.80 = $4.00.
