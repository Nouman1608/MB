These notes condense subtopic **4.4 Similarity** from Topic 4 of the Cambridge IGCSE Mathematics 0580 syllabus for 2025, 2026 and 2027. Core (C4.4) calculates lengths of similar shapes. **Extended** (E4.4) also uses the relationships between lengths, areas and volumes of similar shapes and solids, and solves problems and gives simple explanations, including showing that two triangles are similar using geometric reasons; those items are marked *(Extended)*. For worked examples, read the [similarity study guide](/resources/cambridge-igcse-mathematics-0580-similarity-cambridge-igcse-maths/); for exam-style questions, use the [similarity practice questions](/resources/cambridge-igcse-mathematics-0580-similarity-cambridge-igcse-maths-practice/).

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Similar | Same shape: equal angles, lengths in the same ratio |
| Congruent | Same shape and same size (scale factor 1) |
| Scale factor, k | New length ÷ corresponding original length |
| Corresponding sides | Sides in matching positions; in triangles, opposite equal angles |
| *(Extended)* Area factor | k² |
| *(Extended)* Volume factor | k³ |

## Lengths (Core and Extended)

```
1. Match corresponding sides (by equal angles).
2. k = new / original, from a pair of KNOWN corresponding sides.
3. Unknown length = corresponding length x k   (or / k going back).
```

**Nested triangles** (DE parallel to BC, D on AB, E on AC): compare triangle ADE with triangle ABC.

```
AD = 3, AB = 9, DE = 4      k = 9/3 = 3      BC = 4 x 3 = 12
Use AB (whole side), not DB (part of a side).
```

**Crossed lines** (AB parallel to DE, AE and BD crossing at C): triangle ABC and triangle EDC; the second is upside down.

```
AC = 4, CE = 10, AB = 6     k = 10/4 = 2.5   DE = 6 x 2.5 = 15
```

**Rectangles and other shapes**: compare length with length and width with width.

```
Similar rectangles 8 x 5 and 20 x w:  k = 20/8 = 2.5,  w = 12.5
```

## *(Extended)* Showing triangles are similar

Two pairs of equal angles is enough (the third pair then follows from the angle sum). Give a reason for each:

| Reason | When to use it |
|---|---|
| Vertically opposite angles are equal | Two straight lines cross |
| Alternate angles are equal | Parallel lines, Z shape |
| Corresponding angles are equal | Parallel lines, F shape |
| Common angle | The triangles share a vertex angle |
| Angles in the same segment are equal | Two chords crossing inside a circle |

## *(Extended)* Areas and volumes

```
length factor  k
area factor    k^2        (surface area too)
volume factor  k^3        (capacity too)

From an area ratio:   k = sqrt(area ratio)
From a volume ratio:  k = cube root(volume ratio)
```

| Given | Find | Steps |
|---|---|---|
| lengths 4 and 6 | area of large (small = 32) | k = 1.5, k² = 2.25, 32 × 2.25 = 72 |
| areas 18 and 50 | length of large (small = 6) | k² = 50/18 = 25/9, k = 5/3, 6 × 5/3 = 10 |
| volumes 16 and 54 | surface area of large (small = 20) | k³ = 54/16 = 27/8, k = 3/2, k² = 9/4, 20 × 9/4 = 45 |
| heights 10 and 5 | volume of small (large = 400) | k = ½, k³ = 1/8, 400 × 1/8 = 50 |

**Frustum**: whole cone minus the small similar cone cut from the top.

## Exam traps

- Adding the difference instead of multiplying by k.
- Using DB instead of AB in nested triangles.
- Matching sides by their position on the page instead of by equal angles.
- Applying k to an area or volume.
- Square-rooting a volume ratio.
- Writing similarity reasons vaguely ("parallel lines") instead of naming the angle fact ("alternate angles").
- Rounding k before squaring or cubing it.

## Self-test

1. Rectangle A is 6 cm by 4 cm. Similar rectangle B is 15 cm long. Find its width.
2. Triangles PQR and XYZ are similar with PQ = 5, XY = 12.5 and QR = 8. Find YZ.
3. In triangle ABC, DE is parallel to BC with D on AB. AD = 2, DB = 6 and DE = 3. Find BC.
4. AB is parallel to DE, and AE and BD cross at C. AC = 3, CE = 12, CD = 10. Find BC.
5. A shape is enlarged by scale factor 0.4. A side was 15 cm. How long is it now?
6. Are triangles with sides 3, 4, 5 and 6, 8, 9 similar?
7. *(Extended)* Similar shapes have k = 3. The small area is 7 cm². Find the large area.
8. *(Extended)* Similar solids have k = 2. The large volume is 240 cm³. Find the small volume.
9. *(Extended)* Similar triangles have areas 12 cm² and 75 cm². Find the length scale factor.
10. *(Extended)* Similar cylinders have volumes 40 cm³ and 135 cm³. The smaller has height 6 cm. Find the height of the larger.
11. *(Extended)* Give a reason why angle BAC = angle DEC in question 4.
12. *(Extended)* A cone has volume 640 cm³. The top half of its height is cut off. Find the volume of the frustum.

### Answers

1. k = 15/6 = 2.5: width **10 cm**
2. k = 2.5: YZ = **20**
3. AB = 8, k = 8/2 = 4: BC = **12**
4. k = 12/3 = 4; CD corresponds to BC, so BC = 10 ÷ 4 = **2.5**
5. 15 × 0.4 = **6 cm**
6. 6/3 = 2, 8/4 = 2, but 9/5 = 1.8: **no**
7. 7 × 9 = **63 cm²**
8. 240 ÷ 8 = **30 cm³**
9. k² = 75/12 = 25/4: **k = 5/2**
10. k³ = 135/40 = 27/8, k = 3/2: **9 cm**
11. **Alternate angles** (AB parallel to DE)
12. Small cone: 640 × (½)³ = 80; frustum = **560 cm³**
