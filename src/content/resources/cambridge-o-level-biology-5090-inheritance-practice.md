B: an alternative form of a gene

C: the observable features of an organism

D: a section of a chromosome

B: Aa

C: aa

**3.** State what is meant by the term phenotype. **[1]**

**4.** State the sex chromosomes of a human male. **[1]**

## Section B

**6.** In a species of mouse, black fur (B) is dominant to brown fur (b). Two black mice, both with genotype Bb, are crossed.

**(a)** State the genotypes of the gametes that each parent can make. **[1]**

**(b)** Draw a Punnett square for this cross. **[2]**

**(c)** State the ratio of black to brown offspring. **[1]**

**(d)** The mice have 16 offspring. State how many you would expect to be brown. **[1]**

**(e)** In fact, 7 of the 16 offspring are brown. Explain why this differs from the expected number. **[2]**

**7.** A black mouse is crossed with a brown mouse. Half of their offspring are black and half are brown.

**(a)** State the genotype of the brown parent. **[1]**

**(b)** State the genotype of the black parent and explain how you know. **[2]**

**(c)** Complete a genetic diagram for this cross, showing the parents' gametes and the offspring genotypes. **[3]**

**8.** In humans, the ABO blood group is controlled by three alleles: IA, IB and Io. A woman has blood group A, genotype IAIo. Her partner has blood group B, genotype IBIo.

**(a)** State what is meant by codominance. **[1]**

**(b)** Draw a Punnett square to show the possible genotypes of their children. **[2]**

**(c)** State the probability that a child will have blood group O. **[1]**

**(d)** State the blood group of a child with genotype IAIB, and explain why it is an example of codominance. **[2]**

**9.** A couple have three daughters and are expecting a fourth child.

**(a)** State the probability that the fourth child will be a boy, and explain your answer. **[2]**

**(b)** State which parent determines the sex of a child. **[1]**

**10.** Sickle cell anaemia is caused by a gene mutation.

**(a)** State what is meant by a gene mutation. **[1]**

**(b)** Describe how a mutation in a gene could result in a change to the red blood cells. **[3]**

**11.** State **two** factors that increase the rate of mutation. **[2]**

**12.** State **four** sources of genetic variation in a population. **[4]**

**13.** Two pea plants that are homozygous for round seeds and homozygous for wrinkled seeds respectively are crossed, and the offspring (generation 1) are then crossed together.

**(a)** State the genotype and phenotype of the generation 1 plants (round is dominant). **[2]**

**(b)** Predict the phenotype ratio of generation 2. **[1]**

**(c)** In an experiment, generation 2 had 1 440 plants. Calculate the expected number of wrinkled plants. **[2]**

## Answers

**1.** B [1]. **[1]**

**2.** B [1]. **[1]**

**3.** The observable features of an organism [1]. **[1]**

**4.** XY [1]. **[1]**

**5.** 47 [1]. **[1]**

**6.**

**(a)** B or b [1].

**(b)** Rows and columns B, b with offspring BB, Bb, Bb, bb [2 marks: 1 for correct gametes shown in the square, 1 for correct offspring].

**(c)** 3 black : 1 brown [1].

**(d)** 16 divided by 4 = 4 [1].

**(e)** Fertilisation is random [1], and with a small number of offspring the actual ratio is likely to differ from the expected one by chance [1]. **[7]**

**7.**

**(a)** bb [1].

**(b)** Bb [1], because half the offspring are brown (bb), so each received a b from the black parent, which must therefore carry b [1].

**(c)** Parents Bb x bb [1]. Gametes B, b and b, b [1]. Offspring Bb, Bb, bb, bb [1]. **[6]**

**8.**

**(a)** Both alleles are expressed in the phenotype of a heterozygous individual [1].

**(b)** Gametes IA, Io and IB, Io [1]. Offspring IAIB, IAIo, IBIo, IoIo [1].

**(c)** 1 in 4 (25 per cent) [1].

**(d)** Blood group AB [1], because both alleles IA and IB are expressed [1]. **[6]**

**9.**

**(a)** 1 in 2, or 50 per cent [1], because each child has an equal chance of receiving an X or a Y from the father, and previous children do not affect it [1].

**(b)** The father [1]. **[3]**

**10.**

**(a)** A random change in the base sequence of DNA [1].

**(b)** The change in the sequence of bases alters the sequence of amino acids in a protein [1], so the haemoglobin has a different shape [1], and the red blood cells become sickle shaped [1]. **[4]**

**11.** Ionising radiation [1] and some chemicals [1]. **[2]**

**12.** Mutation [1], meiosis [1], random mating [1], random fertilisation [1]. **[4]**

**13.**

**(a)** Rr [1], round [1].

**(b)** 3 round : 1 wrinkled [1].

**(c)** 1 440 divided by 4 = 360 [1] wrinkled plants (a quarter of the total) [1]. **[5]**

## Where marks are usually lost

- Writing the gametes of a heterozygous parent as a pair of alleles.
- Forgetting to give the key for the letters.
- Giving the genotype ratio when the question asks for the phenotype ratio.
- Saying a small family should show exactly 3 : 1.
- Saying that the mother determines the sex of the baby.
