Cambridge O Level Pakistan Studies (2059) has two compulsory, externally assessed papers, both answered in English and not tiered. Paper 1, The History and Culture of Pakistan, and Paper 2, The Environment of Pakistan, are each 1 hour 30 minutes, 75 marks and 50%. The 2026 edition is single-year and is available in the June and November series. Topic support is available on [The History and Culture of Pakistan](/resources/o-level-pakistan-studies-history-and-culture-of-pakistan/), [The Emergence of Pakistan, 1906-47](/resources/o-level-pakistan-studies-emergence-of-pakistan-1906-47/) and [The Land of Pakistan](/resources/o-level-pakistan-studies-land-of-pakistan/).

## Paper 1: The History and Culture of Pakistan (75 marks, 50%)

Paper 1 has a compulsory Section A of 25 marks, a source-based question, and Section B of 50 marks, where you answer two of four essay-style questions. Section B therefore asks for two extended answers, which means that 50 marks are earned across just two pieces of writing. The choice matters: before you start, read all four and choose the two for which you can give the most accurate, developed and balanced answers.

Revision for this paper should be organised chronologically and thematically. Build a timeline for the move to independence and the years that followed, then add the causes, consequences and the people involved. Include the cultural content in the title of the paper, not only political events.

## Paper 2: The Environment of Pakistan (75 marks, 50%)

Paper 2 requires three of five questions, each worth 25 marks. Revision has to cover the full range because you cannot be sure which three will suit you. See the pages on [Agricultural Development](/resources/o-level-pakistan-studies-agricultural-development/), [Industrial Development](/resources/o-level-pakistan-studies-industrial-development/) and the [Land revision notes](/resources/o-level-pakistan-studies-land-revision-notes/). Learn named examples, locations and figures, since environment answers are strengthened by specific detail.

## Timing and marks-per-minute

- Each paper: 90 minutes / 75 marks = 1.2 minutes per mark.
- Paper 1 Section A: 25 x 1.2 = 30 minutes.
- Paper 1 Section B: 50 x 1.2 = 60 minutes, which is about 30 minutes for each of the two essays if they are equally weighted (the record gives 50 marks across the two, so check the specification for the split of marks).
- Paper 2: 3 x 25 marks = 75 marks, and 25 x 1.2 = 30 minutes per question.

The sums use the whole 90 minutes, so in practice shave a little from each answer to leave a few minutes to read and choose. Every mark on each paper is worth 50 / 75 = about 0.67% of the qualification.

## Command words and question styles

- **Describe**: give features or events in detail.
- **Explain**: give reasons, each developed with a consequence.
- **Why** and **how far**: reach a supported judgement after weighing factors.
- **Source questions**: use the source and your own knowledge together; evaluate it, do not paraphrase it.
- **Essay-style questions**: introduce your line, develop several points with evidence and conclude.

## Worked practice scenario

An original Section B style essay plan: "How important was the Lahore Resolution of 1940 in the movement for a separate Muslim homeland?"

Outline plan:

1. Introduction: state the Resolution was a turning point in aim, but not the only factor.
2. Point one: it set out a clear political goal for the Muslim League, in March 1940 at Lahore.
3. Point two: it gave the movement a focus for mobilising support after 1940.
4. Counter-point: events such as the 1946 elections and negotiations with the British and Congress also shaped the outcome.
5. Judgement: the Resolution mattered greatly as a declaration of aim, but achieving the goal depended on later events.

Model paragraph: "The Lahore Resolution of 1940 was important chiefly because it turned a general sense of Muslim concern into a stated political aim. Before it, demands were framed around safeguards within a united India; afterwards, the Muslim League could point to a declared goal around which supporters could rally. This mattered because a movement needs a clear objective to organise campaigns and elections. However, a declaration alone did not create a state, and later political developments, including elections and negotiations, determined how the aim was pursued. The Resolution was therefore a necessary foundation rather than a complete explanation."

This follows the pattern point, evidence, explanation, link back to the question.

## Before/during exam checklist

- Note that both papers are 1 hour 30 minutes and 75 marks.
- Build a dated timeline for Paper 1 and a fact sheet of places and figures for Paper 2.
- Practise source questions using the source and your own knowledge.
- On Paper 1 Section B, choose two of four essays after reading all of them.
- On Paper 2, choose three of five questions after reading all of them.
- Plan each essay in a few lines first.
- Write in English, with clear paragraphs and a conclusion.
- Count your questions before time ends: one compulsory Section A plus two Section B on Paper 1, and three on Paper 2.

2. How many Paper 2 questions do you answer, and what is each worth?
3. What is the pace in minutes per mark?
4. In which series is the 2026 edition available?

**Answers:** 1. Two, from four, for 50 marks in total. 2. Three of five, 25 marks each. 3. 1.2 minutes per mark, since 90 / 75 = 1.2. 4. The June and November series.

## Frequently asked questions

**How many papers are there in Cambridge O Level Pakistan Studies 2059?**
There are two compulsory, externally assessed papers, each 1 hour 30 minutes, 75 marks and 50%. All answers are written in English.

**What does Paper 1 Section B involve?**
Section B is worth 50 marks and asks you to answer two of four essay-style questions. Section A is a compulsory 25-mark source-based question.

**What does Paper 2 involve?**
You answer three of five questions, each worth 25 marks. Paper 2 is The Environment of Pakistan.

**Which series can I sit?**
The 2026 edition is examined in June and November. Cambridge has also published later single-year and bundled editions, so confirm which applies to your year.

**Does this have the same structure as IGCSE Pakistan Studies 0448?**
Yes, the paper titles, timing and mark split match, according to the verified record. They are separate qualifications with their own codes.
