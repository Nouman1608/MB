Cambridge A Level Mathematics (9709, examination series 2026-2027) is route-based, with six externally assessed written papers. Paper 1 Pure Mathematics 1 (1 hour 50 minutes, 75 marks) and Paper 3 Pure Mathematics 3 (1 hour 50 minutes, 75 marks) are each 30% of the A Level. Paper 2 Pure Mathematics 2, Paper 4 Mechanics, Paper 5 Probability & Statistics 1 and Paper 6 Probability & Statistics 2 are each 1 hour 15 minutes and 50 marks. This page explains which papers you sit and how to prepare, alongside the topic pages on [Pure Mathematics 1 functions](/resources/a-level-mathematics-pure-mathematics-1-functions/), [Pure Mathematics 1 coordinate geometry](/resources/a-level-mathematics-pure-mathematics-1-coordinate-geometry/) and [quadratics revision notes](/resources/a-level-mathematics-quadratics-revision-notes/).

## Know your route before you plan revision

The full A Level is always Paper 1 and Paper 3 (both compulsory, 30% + 30% = 60%), Paper 5 Probability & Statistics 1 (compulsory, 20%), plus either Paper 4 Mechanics or Paper 6 Probability & Statistics 2 (20% each). These two are mutually exclusive: Paper 6 cannot be combined with Paper 4 because it depends on Paper 5 content. The total is 30 + 30 + 20 + 20 = 100%.

The AS Level is Paper 1 (60% of the AS) plus one of Paper 2, Paper 4 or Paper 5 (each 40% of the AS). Only Papers 1 + 4 or Papers 1 + 5 can be carried forward as the first stage of a staged A Level. The route of Papers 1 + 2 is a standalone "AS Level, Pure Mathematics only" and cannot be carried forward to complete the A Level. Paper 2 carries 0% towards the A Level, and Paper 3 and Paper 6 have no AS weighting.

## Papers 1 and 3: the pure mathematics core

Papers 1 and 3 are the long papers: 110 minutes for 75 marks, or 110 / 75 = 1.47 minutes per mark. Paper 3 is compulsory for the A Level only and is not offered at AS. These papers reward algebraic fluency, so drill the foundations until they are automatic. Pages such as [Pure Mathematics 1 quadratics](/resources/a-level-mathematics-pure-mathematics-1-quadratics/) cover content that recurs in other questions: discriminants, completing the square and the intersection of lines and curves.

## Paper 2: Pure Mathematics 2 (AS route only)

Paper 2 is 75 minutes for 50 marks (1.5 minutes per mark) and is offered only as part of the AS Level, on the Pure Mathematics only route. If you are on the full A Level, you do not sit it, though the revision material in [Pure Mathematics 2](/resources/a-level-mathematics-pure-mathematics-2/) still helps you see how topics build.

## Papers 4, 5 and 6: Mechanics and Probability & Statistics

Each of these is 75 minutes for 50 marks, so 75 / 50 = 1.5 minutes per mark. Mechanics and Statistics need different habits. In Mechanics, draw a clear force or motion diagram first and state your sign convention; the [Mechanics study guide](/resources/a-level-maths-9709-mechanics/) covers the content. In Probability & Statistics, define events and distributions in words before computing; the [Probability & Statistics 1 study guide](/resources/a-level-maths-9709-probability-statistics-1/) gives the topic coverage. Paper 6 is offered only as part of the A Level.

## Timing and marks per minute

- Papers 1 and 3: 110 minutes / 75 marks = 1.47 minutes per mark.
- Papers 2, 4, 5 and 6: 75 minutes / 50 marks = 1.5 minutes per mark.
- Full A Level (Papers 1, 3, 5 and either 4 or 6): 110 + 110 + 75 + 75 = 370 minutes for 75 + 75 + 50 + 50 = 250 marks.

A five-mark part therefore warrants about seven to eight minutes. If a question stalls for longer, mark it, move on and return with fresh eyes.

## Command words and question styles

Mathematics papers use a small set of instructions. "Show that" gives you the answer and demands a complete, logical route to it, so every step matters. "Find" and "calculate" want a result with working. "Determine" and "solve" want a method that reaches exact values. "Sketch" wants key features labelled, not accurate plotting. Method marks are generally available for correct working even when the final answer is wrong, so write each step. Check the current syllabus and formula list for what you must memorise and what is supplied.

## Worked practice scenario

Original question: "The line y = 2x + k is a tangent to the curve y = x^2 - 4x + 7. Find the value of k and the coordinates of the point of contact."

```
Step 1: set the expressions equal.
  x^2 - 4x + 7 = 2x + k
  x^2 - 6x + (7 - k) = 0

Step 2: a tangent touches once, so the discriminant is zero.
  b^2 - 4ac = (-6)^2 - 4(1)(7 - k) = 36 - 28 + 4k = 8 + 4k
  8 + 4k = 0  ->  k = -2

Step 3: repeated root.
  x^2 - 6x + 9 = 0  ->  (x - 3)^2 = 0  ->  x = 3

Step 4: find y.
  y = 2(3) + (-2) = 4
  Check on the curve: 9 - 12 + 7 = 4  (matches)
```

So k = -2 and the point of contact is (3, 4). The check in Step 4 confirms both expressions give the same y-value. A second check uses the gradient: dy/dx = 2x - 4 equals 2 when x = 3, matching the line's gradient.

## Before/during exam checklist

- Confirm exactly which papers your route requires, and that your choice of Paper 4 or Paper 6 is a permitted pairing.
- Practise full timed papers at 1.47 or 1.5 minutes per mark.
- Learn which results are in the formula list and which you must recall.
- Show every step of working, including unit and rounding statements where asked.
- Read "show that" questions twice and avoid working backwards from the answer.
- Check answers against the question: sensible size, correct form, required accuracy.
- Leave a few minutes at the end for returning to skipped parts.

## Frequently asked questions

**How many papers are there in Cambridge A Level Mathematics 9709?**
There are six written papers, all externally assessed: Pure Mathematics 1, 2 and 3, Mechanics, Probability & Statistics 1 and Probability & Statistics 2.

**Which papers make up the full A Level?**
Papers 1 and 3 (30% each), Paper 5 (20%) and either Paper 4 or Paper 6 (20%), totalling 100%.

**Which papers make up the AS Level?**
Paper 1 (60% of the AS Level) plus one of Paper 2, Paper 4 or Paper 5 (40% of the AS Level).

**Can I carry my AS result forward?**
Only if the second paper was Paper 4 or Paper 5. The Papers 1 + 2 route is a standalone AS Level and cannot be carried forward.

**How long are the papers?**
Papers 1 and 3 are 1 hour 50 minutes and 75 marks each. Papers 2, 4, 5 and 6 are 1 hour 15 minutes and 50 marks each.
