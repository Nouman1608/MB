## Questions

### Section A: short-answer questions

**1.** Define the term *delayering*. **[2]**

**2.** Distinguish between span of control and chain of command. **[2]**

**3.** Explain one advantage and one disadvantage of bureaucracy for a hospital. **[4]**

**4.** A business with a span of control of 6 at every level has four levels of hierarchy. Calculate the maximum number of employees it can hold. **[2]**

**5.** Explain one reason why a matrix structure might lead to conflict. **[2]**

### Section B: structured questions

Questions 6 to 10 use the case below.

Calloway Outdoor is an invented maker of camping equipment. Its chart is:

```
Managing director
  -> Operations director
       -> 3 production managers, each with 3 supervisors
            -> each supervisor has 10 operatives
  -> Marketing director
       -> 6 marketing staff
  -> Finance director
       -> 4 finance staff
```

Calloway sells mainly in its home country through independent shops. It plans to sell directly to customers in four other countries, each with different product safety laws and climates.

**6.** Using the chart:

**(a)** Calculate the total number of employees. **[2]**

**(b)** State the number of levels of hierarchy in the operations branch. **[1]**

**(c)** Calculate the number of employees below the Operations director. **[1]**

**7.** Explain what the chart suggests about communication between an operative and the managing director. **[2]**

**8.** Calloway is organized by function. Explain one disadvantage of this for its plan to sell in four other countries. **[2]**

**9.** Calloway's board is considering removing the production manager level. Analyse the effects of this delayering on the business. **[6]**

**10.** Discuss whether Calloway should reorganize by region to support its plan to sell in four other countries. **[8]**

**11.** Distinguish between a tall structure and a flat structure, using one effect on employee motivation. **[3]**

**12.** (HL only) Pellbrook Events, an invented festival organiser, has 25 permanent staff but needs about 400 workers for three weeks each summer. Explain how Handy's Shamrock Organization could apply to Pellbrook. **[6]**

## Answers

### Section A

**1.** Removing one or more levels of hierarchy from an organisation [1], usually middle management, which widens spans of control and shortens the chain of command [1]. **[2]**

**2.** Span of control is the number of subordinates who report directly to one manager [1], while chain of command is the line of authority from the top of the organisation down to each employee [1]. **[2]**

**3.** Advantage: clear procedures [1] mean every patient is treated to the same safety standards, which matters where errors harm lives [1]. Disadvantage: rigid rules slow decisions [1], so staff cannot adapt quickly in an emergency that the rules did not foresee [1]. **[4]**

**4.** 1 + 6 + 36 + 216 [1] = **259 employees** [1] **[2]**
*Examiner insight:* Each level multiplies the one above by 6. A common error is 6 × 4 = 24.

**5.** Employees report to both a functional manager and a project manager [1], so they may receive conflicting instructions or priorities, causing confusion and tension [1]. **[2]**

### Section B

**6. (a)** 1 + 3 directors + 3 managers + 9 supervisors + 90 operatives + 6 + 4 [1] = **116** [1]

**(b)** **5 levels** (managing director, Operations director, production manager, supervisor, operative) [1]

**(c)** 3 + 9 + 90 = **102** [1] **[4]**
*Examiner insight:* Count supervisors: 3 managers × 3 supervisors = 9, and operatives: 9 × 10 = 90.

**7.** An operative's message passes through four links: supervisor, production manager, Operations director, managing director [1]. This long chain makes communication slow and increases the risk that the message is distorted or filtered [1]. **[2]**

**8.** Functional departments are organized around tasks, not countries [1], so no team specializes in each country's safety laws and climate, and decisions may ignore local needs [1]. **[2]**

**9.** The 3 production managers would be removed, so 9 supervisors report directly to the Operations director, whose span rises from 3 to 9 [1]. The chain of command shortens from five levels to four, so messages travel faster [1]. Salary costs fall, which could improve profit [1]. However, the Operations director may be overloaded with 9 direct reports and less time for planning [1]. Supervisors gain responsibility, which may motivate some [1], but loss of a promotion step and the experience of the removed managers may lower morale and quality [1]. **[6]**
*Examiner insight:* "Analyse" needs both effects and a chain of reasoning, not a list. Using the case numbers (3 to 9) shows application.

**10.** For: different safety laws in each country need local knowledge, which a regional structure provides [1]; regional teams can adapt products to local climates [1]; decisions are made closer to customers, so responses are faster [1]. Against: each region would duplicate marketing and finance roles, raising costs for a business of only 116 staff [1]; the four markets are new and small, so full regional divisions may be premature [1]; production is in one country, so operations should stay functional [1]. A middle option: keep production functional and create regional sales and compliance teams [1]. Judgement with condition, for example: a partial regional structure is better now, moving to full regional divisions only if overseas sales grow to a large share of revenue [1]. **[8]**

**11.** A tall structure has many levels and narrow spans of control, while a flat structure has few levels and wide spans [1]. In a flat structure employees usually have more responsibility and less close supervision [1], which can motivate those who value independence, while a tall structure offers more promotion steps that can motivate through career progression [1]. **[3]**

**12.** Core: the 25 permanent staff who plan the festival, book performers and manage safety, kept on secure contracts because their knowledge is essential [1][1]. Contractual fringe: outside firms paid for specific services such as security, stage building or catering [1][1]. Flexible labour force: about 400 temporary workers hired for three weeks to run gates, bars and cleaning [1]. Effect: Pellbrook avoids paying 400 staff all year, but must manage contracts carefully and may get weaker commitment from temporary workers [1]. **[6]**

## Where marks are usually lost

* Adding up a chart without multiplying through the levels.
* Counting all staff in a branch as one manager's span.
* Stating effects of delayering without using the case figures.
* Recommending a structure without weighing costs, especially duplication of roles.
* Naming Shamrock leaves without applying them to the business.
