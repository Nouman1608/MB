This page is a guide to OCR A Level Business (H431) past papers from June 2023 to June 2026. For each series you get the grade thresholds, copied from OCR's grade-boundary documents, and, for June 2023 to June 2025, a short account of what examiners reported, written by us from OCR's own examiners' reports. We do not host any papers. Every paper, mark scheme and report is on OCR's site, linked below.

## Where to get the official papers

- [OCR A Level Business (H031, H431) qualification page](https://www.ocr.org.uk/qualifications/as-and-a-level/business-h031-h431-from-2015/)
- [OCR A Level Business assessment page, with question papers, mark schemes and examiners' reports](https://www.ocr.org.uk/qualifications/as-and-a-level/business-h031-h431-from-2015/assessment/)
- [OCR grade boundaries](https://www.ocr.org.uk/administration/grade-boundaries/) and the [grade boundaries archive](https://www.ocr.org.uk/administration/grade-boundaries/grade-boundaries-archive/grade-boundaries-archive.aspx)

Use paper, mark scheme and examiners' report together. Sit the paper under timed conditions, mark it strictly with the mark scheme, then read the report for that component to see why answers that looked reasonable still lost marks. Business papers are built around a resource booklet, so practise reading the booklet before you start writing. Our notes for each series are a quick way into the report, not a replacement for it.

Check which specification a paper was set on. H431 is the specification being withdrawn: OCR's records give its final assessment as summer 2027, with a replacement, H436, taught from September 2026 and first assessed in 2028. H436 has no past papers or boundaries yet, so every series below is H431. H431 has three written papers of 2 hours and 80 marks each, all equally weighted: Operating in a local business environment (H431/01), The UK business environment (H431/02) and The global business environment (H431/03), 240 marks in total. H436 has different papers (Business Activity, Marketing and People; Operations, Finance and External Influences; Strategy, Risk and Managing Change), so do not mix the two. For topic study, see our [accounting and financial considerations notes](/resources/ocr-a-level-business-accounting-and-financial-considerations/).

## June 2025

The June 2025 reports describe papers where knowledge and calculations had improved, but where evaluation and use of the resource booklet still separated the grades. Examiners also pointed to scripts that were hard to read or followed no clear structure.

**Where marks were lost**

- Paper 1 (H431/01): theory-only answers that could not earn analysis or evaluation marks; little use of the case study beyond naming what the business does; generic recommendations that did not suit a small business; lists of points in place of developed arguments; everyday phrases used instead of subject terms. Stronger scripts opened by showing the key concept, built chains of analysis and showed working.
- Paper 2 (H431/02): very general answers that did not address the exact question; gaps in subject knowledge; copying the resource booklet instead of applying it; conclusions that merely summarised earlier points instead of reaching a decision; 'explain' questions answered by stating; confusion between what a business plan is and how it is used; some questions left blank.
- Paper 3 (H431/03): not knowing key terms such as capacity management, or the difference between recruitment and selection; analysing too many points, which left too little time for evaluation; saving all the evaluation for the end; answering about another part of the marketing mix when the question was about product. Stronger scripts showed calculation working stage by stage and kept to a few well-chosen points.

## June 2024

In June 2024 examiners saw better exam technique on long answers and better quantitative work, but the multiple-choice section, evaluation and context use continued to separate candidates.

**Where marks were lost**

- Paper 1 (H431/01): purely theoretical answers; not reading or using the resource booklet; weak results on the multiple-choice questions; answering what the candidate wanted to write rather than what was asked. Candidates who did well wrote focused answers and showed working.
- Paper 2 (H431/02): significant gaps in knowledge; copying or ignoring the context; no explicit knowledge of the concept tested in the Section B questions; many numerical questions not attempted. In Section A, monetary motivation methods confused with non-monetary ones, and a leadership term mixed up with autocratic leadership. The final strategy question was the weakest.
- Paper 3 (H431/03): context forgotten on lower-mark questions; no knowledge of Ansoff's matrix, so no marks on that question; trying to cover too many points in a 20-mark answer when two fully developed chains of analysis are enough for the analysis marks; not reading a question closely enough and writing about an unrelated event.

## June 2023

The June 2023 reports describe a fair set of papers on which subject knowledge was better than usual. Evaluation in the longer questions was the limiting factor on many otherwise good scripts.

**Where marks were lost**

- Paper 1 (H431/01): the two calculation-based multiple-choice questions; theory-only answers without analysis or evaluation; ticking two boxes in a multiple-choice question, which earns nothing, and leaving boxes empty although there is no negative marking.
- Paper 2 (H431/02): AIDA as a marketing technique; answers that were too brief, or too long and unfocused; generic Section B responses; weak quantitative work; inconsistent evaluation in the 15-mark questions; omitted questions.
- Paper 3 (H431/03): not applying answers to the case study on shorter questions, which limited them to knowledge marks; the elasticity calculation and the total float calculation; not understanding that an 'analyse' question needs knowledge, application and analysis; trying to cover every stakeholder. Stronger scripts placed short evaluations through the answer and reached a justified judgement.

## Frequently asked questions

**Where can I find OCR H431 past papers and mark schemes?**
On the assessment page for OCR A Level Business, linked above, which lists question papers, mark schemes and examiners' reports by series.

**Is H431 still being examined?**
OCR's records give summer 2027 as the final H431 assessment. H436, the replacement, is first assessed in 2028. Papers for H436 are not yet available, so use H431 papers for H431 revision and check which specification your centre is entered for.

**How is the overall H431 grade decided?**
The three papers are added to give a mark out of 240, and the overall boundaries in each table apply to that total.

**What mistakes do examiners report most often?**
Answers that stay theoretical when the resource booklet should be used, evaluation that only summarises, too many analysed points with no time left for judgement, and calculations without working.

**Can grade thresholds tell me what mark I need next time?**
No. Thresholds change every series and are only a guide for the series they belong to.
