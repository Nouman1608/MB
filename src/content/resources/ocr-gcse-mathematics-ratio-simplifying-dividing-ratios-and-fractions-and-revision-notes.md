## Definitions and facts

- **Ratio**: compares quantities, written a : b. Order matters. 2 : 5 is not 5 : 2.
- **Equivalent ratios**: 3 : 4, 6 : 8 and 1.5 : 2 all describe the same comparison.
- **Simplest form**: whole numbers with no common factor other than 1.
- **Form 1 : n**: first part is 1. n can be a decimal or fraction.
- **Part-to-part** compares one part with another (3 : 4). **Part-to-whole** compares a part with the total (3/7).
- A constant ratio y : x = b : a means y = (b/a) x, a straight line through the origin.

## Method in steps

### Simplifying a ratio

1. Convert to the same unit.
2. Clear decimals or fractions by multiplying.
3. Divide by the highest common factor.

Reminder: 2.5 km : 500 m is 2500 : 500, which simplifies to **5 : 1**. And 72 : 108 simplifies to **2 : 3**. For three parts, 35 : 28 : 21 simplifies to **5 : 4 : 3** by dividing by 7.

Fractions: 3/4 : 5/8. Multiply by 8 to get 6 : 5, so the answer is **6 : 5**.

### Writing as 1 : n

Divide both parts by the first part. For 25 : 40, 40 ÷ 25 = 1.6, so the ratio is **1 : 1.6**. Check by multiplying back: 25 × 1.6 = 40.

### Sharing in a ratio

```
1. Total parts = sum of the ratio numbers
2. One part   = quantity ÷ total parts
3. Each share = ratio number × one part
4. Check      = shares add up to the quantity
```

Reminder: share £510 in the ratio 1 : 2 : 3. Total parts 6, one part 85, shares **£85, £170, £255**. Check: 85 + 170 + 255 = 510.

### Using a difference

If the shares differ by d and the ratio is a : b, then (a - b) parts = d. Ratio 5 : 2 and difference 36: 3 parts = 36, one part = 12, shares **60 and 24**.

### Using one known share

Ratio 5 : 3, smaller share 27. Smaller share is 3 parts, so one part = 9. Larger share = 5 × 9 = **45**.

### Ratio to fractions

For a : b, the fractions of the whole are a/(a + b) and b/(a + b). For 4 : 3 the whole has 7 parts. Of 245, the first share is 4/7 × 245 = 140 and the second is 3/7 × 245 = 105. Check: 140 + 105 = 245.

### Scaling a recipe

Find the amount for one portion, then multiply. Rice for 4 people is 280 g. One portion is 280 ÷ 4 = 70 g, so for 7 people you need 70 × 7 = **490 g**.

### Changing ratios

Find the real quantities, change them, then write the new ratio. Do not add to the ratio numbers.

### Ratio and linear functions

| Ratio of x : y | Equation | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 1 : n | y = nx | 1 : 4 gives y = 4x |
| a : b | y = (b/a) x | 3 : 8 gives y = 8x/3; x = 9 gives y = 24 |

A line y = mx + c with c not zero does not describe a constant ratio.

## Worked reminders

### Known share, unknown share

Jo and Kit have pocket money in the ratio 3 : 7. Jo has £18. One part is 18 ÷ 3 = £6, so Kit has 7 × 6 = **£42**.

### Total given, ratio given

A drink is made from concentrate and water in the ratio 1 : 4, and 650 ml is made in total. There are 5 parts, so one part is 650 ÷ 5 = 130 ml. Concentrate is **130 ml** and water is 4 × 130 = **520 ml**.

### Changing ratio

A library has fiction and non-fiction books in the ratio 5 : 2, with 35 fiction books. One part is 7, so there are 2 × 7 = 14 non-fiction books. After 14 more non-fiction books arrive there are 28. The new ratio is 35 : 28, which simplifies by 7 to **5 : 4**.

## Must-know distinctions

| Idea | Compare | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Part-to-part and part-to-whole | 3 : 4 and 3/7 | The denominator of the fraction is the sum of the parts |
| Ratio and amount | 2 : 3 and "2 and 3" | A ratio gives relative size, not actual size |
| Order of a ratio | cats : dogs and dogs : cats | Reversing the order reverses the answer |
| Ratio and difference | difference in parts | A difference of 36 means (a - b) parts, not 36 split in total |

## Checks to build into every answer

- **Sum check**: after sharing, add the shares. They must equal the original quantity.
- **Ratio check**: divide your two answers back down. If you shared in the ratio 3 : 7, the shares should simplify to 3 : 7.
- **Size check**: the larger ratio number must get the larger share. If it does not, you have swapped the order.
- **Sense check**: a recipe for more people needs more of every ingredient, and a recipe for fewer people needs less.
- **Unit check**: write the unit on a share or an amount, but never on a ratio.

## Choosing a method

| The question gives you | Start by finding |
|---|---|
| The total and the ratio | The total number of parts, then one part |
| One share and the ratio | The value of one part from that share |
| The difference between shares | The number of parts the difference represents |
| A fraction of a whole | The ratio of the part to the rest, using the denominator minus the numerator |
| A recipe for one group size | The amount for one portion |

## Quick self-test

Answer these without looking, then check below.

1. Simplify 90 : 60.
2. Simplify 1.2 m : 30 cm.
3. Write 14 : 35 in the form 1 : n.
4. Share 150 in the ratio 2 : 3.
5. Share 84 in the ratio 1 : 2 : 4.
6. Find the missing number: 3 : 5 = 21 : ?
7. What fraction of the total is the larger part when two parts are in the ratio 4 : 7?
8. Two quantities are in the ratio 5 : 3 and the smaller is 27. Find the larger.
9. Two shares are in the ratio 3 : 2 and differ by 14. Find both shares.
10. A pancake mix for 4 people uses 300 ml of milk. How much milk is needed for 10 people?
11. Quantities x and y are in the ratio 2 : 5. Write y in terms of x, and find y when x = 8.

### Answers

1. 90 : 60 divided by 30 gives **3 : 2**.
2. 1.2 m = 120 cm, so 120 : 30 = **4 : 1**.
3. 14 : 35 = 2 : 5, so 35 ÷ 14 = 2.5 and the ratio is **1 : 2.5**.
4. 5 parts, one part 30, shares **60 and 90**.
5. 7 parts, one part 12, shares **12, 24 and 48**.
6. 21 ÷ 3 = 7, so 5 × 7 = **35**.
7. The whole has 11 parts, so the larger part is **7/11**.
8. 27 ÷ 3 = 9, so 5 × 9 = **45**.
9. 1 part = 14, so shares are 3 × 14 = **42** and 2 × 14 = **28**.
10. 300 ÷ 4 = 75 ml per person, 75 × 10 = **750 ml**.
11. y = 5x/2, and when x = 8, y = **20**.

## Where marks are usually lost

- Leaving units in different forms, such as cm and m, before simplifying.
- Stopping at a ratio that can still be simplified.
- Dividing by one number from the ratio rather than by the total number of parts.
- Using 2/3 for the fraction in a 2 : 3 split, when the fraction is 2/5.
- Giving the shares in the wrong order, so the larger share goes to the smaller part.
- Finding a difference in parts but forgetting to multiply by the value of one part.
- Adding an extra quantity to a ratio number rather than to the real amount.
- Skipping the check that the shares add up to the total.
- Not showing the value of one part, which leaves nothing to credit if an arithmetic slip occurs later.
- Writing 1 : n with n the wrong way up. Divide the second part by the first.
