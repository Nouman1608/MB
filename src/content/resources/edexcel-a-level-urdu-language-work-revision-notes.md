Condensed recall for **sub-theme 1.2** of Theme 1 in Pearson Edexcel A Level Urdu (9UR0): work (کام). For fuller explanation and a model paragraph, read the [Theme 1 study guide](/resources/edexcel-a-level-urdu-language-theme-1-study-guide/).

## Key ideas

- **Types of work**: agriculture, industry, services, informal work and self-employment, and work abroad.
- **خواتین کی ملازمت** = women's employment, with its barriers and opportunities.
- **بے روزگاری** = unemployment; pressure on young people and the role of skills and training.
- Technology, freelancing and changes in how and where people work.

## Argument frame

State the change (for example in who works and where), give an opportunity and a barrier, then a reasoned opinion. Use **میرے خیال میں** for the opinion and **اس کے علاوہ** to add a second point.

## Grammar to control

- Keep gender agreement in compound verbs and passives.
- Prefer simple structures you can control to ambitious ones you cannot.
- Check script details: a misplaced dot or missing diacritic can change a word.

## Research and sources

Do not quote a figure you cannot trace to a named source. Your own research file should hold named sources, dates and figures you have checked yourself.

## Exam traps

- Listing jobs without explaining their importance.
- Asserting opinions about unemployment with no reasons or evidence.
- Learning isolated words without sentences to use them in.

## Self-test

1. Give the Urdu for 'unemployment' and 'livelihood'.
2. What does خواتین کی ملازمت refer to?
3. Translate 'salary' and 'skill' into Urdu.
4. Name two types of work you could discuss.
5. Why avoid quoting a statistic you cannot trace?

**Answers:** 1. بے روزگاری and روزگار. 2. Women's employment. 3. تنخواہ and ہنر. 4. For example, agriculture and services, or self-employment and work abroad. 5. It cannot be checked, and the research file should contain only named, dated, checked sources.
