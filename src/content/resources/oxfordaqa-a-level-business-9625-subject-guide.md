OxfordAQA International AS and A-level Business is a modular course about how organisations are run
and how they choose and carry out strategy. It begins with the nature of business and the main
functional areas (marketing, operations, human resources and finance) and then moves at A2 to
strategic analysis, choice and implementation. Two specifications are currently in use. The revised
**9725** specification was first taught in September 2026, with first AS exams in May/June 2027 and
first A-level exams in May/June 2028. The legacy **9625** specification is in teach-out for students
already part-way through it. Both are assessed by four written papers, two AS papers worth 20% of
the A-level each and two A2 papers worth 30% each, but the paper lengths and mark totals differ.

## How the course is organised

The topic list below follows the 9725 specification. Students finishing 9625 should check the exact
content of their remaining papers with their teacher.

**AS stage (topics 1 to 5)** covers the functional foundations of business:

- **What is business?** The nature and purpose of business, types of business ownership and the
  external environment.
- **Marketing.** Marketing objectives and plans, marketing data, segmentation, targeting and
  positioning, and the marketing mix.
- **Operational performance.** Operations management, operations competitiveness, and inventory
  and supply chain management.
- **Human resources.** Human resource objectives and data, organisational design, motivation and
  employer-employee relations.
- **Finance.** Sources of finance, break-even analysis, and profit and cash.

**A2 stage (topics 6 to 16)** follows the logic of strategic management. It starts with mission,
objectives and SWOT analysis, then analyses a business's existing internal position, its industry
environment, and the wider external environment for opportunities and threats, along with analysing
future sales. From there it turns to strategic options (which markets to compete in and which
products to offer), strategic positioning (how to compete), deciding on a strategic investment, and
types of strategies, before ending with implementing a strategy and change, risk and uncertainty.

## How it's assessed

**9725 (current specification)**

| Paper | Title | Time | Marks | Weighting (A-level) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AS Unit 1 | Business and Markets | 75 minutes | 60 | 20% |
| AS Unit 2 | Managing Operations, Human Resources and Finance | 75 minutes | 60 | 20% |
| A-level Unit 3 | Business Analysis | 105 minutes | 80 | 30% |
| A-level Unit 4 | Strategy | 105 minutes | 80 | 30% |

The first exam opportunity for the AS units is May/June 2027 and for the A-level units May/June
2028. OxfordAQA explains that, following teacher feedback, each A2 paper has separate topics and a
simplified, fully case-study-based structure compared with the legacy specification.

**9625 (legacy specification, teach-out)**

| Paper | Title | Time | Marks | Weighting (A-level) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AS Paper 1 | Business and Markets | 90 minutes | 80 | 20% |
| AS Paper 2 | Managing Operations, Human Resources and Finance | 90 minutes | 80 | 20% |
| A-level Paper 1 | Business Strategy | 105 minutes | 80 | 30% |
| A-level Paper 2 | Business Decision Making | 105 minutes | 80 | 30% |

For 9625, the final May/June AS exams were in 2026, with AS resits available in January 2027. The
final May/June A2 exams are in 2027, with A2 resits in January 2028. OxfordAQA states that there will
be no exams for 9625 after January 2028. Both specifications are modular, untiered and assessed
entirely by externally marked written papers.

## How the parts fit together

The AS functional topics are the raw material for A2 strategy. A strategic question about entering a
new market draws on marketing data and segmentation; a question about growth or efficiency draws on
operations, supply chains and human resources; and almost every strategic decision needs finance,
whether through break-even analysis, sources of finance or the distinction between profit and cash.

The A2 topics themselves form a sequence: analyse the business and its environment, choose a
direction and a way of competing, decide whether an investment is worthwhile, then implement the
strategy while managing change, risk and uncertainty. Learning them in that order makes the logic of
each paper easier to follow.

A skill that runs throughout is applying theory to a specific business. Because the 9725 A2 papers
are fully case-study-based, practising analysis and evaluation in context, rather than learning
definitions in isolation, matters from the first term onwards.

## Planning your study

For 9725 students, a sensible first year works through the five AS topics, beginning with What is
business? and the external environment before moving through marketing, operations, human resources
and finance. Finance often benefits from extra practice, because break-even and profit-and-cash
questions reward accuracy.

The second year follows the A2 sequence from mission and SWOT analysis through to implementation and
change. Keep returning to AS content, since A2 case studies assume it. Read about real businesses
regularly and practise linking what you read to the topic you are studying.

Start timed practice on each paper once its content has been taught. If you are completing 9625
instead, plan around its remaining exam and resit dates above. The exam-preparation page covers how
the two specifications' papers differ and how to approach a case study.

## Frequently asked questions

**What is the difference between OxfordAQA Business 9625 and 9725?**
9725 is the revised specification, first taught in September 2026. 9625 is the legacy specification in teach-out. Both have four papers weighted 20%, 20%, 30% and 30%, but the 9725 AS papers are 75 minutes and 60 marks, while the 9625 AS papers were 90 minutes and 80 marks.

**When are the first 9725 exams?**
The first AS exams are in May/June 2027 and the first A-level exams in May/June 2028.

**When are the last 9625 exams?**
The final May/June A2 exams are in 2027. Resits are available for AS in January 2027 and for A2 in January 2028, and there will be no 9625 exams after January 2028.

**Are the 9725 A2 papers case-study-based?**
Yes. OxfordAQA describes each 9725 A2 paper as having separate topics and a simplified, fully case-study-based structure.

**Is OxfordAQA A-level Business modular?**
Yes. Both specifications are modular, with two AS papers and two A2 papers making up the full A-level.
