Condensed notes for subtopic **1.9 Estimation** of Cambridge O Level Mathematics (Syllabus D) 4024, 2025 to 2027. The [Estimation study guide](/resources/cambridge-o-level-mathematics-4024-estimation/) explains each method with worked examples; the [Estimation practice questions](/resources/cambridge-o-level-mathematics-4024-estimation-practice/) give exam-style questions with mark schemes.

## Syllabus outcomes

1. Round values to a specified degree of accuracy (decimal places and significant figures).
2. Make estimates for calculations involving numbers, quantities and measurements.
3. Round answers to a reasonable degree of accuracy in the context of a given problem.

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Decimal place (d.p.) | digit after the decimal point | 3.14: 2 d.p. |
| Significant figure (s.f.) | digits counted from the first non-zero digit | 0.0305: 3 s.f. |
| Estimate | approximate value found with simple numbers | 60 × 3 ÷ 0.2 = 900 |
| Degree of accuracy | the place value or number of figures given | nearest 10, 2 s.f. |
| Placeholder zero | zero that keeps the place value | the zeros in 31 000 |

## Rounding rule

Look at the digit **after** the rounding place. 5 or more: round up. 4 or less: round down. Keep the place value with zeros in whole numbers.

| Number | Rounded to | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 47 650 | nearest 1000 | 48 000 |
| 8 249 | nearest 100 | 8 200 |
| 365 | nearest 10 | 370 |
| 6.0495 | 2 d.p. | 6.05 |
| 3.14159 | 3 d.p. | 3.142 |
| 0.04567 | 3 d.p. | 0.046 |
| 30 970 | 3 s.f. | 31 000 |
| 0.004052 | 2 s.f. | 0.0041 |
| 7 349 | 2 s.f. | 7 300 |
| 7 349 | 1 s.f. | 7 000 |
| 0.0599 | 1 s.f. | 0.06 |

Leading zeros are not significant. Zeros between digits are.

## Method for estimating

1. Round every number to 1 significant figure.
2. For roots, round to the nearest perfect square (or cube).
3. Do the easy calculation.
4. Write down the rounded values and the result.

| Calculation | Rounded | Estimate | Exact (3 s.f.) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 58.7 × 3.12 ÷ 0.205 | 60 × 3 ÷ 0.2 | 900 | 893 |
| √82.5 ÷ 0.48 | 9 ÷ 0.5 | 18 | 18.9 |
| 19.6 × 0.51 ÷ 4.1 | 20 × 0.5 ÷ 4 | 2.5 | 2.44 |
| 4.3² + 2.7² | 4² + 3² | 25 | 25.8 |
| 8.7 × 4.2 | 9 × 4 | 36 | 36.5 |
| 80.2 × 6.1 ÷ 0.39 | 80 × 6 ÷ 0.4 | 1200 | 1250 |

## Effect of rounding direction

| Rounded item | Rounded up | Rounded down |
|---|---|---|
| number being multiplied | estimate bigger | estimate smaller |
| divisor (denominator) | estimate smaller | estimate bigger |
| number being subtracted | estimate smaller | estimate bigger |

Use this to say if an estimate is larger or smaller than the exact value, but only when all the effects point the same way.

## Check an answer

If your answer is far from the estimate, look for a misplaced decimal point or a slip in the calculation.

Example: 38.2 × 49.7 is about 40 × 50 = 2000. A claimed answer of 18 985.4 is wrong. The correct answer is 1898.54.

## Sensible rounding in context

| Context | Rule | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Money | nearest cent (2 d.p.) | $237.50 ÷ 7 = $33.93 |
| Number of vehicles needed | round up | 213 ÷ 45 = 4.73, so 5 coaches |
| How many full items | round down | 23 kg ÷ 5 kg = 4.6, so 4 full bags |
| Measurements | match the data | lengths to 1 d.p., answer to 1 d.p. |
| Final answers on Paper 2 | 3 s.f. | 893.385... = 893 |
| Angles in degrees | 1 d.p. | 47.256° = 47.3° |

Do not round in the middle of a calculation. Round only the final answer.

## Rounding and standard form

| Number | Rounding | Standard form |
|---|---|---|
| 7 805 621 000 | 2 s.f. | 7.8 × 10⁹ |
| 0.00389462 | 3 s.f. | 3.89 × 10⁻³ |
| 604 300 | 1 s.f. | 6 × 10⁵ |

Decimal places are poor for small numbers: 0.00389462 to 2 d.p. is 0.00, which hides the value.

## Exam traps

- Show the rounded values in an estimate. Without them the method mark is lost.
- Do not give the exact answer when an estimate is asked for.
- 0.205 to 1 s.f. is 0.2, not 0.3.
- 30 970 to 3 s.f. is 31 000, not 31.
- Leading zeros do not count as significant.
- Never round before the end of a multi-step calculation.
- Round up when you need to cover a whole number of items.
- Check whether the question asks for d.p. or s.f.

## Self-test

1. Round 52 846 to the nearest hundred.
2. Round 0.07351 to 2 significant figures.
3. Round 9.996 to 2 decimal places.
4. Round 0.0508 to 1 significant figure.
5. Estimate 7.9 × 31.2 ÷ 0.42.
6. Estimate √(37.6 × 1.02) using a square number.
7. A box holds 12 eggs. How many boxes are needed for 100 eggs?
8. Write 4 530 000 to 2 significant figures in standard form.
9. Work out 6.45 ÷ 0.87 to 3 s.f.
10. A calculator shows 12.34567. Give it to 4 s.f.

**Answers**

1. **52 800**.
2. **0.074**.
3. **10.00**.
4. **0.05**.
5. 8 × 30 ÷ 0.4 = **600**.
6. 37.6 × 1.02 is about 36 × 1 = 36 and √36 = **6**. (Using 40 × 1 gives 40, which is not a square, so 36 is the better choice.)
7. 100 ÷ 12 = 8.33, so **9 boxes**.
8. **4.5 × 10⁶**.
9. **7.41** (7.4137...).
10. **12.35**.
