These questions cover section 4.3, Scale drawings, of the Cambridge O Level Mathematics (Syllabus D) 4024 syllabus for 2025, 2026 and 2027. The method is explained in the [scale drawings study guide](/resources/cambridge-o-level-mathematics-4024-scale-drawings/), and the [scale drawings revision notes](/resources/cambridge-o-level-mathematics-4024-scale-drawings-revision-notes/) summarise it. Bearings are measured clockwise from north and written with three figures. Give non-exact answers to 3 significant figures, and angles to 1 decimal place, unless told otherwise.

## Section A

**1.** A map has a scale of 1 : 50 000. Two villages are 6.4 cm apart on the map. Find the real distance in kilometres. **[2]**

**2.** On a map with scale 1 : 25 000, a track is 4.5 km long. How long is it on the map, in centimetres? **[2]**

**3.** The bearing of B from A is 135°. Write down the bearing of A from B. **[1]**

**4.** The bearing of D from C is 248°. Find the bearing of C from D. **[2]**

**5.** Write down the three-figure bearing of

**(a)** due west,

**(b)** north-east. **[2]**

**6.** A scale drawing uses 1 cm to represent 8 m. A wall is 36 m long, and a drive measures 7.25 cm on the drawing. Find the length of the wall on the drawing and the real length of the drive. **[3]**

## Section B

**7.** A boat sails from A to B, a distance of 8 km on a bearing of 060°. It then sails 6 km on a bearing of 150° to C.

**(a)** Show that angle ABC = 90°.

**(b)** Find the distance AC.

**(c)** Find the bearing of C from A, to the nearest degree.

**(d)** Find the bearing of A from C, to the nearest degree. **[7]**

**8.** A map has a scale of 1 : 200 000. A lake has an area of 3.5 cm² on the map. Find the real area in square kilometres. **[3]**

**9.** Town Q is 120 km due east of town P. Town R is 90 km due north of Q. Find the distance PR and the bearing of R from P, to the nearest degree. **[4]**

**10.** A plan has a scale of 1 : 2500. A field is a rectangle that measures 12 cm by 8 cm on the plan.

**(a)** Find the real dimensions in metres.

**(b)** Find the real area in hectares. (1 hectare = 10 000 m².) **[4]**

**11.** The bearing of B from A is 040° and AB = 10 km. The bearing of C from B is 130° and BC = 10 km. Find the bearing of A from C. **[4]**

**12.** The scale of a plan is 1 : 500. A path is 15 m long in real life. Find its length on the plan in centimetres, and find the scale as a statement of the form "1 cm represents ... m". **[3]**

**13.** A map has a scale of 1 : 40 000.

**(a)** How many metres does 1 cm on the map represent?

**(b)** A coastal path measures 12.5 cm on the map. Find its real length in kilometres. **[3]**

**14.** A pupil makes a scale drawing, with 1 cm representing 10 m, to find the height of a tower. The pupil stands 40 m from its base on level ground, and the angle of elevation of the top is 35°.

**(a)** What length should the pupil draw for the horizontal line?

**(b)** The vertical line in the drawing measures 2.8 cm. Find the height of the tower.

**(c)** Work out 40 × tan 35° to 3 significant figures and comment on your answer to (b). **[4]**

## Answers

**1.** 6.4 × 50 000 = 320 000 cm [1]. That is 3.2 km, so the distance is **3.2 km** [1].

**2.** 4.5 km = 450 000 cm [1]. 450 000 ÷ 25 000 = **18 cm** [1].

**3.** 135° + 180° = **315°** [1].

**4.** 248° − 180° [1] = **068°** [1].

**5. (a)** **270°** [1]. **(b)** **045°** [1].

**6.** Wall: 36 ÷ 8 = **4.5 cm** [1]. Drive: 7.25 × 8 = **58 m** [1][1].

**7. (a)** The bearing of A from B is 060° + 180° = 240° [1]. 240° − 150° = 90° [1].

**(b)** AC² = 8² + 6² = 100 [1], so **AC = 10 km** [1].

**(c)** Angle BAC: tan = 6/8, so BAC = 36.87° [1]. Bearing of C from A = 60° + 36.87° = 96.87° ≈ **097°** [1].

**(d)** 96.87° + 180° = 276.87° ≈ **277°** [1].

*Examiner insight:* The bearing of A from C can also be found as 180° + 097° = 277°.

**8.** 1 cm represents 200 000 cm = 2 km [1], so 1 cm² represents 4 km² [1]. Area = 3.5 × 4 = **14 km²** [1].

**9.** PR² = 120² + 90² = 22 500, so **PR = 150 km** [1][1]. The angle between north at P and PR has tan = 120/90, so it is 53.13° [1]. The bearing is **053°** [1].

**10. (a)** 1 cm represents 2500 cm = 25 m, so the field is **300 m by 200 m** [1][1].

**(b)** Area = 60 000 m² [1] = **6 hectares** [1].

**11.** The bearing of A from B is 220°, and the bearing of C from B is 130°, so angle ABC = 90° [1]. The triangle is right-angled and isosceles, so angle BCA = 45° [1]. The bearing of B from C is 310° [1]. The bearing of A from C = 310° − 45° = **265°** [1].

**12.** 15 m = 1500 cm, so the length on the plan is 1500 ÷ 500 = **3 cm** [1]. 1 cm represents 500 cm = **5 m** [1][1].

**13. (a)** 40 000 cm = **400 m** [1]. **(b)** 12.5 × 400 = 5000 m [1] = **5 km** [1].

**14. (a)** 40 ÷ 10 = **4 cm** [1]. **(b)** 2.8 × 10 = **28 m** [1]. **(c)** 40 × tan 35° = **28.0 m** [1]. The drawing agrees with the calculation, which shows the drawing was accurate [1].

## Where marks are usually lost

- Measuring the angle from the wrong north line or anticlockwise.
- Writing 68° instead of 068°.
- Failing to subtract 360° after adding 180° to a bearing over 180°.
- Multiplying an area by the length scale rather than its square.
- Leaving a distance in the wrong unit.
- Using the bearing of B from A as if it were the bearing of A from B.
