Condensed recall for **sub-theme 3.3** of Theme 3 in Pearson Edexcel A Level Urdu (9UR0): emigration from Pakistan (پاکستان سے ہجرت), which is the research subject for this theme. For fuller explanation and a model paragraph, read the [Theme 3 study guide](/resources/edexcel-a-level-urdu-language-theme-3-study-guide/).

## Research rules

- Theme 3 research must be **based on Pakistan** and written in Urdu.
- Keep a file of named sources, dates and figures you have checked yourself; never quote a statistic you cannot trace.
- Prepare points for and against emigration so you can write a balanced response to any statement.

## Key ideas

| Idea | Notes |
|---|---|
| Push factors | Reasons for leaving: limited jobs, lower wages, insecurity, the wish for further study |
| Pull factors | Reasons for a destination: higher pay, family links, better services |
| Destinations | Gulf region, Europe and North America, with different patterns of work and settlement; check examples against sources |
| Remittances (ترسیلاتِ زر) | Money sent home; effect on families and the economy |
| Brain drain (ذہین افراد کا انخلا) | Loss of skilled workers; debate over return or contact abroad |
| Families | Effects on families left behind and on communities abroad |

## Terminology

- **تارکِ وطن** = emigrant (one who leaves). Do not confuse with immigration (arriving).

## Exam traps

- Research that wanders to other countries.
- Mixing up emigration and immigration in terms and in the research focus.
- One-sided answers; give arguments for and against.

## Self-test

1. Which country must Theme 3 research be based on?
2. What are remittances?
3. Name two push factors and one pull factor.
4. Give the Urdu for brain drain as used in the guide.
5. Is تارکِ وطن an emigrant or an immigrant?

**Answers:** 1. Pakistan. 2. Money that emigrants send home to their families. 3. For example, limited jobs and lower wages (push); higher pay abroad (pull). 4. ذہین افراد کا انخلا. 5. An emigrant, someone who leaves their country.
