# Experiences: fast facts

The model texts are in English and every example is only an illustration of a skill; do the language-producing tasks in the target language of your school course and ask your school teacher to check them. The brief's recommended topics are optional and its possible questions are only possible, so your teacher chooses what to teach.

[Language B course hub](/boards/ib/ib-dp/language-b/) · [printable checklist](/checklists/ib/ib-dp/language-b/). Applies to SL and HL, first assessment 2020, examined until the new syllabus is first assessed in May 2029. Companion to the reviewed [Experiences revision notes](/resources/ib-dp-language-b-experiences-revision-notes/).

## Theme facts

- Prescribed theme number 2 of 5 (identities, experiences, human ingenuity, social organization, sharing the planet).
- Guiding principle: "Explore and tell the stories of the events, experiences and journeys that shape our lives."
- Six optional recommended topics: leisure activities; holidays and travel; life stories; rites of passage; customs and traditions; migration.
- Possible questions: "How does our past shape our present and our future?" and "How and why do different cultures mark important moments in life?"

## Assessment facts that matter for this theme

| Component | Weight | Marks | SL | HL |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Paper 1 writing | 25% | 30 | 1 h 15 min, 250 to 400 words | 1 h 30 min, 450 to 600 words |
| Paper 2 listening | 25% | 25 | 45 min | 1 h |
| Paper 2 reading | 25% | 40 | 1 h | 1 h |
| Individual oral | 25% | 30 | visual image, 15 min preparation | literary extract, 20 min preparation |

Oral criteria: A language 12; B1 message (visual stimulus at SL, literary extract at HL) 6; B2 message, conversation 6; C interactive skills 6.

## Skill checklist for narrative

- Completed events against background or habit.
- Time markers: past, bridge (since then), present or future.
- Reflection: "Looking back, I realise that..."
- Reporting what others said.
- Evaluative language, not only nouns and verbs.

## Traps

- Preparing the wrong oral format for your level.
- Budgeting Paper 2 time by the 25/25 split instead of raw marks (listening 25, reading 40).

## Self-test

1. Name three of the six recommended topics. 2. What is Paper 1's SL word range? 3. How many marks is criterion A of the oral? 4. What stimulus does the HL oral use?

**Answers:** 1. Any three from the list. 2. 250 to 400. 3. 12. 4. An excerpt from one of the two literary works.
