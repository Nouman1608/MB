## Section A

**1.** Which **one** of the following is written by a job applicant rather than by the business? Select one answer.

A: Job description

B: Person specification

C: Curriculum vitae (CV)

D: Application form

**[1]**

**2.** Define the term "person specification". **[2]**

**3.** At Loxley Engineering, Priya organises the daily work of a team of six machine operators and checks the quality of their work. State Priya's job role. **[1]**

**4.** A vacancy at a sports shop attracted 90 applicants, and 15 were shortlisted. Calculate the percentage of applicants who were shortlisted. Give your answer to one decimal place. **[2]**

**5.** A recruitment agency charges a fee of 18% of the first year's salary. The job pays £24,000 a year. Calculate the agency's fee. **[2]**

**6.** Explain **one** disadvantage to a business of recruiting internally. **[3]**

**7.** Explain **one** reason why a business might ask candidates to complete an application form rather than send a CV. **[3]**

## Section B

**8.** Explain **one** difference between the responsibilities of a director and those of a senior manager. **[3]**

**9.** Kellow Software has 40 staff and wants to start selling in Europe. No one at the business has experience of selling abroad. The directors plan to appoint a new Head of International Sales.

**(a)** Outline **one** reason why Kellow should use external recruitment for this post. **[2]**

**(b)** An advert on a specialist job website costs £1,200 and is expected to attract 5 suitable applicants. Calculate the cost per suitable applicant. **[2]**

**10.** Marlow Leisure is a gym that needs a new receptionist. Its job description lists: answering the phone, signing in members, taking payments, and reporting to the front-of-house supervisor. Its person specification lists: good communication skills (essential), experience of handling cash (essential), first-aid certificate (desirable).

**(a)** Identify **one** item from the job description and **one** item from the person specification. **[2]**

**(b)** Analyse how the person specification helps Marlow Leisure to shortlist candidates. **[4]**

**11.** Pennant Bakeries is opening a new shop and needs a shop manager on £29,000 a year. It is considering two options.

Option 1: promote Tom, an existing supervisor with five years at Pennant, and advertise his supervisor post on social media for £150.

Option 2: use a recruitment agency, which charges 15% of the first year's salary, to find an experienced manager from a rival bakery chain.

Justify which **one** of these options Pennant Bakeries should choose. **[9]**

**12.** Fairway Care Homes is growing quickly and finds it hard to fill operational care worker posts. At present it advertises only in a local newspaper and asks applicants to send a CV. Many applicants do not have the care certificate the job needs, and managers spend a long time reading CVs.

Evaluate whether changing its recruitment documents and methods would help Fairway Care Homes recruit effectively. **[9]**

## Answers

**1.** **C** [1] **[1]**

*Examiner insight:* The business designs the application form even though the candidate fills it in.

**2.** A document listing the qualifications, skills, experience and qualities [1] that the ideal person for a job should have [1]. **[2]**

**3.** Supervisor or team leader [1] **[1]**

**4.** 15 ÷ 90 × 100 [1] = **16.7%** [1] **[2]**

**5.** 0.18 × £24,000 [1] = **£4,320** [1] **[2]**

**6.** An internal appointment leaves the employee's old job empty [1]. The business must then recruit again to fill that post [1], which adds time and cost, so the overall saving is smaller than it first appears [1]. **[3]**

*Other creditable points:* no new ideas or skills are brought in; colleagues who were not chosen may be demotivated.

**7.** An application form asks every candidate the same questions [1], so the business receives the same information from each person [1]. This makes it quicker and fairer to compare candidates against the person specification [1]. **[3]**

**8.** Directors are responsible for the long-term aims and direction of the whole business [1], whereas a senior manager is responsible for one function or area, such as marketing [1]. The senior manager turns the directors' decisions into plans for their own department [1]. **[3]**

**9. (a)** No one at Kellow has experience of selling abroad [1], so an outside candidate is needed to bring the knowledge of European markets that the business lacks [1].

**(b)** £1,200 ÷ 5 [1] = **£240** per suitable applicant [1] **[4]**

**10. (a)** Job description: any one of answering the phone, signing in members, taking payments, reporting to the front-of-house supervisor [1]. Person specification: any one of communication skills, cash-handling experience, first-aid certificate [1].

**(b)** Marlow can check each application against the two essential criteria [1] and remove candidates without cash-handling experience or good communication skills [1]. This reduces the number of applicants to interview, saving managers' time [1]. The desirable first-aid certificate can then be used to choose between candidates who meet both essentials, helping the gym appoint someone who can also help members in an emergency [1]. **[6]**

**11.** Option 1 costs about £150 [1], while Option 2 costs 0.15 × £29,000 = £4,350 [1], so Option 1 is £4,200 cheaper [1]. Tom knows Pennant's products and procedures, so the new shop could run smoothly from the start [1], and his promotion shows other staff that they can progress, which may motivate them [1]. However, Tom has supervised but not managed a shop [1], and Pennant must fill his supervisor post too. Option 2 brings an experienced manager who may have new ideas from a rival chain [1], although they would need time to learn Pennant's ways and may not fit in [1]. Judgement: Option 1, because it costs far less and Pennant already knows Tom's ability over five years; it is the better choice provided Tom is given training for the extra responsibility [1]. **[9]**

**12.** Changing the person specification to make the care certificate essential, and stating it in the advert, would stop many unsuitable people applying [1], so fewer applications need reading [1]. An application form with a question about the certificate would let managers check it at a glance [1] and compare candidates more quickly than reading CVs [1]. A local newspaper reaches few readers, especially younger people [1], so adding a job website or social media would widen the pool of qualified applicants [1]. These methods cost money, and a longer form may put some applicants off [1]. Changing documents and methods will not help if there are simply too few qualified carers locally [1]. Judgement: the changes would help, because the main problems are unsuitable applicants and time spent reading CVs, both of which the documents fix; but if the shortage of certificated carers is local, Fairway may also need to offer training or better pay [1]. **[9]**

*Examiner insight:* In Evaluate, link each change to a problem stated in the case, then say what the result depends on.

## Where marks are usually lost

- Saying the application form is written by the candidate.
- Swapping job description and person specification in Question 10(a).
- Writing "use external recruitment" without naming a method or linking it to a skills gap.
- In Question 11, ignoring that Option 1 creates another vacancy.
- Giving a conclusion in Evaluate with no condition on which it depends.
