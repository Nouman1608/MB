These notes condense sub-topic 4(f) Carboxylic acids (points 4.34C to 4.37C) of the Pearson Edexcel International GCSE Chemistry (4CH1) specification, Issue 3 (September 2024). Every point has a C reference, so the whole sub-topic is **Paper 2 only**. Worked examples and model answers are in the [carboxylic acids study guide](/resources/edexcel-igcse-chemistry-4ch1-carboxylic-acids/); then try the [carboxylic acids practice questions](/resources/edexcel-igcse-chemistry-4ch1-carboxylic-acids-practice/).

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Carboxylic acid | Organic compound with the -COOH group |
| Carboxyl group | C(=O)OH: a C=O and an O-H on the same carbon |
| Ethanoate | Salt of ethanoic acid |
| Vinegar | Aqueous solution containing ethanoic acid |
| Weak acid | Acid that ionises only partly in water |

## Members of the series

| Name | Formula | Structural formula | Mr |
|---|---|---|---|
| Methanoic acid | HCOOH | HCOOH | 46 |
| Ethanoic acid | CH₃COOH | CH₃COOH | 60 |
| Propanoic acid | C₂H₅COOH | CH₃CH₂COOH | 74 |
| Butanoic acid | C₃H₇COOH | CH₃CH₂CH₂COOH | 88 |

- **General formula:** CₙH₂ₙ₊₁COOH.
- **Naming:** count **all** carbons including the carboxyl carbon. CH₃COOH has 2 carbons, so ethanoic.
- **Ending:** -oic acid.
- In displayed formulae: one C=O and one O-H on the same carbon. The O-H hydrogen must be drawn.

## Reactions of aqueous carboxylic acids

| Reactant | Products | Observation |
|---|---|---|
| Metal (magnesium, zinc) | Salt (ethanoate) + hydrogen | Metal dissolves; bubbles; squeaky pop with a lit splint |
| Metal carbonate (calcium carbonate, sodium carbonate) | Salt + water + carbon dioxide | Fizzing; limewater turns milky |

```
Mg + 2CH3COOH → (CH3COO)2Mg + H2
CaCO3 + 2CH3COOH → (CH3COO)2Ca + H2O + CO2
```

- Carboxylic acids are **weak acids**, so reactions are slower than with a strong acid of the same concentration.
- The salts are named from the acid: ethanoic acid gives ethanoates; propanoic acid gives propanoates.
- The ethanoate ion, CH₃COO⁻, has mass 59.

## Vinegar

- An aqueous solution containing ethanoic acid (typically about 4 to 8% by mass).
- Made when ethanol in wine or cider is oxidised by bacteria in air.
- Uses: flavouring and preserving food; removing limescale.

## Calculation pattern

1. Moles = mass / Mr (Mg 24, Zn 65, CaCO₃ 100, Na₂CO₃ 106, CH₃COOH 60).
2. Use the equation ratio. Remember 2 mol of acid reacts with each 1 mol of metal or carbonate.
3. Gas volume at room temperature and pressure = moles x 24,000 cm³.
4. Mass of salt = moles x Mr of salt.

## Link to other sub-topics

- Ethanol oxidises to ethanoic acid (microbial oxidation or dichromate(VI)).
- Ethanoic acid + ethanol (acid catalyst) gives ethyl ethanoate (an ester).
- Acid reactions with metals and carbonates follow the same pattern as dilute mineral acids in Topic 2.

## Answer frames

- **Observation for carbonate:** "fizzing; gas turns limewater milky".
- **Observation for metal:** "bubbles of gas; metal dissolves; lit splint pops".
- **Naming:** "count all the carbons, add -oic acid".
- **Identify vinegar:** "an aqueous solution of ethanoic acid".

## Exam traps

- CH₃COOH is ethanoic acid, not methanoic acid.
- Salts are ethanoates, not "ethanates".
- Do not forget water in the carbonate equation or the factor 2 before the acid.
- The functional group is -COOH, not -COH.
- Weak acid does not mean dilute, and strong does not mean concentrated.
- The whole sub-topic is Paper 2 only.

## Self-test

1. State the functional group in carboxylic acids.
2. Name CH₃CH₂COOH.
3. Draw the structural formula of butanoic acid.
4. Write the balanced equation for magnesium reacting with ethanoic acid.
5. 0.65 g of zinc (Ar = 65) reacts with excess ethanoic acid. Calculate the volume of hydrogen formed at room temperature and pressure.
6. Calculate the mass of zinc ethanoate formed in question 5 (Mr = 183).
7. State what you would observe when sodium carbonate is added to ethanoic acid.
8. Name the products of the reaction in question 7.
9. What is vinegar?
10. Name the type of compound formed when ethanoic acid reacts with ethanol.

### Answers

1. -COOH (carboxyl).
2. Propanoic acid.
3. CH₃CH₂CH₂COOH.
4. Mg + 2CH₃COOH → (CH₃COO)₂Mg + H₂.
5. Moles of Zn = 0.65 / 65 = 0.010 mol; moles of H₂ = 0.010 mol; volume = 0.010 x 24,000 = **240 cm³**.
6. 0.010 x 183 = **1.83 g**.
7. Fizzing (effervescence); the solid dissolves.
8. Sodium ethanoate, water and carbon dioxide.
9. An aqueous solution containing ethanoic acid.
10. An ester (ethyl ethanoate).
