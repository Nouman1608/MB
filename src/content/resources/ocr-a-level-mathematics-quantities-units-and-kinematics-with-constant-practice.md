## Questions

**1.** State the SI unit, with its symbol, of each quantity.

**(a)** mass **[1]**
**(b)** acceleration **[1]**
**(c)** the moment of a force (A Level only) **[1]**

**2.** A skier covers 3.6 km in 12 minutes at a steady speed. Find the speed in m s⁻¹. **[3]**

**3.** A cyclist rides along a straight path. Measured from a fixed point O, she starts at position −3 m, reaches +10 m and then stops at +2 m. The whole ride takes 20 s. Find her displacement, the distance she travels, her average speed and her average velocity. **[4]**

**4.** A particle moves in a straight line. Its velocity-time graph is made of straight segments joining (0, 0), (4, 10), (10, 10) and (16, −2), with t in seconds and v in m s⁻¹.

**(a)** Find the acceleration in the first stage and in the last stage. **[2]**
**(b)** Find the displacement from the start at t = 16. **[2]**
**(c)** Find the total distance travelled. **[2]**

**5.** Starting from v = u + at and s = ½(u + v)t, show that v² = u² + 2as. **[3]**

**6.** A car travelling at 24 m s⁻¹ brakes with constant deceleration and stops after 72 m. Find the deceleration and the time the car takes to stop. **[3]**

**7.** A particle moves in a straight line with constant acceleration. It passes O with velocity 5 m s⁻¹. Four seconds later it is 36 m from O, further along the line. Find the acceleration and the velocity at that time. **[4]**

**8.** A particle moves on a straight line with constant acceleration −1.5 m s⁻². At t = 0 it is at O with velocity 9 m s⁻¹ in the positive direction.

**(a)** Find the time at which it is instantaneously at rest and its displacement from O then. **[2]**
**(b)** Find the times at which it is 24 m from O in the positive direction. **[3]**
**(c)** Find the distance it has travelled by the time it returns to O. **[2]**

**9.** Two skaters move along the same straight line. At time t = 0, skater A is 16 m ahead of skater B. A moves at a constant 4 m s⁻¹. B starts from rest and moves with constant acceleration 1.5 m s⁻².

**(a)** Write expressions for the distance each skater has moved from B's starting point after t seconds. **[2]**
**(b)** Find the time at which B catches A. **[2]**
**(c)** Find B's speed and the distance B has travelled at that time. **[2]**

**10.** (A Level only) A particle P has position vector (−2, 5) m at t = 0 and velocity (4, −1) m s⁻¹. It moves with constant acceleration (−0.5, 0.5) m s⁻².

**(a)** Find the velocity and position vector of P at t = 6. **[4]**
**(b)** Find the time at which P moves parallel to the i direction and its position vector then. **[3]**
**(c)** Find the speed of P at t = 4. **[1]**

**11.** A tram starts from rest at stop A, accelerates uniformly for 20 s to speed V m s⁻¹, travels at constant speed, then decelerates uniformly to rest at stop B in 10 s. The whole journey takes 100 s and the distance AB is 1190 m.

**(a)** Find the time spent at constant speed. **[1]**
**(b)** Find V. **[3]**
**(c)** Find the acceleration in the first stage and in the last stage. **[2]**
**(d)** Find the distance travelled at constant speed. **[1]**

## Answers

**1. (a)** kilogram, **kg** [1]
**(b)** metres per second squared, **m s⁻²** [1]
**(c)** newton metre, **N m** [1]
*Examiner insight:* Give the correct symbol or name in full; "N/m" or "m/s" for acceleration describes a different quantity.

**2.** 3.6 km = 3600 m and 12 minutes = 720 s [1]
speed = 3600 ÷ 720 [1]
**5 m s⁻¹** [1]
*Examiner insight:* Show both conversions separately. A correct final answer with no visible conversion is a risk if one conversion slips.

**3.** Displacement = 2 − (−3) = **5 m** [1]
Distance = 13 + 8 = **21 m** [1]
Average speed = 21 ÷ 20 = **1.05 m s⁻¹** [1]
Average velocity = 5 ÷ 20 = **0.25 m s⁻¹** in the positive direction [1]
*Examiner insight:* Be sure of which quantity each word asks for. Using displacement in the average speed is an easy slip to make.

**4. (a)** First stage a = 10 ÷ 4 = **2.5 m s⁻²** [1]
Last stage a = (−2 − 10) ÷ 6 = **−2 m s⁻²** [1]
**(b)** The velocity is zero when 10 − 2(t − 10) = 0, so t = 15 [1]
Areas: 20, 60, 25 and −1 give displacement **104 m** [1]
**(c)** The particle changes direction at t = 15, so the last area counts as distance [1]
Distance = 20 + 60 + 25 + 1 = **106 m** [1]
*Examiner insight:* Find where the line crosses the axis before taking areas. Adding a negative area into the distance is a common slip.

**5.** From v = u + at, t = (v − u)/a [1]
Substitute: s = ½(u + v)(v − u)/a [1]
2as = v² − u², so **v² = u² + 2as** [1]
*Examiner insight:* A "show that" needs every line of the algebra. Stating the final result alone does not show the reasoning.

**6.** Take the positive direction as the direction of motion: u = 24, v = 0, s = 72 [1]
0 = 576 + 2a × 72, so a = −4 [1]
Deceleration **4 m s⁻²**, and t = 24 ÷ 4 = **6 s** [1]
*Examiner insight:* Say whether the answer is a deceleration or an acceleration. A bare "−4" or "4" invites doubt about the direction.

**7.** u = 5, t = 4, s = 36 [1]
36 = 20 + ½ × a × 16 [1]
**a = 2 m s⁻²** [1]
v = 5 + 2 × 4 = **13 m s⁻¹** [1]
*Examiner insight:* Write down the list of known values first. It shows the formula choice, and a later arithmetic slip is easier to follow.

**8. (a)** v = 0: 0 = 9 − 1.5t, so **t = 6** [1]
s = 9 × 6 − 0.75 × 36 = **27 m** [1]
**(b)** 9t − 0.75t² = 24 [1]
t² − 12t + 32 = 0 [1]
**t = 4 and t = 8** [1]
**(c)** It returns to O when 9t − 0.75t² = 0, so t = 12 [1]
Distance = 27 + 27 = **54 m** [1]
*Examiner insight:* Quote both roots of the quadratic in part (b) and say what each means. For part (c), the distance is not the displacement, which is zero.

**9. (a)** Take B's starting point as origin. A: 16 + 4t. B: ½ × 1.5 × t² = 0.75t² **[1] [1]**
**(b)** 0.75t² = 16 + 4t, so 3t² − 16t − 64 = 0 [1]
(3t + 8)(t − 8) = 0, so **t = 8 s** (the negative root is rejected) [1]
**(c)** v = 1.5 × 8 = **12 m s⁻¹** [1]
s = 0.75 × 64 = **48 m** [1]
*Examiner insight:* Reject the negative root with a reason. Check the position of A as well, which is 16 + 32 = 48 m, to confirm the answer.

**10. (a)** v = (4, −1) + 6(−0.5, 0.5) = **(1, 2) m s⁻¹** **[1] [1]**
r = (−2, 5) + 6(4, −1) + 18(−0.5, 0.5) [1]
= **(13, 8) m** [1]
**(b)** Parallel to i means the j-component of velocity is zero: −1 + 0.5t = 0, so **t = 2** [1]
r = (−2, 5) + 2(4, −1) + 2(−0.5, 0.5) [1]
= **(5, 4) m** [1]
**(c)** At t = 4, v = (2, 1), so speed = **√5 ≈ 2.24 m s⁻¹** [1]
*Examiner insight:* The position vector includes the starting position. Giving only the displacement from the starting point answers a different question.

**11. (a)** 100 − 20 − 10 = **70 s** [1]
**(b)** Area of the trapezium: ½ × V × (100 + 70) = 1190 **[1] [1]**
**V = 14** [1]
**(c)** First stage 14 ÷ 20 = **0.7 m s⁻²** [1]
Last stage −14 ÷ 10 = **−1.4 m s⁻²** [1]
**(d)** 14 × 70 = **980 m** [1]
*Examiner insight:* Sketch the velocity-time graph and label the three durations. The parallel sides of the trapezium are 100 and 70, which is easy to mix up.

## Where marks are usually lost

- Not converting km, minutes or grams to SI before substituting.
- Using displacement where a question wants distance, and the reverse.
- Taking the area below the time axis as positive when finding displacement.
- Using the constant acceleration formulae for motion that has stages with different accelerations, instead of treating each stage separately.
- Losing the sign of a deceleration when moving in the positive direction.
- Giving only one root of a quadratic in t and ignoring the second valid time.
- Leaving out units, or giving a speed as a vector.
- Forgetting the starting position when asked for a position vector.
- Skipping lines in a "show that" derivation.
