## Section B

**6.** The table shows the energy content of some foods.

| Food | Energy per 100 g (kJ) |
|---|---|
| Rice | 1500 |
| Cheese | 1700 |
| Banana | 400 |
| Cooking oil | 3700 |

**(a)** Calculate the energy in 200 g of rice. **[1]**

**(b)** Calculate the energy in a meal of 150 g rice, 40 g cheese and 10 g cooking oil. Show your working. **[3]**

**(c)** Explain why the oil provides more energy per gram than the rice. **[1]**

**(d)** State the main nutrient in rice. **[1]**

**7.** A child living in a city with little sunshine is found to have bent legs and weak bones.

**(a)** Name the disease. **[1]**

**(b)** State **two** nutrients whose lack could cause this disease. **[2]**

**(c)** Explain how one of these nutrients helps to prevent the disease. **[2]**

**(d)** Suggest **two** foods that would help to prevent the disease. **[2]**

**8.** A woman complains of tiredness and looks pale. A doctor finds that she has anaemia.

**(a)** Name the mineral salt that is probably lacking from her diet. **[1]**

**(b)** Explain why a lack of this mineral causes tiredness. **[3]**

**(c)** State **two** foods that are good sources of this mineral. **[2]**

**9.** Explain what is meant by a balanced diet and explain why a pregnant woman needs a different diet from a man of the same age who has a desk job. **[4]**

**10.** A sailor on a long voyage with no fresh food develops bleeding gums and slow healing of cuts.

**(a)** Name the deficiency disease. **[1]**

**(b)** Identify the vitamin that was lacking. **[1]**

**(c)** Suggest how the disease could be prevented. **[2]**

**11.** A table shows daily energy needs: a teenage boy 12 000 kJ and an office worker 10 000 kJ.

**(a)** Calculate the percentage more energy the boy needs than the office worker. **[2]**

**(b)** Suggest **two** reasons why the boy needs more energy. **[2]**

**12.** A student says, "Fibre is not digested, so it is a waste of food." Explain why this statement is incorrect. **[2]**

## Answers

**1.** B [1]. **[1]**

**2.** C [1]. **[1]**

**3.** Any two of: meat; fish; eggs; milk; cheese; beans; nuts [1 each]. **[2]**

**4.** Fibre adds bulk to the food [1], so that the muscles in the gut wall can push it along (by peristalsis), which prevents constipation [1]. **[2]**

**5.** Any two of: energy source or store [1]; insulation under the skin to reduce heat loss [1]; part of cell membranes [1]. **[2]**

**6.**

**(a)** 2 x 1500 = 3000 kJ [1].

**(b)** Rice: 1.5 x 1500 = 2250 kJ, and cheese: 0.4 x 1700 = 680 kJ [1]. Oil: 0.1 x 3700 = 370 kJ [1]. Total = 2250 + 680 + 370 = 3300 kJ [1].

**(c)** Oil is a lipid, which stores more energy per gram than carbohydrate [1].

**(d)** Carbohydrate [1]. **[6]**

**7.**

**(a)** Rickets [1].

**(b)** Vitamin D [1]; calcium [1].

**(c)** Vitamin D helps the intestine to absorb calcium [1], so more calcium reaches the bones, which makes them hard [1]. (Accept: calcium is needed to make bones and teeth hard.)

**(d)** Any two of: milk; cheese; oily fish; eggs; green vegetables [1 each]. **[7]**

**8.**

**(a)** Iron [1].

**(b)** Iron is needed to make haemoglobin [1]. With less haemoglobin, the red blood cells carry less oxygen [1], so less aerobic respiration takes place and less energy is released, which makes the woman tired [1].

**(c)** Any two of: red meat; liver; spinach; beans [1 each]. **[6]**

**9.** A balanced diet contains all the nutrients in the correct proportions [1] and the right amount of energy for the person [1]. A pregnant woman needs more protein, calcium and iron, because the baby is growing and blood is needed [1]. The man with a desk job uses less energy and needs fewer of these nutrients, so his diet is different [1]. **[4]**

**10.**

**(a)** Scurvy [1].

**(b)** Vitamin C [1].

**(c)** Eat fresh fruit and vegetables, such as citrus fruits, which contain vitamin C [1], so that the sailor gets a regular supply [1]. **[4]**

**11.**

**(a)** Difference = 12 000 - 10 000 = 2 000 kJ [1]. Percentage = 2 000 divided by 10 000 x 100 = 20 per cent [1].

**(b)** Any two of: he is growing [1]; he is probably more active, so his muscles use more energy [1]; a larger body uses more energy [1]. **[4]**

**12.** Fibre adds bulk to the food in the gut [1], so that the muscles of the gut wall can push the food along by peristalsis, which helps to prevent constipation [1]. **[2]**

## Where marks are usually lost

- Mixing up scurvy, rickets and anaemia and their nutrients.
- Saying fibre is digested or gives energy.
- Giving "for bones" without saying that vitamin D helps calcium absorption.
- Not showing the working in an energy calculation.
- Describing a balanced diet as one that contains "lots of vegetables" only.
