**These notes condense Depth Study C of Cambridge O Level History 2147, Russia 1905-41.** They follow the four key questions -- the fall of the Tsar, the Bolsheviks in power, Stalin's control, and Stalin's economic policies -- in a format built for Paper 1 Section B (one three-part question: 4 + 6 + 10 marks). See the [Depth Study C study guide](/resources/o-level-world-history-depth-study-russia-1905-41/) for full explanations and [Depth Study C practice questions](/resources/o-level-world-history-depth-study-russia-1905-41-practice/) to test yourself.

## Syllabus coverage

**CAMBRIDGE O LEVEL HISTORY (2147), 2024-2026 -- DEPTH STUDY C**

1. Why did the Tsarist regime collapse in 1917?
2. How did the Bolsheviks gain power, and how did they consolidate their rule?
3. How did Stalin gain and hold on to power?
4. What was the impact of Stalin's economic policies?

## Key question 1: why the Tsar fell -- long and short term

| Long-term | Short-term (war) |
|---|---|
| Autocracy unwilling to share power | Defeats, casualties, poor supplies |
| Poverty of peasants and workers | Nicholas as commander-in-chief (1915) |
| Revolutionary parties growing | Tsarina and Rasputin discredit the court |
| Duma ignored after 1905 | Food and fuel shortages, inflation |
| | Army mutiny in Petrograd, March 1917 |

**Why he survived 1905 but not 1917:** in 1905 the army stayed loyal and the opposition was divided; in 1917 the army joined the revolution and almost no one defended the Tsar.

## Key question 2: Bolsheviks

**Why they won in November 1917:** Provisional Government's failures; Kornilov; Lenin's slogans; Trotsky's MRC.

**Reds vs Whites:**

| Reds | Whites |
|---|---|
| Central area, railways, factories | Scattered round the edges |
| One leadership; Trotsky's Red Army | Rival generals, different aims |
| War Communism fed the army | Linked to landlords and the Tsar |
| Propaganda: defending Russia from foreigners | Foreign help seen as interference |

**NEP:** a success in recovery (grain, small trade) but a political retreat; Kronstadt showed why Lenin had to change course.

## Key question 3: Stalin

**Stalin's advantages over Trotsky:** General Secretary (appointments); Lenin's funeral; Testament suppressed; alliances (with Zinoviev and Kamenev, then Bukharin); "Socialism in One Country" more popular than "Permanent Revolution".

**Control methods:** Purges and show trials; NKVD and Gulag; cult of personality; censorship and rewritten history; socialist realism.

## Key question 4: Five-Year Plans and collectivisation

| | Successes | Failures and costs |
|---|---|---|
| Five-Year Plans (from 1928) | Huge rises in coal, steel, oil, electricity; new cities such as Magnitogorsk; stronger defence | Poor quality; few consumer goods; harsh discipline; false figures |
| Collectivisation (from 1929) | Grain for cities and export; state control of countryside; labour freed for industry | Kulaks deported; livestock slaughtered; famine 1932-33 killing millions |

**Groups:** workers (discipline, some rewards); peasants (lost land, famine); women (jobs plus housework); minorities (Russification, deportations).

## Worked example: a (b) answer frame

*Explain why the Bolsheviks won the Civil War.* **[6]**

Reason -> evidence -> link, three times: (1) **geography** -- the Reds held Moscow and Petrograd and the railways, *so* they could move troops quickly; (2) **leadership** -- Trotsky's Red Army used discipline and former Tsarist officers, *so* it fought effectively; (3) **White weaknesses** -- divided generals far apart, *so* they never co-ordinated attacks.

## Exam traps

- Using "February" and "October" without knowing that 2147 says March and November.
- Thinking Lenin led the March Revolution -- he was in Switzerland.
- Saying the Purges brought Stalin to power; they came after he had won.
- Giving only successes or only costs in a "how successful" question.

## Frequently asked questions

**What was the October Manifesto?**
Nicholas II's 1905 promise of a Duma and civil rights, which split the opposition.

**Why was Kronstadt important?**
Loyal Bolshevik sailors rebelled in 1921, showing Lenin that War Communism had to end.

**Who were the kulaks?**
Better-off peasants whom Stalin blamed for grain shortages and removed during collectivisation.

## Self-test

1. Which agreement ended Russia's war with Germany? *Brest-Litovsk, March 1918.*
2. Who commanded the Red Army? *Trotsky.*
3. When did the NEP begin? *1921.*
4. Whose murder in 1934 began the Great Terror? *Kirov's.*
5. When did the first Five-Year Plan begin? *1928.*
