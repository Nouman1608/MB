**These notes condense Truman, Eisenhower and post-war USA, 1945-61, the first topic of the Paper 4 American option in Cambridge 9489.** For explanation see the [1945-61 study guide](/resources/cambridge-a-level-world-history-9489-truman-eisenhower-postwar-usa/); for essays and short questions see the [1945-61 practice questions](/resources/cambridge-a-level-world-history-9489-truman-eisenhower-postwar-usa-practice/).

## Syllabus coverage

- 4.1 How well did Truman deal with the internal issues facing post-war US?
- 4.2 How effective was Eisenhower as President?
- 4.3 How far were the years 1945-61 ones of economic and social change?
- 4.4 How different were the foreign policies of Truman and Eisenhower?

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Fair Deal | Truman's programme (named 1949) extending the New Deal |
| Taft-Hartley Act | 1947 law restricting unions, passed over Truman's veto |
| Loyalty Program | Truman's 1947 screening of federal employees for disloyalty |
| HUAC | House Un-American Activities Committee |
| McCarthyism | Reckless accusations of communist sympathy without evidence |
| Dixiecrats | Southern Democrats who backed Strom Thurmond in 1948 |
| Modern Republicanism | Eisenhower's acceptance of the New Deal with fiscal restraint |
| Military-industrial complex | Eisenhower's 1961 warning about defence industries and the military gaining influence |
| Containment | Policy of stopping the spread of communism |
| New Look | Eisenhower's defence policy relying on nuclear deterrence |
| Massive retaliation | Threat of nuclear response to communist aggression (Dulles, 1954) |
| Domino theory | Belief that one country's fall to communism would lead its neighbours to fall |
| Baby boom | High post-war birth rate, c.1946-64 |
| Suburbanisation | Movement of families to new suburbs such as Levittown |

## 4.1 Truman at home -- verdict grid

| Issue | Achieved | Not achieved |
|---|---|---|
| Economy | Employment Act; reconversion without depression | Inflation 1946; Taft-Hartley |
| Welfare | Housing Act 1949; minimum wage 75 cents; Social Security extended 1950 | Health insurance; aid to education; Brannan Plan |
| Red Scare | Loyalty Program; vetoed McCarran Act (overridden) | McCarthy's rise damaged his administration |
| Civil rights | EO 9981; *To Secure These Rights*; NAACP speech | No legislation through Congress |

**Why he won in 1948:** whistle-stop campaign, "do-nothing Congress", New Deal coalition held (farmers, labour, Black voters in the north), anti-communist record, Dewey's complacency.

## 4.2 Eisenhower -- effective or passive?

- **Effective:** two landslide wins; HEW; Social Security extended; interstate highways; balanced budgets; ended the Korean War; troops to Little Rock; 1957 Civil Rights Act.
- **Passive:** slow to confront McCarthy publicly; lukewarm on *Brown*; the 1957 Act was weak; recession in 1957-58.

## 4.3 Society -- four themes

1. **Consumerism:** cars, TVs, appliances; credit and debt; suburbs and inner-city decline.
2. **Women:** domestic ideal, yet more married women working and studying.
3. **Youth:** teenage market, TV and film, rock and roll.
4. **Poverty:** rural South and Southwest, inner cities, unskilled workers, women and ethnic minorities.

## 4.4 Foreign policy -- continuity and change

| | Truman | Eisenhower |
|---|---|---|
| Aim | Containment | Containment |
| Main method | Aid, alliances, conventional rearmament (NSC-68) | Nuclear deterrence, covert action, summitry |
| Asia | Korean War; crossing the 38th parallel | Armistice; domino theory; backing Diem |
| Europe | Marshall Aid, Berlin airlift, NATO | Berlin ultimatum 1958-59; no action over Hungary |
| Middle East / Latin America | Truman Doctrine (Greece, Turkey) | Iran 1953; Suez 1956; Guatemala 1954; Cuba |

## Exam traps

- **Brown v. Board** was a court decision; Eisenhower's role was enforcement at Little Rock.
- **McCarthy** was a senator, not a member of HUAC.
- **1948:** Truman beat a three-way split in his own party, not just Dewey.
- **Prosperity:** always set growth against persistent poverty.
- **Difference questions:** compare aims and methods separately.

## Self-test

1. What did the Employment Act of 1946 create? *The Council of Economic Advisers.*
2. Which 1947 law restricted unions? *The Taft-Hartley Act.*
3. Who led the Dixiecrats in 1948? *Strom Thurmond.*
4. What did Executive Order 9981 do? *Ordered equal treatment and opportunity in the armed forces.*
5. Which spy case led to a perjury conviction in 1950? *Alger Hiss.*
6. What brought McCarthy down in 1954? *The televised Army-McCarthy hearings, Murrow's criticism and Senate censure.*
7. Name two Eisenhower slogans or promises of 1952. *"I like Ike"; "I shall go to Korea".*
8. Which department did Eisenhower create in 1953? *Health, Education and Welfare.*
9. Where did Eisenhower send federal troops in 1957? *Little Rock, Arkansas.*
10. Which organisation grew out of the 1960 sit-ins? *SNCC.*
11. Where did the CIA back coups in 1953 and 1954? *Iran and Guatemala.*
12. What ended the 1960 Paris summit? *The shooting down of a U-2 spy plane over the USSR.*
