These notes condense subtopic **2.8 Proportion** of the Cambridge IGCSE Mathematics 0580 syllabus (examination in 2025, 2026 and 2027). It is **Extended only** (E2.8); the syllabus has no Core content at C2.8. The statement is to express direct and inverse proportion in algebraic terms and use this form to find unknown quantities. The syllabus notes say it includes linear, square, square root, cube and cube root proportion, and that knowledge of the symbol ∝ is required.

For full worked examples, read the [proportion study guide](/resources/igcse-mathematics-0580-proportion/); for marked exam-style questions, use the [proportion practice questions](/resources/cambridge-igcse-mathematics-0580-proportion-cambridge-igcse-maths-practice/).

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ∝ | "is proportional to"; not an equals sign |
| Directly proportional | As one quantity increases, the other increases in a fixed way; y = k × (something) |
| Inversely proportional | As one increases, the other decreases; y = k ÷ (something) |
| Constant of proportionality, k | The fixed number that turns ∝ into = |
| "Varies as" | Means the same as "is proportional to" |

## The ten forms

| Direct | Equation | Inverse | Equation |
|---|---|---|---|
| y ∝ x | y = kx | y ∝ 1/x | y = k/x |
| y ∝ x² | y = kx² | y ∝ 1/x² | y = k/x² |
| y ∝ √x | y = k√x | y ∝ 1/√x | y = k/√x |
| y ∝ x³ | y = kx³ | y ∝ 1/x³ | y = k/x³ |
| y ∝ ∛x | y = k∛x | y ∝ 1/∛x | y = k/∛x |

The quantity can be a bracket: "y is proportional to the square of (x + 1)" means y = k(x + 1)².

## The method

```
1. Write the proportion with ∝, then as an equation with k.
2. Substitute the given pair of values.
3. Solve for k and write the full formula with k replaced.
4. Substitute the new value; if finding the inside variable,
   undo operations in reverse (multiply out the denominator,
   divide by k, then square, cube or take the root).
```

Quick example: y ∝ 1/x², y = 5 when x = 4. Then 5 = k/16, so k = 80 and y = 80/x². When x = 2, y = 80/4 = 20. When y = 0.8, x² = 80/0.8 = 100, so x = 10.

## Effect of a change (no k needed)

Multiply x by a factor m, then apply the same power to m.

| Relationship | x multiplied by m | y multiplied by |
|---|---|---|
| y = kx² | m | m² |
| y = kx³ | m | m³ |
| y = k√x | m | √m |
| y = k/x | m | 1/m |
| y = k/x² | m | 1/m² |

Percentage changes use the multiplier: a 20% increase in x is m = 1.2. If y ∝ x², y is multiplied by 1.2² = 1.44, a **44%** increase (not 40%).

## Recognising the form from a table

- If y rises as x rises, test y ÷ x, y ÷ x², y ÷ x³, y ÷ √x: the form whose result is constant is the one.
- If y falls as x rises, test y × x, y × x², y × √x, …: the constant product gives k.

Example: x = 1, 2, 3 and y = 36, 9, 4. y × x = 36, 18, 12 (not constant); y × x² = 36, 36, 36. So **y = 36/x²**.

## Exam traps

- **Leaving ∝ in the working.** You cannot substitute into y ∝ x². Write y = kx² first.
- **Giving k instead of the formula.** "Find y in terms of x" needs y = 3x², not k = 3.
- **Inverse forms upside down.** "Inversely proportional to the square of d" is k/d², not kd² and not k/√d.
- **The last step for an inside variable.** From √q = 7, q = 49; from x² = 100, x = 10 (positive if x is a length or the question says so).
- **Squaring part of a bracket.** For y = k(x + 2)², work out (x + 2) first, then square it.
- **Percentage traps.** A 10% rise in x with y ∝ x² is a 21% rise in y, because 1.1² = 1.21.
- **Rounding k early** on Paper 4. Keep the exact value (or a fraction) until the final answer.

## Self-test

1. Write "a is inversely proportional to the square of b" using ∝ and as an equation.
2. y is directly proportional to x, and y = 18 when x = 4. Find y when x = 10.
3. y is proportional to x², and y = 12 when x = 2. Find y when x = 5.
4. y is proportional to √x, and y = 10 when x = 25. Find x when y = 14.
5. y is inversely proportional to x, and y = 8 when x = 3. Find y when x = 12.
6. y is inversely proportional to x², and y = 5 when x = 4. Find x when y = 0.8, given x > 0.
7. y is proportional to x³, and y = 40 when x = 2. Find y when x = 3.
8. y is inversely proportional to ∛x, and y = 3 when x = 64. Find y when x = 8.
9. y is proportional to x³. What happens to y when x is halved?
10. y is inversely proportional to √x. What happens to y when x is multiplied by 9?
11. y is proportional to x². x increases by 20%. Find the percentage increase in y.
12. For the values x = 1, 2, 3 and y = 36, 9, 4, find y in terms of x.

### Answers

1. **a ∝ 1/b²**, **a = k/b²**.
2. k = 18/4 = 4.5; y = 4.5 × 10 = **45**.
3. 12 = 4k, k = 3; y = 3 × 25 = **75**.
4. 10 = 5k, k = 2; 14 = 2√x, √x = 7, **x = 49**.
5. k = 24; y = 24/12 = **2**.
6. k = 80; x² = 80/0.8 = 100, **x = 10**.
7. 40 = 8k, k = 5; y = 5 × 27 = **135**.
8. 3 = k/4, k = 12; y = 12/∛8 = 12/2 = **6**.
9. y is multiplied by (½)³, so **y is divided by 8**.
10. y is multiplied by 1/√9, so **y is divided by 3**.
11. 1.2² = 1.44, a **44%** increase.
12. y × x² = 36 each time, so **y = 36/x²**.
