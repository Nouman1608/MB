These notes condense section 2.01, Fractions, of OCR GCSE (9-1) Mathematics J560, Version 2.1 (August 2026): 2.01a equivalent fractions, 2.01b calculations with fractions, and 2.01c fractions of a quantity. All of it is content for **both tiers**, including the second-column statements on more complex calculations with improper fractions and on fractions greater than 1.

Explanations and worked examples are in the [2.01 study guide](/resources/ocr-gcse-mathematics-j560-fractions/); exam-style questions are in the [2.01 practice questions](/resources/ocr-gcse-mathematics-j560-fractions-practice/). For fractions together with decimals, see the [fractions and decimal fractions revision notes](/resources/ocr-gcse-mathematics-fractions-and-decimal-fractions-revision-notes/).

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Numerator | Top number: how many parts | 3 in 3/8 |
| Denominator | Bottom number: how many equal parts in the whole | 8 in 3/8 |
| Proper fraction | Numerator smaller than denominator | 5/9 |
| Improper fraction | Numerator larger than (or equal to) denominator | 29/8 |
| Mixed number | Whole number and a proper fraction | 3 5/8 |
| Equivalent fractions | Different forms, same value | 3/7 = 36/84 |
| Simplest form | Numerator and denominator have no common factor except 1 | 3/7 |
| Common denominator | A shared denominator, ideally the LCM of the denominators | 24 for sixths and eighths |
| Reciprocal | The fraction turned upside down | Reciprocal of 3/8 is 8/3 |

## Conversions

```
mixed → improper:  whole × denominator + numerator, over the denominator
                   3 5/8 = (3 × 8 + 5)/8 = 29/8
improper → mixed:  divide; the remainder is the new numerator
                   47/6: 47 ÷ 6 = 7 r 5, so 7 5/6
simplify:          divide top and bottom by the HCF
                   36/84: HCF 12, so 3/7
equivalence test:  a/b = c/d when a × d = b × c
```

## The four operations

| Operation | Method | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Add / subtract | Common denominator, then add or subtract numerators | 5/6 − 3/8 = 20/24 − 9/24 = 11/24 |
| Multiply | Cancel, then multiply tops and bottoms | 4/9 × 27/8 = 1/2 × 3/1 = 3/2 |
| Divide | Multiply by the reciprocal of the divisor | −9/4 ÷ 3/8 = −9/4 × 8/3 = −6 |
| Mixed numbers | Convert to improper fractions first | 1 3/5 + 2 2/3 = 8/5 + 8/3 |
| Negative fractions | Same sign rules as integers | (−2/3) × (−3/4) = 1/2 |

**Order of operations still applies:** in 3/4 − 1/3 × 3/8, multiply first: 3/4 − 1/8 = 5/8.

## Fractions of a quantity

```
a/b of Q = Q ÷ b × a
"of what is left": find what is left first, then take the fraction
fraction > 1: same method; the answer is bigger than Q
whole from a part: if a/b of N = P, then N = P ÷ a/b = P × b/a
```

## One quantity as a fraction of another

```
1. Put both quantities in the same units.
2. Part over whole ("of the whole").
3. Simplify.
```

Reminder: 35 cm of 2.1 m is 35/210 = 1/6.

## Multi-step reminders

**Successive fractions.** A tank holds 600 litres. 1/4 is used on Monday and 2/3 of what remains on Tuesday.

```
Monday:  600 ÷ 4 = 150 used, 450 left
Tuesday: 450 ÷ 3 × 2 = 300 used, 150 left
fraction left: 150/600 = 1/4
```

Quick check by multiplying the fractions kept: 3/4 × 1/3 = 1/4.

**Mixed operations.** In 1 1/2 + 2/3 × 3 3/4, convert, then multiply first: 2/3 × 15/4 = 5/2, and 3/2 + 5/2 = 4.

**Which operation?**

| The question asks | Calculation |
|---|---|
| How many pieces of 3/8 m fit in 6 m? | 6 ÷ 3/8 = 16 |
| What is 3/8 of 6 m? | 6 × 3/8 = 2 1/4 m |
| 3/8 of a length is 6 m; how long is it? | 6 ÷ 3/8 = 16 m |
| What fraction of 16 m is 6 m? | 6/16 = 3/8 |

## Exam traps

- **Adding the denominators:** 1/2 + 1/3 is not 2/5; with a common denominator it is 5/6.
- **Multiplying mixed numbers part by part:** convert first.
- **Inverting the wrong fraction** in a division.
- **Dropping a negative sign** in a mixed-number subtraction such as 1 1/4 − 3 2/3.
- **Order of operations ignored** in a mixed calculation.
- **"Of what is left"** applied to the original amount.
- **Units not matched** in "as a fraction of".
- **Not simplified** when the question asks for the simplest form.
- **Improper answer** left when a mixed number is asked for (or the other way round).

## Checks

- A product of a number and a proper fraction is smaller than the number; with a fraction above 1 it is larger.
- Dividing by a proper fraction gives a larger answer.
- Rough decimal check: 1 3/5 + 2 2/3 ≈ 1.6 + 2.7 = 4.3, and 4 4/15 ≈ 4.27.

## Quick self-test

1. Simplify 36/84 fully.
2. Write 3 5/8 as an improper fraction.
3. Write 47/6 as a mixed number.
4. Work out 5/6 − 3/8.
5. Work out 1 3/5 + 2 2/3. Give your answer as a mixed number.
6. Work out 1 1/4 − 3 2/3.
7. Work out 4/9 × 3 3/8.
8. Work out −2 1/4 ÷ 3/8.
9. Work out 3/4 − 1/3 × 3/8.
10. Find 7/12 of £8.40.
11. Find 9/5 of 65 kg.
12. Write 35 cm as a fraction of 2.1 m, in its simplest form.

### Answers

1. **3/7** (divide by 12).
2. **29/8**.
3. **7 5/6**.
4. 20/24 − 9/24 = **11/24**.
5. 24/15 + 40/15 = 64/15 = **4 4/15**.
6. 15/12 − 44/12 = −29/12 = **−2 5/12**.
7. 4/9 × 27/8 = **3/2** = 1 1/2.
8. −9/4 × 8/3 = **−6**.
9. 3/4 − 1/8 = **5/8**.
10. £8.40 ÷ 12 = £0.70, × 7 = **£4.90**.
11. 65 ÷ 5 = 13, × 9 = **117 kg**.
12. 2.1 m = 210 cm, so 35/210 = **1/6**.
