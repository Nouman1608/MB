These questions cover sub-topic **3.3 Bank reconciliation**. Cambridge 0452 papers are not divided into lettered sections, so Sections A and B below simply group shorter and longer questions. The number in square brackets after the last part of each question is the total for the whole question. Businesses and figures are invented; amounts are in dollars.

Revise first with the [3.3 study guide](/resources/cambridge-igcse-accounting-0452-bank-reconciliation/) and the [3.3 revision notes](/resources/cambridge-igcse-accounting-0452-bank-reconciliation-revision-notes/).

## Section A

**1.** Define the term unpresented cheque. **[1]**

**2.** Which item requires the cash book to be updated?

A: an unpresented cheque

B: an uncredited deposit

C: a standing order

D: a bank error **[1]**

**3.** A bank statement shows a closing balance of "1,200 OD". What does this mean?

A: the business has 1,200 in its account

B: the business owes the bank 1,200

C: 1,200 has been paid in but not credited

D: cheques of 1,200 have not been presented **[1]**

**4.** State **two** purposes of preparing a bank reconciliation statement. **[2]**

**5.** Explain the difference between a standing order and a direct debit. **[2]**

**6.** The updated cash book shows a debit balance of 4,260. Unpresented cheques total 1,140 and there is an uncredited deposit of 720. Calculate the balance on the bank statement. **[2]**

## Section B

**7.** On 31 August 2027 the bank column of Bayview Bakery's cash book showed a debit balance of 2,315. The bank statement showed these items not in the cash book: bank charges 38; interest received 12; a direct debit for electricity 214; a standing order for a loan repayment 300; a credit transfer from a customer, Hilltop Hotel, 640. It was also found that a receipt of 485 from a customer had been entered in the cash book as 458.

**(a)** Prepare the updated bank columns of the cash book, balanced at 31 August 2027.

**(b)** State where the updated balance appears in the statement of financial position. **[8]**

**8.** Using your answer to Question 7, prepare Bayview Bakery's bank reconciliation statement at 31 August 2027, given that:

- cheques of 515 and 1,060 had not been presented;
- a deposit of 890 made on 31 August had not been credited by the bank;
- the bank had wrongly credited Bayview's account with a deposit of 150 belonging to another business. **[6]**

**9.** At 31 March 2027 Corbel Joinery's updated cash book shows a bank overdraft of 2,140. Unpresented cheques total 830, an uncredited deposit is 1,490, and the bank charged a fee of 65 twice in error.

**(a)** Prepare the bank reconciliation statement at 31 March 2027.

**(b)** State how the bank balance is shown in the statement of financial position at 31 March 2027. **[6]**

**10.** At 30 June 2027 Ferris Florists' bank statement shows a balance of 5,420 in credit. Unpresented cheques total 1,960 and an uncredited deposit is 1,100. The bank recorded a cheque for 340 written by Ferris Florists as 430.

Prepare a bank reconciliation statement starting with the bank statement balance and ending with the balance that should appear in the cash book. **[5]**

**11.** For each item, state whether it is dealt with by updating the cash book or by including it in the bank reconciliation statement.

(i) Bank charges.

(ii) A cheque sent to a supplier on the last day of the month.

(iii) A credit transfer from a customer.

(iv) A deposit wrongly credited to the business by the bank.

(v) A cheque payment recorded in the cash book at the wrong amount.

(vi) Takings paid in on the last afternoon of the month. **[6]**

**12.** Bayview Bakery now receives most payments by card and pays its suppliers by online transfer instead of by cheque. Its accounting software downloads the bank statement daily.

**(a)** Explain **two** ways these changes affect Bayview Bakery's bank reconciliation.

**(b)** The owner says that reconciliation is no longer needed because the software matches transactions automatically. Evaluate this view. **[7]**

## Answers

**1.** A cheque written and entered in the cash book that has not yet been paid by the bank (not yet on the bank statement) [1]. Total: 1 mark.

**2.** **C** [1]. Total: 1 mark.

**3.** **B** [1]. Total: 1 mark.

**4.** Any two [2]: to explain the difference between the cash book and bank statement balances; to find errors or omissions in the cash book; to find errors made by the bank; to confirm the correct bank balance for the statement of financial position; to identify long-unpresented cheques or missing deposits. Total: 2 marks.

**5.** A standing order is a fixed amount paid regularly on the account holder's instruction [1]; a direct debit is collected by the payee, and the amount and date may vary [1]. Total: 2 marks.

**6.** 4,260 + 1,140 - 720 [1] = **4,680** [1]. Total: 2 marks.

**7. (a)**

```
Cash book (bank columns)
Date     Details                     $   Date     Details                     $
2027                                     2027
31 Aug   Balance b/d             2,315   31 Aug   Bank charges               38
31 Aug   Interest received          12   31 Aug   Electricity (DD)          214
31 Aug   Hilltop Hotel             640   31 Aug   Loan (SO)                 300
31 Aug   Customer (error)           27   31 Aug   Balance c/d             2,442
                                 -----                                    -----
                                 2,994                                    2,994
                                 =====                                    =====
1 Sep    Balance b/d             2,442
```

Interest received and credit transfer on the debit side [1]; error correction of 27 on the debit side [1]; bank charges on the credit side [1]; direct debit on the credit side [1]; standing order on the credit side [1]; balance c/d 2,442 [1]; balance b/d on the debit side [1].

**(b)** As a current asset (bank), 2,442 [1]. Total: 8 marks.

*Examiner insight:* The receipt was recorded 27 too low, so the cash book is debited with 27, not with 485.

**8.**

```
Bayview Bakery
Bank reconciliation statement at 31 August 2027
                                                      $          $
Balance as per updated cash book                              2,442
Add unpresented cheques: 515 + 1,060                          1,575
Add deposit wrongly credited by the bank                        150
                                                             ------
                                                              4,167
Less uncredited deposit                                         890
                                                             ------
Balance as per bank statement                                 3,277
                                                             ======
```

Heading [1]; updated cash book balance 2,442 [1]; unpresented cheques added [1]; bank error added [1]; uncredited deposit deducted [1]; bank statement balance 3,277 [1]. Total: 6 marks.

*Examiner insight:* The wrongly credited deposit has raised the bank's balance only, so it is added when working towards the bank statement figure.

**9. (a)**

```
Corbel Joinery
Bank reconciliation statement at 31 March 2027
                                                      $          $
Balance as per updated cash book (overdraft)                (2,140)
Add unpresented cheques                                         830
                                                             ------
                                                             (1,310)
Less uncredited deposit                              1,490
Less fee charged twice in error                         65
                                                              1,555
                                                             ------
Balance as per bank statement (overdraft)                    (2,865)
                                                             ======
```

Opening overdraft shown as negative [1]; unpresented cheques added [1]; uncredited deposit deducted [1]; bank error of 65 deducted [1]; closing overdraft 2,865 [1].

**(b)** As a current liability (bank overdraft) of 2,140 [1]. Total: 6 marks.

**10.**

```
Ferris Florists
Bank reconciliation statement at 30 June 2027
                                                      $          $
Balance as per bank statement                                 5,420
Less unpresented cheques                                      1,960
                                                             ------
                                                              3,460
Add uncredited deposit                               1,100
Add bank error (430 - 340)                              90
                                                              1,190
                                                             ------
Balance as per cash book                                      4,650
                                                             ======
```

Starts with 5,420 [1]; unpresented cheques deducted [1]; uncredited deposit added [1]; bank error of 90 added [1]; cash book balance 4,650 [1]. Total: 5 marks.

*Examiner insight:* Starting from the bank statement reverses every sign compared with starting from the cash book.

**11.** (i) Cash book [1]; (ii) reconciliation statement (unpresented cheque) [1]; (iii) cash book [1]; (iv) reconciliation statement (bank error) [1]; (v) cash book [1]; (vi) reconciliation statement (uncredited deposit) [1]. Total: 6 marks.

**12. (a)** Two ways, each identified [1] and explained [1], for example: card receipts and online payments appear on the statement before or as they are recorded, so most work is checking and recording statement items rather than tracing cheques [2]; with no cheques there will be few unpresented cheques or uncredited deposits, so differences are smaller [2]; daily downloads allow reconciliation every day, so errors and unauthorised payments are found sooner.

**(b)** Automatic matching saves time and reduces arithmetic errors [1]. However, the software can match items wrongly, cannot detect bank errors or fraud by itself, and card takings may still arrive a day or more later, creating timing differences [1]. Reconciliation is therefore still needed, though it is quicker; the owner's view is not correct [1]. Total: 7 marks.

## Where marks are usually lost

- Putting unpresented cheques or uncredited deposits in the updated cash book.
- Reading the bank statement as if its debits were receipts.
- Losing the brackets when an overdraft is reconciled.
- Recording the full amount of a cash book error instead of the difference.
- Showing the bank statement figure, not the updated cash book figure, in the statement of financial position.
