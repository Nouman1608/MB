## Section A

**1.** Find the gradient of the line through A(1, 2) and B(5, 10). **[2]**

**2.** Find the gradient of the line through P(-3, 4) and Q(5, -2). **[2]**

**3.** Write down the gradient of:

**(a)** the line through (-4, 3) and (6, 3) **[1]**

**(b)** the line through (2, 5) and (2, 9) **[1]**

**4.** Find the gradient of the line through:

**(a)** (-2, -7) and (4, 5) **[2]**

**(b)** (1, -3) and (-5, 9) **[2]**

**5.** State whether lines with these gradients slope up, slope down or are horizontal, as you read from left to right.

**(a)** 2 **[1]**

**(b)** -3 **[1]**

**(c)** 0 **[1]**

**6.** A line crosses the y-axis at (0, 12) and the x-axis at (6, 0). Find its gradient. **[2]**

## Section B

**7.** Show whether the points A(0, -1), B(2, 3) and C(5, 9) lie on a straight line. **[3]**

**8.** The line through (2, k) and (6, 9) has gradient 3/2. Find k. **[3]**

**9.** The line through (3, k) and (7, 1) has gradient -2. Find k. **[3]**

**10.** A distance-time graph is a straight line from (0, 0) to (2.5, 40). Time is in hours and distance is in kilometres. Find the gradient and say what it represents. **[2]**

**11.** A road rises 120 m over a horizontal distance of 2400 m. Find the gradient as a fraction in simplest form and as a percentage. **[3]**

**12.** A line passes through (0, 1) and (3, k). It is parallel to the line through (1, 2) and (4, 8). Find k. **[3]**

**13.** Line A passes through (0, 0) and (4, 10). Line B passes through (0, 0) and (5, 11). Which line is steeper? Show your working. **[3]**

**14.** A student finds the gradient of the line through (1, 5) and (4, -1) as 6 ÷ 3 = 2. Explain the mistake and find the correct gradient. **[2]**

## Answers

**1.** change in y = 10 - 2 = 8 and change in x = 5 - 1 = 4 **[1]**; gradient = **2** **[1]** **[2]**

**2.** change in y = -2 - 4 = -6 and change in x = 5 - (-3) = 8 **[1]**; gradient = **-3/4** **[1]** **[2]**

*Examiner insight:* 5 - (-3) is 8, not 2. The line goes down from left to right, so the gradient is negative.

**3. (a)** **0** **[1]**

**(b)** **undefined** (the line is vertical) **[1]** **[2]**

**4. (a)** (5 - (-7)) ÷ (4 - (-2)) = 12 ÷ 6 **[1]** = **2** **[1]**

**(b)** (9 - (-3)) ÷ (-5 - 1) = 12 ÷ (-6) **[1]** = **-2** **[1]** **[4]**

**5. (a)** **slopes up** **[1]**

**(b)** **slopes down** **[1]**

**(c)** **horizontal** **[1]** **[3]**

**6.** rise = 0 - 12 = -12 and run = 6 - 0 = 6 **[1]**; gradient = **-2** **[1]** **[2]**

**7.** gradient AB = 4 ÷ 2 = 2 **[1]**; gradient BC = 6 ÷ 3 = 2 **[1]**; the gradients are equal and B is common, so the points are **on one straight line** **[1]** **[3]**

**8.** (9 - k) ÷ 4 = 3/2 **[1]**; 9 - k = 6 **[1]**; **k = 3** **[1]** **[3]**

**9.** (1 - k) ÷ 4 = -2 **[1]**; 1 - k = -8 **[1]**; **k = 9** **[1]** **[3]**

*Check:* the points (3, 9) and (7, 1) give (1 - 9) ÷ (7 - 3) = -2.

**10.** gradient = 40 ÷ 2.5 = **16** **[1]**; it is the speed, **16 km/h** **[1]** **[2]**

**11.** gradient = 120 ÷ 2400 **[1]** = **1/20** **[1]** = **5%** **[1]** **[3]**

**12.** gradient of the given line = (8 - 2) ÷ (4 - 1) = 2 **[1]**; (k - 1) ÷ 3 = 2 **[1]**; **k = 7** **[1]** **[3]**

**13.** gradient A = 10 ÷ 4 = 2.5 **[1]**; gradient B = 11 ÷ 5 = 2.2 **[1]**; **line A** is steeper **[1]** **[3]**

**14.** The change in y is -1 - 5 = -6, not +6 **[1]**; the correct gradient is -6 ÷ 3 = **-2** **[1]** **[2]**

## Where marks are usually lost

- Putting the x-change on the top in Question 1.
- Writing 5 - (-3) as 2 in Question 2.
- Giving 0 as the gradient of a vertical line in Question 3(b).
- Losing the negative sign in Questions 4(b) and 6.
- Subtracting in opposite orders on the top and bottom in Question 4.
- Setting up the equation as (k - 9) ÷ 4 = 3/2 without checking the order in Question 8.
- Ignoring the units in Question 10.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I use any two points on the line?**
Yes. The gradient of a straight line is the same wherever you measure it. Points far apart give a more accurate answer when you read from a graph.

**What is the gradient of a vertical line?**
It is undefined, because the change in x is zero and you cannot divide by zero.
