These notes cover **Exercise 5, the Writing exercise**, of 3248 Paper 1: "a short piece of functional
prose such as an email, of approximately 150 words", from "a stimulus of short prompts and/or
pictures", with "a purpose, format and audience" specified. The paper overview is in the
[O Level Urdu Paper 1 study guide](/resources/o-level-cambridge-urdu-first-and-second-language/); the
full method and a model email are in the
[Exercise 5 study guide](/resources/cambridge-o-level-urdu-language-3248-exercise-5-writing-exercise/).

## Task facts

- **14 marks**, the highest tariff on Paper 1.
- About **150 words** of functional prose.
- Stimulus: **short prompts and/or pictures**.
- **Purpose, format and audience** are always given.
- Objectives **W1–W5**; how the marks divide is not published.
- Answer in Urdu; no dictionaries.

## The objectives as a checklist

- **W1** every prompt covered, clearly
- **W2** paragraphs, with varied linking devices
- **W3** accurate grammar and vocabulary in context, with some range
- **W4** Urdu punctuation and correct spelling
- **W5** register and format right for purpose and audience

## Purpose, format, audience

| Ask | Examples |
|---|---|
| Purpose (*مقصد*) | invite, thank, apologise, inform, request, describe, recommend |
| Format (*طرزِ تحریر*) | email, letter, blog post, short article |
| Audience (*مخاطب*) | friend, relative, teacher, principal, website readers |

## Register tables

**Informal**

| Part | Urdu |
|---|---|
| Greeting | پیاری سارہ، / عزیز دوست احمد، |
| Opening | کیسے ہو؟ امید ہے سب خیریت ہے۔ |
| Address | تم، تمھارا، تمھیں |
| Imperative | آؤ، بتاؤ، لکھنا |
| Closing | تمھارا دوست، ... / جلد جواب دینا! |

**Formal**

| Part | Urdu |
|---|---|
| Greeting | محترم استاد صاحب، / محترمہ پرنسپل صاحبہ، |
| Opening | السلام علیکم۔ امید ہے آپ بخیریت ہوں گے۔ |
| Address | آپ، آپ کا، آپ کو |
| Imperative | تشریف لائیے، بتائیے، فرمائیے |
| Closing | والسلام، آپ کا شاگرد ... / شکریہ کے ساتھ |

Respectful verbs: *فرمانا*، *تشریف لانا*، *عنایت کرنا*. Use them with *آپ*, never with *تم*.

## Functional phrases by purpose

| Purpose | Informal | Formal |
|---|---|---|
| invite | کیا تم ... آؤ گے؟ | آپ کو ... کی دعوت دیتے ہوئے خوشی ہو رہی ہے۔ |
| thank | تمھارا بہت شکریہ! | میں آپ کا تہِ دل سے شکر گزار ہوں۔ |
| apologise | معاف کرنا، میں ... | میں ... پر معذرت خواہ ہوں۔ |
| request | کیا تم ... کر دو گے؟ | گزارش ہے کہ ... |
| recommend | تمھیں ضرور ... کرنا چاہیے۔ | میری تجویز ہے کہ ... |
| give news | ایک خوش خبری سنو! | آپ کو یہ بتاتے ہوئے خوشی ہے کہ ... |

## Timing

Paper 1 allows 1 hour 45 minutes for 50 marks. Exercise 5 carries 14, so protect about 25 minutes for
it: 3 to plan, about 18 to write, 4 to check. Running out of time here costs more than anywhere else
on the paper.

## Format features

- **Email**: subject (*موضوع*), greeting, paragraphs, closing and name.
- **Letter**: greeting and closing as above; for a formal letter, a clear statement of purpose early.
- **Blog post**: a title, a personal voice, perhaps a question to readers at the end.
- **Short article**: a title, an engaging first sentence, a neutral or semi-formal tone.
- **Review**: what is being reviewed, what was good, what was weak, and a recommendation, usually
  in a semi-formal tone for readers who have not yet seen or tried it.

## Connectives for a short piece

| Job | Urdu |
|---|---|
| add | اس کے علاوہ، ساتھ ہی، یہی نہیں بلکہ |
| explain | کیونکہ، اس لیے کہ، اسی وجہ سے |
| contrast | لیکن، البتہ، تاہم |
| sequence | سب سے پہلے، اس کے بعد، آخر میں |
| conclude | امید ہے، بس یہی کہنا تھا |

## Shape of 150 words (practice guide)

Opening line (10–15 words) · prompt 1 (about 40) · prompt 2 (about 40) · prompt 3 (about 40) · closing
(10–15).

## Exam traps

- Leaving out a prompt, often the last.
- Switching between *تم* and *آپ*.
- An email without a greeting or closing; a blog without a title.
- Writing far more than 150 words and leaving no time to check.
- A memorised paragraph that does not fit the task.
- Agreement errors (*میلہ لگ رہی ہے*), missing *نے* with transitive past verbs.
- English full stops in Urdu text.

## Self-test

1. How many marks is Exercise 5 worth, and roughly how many words?
2. What three things does the task always specify?
3. Which objective covers register and format?
4. Which objective covers punctuation and spelling?
5. Give an informal and a formal greeting.
6. Rewrite formally: *کل میرے گھر آؤ۔*
7. What can pictures in the stimulus be used for?
8. Name two features an email should have.
9. Give a connective for contrast and one for explanation.
10. Correct: *ہمارے اسکول میں ایک مقابلہ ہو رہی ہے۔*
11. What is the Urdu question mark?
12. Why is a memorised answer risky?

**Answers**

1. 14 marks; about 150 words.
2. Purpose, format and audience.
3. W5.
4. W4.
5. Informal: *پیاری سارہ،*; formal: *محترمہ پرنسپل صاحبہ،*.
6. *کل میرے گھر تشریف لائیے۔*
7. As content: describing, explaining or giving opinions on what they show.
8. Two of: subject line, greeting, paragraphs, closing and name.
9. Contrast: *لیکن* / *البتہ* / *تاہم*; explanation: *کیونکہ* / *اس لیے کہ*.
10. *ہمارے اسکول میں ایک مقابلہ ہو رہا ہے۔* (*مقابلہ* is masculine.)
11. ؟
12. It may not answer the specific prompts, purpose or audience, so W1 and W5 suffer.
