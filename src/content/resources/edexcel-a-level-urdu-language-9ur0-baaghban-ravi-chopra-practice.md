> **These are original questions written for Marlbridge** for practice on the prescribed film باغبان
> (*Baaghban*) for Paper 2 Section C. They are not past-paper questions. Questions 1–10 use practice
> marks; question 11 follows the specification's essay format (45 marks on three grids). Your
> evidence must come from your own viewing of the film.

## Section A

**1.** State the name and year the specification gives for باغبان, and explain why you should not describe that name as the "director" on the strength of the list alone. **[3]**

**2.** Give the English meaning of: (a) تقابلی کردار (b) متوازی تدوین (c) جذباتی شدت (d) یک رخا (e) اخلاقی سبق **[5]**

**3.** Explain the image in the title **باغبان** and how it relates to the parents' expectations. **[3]**

**4.** Correct the error in each sentence.
(a) فلم میں بیٹوں کی بے حسی بہت واضح دکھایا گیا ہے۔
(b) راج اور پوجا کو الگ الگ رہنی پڑتی ہے۔
(c) آخری تقریر فلم کا پیغام کھل کر بیان کرتا ہے۔ **[3]**

**5.** Name **two** ways in which songs function in the film. **[2]**

**6.** What is a **foil**, and which character acts as a foil to the four sons? **[2]**

## Section B

**7.** Translate into Urdu:

> The film persuades the audience through strong emotions and clear moral contrasts. However, some viewers feel that the sons are shown in a one-sided way.

**[8]**

**8.** Write **two** sentences in Urdu about the final speech: one arguing that it strengthens the film, and one arguing that it weakens it. **[4]**

**9.** Explain how casting contributes to the audience's sympathy for the parents. **[2]**

**10.** This paragraph agrees with the film but does not evaluate it. Identify the weakness and add **two** sentences in Urdu that evaluate.

> فلم "باغبان" بہت اچھی فلم ہے۔ اس میں دکھایا گیا ہے کہ اولاد کو والدین کی خدمت کرنی چاہیے۔ یہ بات بالکل درست ہے۔

**[4]**

**11.** Answer in Urdu, in about 320–370 words.

**«"باغبان" بزرگوں کے ساتھ سلوک کا ایک سچا آئینہ ہے، مگر یہ آئینہ صرف ایک رخ دکھاتا ہے۔» اس بیان کا جائزہ لیجیے۔**

**[45]**

## Answers

**1.** روی چوپڑہ (Ravi Chopra) [1]; 2003 [1]; the specification gives each film as a name and a year only, with no role label, so the list itself does not say what role the person had [1].

**2.** (a) foil, a character who highlights another by contrast [1] (b) cross-cutting between parallel scenes [1] (c) emotional intensity [1] (d) one-sided [1] (e) moral lesson [1]

**3.** باغبان means "gardener" [1]; parents are imagined as gardeners who plant, water and protect their children like trees [1], expecting shade and fruit, that is support and care, in old age [1].

**4.** (a) دکھائی گئی ہے: agrees with بے حسی, feminine [1] (b) رہنا پڑتا ہے: the infinitive takes default masculine singular here [1] (c) بیان کرتی ہے: تقریر is feminine [1]

**5.** Any two, one mark each: marking celebration and family unity early in the film (for example the Holi song); expressing longing during separation (for example *Main Yahan Tu Wahan*); voicing feelings that dialogue does not; marking turning points.

**6.** A character who highlights another by contrast [1]; Alok, whose devotion to Raj and Pooja contrasts with the sons' ingratitude [1].

**7.** Model: **فلم شدید جذبات اور واضح اخلاقی تضادات کے ذریعے ناظرین کو قائل کرتی ہے۔ تاہم بعض ناظرین کا خیال ہے کہ بیٹوں کو یک رخے انداز میں پیش کیا گیا ہے۔**

Marking: فلم ... قائل کرتی ہے, feminine agreement with فلم [1]؛ شدید جذبات [1]؛ واضح اخلاقی تضادات کے ذریعے [1]؛ ناظرین کو [1]؛ تاہم [1]؛ بعض ناظرین کا خیال ہے کہ [1]؛ بیٹوں کو [1]؛ یک رخے انداز میں پیش کیا گیا ہے [1].

**8.** Strengthens, for example: **آخری تقریر فلم کو ایک طاقتور نقطۂ عروج دیتی ہے، کیونکہ راج کی ساری تکلیف ایک واضح پیغام میں ڈھل جاتی ہے** [1], with a reason [1]. Weakens, for example: **دوسری طرف یہ تقریر ناظرین کو خود سوچنے کا موقع نہیں دیتی، کیونکہ فلم اپنا پیغام براہِ راست بتا دیتی ہے** [1], with a reason [1].

**9.** The parents are played by two of the most famous stars of Hindi cinema [1], whose status and screen presence give the characters dignity and draw the audience's sympathy before the story develops [1].

**10.** Weakness: it accepts the film's message without analysing how it is delivered or whether its picture is fair [1]; it gives no evidence [1]. Evaluative sentences, for example: **فلم اپنا پیغام گانوں اور متضاد مناظر کے ذریعے بڑی کامیابی سے پہنچاتی ہے۔ مگر بیٹوں کی مجبوریوں کو تقریباً نظر انداز کر دیا گیا ہے، اس لیے تصویر کچھ یک رخی ہو جاتی ہے۔** [2: one for an evaluated strength with technique, one for a qualified criticism].

**11.** Marked on three levels-based grids: critical and analytical response (AO4) [20]; range of grammatical structures and vocabulary, including cinematic terminology (AO3) [15]; accuracy of language (AO3) [10]. Total 45.

**Indicative content.** Credit any well-argued response supported by evidence from the film. Likely points:

- **"A true mirror":** the parents' sacrifices and their treatment after retirement reflect real anxieties about care for the elderly as families change; the separation of the couple dramatises loss of dignity and companionship; links to Theme 1, family.
- **Technique that creates the mirror's power:** casting of the parents; songs of celebration and of separation; cross-cutting between the parents' loneliness and the sons' comfort; the final speech.
- **"Only one side":** the sons are presented with little explanation of their own pressures; the daughters-in-law are largely seen from the parents' point of view; Alok is an idealised foil; melodrama simplifies moral choices.
- **Counter-argument:** a melodrama may legitimately exaggerate to make a point; the one-sidedness may be the film's deliberate strategy.
- **Conclusion:** a judgement on how far the statement is true, linked to the arguments.

At the top of the AO4 range, the answer evaluates rather than only agreeing, and each point is justified with a specific scene.
