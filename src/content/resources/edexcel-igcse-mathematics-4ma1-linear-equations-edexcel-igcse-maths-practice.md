**1.** Solve 5x + 9 = 44. **[2]**

**2.** Solve 8 − 3y = 2y − 17. **[2]**

**3.** Solve 4(x − 3) = 2(x + 5). **[2]**

## Section B

**7.** Solve 2(3x + 1) − 5(x − 2) = 3(x + 6). **[3]**

**8.** Solve x/2 + x/5 = 14. **[3]**

**9.** Solve (4x − 1)/3 − (x + 5)/4 = 5/3. **[3]**

**10.** Solve 10/(x + 3) = 4/(x − 3). **[3]**

**11.** The angles of a triangle are (3x − 10)°, (2x + 5)° and (x + 35)°.

**(a)** Form an equation in x and solve it. **[2]**

**(b)** Show that the triangle is not isosceles. **[1]**

**12.** Solve (x − 2)/4 + (2x + 3)/6 = 7/12. **[3]**

**13.** Hana is three times as old as her son. In 12 years' time, Hana will be twice as old as her son.

**(a)** Using s for the son's age now, show that 3s + 12 = 2(s + 12). **[2]**

**(b)** Find Hana's age now. **[2]**

**14.** A concert ticket costs £x. A booking fee of £2.50 is added to each ticket. Five tickets, including booking fees, cost £96.25 in total. (Prices invented.)

**(a)** Form an equation in x. **[1]**

**(b)** Solve your equation to find the price of one ticket before the booking fee. **[2]**

## Answers

**1.** 5x = 35 [1]; **x = 7** [1].

**2.** 8 + 17 = 2y + 3y, so 25 = 5y [1]; **y = 5** [1].

**3.** 4x − 12 = 2x + 10 [1]; 2x = 22, **x = 11** [1].

**4.** x/3 = 7 [1]; **x = 21** [1].

**5.** 2x + 7 = 15 [1]; 2x = 8, **x = 4** [1].

**6.** 6 − x + 4 = 3x, so 10 = 4x [1]; **x = 2.5** [1].

**7.** 6x + 2 − 5x + 10 = 3x + 18 [1]; x + 12 = 3x + 18, so −6 = 2x [1]; **x = −3** [1].

**8.** Multiply by 10: 5x + 2x = 140 [1]; 7x = 140 [1]; **x = 20** [1].

**9.** Multiply by 12: 4(4x − 1) − 3(x + 5) = 20 [1]; 16x − 4 − 3x − 15 = 20, so 13x = 39 [1]; **x = 3** [1].

**10.** 10(x − 3) = 4(x + 3) [1]; 10x − 30 = 4x + 12, so 6x = 42 [1]; **x = 7** [1].

**11. (a)** (3x − 10) + (2x + 5) + (x + 35) = 180, so 6x + 30 = 180 [1]; **x = 25** [1].

**(b)** The angles are 65°, 55° and 60°; no two are equal, so the triangle is not isosceles [1].

**12.** Multiply by 12: 3(x − 2) + 2(2x + 3) = 7 [1]; 3x − 6 + 4x + 6 = 7, so 7x = 7 [1]; **x = 1** [1].

**13. (a)** Hana is 3s now, so in 12 years she is 3s + 12 and her son is s + 12 [1]; "twice as old" gives 3s + 12 = 2(s + 12) [1].

**(b)** 3s + 12 = 2s + 24, so s = 12 [1]; Hana is 3 × 12 = **36** [1].

**14. (a)** **5(x + 2.5) = 96.25** (or 5x + 12.5 = 96.25) [1].

**(b)** x + 2.5 = 19.25 [1]; **x = 16.75**, so a ticket costs **£16.75** before the fee [1].

## Where marks are usually lost

- Question 2: moving −17 across without changing its sign, which gives 8 − 17 = 5y and y = −1.8.
- Question 6: expanding −(x − 4) as −x − 4. The minus sign multiplies −4 as well, giving +4.
- Question 7: writing −5(x − 2) as −5x − 10, which leads to x = −13.
- Question 8: multiplying only one fraction by 10, or forgetting to multiply 14.
- Question 9: dropping the bracket round x + 5 after clearing fractions, so −3 × 5 becomes +15, or multiplying 5/3 by 12 to get 5 instead of 20.
- Question 10: adding the fractions instead of cross-multiplying, or multiplying out 4(x + 3) as 4x + 3.
- Question 11: finding x = 25 but not working out the three angles for part (b).
- Question 13: adding 12 to Hana's age only, or giving the son's age as the final answer.
- Question 14: writing 5x + 2.5 = 96.25, which adds the booking fee only once instead of five times.

## Checking each answer

Every answer on this page can be checked in the original equation. For question 9 with x = 3: 11/3 − 8/4 = 11/3 − 2 = 5/3, which matches the right-hand side. For question 10 with x = 7: 10/10 = 1 and 4/4 = 1. For question 12 with x = 1: −1/4 + 5/6 = −3/12 + 10/12 = 7/12. For question 14: 5 × (16.75 + 2.50) = 5 × 19.25 = 96.25. For question 13: Hana 36 and son 12; in 12 years they are 48 and 24, and 48 is twice 24. Checking in the story rather than in your own equation also catches an equation that was set up wrongly, which substituting into your own line would not reveal.
