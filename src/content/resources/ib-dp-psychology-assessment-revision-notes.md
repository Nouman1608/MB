Condensed for quick recall of how the course is assessed. For the full subject overview, use the
[IB DP Psychology subject overview](/resources/ib-dp-psychology-subject-guide/).

## SL vs HL weightings

| Component | SL | HL |
|---|---|---|
| Paper 1 (3 short-answer + 1 essay, core approaches) | 50% | 40% |
| Paper 2 (1 essay, chosen option) | 25% | 20% |
| Paper 3 (HL only: 3 short-answer, research methodology) | — | 20% |
| Internal assessment (experimental study) | 25% | 20% |

External assessment: 75% (SL) / 80% (HL). Internal: 25% (SL) / 20% (HL).

## The four options (Paper 2 — choose one)

**Abnormal · developmental · health · relationships psychology.** Each applies the biological,
cognitive and sociocultural approaches to a specific applied area.

## The internal assessment

- Students design, carry out (usually in a small group), analyse and **individually write up** a
  simple experiment.
- Then evaluate it and suggest modifications.
- **Externally moderated** by IB examiners, not marked solely by your own teacher.

## HL-only: Paper 3 and extra AOs

Paper 3 tests **research methodology** specifically. At HL, students are additionally expected to
analyse qualitative/quantitative methods and evaluate research scenarios methodologically and
ethically.

## Exam traps

- Revising all four options for Paper 2 instead of the single option your course actually covers.
- Treating the experimental study as purely a group task, when the write-up must be individual.
- HL students underpreparing Paper 3's methodology focus, which is distinct from Papers 1-2's content focus.

## Self-test

1. What four assessment objectives run across all components?
2. What must be individually completed in the internal assessment, even though the experiment itself is usually a group activity?
3. Name the four options examined in Paper 2.
4. What does HL Paper 3 specifically assess?

**Answers:** 1. Knowledge and comprehension of content; application and analysis of theories/research; synthesis and evaluation of research; selection and use of research skills. 2. The write-up and analysis. 3. Abnormal psychology, developmental psychology, health psychology, psychology of human relationships. 4. Research methodology — three short-answer questions.
