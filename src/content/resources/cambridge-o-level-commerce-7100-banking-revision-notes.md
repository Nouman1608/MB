Condensed notes for sub-topic 5.2, Banking: services, payment methods, bank finance and digital banking.

## Four facts to start with

1. Accounts: **current** (everyday) and **deposit / savings** (earn interest).
2. Payments include cash, cheque, credit card, debit card, store card, credit transfer, standing order, direct debit, electronic transfer and documentary credit.
3. Bank finance: loan, overdraft, mortgage and hire purchase.
4. Digital banking is internet and mobile banking.

## Key terms

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Interest | Payment for the use of money |
| Current account | Account for everyday transactions |
| Deposit / savings account | Account for saving; earns interest |
| ATM | Machine for cash withdrawals and deposits |
| Night safe | Secure deposit box outside the bank |
| Standing order | Fixed payment ordered by the payer at set times |
| Direct debit | Collection of varying amounts by the receiver |
| Documentary credit | Bank promise to pay an exporter on receipt of documents |
| Overdraft | Permission to spend more than the balance, up to a limit |
| Mortgage | Long-term loan to buy property |
| Hire purchase | Pay by deposit and instalments; own the goods after the last payment |

## Services at a glance

| Heading | Services |
|---|---|
| Accounts | Current; deposit / savings |
| Customer services | ATM; night safe; foreign currency; financial advice; telephone banking |
| Payments | Cash; cheque; credit card; debit card; store card; credit transfer; standing order; direct debit; electronic transfer; documentary credit |
| Finance | Loan; overdraft; mortgage; hire purchase |

## Payment cards

| Card | Money comes from | Interest? |
|---|---|---|
| Debit | Your bank account now | No |
| Credit | Card company (borrowed) | Yes, if not paid in full |
| Store | Shop's credit | Often high |

## Matching service to situation

| Situation | Service |
|---|---|
| Deposit takings after closing | Night safe |
| Short-term cash shortage | Overdraft |
| Buy a house | Mortgage |
| Exporter wants payment security | Documentary credit |
| Same rent each month | Standing order |
| Bill that changes | Direct debit |

## Digital banking

| Advantages | Disadvantages |
|---|---|
| 24-hour access | Needs internet and device |
| Fast | Fraud and hacking risk |
| No travel | Excludes some customers |
| Lower costs | Breakdowns |

Impact: branch closures; more digital-only banks; less cash (cashless society).

## Worked examples

**Interest.** $2,000 at 3% for one year: $60.

**Loan.** $6,000 at 8% for 2 years: interest $960, total $6,960, monthly $290 over 24 months.

**Hire purchase.** Deposit $200 + 12 × $45 = $740. Cash price $650. Extra $90.

## Model answers to learn

**Difference between debit card and credit card.** A debit card takes money from the holder's account immediately, so no borrowing is involved. A credit card lets the holder borrow from the card company and repay later, with interest if not repaid on time.

**One impact of digital banking.** Bank branches close because fewer customers visit, which saves the bank money but makes it harder for people without internet access.

## Developing a banking point

| Weak | Developed |
|---|---|
| "A night safe is safe" | "A night safe lets the shopkeeper deposit takings after the bank closes, so cash is not left in the shop overnight" |
| "Overdraft is useful" | "An overdraft covers a short gap between paying suppliers and receiving customer payments, with interest only on the amount used" |
| "Internet banking is easy" | "Customers can pay bills at any time without travelling, which saves time" |

## Cash flow case: which finance?

*Invented example.* A bakery needs (a) $500 to cover wages until customers pay, (b) a $12,000 oven and (c) a $90,000 shop to buy.

| Need | Suitable source | Why |
|---|---|---|
| (a) Short gap in cash | Overdraft | Flexible and short-term |
| (b) Oven | Loan or hire purchase | Fixed amount, repaid over time |
| (c) Shop | Mortgage | Long-term loan secured on the property |

## Exam traps

- **Standing order and direct debit swapped.**
- **Credit card as debit card.**
- **Mortgage as short term.**
- **Service named, not justified.**
- **Interest ignored** in total repaid.
- **No disadvantages** for digital banking.

## Self-test

1. What is a current account used for?
2. What is a night safe?
3. Name two customer services.
4. State the difference between a standing order and a direct debit.
5. What is a documentary credit?
6. What is an overdraft?
7. What is a mortgage?
8. Name two types of digital banking.
9. Give one disadvantage of digital banking.
10. What is meant by a cashless society?

**Answers**

1. Everyday transactions such as receiving wages and paying bills.
2. A secure box where a business can deposit takings after the bank has closed.
3. Any two: ATM, night safe, foreign currency, financial advice, telephone banking.
4. A standing order is a fixed amount ordered by the payer; a direct debit is a collection of amounts that may vary, by the receiver.
5. A bank promise to pay an exporter once the right documents are shown.
6. Permission to spend more than the balance, up to a limit, with interest on the amount overdrawn.
7. A long-term loan for buying property.
8. Internet banking and mobile banking.
9. Any one: needs internet access, fraud risk, excludes some customers, technical failure.
10. A society where most payments are made without cash.
