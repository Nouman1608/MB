**These notes condense content area 3.1.1.4 of 9237: "the qualities, importance and role of angels, including Jibril (AS), Mika'il (AS), Isra'fil (AS), Malak-al-Mowth (angel of death) (AS) and Kiraman Katibeen (AS)" and "the Hadith of Jibril, including its key teachings".** The full explanation is in the [Belief in angels study guide](/resources/oxfordaqa-igcse-islamiyat-9237-belief-in-angels/).

## What to know first

- **Angels (*malaikah*)** are beings made from light who obey Allah without fail. They are unseen (*ghayb*).
- Belief in them is the **second** of the six key beliefs, in Qur'an 2:285.
- They are **not divine**, not "daughters of Allah" (43:19), and not worshipped.

## Qualities in six words

**Light. Obedient. Tireless. Winged. Unseen. Honoured.**

| Quality | Proof |
|---|---|
| Created from light | Sahih Muslim |
| Never disobey | 66:6 |
| Never tire of worship | 21:20 |
| Wings, "two or three or four" | 35:1 |
| Can take human form | 19:17 (Maryam) |
| Do not eat | 11:69-70 (guests of Ibraheem) |

## Five angels, five roles

| Angel | Role | Evidence | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Jibril** | Brings revelation to prophets, above all the Qur'an | 2:97 | Trust in revelation |
| **Mika'il** | Rain and provision (tradition) | 2:98 | Sustenance is from Allah |
| **Isra'fil** | Blows the trumpet on the Last Day | 39:68; Hadith | Link to the Last Day |
| **Malak-al-Mowth** | Takes souls at the appointed time | 32:11 | Death is decreed |
| **Kiraman Katibeen** | Record deeds, one on each shoulder | 82:10-12; 50:17-18 | Accountability |

## Other roles

- Praise Allah and ask forgiveness for believers (40:7).
- Helped the Muslims at Badr (8:9).
- Guard people by Allah's command (13:11).
- Greet the righteous in Paradise.

## Importance (use as paragraph starters)

1. **Part of faith:** the Qur'an lists it.
2. **Supports the Qur'an:** Jibril brought it.
3. **Accountability:** deeds are recorded.
4. **Comfort:** angels support believers.
5. **Allah's greatness:** angels serve Him.
6. **Model of obedience:** they never disobey.

## The Hadith of Jibril

A stranger in white clothes, with no sign of travel, sat with the Prophet (pbuh) and asked four questions, and then the Prophet (pbuh) said, "That was Jibril who came to teach the people their religion."

| Question | Answer | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Iman | Belief in Allah, angels, His messengers, the Resurrection (Muslim's report adds the books and the decree) | What the heart believes |
| Islam | Worship Allah alone, prayer, Zakah, fasting Ramadhan (Muslim's report adds Shahadah and Hajj) | What the body does |
| Ihsan | Worship as if you see Allah; if not, He sees you | Excellence and sincerity |
| The Hour | Only Allah knows; signs are given | Humility |

**Three levels of religion:** Islam (practice), Iman (belief), Ihsan (excellence).

**Key lessons:** faith and action belong together; an angel can appear as a man; the Hour is hidden.

**Link:** the Hadith of Jibril is also the passage for 3.1.2.1 (the Five Pillars). In this topic, focus on what it says about angels and belief.

## Exam traps

- Angels have no free will; humans and jinn do.
- Iblis was a jinn (18:50), not an angel.
- Do not say Muhammad (pbuh) saw Jibril in his true form every time. He usually came as a man or as a sound.
- Do not treat the Hadith of Jibril as a story only: give the four questions.
- Give role plus reason, not role alone.

## Model answer shapes

- **2 marks "Give two qualities of angels":** They obey Allah completely; they are made from light.
- **4 marks "Explain the role of Jibril":** He brought revelation to the prophets, including the Qur'an to Muhammad (pbuh) [1 and 1], so Muslims believe the Qur'an is Allah's word, delivered exactly [1 and 1].
- **6 marks:** two roles with importance, plus a source (for example 82:10-12).
- **12 marks:** group roles under revelation, accountability and the unseen, tie to "messengers having wings" and "Creator of the heavens and the earth", consider whether belief in angels is as important as other beliefs, and judge.

## A model 6-mark paragraph

**Question:** Explain **two** roles of angels. Apply **one** teaching. **[6]**

Jibril (AS) brings revelation from Allah to the prophets, and he brought the Qur'an to Hazrat Muhammad (pbuh) [1]. This matters because Muslims believe the Qur'an is Allah's word, delivered without change [1]. The Kiraman Katibeen record the words and deeds of every person [1], so Muslims believe that nothing is forgotten and they try to behave well even when alone [1]. The Qur'an says, "they know whatever you do" (82:12) [1], which shows that the recording is complete and that people will be held to account for it [1].

**Why it scores:** two different roles, each followed by its importance, and one quotation applied to the second.

## Angels and the other key beliefs

- **Tawhid:** angels are servants, so they remind a Muslim that only Allah is divine.
- **Kutub and Risalah:** angels carried the books to the messengers.
- **Yaum e Akhirah:** Isra'fil's trumpet and the recorders' books belong to the Last Day.
- **Al-Qadr:** angels act only by Allah's command, which links to the decree.

## Self-test

1. What are angels made from?
2. Which verse says the angels have wings in twos, threes or fours?
3. Which angel is linked to rain?
4. Which angel will blow the trumpet?
5. What does *Kiraman Katibeen* mean?
6. Name the four questions Jibril asked.
7. What did the Prophet (pbuh) say the stranger had come to do?
8. Why are angels not worshipped?
9. Give two ways in which belief in angels changes behaviour.
10. Which verse names Mika'il?

**Answers:** 1. Light. 2. Qur'an 35:1. 3. Mika'il. 4. Isra'fil. 5. The noble recorders. 6. Iman (faith), Islam, Ihsan and the Hour. 7. To teach the people their religion. 8. Because they are creations of Allah, and only Allah is worshipped. 9. Any two: awareness that deeds are recorded; good conduct when unobserved; trust in revelation; comfort in prayer. 10. Qur'an 2:98.
