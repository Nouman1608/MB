Condensed recall for **5.6** in the prescribed literary texts and films of Pearson Edexcel A Level Urdu (9UR0), Paper 2. For the full explanation, read the [Translation into Urdu and Written Response to Works study guide](/resources/a-level-edexcel-urdu-translation-and-works/) and the [Paper 2 revision notes](/resources/edexcel-a-level-urdu-translation-works-revision-notes/).

## The work

- **Title:** بجرنگی بھائی جان (*Bajrangi Bhaijaan*)
- **Name given in the specification:** کبیر خان (Kabir Khan)
- **Year:** 2015
- **Form:** a prescribed film in Appendix 1.

The specification gives each film as a name and a year only, with no role label, so do not write a name in as 'director' on the strength of the list alone.

## How it is examined

- Paper 2 is 2 hours 40 minutes, 110 marks, 30% of the A Level.
- Section C (film) is worth 45 marks and about 1 hour 5 minutes; you choose from two questions.
- You write exactly **two** extended responses: two literary texts, or one literary text and one film. Two films is never allowed.
- Marking criteria: critical and analytical response with cultural or social insight (AO4), range of grammatical structures and vocabulary (AO3), and accuracy of language (AO3). There are no AO1 or AO2 marks on Paper 2.

## Approach and skills

- Prepare against five angles: **form and technique, key themes and issues, characterisation, plot structure, social and cultural setting**.
- Structure each answer as **position, evidence, cultural connection**: state your stance, support it with two or three specific points (not a retelling), then link the writer's or film's treatment of an issue to a broader social or cultural theme.
- Write in controlled Urdu with correct agreement, tense and spelling, and plan before you write.
- Use your own prepared references from the work; this page does not replace studying it.

## Exam traps

- Retelling the story without connecting it to a theme weakens the AO4 mark.
- Attempting three responses, or choosing two films.
- Running over time on the first response and squeezing the second.

## Self-test

1. Which name and year does the specification give for بجرنگی بھائی جان?
2. Which combinations of works are allowed?
3. How long should you spend on each extended response?
4. How long is Paper 2 and how many marks?
5. What is the weight of Paper 2?

**Answers:** 1. کبیر خان, 2015. 2. Two literary texts, or one literary text and one film. 3. About 1 hour 5 minutes. 4. 2 hours 40 minutes, 110 marks. 5. 30% of the A Level.
