## Section A

**1.** List the **eight** characteristics of living organisms. **[4]**

**2.** Name the five kingdoms and give one example organism from each. **[5]**

## Section B

**3.** Compare a plant cell and a fungal cell, giving **three** differences. **[3]**

**4.** Viruses are not placed in any kingdom.

**(a)** Give **three** reasons why viruses are not considered living. **[3]**
**(b)** Describe the basic structure of a virus. **[2]**
**(c)** Name one virus and the disease it causes. **[1]**

**5.** Fungi and bacteria both act as decomposers.

**(a)** Explain how a fungus obtains its nutrition. **[3]**
**(b)** Explain the importance of decomposers in an ecosystem. **[2]**

**6.** For each pathogen type, name a disease and its causative organism: bacterium, fungus, protoctist. **[6]**

## Answers

**1.** **M**ovement, **R**espiration, **S**ensitivity, **G**rowth, **R**eproduction, **E**xcretion, **N**utrition, **C**ontrol of internal conditions [4 marks — half a mark each, or 1 mark per two correct].

**2. Animals** — e.g. a human [1]. **Plants** — e.g. a maize plant [1]. **Fungi** — e.g. yeast or Mucor [1]. **Protoctists** — e.g. Amoeba or Chlorella [1]. **Prokaryotes/bacteria** — e.g. Lactobacillus or Pneumococcus [1].

**3.** Plant cell walls are **cellulose**, fungal walls are **chitin** [1]. Plant cells contain **chloroplasts**, fungal cells do not [1]. Plants store carbohydrate as **starch**, fungi as **glycogen** [1].

**4. (a)** They **cannot reproduce independently** — only inside a host cell [1]; they have **no cytoplasm or organelles and do not respire** [1]; they do not **grow, excrete or respond to stimuli** [1].
**(b)** A **protein coat** [1] surrounding a core of **genetic material — DNA or RNA** [1].
**(c)** Any correct pair, e.g. **HIV → AIDS**, or **influenza virus → flu**, or **tobacco mosaic virus → TMV in plants** [1].

**5. (a)** It secretes **extracellular enzymes onto the food** [1]; these **digest the large insoluble molecules outside the organism** [1]; the **soluble products are then absorbed** — this is **saprotrophic nutrition** [1].
**(b)** They **break down dead organisms and waste** [1], **releasing mineral ions back into the soil** for plants to reabsorb, so nutrients are recycled [1].

**6. Bacterium** — e.g. **cholera**, caused by *Vibrio cholerae* [1] [1]. **Fungus** — e.g. **athlete's foot**, caused by a *Tinea* fungus [1] [1]. **Protoctist** — e.g. **malaria**, caused by *Plasmodium* [1] [1].

## Where marks are usually lost

- Missing one of the eight characteristics — learn MRS GREN plus control of internal conditions.
- Saying fungal walls are made of cellulose.
- Claiming viruses have a cell wall.
- Confusing the pathogen with the vector — mosquitoes carry *Plasmodium*, they are not the pathogen.
