These questions cover section 5.2, Area and perimeter, of the Cambridge O Level Mathematics (Syllabus D) 4024 syllabus for 2025, 2026 and 2027. The methods are explained in the [area and perimeter study guide](/resources/cambridge-o-level-mathematics-4024-area-and-perimeter/), and the [area and perimeter revision notes](/resources/cambridge-o-level-mathematics-4024-area-and-perimeter-revision-notes/) list the formulas. Give non-exact answers to 3 significant figures unless told otherwise, and include units.

## Section A

**1.** A rectangle is 12.5 cm long and 6.4 cm wide. Find its area and its perimeter. **[2]**

**2.** A triangle has a base of 15 cm and a perpendicular height of 8.4 cm. Find its area. **[2]**

**3.** A parallelogram has a base of 11 cm, a perpendicular height of 4.5 cm and a slanting side of 5 cm. Find its area and its perimeter. **[3]**

**4.** A trapezium has parallel sides of 7 cm and 12 cm. The distance between them is 6 cm. Find its area. **[2]**

**5.** A triangle has area 40 cm² and perpendicular height 8 cm. Find the length of its base. **[2]**

**6.** A rectangle has area 91.8 cm² and width 5.4 cm. Find its length. **[2]**

## Section B

**7.** A rectangle has a length that is 5 cm greater than its width. Its perimeter is 50 cm. Find its width, length and area. **[4]**

**8.** An isosceles triangle has sides of 17 cm, 17 cm and 16 cm.

**(a)** Find the perpendicular height of the triangle.

**(b)** Find its area and its perimeter. **[4]**

**9.** An isosceles trapezium has parallel sides of 9 cm and 21 cm. The sloping sides are each 10 cm.

**(a)** Show that the distance between the parallel sides is 8 cm.

**(b)** Find the area of the trapezium.

**(c)** Find the perimeter of the trapezium. **[5]**

**10.** A rectangular lawn is 12 m by 9 m. A path of width 1.5 m goes all round the outside. Find the area of the path. **[4]**

**11.** In a triangle with sides 13 cm, 14 cm and 15 cm, the perpendicular height from the side of 14 cm is 12 cm. Find the area of the triangle, and then find the perpendicular height from the side of 13 cm, giving your answer to 3 significant figures. **[3]**

**12.** A room is 5.2 m long and 3.5 m wide. Carpet costs $14.50 per square metre. Find the cost of carpeting the room. **[3]**

**13.** A rectangle has perimeter 34 cm and area 60 cm². Find the lengths of its sides. **[4]**

**14.** A parallelogram has a base of 15 cm and area 105 cm². Find its perpendicular height. **[2]**

**15.** A rhombus has sides of 10 cm and a perpendicular height of 8 cm between two opposite sides. Find its area and its perimeter. **[3]**

**16.** A trapezium has parallel sides of x cm and (x + 4) cm, and a distance of 7 cm between them. Its area is 63 cm². Find x and the lengths of the parallel sides. **[4]**

**17.** A triangle has vertices A (1, 1), B (7, 1) and C (4, 5). Find its area and its perimeter. **[4]**

## Answers

**1.** Area = 12.5 × 6.4 = **80 cm²** [1]. Perimeter = 2(12.5 + 6.4) = **37.8 cm** [1].

**2.** Area = 1/2 × 15 × 8.4 [1] = **63 cm²** [1].

**3.** Area = 11 × 4.5 = **49.5 cm²** [1]. Perimeter = 2(11 + 5) [1] = **32 cm** [1].

**4.** Area = 1/2 × (7 + 12) × 6 [1] = **57 cm²** [1].

**5.** 1/2 × b × 8 = 40 [1], so 4b = 40 and **b = 10 cm** [1].

**6.** length × 5.4 = 91.8 [1], so length = **17 cm** [1].

**7.** Let the width be x, so the length is x + 5 [1]. 2(2x + 5) = 50, so 4x + 10 = 50 [1]. Width **10 cm** and length **15 cm** [1]. Area = **150 cm²** [1].

**8. (a)** Half the base = 8 cm [1]. h² = 17² − 8² = 225, so **h = 15 cm** [1].

**(b)** Area = 1/2 × 16 × 15 = **120 cm²** [1]. Perimeter = 17 + 17 + 16 = **50 cm** [1].

**9. (a)** The overhang at each end is (21 − 9) ÷ 2 = 6 cm [1]. h² = 10² − 6² = 64, so h = 8 cm [1].

**(b)** Area = 1/2 × (9 + 21) × 8 = **120 cm²** [1].

**(c)** Perimeter = 9 + 21 + 10 + 10 = **50 cm** [1][1].

**10.** Outer rectangle = (12 + 3) × (9 + 3) = 15 × 12 = 180 m² [1][1]. Lawn = 12 × 9 = 108 m² [1]. Path = 180 − 108 = **72 m²** [1].

**11.** Area = 1/2 × 14 × 12 = **84 cm²** [1]. 1/2 × 13 × h = 84 [1], so h = 168 ÷ 13 = **12.9 cm** [1].

**12.** Area = 5.2 × 3.5 = 18.2 m² [1]. Cost = 18.2 × 14.50 [1] = **$263.90** [1].

**13.** l + w = 17 [1]. l(17 − l) = 60, so l² − 17l + 60 = 0 [1]. (l − 5)(l − 12) = 0 [1]. The sides are **5 cm and 12 cm** [1].

**14.** 15 × h = 105 [1], so **h = 7 cm** [1].

**15.** A rhombus is a parallelogram, so area = 10 × 8 = **80 cm²** [1][1]. Perimeter = 4 × 10 = **40 cm** [1].

**16.** 1/2 × (x + x + 4) × 7 = 63 [1]. 7(x + 2) = 63, so x + 2 = 9 [1]. **x = 7** [1]. The parallel sides are **7 cm and 11 cm** [1].

**17.** AB = 6 and the height from C to AB is 5 − 1 = 4, so area = 1/2 × 6 × 4 = **12 square units** [1][1]. AC = √(3² + 4²) = 5 and BC = √(3² + 4²) = 5 [1]. Perimeter = 6 + 5 + 5 = **16 units** [1].

## Where marks are usually lost

- Using the slanting side instead of the perpendicular height in an area.
- Forgetting the factor 1/2 for triangles and trapezia.
- Taking the perimeter of a parallelogram as 2 × (base + height).
- Forgetting to double the width of a path when finding the outer dimensions.
- Giving an area in cm, or a perimeter in cm².
