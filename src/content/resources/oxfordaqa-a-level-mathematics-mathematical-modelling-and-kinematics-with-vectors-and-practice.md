The specification places this content in International A-level Unit M2 (Mechanics); it is not examined at International AS. Distances are in metres and times in seconds unless stated, and where needed take g = 9.8 m s⁻².

## Questions

**1.** Two crates are towed along a level runway by a rope. The crates are modelled as particles, the rope as light and inextensible, and the runway as smooth.

**(a)** State what "light" tells you about the tension in the rope. **[1]**
**(b)** State what "inextensible" tells you about the motion of the crates. **[1]**
**(c)** The runway is in fact rough. State the effect on the model's predicted acceleration. **[1]**
**(d)** Give one reason why treating a crate as a particle may be reasonable here. **[1]**

**2.** A stone is projected straight up at 21 m s⁻¹ from ground level. Model it as a particle moving under gravity.

**(a)** Find the greatest height reached and the time taken to return to ground level, to 3 significant figures where needed. **[3]**
**(b)** The stone is measured reaching 20.3 m. Comment on the model and suggest one improvement. **[2]**

**3.** A particle has velocity v = (6t² − 42t + 72; 4t − 12; 2t − 6) for t ≥ 0, and is at the origin when t = 0.

**(a)** Show that the particle is at rest only when t = 3. **[3]**
**(b)** Find the position vector of the particle when t = 3. **[2]**
**(c)** Find the acceleration at t = 3. **[1]**

**4.** A particle moves on a smooth horizontal plane. It is at O when t = 0, with velocity −4i + 7j. Its acceleration is 6t i + 4j.

**(a)** Obtain v as a function of t. **[3]**
**(b)** Show that its speed when t = 2 is 17 m s⁻¹. **[2]**
**(c)** Show that it is on the y-axis when t = 2 and find its distance from O. **[2]**

**5.** A particle has position vector r = 3cos(2t)i + 3sin(2t)j relative to O.

**(a)** Find its velocity and acceleration. **[2]**
**(b)** Show that its speed is constant and find it. **[2]**
**(c)** Show that a = −4r and state the direction of the acceleration. **[2]**
**(d)** Describe the path and find the magnitude of the acceleration. **[2]**

**6.** A particle moves on a straight line through O. Its displacement from O is x and its velocity is v = 6t − 3t² when t ≥ 0. When t = 0, x = 1.

**(a)** Find the positive time at which it is instantaneously at rest. **[2]**
**(b)** Find x in terms of t. **[2]**
**(c)** Calculate how far it travels altogether during the first 4 seconds. **[3]**

**7.** A particle has velocity v = (3t − 10)i + (4t − 5)j and is at 2i + j when t = 0.

**(a)** Show that the acceleration is constant and find its magnitude. **[2]**
**(b)** Show that |v|² = 25(t − 2)² + 25. **[2]**
**(c)** Find the least speed and the time at which it occurs. **[2]**
**(d)** Find the position vector at that time. **[2]**

**8.** Particles A and B have position vectors r_A = (t² + t)i + (5t − 4)j and r_B = (4t − 2)i + t²j.

**(a)** Find the values of t for which the i components agree, and for which the j components agree. **[4]**
**(b)** Show that A and B collide and find the position. **[2]**
**(c)** Find the speed of each particle at the collision. **[2]**
**(d)** State a modelling assumption behind "collide" meaning "same position". **[1]**

**9.** A buggy P moves on smooth ground with acceleration (6t − 6)i + 4j. At t = 0 it is at O with velocity 3i − 8j.

**(a)** Find the velocity of P at time t. **[3]**
**(b)** Find the position vector of P at time t. **[2]**
**(c)** Show that P moves parallel to i when t = 2, and find its position and speed then. **[3]**
**(d)** Show that P is next on the x-axis when t = 4 and find its distance from O. **[2]**

**10.** A drone has position vector r = (t² − 2t)i + 3tj + (6 − 3t²/2)k, where k is vertically upwards and the ground is the plane k = 0.

**(a)** Find its velocity and acceleration. **[2]**
**(b)** Find when it reaches the ground and its position then. **[2]**
**(c)** Find its speed on reaching the ground. **[2]**
**(d)** The drone is modelled as a particle. State what this ignores. **[1]**

## Answers

**1. (a)** The tension is the same throughout the rope. [1]
**(b)** The crates have the same speed and acceleration along the rope while it is taut. [1]
**(c)** Friction opposes motion, so the model overestimates the acceleration. [1]
**(d)** The crate is small compared with the runway length, so its size and rotation can be ignored. [1]
*Examiner insight:* A modelling answer is stronger when it names the effect on the result, as in part (c), rather than only repeating the word.

**2. (a)** 0 = 21² − 2(9.8)h [1], so **h = 22.5 m [1]**. Time to return = 2 × 21/9.8 = **4.29 s [1]**.
**(b)** The model predicts 22.5 m but 20.3 m was measured, so it overestimates [1]. Air resistance is ignored; include a resistive force [1].
*Examiner insight:* A comment needs a comparison with the data, a named assumption and a direction, so give all three.

**3. (a)** i: 6(t − 3)(t − 4) = 0 gives t = 3 or 4 [1]. j: t = 3. k: t = 3 [1]. Only t = 3 is common (at t = 4 the j component is 4), so rest occurs only at **t = 3 [1]**.
**(b)** r = (2t³ − 21t² + 72t; 2t² − 12t; t² − 6t) [1], so at t = 3, **r = (81; −18; −9) [1]**.
**(c)** a = (12t − 42; 4; 2) = **(−6; 4; 2)** at t = 3 [1].
*Examiner insight:* "Show that" needs every component checked; testing only the i component leaves the argument incomplete.

**4. (a)** v = (3t² + c)i + (4t + d)j [1]; v(0) gives c = −4, d = 7 [1]; **v = (3t² − 4)i + (4t + 7)j [1]**.
**(b)** v(2) = 8i + 15j [1], speed = √(64 + 225) = **17 [1]**.
**(c)** r = (t³ − 4t)i + (2t² + 7t)j [1]; at t = 2, r = 0i + 22j, so it is on the y-axis, **22 m** from O [1].
*Examiner insight:* When asked to show a value, display the step that produces it (here √289) and then state the value.

**5. (a)** **v = −6sin(2t)i + 6cos(2t)j [1]**, **a = −12cos(2t)i − 12sin(2t)j [1]**.
**(b)** |v|² = 36(sin²2t + cos²2t) [1] = 36, so the speed is **6 m s⁻¹** for all t [1].
**(c)** a = −4(3cos2t i + 3sin2t j) = −4r [1]; the acceleration is directed towards O [1].
**(d)** |r|² = 9, so a circle of centre O and radius 3 [1]; |a| = 4 × 3 = **12 m s⁻²** [1].
*Examiner insight:* Use the identity sin² + cos² = 1 explicitly; the constant speed is only demonstrated once it appears in your working.

**6. (a)** 3t(2 − t) = 0 [1], so **t = 2 [1]**.
**(b)** x = 3t² − t³ + c [1]; x(0) = 1 gives **x = 3t² − t³ + 1 [1]**.
**(c)** x(2) = 5 and x(4) = −15 [1]. The particle moves forward 5 − 1 = 4 m, then back 5 − (−15) = 20 m [1]; total = **24 m [1]**.
*Examiner insight:* Distance travelled differs from x(4) − x(0); a change of direction at t = 2 means the path must be split there.

**7. (a)** a = 3i + 4j, which has no t in it, so it is constant [1]; |a| = **5 m s⁻² [1]**.
**(b)** |v|² = (3t − 10)² + (4t − 5)² = 25t² − 100t + 125 [1] = 25(t − 2)² + 25 [1].
**(c)** The least value of |v|² is 25 at t = 2 [1], so the **least speed is 5 m s⁻¹ at t = 2 [1]**.
**(d)** r = (3t²/2 − 10t + 2)i + (2t² − 5t + 1)j [1]; at t = 2, **r = −12i − j [1]**.
*Examiner insight:* Take the square root at the end of part (c); quoting 25 as the speed confuses |v|² with |v|.

**8. (a)** i: t² + t = 4t − 2, so t² − 3t + 2 = 0 [1], giving **t = 1 or 2 [1]**. j: 5t − 4 = t², so t² − 5t + 4 = 0 [1], giving **t = 1 or 4 [1]**.
**(b)** Only t = 1 is common to both lists, so they collide at t = 1 [1], at **2i + j [1]**.
**(c)** v_A = (2t + 1)i + 5j, so at t = 1 it is 3i + 5j and the speed is **√34 [1]**. v_B = 4i + 2tj, so it is 4i + 2j and the speed is **2√5 [1]**.
**(d)** The particles are treated as points with no size [1].
*Examiner insight:* A collision needs one time common to both component equations; accepting t = 2 or t = 4 from one equation alone would be a false conclusion.

**9. (a)** v = (3t² − 6t + c)i + (4t + d)j [1]; c = 3, d = −8 from v(0) [1]; **v = (3t² − 6t + 3)i + (4t − 8)j [1]**.
**(b)** r = (t³ − 3t² + 3t)i + (2t² − 8t)j with zero constants as r(0) = 0 [1]; this is the **position vector [1]**.
**(c)** The j component of v is 4(t − 2), which is zero at t = 2 [1]; r(2) = **2i − 8j [1]**; v(2) = 3i, so the speed is **3 m s⁻¹ [1]**.
**(d)** y = 2t(t − 4) = 0 gives t = 4 (t = 0 is the start) [1]; x = 64 − 48 + 12 = 28, so **28 m** from O [1].
*Examiner insight:* State why t = 0 is excluded in part (d), since the phrase "next on the x-axis" refers to a later time.

**10. (a)** **v = (2t − 2)i + 3j − 3tk [1]**, **a = 2i − 3k [1]**.
**(b)** k component zero: 6 = 3t²/2, so t² = 4 and **t = 2 [1]** (t = −2 rejected); r(2) = **6j [1]**.
**(c)** v(2) = 2i + 3j − 6k [1]; speed = √(4 + 9 + 36) = **7 m s⁻¹ [1]**.
**(d)** It ignores the drone's size, shape and rotation [1].
*Examiner insight:* Reject the negative root explicitly, because the model is only defined for t ≥ 0.

## Where marks are usually lost

- Using constant acceleration equations when a depends on t.
- Leaving out the vector constant of integration, or finding only one of its components.
- Declaring "at rest" after checking a single component of the velocity.
- Accepting a collision from one component equation, or from two different values of t.
- Confusing |r|, the distance from O, with the distance travelled.
- Giving |v|² as the speed, or leaving a speed as a vector.
- Ignoring a change in the direction of motion when finding total distance.
- Calling a model unrealistic while naming neither the assumption nor its effect on the answer.
