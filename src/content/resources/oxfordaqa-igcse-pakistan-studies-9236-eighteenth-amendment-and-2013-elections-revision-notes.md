These notes condense one content point from key development 3.1.2.2 of the 9236 specification:
"changes under Asif Ali Zardari, including the 18th and 19th Amendments; the 2013 elections, and the
policies of the Pakistan Muslim League (PML) led government". The full explanation is in the
[Zardari and the 2013 elections study guide](/resources/oxfordaqa-igcse-pakistan-studies-9236-eighteenth-amendment-and-2013-elections/);
the wider topic is in the
[Pakistan into the 21st Century revision notes](/resources/igcse-oxfordaqa-pakistan-studies-pakistan-into-the-21st-century-revision-notes/).

## Timeline

| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| Dec 2007 | Benazir Bhutto assassinated in Rawalpindi |
| Feb 2008 | PPP wins most seats; Gilani becomes Prime Minister |
| Aug–Sep 2008 | Musharraf resigns; Zardari elected President |
| 2008 | Benazir Income Support Programme set up |
| Mar 2009 | Chief Justice Iftikhar Chaudhry restored |
| 2009 | 7th NFC Award; Balochistan package; Gilgit-Baltistan Order |
| Apr 2010 | 18th Amendment |
| 2010–11 | 19th Amendment |
| 2012 | 20th Amendment; Gilani disqualified, Raja Pervaiz Ashraf PM |

| Sep 2013 | Zardari hands over to President Mamnoon Hussain |
| 2014 | Zarb-e-Azb; Army Public School attack; National Action Plan |

| Jul 2017 | Nawaz Sharif disqualified; Shahid Khaqan Abbasi PM |
| 2018 | 25th Amendment merges FATA into Khyber Pakhtunkhwa |

## What each amendment did

| Amendment | Main changes |
|---|---|
| 18th (2010) | President's discretionary power to dissolve the National Assembly removed; Prime Minister chief executive; Concurrent List abolished and subjects devolved; NWFP renamed Khyber Pakhtunkhwa; Article 25A right to education aged 5–16; suspending the constitution made high treason; Judicial Commission for appointing judges; third-term bar removed |
| 19th (2010–11) | Judicial appointments procedure adjusted, giving senior judges a greater role, after Supreme Court concerns |
| 20th (2012) | Election Commission strengthened; rules for caretaker governments |
| 21st (2015) | Military courts for terrorism cases (PML-N period) |
| 25th (2018) | FATA merged into Khyber Pakhtunkhwa (PML-N period) |

## Why 2013 mattered

- First handover from an elected government that **completed its term** to another elected
  government.
- PML-N won; **Nawaz Sharif's third term**, possible only because the 18th Amendment removed the bar.
- **PTI** became a major force and governed Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.
- A civilian President (Zardari) completed his term and handed over peacefully.

## PML-N policies (2013–18)

- **Energy:** coal, hydel and LNG projects to reduce load-shedding.
- **Economy:** IMF programme from 2013; CPEC from 2015.
- **Security:** Zarb-e-Azb (2014); National Action Plan; Karachi operation.
- **Infrastructure:** motorways; metro bus systems.
- **Governance:** FATA merger (2018).

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Amendment | A formal change to the constitution passed by parliament |
| Devolution | Transfer of powers from the federal government to the provinces |
| Concurrent List | Subjects on which both federal and provincial governments could legislate; abolished in 2010 |
| NFC Award | Agreement dividing federal revenue between the provinces |
| Load-shedding | Planned power cuts caused by electricity shortages |
| Disqualification | Removal from office by court ruling |
| Caretaker government | Temporary neutral government that runs the country during an election |
| National Action Plan | Counter-terrorism plan agreed after the Peshawar school attack in 2014 |

## Judging success: quick balance

- **Democracy:** restored by the 18th Amendment and proved by the 2013 handover; but two Prime
  Ministers were removed by the courts.
- **Provinces:** more money and powers; but capacity and Balochistan's grievances remained problems.
- **Security:** violence fell after 2014; but military courts raised rights concerns.
- **Economy and energy:** more power generation and CPEC investment; but debt and IMF dependence grew.

Use the pattern "achievement, but limit, so judgement" in 9-mark answers.

## Exam traps

- Calling Zardari Prime Minister.
- Mixing the 18th and 19th Amendments.
- Saying 2013 was the first election, rather than the first transfer between elected governments
  that completed a term.
- Putting the FATA merger under PTI; it was passed in 2018 under the PML-N.
- Listing policies without weighing success and failure.

## Self-test

1. Who became Prime Minister after the 2008 election?
2. In which year did Zardari become President?
3. What was BISP, and who received its payments?
4. What did the 7th NFC Award change?
5. Which presidential power did the 18th Amendment remove?
6. What was NWFP renamed?
7. What does Article 25A guarantee?
8. What was the main purpose of the 19th Amendment?
9. Why was Gilani disqualified in 2012?
10. Why was the 2013 election a landmark?
11. Name two security measures of the PML-N government.
12. Which amendment merged FATA into Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, and when?

## Answers

1. Yousaf Raza Gilani.
2. 2008.
3. The Benazir Income Support Programme, giving cash payments to poor families, paid to women.
4. It gave provinces a larger share of federal revenue and used several criteria, not population
   alone.
5. The President's discretionary power to dissolve the National Assembly.
6. Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.
7. Free and compulsory education for children aged 5 to 16.
8. To adjust the method of appointing judges, giving senior judges a greater role.
9. He was convicted of contempt of court for refusing to ask Swiss authorities to reopen cases
   against Zardari.
10. It was the first handover from an elected government that had completed its term to another
    elected government.
11. Any two: Zarb-e-Azb; the National Action Plan; military courts; the Karachi operation.
12. The 25th Amendment, in 2018.
