**These notes support Paper 2 Question 1 of Cambridge O Level Islamiyat 2058, Major Teachings in the
Hadiths of the Prophet.** Each of the twenty set Hadiths is listed with a keyword cue (the phrase
that helps you recognise it in the exam), its teaching, and a modern example of putting it into
practice. Cover the right-hand columns and test yourself.

## Syllabus coverage

**2058 Paper 2, Topic 1 (2026–2027 syllabus):** twenty Hadiths from al-Bukhari and Muslim on
individual conduct and life in the community; candidates answer on two of four set passages,
explaining teaching and practice (8 marks).

## Answer formula

**Teaching (about 4 marks):** state the main message, explain key words, and say what belief lies
behind it.
**Application (about 4 marks):** give one or two specific actions a Muslim might take today, with a
supporting verse or Hadith if possible.

## Worked example

**Hadith:** "God will not show mercy to him who does not show mercy to others."

**Teaching:** The Hadith teaches that showing mercy is a condition of receiving Allah's mercy. Allah
is al-Rahman and al-Rahim, and He wants His servants to reflect this quality. A person who is harsh,
cruel or unforgiving cannot expect forgiveness on the Day of Judgement.

**Application:** Muslims should be patient with family members, forgive people who wrong them and
help those who suffer. Mercy includes animals; the Prophet (pbuh) taught kindness even to a thirsty
dog. Teachers and parents should correct gently rather than harshly. This links to the Hadith "Be
gentle and do not be hard."

## Linking Hadiths to the Qur'an

- **Brotherhood and unity:** Qur'an 49.10, "The believers are but a single brotherhood."
- **Speech:** Qur'an 49.11–12 forbids mockery and backbiting.
- **Giving:** Qur'an 2.177 includes spending on orphans, the needy and travellers in righteousness.
- **Commanding good:** Qur'an 3.104 calls for a group inviting to good and forbidding wrong.

## Common mistakes and exam traps

- **Choosing a Hadith you only half-remember.** Pick the two you can explain and apply most fully.
- **Repeating the teaching in the application.** The application must describe action.
- **Very general examples.** "Be kind" is weak; "visit a sick neighbour and offer to shop for them" is strong.
- **Over-long answers.** This question is worth 8 marks; keep time for the 14-mark essays.

## Frequently asked questions

**What does haya mean?** Modesty or a healthy sense of shame that keeps a person from doing wrong.

**Why is caring for orphans so emphasised?** The Qur'an repeatedly commands kindness to orphans, the
Prophet (pbuh) grew up an orphan, and this Hadith promises a special place near him in paradise.
