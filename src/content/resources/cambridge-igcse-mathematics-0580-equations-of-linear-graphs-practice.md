These questions cover subtopic 3.5 Equations of linear graphs of the Cambridge IGCSE Mathematics 0580 syllabus for examination in 2025, 2026 and 2027. Questions marked **(Extended)** use lines in forms such as ax + by = c, or find an equation from two points without a graph; the others suit both tiers. Each question is labelled (non-calculator) or (calculator). Give every equation in a fully simplified form.

## Section A

**1.** (non-calculator) Write down the gradient and the y-intercept of the line y = –3x + 8. **[2]**

**2.** (non-calculator) Write down the equation of the line with gradient ½ that crosses the y-axis at (0, –4). **[1]**

**3.** (non-calculator) A straight line drawn on a grid crosses the y-axis at (0, 2) and passes through the grid point (3, –4). Find the equation of the line. **[2]**

**4.** (non-calculator) Write down the equation of

**(a)** the x-axis, **[1]**

**(b)** the vertical line through (7, –1). **[1]**

**5.** (non-calculator) The point (k, 11) lies on the line y = 4x – 5. Find the value of k. **[2]**

**6.** (Extended) (non-calculator) Find the gradient and the y-intercept of the line 6x + 3y = 15. **[2]**

**7.** (Extended) (non-calculator) Find the gradient and the y-intercept of the line 2x – 5y = 10. **[2]**

## Section B

**8.** (calculator) A phone plan costs C dollars per month for m minutes of calls, where C = 0.08m + 12.

**(a)** State what the number 0.08 represents. **[1]**

**(b)** State what the number 12 represents. **[1]**

**(c)** Find the cost of 250 minutes of calls in a month. **[1]**

**(d)** One month the cost was $30.40. Find the number of minutes used. **[2]**

**9.** (non-calculator) A straight line drawn on a grid passes through the grid points (–2, 8), (0, 5) and (4, –1).

**(a)** Find the equation of the line in the form y = mx + c. **[3]**

**(b)** Find the x-coordinate of the point where the line crosses the x-axis. **[1]**

**10.** (Extended) (non-calculator) A line passes through (–3, –1) and (5, 3).

**(a)** Calculate its gradient. **[1]**

**(b)** Find its equation in the form y = mx + c. **[2]**

**(c)** Write the equation in the form ax + by = c, where a, b and c are integers. **[1]**

**11.** (Extended) (non-calculator) A line has equation 3x + 2y = 12.

**(a)** Find the coordinates of the points where the line crosses the x-axis and the y-axis. **[2]**

**(b)** Find the gradient of the line. **[1]**

**12.** (non-calculator) Water drains steadily from a tank. A straight-line graph of depth D cm against time t minutes passes through (0, 90) and (20, 60).

**(a)** Find the equation of the line in the form D = mt + c. **[2]**

**(b)** Explain what the gradient tells you about the water. **[1]**

**(c)** Find the time at which the tank is empty. **[1]**

**13.** (Extended) (non-calculator) Line L passes through (2, 9) and has the same y-intercept as the line y = 5x – 3. Find the equation of L. **[3]**

## Answers

**1.** Gradient **–3** [1]; y-intercept **8** [1]

**2.** **y = ½x – 4** [1]

**3.** m = (–4 – 2)/3 = –2 [1]; **y = –2x + 2** [1]

**4. (a)** **y = 0** [1]

**(b)** **x = 7** [1]

*Examiner insight:* the x-axis is where every y-coordinate is 0, so its equation is y = 0, not x = 0.

**5.** 11 = 4k – 5 [1]; **k = 4** [1]

**6.** 3y = –6x + 15, so y = –2x + 5 [1]; gradient **–2**, y-intercept **5** [1]

**7.** –5y = 10 – 2x, so y = 0.4x – 2 [1]; gradient **0.4** (or 2/5), y-intercept **–2** [1]

*Examiner insight:* reading the gradient as 2 from the original equation is the usual error; the coefficient of x is the gradient only when y is the subject.

**8. (a)** The **cost per minute**: 8 cents ($0.08) for each minute [1]

**(b)** The **fixed monthly charge** of $12, paid even with no calls [1]

**(c)** 0.08 × 250 + 12 = **$32** [1]

**(d)** 0.08m = 30.40 – 12 = 18.40 [1]; m = 18.40 ÷ 0.08 = **230 minutes** [1]

**9. (a)** c = 5 [1]; m = (5 – 8)/(0 – (–2)) = –3/2 [1]; **y = –1.5x + 5** (or y = –(3/2)x + 5) [1]

**(b)** 0 = –1.5x + 5, so x = 5 ÷ 1.5 = **10/3** (3⅓) [1]

*Examiner insight:* check with (4, –1): –1.5 × 4 + 5 = –1. Every given point must satisfy your equation.

**10. (a)** (3 – (–1))/(5 – (–3)) = 4/8 = **½** [1]

**(b)** 3 = ½(5) + c, so c = ½ [1]; **y = ½x + ½** [1]

**(c)** 2y = x + 1, so **x – 2y = –1** (or –x + 2y = 1) [1]

**11. (a)** y = 0 gives x = 4: **(4, 0)** [1]; x = 0 gives y = 6: **(0, 6)** [1]

**(b)** From (0, 6) to (4, 0): –6/4 = **–3/2** [1]

**12. (a)** c = 90 and m = (60 – 90)/20 = –1.5 [1]; **D = –1.5t + 90** [1]

**(b)** The depth **falls by 1.5 cm every minute** [1]

**(c)** –1.5t + 90 = 0, so t = **60 minutes** [1]

*Examiner insight:* "the gradient is negative" restates the graph; the mark needs the rate, its units and the direction of change.

**13.** c = –3 [1]; 9 = 2m – 3, so m = 6 [1]; **y = 6x – 3** [1]

## Where marks are usually lost

- Reading the gradient from ax + by = c without rearranging.
- Confusing the y-intercept with the x-intercept.
- Giving m and c but not the equation.
- Leaving fractions unsimplified.
- Describing a gradient in context without units or direction.
- Writing x = 0 for the x-axis.
