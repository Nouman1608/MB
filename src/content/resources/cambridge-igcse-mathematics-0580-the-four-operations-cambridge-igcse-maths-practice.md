These questions cover subtopic 1.6 The four operations in the Cambridge IGCSE Mathematics 0580 syllabus for 2025, 2026 and 2027. Core (C1.6) and Extended (E1.6) content is identical, so every question suits both tiers. All questions are non-calculator: show your working, and give fractions in their simplest form.

**10.** (non-calculator) At midnight the temperature was −7.5 °C. By 10 am it had risen by 11.2 °C. By 10 pm it had fallen by 6.8 °C from its 10 am value.

**(a)** Find the temperature at 10 am. **[1]**

**(b)** Find the temperature at 10 pm. **[1]**

**11.** (non-calculator) A water tank holds 240 litres when full. It is 5/8 full. Then 2/5 of the water in the tank is used.

**(a)** How many litres remain in the tank? **[2]**

**(b)** What fraction of the full tank is this? Give your answer in its simplest form. **[1]**

**12.** (non-calculator) Noor's bank account is overdrawn: its balance is −$38.40. She pays in $125.75, then pays three bills of $29.95 each. Find the final balance. **[3]**

**13.** (non-calculator) Show that (2.5 × 1.2 − 0.6) ÷ 0.08 = 30. **[3]**

**14.** (non-calculator) Work out (6² − 4 × 3) / (−2 − 4). **[2]**

## Answers

**1. (a)** 7 + 18 = **25** [1]

**(b)** 48 ÷ 8 = 6, 6 × 3 = **18** [1]

**(c)** 9 − 16 = −7, 5 × (−7) = **−35** [1]

*Examiner insight:* in (b), division and multiplication are worked left to right, so 48 ÷ 24 = 2 is wrong.

**2. (a)** **5** [1]

**(b)** **−42** [1]

**(c)** 5 − 8 = **−3** [1]

**3.** 8/18 + 3/18 [1] = **11/18** [1]

**4. (a)** 7 × 6 = 42, three decimal places: **0.042** [1]

**(b)** 630 ÷ 9 = **70** [1]

*Examiner insight:* scaling both numbers by 100 keeps the answer unchanged; 0.7 or 7 shows the scale was applied to only one number.

**5.** **3 + 5 × (4 − 2) = 13** [1]

**6.** 7/4 × 12/5 = 84/20 [1] = 21/5 = **4⅕** [1]

**7.** 25/6 − 11/4 [1] = 50/12 − 33/12 [1] = 17/12 = **1 5/12** [1]

*Examiner insight:* subtracting whole numbers first gives 2 + (1/6 − 3/4), where the fraction part is negative; take care or use improper fractions.

**8.** 18/5 ÷ 9/4 [1] = 18/5 × 4/9 [1] = 72/45 = 8/5 = **1⅗** [1]

**9. (a)** 476 × 30 = 14 280 and 476 × 8 = 3808 [1]; **18 088** [1]

**(b)** 24 × 100 = 2400, remainder 552; 24 × 23 = 552 [1]; **123** [1]

**10. (a)** −7.5 + 11.2 = **3.7 °C** [1]

**(b)** 3.7 − 6.8 = **−3.1 °C** [1]

**(c)** 3.7 − (−7.5) = **11.2** degrees [1]

*Examiner insight:* in (b), start from the 10 am value, not the midnight value; the question says "from its 10 am value".

**11. (a)** 5/8 × 240 = 150 litres in the tank [1]; 2/5 × 150 = 60 used, so **90 litres** remain [1]

**(b)** 90/240 = **3/8** [1]

**12.** −38.40 + 125.75 = 87.35 [1]; bills 3 × 29.95 = 89.85 [1]; 87.35 − 89.85 = **−$2.50** (overdrawn by $2.50) [1]

*Examiner insight:* 3 × 29.95 is quickest as 3 × 30 − 3 × 0.05 = 90 − 0.15 = 89.85.

**13.** 2.5 × 1.2 = 3 [1]; 3 − 0.6 = 2.4 [1]; 2.4 ÷ 0.08 = 240 ÷ 8 = 30 [1]

*Examiner insight:* in a "show that", each value must be seen; writing only "= 30" earns nothing.

**14.** Numerator 36 − 12 = 24 and denominator −6 [1]; 24 ÷ (−6) = **−4** [1]

## Where marks are usually lost

- Working strictly left to right instead of following the order of operations.
- Sign errors when subtracting or dividing negatives.
- Adding fractions without a common denominator, or not converting mixed numbers before × and ÷.
- Misplacing the decimal point in decimal multiplication or division.
- In multi-step contexts, starting a later step from the wrong value.

## Approaching four operations questions

Write one operation per line. For fractions, convert mixed numbers immediately and cancel before multiplying; for decimals, count decimal places or scale before dividing. In practical questions, underline the starting value and each change, and check the final answer against a rough estimate.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is −$2.50 an acceptable way to write an overdrawn balance?**
Yes. A negative balance shows the account is overdrawn; "overdrawn by $2.50" is equally clear. Always include the units.
