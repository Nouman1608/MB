## Section A

**1.** Which change would make the rate of diffusion of a gas across a membrane increase?

A: a smaller surface area

B: a lower temperature

C: a steeper concentration gradient

D: a thicker membrane

**[1]**

**2.** A plant cell is placed in a very concentrated sugar solution. What is the cell called after it has lost so much water that the membrane pulls away from the wall?

A: flaccid

B: plasmolysed

C: turgid

D: ruptured

**[1]**

**3.** Define diffusion. **[2]**

**4.** State **two** roles of water as a solvent in organisms. **[2]**

**5.** Explain why a plant that is not watered wilts. **[3]**

## Section B

**6.** A student placed cylinders of potato in sucrose solutions for one hour. The table shows the results.

| Sucrose (mol per dm cubed) | Start mass (g) | End mass (g) |
|---|---|---|
| 0.0 | 2.50 | 2.85 |
| 0.2 | 2.60 | 2.60 |
| 0.4 | 2.40 | 2.16 |

**(a)** Calculate the percentage change in mass for the cylinder in 0.4 mol per dm cubed sucrose. Show your working. **[2]**

**(b)** Explain why the cylinder in pure water gained mass. **[3]**

**(c)** State the concentration of sucrose that has the same water potential as the potato cells, and explain your choice. **[2]**

**(d)** State **two** variables the student should keep the same. **[2]**

**7.** Three agar cubes with sides of 1 cm, 2 cm and 4 cm were placed in dilute acid.

**(a)** Calculate the surface area to volume ratio of the 4 cm cube. **[2]**

**(b)** Explain why the 1 cm cube changes colour all the way through more quickly than the 4 cm cube. **[3]**

**8.** A student fills a dialysis tubing bag with starch and glucose solution and places it in a beaker of water. After 30 minutes the water is tested.

**(a)** State the result expected when iodine solution is added to the water. **[1]**

**(b)** Describe the test that shows glucose is present in the water, and give the positive result. **[2]**

**(c)** Explain what these results show about dialysis tubing. **[2]**

**9.** Explain why a red blood cell in pure water bursts but a plant root hair cell in pure water does not. **[4]**

**10.** Describe the effect of temperature on the rate of diffusion and explain the effect in terms of kinetic energy. **[3]**

## Answers

**1.** C [1]. **[1]**

**2.** B [1]. **[1]**

**3.** The net movement of molecules or ions from a region of higher concentration to a region of lower concentration [1], down a concentration gradient, as a result of random movement [1]. **[2]**

**4.** Any two of: digestion, because enzymes and food are dissolved in water [1]; excretion, because urea dissolves in water and leaves in urine [1]; transport, because substances dissolve in water in blood or in sap [1]. **[2]**

**5.** Without water the cells lose water [1]. The cells stop being turgid and become flaccid, so the turgor pressure falls [1]. The plant is no longer supported by the pressure of water pushing on the cell walls, so it wilts [1]. **[3]**

**6.**

**(a)** Change = 2.16 - 2.40 = -0.24 g [1]. Percentage change = -0.24 divided by 2.40 x 100 = -10.0 per cent [1].

**(b)** The water potential outside is higher than inside the potato cells [1]. Water moves into the cells by osmosis [1], through the partially permeable membrane, so the mass increases [1].

**(c)** 0.2 mol per dm cubed [1], because the mass did not change, which means there was no net movement of water [1].

**(d)** Any two of: size of cylinder; volume of solution; time in solution; temperature; method of drying [1 each]. **[9]**

**7.**

**(a)** Surface area = 6 x 4 x 4 = 96 cm squared; volume = 4 x 4 x 4 = 64 cm cubed [1]. Ratio = 96 : 64 = 1.5 : 1 [1].

**(b)** The 1 cm cube has a ratio of 6 : 1 and the 4 cm cube has 1.5 : 1, so the small cube has a larger surface area for its volume [1]. Acid has a shorter distance to diffuse to the centre [1], so the whole cube is reached more quickly [1]. **[5]**

**8.**

**(a)** No colour change; it stays orange-brown [1].

**(b)** Add Benedict's solution and heat [1]. The blue solution turns green, yellow or orange-red [1].

**(c)** Glucose molecules are small enough to pass through the tubing, but starch molecules are too large [1]. The tubing is partially permeable [1]. **[5]**

**9.** In pure water the water potential outside is higher than inside the cell, so water enters both cells by osmosis [1]. The red blood cell has no cell wall [1], so it swells until the membrane bursts [1]. The root hair cell has a strong cellulose cell wall that resists expansion, so it becomes turgid instead of bursting [1]. **[4]**

**10.** As temperature rises, the rate of diffusion increases [1]. The particles have more kinetic energy [1] and so move faster, giving more movement down the concentration gradient in the same time [1]. **[3]**

## Where marks are usually lost

- Leaving out the **partially permeable membrane** when explaining osmosis.
- Confusing a flaccid cell with a plasmolysed cell.
- Dividing by the end mass instead of the start mass in percentage change.
- Writing that diffusion uses energy from respiration.
- Describing the iodine and Benedict's results the wrong way round.
