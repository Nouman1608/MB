## Section A - short questions

**1.** Imported goods are stored under customs control and import duty is paid only when they are released. This is a: **[1]**
A cold store   B bonded warehouse   C cash and carry   D regional distribution centre

**2.** Which is a use of automation in a warehouse? **[1]**
A bulk breaking   B voice picking   C trade credit   D import duty

**3.** A grain merchant stores wheat after the harvest and sells it throughout the year. Which function of warehousing does this show? **[1]**
A logistics postponement   B protecting supply   C link with seasonal production   D automated sorting

**4.** State **two** types of goods that need cold storage. **[2]**

**5.** Define logistics postponement. **[2]**

**6.** Identify **two** benefits to a business of warehouse automation. **[2]**

## Section B - structured questions

**7.** *Green Valley Farms harvests apples in autumn. It stores them in a warehouse and sells them until the following summer.*
Explain **two** ways in which warehousing helps Green Valley Farms. **[4]**

**8.** Describe **two** differences between a cash and carry warehouse and a regional distribution centre. **[4]**

**9.** Explain how a bonded warehouse helps an importer of wine. **[4]**

**10.** *A sports retailer's warehouse employs 10 pickers at $80 a day each. A new automated sorting system would let it use 4 pickers, but running the system costs $260 a day.*
(a) Calculate the daily labour cost before automation. **[1]**
(b) Calculate the daily cost with automation (labour plus running cost). **[2]**
(c) Calculate the daily saving. **[1]**
(d) State **two** other benefits, not about cost, that automation might bring. **[2]**

**11.** *Brightway Foods supplies 60 supermarkets in a region. It delivers to a regional distribution centre, not to each shop.*
(a) Explain **two** reasons why the supermarkets benefit from using a regional distribution centre. **[4]**
(b) Explain **one** way in which the warehouse helps to protect supply to the shops. **[2]**

**12.** Evaluate whether a small firm should invest in warehouse automation. **[6]**

## Answers

**1.** B. Customs control and delayed duty identify a bonded warehouse. **[1]**

**2.** B. Voice picking is one of the three uses, with robotics and automated sorting. **[1]**

**3.** C. Production is seasonal, while sales continue all year. **[1]**

**4.** Any two: meat [1]; fish [1]; dairy products [1]; fruit and vegetables [1]; medicines [1]; frozen foods [1]. **[max 2]**

**5.** Delaying the final stage such as packing, labelling or assembly [1] until an order is known [1]. **[2]**

**6.** Any two: lower labour costs [1]; less human error [1]; improved picking accuracy [1]; improved inventory control [1]; improved customer service [1]. **[max 2]**

**7.** Any two, 2 marks each:
Seasonal production: apples are picked at one time of year [1], so storing them lets the farm sell all year instead of only in autumn [1].
Price stability: if all apples were sold at harvest, prices would fall [1], so storing them and releasing them later helps to keep a better price [1].
Cold storage: low temperatures keep apples fresh for months [1], so fewer are wasted [1].
Demand: stock can be released when demand is highest [1], so the farm earns more [1]. **[max 4]**

**8.** Any two differences, 2 marks each (both sides stated):
Customers: cash and carry sells to trade customers who walk in and choose goods [1]; an RDC supplies the chain's own shops and does not sell to the public or other traders [1].
Payment: cash and carry customers pay immediately [1]; an RDC's shops are supplied within the same business and settle through the company accounts [1].
Delivery: cash and carry customers collect their own goods [1]; an RDC sends goods out to the shops on its own transport [1].
Purpose: cash and carry is a low-price wholesale outlet [1]; an RDC is a central hub that sorts and distributes stock to shops [1]. **[max 4]**

**9.** Goods imported from abroad are normally taxed with import duty when they arrive [1]. In a bonded warehouse the wine is stored under customs control and the duty is delayed until the wine is released for sale [1]. The importer does not have to pay a large tax bill before it has sold any wine [1], which improves its cash flow (or, if the wine is re-exported, no duty may be payable) [1]. **[4]**

**10.** (a) 10 x 80 = **$800** [1]. (1 mark)
(b) Labour: 4 x 80 = 320 [1]; plus running cost 320 + 260 = **$580** [1]. (2 marks)
(c) 800 - 580 = **$220** per day [1]. (1 mark)
(d) Any two: fewer errors in sorting [1]; faster delivery to customers [1]; better stock records [1]; improved customer service [1]. (2 marks) **[6]**

**11.** (a) Any two, 2 marks each: each shop receives one delivery of mixed goods instead of many separate ones [1], which saves unloading time and staff costs [1]; the RDC can send goods in the quantities each shop needs [1], so shops hold less stock and need less storage space [1]; regular deliveries from the RDC keep shelves filled [1], so fewer sales are lost [1]. (max 4 marks)
(b) The RDC holds a reserve of stock [1], so the shops can still be supplied if a supplier is late or a delivery is delayed [1]. (2 marks) **[6]**

**12.** Up to 2 for benefits, up to 2 for limits, up to 2 for a justified conclusion.
Benefits: lower labour costs [1]; fewer errors and better accuracy [1]; improved stock control and customer service [1].
Limits: equipment, installation and training costs are high for a small firm [1]; low order volumes may mean the machines are not used enough to repay the cost [1]; a breakdown could stop the warehouse [1].
Conclusion: for example, a small firm with high labour costs and many orders may gain, but one with few orders should use cheaper options such as barcode scanners first [2]. **[6]**

## Where marks are usually lost

- Writing "stores goods" with no named function.
- Treating a bonded warehouse as a warehouse that simply holds imports, with no mention of customs duty.
- Saying cash and carry delivers goods.
- Listing benefits of automation without saying how the machine causes them.
- In calculations, adding only the running cost and forgetting the remaining wages.

## Frequently asked questions

**What are the four types of warehouse in 0715?** Bonded, cold storage, cash and carry and regional distribution centres.

**What are the three uses of automation?** Robotics, automated sorting and voice picking.
