These questions cover section **3.1.2.1 Relative atomic mass and relative molecular mass** of the AQA AS and A-level Chemistry specification (7404/7405), for teaching from September 2015. It is AS content, examined on Paper 1. Learn the ideas first in the [Relative atomic mass and relative molecular mass study guide](/resources/aqa-a-level-chemistry-7405-relative-atomic-mass-and-relative-molecular-mass/) and the [revision notes](/resources/aqa-a-level-chemistry-7405-relative-atomic-mass-and-relative-molecular-mass-revision-notes/). Use Aᵣ values H = 1.0, C = 12.0, N = 14.0, O = 16.0, Na = 23.0, P = 31.0, S = 32.1, Cl = 35.5, Ca = 40.1, Co = 58.9, Pb = 207.2.

## Questions

**1.** Define the term *relative molecular mass*. **[2]**

**2.** Bromine has two isotopes, of mass 79 and 81. Explain why the relative atomic mass of bromine is 79.9 and not a whole number. **[2]**

**3.** State the correct term for the relative mass of each substance.

**(a)** methane, CH₄ **[1]**

**(b)** calcium chloride, CaCl₂ **[1]**

**4.** Calculate the relative formula mass of each compound.

**(a)** ammonium phosphate, (NH₄)₃PO₄ **[1]**

**(b)** lead(II) nitrate, Pb(NO₃)₂ **[1]**

**(c)** hydrated cobalt(II) chloride, CoCl₂·6H₂O **[1]**

**5.** Aspirin has the molecular formula C₉H₈O₄.

**(a)** Calculate its relative molecular mass. **[1]**

**(b)** Calculate the percentage by mass of oxygen in aspirin. Give your answer to 3 significant figures. **[1]**

**6.** Calculate the percentage by mass of water in hydrated sodium sulfate, Na₂SO₄·10H₂O. Give your answer to 3 significant figures. **[2]**

**7.** A metal oxide has the formula M₂O₃ and a relative formula mass of 152.0. Calculate the relative atomic mass of M and identify the metal. **[2]**

**8.** Hydrated calcium chloride, CaCl₂·xH₂O, has a relative formula mass of 219.1. Calculate the value of x. **[2]**

**9.** An alkane has the general formula CₙH₂ₙ₊₂ and a relative molecular mass of 72.0. Deduce its molecular formula. **[2]**

**10.** Two fertilisers are ammonium nitrate, NH₄NO₃, and ammonium chloride, NH₄Cl. Show, by calculation, which contains the greater percentage by mass of nitrogen. **[3]**

**11.** A student writes: "Relative atomic mass is the mass of an atom compared with carbon." Give two changes needed to make this a full definition. **[2]**

**12.** The mass of one atom of carbon-12 is 1.993 × 10⁻²³ g.

**(a)** The average mass of an atom of element Z is 3.82 × 10⁻²³ g. Calculate the relative atomic mass of Z and identify it. **[3]**

**(b)** The average mass of one molecule of a gas is 7.31 × 10⁻²³ g. Calculate its relative molecular mass. **[1]**

## Answers

**1.** The average mass of a molecule [1] compared with one twelfth of the mass of an atom of carbon-12 [1]. **[2]**

*Examiner insight:* "Compared with carbon-12" without "one twelfth" gives a value twelve times too small, so the second mark is lost.

**2.** Bromine is a mixture of isotopes of mass 79 and 81 [1]; Aᵣ is the weighted mean of their masses according to their abundances, so it lies between them [1]. **[2]**

**3. (a)** Relative molecular mass [1]

**(b)** Relative formula mass, because calcium chloride is ionic [1]. **[2]**

**4. (a)** 3 × (14.0 + 4.0) + 31.0 + 4 × 16.0 = **149.0** [1]

**(b)** 207.2 + 2 × (14.0 + 48.0) = **331.2** [1]

**(c)** 58.9 + 71.0 + 6 × 18.0 = **237.9** [1]. **[3]**

*Examiner insight:* In (a) the bracket multiplies both N and H; a total of 113.0 shows that only one NH₄ was counted, and 141.0 shows the subscript applied to N only.

**5. (a)** 9 × 12.0 + 8 × 1.0 + 4 × 16.0 = **180.0** [1]

**(b)** 64.0 ÷ 180.0 × 100 = **35.6%** [1]. **[2]**

**6.** Na₂SO₄ = 46.0 + 32.1 + 64.0 = 142.1, so formula mass = 142.1 + 180.0 = 322.1 [1]; % water = 180.0 ÷ 322.1 × 100 = **55.9%** [1]. **[2]**

*Examiner insight:* Dividing 180.0 by 142.1 (the anhydrous salt) gives 127%, which cannot be a percentage of the whole.

**7.** 2 × Aᵣ(M) = 152.0 − 48.0 = 104.0, so Aᵣ(M) = 52.0 [1]; M is **chromium** [1]. **[2]**

**8.** CaCl₂ = 40.1 + 71.0 = 111.1 [1]; x = (219.1 − 111.1) ÷ 18.0 = **6** [1]. **[2]**

**9.** 12.0n + 1.0(2n + 2) = 72.0, so 14n + 2 = 72 [1]; n = 5, so **C₅H₁₂** [1]. **[2]**

**10.** NH₄NO₃: Mᵣ = 80.0, % N = 28.0 ÷ 80.0 × 100 = 35.0% [1]. NH₄Cl: Mᵣ = 53.5, % N = 14.0 ÷ 53.5 × 100 = 26.2% [1]. Ammonium nitrate has the greater percentage of nitrogen [1]. **[3]**

*Examiner insight:* Ammonium nitrate has two nitrogen atoms; using 14.0 instead of 28.0 gives 17.5% and the wrong conclusion.

**11.** Any two: say "average" (or weighted mean) mass of an atom [1]; compare with one twelfth of the mass [1]; specify an atom of carbon-12, not just carbon [1]. **[2]**

**12. (a)** One twelfth of the mass of a carbon-12 atom = 1.993 × 10⁻²³ ÷ 12 = 1.661 × 10⁻²⁴ g [1]; Aᵣ = 3.82 × 10⁻²³ ÷ 1.661 × 10⁻²⁴ = **23.0** [1]; Z is **sodium** [1]

**(b)** 7.31 × 10⁻²³ ÷ 1.661 × 10⁻²⁴ = **44.0** [1]. **[4]**

*Examiner insight:* This question applies the definition directly. Dividing by the whole mass of the carbon-12 atom, not one twelfth of it, gives 1.92.

## Where marks are usually lost

- Definitions without "average", "one twelfth" or "carbon-12".
- Calling an ionic compound's relative mass a relative molecular mass.
- Brackets applied to only the first atom inside.
- Multiplying by the water in a hydrate instead of adding it.
- Forgetting that a formula contains more than one atom of the element in percentage calculations.
