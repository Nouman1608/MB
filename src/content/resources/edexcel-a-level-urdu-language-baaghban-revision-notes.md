Revision notes for **5.5** in the prescribed literary texts and films of Pearson Edexcel A Level Urdu (9UR0), Paper 2. For the full explanation of the paper, read the [Translation into Urdu and Written Response to Works study guide](/resources/a-level-edexcel-urdu-translation-and-works/) and the [Paper 2 revision notes](/resources/edexcel-a-level-urdu-translation-works-revision-notes/).

## The work

- **Title:** باغبان (*Baaghban*)
- **Name given in the specification:** روی چوپڑہ (Ravi Chopra)
- **Year:** 2003
- **Form:** a prescribed film in Appendix 1.

The specification gives each film as a name and a year only, with no role label, so do not write a name in as 'director' on the strength of the list alone.

## What the film is about

*Baaghban* is a Hindi-language family drama. Its central couple, **راج** (Raj) and **پوجا** (Pooja) Malhotra, played by Amitabh Bachchan and Hema Malini, have devoted their lives to bringing up four sons. When Raj retires, the couple hope their children will support them in old age. Instead, the sons treat them as a burden and arrange for the parents to live apart, each with a different son. The separation, the couple's loyalty to each other, and the contrast between the sons and **آلوک** (Alok), a young man the couple had cared for as a boy and who treats them with real devotion, drive the story. Raj turns his experience into a book, and its success allows him to speak publicly about the duty of children towards their parents.

Use this outline only for orientation. In the exam you need your own specific references to scenes and moments, gathered from watching the film closely.

## The title

**باغبان** means 'gardener'. The image suggests parents as gardeners who plant, water and protect their children as a gardener tends trees, expecting that the trees will one day give shade and fruit. Ask whether the film shows this expectation as fair, and what happens when it is disappointed.

## Key themes and issues

- **Duty to parents and elders**: the film's central question is what children owe to parents who sacrificed for them. Link this directly to Theme 1, sub-theme 1.1, [family](/resources/edexcel-a-level-urdu-language-family-revision-notes/).
- **Joint family and changing values**: the sons' attitudes reflect a shift towards independence, money and personal comfort; the film tends to present this shift critically.
- **Marriage as companionship**: Raj and Pooja's relationship, tested by separation, is presented as an ideal of lifelong partnership.
- **Gratitude and ingratitude**: the contrast between the sons and Alok suggests that loyalty is shown through actions, not only through blood ties.
- **Old age and independence**: Raj's financial dependence after retirement raises the question of how elderly people can keep their dignity.

## Form and technique

- **Melodrama (جذباتیت)**: strong emotions, clear moral contrasts and music are used to move the audience. You may evaluate this: does it make the message powerful, or one-sided?
- **Songs (گانے)**: as in much Indian cinema, songs express feelings that dialogue does not, and mark key moments of joy or separation.
- **Contrast and parallel scenes**: scenes of the parents apart, and of the sons' comfortable lives, are set against each other to guide the audience's sympathy.
- **Final speech**: the film ends with a public statement of its message. Consider whether this direct approach strengthens or weakens the film.
- **Acting and casting**: two very well-known stars in the parents' roles give the story weight and draw the audience's sympathy.

## How it is examined

- Paper 2 is 2 hours 40 minutes, 110 marks, 30% of the A Level.
- Section C (film) is worth 45 marks and about 1 hour 5 minutes; you choose from two questions.
- You write exactly **two** extended responses: two literary texts, or one literary text and one film. Two films is never allowed.
- Marking criteria: critical and analytical response with cultural or social insight (AO4), range of grammatical structures and vocabulary (AO3), and accuracy of language (AO3). There are no AO1 or AO2 marks on Paper 2.

## Approach and skills

- Prepare against five angles: **form and technique, key themes and issues, characterisation, plot structure, social and cultural setting**.
- Structure each answer as **position, evidence, cultural connection**: state your stance, support it with two or three specific points (not a retelling), then link the writer's or film's treatment of an issue to a broader social or cultural theme.
- Write in controlled Urdu with correct agreement, tense and spelling, and plan before you write.
- Use your own prepared references from the work; this page does not replace studying it.

## Useful sentences with grammar notes

1. **فلم میں والدین اور اولاد کے رشتے کو مرکزی موضوع بنایا گیا ہے۔** (The film makes the relationship between parents and children its central theme.) Passive with کو, so masculine singular: بنایا گیا ہے.
2. **بیٹے اپنے بوڑھے والدین کو بوجھ سمجھنے لگتے ہیں۔** (The sons begin to regard their elderly parents as a burden.) **سمجھنے لگنا** means 'to begin to regard'.
3. **راج اور پوجا کو مجبوراً الگ الگ گھروں میں رہنا پڑتا ہے۔** (Raj and Pooja are forced to live in separate houses.) Obligation with کو + infinitive + پڑنا.
4. **فلم کا اختتام کچھ زیادہ جذباتی ہے، مگر اس کا پیغام واضح ہے۔** (The film's ending is rather too emotional, but its message is clear.) A balanced evaluation in one sentence.

## Model paragraph

Task: how does the film present the relationship between parents and children?

فلم "باغبان" میں والدین اور اولاد کے رشتے کو مرکزی موضوع بنایا گیا ہے۔ راج اور پوجا ساری زندگی اپنے بیٹوں کی پرورش اور تعلیم کے لیے قربانیاں دیتے ہیں، لیکن ریٹائرمنٹ کے بعد جب انھیں سہارے کی ضرورت ہوتی ہے تو بیٹے انھیں بوجھ سمجھنے لگتے ہیں اور دونوں کو الگ الگ گھروں میں رہنا پڑتا ہے۔ فلم کا عنوان بھی معنی خیز ہے، کیونکہ باغبان کی طرح والدین بچوں کو پروان چڑھاتے ہیں مگر بدلے میں ہمیشہ پھل نہیں پاتے۔ میرے خیال میں یہ فلم برصغیر کے خاندانی نظام میں آنے والی تبدیلیوں پر ایک سوال اٹھاتی ہے: کیا جدید زندگی میں بزرگوں کی عزت اور دیکھ بھال کی روایت کمزور ہو رہی ہے؟

**Translation:** In the film *Baaghban*, the relationship between parents and children is the central theme. Raj and Pooja make sacrifices all their lives for their sons' upbringing and education, but after retirement, when they need support, the sons begin to see them as a burden and the two have to live in separate houses. The film's title is also meaningful, because like a gardener, parents raise their children but do not always receive fruit in return. In my view the film raises a question about the changes taking place in the family system of the subcontinent: is the tradition of respecting and caring for elders becoming weaker in modern life?

**Why it works:** a clear position, evidence from the story, analysis of the title, and a cultural connection to the joint family system. In a real answer, add one or two specific scenes and comment on a technique such as music or contrast.

## Essay prompts to prepare

- «فلم "باغبان" میں بزرگوں کے ساتھ سلوک کی عکاسی کا تنقیدی جائزہ لیجیے۔» (Critically evaluate the portrayal of the treatment of elders in *Baaghban*.)
- «"باغبان" کا پیغام آج کے معاشرے کے لیے پہلے سے زیادہ اہم ہے۔» (The message of *Baaghban* matters more to today's society than ever.) To what extent do you agree?
- How does the film use the character of Alok to develop its themes?
- Is the film's emotional style a strength or a weakness?

## Exam traps

- Retelling the story without connecting it to a theme weakens the AO4 mark.
- Attempting three responses, or choosing two films.
- Running over time on the first response and squeezing the second.
- Calling Ravi Chopra the director on the strength of the specification list alone.
- Analysing only the story and never the film techniques (music, contrast, acting, the final speech).
- Writing that all young people are ungrateful; the film itself offers Alok as a counter-example.
