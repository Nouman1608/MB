## The ten formulas

Only the syllabus formulas are accepted.

**Profitability**

| Ratio | Formula | Unit |
|---|---|---|
| Gross profit margin | gross profit ÷ revenue × 100 | % |
| Mark-up | gross profit ÷ cost of sales × 100 | % |
| Profit margin | profit for the year ÷ revenue × 100 | % |
| Return on capital employed | profit for the year before interest ÷ capital employed × 100 | % |

Capital employed = issued shares + reserves + non-current liabilities.

**Liquidity**

| Ratio | Formula | Unit |
|---|---|---|
| Current (working capital) ratio | current assets ÷ current liabilities | x : 1 |
| Acid test (liquid) ratio | (current assets − inventory) ÷ current liabilities | x : 1 |

**Efficiency**

| Ratio | Formula | Unit |
|---|---|---|
| Rate of inventory turnover | cost of sales ÷ average inventory | times |
| Inventory turnover | average inventory ÷ cost of sales × 365 | days |
| Trade receivables turnover | trade receivables ÷ credit sales × 365 | days |
| Trade payables turnover | trade payables × 365 ÷ credit purchases | days |

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Average inventory | (opening inventory + closing inventory) ÷ 2 |
| Credit sales | Revenue less cash sales |
| Credit purchases | Purchases less cash purchases |
| Profit before interest | Profit for the year + interest on non-current liabilities |
| Liquid assets | Current assets less inventory |
| Working capital | Current assets less current liabilities |

## Where each figure comes from

| Figure | Source |
|---|---|
| Revenue, cost of sales, gross profit | Statement of profit or loss, trading section |
| Profit for the year | Statement of profit or loss, bottom line |
| Interest on non-current liabilities | Statement of profit or loss (finance costs or expenses) |
| Opening and closing inventory | Cost of sales calculation (closing also in current assets) |
| Trade receivables, trade payables | Statement of financial position, current items |
| Current assets and current liabilities | Statement of financial position totals |
| Issued shares, reserves, non-current liabilities | Statement of financial position, equity and liabilities |
| Credit sales and credit purchases | Usually given in the question, or total less cash |

## What each ratio tells you

- **Gross profit margin / mark-up:** how much gross profit the business makes on its
  trading; driven by selling prices and purchase costs.
- **Profit margin:** how well expenses are controlled as well as trading.
- **ROCE:** how efficiently all long-term finance is used to earn profit.
- **Current ratio:** whether current assets cover current liabilities.
- **Acid test:** the same, without relying on selling inventory.
- **Inventory turnover:** how quickly goods sell; days = 365 ÷ rate.
- **Receivables days:** how quickly customers pay.
- **Payables days:** how quickly the business pays suppliers.

## Rearrangements

| To find | Use |
|---|---|
| Current assets | current ratio × current liabilities |
| Inventory | current assets − (acid test × current liabilities) |
| Trade receivables | credit sales × days ÷ 365 |
| Trade payables | credit purchases × days ÷ 365 |
| Average inventory | cost of sales ÷ rate of inventory turnover |
| Mark-up from margin | margin ÷ (100 − margin) × 100 |
| Margin from mark-up | mark-up ÷ (100 + mark-up) × 100 |

## Worked reminder

Revenue 240,000 (cash sales 40,000); cost of sales 150,000; opening inventory 18,000;
closing inventory 22,000; trade receivables 25,000.

```
Gross profit margin    90,000 ÷ 240,000 × 100       = 37.50%
Mark-up                90,000 ÷ 150,000 × 100       = 60.00%
Average inventory      (18,000 + 22,000) ÷ 2        = 20,000
Rate of turnover       150,000 ÷ 20,000             = 7.50 times
Receivables days       25,000 ÷ 200,000 × 365       = 45.63 days
```

## Exam traps

- **Average**, not closing, inventory in the two turnover ratios.
- **Credit** figures, not totals, in receivables and payables days.
- **Trade** balances only; leave out other receivables and other payables.
- **Add back interest** on non-current liabilities for ROCE.
- **Units:** %, x : 1, times, days. A missing unit can cost the mark.
- **Two decimal places** unless told otherwise; round only at the end.
- **Mark-up > margin** always, for the same gross profit.

## Self-test

1. State the formula for the acid test ratio.
2. Revenue 60,000; gross profit 15,000. Calculate the gross profit margin.
3. Using the figures in question 2, calculate the mark-up.
4. Profit for the year 18,000; debenture interest 2,000; capital employed 160,000.
   Calculate ROCE.
5. Current assets 42,000; current liabilities 20,000. Calculate the current ratio.
6. Opening inventory 9,000; closing 11,000; cost of sales 80,000. Calculate the rate
   of inventory turnover.
7. Using question 6, calculate inventory turnover in days.
8. Trade payables 14,000; credit purchases 127,750. Calculate payables days.
9. Which figure is excluded from current assets in the acid test?
10. Convert a margin of 20% to a mark-up.
11. Name the three groups the ten ratios fall into.
12. Capital employed: shares 100,000; reserves 45,000; loan 30,000. Calculate it.

### Answers

1. (Current assets − inventory) ÷ current liabilities.
2. **25.00%**.
3. 15,000 ÷ 45,000 × 100 = **33.33%**.
4. 20,000 ÷ 160,000 × 100 = **12.50%**.
5. **2.10 : 1**.
6. 80,000 ÷ 10,000 = **8.00 times**.
7. 10,000 ÷ 80,000 × 365 = **45.63 days**.
8. 14,000 × 365 ÷ 127,750 = **40.00 days**.
9. Inventory.
10. 20 ÷ 80 × 100 = **25.00%**.
11. Profitability, liquidity and efficiency.
12. **175,000**.
