|---|---|

## Worked example: describe two features

*Describe two features of Prohibition.* (6 marks)

1. The **18th Amendment** and the Volstead Act banned the manufacture and sale of alcohol from 1920,
   but enforcement was weak, with few federal agents for a huge country.
2. **Organised crime** grew rich from bootlegging and speakeasies; Al Capone controlled much of
   Chicago's trade, and violence such as the St Valentine's Day Massacre (1929) turned opinion against
   the law, which was repealed in 1933.

## New Deal: achievements and shortcomings

| Achievements | Shortcomings |
|---|---|
| Banking crisis ended; confidence restored by the Emergency Banking Act and fireside chats | Unemployment never fell below about 14% in the 1930s |
| Millions given work through the CCC, PWA and WPA | Recession of 1937–38 after spending cuts |
| TVA and rural electrification modernised poor regions | AAA payments helped landowners more than sharecroppers |
| Social Security and the Wagner Act created lasting reform | Black Americans and women often received less help |
| Federal government took responsibility for welfare | Full recovery came only with rearmament |

## Common mistakes and exam traps

- **Calling Hoover "do-nothing".** He did intervene (the RFC), though not on the scale needed.
- **Confusing the First and Second New Deals.** Social Security, the WPA and the Wagner Act are 1935.
- **Treating opposition as one group.** Some thought the New Deal went too far (Liberty League,
  Supreme Court), others not far enough (Long, Coughlin).
- **Using the Crash as the only cause of the Depression.** It was the trigger; over-production and
  weak banks were deeper causes.
- **Missing provenance.** A government poster for the NRA or a Republican cartoon will give a one-sided
  view — use this when cross-referencing.

## Frequently asked questions

**Why did the Crash happen in 1929?**
Speculation and margin buying pushed prices above real values;
when confidence went, panic selling followed.

**What was the TVA's importance?**
It showed how federal planning could transform a poor region with
dams, jobs and electricity.

**Why did the Supreme Court oppose Roosevelt?**
It ruled that some New Deal laws gave the federal
government powers the Constitution reserved to the states.
