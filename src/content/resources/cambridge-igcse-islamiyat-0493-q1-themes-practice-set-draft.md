> **DRAFT for teacher review.** Original questions written for Marlbridge, not past-paper questions. Not yet reviewed by a teacher and not for publication. Read each passage in the syllabus Appendix 1 (Arabic and translation) before answering.

**How to use this set.** It covers passages not used in the first practice set: Suras 1, 2.21-22, 5.110, 108 and 114.

## Section A: Short questions

**1.** Name the group that contains Sura 1 and the group that contains Sura 108. **[2]**

**2.** Sura 1 has three movements. Name them. **[3]**

**3.** Sura 2.21-22 names blessings Allah has given. Give three of them. **[3]**

**4.** Sura 114 is a prayer for refuge. Whom does the believer seek refuge with, and from what? **[3]**

**5.** Sura 5.110 recalls favours to Isa. Give two, and explain why the passage says they happened. **[3]**

**6.** Sura 108 asks for two responses to what has been given. Name them. **[2]**

## Section B: Exam-style

**7.** Sura 1. (a) Explain the main theme or themes. **[4]** (b) Explain its importance in the life of a Muslim today. **[4]**

**8.** Sura 2.21-22. (a) Explain the main theme or themes. **[4]** (b) Explain its importance in the life of a Muslim today. **[4]**

**9.** Sura 114. (a) Explain the main theme or themes. **[4]** (b) Explain its importance in the life of a Muslim today. **[4]**

## Section C: Evaluate

**10.** "Sura 5.110 is mainly about Isa, so it says nothing about Allah." Explain why this is a weak reading of the passage. **[3]**

## Answers

**1.** Sura 1: Allah's relationship with the created world [1]; Sura 108: Allah's Messengers [1].

**2.** Praise of Allah as Lord, Merciful and Master of the Day of Judgement [1]; a pledge to worship and seek help from Him alone [1]; a request for guidance on the straight path [1].

**3.** Any three: creation of people [1]; the earth as a resting place [1]; the sky as a canopy [1]; rain [1]; fruits [1].

**4.** The Lord, King and God of mankind [1-2]; from the whisperer who withdraws, among jinn and people [1].

**5.** Any two from: support of the holy spirit, speaking in the cradle, knowledge of the Book and wisdom, the Torah and Gospel, miracles such as the clay bird, healing the blind and lepers, raising the dead [1 each, up to 2]; all by Allah's permission [1].

**6.** Prayer [1] and sacrifice [1].

**7.** (a) Praise of Allah's mercy and lordship [1]; the day of judgement [1]; worship and help from Allah alone [1]; request for guidance [1]. (b) Recited in every rak'a of prayer, renewing the relationship between worshipper and Lord [up to 2]; a daily prayer for guidance [up to 2].

**8.** (a) Worship Allah who created people [1]; His blessings (earth, sky, rain, fruits) are reasons for worship [1]; do not set up rivals to Him [1]; creation shows His care [1]. (b) Gratitude for blessings [up to 2]; care for the environment as part of that gratitude [up to 2].

**9.** (a) Seeking refuge with Allah as Lord, King and God of mankind [1-2]; protection from the whisperer's evil suggestions [1-2]. (b) A prayer for protection from temptation [up to 2]; reminds a Muslim to rely on Allah for protection [up to 2].

**10.** The favours are given by Allah [1]; each miracle depends on Allah's permission [1]; so the passage shows Allah's power while honouring Isa as a prophet [1].
