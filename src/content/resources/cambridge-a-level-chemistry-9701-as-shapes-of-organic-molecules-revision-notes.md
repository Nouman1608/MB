**Syllabus:** Cambridge International AS & A Level Chemistry 9701, 2025–2027, **AS Level** subtopic
13.3: chain types; shapes and bond angles at sp, sp² and sp³ atoms; arrangement of σ and π bonds; the
term planar.

## Chain types in one line each

- **Straight-chained:** no carbon bonded to more than two other carbons (pentane).
- **Branched:** at least one carbon bonded to three or four carbons (2,2-dimethylpropane).
- **Cyclic:** carbons joined in a ring (cyclopentane, C₅H₁₀).

A straight chain of sp³ carbons is a zig-zag, not a straight line. A ring with no double bond has the
general formula CₙH₂ₙ, the same as an alkene.

## The hybridisation table to learn

| Atoms bonded to the carbon | Hybridisation | Shape | Angle | Unhybridised p orbitals | Typical bond types |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | sp³ | tetrahedral | 109.5° | 0 | four σ |
| 3 | sp² | trigonal planar | 120° | 1 | three σ + one π |
| 2 | sp | linear | 180° | 2 | two σ + two π |

How hybrid orbitals form: promote one 2s electron to 2p (2s¹ 2p³), then mix the 2s orbital with three,
two or one 2p orbitals. The hybrid orbitals repel and spread out as far apart as possible.

## σ and π bonds compared

| | σ bond | π bond |
|---|---|---|
| Overlap | end-on (head-on) | sideways, between parallel p orbitals |
| Electron density | on the line between the nuclei | above and below that line |
| Present in | every single, double and triple bond (one each) | double bonds (one), triple bonds (two) |
| Relative strength | stronger | weaker |
| Rotation about the bond | free | prevented: rotating would break the overlap |

Bond counting rule: **single = 1σ; double = 1σ + 1π; triple = 1σ + 2π.**

Data booklet check: C–C 350, C=C 610, C≡C 840 kJ mol⁻¹ (average values). Each extra bond adds less
than the σ bond does, showing π bonds are weaker.

## Planar: what it does and does not cover

- **Planar** means all the atoms lie in one plane.
- At a C=C, the two carbons and the four atoms attached to them are coplanar, because the p orbitals
  must be parallel to overlap.
- Ethene, C₂H₄, is planar. Buta-1,3-diene, CH₂=CH–CH=CH₂, has only sp² carbons and can be planar.
- Any sp³ carbon (CH₃, CH₂ in a chain) means the whole molecule is not planar.
- sp atoms are linear: in but-2-yne, CH₃–C≡C–CH₃, the four carbons lie on one straight line, but the
  CH₃ hydrogens are tetrahedral around their carbons.

## Hybridisation of atoms other than carbon

| Group | Atom | Hybridisation | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| C=O (aldehyde, ketone, acid, ester) | carbonyl carbon | sp² | carbon and its three neighbours in one plane, about 120° |
| C≡N (nitrile) | carbon and nitrogen | sp | R–C≡N is linear at the nitrile carbon |
| –NH₂ (amine) | nitrogen | sp³ | three bonds + one lone pair; angle about 107° |
| –OH (alcohol) | oxygen | sp³ | two bonds + two lone pairs; angle about 104.5° |

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Hybridisation | mixing of an atom's s and p orbitals to form a set of equivalent hybrid orbitals |
| sp³ / sp² / sp | hybrid sets made from one s orbital with three, two or one p orbitals |
| σ bond | covalent bond from end-on overlap, electron density along the internuclear axis |
| π bond | covalent bond from sideways overlap of parallel p orbitals, density above and below the axis |
| Planar | all atoms in the same plane |
| Straight-chained | carbons in one unbranched chain |
| Branched | a carbon chain with at least one side chain |
| Cyclic | carbon atoms joined in a ring |

## Exam traps

- A double bond is **one σ plus one π**, never "two σ bonds" or "two π bonds".
- Count σ bonds from **every** bond, C–H included. Ethane has 7 σ bonds; ethene has 5 σ and 1 π.
- Do not call propene, propanone or ethanal planar: each contains an sp³ CH₃ carbon.
- When asked to explain planarity, mention the **parallel p orbitals** that must overlap sideways.
  "It has a double bond" is not enough.
- The angle at an sp² carbon is 120°, not 109.5°. The 107° and 104.5° values are for N and O with lone
  pairs.
- Cyclohexane is not flat: its carbons are sp³ with angles of about 109.5°, so the ring is puckered.
- π electron density is drawn as two lobes either side of the σ bond, not between the nuclei.

## Self-test

1. State the hybridisation of each carbon in CH₃CH=CH₂.
2. How many σ bonds and how many π bonds are there in ethyne, C₂H₂?
3. Give the bond angle H–C–H in methane and H–C–H in ethene.
4. Describe how a π bond forms.
5. Explain why a π bond is weaker than a σ bond.
6. Is propanone, CH₃COCH₃, planar? Explain.
7. Count the σ and π bonds in propanal, CH₃CH₂CHO.
8. Classify each as straight-chained, branched or cyclic: (a) 2-methylbutane, (b) cyclobutane, (c) heptane.
9. State the shape around the carbon atom of the nitrile group in ethanenitrile, CH₃CN.
10. Explain why all four carbon atoms in but-2-yne lie on a straight line.
11. How many unhybridised p orbitals does an sp carbon have, and what do they form?
12. Use the data-booklet values C–C 350 and C=C 610 kJ mol⁻¹ to estimate the strength of the π bond in
    C=C.

**Answers:**

1. CH₃ carbon sp³; both CH= carbons sp².
2. 3 σ (two C–H and one in C≡C) and 2 π.
3. 109.5° in methane; 120° in ethene.
4. Sideways overlap of two parallel unhybridised p orbitals on adjacent atoms, giving electron density
   above and below the line joining the nuclei.
5. Sideways overlap of p orbitals is less effective than end-on overlap, so the electrons are held less
   strongly between the nuclei.
6. No. The carbonyl carbon is sp² and trigonal planar, but each CH₃ carbon is sp³ and tetrahedral, so
   some hydrogens lie out of the plane.
7. Bonds: six C–H, two C–C, one C=O. σ = 6 + 2 + 1 = 9; π = 1.
8. (a) branched; (b) cyclic; (c) straight-chained.
9. Linear, 180° (the carbon is sp).
10. The two middle carbons are sp hybridised, so C–C≡C–C has 180° angles at both of them, putting all
    four carbons in line.
11. Two; they overlap sideways with p orbitals on the neighbouring atom to form two π bonds at right
    angles to each other.
12. 610 − 350 = 260 kJ mol⁻¹, less than the σ bond, consistent with π bonds being weaker.

## Frequently asked questions

**How do I decide whether a carbon is sp, sp² or sp³ in Cambridge 9701?**
Count the atoms bonded to it: four means sp³, three means sp² and two means sp. This works because each
bonded atom uses one hybrid orbital for its σ bond.

**Is benzene part of 13.3?**
13.3 is about molecules containing sp, sp² and sp³ atoms in general, with ethene as the named planar
example. Benzene's delocalised structure is studied in the A Level arenes topic.
