## Section A - multiple choice

**1.** A supermarket sells bread below cost to attract shoppers who then buy other goods. This is a: **[1]**
A free sample   B loss leader   C coupon   D competition

**2.** Which source of finance involves selling unpaid customer invoices to a specialist company? **[1]**
A leasing   B debt factoring   C crowdfunding   D debentures

**3.** Which payment method is best for paying the same rent every month? **[1]**
A direct debit   B standing order   C store card   D documentary credit

**4.** A product costs $50 and sells for $65. The mark-up is: **[1]**
A 15%   B 23%   C 30%   D 77%

## Section B - structured questions

**5.** *Two rival paint brands advertise: Brand A says "Our paint covers twice the area of Brand B"; the
national paint association runs adverts saying "Paint protects your home".*
(a) Identify the type of advertising used by each. **[2]**
(b) Explain **one** advantage and **one** disadvantage of competitive advertising. **[4]**

**6.** *Sana opens a mobile phone repair shop in a small town.*

**7.** Explain why a perfume company might use the romantic and social acceptance methods of appeal. **[4]**

**8.** *Ocean Traders imports furniture and has a current account with its bank.*
(a) Explain **two** banking services, other than the current account, that Ocean Traders could use. **[4]**
(b) Explain **two** impacts of the growth of digital banking on businesses like Ocean Traders. **[4]**

**9.** (a) State **three** purposes of insurance. **[3]**
(b) A warehouse is insured for $400 000. The premium rate is 0.6%. Calculate the annual premium. **[2]**
(c) Explain the difference between employer's liability and public liability insurance. **[4]**

**10.** Describe the purpose of a proposal form. **[2]**

**11.** *Tech Start Ltd, a new software company, needs $500 000 to grow.* Discuss whether venture capital or a

(a) Calculate gross profit and gross profit margin. **[3]**

## Answers

**1.** B. **[1]**

**2.** B. **[1]**

**3.** B. **[1]**

**4.** C - (65 - 50) / 50 x 100 = 30%. **[1]**

**5.** (a) Brand A: competitive **[1]**; association: collective (generic) **[1]**.
(b) Advantage: can attract customers from rivals by showing why the brand is better **[2]**. Disadvantage:
rivals may respond with their own adverts or legal complaints, increasing costs **[2]**.

**6.** (a) Any two justified: leaflets or flyers - cheap and target the local town **[2]**; social media
advertising - low cost, reaches young phone users, easy to change **[2]**; signboard or window display -
catches passing customers **[2]**; word of mouth - free and trusted locally **[2]**. **[max 4]**
(b) Any two explained: special offer on a first repair **[2]**; free gift such as a screen protector **[2]**;
coupons in leaflets **[2]**; competitions on social media **[2]**. **[max 4]**

**7.** Romantic appeal links the perfume to love and attraction **[2]**, encouraging buying as a gift or to feel
attractive; social acceptance suggests the perfume helps people fit in and be admired **[2]**.

**8.** (a) Any two explained: documentary credit to pay overseas suppliers safely **[2]**; foreign currency
services for paying in other currencies **[2]**; overdraft to cover shortages of cash while waiting for
sales **[2]**; loan to expand **[2]**; electronic transfer for quick payments **[2]**. **[max 4]**
(b) Any two explained: payments and transfers can be made at any time, saving time **[2]**; local branch
closures make face-to-face advice harder **[2]**; customers pay less by cash, so less cash handling **[2]**;
risk of fraud means more security is needed **[2]**. **[max 4]**

**9.** (a) Any three: risk reduction; compensation; financial protection; business confidence; investment.
**[3]**
(b) 400 000 x 0.6 / 100 = **$2 400**. **[2]**
(c) Employer's liability covers claims by employees injured or made ill at work **[2]**; public liability
covers claims by members of the public injured on the premises or by the business's activities **[2]**.

**10.** It is the insurance application form **[1]** on which the business gives all material facts so the
insurer can assess the risk and set a premium **[1]**.

**11.** **[6]** Venture capital: suits a risky, fast-growing business; no fixed repayments; investors may offer
expertise; but the owners give up a share of ownership and control. Bank loan: owners keep control; but
banks may refuse a new firm with few assets, and interest and repayments must be paid even if sales are
slow. Conclusion: venture capital is likely to be more suitable for a new software company with high
growth potential but limited security.

**12.** (a) Gross profit = 90 000 - 63 000 = **$27 000** **[1]**; margin = 27 000 / 90 000 x 100 = **30%** **[2]**.
(b) 27 000 - 18 000 = **$9 000**. **[1]**
(c) Any two explained: raise selling prices if customers will accept it **[2]**; buy in bulk or find cheaper
suppliers to lower cost of sales **[2]**; reduce waste of perishable stock **[2]**; sell more high-margin
products **[2]**. **[max 4]**

**13.** Current assets minus current liabilities **[1]**. It shows whether the retailer can pay short-term debts
such as suppliers **[1]** and buy stock to keep trading **[1]**.

## A note on exam technique for this topic

Calculation questions carry method marks - write the formula, show the working, then the answer with $ or
%. In "recommend" questions, tie each choice to the business: a small-town repair shop does not need TV
advertising.

## Frequently asked questions

**Which banking payment methods are in 7100?** Cash, cheque, credit card, debit card, store card, credit
transfer, standing order, direct debit, electronic transfer and documentary credit (letter of credit).

**What is the difference between debt factoring and an overdraft?** Debt factoring raises cash by selling
unpaid invoices; an overdraft lets a business spend more than it has in its current account up to a limit.

**Which calculations must I know?** Revenue, gross profit, gross profit margin, profit for the year, working
capital and mark-up percentage.
