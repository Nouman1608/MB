## Where marks are usually lost

- Removing 3d electrons before 4s in transition-element ions.
- Forgetting the Cr and Cu exceptions, or writing them for the ions.
- Pairing electrons before each orbital in a sub-shell has one.
- Explaining the Cr configuration as "more stable" without referring to energy and repulsion.
- Describing p orbitals without saying they lie along axes at right angles.

## Frequently asked questions

**Why is 4s filled before 3d but emptied first in ions?**
In potassium and calcium the 4s sub-shell is lower in energy, so it fills first. Once 3d contains electrons, 4s is the higher-energy, outer sub-shell, so its electrons are removed first when an ion forms.

**How do I count unpaired electrons quickly?**
Draw the outer sub-shell in boxes, place one electron in each box before pairing, and count the boxes with a single arrow.
