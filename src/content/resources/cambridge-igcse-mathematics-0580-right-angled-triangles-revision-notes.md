Angles are in degrees; answers in degrees to **1 decimal place**.

## The ratios

```
sin θ = opposite / hypotenuse
cos θ = adjacent / hypotenuse
tan θ = opposite / adjacent
```

The ratios are not on the List of formulas, so learn them.

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Hypotenuse | Side opposite the right angle; the longest side |
| Opposite | Side across from the angle you are using |
| Adjacent | Side next to the angle you are using, not the hypotenuse |
| Inverse function | sin⁻¹, cos⁻¹, tan⁻¹: turn a ratio back into an angle |
| Bearing | Angle measured clockwise from north, written with three figures |
| Angle of elevation | Up from the horizontal to a line of sight (Extended) |
| Angle of depression | Down from the horizontal to a line of sight (Extended) |

## Choosing and using a ratio

```
1. Mark the angle θ (given or wanted).
2. Label H (opposite the right angle), O (opposite θ), A (next to θ).
3. Circle the two sides involved (known + wanted).
4. O and H -> sin   A and H -> cos   O and A -> tan
5. Write the equation, e.g. tan θ = O/A.
6. Side on top -> multiply.  Side on bottom -> divide.  Angle -> inverse.
```

| Unknown | Example | Calculation |
|---|---|---|
| top of the fraction | sin 40° = x / 20 | x = 20 sin 40° |
| bottom of the fraction | sin 28° = 9 / x | x = 9 / sin 28° |
| angle | tan θ = 12 / 5 | θ = tan⁻¹(12 / 5) |

## Bearings

- Measured from **north**, **clockwise**, three figures: 070°, not 70°.
- Back bearing: add 180° if the bearing is less than 180°, subtract 180° if more. The bearing of A from B, when B from A is 070°, is 250°.
- Draw a north line at the point the bearing is measured **from**.
- The north–south and east–west lines make the right angle.

**Worked reminder.** From P, Q is 5 km east and 8 km south. The angle below east is tan⁻¹(8/5) = 57.99…°, so the bearing of Q from P is 90° + 57.99…° = **148.0°**.

## Extended: shortest distance and elevation

- The shortest distance from a point to a line is the **perpendicular** distance: every other route is a hypotenuse, so it is longer.
- Elevation and depression are measured from the **horizontal**.
- Angle of depression from A to B = angle of elevation from B to A (alternate angles).

**Worked reminder.** From the top of a lighthouse 48 m high, the angle of depression of a boat is 22°. The boat is 48 / tan 22° = **119 m** from the foot of the lighthouse.

## Exam traps

- Labelling sides from the wrong angle.
- Multiplying when the unknown is on the bottom of the ratio.
- Calculator in radians; check that sin 30 gives 0.5.
- Using sin⁻¹ of a value greater than 1 (the calculator shows an error: a side was mislabelled).
- Rounding an angle to 3 significant figures instead of 1 decimal place: 147.99…° is 148.0°, and 8.123…° is 8.1°, not 8.12°.
- Angle of depression placed at the wrong vertex.
- Bearings measured anticlockwise or from east, or written with two figures.
- Rounding a length before using it in a second triangle.

## Self-test

1. Hypotenuse 20 cm, angle 40°. Find the side opposite the angle.
2. Adjacent side 15 cm, angle 55°. Find the opposite side.
3. Opposite side 9 cm, angle 28°. Find the hypotenuse.
4. Adjacent 6.4 cm, hypotenuse 10.5 cm. Find the angle.
5. Opposite 3.2 cm, hypotenuse 7.8 cm. Find the angle.
6. Opposite 12 cm, adjacent 5 cm. Find the angle.
7. The bearing of B from A is 070°. Find the bearing of A from B.
8. Which ratio links the opposite side and the hypotenuse?
9. (Extended) From 25 m away on level ground, the angle of elevation of the top of a tree is 35°. Find the height of the tree.
10. (Extended) A point is 15 m from A along a line making 40° with a straight road through A. Find the shortest distance from the point to the road.
11. (Extended) The angle of elevation of a cliff top from a boat is 14°. What is the angle of depression of the boat from the cliff top?
12. Why can sin x never equal 1.2?

### Answers

1. 20 sin 40° = **12.9 cm**
2. 15 tan 55° = **21.4 cm**
3. 9 / sin 28° = **19.2 cm**
4. cos⁻¹(6.4 / 10.5) = **52.4°**
5. sin⁻¹(3.2 / 7.8) = **24.2°**
6. tan⁻¹(12 / 5) = **67.4°**
7. 070° + 180° = **250°**
8. **Sine**
9. 25 tan 35° = **17.5 m**
10. 15 sin 40° = **9.64 m**
11. **14°** (alternate angles between horizontal lines)
12. In a right-angled triangle the opposite side is shorter than the hypotenuse, so O/H is always **less than 1** for an acute angle.
