## Where marks are usually lost

- Not showing the rounded numbers in Questions 3, 4, 5 and 12.
- Rounding 0.205 to 0.3 in Question 3.
- Writing 31 instead of 31 000 in Question 2(a).
- Rounding 4.73 coaches down to 4 in Question 7.
- Rounding part-way through the calculation in Question 9.
- Using decimal places when the question asks for significant figures in Question 14.

## Frequently asked questions

**Should I always round to 1 significant figure when estimating?**
Usually, yes, because that makes the arithmetic simple. For roots, use a nearby perfect square or cube instead.

**What is the difference between decimal places and significant figures?**
Decimal places count digits after the decimal point. Significant figures count from the first non-zero digit, wherever the decimal point is.
