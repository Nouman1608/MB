Condensed notes for sub-topic 4.1, Transport: functions, methods, choice factors and containerisation.

## Four facts to start with

1. Transport has three functions: **time**, **place** and **quantity**.
2. Methods: road, rail, air, sea and pipeline.
3. The choice depends on the goods, urgency, cost, security, distance and reliability of the transporter.
4. **Containerisation** uses standard containers moved between ship, rail and road, with **transshipment** at hubs.

## Key terms

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Transport | Movement of goods and people from one place to another |
| Courier | Business that delivers parcels and documents quickly |
| Cargo liner | Ship on a fixed route and timetable carrying general cargo |
| Tanker | Ship that carries liquids such as oil |
| Container ship | Ship that carries standard containers |
| Pipeline | Pipe for moving liquids or gases |
| Containerisation | Moving goods in standard-sized containers |
| Transshipment | Moving goods from one ship or vehicle to another during a journey |

## Functions of transport

| Function | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Time | Goods arrive when needed |
| Place | Goods move to where they are wanted |
| Quantity | Goods move in the right amounts |

## Methods compared

| Method | Best for | Main plus | Main minus |
|---|---|---|---|
| Road | Short and medium trips; door-to-door | Flexible | Traffic; small loads |
| Rail | Heavy bulk goods over land | Cheap for bulk | Fixed routes |
| Air | Urgent, perishable, high-value | Fastest | Most expensive |
| Sea | Heavy goods overseas | Cheapest for bulk | Slowest |
| Pipeline | Liquids and gases | Continuous and cheap to run | Costly to build; fixed route |

## Choice factors

Nature of goods; urgency; cost; security; distance; reliability of transporter.

## Containerisation

| Advantages | Disadvantages |
|---|---|
| Fast loading and unloading | Expensive equipment |
| Protection from damage and theft | Needs large ports and roads |
| Easy to switch methods | Empty containers must be returned |
| Standard sizes | Unsuitable for oversized items |

## Worked examples

**Air versus sea.** 500 kg at $4.20 per kg by air = $2,100. At $0.60 per kg by sea = $300. Air is $1,800 dearer but 23 days faster (2 days against 25).

**Cost per unit.** $3,600 ÷ 2,400 boxes = $1.50 per box. Only 1,800 boxes in the container: $3,600 ÷ 1,800 = $2.00 per box.

## Model answers to learn

**Why might a business use rail rather than road for coal?** Coal is heavy and bulky and travels a long distance. Rail can carry large loads cheaply and is not slowed by road traffic.

**Why is containerisation useful for exporters?** Goods are packed once and moved by ship, train and lorry without being unpacked, so loading is quick and the goods are protected from theft and damage.

## Exam traps

- **General answers** with no link to the goods.
- **Sea is fast.** It is cheap and slow.
- **Pipeline for any goods.** Only liquids and gases.
- **Containerisation as a vehicle.**
- **Transshipment as unpacking.** It is transfer between ships or vehicles.
- **Disadvantages omitted** in an evaluation.

## Self-test

1. State the three functions of transport.
2. Name two methods of sea transport.
3. Give one advantage of road transport.
4. Give one disadvantage of rail transport.
5. Why is air transport suitable for fresh flowers?
6. Name the type of goods that pipelines carry.
7. List four factors affecting the choice of transport.
8. What is containerisation?
9. What is transshipment?
10. Why is a full container cheaper per unit?

**Answers**

1. Time, place, quantity.
2. Any two: ferry, tanker, container ship, cargo liner.
3. Any one: door-to-door, flexible routes, suits small loads.
4. Any one: fixed routes and timetables, needs other transport to reach the door.
5. They are perishable and need to arrive quickly.
6. Liquids and gases.
7. Any four: nature of goods, urgency, cost, security, distance, reliability of transporter.
8. Moving goods in standard-sized containers.
9. Transferring goods from one ship or vehicle to another during the journey.
10. The same transport cost is shared over more units.

## Command words to expect

| Command word | What to write |
|---|---|
| Identify, State, Give | A short answer with no explanation |
| Define | A precise meaning |
| Describe | The main points or features |
| Explain | A reason, linked to the situation, with a development |
| Analyse | Break the issue into parts and show how they connect |
| Assess, Consider | A reasoned judgement about importance or effect |
| Discuss | Both sides of an issue, in a structured way |
| Justify | A case backed with evidence |
| Evaluate | A judgement of value, ending with a conclusion |
