These questions cover section 5.5 Clubs and societies of the Cambridge O Level Accounting 7707 syllabus for 2027 to 2029. Questions 9 to 11 follow one society through a year. Revise first with the [clubs and societies study guide](/resources/cambridge-o-level-accounting-7707-clubs-and-societies/) and the [clubs and societies revision notes](/resources/cambridge-o-level-accounting-7707-clubs-and-societies-revision-notes/). All clubs and figures are invented, and amounts are in dollars.

## Section A: Short-answer questions

**1.** State what is meant by a receipts and payments account. **[2]**

**2.** At 1 January 2028 Brookside Bowls Club had: premises $40,000; equipment $6,500; bar inventory $1,100; subscriptions in arrears $320; bank overdraft $850; subscriptions in advance $140; rates owing $260. Calculate the accumulated fund at 1 January 2028. **[3]**

**3.** A club received $12,600 in subscriptions during the year. At the start of the year subscriptions in arrears were $450 and in advance $300. At the end of the year subscriptions in arrears were $520 and in advance $780. Calculate the subscriptions income for the year. **[3]**

**4.** State where each item appears in a club's statement of financial position.

**(a)** Subscriptions in arrears. **[1]**

**(b)** Subscriptions in advance. **[1]**

**5.** Which item is shown in a club's income and expenditure account?

A: the cost of a new mower

B: the closing bank balance

C: depreciation of the mower

D: subscriptions received for next year

**[1]**

**6.** Explain why a club works out the profit on its café separately instead of showing café sales and purchases in the income and expenditure account. **[2]**

## Section B: Structured questions

**7.** Hillcrest Photographic Society has a year ending 30 September. At 1 October 2028 subscriptions in arrears were $180 and subscriptions in advance were $95. During the year $5,460 was received in subscriptions. At 30 September 2029 subscriptions in arrears were $240 and subscriptions in advance were $135.

**(a)** Prepare the subscriptions account for the year ended 30 September 2029. **[5]**

**(b)** Explain why the amount transferred to the income and expenditure account differs from the cash received. **[2]**

**8.** Westbury Cricket Club runs a café. For the year ended 31 March 2029:

| Item | $ |
|---|---|
| Café sales | 14,250 |
| Café inventory 1 April 2028 | 860 |
| Café inventory 31 March 2029 | 940 |
| Paid to café suppliers | 7,620 |
| Owing to café suppliers 1 April 2028 | 540 |
| Owing to café suppliers 31 March 2029 | 690 |
| Wages of café assistant | 2,800 |

**(a)** Calculate the café purchases for the year. **[2]**

**(b)** Calculate the profit on the café for the year. **[4]**

**9.** Ashby Drama Society had $1,840 in the bank and equipment with a book value of $3,000 at 1 January 2028. It had no other assets or liabilities. During 2028 it received subscriptions $4,320 and ticket sales for its play $3,150. It paid hall hire $2,400, costumes and scenery for the play $1,280, new lighting equipment $2,250, printing $390 and insurance $600.

**(a)** Prepare the receipts and payments account for the year ended 31 December 2028. **[5]**

**(b)** The treasurer says the society made a profit of $550 because the bank balance rose by $550. Explain why this is incorrect. **[2]**

**10.** For Ashby Drama Society (Question 9), the following information is available at 31 December 2028:

- subscriptions income for the year, after adjustment, is $4,410
- hall hire of $200 is owing
- insurance of $150 is prepaid
- all equipment is depreciated at 20% of its year-end book value before depreciation, $5,250.

Prepare the income and expenditure account for the year ended 31 December 2028. **[7]**

**11.** For Ashby Drama Society, subscriptions of $90 are in arrears at 31 December 2028 and the surplus for the year is $1,790.

**(a)** Calculate the accumulated fund at 1 January 2028. **[1]**

**(b)** Prepare the statement of financial position at 31 December 2028. **[6]**

**12.** The committee of a gardening club reports a deficit of $1,200 for the year, but the club's bank balance has risen.

**(a)** Explain **two** reasons why the bank balance could rise even though the club made a deficit. **[4]**

**(b)** Suggest **two** actions the committee could take to turn the deficit into a surplus. **[2]**

## Answers

**1.** A summary of the cash or bank transactions of a club for the year [1], starting with the opening balance and ending with the closing cash or bank balance [1]. **[2]**

**2.** Assets = 40,000 + 6,500 + 1,100 + 320 = $47,920 [1]. Liabilities = 850 + 140 + 260 = $1,250 [1]. Accumulated fund = **$46,670** [1]. **[3]**

*Examiner insight:* The overdraft is a liability; adding it to the assets is the most common slip.

**3.** 12,600 - 450 + 300 [1] + 520 - 780 [1] = **$12,190** [1]. **[3]**

**4. (a)** Current assets, as an other receivable [1].

**(b)** Current liabilities, as an other payable [1]. **[2]**

**5.** C [1]. **[1]**

**6.** It shows whether the café itself made a profit or a loss [1], so the committee can decide whether to change prices or keep running it; only the net result is income of the club [1]. **[2]**

**7. (a)**

```
Subscriptions account
Date        Details        USD    Date        Details       USD
1 Oct 2028  Balance b/d    180    1 Oct 2028  Balance b/d    95
30 Sep 2029 Income and            2028-29     Bank        5,460
            expenditure  5,480    30 Sep 2029 Balance c/d   240
30 Sep 2029 Balance c/d    135
                         5,795                            5,795
1 Oct 2029  Balance b/d    240    1 Oct 2029  Balance b/d   135
```

Marks: opening arrears and advance on the correct sides [1]; bank $5,460 [1]; closing arrears and advance c/d [1]; transfer to income and expenditure $5,480 [1]; balances b/d at 1 October 2029 [1].

**(b)** The cash received includes $180 earned last year and leaves out $95 received last year for this year [1]. It also excludes the $240 still owed for this year and includes $135 paid in advance for next year, so the accruals basis gives a different figure [1]. **[7]**

**8. (a)** 7,620 - 540 + 690 [1] = **$7,770** [1].

**(b)** Cost of sales = 860 + 7,770 - 940 [1] = $7,690 [1]. Gross profit = 14,250 - 7,690 = $6,560 [1]. Profit on café = 6,560 - 2,800 = **$3,760** [1]. **[6]**

**9. (a)**

```
Ashby Drama Society
Receipts and payments account for the year ended 31 December 2028
Receipts                 USD    Payments                     USD
Balance b/d            1,840    Hall hire                  2,400
Subscriptions          4,320    Costumes and scenery       1,280
Play ticket sales      3,150    Lighting equipment         2,250
                                Printing                     390
                                Insurance                    600
                                Balance c/d                2,390
                       9,310                               9,310
```

Marks: opening balance [1]; receipts [1]; payments including the equipment [1]; closing balance $2,390 [1]; totals $9,310 [1].

**(b)** The rise in the bank balance includes capital spending and ignores non-cash items [1]; the $2,250 equipment is an asset, not an expense, while depreciation, accruals and prepayments are left out, so the surplus must be found from an income and expenditure account (it is $1,790) [1]. **[7]**

**10.**

```
Ashby Drama Society
Income and expenditure account for the year ended 31 December 2028
                                                USD        USD
Income
  Subscriptions                                          4,410
  Surplus on play (3,150 - 1,280)                        1,870
                                                         6,280
Less Expenditure
  Hall hire (2,400 + 200)                     2,600
  Insurance (600 - 150)                         450
  Printing                                      390
  Depreciation of equipment (5,250 x 20%)     1,050
                                                         4,490
Surplus of income over expenditure                       1,790
```

Marks: subscriptions [1]; play surplus $1,870 [1]; hall hire $2,600 [1]; insurance $450 [1]; depreciation $1,050 [1]; total expenditure $4,490 [1]; surplus $1,790, correctly labelled [1]. **[7]**

*Examiner insight:* The new lighting equipment appears only through its share of depreciation.

**11. (a)** 3,000 + 1,840 = **$4,840** [1].

**(b)**

```
Ashby Drama Society
Statement of financial position at 31 December 2028
                                                USD        USD
Non-current assets
  Equipment (5,250 - 1,050)                              4,200
Current assets
  Subscriptions in arrears                       90
  Insurance prepaid                             150
  Bank                                        2,390
                                                         2,630
Total assets                                             6,830
Accumulated fund
  Balance at 1 January 2028                   4,840
  Add Surplus for the year                    1,790
                                                         6,630
Current liabilities
  Hall hire owing                                          200
Total accumulated fund and liabilities                   6,830
```

Marks: equipment $4,200 [1]; current assets $2,630 [1]; accumulated fund heading and opening balance [1]; surplus added, $6,630 [1]; hall hire owing as a current liability [1]; both totals $6,830 [1]. **[7]**

**12. (a)** Any two, each explained: depreciation is expenditure that involves no payment [1], so it creates part of the deficit without reducing the bank [1]; expenses owing at the year end are charged but not yet paid [1], so the cash has not yet left [1]; subscriptions received in advance for next year increase the bank but are not this year's income; a loan was received, which increases the bank but is a liability, not income.

**(b)** Any two: raise the subscription [1]; increase café or event prices, or hold more fund-raising events [1]; reduce running costs, such as hall hire. **[6]**
