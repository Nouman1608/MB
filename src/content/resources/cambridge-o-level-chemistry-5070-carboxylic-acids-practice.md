**12.** Two colourless liquids, A and B, are ethanol and aqueous ethanoic acid, but the labels have fallen off. Describe **two** different chemical tests to identify them, giving the result for each liquid. **[4]**

## Where marks are usually lost

- Writing "-oic" in salt names.
- Missing brackets in the formulae of salts of 2+ metals.
- Giving the wrong gas: hydrogen with metals, carbon dioxide with carbonates.
- Leaving water out of esterification, or naming the ester acid part first.
- Describing a test with the result for only one of the two liquids.
