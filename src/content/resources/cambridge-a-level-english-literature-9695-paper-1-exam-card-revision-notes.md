# Paper 1 exam card

## Structure

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Paper | Drama and Poetry, written, 2 hours |
| Total | 50 marks (Section A 25, Section B 25) |
| Questions | One from Section A (Drama), one from Section B (Poetry) |
| Choice | On each set text, question (a) or (b); (b) is based on a passage or poem on the paper |
| Allowed | No set texts; no dictionaries |
| Weight | 50 per cent of AS Level; 25 per cent of A Level |

## Assessment objectives (each 25 per cent of the paper)

- **AO1** Knowledge and understanding, with appreciation of relevant contexts
- **AO2** Analysis of writers' choices of language, form and structure
- **AO3** Personal response: informed, independent opinions and interpretations
- **AO4** Communication: relevant, structured and supported

## Command words and the syllabus meanings

| Word | Meaning in the syllabus |
|---|---|
| Analyse | Examine in detail to show meaning, identify elements and the relationship between them |
| Assess | Make an informed judgement |
| Comment | Give an informed opinion |
| Compare | Identify or comment on similarities and/or differences |
| Consider | Review and respond to given information |
| Discuss | Write about issues or topics in depth in a structured way |
| Examine | Investigate closely, in detail |
| Explain | Set out purposes or reasons; make the relationships between things evident |

The syllabus adds that phrases such as "In what ways ...?" and "How far, and in what ways ...?" may also be seen.

## The one-line check for any paragraph

Point, evidence (a short quotation or precise reference), named choice, effect, link back to the question.

## Self-test

1. How many marks is each section worth? *25.*
2. Which AO is not assessed on Paper 1? *AO5.*
3. What does the (b) question print? *A passage or a poem from the set text.*
4. What does "Examine" mean in the syllabus? *Investigate closely, in detail.*
5. Can you take your set text into the exam? *No.*

*Sources: Cambridge International, AS & A Level Literature in English 9695 syllabus for 2024, 2025 and 2026 (sections 3 and 4, including command words).*
