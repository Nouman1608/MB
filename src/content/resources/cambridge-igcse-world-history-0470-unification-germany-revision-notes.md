**Key question 1.3, Cambridge IGCSE History 0470:** "How was Germany unified?" Learn German nationalism, the
Zollverein, 1848 in Prussia, the Frankfurt Parliament, Austria's recovery by 1850, and Bismarck's foreign policy to
1871. Full explanations are in the [Germany study guide](/resources/cambridge-igcse-world-history-0470-unification-germany/);
practise with the [Germany practice questions](/resources/cambridge-igcse-world-history-0470-unification-germany-practice/).

## Bismarck's method in three wars

| | Denmark, 1864 | Austria, 1866 | France, 1870-71 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Excuse | Danish attempt to bind Schleswig to Denmark | Dispute over Holstein after Gastein | Hohenzollern candidature and the Ems Telegram |
| Diplomacy | Allied with Austria | Russia friendly, France neutral (Biarritz), Italy allied | France isolated; southern states join Prussia |
| Military key | Joint Austro-Prussian forces | Railways, needle gun, Moltke at Königgrätz | Rapid mobilisation; Sedan; siege of Paris |
| Result | Duchies taken from Denmark | Austria excluded; North German Confederation | German Empire; Alsace-Lorraine |

## Key people

| Person | Remember |
|---|---|
| Frederick William IV | King of Prussia; yielded in March 1848, then restored control; refused Frankfurt's crown |
| William I | King from 1861 (regent from 1858); appointed Bismarck; German Emperor 1871 |
| Bismarck | Minister-President from 1862; "iron and blood"; architect of the three wars |
| Roon | War minister who planned the army reforms |
| Moltke | Chief of the general staff; used railways to move armies fast |
| Napoleon III | French emperor; neutral in 1866; defeated and captured at Sedan |
| Prince Leopold | Hohenzollern prince offered the Spanish throne in 1870 |

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| German Confederation | Loose league of German states, 1815-66, under Austrian presidency |
| Zollverein | Prussian-led customs union from 1834 |
| Grossdeutsch / Kleindeutsch | A Germany with or without Austria |
| Frankfurt Parliament | Elected German national assembly, 1848-49 |
| Olmütz | 1850 agreement in which Prussia backed down before Austria |
| Realpolitik | Politics based on power and practical advantage, not ideals |
| North German Confederation | Prussian-led union of northern states, 1867 |
| Ems Telegram | Report of William I's talk with the French ambassador, edited and published by Bismarck |

## Why 1848-50 failed and 1866-71 succeeded

| 1848-50 | 1862-71 |
|---|---|
| Elected assembly with no army | Prussian state with a modern army |
| Austria strong and backed by Russia | Austria isolated in 1866 |
| Frederick William IV hesitant | William I backing a determined Bismarck |
| Liberal methods: debates and constitutions | War and diplomacy |

## One-line answers to the focus points

- **Why was Germany not unified in 1848-50?** Frankfurt had no army, Frederick William IV refused its crown, and
  Austria, backed by Russia, restored its leadership at Olmütz.
- **How did Bismarck bring about Austria's defeat?** By isolating Austria, allying with Italy and using the Holstein
  dispute to force a war that Prussia's army won quickly.
- **How did he bring about France's defeat?** By using the Hohenzollern candidature and the Ems Telegram to make France
  declare war alone, bringing the southern states in on Prussia's side.
- **How far was he responsible?** He chose the timing and methods; Prussia's economy and army and his enemies'
  mistakes made success possible.

## Exam traps

- **Denmark 1864:** Austria fought with Prussia, not against it.
- **Treaty of Prague:** Austria lost Venetia to Italy, not land to Prussia.
- **The southern states** were not in the North German Confederation.
- **The Ems Telegram** was a real report that Bismarck edited, not a forgery from nothing.
- **"How far was Bismarck responsible?"** needs other factors: the Zollverein, the army, Austrian and French mistakes.

## Self-test

1. When did the Zollverein come into force, and which great power stayed outside? *1834; Austria.*
2. Give one way the Zollverein helped Prussia. *It made Prussia the economic leader of Germany, encouraged railways,
   or gave wealth for the army (any one).*
3. Why did the Frankfurt Parliament depend on Prussia in 1848? *It had no army, so it needed Prussian troops to fight
   Denmark over Schleswig-Holstein.*
4. What happened at Olmütz in 1850? *Prussia gave up the Erfurt Union under Austrian and Russian pressure; the
   Austrian-led Confederation was restored.*
5. Why was Bismarck appointed in 1862? *To force through army reforms that the liberal parliament would not fund.*
6. What did the Convention of Gastein decide? *Prussia would run Schleswig and Austria would run Holstein.*
7. How did Bismarck secure Italy's help in 1866? *He promised Venetia if Italy attacked Austria.*
8. Name the decisive battle of 1866. *Königgrätz (Sadowa).*
9. Why was the Treaty of Prague lenient? *So that Austria would not seek revenge and could become a future ally.*
10. Who was offered the Spanish throne in 1870? *Prince Leopold of Hohenzollern-Sigmaringen.*
11. Why did the southern states join Prussia in 1870? *France declared war and appeared the aggressor, triggering
    their alliances.*
12. What did Germany gain at the Treaty of Frankfurt? *Alsace and part of Lorraine and a 5 billion franc indemnity.*
