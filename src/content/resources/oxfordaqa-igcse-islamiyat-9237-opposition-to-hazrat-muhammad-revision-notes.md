**These notes condense content area 3.2.1.5 of 9237: "The reasons for the opposition he faced from the leaders of Makkah, including the boycott of Banu Hashim, the events at Ta'if and the plots to kill Hazrat Muhammad (pbuh)."** The full account is in the [opposition study guide](/resources/oxfordaqa-igcse-islamiyat-9237-opposition-to-hazrat-muhammad/).

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Quraysh | The ruling tribe of Makkah |
| Banu Hashim | The Prophet's clan |
| Boycott | Cutting off trade, marriage and contact with a group |
| Shi'b Abi Talib | The valley where the boycotted clans lived |
| Ta'if | Town about sixty miles from Makkah; home of the Thaqif tribe |
| Year of Sorrow | The year in which Abu Talib and Khadija (RA) died |
| Dar al-Nadwa | The Quraysh council house |
| Negus | The Christian king of Abyssinia |

## Reasons for opposition

| Type | Reason |
|---|---|
| Religious | Tawhid rejected the ancestors' idols; teaching of the Day of Judgement |
| Economic | Fear of losing trade from pilgrims to the idols |
| Political and tribal | Fear of losing leadership; rivalry with Banu Hashim |
| Social | Equality threatened the position of the rich over slaves and the poor |

## Key opponents

- **Abu Lahab:** the Prophet's uncle; insulted him at Safa; his wife Umm Jamil spread thorns and slander (Surah 111).
- **Abu Jahl:** leading enemy; proposed the plot to kill the Prophet (pbuh).
- **Umayyah ibn Khalaf:** tortured Bilal.

## Methods used

1. Mockery and insults.
2. Offers of wealth and leadership (refused).
3. Pressure on Abu Talib.
4. Torture of weak Muslims: Bilal, Sumayyah and Yasir (Sumayyah: the first martyr), Ammar.
5. Boycott of Banu Hashim.
6. Attacks after Abu Talib's death.
7. Plot to kill the Prophet (pbuh).

**Abyssinia (about 615 CE):** a group of Muslims migrated and were protected by the Negus.

## The boycott of Banu Hashim

| Point | Detail |
|---|---|
| When | About 616 to 619 CE; three years |
| Who | Banu Hashim and Banu Muttalib, who protected the Prophet (pbuh) |
| Terms | No trade, no marriage, no speech, no food sold to them |
| Where | The valley of Abu Talib |
| Effects | Hunger, illness, children in distress, loss of trade |
| End | Some Quraysh were ashamed and cancelled it; the document was said to have been eaten by termites except for the name of Allah |
| Importance | Showed clan loyalty and the patience of the Muslims |

## The Year of Sorrow

- About 619 CE, Abu Talib died, and Khadija (RA) died soon after.
- Loss of protector and comfort.
- The Quraysh felt free to attack him more.

## Ta'if

1. The Prophet (pbuh) and Zayd (RA) travelled to Ta'if to seek support.
2. The leaders of Thaqif rejected him.
3. Children and slaves stoned them; his feet bled.
4. He sheltered in a vineyard and made a prayer of complaint to Allah.
5. The angel of the mountains offered to crush Makkah; he refused.
6. **Lesson:** patience, forgiveness and hope for guidance.

## The plots to kill the Prophet (pbuh)

- Meeting at the Dar al-Nadwa; Abu Jahl's plan.
- One young man from each clan would strike together to share the blame.
- Allah informed the Prophet (pbuh) (8:30).
- He left with Abu Bakr (RA); Ali (RA) slept in his bed.
- The plot failed; the Hijrah followed.

## Using the prescribed passage, Qur'an 111:1-5

| Words | Use for |
|---|---|
| "May the hands of Abu Lahab be ruined" | Allah's judgement on a leading opponent |
| "His wealth will not avail him" | Wealth cannot save from Allah |
| "burn in a Fire of [blazing] flame" | Punishment in the Hereafter |
| "his wife ... the carrier of firewood" | Those who help the opposition share responsibility |
| The whole surah | Allah defends His Messenger; family ties do not protect from truth |

## Lessons from the opposition

- Patience in persecution (Bilal, Sumayyah).
- Trust in Allah (Ta'if).
- Forgiveness (refusing to destroy Makkah).
- Loyalty (Abu Talib and the clans in the boycott).
- Truth does not depend on majority approval.

## How to build a 6-mark answer

1. Give the first reason and explain it with a detail (AO1).
2. Give the second reason and explain it (AO1).
3. Add one quotation, such as 111:2 or 8:30, and apply it (AO2).

**Mini example (economic reason):** "The Quraysh depended on trade from pilgrims who came to the idols. If people worshipped one God, this income would be lost, so the leaders opposed the message."

## Responses of the Prophet (pbuh)

| Challenge | Response |
|---|---|
| Insults | Patience and continued preaching |
| Offers of wealth and power | Refusal; he would not stop his mission |
| Torture of followers | Advised migration to Abyssinia; comfort and encouragement |
| Boycott | Remained steadfast with his clan |
| Ta'if | Prayed to Allah; refused revenge |
| Plot to kill | Trusted Allah; left for Madinah |

## Exam traps

- **Boycott**: in Makkah, in the valley; **Ta'if**: a journey to another town.
- The Year of Sorrow = deaths of **Abu Talib and Khadija (RA)**.
- Abu Talib **protected** but **did not accept Islam**.
- Sumayyah is the **first martyr**.
- Surah 111 is about **Abu Lahab**, the Prophet's uncle.
- Give more than one type of reason (religious, economic, political, social).

## Self-test

1. Name two reasons why the Quraysh opposed the Prophet (pbuh).
2. Who was the first martyr of Islam?
3. For how long did the boycott last?
4. What was in the document of the boycott?
5. Which two people died in the Year of Sorrow?
6. Which town did he visit to seek support?
7. Who travelled with him to Ta'if?
8. What did the angel of the mountains offer, and what did he answer?
9. Who proposed that one man from each clan should strike?
10. Who is named in Qur'an 111?

**Answers:** 1. Any two: religious (idols, ancestors); economic (pilgrim trade); political (leadership); social (equality). 2. Sumayyah. 3. About three years. 4. No trade, marriage or contact with Banu Hashim and Banu Muttalib. 5. Abu Talib and Khadija (RA). 6. Ta'if. 7. Zayd ibn Harithah (RA). 8. To crush the two mountains over Makkah; he refused and hoped for people who would worship Allah alone. 9. Abu Jahl. 10. Abu Lahab.
