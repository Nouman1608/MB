# Global contexts: lenses practice

Examples are written in English. Do any task that asks you to produce language in the language your school course uses, and ask your school teacher to check it. MYP has no prescribed content list: schools design their own units, so ask your teacher which concepts and contexts your units use. Marks in brackets are a revision aid, not IB marks.

Course hub: [/boards/ib/ib-myp/myp-language-acquisition/](/boards/ib/ib-myp/myp-language-acquisition/).

## Questions

**1.** List the six global contexts in the IB brief. **[3]**

**2.** Match each topic to the most fitting context and give a reason: (a) a family recipe handed down; (b) a school's recycling scheme; (c) a town's free bus pass for students. **[6]**

**3.** The topic is "mobile phones". Write one question you could discuss through *scientific and technical innovation* and one through *identities and relationships*. **[2]**

**4.** Plan a three-sentence spoken answer on "my favourite place" at personal, local and global scale. **[3]**

**5.** Explain why "tourism" can sit under more than one context. **[3]**

**6.** Read this original text. *"Every spring the village paints its doors a new colour. Visitors come from other towns to photograph them, but the baker says the noise starts too early."* Name two contexts the text touches and explain. **[4]**

## Model answers

**1.** Identities and relationships; orientation in space and time; personal and cultural expression; scientific and technical innovation; globalization and sustainability; fairness and development [3: all six; 2: four or five; 1: two or three].

**2.** (a) Personal and cultural expression, or identities and relationships: family and tradition [2]. (b) Globalization and sustainability: environment [2]. (c) Fairness and development: access [2].

**3.** For example: "How have phones changed the way we study?" [1]; "How do phones change friendships?" [1].

**4.** Personal: why you like it [1]; local: where it is in your town [1]; global: how other places have similar spaces [1].

**5.** Journeys fit orientation in space and time [1]; effects on places fit globalization and sustainability [1]; who benefits fits fairness and development [1].

**6.** Personal and cultural expression (painting doors) [2]; globalization and sustainability or fairness and development (visitors' effects on residents) [2].
