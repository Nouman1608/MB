**These notes condense content area 3.1.1.3 of 9237: "the meaning and importance of the 99 names of Allah, including omnipotence (Qudrah), Allah as creator (Al-Khaliq), Allah as most gracious (Ar-Rehman), Allah as merciful (Ar-Raheem) and Allah as all-knowing (Al-Aleem)".** The full explanation is in the [Belief in Allah and the 99 names study guide](/resources/oxfordaqa-igcse-islamiyat-9237-concept-of-belief-in-allah/).

## Core facts

- Allah has **99 names to learn**; the Hadith (Bukhari and Muslim) says whoever memorises them enters Paradise. This does not limit Allah to 99 names.
- Qur'an 7:180: "To Allah belong the best names, so invoke Him by them."
- Qur'an 42:11: "There is nothing like unto Him." Names describe qualities that are perfect in Allah and cannot be compared with human qualities.
- Muslims use the names in **prayer (du'a)**, in **Bismillah ir-Rahman ir-Raheem**, and in **personal names** such as Abdur-Rahman.

## The five names: one page

| Name | Attribute | Meaning | Proof | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Al-Qadir** | **Qudrah** (omnipotence) | All-powerful; nothing is beyond Him | 2:20; 36:82 ("Be, and it is") | Hope, humility, trust |
| **Al-Khaliq** | Creating | Brings everything into being and sustains it | 59:24; 13:16 | Gratitude, care for creation |
| **Ar-Rehman** | Wide mercy | Mercy for all creation in this world | 59:22; 7:156 | Kindness to everyone |
| **Ar-Raheem** | Special, lasting mercy | Mercy especially for believers, and in the Hereafter | 33:43; 39:53 | Hope of forgiveness |
| **Al-Aleem** | Knowledge | Knows seen and unseen, open and secret | 59:22; 6:59 | Honesty, comfort |

## Remember by contrast

- **Ar-Rehman** = *all creation, now.*
- **Ar-Raheem** = *believers, especially on the Last Day.*
- **Al-Khaliq** = *makes from nothing.* **Al-Bari'** = *originates;* **Al-Musawwir** = *gives form.* (All in 59:24.)
- **Qudrah** is the *attribute* (power); **Al-Qadir** is the *name.*

## Mercy fact to quote

A Hadith in Bukhari and Muslim says Allah created a hundred parts of mercy and sent one to earth. That one part is why creatures show kindness, even to their young. He keeps ninety-nine for the Last Day.

## Passage: Qur'an 59:22-24

| Phrase | Use it for |
|---|---|
| "no deity" | Tawhid |
| "Knower of the unseen and the witnessed" | Al-Aleem |
| "the Entirely Merciful, the Especially Merciful" | Ar-Rehman and Ar-Raheem |
| "the Exalted in Might, the Compeller, the Superior" | Qudrah |
| "the Creator, the Producer, the Fashioner" | Al-Khaliq |
| "Exalted is Allah above whatever they associate with Him" | Rejection of Shirk |
| "Whatever is in the heavens and earth is exalting Him" | All creation praises Allah |

**Balance:** mercy beside majesty means a Muslim hopes and respects at the same time.

## How the names change behaviour

1. **Honesty:** Allah is Al-Aleem, so secrets are not hidden.
2. **Humility:** Allah has Qudrah, so wealth and power do not make a person great.
3. **Gratitude and care:** Allah is the Creator, so life is a gift.
4. **Kindness:** Ar-Rehman and Ar-Raheem teach Muslims to show mercy, as in the Hadith that Allah shows mercy to those who show mercy.
5. **Hope:** do not despair of Allah's mercy (39:53).
6. **Trust:** a Muslim works, then relies on Allah.

## Exam traps

- Give the **meaning** and the **effect**: names are only half the answer.
- Do not say Ar-Rehman and Ar-Raheem are the same thing; their emphasis differs.
- Do not say Allah is "like" a ruler or a father in a literal way.
- Do not list lots of names with no explanation.
- Quote phrases from the passage; do not copy out the whole passage.

## Quick model answers

- **2 marks "Give two names of Allah":** Al-Khaliq; Al-Aleem.
- **4 marks "Explain why belief in Allah as Al-Khaliq is important":** Muslims believe that everything exists because Allah created it, so they see their life and the world as a gift [1 and 1]. This leads to thanks and to caring for the environment as a trust [1 and 1].
- **6 marks:** two points explained, plus a source such as "to Him belong the best names" (59:24).
- **12 marks:** choose three or four names, use the passage for each, then weigh up whether mercy or power is more important in shaping Muslim life, and conclude.

## A model 6-mark paragraph

**Question:** Explain **two** reasons why belief in the names Al-Khaliq and Qudrah is important. Apply **one** teaching. **[6]**

Belief in Allah as Al-Khaliq is important because it makes a Muslim thankful [1]. Everything in the world, including life and health, is a gift from the Creator, so it should be used with care and not wasted [1]. Belief in Qudrah is important because it gives hope [1], since nothing is beyond Allah's power, so a Muslim facing illness or failure does not give up but asks Allah for help while making an effort [1]. The Qur'an says that "Allah is over all things competent" (2:20) [1], which shows that every difficulty, however large, is within His power to change [1].

**Why it scores:** each reason is identified and explained, and the quotation is used to prove the second reason.

## Linking the names to other topics

- **Tawhid (3.1.1.1):** the third type of Tawhid is oneness of names and qualities, so these notes are its detail.
- **Angels (3.1.1.4):** angels carry out Allah's commands, which depends on Qudrah.
- **Al-Qadr (3.1.1.5):** Al-Aleem is the basis of the decree, since Allah knows all that will happen.
- **Life after death (3.1.1.6):** Ar-Raheem gives hope of forgiveness on the Last Day.
- **Salah (3.1.2.3):** the opening chapter in every rak'ah says "the Entirely Merciful, the Especially Merciful".

## Self-test

1. How many names of Allah does the Hadith say believers should learn?
2. Which verse says to call on Allah by His best names?
3. What does Qudrah mean?
4. Which name means Creator?
5. How do Ar-Rehman and Ar-Raheem differ?
6. Which verse says "no leaf falls but that He knows it"?
7. Give two ways that belief in Al-Aleem affects behaviour.
8. Which phrase in 59:22-24 rejects Shirk?
9. What is a *khalifah*?
10. Name two ways Muslims keep the names in daily life.

**Answers:** 1. Ninety-nine. 2. Qur'an 7:180. 3. Power; the ability to do all things. 4. Al-Khaliq. 5. Ar-Rehman is mercy to all creation in this world; Ar-Raheem is special mercy for believers, lasting into the Hereafter. 6. Qur'an 6:59. 7. Any two: honesty; sincerity; comfort; awareness of conscience. 8. "Exalted is Allah above whatever they associate with Him." 9. A steward or trustee of the earth. 10. Any two: starting with Bismillah; praying with the names; naming children with "Abd" and a name of Allah; learning the names.
