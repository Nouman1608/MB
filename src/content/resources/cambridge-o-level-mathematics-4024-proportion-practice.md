## Section A

**1.** Write each statement as an equation, using k for the constant.

**(a)** w is directly proportional to the cube root of v. **[1]**

**(b)** P is inversely proportional to the square of r. **[1]**

**2.** y is directly proportional to x. When x = 4, y = 14. Find y when x = 10. **[3]**

**3.** y is inversely proportional to x. When x = 4, y = 9.

**(a)** Find an equation connecting y and x. **[2]**

**(b)** Find y when x = 12. **[1]**

**4.** y ∝ x². When x = 4, y = 48.

**(a)** Find y when x = 7. **[2]**

**(b)** Find the positive value of x when y = 27. **[2]**

**5.** y ∝ x³. Describe the effect on y when x is halved. **[1]**

## Section B

**6.** The distance d km a person can see to the horizon is directly proportional to the square root of their height h m above sea level. When h = 36, d = 24.

**(a)** Find d when h = 81. **[3]**

**(b)** Find h when d = 10. **[2]**

**7.** The brightness I of a light is inversely proportional to the square of the distance d from it. When d = 2, I = 36.

**(a)** Find I when d = 3. **[3]**

**(b)** Find d when I = 1. **[2]**

**8.** The volume V cm³ of a solid is directly proportional to the cube of its height r cm. When r = 2, V = 32.

**(a)** Find V when r = 1.5. **[3]**

**(b)** Find r when V = 500. **[2]**

**9.** y is directly proportional to the cube root of x. When x = 8, y = 10. Find x when y = 15. **[3]**

**10.** y ∝ x². x is increased by 50%. Find the percentage increase in y. **[2]**

**11.** The cost per person of hiring a minibus is inversely proportional to the number of people sharing it. When 15 people share, each pays $24.

**(a)** Find the cost per person when 20 people share. **[2]**

**(b)** Explain what the constant of proportionality represents in this context. **[1]**

**12.** y is directly proportional to the square of x. When x = 1.5, y = 7.2. Find y when x = 4.3. **[3]**

**13.** The table shows values of x and y.

| x | 1 | 4 | 9 | 16 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| y | 12 | 6 | 4 | 3 |

**(a)** Show that y is inversely proportional to the square root of x. **[2]**

**(b)** Find y when x = 36. **[1]**

## Answers

**1. (a)** **w = k∛v** [1]

**(b)** **P = k/r²** [1] **[2]**

**2.** y = kx [1]; 14 = 4k, k = 3.5 [1]; y = 3.5 × 10 = **35** [1] **[3]**

**3. (a)** y = k/x, 9 = k/4 [1]; k = 36, **y = 36/x** [1]

**(b)** y = 36/12 = **3** [1] **[3]**

**4. (a)** 48 = 16k, k = 3 [1]; y = 3 × 49 = **147** [1]

**(b)** 27 = 3x², x² = 9 [1]; **x = 3** [1] **[4]**

**5.** y is multiplied by (½)³, so **y is divided by 8** [1] **[1]**

**6. (a)** d = k√h; 24 = 6k, k = 4 [1]; d = 4√81 [1] = 4 × 9 = **36 km** [1]

**(b)** 10 = 4√h, √h = 2.5 [1]; **h = 6.25 m** [1] **[5]**

**7. (a)** I = k/d²; 36 = k/4, k = 144 [1]; I = 144/3² [1] = **16** [1]

**(b)** 1 = 144/d², d² = 144 [1]; **d = 12** [1] **[5]**

*Examiner insight:* Moving three times further away (from 2 to 6) would divide the brightness by 9. Checking a result like this against the effect-of-change rule catches errors.

**8. (a)** V = kr³; 32 = 8k, k = 4 [1]; V = 4 × 1.5³ = 4 × 3.375 [1] = **13.5 cm³** [1]

**(b)** 500 = 4r³, r³ = 125 [1]; **r = 5 cm** [1] **[5]**

**9.** y = k∛x; 10 = 2k, k = 5 [1]; 15 = 5∛x, ∛x = 3 [1]; **x = 27** [1] **[3]**

**10.** Multiplier for x is 1.5, so for y it is 1.5² = 2.25 [1]; y increases by **125%** [1] **[2]**

*Examiner insight:* A multiplier of 2.25 means y is 225% of its old value, an increase of 125%. Writing 225% or 100% loses the mark.

**11. (a)** c = k/n; 24 = k/15, k = 360 [1]; c = 360/20 = **$18** [1]

**(b)** k = 360 is the **total cost of hiring the minibus**, in dollars, which stays the same however many people share it [1] **[3]**

**12.** y = kx²; 7.2 = k × 2.25, k = 3.2 [1]; y = 3.2 × 4.3² = 3.2 × 18.49 [1] = 59.168 = **59.2** (3 s.f.) [1] **[3]**

**13. (a)** y√x: 12 × 1 = 12, 6 × 2 = 12, 4 × 3 = 12, 3 × 4 = 12 [1]. The product is constant, so y = 12/√x and y is inversely proportional to √x [1].

**(b)** y = 12/√36 = 12/6 = **2** [1] **[3]**

## Where marks are usually lost

- Not squaring or cubing the given value when finding k (Questions 4, 7 and 8).
- Squaring before dividing in Question 6(b), then making an arithmetic slip.
- Giving a negative distance or height.
- Writing 50% or 225% in Question 10 instead of 125%.
- In Question 13, checking only one pair of values. A relationship must hold for every pair.
- Rounding k in Question 12. Here k = 3.2 is exact; keep it so.

## Frequently asked questions

**How do I know whether a question means direct or inverse proportion?**
Read the wording: "inversely proportional" always means divide. In context, if one quantity goes down as the other goes up and their product is fixed, it is inverse.

**Do I always need to find k?**
No. If the question only asks how y changes when x is multiplied by a number, apply the same power to that number. Otherwise, find k first.

**Is the ∝ symbol tested in 4024?**
Yes. The syllabus says knowledge of the proportional symbol is required, so a question may be written as y ∝ x² instead of in words.
