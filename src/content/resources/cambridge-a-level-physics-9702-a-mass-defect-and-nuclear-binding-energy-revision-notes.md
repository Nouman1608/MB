Subtopic 23.1 of Cambridge International AS & A Level Physics 9702 (2025–2027) covers mass-energy equivalence, nuclear equations, mass defect and binding energy, the binding energy per nucleon curve, and the energy released in fission and fusion. These notes are condensed for recall. For full explanations and worked examples, read the [Mass defect and binding energy study guide](/resources/cambridge-a-level-physics-9702-a-mass-defect-and-nuclear-binding-energy/); to apply the ideas, try the [Mass defect and binding energy practice questions](/resources/cambridge-a-level-physics-9702-a-mass-defect-and-nuclear-binding-energy-practice/).

## Equations and data

```
E = mc²            ΔE = c² Δm
Δm = Z mₚ + (A − Z) mₙ − m_nucleus
binding energy per nucleon = binding energy / A
1 u = 1.66 × 10⁻²⁷ kg      c = 3.00 × 10⁸ m s⁻¹
1 MeV = 1.60 × 10⁻¹³ J     1 u × c² = 1.49 × 10⁻¹⁰ J (data-list values)
```

## Definitions to learn word for word

- **Mass defect**: the difference between the total mass of the separate nucleons and the mass of the nucleus.
- **Binding energy**: the minimum energy required to separate a nucleus into its constituent protons and neutrons.
- **Nuclear fusion**: the joining of two light nuclei to form a heavier nucleus.
- **Nuclear fission**: the splitting of a heavy nucleus into two lighter nuclei of similar mass (usually triggered by absorbing a neutron).

## Nuclear equations: conservation rules

| Conserved | Not separately conserved |
|---|---|
| Nucleon number A (top numbers) | Mass |
| Proton number Z, that is charge (bottom numbers) | Kinetic energy |
| Mass-energy in total | |

Particle symbols: ¹₁p, ¹₀n, ⁴₂He, ⁰₋₁e (β⁻), ⁰₊₁e (β⁺).

Syllabus example: ¹⁴₇N + ⁴₂He → ¹⁷₈O + ¹₁H (A: 18 = 18; Z: 9 = 9).

## Method: binding energy of a nucleus

1. Count protons Z and neutrons A − Z.
2. Total mass of separate nucleons = Z mₚ + (A − Z) mₙ.
3. Subtract the mass of the nucleus (keep all decimal places).
4. Convert u to kg (× 1.66 × 10⁻²⁷).
5. Multiply by c² for joules; divide by 1.60 × 10⁻¹³ for MeV.
6. Divide by A for binding energy per nucleon.

**Helium-4 check:** Δm = 0.030376 u; binding energy = 4.54 × 10⁻¹² J = 28.4 MeV; per nucleon 7.09 MeV.

## Method: energy released in a reaction

1. Add the masses of everything before; add the masses of everything after.
2. Δm = before − after.
3. Positive Δm: energy released = c²Δm. Negative Δm: energy must be supplied.

**Deuterium-tritium fusion:** Δm = 0.018883 u → 2.82 × 10⁻¹² J (17.6 MeV) released.

## The curve: what to sketch

| Region | Shape | Approximate values |
|---|---|---|
| A = 2 | Low start | ²₁H about 1.1 MeV |
| A = 4 | Isolated high point | ⁴₂He about 7.1 MeV |
| A ≈ 56 | Broad maximum | about 8.8 MeV (iron); most stable |
| A ≈ 235 | Gentle fall | uranium about 7.6 MeV |

**Reading it:**

- Higher binding energy per nucleon = more tightly bound = more stable.
- Fusion (left side) and fission (right side) both move **towards the peak**.
- Products have higher binding energy per nucleon, so higher total binding energy, so less mass: the difference is released.

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Nucleon | A proton or a neutron |
| Nucleon number A | Number of protons plus neutrons |
| Proton number Z | Number of protons |
| Unified atomic mass unit | 1/12 of the mass of a carbon-12 atom, 1.66 × 10⁻²⁷ kg |
| Mass-energy equivalence | Any change in energy ΔE has an associated change in mass ΔE/c² |
| Binding energy per nucleon | Binding energy divided by nucleon number |

## Exam traps

- "Binding energy is the energy stored in the nucleus": wrong. It is the energy needed to separate it.
- Mass defect calculated as nucleus minus nucleons, giving a negative value.
- Rounding masses to four significant figures before subtracting.
- Using atomic masses for some particles and nuclear masses for others.
- Forgetting the neutrons emitted in a fission equation when finding Δm.
- Comparing total binding energies to judge stability: use **per nucleon** values.
- Sketching the peak at uranium, or drawing the curve as symmetrical.
- Converting MeV to J with 1.60 × 10⁻¹⁹ instead of 1.60 × 10⁻¹³.
