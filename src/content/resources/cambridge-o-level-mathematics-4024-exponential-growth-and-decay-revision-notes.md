Condensed notes for subtopic **1.17 Exponential growth and decay** of Cambridge O Level Mathematics (Syllabus D) 4024, 2025 to 2027. The [Exponential growth and decay study guide](/resources/cambridge-o-level-mathematics-4024-exponential-growth-and-decay/) explains each method with worked examples; the [Exponential growth and decay practice questions](/resources/cambridge-o-level-mathematics-4024-exponential-growth-and-decay-practice/) give exam-style questions with mark schemes.

## Syllabus outcome

Use exponential growth and decay, for example depreciation and population change. Knowledge of e is not required.

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Exponential growth | repeated multiplication by a factor greater than 1 |
| Exponential decay | repeated multiplication by a factor between 0 and 1 |
| Multiplier (growth factor) | the number multiplied by each period |
| Depreciation | fall in value over time |
| Period | the time interval to which the rate applies |
| Doubling time, half-life | time for a quantity to double or halve |

## Model

```
value after n periods = A x r^n
```

A is the starting value and r is the multiplier.

| Change | r | Type |
|---|---|---|
| +2.5% | 1.025 | growth |
| +4% | 1.04 | growth |
| doubling | 2 | growth |
| -15% | 0.85 | decay |
| -5% | 0.95 | decay |
| halving | 0.5 | decay |
| -20% | 0.8 | decay |

Multiplier for a rise of p%: 1 + p/100. For a fall of p%: 1 - p/100.

## Worked examples

| Question | Working | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| 12 000 grows 2.5% a year, 10 years | 12 000 × 1.025¹⁰ | 15 361 |
| 500 doubles every 3 h, 12 h | 500 × 2⁴ | 8 000 |
| $250 000 rises 4% a year, 3 years | 250 000 × 1.04³ | $281 216 |
| $18 000 loses 15% a year, 3 years | 18 000 × 0.85³ | $11 054.25 |
| 640 g halves every 4 days, 12 days | 640 × 0.5³ | 80 g |
| 60 000 falls 5% a year, 4 years | 60 000 × 0.95⁴ | 48 870 |
| $2 500 at 3% compound, 5 years | 2 500 × 1.03⁵ | $2 898.19 |

Match n to the period: halving every 4 days with 12 days gives n = 3.

## Finding the rate

If a value changes from V₀ to V₁ in n equal periods, then r^n = V₁ ÷ V₀.

| Data | r^n | r | Percentage change |
|---|---|---|---|
| 8 000 to 5 832 in 3 years | 0.729 | 0.9 | 10% decrease |
| 500 to 864 in 3 years | 1.728 | 1.2 | 20% increase |
| 100 to 121 in 2 years | 1.21 | 1.1 | 10% increase |

Use trial with the cube or square roots you know, or take the root on the calculator.

## Finding the number of periods

Try values of n in order until you pass the target. Show at least two lines.

| Question | Table | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| $3 000 at 8%: first year over $6 000 | n = 9: 5 997.01; n = 10: 6 476.77 | 10 years |
| $40 000 loses 20% a year: first year below half | n = 3: 20 480; n = 4: 16 384 | 4 years |
| $5 000 at 6%: first year over $8 000 | n = 8: 7 969.24; n = 9: 8 447.39 | 9 years |

Logarithms are not needed.

## Linear versus exponential

| Year | Linear (+$120 per year) | Exponential (+10% per year) |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | $1 200 | $1 200 |
| 1 | $1 320 | $1 320 |
| 2 | $1 440 | $1 452 |
| 3 | $1 560 | $1 597.20 |

Exponential change adds a bigger amount each period when the quantity is growing.

## Two stages

Multiply the stages: $1 000 at 5% for 2 years then 3% for 3 years:

```
1000 x 1.05^2 x 1.03^3 = $1 204.73
```

## Expression to remember

Growth: value = P × (1 + r/100)ⁿ. Decay: value = P × (1 - r/100)ⁿ. These are the same as compound interest.

## Exam traps

- A 5% rise has multiplier 1.05, not 1.5.
- A 15% fall has multiplier 0.85, not 0.15.
- Count periods, not hours or days.
- Do not use linear working (A × (1 - 0.15 × n)).
- For "how many years", check the previous year as well.
- Keep the full value until the final line.
- Round to a sensible form: whole people, cents for money.
- Always write the multiplier line.

## Self-test

1. Write the multiplier for a 6% increase.
2. Write the multiplier for a 12% decrease.
3. A value of $5 000 increases by 4% a year. Find it after 3 years.
4. A car worth $12 000 loses 10% per year. Find the value after 2 years.
5. A culture of 80 cells doubles every 2 hours. Find the number after 8 hours.
6. A value changes from 400 to 324 in 2 equal steps of the same percentage decrease. Find the percentage decrease.
7. A population of 2 000 grows by 20% a year. After how many whole years does it first exceed 4 000?
8. Write an expression for the value after n years of $P that loses 7% each year.

**Answers**

1. **1.06**.
2. **0.88**.
3. 5 000 × 1.04³ = **$5 624.32**.
4. 12 000 × 0.9² = **$9 720**.
5. 80 × 2⁴ = **1 280**.
6. r² = 324 ÷ 400 = 0.81, so r = 0.9, a decrease of **10%**.
7. 1.2⁴ = 2.0736 (over 2), while 1.2³ = 1.728 (under 2): **4 years**.
8. **P × 0.93ⁿ**.
