This set covers **section 1.10, Applying number**, of the Edexcel International GCSE Mathematics A (4MA1) specification, Issue 2. It is Foundation Tier content that the Higher Tier also assumes, so every question is for both tiers. Exchange rates here are for these questions only. Give money answers to the nearest penny.

## Section A

**1.** Change

**(a)** 2.08 tonnes into kilograms **[1]**

**(b)** 640 ml into litres **[1]**

**(c)** 1.5 m³ into litres. **[1]**

**2.** A rectangular noticeboard measures 85 cm by 60 cm. Work out its area in m². **[2]**

**3.**

**(a)** Write 1 hour 48 minutes in hours as a decimal. **[1]**

**(b)** Write 2.15 hours in hours and minutes. **[1]**

**4.** A flight leaves at 23:35 and the journey takes 3 hours 50 minutes. The arrival airport is in the same time zone.

**(a)** Work out the arrival time, using the 24-hour clock. **[1]**

**(b)** Write the arrival time using the 12-hour clock. **[1]**

**5.** The exchange rate is £1 = 103 Indian rupees. Change 12 000 rupees into pounds. **[2]**

**6.** A 2.4 m plank is cut into as many 45 cm lengths as possible. How many lengths are cut, and how much of the plank is left over? **[2]**

## Section B

**7.** Ravi's basic rate of pay is £11.80 per hour for a 37.5-hour week. Overtime is paid at time and a half. One week he works 43 hours. Work out his pay for that week. **[4]**

**8.** A chicken needs 45 minutes of cooking per kilogram, plus an extra 25 minutes. A 2.4 kg chicken must be ready at 19:15. Work out the latest time it can go into the oven. **[3]**

**9.** A garden fence has 8 panels. Each panel is a rectangle 1.8 m wide and 1.5 m high. Both sides of every panel are to be painted with one coat. One litre of paint covers 6 m², and paint is sold in 3-litre tins costing £21.50 each. Work out the cost of the paint needed. **[4]**

**10.** A car hire company charges £38 per day plus 15p for each kilometre driven over 200 km. Lucia hires a car for 5 days and drives 640 km. Work out the total cost. **[3]**

**11.** A laptop costs 899 US dollars in New York and £720 in London. The exchange rate is £1 = 1.24 US dollars. In which city is the laptop cheaper, and by how much in pounds? **[3]**

**12.** A shower uses 9.5 litres of water per minute. Each of the 4 people in a family has an 8-minute shower every day. Water costs £1.95 per m³. Work out the cost of the water used for the family's showers in one week. **[4]**

**13.** A taxi fare is £3.50 plus £1.85 per kilometre. Three friends share the fare for a 7.2 km journey equally. How much should each pay so that the whole fare is covered? **[3]**

## Answers

**1. (a)** **2080 kg** [1]

**(b)** **0.64 litres** [1]

**(c)** **1500 litres** [1]

**2.** 0.85 m × 0.6 m [1] = **0.51 m²** [1]

**3. (a)** 48 ÷ 60 = 0.8, so **1.8 hours** [1]

**(b)** 0.15 × 60 = 9, so **2 hours 9 minutes** [1]

**4. (a)** 23:35 + 25 min = 00:00, then + 3 h 25 min = **03:25** [1]

**(b)** **3:25 am** [1]

**5.** 12 000 ÷ 103 [1] = 116.504…, so **£116.50** [1]

**6.** 240 ÷ 45 = 5 remainder 15 [1]; **5 lengths, with 15 cm left over** [1]

**7.** Basic pay: 37.5 × 11.80 = £442.50 [1]; overtime rate: 1.5 × 11.80 = £17.70 [1]; overtime: 5.5 × 17.70 = £97.35 [1]; total **£539.85** [1]

**8.** 45 × 2.4 + 25 = 133 minutes [1] = 2 h 13 min [1]; 19:15 − 2 h 13 min = **17:02** [1]

**9.** One side of one panel = 1.8 × 1.5 = 2.7 m² [1]; total area = 2.7 × 8 × 2 = 43.2 m² [1]; paint = 43.2 ÷ 6 = 7.2 litres, so 3 tins (9 litres) are needed [1]; cost = 3 × 21.50 = **£64.50** [1]

**10.** Daily charge: 5 × 38 = £190 [1]; extra distance 640 − 200 = 440 km, costing 440 × 0.15 = £66 [1]; total **£256** [1]

**11.** 899 ÷ 1.24 [1] = £725.00 [1]; **London is cheaper, by £5.00** [1]

**12.** Water per day: 9.5 × 8 × 4 = 304 litres [1]; per week: 304 × 7 = 2128 litres [1] = 2.128 m³ [1]; cost: 2.128 × 1.95 = 4.1496, so **£4.15** [1]

**13.** Fare = 3.50 + 1.85 × 7.2 = 3.50 + 13.32 [1] = £16.82 [1]; 16.82 ÷ 3 = 5.6066…, so each pays **£5.61** [1]

## Where marks are usually lost

- Multiplying 85 × 60 = 5100 and dividing by 100 in question 2, giving 51 m² instead of 0.51 m².
- Writing 1.48 hours in question 3(a).
- Giving 27:25 in question 4(a) instead of passing midnight.
- Paying all 43 hours at the overtime rate in question 7.
- Painting only one side, or rounding 2.4 tins down, in question 9.
- Charging 15p for all 640 km in question 10.
- Forgetting to convert litres to m³ in question 12.
