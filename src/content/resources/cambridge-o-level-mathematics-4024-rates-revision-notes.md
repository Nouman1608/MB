Condensed notes for subtopic **1.12 Rates** of Cambridge O Level Mathematics (Syllabus D) 4024, 2025 to 2027. The [Rates study guide](/resources/cambridge-o-level-mathematics-4024-rates/) explains each rate with worked examples; the [Rates practice questions](/resources/cambridge-o-level-mathematics-4024-rates-practice/) give exam-style questions with mark schemes.

## Syllabus outcomes

1. Use common measures of rate: hourly rates of pay, exchange rates, flow rates, fuel consumption.
2. Apply other measures of rate: pressure, density, population density. Required formulas will be given in the question.
3. Solve problems involving average speed. Knowledge of the speed, distance and time formula is required.

Rates are written in forms such as m/s and g/cm³.

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning | Example unit |
|---|---|---|
| Rate | quantity per unit of another quantity | $ per hour |
| Exchange rate | value of one currency in another | 1 dollar = 4.5 francs |
| Flow rate | volume per unit of time | litres per minute |
| Fuel consumption | fuel per distance, or distance per fuel | L per 100 km, km per L |
| Density | mass per unit volume | g/cm³ |
| Pressure | force per unit area | N/m² |
| Population density | people per unit area | people per km² |
| Average speed | total distance ÷ total time | km/h |

## Formulas

| Quantity | Formula | Given in exam? |
|---|---|---|
| Speed | distance ÷ time | **must know** |
| Distance | speed × time | **must know** |
| Time | distance ÷ speed | **must know** |
| Density | mass ÷ volume | given in question |
| Pressure | force ÷ area | given in question |
| Population density | population ÷ area | given in question |

Rearrange as needed: mass = density × volume; force = pressure × area.

## Method

1. Identify the rate and its units.
2. Find the rate for one unit (or use the rate directly).
3. Multiply or divide.
4. Check the units of the answer.

Unit check: dollars per hour × hours = dollars.

## Worked examples

| Type | Question | Working | Answer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pay | $8.40/h for 37.5 h | 8.40 × 37.5 | $315 |
| Overtime | 6 h at 1.5 × rate | 8.40 × 1.5 × 6 | $75.60 |
| Exchange | $80 at $1 = 4.5 francs | 80 × 4.5 | 360 francs |
| Exchange back | 1 125 francs | 1 125 ÷ 4.5 | $250 |
| Flow | 540 L at 12 L/min | 540 ÷ 12 | 45 min |
| Net flow | 15 L/min in, 6 L/min out, 450 L | 450 ÷ 9 | 50 min |
| Fuel | 6.4 L per 100 km, 350 km | 6.4 × 3.5 | 22.4 L |
| Fuel range | 56 L at 6.4 L per 100 km | 56 ÷ 6.4 × 100 | 875 km |
| Density | 540 g in 200 cm³ | 540 ÷ 200 | 2.7 g/cm³ |
| Mass | 125 cm³ at 8.0 g/cm³ | 8.0 × 125 | 1 000 g |
| Pressure | 600 N on 0.25 m² | 600 ÷ 0.25 | 2 400 N/m² |
| Population density | 1 200 000 people in 480 km² | 1 200 000 ÷ 480 | 2 500 per km² |

## Speed, distance and time

Triangle: distance on top, speed and time on the bottom.

| Find | Cover | Formula |
|---|---|---|
| Speed | speed | distance ÷ time |
| Distance | distance | speed × time |
| Time | time | distance ÷ speed |

Time conversions:

| Time | In hours |
|---|---|
| 30 min | 0.5 |
| 45 min | 0.75 |
| 1 h 45 min | 1.75 |
| 20 min | 1/3 (0.333...) |
| 2 h 12 min | 2.2 |

Decimal hours back to minutes: multiply the decimal part by 60. For example, 0.875 h = 52.5 min.

### Average speed

Average speed = total distance ÷ total time.

```
120 km at 60 km/h  →  2 h
90 km at 45 km/h   →  2 h
average = 210 ÷ 4 = 52.5 km/h
```

The mean of 60 and 45 is also 52.5 here because the times are equal. If the times differ, the mean of the speeds is wrong.

### km/h and m/s

| Change | Rule | Example |
|---|---|---|
| km/h to m/s | ÷ 3.6 | 72 km/h = 20 m/s |
| m/s to km/h | × 3.6 | 15 m/s = 54 km/h |

## Currency conversion rule

If 1 unit of A = r units of B, then A → B means multiply by r, and B → A means divide by r. Sense check: the currency with the smaller value gives the bigger number.

## Exam traps

- 1 hour 45 minutes is 1.75 hours, not 1.45.
- Do not average two speeds unless the times are equal.
- Convert units before using a formula.
- Exchange the right way round.
- Subtract the outflow for a net flow rate.
- Show the formula, the substitution and the answer.
- Quote the unit: km/h, g/cm³, N/m².
- Check whether the fuel figure is per 100 km or per litre.

## Self-test

1. A plumber earns $14.50 per hour. How much does he earn in 6.5 hours?
2. $1 = 4.5 francs. How many francs is $36?
3. A tank fills at 8 litres per minute. How long does it take to put in 250 litres?
4. A car uses 8 litres per 100 km. How much fuel does it use for 275 km?
5. A stone has mass 270 g and volume 100 cm³. Find its density.
6. A cyclist rides 36 km in 2 h 15 min. Find the average speed.
7. Change 90 km/h to m/s.
8. A car travels at 40 km/h for 1 hour and then 60 km/h for 3 hours. Find the average speed.

**Answers**

1. 14.50 × 6.5 = **$94.25**.
2. 36 × 4.5 = **162 francs**.
3. 250 ÷ 8 = **31.25 minutes** (31 min 15 s).
4. 8 × 2.75 = **22 litres**.
5. 270 ÷ 100 = **2.7 g/cm³**.
6. 2 h 15 min = 2.25 h, 36 ÷ 2.25 = **16 km/h**.
7. 90 ÷ 3.6 = **25 m/s**.
8. Distance = 40 + 180 = 220 km; time = 4 h; **55 km/h**.
