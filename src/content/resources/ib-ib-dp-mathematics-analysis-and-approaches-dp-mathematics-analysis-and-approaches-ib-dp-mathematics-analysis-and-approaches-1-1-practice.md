This practice set covers syllabus section **1.1** of IB DP Mathematics: Analysis and Approaches (first
assessment 2021), common to SL and HL: operations with numbers in the form a × 10ᵏ, where 1 ≤ a < 10 and
k ∈ ℤ. Section A is calculator-free, like Paper 1; Section B expects technology, like Paper 2. Give every
answer in the form a × 10ᵏ unless told otherwise, exactly or to 3 significant figures.

Learn the methods first in the [section 1.1 study guide](/resources/ib-ib-dp-mathematics-analysis-and-approaches-dp-mathematics-analysis-and-approaches-ib-dp-mathematics-analysis-and-approaches-1-1/)
and the [section 1.1 revision notes](/resources/ib-ib-dp-mathematics-analysis-and-approaches-dp-mathematics-analysis-and-approaches-ib-dp-mathematics-analysis-and-approaches-1-1-revision-notes/).

## Section A

**1.** (calculator-free) Write each number in the form a × 10ᵏ.

**(a)** 0.000 052 **[1]**
**(b)** 47 × 10⁶ **[1]**

**2.** (calculator-free) Calculate (3.2 × 10⁻⁴) × (2.5 × 10⁹). **[2]**

**3.** (calculator-free) Calculate (7.2 × 10⁵) ÷ (1.8 × 10⁻²). **[2]**

**4.** (calculator-free) Calculate 8.4 × 10⁶ + 5.0 × 10⁵. **[2]**

**5.** (calculator-free)

**(a)** Calculate (4 × 10³)³. **[2]**
**(b)** Calculate √(8.1 × 10⁻⁵). **[2]**

**6.** (calculator-free) Calculate 2.4 × 10⁻³ − 9 × 10⁻⁴. **[2]**

**7.** (calculator-free) A student writes 3.6 × 10⁴ + 2.1 × 10³ = 5.7 × 10⁷. Identify the error and give the
correct answer. **[2]**

## Section B

**8.** (calculator allowed) The mean distance from the Sun to the Earth is 1.496 × 10¹¹ m. Light travels at
2.998 × 10⁸ m s⁻¹.

**(a)** Find the time light takes to travel from the Sun to the Earth, in seconds, to 3 significant
figures. **[2]**
**(b)** Convert your answer to minutes. **[1]**

**9.** (calculator allowed) Model the Earth as a sphere of radius 6.371 × 10⁶ m.

**(a)** Find its surface area, using S = 4πr². **[2]**
**(b)** Find its volume, using V = (4/3)πr³. **[2]**

**10.** (calculator allowed) The mass of an electron is 9.109 × 10⁻³¹ kg. Find the total mass of
6.022 × 10²³ electrons. **[2]**

**11.** (calculator allowed) A bacterium is 2.5 × 10⁻⁶ m long and a virus particle is 5.0 × 10⁻⁹ m across.

**(a)** Find how many times longer the bacterium is than the virus. **[2]**
**(b)** Show how an estimate using the powers of 10 confirms the size of your answer. **[1]**

**12.** (calculator allowed) A storage drive holds 2 × 10¹² bytes. Each photo uses 4.5 × 10⁶ bytes.

**(a)** Find the maximum number of whole photos the drive can store. **[2]**
**(b)** Write your answer to (a) in the form a × 10ᵏ, correct to 3 significant figures. **[1]**

## Answers

**1. (a)** **5.2 × 10⁻⁵** [1]
**(b)** 47 × 10⁶ = **4.7 × 10⁷** [1]

**2.** 3.2 × 2.5 = 8 and 10⁻⁴⁺⁹ = 10⁵ [1]; **8 × 10⁵** [1] **[2]**

**3.** 7.2 ÷ 1.8 = 4 and 10⁵⁻⁽⁻²⁾ = 10⁷ [1]; **4 × 10⁷** [1] **[2]**

**4.** 8.4 × 10⁶ + 0.5 × 10⁶ [1] = **8.9 × 10⁶** [1] **[2]**
*Examiner insight:* Converting both numbers to ordinary form (8 400 000 + 500 000) is also valid, but the
final answer must still be returned to the form a × 10ᵏ.

**5. (a)** 4³ × 10⁹ = 64 × 10⁹ [1] = **6.4 × 10¹⁰** [1]
**(b)** √(81 × 10⁻⁶) [1] = **9 × 10⁻³** [1]
*Examiner insight:* Rewriting 8.1 × 10⁻⁵ as 81 × 10⁻⁶ makes the power even; trying √8.1 by hand leads
nowhere.

**6.** 2.4 × 10⁻³ − 0.9 × 10⁻³ [1] = **1.5 × 10⁻³** [1] **[2]**

**7.** The student multiplied the powers of 10 (added the indices) instead of adding the numbers [1].
3.6 × 10⁴ + 0.21 × 10⁴ = **3.81 × 10⁴** [1] **[2]**

**8. (a)** t = (1.496 × 10¹¹) ÷ (2.998 × 10⁸) [1] = 498.99… = **499 s** (4.99 × 10² s) [1]
**(b)** 498.99… ÷ 60 = **8.32 minutes** [1]
*Examiner insight:* Use the unrounded 498.99… in (b); 499 ÷ 60 also gives 8.32 here, but early rounding
can change the third figure in other questions.

**9. (a)** 4π(6.371 × 10⁶)² [1] = **5.10 × 10¹⁴ m²** [1]
**(b)** (4/3)π(6.371 × 10⁶)³ [1] = **1.08 × 10²¹ m³** [1]

**10.** (9.109 × 10⁻³¹) × (6.022 × 10²³) [1] = 5.4854… × 10⁻⁷ = **5.49 × 10⁻⁷ kg** [1] **[2]**
*Examiner insight:* A quick size check, 9 × 6 ≈ 54 and 10⁻³¹ × 10²³ = 10⁻⁸, gives about 5 × 10⁻⁷, which
confirms the GDC answer.

**11. (a)** (2.5 × 10⁻⁶) ÷ (5.0 × 10⁻⁹) [1] = **500** (5.00 × 10²) times [1]
**(b)** 2.5 ÷ 5 = 0.5 and 10⁻⁶⁻⁽⁻⁹⁾ = 10³, so 0.5 × 10³ = 5 × 10² [1]

**12. (a)** (2 × 10¹²) ÷ (4.5 × 10⁶) = 444 444.4… [1], so **444 444 photos** (round down, as only whole
photos fit) [1]
**(b)** **4.44 × 10⁵** [1]
*Examiner insight:* Rounding 444 444.4 up would claim space for a photo that does not fit; in context,
round down.

## Where marks are usually lost

- Leaving a outside the range 1 ≤ a < 10 after the arithmetic.
- Copying GDC notation into the final answer.
- Adding indices when adding numbers.
- Losing the sign when subtracting a negative index.
- Rounding a count up in a context where only whole items fit.

## Frequently asked questions

**Must every answer be in the form a × 10ᵏ?**
Only when the question asks for it, or when the number is very large or very small. An answer such as 8.32
minutes is fine as it is.

**Is a = 1 allowed?**
Yes. The condition is 1 ≤ a < 10, so 1 × 10⁵ is in the form, but 10 × 10⁴ is not.
