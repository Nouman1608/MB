**These notes condense content area 3.2.1.3 of 9237: "The details of the call to Prophethood in Cave of Hira, including his encounter with Jibril (AS)" and "Hazrat Muhammad's (pbuh) reaction and the role of Hazrat Khadija (RA) and Waraqa."** The full account is in the [call to prophethood study guide](/resources/oxfordaqa-igcse-islamiyat-9237-the-call-to-prophethood-nabuat/).

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Nabuat | Prophethood |
| Wahy | Revelation |
| Hira | The cave on Jabal al-Nur where the first revelation came |
| Jibril (AS) | The angel of revelation |
| Iqra | "Read" or "Recite": the first word of revelation |
| Zammiluni | "Cover me": the Prophet's (pbuh) words to Khadija (RA) |
| Namus | The angel of revelation, as Waraqa called him |
| Fatrah | The pause in revelation |
| Ummi | Unlettered |

## Sequence of events

1. About 610 CE, aged 40, in **Ramadhan**, he is in retreat in the **Cave of Hira**.
2. **Jibril (AS)** appears: "Iqra".
3. "I am not one who can read": said three times.
4. Each time Jibril (AS) holds him tightly and releases him.
5. Jibril (AS) recites **96:1-5**.
6. The Prophet (pbuh) descends the mountain, trembling.
7. At home he says "Cover me" to **Khadija (RA)**.
8. Khadija (RA) comforts him and believes him.
9. She takes him to **Waraqa ibn Nawfal**, who confirms the revelation and warns of opposition.
10. A pause (**fatrah**); then revelation resumes with 74:1-2: "arise and warn".

## The Prophet's (pbuh) reaction

- Fear and confusion: he had not expected to be a prophet.
- He did not claim any greatness; he sought the comfort of his wife.
- It shows his honesty and humanity.
- It supports the belief that revelation came from Allah, not from his own thoughts.

## The role of Khadija (RA)

| Action | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Comforted him | "Allah will never disgrace you" |
| Named his qualities | Keeps ties of kinship, bears the burdens of the weak, helps the poor, honours guests, helps in calamity |
| Believed him | First person to accept Islam |
| Took him to Waraqa | Sought knowledge of scripture |

## The role of Waraqa ibn Nawfal

- Cousin of Khadija (RA); a Christian who had studied scripture; old and blind.
- Said it was the same angel (Namus) who came to Musa (AS).
- Warned that the people would drive him out.
- Promised support if he lived; he died soon after.
- Importance: **confirmation** from outside the family and a link to earlier prophets.

## The first believers

| Person | Detail |
|---|---|
| Khadija (RA) | First adult believer |
| Ali (RA) | Boy of about ten in the Prophet's (pbuh) household |
| Zayd ibn Harithah (RA) | Freed servant and adopted son |
| Abu Bakr (RA) | Closest friend; accepted without hesitation |

## Using the prescribed passage, Qur'an 96:1-5

| Words | Use for |
|---|---|
| "Recite in the name of your Lord who created" | First command; Allah as Creator; faith linked to knowledge |
| "Created man from a clinging substance" | Humble origin of humans; humility |
| "your Lord is the most Generous" | Allah's generosity in giving knowledge |
| "Who taught by the pen" | Writing and education are gifts of Allah |
| "Taught man that which he knew not" | Revelation is knowledge that humans could not reach themselves |

**Link to the unlettered Prophet:** he could not read, so the Qur'an cannot be his own composition (7:157).

## Importance of the call

- Start of the final revelation and of Islam.
- Confirms Muhammad (pbuh) as Prophet and Messenger.
- Shows the high value of learning.
- Connects him to earlier prophets such as Musa (AS).
- Shows the importance of supportive family and friends.

## Why each person matters: one line each

- **Jibril (AS):** the means of revelation; he delivered the words of Allah.
- **Khadija (RA):** emotional support, belief and practical help in the first hours.
- **Waraqa:** scholarly confirmation and a warning about the future.
- **Abu Bakr (RA):** early proof that those who knew his character trusted him.

## How to build a 6-mark answer

1. Give the first reason and explain it with a detail from the story (AO1).
2. Give the second reason and explain it (AO1).
3. Add one quotation, such as 96:1 or 93:8, and say how it supports your point (AO2).

**Mini example (Waraqa):** "Waraqa confirmed that the angel was the one who came to Musa. This reassured the Prophet (pbuh) that he was not mistaken, and showed that his message belonged to the line of earlier prophets."

## Exam traps

- **Hira** is the cave of revelation; **Thawr** is the cave of the Hijrah.
- The Prophet (pbuh) said "I am not one who can read" because he was **unlettered**.
- Khadija (RA) was the first **believer**; Abu Bakr (RA) was the first adult **man** to believe.
- Waraqa **warned** and **promised support**: give both.
- The first verses speak of **reading and knowledge**, not rules or ritual.
- It happened in **Ramadhan**, in about 610 CE.

## Self-test

1. In which cave did the first revelation come?
2. Who was the angel of revelation?
3. What was the first word of revelation?
4. What did the Prophet (pbuh) reply?
5. How many times did the angel hold him tightly?
6. What did he say to Khadija (RA) when he returned home?
7. Give two qualities that Khadija (RA) named in comforting him.
8. Who was Waraqa ibn Nawfal?
9. What did Waraqa warn him about?
10. Which prophet did Waraqa link him with?

**Answers:** 1. The Cave of Hira. 2. Jibril (AS). 3. Iqra ("Read" or "Recite"). 4. "I am not one who can read." 5. Three times. 6. "Cover me, cover me." 7. Any two: keeps ties with relatives; bears the burdens of the weak; helps the poor; honours guests; helps in calamity. 8. Khadija's (RA) cousin, a Christian who had studied the scriptures. 9. That his people would drive him out and treat him with hostility. 10. Musa (AS).
