These notes condense subtopic 5.2 of Cambridge IGCSE ICT 0417: the four named health problems from prolonged use of IT equipment, their causes and how to prevent them. For the full explanation of why each problem happens, read the [health problems study guide](/resources/cambridge-igcse-ict-0417-health-problems/), then try the [health problems practice questions](/resources/cambridge-igcse-ict-0417-health-problems-practice/).

## Syllabus coverage

| Syllabus wording | What you must be able to do |
|---|---|
| RSI, back problems, eye problems, headaches | Name and describe each problem |
| The causes of these health issues | Give specific causes, ideally linked to a scenario |
| Strategies for preventing them | Give a prevention that matches each cause |

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| RSI (repetitive strain injury) | Pain, stiffness or swelling in fingers, wrists, arms or shoulders from repeating the same action |
| Ergonomic equipment | Keyboards, mice and chairs designed to keep the body in a natural, comfortable position |
| Wrist rest | A pad in front of the keyboard or mouse that keeps the wrist supported and straight |
| Anti-glare screen | A screen or filter that reduces reflections from lights and windows |
| Eye strain | Tired, sore or aching eyes from focusing on a screen for a long time |
| Posture | The position of the body when sitting; good posture keeps the back straight and the neck level |
| Footrest | A support that lets a user's feet rest flat when the chair is raised to desk height |

## The four problems at a glance

### RSI

- **Causes:** long periods of typing; repeated clicking and scrolling; wrists bent while typing; heavy texting on touch screens.
- **Prevention:** breaks and hand exercises; ergonomic keyboard and mouse; wrist rest; correct arm and wrist position; voice recognition software instead of typing.

### Back problems (including neck and shoulders)

- **Causes:** sitting still for long periods; slouching; chair with poor back support or wrong height; screen too low or high; laptop used on the lap.
- **Prevention:** adjustable chair with lower-back support; footrest; screen raised or tilted to eye level; laptop stand; good posture; breaks to stand and stretch.

### Eye problems

- **Causes:** staring at a screen for long periods; blinking less, so eyes dry; glare and reflections; poor lighting; badly set brightness or contrast; small text; flickering screen.
- **Prevention:** breaks and looking at distant objects; anti-glare screen or filter; blinds and better screen position; adjust brightness, contrast and text size; screen without flicker; regular eye tests.

### Headaches

- **Causes:** eye strain; glare; poor lighting; tension from poor posture; long sessions without breaks.
- **Prevention:** anti-glare screen; better lighting; adjust screen settings; breaks; good posture; regular eye tests.

Use this to avoid repeating one prevention when a question asks for two different ones.

## Answer pattern

**Cause** (what the user does or what the setting is) **+ problem** (which part of the body) **+ prevention** (what to change) **+ reason** (why it removes the cause).

Example: the monitor sits too low, so the user bends his neck for hours and develops neck and back pain; raising the monitor to eye level keeps the neck straight.

## Exam traps

- Writing "take breaks" for every problem. It earns credit once; give a second, different prevention.
- Mismatching cause and prevention, for example "use a wrist rest" for eye strain.
- Writing "using a computer too much" as a cause. Name the specific action or setting.
- Bringing in physical safety hazards (electrocution, fire, tripping over cables). These are Topic 8.
- Confusing RSI with back pain. RSI affects the hands, wrists and arms.
- Ignoring the scenario. If the question mentions a window behind the screen, glare is the expected cause.

## Self-test

1. What do the letters RSI stand for?
2. Name the parts of the body most affected by RSI.
3. Give two causes of RSI.
4. Describe how a wrist rest helps prevent RSI.
5. Give two causes of back problems from IT use.
6. Explain why using a laptop on the lap can cause neck pain.
7. Give two causes of eye strain.
8. Name two pieces of equipment that help prevent eye problems.
9. Explain why voice recognition software can reduce RSI.
10. Give one cause of headaches that is also a cause of eye problems.
11. A user's chair is raised so her arms are level with the desk, but her feet no longer touch the floor. What should she use?
12. Why should an answer on headaches not be classed as a physical safety issue?

### Answers

1. Repetitive strain injury.
2. Fingers, wrists, arms and shoulders.
3. Any two: typing for long periods; repeated clicking; wrists bent while typing; heavy texting on a touch screen.
4. It supports the wrist and keeps it straight, so the tendons are not strained in a bent position.
5. Any two: slouching; sitting still for long periods; chair without back support or at the wrong height; screen too low or too high.
6. The user has to look down at the screen, so the neck is bent forward for long periods.
7. Any two: long periods looking at a screen; glare; poor lighting; small text; flickering screen.
8. Any two: anti-glare screen or filter; window blinds; a screen without flicker.
9. The user speaks instead of typing, so the fingers and wrists make fewer repeated movements.
10. Glare from lights or windows (also eye strain or poor lighting).
11. A footrest.
12. Headaches are a health issue affecting the user's body over time; physical safety covers hazards such as electrocution, fire and tripping that cause immediate injury.
