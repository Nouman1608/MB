These notes condense **section 1.2, Fractions**, of the Pearson Edexcel International GCSE Mathematics A (4MA1) specification, Issue 2. It is Foundation Tier content that the Higher Tier also assumes. For full explanations and worked examples, use the [section 1.2 study guide](/resources/edexcel-igcse-mathematics-4ma1-fractions/).

## Condensed recall: the methods

**Simplify (1.2 A).** Divide top and bottom by their HCF. 45/75: HCF 15, so 3/5.

**Mixed ↔ improper (1.2 B).**

- 3 5/8 = (3 × 8 + 5)/8 = 29/8
- 29/8 = 3 remainder 5 = 3 5/8

**Common denominator (1.2 C).** Use the LCM of the denominators. For sixths and fifteenths, use 30.

**Order (1.2 D).** Same denominator, then compare numerators; or convert each to a decimal. Answer with the original fractions.

**Fraction of an amount (1.2 D).** ÷ bottom, × top. 3/7 of 91 = 13 × 3 = 39.

**One number as a fraction of another (1.2 E).** part/whole, same units, then simplify. 400 g of 2 kg = 400/2000 = 1/5.

**Add or subtract (1.2 F).** Common denominator, then combine numerators. With mixed numbers, either do wholes and fractions separately (borrow if needed) or use improper fractions.

**Convert (1.2 G).** top ÷ bottom = decimal; × 100 = percentage.

**Unit fractions (1.2 H).** ÷ n is the same as × 1/n. The reciprocal of a/b is b/a; a number times its reciprocal is 1.

**Multiply (1.2 I).** Make improper, cancel across, multiply tops and bottoms.

**Divide (1.2 I).** Make improper, multiply by the reciprocal of the second fraction.

## Problem patterns

- **"Of the rest":** multiply fractions. 3/4 of the remaining 2/5 = 3/10 of the whole.
- **Reverse:** if a/b of x is known, divide by a/b. 4/9 of x = 36 gives x = 81.
- **How many fit:** divide. 3 3/4 m of ribbon cut into 5/8 m pieces: 15/4 ÷ 5/8 = 6 pieces.
- **Fraction left:** 1 − (sum of fractions used), with a common denominator.

## Worked recall

**Ordering a mixed list.** Order 0.6, 5/8 and 3/5: 5/8 = 0.625 and 3/5 = 0.6, so 0.6 and 3/5 are equal and 5/8 is the largest.

**A fraction of a fraction.** In a school, 3/5 of the students are girls and 1/4 of the girls play hockey. The fraction of all students who are girls playing hockey is 1/4 × 3/5 = 3/20.

**Subtracting with borrowing.** 4 1/5 − 1 2/3: 1/5 < 2/3, so write 4 1/5 = 3 6/5, then 3 18/15 − 1 10/15 = 2 8/15.

**Dividing to compare.** How many times does 3/8 go into 2 1/4? 9/4 ÷ 3/8 = 9/4 × 8/3 = 6. Division answers "how many of these fit".

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Numerator | The top number of a fraction |
| Denominator | The bottom number; how many equal parts make a whole |
| Equivalent fractions | Fractions with the same value, such as 2/3 and 10/15 |
| Simplest form (lowest terms) | Numerator and denominator have no common factor other than 1 |
| Vulgar fraction | A fraction written as one whole number over another |
| Improper fraction | A fraction whose numerator is larger than its denominator |
| Mixed number | A whole number and a proper fraction, such as 2 3/5 |
| Common denominator | A shared denominator, ideally the LCM of the denominators |
| Unit fraction | A fraction with numerator 1 |
| Reciprocal (multiplicative inverse) | The number that multiplies to give 1: the reciprocal of 7 is 1/7 |

## Exam traps

- **Never add denominators.** 2/5 + 1/3 = 6/15 + 5/15 = 11/15.
- **Mixed numbers in × and ÷.** Always convert to improper first.
- **Flip only the second fraction** when dividing.
- **Borrowing in subtraction.** 5 1/4 − 2 2/3: 1/4 < 2/3, so borrow, or use improper fractions.
- **Units.** Convert before writing "part over whole": 20 minutes of 2 hours is 20/120 = 1/6.
- **Unfinished answers.** Simplify fully, and convert improper answers if a mixed number is asked for.
- **Of the remainder.** Do not take the second fraction of the original whole.

## Self-test

1. Simplify 72/120.
2. Write 5 4/9 as an improper fraction.
3. Write 53/8 as a mixed number.
4. Which is larger, 5/7 or 7/10? Show how you decide.
5. Find 4/9 of 216.
6. Write 750 m as a fraction of 2 km, in its simplest form.
7. Work out 2 3/4 + 1 5/6.
8. Work out 4 2/5 − 1 3/4.
9. Work out 1 1/5 × 2 1/2.
10. Work out 2 2/3 ÷ 4.
11. Write 9/16 as a decimal and as a percentage.
12. 5/6 of a number is 65. Find the number.

**Answers**

1. HCF 24: 3/5.
2. 49/9.
3. 6 5/8.
4. 50/70 and 49/70, so 5/7 is larger.
5. 216 ÷ 9 × 4 = 96.
6. 750/2000 = 3/8.
7. 3 + (9/12 + 10/12) = 3 + 19/12 = 4 7/12.
8. 22/5 − 7/4 = 88/20 − 35/20 = 53/20 = 2 13/20.
9. 6/5 × 5/2 = 3.
10. 8/3 × 1/4 = 2/3.
11. 0.5625 and 56.25%.
12. 65 ÷ 5 × 6 = 78.
