Condensed notes for subtopic **1.6 The four operations** of Cambridge O Level Mathematics (Syllabus D) 4024, 2025 to 2027. The [The four operations study guide](/resources/cambridge-o-level-mathematics-4024-the-four-operations/) explains each method with worked examples; the [The four operations practice questions](/resources/cambridge-o-level-mathematics-4024-the-four-operations-practice/) give exam-style questions with mark schemes.

## Syllabus outcome

Use the four operations for calculations with integers, fractions and decimals, including the correct ordering of operations and the use of brackets. This includes negative numbers, improper fractions, mixed numbers and practical situations such as temperature changes.

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Sum | result of addition | sum of 4 and 9 is 13 |
| Difference | result of subtraction | difference of 9 and 4 is 5 |
| Product | result of multiplication | product of 4 and 9 is 36 |
| Quotient | result of division | quotient of 36 and 4 is 9 |
| Reciprocal | fraction turned upside down | reciprocal of 2/5 is 5/2 |
| Improper fraction | numerator at least the denominator | 11/4 |
| Mixed number | whole number plus fraction | 2 3/4 |

## Sign rules

| Operation | Result |
|---|---|
| a - (-b) | a + b |
| a + (-b) | a - b |
| (+) × (+), (-) × (-) | positive |
| (+) × (-), (-) × (+) | negative |
| (-) ÷ (-) | positive |
| (-) ÷ (+) | negative |

Examples: -6 - (-9) = 3; (-4) × (-7) = 28; (-36) ÷ (-9) = 4; (-20) ÷ 4 = -5; (-2) × (-3) × (-4) = -24.

## Order of operations

| Step | What | Note |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Brackets | includes fraction bars and roots |
| 2 | Indices | powers and roots |
| 3 | × and ÷ | left to right |
| 4 | + and - | left to right |

```
8 + 3 x (12 - 7)^2 ÷ 5 = 8 + 3 x 25 ÷ 5 = 8 + 15 = 23
(-3)^2 - 2 x (-5)      = 9 + 10 = 19
2 - 3 x (-4)           = 2 + 12 = 14
12 ÷ 3 x 2             = 4 x 2 = 8
(7 + 5)/(10 - 4)       = 12/6 = 2
```

Effect of brackets on 2 + 3 × 4 + 5:

| Expression | Value |
|---|---|
| 2 + 3 × 4 + 5 | 19 |
| (2 + 3) × 4 + 5 | 25 |
| 2 + 3 × (4 + 5) | 29 |
| (2 + 3) × (4 + 5) | 45 |

## Fractions

| Operation | Method | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Add or subtract | common denominator, then combine numerators | 11/4 + 11/6 = 33/12 + 22/12 = 55/12 |
| Multiply | top × top, bottom × bottom; cancel first | 10/3 × 9/4 = 15/2 |
| Divide | multiply by the reciprocal | 5/6 ÷ 5/2 = 5/6 × 2/5 = 1/3 |
| Fraction of amount | multiply | 3/8 of 64 = 24 |
| Mixed numbers | convert to improper first | 3 1/3 = 10/3 |

Worked results:

| Calculation | Answer |
|---|---|
| 2 3/4 + 1 5/6 | 4 7/12 |
| 4 1/5 - 1 3/4 | 2 9/20 |
| 3 1/3 × 2 1/4 | 7 1/2 |
| 5/6 ÷ 2 1/2 | 1/3 |
| (5/6 - 1/4) ÷ 7/12 | 1 |
| 7/8 - 1/3 | 13/24 |
| 2/3 × 9/10 | 3/5 |

Remember: you can only add or subtract fractions with the same denominator. For multiplication and division, never use a common denominator.

## Decimals

| Operation | Method |
|---|---|
| Add or subtract | line up decimal points; add zeros |
| Multiply | multiply as integers; total the decimal places |
| Divide | multiply both numbers by a power of 10 to make the divisor whole |

```
0.36 x 0.25: 36 x 25 = 900; four decimal places → 0.0900 = 0.09
4.8 ÷ 0.06 = 480 ÷ 6 = 80
12.5 - 3.875 = 8.625
```

Estimate to check the decimal point: 0.36 × 0.25 is near 0.4 × 0.25 = 0.1.

## Practical situations

| Situation | Calculation |
|---|---|
| Temperature -3 °C rises 2.5 °C per hour for 4 h | -3 + 4 × 2.5 = 7 °C |
| Then falls 6 °C | 7 - 6 = 1 °C |
| From 6 °C, falls 4 °C per hour for 3 h | 6 - 12 = -6 °C |
| Lift from floor -2 to floor 7 | 7 - (-2) = 9 floors |
| Bank balance -$45 then pays in $120 | -45 + 120 = $75 |

Include the unit with the answer.

## Calculator (Paper 2)

- Use bracket keys round numerators and denominators.
- Use the fraction key for exact fraction work, if your calculator has one.
- Do not round part way through.
- Check by estimating.

## Exam traps

- Adding numerators and denominators: 1/4 + 1/6 is 5/12, not 2/10.
- Multiplying whole parts and fractional parts separately in mixed numbers.
- Flipping the first fraction in a division.
- Doing addition before multiplication.
- Treating 12 ÷ 3 × 2 as 12 ÷ 6.
- Losing a minus sign: -5 - 3 = -8, not -2.
- Leaving a fraction answer uncancelled.

7. **5.35**.
8. **0.028**.
9. 36 ÷ 9 = **4**.
