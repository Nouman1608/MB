Cambridge O Level Urdu is two separate, simultaneously current syllabuses, and the first job is to know which one you are sitting. 3247 First Language Urdu is for candidates with Urdu as their mother tongue: Paper 1 Reading and Writing (1 hour 30 minutes, 50 marks, 50%) and Paper 2 Texts (2 hours, 50 marks, 50%). 3248 Second Language Urdu is for learners with Urdu as an additional language: Paper 1 Reading and Writing (1 hour 45 minutes, 50 marks, 50%) and Paper 2 Grammar, Writing and Translation (1 hour 30 minutes, 50 marks, 50%). The notes below sit alongside the site's [Urdu First and Second Language overview](/resources/o-level-cambridge-urdu-first-and-second-language/) and the [Paper 1 revision notes](/resources/o-level-cambridge-urdu-first-and-second-language-revision-notes/).

## Both syllabuses: what is shared

In both 3247 and 3248 every answer is written in Urdu, each paper is a written exam, and the two papers are weighted equally at 50% each, so neither paper can be neglected. Grades run from A* to E only. 3248 does not permit dictionaries. 3247 is available in the June series. Because the papers are equally weighted and equal in marks, your revision time should be split roughly evenly between them, adjusted only for your own weaker paper.

## 3247 First Language: Paper 1 Reading and Writing

Paper 1 is 90 minutes for 50 marks, which is 90 / 50 = 1.8 minutes per mark. As a mother-tongue paper, it rewards control of register, accurate spelling and clear organisation rather than basic vocabulary. **Preparation priority**: practise reading a passage once for gist, once for detail, and writing under timed conditions so that the writing task is never squeezed. Reserve a few minutes at the end to proofread: dots on letters, hamza, and sentence-final punctuation are easy marks to lose through haste.

## 3247 First Language: Paper 2 Texts

Paper 2 is 120 minutes for 50 marks, or 120 / 50 = 2.4 minutes per mark, the most generous allowance of the four papers. The syllabus topic list names the content as an unseen passage, poetry and prose. **Preparation priority**: for the prescribed material, learn your texts well enough to quote briefly and accurately, and for the unseen passage practise annotating quickly. The extra time per mark is for planning and checking, not for writing at greater length than the marks justify. The [Paper 2 Texts page](/resources/o-level-cambridge-urdu-first-language-paper-2-texts/) and its [revision notes](/resources/o-level-urdu-first-language-paper-2-texts-revision-notes/) cover this paper in more detail.

## 3248 Second Language: Paper 1 Reading and Writing

Paper 1 is 105 minutes for 50 marks, which is 105 / 50 = 2.1 minutes per mark. Since Urdu is an additional language for these candidates, the generous time is there to let you read carefully and compose accurately in Urdu without a dictionary. **Preparation priority**: build a bank of connectives and sentence patterns you can reproduce correctly under pressure, and practise reading short passages aloud in your head so that you notice meaning, not only individual words.

## 3248 Second Language: Paper 2 Grammar, Writing and Translation

Paper 2 is 90 minutes for 50 marks: 90 / 50 = 1.8 minutes per mark. The title tells you the three strands: grammar, writing and translation. **Preparation priority**: treat grammar as something to apply, not recite. Practise short, accurate written pieces, and for translation work phrase by phrase, keeping the meaning of the whole sentence in view. The [Second Language Paper 2 page](/resources/o-level-cambridge-urdu-second-language-paper-2-grammar-writing-translation/) and [Paper 2 revision notes](/resources/o-level-urdu-second-language-paper-2-revision-notes/) go further.

## Question styles and command words

Without reproducing any particular paper, the following habits apply in any language paper. Read the instruction line for the verb: answers that "explain" need a reason, answers that "describe" need detail, and a question that asks you to write in a stated form (a letter, a report, an article) needs the conventions of that form. Check how many marks a question carries and size your answer to match. In a translation, keep the tense and number of the original and do not leave gaps.

## Worked practice scenario: planning a short piece of writing

Task (original): "Write a short piece of about 150 words explaining why your neighbourhood needs to keep its streets clean."

```
Plan (5 minutes):
Opening - state the problem in one sentence.
Cause   - why litter stays on the streets.
Effect  - health and appearance of the neighbourhood.
Remedy  - what each household can do.
Close   - one forward-looking sentence.
```

Model paragraph (middle section):

صفائی ایمان کا حصہ ہے، مگر ہمارے محلے کی گلیوں میں کوڑا کرکٹ جگہ جگہ پڑا رہتا ہے۔ اس کی بڑی وجہ یہ ہے کہ لوگ کوڑا مقررہ جگہ پر نہیں پھینکتے۔ گندگی سے بیماریاں پھیلتی ہیں اور بچوں کا کھیلنا بھی مشکل ہو جاتا ہے۔ اگر ہر گھر اپنی ذمہ داری سمجھے تو گلیاں صاف رہ سکتی ہیں۔

The paragraph moves from problem to cause to effect to remedy, one idea per sentence, and uses linking phrases (مگر, اس کی بڑی وجہ, اگر) that a marker can see. A five-minute plan like this protects you from drifting.

## Before/during exam checklist

- Confirm whether you are sitting 3247 or 3248 and which paper comes first.
- Revise equally for both papers; each is 50 marks and 50%.
- Practise writing by hand in Urdu at timed speed, as every answer is in Urdu.
- 3248 candidates: practise without a dictionary, as dictionaries are not permitted.
- Make a short plan before every extended piece of writing.
- Allow a few minutes at the end of each paper to proofread spelling and punctuation.
- Check that every question has an attempt; do not leave blanks.
- Bring the permitted equipment only, and check your timetable for the June series.

## Frequently asked questions

**What is the difference between Cambridge O Level Urdu 3247 and 3248?**
3247 is First Language Urdu, for candidates with Urdu as their mother tongue, and 3248 is Second Language Urdu, for learners with Urdu as an additional language. They are distinct syllabuses that are both current.

**How are the papers weighted?**
Each syllabus has two written papers of 50 marks, each worth 50% of the grade. 3247 Paper 1 is 1 hour 30 minutes and Paper 2 is 2 hours. 3248 Paper 1 is 1 hour 45 minutes and Paper 2 is 1 hour 30 minutes.

**In which language are the answers written?**
All answers are written in Urdu in both syllabuses.

**Can I use a dictionary?**
Not in 3248, where dictionaries are not permitted.

**What grades are available?**
Grades run from A* to E.
