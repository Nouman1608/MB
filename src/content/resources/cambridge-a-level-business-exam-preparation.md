Cambridge A-Level Business 9609 has four externally assessed papers: Paper 1 Business Concepts 1 (1 hour 15 minutes, 40 marks, 20% of the A Level), Paper 2 Business Concepts 2 (1 hour 30 minutes, 60 marks, 30%), Paper 3 Business Decision-Making (1 hour 45 minutes, 60 marks, 30%) and Paper 4 Business Strategy (1 hour 15 minutes, 40 marks, 20%). The weightings sum to 100% of the A Level. Revise using our pages on [business and its environment](/resources/a-level-business-business-and-its-environment/), [marketing](/resources/a-level-business-marketing/) and [business finance and sources of finance](/resources/cambridge-a-level-business-business-finance-and-sources-of-finance/).

## AS Level and A Level routes

Papers 1 and 2 form the AS Level and can be a standalone qualification, with Paper 1 worth 40% and Paper 2 worth 60% of the AS in that route. Papers 3 and 4 complete the full A Level and count only towards the A Level, which can be completed in a later series. The percentages used on this page are the percentages of the full A Level.

## Paper 1: Business Concepts 1

Paper 1 is 75 minutes and 40 marks, 20% of the A Level. **How to spend the time:** 75 / 40 = 1.875 minutes per mark. With a relatively small mark total in a fairly generous time, spend the first minutes reading every question and make sure that each answer is developed to match the marks offered, using business vocabulary accurately.

## Paper 2: Business Concepts 2

Paper 2 is 90 minutes and 60 marks, 30% of the A Level. **How to spend the time:** 90 / 60 = 1.5 minutes per mark. It is the heavier of the two AS papers, so give it time in revision as well as on the day. Keep a tight link between the question, the context given and the business concepts you apply.

## Paper 3: Business Decision-Making

Paper 3 is 105 minutes and 60 marks, 30% of the A Level, and counts only towards the full A Level. **How to spend the time:** 105 / 60 = 1.75 minutes per mark. As the title suggests, it rewards decisions backed by evidence, so practise using numerical data, such as investment appraisal and finance figures, to support a recommendation.

## Paper 4: Business Strategy

Paper 4 is 75 minutes and 40 marks, 20% of the A Level, and also counts only towards the full A Level. **How to spend the time:** 75 / 40 = 1.875 minutes per mark. Treat strategy as the integration of the whole course: link objectives, marketing, operations, people and finance rather than answering each in isolation.

## Command words and question styles

Business answers move up through levels. "Define" or "State" needs a precise statement. "Explain" needs a reason. "Analyse" needs a developed chain of consequences in the context. "Evaluate" and "Recommend" need a weighed judgement with a justified conclusion. Apply the context in every answer and show your calculations in full.

## Worked practice scenario: payback and average rate of return

**Question:** Meridian Print is considering a machine costing 120,000 dollars. Expected net cash flows are 30,000, 45,000, 50,000, 40,000 and 35,000 dollars in years 1 to 5. (a) Calculate the payback period. (b) Calculate the average rate of return. (c) Make a recommendation.

**Model answer:**

```
(a) Cumulative cash: Y1 30,000; Y2 75,000; Y3 125,000
    After 2 years 45,000 is still needed; Year 3 brings 50,000.
    Payback = 2 + 45,000 / 50,000 = 2.9 years (about 2 years 11 months)

(b) Total cash inflow = 30,000 + 45,000 + 50,000 + 40,000 + 35,000 = 200,000
    Total profit = 200,000 - 120,000 = 80,000
    Average annual profit = 80,000 / 5 = 16,000
    ARR = 16,000 / 120,000 x 100 = 13.33%
```

(c) Recommendation: payback within three years is encouraging and the return is positive, so the machine looks worthwhile if the cash-flow forecasts are reliable. However, forecasts are uncertain, and neither method accounts for the time value of money. The decision should also be compared with the cost of finance and any alternative project before committing.

## Planning your final weeks

Begin with the AS papers if you are on the staged route, since Papers 1 and 2 carry 50% of the A Level between them. Build a topic grid for the five content areas, from business and its environment to finance and accounting, and tick off definitions, calculations and application examples separately. For Paper 3, collect a small set of numerical techniques and practise writing a recommendation in three parts: the evidence, the risks and the decision. For Paper 4, practise connecting areas, for example showing how a marketing strategy depends on available finance and operational capacity. Finish with at least one timed attempt at each paper you will sit.

## Before/during exam checklist

- Confirm which route you are on and which papers you sit.
- Learn the formulae for investment appraisal, ratios and break-even.
- Practise one paper per week closer to the exam.
- Prepare a list of command words with example response lengths.
- Underline the business named in each question.
- Show all calculations with units.
- Close every longer answer with a justified judgement.
- Keep checking time against the minutes-per-mark rates.

## Frequently asked questions

**How many papers are there in Cambridge A-Level Business 9609?**
There are four externally assessed written papers: Business Concepts 1, Business Concepts 2, Business Decision-Making and Business Strategy.

**Which papers make up the AS Level?**
Papers 1 and 2, which can be a standalone qualification or the first half of the full A Level.

**What are the A Level weightings?**
20% for Paper 1, 30% for Paper 2, 30% for Paper 3 and 20% for Paper 4.

**How long is each paper?**
Paper 1 is 1 hour 15 minutes, Paper 2 is 1 hour 30 minutes, Paper 3 is 1 hour 45 minutes and Paper 4 is 1 hour 15 minutes.

**When was this syllabus first assessed?**
The first assessment is 2026, and the syllabus is for examination in 2026, 2027 and 2028.
