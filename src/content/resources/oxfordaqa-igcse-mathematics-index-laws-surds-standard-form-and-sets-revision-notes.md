## N6: Index laws

**Method for mixed terms**

1. Handle the numbers (coefficients) on their own.
2. Handle each letter separately: add indices to multiply, subtract to divide.
3. If the answer must have positive indices, move each negative-index factor into the denominator.

Reminder: 4a³ × 6a⁻⁷ = 24a⁻⁴ = 24/a⁴. The coefficients multiply (4 × 6) but the indices only add (3 + (−7) = −4).

**Must-know distinctions**

- 2⁻³ = 1/8 (a small positive number), not −8 and not −6.
- 7⁰ = 1, not 0.
- (5/3)⁻² means flip then square: (3/5)² = 9/25.
- The laws need the same base. 2⁴ × 3⁴ cannot be combined by adding indices.
- To solve equations such as 2^(x+1) = 1/16, write both sides as powers of the same base, then equate the indices: x + 1 = −4, so x = −5.

**Fractional powers (Extension only).** Take the root first, then the power. Reminder: 32^(2/5): 32^(1/5) = 2, and 2² = 4. A negative fractional power is a reciprocal as well: 4^(−3/2) = 1/(4^(3/2)) = 1/8.

## N7: Exact calculation

**Fractions: method in steps**

1. Convert each mixed number to an improper fraction.
2. Adding or subtracting needs a common denominator. Multiplying or dividing is easier if you cancel first.
3. To divide, invert the second fraction and multiply.
4. Give the final answer as a fraction (simplified), as a mixed number if the question suggests it, and never as a rounded decimal when "exact" is asked for.

Reminder: 1½ × 2⅔ = 3/2 × 8/3 = 4. Reminder: 2/3 ÷ 4/9 = 2/3 × 9/4 = 3/2.

**Surds (Extension only): method in steps**

1. Simplify each surd by taking out the largest square factor (√45 = √9 × √5 = 3√5).
2. Combine only like surds: 3√5 + 2√5 = 5√5.
3. When expanding, remember √a × √a = a.
4. To rationalise, multiply top and bottom by the surd (single surd) or by the conjugate (two-term denominator).

| Denominator | Multiply top and bottom by | Result |
|---|---|---|
| √6 | √6 | denominator 6 |
| 4 + √3 | 4 − √3 | denominator 13 |
| 5 − √2 | 5 + √2 | denominator 23 |

Reminder: 9/√6 = 9√6/6 = 3√6/2. Cancel after rationalising.

Reminder for a two-term denominator: 8/(4 + √3) = 8(4 − √3)/13 = (32 − 8√3)/13. There is nothing to cancel here, because 13 does not divide 32 and 8.

## N8: Standard form

**Converting**

- Move the decimal point until you have a number from 1 up to (not including) 10.
- Count the places moved. Large number: positive power. Number between 0 and 1: negative power.
- Reminder: 0.00805 = 8.05 × 10⁻³ and 3.7 × 10⁶ = 3 700 000.

**Calculating**

- Multiply or divide: do the A values, then the powers of 10, then tidy A back into the range 1 to 10.
- Add or subtract: make the powers of 10 equal first. The powers themselves do not get added.
- Reminder: (6 × 10⁴) × (5 × 10⁻⁷) = 30 × 10⁻³ = 3 × 10⁻².

**Calculator displays.** A display such as 2.4E-5 means 2.4 × 10⁻⁵. In your answer, write the × and the power of 10 in full.

**Worked reminder (add and subtract).** (5.2 × 10⁵) − (3 × 10⁴): write 3 × 10⁴ as 0.3 × 10⁵. Then 5.2 × 10⁵ − 0.3 × 10⁵ = 4.9 × 10⁵. For a small-number case, (2 × 10⁻³) + (5 × 10⁻⁴) = 2 × 10⁻³ + 0.5 × 10⁻³ = 2.5 × 10⁻³.

**Comparing sizes.** Compare the powers of 10 first. If the powers are equal, compare the A parts. For example, 8.1 × 10⁻³ is smaller than 2 × 10⁻², because −3 < −2.

## N9: Sets

| Notation | Reading | How to find it |
|---|---|---|
| ξ | universal set | the full list or the rectangle |
| A′ | not in A | everything in ξ outside circle A |
| A ∪ B | A or B (or both) | all regions in either circle |
| A ∩ B | A and B | the overlap only |
| n(A) | number of elements in A | count the elements, or add the region totals |

**Method for Venn diagram problems**

1. Draw ξ as a rectangle and a circle for each set. Overlap circles even if the overlap may be empty.
2. Fill the central region first (all sets), then the pairs-only regions by subtraction, then the single-set regions.
3. The outside region is the total minus everything inside the circles.
4. Check that all regions add up to n(ξ).

Reminder for two sets: if 30 pupils study Spanish or French, 18 study Spanish and 16 study French, then both = 18 + 16 − 30 = 4.

**Worked reminder (three sets).** In a group of 30, 12 take art, 10 take music, 7 take drama. 3 take art and music only, and 2 take all three. Nobody takes drama and music without art, and 4 take art and drama only. Then art only = 12 − 3 − 2 − 4 = 3, music only = 10 − 3 − 2 = 5, and drama only = 7 − 4 − 2 = 1. Inside the circles: 3 + 5 + 1 + 3 + 4 + 2 + 0 = 18, so 30 − 18 = 12 take none of them.

**Describing regions.** (A ∪ B)′ is the outside region only. A′ ∩ B is "in B but not in A", which is the part of circle B outside the overlap.

## Quick self-test

Try these without looking at the answers. Questions 4 to 7 are Extension only.

1. Simplify 4a³ × 6a⁻⁷, giving your answer with a positive index.
2. Find the value of 9⁰ + 2⁻¹.
3. Simplify x⁹ ÷ x⁻².
4. (Extension only) Evaluate 32^(2/5).
5. (Extension only) Simplify √45 + √20.
6. (Extension only) Rationalise 9/√6 and simplify.
7. (Extension only) Expand and simplify (3 − √2)(3 + √2).
8. Calculate 1½ × 2⅔.
9. Write 0.00805 in standard form.
10. Calculate (6 × 10⁴) × (5 × 10⁻⁷), giving your answer in standard form.
11. Calculate (8.4 × 10⁶) ÷ (2 × 10⁻²), giving your answer in standard form.
12. ξ = {1, 2, …, 10}, A = {1, 3, 5, 7, 9}, B = {2, 3, 5, 7}. (a) List A ∩ B. (b) Find n(A ∪ B). (c) List (A ∪ B)′.

### Answers

1. 24a⁻⁴ = **24/a⁴**
2. 1 + 1/2 = **3/2** (or 1.5)
3. 9 − (−2) = **x¹¹**
4. 32^(1/5) = 2, then 2² = **4**
5. 3√5 + 2√5 = **5√5**
6. 9√6/6 = **3√6/2**
7. 9 − 2 = **7**
8. 3/2 × 8/3 = **4**
9. **8.05 × 10⁻³**
10. 30 × 10⁻³ = **3 × 10⁻²**
11. 4.2 × 10⁶⁻⁽⁻²⁾ = **4.2 × 10⁸**
12. (a) **{3, 5, 7}**. (b) A ∪ B = {1, 2, 3, 5, 7, 9}, so **6**. (c) **{4, 6, 8, 10}**

## Where marks are usually lost

- Multiplying the powers instead of adding them in aᵐ × aⁿ, or adding coefficients that should be multiplied.
- Turning a negative index into a negative number.
- Using a fractional power without taking the root first, which makes the numbers large and error-prone.
- Giving a rounded decimal when the question asks for an exact value, or rounding part-way through a calculation.
- Not simplifying surds fully (leaving √72 instead of 6√2), or not rationalising when told to.
- Stopping at a result like 12.8 × 10³ and leaving it as an answer in standard form.
- Adding or subtracting standard-form numbers without matching the powers of 10.
- Copying a calculator display such as 2.4E-5 as the final answer instead of writing 2.4 × 10⁻⁵.
- In Venn diagrams, filling the single-set regions before the overlap, so the totals do not add up.
- Using A ∪ B when the question says "and" (that is A ∩ B), or the reverse.
