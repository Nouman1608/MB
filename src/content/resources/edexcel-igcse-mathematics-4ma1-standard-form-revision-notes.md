These notes condense **section 1.9, Standard form**, of the Pearson Edexcel International GCSE Mathematics A (4MA1) specification, Issue 2. Calculating with and interpreting numbers in standard form is for both tiers; solving problems involving standard form is **Higher tier only**. For explanations and worked examples, use the [section 1.9 study guide](/resources/edexcel-igcse-mathematics-4ma1-standard-form/).

## Condensed recall: the form

**a × 10ⁿ, with 1 ≤ a < 10 and n an integer.**

| Number | Standard form | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 3 080 000 | 3.08 × 10⁶ | point moves 6 places left |
| 0.000 507 | 5.07 × 10⁻⁴ | point moves 4 places right |
| 7.5 | 7.5 × 10⁰ | already between 1 and 10 |
| 46 × 10³ | 4.6 × 10⁴ | a too big: ÷ 10, power + 1 |
| 0.9 × 10⁻⁵ | 9 × 10⁻⁶ | a too small: × 10, power − 1 |

- Positive n: a number of 10 or more. Negative n: a number between 0 and 1.
- To write as an ordinary number, move the point n places (right if n > 0, left if n < 0).

## Condensed recall: comparing and calculating

- **Order:** compare powers first, then a. 2 × 10⁻³ > 9 × 10⁻⁴.
- **Multiply:** (a × 10ᵐ)(b × 10ⁿ) = ab × 10ᵐ⁺ⁿ, then adjust. (4 × 10³)(5 × 10⁻⁸) = 20 × 10⁻⁵ = 2 × 10⁻⁴.
- **Divide:** (a × 10ᵐ) ÷ (b × 10ⁿ) = (a ÷ b) × 10ᵐ⁻ⁿ, then adjust. (1.5 × 10⁶) ÷ (5 × 10²) = 0.3 × 10⁴ = 3 × 10³.
- **Add or subtract:** convert to ordinary numbers, or match the powers. 3.4 × 10⁴ − 8 × 10³ = 34 000 − 8000 = 2.6 × 10⁴.
- **Square root:** make the power even first. √(2.5 × 10⁹) = √(25 × 10⁸) = 5 × 10⁴.
- **Calculator:** use the ×10ˣ or EXP key; bracket each number; write the result as a × 10ⁿ, not "E".

## Condensed recall: problems (Higher tier only)

- Decide the operation from the words: "how many times bigger" → divide; "total" → multiply or add.
- Convert units before calculating: 1 kg = 10³ g, 1 km = 10³ m, 1 tonne = 10³ kg.
- Percentages: multiply by the multiplier. 4.2 × 10⁶ increased by 5% = 4.41 × 10⁶.
- Give the answer in the form the question asks (standard form, ordinary number or significant figures).

## Worked recall

**Ordering (both tiers).** Order 6.1 × 10³, 5.9 × 10⁴, 610 and 0.6 × 10⁴. Write all in standard form: 6.1 × 10³, 5.9 × 10⁴, 6.1 × 10², 6 × 10³. Smallest first: 610, 0.6 × 10⁴, 6.1 × 10³, 5.9 × 10⁴.

**Context (Higher).** A hard drive holds 2 × 10¹² bytes. A photo uses 4 × 10⁶ bytes. Number of photos = (2 × 10¹²) ÷ (4 × 10⁶) = 0.5 × 10⁶ = 5 × 10⁵.

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Standard form (standard index form) | A number written as a × 10ⁿ with 1 ≤ a < 10 and n an integer |
| Index (power) of 10 | The value n, telling you how many places the digits move |
| Ordinary number | The number written out in full, such as 0.000 507 |
| Integer | A whole number, positive, negative or zero |
| ×10ˣ / EXP key | The calculator key that enters a power of 10 |

## Exam traps

- **a must be less than 10:** 12 × 10⁵ is not standard form; it is 1.2 × 10⁶.
- **a must be at least 1:** 0.8 × 10³ is not standard form; it is 8 × 10².
- **Negative powers:** 6 × 10⁻² = 0.06, not −600.
- **Powers do not add when you add numbers.** Match the powers first.
- **Adjust at the end** of every multiplication and division.
- **Do not type "× 10"** before the ×10ˣ key.

## Self-test

1. Write 92 400 000 in standard form.
2. Write 0.000 006 1 in standard form.
3. Write 2.08 × 10⁻⁴ as an ordinary number.
4. Write 0.57 × 10⁸ in standard form.
5. Which is bigger, 7.2 × 10⁻⁵ or 1.1 × 10⁻⁴?
6. Work out (8 × 10⁵) × (3 × 10⁻²), giving your answer in standard form.
7. Work out (6.3 × 10⁻³) ÷ (9 × 10⁴), giving your answer in standard form.
8. Work out 5.1 × 10⁶ + 9.6 × 10⁵, giving your answer in standard form.
9. Work out √(8.1 × 10⁹).
10. (Higher) A virus is 1.2 × 10⁻⁷ m long. How many would fit end to end along 3 mm?
11. (Higher) A company's profit of 3.6 × 10⁸ dollars is shared equally among 4.5 × 10⁴ workers. How much does each receive?
12. (Higher) y = 3 × 10ᵐ. Write y² in standard form in terms of m.

**Answers**

1. 9.24 × 10⁷.
2. 6.1 × 10⁻⁶.
3. 0.000 208.
4. 5.7 × 10⁷.
5. 1.1 × 10⁻⁴ (power −4 is greater than −5).
6. 24 × 10³ = 2.4 × 10⁴.
7. 0.7 × 10⁻⁷ = 7 × 10⁻⁸.
8. 5 100 000 + 960 000 = 6 060 000 = 6.06 × 10⁶.
9. √(81 × 10⁸) = 9 × 10⁴.
10. 3 mm = 3 × 10⁻³ m; (3 × 10⁻³) ÷ (1.2 × 10⁻⁷) = 2.5 × 10⁴.
11. (3.6 × 10⁸) ÷ (4.5 × 10⁴) = 8000 dollars.
12. 9 × 10²ᵐ.
