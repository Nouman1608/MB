## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Equivalent fractions | Fractions with the same value, such as 3/4 and 18/24 |
| Simplest form | Numerator and denominator have no common factor except 1 |
| Terminating decimal | A decimal that ends, such as 0.375 |
| Recurring decimal | A decimal with a digit or block of digits that repeats for ever |
| Operator | A fraction, decimal or percentage used to multiply a quantity ("of") |
| Multiplier | The single decimal that does a percentage change in one step |
| Improper fraction | A fraction greater than 1, such as 9/8 |

## Converting: the methods

- **Fraction → decimal:** divide numerator by denominator. Non-calculator shortcut: scale the denominator to 10, 100 or 1,000. 13/16 = 0.8125 by short division.
- **Decimal → fraction:** write over 10, 100 or 1,000, then simplify. 0.24 = 24/100 = 6/25.
- **Decimal → percentage:** × 100. 2.75 = 275%.
- **Percentage → fraction:** write over 100 and simplify. 15% = 15/100 = 3/20.
- **Simplify** by dividing top and bottom by the highest common factor: 18/48 = 3/8 (HCF 6).

## Ordering mixed forms

1. Convert every value to a decimal.
2. Write all to the same number of decimal places.
3. Order by place value.
4. Write the answer using the original forms.

## Recurring decimals (Extension only)

**Which fractions recur?** In simplest form, a denominator with any prime factor other than 2 and 5.

**Recurring → fraction, method:**

1. Let x = the decimal.
2. Multiply by 10 if one digit repeats, 100 if two repeat, 1,000 if three repeat.
3. If the repeat does not start straight after the decimal point, use two multiples whose recurring tails line up.
4. Subtract to remove the recurring part, then solve and simplify.

**Worked reminders**

```
0.777...    x = 0.777...,  10x = 7.777...   9x = 7      x = 7/9
0.212121... 100x = 21.2121...               99x = 21    x = 21/99 = 7/33
0.8333...   10x = 8.333..., 100x = 83.333... 90x = 75   x = 75/90 = 5/6
```

**Patterns to recognise:** one repeating digit d gives d/9; two repeating digits ab give ab/99.

## Operators (N13)

- "Of" means multiply: 3/20 of 900 = 900 × 3/20 = 135, and 15% of 900 = 0.15 × 900 = 135.
- Operators greater than 1 increase: 9/8 of 64 = 72.
- One after another: multiply the operators. 80% of 45% of 500 = 0.8 × 0.45 × 500 = 180.
- "Of the remainder": find what is left first. If 1/4 is spent and then 1/3 of the rest, the fraction left is 3/4 × 2/3 = 1/2.

## One quantity as a fraction or percentage of another (N14)

1. Same units first.
2. Fraction = part ÷ whole, where the whole is the quantity after "of" or "compared with".
3. Simplify, or × 100 for a percentage.

Worked reminder: 36 minutes as a percentage of 2.5 hours. 2.5 hours = 150 minutes. 36/150 = 6/25 = 24%.

Above 1 or 100%: 35 kg compared with 28 kg is 35/28 = 5/4 = 125%.

## Link to N15

Percentage change = change ÷ original × 100. Increase by r%: multiply by (1 + r/100). Decrease by r%: multiply by (1 − r/100). Reverse percentages (Extension only): divide by the multiplier. Full treatment is in the [percentage change and interest revision notes](/resources/oxfordaqa-igcse-mathematics-percentage-change-interest-and-exponential-growth-and-revision-notes/).

## Exam traps

- 0.7 > 0.65 even though 65 > 7: compare place value.
- 40% then 15% is not 55%: multiply, don't add.
- "Of the remainder" changes the whole.
- Percentage of "A compared with B" puts B underneath.
- Recurring decimals with two repeating digits need 100x, not 10x.
- "Simplest form" means fully simplified: 21/99 must become 7/33.
- Converting 0.4% to 0.4 or 0.04 instead of 0.004.

## Quick self-test

1. Write 7/20 as a decimal and a percentage.
2. Write 0.36 as a fraction in its simplest form.
3. Write 2.4% as a decimal.
4. Order, smallest first: 0.7, 2/3, 68%, 0.69.
5. Find 3/8 of 96.
6. Find 30% of 5% of 4,000.
7. A bag of 120 counters is 1/4 red. 2/5 of the rest are blue. How many are blue?
8. Write 45 cm as a fraction of 1.8 m.
9. Write 54 as a percentage of 40.
10. (Extension only) Write 0.555... as a fraction.
11. (Extension only) Write 0.272727... as a fraction in its simplest form.
12. (Extension only) Explain why 5/12 gives a recurring decimal.

### Answers

1. 0.35 and 35%.
2. 36/100 = 9/25.
3. 0.024.
4. 2/3 (0.667), 68% (0.68), 0.69, 0.7.
5. 96 ÷ 8 × 3 = 36.
6. 0.3 × 0.05 × 4,000 = 60.
7. Red = 30, rest = 90, blue = 2/5 × 90 = 36.
8. 180 cm, so 45/180 = 1/4.
9. 54/40 × 100 = 135%.
10. 9x = 5, so 5/9.
11. 99x = 27, so 27/99 = 3/11.
12. 12 = 2 × 2 × 3, which has the prime factor 3 (other than 2 and 5), and 5/12 is already in its simplest form.

## Frequently asked questions

**Are recurring decimals on the Core tier of 9260?**
No. Converting between fractions and recurring decimals is in the Extension column of N12. Terminating decimals are Core.

**How do I know which number goes on the bottom of the fraction?**
The quantity after "of" or "compared with" is the whole, so it goes on the bottom.
