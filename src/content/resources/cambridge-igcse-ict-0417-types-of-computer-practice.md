**6.** A gaming enthusiast wants a computer that can be upgraded with a new graphics card every two years.

**8.** A farmer uses a smartphone app to control a drone that photographs crops.

**(a)** Describe how the smartphone controls the drone. **[2]**

**(a)** Explain why a laptop is more suitable than a desktop for this journalist. **[2]**

**(b)** Give **one** reason why the company still chooses smartphones for its sales staff. **[1]**

## Answers

**1.** How easily a computer can be carried and used in different places. **[1]**

**2.** B: desktop. **[1]**

**3.** Any two: a smartphone with a large screen (1); size between a smartphone and a small tablet (1); can make calls over the mobile network (1); touch screen input (1). **[2]**

**4.** Any two: screen or monitor (1); keyboard (1); pointing device or touchpad (1); camera or webcam (1); speakers or microphone (1). **[2]**

**5.** Any two: office and business management (1); education (1); gaming (1); entertainment (1); remotely controlled devices (1). **[2]**

**6. (a)** Desktop computer. **[1]**

**(b)** A desktop's case has room for components to be removed and replaced, so a new graphics card can be fitted (1), whereas a laptop's components are packed tightly or fixed in place, so the graphics hardware usually cannot be upgraded (1). **[2]**

**7. (a)** Any two: touch screen is easy for young children to learn (1); light and easy to carry around the classroom (1); long battery life lasts the school day (1); educational apps are designed for touch use (1); quick to start up (1). **[2]**

**(b)** Any one: on-screen keyboard is harder for typing longer work (1); less processing power or storage (1); screens can crack if dropped (1); fewer ports to connect devices such as printers (1). **[1]**

**8. (a)** An app on the smartphone sends commands to the drone wirelessly (1), for example to change its direction, height or to take a photograph, and the drone's images can be shown on the phone's screen (1). **[2]**

**(b)** Any one: lighter and easier to hold while standing in a field (1); has its own mobile network connection (1); touch controls in the app are quick to use (1). **[1]**

**9. (a)** The laptop is portable and battery powered (1), so the same computer can be used at home and at events in other cities, with the articles stored on it (1). **[2]**

**(b)** Any two: smaller screen and keyboard than a desktop (1); harder to upgrade (1); more expensive for the same performance (1); more easily lost, stolen or damaged while travelling (1); battery needs recharging (1). **[2]**

**10.** Any three, each a contrast: a tablet is portable whereas a desktop stays in one place (1); a tablet uses a touch screen whereas a desktop uses a keyboard and mouse (1); a tablet runs on a battery whereas a desktop uses mains power (1); a tablet is hard to upgrade whereas a desktop is expandable (1); a tablet has a smaller screen whereas a desktop usually has a large monitor (1); a tablet has less processing power than a typical desktop (1). **[3]**

**11. (a)** Smartphones are small and carried outside the office, so they are more easily lost or stolen (1); anyone who finds the phone could access the customer details, whereas desktops stay inside the company's building (1). **[2]**

**(b)** Any one: staff can look up and update customer details while visiting customers (1); staff can contact customers and the office from anywhere (1). **[1]**

**12.** Tablets, advantages, up to 2: records updated at the patient's bedside, so less risk of forgetting details (1); portable and light to carry between wards (1); touch screen is quick to use (1). Tablets, disadvantages, up to 2: may be lost, stolen or dropped (1); batteries must be charged (1); on-screen keyboard is slow for long notes (1). Desktops, advantages, up to 2: full keyboard and large screen for detailed records (1); less likely to be lost or stolen, so data is more secure (1); no battery to charge (1). Desktops, disadvantages, up to 2: nurses must walk back to the station (1); details may be forgotten before they are entered (1). Recommendation with a reason linked to the scenario (1). **[6]** Award a maximum of 6 marks from these points; answers that cover only one option are limited to 3 marks.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is a laptop always worse than a desktop?**
No. The comparison depends on the user. A laptop wins on portability; a desktop wins on expandability, ergonomics and performance for the price.

**Do I need to know screen sizes in inches?**
No. Describe screen sizes in relative terms: smaller than a tablet, larger than a smartphone.
