## Questions

**1.** Photosynthesis takes place in the leaves of green plants.

**(a)** State the word equation for photosynthesis. **[1]**
**(b)** Write the balanced chemical symbol equation for photosynthesis. **[2]**

**2.** A farmer's crop has yellow leaves and is growing slowly. Soil tests show it is short of two mineral ions. State why the plant needs:

**(a)** magnesium ions **[1]**
**(b)** nitrate ions **[1]**

**3.** Copy and complete the table.

| Component | One good source | One function in the body |
|---|---|---|
| Vitamin C | ............ | ............ |
| Iron | ............ | ............ |

**[4]**

**4.** Explain how **two** features of the mesophyll layers of a leaf adapt it for photosynthesis. **[4]**

**5.** A student investigates the effect of light intensity on photosynthesis in pondweed. The pondweed is in water containing sodium hydrogencarbonate. She counts the bubbles released per minute with a lamp at different distances.

| Distance of lamp (cm) | Trial 1 | Trial 2 | Trial 3 | Mean |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10 | 48 | 52 | 50 | 50 |
| 20 | 31 | 29 | 33 | 31 |
| 30 | 18 | 21 | 15 | ...... |
| 40 | 10 | 12 | 8 | 10 |

**(a)** Calculate the mean number of bubbles per minute at 30 cm. **[1]**
**(b)** Calculate the percentage decrease in the mean rate when the lamp is moved from 10 cm to 40 cm. **[2]**
**(c)** Explain the effect of moving the lamp further away on the rate of photosynthesis. **[2]**
**(d)** Give **two** variables the student should keep the same. **[2]**
**(e)** Describe how the student could show the gas is oxygen. **[1]**

**6.** A plant with variegated leaves (green in the middle, white around the edge) is kept in the dark for two days. A strip of foil is then fixed across one leaf, and the plant is left in bright light for six hours. The leaf is then tested for starch.

**(a)** Explain why the plant was kept in the dark for two days. **[2]**
**(b)** During the test, the leaf is placed in hot ethanol. Explain why ethanol is used, and why it is heated in a water bath. **[2]**
**(c)** Predict which part of the leaf turns blue-black with iodine. Explain your answer. **[2]**

**7.** This question is about the human alimentary canal.

**(a)** Name the part where:

**(i)** most water is absorbed from undigested food **[1]**
**(ii)** bile is stored **[1]**
**(iii)** enzymes from the pancreas are released into the gut **[1]**
**(iv)** faeces are stored before egestion **[1]**

**(b)** Explain how food is moved along the oesophagus. **[2]**

**8.** Complete the table about digestive enzymes.

| Enzyme | Substrate | Product(s) |
|---|---|---|
| Amylase | Starch | ............ |
| ............ | Maltose | Glucose |
| Protease | Protein | ............ |
| Lipase | Lipid | ............ |

**[4]**

**9.** A person has their gall bladder removed. Bile still reaches the duodenum, but in a slow trickle rather than in larger amounts after a meal.

**(a)** State where bile is produced. **[1]**
**(b)** Explain why this person may digest fatty meals more slowly. **[2]**
**(c)** Bile also affects the pH in the duodenum. Explain why this matters for digestion. **[1]**

**10.** Explain how the structure of a villus adapts the small intestine for the absorption of digested food. **[4]**

**11.** (Paper 2 only) A student burns a 0.40 g piece of cashew nut under a boiling tube containing 20 cm³ of water. The water temperature rises from 22.5 °C to 49.5 °C. It takes 4.2 J to raise the temperature of 1 g of water by 1 °C.

**(a)** Calculate the temperature rise. **[1]**
**(b)** Calculate the energy released by the cashew nut, in J. **[2]**
**(c)** Calculate the energy released per gram of cashew nut, in kJ/g. **[1]**
**(d)** The packet states that cashew nuts contain 23 kJ/g. Give **two** reasons why the student's value is lower. **[2]**
**(e)** Suggest **one** change to the method that would make the value more accurate. **[1]**

**12.** The table shows the daily energy requirement of four people.

| Person | Description | Energy requirement (MJ per day) |
|---|---|---|
| A | Woman, 30 years, office worker | 9.5 |
| B | Woman, 30 years, building worker | 13.0 |
| C | Woman, 75 years, retired | 8.0 |
| D | Woman, 30 years, office worker, pregnant | 10.0 |

**(a)** Calculate the percentage by which B's energy requirement is greater than A's. Give your answer to 3 significant figures. **[2]**
**(b)** Explain why B needs more energy than A. **[2]**
**(c)** Explain why D needs more energy than A. **[1]**
**(d)** Person D is advised to eat more foods rich in iron and in calcium. Explain why each is needed. **[2]**

## Answers

**1. (a)** carbon dioxide + water → glucose + oxygen [1]
**(b)** Correct formulae: CO₂, H₂O, C₆H₁₂O₆ and O₂ [1]. Balanced: **6CO₂ + 6H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂** [1].
*Examiner insight:* The balancing mark depends on correct formulae; light and chlorophyll may be written over the arrow but are not credited as reactants.

**2. (a)** To make **chlorophyll** [1].
**(b)** To make **amino acids** (for proteins) [1].
*Examiner insight:* "For healthy leaves" or "for growth" alone is too vague for either mark; name the molecule made.

**3.** Vitamin C: source, e.g. oranges, lemons or other citrus fruit [1]; function, e.g. healthy skin and gums / prevents scurvy [1].
Iron: source, e.g. red meat, liver or spinach [1]; function: making haemoglobin (in red blood cells) [1].
*Examiner insight:* "For blood" does not score the iron function mark; haemoglobin or carrying oxygen in red blood cells is needed.

**4.** Palisade cells contain many chloroplasts [1], so they absorb a lot of light for photosynthesis [1].
Spongy mesophyll has air spaces [1], so carbon dioxide can diffuse to the photosynthesising cells [1].
Also accept, as either feature: the palisade layer is near the upper surface, where light intensity is highest.
*Examiner insight:* Each explanation mark is linked to its feature, so two features with no explanation score 2 out of 4, not 4.

**5. (a)** (18 + 21 + 15) ÷ 3 = **18** bubbles per minute [1].
**(b)** (50 − 10) ÷ 50 × 100 [1] = **80%** [1].
**(c)** The rate decreases as the lamp moves further away [1] because light intensity falls, and light is the limiting factor [1].
**(d)** Any two: temperature; amount of sodium hydrogencarbonate (carbon dioxide); type or length of pondweed; time allowed to adjust [1][1].
**(e)** Collect the gas; it relights a glowing splint [1].
*Examiner insight:* In (b), an answer of 400% (dividing by the 40 cm value) scores 0; one method mark can be given for the correct subtraction set over 50.

**6. (a)** To use up / remove the starch already in the leaf [1], so any starch found must have been made during the six hours in light [1].
**(b)** Ethanol removes the chlorophyll, so the colour change can be seen [1]. Ethanol is flammable, so it must not be heated with a naked flame [1].
**(c)** Only the green part that was not covered by foil [1]. Photosynthesis needs both chlorophyll and light: the white edge has no chlorophyll, and the covered part had no light [1].
*Examiner insight:* In (c) the explanation mark needs both requirements; naming only light or only chlorophyll does not explain the whole result.

**7. (a) (i)** Colon / large intestine [1]
**(ii)** Gall bladder [1]
**(iii)** Duodenum [1]
**(iv)** Rectum [1]
**(b)** Peristalsis: waves of contraction and relaxation of the muscles in the oesophagus wall [1]. Circular muscle contracts behind the food, squeezing it forward [1].
*Examiner insight:* "Liver" for (a)(ii) is a common wrong answer: the question asks where bile is stored, not where it is made.

**8.** Amylase product: **maltose** [1]. Enzyme for maltose: **maltase** [1]. Protease product: **amino acids** [1]. Lipase products: **fatty acids and glycerol** [1].
*Examiner insight:* Glucose is not accepted as the product of amylase; both fatty acids and glycerol are needed for the lipase mark.

**9. (a)** The liver [1].
**(b)** Less bile is available to emulsify fat in a meal, so fat stays in larger drops [1]. Smaller surface area for lipase to act on, so digestion is slower [1].
**(c)** Bile is alkaline and neutralises stomach acid, giving the pH at which enzymes in the small intestine work best [1].
*Examiner insight:* Saying bile "breaks down" or "digests" fat gets no credit; the mark is for emulsifying and the surface area effect on lipase.

**10.** Villi (and microvilli) give a large surface area [1].
The wall is one cell thick, giving a short diffusion distance [1].
A dense capillary network carries absorbed food away [1], keeping a steep concentration gradient [1].
Also accept, as an alternative to any one point: a lacteal absorbs fatty acids and glycerol.
*Examiner insight:* An "explain" question needs the effect as well as the feature: "thin wall" alone is usually not enough; add "short diffusion distance".

**11. (a)** 49.5 − 22.5 = **27.0 °C** [1].
**(b)** 20 × 4.2 × 27.0 [1] = **2268 J** [1].
**(c)** 2268 ÷ 0.40 = 5670 J/g = **5.67 kJ/g** [1].
**(d)** Any two: heat lost to the air; heat used to warm the boiling tube; the nut did not burn completely; some energy lost as light [1][1].
**(e)** Any one: insulate or use a draught shield around the tube; keep the flame close to the tube; relight the food until it burns completely; use a calorimeter [1].
*Examiner insight:* Allow error carried forward from (a) into (b) and (c); a correct number with the wrong unit (5670 kJ/g) loses the mark in (c).

**12. (a)** (13.0 − 9.5) ÷ 9.5 × 100 [1] = **36.8%** [1].
**(b)** B is more physically active [1], so her muscles respire more and need more energy [1].
**(c)** Extra energy is needed for the growth of the fetus (and placenta) / to carry the extra mass [1].
**(d)** Iron is needed to make haemoglobin, for her own and the fetus's red blood cells [1]. Calcium is needed for the fetus's developing bones and teeth [1].
*Examiner insight:* In (b), "B does more work" without linking it to respiration or energy use in muscles usually gains only the first mark.

## Where marks are usually lost

- Putting light or chlorophyll in the photosynthesis equation as a reactant.
- Unbalanced symbol equations, or writing O instead of O₂.
- Calling the x-axis factor limiting where the graph has levelled off.
- Mixing up magnesium (chlorophyll) and nitrate (amino acids).
- Forgetting to say why destarching matters: starch already present would give a false result.
- Naming the liver as the store of bile, or saying bile digests fat.
- Giving glucose as the product of amylase.
- Listing villus features without explaining how each speeds up absorption.
- Dividing by the wrong value in percentage-change questions.
- In the food energy practical, forgetting to divide by the mass of food, or giving J when kJ was asked for.
