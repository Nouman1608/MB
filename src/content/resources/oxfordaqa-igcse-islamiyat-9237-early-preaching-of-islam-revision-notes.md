**These notes condense content area 3.2.1.4 of 9237: "The private and public preaching and Hazrat Muhammad's (pbuh) invitation to Islam, his call to Tawhid and rejection of idol worshiping" and "The early teachings of Islam, including the rights for women, the outlawing of alcohol and gambling."** The full account is in the [early preaching study guide](/resources/oxfordaqa-igcse-islamiyat-9237-early-preaching-of-islam/).

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Tawhid | The oneness of Allah |
| Shirk | Associating partners with Allah |
| Dar al-Arqam | House of al-Arqam, near Safa, used for secret meetings |
| Safa | The hill from which the Prophet (pbuh) first called the Quraysh in public |
| Mahr | The marriage gift paid by the groom to the bride |
| Khamr | Intoxicants; alcohol |
| Maysir | Gambling |
| Fatihah | The opening surah (1:1-7) |

## Two phases

| | Private | Public |
|---|---|---|
| Length | About three years | From then on |
| Method | Personal invitation to trusted people; secret meetings | Meal for family; call from Mount Safa; preaching at the Ka'aba |
| Reason | Community small; risk of attack; time to teach | Allah's command (26:214; 15:94) |
| Response | Close family and friends believed | Insults from Abu Lahab; growing opposition |

## The first believers

- **Khadija (RA):** first.
- **Ali (RA):** first boy.
- **Zayd ibn Harithah (RA):** freed servant.
- **Abu Bakr (RA):** friend; brought others, such as Uthman, Zubair, Abdur Rahman ibn Awf, Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas and Talha.
- Many early believers were young, poor or slaves, such as **Bilal** and **Ammar** and his parents.

## The public call

1. Command to warn the closest relatives (26:214).
2. Meal for Banu Hashim: call to Islam.
3. Mount Safa: the Quraysh agree he has never lied; he warns them.
4. Abu Lahab insults him; Surah 111 follows.
5. Open preaching at the Ka'aba and to pilgrims.

## Tawhid and the rejection of idols

- Allah alone creates, sustains and is worshipped.
- Idols are powerless and are "only names" (53:23).
- No go-between is needed between a person and Allah.
- Every person answers to Allah on the Day of Judgement.
- **Effect:** it threatened ancestral tradition and the income and status of the Quraysh.

## Using the prescribed passage, Qur'an 1:1-7

| Words | Use for |
|---|---|
| "the Entirely Merciful, the Especially Merciful" | Allah's mercy; compassion in the invitation |
| "Lord of the worlds" | Tawhid; Allah is Lord of all |
| "Sovereign of the Day of Recompense" | Accountability; the Hereafter |
| "It is You we worship and You we ask for help" | Worship of Allah alone; no idols |
| "Guide us to the straight path" | Need for guidance; the role of revelation |
| "those who have earned [Your] anger ... those who are astray" | Warning against rejecting the truth |

**Note:** the surah is recited in every prayer, so the core message is repeated daily.

## Early teachings on women

| Right | Source |
|---|---|
| Ban on infanticide | 17:31; 81:8-9 |
| Equal reward for deeds | 3:195; 33:35 |
| Share in inheritance | 4:7; 4:11 |
| Own property; receive the mahr | 4:4 |
| Consent in marriage | Teaching of the Prophet (pbuh) |
| Respect for mothers | "Your mother" named three times (hadith) |

## Alcohol and gambling

| Stage | Teaching |
|---|---|
| Stage 1 | Great sin and some benefit, but the sin is greater (2:219) |
| Stage 2 | Do not pray while intoxicated (4:43) |
| Stage 3 | Complete ban: defilement from the work of Satan (5:90-91) |

**Reasons for the ban:** hatred and enmity between people; distraction from remembrance of Allah and prayer; waste of money; harm to family.

## Why the early preaching matters

- It established **Tawhid** as the foundation of Islam.
- It built a faithful core community before open opposition.
- It addressed social wrongs: the position of women, drink and gambling.
- It shows a method: patience, wisdom and starting with those who know your character.

## Lessons from the early preaching

- **Start with those who know you:** the first believers trusted his character.
- **Be patient and wise:** the private phase prepared the community.
- **Call kindly:** "invite to the way of your Lord with wisdom and good instruction" (16:125).
- **Reform society:** Islam also corrected social wrongs, not only beliefs.

## How to build a 6-mark answer

1. State the first reason or teaching and explain it (AO1).
2. State the second and explain it (AO1).
3. Add one quotation, such as 17:31, 5:90 or 1:2, and apply it (AO2).

**Mini example (women's rights):** "Islam gave women a share of inheritance. Before this they were often treated as property, so the right gave them security and respect (4:7)."

## Exam traps

- Private preaching lasted about **three years**; do not say it was one year or ten.
- The call from **Mount Safa** is the public start.
- Abu Lahab's insult is linked with **Surah 111**.
- Alcohol was banned in **stages**, not at once.
- Name a **source** for each right of women.
- Say what Tawhid **meant** (no idols, no go-betweens, accountability), not just "one God".

## Self-test

1. How long did the private phase last?
2. Name two of the first believers.
3. Where did the early believers meet in secret?
4. From which hill did the Prophet (pbuh) first call the Quraysh?
5. Which uncle insulted him?
6. What is Tawhid?
7. Give two rights of women given by Islam.
8. In how many stages was alcohol banned?
9. Give one reason why alcohol and gambling were banned.
10. Which surah is the prescribed passage for early preaching?

**Answers:** 1. About three years. 2. Any two: Khadija, Ali, Zayd, Abu Bakr. 3. The house of al-Arqam. 4. Mount Safa. 5. Abu Lahab. 6. The oneness of Allah. 7. Any two: ban on infanticide; share of inheritance; owning property; receiving the mahr; consent in marriage; equal reward. 8. Three stages. 9. Any one: causes hatred and enmity; distracts from prayer and remembrance of Allah; wastes wealth; harms families. 10. Surah al-Fatihah (1:1-7).
