These notes cover **Exercise 2, Multiple matching**, of 3248 Paper 1: "candidates read a series of short paragraphs followed by a series of sentences, then match each sentence to one of the paragraphs". For the whole paper see the [O Level Urdu Paper 1 study guide](/resources/o-level-cambridge-urdu-first-and-second-language/); for a full worked example see the [Exercise 2 Multiple matching study guide](/resources/cambridge-o-level-urdu-language-3248-exercise-2-multiple-matching/).

## Task facts

| Fact | Detail |
|---|---|
| Marks | 9 |
| Objectives | R1, R2, R3 |
| Texts | a series of short paragraphs, then a series of sentences |
| Task | match each sentence to one of the paragraphs |
| Language | questions in Urdu; dictionaries not allowed |
| Paper | 1 hour 45 minutes, 50 marks, five compulsory exercises |
| Not printed in the syllabus | the number of paragraphs and sentences, and whether a paragraph can be used twice: read the rubric |

## What each objective means here

- **R1:** find the detail that the sentence points to.
- **R2:** link an opinion, feeling or attitude to the right speaker.
- **R3:** see the connection between an idea in a sentence and an idea in a paragraph, even when the words differ.

## Six-step method

1. Read **all paragraphs** first and note the topic, problem and feeling of each.
2. Read **one sentence** and decide its idea.
3. Find the paragraph with that idea, using **meaning**, not repeated words.
4. Test: is **every part** of the sentence true of that paragraph?
5. Use **elimination** if the rubric says a letter is used only once.
6. Come back to doubtful sentences last.

## Paraphrase patterns

| Paragraph says | Sentence says | Pattern |
|---|---|---|
| اپنے چچا سے سیکھا | کسی رشتہ دار سے ہنر ملا | specific becomes general |
| ڈاک کا خرچ ایک مسئلہ ہے | رقم کی تنگی کا سامنا ہے | cause becomes category |
| تین سو کتابیں بھیج چکی ہوں | سیکڑوں چیزیں پہنچائی ہیں | number becomes approximation |
| سردیوں میں جلدی اٹھنا مشکل ہے | موسم کے باعث صبح کی دشواری | detail becomes general reason |
| پہلے گھبراتے ہیں، پھر اعتماد بڑھتا ہے | وقت کے ساتھ تبدیلی آتی ہے | story becomes summary |

## Clue words

| Word or phrase | Think |
|---|---|
| *کوئی ... نہیں* | a refusal or absence |
| *صرف* | a limit |
| *اب تک* | total so far |
| *امید* | hope about the future |
| *خدشہ / ڈر* | fear |
| *پہلے ... اب* | change over time |
| *مگر / لیکن / البتہ* | contrast: the second part is often the point |
| *اس لیے / کیونکہ* | reason and result |

## Types of idea sentences test

| Type | Example |
|---|---|
| A fact about the person | learned a skill from a relative |
| A problem | cost, time, weather, tiredness |
| A feeling | pride, worry, joy |
| A hope or plan | wishes for a better building |
| A result | gains confidence, makes friends |

## Typical traps

| Trap | What to do |
|---|---|
| The same word appears in two paragraphs | Check the full idea, not the word |
| Two paragraphs share a feeling | Look for the second detail in the sentence |
| A number matches the wrong paragraph | Check what the number counts |
| A sentence has two ideas | Both must match |
| A letter used already | Check the rubric before reusing |

## Mini example to rehearse

Paragraph: *میں اپنے محلے کے بچوں کو شام کو مفت ٹیوشن پڑھاتی ہوں۔ شروع میں کوئی بچہ نہیں آتا تھا، مگر اب بیس بچے آتے ہیں۔*

| Sentence | Match? | Why |
|---|---|---|
| اس شخص کے کام کو وقت کے ساتھ لوگوں نے قبول کیا ہے | yes | *پہلے کوئی نہیں، اب بیس*: a change over time |
| اس شخص کو اپنے کام کے بدلے پیسے ملتے ہیں | no | *مفت* contradicts it |
| اس شخص نے اپنا کام اپنے گھر کے قریب شروع کیا | yes | *اپنے محلے* |
| اس شخص کے پاس بہت زیادہ بچے ہیں | no | twenty is not stated as too many |

Practise this on any short paragraph: write four sentences, two that match and two that nearly match, and say why.

## Time plan

| Stage | Share of the time |
|---|---|
| Read the paragraphs and note key ideas | about half |
| Match the easy sentences | a quarter |
| Eliminate and check the rest | a quarter |

If one sentence will not match, move on and return to it. The remaining letters often make the answer clear.

## Exam traps

- Matching by one repeated word.
- Ignoring half of a two-part sentence.
- Reusing a letter when it is not allowed.
- Leaving a sentence blank. Make an informed guess.
- Spending too long. It is 9 of 50 marks.

## Self-test

1. How many marks is Exercise 2 worth?
2. Which three reading objectives does it test?
3. Why is matching by repeated words risky?
4. A paragraph says *تین سو کتابیں*. Which sentence phrase might match it: *چند چیزیں* or *سیکڑوں چیزیں*?
5. What does *امید* usually signal?
6. What should you read first, the paragraphs or the sentences?
7. A sentence says the speaker has a fear about money. The paragraph says *ڈاک کا خرچ ایک مسئلہ ہے*. Is this a match? Why?
8. What should you check before using a letter twice?
9. A sentence has two parts and only one fits the paragraph. What do you do?
10. Give the English for *مطابقت*.

**Answers**

1. Nine.
2. R1, R2 and R3.
3. The sentences paraphrase the paragraphs, and the same word can appear in more than one paragraph.
4. *سیکڑوں چیزیں*.
5. A hope about the future, not a fact.
6. The paragraphs.
7. Yes. Postage cost is a money problem, so the idea matches even though the words differ.
8. The rubric, which says whether a paragraph can be used more than once.
9. It is not a match; look for a paragraph where both parts are true.
10. Matching.
