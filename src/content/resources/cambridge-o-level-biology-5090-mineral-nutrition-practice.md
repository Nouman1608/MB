## Section A

**1.** Which ion is needed by a plant to make chlorophyll?

A: calcium

B: magnesium

C: nitrate

D: potassium

**[1]**

**2.** Which substance made by a plant contains nitrogen taken from the soil?

A: cellulose

B: glucose

C: protein

D: starch

**[1]**

**3.** State why nitrate ions are needed by plants. **[2]**

**4.** State the effect of magnesium deficiency on the leaves of a plant. **[1]**

**5.** Explain why a plant that lacks magnesium ions grows more slowly than a healthy plant. **[3]**

## Section B

**6.** A student grew identical bean seedlings for four weeks in three different culture solutions. All other conditions were the same. The table shows the results.

| Solution | Mean height (cm) | Colour of leaves |
|---|---|---|
| Complete (all ions) | 30.0 | dark green |
| Without nitrate | 12.0 | pale green to yellow |
| Without magnesium | 21.0 | yellow with green veins |

**(a)** State the purpose of the complete solution. **[1]**

**(b)** Calculate the percentage reduction in mean height for the seedlings grown without nitrate. **[2]**

**(c)** Calculate the percentage reduction in mean height for the seedlings grown without magnesium. **[2]**

**(d)** Explain why the seedlings grown without nitrate are much shorter. **[3]**

**(e)** Explain the colour of the leaves of the seedlings grown without magnesium. **[2]**

**(f)** State **two** variables, other than the ions, that should be kept the same in the three solutions. **[2]**

**7.** Air was bubbled through the culture solutions in question 6.

**(a)** Suggest why air was bubbled through the solutions. **[2]**

**(b)** Name the process by which the roots take up nitrate ions from the solution. **[1]**

**8.** A farmer grows wheat on the same field every year and adds no fertiliser. Over time the plants become stunted and have yellow leaves.

**(a)** Suggest which mineral ion is lacking from the soil and give a reason for your answer. **[2]**

**(b)** Explain why adding a fertiliser containing this ion would improve growth. **[3]**

**9.** A student says, "Nitrate is food for plants because it makes them grow." Evaluate this statement. **[3]**

**10.** Describe the route taken by nitrate ions from the soil to the leaf cells of a plant. **[3]**

**11.** Describe a water culture experiment that could be used to show that a plant needs magnesium ions. **[4]**

## Answers

**1.** B [1]. **[1]**

**2.** C [1]. **[1]**

**3.** To make amino acids [1], which are joined to make proteins [1]. **[2]**

**4.** The leaves turn yellow [1]. **[1]**

**5.** Magnesium is needed to make chlorophyll [1]. Without it, less light is absorbed [1], so photosynthesis is slower and less glucose is made for growth [1]. **[3]**

**6.**

**(a)** It is the control, showing normal growth [1].

**(b)** Reduction = 30.0 - 12.0 = 18.0 cm [1]. Percentage = 18.0 divided by 30.0 x 100 = 60 per cent [1].

**(c)** Reduction = 30.0 - 21.0 = 9.0 cm [1]. Percentage = 9.0 divided by 30.0 x 100 = 30 per cent [1].

**(d)** Nitrate contains nitrogen needed to make amino acids [1]. Without them the plant cannot make enough protein [1], so it cannot make new cells and enzymes and growth is stunted [1].

**(e)** Magnesium is needed to make chlorophyll [1]. Without it, the leaves have less green pigment, so they look yellow [1].

**(f)** Any two of: light intensity; temperature; volume of solution; the species or age of seedlings; time [1 each]. **[12]**

**7.**

**(a)** The roots need oxygen for aerobic respiration [1], which releases energy for active transport of the ions [1].

**(b)** Active transport [1]. **[3]**

**8.**

**(a)** Nitrate [1], because stunted growth shows a shortage of protein, and yellow leaves can also result from a shortage of nitrate [1].

**(b)** The fertiliser would supply nitrate ions that the plants take up [1]. They make amino acids and then proteins [1], which are needed for enzymes and new cells, so growth increases [1]. **[5]**

**9.** The statement is incorrect [1]. Plants make their own food (glucose) by photosynthesis, and nitrate does not provide energy [1]. Nitrate is a raw material needed to make amino acids and proteins, which are needed for growth [1]. **[3]**

**10.** Nitrate ions are taken into the root hair cells by active transport [1], move across the root to the xylem [1], and are carried up the xylem to the leaf where they enter the cells [1]. **[3]**

**11.** Grow identical seedlings in two solutions: a complete solution and one without magnesium [1]. The complete solution is the control [1]. Keep light, temperature, volume of solution and time the same, and aerate the solutions [1]. After several weeks compare the leaf colour and height or mass; the seedlings without magnesium should have yellow leaves and less growth [1]. **[4]**

## Where marks are usually lost

- Calling mineral ions "plant food".
- Missing out amino acids when explaining why nitrate is needed.
- Not naming chlorophyll as the substance that needs magnesium.
- Dividing by the test value, not the control value, when finding the percentage reduction.
- Forgetting that the control is the complete solution.
