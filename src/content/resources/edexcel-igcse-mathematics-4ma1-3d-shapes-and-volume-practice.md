## Section A

**1.** **(a)** Write down the name of the solid that has one flat face, one curved face and one vertex. **[1]**

**(b)** How many edges does a square-based pyramid have? **[1]**

**(c)** How many vertices does a triangular prism have? **[1]**

**2.** A cuboid measures 9 cm by 4 cm by 6 cm. Work out its total surface area. **[3]**

**3.** A prism has a cross-section that is a right-angled triangle with sides 5 cm, 12 cm and 13 cm. The prism is 20 cm long.

**(a)** Work out the volume of the prism. **[2]**

**(b)** Work out the total surface area of the prism. **[3]**

**4.** A cylinder has a radius of 5 cm and a height of 12 cm. Work out, to 3 significant figures,

**(a)** its volume, **[2]**

**(b)** its total surface area. **[3]**

**5.** **(a)** Convert 3.6 m³ to cm³. **[1]**

**(b)** Convert 12 500 cm³ to litres. **[1]**

**(c)** Convert 2.4 litres to cm³. **[1]**

## Section B

**6.** A water tank is a cuboid 1.2 m long, 0.8 m wide and 0.5 m high.

**(a)** Work out the volume of the tank in m³. **[2]**

**(b)** The tank is full. How many litres of water does it hold? **[1]**

**7.** A cylindrical can has a radius of 3 cm and a height of 20 cm. Work out its capacity in litres, to 3 significant figures. **[3]**

**8.** A paint tin has no lid. It is a cylinder with a radius of 4 cm and a height of 9 cm. Work out the area of metal needed to make the tin, to 3 significant figures. **[3]**

**9.** A solid metal cube of side 8 cm is melted and made into a cuboid with a base 16 cm by 4 cm. Work out the height of the cuboid. **[3]**

**10.** A cylinder has a volume of 1000 cm³ and a height of 10 cm. Work out its radius, to 3 significant figures. **[3]**

**11.** (Higher tier only) A cone has a base radius of 7 cm and a vertical height of 24 cm.

**(a)** Show that its slant height is 25 cm. **[2]**

**(b)** Work out the volume of the cone, to 3 significant figures. **[2]**

**(c)** Work out the total surface area of the cone, to 3 significant figures. **[3]**

**12.** (Higher tier only) A solid hemisphere has a radius of 9 cm. Work out, to 3 significant figures,

**(a)** its volume, **[2]**

**(b)** its total surface area, including the flat face. **[3]**

**13.** (Higher tier only) A toy is made from a cone on top of a hemisphere. Both have a radius of 3 cm, and the cone has a vertical height of 4 cm. The flat face of the cone sits on the flat face of the hemisphere.

**(a)** Work out the volume of the toy, in terms of π. **[4]**

**(b)** Work out the surface area of the toy, in terms of π. **[3]**

## Answers

**1. (a)** A cone [1].

**(b)** 8 edges [1].

**(c)** 6 vertices [1].

**2.** Faces: 9 × 4 = 36, 9 × 6 = 54, 4 × 6 = 24 [1]. Total of three different faces = 114 [1]. Surface area = 2 × 114 = **228 cm²** [1].

**3. (a)** Cross-section area = ½ × 5 × 12 = 30 cm² [1]. Volume = 30 × 20 = **600 cm³** [1].

**(b)** Two triangles: 2 × 30 = 60 [1]. Three rectangles: 20 × (5 + 12 + 13) = 600 [1]. Total = **660 cm²** [1].

**4. (a)** V = π × 5² × 12 [1] = 300π = 942.47... = **942 cm³** [1].

**(b)** Curved surface 2π × 5 × 12 = 120π [1]; two ends 2π × 25 = 50π [1]; total = 170π = 534.07... = **534 cm²** [1].

**5. (a)** 3.6 × 1 000 000 = **3 600 000 cm³** [1].

**(b)** 12 500 ÷ 1000 = **12.5 litres** [1].

**(c)** 2.4 × 1000 = **2400 cm³** [1].

**6. (a)** 1.2 × 0.8 × 0.5 [1] = **0.48 m³** [1].

**(b)** 1 m³ = 1000 litres, so **480 litres** [1].

**7.** V = π × 3² × 20 = 180π [1] = 565.48... cm³ [1]. Divide by 1000: **0.565 litres** [1].

**8.** Open tin, so there is only one circular end [1]. Area = π × 4² + 2π × 4 × 9 = 16π + 72π [1] = 88π = 276.46... = **276 cm²** [1].

**9.** Volume of the cube = 8³ = 512 cm³ [1]. 16 × 4 × h = 512, so 64h = 512 [1]. h = **8 cm** [1].

**10.** 1000 = π × r² × 10 [1]. r² = 1000 ÷ (10π) = 31.83... [1]. r = **5.64 cm** [1].

**11. (a)** l² = 7² + 24² = 49 + 576 = 625 [1]. l = √625 = **25 cm** [1].

**(b)** V = ⅓ × π × 49 × 24 [1] = 392π = 1231.5... = **1230 cm³** [1].

**(c)** Curved surface = π × 7 × 25 = 175π [1]. Base = π × 49 = 49π [1]. Total = 224π = 703.7... = **704 cm²** [1].

**12. (a)** V = 2/3 × π × 9³ [1] = 486π = 1526.8... = **1530 cm³** [1].

**(b)** Curved surface = 2π × 81 = 162π [1]. Flat face = π × 81 = 81π [1]. Total = 243π = 763.4... = **763 cm²** [1].

**13. (a)** Cone: ⅓ × π × 3² × 4 = 12π [1]. Hemisphere: 2/3 × π × 3³ = 18π [1]. Add: [1]. Volume = **30π cm³** [1].

**(b)** Slant height of the cone = √(9 + 16) = 5 cm [1]. Curved surface of the cone = π × 3 × 5 = 15π; curved surface of the hemisphere = 2π × 9 = 18π [1]. The joined flat faces are inside, so total = **33π cm²** [1].

## Where marks are usually lost

- Question 2: finding the area of only three faces and not doubling.
- Question 3(b): leaving out the two triangular ends, or using the slant side only once.
- Question 4(b): counting the curved surface only.
- Question 6: multiplying in centimetres and then calling the answer m³.
- Question 8: including a lid for a tin that has none.
- Question 11: using 24 cm instead of the slant height 25 cm in the curved surface area.
- Question 12(b): leaving out the flat face. The question says to include it.
- Question 13(b): adding the flat circular faces that are joined together.

## Frequently asked questions

**Which of these questions test Higher Tier content only?**
Questions 11, 12 and 13, which use cones and hemispheres. Questions 1 to 10 test content that both tiers cover.

**Do I need to remember the formula for the curved surface area of a cone?**
It is on the Higher Tier formulae sheet as πrl, but you must know that l is the slant height and be able to find it with Pythagoras' theorem.
