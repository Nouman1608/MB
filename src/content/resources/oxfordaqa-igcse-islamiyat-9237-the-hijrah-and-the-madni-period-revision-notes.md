**These notes condense content area 3.2.1.7 of 9237 as a sequence of cause and effect: "The events leading up to the Hijrah including the pledges of Aqaba", the journey "including the roles of Hazrat Abu Bakr (RA) and Hazrat Ali (RA)", "Early life in Madinah, including Hazrat Muhammad's (pbuh) role as a statesman and the establishment of the Charter of Madinah" and "The establishment of the first mosque in Madinah."** The full timeline is in the [study guide](/resources/oxfordaqa-igcse-islamiyat-9237-the-hijrah-and-the-madni-period/).

## Cause and effect chain

| Event | Led to |
|---|---|
| Death of Abu Talib and Khadija (RA) | Loss of protection; more attacks |
| Rejection at Ta'if | Need for another base |
| Khazraj men meet the Prophet (pbuh) at Aqaba | Islam spreads in Yathrib |
| First pledge (twelve men) | Mus'ab ibn Umair (RA) teaches there |
| Second pledge (more than seventy men, two women) | Promise of protection; invitation to Yathrib |
| Muslims leave in small groups | The Quraysh fear the Prophet (pbuh) will gather strength |
| Quraysh plot | Departure of the Prophet (pbuh) and Abu Bakr (RA) |
| Journey and arrival | Quba mosque; entry into Madinah |
| Prophet's Mosque, brotherhood, Charter | A united community and state |

## Aqaba: pledges compared

| | First | Second |
|---|---|---|
| People | Twelve men | More than seventy men and two women |
| Promises | No shirk, theft, adultery, killing children, slander; obey in good | Protect the Prophet (pbuh) as their own families |
| Result | Mus'ab sent to teach | Invitation to live in Yathrib; twelve leaders appointed |

## Journey: day by day

1. **Night of the plot:** Ali (RA) sleeps in the Prophet's bed; the Prophet (pbuh) slips out.
2. **Same night:** meets Abu Bakr (RA); they head south.
3. **Three nights in Thawr:** Abdullah brings news; Asma (RA) brings food; Amir ibn Fuhayra hides the tracks.
4. **At the cave mouth:** "Do not grieve; indeed Allah is with us" (9:40).
5. **Guide:** Abdullah ibn Urayqit leads them by the coast road.
6. **Pursuit:** the reward is one hundred camels; Suraqa's horse sinks; he turns back.
7. **Quba:** first mosque; Ali (RA) joins them.
8. **Madinah:** the camel kneels; land from two orphans; stay with Abu Ayyub al-Ansari (RA).

## The roles of Ali (RA) and Abu Bakr (RA)

| | Ali (RA) | Abu Bakr (RA) |
|---|---|---|
| Role | Slept in the bed; returned the trusts; followed on foot | Prepared camels and a guide; travelled with the Prophet (pbuh); hid in Thawr |
| Quality | Courage and honesty | Loyalty and planning |
| Effect | Plot failed; the Prophet's trustworthiness upheld | Journey succeeded; his family supported it |

## Two mosques

| | Quba | Prophet's Mosque (Madinah) |
|---|---|---|
| Built | On arrival at the edge of Madinah | On land of two orphans, paid for |
| Importance | First mosque in Islam | Centre of community life |
| Uses | Prayer | Prayer, learning, courts, meetings, delegations, the suffah |

**Qur'an 9:108:** "a mosque founded on righteousness from the first day" and "men who love to purify themselves". Many scholars say Quba; others say the Prophet's Mosque.

## Statesman: four actions

1. Built the mosque as the community's centre.
2. Created brotherhood between the Muhajirun and the Ansar.
3. Agreed the Charter of Madinah.
4. Organised defence and relations with other tribes.

## Charter of Madinah

| Term | Effect |
|---|---|
| One Ummah | Ended the Aws and Khazraj feud |
| Jewish tribes keep their religion | Religious freedom |
| Joint defence | Security for all |
| No help for the Quraysh | Protected the city from the enemy |
| Help for the oppressed | Justice |
| Disputes to Allah and the Prophet (pbuh) | One system of law |
| Madinah a sacred area | Protected status |

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Hijrah | Migration from Makkah to Madinah, 622 CE |
| Muhajirun | Migrants from Makkah |
| Ansar | Helpers of Madinah |
| Bay'ah | Pledge of allegiance |
| Thawr | Cave of hiding |
| Quba | Village where the first mosque was built |
| Suffah | Covered area for poor Muslims in the Prophet's Mosque |
| Madinat an-Nabi | City of the Prophet |

## Exam traps

- **Quba** was the first mosque; the Prophet's Mosque came after.
- The cave is **Thawr**, not Hira.
- The second pledge added **protection**.
- Ali (RA) did **two things**: slept in the bed and returned trusts.
- The Charter included **non-Muslims**.
- Dates: the Hijrah was in **622 CE**; Muslims count their calendar from it.

## Self-test

1. What did Mus'ab ibn Umair (RA) do in Yathrib?
2. How many people took part in the second pledge of Aqaba?
3. In which cave did the Prophet (pbuh) hide?
4. Who brought food to the cave?
5. How did Ali (RA) help the plan to succeed?
6. Which man pursued the travellers and turned back?
7. Where was the first mosque built?
8. Who hosted the Prophet (pbuh) in Madinah?
9. Give two terms of the Charter of Madinah.
10. Which verse is the prescribed passage?

**Answers:** 1. He taught the Qur'an and Islam. 2. More than seventy men and two women. 3. Thawr. 4. Asma (RA), daughter of Abu Bakr. 5. He slept in the Prophet's bed so that the watchers thought he was still there. 6. Suraqa ibn Malik. 7. Quba. 8. Abu Ayyub al-Ansari (RA). 9. Any two: one community; Jewish tribes keep their religion; joint defence; no help for the Quraysh; help for the oppressed; disputes to the Prophet (pbuh); Madinah a sacred area. 10. Qur'an 9:108.
