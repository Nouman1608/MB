Condensed notes for **Part 1: Presentation** of the optional Component 5 Speaking test of Cambridge IGCSE Urdu as a Second Language (0539). For fuller explanations and a worked example, use the [Part 1 Presentation study guide](/resources/cambridge-igcse-urdu-language-0539-part-1-presentation/).

## Part 1 in a box

```
Part 1  Presentation
Time        2 to 3 minutes
Marks       20 (of 60 for Speaking)
Topic       chosen by the candidate
Objectives  S1, S2, S3, S4, S5
Language    Urdu throughout; test is recorded; no dictionaries
```

- Speaking is optional and **separately endorsed** (grade 1 high to 5 low). It does not change the overall qualification grade.
- Marked by a teacher or examiner at the centre; externally moderated.

## The speaking objectives

| Objective | Meaning |
|---|---|
| S1 | communicate ideas and opinions clearly, accurately and effectively |
| S2 | develop responses and link ideas with appropriate linking devices |
| S3 | use a range of grammatical structures and vocabulary accurately and effectively |
| S4 | show control of pronunciation and intonation |
| S5 | engage in conversation and contribute to help move it forward |

## Rules to remember

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Topic | chosen by the candidate; must reflect an aspect or aspects of life in an Urdu-speaking community or culture |
| Written copy | **not allowed** |
| Cue card | one only, in Urdu, no larger than a postcard, a **maximum of five headings** |
| Illustrative material | no more than three or four items (maps, diagrams, statistics, pictures); must **not** give extra written support |
| Dictionaries | not allowed |

## Topic tests

1. **Culture:** is it about Urdu-speaking life or culture?
2. **Interest:** do you care about it?
3. **Depth:** can you fill two to three minutes, and more in Part 2?
4. **Language:** can you control the Urdu you need?

Suitable examples: a festival, food, craft, poet, family custom, city, music, a traditional job, a market.

## Five-part structure

| Part | Content | Language to use |
|---|---|---|
| Opening | greeting, topic, why chosen | السلام علیکم، آج میں ... کے بارے میں بتاؤں گا / گی |
| Background | what it is | یہ ایک ایسی ... ہے جس میں ... |
| Detail | two or three facts and an example | مثال کے طور پر |
| Opinion | view with a reason | میرے خیال میں ... کیونکہ ... |
| Close | summary or hope | آخر میں، خلاصہ یہ ہے کہ، شکریہ |

## Linking phrases

| Function | Phrases |
|---|---|
| Order | سب سے پہلے، پھر، اس کے بعد، آخر میں |
| Add | اس کے علاوہ، نیز |
| Contrast | لیکن، البتہ، تاہم |
| Reason | کیونکہ، اس لیے |
| Example | مثال کے طور پر، جیسے |
| Opinion | میرے خیال میں، میری رائے ہے کہ |

## Assessment criteria

| Criterion | Marks | Looks for |
|---|---|---|
| Content and presentation | 10 | coverage; ideas sequenced and linked; facts, ideas and opinions; holds interest |
| Quality of language | 10 | vocabulary; pronunciation and intonation; structures; errors that do or do not impede communication |

Bands for each criterion: 9 to 10 very good; 7 to 8 good; 5 to 6 satisfactory; 3 to 4 weak; 1 to 2 poor; 0 no rewardable content. Examiners start at the bottom and work upwards, giving credit for what the candidate can do.

## Delivery checklist

- Look at the examiner; glance at the card.
- Slow, clear speech with pauses.
- Stress important words; let the voice fall at the end of a sentence.
- Practise the hard sounds: *ق، خ، غ، ٹ، ڈ، ڑ*.
- Time yourself.
- End clearly, not abruptly.

## Typical faults

| Fault | Fix |
|---|---|
| Memorised speech | practise from five headings only |
| Facts but no opinion | add *میرے خیال میں ... کیونکہ ...* |
| Too general a topic | narrow it to a specific cultural subject |
| More than five headings | cut to five |
| Written notes on illustrative material | use pictures and numbers only |
| Under two minutes | add an example and a reason to each part |

## Exam traps

- Prepare for the follow-up questions: Part 2 is about your presentation.
- Avoid a topic that overlaps with the general conversation topics you may be asked.
- Do not read from the illustrative material.
- Do not rush: speed does not earn marks.

## Self-test

1. How long is the Part 1 presentation?
2. How many marks is it worth?
3. Who chooses the topic?
4. What must the topic reflect?
5. Is a written copy of the presentation allowed?
6. What is the maximum number of headings on the cue card?
7. How many items of illustrative material are allowed?
8. Give **two** features of a presentation in the top band.
9. Which two criteria are used for Part 1?
10. Name **two** linking phrases in Urdu.

**Answers**

1. Two to three minutes.
2. Twenty marks.
3. The candidate.
4. An aspect or aspects of life in an Urdu-speaking community or Urdu-speaking culture.
5. No.
6. Five.
7. No more than three or four.
8. Any two: thorough coverage; ideas well sequenced and linked; a range of facts, ideas and opinions; an engaging presentation; wide and precise vocabulary; clear pronunciation and intonation; complex structures used accurately.
9. Content and presentation, and quality of language.
10. Any two, for example *اس کے علاوہ*، *کیونکہ*، *لیکن*، *آخر میں*.
