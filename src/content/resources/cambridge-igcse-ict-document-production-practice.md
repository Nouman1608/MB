## Section B: structured questions

**5.** A magazine article has a title across the full page width and the body text in three columns.

**(a)** Explain why a section break is needed after the title. **[2]**
**(b)** The second column ends half-way down the page with only one line of a paragraph at the bottom. Describe how a column break could improve this. **[1]**

**6.** A price list contains these values: 4.5, 125.99, 18.25.

**(a)** Name the tab that should be used to align them and describe its effect. **[2]**
**(b)** The list also includes item names. Describe how a hanging paragraph could be used for long item descriptions. **[2]**

**7.** A student uses *find and replace* to change every occurrence of "Ali" to "Aliya".

**(a)** Explain why she should select *whole words only*. **[2]**
**(b)** Explain when *match case* should be used. **[1]**

**8.** A 40-page booklet will be printed on both sides of the paper and bound.

**(a)** Describe how the margins should be set. **[2]**
**(b)** The booklet has a contents page. Explain how bookmarks and hyperlinks could help a reader of the electronic version. **[2]**

## Section C: practical-style tasks

**9.** Open any document of at least two pages. Set it to A4, landscape, with all margins 2.5 cm. Change the body text to 1.5 line spacing with 6 pt after each paragraph. **[4]**

**10.** Create a numbered list of five steps, then change it to a bulleted list. Make the first word of each item bold. **[3]**

**11.** Insert a bookmark called *Summary* at the last heading. Create a hyperlink from the word "summary" on page 1 to the bookmark. Then delete a hyperlink you did not need. **[3]**

## Worked answers

**1.** Left (1), right (1), centred (1), decimal (1). **[4]**

**2.** To force the following text to start at the top of the next column. **[1]**

**3.** Any suitable example, for example the 2 in H₂O or in CO₂. **[1]**

**4.** To add extra space on the inside edge of the page for binding, so text is not lost in the binding. **[1]**

**5. (a)** The title must be in one column and the body in three columns (1); a section break allows different column settings in different parts of the same page (1). **[2]**
**(b)** Insert a column break before the line so that the whole paragraph starts together at the top of the next column (removing the orphan). **[1]**

**6. (a)** Decimal tab (1); it lines up the numbers on their decimal points so that units, tens and decimals line up in a column (1). **[2]**
**(b)** The first line (the item name) starts at the margin (1) and the remaining lines of the description are indented, so each item name stands out at the left (1). **[2]**

**7. (a)** Without it, "Ali" inside other words (for example "Alice" or "quality") would also be changed (1), producing errors such as "Aliyace" (1). **[2]**
**(b)** When only text with exactly the same capitals should be changed, for example "Ali" but not "ali" in a different word. **[1]**

**8. (a)** Set mirrored margins (inside and outside) (1) and add a gutter margin on the inside edge for the binding (1). **[2]**
**(b)** Bookmarks mark the start of each section (1); hyperlinks in the contents page jump straight to those bookmarks, so the reader can move to a section without scrolling (1). **[2]**

**9.** 1 mark each: A4 size; landscape orientation; all four margins 2.5 cm; body text 1.5 line spacing with 6 pt after (both needed). **[4]**

**10.** 1 mark each: numbered list of five items created; changed to bullets (no numbers remaining); first word of each item bold, nothing else bold. **[3]**

**11.** 1 mark each: bookmark named *Summary* at the correct heading; hyperlink from "summary" goes to the bookmark (tested); unwanted hyperlink removed (text remains, link gone). **[3]**

## Frequently asked questions

**Why not just press Enter until text moves to the next page?**
Extra blank lines move when text is edited, so the layout breaks. A page or column break keeps the text in the right place, and examiners look for correct use of breaks.

**Which paper tests document production?**
Paper 2 (Document Production, Databases and Presentations), a practical test of 2 hours 15 minutes worth 70 marks.

**Is a gutter the same as a wide left margin?**
No. A gutter is extra space added specifically on the binding edge; with mirrored margins it is always on the inside of each page.
