Condensed notes for subtopic **1.15 Time** of Cambridge O Level Mathematics (Syllabus D) 4024, 2025 to 2027. The [Time study guide](/resources/cambridge-o-level-mathematics-4024-time/) explains each method with worked examples; the [Time practice questions](/resources/cambridge-o-level-mathematics-4024-time-practice/) give exam-style questions with mark schemes.

## Syllabus outcomes

1. Calculate with time (s, min, h, days, weeks, months, years), including the relationship between units. 1 year = 365 days.
2. Calculate times in terms of the 24-hour and 12-hour clock (3.15 a.m. is 03 15; 3.15 p.m. is 15 15).
3. Read clocks and timetables, including time zones, local times and time differences.

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Duration | length of time between two moments |
| 24-hour clock | clock from 00 00 to 23 59 |
| a.m. / p.m. | before / after noon |
| Timetable | list of departure and arrival times |
| Time zone | region with a standard local time |
| Time difference | hours ahead of or behind another place |

## Units

| Conversion | Value |
|---|---|
| 1 min | 60 s |
| 1 h | 60 min = 3 600 s |
| 1 day | 24 h = 1 440 min = 86 400 s |
| 1 week | 7 days |
| 1 year | 365 days |
| 365 days | 8 760 h = 52 weeks 1 day |

Method for conversions: multiply to go to a smaller unit, divide to go to a larger one.

| Task | Working | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| 5 400 s in h and min | 5 400 ÷ 60 = 90 min | 1 h 30 min |
| 23 456 s | 6 h (21 600), 30 min (1 800), 56 s | 6 h 30 min 56 s |
| 3 days in seconds | 3 × 86 400 | 259 200 s |
| 2 h 15 min in seconds | 8 100 | 8 100 s |
| 365 days in hours | 365 × 24 | 8 760 h |

## Decimals and time

| Time | Decimal hours |
|---|---|
| 15 min | 0.25 |
| 30 min | 0.5 |
| 45 min | 0.75 |
| 24 min | 0.4 |
| 2 h 24 min | 2.4 |

Time is base 60, not base 10. Decimal part × 60 = minutes. Minutes ÷ 60 = decimal part of an hour.

## Clock conversion

| 12-hour | 24-hour |
|---|---|
| 12.25 a.m. | 00 25 |
| 3.15 a.m. | 03 15 |
| 12.00 noon | 12 00 |
| 3.15 p.m. | 15 15 |
| 9.40 p.m. | 21 40 |
| 11.59 p.m. | 23 59 |

For p.m. times after 1 p.m., add 12 hours. To change back, subtract 12 from hours 13 to 23.

## Duration method

1. Count up to the next whole hour.
2. Count whole hours.
3. Add the minutes at the end.
4. Total, carrying 60 minutes into an hour.

| From | To | Working | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| 09 35 | 22 50 | 25 min + 12 h + 50 min | 13 h 15 min |
| 21 40 | 03 15 | 2 h 20 min + 3 h 15 min | 5 h 35 min |
| 11 45 | 13 10 | 15 min + 1 h + 10 min | 1 h 25 min |
| 23 50 | 06 15 | 10 min + 6 h 15 min | 6 h 25 min |

If the later time has a smaller number on the clock, the journey crosses midnight.

## Arrival time method

Start time + duration: add hours, then minutes, then carry.

| Start | Duration | Arrival |
|---|---|---|
| 08 45 | 3 h 40 min | 12 25 |
| 06 50 | 2 h 25 min | 09 15 |
| 19 45 | 2 h 18 min | 22 03 |
| 22 30 | 3 h 50 min | 02 20 next day |

## Time zones

| Situation | Rule |
|---|---|
| Destination ahead by h hours | local time at destination = departure time + flight time + h |
| Destination behind by h hours | local time = departure time + flight time - h |
| City A is 3 h behind City B | A's clock = B's clock - 3 h |

Example: leaves 22 40, flight 9 h 35 min, destination 5 h ahead: 22 40 + 9 h 35 = 08 15 next day; + 5 h = 13 15 local time, next day.

Check that the answer is sensible: a place ahead has a later clock time.

## Repeated durations

| Task | Working | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| 4 jobs of 3 h 50 min | 12 h + 200 min | 15 h 20 min |
| 5 lessons of 55 min | 275 min | 4 h 35 min |
| 7 days at 6 h 45 min | 42 h + 315 min | 47 h 15 min |

## Exam traps

- 2 h 30 min is 2.5 hours, not 2.30.
- Do not subtract directly when crossing midnight.
- Carry 60 minutes, not 100.
- 12.00 a.m. is midnight (00 00); 12.00 p.m. is noon (12 00).
- Write 24-hour times with four digits.
- Say "next day" when the arrival passes midnight.
- Time zone direction: a place ahead has a later time.
- Use 365 days for a year.

## Self-test

1. Write 6.45 p.m. in the 24-hour clock.
2. Write 00 40 in the 12-hour clock.
3. Find the time from 08 50 to 11 15.
4. A bus leaves at 14 35 and takes 2 hours 50 minutes. When does it arrive?
5. How many minutes are there in 4 hours 20 minutes?
6. Write 2.3 hours in hours and minutes.
7. A plane leaves at 20 15 and flies for 6 h 50 min. The arrival city is 4 hours behind the departure city. What is the local arrival time?
8. How many weeks and days are there in 100 days?

**Answers**

1. **18 45**.
2. **12.40 a.m.**
3. 08 50 to 11 15 = 10 min + 2 h + 15 min = **2 h 25 min**.
4. 14 35 + 2 h 50 min = **17 25**.
5. 4 × 60 + 20 = **260 minutes**.
6. 0.3 × 60 = 18, so **2 hours 18 minutes**.
7. 20 15 + 6 h 50 min = 03 05 next day; minus 4 h = **23 05** the same day as departure.
8. 100 ÷ 7 = 14 remainder 2, so **14 weeks 2 days**.
