**These notes condense The USA, 1974-93, the third topic of the Paper 4 American option in Cambridge 9489.** For explanation and a worked judgement paragraph see the [1974-93 study guide](/resources/cambridge-a-level-world-history-9489-the-usa-1974-93/); for essays and short questions see the [1974-93 practice questions](/resources/cambridge-a-level-world-history-9489-the-usa-1974-93-practice/).

## Syllabus coverage

- 6.1 How significant were the problems facing the US in the late 1970s?
- 6.2 How great was the impact of the New Conservatism, 1981-92?
- 6.3 How effective were Carter's foreign policies, 1977-81?
- 6.4 Why did relations between the US and the USSR change?

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Stagflation | High inflation and high unemployment at the same time |
| Busing | Transporting pupils to other districts to desegregate schools |
| New Right | Coalition of religious conservatives, tax rebels and free-market thinkers |
| Moral Majority | Jerry Falwell's evangelical political organisation (1979) |
| Supply-side economics | Theory that tax cuts raise output, investment and eventually revenue |
| Reaganomics | Reagan's tax cuts, domestic spending cuts, deregulation and tight money |
| Keynesian consensus | Post-war acceptance that government spending should manage demand |
| Deregulation | Removing government rules on industries such as airlines and banking |
| Shuttle diplomacy | Kissinger's travel between Middle East capitals to broker deals, 1973-75 |
| Détente | Easing of Cold War tension, c.1969-79 |
| Second Cold War | Renewed confrontation, c.1979-85 |
| Reagan Doctrine | Support for anti-communist insurgents ("rollback") |
| SDI | Strategic Defense Initiative, Reagan's proposed missile shield (1983) |
| INF Treaty | 1987 agreement eliminating intermediate-range nuclear missiles |

## 6.1 Late-1970s problems -- ranking

1. **Economic:** stagflation, oil shocks (1973, 1979), high interest rates.
2. **Political:** post-Watergate distrust; the Nixon pardon; Carter's poor relations with Congress.
3. **Social:** busing conflicts; reaction against abortion rights, gay rights and the counter-culture.
4. **International:** Iran and Afghanistan made the USA look weak.

**Why Reagan won in 1980:** personal appeal, supply-side promises, Carter's failures, New Right mobilisation.

## 6.2 New Conservatism -- impact checklist

- **Taxes:** top rate 70 to 50 per cent (1981) to 28 per cent (1986).
- **Spending:** domestic cuts but defence growth; deficits and debt soared.
- **Labour:** PATCO controllers dismissed; union power declined.
- **Social:** opposition to busing and affirmative action; slow response to AIDS; MLK holiday signed.
- **Bush:** tax rise in 1990; ADA and Clean Air amendments; recession; lost in 1992.
- **Democrats:** 1980 split; Mondale's tax pledge (1984); loss of the white South; DLC founded (1985).

## 6.4 US-Soviet relations -- why they changed

- **Détente motives:** cost, parity, Vietnam, the Sino-Soviet split.
- **Détente collapse:** Angola and Ethiopia, Afghanistan, SALT II unratified, ideological revival, Reagan's election.
- **Rollback:** Nicaragua (Contras), Afghanistan (mujahideen), Angola (UNITA).
- **Thaw:** Gorbachev's reforms and need to cut defence; summits; INF Treaty; Reagan's turn to negotiation.

## Exam traps

- **Reagan cut taxes, not total spending.**
- **Camp David** was Egypt-Israel only.
- **SALT II** was signed in 1979 but never ratified.
- **Iran-Contra** combined arms sales to Iran with illegal funding of the Contras.
- **"Why did relations change?"** needs both Soviet and American causes.

## Self-test

1. What was stagflation? *Simultaneous high inflation and high unemployment.*
2. What damaged Ford's reputation in September 1974? *His pardon of Nixon.*
3. Which department did Carter create in 1977? *The Department of Energy.*
4. Who founded the Moral Majority? *Jerry Falwell.*
5. What question did Reagan ask voters in the 1980 debate? *Whether they were better off than four years earlier.*
6. What did the 1981 tax act do to the top rate? *Cut it from 70 to 50 per cent.*
7. How did Reagan respond to the 1981 air traffic controllers' strike? *He dismissed more than 11,000 strikers.*
8. Which two Bush laws of 1990 are on the syllabus? *The Americans with Disabilities Act and the Clean Air Act amendments.*
9. Which two leaders signed the 1979 peace treaty brokered at Camp David? *Sadat and Begin.*
10. How long were the American hostages held in Iran? *444 days.*
11. What did the Helsinki Accords recognise? *Europe's post-war borders, with commitments on human rights.*
12. What did the INF Treaty eliminate? *Intermediate-range nuclear missiles.*
