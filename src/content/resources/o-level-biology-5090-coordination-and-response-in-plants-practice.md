## Questions

**1.** Define the term *gravitropism*. **[2]**

**2.** Copy and complete the table using the words *positive* or *negative*.

| Part of plant | Response to gravity | Response to light from one side |
|---|---|---|
| Shoot | ........ | ........ |
| Root | ........ | not required |

**[3]**

**3.** This question is about auxin.

**(a)** State where auxin is made. **[1]**
**(b)** State the effect of auxin on the cells it reaches. **[1]**
**(c)** A shoot is lit from the left only. State which side of the shoot has more auxin. **[1]**

**4.** Explain how auxin causes a shoot to grow towards light coming from one side. **[5]**

**5.** A student grew three groups of oat seedlings in light from one side for two days and measured the angle of curvature of each shoot towards the light.

| Group | Treatment of shoot tip | Angles of curvature / ° |
|---|---|---|
| A | No treatment | 38, 42, 35, 45 |
| B | Covered with a transparent cap | 36, 41, 39, 40 |
| C | Covered with a light-proof cap | 0, 2, 0, 2 |

**(a)** Calculate the mean angle of curvature for group A. **[1]**
**(b)** Explain the results for group C. **[3]**
**(c)** Suggest why the student included group B. **[2]**

**6.** A student measured cells in the region just below the tip of a shoot that had bent. The mean cell length on one side was 45 µm and on the other side was 72 µm.

**(a)** Calculate the percentage by which the longer cells exceed the shorter cells in length. **[2]**
**(b)** The shoot had bent towards a lamp. State which side, lit or shaded, had the 72 µm cells, and explain your answer. **[2]**

**7.** A potted seedling is placed on its side in a dark cupboard for two days.

**(a)** Describe what happens to the direction of growth of the shoot and of the root. **[2]**
**(b)** Explain the response of the shoot in terms of auxin. **[3]**
**(c)** Suggest why the seedling was kept in the dark. **[1]**

**8.** Plan an investigation to find out whether cress shoots show phototropism. You are provided with cress seedlings growing in small pots, cardboard boxes, scissors, a lamp and a protractor. **[6]**

**9.** Bean seeds were pinned to damp cotton wool in two dishes, with their young roots pointing horizontally. Both dishes were kept in the dark. Dish P was kept still. Dish Q was fixed to a clinostat.

**(a)** Describe the expected direction of root growth in dish P and in dish Q. **[2]**
**(b)** Explain why dish Q was set up. **[2]**
**(c)** One root in dish P grew from 18 mm to 42 mm long in 3 days. Calculate its mean growth rate. **[2]**
**(d)** Suggest one source of error in measuring the length of a curved root. **[1]**

**10.** A student laid five bean seedlings on their sides in the dark. After two days the roots had curved downwards by these angles: 84°, 90°, 78°, 88°, 85°.

**(a)** Calculate the mean angle of curvature. **[2]**
**(b)** Name this response and state whether it is positive or negative. **[2]**
**(c)** Explain how this response benefits the plant. **[2]**
**(d)** Suggest why the student used five seedlings rather than one. **[1]**
**(e)** State one variable that should be kept constant for all five seedlings. **[1]**

**11.** Scientists measured the auxin in the two halves of a shoot tip that had been lit from one side. The lit half contained 35 arbitrary units of auxin and the shaded half contained 65 arbitrary units.

**(a)** Calculate the percentage of the total auxin that was in the shaded half. **[2]**
**(b)** Explain how this difference leads to the shoot bending. **[3]**
**(c)** The shoot is then given light evenly from all sides. Predict how the auxin would be distributed and how the shoot would grow. **[2]**

## Answers

**1.** A response in which parts of a plant grow [1] towards or away from gravity [1]. **[2]**
*Examiner insight:* "Towards or away from" is needed for the second mark; "the plant grows downwards" describes only a root and is not a definition.

**2.** Shoot, gravity: **negative** [1]. Shoot, light: **positive** [1]. Root, gravity: **positive** [1]. **[3]**
*Examiner insight:* In this answer each cell carries its own mark, so writing the root and shoot gravity responses the wrong way round costs two marks.

**3. (a)** In the **shoot tip** [1].
**(b)** It stimulates cell **elongation** [1].
**(c)** The **right** side, which is shaded [1].
*Examiner insight:* "Makes the cells grow" is usually too vague for (b); the creditworthy idea is elongation, and "makes cells divide" scores nothing.

**4.**
- Auxin is made in the shoot tip; [1]
- light causes auxin to be unequally distributed, with more on the shaded side; [1]
- auxin spreads back from the tip to the region below; [1]
- cells on the shaded side elongate more than cells on the lit side; [1]
- so the shaded side grows longer and the shoot bends towards the light. [1]
**[5]**
*Examiner insight:* The bending point only scores when it follows from a stated difference between the two sides; "auxin makes the shoot bend" with no reference to the shaded side earns little.

**5. (a)** (38 + 42 + 35 + 45) ÷ 4 = 160 ÷ 4 = **40°** [1]
**(b)** The shoots grew straight / did not bend [1]. Light could not reach the tip, so auxin was not unequally distributed [1]. Auxin spread back evenly, so cells on all sides elongated equally [1].
**(c)** To show that the cap itself does not stop bending [1]; so the lack of bending in C is caused by keeping light from the tip [1].
*Examiner insight:* For (c), "as a control" alone earns at most one mark; say what the control rules out (the effect of the cap, not of the light).

**6. (a)** (72 − 45) ÷ 45 × 100 [1] = **60%** [1]
**(b)** The **shaded** side [1]. More auxin collects on the shaded side and auxin stimulates cell elongation, so cells there are longer [1].
*Examiner insight:* Dividing by 72 instead of 45 gives 37.5%, which is a different comparison; use the value the question compares against, and expect to lose the accuracy mark for the wrong base.

**7. (a)** The shoot curves **upwards** [1]; the root curves **downwards** [1].
**(b)** Auxin is made in the shoot tip and spreads back [1]. Gravity causes more auxin on the lower side [1]. Cells on the lower side elongate more, so the shoot curves upwards [1].
**(c)** So that light cannot cause a response; only gravity affects the direction of growth [1].
*Examiner insight:* In (b) the side must be named; an answer that says auxin is "unequally distributed" without saying which side has more cannot score the second or third mark.

**8.**
- Put one group of seedlings in a box with a slit cut in one side, with the lamp shining through the slit, so light comes from one side; [1]
- control: an identical group in a box with no slit, or lit evenly from all sides; [1]
- keep at least two named variables the same, for example temperature, watering, type and age of seedlings; [1]
- leave both groups for the same stated time, for example two or three days; [1]
- measure the angle of curvature of each shoot from the vertical with the protractor; [1]
- use several seedlings in each group and calculate a mean angle. [1]
**[6]**
*Examiner insight:* Each controlled variable must be named; "keep everything else the same" usually scores nothing, and the control group needs to be described, not just mentioned.

**9. (a)** Dish P: roots curve downwards [1]. Dish Q: roots keep growing horizontally / in the direction they started [1].
**(b)** The clinostat turns the seeds so gravity acts on all sides equally [1]; this shows that the downward curve in P is caused by gravity [1].
**(c)** (42 − 18) ÷ 3 [1] = **8 mm per day** [1]
**(d)** A ruler cannot follow the curve / thread laid along the root may stretch / the start of the root is hard to judge [1].
*Examiner insight:* The rate needs units; "8" alone loses the accuracy mark even when the working is correct.

**10. (a)** (84 + 90 + 78 + 88 + 85) ÷ 5 [1] = 425 ÷ 5 = **85°** [1]
**(b)** Gravitropism [1]; positive [1].
**(c)** The root grows down into the soil [1]; to anchor the plant / to absorb water and mineral ions [1].
**(d)** Seedlings vary, so a mean of several is more reliable [1].
**(e)** Temperature / type or age of seedling / time / moisture of the cotton wool [1].
**[8]**
*Examiner insight:* Part (b) has two separate marks, one for the name and one for the direction; "gravitropism" with no direction scores only one.

**11. (a)** 65 ÷ (35 + 65) × 100 [1] = **65%** [1]
**(b)** Auxin spreads back from the tip to the region below [1]. More auxin on the shaded side stimulates more cell elongation there [1]. The shaded side grows longer, so the shoot bends towards the light [1].
**(c)** Auxin evenly distributed [1]; cells elongate equally on all sides, so the shoot grows straight up [1].
**[7]**
*Examiner insight:* In (a), the divisor is the total (100 units), not the lit-side value; dividing 65 by 35 gives a ratio, not the percentage asked for.

## Where marks are usually lost

- Definitions of a tropism that leave out "grow" or "towards or away from".
- Writing "auxin is made in the tip" without the word **shoot**.
- Saying auxin makes cells divide, rather than stimulating cell **elongation**.
- Not naming the side (shaded, or lower) that has more auxin.
- Saying the shoot bends towards the side with more auxin; it bends **away from** it.
- Swapping positive and negative for roots and shoots.
- In planning answers, writing "keep everything the same" instead of naming each controlled variable.
- Forgetting a control (even light, or a clinostat), or not keeping a gravitropism test in the dark.
- Leaving units off a mean angle (°) or a growth rate (mm per day).
