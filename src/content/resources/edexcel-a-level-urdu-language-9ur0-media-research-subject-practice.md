**3.** Give the English meaning of: (a) اداریہ (b) کالم نگار (c) ہتکِ عزت (d) تحقیقاتی صحافت (e) سیاسی شعور **[5]**

(c) آزادیٔ صحافت جمہوریت کے لیے ضروری ہیں۔ **[3]**

## Answers

**1.** آزادیٔ اظہار, freedom of expression [1]؛ سیاست پر پرنٹ میڈیا کے اثرات, the effects of print media on politics [1]؛ سوشل میڈیا کے نتیجے میں بڑھتا ہوا سیاسی شعور, growing political awareness as a result of social media [1]

**2.** Either Pakistan [1] or one area of India where Urdu is an official language [1]; if more than one is used, examiners credit only the content on the area that gains the highest mark, so the rest is wasted [1].

**3.** (a) editorial [1] (b) columnist [1] (c) defamation [1] (d) investigative journalism [1] (e) political awareness [1]

**4.** (a) تنقید کی: with نے the verb agrees with تنقید, which is feminine [1] (b) پھیلتی ہیں: agrees with خبریں [1] (c) ضروری ہے: the subject آزادیٔ صحافت is singular [1]

**5.** Candidates research different places (Pakistan or one Urdu-speaking area of India) [1], so the text must be equally relevant to all of them, leaving the specific evidence to each candidate's research [1].

**6.** Awareness is knowing about political events and issues [1]; understanding is judging evidence and arguments well [1]; participation is acting, for example voting or campaigning [1]. Social media may increase awareness most, because disinformation and echo chambers can limit understanding [1].

**7.** Model: "A journalist's job is not only to report the news but also to question the powerful. When newspapers expose corruption in a government project, the public find out where their tax money is being spent."

Marking: a journalist's job [1]; is not only to report the news [1]; but also to question [1]; the powerful [1]; when newspapers expose [1]; corruption in a government project [1]; the public find out [1]; where their tax money is being spent [1].

**8.** One mark per heading completed with a relevant, accurate entry, to [4]. Example: **مرکزی نکتہ:** قانون آزادیٔ اظہار کی ضمانت دیتا ہے مگر اس پر پابندیاں بھی لگاتا ہے۔ **ثبوت:** [source, date]: ایک چینل کی نشریات عارضی طور پر معطل کی گئیں۔ **دوسری رائے:** حکام کا کہنا ہے کہ یہ اقدام نفرت انگیز مواد روکنے کے لیے تھا۔ **میرا نتیجہ:** پابندیاں واضح اصولوں کے تحت اور عدالت کی نگرانی میں ہونی چاہییں۔

**9.** For, any two: young people follow and discuss politics on their phones; citizens record and share events, holding officials to account; campaigns spread quickly; politicians speak directly to voters [2]. Complicating, any two: disinformation and rumours; echo chambers and polarisation; abuse and intimidation of journalists or activists; restrictions on platforms [2].

**10.** Improved evaluation, for example: **متن میں سوشل میڈیا کے مثبت اور منفی دونوں پہلوؤں کا متوازن جائزہ لیا گیا ہے** [1], with precise vocabulary and correct passive agreement [1]. Research sentence, for example: **میری تحقیق کے مطابق انتخابات کے دوران بہت سی غلط خبریں سوشل میڈیا ہی سے پھیلیں** [1], clearly signalled as research and about one area only [1].

**11.** Marked on three levels-based grids, applied best-fit to the whole response: AO4, knowledge and understanding of society and culture through research [20]; AO2, understanding of and response to the unseen text [10]; AO3, accuracy and range of language [10]. Total 40.

**Indicative content.**

- **From the text (AO2):** newspaper reading as a morning habit; politicians judging opinion through editorials and columns; young readers moving to short videos and posts; falling circulation; the view that print has lost its grip; the counter-view that much social media news originates in newspaper reports, so influence has changed route; the point that newspapers check news before publication while social media often skips this step.
- **From research (AO4), on one area only:** a named case where a newspaper report had a political effect (an inquiry, a resignation, a court case); the role of columnists and editorials in the researched area; newspapers' move online; examples of social media spreading unverified political news; any regulation or restriction affecting print or online media there.
- **Evaluation:** strong answers reject the word **ختم** as too absolute, arguing that print's influence has shifted rather than ended, or argue the opposite with evidence. The conclusion should answer the statement directly and follow from the paragraphs.

**Model-answer outline (in Urdu):** opening that sets out the text's two views; paragraph 1 on decline in readership (text plus research); paragraph 2 on newspapers' reports feeding social media and setting the agenda (research example); paragraph 3 on verification as print's continuing strength; conclusion: **اثر ختم نہیں ہوا، اس کا راستہ بدلا ہے**.
