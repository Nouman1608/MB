## Section A — Vocabulary and grammar

**1.** Give the Urdu for: subject (school), timetable, college, doctor, ambition. **[5]**

**2.** Complete each sentence with the correct future-tense or "would like to" form:

**(a)** میں کالج میں اپنی تعلیم ______ (جاری رکھنا — will continue) **[2]**
**(b)** میں ڈاکٹر ______ (بننا — would like to become) کیونکہ میں لوگوں کی مدد کرنا چاہتا ہوں۔ **[2]**

**3.** Rewrite as a conditional sentence: "میں محنت کروں گا۔ میں کامیاب ہو جاؤں گا۔" (join using
"اگر...تو..."). **[3]**

## Section B — Comprehension and writing

**4.** Read the short passage (in the style of a Theme 3 reading task), then answer in English:

> "میرا پسندیدہ مضمون سائنس ہے کیونکہ مجھے تجربات کرنا پسند ہے۔ اسکول کے بعد، میں کالج میں سائنس پڑھوں
> گا۔ میں ڈاکٹر بننا چاہتا ہوں کیونکہ میں لوگوں کی مدد کرنا چاہتا ہوں۔ اگر میں محنت کروں تو مجھے یقین
> ہے کہ میں اپنا مقصد حاصل کر لوں گا۔"

**(a)** What is the writer's favourite subject, and what reason do they give? **[2]**
**(b)** What is the writer's post-16 plan? **[2]**
**(c)** Identify the writer's career ambition, its justification, and the conditional statement at the
end. **[3]**

**5.** Write approximately 90 words in Urdu about your current studies and your future plans, naming a
favourite subject with a reason, a post-16 plan, and a career ambition with justification. **[15]**

## Section C — School life and comparisons

**6.** Translate into English: "میرے نئے اسکول میں لائبریری اور کھیل کا میدان دونوں پرانے اسکول سے
بہتر ہیں، لیکن یونیفارم کے قوانین زیادہ سخت ہیں۔" **[5]**

**7.** Explain why this sentence belongs to sub-topic 3.2 (Life at School/College) rather than 3.1 (My
Studies), and identify the comparative structure used. **[3]**

**8.** A classmate writes: "میں مستقبل میں انجینئر بننا چاہتا ہوں۔" and stops there. Explain what is
missing from this response for full justification marks, and rewrite it to include the missing
element. **[3]**

## Worked answers

**1.** مضمون (subject) **[1]**، ٹائم ٹیبل (timetable) **[1]**، کالج (college) **[1]**، ڈاکٹر (doctor)
**[1]**، مقصد/عزم (ambition) **[1]**.

**2. (a)** میں کالج میں اپنی تعلیم **جاری رکھوں گا** **[2]** (future tense, correct verb agreement).
**(b)** میں ڈاکٹر **بننا چاہتا ہوں** کیونکہ میں لوگوں کی مدد کرنا چاہتا ہوں۔ **[2]**

**3.** اگر میں محنت کروں تو میں کامیاب ہو جاؤں گا۔ ("If I work hard, I will succeed.") **[3]** (correct
conditional joining, matching verb forms in both clauses).

**4. (a)** Science, because the writer enjoys doing experiments **[2]**.
**(b)** The writer will continue studying science at college after school **[2]**.
**(c)** Ambition: to become a doctor **[1]**; justification: because the writer wants to help people
**[1]**; conditional statement: if the writer works hard, they believe they will achieve their goal
**[1]**.

**5.** Full marks require: a named favourite subject with a reason introduced by "کیونکہ" **[3–4]**; a
clearly stated post-16 plan in the future tense **[3–4]**; a career ambition using "بننا چاہتا
ہوں/چاہتی ہوں" with a justification **[4–5]**; and ideally a closing conditional statement linking
effort to the outcome **[2–3]**. Marks are lost for naming a subject or job with no reason attached,
or for describing current studies only without ever moving into the future tense. A response that
completes all elements in a natural sequence — present-tense subject and reason, future-tense plan,
career ambition with justification — closely matches the assessment objectives for this task type.

**6.** "At my new school, both the library and the sports field are better than at my old school
**[3]**, but the uniform rules are stricter **[2]**." (One mark each for the two correctly rendered
comparatives, one for correct sense overall, two for "stricter" and overall fluency.)

**7.** The sentence belongs to 3.2 because it concerns school facilities (library, sports field) and
rules (uniform), not subject content or opinions on subjects, which would place it in 3.1 **[2]**. The
comparative structure used is "بہتر ہیں" (are better) and "زیادہ سخت ہیں" (are stricter), comparing the
new school directly against the old one **[1]**.

**8.** The response names a career ambition (to become an engineer) but gives no reason or
justification, which is what examiners specifically reward beyond a bare statement **[1]**. A stronger
version adds a reason with "کیونکہ": "میں مستقبل میں انجینئر بننا چاہتا ہوں کیونکہ مجھے ریاضی اور
مسائل حل کرنا پسند ہے۔" ("I would like to become an engineer in future because I like maths and
solving problems.") **[2]**

## A note on marking approach

As with Theme 2, examiners credit communication of meaning ahead of grammatical perfection: a response
that includes all four required elements (subject/reason, post-16 plan, career ambition/justification,
and ideally a conditional) with a minor verb-agreement slip scores higher than a technically accurate
response that only describes current studies and never reaches the future-tense material this theme
specifically tests. When self-marking a practice response, check first whether every required element
is present, then check accuracy — a missing career ambition or an all-present-tense answer costs more
than a handful of small grammar errors spread across an otherwise complete response. This applies equally across all four sub-topics of the theme, so a self-marking checklist covering subject-plus-reason, post-16 plan, career ambition-plus-justification, and a comparative or conditional structure is a more reliable revision habit than proofreading for spelling alone.
