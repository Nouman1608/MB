## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Point | A position with no size |
| Line | Straight, endless in both directions |
| Line segment | Part of a line between two end points |
| Vertex (plural vertices) | A corner of a shape or solid |
| Edge | Where two faces of a solid meet, or a side of a 2D shape |
| Plane | A flat surface extending for ever |
| Parallel | Always the same distance apart; never meet (∥) |
| Perpendicular | Meeting at a right angle (⊥) |
| Face | A flat surface of a solid |
| Surface | Any outer part of a solid, flat or curved |
| Regular polygon | All sides equal and all angles equal |

## Diagram marks

- Small square: right angle.
- Arrows: parallel lines (single arrows for one pair, double for a second pair).
- Dashes: equal lengths (single and double dashes for different pairs).
- Arcs: equal angles.
- "Not drawn accurately": do not measure; reason from facts.

## Angle conventions

| Type | Size |
|---|---|
| Acute | 0° < angle < 90° |
| Right | 90° |
| Obtuse | 90° < angle < 180° |
| Reflex | 180° < angle < 360° |

- Angle ABC: vertex B in the middle; same as angle CBA.
- One letter only when a single angle sits at that vertex.
- Reflex angle at a point = 360° − the angle inside.
- Triangle ABC: side a = BC (opposite A), b = CA, c = AB.

## Shape names

```
Vertices are listed in order round the shape.
ABCD: sides AB, BC, CD, DA; diagonals AC and BD.
```

| Triangle | Property |
|---|---|
| Equilateral | 3 equal sides, 3 angles of 60° |
| Isosceles | 2 equal sides, 2 equal base angles |
| Scalene | No equal sides, no equal angles |

| Quadrilateral | Defining idea | Also counts as |
|---|---|---|
| Square | 4 equal sides, 4 right angles | rectangle, rhombus, parallelogram, kite |
| Rectangle | 4 right angles | parallelogram |
| Rhombus | 4 equal sides | parallelogram, kite |
| Parallelogram | 2 pairs of parallel sides | |
| Kite | 2 pairs of equal adjacent sides | |
| Trapezium | a pair of parallel sides | |

Polygons: 5 sides pentagon, 6 hexagon, 8 octagon. Give the most specific name the facts allow.

## Solids

| Solid | Faces or surfaces | Edges | Vertices |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cube | 6 | 12 | 8 |
| Hexagonal prism | 8 | 18 | 12 |
| Square-based pyramid | 5 | 8 | 5 |
| Cylinder | 2 flat, 1 curved | 2 curved | 0 |
| Cone | 1 flat, 1 curved | 1 curved | 1 (apex) |
| Sphere | 1 curved | 0 | 0 |

## Instruments

- Ruler: from zero; nearest millimetre.
- Protractor: centre on the vertex; base line on one arm; read the scale starting at 0° on that arm; sanity-check acute or obtuse.
- Compasses: fixed radius; leave arcs visible.
- Reflex angle x: draw 360° − x and mark the outside.

## Coordinates

- (x, y): across, then up.
- Horizontal length = difference in x; vertical length = difference in y.
- Midpoint = average of the x-coordinates, average of the y-coordinates.
- Equal "steps" (same across and same up) mean parallel and equal segments.
- Reflection in the x-axis: (x, y) → (x, −y). In the y-axis: (x, y) → (−x, y). In y = x: (x, y) → (y, x).
- Reflections, rotations and translations keep lengths, angles and area.

## Exam traps

- Vertex letter not in the middle of an angle name.
- Letters out of order, so a diagonal is taken as a side.
- Calling a rhombus or rectangle regular.
- Measuring a diagram labelled "not drawn accurately".
- Assuming lines are parallel or perpendicular without a mark or a stated fact.
- Reading the outer protractor scale instead of the inner one (or the reverse).
- Writing (y, x) instead of (x, y).

## Quick self-test

1. Angle PQR = 128°. Which letter is the vertex, and what type of angle is it?
2. Find the reflex angle at Q in question 1.
3. In triangle XYZ, which side is called y?
4. In rectangle KLMN, name the two diagonals.
5. In parallelogram PQRS, which side is parallel to QR?
6. A quadrilateral has four equal sides and no right angles. Give its most specific name.
7. Is a rectangle that is not a square a regular polygon? Give a reason.
8. How many faces, edges and vertices has a hexagonal prism?
9. Which solid has one flat face, one curved surface and one vertex?
10. A(2, 3) and B(2, 9). Find the length AB and the midpoint of AB.
11. Reflect (5, −2) in the line y = x.
12. Which marks on a diagram show that two sides are equal?

### Answers

1. Q; obtuse
2. 360° − 128° = 232°
3. ZX (the side opposite angle Y)
4. KM and LN
5. SP (equivalently PS)
6. Rhombus
7. No: its angles are equal but its sides are not all equal
8. 8 faces, 18 edges, 12 vertices
9. Cone
10. 6 units; (2, 6)
11. (−2, 5)
12. Matching dashes (hatch marks) on the two sides
