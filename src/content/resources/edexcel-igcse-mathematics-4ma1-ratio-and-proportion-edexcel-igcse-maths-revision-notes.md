These notes condense **section 1.7, Ratio and proportion**, of the Pearson Edexcel International GCSE Mathematics A (4MA1) specification, Issue 2. It is Foundation Tier content that the Higher Tier also assumes, so it applies to both tiers. For explanations and worked examples, use the [section 1.7 study guide](/resources/edexcel-igcse-mathematics-4ma1-ratio-and-proportion-edexcel-igcse-maths/).

## Condensed recall: ratio notation (1.7 A)

- **Simplify:** same units, then divide by the HCF. 40 min : 2 h = 40 : 120 = 1 : 3.
- **Remove decimals or fractions:** multiply every part by the same number. 1.2 : 0.8 = 12 : 8 = 3 : 2; 2/5 : 1/4 = 8 : 5.
- **1 : n:** divide both parts by the first. 5 : 13 = 1 : 2.6.
- **Ratio → fraction:** in a : b, first share = a/(a + b) of the total.
- **Fraction → ratio:** if 3/8 are adults, adults : children = 3 : 5.

## Condensed recall: sharing (1.7 B)

| You are given | Divide it by | Example (ratio 4 : 7) |
|---|---|---|
| The total | total parts (11) | total 66 → 1 part = 6 → 24 and 42 |
| One share | that share's parts | larger is 56 → 1 part = 8 → smaller 32 |
| The difference | difference in parts (3) | difference 18 → 1 part = 6 → 24 and 42 |

- Three-part ratios work the same way: 2 : 3 : 5 has 10 parts.
- **Combine** a : b and b : c by scaling until the shared middle term matches.
- **Changing ratio:** let one part = k, write the new ratio as an equation, solve for k.

## Condensed recall: proportion (1.7 C, D)

- **Direct proportion:** the ratio of the two quantities is constant; one doubles when the other doubles.
- **Unitary method:** find one unit, then multiply.
- **Table:** find k = y ÷ x from a full pair; then y = kx or x = y ÷ k.
- **Best buy:** compare the price of the same amount (per item, per 100 g, per litre).
- **Recipes:** for "how many can be made", divide each available amount by the amount per item and take the smallest whole number.

## Condensed recall: maps and scales (1.7 E)

- 1 : n means real = map × n (in the same units).
- 1 m = 100 cm; 1 km = 100 000 cm.
- 1 : 50 000 → 1 cm represents 500 m; 1 : 25 000 → 1 cm represents 250 m.

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Ratio | A comparison of two or more quantities, written a : b |
| Simplest form | Whole-number parts with no common factor other than 1 |
| Part | One unit of a ratio; a : b has a + b parts |
| Unitary method | Finding the value for one item, then scaling up |
| Direct proportion | Two quantities whose ratio stays constant |
| Constant of proportionality | The fixed value k in y = kx |
| Scale | A ratio linking a map or drawing length to the real length |
| Scale diagram | A drawing in which every length is reduced by the same ratio |

## Exam traps

- **Total parts, not one part.** Sharing £90 in 2 : 7: divide by 9, not by 2 or 7.
- **Order matters.** 3 : 5 for boys : girls is not girls : boys.
- **Units before simplifying.** 50 cm : 2 m is 1 : 4, not 25 : 1.
- **Fraction of what?** In 4 : 7, the first is 4/11 of the total but 4/7 of the second.
- **Combine properly.** a : b = 2 : 3 and b : c = 4 : 5 give 8 : 12 : 15, not 2 : 3 : 5.
- **Map units.** 3 cm at 1 : 50 000 is 150 000 cm = 1.5 km, not 150 km.
- **Recipe limits.** Round down: you cannot make 32.6 biscuits.

## Self-test

1. Simplify 1.8 litres : 450 ml.
2. Write 6 : 45 in the form 1 : n.
3. Simplify 3/4 : 5/6.
4. Share 84 sweets in the ratio 4 : 3.
5. A club has boys and girls in the ratio 5 : 4. There are 63 members. How many are girls?
6. In a ratio 9 : 4, the difference between the two quantities is 35. Find both quantities.
7. p : q = 5 : 2 and q : r = 3 : 4. Find p : q : r.
8. What fraction of a 2 : 3 : 7 mixture is the third ingredient?
9. Five notebooks cost £7.15. Find the cost of 8.
10. y varies directly as x, and y = 9 when x = 6. Find y when x = 14.
11. On a 1 : 250 000 map, two towns are 6.8 cm apart. Find the real distance in km.
12. A wall 4.5 m long is drawn at a scale of 1 : 30. How long is it on the drawing?

**Answers**

1. 1800 : 450 = 4 : 1.
2. 1 : 7.5.
3. 9/12 : 10/12 = 9 : 10.
4. 7 parts, 1 part = 12: 48 and 36.
5. 9 parts, 1 part = 7: 4 × 7 = 28 girls.
6. Difference 5 parts = 35, so 1 part = 7: 63 and 28.
7. 15 : 6 and 6 : 8, so 15 : 6 : 8.
8. 7/12.
9. 1 notebook = £1.43; 8 cost £11.44.
10. k = 9 ÷ 6 = 1.5, so y = 1.5 × 14 = 21.
11. 6.8 × 250 000 = 1 700 000 cm = 17 km.
12. 450 cm ÷ 30 = 15 cm.
