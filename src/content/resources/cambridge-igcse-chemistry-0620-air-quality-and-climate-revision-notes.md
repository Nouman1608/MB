**Syllabus:** Cambridge IGCSE Chemistry 0620, 2026–2028, subtopic 10.3 Air quality and climate.

## Clean, dry air

| Gas | Approximate % |
|---|---|
| nitrogen, N₂ | 78 |
| oxygen, O₂ | 21 |
| noble gases and carbon dioxide | the remainder |

**Oxygen experiment:** pass air over hot copper; the copper removes the oxygen (forming black CuO); volume falls by about 21 %. Let the gas cool before reading.

## Pollutants

| Pollutant | Source | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| CO₂ | complete combustion of carbon fuels | global warming → climate change |
| CO | incomplete combustion of carbon fuels | toxic |
| particulates | incomplete combustion of carbon fuels | respiratory problems; cancer risk |
| CH₄ | decomposing vegetation; animal digestion | global warming → climate change |
| oxides of nitrogen | car engines | acid rain; photochemical smog; respiratory problems |
| SO₂ | burning fossil fuels containing sulfur compounds | acid rain |

**Quick pairs:**

- Acid rain: SO₂ and oxides of nitrogen.
- Global warming: CO₂ and CH₄.
- Incomplete combustion: CO and particulates.

## Strategies

**Climate change:**

- plant trees → photosynthesis removes CO₂;
- less livestock farming → less CH₄;
- fewer fossil fuels → less CO₂;
- more hydrogen → burns to water only (2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O);
- more renewable energy (wind, solar) → no fuel burned.

**Acid rain:**

- catalytic converters → remove oxides of nitrogen;
- low-sulfur fuels → less SO₂ formed;
- flue gas desulfurisation → calcium oxide (basic) reacts with SO₂ (acidic): CaO + SO₂ → CaSO₃.

## Photosynthesis

- Carbon dioxide + water → glucose + oxygen.
- Needs **chlorophyll** and **light energy** (conditions, not reactants).
- *(Extended)* 6CO₂ + 6H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂.

## Greenhouse effect *(Extended)*

1. Some of the Sun's energy is reflected; the rest is absorbed by the surface.
2. The surface emits thermal energy.
3. CO₂ and CH₄ absorb some of this thermal energy…
4. …and re-emit it in all directions, including back to Earth.
5. Thermal energy loss to space is reduced → global warming.

## Oxides of nitrogen *(Extended)*

- Formed in engines: **high temperature** makes N₂ and O₂ from the air react: N₂ + O₂ → 2NO.
- Removed by catalytic converter: **2CO + 2NO → 2CO₂ + N₂**.
- NO is reduced (loses O); CO is oxidised (gains O).

## Answer frames

**"State the source of X" (1 mark):** use the syllabus wording, including "complete" or "incomplete" for combustion products.

**"Explain a strategy" (2 marks):** name it, then give the chemistry. "Low-sulfur fuels, because less sulfur dioxide forms when they burn."

**"Describe how CO₂ causes global warming" (Extended, about 4 marks):** absorbed by surface → thermal energy emitted → absorbed and re-emitted by CO₂ → less lost to space.

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Complete combustion | burning with enough oxygen; carbon in the fuel becomes CO₂ |
| Incomplete combustion | burning with too little oxygen; CO and/or particulates form |
| Particulates | tiny solid particles, such as soot, in the air |
| Acid rain | rain made acidic by dissolved SO₂ and oxides of nitrogen |
| Photochemical smog | haze formed from pollutants, including oxides of nitrogen, in sunlight |
| Greenhouse gas | a gas that absorbs and re-emits thermal energy, such as CO₂ or CH₄ |
| Flue gas desulfurisation | removing SO₂ from power station waste gases using calcium oxide |
| Catalytic converter | device in a car exhaust with a catalyst that converts harmful gases into less harmful ones |

## Exam traps

- **Remainder of air** = noble gases **and** CO₂, not "1 % CO₂".
- **CO₂ effect** = global warming leading to climate change, **not** acid rain.
- **Methane source** = vegetation and digestion, **not** cars.
- **Catalytic converters produce CO₂;** they remove CO and NO.
- **Strategy + reason** for every "explain" mark.
- *(Extended)* Use **absorb**, **re-emit** and **loss to space**, not just "trap heat".
- *(Extended)* Oxides of nitrogen need **high temperature**; nitrogen comes from the **air**, not the fuel.

## Self-test

1. State the approximate percentages of nitrogen and oxygen in clean, dry air.
2. Name **two** pollutants produced by incomplete combustion of a carbon fuel.
3. State the source of sulfur dioxide in the air.
4. State **one** adverse effect of particulates.
5. Name **two** sources of methane.
6. Explain how planting trees helps to reduce climate change.
7. Explain why using hydrogen as a fuel reduces carbon dioxide emissions.
8. Write the word equation for photosynthesis.
9. *(Extended)* Write the balanced symbol equation for photosynthesis.
10. *(Extended)* Explain how oxides of nitrogen form in a car engine.
11. *(Extended)* Identify the substance that is reduced in 2CO + 2NO → 2CO₂ + N₂.
12. *(Extended)* Explain how greenhouse gases reduce thermal energy loss to space.

**Answers:**

1. Nitrogen 78 %; oxygen 21 %.
2. Carbon monoxide; particulates.
3. Combustion of fossil fuels that contain sulfur compounds.
4. Increased risk of respiratory problems or cancer.
5. Decomposition of vegetation; waste gases from digestion in animals.
6. Trees take in carbon dioxide for photosynthesis, removing it from the air.
7. Hydrogen burns to form only water, so no carbon dioxide is produced.
8. carbon dioxide + water → glucose + oxygen.
9. 6CO₂ + 6H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂.
10. At the high temperature in the engine, nitrogen and oxygen from the air react to form nitrogen monoxide.
11. Nitrogen monoxide, NO (it loses oxygen).
12. They absorb thermal energy emitted by the Earth's surface and re-emit it in all directions, including back to Earth, so less escapes to space.
