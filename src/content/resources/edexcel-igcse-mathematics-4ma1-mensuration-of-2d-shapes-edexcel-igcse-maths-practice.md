## Section A

**1.** Convert

**(a)** 4.2 m² to cm², **[1]**

**(b)** 65 000 mm² to cm², **[1]**

**(c)** 0.0035 km² to m². **[1]**

**2.** A rectangular floor measures 6.4 m by 3.5 m. It is covered with square tiles, each of side 40 cm. Work out the number of tiles needed. Assume no tiles are cut or wasted. **[3]**

**3.** A shape is made by cutting a rectangle 6 cm by 4 cm from one corner of a rectangle 15 cm by 10 cm.

**(a)** Work out the perimeter of the shape. **[2]**

**(b)** Work out the area of the shape. **[2]**

**4.** A triangle has an area of 54 cm² and a base of 12 cm. Work out its perpendicular height. **[2]**

**5.** A parallelogram has a base of 15 cm, a perpendicular height of 8.4 cm and a slanting side of 10 cm.

**(a)** Work out its area. **[2]**

**(b)** Work out its perimeter. **[1]**

## Section B

**6.** **(a)** A trapezium has parallel sides of 9.5 cm and 14.5 cm. The distance between them is 6 cm. Work out the area. **[2]**

**(b)** Another trapezium has an area of 91 cm². Its height is 7 cm and one parallel side is 10 cm. Work out the length of the other parallel side. **[3]**

**7.** A circle has a diameter of 18 cm. Work out, to 3 significant figures,

**(a)** the circumference, **[2]**

**(b)** the area. **[2]**

**8.** A bicycle wheel has a diameter of 66 cm. Work out how many complete turns the wheel makes when the bicycle travels 1 km. **[3]**

**9.** A semicircle has a diameter of 14 cm. Work out, to 3 significant figures,

**(a)** its perimeter, **[3]**

**(b)** its area. **[2]**

**10.** A window is a rectangle 1.2 m wide and 1.5 m high with a semicircle on top. The diameter of the semicircle is the 1.2 m width of the rectangle.

**(a)** Work out the area of the window, to 3 significant figures. **[3]**

**(b)** Work out the total length of the frame around the outside of the window, to 3 significant figures. **[3]**

**11.** A circular pond has a radius of 4 m. A path of width 1.5 m goes all the way round the pond. Work out the area of the path, to 3 significant figures. **[3]**

**12.** (Higher tier only) A sector of a circle has a radius of 12 cm and an angle of 75°. Work out, to 3 significant figures,

**(a)** the arc length, **[2]**

**(b)** the area of the sector. **[2]**

**13.** (Higher tier only)

**(a)** An arc of length 22 cm is cut from a circle of radius 9 cm. Work out the angle of the sector, to 1 decimal place. **[3]**

**(b)** A sector with an angle of 100° has an area of 40 cm². Work out the radius of the circle, to 3 significant figures. **[3]**

## Answers

**1. (a)** 4.2 × 10 000 = **42 000 cm²** [1].

**(b)** 65 000 ÷ 100 = **650 cm²** [1].

**(c)** 0.0035 × 1 000 000 = **3500 m²** [1].

**2.** Floor area = 6.4 × 3.5 = 22.4 m² [1]. Tile area = 0.4 × 0.4 = 0.16 m² (or work in cm²: 640 × 350 = 224 000 and 40 × 40 = 1600) [1]. Number of tiles = 22.4 ÷ 0.16 = **140** [1].

**3. (a)** The cut-out is at a corner, so the perimeter equals that of the large rectangle [1]: 2 × (15 + 10) = **50 cm** [1].

**(b)** 15 × 10 − 6 × 4 = 150 − 24 [1] = **126 cm²** [1].

**4.** 54 = ½ × 12 × h [1], so 6h = 54 and h = **9 cm** [1].

**5. (a)** Area = base × perpendicular height = 15 × 8.4 [1] = **126 cm²** [1].

**(b)** Perimeter = 2 × (15 + 10) = **50 cm** [1].

**6. (a)** ½ × (9.5 + 14.5) × 6 [1] = ½ × 24 × 6 = **72 cm²** [1].

**(b)** 91 = ½ × (a + 10) × 7 [1]; a + 10 = 91 × 2 ÷ 7 = 26 [1]; a = **16 cm** [1].

**7. (a)** C = π × 18 [1] = 56.548... = **56.5 cm** [1].

**(b)** r = 9, so A = π × 9² = 81π [1] = 254.469... = **254 cm²** [1].

**8.** Circumference = π × 66 = 207.345... cm [1]. 1 km = 100 000 cm [1]. 100 000 ÷ 207.345... = 482.28..., so **482 complete turns** [1].

**9. (a)** r = 7. Curved edge = π × 7 = 21.991... [1]. Add the diameter, 14 [1]. Perimeter = 35.991... = **36.0 cm** [1].

**(b)** ½ × π × 7² = 24.5π [1] = 76.969... = **77.0 cm²** [1].

**10. (a)** Rectangle: 1.2 × 1.5 = 1.8 m² [1]. Semicircle, r = 0.6: ½ × π × 0.36 = 0.5654... m² [1]. Total = 2.3654... = **2.37 m²** [1].

**(b)** Two sides of 1.5 and the bottom of 1.2 give 4.2 m [1]. Curved top = π × 0.6 = 1.884... m [1]. Total = 6.084... = **6.08 m** [1]. The top edge of the rectangle is not part of the outside.

**11.** Outer radius = 4 + 1.5 = 5.5 m [1]. Path area = π × 5.5² − π × 4² = π × (30.25 − 16) [1] = 14.25π = 44.767... = **44.8 m²** [1].

**12. (a)** Arc length = 75/360 × 2 × π × 12 [1] = 15.707... = **15.7 cm** [1].

**(b)** Area = 75/360 × π × 12² [1] = 94.247... = **94.2 cm²** [1].

**13. (a)** 22 = θ/360 × 2 × π × 9 [1]; θ = 22 × 360 ÷ (18π) [1] = 140.056... = **140.1°** [1].

**(b)** 40 = 100/360 × π × r² [1]; r² = 40 × 360 ÷ (100π) = 45.836... [1]; r = **6.77 cm** [1].

## Where marks are usually lost

- Question 1(a) and 1(c): using 100 or 1000 instead of the squared factors 10 000 and 1 000 000.
- Question 2: dividing 22.4 m² by 40 (a length in cm) instead of the tile area.
- Question 3(a): subtracting the cut-out lengths from the perimeter. A corner cut leaves it unchanged.
- Question 5: using the slanting side 10 cm as the height in the area.
- Question 7(b): squaring the diameter, which gives four times the correct area.
- Question 9(a): leaving out the 14 cm diameter.
- Question 10(b): adding the top edge of the rectangle, which is inside the window shape.
- Question 13: rearranging the sector formula wrongly. Multiply by 360 and divide by the rest.

## Frequently asked questions

**Which of these questions test Higher Tier content only?**
Questions 12 and 13, which use arc length and sector area. Questions 1 to 11 test content that both tiers cover.

**Should I use the π button or 3.14?**
Use the π button on your calculator and round only at the end. If a question asks for an answer in terms of π, leave π in the answer.
