Condensed notes for subtopic **1.8 Standard form** of Cambridge O Level Mathematics (Syllabus D) 4024, 2025 to 2027. The [Standard form study guide](/resources/cambridge-o-level-mathematics-4024-standard-form/) explains each method with worked examples; the [Standard form practice questions](/resources/cambridge-o-level-mathematics-4024-standard-form-practice/) give exam-style questions with mark schemes.

## Syllabus outcomes

1. Use the standard form A × 10ⁿ where n is a positive or negative integer and 1 ⩽ A < 10.
2. Convert numbers into and out of standard form.
3. Calculate with values in standard form.

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Standard form | A × 10ⁿ with 1 ⩽ A < 10 and n an integer | 4.5 × 10⁵ |
| Front number (A) | number between 1 and 10 | 4.5 |
| Power of 10 | tells the size | 10⁵ = 100 000 |
| Significant figures | digits that count from the first non-zero digit | 3.15 × 10⁷ has 3 |

## Converting into standard form

| Number | Steps | Standard form |
|---|---|---|
| 450 000 | point after 4; 5 places left | 4.5 × 10⁵ |
| 0.00072 | point after 7; 4 places right | 7.2 × 10⁻⁴ |
| 6 000 000 000 | point after 6; 9 places | 6 × 10⁹ |
| 83 | 1 place | 8.3 × 10¹ |
| 0.5 | 1 place right | 5 × 10⁻¹ |
| 12.6 million | 12 600 000 | 1.26 × 10⁷ |

Rule of thumb: numbers of 10 or more have a positive n; numbers below 1 have a negative n; numbers from 1 up to 10 have n = 0.

## Converting out of standard form

| Standard form | Ordinary number |
|---|---|
| 3.8 × 10⁶ | 3 800 000 |
| 5.1 × 10⁻³ | 0.0051 |
| 2.04 × 10² | 204 |
| 9 × 10⁻¹ | 0.9 |
| 1.2 × 10⁻⁵ | 0.000012 |

## Fixing numbers that are nearly in standard form

| Given | Fix | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 45 × 10³ | 45 = 4.5 × 10 | 4.5 × 10⁴ |
| 0.62 × 10⁵ | 0.62 = 6.2 × 10⁻¹ | 6.2 × 10⁴ |
| 30 × 10⁹ | 30 = 3 × 10 | 3 × 10¹⁰ |
| 0.7 × 10⁻⁸ | 0.7 = 7 × 10⁻¹ | 7 × 10⁻⁹ |

## Calculating

| Operation | Method | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Multiply | multiply fronts, add powers | (3 × 10⁴)(2.5 × 10³) = 7.5 × 10⁷ |
| Divide | divide fronts, subtract powers | (9.6 × 10⁷) ÷ (1.2 × 10³) = 8 × 10⁴ |
| Add or subtract | match powers (or use ordinary numbers) | 3.2 × 10⁵ + 4.5 × 10⁴ = 3.65 × 10⁵ |
| Power | power the front, multiply the index | (2.5 × 10⁶)² = 6.25 × 10¹² |
| Square root | root the front, halve the index | √(1.6 × 10⁷) = 4 × 10³ |

After every calculation, check that 1 ⩽ A < 10.

### Adjust after multiplying and dividing

```
(6 x 10^5)(5 x 10^4) = 30 x 10^9 = 3 x 10^10
(4.2 x 10^-3) ÷ (6 x 10^5) = 0.7 x 10^-8 = 7 x 10^-9
```

### Subtracting

```
6.1 x 10^-3 - 2.4 x 10^-4
= 0.0061 - 0.00024
= 0.00586
= 5.86 x 10^-3
```

## Comparing numbers in standard form

Compare the powers of 10 first, then the front numbers.

| Pair | Larger |
|---|---|
| 7.2 × 10⁻³ and 8.1 × 10⁻⁴ | 7.2 × 10⁻³ (power -3 > -4) |
| 6.1 × 10⁴ and 5.9 × 10⁴ | 6.1 × 10⁴ (same power; 6.1 > 5.9) |
| 9.8 × 10³ and 1.1 × 10⁴ | 1.1 × 10⁴ |

## Context facts and formulas

| Situation | Relationship |
|---|---|
| Time | distance ÷ speed |
| Number of items | total ÷ size of one |
| Light from the Sun | 1.5 × 10¹¹ ÷ 3 × 10⁸ = 5 × 10² s |
| Seconds in 365 days | 365 × 24 × 3600 = 3.1536 × 10⁷ |

## Calculator (Paper 2)

- Use the EXP (or ×10ˣ) key. Do not type "×10" and then the power.
- Put brackets round a divisor in standard form.
- Write the final answer as A × 10ⁿ. Do not copy "E" notation from the display.
- Round A to the accuracy asked: 3 s.f. unless told otherwise.

Example: (6.7 × 10⁸) ÷ (3.2 × 10⁻⁵) = 2.09375 × 10¹³ = 2.09 × 10¹³ (3 s.f.).

## Exam traps

- 0.0051 is 5.1 × 10⁻³, not 5.1 × 10³.
- 30 × 10⁹ is not standard form; it is 3 × 10¹⁰.
- When dividing powers of 10, subtract the indices: 10⁻³ ÷ 10⁵ = 10⁻⁸.
- Do not add indices when adding numbers.
- Count zeros carefully in billions and millionths.
- Write 3.15 × 10⁷, not 3.15E7.
- Round the front number, not the whole number.
- Show the working line with the front numbers.

## Self-test

1. Write 7 200 000 in standard form.
2. Write 0.000 305 in standard form.
3. Write 4.6 × 10⁻² as an ordinary number.
4. Write 58 × 10⁴ in standard form.
5. Work out (4 × 10³) × (3 × 10⁵).
6. Work out (8 × 10⁹) ÷ (2 × 10⁴).
7. Work out (5 × 10⁻²) ÷ (4 × 10³), in standard form.
8. Work out 2.5 × 10⁶ + 3.5 × 10⁵.
9. Which is larger, 3.9 × 10⁻⁵ or 1.2 × 10⁻⁴?
10. A virus is 1.2 × 10⁻⁷ m long. How many in a row would be 6 × 10⁻³ m long?

**Answers**

1. **7.2 × 10⁶**.
2. **3.05 × 10⁻⁴**.
3. **0.046**.
4. **5.8 × 10⁵**.
5. 12 × 10⁸ = **1.2 × 10⁹**.
6. **4 × 10⁵**.
7. 5 ÷ 4 = 1.25 and 10⁻² ÷ 10³ = 10⁻⁵, so **1.25 × 10⁻⁵**.
8. 2 500 000 + 350 000 = 2 850 000 = **2.85 × 10⁶**.
9. **1.2 × 10⁻⁴**, because the power -4 is greater than -5.
10. 6 × 10⁻³ ÷ 1.2 × 10⁻⁷ = 5 × 10⁴, so **50 000** viruses.
