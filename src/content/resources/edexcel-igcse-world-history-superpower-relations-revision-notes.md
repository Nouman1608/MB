## The three conferences compared

| | Tehran (Nov–Dec 1943) | Yalta (Feb 1945) | Potsdam (July–Aug 1945) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Leaders | Churchill, Roosevelt, Stalin | Churchill, Roosevelt, Stalin | Churchill then Attlee, Truman, Stalin |
| Agreed | Second front in France; Poland's borders move west | Four zones of Germany and Berlin; free elections; UN; USSR to fight Japan | Germany demilitarised; zones confirmed; war-crimes trials |

## Crisis summaries: cause → events → outcome

- **Berlin 1948–49:** new Western currency → blockade → airlift → blockade lifted; NATO; two
  Germanys.
- **Hungary 1956:** de-Stalinisation and Nagy's reforms → Warsaw Pact withdrawal announced → Soviet
  invasion → Kádár installed; West does not act.
- **Berlin 1961:** refugee drain → Wall built → flow stopped; division symbolised.
- **Cuba 1962:** Bay of Pigs, Soviet missiles → quarantine and negotiation → missiles removed;
  Hotline and Test Ban follow.
- **Czechoslovakia 1968:** Prague Spring → invasion → Brezhnev Doctrine; Western protests only.

## Worked example: two effects

*Explain two effects of the Berlin Crisis of 1948–49.*

1. **The creation of NATO (1949):** the blockade convinced Western states that they needed a
   permanent military alliance, committing the USA to defending Western Europe.
2. **The division of Germany:** the FRG and GDR were set up in 1949, making the partition of Germany
   formal and turning Berlin into a long-term flashpoint.

## Common mistakes and exam traps

- **Mixing up Yalta and Potsdam.** Yalta = cooperation with Roosevelt; Potsdam = suspicion with
  Truman and the atomic bomb.
- **Calling the Marshall Plan military aid.** It was economic aid; the Truman Doctrine was the
  political commitment.
- **Saying the West helped Hungary.** It condemned the invasion but did not intervene.
- **Over-stating SALT I.** It limited future growth; it did not cut existing weapons.
- **Ending too late.** The depth study ends in 1972; Afghanistan and Reagan are outside it.

## Frequently asked questions

**Why did Stalin want control of Eastern Europe?**
As a buffer zone after two German invasions, and
to spread communism.

**What made the Cuban Missile Crisis end peacefully?**
Kennedy chose a quarantine over air strikes,
and both leaders compromised — including the secret deal on US missiles in Turkey.

**What did détente achieve by 1972?**
Treaties limiting testing, space weapons, proliferation and
strategic missiles — but no end to rivalry.
