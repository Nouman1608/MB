These questions cover section 3 of Unit P2 (YMA01, Issue 3): the coordinate geometry of the circle and its three properties. Revise first with the [P2 circle study guide](/resources/edexcel-a-level-mathematics-yma01-p2-coordinate-geometry/) and the [P2 circle revision notes](/resources/edexcel-a-level-mathematics-yma01-p2-coordinate-geometry-revision-notes/).

## Section A

**1.** Write down the coordinates of the centre and the exact radius of the circle (x + 3)² + (y − 7)² = 18. **[2]**

**2.** Find the centre and radius of the circle x² + y² + 8x − 2y − 8 = 0. **[3]**

**3.** A circle has centre (2, −5) and passes through the point (−1, −1). Find its equation. **[2]**

**4.** Find the set of values of k for which x² + y² − 6x + 4y + k = 0 is the equation of a circle. **[2]**

## Section B

**5.** The points A(−3, 2) and B(5, 8) are the ends of a diameter of the circle C.

**(a)** Find an equation for C. **[3]**

**(b)** Show that the point P(4, 1) lies on C. **[1]**

**(c)** State the size of angle APB, giving a reason. **[1]**

**6.** The circle C has equation (x − 3)² + (y + 1)² = 10 and centre Q.

**(a)** Verify that P(4, 2) lies on C. **[1]**

**(b)** Find an equation of the tangent to C at P, in the form ax + by + c = 0 where a, b and c are integers. **[4]**

**(c)** The tangent meets the x-axis at R. Find the coordinates of R. **[1]**

**(d)** Find the area of triangle QPR. **[2]**

**7.** The circle C has equation x² + y² − 4x − 6y − 12 = 0.

**(a)** Find the centre and radius of C. **[3]**

**(b)** The chord AB of C has midpoint M(5, 5). Find an equation of the line AB. **[3]**

**(c)** Find the exact length of AB. **[3]**

**8.** A circle passes through the points A(0, 4) and B(6, 4). Its centre lies on the line y = x − 1. Find an equation of the circle. **[4]**

**9.** Find the values of k for which the line y = 2x + k is a tangent to the circle x² + y² = 20. **[5]**

**10.** The circle C has equation (x + 2)² + (y − 1)² = 36 and T is the point (4, 9).

**(a)** Show that T lies outside C. **[1]**

**(b)** Find the length of a tangent from T to C. **[3]**

**11.** A circle has centre (−3, 4) and touches the x-axis.

**(a)** Write down an equation of the circle. **[2]**

**(b)** Find the exact y-coordinates of the points where the circle crosses the y-axis. **[3]**

**12.** Show that the line y = x + 6 does not meet the circle (x − 2)² + (y + 1)² = 9. **[4]**

## Answers

**1.** Centre **(−3, 7)** [1]; radius √18 = **3√2** [1].

**2.** (x + 4)² − 16 + (y − 1)² − 1 − 8 = 0 [1], so (x + 4)² + (y − 1)² = 25. Centre **(−4, 1)** [1], radius **5** [1].

**3.** r² = (−1 − 2)² + (−1 + 5)² = 9 + 16 = 25 [1], so **(x − 2)² + (y + 5)² = 25** [1].

**4.** (x − 3)² + (y + 2)² = 13 − k [1]. For a circle r² > 0, so **k < 13** [1].

**5. (a)** Centre = midpoint of AB = (1, 5) [1]. AB = √(8² + 6²) = 10, so r = 5 [1]. **(x − 1)² + (y − 5)² = 25** [1].

**(b)** (4 − 1)² + (1 − 5)² = 9 + 16 = 25, so P lies on C [1].

**(c)** **90°**, because AB is a diameter and the angle in a semicircle is a right angle [1].

Total: 5

**6. (a)** (4 − 3)² + (2 + 1)² = 1 + 9 = 10, so P lies on C [1].

**(b)** Q is (3, −1). Gradient of QP = (2 − (−1))/(4 − 3) = 3 [1]. The tangent is perpendicular to the radius, so its gradient is −⅓ [1]. y − 2 = −⅓(x − 4) [1], so 3y − 6 = −x + 4, giving **x + 3y − 10 = 0** [1].

**(c)** y = 0 gives x = 10: **R(10, 0)** [1].

**(d)** Angle QPR = 90° (radius ⟂ tangent). QP = √10 and PR = √((10 − 4)² + (0 − 2)²) = √40 = 2√10 [1]. Area = ½ × √10 × 2√10 = **10** [1].

Total: 8

**7. (a)** (x − 2)² − 4 + (y − 3)² − 9 − 12 = 0 [1], so (x − 2)² + (y − 3)² = 25. Centre **(2, 3)** [1], radius **5** [1].

**(b)** Let the centre be K(2, 3). Gradient of KM = (5 − 3)/(5 − 2) = ⅔ [1]. KM is perpendicular to AB, so the gradient of AB is −3/2 [1]. y − 5 = −3/2 (x − 5), so **3x + 2y − 25 = 0** [1].

**(c)** KM² = 3² + 2² = 13 [1]. AM² = 25 − 13 = 12, so AM = 2√3 [1]. **AB = 4√3** [1].

Total: 9

**8.** The perpendicular bisector of AB passes through the centre; AB is horizontal with midpoint (3, 4), so the bisector is x = 3 [1]. Meeting y = x − 1 gives the centre (3, 2) [1]. r² = (3 − 0)² + (2 − 4)² = 13 [1]. **(x − 3)² + (y − 2)² = 13** [1].

**9.** Substituting: x² + (2x + k)² = 20 [1], so 5x² + 4kx + k² − 20 = 0 [1]. For a tangent, the discriminant is zero: (4k)² − 4(5)(k² − 20) = 0 [1]. 16k² − 20k² + 400 = 0, so k² = 100 [1], and **k = 10 or k = −10** [1].

**10. (a)** The centre is K(−2, 1) and r = 6. KT² = 6² + 8² = 100, so KT = 10 > 6, and T is outside C [1].

**(b)** The tangent from T meets the radius at a right angle [1], so tangent length² = KT² − r² = 100 − 36 = 64 [1]. Length = **8** [1].

Total: 4

**11. (a)** The circle touches the x-axis, so r equals the distance from the centre to the axis, 4 [1]. **(x + 3)² + (y − 4)² = 16** [1].

**(b)** x = 0: 9 + (y − 4)² = 16 [1], so (y − 4)² = 7 [1] and **y = 4 ± √7** [1].

Total: 5

**12.** Substituting y = x + 6: (x − 2)² + (x + 7)² = 9 [1]. Expanding: 2x² + 10x + 53 = 9, so x² + 5x + 22 = 0 [1]. Discriminant = 25 − 88 = −63 [1]. This is negative, so there are no real solutions and the line does not meet the circle [1].

## Where marks are usually lost

- Giving the centre of (x + 3)² + (y − 7)² = 18 as (3, −7), or the radius as 18.
- In Question 6, using the radius gradient 3 as the tangent gradient.
- In Question 7(c), stopping at AM = 2√3 instead of doubling to find the whole chord.
- In Question 9, not expanding (2x + k)² fully, or setting the discriminant greater than zero instead of equal to zero.
- In Question 12, finding the discriminant but not stating what a negative value means for the line and the circle.
