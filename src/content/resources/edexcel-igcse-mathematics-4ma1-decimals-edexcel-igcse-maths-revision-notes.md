These notes condense **section 1.3, Decimals**, of the Pearson Edexcel International GCSE Mathematics A (4MA1) specification, Issue 2. Statements 1.3 A–E are for both tiers; converting recurring decimals into fractions is **Higher tier only**. For full explanations and worked examples, use the [section 1.3 study guide](/resources/edexcel-igcse-mathematics-4ma1-decimals-edexcel-igcse-maths/).

## Condensed recall: place value and ordering

- After the point: tenths (0.1), hundredths (0.01), thousandths (0.001), ten-thousandths (0.0001).
- In 7.4096 the 9 is worth 9 thousandths = 0.009.
- × 10ⁿ moves digits n places left; ÷ 10ⁿ moves them n places right.
- **Order:** pad with zeros to the same number of decimal places, then compare. 0.3 = 0.300 > 0.299.
- **Negatives:** the larger the size, the smaller the number. −1.25 < −1.2.

## Condensed recall: calculating

- **× decimals:** multiply as whole numbers; total decimal places in the question = decimal places in the answer. 1.2 × 0.05 = 0.060 = 0.06.
- **÷ decimals:** scale both numbers until the divisor is whole. 0.56 ÷ 0.007 = 560 ÷ 7 = 80.
- **Check:** × by a number between 0 and 1 makes smaller; ÷ by it makes bigger.
- **From a known fact:** 48 × 15 = 720, so 4.8 × 0.15 = 0.72 and 72 ÷ 1.5 = 48.

## Condensed recall: conversions

- **Decimal → percentage:** × 100. 0.075 = 7.5%; 1.4 = 140%.
- **Terminating decimal → fraction:** over 10, 100 or 1000 by the last place, then simplify. 0.36 = 36/100 = 9/25; 0.625 = 625/1000 = 5/8.
- Every terminating decimal is a fraction, and in simplest form its denominator has only 2s and 5s as prime factors.

## Condensed recall: recurring decimals (Higher tier only)

| Decimal | Multiply by | Subtract | Fraction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.7̇ = 0.777… | 10 | 10x − x = 7 | 7/9 |
| 0.4̇5̇ = 0.4545… | 100 | 100x − x = 45 | 45/99 = 5/11 |
| 0.1̇23̇ = 0.123123… | 1000 | 1000x − x = 123 | 123/999 = 41/333 |
| 0.36̇ = 0.3666… | 10 and 100 | 100x − 10x = 33 | 33/90 = 11/30 |

- Repeat starts right after the point: block over the same number of 9s.
- Non-repeating digit first: use two multiples (10x and 100x) whose tails match.
- "Prove" or "show that": write out the full subtraction.

## Worked recall

**Ordering with a mix of forms.** Order 0.38, 3/8 and 37.5%. Convert each to a decimal: 3/8 = 0.375 and 37.5% = 0.375, so 3/8 and 37.5% are equal and both are smaller than 0.38. Equal values can appear in an ordering list; write them side by side.

**Checking a decimal calculation.** 0.42 × 0.3 = 0.126. Estimate: 0.4 × 0.3 = 0.12, so 0.126 is sensible; 1.26 or 0.0126 would be out by a factor of 10.

**Dividing by a decimal.** 2.76 ÷ 0.12: multiply both by 100 to get 276 ÷ 12 = 23. Since 0.12 is less than 1, the answer is bigger than 2.76, as expected.

**Decimal to percentage and back.** 0.065 = 6.5% and 1.25 = 125%. Going the other way, 0.8% = 0.008: divide by 100 by moving the digits two places right.

**Why some fractions recur.** 1/8 = 0.125 terminates because 8 = 2³. 1/12 = 0.083̇ recurs because 12 = 2² × 3 contains the prime 3.

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Decimal point | Separates whole-number columns from fractional columns |
| Decimal place | A column after the point; 3.142 has three decimal places |
| Place holder | A zero that keeps other digits in their columns, as in 4.05 |
| Terminating decimal | A decimal that ends, such as 0.875 |
| Recurring decimal | A decimal with a block that repeats forever, such as 0.272727… |
| Dot notation | Dots over the first and last digits of the repeating block |
| Simplest form | A fraction cancelled until the HCF of top and bottom is 1 |

## Exam traps

- **Length is not size.** 0.199 < 0.2.
- **Decimal places when multiplying.** 0.4 × 0.4 = 0.16, not 1.6.
- **Thousandths.** 0.035 = 35/1000 = 7/200, not 35/100.
- **Percentages above 100.** 2.3 = 230%.
- **Wrong multiplier.** For a two-digit repeat, use 100x; for three digits, 1000x.
- **Unsimplified answers.** 45/99 must become 5/11 when "simplest form" is asked.

## Self-test

1. Write down the value of the 3 in 0.0739.
2. Order 0.45, 0.405, 0.054, 0.5, 0.045, smallest first.
3. Order −2.3, −2.03, −2.33, −2.303, smallest first.
4. Work out 0.08 × 0.6.
5. Work out 3.6 ÷ 0.009.
6. Given 26 × 35 = 910, write down 2.6 × 0.35.
7. Write 0.88 as a fraction in its simplest form.
8. Write 0.0125 as a percentage and as a fraction in its simplest form.
9. Explain why 0.7 is a fraction.
10. (Higher) Write 0.2̇ as a fraction.
11. (Higher) Write 0.5̇1̇ as a fraction in its simplest form.
12. (Higher) Write 0.73̇ as a fraction in its simplest form.

**Answers**

1. 3 thousandths, 0.003.
2. 0.045, 0.054, 0.405, 0.45, 0.5.
3. −2.33, −2.303, −2.3, −2.03.
4. 8 × 6 = 48 with three decimal places: 0.048.
5. 3600 ÷ 9 = 400.
6. 0.910 = 0.91.
7. 88/100 = 22/25.
8. 1.25%; 125/10 000 = 1/80.
9. 0.7 = 7/10, a whole number over a whole number.
10. 2/9.
11. 51/99 = 17/33.
12. 10x = 7.333…, 100x = 73.333…, 90x = 66, so x = 66/90 = 11/15.
