Pearson Edexcel A Level Urdu (9UR0) is a UK A Level for students who want
to develop their Urdu to an advanced level through the study of Pakistani society, culture, migration and politics,
and of prescribed literary works and films. Its full title describes it as
an A Level in Urdu (listening, reading and writing): speaking is not
assessed. It is a linear qualification, and all three papers are sat in the
same May/June series. Paper 1 lasts 150 minutes (80 marks, 40%), Paper 2
lasts 160 minutes (110 marks, 30%) and Paper 3 lasts 135 minutes (60 marks,
30%). The specification was first taught in September 2018 and first
assessed in May/June 2020.

Unlike the Pearson Edexcel International A Levels, 9UR0 is a UK-regulated
Advanced GCE and is not divided into AS and A2 units.

## How the course is organised

The content is built around four themes and a list of prescribed works.

- **Theme 1: پاکستانی معاشرے کا ارتقا** (evolving Pakistani society) covers
  خاندان (family), کام (work) and equal access to education in Pakistan.
- **Theme 2: اردو بولنے والی دُنیا میں تمدن اور فنونِ لطیفہ** (art and culture in
  the Urdu-speaking world) covers لوک رسوم و روایت (folk customs and
  traditions), مقبول کلچر (popular culture) and میڈیا (media).
- **Theme 3: (ہجرت ترکِ وطن) اور کثیر الثقافتی معاشرہ** (immigration and the
  multicultural society) covers the positive aspects of a diverse society,
  trends and attitudes towards migration, and migration from Pakistan.
- **Theme 4: پاکستانی سیاست کے رُخ** (aspects of Pakistani politics) covers
  قیامِ پاکستان (1947), the founding of Pakistan; جاگیرداری نظام, the feudal
  system; and measures to address environmental problems in Pakistan.
- **Prescribed literary texts and films**: the novel آنگن by خدیجہ مستور;
  essays from پطرس کے مضامین by احمد شاہ پطرس بخاری (سویرے جو کل آنکھ میری کھُلی؛
  اُردو کی آخری کتاب؛ مُرید پُور کا پیر؛ مرحُوم کی یاد میں؛ لاہور کا جغرافیہ); the
  play بساط by باصر کاظمی; and the films بن روئے (مومنہ دُرید), باغبان (روی چوپڑہ)
  and بجرنگی بھائی جان (کبیر خان).

The themes supply the topics for reading, listening, translation and
research; the prescribed works are studied in depth for the written response
in Paper 2.

## How it's assessed

All three papers are written exams, externally assessed and taken together.

| Paper | Title | Duration | Marks | Weighting |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 9UR0/01 | Paper 1: Translation into English, Reading Comprehension and Writing (Research Question) in Urdu | 150 minutes (2h30) | 80 | 40% |
| 9UR0/02 | Paper 2: Translation into Urdu and Written Response to Works | 160 minutes (2h40) | 110 | 30% |
| 9UR0/03 | Paper 3: Listening, Reading and Writing in Urdu | 135 minutes (2h15) | 60 | 30% |

Inside each paper the marks divide as follows.

- **Paper 1**: translation into English (20 marks), reading (20 marks) and a
  writing question based on independent research (40 marks).
- **Paper 2**: translation into Urdu (20 marks) and a written response either
  to two prescribed literary texts or to one text and one film (90 marks in
  total, across Sections B and C). The choice of works does not change the
  paper's marks or weighting.
- **Paper 3**: listening comprehension (30 marks) and a combined listening,
  reading and writing question (30 marks). This is the only paper with
  assessed listening.

Note that Paper 2 has the most marks but not the highest weighting: Paper 1,
with 80 marks, carries 40%, while Papers 2 and 3 carry 30% each.

## How the parts fit together

The course connects language skills and content at every point. The four
themes provide the vocabulary and cultural knowledge needed for the reading
and listening texts, for translation in both directions and for the research
question. Studying, for example, the debate on equal access to education or
the changing patterns of migration from Pakistan gives you both the language
and the ideas to write convincingly.

Translation runs in two directions across two papers: into English in Paper 1
and into Urdu in Paper 2. Together they test precise understanding of
grammar and vocabulary in both languages. The independent research question
in Paper 1 rewards sustained reading and research carried out over the
course, while the response to works in Paper 2 rewards close knowledge of the
prescribed texts and films and the ability to analyse them in well-organised
Urdu.

Listening is assessed only in Paper 3, but it supports everything else:
regular listening to Urdu media widens vocabulary and helps with the themes,
especially Theme 2's focus on popular culture and media.

## Planning your study

Because all papers are taken in a single series at the end of the course,
you can plan the two years as one programme. A sensible approach is to work
through two themes in the first year alongside the first of your chosen
works, then the remaining two themes and the second work in the second year.
Agree the focus of your independent research with your teacher in good
time, so that reading for it can build up steadily.

Practise translation in both directions every week, and keep a vocabulary
notebook organised by theme. Build listening into your routine from the
start rather than leaving it until the final term. Begin timed practice on
each paper once the themes and works have been covered, and use the
exam-preparation page for advice on dividing time within each paper.

## Frequently asked questions

**How many papers are in Pearson Edexcel A Level Urdu 9UR0?**
There are three papers: Paper 1 (150 minutes, 80 marks, 40%), Paper 2 (160 minutes, 110 marks, 30%) and Paper 3 (135 minutes, 60 marks, 30%). All are taken in the same May/June series.

**Is speaking assessed in A Level Urdu 9UR0?**
No. The qualification assesses listening, reading and writing. Speaking is not assessed.

**Is 9UR0 an International A Level?**
No. It is a UK-regulated Pearson Edexcel A Level (Advanced GCE), not part of the International Advanced Level suite, and it is not split into AS and A2 units.

**What do I write about in Paper 2?**
After a translation into Urdu (20 marks), you write a response either to two prescribed literary texts or to one text and one film, worth 90 marks in total.

**When was 9UR0 first assessed?**
It was first taught in September 2018 and first assessed in May/June 2020.
