## Section A

**1.** Let f(x) = 2(x − 3)² + 5. Write down:

**(a)** the coordinates of the vertex **[1]**
**(b)** the equation of the axis of symmetry **[1]**
**(c)** the y-intercept. **[1]**

**2.** Let f(x) = −(x + 4)(x − 2).

**(a)** Write down the x-intercepts. **[1]**
**(b)** Find the equation of the axis of symmetry. **[1]**
**(c)** Find the maximum value of f. **[1]**

**3.** Write x² − 10x + 18 in the form (x − h)² + k. **[2]**

**4.** Write 2x² + 12x + 11 in the form a(x − h)² + k. **[3]**

**5.** A parabola has vertex (2, −3) and passes through (4, 5).

**(a)** Find its equation in the form y = a(x − h)² + k. **[2]**
**(b)** Write the equation in the form y = ax² + bx + c. **[1]**

**6.** A quadratic function f has f(1) = f(7) and minimum value −2.

**(a)** Write down the equation of the axis of symmetry. **[1]**
**(b)** Write down the coordinates of the vertex. **[1]**

## Section B

**7.** Let f(x) = 3x² − 6x − 9.

**(a)** Write f(x) in the form a(x − p)(x − q). **[2]**
**(b)** Write f(x) in the form a(x − h)² + k. **[2]**
**(c)** Write down the x-intercepts, the y-intercept and the vertex of the graph. **[3]**

**8.** A parabola has x-intercepts (−3, 0) and (5, 0) and y-intercept (0, 30).

**(a)** Find its equation in factorised form. **[2]**
**(b)** Find the coordinates of its vertex. **[2]**
**(c)** Write down the range of the function. **[1]**

**9.** (calculator allowed) A ball's height in metres t seconds after it is thrown is h(t) = −4.9t² + 19.6t + 1.5.

**(a)** Write h(t) in the form a(t − p)² + q. **[2]**
**(b)** Write down the maximum height and the time at which it occurs. **[2]**
**(c)** Find the time at which the ball reaches the ground. **[2]**
**(d)** Find the length of time for which the ball is more than 15 m above the ground. **[2]**

**10.** In an invented model, a shop sells 600 − 25p items per day when the price is p dollars, for 0 ≤ p ≤ 24.

**(a)** Show that the daily revenue is R(p) = −25p² + 600p. **[1]**
**(b)** Find the price that maximises revenue, and the maximum revenue. **[3]**
**(c)** Find the prices that give a revenue of $2000. **[2]**

**11.** The graph of f(x) = x² + bx + c has vertex (−3, 4).

**(a)** Find b and c. **[3]**
**(b)** Describe the transformations that map the graph of y = x² onto the graph of y = f(x). **[2]**

**12.** The graph of a quadratic function passes through (0, 7), (2, −1) and (4, 7), and (2, −1) is its vertex.

**(a)** Explain why the axis of symmetry is x = 2. **[1]**
**(b)** Find the equation of the graph in the form y = a(x − h)² + k. **[3]**
**(c)** Write the equation in the form y = ax² + bx + c. **[1]**

## Answers

**1. (a)** **(3, 5)** [1]
**(b)** **x = 3** [1]
**(c)** 2(9) + 5 = 23, so **(0, 23)** [1]

**2. (a)** **x = −4 and x = 2** [1]
**(b)** **x = −1** [1]
**(c)** f(−1) = −(3)(−3) = **9** [1]

**3.** (x − 5)² − 25 + 18 [1] = **(x − 5)² − 7** [1]

**4.** 2(x² + 6x) + 11 [1] = 2[(x + 3)² − 9] + 11 [1] = **2(x + 3)² − 7** [1]

**5. (a)** y = a(x − 2)² − 3 and 5 = 4a − 3, so a = 2 [1]: **y = 2(x − 2)² − 3** [1]
**(b)** **y = 2x² − 8x + 5** [1]

**6. (a)** **x = 4** [1]
**(b)** **(4, −2)** [1]

**7. (a)** 3(x² − 2x − 3) [1] = **3(x − 3)(x + 1)** [1]
**(b)** 3[(x − 1)² − 1] − 9 [1] = **3(x − 1)² − 12** [1]
**(c)** x-intercepts **(3, 0), (−1, 0)** [1]; y-intercept **(0, −9)** [1]; vertex **(1, −12)** [1]

**8. (a)** f(x) = a(x + 3)(x − 5) and f(0) = −15a = 30, so a = −2 [1]: **f(x) = −2(x + 3)(x − 5)** [1]
**(b)** Axis x = 1 [1]; f(1) = −2(4)(−4) = 32, vertex **(1, 32)** [1]
**(c)** **f(x) ≤ 32** [1]

**9. (a)** −4.9(t² − 4t) + 1.5 = −4.9[(t − 2)² − 4] + 1.5 [1] = **−4.9(t − 2)² + 21.1** [1]
**(b)** **21.1 m** [1] at **t = 2 s** [1]
**(c)** −4.9(t − 2)² + 21.1 = 0, so (t − 2)² = 4.306… [1]; t = **4.08 s** (the negative root is rejected) [1]
**(d)** h(t) = 15 at t = 0.884 and t = 3.12 [1]; time above 15 m = **2.23 s** [1]

**10. (a)** R = p(600 − 25p) = −25p² + 600p [1]
**(b)** R = −25p(p − 24), so the zeros are 0 and 24 [1] and the axis is p = 12 [1]; maximum revenue R(12) = 12 × 300 = **$3600** at **p = $12** [1]
**(c)** −25p² + 600p = 2000 gives p² − 24p + 80 = 0, so (p − 4)(p − 20) = 0 [1]: **p = $4 or $20** [1]

**11. (a)** f(x) = (x + 3)² + 4 [1] = x² + 6x + 13 [1], so **b = 6, c = 13** [1]
**(b)** Translation 3 units left [1] and 4 units up [1]
*Examiner insight:* "Translation by 3" without a direction earns no mark; state left or right, or give the vector.

**12. (a)** (0, 7) and (4, 7) have equal y-values, so the axis is halfway between x = 0 and x = 4 [1]
**(b)** y = a(x − 2)² − 1 [1]; through (0, 7): 4a − 1 = 7 [1], so **y = 2(x − 2)² − 1** [1]
**(c)** **y = 2x² − 8x + 7** [1]

## Where marks are usually lost

- The sign of h in a(x + h)² + k read the wrong way.
- In completing the square with a ≠ 1, forgetting to multiply the subtracted square by a, as in question 4.
- The value of a left out when building from roots, as in question 8.
- Negative times kept in a model, as in question 9.
- Transformations described without directions or scale factors.
