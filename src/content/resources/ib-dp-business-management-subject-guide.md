IB Diploma Programme Business Management is designed to meet the needs
of students who want to develop knowledge of business content, concepts
and tools to support real business decision-making. It is built around
four interdisciplinary concepts -- creativity, change, ethics and
sustainability -- explored from a business perspective.

## What the course covers

Business Management focuses on business functions, management
processes and decision-making in contexts of strategic uncertainty.
Students examine how internal and external factors shape business
decisions, and how those decisions affect a range of internal and
external stakeholders. Emphasis is placed on strategic decision-making
alongside the operational business functions of human resource
management, finance and accounts, marketing, and operations
management.

## Aims

The course aims to enable students to:

- develop as confident, creative and compassionate business leaders,
  entrepreneurs, social entrepreneurs and change agents
- foster an informed understanding of ethical and sustainable business
  practices
- explore the connections between individuals, businesses and society
- engage with decision-making as a process and a skill.
