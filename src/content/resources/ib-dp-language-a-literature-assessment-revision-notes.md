Condensed for quick recall of how the course is assessed. For the full subject overview, use the
[IB DP Language A: Literature subject overview](/resources/ib-dp-language-a-literature-subject-guide/).

## SL vs HL weightings

| Component | SL | HL |
|---|---|---|
| Paper 1 (guided analysis, two unseen literary extracts) | 35% | 35% |
| Paper 2 (comparative essay, studied literary works) | 35% | 25% |
| HL essay (independent coursework, 1,200-1,500 words) | — | 20% |
| Individual oral | 30% | 20% |

## How this differs from Language A: Language and Literature

Same overall structure and weightings, but Paper 1 and Paper 2 here focus **exclusively on
literary texts** — Language and Literature's Paper 1 works with a wider range of text types.

## The individual oral

- A prepared spoken response exploring how a **global issue of the student's choosing** is
  presented in an extract from a studied literary work, then **extended to a second work**.

## Exam traps

- Confusing this course's exclusively-literary Paper 1 with Language A: Language and Literature's broader text-type range.
- Treating the individual oral as a single-text analysis, when it requires extending the discussion to a second studied work.
- HL students skipping dedicated planning time for the HL essay, since it's independent coursework rather than an in-class exam.

## Self-test

1. What kind of texts does Paper 1 focus on in this course?
2. What specifically distinguishes HL assessment from SL?
3. How many literary works must the individual oral eventually connect?
4. What are Paper 1 and Paper 2 weighted at, at SL?

**Answers:** 1. Two previously unseen literary extracts. 2. The HL essay — independent written coursework of 1,200-1,500 words, only completed at Higher Level. 3. Two — the oral begins with one extract and extends the discussion to a second work. 4. Paper 1: 35%, Paper 2: 35%.
