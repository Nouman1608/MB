## British policy: concession and repression

| Concession | Repression |
|---|---|
| 1917 promise of responsible government | Rowlatt Acts (1919) |
| 1919 Act (dyarchy) | Amritsar (1919) |
| Round Table Conferences; Gandhi–Irwin Pact | Mass arrests after the Salt March |
| 1935 Act | Arrest of Congress leaders after Quit India |
| Cripps offer (1942); Cabinet Mission (1946) | — |

Use this pattern in essays: British policy mixed reform with force, and each act of repression
strengthened nationalism.

## Worked example: two effects

*Explain two effects of the 1937 elections.*

1. Congress formed governments in most provinces, giving it experience of power and showing it
   was the dominant nationalist party.
2. The Muslim League's poor result, and Congress's refusal to share power with it, persuaded
   Jinnah that Muslims would be a permanent minority in a Congress-run India — pushing the League
   towards the Lahore Resolution of 1940.

## Common mistakes and exam traps

- **Confusing the 1919 and 1935 Acts.** 1919 = dyarchy in the provinces; 1935 = provincial
  autonomy and a proposed federation.
- **Calling Jinnah a separatist from the start.** He was a Congress member until 1920 and only
  demanded Pakistan from 1940.
- **Forgetting Britain's position after 1945.** War debts and the Labour government's priorities
  matter as much as Indian pressure.
- **Exaggerating Quit India's success.** It was suppressed quickly; its significance lay in showing
  Indian determination and in the League's gains while Congress leaders were imprisoned.
- **Inventing casualty figures.** Use "the official figure of 379" for Amritsar and "hundreds of
  thousands" for partition rather than precise invented numbers.

## Frequently asked questions

**What was the significance of the Salt March?**
It made the salt tax a symbol of unjust rule,
drew worldwide attention and brought mass participation into civil disobedience.

**Why did the Cabinet Mission fail?**
Congress and the League interpreted the grouping plan
differently, and trust collapsed after Nehru's statement in July 1946.

**What problems did partition cause?**
Mass violence, the displacement of millions, and disputes
over Princely States, especially Kashmir.
