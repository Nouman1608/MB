Condensed for quick recall of how the course is assessed. For the full subject overview, use the
[IB DP Business Management subject overview](/resources/ib-dp-business-management-subject-guide/).

## SL vs HL weightings

| Component | SL | HL |
|---|---|---|
| Paper 1 (pre-seen case study) | 35% | 25% |
| Paper 2 (stimulus material + extended response) | 35% | 30% |
| Paper 3 (social-enterprise stimulus) | — | 25% |
| Internal assessment | 30% | 20% |

## Paper 1's distinctive feature

The Paper 1 case study is **released in advance** — this tests research and preparation on a
known organisation, a different skill from Paper 2 (and HL Paper 3), which respond to **unseen**
stimulus material under timed conditions.

## Internal assessment differs by level, not just weighting

- **SL:** a written commentary applying course concepts to a real business.
- **HL:** a full research project, again on a real organisation — a different task, not just a
  longer version of the SL commentary.

Both are marked by your own teacher first, then externally moderated by IB examiners.

## The six underpinning concepts

Change, culture, ethics, globalization, innovation, strategy — these run through the **whole**
course and both papers and the IA, rather than being confined to one unit.

## Self-test

1. Why does Paper 1 test a different skill from Paper 2/Paper 3?
2. Name the internal assessment task at HL, and how it differs from the SL task.
3. List the six underpinning concepts.
4. How is the internal assessment marked?

**Answers:** 1. Because the Paper 1 case study is released in advance, testing research and prepared analysis, while Paper 2 (and HL Paper 3) use unseen stimulus material under timed exam conditions. 2. A research project on a real organisation, differing in kind (not just length) from the SL written commentary. 3. Change, culture, ethics, globalization, innovation, strategy. 4. First by the student's own teacher, then externally moderated by IB examiners.
