# Question 1: choosing and planning your two passages

**Paper 1 of Cambridge IGCSE Islamiyat 0493 has five questions. You must answer Question 1, Question 2 and two others, and the paper totals 50 marks.** Question 1 (major themes of the Qur'an) is worth 8 marks. Three passages are set from Appendix 1 and you may choose any two. For each, the syllabus asks you to explore the major theme or themes and their importance in the lives of Muslims today. The other questions on the paper are Question 2 (history and importance of the Qur'an, 14 marks), Question 3 (life and importance of the Prophet (pbuh), 14 marks) and Question 4 (the first Islamic community, 14 marks), with one or two questions set on each of Questions 3 and 4.

Related: [Major Themes of the Qur'an revision notes](/resources/cambridge-igcse-islamiyat-major-themes-of-the-quran-revision-notes/).

## Step 1: choose in under a minute

When you see the three passages, find each one's group first.

| Group | Appendix 1 passages |
|---|---|
| Allah in himself | 1 Sura 2.255; 2 Sura 6.101-103; 3 Sura 41.37; 4 Sura 42.4-5; 5 Sura 112 |
| Allah's relationship with the created world | 6 Sura 1; 7 Sura 2.21-22; 8 Sura 96.1-5; 9 Sura 99; 10 Sura 114 |
| Allah's Messengers | 11 Sura 2.30-37; 12 Sura 6.75-79; 13 Sura 5.110; 14 Sura 93; 15 Sura 108 |

Choose the two passages for which you can state a theme **and** a modern application most clearly. Do not choose the longer passage just because more is printed: the marks are for theme and importance, not for retelling.

## Step 2: plan each answer in a four-box grid

| Box | What to write | Example for Sura 112 |
|---|---|---|
| Group | One phrase | Allah in himself |
| Theme | One sentence, in your words | Allah is one: no partner, no offspring, no equal |
| Evidence | A verse number or Arabic word from the printed passage | The statement that He does not beget and is not begotten |
| Importance today | An action and the reason | Worship and trust directed to Allah alone |

A full plan takes about two minutes. The paper has 50 marks, so a rough guide of one and a half to two minutes per mark gives Question 1 about 12 to 16 minutes in all. (This pacing is Marlbridge's own arithmetic, not a Cambridge instruction; see the note below.)

## Step 3: know what each part is marked for

The syllabus gives Question 1(a) a maximum of 4 marks, for knowledge (AO1). Level 4 describes a well-structured, clear and comprehensive response with extensive and accurate knowledge, and it is "likely" to quote Qur'an verses or Hadiths in support. Part (b) is marked for understanding (AO2): the top level, worth 3 to 4 marks, asks for a clear understanding of the question and the use of your own opinions with reasoning, while the lower level describes descriptive and factual references with limited discussion.

## Worked plan: Sura 93

- **Group:** Allah's Messengers.
- **Theme:** Allah reassures the Prophet (pbuh) that He has not abandoned him and that the hereafter will be better; He recalls His favours to him as an orphan (shelter, guidance and provision).
- **Evidence:** the verses recalling those favours, and the duties that follow (not oppressing the orphan, not turning away the one who asks).
- **Importance today:** comfort in hard times, and care for orphans and the needy as a Muslim duty.

## Common mistakes

- Choosing by length rather than by what you can explain.
- Writing a part (b) that restates part (a).
- Giving the same application for both passages.
- Not showing any link to the Arabic.
