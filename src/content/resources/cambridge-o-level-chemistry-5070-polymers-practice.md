**7.** Propene, CH₂=CHCH₃, forms an addition polymer.

**(a)** Name the polymer. **[1]**

**(b)** Draw the repeat unit of the polymer, showing all atoms and bonds. **[2]**

**(c)** Explain why this is called addition polymerisation. **[1]**

**(c)** State how many monomer molecules were used to make this section of chain. **[1]**

*Examiner insight:* the repeat unit of an addition polymer always has the same molecular formula as its monomer, because nothing is lost when the monomers join.

## Where marks are usually lost

- Drawing CH₃ into the main chain of poly(propene).
- Leaving C=C in an addition repeat unit, or forgetting brackets and *n*.
- Saying addition polymerisation gives water.
- Splitting condensation polymers at the wrong bond when deducing monomers.
- Calling proteins or nylon polyesters.
