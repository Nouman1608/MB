Condensed notes for subtopic **3.1 Coordinates** of Cambridge O Level Mathematics (Syllabus D) 4024, 2025 to 2027. The [Coordinates study guide](/resources/cambridge-o-level-mathematics-4024-coordinates/) explains each idea with worked examples; the [Coordinates practice questions](/resources/cambridge-o-level-mathematics-4024-coordinates-practice/) give exam-style questions with mark schemes.

## Syllabus outcome

Use and interpret Cartesian coordinates in two dimensions.

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Cartesian plane | grid made by two perpendicular number lines | x-axis and y-axis |
| Origin | where the axes cross | (0, 0) |
| Coordinates | ordered pair (x, y) | (-3, 2) |
| x-coordinate | distance across | -3 |
| y-coordinate | distance up or down | 2 |
| Quadrant | one of four regions | 2nd quadrant |
| Vertex | corner of a shape | A(1, 2) |

## Reading and plotting

1. Start at the origin.
2. Move along the x-axis by the x-value (right if positive, left if negative).
3. Move up or down by the y-value (up if positive, down if negative).

Check the scale on each axis. Do not assume one square is one unit.

## Quadrants

| Quadrant | Position | Signs |
|---|---|---|
| 1st | top right | (+, +) |
| 2nd | top left | (-, +) |
| 3rd | bottom left | (-, -) |
| 4th | bottom right | (+, -) |

Points on the axes are in no quadrant: y = 0 is on the x-axis, x = 0 is on the y-axis.

| Point | Location |
|---|---|
| (-3, 2) | 2nd quadrant |
| (4, -1) | 4th quadrant |
| (-2, -6) | 3rd quadrant |
| (0, -5) | on the negative y-axis |
| (6, 0) | on the positive x-axis |

## Special lines

| Line | Description | Points |
|---|---|---|
| y = 4 | horizontal | (-3, 4), (0, 4), (5, 4) |
| x = -2 | vertical | (-2, 1), (-2, 7) |
| y = 0 | the x-axis | (k, 0) |
| x = 0 | the y-axis | (0, k) |

Lines x = a and y = b cross at (a, b). Example: x = -2 and y = 3 cross at (-2, 3).

## Distance on a horizontal or vertical line

| Points | Same | Distance |
|---|---|---|
| (-3, 4) and (5, 4) | y | 5 - (-3) = 8 |
| (-2, 1) and (-2, 7) | x | 7 - 1 = 6 |
| (-1, -4) and (-1, 3) | x | 3 - (-4) = 7 |

Subtract the smaller from the larger. Watch the negatives.

## Finding the fourth vertex

| Shape | Property to use | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Rectangle | sides parallel to axes if two sides are | A(-2, 1), B(4, 1), C(4, -3) gives D(-2, -3) |
| Parallelogram | opposite sides equal and parallel; same step | A(1, 2), B(5, 2), C(7, 5) gives D(3, 5) |
| Square | equal sides, right angles | A(0, 0), B(5, 0) gives C(5, 5), D(0, 5) |
| Kite | one diagonal is the line of symmetry | A(0, 4), C(0, -2), B(3, 0) gives D(-3, 0) |

Parallelogram step: from B to C is +2 across and +3 up, so D = A + (2, 3).

## Areas from coordinates

| Shape | Data | Area |
|---|---|---|
| Rectangle | width 6, height 4 | 24 |
| Triangle | (0, 0), (6, 0), (6, 8) | 1/2 × 6 × 8 = 24 |
| Kite | diagonals 6 and 6 | 1/2 × 6 × 6 = 18 |
| Isosceles triangle | P(-4, 0), Q(4, 0), R(0, 6) | 1/2 × 8 × 6 = 24 |

## Moving and reflecting

| Change | Rule | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Translate 3 left, 4 down | (x - 3, y - 4) | (2, 5) to (-1, 1) |
| Reflect in x-axis | (x, -y) | (3, -2) to (3, 2) |
| Reflect in y-axis | (-x, y) | (3, -2) to (-3, -2) |
| Reflect in both axes | (-x, -y) | (3, -2) to (-3, 2) |

## Unknown coordinates

| Condition | Set up | Example |
|---|---|---|
| on the y-axis | x = 0 | (2a - 6, a + 1): a = 3, point (0, 4) |
| on the x-axis | y = 0 | (k + 1, 2k - 4): k = 2, point (3, 0) |
| on the line y = x | x = y | (p, 2p - 5): p = 5, point (5, 5) |

## Table of values

For y = 2x - 1:

| x | -2 | -1 | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| y | -5 | -3 | -1 | 1 | 3 | 5 |

Points: (-2, -5), (-1, -3), (0, -1), (1, 1), (2, 3), (3, 5).

## Exam traps

- (x, y): across first, then up or down.
- (2, 5) and (5, 2) are different points.
- Read both scales; do not assume 1 square = 1 unit.
- 5 - (-3) = 8, not 2.
- A point on an axis is not in a quadrant.
- Reflection in the x-axis changes the sign of y, not x.
- Use brackets and a comma when writing coordinates.
- Make a quick sketch.

## Self-test

1. In which quadrant is (-7, 3)?
2. Write down the coordinates of the point 4 units left of the origin on the x-axis.
3. Find the distance between (2, -3) and (2, 5).
4. P(1, 1), Q(6, 1), R(6, 4) are vertices of a rectangle PQRS. Find S.
5. Find the coordinates where x = 5 and y = -2 cross.
6. Reflect (-4, 6) in the x-axis.
7. The point (m + 4, 3m) lies on the y-axis. Find m.
8. A parallelogram has vertices A(0, 0), B(4, 1), C(7, 5). Find D.

**Answers**

1. **2nd quadrant**.
2. **(-4, 0)**.
3. 5 - (-3) = **8 units**.
4. **S(1, 4)**.
5. **(5, -2)**.
6. **(-4, -6)**.
7. m + 4 = 0, so **m = -4**; the point is (0, -12).
8. B to C is +3, +4, so D = A + (3, 4) = **(3, 4)**.
