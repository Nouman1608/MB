These questions cover section 2 of Unit M1: Mechanics 1 in Pearson Edexcel International A Level Mathematics (YMA01): magnitude and direction of a vector, resultants, resolving, the unit vectors i and j, and vectors applied to displacement, velocity, acceleration and force in a plane. Unless stated otherwise, i is due east and j is due north. Give non-exact answers to 3 significant figures and angles to 1 decimal place.

Related: [M1 vectors study guide](/resources/edexcel-a-level-mathematics-yma01-m1-vectors-in-mechanics/) and [M1 vectors revision notes](/resources/edexcel-a-level-mathematics-yma01-m1-vectors-in-mechanics-revision-notes/).

## Section A

**1.** A particle moves with velocity (−6i − 8j) m s⁻¹. Find its speed and the bearing on which it is moving. **[3]**

**2.** A boat moves at 12 m s⁻¹ on a bearing of 300°. Write its velocity in the form (ai + bj) m s⁻¹. **[2]**

**3.** Two forces of magnitude 7 N and 4 N act on a particle. The angle between their directions is 50°. Find the magnitude of the resultant and the angle it makes with the 7 N force. **[4]**

**4.** A particle moves with speed 10 m s⁻¹ in the direction of the vector (−i + 2j). Find its velocity in exact form. **[2]**

**5.** A particle moves with constant acceleration. At t = 0 its velocity is (3i − 2j) m s⁻¹, and at t = 4 s its velocity is (−5i + 10j) m s⁻¹. Find the acceleration and its magnitude. **[3]**

**6.** Three forces (4i − 3j) N, (−7i + 2j) N and (ai + 5j) N act on a particle. Their resultant is parallel to j. Find a and the magnitude of the resultant. **[3]**

## Section B

**7.** Two forces F₁ = (2i − 5j) N and F₂ = (qi + 9j) N act on a particle. Their resultant R is parallel to (i − 2j).

**(a)** Find the value of q. **[3]**

**(b)** Find the magnitude of R and the angle R makes with the positive i direction. **[3]**

**8.** At time t = 0, particle P is at (2i + 5j) m and particle Q is at (−4i − 3j) m. P moves with constant velocity (3i − j) m s⁻¹ and Q with constant velocity (5i + 3j) m s⁻¹.

**(a)** Find the vector from P to Q at time t seconds. **[2]**

**(b)** Show that Q is due west of P when t = 2, and find the distance PQ at that time. **[3]**

**(c)** Find the value of t, other than t = 0, at which P and Q are 10 m apart. **[3]**

**9.** At noon boat A is at (i + 7j) km and moves with constant velocity (3i − j) km h⁻¹. At the same time boat B is at (10i + 4j) km and moves with constant velocity (−i + j) km h⁻¹.

**(a)** Show that B is due north of A at 14:15. **[4]**

**(b)** Explain why the boats do not collide. **[1]**

**(c)** Find the distance between the boats at 14:15. **[1]**

**10.** A particle has initial velocity (2i − 3j) m s⁻¹ and moves with constant acceleration (i + 2j) m s⁻².

**(a)** Find its velocity when t = 3 s. **[2]**

**(b)** Find its speed when t = 3 s. **[1]**

**(c)** Find the time at which it is moving parallel to i. **[2]**

**(d)** Find the time at which it is moving parallel to (i + j). **[2]**

**11.** A boat moves with constant velocity. At 09:00 it is at the point with position vector (3i + 2j) km and at 09:30 it is at (9i − 6j) km.

**(a)** Find the velocity of the boat. **[2]**

**(b)** Find its speed and the bearing on which it moves. **[3]**

**(c)** Find its position vector at 10:15. **[2]**

**12.** Three horizontal forces act on a particle: 10 N in the direction of i, 8 N at 60° above the i direction (towards j), and 6 N in the direction of −j.

**(a)** Find the resultant force in the form (ai + bj) N. **[3]**

**(b)** Find the magnitude of the resultant and the angle it makes with i. **[3]**

## Answers

**1.** Speed = √(36 + 64) = **10 m s⁻¹** [1].

The vector points south-west. Angle west of south: tan⁻¹(6/8) = 36.9° [1], so the bearing is 180° + 36.9° = **216.9°** [1].

**2.** East component 12 sin 300° = −10.4; north component 12 cos 300° = 6 [1].

Velocity = **(−10.4i + 6j) m s⁻¹** [1].

**3.** Angle in the vector triangle = 180° − 50° = 130° [1].

R² = 49 + 16 − 2(7)(4) cos 130° = 101.0, so **R = 10.0 N** [1].

sin θ/4 = sin 130°/10.05 [1], so **θ = 17.8°** [1].

**4.** |−i + 2j| = √5 [1], so v = 10(−i + 2j)/√5 = **(−2√5 i + 4√5 j) m s⁻¹** [1].

**5.** a = ((−5 − 3)i + (10 + 2)j)/4 [1] = **(−2i + 3j) m s⁻²** [1].

Magnitude = √13 = **3.61 m s⁻²** [1].

**6.** Parallel to j, so the i component is zero: 4 − 7 + a = 0, giving **a = 3** [1].

j component: −3 + 2 + 5 = 4 [1], so the resultant is 4j and its magnitude is **4 N** [1].

**7. (a)** R = (2 + q)i + 4j [1].

Parallel to (i − 2j): (2 + q)/4 = 1/(−2) [1], so 2 + q = −2 and **q = −4** [1].

**(b)** R = (−2i + 4j), |R| = √20 = **4.47 N** [1].

R points up and to the left; tan⁻¹(4/2) = 63.4° above the negative i direction [1], so the angle with the positive i direction is **116.6°** [1].

**8. (a)** r_P = (2 + 3t)i + (5 − t)j and r_Q = (−4 + 5t)i + (−3 + 3t)j [1].

r_Q − r_P = **(2t − 6)i + (4t − 8)j** [1].

**(b)** At t = 2: r_Q − r_P = −2i + 0j [1].

The j component is zero and the i component is negative, so Q is due west of P [1]. PQ = **2 m** [1].

**(c)** (2t − 6)² + (4t − 8)² = 100 [1].

20t² − 88t + 100 = 100, so 20t² − 88t = 0 and 4t(5t − 22) = 0 [1].

Other than t = 0: **t = 4.4 s** [1].

**9. (a)** r_A = (1 + 3t)i + (7 − t)j and r_B = (10 − t)i + (4 + t)j, with t in hours after noon [1].

r_B − r_A = (9 − 4t)i + (2t − 3)j [1].

Due north: 9 − 4t = 0, so t = 2.25 h, which is 14:15 [1]. Then the j component is 2(2.25) − 3 = 1.5 > 0, so B is due north of A [1].

**(b)** At the only time the i components are equal, the j components differ (by 1.5 km), so the position vectors are never equal [1].

**(c)** **1.5 km** [1].

**10. (a)** v = (2i − 3j) + 3(i + 2j) [1] = **(5i + 3j) m s⁻¹** [1].

**(b)** √(25 + 9) = **5.83 m s⁻¹** [1].

**(c)** v = (2 + t)i + (−3 + 2t)j. Parallel to i: −3 + 2t = 0 [1], so **t = 1.5 s** [1].

**(d)** Parallel to (i + j): 2 + t = −3 + 2t [1], so **t = 5 s**, when v = 7i + 7j [1].

**11. (a)** v = ((9i − 6j) − (3i + 2j))/0.5 [1] = **(12i − 16j) km h⁻¹** [1].

**(b)** Speed = √(144 + 256) = **20 km h⁻¹** [1].

South-east; angle east of south = tan⁻¹(12/16) = 36.9° [1], so the bearing is **143.1°** [1].

**(c)** 10:15 is 1.25 h after 09:00: r = (3i + 2j) + 1.25(12i − 16j) [1] = **(18i − 18j) km** [1].

**12. (a)** The 8 N force is 8 cos 60° i + 8 sin 60° j = 4i + 6.93j [1].

Sum: (10 + 4 + 0)i + (0 + 6.93 − 6)j [1] = **(14i + 0.928j) N** [1].

**(b)** |R| = √(14² + 0.928²) [1] = **14.0 N** [1].

Angle with i: tan⁻¹(0.928/14) = **3.8°** [1], on the j side of i.

## Where marks are usually lost

- Giving a bearing from the calculator's tan⁻¹ without placing the vector in its quadrant.
- Using 50° rather than 130° inside the vector triangle in a cosine rule question.
- Subtracting velocities the wrong way round when finding an acceleration.
- Checking only one component when deciding whether objects meet.
- Forgetting to convert minutes to hours, as in 30 minutes = 0.5 h.
- Giving a velocity when the question asks for a speed.
