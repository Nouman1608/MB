**These notes condense content area 3.2.2.3 of 9237: "The relationship of the Hadith to the Qur'an as a source of guidance and Shariah law, including the importance of the teachings and Sunnah of Hazrat Muhammad (pbuh)."** The full explanation is in the [study guide](/resources/oxfordaqa-igcse-islamiyat-9237-hadith-and-sunnah-as-source-of-shariah/).

## Three key terms

| Term | Meaning in one line |
|---|---|
| Hadith | A report of what the Prophet (pbuh) said, did, approved or was like |
| Sunnah | The Prophet's (pbuh) way and example, recorded in the Hadith |
| Shariah | The law or path of Islam, drawn from the Qur'an and Sunnah first, then Ijma and Qiyas |

## How the Hadith relates to the Qur'an

| Role | Explanation | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Explains | Gives the detail of general commands | Prayer: "Pray as you have seen me praying" (Bukhari 631) |
| Specifies | Limits or clarifies a general rule | A murderer does not inherit from his victim |
| Adds | Gives guidance not stated in the Qur'an | A woman and her paternal aunt may not be married together |
| Confirms | Repeats and reinforces Qur'anic teaching | Honesty, kindness, prayer, fasting |

**Order of authority:** the Qur'an first; the Sunnah second; the Sunnah cannot contradict the Qur'an.

**Hadith of Mu'adh ibn Jabal (RA) (Abu Dawud):** judge by the Book, then the Sunnah, then reasoning; the Prophet (pbuh) approved.

## Why Muslims follow the Sunnah: Qur'anic evidence

| Verse | Message |
|---|---|
| 4:59 | Obey Allah and obey the Messenger |
| 4:80 | He who obeys the Messenger has obeyed Allah |
| 33:21 | The Messenger is an excellent pattern |
| 3:31 | Follow the Prophet if you love Allah |
| 59:7 | Take what the Messenger gives; refrain from what he forbids |
| 16:44 | The Prophet was sent to explain the message |

Hazrat Aisha (RA) said that his character was the Qur'an (Muslim).

## Types of Sunnah

- **Sayings:** what he said.
- **Actions:** what he did.
- **Approvals:** what he saw and did not forbid.
- **Character:** how he was described.
- **Rank:** some Sunnah is obligatory (such as the five prayers) and some recommended (such as voluntary night prayer).

## Importance of the Prophet's (pbuh) teachings and Sunnah

1. **Practical guidance** for the Five Pillars.
2. **Correct interpretation** of the Qur'an.
3. **A model of character** in family, trade, war and peace.
4. **A basis for law** in worship, family, crime and trade.
5. **Unity** in the basic form of Muslim worship.

## The Sunnah in the Five Pillars

| Pillar | What the Qur'an gives | What the Sunnah adds |
|---|---|---|
| Shahadah | The belief in one God and the Prophet's (pbuh) message | How it is said and taught |
| Salah | The command to pray | Times, form, rak'ahs and the call to prayer |
| Sawm | The command to fast in Ramadhan | The pre-dawn meal, breaking the fast, voluntary fasts |
| Zakah | The command to give | Minimum amount (nisab) and rates |
| Hajj | The command to perform pilgrimage | The order and rites of Hajj |

Without the Sunnah the pillars would be known by name and not by practice.

## The prescribed passage: Qur'an 53:1-6

| Phrase | Point |
|---|---|
| "has not strayed, nor has he erred" | The Prophet (pbuh) was guided and protected in his message |
| "nor does he speak from [his own] inclination" | His religious teaching is not personal opinion |
| "it is not but a revelation revealed" | Many scholars: this supports the authority of the Sunnah as well as the Qur'an |
| "taught to him by one intense in strength" | Jibril (AS) was the means of teaching |
| "he rose to [his] true form" | He saw Jibril (AS) in his real form |

**Careful wording:** all agree that the passage proves the Qur'an is revelation; many scholars also use it to support the Sunnah; some limit it to the Qur'an.

## Evaluating "The Qur'an is enough"

| For | Against |
|---|---|
| The Qur'an is complete (5:3) and protected (15:9) | The Qur'an commands obedience to the Messenger (4:59, 59:7) |
| Hadith were written later and include weak reports | The Qur'an does not give the details of prayer or Zakah |
| Avoids human additions | Mu'adh's Hadith puts the Sunnah second |
| | Scholars grade Hadith to remove weak reports |

**Judgement:** the Qur'an is the foundation, but it points to the Sunnah for practice.

## Model answer: a 4-mark question

**Question:** Explain **two** reasons why the Sunnah is important to Muslims. **[4]**

> The Sunnah explains how to carry out the Qur'an's commands [1]: it shows how to pray and perform Hajj, which the Qur'an commands but does not describe in detail [1]. It also gives an example of character to follow [1], because the Qur'an calls the Messenger of Allah an excellent pattern (33:21) [1].

## Exam traps

- Hadith is a report; Sunnah is the practice. Do not use them as if identical.
- The Sunnah never overrides the Qur'an.
- Do not say that all Hadith are equally reliable.
- Use a Qur'anic verse for why Muslims follow the Prophet (pbuh), not just "because he was the Prophet".
- Be careful in how you use 53:3-4: say "many scholars use this to support".

## Self-test

1. What is the difference between Hadith and Sunnah?
2. Define Shariah.
3. Give one example of the Hadith explaining the Qur'an.
4. Which verse says the Messenger is an excellent pattern?
5. What did Mu'adh ibn Jabal (RA) say he would judge by, in order?
6. Name the four kinds of Sunnah.
7. What did Aisha (RA) say about the Prophet's (pbuh) character?
8. Which phrase in 53:1-6 says the Prophet (pbuh) does not speak from personal desire?
9. Give one argument for and one against the claim that the Qur'an is enough.
10. Why can the Sunnah not overrule the Qur'an?

**Answers:** 1. Hadith is the report; Sunnah is the way or practice that it records. 2. The law or path of Islam given by Allah, drawn from the Qur'an, Sunnah, Ijma and Qiyas. 3. Any one: prayer; Zakah rates; Hajj rites. 4. Qur'an 33:21. 5. The Book of Allah, the Sunnah, then his own reasoning. 6. Sayings, actions, approvals and character (description). 7. That his character was the Qur'an. 8. "Nor does he speak from [his own] inclination." 9. For: the Qur'an is complete and protected; against: it commands obedience to the Messenger and lacks details of worship. 10. The Qur'an is the first source and the word of Allah; the Sunnah explains it.
