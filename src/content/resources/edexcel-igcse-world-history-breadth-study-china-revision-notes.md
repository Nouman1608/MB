## Worked example: explain two ways different

*Explain two ways in which Chinese agriculture in 1952 was different from Chinese agriculture in 1958.*
(6 marks)

1. **Ownership:** in 1952, after the Agrarian Reform Law, most peasants farmed their own small plots; by
   1958 land was owned collectively in huge communes.
2. **Organisation and targets:** in 1952 families decided what to grow; in 1958 communes followed state
   targets and peasants were diverted to backyard furnaces.

## Essay plan in outline

*"Mao's leadership was the main reason for change in China in the years 1934–76." How far do you agree?*

- **For:** Long March and Yan'an; victory in 1949; land reform, collectives, the Great Leap and the
  Cultural Revolution were Mao's initiatives.
- **Against:** Japan's invasion weakened the GMD; GMD corruption; Soviet aid and then the split; other
  leaders such as Zhou Enlai, Liu Shaoqi and Deng.
- **Judgement:** Mao drove most change, but often with results he did not intend.

## Common mistakes and exam traps

- **Confusing the Great Leap (1958–62) with the Cultural Revolution (1966–76).**
- **Calling the 1911 Revolution a communist revolution.**
- **Treating the GMD as always weak.** It won the Northern Expedition and nearly destroyed the CCP in 1934.
- **Inventing exact death tolls.** Use "tens of millions" for the famine.
- **Answering (c) from one topic.**

## Frequently asked questions

**Why was the Long March significant?** It saved the CCP, made Mao leader and became a propaganda legend.

**Why did the Great Leap fail?** Unrealistic targets, false reporting, poor-quality steel and neglect of
harvests.

**What did Deng change?** The economy, through markets and foreign investment; not one-party rule.
