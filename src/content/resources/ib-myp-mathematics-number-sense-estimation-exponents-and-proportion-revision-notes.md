## Number sets and forms

| Symbol | Set | Example members |
|---|---|---|
| N | positive integers and zero | 0, 4, 250 |
| Z | integers | -9, 0, 6 |
| Q | rational numbers (a/b) | 3/8, -0.25, 0.7 recurring |
| Q' | irrational numbers | √2, π, √20 |
| R | real numbers (Q and Q') | all of the above |

- A decimal is rational if it stops or recurs. Irrational decimals neither stop nor recur.
- |x| is the distance from 0. |a - b| is the distance between a and b.
- **Recurring decimal to fraction, in steps:** let x equal the decimal; multiply by 10 or 100 or 1000 so the recurring tails match; subtract; divide.
- Worked reminder: x = 0.4545... gives 100x - x = 45, so x = 45/99 = 5/11.

## Factors, primes, GCF, LCM

| Task | Method |
|---|---|
| Prime factorization | divide by 2, 3, 5, 7 ... until 1 is left; write with powers |
| GCF | shared primes, lowest powers |
| LCM | all primes, highest powers |
| Check | GCF × LCM = product of the two numbers |

Worked reminder: 60 = 2² × 3 × 5 and 150 = 2 × 3 × 5². GCF = 2 × 3 × 5 = 30; LCM = 2² × 3 × 5² = 300; 30 × 300 = 9000 = 60 × 150.

## Roots, surds and rationalizing

| Rule | Example |
|---|---|
| √(ab) = √a × √b | √48 = √16 × √3 = 4√3 |
| √(a/b) = √a / √b | √(5/9) = √5 / 3 |
| like surds combine | 2√3 + 5√3 = 7√3 |
| (a + √b)(a - √b) = a² - b | (4 + √2)(4 - √2) = 14 |

Method for simplifying: find the largest square factor, take its root outside, leave the rest inside.
**enr** Rationalizing: multiply top and bottom by √b (single surd) or by the conjugate (a binomial). 4/√2 = 4√2/2 = 2√2.
Note: √(a + b) is not √a + √b.

## Laws of exponents and standard form

| Law | Note |
|---|---|
| aᵐ × aⁿ = aᵐ⁺ⁿ | the bases must match |
| aᵐ ÷ aⁿ = aᵐ⁻ⁿ | subtract, even if it goes negative |
| (aᵐ)ⁿ = aᵐⁿ | multiply powers |
| (ab)ⁿ = aⁿbⁿ | applies to every factor in the bracket |
| a⁰ = 1 | a not 0 |
| a⁻ⁿ = 1/aⁿ | negative power means reciprocal, not a negative number |
| a^(m/n) **ext** | root first (the denominator n), then the power (the numerator m) |

Standard form is written a × 10ᵏ, with a between 1 (included) and 10 (excluded) and k a whole number, positive or negative.
- To multiply or divide, handle the numbers and the powers of 10 separately, then tidy.
- To add or subtract, write both with the same power of 10 first.
- Worked reminder: 5 × 10⁻³ ÷ (2 × 10²) = 2.5 × 10⁻⁵.

## Logarithms (ext)

- log_a x = y means aʸ = x. So log₃ 81 = 4 and log_a 1 = 0.
- Adding logs multiplies the arguments, subtracting logs divides them, and a multiplier in front becomes a power: log(xy) = log x + log y, log(x/y) = log x - log y, log(xⁿ) = n log x.
- To solve aˣ = b: x = log b / log a, evaluated with technology.
- Worked reminder: 3ˣ = 20 gives x = log 20 / log 3 = 2.73 (3 s.f.).
- Logarithms and exponents are inverses; this is why logs appear in models of growth and decay.

## Inequalities on a number line

| Symbol | Circle |
|---|---|
| < or > | open (end value excluded) |
| ≤ or ≥ | closed (end value included) |

- Double inequality: 1 ≤ x < 6 is one segment between two circles.
- Compound with "or": x ≤ -2 or x > 4 is two rays pointing outwards.
- To list integers, check each end value against the circle type.

**Number sequences:** find the term-to-term difference; if the differences are not constant, look at the differences of the differences. Describe in words, then predict the next terms.

## Estimation, rounding and units

- Estimate by rounding each number to 1 s.f. first.
- Decimal places are counted after the point. Significant figures are counted from the first digit that is not zero. Look only at the next digit; 5 or more rounds up.
- Do not round in the middle of working. Round once, at the end, to the accuracy the question asks for or the data supports.
- Conversions: length ×1000 (km to m); area factor is squared (1 m² = 10 000 cm²); volume factor is cubed (1 m³ = 1000 litres); speed km/h to m/s divide by 3.6.
- Time zones: convert to the departure city's clock, add the duration, then shift by the difference in UTC offsets.

## Accuracy, errors, bounds

| Quantity | Formula |
|---|---|
| Absolute error | the size of (measured - true), always positive |
| Percentage error | absolute error ÷ true × 100 |
| Rounded to nearest u | x - u/2 ≤ value < x + u/2 |

**ext** Bounds of calculations:
- a + b: add lowers, add uppers.
- a - b: lower = lower a - upper b; upper = upper a - lower b.
- a × b: multiply lowers, multiply uppers.
- a ÷ b: lower = lower a ÷ upper b; upper = upper a ÷ lower b.

Worked reminder (**ext**): a rectangle is 12 cm by 7 cm, both to the nearest cm. Area upper bound = 12.5 × 7.5 = 93.75 cm². Lower bound = 11.5 × 6.5 = 74.75 cm². The nominal area, 84 cm², sits between them. Report the answer with a sensible number of figures, such as "about 84 cm², to 2 s.f.", and explain that the data limit the accuracy.

Commenting on validity: a result is only as accurate as the least accurate measurement used. Say which measurement limits it.

## Ratio, percentage, proportion and money

| Idea | Method |
|---|---|
| Share in ratio a : b | add parts, divide, multiply |
| Increase by p% | × (1 + p/100) |
| Decrease by p% | × (1 - p/100) |
| Reverse percentage | divide by the multiplier |
| Direct y = kx | y ÷ x constant; graph is a straight line through the origin |
| Inverse y = k/x | xy constant; graph is a curve |
| Simple interest | P × r × t |
| Compound interest | A = P(1 + r)ⁿ |

Worked reminder: 300 after a 25% increase means original = 300 ÷ 1.25 = 240.

Worked reminder (compound interest): 800 at 5% for 3 years gives 800 × 1.05³ = 926.10, so the interest is 126.10. Simple interest would be 800 × 0.05 × 3 = 120.

Worked reminder (inverse proportion): 8 taps fill a tank in 15 minutes. Taps × time is constant at 120, so 10 taps take 120 ÷ 10 = 12 minutes.

## Number properties and bases

- Commutative: a + b = b + a and ab = ba. Associative: grouping can change. Distributive: a(b + c) = ab + ac.
- Subtraction and division are not commutative or associative.
- **enr** Place values in base b are powers of b. 312₄ = 3 × 16 + 1 × 4 + 2 = 54. To convert from base 10, divide repeatedly by b and read remainders upwards.

## Quick self-test

1. Is 0.1 recurring (0.111...) in N, Z, Q, Q' or R? Name all that apply.
2. Write 0.2777... (7 recurring) as a fraction.
3. Find the GCF and LCM of 36 and 90.
4. Simplify √75 + √12.
5. Simplify (4x³y⁻²)² ÷ (2xy).
6. Write 3.6 × 10⁻³ × 5 × 10⁷ in standard form.
7. Round 0.0070468 to 3 s.f.
8. Share 840 in the ratio 2 : 3 : 7.
9. A price falls 20% to 360. What was it before?
10. y is inversely proportional to x and y = 9 when x = 4. Find y when x = 10.
11. **ext** Evaluate 81^(3/4) and log₂ 32.
12. **ext** A length is 20 cm to the nearest cm. Give its bounds.

### Answers

1. It equals 1/9, so it is in Q and R only.
2. x = 0.2777...: 100x = 27.777..., 10x = 2.777...; 90x = 25; x = 25/90 = **5/18**.
3. 36 = 2² × 3², 90 = 2 × 3² × 5. GCF = 2 × 9 = **18**; LCM = 4 × 9 × 5 = **180**.
4. 5√3 + 2√3 = **7√3**.
5. 16x⁶y⁻⁴ ÷ (2xy) = **8x⁵y⁻⁵** = 8x⁵/y⁵.
6. 3.6 × 5 = 18; 10⁻³ × 10⁷ = 10⁴; 18 × 10⁴ = **1.8 × 10⁵**.
7. **0.00705** (the digit after 0.00704 is 6, so round up).
8. 12 parts, 70 each: **140, 210, 490**.
9. 360 ÷ 0.8 = **450**.
10. k = 36; y = 36/10 = **3.6**.
11. 81^(3/4) = 3³ = **27**; log₂ 32 = **5**.
12. **19.5 ≤ length < 20.5** cm.

## Where marks are usually lost

- Stopping at 16.5 × 10⁷ without converting to standard form.
- Treating a negative exponent as a negative answer.
- Rounding a recurring decimal and calling it exact in a "show" or "justify" step.
- Forgetting that unit conversions for area and volume use the squared or cubed factor.
- Rounding before the final step, so the last digits are wrong.
- Mixing up the bounds in a division.
- Leaving out units, or giving too many significant figures for rounded data.
- In Criterion D tasks, giving an answer without commenting on whether it is reasonable in the context.
