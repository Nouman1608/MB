**HL only.** These notes cover Regional study 5, Unification of Germany and Italy (1815 to 1871), in the Europe option examined on HL Paper 3. The study names both countries. See the [study guide](/resources/ib-dp-history-2028-german-italian-unification/) for fuller explanation.

## What Paper 3 wants

Two 15-mark essays from different regional studies in two hours. The top band wants an in-depth understanding of the argument, a balanced evaluation of perspectives and a substantiated judgement. Command terms include "To what extent", "Discuss", "Examine", "Explain" and "Analyse".

## Congress System: points to learn

- **Vienna:** Austria gained Lombardy and Venetia; German Confederation of 39 states; Prussia enlarged in the Rhineland.
- **Metternich:** valued stability; used the Holy Alliance and congresses; Austrian troops crushed revolts in Naples and Piedmont; Carlsbad Decrees (1819).
- **Impact:** delayed change but also produced resentment.

## Nationalism and liberalism before 1848

| Theme | Italy | Germany |
|---|---|---|
| Ideas | Napoleonic memories; cultural revival | Romanticism; resistance to Napoleon |
| Movements | Carbonari; Young Italy | Burschenschaften; Hambach Festival |
| Leaders | Mazzini (republic); Gioberti (papal federation) | Rulers resisted; liberals in states |
| Key force | Papacy (Pius IX) | Prussia and the Zollverein |
| Weakness | Little mass support; Austrian force | Divided states; censorship |

## 1848 to 1849

- **Italy:** war by Charles Albert; defeat at Custoza and Novara; Roman Republic fell to French troops; Piedmont kept the *Statuto*.
- **Germany:** Frankfurt Parliament split over Kleindeutsch and Grossdeutsch; Frederick William IV refused the crown; autocracy returned.
- **Why failure:** divided middle classes, no armies, peasants and workers wanted different things, foreign and Austrian force.
- **Legacy:** nationalism moved toward governments; Piedmont and Prussia had constitutions.

## Unification of Italy

- **Cavour:** economic and military modernisation; Crimean War diplomacy; Plombières alliance with Napoleon III; 1859 war.
- **Garibaldi:** Sicily and Naples (1860); handed his conquests to Victor Emmanuel II.
- **Foreign influence:** France's help in 1859; Prussia's war in 1866; French withdrawal from Rome in 1870.
- **Social and economic:** Piedmont's strength; poorer south; low voting rights; "making Italians" was still to be done.

## Unification of Germany

- **Bismarck:** army conflict (1862); "iron and blood"; diplomacy to isolate opponents.
- **Army:** Roon and Moltke; railways, telegraph, rifles.
- **Wars:** Denmark (1864), Austria (1866), France (1870 to 1871).
- **Ems telegram:** used to provoke France; the southern states joined.
- **Result:** Empire proclaimed at Versailles (18 January 1871); Alsace and Lorraine annexed.

## Historians' views

- Treitschke: unification as Prussia's mission.
- A.J.P. Taylor: Bismarck as opportunist.
- Wehler: social and economic forces and elite interests.
- Mack Smith: Cavour's calculation. Gramsci: passive revolution.

## Question wording and how to respond

| Question starts | What it needs |
|---|---|
| "To what extent was ... the main reason for unification?" | Rank the named factor against others; judge |
| "Examine the role of ..." | Explain the person's actions, limits and the role of others |
| "Discuss the impact of 1848" | Short-term failure and long-term effects, for both countries if the question asks |
| "Compare ..." | Similarities and differences with evidence on both sides |

## Common mistakes

- Treating unification as the plan of one man (Cavour or Bismarck).
- Ignoring foreign influence and the role of war.
- Treating 1848 as pure failure with no legacy.
- Describing events without ranking causes.
- Writing only about Germany or only about Italy when the question needs both.

## Self-test

1. What was the Zollverein?
2. Why did Metternich oppose nationalism?
3. What did Frederick William IV do with the crown offered by Frankfurt?
4. Give two ways Cavour won foreign support.
5. Why did Garibaldi hand over his conquests?
6. What was the Ems telegram used for?

**Answers:** 1. A customs union led by Prussia, covering most German states by 1834 but not Austria. 2. A multinational empire could be broken up by it. 3. He refused it in 1849. 4. For example the Crimean War and the Plombières alliance with Napoleon III. 5. He was committed to unification under Victor Emmanuel II and Cavour had moved Piedmontese troops to prevent a clash over Rome. 6. To provoke France into declaring war in 1870.

## Frequently asked questions

**Which person matters most?**
Do not decide before you read the question. Rank individuals against wars, diplomacy and economic strength.

**How much about 1848 should I include?**
Enough to explain failure and legacy for each country.
