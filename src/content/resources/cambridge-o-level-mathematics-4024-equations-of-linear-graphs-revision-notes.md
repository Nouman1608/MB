Condensed notes for subtopic **3.5 Equations of linear graphs** of Cambridge O Level Mathematics (Syllabus D) 4024, 2025 to 2027. The [Equations of linear graphs study guide](/resources/cambridge-o-level-mathematics-4024-equations-of-linear-graphs/) explains each method with worked examples; the [Equations of linear graphs practice questions](/resources/cambridge-o-level-mathematics-4024-equations-of-linear-graphs-practice/) give exam-style questions with mark schemes.

## Syllabus outcome

Interpret and obtain the equation of a straight-line graph. Questions may use and request lines in the forms ax + by = c, y = mx + c and x = k, may give the graph, and may ask for the gradient or y-intercept from an equation. Give equations in fully simplified form.

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Gradient (m) | change in y per 1 unit of x | 2 |
| y-intercept (c) | y-value where the line crosses the y-axis | (0, 3) |
| Equation of a line | rule linking x and y for every point | y = 2x + 3 |
| Subject | the letter on its own on one side | y in y = 2x + 3 |
| Simplified form | no unnecessary fractions, brackets or common factors | y = 3x - 2 |

## Forms of a straight line

| Form | Example | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| y = mx + c | y = 2x + 3 | m gradient, c y-intercept |
| ax + by = c | 3x - 2y = 6 | integers a, b, c |
| y = k | y = 5 | horizontal; gradient 0 |
| x = k | x = -2 | vertical; gradient undefined |
| y = mx | y = 4x | through the origin |

## Gradient and y-intercept from an equation

| Equation | Rearranged | m | c |
|---|---|---|---|
| y = 5 - 2x | y = -2x + 5 | -2 | 5 |
| 3x - 4y = 24 | y = (3/4)x - 6 | 3/4 | -6 |
| 4x + 5y = 20 | y = -(4/5)x + 4 | -4/5 | 4 |
| 2y = 6x - 4 | y = 3x - 2 | 3 | -2 |
| x + 2y = 8 | y = -x/2 + 4 | -1/2 | 4 |
| y - 3x = 7 | y = 3x + 7 | 3 | 7 |

Divide every term when rearranging, and flip every sign when dividing by a negative.

## Finding an equation: three methods

| Given | Steps | Example |
|---|---|---|
| m and c | write y = mx + c | m = 2, c = 3: y = 2x + 3 |
| m and a point | substitute the point, solve for c | m = -3 through (2, -1): c = 5, y = -3x + 5 |
| two points | find m, then substitute one point | (-1, 5), (3, -3): m = -2, c = 3, y = -2x + 3 |
| graph | read c; find m from two points | crosses (0, 3), passes (2, 7): y = 2x + 3 |

Check with a second point on the line.

Alternative for a point (x₁, y₁): y - y₁ = m(x - x₁).

## Converting between forms

| From | To | Working |
|---|---|---|
| y = (3/2)x - 3 | 3x - 2y = 6 | multiply by 2: 2y = 3x - 6; rearrange |
| y = (2/3)x + 4 | 2x - 3y = -12 | multiply by 3: 3y = 2x + 12 |
| 3x - 2y = 6 | y = (3/2)x - 3 | -2y = -3x + 6; divide by -2 |

## Intercepts from the equation

| Equation | x = 0 | y = 0 |
|---|---|---|
| y = -2x + 3 | (0, 3) | (1.5, 0) |
| 3x - 2y = 6 | (0, -3) | (2, 0) |
| y = 2x - 2 | (0, -2) | (1, 0) |

## Real-life equations

Gradient = rate of change, intercept = fixed or starting amount.

| Situation | Equation | Reading |
|---|---|---|
| Taxi: $3 plus $2.50 per km | C = 2.5d + 3 | m = 2.5, c = 3; 8 km costs $23 |
| Phone: $12 plus $0.15 per min | C = 0.15t + 12 | m = 0.15, c = 12 |

## Checking a point

Substitute into the equation. If both sides are equal, the point is on the line. For 3x - 2y = 6: (4, 3) gives 12 - 6 = 6, so on the line; (6, -3) gives 18 + 6 = 24, so not.

## Exam traps

- Gradient of 3x - 4y = 24 is 3/4, not 3.
- Do not swap m and c.
- Rearranging: divide every term, including the right-hand side.
- Gradient order: same subtraction order on top and bottom.
- Vertical lines are x = k, never y = something.
- "Fully simplified" means cancel common factors.
- Always check with a second point.
- Write the equation with y = on the left, unless another form is asked for.

## Self-test

1. Write down the gradient and y-intercept of y = 4x - 7.
2. Find the gradient of 6x + 3y = 12.
3. Find the equation of the line with gradient 2 through (0, -5).
4. Find the equation of the line with gradient -1/2 through (4, 1).
5. Find the equation of the line through (1, 4) and (3, 10).
6. Write y = (2/5)x + 2 in the form ax + by = c, with integers.
7. Where does 5x - 2y = 10 cross each axis?
8. A plumber charges $40 call-out plus $25 per hour. Write the cost C for h hours.

**Answers**

1. Gradient **4**, y-intercept **-7**.
2. 3y = -6x + 12, y = -2x + 4, gradient **-2**.
3. **y = 2x - 5**.
4. 1 = -2 + c, c = 3, so **y = -x/2 + 3**.
5. m = 6/2 = 3, 4 = 3 + c, c = 1, so **y = 3x + 1**.
6. 5y = 2x + 10, so **2x - 5y = -10**.
7. x = 0 gives y = -5, so **(0, -5)**; y = 0 gives x = 2, so **(2, 0)**.
8. **C = 25h + 40**.
