## Questions

**1.** Convert: **(a)** 2.6 m² to cm² **[1]**; **(b)** 0.8 m³ to litres **[1]**; **(c)** 3 hours 24 minutes to hours as a decimal **[1]**.

**2.** The exchange rate is £1 = 1.30 US dollars. A jacket costs 84.50 US dollars in New York and £62 in Leeds. Which price is lower, and by how much in pounds? **[3]**

**3.**

**(a)** Priya earns £11.20 per hour. She works for 6 hours 45 minutes. Find her pay. **[2]**
**(b)** A 250 g jar of coffee costs £3.40 and a 400 g jar costs £5.20. Which jar is the better value? Show your working. **[3]**

**4.** A train travels 135 km in 1 hour 30 minutes at a steady speed.

**(a)** Find its speed in km/h. **[2]**
**(b)** Write this speed in m/s. **[2]**

**5.** A solid metal cube has edges of 5 cm and a mass of 1 kg.

**(a)** Find its density in g/cm³. **[2]**
**(b)** Write the density in kg/m³. **[1]**
**(c)** A force of 750 N acts on an area of 0.3 m². Find the pressure in N/m². **[2]**

**6.**

**(a)** A rope is x metres long. It is cut into pieces of 20 cm. Write an expression for the number of pieces. **[2]**
**(b)** Water flows from a tap at a litres per minute. Write this rate in cm³ per second, as a single fraction. **[2]**

**7.** A walking map has a scale of 1 : 30 000.

**(a)** Two huts are 6.4 cm apart on the map. Find the real distance in km. **[2]**
**(b)** A real path is 4.5 km long. Find its length on the map in cm. **[2]**
**(c)** How many metres does 1 cm on the map represent? **[1]**

**8.** The bearing of port B from port A is 138°. Port C is such that the bearing of C from B is 228°.

**(a)** Find the bearing of A from B. **[2]**
**(b)** Show that angle ABC is 90°. **[2]**
**(c)** AB = 10 km and BC = 24 km. Find the distance AC. **[2]**

**9.** An L-shaped field is drawn with right angles only. Going round from the bottom left corner, the sides in order are 11 m, 3 m, 6 m, b, c and 8 m. The 11 m side is the full width and the 8 m side is the full height. Sketch the field, then find b and c and the perimeter. **[3]**

**10.**

**(a)** A circle has radius 6.5 cm. Give its circumference as a multiple of π and as a decimal to 3 significant figures. **[2]**
**(b)** A wheel has circumference 2.2 m. How many complete turns must it make to travel at least 1 km? **[2]**
**(c)** A sector has radius 12 cm and angle 75°. Find its arc length in terms of π. **[2]**
**(d)** Find the perimeter of this sector to 3 significant figures. **[1]**

**11.** A garden bed is a rectangle 8 m by 5 m with a semicircle added on one of the 5 m sides. Edging is placed round the whole outside of the bed, except for a 1.2 m gap for a gate. Edging costs £14.50 per metre. Find the cost of the edging, to the nearest pound. **[7]**

**12.** A circular running track has radius 35 m. An athlete runs 3 laps in 9 minutes at a steady speed.

**(a)** Find the distance run, to 3 significant figures. **[3]**
**(b)** Find the speed in m/min. **[1]**
**(c)** Find the speed in km/h, to 3 significant figures. **[2]**

## Answers

**1. (a)** 1 m² = 10 000 cm², so 2.6 × 10 000 = **26 000 cm²** [1]. **(b)** 1 m³ = 1000 litres, so **800 litres** [1]. **(c)** 24 ÷ 60 = 0.4, so **3.4 hours** [1]. **[3]**
*Examiner insight:* Answers to conversions need the unit, and a clear check is whether the number is bigger when the unit gets smaller.

**2.** Convert the dollar price to pounds: 84.50 ÷ 1.30 = £65 [1]. Compare £65 with £62 [1]. The Leeds jacket is cheaper by **£3** [1]. **[3]**
*Examiner insight:* Compare prices in the same currency and say which is cheaper in a sentence, not just a number.

**3. (a)** 6 h 45 min = 6.75 h [1]. 11.20 × 6.75 = **£75.60** [1]. **(b)** 250 g jar: 340 ÷ 250 = 1.36 p per gram [1]. 400 g jar: 520 ÷ 400 = 1.30 p per gram [1]. The **400 g jar** is better value, since it costs less per gram [1]. **[5]**
*Examiner insight:* Compare costs for the same amount, and finish with a statement naming the better buy.

**4. (a)** 1 h 30 min = 1.5 h [1]. 135 ÷ 1.5 = **90 km/h** [1]. **(b)** 90 km = 90 000 m [1]. 90 000 ÷ 3600 = **25 m/s** [1]. **[4]**
*Examiner insight:* Show the conversion of both the top and the bottom of km/h, so the working is clear even if an arithmetic slip happens.

**5. (a)** Volume = 5 × 5 × 5 = 125 cm³ [1]. Mass = 1000 g, so density = 1000 ÷ 125 = **8 g/cm³** [1]. **(b)** 8 × 1000 = **8000 kg/m³** [1]. **(c)** Pressure = force ÷ area = 750 ÷ 0.3 [1] = **2500 N/m²** [1]. **[5]**
*Examiner insight:* Change the mass to grams before dividing when the answer is wanted in g/cm³.

**6. (a)** x metres = 100x cm [1]. Number of pieces = 100x ÷ 20 = **5x** [1]. **(b)** a litres = 1000a cm³ and 1 minute = 60 seconds [1]. Rate = 1000a/60 = **50a/3 cm³ per second** [1]. **[4]**
*Examiner insight:* Write the unit conversion next to the letter, so each factor you use is visible.

**7. (a)** 6.4 × 30 000 = 192 000 cm [1] = **1.92 km** [1]. **(b)** 4.5 km = 450 000 cm [1]. 450 000 ÷ 30 000 = **15 cm** [1]. **(c)** 30 000 cm = **300 m** [1]. **[5]**
*Examiner insight:* Convert cm to km at the end, using 100 000 cm = 1 km, and state the unit.

**8. (a)** Back bearing = 138° + 180° [1] = **318°** [1]. **(b)** At B, the bearing of A is 318° and the bearing of C is 228° [1]. The angle between them is 318° − 228° = 90°, so angle ABC is **90°** [1]. **(c)** Pythagoras: AC² = 10² + 24² = 676 [1]. AC = **26 km** [1]. **[6]**
*Examiner insight:* On a "show that" question, state both bearings at B and the subtraction, not only the final angle.

**9.** The vertical sides on the right total the full height of 8 m, so b = 8 − 3 = **5 m** [1]. The horizontal sides along the top total the full width of 11 m, so c = 11 − 6 = **5 m** [1]. Perimeter = 11 + 3 + 6 + 5 + 5 + 8 = **38 m** [1]. **[3]**
*Examiner insight:* Mark every missing length on a sketch and then add all six sides. Counting from the sketch avoids missing a side.

**10. (a)** C = 2π × 6.5 = **13π cm** [1] = **40.8 cm** [1]. **(b)** 1000 ÷ 2.2 = 454.5... [1]. A whole number of turns is needed, so **455 turns** [1]. **(c)** Arc = 75/360 × 2π × 12 [1] = 5/24 × 24π = **5π cm** [1]. **(d)** Perimeter = 5π + 24 = **39.7 cm** [1]. **[7]**
*Examiner insight:* When the question asks for a number of complete turns, a rounded-down answer would not reach 1 km, so think about what the context needs.

**11.** The semicircle has diameter 5 m, so radius 2.5 m [1]. Arc = π × 2.5 = 7.854 m [1]. The three straight sides are 8 + 8 + 5 = 21 m [1]. The 5 m side where the semicircle is attached is inside the outline, so it is not edged [1]. Outside perimeter = 21 + 7.854 = 28.854 m [1]. Edging needed = 28.854 − 1.2 = 27.654 m [1]. Cost = 27.654 × 14.50 = 400.98, so **£401** [1]. **[7]**
*Examiner insight:* Keep the full unrounded value until the last step. Rounding the length early can change the final pound figure.

**12. (a)** Circumference = 2π × 35 = 70π m [1]. Three laps: 3 × 70π = 210π [1] = **660 m** [1]. **(b)** 210π ÷ 9 = **73.3 m/min** [1]. **(c)** Speed = 210π ÷ 9 × 60 = 4398 m/h [1] = **4.40 km/h** [1]. **[6]**
*Examiner insight:* Take the unrounded distance into the speed calculation, and convert m/min to km/h by two separate, stated steps.

## Where marks are usually lost

- Using 100 or 1000 for an area or volume conversion.
- Converting time as a decimal without dividing minutes by 60.
- Using the exchange rate the wrong way round, so the answer is not sensible.
- Failing to compare unit prices for the same quantity.
- Not converting the top and bottom of a compound unit.
- Measuring a bearing from the wrong point, or forgetting the three figures.
- Using the diameter in 2πr.
- Giving the arc alone as the perimeter of a sector, or counting an inside edge.
- Rounding too early, or giving a final answer with no units.
