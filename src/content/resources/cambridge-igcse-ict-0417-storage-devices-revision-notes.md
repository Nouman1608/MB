## Device or medium?

The **device** reads and writes. The **medium** holds the data. A DVD drive (device) reads a DVD (medium). In a hard disk drive or SSD, the medium is sealed inside the device.

## Access methods

- **Direct (random) access**: goes straight to the data. Hard disk drives, optical drives, SSDs.
- **Serial (sequential) access**: reads through the data in order. Magnetic tape.

## Magnetic devices

**Fixed hard disk drive**

- Spinning platters; read-write heads on a moving arm; direct access; inside the computer.
- Uses: desktops, servers, network storage.
- For: huge capacity, low cost per GB, fast direct access. Against: moving parts (head crash if knocked), slower, louder and more power-hungry than SSD.

**Portable hard disk drive**

- Same technology, external, USB connection.
- Uses: backups, moving large files, extra laptop storage.
- For: large and cheap; backup kept away from the computer. Against: damaged if dropped; lost or stolen; slower than a portable SSD.

**Magnetic tape drive**

- Tape cartridge wound past a read-write head; serial access.
- Uses: backing up and archiving very large amounts of data; data processed in order.
- For: enormous capacity, very low cost per GB, robust cartridges kept off-site for years. Against: very slow to find one file; expensive drive; unsuitable for frequently changed files.

## Optical devices

**CD, DVD and Blu-ray drives**

- Laser reads pits and lands on a spinning disc; writers change a dye layer.
- Blu-ray uses a blue-violet laser with a shorter wavelength than the red laser of CD and DVD, so smaller pits, more data.
- Uses: music, films, games, software installation, read-only distribution, photo archives.
- For: cheap portable discs; read-only discs cannot be changed. Against: slower and smaller than hard drives; many laptops lack drives; drives read only some formats.

## Solid-state devices

**Fixed SSD**

- Flash memory; no moving parts; direct access.
- Uses: laptops, tablets, phones, fast desktops and servers.
- For: very fast, robust, silent, low power, light. Against: higher cost per GB; limited number of writes per memory area; less capacity for the same price.

**Portable SSD, pen drive, flash drive**

- External flash memory, usually USB.
- Uses: carrying and transferring files, small backups.
- For: very portable, robust, fast, no power supply needed. Against: easily lost or stolen; spreads viruses; smaller capacity for the price than a portable hard drive.

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Storage device | Hardware that reads data from and writes data to a storage medium |
| Platter | Rigid magnetic disk inside a hard disk drive |
| Read-write head | Part that reads or changes magnetised areas |
| Head crash | Damage when a read-write head touches the spinning platter |
| Direct access | Going straight to any data item |
| Serial access | Reading data in order until the item is found |
| Pits and lands | Raised and lowered areas on an optical disc read by a laser |
| Flash memory | Non-volatile solid-state memory with no moving parts |
| Archive | Long-term store of data that is rarely accessed |

## Exam traps

- A CD, DVD or Blu-ray disc is a **medium**; the drive is the **device**.
- Tape is **serial** access, not direct.
- SSDs use flash memory, not magnetism, and are usually **dearer** per gigabyte.
- Blu-ray holds more because of the **shorter wavelength** of its laser, not because the disc is bigger.
- Every advantage needs its reason: "fast **because** there are no moving parts".
- Losing a pen drive is a data security risk: mention encryption in an answer about protecting data.

## Self-test

1. What does a storage device do?
2. State the difference between direct and serial access.
3. Which named device uses serial access?
4. Describe how a hard disk drive reads data.
5. Give **two** uses of a magnetic tape drive.
6. Why is magnetic tape unsuitable for a file that is opened many times a day?
7. Why can a Blu-ray disc hold more data than a DVD?
8. Give **one** advantage of read-only optical discs for a software publisher.
9. Give **three** advantages of an SSD over a hard disk drive in a laptop.
10. Give **one** disadvantage of an SSD.
11. Give **two** risks of carrying files on a pen drive.
12. Why is a portable hard drive used for backups?

## Self-test answers

1. It reads data from and writes data to a storage medium.
2. Direct access goes straight to any data item; serial access reads through data in order until it reaches the item.
3. The magnetic tape drive.
4. The platters spin and a read-write head on a moving arm detects the magnetised areas on the surface.
5. Any two: backing up large amounts of data; archiving; storing data processed in order, such as billing records.
6. Serial access means the tape must be wound to the file each time, which is very slow.
7. Its blue-violet laser has a shorter wavelength, so smaller pits can be read and packed more closely.
8. The data cannot be changed or deleted by the customer.
9. Any three: faster access; no moving parts so robust; silent; lower power so longer battery life; lighter.
10. Higher cost per gigabyte, or a limited number of writes, or less capacity for the same price.
11. Any two: lost or stolen, exposing data; can carry viruses; can be damaged.
12. It has a large capacity at low cost and can be kept away from the computer, so the copy survives if the computer is lost or damaged.
