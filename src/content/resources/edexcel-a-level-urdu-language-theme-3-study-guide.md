This guide covers **Theme 3, Immigration and the multicultural society (ہجرت ترکِ وطن اور کثیر الثقافتی معاشرہ)**, in Pearson Edexcel GCE A-Level Urdu (9UR0), for teaching from 2018. Its sub-themes are the positive features of a diverse society (ایک متنوع اور گوناگوں معاشرے کے مثبت پہلو), attitudes towards immigration (ہجرت کی جانب رجحانات و طرزِ فکر) and emigration from Pakistan (پاکستان سے ہجرت), the last being the research subject. You will need the vocabulary of migration, a balanced way of discussing it and accurate Urdu to express an argument.

## Where this fits in 9UR0

Theme 3 is one of the content themes of the qualification. As the [Paper 1 study guide](/resources/a-level-edexcel-urdu-translation-and-reading/) explains, research for Themes 1, 3 and 4 must be based on Pakistan, which suits the emigration sub-theme. Sources on this theme also appear in the listening and reading work covered in the [Paper 3 study guide](/resources/a-level-edexcel-urdu-listening-reading-writing/), where you summarise viewpoints and evaluate them. Practise stating a position and acknowledging a different one, since that skill serves every part of this theme.

## Sub-theme 3.1: positive features of a diverse society

Ideas to discuss in Urdu:

- **Cultural variety**: languages, food, dress, music and festivals shared across communities.
- **Economic contribution**: skills, enterprise, labour and new markets that migrants bring.
- **Learning and understanding**: contact between communities can reduce prejudice and widen knowledge.
- **Shared citizenship**: how a society balances common values with respect for difference.

| Urdu | Meaning |
|---|---|
| کثیر الثقافتی | multicultural |
| تنوع | diversity |
| رواداری | tolerance |
| باہمی احترام | mutual respect |
| ثقافت | culture |
| زبان | language |
| تہوار | festival |
| شہریت | citizenship |

## Sub-theme 3.2: attitudes towards immigration

Ideas to discuss:

- **Welcoming and supportive attitudes**: seeing migrants as contributors and neighbours.
- **Worries and objections**: concern about jobs, housing, public services, or change in local identity.
- **Prejudice and discrimination**: how stereotypes and unfair treatment affect people's lives, and what law, schools and communities can do about them.
- **Integration and identity**: whether newcomers keep their own customs while taking part in wider society, and how second and third generations see themselves.
- **The role of the media and politicians** in shaping public attitudes.

| Urdu | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ہجرت | migration |
| تارکِ وطن | emigrant |
| مہاجر | migrant |
| تعصب | prejudice |
| امتیازی سلوک | discriminatory treatment |
| انضمام | integration |
| شناخت | identity |
| رجحان | attitude, trend |

You can discuss attitudes without claiming that one view is the only reasonable one. State what people on each side say, give the reasons they offer, and then say which you find more convincing and why.

## Sub-theme 3.3: emigration from Pakistan

This is the research subject. Your research must cover the aspects set by the specification, be based on Pakistan, and be written in Urdu. Points to research:

- **Push and pull factors**: reasons people leave, such as limited jobs, lower wages, insecurity or the wish for further study, and reasons they choose a destination, such as higher pay, family links, or better services.
- **Main destinations**: countries in the Gulf region and in Europe and North America, with different patterns of work and settlement. Check which examples you use against your sources.
- **Remittances** (**ترسیلاتِ زر**): money sent home, and its effect on families and the economy.
- **Skilled emigration**: the loss of skilled workers, often called brain drain (**ذہین افراد کا انخلا**), and the debate about whether return or contact abroad can offset it.
- **Effects on families** left behind and on communities abroad.

Keep a file of named sources, dates and figures that you have checked yourself, and never quote a statistic you cannot trace. Prepare points for and against emigration so you can write a balanced response on any statement.

## Worked example: a model paragraph, with annotation

Task: write a short response to the statement that migration makes a society richer.

ہجرت کرنے والے لوگ نئے ملک میں اپنی زبان، کھانے اور تہوار بھی ساتھ لے جاتے ہیں۔ اس سے معاشرہ زیادہ رنگا رنگ بن جاتا ہے۔ تاہم بعض لوگ اس تنوع کو شک کی نظر سے دیکھتے ہیں، کیونکہ انھیں اپنی نوکریوں اور اپنی شناخت کی فکر ہوتی ہے۔ میرے خیال میں باہمی احترام اور مکالمے سے یہ خدشات کم ہو سکتے ہیں۔

What makes it work:

- **Structure**: a point about contribution, its result, a counter-view introduced with تاہم, and a reasoned conclusion.
- **Agreement**: ہجرت کرنے والے لوگ takes a masculine plural verb, لے جاتے ہیں; معاشرہ is masculine singular, so بن جاتا ہے.
- **Connectives**: تاہم, کیونکہ and میرے خیال میں mark contrast, reason and opinion.
- **Postposition**: انھیں ... فکر ہوتی ہے expresses concern with a dative construction, a feature to practise because it differs from English.

## Useful connectives and phrases

Learn these as ready-made building blocks and practise using each in a sentence of your own.

| Urdu | Use |
|---|---|
| اس کے برعکس | in contrast |
| اس کے علاوہ | in addition |
| نتیجتاً | as a result |
| میرے خیال میں | in my opinion |
| ایک طرف ... دوسری طرف | on one hand ... on the other |
| آخر میں | finally |

Build each paragraph around one of these links so that your argument can be followed from the first sentence to the last.

## Common mistakes

- Presenting only one view, or asserting opinions with no reasons.
- Confusing emigration (leaving) with immigration (arriving) in the Urdu terms and in the research focus.
- Using generalisations about whole communities rather than specific, accurate evidence.
- Research that wanders to other countries instead of Pakistan.
- Careless script, gender agreement or tense in longer answers.

## Exam technique

Plan a clear argument, give each paragraph one idea, and use connectives of contrast and result. Write respectfully about different groups, and support claims with evidence from your research and from the source text where one is given. Check the length against the task's word range and leave time for script and agreement checks.

## Check your understanding

1. What does کثیر الثقافتی معاشرہ mean?
   *Answer: A multicultural society.*
2. Give the Urdu for "prejudice" and for "integration".
   *Answer: تعصب and انضمام.*
3. Which country must your research cover for Theme 3?
   *Answer: Pakistan.*
4. Name two push factors and one pull factor in emigration.
   *Answer: For example, limited jobs and lower wages (push); higher pay abroad (pull).*
5. What are remittances?
   *Answer: Money that emigrants send home to their families.*
6. Translate into Urdu: "a diverse society respects everyone".
   *Answer: ایک متنوع معاشرہ سب کا احترام کرتا ہے۔*
