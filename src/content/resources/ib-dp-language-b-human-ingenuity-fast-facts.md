# Human ingenuity: fast facts

The model texts are in English and every example is only an illustration of a skill; do the language-producing tasks in the target language of your school course and ask your school teacher to check them. The brief's recommended topics are optional and its possible questions are only possible, so your teacher chooses what to teach.

[Language B course hub](/boards/ib/ib-dp/language-b/) · [printable checklist](/checklists/ib/ib-dp/language-b/). Applies to SL and HL, first assessment 2020, examined until the new syllabus is first assessed in May 2029. Companion to the reviewed [Human ingenuity revision notes](/resources/ib-dp-language-b-human-ingenuity-revision-notes/).

## Theme facts

- Prescribed theme 3 of 5.
- Guiding principle: explore the ways in which human creativity and innovation affect our world.
- Five optional recommended topics: entertainment; artistic expressions; communication and media; technology; scientific innovation.
- Possible questions: what can we learn about a culture through its artistic expression? How do the media change the way we relate to each other?
- Conceptual understandings in the Language B course: context, audience, purpose, meaning, variation.

## Assessment facts

- Paper 1: 25%, one task from three, 30 marks.
- Paper 2: 50%, listening 25 marks (25%), reading 40 marks (25%).
- Individual oral: 25%, 30 marks; A language 12, B1 message 6, B2 message, conversation 6, C interactive skills 6.
- HL only: two literary works originally written in the target language.

## Argument frame

Claim, evidence, effect, counterpoint, judgement.

## Useful structures (describe in your target language)

- Comparing past and present technology.
- Cause and effect.
- Giving and softening an opinion.
- Concession: "although", "even though".

## Traps

- Treating the topic list as a fence: an unseen text can approach creativity or innovation from any angle.
- Giving only positive effects.

## Self-test

1. Which two possible questions does the brief give? 2. How many marks is the reading section? 3. What is the HL-only requirement?

**Answers:** 1. Culture and artistic expression; media and relationships. 2. 40. 3. Study of two literary works.
