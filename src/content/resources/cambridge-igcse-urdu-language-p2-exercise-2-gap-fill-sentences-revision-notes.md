## The exercise in one box

```
Paper 2, Exercise 2 – Gap-fill sentences          8 marks
Hear       ONE longer spoken text, e.g. conversation, interview,
           monologue, formal talk
Write      the missing word(s) in notes or sentences on the paper
Assesses   L1 select information, L2 ideas, opinions and attitudes
Heard      twice, with pauses to read and write
```

## Five facts to remember

1. The gaps follow the **order of the recording**.
2. Each gap is usually **one to three words** of key content.
3. The printed words **around** the gap are often **reworded**; the gap content is what was said.
4. Urdu grammar around the gap tells you **what kind of word** is missing.
5. Some gaps test **L2**: a feeling, opinion or reason, not only a fact.

## Grammar clues at a glance

- **Feminine verb (ہوتی، جاتی، تھی):** a feminine noun is missing.
- **ہیں / تھے / جاتے:** plural.
- **ہر ___ کو:** a day or time unit.
- **___ کی تعداد / کتنے:** a number.
- **___ کی وجہ سے:** a cause (noun).
- **کے ___ پر / میں:** an oblique noun (دعوت ناموں، کمروں).
- **Feminine future (سنائیں گی):** a female person (شاعرہ، استانی).

## Traps in longer texts

- **Then and now:** شروع میں چھ ... اب پچیس. Read the time word in the printed sentence.
- **List and exception:** "mostly vegetables ... in winter, carrots too". Match the condition.
- **Cause and effect:** after کی وجہ سے, write the cause.
- **Self-correction:** نہیں، معاف کیجیے ... The corrected detail counts.

## The routine

1. **Pause:** read all gaps; underline اب / پہلے / سردیوں میں; pencil the kind of answer.
2. **First hearing:** follow in order; jot lightly; skip and move on if lost.
3. **Second hearing:** fill and confirm.
4. **After:** does each answer fit the grammar of its sentence?

## Notes or sentences?

- **Notes** (heading + colon): write only the content, e.g. 10,000 or کہانیاں.
- **Sentences:** the answer must read as part of the sentence, so use the right form, e.g. oblique plural
  after a postposition.
- Never rewrite the whole sentence.

## Exam traps

- Waiting to hear the printed sentence word for word.
- Writing the "then" figure for a "now" gap.
- Copying a whole clause, which may include a wrong detail.
- A noun whose gender or number clashes with the verb.
- Giving up after one missed gap instead of rejoining at the next topic.

## What a creditable answer looks like

The syllabus gives the exercise total (8 marks) and the objectives. This page does not reproduce any
official mark scheme, so work to these safe principles:

- **The detail must be the one heard.** A gap about the current number is not answered by an earlier
  number, however accurately you caught it.
- **The answer must be unambiguous.** Write clearly enough that the word cannot be read as a different
  one: ستر (70) and سترہ (17) differ by one letter.
- **The answer must fit.** If the printed sentence already contains سے زیادہ or بجے, do not repeat it
  inside the gap.
- **One answer per gap.** Two alternatives, one right and one wrong, leave the examiner unable to tell
  which you meant.

## Step-by-step method

1. **Read the title or context line** in the pause. It tells you who is speaking and about what, for
   example a librarian in an interview.
2. **Label each gap** in pencil: number, day, time, place, person, thing, reason or feeling.
3. **Circle the time words** in the printed sentences (پہلے، شروع میں، اب، آج کل، اگلے سال). These
   are where then-and-now traps sit.
4. **First hearing:** write the key word lightly, even in Roman letters or figures if that is
   quicker, and keep moving with the order of the text.
5. **Second hearing:** listen hardest for the gaps you are least sure of and for any self-correction.
6. **Final check:** rewrite each answer neatly in Urdu script, and read the whole sentence with your
   word in it to test gender, number and the oblique form.

## Worked example

**Context:** an interview with a librarian about a small local library. (The recording below is
written for this page; you would hear it, not read it.)

> **لائبریرین:** یہ لائبریری تین سال پہلے کھلی تھی۔ شروع میں ہمارے پاس صرف پانچ سو کتابیں تھیں،
> اب دو ہزار سے زیادہ ہیں۔ ہر جمعہ کو بچوں کے لیے کہانیاں پڑھی جاتی ہیں۔ پہلے یہ پروگرام صبح ہوتا
> تھا، لیکن اب شام چار بجے ہوتا ہے، کیونکہ صبح بچے اسکول میں ہوتے ہیں۔ ہمارا سب سے بڑا مسئلہ جگہ
> کی کمی ہے۔

**English gloss:** "This library opened three years ago. At first we had only five hundred books; now
there are more than two thousand. Every Friday stories are read for children. This programme used to
be in the morning, but now it is at four in the afternoon, because in the morning the children are at
school. Our biggest problem is the lack of space."

| Printed sentence | Clue before listening | Answer | Trap avoided |
|---|---|---|---|
| لائبریری ______ پہلے کھلی۔ | پہلے after the gap: a length of time | تین سال | none: the verb is reworded (کھلی تھی → کھلی) |
| اب لائبریری میں ______ سے زیادہ کتابیں ہیں۔ | اب + number | دو ہزار | پانچ سو is the "then" figure |
| ہر ______ کو بچوں کے لیے کہانیاں پڑھی جاتی ہیں۔ | ہر ___ کو: a day | جمعہ | none |
| یہ پروگرام اب ______ ہوتا ہے۔ | اب: the new time | شام چار بجے | صبح is the old time |
| وقت اس لیے بدلا کہ صبح بچے ______ ہوتے ہیں۔ | a place or state | اسکول میں | the reason follows کیونکہ in the recording |
| لائبریری کو سب سے زیادہ پریشانی ______ کی وجہ سے ہے۔ | کی وجہ سے: a cause, a noun | جگہ کی کمی | the printed sentence rewords "مسئلہ" as "پریشانی" |

Notice that only one printed sentence repeats the speaker's wording closely. The rest change the verb,
the order or the key noun, which is exactly why you predict the *kind* of word rather than wait for a
matching sentence.

## Signal words and what follows them

| Signal | Meaning | What to listen for next |
|---|---|---|
| شروع میں / پہلے | at first / before | an old detail, often replaced |
| اب / آج کل | now / these days | the current detail |
| کیونکہ | because | a reason (an L2 gap) |
| اس لیے | so, therefore | a result |
| کے بجائے | instead of | the old detail comes before it |
| البتہ / لیکن | however / but | an exception or condition |
| معاف کیجیے / میرا مطلب ہے | sorry / I mean | a corrected detail |

## More exam traps

- Writing سے زیادہ inside a gap that is already followed by سے زیادہ in print.
- Answering with the speaker's whole reason when the gap wants only the noun (جگہ کی کمی, not "ہمارے
  پاس جگہ کم ہے کیونکہ ...").
- Leaving a Roman-letter note from the first hearing instead of rewriting it in Urdu script.
- Dropping the postposition the sentence needs (اسکول instead of اسکول میں where the verb ہوتے ہیں
  needs a place).

## Self-test

1. How many hearings do you get, and what should each be used for?
2. In "باغ میں ______ اگائی جاتی ہیں", what kind of word is missing?
3. Heard: "شروع میں ہم دس تھے، اب تیس ہیں۔" Printed: "اب ارکان کی تعداد ______ ہے۔" What is the answer?
4. Why might the words around the gap differ from what the speaker says?
5. What should you do if you lose your place?
6. Name the two assessment objectives for Exercise 2.
7. Heard: "ہم ہر منگل کو ملتے ہیں۔" Printed: "ہر ______ کو ملاقات ہوتی ہے۔" What is the answer?
8. What kind of word fits "نئے ______ خریدے جاتے ہیں"?
9. Heard: "ہمارے کلب میں تیس ... نہیں، معاف کیجیے، چالیس ارکان ہیں۔" Printed: "کلب میں ______ ارکان
   ہیں۔" What do you write?
10. Heard: "پہلے یہ پروگرام صبح ہوتا تھا، اب شام کو ہوتا ہے۔" Printed: "پروگرام اب ______ ہوتا ہے۔"
    What is the answer, and which word is the trap?
11. Why is it risky to copy a whole clause from the recording into a gap?
12. A printed sentence reads "میچ ______ کی وجہ سے ملتوی ہوا۔" What kind of answer is needed?

**Answers:** 1. Two: the first to follow the text and fill what you can, the second to fill gaps and
confirm details. 2. A feminine plural noun (the verb اگائی جاتی ہیں is feminine plural), for example
سبزیاں. 3. تیس (30): اب asks for the current number, not دس. 4. The question paper rewords the recording so
that you show understanding rather than only matching sounds; the missing content is still what was
said. 5. Listen for the topic of the next printed sentence and rejoin there, since the gaps follow the
order of the text. 6. L1 (identify and select relevant information) and L2 (understand ideas, opinions
and attitudes). 7. منگل (Tuesday): ہر ___ کو asks for a day. 8. A masculine plural noun, because خریدے
جاتے ہیں is masculine plural, for example پودے or بیج. 9. چالیس (40): the speaker corrects himself, and the
corrected detail counts. 10. شام (or شام کو); صبح is the old time, signalled by پہلے. 11. A long clause
may contain a second, wrong detail, and the examiner cannot tell which part is your answer. 12. A cause,
expressed as a noun, for example بارش.
