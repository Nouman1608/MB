Condensed notes for the final weeks. For the full explanation and a worked pair of speakers, use the
[Exercise 3 multiple matching study guide](/resources/cambridge-igcse-urdu-language-p2-exercise-3-multiple-matching/).

## The exercise in one box

```
Paper 2, Exercise 3 – Multiple matching           6 marks
Hear       six short extracts, one speaker each, usually on one topic
Do         match each speaker to the appropriate content (an option)
Assesses   L3 connections between ideas, opinions and attitudes
           L4 what is implied: gist, purpose, intention, feelings
Heard      twice, with pauses to read and write
```

The syllabus does not state the number of options. Be ready for extra options that are not needed.

## L3 and L4 in one line each

- **L3:** the option must capture the *connection*, such as "likes it, but ...", not just one half.
- **L4:** the speaker *implies* the view (a rhetorical question, a complaint); the option states it plainly.

## Key terms

| Urdu | English |
|---|---|
| بولنے والا / والی | speaker |
| رائے | opinion |
| متفق / متفق نہیں | agrees / disagrees |
| شک | doubt |
| افسوس | regret |
| رویہ | attitude |
| رائے بدل گئی | the view has changed |

## Attitude signals

- **لیکن، مگر، پر، البتہ:** the real point comes next.
- **... تو ہے، لیکن ...:** a concession, then a reservation.
- **پہلے ... اب ...:** a change of mind; match the **اب** view.
- **لوگ کہتے ہیں / سنا ہے:** a reported view, often rejected next.
- **شاید، پتا نہیں، مجھے نہیں لگتا:** doubt.
- **کاش:** regret or a wish.
- **کیا فائدہ اگر ...؟:** an implied negative judgement.
- **بالکل، ہرگز، واقعی:** strong emphasis.

## Paraphrase pairs to recognise

| Heard | Printed option |
|---|---|
| بہت پیسے لگتے ہیں | مہنگا ہے |
| وقت ہی نہیں بچتا | وقت کی کمی ہے |
| مجھے تو کبھی کچھ نہیں ہوا | ایک عام خیال سے متفق نہیں |
| اب عادت ہو گئی ہے | رائے بدل گئی |
| دل وہیں لگتا ہے | جذباتی تعلق ہے |

## The routine

1. **Pause:** read every option; underline the word that makes it different.
2. **First hearing:** a 3–4 word summary per speaker, plus a sign: + / − / ± / →.
3. **Second hearing:** confirm; re-listen to uncertain speakers.
4. **After:** match sure ones first, cross them off, eliminate the rest; no blanks.

## The keyword trap

All six speakers share a topic, so options share its vocabulary. **Match the speaker's overall point, not
a shared word.** If the only reason for a match is one word in common, check again.

## Exam traps

- Choosing the positive half of a "yes, but" speaker.
- Taking a reported view (لوگ کہتے ہیں) as the speaker's own.
- Matching the speaker's old view when they have changed their mind.
- Using one option twice (when each is to be used once) and leaving a speaker unmatched.
- Leaving a blank instead of eliminating.

## What the exercise rewards

The exercise carries 6 marks across six speakers, so every speaker counts. What earns the match is
understanding the speaker's **position**: whether they are for or against, partly convinced, doubtful,
regretful or changed. A speaker who mentions a fact from an option without holding that view does not
match it. This is why Exercise 3 is the listening exercise most likely to catch students who listen
only for vocabulary.

## Worked example

**Topic:** school uniform. Four of the speakers are shown, with five options, so one option is not
needed. (The extracts are written for this page; on the day you would hear them.)

| Speaker | Heard | English gloss |
|---|---|---|
| 1 (girl) | پہلے مجھے وردی بالکل اچھی نہیں لگتی تھی، لیکن اب میں سوچتی ہوں کہ صبح کپڑے چننے کا وقت بچ جاتا ہے۔ | At first I didn't like the uniform at all, but now I think it saves the time spent choosing clothes in the morning. |
| 2 (boy) | لوگ کہتے ہیں کہ وردی سے سب برابر لگتے ہیں۔ مجھے نہیں لگتا۔ جوتوں اور بستوں سے فرق صاف نظر آتا ہے۔ | People say uniform makes everyone look equal. I don't think so. You can clearly see the difference from shoes and bags. |
| 3 (girl) | وردی تو اچھی ہے، لیکن گرمیوں میں یہ کپڑا بہت گرم ہوتا ہے۔ | The uniform is fine, but in summer this fabric is very hot. |
| 4 (boy) | کاش ہمیں ہفتے میں ایک دن اپنے کپڑے پہننے کی اجازت ہوتی! | I wish we were allowed to wear our own clothes one day a week! |

**Options**

- **A** اس کی رائے وقت کے ساتھ بدل گئی ہے۔ (Their view has changed over time.)
- **B** وہ ایک عام خیال سے متفق نہیں۔ (They disagree with a common idea.)
- **C** وہ وردی کو پسند کرتی ہے مگر ایک مسئلے کی شکایت کرتی ہے۔ (She likes the uniform but complains
  about one problem.)
- **D** وہ کسی تبدیلی کی خواہش رکھتا ہے۔ (He wishes for a change.)
- **E** وہ سمجھتا ہے کہ وردی مہنگی ہے۔ (He thinks uniform is expensive.)

**Answers and reasoning**

1. **A.** The signal is پہلے ... لیکن اب: the old dislike is replaced by a practical reason to accept it.
2. **B.** لوگ کہتے ہیں introduces the common idea; مجھے نہیں لگتا rejects it. The shoes and bags might
   tempt you towards E, but nothing is said about price: that is the keyword trap.
3. **C.** تو ہے، لیکن is a concession followed by a reservation (L3: both halves).
4. **D.** کاش signals a wish; the change he wants is never stated as a demand (L4: implied).

E is the extra option. If you had matched speaker 2 to E, you would have matched a topic (clothes and
what they cost) rather than a view.

## Step-by-step method in detail

1. **Read the options for their verbs and attitude words**: بدل گئی، متفق نہیں، شکایت، خواہش، فکر. These,
   not the topic nouns, separate the options.
2. **Translate each option into a sign** in the margin: → (changed), ✗ (disagrees), ± (yes, but),
   ★ (wish), ? (doubt).
3. **First hearing:** after each speaker, write the sign you hear and two or three key words.
4. **Second hearing:** confirm the sign and listen for the word that carries the turn: لیکن، اب، کاش، مجھے
   نہیں لگتا.
5. **Match the clear signs first**, cross off those options, then decide the doubtful speakers between
   what is left.

## Option vocabulary

| Urdu | English |
|---|---|
| شکایت | complaint |
| خواہش | wish, desire |
| فکر / تشویش | worry / concern |
| حمایت کرنا | to support |
| مخالفت کرنا | to oppose |
| تجربہ | experience |
| فائدہ / نقصان | advantage / disadvantage |
| یقین نہیں | not sure |
| مطمئن | satisfied |
| فیصلہ نہیں کر پایا | has not been able to decide |

## More exam traps

- Treating a speaker's description of a problem as a complaint when they go on to say it does not
  bother them (مجھے اس سے کوئی فرق نہیں پڑتا, "it makes no difference to me").
- Matching a girl speaker to an option written with a masculine verb, or the reverse, when the options
  are gendered: the verb ending can confirm or rule out a match.
- Missing a wish expressed with کاش or with ہوتا تو, because no verb such as چاہنا is used.

## Self-test

1. Which two assessment objectives does Exercise 3 test?
2. A speaker says: "یہ ٹھیک ہے کہ شہر میں سہولتیں زیادہ ہیں، لیکن یہاں سکون نہیں۔" Summarise their view.
3. What should you listen for after لوگ کہتے ہیں?
4. Why can the topic word not separate the speakers?
5. A speaker says "پہلے مجھے یہ بالکل پسند نہیں تھا، اب روز کرتا ہوں۔" Which view do you match?
6. What do you do with the last one or two speakers you are unsure about?
7. What does a speaker signal with "کاش ..."?
8. A speaker says: "سنا ہے کہ یہ جگہ خطرناک ہے، مگر میں تو کئی بار گئی ہوں اور کبھی کوئی مسئلہ نہیں
   ہوا۔" Which kind of option matches?
9. Give the Urdu for "complaint" and for "wish".
10. Why do the option verbs matter more than the option nouns?
11. An option reads "وہ اپنا فیصلہ نہیں کر پایا". What would you expect to hear?

**Answers:** 1. L3 (connections between ideas, opinions and attitudes) and L4 (what is implied but not
directly stated). 2. Accepts that the city has more facilities, but feels it lacks peace: a mixed view
with a reservation. 3. Whether the speaker goes on to accept or reject that reported view; their own
opinion usually follows. 4. Because the six speakers usually talk about the same topic, so they differ in
attitude, not subject. 5. The current one, after اب: he now likes it and does it every day; his view has
changed. 6. Cross off the options already used and choose by elimination; never leave a blank. 7. A wish or regret: something they would like to be
different. 8. One saying she disagrees with a common or reported view: سنا ہے gives the reported view and
مگر میں تو ... rejects it from her own experience. 9. شکایت; خواہش. 10. All the speakers share a topic,
so the nouns overlap; the verbs and attitude words (بدل گئی، متفق نہیں، شکایت) carry the position that
separates them. 11. Doubt or indecision, for example پتا نہیں or a speaker weighing two sides without
choosing.
