## Section A

**1.** Name, in Urdu, the three aspects of this research subject given in the specification, and translate each into English. **[3]**

**2.** State **four** rules from the specification that govern the independent research or its use in the exam. **[4]**

**3.** Give the Urdu for: (a) parallel systems (b) religious seminaries (c) scholarships (plural) (d) fundamental right (e) the poor (plural, as in the specification) **[5]**

**4.** Rewrite each sentence correctly.
(a) لڑکیوں کی تعلیم پر توجہ دیا جانا چاہیے۔
(b) سرکاری اسکولوں میں سہولیات کم ہے۔
(c) حکومت نے نئے اسکول بنائی۔ **[3]**

**5.** Explain why "free" schooling may still be out of reach for a poor family, giving **two** hidden costs. **[3]**

**6.** A student writes in Section C: "برطانیہ میں تمام بچوں کو مفت تعلیم ملتی ہے، اس لیے پاکستان کو بھی ایسا کرنا چاہیے۔" Explain why this sentence earns little credit for AO4. **[2]**

## Answers

**1.** تعلیم کے متوازی نظام, parallel systems of education [1]؛ لڑکیوں، لڑکوں کے لیے یکساں مواقع, equal opportunities for girls and boys [1]؛ غربا کے لئے بہتر مواقع, better opportunities for the poor [1]

**2.** Any four of the following, one mark each, to a maximum of [4]: all three aspects must be researched; the research must be based on Pakistan only; it must be independent, with no content or sources provided by teachers; it must draw on at least two authentic sources in Urdu; it must not use the Paper 2 literary text or film; no notes or dictionary may be taken into the exam; the answer should be about 320–370 words.

**3.** (a) متوازی نظام [1] (b) دینی مدارس [1] (c) وظائف [1] (d) بنیادی حق [1] (e) غربا [1]

**4.** (a) توجہ دی جانی چاہیے: توجہ is feminine [1] (b) سہولیات کم ہیں: plural subject [1] (c) اسکول بنائے: with نے the verb agrees with the masculine plural object [1]

**5.** Even without fees, schooling has other costs [1]; any two of: uniforms, books or stationery, transport, and the income the child could have earned by working [2].

**6.** For Theme 1 the response must be based on Pakistan only, and content on another country gains no marks [1]; the sentence also offers an opinion without research evidence about Pakistan [1].

**7.** Model: "In rural areas many parents do not send their daughters to school after primary, because there is no middle school nearby. Where a school is close and female teachers are available, the enrolment rate of girls is markedly better."

Marking: in rural areas [1]; many parents do not send their daughters to school [1]; after primary [1]; because there is no middle school nearby [1]; where a school is close [1]; and female teachers are available [1]; the enrolment rate of girls [1]; is markedly better [1].

**8.** Any three, one mark each: میری تحقیق کے مطابق؛ اپنی تحقیق کے دوران میں نے پڑھا کہ؛ ایک اخباری رپورٹ کے مطابق؛ میں نے جن ذرائع کا مطالعہ کیا ان سے پتا چلتا ہے کہ؛ ایک ٹی وی پروگرام میں بتایا گیا کہ.

**9.** Increase inequality: children in different systems receive different languages, curricula and prospects [1], so class divisions are reproduced from one generation to the next [1]. Widen access: private, non-profit schools and seminaries reach children whom government schools have not reached [1], for example by offering free places, food or lodging to poor children [1].

**10.** Improved sentence, for example: **عبارت سے معلوم ہوتا ہے کہ گاؤں کے اسکول میں اساتذہ کی شدید کمی ہے** [1], rephrased rather than copied [1]. Research link, for example: **میری تحقیق کے مطابق بہت سے دیہی اسکولوں میں ایک یا دو اساتذہ کئی جماعتوں کو پڑھاتے ہیں** [1], clearly signalled as research [1].

**11.** Marked on three levels-based grids, applied best-fit to the whole response: AO4, knowledge and understanding of society and culture through research [20]; AO2, understanding of and response to the unseen text [10]; AO3, accuracy and range of language [10]. Total 40.

**Indicative content.** Credit any well-argued response. Likely points:

- **From the text (AO2):** the stipend has kept Samina in school; the building is dilapidated and two classes share one room; the science teacher post has been vacant for two years; the teacher's view that access without quality will not let these pupils compete; the district officer's view that access had to come first.
- **From research (AO4):** cash support linked to attendance and what sources say about its effect; teacher shortages and absence in rural schools; the gap between government and private schools (parallel systems); girls' additional barriers in poor rural families; Article 25A and how far the state meets it.
- **Evaluation:** a strong answer accepts that access is a necessary first step but argues that without trained teachers, buildings and materials, unequal outcomes continue; or argues the opposite with evidence. It should reach a clear conclusion, for example that access and quality must be pursued together.

**Model-answer outline (in Urdu):** opening that cites the text's two views; paragraph 1 on access, research on attendance-linked support; paragraph 2 on quality, research on teacher shortages and the private–government gap; paragraph 3 on girls in poor families as a double disadvantage; conclusion: **رسائی پہلا قدم ہے، منزل نہیں**.
