These questions cover subtopic 1.16 Money of the Cambridge IGCSE Mathematics 0580 syllabus for examination in 2025, 2026 and 2027. The content is the same for Core and Extended, so every question suits both tiers. All prices and exchange rates are invented. Give money answers to the nearest cent unless the question says otherwise.

## Section A

**1.** (non-calculator)

**(a)** A calculator display shows 7.3 for an amount in dollars. Write the amount correctly. **[1]**

**(b)** Write 1250 cents in dollars. **[1]**

**2.** (non-calculator) Maya buys 3 sandwiches at $2.85 each and 2 drinks at $1.40 each. She pays with a $20 note. Find her change. **[2]**

**3.** (non-calculator) On one day, $1 = 0.85 euros.

**(a)** Convert $240 to euros. **[1]**

**(b)** Convert 153 euros to dollars. **[1]**

**4.** (calculator) A phone plan costs $12.99 per month. This includes 200 text messages. Each extra text costs $0.08. Leah sends 287 texts in one month. Find her bill. **[2]**

**5.** (non-calculator) A youth club has $150 to spend on concert tickets. Each ticket costs $13.50. Find the greatest number of tickets the club can buy. **[2]**

**6.** (calculator) Six people share a bill of $142.75 equally. Each person pays the same whole number of cents, and the full bill must be paid. How much does each person pay? **[2]**

## Section B

**7.** (calculator) A water company charges a standing charge of $9.60 per month plus $2.14 for each cubic metre of water used.

**(a)** At the start of a month the meter reads 1846 m³. At the end it reads 1873 m³. Find the bill for the month. **[3]**

**(b)** The next month's bill is $79.15. Find the amount of water used that month. **[2]**

**8.** (non-calculator) Ravi needs 8 notebooks. Notebooks cost $3.60 each in both shops below.

- Shop A: "Buy 3, get 1 free".
- Shop B: 20% off every notebook.

Which shop is cheaper for 8 notebooks, and by how much? **[4]**

**9.** (calculator) On one day, $1 = 0.80 pounds and $1 = 7.25 yuan.

**(a)** Convert 600 pounds to yuan. **[3]**

**(b)** Find the number of yuan for 1 pound. Give your answer correct to 3 significant figures. **[1]**

**10.** (calculator) A laptop costs $899 in one country and 3200 dirhams in another. On the same day, $1 = 3.67 dirhams. In which country is the laptop cheaper, and by how much in dollars? **[3]**

**11.** (calculator) A bureau charges 2% commission on the amount of money changed. The commission is taken in pounds before the exchange. Lina changes £500 into euros at £1 = 1.16 euros. How many euros does she receive? **[3]**

**12.** (calculator) Ben has $1500 for a holiday. Flights cost $640. A hotel costs 85 euros per night for 7 nights. On one day, $1 = 0.92 euros.

**(a)** Find the cost of the hotel in euros. **[1]**

**(b)** Find how much money Ben has left after paying for the flights and hotel. Give your answer in dollars. **[3]**

## Answers

**1. (a)** **$7.30** [1]

**(b)** **$12.50** [1] **[2]**

**2.** 3 × 2.85 + 2 × 1.40 = 8.55 + 2.80 = $11.35 [1]; 20 − 11.35 = **$8.65** [1] **[2]**

**3. (a)** 240 × 0.85 = **204 euros** [1]

**(b)** 153 ÷ 0.85 = **$180** [1] **[2]**

*Examiner insight:* in (b), 153 × 0.85 = 130.05 goes the wrong way. One dollar is worth less than one euro here, so the number of dollars must be larger than the number of euros.

**4.** Extra texts = 287 − 200 = 87, costing 87 × 0.08 = $6.96 [1]; bill = 12.99 + 6.96 = **$19.95** [1] **[2]**

**5.** 150 ÷ 13.50 = 11.1... [1]; **11 tickets** [1] **[2]**

**6.** 142.75 ÷ 6 = 23.7916... [1]; round **up** to **$23.80** so the bill is covered [1] **[2]**

*Examiner insight:* $23.79 is the nearest cent, but 6 × 23.79 = $142.74, which is 1 cent short. The context decides the direction of rounding.

**7. (a)** Water used = 1873 − 1846 = 27 m³ [1]; 27 × 2.14 = $57.78 [1]; bill = 9.60 + 57.78 = **$67.38** [1]

**(b)** 79.15 − 9.60 = $69.55 [1]; 69.55 ÷ 2.14 = **32.5 m³** [1] **[5]**

**8.** Shop A: 8 notebooks = 2 groups of 4, so pay for 6 [1]; 6 × 3.60 = $21.60 [1]; Shop B: 8 × 3.60 × 0.8 = $23.04 [1]; **Shop A** is cheaper by **$1.44** [1] **[4]**

*Examiner insight:* "buy 3, get 1 free" means one free in every 4 notebooks, not one free in every 3.

**9. (a)** 600 ÷ 0.80 = $750 [1]; 750 × 7.25 [1] = **5437.5 yuan** [1]

**(b)** 7.25 ÷ 0.80 = 9.0625 = **9.06 yuan** [1] **[4]**

**10.** 3200 ÷ 3.67 = 871.93... dollars [1]; 899 − 871.93... = 27.065... [1]; the laptop is cheaper in the **dirham country** by **$27.07** [1] **[3]**

**11.** Commission = 0.02 × 500 = £10 [1]; amount changed = £490 [1]; 490 × 1.16 = **568.40 euros** [1] **[3]**

*Examiner insight:* converting all £500 (580 euros) and then taking 2% off gives the same total here, 568.40 euros, because a percentage commission scales with the amount. With a fixed fee, the order would matter.

**12. (a)** 85 × 7 = **595 euros** [1]

**(b)** 595 ÷ 0.92 = 646.739... dollars [1]; 640 + 646.739... = 1286.739... [1]; 1500 − 1286.739... = **$213.26** [1] **[4]**

## Where marks are usually lost

- Writing $7.3 instead of $7.30.
- Converting in the wrong direction, as in Question 3(b).
- Rounding a shared bill to the nearest cent when it must be rounded up.
- Misreading "buy 3, get 1 free" as one free in every three.
- Forgetting the standing charge in a bill, or the included texts in a phone plan.
- Comparing prices in two currencies without converting one of them.
- Rounding the dollar value of the hotel before subtracting.

## Frequently asked questions

**When do I round money up rather than to the nearest cent?**
When the context needs a whole amount to be covered, such as a shared bill. For the number of items you can afford, round down.

**How do I convert between two currencies when no direct rate is given?**
Convert into the currency both rates share, keep the full calculator value, then convert into the second currency.

**Does the order of a commission and an exchange matter?**
For a percentage commission the result is the same. For a fixed fee, take the fee off in the currency it is charged in, before converting.
