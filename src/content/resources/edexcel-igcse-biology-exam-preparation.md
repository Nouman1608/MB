Pearson Edexcel International GCSE Biology (4BI1) is a linear qualification with two externally assessed, untiered written papers taken in the same series. Paper 1 (4BI1/1B) is 2 hours, 110 marks and 61.1% of the grade; Paper 2 (4BI1/2B) is 1 hour 15 minutes, 70 marks and 38.9%. Paper 1 assesses only the core content, while Paper 2 assesses all content, including the bold-referenced material. The five content areas are the nature and variety of living organisms, structures and functions in living organisms, reproduction and inheritance, ecology and the environment, and use of biological resources. Site resources include [Gas Exchange](/resources/edexcel-igcse-biology-4bi1-gas-exchange/), [Excretion](/resources/edexcel-igcse-biology-4bi1-excretion/) and [Inheritance](/resources/edexcel-igcse-biology-4bi1-inheritance/).

## Why the two papers differ

Both papers are available to every candidate; there is no Foundation or Higher entry. What differs is the content scope. Paper 1 draws only on the core content, so it is the paper in which every candidate can secure most of their marks by knowing the fundamentals well. Paper 2 can draw on everything, including the bold-referenced extension material. **Preparation priority**: build secure core knowledge first, then add the extra material on top, and avoid leaving the bold-referenced content until the final days, because it is examined in Paper 2 alongside core content.

## Paper 1: 2 hours, 110 marks

With 120 minutes for 110 marks you have 120 / 110 = about 1.09 minutes per mark. That is little more than a minute per mark, and since the paper carries 61.1% of the grade it deserves the largest share of revision time. **Preparation priority**: practise answering in full but concise sentences that match the marks, label diagrams accurately, and rehearse the standard practical investigations (for example, testing for nutrients, investigating enzymes or osmosis) so that you can describe a method and name variables. Because Paper 1 is the long paper, build stamina by doing a full two-hour practice under timed conditions at least once.

## Paper 2: 1 hour 15 minutes, 70 marks

Paper 2 gives 75 minutes for 70 marks, or 75 / 70 = about 1.07 minutes per mark. It is shorter, but the pace is tighter and the content scope wider. **Preparation priority**: practise switching rapidly between topics, and make sure you can handle questions that apply bold-referenced ideas to unfamiliar data. Read stimulus information carefully before writing.

## Weightings and arithmetic

The two papers total 110 + 70 = 180 marks and 120 + 75 = 195 minutes. The weightings come from the mark totals: 110 / 180 = 61.1% and 70 / 180 = 38.9%, which add up to 100%. Because the weightings follow the mark totals, every mark is worth the same fraction of the grade (100 / 180 = about 0.56% per mark) on either paper, so treat every mark equally and do not neglect Paper 2 just because it is shorter.

## Question styles and command words

Typical command words are state, name, describe, explain, suggest, calculate and compare. "Describe" asks what happens, and "explain" asks why, using biological reasoning. "Suggest" is used for unfamiliar contexts, where you apply a principle you know. Calculation questions may include percentages, magnification, rates and means. **Preparation priority**: for every topic, practise at least one data-handling question, because interpreting tables and graphs is a skill that recall alone does not develop.

## Worked practice scenario: percentage change in mass

Question (original): "A potato cylinder has an initial mass of 5.20 g. After 24 hours in a concentrated sugar solution its mass is 4.68 g. (a) Calculate the percentage change in mass. (b) Explain the change."

```
(a) Change in mass = 4.68 - 5.20 = -0.52 g
    Percentage change = (-0.52 / 5.20) x 100 = -10.0%
    The mass decreased by 10.0%.

(b) The sugar solution has a lower water potential than the
    potato cells (a more concentrated solution). Water moves out
    of the cells by osmosis, down a water potential gradient,
    across the partially permeable membrane, so the cylinder
    loses mass.
```

The sign and the unit matter: write "decrease of 10.0%" or "-10.0%", and always divide by the initial mass, not the final mass.

## Before/during exam checklist

- Know that Paper 1 is core content only and Paper 2 includes bold-referenced content.
- Plan most revision time around Paper 1's 110 marks.
- Practise full-length timed papers, especially the two-hour Paper 1.
- Revise the standard practicals and how to present results.
- Practise percentage change, magnification and rate calculations.
- Read each command word and the number of marks before writing.
- Label diagrams precisely and use biological terms.
- Use remaining time to check calculations and units.

## Self-test

1. What percentage of the grade is Paper 2?
2. Which paper assesses only the core content?
3. How many minutes per mark does Paper 1 allow?
4. How many marks do the two papers total?
5. In the worked scenario, what is the percentage change in mass?

**Answers:** 1. 38.9%. 2. Paper 1. 3. 120 / 110, about 1.09 minutes per mark. 4. 180 marks. 5. A decrease of 10.0%.

## Frequently asked questions

**How many papers are there in Edexcel IGCSE Biology 4BI1?**
Two: Paper 1 (4BI1/1B, 2 hours, 110 marks, 61.1%) and Paper 2 (4BI1/2B, 1 hour 15 minutes, 70 marks, 38.9%).

**Are there tiers?**
No. Both papers are untiered and available to all candidates.

**Is there a coursework component?**
The assessment structure is made up of the two externally assessed written papers described above.

**Which paper covers bold-referenced content?**
Paper 2 assesses all content, including bold-referenced material.

**When was the specification first assessed?**
First assessment was June 2019, and the current specification is Issue 3.
