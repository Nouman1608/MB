## The Qur'an — key facts

- Revealed over **23 years**, beginning in the Cave of Hira in 610 CE, through the angel **Jibril**.
- **114 surahs**, classified as **Makkan** (shorter, on tawhid, the afterlife, moral conduct) or **Madinan** (longer, on law, community and social order).
- Compiled under **Abu Bakr** after the Battle of Yamama, when many *huffaz* were killed; standardised under **Uthman**, who sent official copies to the main centres and had variants destroyed.

## Themes of the Qur'an

**Tawhid** (oneness of God) · prophethood · the afterlife and judgement · guidance for personal conduct · law and social justice · relations with earlier scriptures.

## The Prophet's life — sequence

```
570  Birth in Makkah
610  First revelation, Cave of Hira
613  Public preaching begins; persecution follows
615  First migration to Abyssinia
619  Year of Sorrow -- deaths of Khadijah and Abu Talib
622  HIJRA to Madinah -- start of the Islamic calendar
622  Constitution of Madinah
624  Battle of Badr      -- victory
625  Battle of Uhud      -- setback; archers left their post
627  Battle of the Trench -- siege withstood
628  Treaty of Hudaybiyyah
630  Conquest of Makkah  -- general amnesty granted
632  Farewell Sermon; death of the Prophet
```

## The first Islamic community

- **Constitution of Madinah** — defined rights and duties of Muslims, Jews and other groups; established Madinah as a single community with the Prophet as arbiter.
- **Muhajirun** (migrants from Makkah) were paired with the **Ansar** (helpers of Madinah) — a practical solution to destitution and a model of brotherhood.
- The **Farewell Sermon** set out equality regardless of race, the sanctity of life and property, rights of women, and the abolition of usury and blood feuds.

## Exam traps

- Don't retell a narrative when the question asks for **significance** — the second half carries the marks.
- Abu Bakr **collected**; Uthman **standardised**. Distinguish the two stages.
- Uhud was a **setback**, not a total defeat.
- The Hijra marks the start of the Islamic calendar, not the Prophet's birth.
- Always support a point with a specific event, date or Qur'anic reference.

## Self-test

1. Who compiled the Qur'an, and who standardised it?
2. Distinguish Makkan from Madinan surahs.
3. Why was the Constitution of Madinah significant?
4. What went wrong at Uhud?
5. Name three principles from the Farewell Sermon.

**Answers:** 1. Abu Bakr compiled it after Yamama; Uthman standardised it and distributed official copies. 2. Makkan are generally shorter and focus on tawhid, the afterlife and moral conduct; Madinan are longer and address law, community organisation and social order. 3. It bound Muslims, Jews and other groups into one community with defined rights and duties, and established the Prophet as arbiter — the first written constitution of the Islamic state. 4. Archers abandoned their position to collect spoils, allowing a counter-attack from the rear. 5. Any three: equality regardless of race, sanctity of life and property, rights of women, abolition of usury, ending of blood feuds.
