## Questions

**1.** State the principle used to predict molecular shape, including the repulsion order. **[3]**

**2.** Give the shape and bond angle of: (a) CO₂, (b) NH₃, (c) SF₆, (d) BeCl₂. **[4]**

**3.** Explain why the bond angle in H₂O (104.5°) is smaller than in NH₃ (107°). **[2]**

**4.** Explain why CCl₄ is non-polar even though each C–Cl bond is polar. **[2]**

**5.** State the two requirements for hydrogen bonding, and explain why HCl does not hydrogen bond despite chlorine being electronegative. **[4]**

**6.** The boiling points of the hydrides of group 16 are: H₂O 100 °C, H₂S −60 °C, H₂Se −41 °C, H₂Te −2 °C.

**(a)** Explain the trend from H₂S to H₂Te. **[2]**
**(b)** Explain why H₂O does not follow this trend. **[2]**

**7.** Explain, in structural terms, why ice is less dense than liquid water. **[3]**

---

## Answers

**1.** Electron pairs **repel** and arrange themselves **as far apart as possible** [1]; **lone pairs repel more strongly than bonding pairs** [1], following the order lone–lone > lone–bond > bond–bond [1].

**2. (a)** Linear, 180° [1]. **(b)** Trigonal pyramidal, 107° [1]. **(c)** Octahedral, 90° [1]. **(d)** Linear, 180° [1].

**3.** Water has **two lone pairs** whereas ammonia has one [1]; each lone pair **repels more strongly than a bonding pair**, so two lone pairs compress the bond angle further [1].

**4.** The molecule is **tetrahedral and symmetrical** [1], so the four bond dipoles are equal and **cancel**, giving no overall dipole [1].

**5.** Hydrogen bonded **directly to N, O or F** [1], and a **lone pair on an N, O or F** of a neighbouring molecule to accept it [1].
HCl fails because **chlorine is not sufficiently electronegative** and, crucially, is **not one of N, O or F** [1] — its lone pairs are too diffuse over a larger atom to accept a hydrogen bond effectively [1].

**6. (a)** Boiling point **increases** from H₂S to H₂Te [1] because the molecules have **more electrons**, so the induced dipole–induced dipole forces between them are stronger [1].

**(b)** Water has **hydrogen bonding** between molecules [1], which is much stronger than the induced dipole forces in the others, so far more energy is needed to separate the molecules [1].

**7.** Each water molecule forms **four hydrogen bonds** in a **tetrahedral arrangement** [1], creating an **open lattice containing holes** [1]. On melting the lattice partly collapses, so the molecules pack more closely and the liquid is denser [1].

## Where marks are usually lost

- Giving the shape without accounting for lone pairs.
- Saying a molecule is polar because its bonds are polar, ignoring symmetry.
- Claiming HCl hydrogen bonds.
- Explaining ice's density without "open lattice" and "tetrahedral".
