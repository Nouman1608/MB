## Answers

**1.** ہجرت کی سیاسی اور معاشی وجوہات, political and economic reasons for emigration [1]؛ اعلیٰ تعلیم یافتہ طبقے کا ترکِ وطن, emigration of the highly educated [1]؛ پاکستانی معاشرے پر وطن لوٹنے والوں کے مثبت اثرات, positive effects of returnees on Pakistani society [1]

**2.** (a) political instability [1] (b) brain drain [1] (c) the highly educated class [1] (d) those who return home, returnees [1] (e) a favourable environment [1]

**3.** Economic: (a) inflation [1] and (c) the wage gap [1]. Political: (b) political instability [1] and (d) fear of terrorism [1].

**4.** (a) چھوڑ دیا: with نے the verb agrees with ملک, masculine [1] (b) کھولیں: agrees with دکانیں, feminine plural [1] (c) اہم ہیں: ترسیلاتِ زر is plural [1]

**5.** Political instability creates uncertainty [1]; uncertainty discourages investment, so fewer jobs are created [1]; with fewer jobs and lower pay, people seek work abroad [1].

**6.** Theme 3 research must be about Pakistan, and content on another country's society gains no marks [1]; the paragraph would only earn credit if it explained the effect on emigration from Pakistan, for example as a pull factor [1].

**7.** Model: "Every year thousands of young doctors and engineers go abroad in search of a better future. The nation's money has been spent on their education, so some people regard this as a national loss. But if these same people return after gaining experience, they can benefit the country even more than before."

Marking: every year [1]; thousands of young doctors and engineers [1]; go abroad in search of a better future [1]; the nation's money has been spent on their education [1]; so some people [1]; regard this as a national loss [1]; but if these same people [1]; return after gaining experience [1]; they can benefit the country even more than before [1].

**8.** Any four, one mark each with example: investment and enterprise (savings used to start a business); skills transfer (trade or professional skills taught locally); founding institutions (clinics, schools, firms); new ideas and civic attitudes; philanthropy (funding hospitals, relief or medical camps) [4].

**9.** Any two, one mark each: difficulty readjusting; qualifications or experience not recognised; return forced by job loss rather than choice; lack of a supportive environment leading people to leave again.

**10.** Text sentence, for example: **عبارت میں ایک ماہر امراضِ قلب کا ذکر ہے جو سہولیات کی کمی کی وجہ سے واپس نہیں آنا چاہتیں** [1], rephrased and grammatically accurate, including feminine respectful plural [1]. Research sentence, for example: **میری تحقیق کے مطابق بیرونِ ملک مقیم کئی پاکستانی ڈاکٹر آن لائن تربیت کے ذریعے مقامی اسپتالوں کی مدد کرتے ہیں** [1], clearly signalled as research and about Pakistan [1].

**11.** Marked on three levels-based grids, applied best-fit to the whole response: AO4, knowledge and understanding of society and culture through research [20]; AO2, understanding of and response to the unseen text [10]; AO3, accuracy and range of language [10]. Total 40.

**Indicative content.**

- **From the text (AO2):** Rashid's twenty years in Saudi Arabia and his transport business employing fifteen local people; his daughter, a cardiologist in Britain, with no plans to return because of facilities and an opaque promotion system; her annual free medical camps and online mentoring of local doctors; Rashid's closing contrast between bringing back money and sharing knowledge.
- **From research (AO4):** political and economic reasons professionals give for leaving; the cost of losing doctors, engineers or academics in a named sector; remittances and investment; examples of emigrants supporting Pakistan from abroad and of returnees founding institutions; schemes aimed at overseas Pakistanis and how effective sources judge them.
- **Evaluation:** strong answers reject **نقصان کے سوا کچھ نہیں** (nothing but a loss) as too absolute: the loss of skills is real, but links, philanthropy and return can turn emigrants into an asset; or argue that the loss outweighs the gains, with evidence. A clear conclusion is required.

**Model-answer outline (in Urdu):** opening that names both figures in the text; paragraph 1 on the real loss (research on shortages, the daughter's reasons); paragraph 2 on contribution from abroad (medical camps, online mentoring, research example); paragraph 3 on returnees (Rashid, research example); conclusion: **نقصان ضرور ہے، مگر رابطہ قائم رہے تو یہی لوگ اثاثہ بن جاتے ہیں**.
