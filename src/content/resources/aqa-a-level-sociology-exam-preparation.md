AQA A-Level Sociology (7192) is linear and not tiered, assessed by three written papers of 2 hours each, 80 marks each and one third of the A-level, 240 marks in total. Paper 1 is Education with Theory and Methods, Paper 2 is Topics in Sociology, and Paper 3 is Crime and Deviance with Theory and Methods. Revise alongside our pages on [the role of education and differential achievement](/resources/a-level-aqa-sociology-role-of-education-and-differential-achievement/), [methods in context](/resources/a-level-sociology-methods-in-context/) and [crime and deviance](/resources/a-level-aqa-sociology-crime-and-deviance/).

## Paper 1: Education with Theory and Methods

Paper 1 is 2 hours and 80 marks, all content compulsory. The marks are divided into Education (50 marks), Methods in Context (20 marks) and Theory and Methods (10 marks). **How to spend the time:** education dominates the paper, so most of your time and revision should go there, but do not neglect the two smaller sections, because the Methods in Context question needs you to apply method strengths and limitations to a specific piece of research in the education setting. Practise linking method to context rather than reciting general evaluation.

## Paper 2: Topics in Sociology

Paper 2 is 2 hours and 80 marks, in two sections of 40 marks. For Section A you answer on one topic chosen from Culture and Identity, Families and Households, Health, or Work, Poverty and Welfare. For Section B you answer on one topic chosen from Beliefs in Society, Global Development, The Media, or Stratification and Differentiation. **How to spend the time:** with both sections worth 40 marks, split the paper evenly, about 60 minutes each. Because you choose one topic per section, a narrow revision plan covering your two topics in depth is workable, but check which topics your centre has taught before committing.

## Paper 3: Crime and Deviance with Theory and Methods

Paper 3 is 2 hours and 80 marks, recorded as 33.34% so that the weightings total exactly 100. All content is compulsory: Crime and Deviance (50 marks) and Theory and Methods (30 marks). **How to spend the time:** Theory and Methods is a larger share here than on Paper 1, so revise the main perspectives and the debate about the nature of sociological research with the same care as the crime content.

## Timing and marks-per-minute

- Each paper: 120 minutes / 80 marks = 1.5 minutes per mark.
- Paper 1: Education 50 x 1.5 = 75 minutes; Methods in Context 20 x 1.5 = 30 minutes; Theory and Methods 10 x 1.5 = 15 minutes. Check: 75 + 30 + 15 = 120.
- Paper 2: each 40-mark section 40 x 1.5 = 60 minutes.
- Paper 3: Crime and Deviance 50 x 1.5 = 75 minutes; Theory and Methods 30 x 1.5 = 45 minutes. Check: 75 + 45 = 120.
- Qualification total: 3 x 80 = 240 marks.

## Command words and question styles

Sociology questions reward a clear ladder of skills. "Outline" and "Identify" need brief, accurate points. "Explain" needs a point and a developed reason. "Analyse" asks you to break an idea or a piece of source material into parts and show how they connect. "Evaluate" needs a balanced judgement using concepts, studies and competing perspectives. Name sociologists and concepts accurately, and always answer the specific question rather than writing down everything you know on the topic.

## Worked practice scenario: planning and writing an essay paragraph

**Question (original):** "Evaluate the view that material deprivation is the main reason for differences in educational achievement between social classes."

**Plan:**

```
Intro   : define material deprivation; state a line of argument
Para 1  : for - costs of resources, housing and diet affect attendance and
          preparation; link to the concept of cultural capital
Para 2  : for - the cost of higher education may deter choices
Para 3  : against - cultural deprivation and the role of parental attitudes
Para 4  : against - school processes: labelling, streaming, the hidden curriculum
Para 5  : counter-view - the interaction of factors rather than one main cause
Conclude: a reasoned judgement answering "main reason"
```

**Model paragraph (Para 1):** Material deprivation can affect achievement by limiting the resources available to a child. A pupil without a quiet place to study, a computer or reliable access to the internet may find homework harder to complete, and poorer housing and diet may affect attendance and concentration. This supports the view that class differences in achievement partly originate outside school in unequal living standards. However, this explanation on its own does not account for why some pupils from low-income households achieve well, which is why the argument must be weighed against cultural and in-school explanations before a judgement is reached.

## Before/during exam checklist

- Confirm the two Paper 2 topics your centre has taught and revise those in depth.
- Make a one-page summary of each major perspective with named sociologists.
- Practise timed essays at 1.5 minutes per mark.
- Learn the key strengths and limitations of each research method.
- For Methods in Context, always name the setting and the research issue.
- Plan every extended answer in two minutes before writing.
- Write a conclusion that answers the question set.
- Check the clock at each section boundary.
