These notes condense syllabus section **1.1** of IB DP Mathematics: Analysis and Approaches (first
assessment 2021), common to SL and HL: operations with numbers in the form a × 10ᵏ, where 1 ≤ a < 10 and k is
an integer. For worked examples and explanations, see the
[section 1.1 study guide](/resources/ib-ib-dp-mathematics-analysis-and-approaches-dp-mathematics-analysis-and-approaches-ib-dp-mathematics-analysis-and-approaches-1-1/);
then practise with the
[section 1.1 practice questions](/resources/ib-ib-dp-mathematics-analysis-and-approaches-dp-mathematics-analysis-and-approaches-ib-dp-mathematics-analysis-and-approaches-1-1-practice/).

## The rules

| Operation | Method | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Multiply | Multiply the a values, **add** the powers | (2 × 10³)(4 × 10⁵) = 8 × 10⁸ |
| Divide | Divide the a values, **subtract** the powers | (6 × 10²) ÷ (3 × 10⁻⁴) = 2 × 10⁶ |
| Add / subtract | Rewrite with the **same power** first | 5 × 10⁴ + 3 × 10³ = 5.3 × 10⁴ |
| Power | Raise a and the power of 10 | (2 × 10⁻³)³ = 8 × 10⁻⁹ |
| Root | Make the power divisible by the root, then root both parts | √(4.9 × 10⁷) = √(49 × 10⁶) = 7 × 10³ |

Then **adjust**: if a ≥ 10, divide a by 10 and add 1 to k; if a < 1, multiply a by 10 and subtract 1 from k.

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| a × 10ᵏ form | 1 ≤ a < 10, k ∈ ℤ (often called standard form or scientific notation) |
| Front number, a | The decimal part; carries all the significant figures |
| Index, k | The integer power of 10; sets the size of the number |
| Calculator notation | A display such as 3.2E−7; not acceptable as a final answer |
| Significant figures | Digits of a that carry meaning; IB default is 3 s.f. |
| Order of magnitude | The power of 10, used to judge size and check answers |

## Converting

- Large number → positive k: 48 300 000 = 4.83 × 10⁷.
- Number between 0 and 1 → negative k: 0.000 0592 = 5.92 × 10⁻⁵.
- Number between 1 and 10 → k = 0: 7.4 = 7.4 × 10⁰.
- Decimal point moves left in a → k goes up; moves right → k goes down.

## Worked reminder

(9.6 × 10⁻²) ÷ (3.2 × 10⁴): 9.6 ÷ 3.2 = 3 and 10⁻²⁻⁴ = 10⁻⁶, so the answer is **3 × 10⁻⁶**. No adjustment is
needed because 3 already lies between 1 and 10.

## Ordering

1. Compare k: larger k means larger number (for positive numbers).
2. If k is equal, compare a.

Example: 8.9 × 10⁻⁶ < 1.2 × 10⁻⁵ < 3.4 × 10⁻⁵.

## Paper 1 or Paper 2?

| Paper 1 (no technology) | Paper 2 (technology required) |
|---|---|
| Exact multiplication, division, powers and simple roots | Contextual calculations with awkward values |
| Adding with a common power | Converting GDC output into the form |
| Estimating sizes | Rounding a to 3 s.f. |

HL Paper 3 also allows technology.

## Accuracy

- Give answers **exactly or to 3 s.f.** unless told otherwise.
- In a × 10ᵏ, all significant figures sit in a: 0.004 718 → 4.72 × 10⁻³ (3 s.f.).
- Keep full values until the last step.

## Must-know distinctions

| Looks similar | Difference |
|---|---|
| 3 × 10⁴ + 2 × 10⁴ and (3 × 10⁴)(2 × 10⁴) | The sum is 5 × 10⁴; the product is 6 × 10⁸ |
| 4.0 × 10³ and 4 × 10³ | Equal in value; 4.0 × 10³ signals 2 significant figures |
| 10⁻³ and −10³ | 0.001 is a small positive number; −1000 is a large negative number |
| Rounding a and rounding k | Only a is rounded; k is an exact integer |
| Estimate and answer | An estimate checks size; the exam answer needs the accuracy asked for |

## Exam traps

- **Calculator notation** in the final answer (for example 6.1E+12) loses the mark; write 6.1 × 10¹².
- **Out-of-range a**: 0.5 × 10⁹ and 45 × 10³ are not in the form.
- **Adding powers in a sum**: 2 × 10⁵ + 3 × 10⁵ = 5 × 10⁵.
- **Sign slips** with negative powers: 10⁴ ÷ 10⁻³ = 10⁷.
- **Wrong adjustment direction**: 0.8 × 10⁻² = 8 × 10⁻³, not 8 × 10⁻¹.
- **Odd power under a square root**: rewrite 10⁹ as 10 × 10⁸ first.

## Quick self-test

1. Is 10.2 × 10⁴ in the required form? If not, correct it.
2. Write 0.000 36 in the form a × 10ᵏ.
3. Calculate (5 × 10⁶)(7 × 10⁻²).
4. Calculate (1.2 × 10⁵) ÷ (4 × 10⁻³).
5. Calculate 6.2 × 10⁵ + 4 × 10⁴.
6. Calculate (5 × 10⁻⁴)².
7. Calculate √(3.6 × 10⁵).
8. Write 0.06 × 10⁸ in the required form.
9. Order 4.1 × 10³, 9.9 × 10², 1.05 × 10³ from smallest.
10. A GDC shows 2.54787E−8. Write this to 3 s.f. in the required form.
11. Estimate (6.1 × 10⁸) ÷ (2.9 × 10³) to one significant figure.
12. What must be true of k?

### Answers

1. No; 1.02 × 10⁵.
2. 3.6 × 10⁻⁴.
3. 35 × 10⁴ = 3.5 × 10⁵.
4. 0.3 × 10⁸ = 3 × 10⁷.
5. 6.2 × 10⁵ + 0.4 × 10⁵ = 6.6 × 10⁵.
6. 25 × 10⁻⁸ = 2.5 × 10⁻⁷.
7. √(36 × 10⁴) = 6 × 10².
8. 6 × 10⁶.
9. 9.9 × 10², 1.05 × 10³, 4.1 × 10³.
10. 2.55 × 10⁻⁸.
11. About 6 × 10⁸ ÷ 3 × 10³ = 2 × 10⁵.
12. It is an integer.
