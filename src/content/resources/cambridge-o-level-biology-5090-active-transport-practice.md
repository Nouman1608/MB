## Section A

**1.** Which description of active transport is correct?

A: movement of water from high to low water potential through a membrane

B: movement of particles from high to low concentration, using no energy

C: movement of particles from low to high concentration, using energy from respiration

D: movement of particles from low to high concentration, using no energy

**[1]**

**2.** Which cell would be expected to carry out most active transport?

A: a cell with a few mitochondria and a thick cell wall

B: a root hair cell with many mitochondria

C: a dead xylem vessel

D: a red blood cell in a capillary

**[1]**

**3.** State where the energy for active transport comes from. **[1]**

**4.** Define active transport. **[3]**

**5.** Name **two** mineral ions that root hair cells absorb by active transport and state one use of each. **[4]**

## Section B

**6.** Root hair cells in a barley plant contain 0.60 millimoles per dm cubed (mmol per dm cubed) of nitrate ions. The soil water around them contains 0.03 mmol per dm cubed.

**(a)** Calculate how many times greater the concentration in the root hair cell is than in the soil water. **[1]**

**(b)** Explain why diffusion alone cannot bring nitrate into the root hair cell. **[2]**

**(c)** Name the process that does bring the nitrate into the cell. **[1]**

**(d)** Explain why the root hair cell needs a large number of mitochondria for this process. **[2]**

**7.** A student placed roots in a solution of potassium ions. One set of roots was in air and the other was in nitrogen gas, which contains no oxygen. The table shows the uptake of ions.

| Condition | Uptake of potassium (units per hour) |
|---|---|
| In air | 6.0 |
| In nitrogen gas | 0.9 |

**(a)** Calculate the percentage decrease in uptake when oxygen is removed. Show your working. **[2]**

**(b)** Explain the results. **[3]**

**(c)** Predict the effect on uptake of lowering the temperature of the solution, and give a reason. **[2]**

**8.** Compare diffusion and active transport. **[4]**

**9.** A gardener finds that a pot plant stands in water, and its leaves turn yellow. Suggest an explanation in terms of ion uptake. **[3]**

**10.** State **two** reasons why a root hair cell is well adapted for taking in ions and water from soil. **[2]**

**11.** A student states: "Active transport is the same as diffusion because both move particles across a membrane." Evaluate this statement, using one similarity and two differences. **[3]**

---

## Answers

**1.** C [1]. **[1]**

**2.** B [1]. **[1]**

**3.** Respiration [1]. **[1]**

**4.** The movement of molecules or ions into or out of a cell through the cell membrane [1], from a region of lower concentration to a region of higher concentration (against the concentration gradient) [1], using energy released during respiration [1]. **[3]**

**5.** Nitrate [1] for making amino acids and so proteins [1]. Magnesium [1] for making chlorophyll [1]. **[4]**

**6.**

**(a)** 0.60 divided by 0.03 = 20 times greater [1].

**(b)** The concentration is higher inside the cell than in the soil water [1], so diffusion would move nitrate out of the cell, down the gradient, instead of into it [1].

**(c)** Active transport [1].

**(d)** Mitochondria carry out aerobic respiration, which releases energy [1]. Active transport uses this energy, so more mitochondria means more energy for moving ions against the gradient [1]. **[6]**

**7.**

**(a)** Decrease = 6.0 - 0.9 = 5.1 [1]. Percentage = 5.1 divided by 6.0 x 100 = 85 per cent [1].

**(b)** Without oxygen, aerobic respiration cannot release much energy [1]. Active transport needs energy from respiration [1], so the uptake of potassium falls [1].

**(c)** Uptake decreases [1], because the enzyme-controlled reactions of respiration are slower, so less energy is released for active transport [1]. **[7]**

**8.** Any four of: diffusion moves particles down a concentration gradient, active transport moves them against it [1]; diffusion needs no energy from respiration, active transport does [1]; diffusion continues without oxygen, active transport slows [1]; diffusion ends with equal concentrations, active transport can build a concentration difference [1]; both move molecules or ions across membranes [1]. **[4]**

**9.** Waterlogged soil contains little oxygen [1]. The roots cannot respire aerobically, so less energy is released for active transport [1]. Less magnesium (needed for chlorophyll) and nitrate are taken in, so the leaves are yellow [1]. **[3]**

**10.** Any two of: the root hair gives a large surface area [1]; many mitochondria release energy for active transport [1]; thin wall and membrane give a short distance [1]. **[2]**

**11.** Similarity: both move particles across a cell membrane [1]. Differences, any two of: diffusion is down the gradient and active transport is against it [1]; active transport needs energy from respiration and diffusion does not [1]; active transport stops without oxygen but diffusion does not [1]. The statement is therefore incorrect, because they are different processes [accept as part of a full answer]. **[3]**

## Where marks are usually lost

- Writing "uses energy" without saying it is released by **respiration**.
- Describing the movement as "from high to low concentration".
- Leaving out the link between mitochondria and the energy supply.
- Giving the uses of the ions without naming the compound they help to make.
- Confusing osmosis (water only) with active transport (ions and molecules).
