These questions cover section 6.2, Right-angled triangles, of the Cambridge O Level Mathematics (Syllabus D) 4024 syllabus for 2025, 2026 and 2027. The methods are explained in the [right-angled triangles study guide](/resources/cambridge-o-level-mathematics-4024-right-angled-triangles/), and the [right-angled triangles revision notes](/resources/cambridge-o-level-mathematics-4024-right-angled-triangles-revision-notes/) summarise them. Give lengths to 3 significant figures and angles to 1 decimal place unless told otherwise. Diagrams are not drawn to scale.

## Section A

**1.** A right-angled triangle has hypotenuse 15 cm and an angle of 40°. Find the side opposite the 40° angle. **[2]**

**2.** In a right-angled triangle, the side adjacent to an angle of 52° is 9 cm. Find the hypotenuse. **[2]**

**3.** In a right-angled triangle, the side opposite angle θ is 6 cm and the side adjacent to θ is 11 cm. Find θ. **[2]**

**4.** In a right-angled triangle, the hypotenuse is 13 cm and a shorter side is 5 cm. Find the angle opposite the 5 cm side. **[2]**

**5.** From a point on level ground 50 m from the foot of a tower, the angle of elevation of the top is 38°. Find the height of the tower. **[2]**

**6.** From the top of a vertical cliff 120 m high, the angle of depression of a boat is 18°. Find the horizontal distance from the boat to the foot of the cliff. **[3]**

## Section B

**7.** A ship sails 35 km on a bearing of 125°. Find how far east and how far south of its starting point it finishes. **[4]**

**8.** An isosceles triangle has two equal sides of 15 cm and a base of 18 cm.

**(a)** Find each base angle.

**(b)** Find the height of the triangle.

**(c)** Find the area of the triangle. **[4]**

**9.** In triangle ABC, angle B = 90°, AB = 7 cm and BC = 24 cm. Find AC, angle BAC and angle ACB. **[4]**

**10.** The point P is 9 cm from A. The line AP makes an angle of 40° with the line AB. Find the shortest distance from P to the line AB. **[2]**

**11.** A ramp makes an angle of 12° with the horizontal ground and rises 1.4 m. Find the length of the ramp. **[3]**

**12.** The angle of elevation of the top of a building from a point A on level ground is 25°. From a point B, 15 m closer to the building and in line with A and the foot of the building, the angle of elevation is 40°. Find the height of the building. **[5]**

**13.** A wire from the top of a vertical pole 8 m high is fixed to the ground and makes an angle of 62° with the ground. Find the length of the wire and the distance of its foot from the base of the pole. **[3]**

**14.** A walker goes 6 km due east and then 8 km due north. Find the distance from the start and the bearing of the finishing point from the start. **[3]**

**15.** A straight line joins (0, 0) to (5, 12). Find the angle between the line and the x-axis, and the length of the line. **[3]**

## Answers

**1.** sin 40° = O/15 [1]. O = 15 × sin 40° = **9.64 cm** [1].

**2.** cos 52° = 9/H [1]. H = 9 ÷ cos 52° = **14.6 cm** [1].

**3.** tan θ = 6/11 [1]. θ = tan⁻¹(6/11) = **28.6°** [1].

**4.** sin θ = 5/13 [1]. θ = sin⁻¹(5/13) = **22.6°** [1].

**5.** tan 38° = h/50 [1]. h = 50 × tan 38° = **39.1 m** [1].

**6.** The angle at the boat inside the triangle is 18° (alternate angles) [1]. tan 18° = 120/d [1]. d = 120 ÷ tan 18° = **369 m** [1].

**7.** East = 35 × sin 125° [1] = **28.7 km** [1]. South = 35 × cos 55° (the angle from the south direction is 180° − 125° = 55°) [1] = **20.1 km** [1].

**8. (a)** Half the base = 9 cm. cos θ = 9/15 [1]. θ = **53.1°** [1].

**(b)** h = √(15² − 9²) = **12 cm** [1].

**(c)** Area = 1/2 × 18 × 12 = **108 cm²** [1].

**9.** AC = √(49 + 576) = **25 cm** [1]. tan A = 24/7 [1], so angle BAC = **73.7°** [1]. Angle ACB = 90° − 73.7° = **16.3°** [1].

**10.** The shortest distance is the perpendicular distance [1]. 9 × sin 40° = **5.79 cm** [1].

**11.** sin 12° = 1.4/L [1]. L = 1.4 ÷ sin 12° [1] = **6.73 m** [1].

**12.** Let the distance from A to the building be d. Then h = d tan 25° and h = (d − 15) tan 40° [1][1]. d tan 25° = (d − 15) tan 40° gives d(tan 40° − tan 25°) = 15 tan 40° [1]. d = 15 × 0.8391 ÷ (0.8391 − 0.4663) = 33.76 [1]. h = 33.76 × tan 25° = **15.7 m** [1].

**13.** sin 62° = 8/L [1]. L = 8 ÷ sin 62° = **9.06 m** [1]. Distance = 8 ÷ tan 62° = **4.25 m** [1].

**14.** Distance = √(36 + 64) = **10 km** [1]. The angle from north = tan⁻¹(6/8) = 36.9° [1]. The bearing is **037°** [1].

**15.** tan θ = 12/5 [1]. θ = **67.4°** [1]. Length = √(25 + 144) = **13** [1].

## Where marks are usually lost

- Using the wrong ratio because the sides were labelled from the wrong angle.
- Having the calculator in the wrong angle mode.
- Dividing the wrong way round when the unknown is the hypotenuse.
- Rounding the ratio before using the inverse function.
- Confusing elevation with depression, or a bearing with an angle from east.
