## The wars compared

| War | Cause | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 1948–49 | Arab rejection of partition and Israel's independence | Israel survives and expands; 700,000 refugees |
| 1956 | Nationalisation of the canal; fedayeen raids | Anglo-French withdrawal; Nasser strengthened |
| 1967 | Closure of Tiran; UN force removed; Arab build-up | Israel takes Sinai, Gaza, West Bank, Golan |
| 1973 | Arab desire to regain land and pride | Early Arab gains; Israel recovers; path to diplomacy |
| 1982 | PLO bases in Lebanon | PLO expelled to Tunis; wider war |
| 2008–09 | Rocket fire from Gaza; Hamas rule | Heavy Palestinian losses; no settlement |

## Worked example: explain two ways different

*Explain two ways in which the territory controlled by Israel in 1949 was different from that controlled
in 1967 (after the Six-Day War).* (6 marks)

1. **Size:** in 1949 Israel held the land within the armistice lines; after June 1967 it also occupied
   Sinai, Gaza, the West Bank, East Jerusalem and the Golan Heights.
2. **Population:** in 1949 Israel's territory had a Jewish majority; after 1967 it ruled over a large
   Palestinian Arab population in the occupied territories.

## Essay plan in outline

*"Disputed territory was the main cause of conflict in the Middle East in the years 1917–2009."*

- **For:** competing claims after Balfour; partition (1947); occupation after 1967; settlements; Jerusalem.
- **Against:** superpower rivalry; Arab nationalism and Nasser; refugees; terrorism and extremism on both sides.
- **Judgement:** most other causes grew out of, or were made worse by, the struggle for land.

## Common mistakes and exam traps

- **Confusing Black September (1970) in Jordan with the group that attacked Munich (1972).**
- **Treating Suez as an Arab–Israeli war only.** Britain, France and the superpowers are central.
- **Saying 1973 was an Arab victory.** It began well for Egypt and Syria, but Israel recovered.
- **Leaving out terrorism as a theme.** It is named in the specification and runs from the 1930s to 2009.
- **Inventing casualty figures.** Use rounded figures only when certain.

## Frequently asked questions

**Why did Britain give up Palestine?** Rising violence, cost, post-war weakness and US pressure.

**Why did Egypt make peace in 1979?** To regain Sinai, gain US aid and end the costs of war.

**What did Oslo achieve?** Mutual recognition and the Palestinian Authority, but not a final settlement.
