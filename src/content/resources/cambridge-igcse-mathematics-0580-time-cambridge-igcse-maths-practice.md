These questions cover subtopic 1.15 Time of the Cambridge IGCSE Mathematics 0580 syllabus for examination in 2025, 2026 and 2027. The content is the same for Core and Extended, so every question suits both tiers. Each question is labelled (non-calculator) or (calculator). The places named in Questions 8 and 9 are fictional.

## Section A

**1.** (non-calculator)

**(a)** Write 7.40 p.m. in the 24-hour clock. **[1]**

**(b)** Write 00 20 in the 12-hour clock. **[1]**

**2.** (non-calculator)

**(a)** Write 3.15 hours in hours and minutes. **[1]**

**(b)** Write 2 hours 42 minutes in hours, as a decimal. **[1]**

**3.** (non-calculator) A lesson starts at 13 50 and ends at 15 05. Find the length of the lesson in minutes. **[2]**

**4.** (non-calculator) A baby is 100 days old.

**(a)** Write this age in complete weeks and days. **[1]**

**(b)** Write this age in hours. **[1]**

**5.** (non-calculator) A night train leaves at 23 48 and arrives at 04 13 the next morning. Find the length of the journey. **[2]**

**6.** (non-calculator)

**(a)** Find the number of seconds in one day. **[1]**

**(b)** Write 1 hour as a fraction of 1 week, in its simplest form. **[1]**

## Section B

**7.** (non-calculator) Part of a ferry timetable is shown. A dash means the ferry does not stop there.

| Ferry | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Harbour | 06 40 | 09 15 | 12 30 | 16 05 |
| North Bay | 07 25 | -- | 13 15 | 16 50 |
| Lighthouse | 08 10 | 10 35 | 14 00 | 17 35 |

**(a)** How long does ferry 1 take to travel from Harbour to Lighthouse? **[1]**

**(b)** How much quicker is ferry 2 than ferry 3 from Harbour to Lighthouse? **[2]**

**(c)** Tom arrives at North Bay at 12 50. How long must he wait for a ferry to Lighthouse, and at what time will he arrive there? **[2]**

**8.** (non-calculator) Quorra is 5 hours 30 minutes ahead of Belmar.

**(a)** It is 10 45 in Belmar. What time is it in Quorra? **[1]**

**(b)** A flight leaves Quorra at 02 10 local time. The flight takes 9 hours 25 minutes. Find the local time in Belmar when the flight lands. **[3]**

**9.** (non-calculator) A flight leaves Belmar at 20 40 local time and lands in Quorra at 13 05 local time the next day. Quorra is 5 hours 30 minutes ahead of Belmar. Find the length of the flight. **[3]**

**10.** (calculator) A computer program runs for 200 000 seconds. Write this time in days, hours, minutes and seconds. **[3]**

**11.** (calculator) Ali works from 08 45 to 17 15, Monday to Friday. Each day he has a 45-minute unpaid lunch break. He is paid $14.20 per hour.

**(a)** Find the number of hours he is paid for each week. **[2]**

**(b)** Find his weekly pay. **[2]**

**12.** (non-calculator) In this question, 1 year = 365 days.

**(a)** Find the number of hours in a year. **[1]**

**(b)** A clock gains 3 seconds every day. After how many days will it be 5 minutes fast? **[2]**

## Answers

**1. (a)** **19 40** [1]

**(b)** **12.20 a.m.** [1] **[2]**

**2. (a)** 0.15 × 60 = 9, so **3 hours 9 minutes** [1]

**(b)** 42 ÷ 60 = 0.7, so **2.7 hours** [1] **[2]**

*Examiner insight:* 3.15 hours is not 3 hours 15 minutes, and 2 h 42 min is not 2.42 hours. Minutes are sixtieths of an hour, not hundredths.

**3.** 13 50 → 14 00 is 10 minutes and 14 00 → 15 05 is 65 minutes [1]; **75 minutes** [1] **[2]**

**4. (a)** 100 ÷ 7 = 14 remainder 2, so **14 weeks 2 days** [1]

**(b)** 100 × 24 = **2400 hours** [1] **[2]**

**5.** 23 48 → 00 00 is 12 minutes; 00 00 → 04 13 is 4 h 13 min [1]; **4 hours 25 minutes** [1] **[2]**

**6. (a)** 24 × 60 × 60 = **86 400** [1]

**(b)** 1 week = 7 × 24 = 168 hours, so **1/168** [1] **[2]**

**7. (a)** 06 40 → 08 10 = **1 hour 30 minutes** [1]

**(b)** Ferry 2: 09 15 → 10 35 = 1 h 20 min; ferry 3: 12 30 → 14 00 = 1 h 30 min [1]; ferry 2 is **10 minutes** quicker [1]

**(c)** Next ferry from North Bay is at 13 15, so he waits **25 minutes** [1]; he arrives at **14 00** [1] **[5]**

*Examiner insight:* ferry 2 does not stop at North Bay, so it cannot be used in (c), even though no time is shown at all in that row.

**8. (a)** 10 45 + 5 h 30 min = **16 15** [1]

**(b)** Quorra time on landing: 02 10 + 9 h 25 min = 11 35 [1]; Belmar is behind, so subtract 5 h 30 min [1]; **06 05** (the same day) [1] **[4]**

**9.** Landing time in Belmar time: 13 05 − 5 h 30 min = 07 35 the next day [1]; 20 40 → 00 00 is 3 h 20 min and 00 00 → 07 35 is 7 h 35 min [1]; flight time **10 hours 55 minutes** [1] **[3]**

*Examiner insight:* subtracting the two local times directly gives 16 h 25 min, which ignores the time difference. Convert both times to one zone first.

**10.** 200 000 ÷ 86 400 = 2 days, remainder 27 200 s [1]; 27 200 ÷ 3600 = 7 hours, remainder 2000 s [1]; 2000 ÷ 60 = 33 minutes, remainder 20 s; **2 days 7 hours 33 minutes 20 seconds** [1] **[3]**

**11. (a)** Each day: 08 45 → 17 15 is 8 h 30 min, minus 45 min = 7 h 45 min [1]; 5 × 7 h 45 min = 38 h 45 min = **38.75 hours** [1]

**(b)** 38.75 × 14.20 [1] = **$550.25** [1] **[4]**

*Examiner insight:* typing 38 h 45 min as 38.45 gives $545.99. Convert the minutes to a decimal of an hour first.

**12. (a)** 365 × 24 = **8760 hours** [1]

**(b)** 5 minutes = 300 seconds [1]; 300 ÷ 3 = **100 days** [1] **[3]**

## Where marks are usually lost

- Treating minutes as hundredths of an hour, as in Questions 2 and 11.
- Subtracting times directly instead of counting on.
- Writing 12.20 a.m. as 12 20 in the 24-hour clock, or 00 20 as 12.20 p.m.
- Using a timetable column for a ferry that does not stop at the station or port in the question.
- Adding a time difference when the destination is behind.
- Subtracting local times from two different zones.

## Frequently asked questions

**How is a time written in the 24-hour clock in 0580?**
As four digits with a space, such as 03 15 for 3.15 a.m. and 15 15 for 3.15 p.m.

**How many days are in a year for 0580 questions?**
The syllabus states 1 year = 365 days.

**What is the safest way to find a duration across midnight?**
Count on to 00 00, then count on from 00 00 to the end time, and add the two parts.
