## The exercise in one box

```
Paper 2, Exercise 4 – Multiple-choice questions     8 marks
Hear       a discussion between two speakers
Answer     multiple-choice comprehension questions, in Urdu
Assesses   L1, L2, L3 and L4: the only exercise testing all four
Heard      twice, with pauses to read and answer
```

The syllabus does not fix the number of questions or options.

## Question types by objective

- **L1 detail:** کب؟ کہاں؟ کتنے؟ One stated fact.
- **L2 opinion or objection:** ... کی کیا رائے ہے؟ / ... کو کس بات پر اعتراض ہے؟
- **L3 connection:** دونوں کس بات پر متفق ہوتے ہیں؟ / آخر میں کیا طے پاتا ہے؟
- **L4 implied:** "..." سے کیا مراد ہے؟ / ... کیسا محسوس کرتا ہے؟ / گفتگو کا مقصد کیا ہے؟

## Key terms

| Urdu | English |
|---|---|
| گفتگو | discussion, conversation |
| تجویز | suggestion |
| اعتراض | objection |
| متفق ہونا | to agree |
| طے پانا | to be decided |
| مقصد | purpose |
| مطلب / مراد | meaning |

## Turn-openers

- **Agree:** بالکل ٹھیک، میں آپ سے متفق ہوں، چلو یہی ٹھیک ہے
- **Partly agree:** یہ خیال برا نہیں، مگر ... / آپ کی بات اپنی جگہ درست ہے، لیکن ...
- **Disagree:** میں ایسا نہیں سمجھتا، یہ ضروری تو نہیں
- **Hesitate:** ہمم ...، پتا نہیں ...
- **Surprise / admiration / annoyance:** ارے! واقعی؟ / واہ! بہت خوب / اُف!

## Feeling words in options

| Urdu | English |
|---|---|
| پُرجوش | excited |
| مایوس | disappointed |
| پریشان | worried |
| حیران | surprised |
| متاثر | impressed |
| مطمئن | satisfied |
| ناراض | annoyed |

The feeling is often **shown, not named**: an exclamation, a complaint, a change of tone.

## The four wrong-option types

1. **Right words, wrong speaker.**
2. **Suggested, then rejected** (wrong if the question asks what was decided).
3. **True, but not the answer to this question.**
4. **Reported view** that the other speaker corrects (سنا ہے ... / لوگ کہتے ہیں ...).

If you can say why each other option is wrong, your choice is safe.

## Mini-example

> **علی:** چلو، اس اتوار کو چڑیا گھر چلتے ہیں۔\
> **ثنا:** یہ خیال برا نہیں، مگر اتوار کو وہاں بہت رش ہوتا ہے۔ ہفتے کو چلیں؟

Question: ثنا کو علی کی تجویز پر کیا اعتراض ہے? The answer is the crowd on Sunday (اتوار کو رش), not the
zoo itself: her turn-opener accepts the idea and the reservation follows مگر.

## Worked example: a full discussion

Two students plan a fund-raising stall. The dialogue and questions are written for this page, with
three options per question for illustration only; the syllabus does not fix the number of options.

> **عمر:** میرا خیال ہے کہ ہم فنڈ جمع کرنے کے لیے کیک بیچیں۔\
> **ماریہ:** یہ خیال برا نہیں، مگر پچھلے سال بھی سب نے کیک ہی بیچے تھے۔ کیوں نہ ہم پرانی کتابوں کا
> اسٹال لگائیں؟\
> **عمر:** کتابیں؟ پتا نہیں ... کیا لوگ پرانی کتابیں خریدیں گے؟\
> **ماریہ:** ضرور! میری امی کہتی ہیں کہ لائبریری کی سیل میں ہمیشہ رش ہوتا ہے۔ اور کتابیں جمع کرنے
> میں کوئی خرچ نہیں۔\
> **عمر:** یہ بات تو ٹھیک ہے۔ چلو، یہی ٹھیک ہے۔ میں کل اسمبلی میں اعلان کر دوں گا۔\
> **ماریہ:** واہ! تم نے تو پوری تیاری کر لی۔

**English gloss:** Umar: "I think we should sell cakes to raise funds." Maria: "That's not a bad idea,
but last year everyone sold cakes too. Why don't we set up a second-hand book stall?" Umar: "Books? I
don't know ... will people buy old books?" Maria: "Definitely! My mum says the library sale is always
crowded. And collecting books costs nothing." Umar: "That's true. All right, that's settled. I'll
announce it in assembly tomorrow." Maria: "Wow! You've got it all planned."

| Question | Options | Answer and reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| ماریہ کو کیک بیچنے پر کیا اعتراض ہے؟ (L2) | A کیک مہنگے ہیں B پچھلے سال بھی یہی کیا گیا تھا C لوگوں کو کیک پسند نہیں | **B**: the reservation follows مگر. A and C are never said. |
| عمر نے کتابوں کے خیال پر پہلے کیسا ردِعمل دیا؟ (L2) | A فوراً مان گیا B شک ظاہر کیا C غصے میں آ گیا | **B**: پتا نہیں and a doubting question. He agrees only later, so A is the wrong time. |
| آخر میں کیا طے پاتا ہے؟ (L3) | A کیک بیچے جائیں گے B پرانی کتابوں کا اسٹال لگے گا C لائبریری میں سیل ہوگی | **B**: A was suggested, then dropped; C uses words from the text but is Maria's mother's example. |
| "واہ! تم نے تو پوری تیاری کر لی" سے ماریہ کا کیا احساس ظاہر ہوتا ہے؟ (L4) | A ناراضی B مایوسی C وہ متاثر ہے | **C**: واہ! and the praise show she is impressed, though she never says متاثر. |

Notice the reported view here (میری امی کہتی ہیں) *supports* Maria's point rather than being
corrected. A reported view is not always wrong: listen to whether the speaker builds on it or rejects
it.

## Step-by-step method in detail

1. **In the pause**, read every stem and label it L1-L4 by its question word: کب/کہاں (detail),
   رائے/اعتراض (opinion), متفق/طے (connection), مراد/محسوس/مقصد (implied).
2. **Write both initials** (ع / م) at the top of your notes.
3. **First hearing:** under each initial, note suggestions (+), objections (−) and the moment of
   agreement (✓).
4. **Second hearing:** check the final lines carefully; decisions and feelings usually come near the
   end.
5. **Test each choice**: say to yourself why the other options are wrong (wrong speaker, dropped
   idea, true but irrelevant, reported view).

| Urdu | English |

| کیوں نہ ہم ...؟ | why don't we ...? |
| یہ بات تو ٹھیک ہے | that's true |
| شک ظاہر کرنا | to express doubt |
| ردِعمل | reaction |
| فیصلہ کرنا | to decide |
| راضی ہونا | to agree, be willing |
| خرچ | cost, expense |
| اعلان کرنا | to announce |

## The routine

1. **Pause:** read the stems; underline names and question words; write both initials.
2. **First hearing:** provisional choices; note who suggests, objects, agrees, changes mind.
3. **Second hearing:** confirm; listen to replies after suggestions and to the final lines.
4. **After:** one answer per question; change only with clear evidence.

## Exam traps

- Assigning a view to the wrong speaker.
- Choosing an idea that was dropped later.
- Taking "یہ خیال برا نہیں، مگر" as agreement.
- Missing a feeling shown only through tone.
- Changing a sound answer on the second hearing.
- Choosing an option because it repeats a word you heard (سیل، لائبریری) without checking who said it
  and why.
- Ignoring the order of events: an early reaction (شک) is not the final position (راضی).

## Self-test

1. Which listening objectives does Exercise 4 assess?
2. A speaker replies "آپ کی بات اپنی جگہ درست ہے، لیکن ...". What is coming?
3. What is a "suggested, then rejected" distractor, and when is it wrong?
4. How might a speaker show they are impressed without saying متاثر?
5. Why write both speakers' initials before the recording starts?
6. When should you change an answer on the second hearing?
7. In the worked dialogue, why is "کیک بیچے جائیں گے" wrong for "آخر میں کیا طے پاتا ہے؟"?
8. What does "کیوں نہ ہم ...؟" introduce?
9. Which question words point to an L4 question?
10. Is a reported view (میری امی کہتی ہیں ...) always a distractor? Explain.
11. Give the Urdu for "reaction" and "to announce".

**Answers:** 1. All four, L1 to L4. 2. A reservation or disagreement: the speaker accepts the point only
partly. 3. An idea that is raised in the discussion and later dropped; it is wrong when the question asks
what was decided or agreed. 4. With an exclamation such as واہ! or بہت خوب, or a remark such as "تم نے تو
سب سوچ رکھا ہے!". 5. So you can note who suggests, objects and agrees, which prevents the most common error
of assigning a view to the wrong speaker. 6. Only when you clearly hear evidence that contradicts your
first choice, not because of a half-remembered word. 7. It was suggested by Umar and then dropped in favour of the
book stall: a "suggested, then rejected" distractor. 8. A suggestion ("why don't we ...?"). 9. Words
asking for meaning, feeling or purpose: مراد، کیسا محسوس کرتا ہے، مقصد. 10. No; it can support the
speaker's point, as Maria's mother's view does. Listen for whether the speaker builds on it or corrects
it. 11. ردِعمل; اعلان کرنا.
