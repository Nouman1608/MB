These questions cover section 7.2, Vectors in two dimensions, of the Cambridge O Level Mathematics (Syllabus D) 4024 syllabus for 2025, 2026 and 2027. Column vectors are typed as (x, y), with x on top; write them as columns in your answers. The methods are in the [vectors in two dimensions study guide](/resources/cambridge-o-level-mathematics-4024-vectors-in-two-dimensions/), and the [vectors in two dimensions revision notes](/resources/cambridge-o-level-mathematics-4024-vectors-in-two-dimensions-revision-notes/) list the rules. Throughout, u = (5, −2) and v = (−3, 4) unless a question says otherwise.

## Section A

**1.** Find u + v and u − v. **[2]**

**2.** Find 4u − 3v. **[2]**

**3.** A is (−2, 3) and B is (4, −5). Write down AB and BA as column vectors. **[2]**

**4.** A translation maps (6, 1) onto (2, 7). Find the vector of the translation and the image of (−3, −3) under it. **[2]**

**5.** The translation (3, −2) is followed by the translation (−7, 5). Find the single equivalent translation and the vector of its inverse. **[2]**

**6.** Which of these are scalar multiples of (2, −3)?

(−4, 6), (6, −9), (4, 6), (1, −1.5) **[2]**

## Section B

**7.** Find k and m such that (3, k) + m(2, −1) = (11, 0). **[3]**

**8.** Find the values of p and q such that pu + qv = (1, 8). **[4]**

**9.** Triangle T has vertices P(1, 2), Q(4, 2) and R(1, 6).

**(a)** T is translated by (−5, 3). Find the coordinates of the image vertices.

**(b)** The image is then translated by a vector w so that P ends up at (0, 0). Find w.

**(c)** Find the single translation that maps T straight to its final position. **[4]**

**10.** a = (x, 2) and b = (3, y), and 2a + b = (7, 1).

**(a)** Find x and y.

**(b)** Hence find a − 2b. **[4]**

**11.** In a quadrilateral ABCD, AB = (3, 1), BC = (−1, 4) and CD = (−4, −2).

**(a)** Find AC.

**(b)** Find AD.

**(c)** Write down DA.

**(d)** Show that AB + BC + CD + DA is the zero vector, and explain why this must happen. **[5]**

**12.** c = (6, −8).

**(a)** Find k such that kc = (−9, 12), and describe how kc compares with c in direction and length.

**(b)** Find the vector d such that 2d − c = (4, 2). **[4]**

**13.** Triangle A has vertices (2, 1), (5, 1) and (2, 3). Triangle B has vertices (−3, 4), (0, 4) and (−3, 6). Describe fully the single transformation that maps triangle A onto triangle B. **[2]**

## Answers

**1.** u + v = **(2, 2)** [1]. u − v = (5 − (−3), −2 − 4) = **(8, −6)** [1].

**2.** 4u = (20, −8) and 3v = (−9, 12) [1], so 4u − 3v = **(29, −20)** [1].

*Examiner insight:* Writing 4u and 3v on a line of their own is what earns the method mark if the final subtraction slips.

**3.** AB = (4 − (−2), −5 − 3) = **(6, −8)** [1]. BA = **(−6, 8)** [1].

**4.** (2 − 6, 7 − 1) = **(−4, 6)** [1]. Image: (−3 − 4, −3 + 6) = **(−7, 3)** [1].

**5.** (3 + (−7), −2 + 5) = **(−4, 3)** [1]. Inverse **(4, −3)** [1].

**6.** **(−4, 6)**, **(6, −9)** and **(1, −1.5)** (−2, 3 and 0.5 times) [2] (one mark for two correct). (4, 6) is not, because 4 ÷ 2 = 2 but 6 ÷ (−3) = −2.

**7.** Top: 3 + 2m = 11 [1], so **m = 4** [1]. Bottom: k − m = 0, so **k = 4** [1].

*Examiner insight:* Solve the equation with one unknown first, then use it in the other.

**8.** Top: 5p − 3q = 1 [1]. Bottom: −2p + 4q = 8 [1]. From the bottom equation, p = 2q − 4; substituting gives 10q − 20 − 3q = 1, so 7q = 21 [1], **q = 3 and p = 2** [1].

*Examiner insight:* Check: 2u + 3v = (10, −4) + (−9, 12) = (1, 8).

**9. (a)** **(−4, 5), (−1, 5), (−4, 9)** [2] (one mark for two correct).

**(b)** From (−4, 5) to (0, 0): **w = (4, −5)** [1].

**(c)** (−5, 3) + (4, −5) = **(−1, −2)** [1]. Check: P(1, 2) moves to (0, 0).

**10. (a)** Top: 2x + 3 = 7, so **x = 2** [1]. Bottom: 4 + y = 1, so **y = −3** [1].

**(b)** 2b = (6, −6) [1], so a − 2b = (2 − 6, 2 − (−6)) = **(−4, 8)** [1].

**11. (a)** AC = AB + BC = **(2, 5)** [1].

**(b)** AD = AC + CD = **(−2, 3)** [1].

**(c)** DA = **(2, −3)** [1].

**(d)** (3, 1) + (−1, 4) + (−4, −2) + (2, −3) = (0, 0) [1]. The route goes A to B to C to D and back to A, so it ends where it started [1].

**12. (a)** 6k = −9, so **k = −1.5** [1]. kc points in the **opposite direction** to c and is **1.5 times as long** [1].

**(b)** 2d = (4, 2) + c = (10, −6) [1], so **d = (5, −3)** [1].

**13.** **Translation** [1] by the vector **(−5, 3)** [1].

*Examiner insight:* "Describe fully" needs both the name of the transformation and its vector. Check with a second vertex: (5, 1) to (0, 4) is also (−5, 3).

## Where marks are usually lost

- Computing start minus end, so the vector points the wrong way.
- Sign errors with double negatives in subtraction.
- Scaling one component only.
- Solving only one of the two component equations.
- Describing a translation without naming it or without the vector.
- Not checking a solution by substituting back.
