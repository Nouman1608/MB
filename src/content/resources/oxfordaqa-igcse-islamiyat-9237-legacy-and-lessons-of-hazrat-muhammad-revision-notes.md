**These notes condense content area 3.2.1.10 of 9237: "the key teachings from his final sermon" and "key teachings from Hazrat Muhammad's life including: treatment of parents, the importance of justice, honesty and patience."** The full explanation is in the [study guide](/resources/oxfordaqa-igcse-islamiyat-9237-legacy-and-lessons-of-hazrat-muhammad/).

## The legacy in four words

1. **Message:** the Qur'an revealed, preserved and declared complete.
2. **Example:** the Sunnah shows how to live the message.
3. **Community:** one Ummah united by faith, not tribe.
4. **Values:** justice, honesty, mercy, patience, family respect.

## The Final Sermon

- **When and where:** the ninth of Dhul-Hijjah, 10 AH (632 CE), at Arafat during the Farewell Pilgrimage.
- **Audience:** a very large crowd of pilgrims; those present were told to pass the message to those absent.
- **Afterwards:** the Prophet (pbuh) died in Madinah about three months later, in 11 AH.
- **Caution:** reports vary in wording; summarise the teachings.

| Teaching | One-line summary |
|---|---|
| Sanctity | Life, property and honour are sacred |
| Trusts | Return what you have been trusted with |
| Interest | Interest is cancelled, starting with the Prophet's own family |
| Revenge | Pre-Islamic blood feuds are ended |
| Women | Treat them well; rights on both sides |
| Equality | Only taqwa makes one person better than another |
| Brotherhood | Muslims are brothers |
| Guidance | Hold fast to the Qur'an (and the Sunnah) |
| Worship | Prayer, fasting, Zakah, Hajj |
| Passing it on | The present tell the absent |

## Qur'an 5:3: the prescribed passage

| Phrase | Meaning |
|---|---|
| "those who disbelieve have despaired of [defeating] your religion" | The opposition has failed; the community is secure |
| "fear them not, but fear Me" | Fear of Allah, not of people |
| "This day I have perfected for you your religion" | The guidance is complete (linked to Arafat) |
| "completed My favour upon you" | Islam is a blessing to be thankful for |
| "forced by severe hunger ... Allah is Forgiving and Merciful" | Rules have mercy built in |

The first part of the verse lists forbidden foods (dead animals, blood, pork, food dedicated to other than Allah and others).

## Four lessons: teaching and example

| Lesson | Qur'an or Hadith | Example from his life |
|---|---|---|
| Parents | Qur'an 17:23-24: good treatment; do not say "uff"; lower the wing of humility. Hadith: "your mother" three times, then "your father" | Honoured the memory of his parents; taught kindness continues after death through prayer and honouring their friends |
| Justice | Qur'an 4:135: stand firm in justice, even against yourselves, parents and relatives. Qur'an 5:8 | Refused to excuse a noble woman's theft; said he would apply the same rule to his own daughter Fatima |
| Honesty | Hadith: truthfulness leads to righteousness and righteousness to Paradise | Called al-Amin and al-Sadiq; returned deposits via Hazrat Ali (RA) at the Hijrah |
| Patience | Qur'an 2:153: Allah is with the patient; 39:10: reward without account | Ta'if, the boycott, bereavements; forgiveness at Makkah |

## Limits and balance

- Obedience to parents has a limit: it does not extend to disobeying Allah, but kindness continues (Qur'an 31:15).
- Justice applies to friends and enemies: hatred must not lead to injustice (Qur'an 5:8).
- Patience is active: planning, effort and prayer, not passive suffering.
- Honesty covers trade, trusts, speech and promises.

## How to use these in answers

- **Name** the teaching; **explain** why it matters; **apply** a source or example.
- Pair each lesson with one source and one event so that you can score on the AO2 marks of a 6-mark question.
- In a 12-mark answer, use Qur'an 5:3 to show that the message is complete, and the lessons to show how it is lived.

## Model answer: a 6-mark question

**Question:** Explain **two** reasons why justice is important in Islam. Use a source of authority. **[6]**

> Justice protects the weak from the strong [1]: the Prophet (pbuh) refused to excuse a noble woman who stole, because societies fall when the powerful escape punishment [1]. It is also a duty to Allah [1], because Muslims must act as witnesses for Allah even against themselves, their parents and relatives [1]. Qur'an 4:135 commands believers to stand firm in justice, which shows that fairness comes before family loyalty [2].

## Linking the lessons to the sermon

- **Parents:** family rights run through the sermon's concern for the home and the treatment of women.
- **Justice:** the cancelling of interest and blood feuds started with the Prophet's (pbuh) own family.
- **Honesty:** the return of trusts is a direct command of the sermon.
- **Patience:** the sermon asks Muslims to hold on to the Qur'an and Sunnah in the face of later disagreement.

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Khutbat al-Wada' | The Farewell Sermon |
| Hajjat al-Wada' | The Farewell Pilgrimage |
| Arafat | The plain where pilgrims stand on the ninth of Dhul-Hijjah |
| Amanat | A trust; something entrusted to a person |
| Al-Amin | The Trustworthy, the Prophet's (pbuh) title before prophethood |
| Sabr | Patience and steadfastness |
| Adl | Justice |
| Birr al-walidayn | Kindness and dutifulness to parents |

## Exam traps

- Do not give the sermon as a story only; list teachings and say why they matter.
- Qur'an 5:3 is not just about food: use the completion of the religion.
- Do not claim exact wording for the sermon.
- Parents, justice, honesty and patience are all named in the specification; do not leave one out.

## Self-test

1. Where was the Final Sermon given?
2. Give two teachings from the Final Sermon.
3. What does the Final Sermon say about racial and tribal pride?
4. Which phrase in Qur'an 5:3 says the religion is complete?
5. Which verse tells believers to stand firm in justice even against parents and relatives?
6. Whom did the Prophet (pbuh) say deserved the best company, according to the Hadith?
7. What was the Prophet (pbuh) called before prophethood, and why?
8. Who returned the deposits left with the Prophet (pbuh) in Makkah?
9. Give one example of the Prophet's (pbuh) patience.
10. What is the limit on obedience to parents?

**Answers:** 1. At Arafat. 2. Any two from the table above, for example the sanctity of life, equality through taqwa, the return of trusts. 3. It is cancelled: no Arab is above a non-Arab, or a white person above a black person, except by taqwa. 4. "This day I have perfected for you your religion." 5. Qur'an 4:135. 6. Your mother, three times, then your father. 7. Al-Amin, the trustworthy, because of his honesty. 8. Hazrat Ali (RA). 9. Any one: the boycott in Makkah; the stoning at Ta'if; the loss of Khadijah (RA) and Abu Talib; forgiving the Makkans. 10. Parents must not be obeyed if they command disobedience to Allah, but kindness continues.
