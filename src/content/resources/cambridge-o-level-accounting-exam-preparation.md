Cambridge O Level Accounting (7707) is assessed by two externally assessed papers and is not tiered: Paper 1, Multiple Choice (1 hour 30 minutes, 40 marks, 30% of the qualification) and Paper 2, Structured Written Paper (1 hour 45 minutes, 100 marks, 70%). The first assessment is in 2027. This page turns that structure into a plan, and it works best alongside the site's pages on [accounting procedures](/resources/igcse-accounting-accounting-procedures/), [bank reconciliation](/resources/igcse-accounting-bank-reconciliation/) and [analysis and interpretation](/resources/igcse-accounting-analysis-and-interpretation/). Those pages are written for the Cambridge IGCSE Accounting 0452 syllabus, whose paper structure matches this O Level for the same series, so use them as a close companion rather than a line-by-line map.

## Paper 1: Multiple Choice (40 marks, 30%)

Paper 1 gives you 90 minutes for 40 marks, and a calculator is allowed. Because every question is multiple choice, you cannot earn marks for a good method that ends in a wrong letter; the paper rewards accuracy under time pressure instead. Many accounting multiple-choice items need a short calculation or a quick judgement about which ledger account is debited and which is credited, so the working is done on scrap space and the answer is a single choice.

Spend the time on getting each item right rather than racing. A sensible routine is a first pass in which you answer everything you can do cleanly, a mark beside any item that needs longer, and a second pass for those. Never leave a question unanswered, since a blank cannot score.

## Paper 2: Structured Written Paper (100 marks, 70%)

Paper 2 is the heavier paper: 1 hour 45 minutes, 100 marks and 70% of the qualification, again with a calculator allowed. It is where you produce full accounting work, such as ledger accounts, financial statements, reconciliations and explanations, so layout, labelling and shown workings matter as much as the figures. Revise across all seven areas of the syllabus, which are the fundamentals of accounting; sources and recording of data; verification of accounting records; accounting procedures; preparation of financial statements; analysis and interpretation; and accounting concepts and modern practice. The record available to us does not say how the 100 marks are divided between questions, so do not assume any particular split; check the specification and a specimen paper for that.

## Timing and marks-per-minute

Work out your pace before the exam, not during it.

- Paper 1: 90 minutes / 40 marks = 2.25 minutes per mark (2 minutes 15 seconds). That is generous per item, which is why a second pass for difficult items is realistic.
- Paper 2: 105 minutes / 100 marks = 1.05 minutes per mark. A 10-mark question therefore deserves roughly 10.5 minutes, and a 4-mark part about 4 minutes.
- Total sitting time across the two papers is 90 + 105 = 195 minutes, for 40 + 100 = 140 marks.

Each paper's value per mark is also worth knowing. Paper 1 is worth 30% over 40 marks, which is 0.75% of the qualification per mark; Paper 2 is worth 70% over 100 marks, which is 0.7% per mark. The two are close, so no mark on either paper is cheap to give away.

## Command words and question styles

Learn what each instruction asks you to do. These are general across Cambridge written papers:

- **State** or **name**: give a short fact, with no explanation.
- **Calculate**: show the working and give the units or currency symbol.
- **Prepare** or **complete**: produce a ledger account, statement or schedule in the correct format, with headings, dates and balancing figures.
- **Explain**: give a reason, linked to the accounting situation in the question.
- **Comment on** or **advise**: use the figures given to support a judgement, rather than describing accounting in general.

## Worked practice scenario

An original example, written in the style of a Paper 2 calculation part.

Question: "A business buys equipment for $48,000 on 1 January. It depreciates equipment at 20% per year using the reducing balance method. (a) Calculate the depreciation charge for each of the first two years and the net book value at the end of year 2. (b) In year 2 the business's revenue was $90,000 and its gross profit was $36,000. Calculate the gross profit margin."

```
(a) Year 1 depreciation = 20% x $48,000          = $9,600
    Net book value end of year 1 = 48,000 - 9,600 = $38,400
    Year 2 depreciation = 20% x $38,400          = $7,680
    Net book value end of year 2 = 38,400 - 7,680 = $30,720

(b) Gross profit margin = gross profit / revenue x 100
                        = 36,000 / 90,000 x 100 = 40%
```

Notice that year 2 depreciation is applied to the reduced balance of $38,400, not the original cost; using $48,000 again would give $9,600 and lose the marks, because that is the straight-line figure. Showing the net book value after each year proves the method.

## Before/during exam checklist

- Confirm both paper times (90 minutes and 105 minutes) and that a calculator is allowed on each.
- Revise all seven syllabus areas; do not rely on a favourite topic.
- Practise laying out ledger accounts and statements until the format is automatic.
- On Paper 1, answer the easy items first, mark the slow ones and return to them.
- On Paper 1, write an answer for every question before time ends.
- On Paper 2, show every step of a calculation, with currency and labels.
- On Paper 2, check that balancing figures and totals agree before moving on.
- Leave a few minutes at the end of Paper 2 to re-read the longer answers.

## Frequently asked questions

**How many papers are there in Cambridge O Level Accounting 7707?**
There are two papers, both externally assessed and not tiered. Paper 1 is Multiple Choice (1 hour 30 minutes, 40 marks, 30%) and Paper 2 is a Structured Written Paper (1 hour 45 minutes, 100 marks, 70%).

**Can I use a calculator in the Accounting papers?**
Yes, the assessment record shows a calculator is allowed on both Paper 1 and Paper 2. Show your working on Paper 2 so that method can be credited.

**When is Cambridge O Level Accounting 7707 first assessed?**
The first assessment is in 2027, under the 2027-2029 syllabus. Candidates are eligible for grades A* to E.

**How is Paper 2 weighted compared with Paper 1?**
Paper 2 is worth 70% and Paper 1 is worth 30%. Paper 2 also has far more marks, 100 against 40, and a longer duration of 1 hour 45 minutes.

**Is O Level Accounting 7707 the same as IGCSE Accounting 0452?**
The paper structure is the same for the same series, according to the verified record. They are separate syllabuses, so always confirm details of content in the 7707 document itself.
