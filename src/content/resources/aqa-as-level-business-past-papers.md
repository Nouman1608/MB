This page is a guide to AQA AS-Level Business past papers. AQA's current AS specification is 7137, taught since September 2026 and first examined in summer 2027, so it has no live past papers yet. Every real past paper, grade boundary and report on the examination in this guide belongs to the specification it replaces, 7131, for the June 2024, June 2025 and June 2026 series. For each series you get the grade thresholds, copied from AQA's grade-boundary documents, and, where AQA has published its report on the examination, a short account of what the examiners saw, written by us. We do not host any papers. Every paper, mark scheme and report is on AQA's own site, linked below.

AQA publishes question papers, inserts, mark schemes and reports on the examination on the assessment resources page for the subject.

Use the three documents together. Sit the paper first, under timed conditions. Mark it strictly with the mark scheme, which tells you what each mark needs. Then read the report on the examination for that paper: it explains why answers that looked reasonable still lost marks. Our notes for each series below are a quick way into that report. If a paper exposes a weak topic, go back to the matching study guide, for example [financial management](/resources/aqa-as-level-business-financial-management/), [people management](/resources/aqa-as-level-business-people-management/) or [operations management](/resources/aqa-as-level-business-operations-management/), and see the [exam preparation guide](/resources/aqa-as-level-business-exam-preparation/) for technique.

Check which specification a paper was set on. The two specifications are both linear and not tiered, but they are different papers. The outgoing 7131 had two compulsory written papers, each 1 hour 30 minutes, 80 marks and 50 per cent of the AS (160 marks in total). Paper 1 had 10 multiple-choice questions, short-answer questions and two data-response questions, and Paper 2 had one compulsory case study with about seven questions. AQA's subject page describes 7131 as outgoing for cohorts taking exams in 2026, so June 2026 was the last series. The new 7137 has two papers, each 1 hour 45 minutes and 80 marks, with a different structure of two case studies in each paper. Only use the 7131 series below as general practice for 7137, and use the 7137 sample materials on AQA's site to see its real format.

AQA gives one set of subject-level boundaries for the AS (out of 160) and, for illustration, notional boundaries for each paper (out of 80). The boundaries run from A to E. Because the qualification is linear, the subject-level boundaries decide the grade. There are no thresholds yet for 7137, and series from 2027 onwards and earlier than June 2024 are not summarised here yet. Older reports are on the 7131 assessment resources page.

## June 2025

The June 2025 reports say Paper 1 discriminated well, with strong candidates giving concise answers that made a clear link to the context, and weaker ones showing imprecise knowledge and few links to the scenario. Time did not appear to be a problem. Paper 2 was accessible, with few unanswered questions, but examiners again noted that untidy handwriting and unreadable figures cannot be credited. The most effective answers were planned, balanced and focused on exactly what the question asked.

**Where marks were lost**

- Paper 7131/1: an explanation of quality assurance that talked about quality in general instead of the advantage of assurance for a high-volume food manufacturer, and answers on span of control that did not tie an increased span to efficiency.
- Paper 7131/1: margin of safety, where over a third of candidates scored nothing and many stopped at contribution per unit or break-even output; by contrast the dividend calculation was done well.
- Paper 7131/1: leadership answers that defined autocratic leadership at length without linking it to a business growing quickly, and some confusion with democratic leadership.
- Paper 7131/1: a longer question on interest rates where weaker answers recycled the case, treated a rise in rates as increasing the size of a loan rather than the cost of servicing it, and confused income elasticity with price elasticity.
- Paper 7131/2: percentage change not calculated correctly, and a gross profit margin calculation that used the right formula with the wrong figures.
- Paper 7131/2: answers on delegated budgets that mostly argued only the positive side, and a judgement question where not all answers picked up the word "future" or made use of it in the conclusion.

## June 2024

The June 2024 reports describe the same pattern: strong candidates were concise and tied each point to the case, and weaker ones gave long definitions or lists. On Paper 1 several multiple-choice questions on cash flow, market mapping and elasticity were hard. On Paper 2, AQA reported that one question tested content that had been removed from the specification for first teaching from September 2023, so every candidate was awarded full marks for it.

**Where marks were lost**

- Paper 7131/1: multiple-choice questions where candidates missed the effect of the phrase "no other changes", did not notice that a monthly net cash flow already included an outflow, or confused market mapping with other concepts.
- Paper 7131/1: an explanation of how competition could raise fixed costs that stayed with general effects of competition, and an operating profit margin calculation that stopped at revenue and variable costs without the expenses.
- Paper 7131/1: income elasticity of +4 explained as a rise in demand without recognising that sales would rise by a greater proportion than incomes, and some mixing with price elasticity.
- Paper 7131/1: a technology and operational efficiency answer that gave a long definition or list of examples instead of linking technology to efficiency in a manufacturing business.
- Paper 7131/2: margin of safety, where more than half of those who attempted it scored nothing, and a promotional mix question answered as if it were about the whole marketing mix or about cost.
- Paper 7131/2: decision tree answers that defined a decision tree at length, supplier and managerial-concern answers that were one-sided or generic, and motivation answers that listed theories without addressing labour productivity or the focus on all businesses.

## Frequently asked questions

**Where can I find AQA AS-Level Business past papers?**
For the outgoing 7131 specification, AQA publishes papers, inserts, mark schemes and reports on the examination on its assessment resources page. The new 7137 has sample assessment materials there but no live past papers yet.

**Are the 7131 papers the same as the 7137 papers?**
No. 7137 has a different structure and first exams in summer 2027, so 7131 papers are general practice only. Use the 7137 sample materials for its real format.

**Which 7131 papers should I sit when practising a series?**
Sit both papers for the series. Each was 1 hour 30 minutes and 80 marks. Mark them with the official mark scheme.

**Are the paper-level thresholds the ones that decide my grade?**
No. The AS is linear, so the subject-level boundaries out of 160 decide the grade. AQA gives the paper-level figures as notional boundaries, for illustration only.

**Can grade thresholds tell me what mark I need next time?**
No. Thresholds change every series and should only be used as a rough guide for the series they belong to, never to predict future ones.
