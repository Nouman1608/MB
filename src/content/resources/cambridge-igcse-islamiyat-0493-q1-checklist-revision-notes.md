# Question 1 checklist

## The format in five lines

- Paper 1 has five questions; answer Question 1, Question 2 and two others. 50 marks in all.
- Question 1 is compulsory: three passages set from Appendix 1, choose any two, 8 marks.
- Each passage: the major theme or themes, and their importance in the lives of Muslims today.
- Passages are printed in Arabic and English; show knowledge of the original as well as the translation.
- Grades run from A* to G for IGCSE.

## Answer shape for each passage

1. Group, then theme in one sentence.
2. A phrase or Arabic word from the passage as evidence.
3. Part (b): an action a Muslim takes today, and why.

## Mistakes to avoid

- Telling the story instead of stating the theme.
- A single theme only, when the passage has more.
- An application that could fit any passage.
- Leaving the Arabic out.

## Self-test

1. How many passages do you answer on, and how many are set? *Two of three.*
2. How many marks is Question 1? *8.*
3. Name the three groups. *Allah in himself; Allah's relationship with the created world; Allah's Messengers.*
4. Name one passage from each group. *Any from the table above.*
