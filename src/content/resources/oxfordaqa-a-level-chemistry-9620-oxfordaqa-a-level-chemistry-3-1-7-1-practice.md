## Section A

**1.** State **two** features of a reversible reaction at dynamic equilibrium. **[2]**

**2.** State Le Chatelier's principle. **[1]**

**3.** Explain why a catalyst does not affect the position of equilibrium. **[2]**

**4.** Explain why a reversible reaction producing a gas does not reach equilibrium in an open beaker. **[1]**

**5.** Predict the effect of increasing the total pressure on the position of each equilibrium.

**(a)** N₂(g) + O₂(g) ⇌ 2NO(g) **[1]**

**(b)** PCl₅(g) ⇌ PCl₃(g) + Cl₂(g) **[1]**

**(c)** 4NH₃(g) + 5O₂(g) ⇌ 4NO(g) + 6H₂O(g) **[1]**

**6.** A student writes: "At equilibrium the reaction has stopped and there are equal amounts of reactants and products." Correct **both** errors. **[2]**

## Section B

**7.** Equal amounts of hydrogen and iodine vapour are sealed in a flask at constant temperature. Describe how the rates of the forward and reverse reactions change from the moment of mixing until equilibrium is reached. **[3]**

**8.** Ethanoic acid and ethanol react in the liquid phase with an acid catalyst:

CH₃COOH + C₂H₅OH ⇌ CH₃COOC₂H₅ + H₂O

**(a)** State and explain the effect of adding more ethanol on the amount of ester at equilibrium. **[2]**

**(b)** Suggest and explain the effect on the amount of ester of removing water from the mixture as it forms. **[2]**

**(c)** State why adding more acid catalyst does not change the equilibrium amount of ester. **[1]**

**9.** Iron(III) ions react with thiocyanate ions to form a deep red complex ion:

Fe³⁺(aq) + SCN⁻(aq) ⇌ [Fe(SCN)]²⁺(aq)

Fe³⁺(aq) is pale yellow and SCN⁻(aq) is colourless.

**(a)** Potassium thiocyanate solution is added to the equilibrium mixture. State and explain the colour change. **[2]**

**(b)** Sodium hydroxide solution is added, which precipitates Fe³⁺ ions as iron(III) hydroxide. Explain why the red colour of the solution fades. **[2]**

**(c)** When the mixture is warmed, it becomes paler. Deduce the sign of ΔH for the forward reaction, explaining your answer. **[2]**

**10.** N₂O₄(g) ⇌ 2NO₂(g); N₂O₄ is colourless and NO₂ is brown. A gas syringe containing the equilibrium mixture is quickly pushed in to half its volume at constant temperature. Describe and explain what is seen immediately and over the next few seconds, and compare the final colour with the original. **[4]**

**11.** Ethanol is made industrially from ethene and steam using an acid catalyst:

C₂H₄(g) + H₂O(g) ⇌ C₂H₅OH(g)        ΔH = −46 kJ mol⁻¹

**(a)** Explain why a moderate temperature, rather than a low temperature, is used. **[3]**

**(b)** Explain why a moderately high pressure is used, rather than a low pressure or a very high pressure. **[3]**

**(c)** Explain why unreacted ethene is separated and recycled. **[1]**

**12.** N₂(g) + O₂(g) ⇌ 2NO(g), ΔH = +181 kJ mol⁻¹. State and explain the effect on the equilibrium yield of nitrogen monoxide of (i) increasing the temperature and (ii) increasing the pressure. **[4]**

## Answers

**1.** The forward and reverse reactions occur at equal rates [1]; the concentrations of reactants and products remain constant [1]

**2.** If a factor affecting a system at equilibrium is changed, the position of equilibrium shifts in the direction that opposes the change [1]

**3.** A catalyst increases the rates of the forward and reverse reactions equally (it lowers both activation energies by the same amount) [1]; so equilibrium is reached sooner, but the composition at equilibrium is unchanged [1]

**4.** The gas escapes, so the reverse reaction cannot occur at the same rate as the forward reaction (the system is not closed) [1]

**5. (a)** No effect: 2 moles of gas on each side [1]

**(b)** Shifts left: 1 mole of gas on the left, 2 on the right [1]

**(c)** Shifts left: 9 moles of gas on the left, 10 on the right [1]

**6.** The reaction has **not** stopped: forward and reverse reactions continue at equal rates [1]; the concentrations are **constant**, not necessarily equal [1]

**7.** At first the forward rate is at its highest and the reverse rate is zero, because there is no HI [1]; as H₂ and I₂ are used up the forward rate falls, and as HI builds up the reverse rate rises [1]; at equilibrium the two rates become equal and then stay constant [1]

**8. (a)** The amount of ester increases [1]; the position shifts to the right to use up some of the added ethanol [1]

**(b)** The amount of ester increases [1]; removing a product shifts the position to the right to replace it [1]

**(c)** A catalyst speeds up the forward and reverse reactions equally, so the position of equilibrium is unchanged [1]

**9. (a)** The solution becomes deeper red [1]; [SCN⁻] increases, so the position shifts right to use up some SCN⁻, forming more [Fe(SCN)]²⁺ [1]

**(b)** [Fe³⁺] falls as the ions are removed [1]; the position shifts left to replace Fe³⁺, so [Fe(SCN)]²⁺ decreases [1]

**(c)** Warming shifts the position left, in the direction that uses up the red complex [1]; increasing the temperature favours the endothermic direction, so the reverse reaction is endothermic and the forward reaction is exothermic: ΔH is **negative** [1]
*Examiner insight:* Work backwards: identify which way the position moved from the colour, then apply the temperature rule.

**10.** Immediately the mixture becomes darker brown [1]; because the same NO₂ molecules are in half the volume, so [NO₂] increases [1]; then the colour fades somewhat, because the higher pressure shifts the position to the left, the side with fewer moles of gas (1 rather than 2) [1]; the final colour is still darker than the original, because the shift only partly opposes the change [1]

**11. (a)** The forward reaction is exothermic, so a low temperature would give a higher equilibrium yield [1]; but at a low temperature the rate would be too slow [1]; a moderate temperature gives an acceptable yield at an acceptable rate [1]

**(b)** There are 2 moles of gas on the left and 1 on the right, so a higher pressure shifts the position right, increasing the yield (and the rate) [1]; a low pressure would give a low yield and slow rate [1]; very high pressures need expensive, thick-walled equipment, use more energy for compression and are less safe [1]

**(c)** Unreacted ethene can be passed through again, so more of it is eventually converted to ethanol, reducing waste and cost [1]

**12.** (i) The yield increases [1]; the forward reaction is endothermic, so a higher temperature shifts the position to the right [1]. (ii) No effect on the yield [1]; there are 2 moles of gas on each side, so neither direction reduces the pressure [1]
*Examiner insight:* For (ii), "no effect" earns the first mark only with the equal-gas-moles reason.

## Where marks are usually lost

- Giving the direction of shift without the reason from the equation.
- Writing "concentrations are equal" for "concentrations are constant".
- Not linking an indirect change (added OH⁻, removed Fe³⁺) to a species in the equation.
- Compromise answers that ignore rate or cost.
- Counting liquid species when applying the pressure rule.
