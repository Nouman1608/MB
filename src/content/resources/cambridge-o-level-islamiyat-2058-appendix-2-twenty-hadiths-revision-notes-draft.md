# The twenty set Hadiths at a glance

Four Hadiths are set in Paper 2 Question 1 and you answer on two (8 marks). Any of the twenty can appear. Part (a) asks what each teaches and part (b) how Muslims act on it.

Related: [Major Teachings in the Hadiths revision notes](/resources/cambridge-o-level-islamiyat-major-teachings-in-the-hadiths-revision-notes/).

| No. | Key idea (summary) | A possible action |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Religion is sincerity: to Allah, His Book, His Messenger, the leaders of the Muslims and the common people | Be honest in worship and in dealings with others |
| 2 | None believes until he wants for his brother what he wants for himself | Share and wish others well |
| 3 | Speak good or keep silent; be generous to the neighbour and the guest | Avoid gossip; host and help neighbours |
| 4 | Obligatory prayer, fasting in Ramadan and keeping to the lawful and forbidden lead to paradise | Keep the basic duties faithfully |
| 5 | Every joint must perform a charity daily: justice, helping, a good word, steps to prayer, removing harm | Do small acts of good each day |
| 6 | Change an evil with the hand, then the tongue, then the heart | Speak against wrong according to ability |
| 7 | The most excellent is the believer who strives in Allah's way with person and property | Give time and wealth for good |
| 8 | Martyrs include those who die naturally, of plague or of cholera, in Allah's way | Take comfort and act in Allah's way |
| 9 | No food is better than that eaten from the work of one's hand | Work honestly |
| 10 | One who manages the affairs of the widow and the poor is like one who strives in Allah's way | Support widows and the poor |
| 11 | The one who brings up an orphan will be near the Prophet (pbuh) in paradise | Sponsor or care for orphans |
| 12 | The instruction to Abu Musa and Mu'adh ibn Jabal when sent to govern parts of Yaman: be gentle, give joy, do not alienate | Lead and teach with gentleness |
| 13 | One who studies the Qur'an is like the owner of tethered camels | Keep up regular study of the Qur'an |
| 14 | Mercy on the man who is kindly when he sells, buys and demands his money back | Be fair and kind in trade |
| 15 | God will not show mercy to him who does not show mercy to others | Show mercy to people and animals |
| 16 | The believers are like a single man | Share the hardships of others |
| 17 | Modesty produces nothing but good | Cultivate modesty |
| 18 | A mustard seed of faith saves from hell; a mustard seed of pride keeps from paradise | Guard against pride |
| 19 | The world is the believer's prison and the unbeliever's paradise | Live with restraint and patience |
| 20 | God looks at hearts and deeds, not forms and possessions | Check intention, not appearance |

## Self-test

1. How many Hadiths are set in the exam, and how many do you answer on? *Four set, two answered.*
2. Which collections record these Hadiths? *Al-Bukhari or Muslim, many by both.*
3. Which Hadith deals with a governor's instructions to Abu Musa and Mu'adh? *No. 12.*
4. Which numbers deal with orphans and widows? *10 and 11.*
5. Which number places the heart above forms and possessions? *20.*

## What a teacher must confirm

- Number 8: the table keeps to the syllabus wording and does not add other examples.
- Whether the actions are the kind credited at part (b).
