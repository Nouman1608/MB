Cambridge IGCSE Geography (0460) is not tiered. Every candidate takes Paper 1, Geographical Themes (1 hour 45 minutes, 75 raw marks weighted to 100, 45%) and Paper 2, Geographical Skills (1 hour 30 minutes, 60 marks, 27.5%), plus either Component 3, Coursework (one assignment of up to 2,000 words, 60 marks, 27.5%) or Paper 4, Alternative to Coursework (1 hour 30 minutes, 60 marks, 27.5%). Content support is available for [The Natural Environment](/resources/igcse-cambridge-geography-natural-environment/), [Development](/resources/igcse-geography-0460-development/) and [Energy](/resources/igcse-geography-0460-energy/).

## Paper 1: Geographical Themes (75 marks, 45%)

Paper 1 covers the three themes of the syllabus: Population and Settlement, The Natural Environment and Economic Development. It is the biggest component. Its 75 raw marks are weighted to 100 by the board, and the 45% weighting is the board's own final figure. Revision should therefore be wide, with named case studies and precise geographical vocabulary for every theme. Use the study guides and revision notes for each, such as [Food production](/resources/igcse-geography-0460-food-production-revision-notes/) and [Environmental risks of economic development](/resources/igcse-geography-0460-environmental-risks-of-economic-development-revision-notes/).

## Paper 2: Geographical Skills (60 marks, 27.5%)

Paper 2 tests geographical skills over 1 hour 30 minutes. Practise the skills you will meet in any geography course: reading maps and graphs, interpreting data, drawing conclusions from resources and describing patterns and trends. The record does not list each skill, so check the specification for exactly which are assessed.

## Component 3 or Paper 4: your route to the final 27.5%

All candidates take one of these two, not both.

- **Component 3, Coursework**: centre-based, one assignment of up to 2,000 words, 60 marks, 27.5%, internally assessed and externally moderated. There is no timed paper. Plan the investigation carefully with your teacher and respect the word limit.
- **Paper 4, Alternative to Coursework**: 1 hour 30 minutes, 60 marks, 27.5%, externally assessed. It is a written paper that stands in for coursework, so practise fieldwork-style planning, data presentation and evaluation questions.

Check with your centre which one you are entered for.

## Timing and marks-per-minute

- Paper 1: 105 minutes / 75 marks = 1.4 minutes per mark.
- Paper 2: 90 minutes / 60 marks = 1.5 minutes per mark.
- Paper 4: 90 minutes / 60 marks = 1.5 minutes per mark.
- Written time if you take Paper 4: 105 + 90 + 90 = 285 minutes. With coursework instead: 105 + 90 = 195 minutes.
- Weighting per mark: Paper 1 is 45 / 75 = 0.6% per raw mark; Paper 2 is 27.5 / 60 = about 0.46% per mark.

A Paper 1 mark is therefore worth more than a Paper 2 mark. Do not abandon Paper 1 extended answers.

## Command words and question styles

- **State** and **name**: a short fact with no development.
- **Describe**: say what a pattern, feature or trend looks like, using figures from a resource.
- **Explain**: give reasons, linked with because or so.
- **Suggest**: apply what you know to an unfamiliar situation.
- **Assess** and **evaluate**: weigh advantages and disadvantages and reach a conclusion.
- **Calculate**: show the working and units.

## Building a revision plan around the weightings

Because Paper 1 is 45% and the two other components are 27.5% each, split your revision time roughly in proportion, but not exactly: Paper 1 needs the most knowledge to be learned, whereas the skills paper and the coursework or Paper 4 route reward regular practice more than memorising. A workable pattern is to revise one theme at a time with a case study for each key idea, then finish each week with a short skills session on maps, graphs or data. If you are on the Paper 4 route, rehearse fieldwork-style questions about planning, methods, presentation and evaluation. If you are on the coursework route, put your effort into the investigation timetable agreed with your teacher.

## Worked practice scenario

An original skills-style calculation.

Question: "A country has a birth rate of 24 per 1,000 and a death rate of 7 per 1,000. (a) Calculate the natural increase as a percentage. (b) A district has a population of 120,000 and an area of 80 km2. Calculate its population density."

```
(a) Natural increase per 1,000 = birth rate - death rate = 24 - 7 = 17
    As a percentage: 17 / 1,000 x 100 = 1.7%

(b) Population density = population / area
                      = 120,000 / 80 = 1,500 people per km2
```

Shown working earns method marks even if you slip, and the unit "per km2" is part of the answer. Percentages of natural increase are written per hundred, not per thousand, so the conversion step in part (a) is where errors usually happen.

## Before/during exam checklist

- Confirm whether you take Component 3 or Paper 4.
- Revise all three themes of Paper 1 with case studies.
- Practise map, graph and data skills for Paper 2 regularly.
- Take a ruler, pencil and calculator if the instructions allow.
- Use the minutes-per-mark figures to time each question.
- Use figures from resources when asked to describe.
- Show calculation working and units.
- For coursework, keep within the 2,000-word limit.

## Self-test

1. What is the raw mark total for Paper 1, and what is its weighting?
2. Which components can replace one another?
3. How many minutes per mark does Paper 2 allow?
4. What is 24 - 7 expressed as a percentage of 1,000?

**Answers:** 1. 75 raw marks, weighted to 100, worth 45%. 2. Component 3, Coursework, and Paper 4, Alternative to Coursework. 3. 1.5 minutes per mark, since 90 / 60 = 1.5. 4. 1.7%, since 17 / 1,000 x 100 = 1.7.

## Frequently asked questions

**How many papers are there in Cambridge IGCSE Geography 0460?**
Paper 1 and Paper 2 are compulsory. Candidates then take either Component 3, Coursework or Paper 4, Alternative to Coursework.

**How is Paper 1 weighted?**
Paper 1 has 75 raw marks, weighted to 100 by the board, and is worth 45%. It lasts 1 hour 45 minutes.

**How long is the coursework?**
Component 3 is one assignment of up to 2,000 words, worth 60 marks and 27.5%. It is internally assessed and externally moderated.

**What is Paper 4?**
It is the Alternative to Coursework, a written paper of 1 hour 30 minutes, 60 marks and 27.5%, externally assessed.

**Which series is covered?**
The record covers the 2025-2026 edition, with first assessment in 2025 and both June and November series. The syllabus is not tiered.
