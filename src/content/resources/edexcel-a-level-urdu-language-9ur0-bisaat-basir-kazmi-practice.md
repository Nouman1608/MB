> **These are original questions written for Marlbridge** for practice on the prescribed play بساط
> (*Bisaat*) for Paper 2 Section B. They are not past-paper questions. Questions 1–10 use practice
> marks; question 11 follows the specification's essay format (45 marks on three grids). Where an
> answer needs evidence from the play, the mark scheme describes what that evidence must do; supply
> it from your own reading.

## Section A

**1.** State the playwright, the form and the date the specification gives for بساط. **[3]**

**2.** Give **three** meanings of the word **بساط**, and one Urdu phrase that uses its figurative meaning. **[4]**

**3.** Give the English meaning of: (a) ڈرامائی تناؤ (b) ڈرامائی ستم ظریفی (c) اسٹیج کی ہدایات (d) مہرہ (e) فیصلہ کن لمحہ **[5]**

**4.** Name **three** ways in which a play reveals character to its audience, other than a narrator's description. **[3]**

**5.** Correct the error in each sentence.
(a) اس منظر میں ڈرامائی تناؤ اپنے عروج پر پہنچ جاتی ہے۔
(b) ڈراما نگار نے کرداروں کی بے بسی دکھایا ہے۔
(c) ناظرین کو اس لمحے کردار کی مجبوری کا احساس ہوتی ہے۔ **[3]**

**6.** May a candidate answer on بساط and one film? May they answer on two films? **[2]**

## Section B

**7.** Translate into Urdu:

> In a play, the audience learns about the characters mainly through their dialogue and actions. A single silence can reveal more than a long speech.

**[8]**

**8.** Using the six scene-analysis questions from the study guide (کون، کہاں، کب؛ مقصد؛ رکاوٹ؛ مکالمے کا انداز؛ تبدیلی؛ ربط), explain what a candidate should write under **مکالمے کا انداز** and **تبدیلی** for any key scene. **[4]**

**9.** Write a one-sentence thesis in Urdu for the question **«عنوان "بساط" ڈرامے کے مرکزی خیال کی کس حد تک ترجمانی کرتا ہے؟»** **[3]**

**10.** A candidate writes: «بساط کا مطلب شطرنج ہے، قالین ہے اور حیثیت ہے۔ اس لیے یہ اچھا عنوان ہے۔» Explain **two** weaknesses and describe how the paragraph should be improved. **[4]**

**11.** Answer in Urdu, in about 320–370 words.

**«ڈرامے "بساط" میں مکالمے صرف کہانی آگے نہیں بڑھاتے بلکہ کرداروں کے باطن کو بھی بے نقاب کرتے ہیں۔» اس بیان کا جائزہ لیجیے۔**

**[45]**

## Answers

**1.** باصر کاظمی (Basir Sultan Kazmi) [1]; a play [1]; 1987 [1].

**2.** A spread cloth or carpet [1]; a chessboard [1]; one's means or capacity [1]; phrase: اپنی بساط کے مطابق (according to one's means) [1].

**3.** (a) dramatic tension [1] (b) dramatic irony, when the audience knows more than a character [1] (c) stage directions [1] (d) a chess piece, pawn [1] (e) decisive moment [1]

**4.** Any three, one mark each: what the character says (dialogue); how they say it (tone, register); what they do on stage (action, gesture); silences and pauses; what other characters say about them; stage directions.

**5.** (a) پہنچ جاتا ہے: تناؤ is masculine [1] (b) دکھائی ہے: with نے the verb agrees with بے بسی, feminine [1] (c) احساس ہوتا ہے: احساس is masculine [1]

**6.** Yes: one literary text and one film is an allowed combination [1]. No: two films is never allowed [1].

**7.** Model: **ڈرامے میں ناظرین کرداروں کے بارے میں زیادہ تر ان کے مکالموں اور اعمال سے جانتے ہیں۔ ایک لمحے کی خاموشی بھی ایک طویل تقریر سے کہیں زیادہ ظاہر کر سکتی ہے۔**

Marking: ڈرامے میں [1]؛ ناظرین [1]؛ کرداروں کے بارے میں [1]؛ زیادہ تر [1]؛ ان کے مکالموں اور اعمال سے جانتے ہیں [1]؛ ایک لمحے کی خاموشی بھی [1]؛ ایک طویل تقریر سے کہیں زیادہ [1]؛ ظاہر کر سکتی ہے, feminine agreeing with خاموشی [1].

**8.** مکالمے کا انداز: how the characters speak, for example formal or informal, direct or evasive, long speeches or short exchanges [1], and what this reveals about their attitude or relationship [1]. تبدیلی: what has changed by the end of the scene, such as a relationship, a decision, the balance of power, or what the audience knows [1], and why that change matters to the play as a whole [1].

**9.** One mark each: a clear position on how far the title expresses the central idea [1]; a named meaning of the title linked to the play [1]; accurate Urdu [1]. Example: **عنوان "بساط" ڈرامے کے مرکزی خیال کی بڑی حد تک ترجمانی کرتا ہے، کیونکہ شطرنج کی بساط کی طرح اس ڈرامے میں بھی کچھ کردار چالیں چلتے ہیں اور کچھ مہروں کی طرح استعمال ہوتے ہیں۔** (The second clause must then be supported with scenes from the play.)

**10.** Weaknesses: it lists meanings without evidence from any scene [1]; the judgement "اچھا عنوان" is vague and not linked to the play's ideas [1]. Improvement: choose the meaning the play develops most fully [1] and support it with a specific scene or line, explaining how it connects to a theme [1].

**11.** Marked on three levels-based grids: critical and analytical response (AO4) [20]; range of grammatical structures and vocabulary, including literary terminology (AO3) [15]; accuracy of language (AO3) [10]. Total 45.

**Indicative approach.** Credit any well-argued response supported by evidence from the play. A strong answer is likely to:

- state a position on both parts of the statement: dialogue moving the plot on, and dialogue revealing inner life;
- analyse two or three specific exchanges, explaining what each reveals about a character's thoughts, desires or conflict, and how the manner of speaking (tone, register, evasion, silence) adds to the words;
- show at least one moment where dialogue advances the action, such as a revelation or decision;
- consider what dialogue cannot do alone, for example where stage directions, action or silence carry the meaning, which allows the statement to be qualified;
- link the analysis to a theme of the play and, where relevant, to the title;
- reach a conclusion that judges how far the statement is true.

At the top of the AO4 range, each point is justified with precisely recalled lines or closely described moments; an answer that discusses dialogue only in general terms, without evidence, will stay in the lower bands.
