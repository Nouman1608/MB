## Questions

### Section A: short questions

**1.** Which of the following is a direct cost?

A: factory rent

B: wages of assembly workers

C: factory supervisor's salary

D: depreciation of factory machinery

**[1]**

**2.** Raw materials consumed were 40,000, direct wages 32,000 and factory
overheads 18,000. What is the prime cost?

A: 58,000

B: 72,000

C: 90,000

D: 50,000

**[1]**

**3.** Explain what is meant by work in progress and why opening work in progress
is added in the manufacturing account. **[2]**

**4.** Raw materials at the start of the year were 6,200. Purchases were 45,300,
carriage inwards 800 and purchases returns 1,100. Raw materials at the end of the
year were 7,400. Calculate the cost of raw materials consumed. **[2]**

**5.** Classify each cost as direct, indirect (factory overhead) or not a factory
cost: wages of machine operators; wages of factory canteen staff; commission paid
to sales staff; timber used to make chairs. **[2]**

**6.** Name the three types of inventory held by a manufacturer, and state in which
statement the finished goods inventory is used to calculate cost of sales. **[2]**

### Section B: structured questions

**7.** Hollins Ceramics provides the following for the year ended 31 December 2028:

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Raw materials at 1 January / 31 December | 4,800 / 5,600 |
| Purchases of raw materials | 38,900 |
| Carriage inwards on raw materials | 650 |
| Direct factory wages | 29,400 |
| Indirect factory wages | 8,700 |
| Factory power | 5,300 |
| Rent and rates of premises | 12,000 |
| Plant and machinery at cost | 40,000 |
| Accumulated depreciation of plant at 1 January | 16,000 |
| Work in progress at 1 January / 31 December | 3,900 / 3,100 |

Direct wages of 600 and factory power of 400 are accrued. Two-thirds of rent and
rates relates to the factory. Plant is depreciated at 15% a year on the reducing
balance.

Prepare the manufacturing account for the year ended 31 December 2028. **[10]**

**8.** Hollins Ceramics also buys some finished vases to resell. Use your factory
cost of production from Question 7 and the following: revenue 168,000; finished
goods inventory at 1 January 9,200 and at 31 December 11,450; purchases of finished
vases 4,000; office salaries 21,300; carriage outwards 2,250. The office share of
rent and rates is one-third.

Prepare the statement of profit or loss for the year ended 31 December 2028. **[5]**

**9.** A manufacturer's closing work in progress was understated by 800. State the
effect on **(a)** factory cost of production **[1]**, **(b)** gross profit **[1]**
and **(c)** current assets. **[1]**

**10.** Factory cost of production was 120,000, factory overheads 35,000, opening
work in progress 4,000 and closing work in progress 6,500. Direct labour was
41,000. Calculate prime cost and the cost of raw materials consumed. **[3]**

**11.** Explain why depreciation of factory machinery is included in the
manufacturing account but depreciation of delivery vans is not. **[2]**

**12.** Insurance of 3,600 was paid during the year for the factory and the office,
of which 600 is prepaid at the year end. The charge is shared factory 5 : office 1.
Calculate the insurance charged in the manufacturing account and in the statement
of profit or loss. **[3]**

## Answers

**1.** B [1]. Their wages can be traced to each unit assembled. **[1]**

**2.** B [1]. 40,000 + 32,000 = 72,000; overheads are not part of prime cost. **[1]**

**3.** Goods that are partly finished at the year end [1]. Opening work in progress
was started last year and is completed this year, so its cost is part of this
year's production of finished goods [1]. **[2]**

**4.** 6,200 + 45,300 + 800 − 1,100 − 7,400 [1] = **43,800** [1]. **[2]**

**5.** Machine operators: direct; timber: direct; canteen staff: indirect; sales
commission: not a factory cost. Two correct [1]; all four correct [1]. **[2]**

**6.** Raw materials, work in progress and finished goods [1]. Finished goods are
used in the statement of profit or loss [1]. **[2]**

**7.**

```
Hollins Ceramics: Manufacturing account for the year ended 31 December 2028
Raw materials at 1 January                                    4,800
Purchases of raw materials                       38,900
Carriage inwards                                    650      39,550
                                                             44,350
Raw materials at 31 December                                 (5,600)
Cost of raw materials consumed                               38,750
Direct labour (29,400 + 600)                                 30,000
Prime cost                                                   68,750
Factory overheads
   Indirect wages                                 8,700
   Power (5,300 + 400)                            5,700
   Rent and rates (12,000 × 2/3)                  8,000
   Depreciation of plant ((40,000 − 16,000) × 15%) 3,600     26,000
                                                             94,750
Add work in progress at 1 January                             3,900
Less work in progress at 31 December                         (3,100)
Factory cost of production                                   95,550
```

Raw materials with carriage inwards [1]; cost consumed 38,750 [1]; direct labour
30,000 [1]; prime cost 68,750 [1]; power 5,700 [1]; rent and rates 8,000 [1];
depreciation 3,600 [1]; total overheads 26,000 [1]; WIP added and deducted
correctly [1]; factory cost of production 95,550 [1]. **[10]**

**8.**

```
Hollins Ceramics: Statement of profit or loss for the year ended 31 December 2028
Revenue                                                     168,000
Less cost of sales
   Inventory of finished goods at 1 January        9,200
   Factory cost of production                     95,550
   Purchases of finished goods                     4,000
                                                 108,750
   Inventory of finished goods at 31 December    (11,450)    97,300
Gross profit                                                 70,700
Less expenses
   Office salaries                                21,300
   Rent and rates (12,000 × 1/3)                   4,000
   Carriage outwards                               2,250     27,550

```

Factory cost of production in cost of sales [1]; purchases of finished goods
included [1]; cost of sales 97,300 [1]; gross profit 70,700 [1]; profit for the
year 43,150 [1]. **[5]**

**9. (a)** Overstated by 800 [1].

**(b)** Understated by 800 [1].

**(c)** Understated by 800 [1]. **[3]**

**10.** Total production cost before WIP = 120,000 + 6,500 − 4,000 = 122,500 [1].
Prime cost = 122,500 − 35,000 = **87,500** [1]. Raw materials consumed = 87,500
− 41,000 = **46,500** [1]. **[3]**

**11.** Factory machinery is used to make the products, so its depreciation is a
cost of production and an indirect factory cost [1]. Delivery vans are used to
deliver goods already made and sold, so their depreciation is a distribution
expense in the statement of profit or loss [1]. **[2]**

**12.** Charge for the year = 3,600 − 600 = 3,000 [1]. Manufacturing account:
3,000 × 5/6 = **2,500** [1]. Statement of profit or loss: 3,000 × 1/6 = **500** [1]. **[3]**

## Where marks are usually lost

- Using purchases instead of raw materials consumed.
- Treating supervisors' wages as direct labour.
- Splitting a cost before adjusting for an accrual or prepayment.
- Putting finished goods inventory in the manufacturing account.
- Showing only one inventory figure in the statement of financial position.
