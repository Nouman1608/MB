**These notes condense content area 3.1.1.6 of 9237: "Muslim beliefs about death, Barzakh, resurrection, the Day of Judgement, Heaven and Hell" and "different Muslim death rituals and their importance".** The full explanation is in the [Belief in life after death study guide](/resources/oxfordaqa-igcse-islamiyat-9237-belief-in-life-after-death/).

## The journey at a glance

**Death → Barzakh → Trumpet and resurrection → Judgement → Jannah or Jahannam**

| Stage | One line | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Death | Soul taken by Malak-al-Mowth at the fixed time | 3:185; 32:11 |
| Barzakh | Barrier between death and resurrection; two angels question the dead | 23:100; Hadith |
| Resurrection | Isra'fil blows the trumpet; bodily rising | 39:68; 75:3-4 |
| Judgement | Book of deeds, scales, witnesses, mercy | 21:47; 69:19-25 |
| Jannah | Gardens, rivers, peace, nearness to Allah | 47:15; 75:22-23 |
| Jahannam | Fire; a just consequence and a warning | 4:56 |

## Why it matters (keep to three headings)

1. **Accountability:** every action is recorded and weighed.
2. **Justice and hope:** wrongs will be put right.
3. **Perspective:** this life is a test (67:2); wealth ends at death.

## Barzakh in detail

- *Barzakh* = barrier (23:100).
- Questions in the grave: Who is your Lord? What is your religion? What do you say about the man sent to you?
- The grave is "a garden from the gardens of Paradise" or "a pit from the pits of Hell" (Tirmidhi).
- It is **not** the final outcome; judgement follows resurrection.

## Judgement in detail

- **Record:** right hand (success) or left (failure) (84:7-10).
- **Scales:** nothing is wasted: "an atom's weight of good" will be seen (99:7-8).
- **Witnesses:** hands, feet and the earth testify (36:65; 99:4).
- **Mercy:** greater than anger; intercession only by Allah's permission.
- **Names of the Day:** Yaum ad-Din, Yaum al-Qiyamah, al-Hashr.

## Jannah and Jahannam

| Jannah | Jahannam |
|---|---|
| Gardens with rivers (water, milk, honey, wine that does not intoxicate) | Fire |
| Peace, no sorrow, reunion with the righteous | Regret and suffering as a consequence of choices |
| Levels, the highest is Firdaus | Described as just and a warning |
| Greatest reward: pleasure of Allah and seeing Him | Many scholars say believing sinners may leave through mercy |

Descriptions use human language; Muslims believe the reality is beyond imagination.

## Passage: Qur'an 80:34-42

| Verses | Message | Use for |
|---|---|---|
| 34-37: "flee from his own siblings ... mother ... father ... wife ... children" | No one can carry another's burden | Individual responsibility |
| "for every man, that Day, will be a matter adequate for him" | Each person is occupied with their own account | Accountability |
| 38-39: "bright ... laughing, rejoicing" | The joy of the successful | Jannah |
| 40-42: "dust ... blackness" | The shame of those who rejected the truth | Jahannam |

## Death rituals in order

1. **Near death:** Shahadah and Qur'an.
2. **After death:** eyes closed, body covered.
3. **Ghusl:** washing by same-gender relatives.
4. **Kafan:** plain white cloth (equality).
5. **Salat al-Janazah:** standing prayer, fard kifayah (community duty).
6. **Burial:** soon, in a simple grave, on the right side facing the Qiblah.
7. **Mourning:** three days (widow: four months and ten days, 2:234); food and support for the family.

**Why each matters:** purity, equality, humility, community, remembering death.

## Differences

- Takbirs in Janazah: **four** (Sunni), **five** (Shi'a).
- Gatherings on set days: custom in some places, not required.
- Grief: tears accepted; wailing and self-harm discouraged.
- Graves: visiting encouraged; building over graves debated.
- Cremation not allowed.

## Exam traps

- Barzakh is not Paradise or Hell; it is a waiting stage.
- Resurrection is bodily.
- Do not state a custom as a rule of Islam.
- Add a reason to each ritual.
- Use at least two phrases from Qur'an 80:34-42 in a 12-mark answer.

## Model answer shapes

- **2 marks "Give two stages after death":** Barzakh; the Day of Judgement.
- **4 marks "Explain why the kafan is important":** It is plain white cloth for everyone [1], which shows that rich and poor are equal before Allah [1], and it is simple, so it avoids display [1] and reminds the living that worldly status ends at death [1].
- **6 marks:** two developed reasons plus a source (for example 99:7-8 or the Hadith on deeds that continue).
- **12 marks:** support the statement, challenge it, use the passage for each point, and give a judgement.

## A model 6-mark paragraph

**Question:** Explain **two** reasons why belief in the Day of Judgement is important. Apply **one** teaching. **[6]**

Belief in the Day of Judgement is important because it makes Muslims accountable for what they do [1]. If every word and deed is recorded and weighed, a Muslim will try to be honest and fair even when no one else can see [1]. It is also important because it gives hope of justice [1]. People who have been wronged and received no justice in this life believe that Allah will put it right on that Day [1]. The Qur'an says, "We place the scales of justice for the Day of Resurrection, so no soul will be treated unjustly at all" (21:47) [1], which assures believers that judgement will be perfectly fair [1].

## Linking to other topics

- **Angels (3.1.1.4):** Isra'fil blows the trumpet, Malak-al-Mowth takes the soul and the Kiraman Katibeen keep the record.
- **Al-Qadr (3.1.1.5):** the time of death is decreed, and judgement concerns choices.
- **Names of Allah (3.1.1.3):** Allah is *Al-Aleem* (knows every deed) and *Ar-Raheem* (merciful), which shapes Muslim hope on the Last Day.
- **Zakah and Salah (3.1.2):** the reward of worship and charity is expected in the Hereafter.

## Self-test

1. What does Barzakh mean?
2. Which angel blows the trumpet?
3. In which hand do the successful receive their book?
4. Which verse says the scales of justice will be set up?
5. What does 80:34-37 teach?
6. Give two features of Jannah.
7. What is fard kifayah?
8. How many takbirs do Sunni Muslims say in the Janazah prayer?
9. How long does a widow observe iddah?
10. Why is the shroud plain white?

**Answers:** 1. Barrier. 2. Isra'fil. 3. The right hand. 4. Qur'an 21:47. 5. People will flee from their families, because each is occupied with their own account. 6. Any two: gardens with rivers; peace; reunion; seeing Allah. 7. A duty on the community: if enough people do it, everyone is relieved. 8. Four. 9. Four months and ten days. 10. To show equality and simplicity.
