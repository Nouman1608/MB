## The exercise in one table

| Feature | Exercise 2 |
|---|---|
| Marks | 9, one per sentence |
| Material | a series of short paragraphs, then a series of sentences |
| Task | match each sentence to one paragraph |
| Objectives | R1, R2, R3 (no R4: answers are stated, but reworded) |
| What you write | a letter for each sentence; no Urdu prose |
| Suggested time | about 12-15 minutes |

**Check the rubric:** a paragraph may be the answer to more than one sentence.

## The method in six steps

1. Read the **sentences** first; note each one's key idea in the margin.
2. Mark sentences with **two parts**.
3. Read each **paragraph** once for who and what.
4. Match the **easy** sentences first.
5. For every match, point to the **evidence** words in the paragraph.
6. Answer **all** nine; no blanks.

## Paraphrase patterns

| Pattern | Paragraph says | Sentence says |
|---|---|---|
| Synonym | مشکل | دشوار |
| Synonym | شروع کیا | آغاز کیا |
| Category | سیب، آم، کیلے | پھل |
| Negated opposite | آسان نہیں تھا | مشکل تھا |
| Feeling shown | تالیاں بجیں تو آنکھوں میں آنسو آ گئے | وہ جذباتی ہو گیا |
| Change | پہلے… اب… | معمول بدل گیا |
| Frequency | ہفتے میں صرف ایک بار | پہلے سے کم |
| Intention | بننا چاہتی ہوں | مستقبل کا منصوبہ |

## Marker words to notice

| Type | Words |
|---|---|
| Time and change | پہلے، اب، شروع میں، آخرکار، ابھی تک، اگلے سال، دو سال پہلے |
| Intention | ارادہ ہے، چاہتا/چاہتی ہوں، سوچ رہا/رہی ہوں، منصوبہ |
| Opinion source | میرے خیال میں (own) / ان کا کہنا ہے، وہ کہتے ہیں (someone else's) |
| Contrast | لیکن، مگر، البتہ، اس کے باوجود |
| Feeling | خوش، مطمئن، پریشان، مایوس، حیران، شکر گزار، فخر |

## The five classic traps

1. **Shared word:** the paragraph that repeats a word from the sentence is often wrong.
2. **Half a match:** only one part of a two-part sentence is true.
3. **Wanted versus did:** چاہتا تھا is not کیا.
4. **Whose opinion:** a parent's or friend's view is not the writer's.
5. **Wrong time:** a past event does not match a sentence about the future.

## Evidence check in action

**Sentence:** "یہ شخص اپنے کام سے مطمئن نہیں ہے۔"

- Paragraph X: "میری نوکری میں تنخواہ اچھی ہے۔" → mentions the job, but says nothing negative. **No.**
- Paragraph Y: "دفتر میرے گھر سے دور ہے، مگر مجھے اپنا کام بہت پسند ہے۔" → a complaint about distance,
  but he likes the work. **No** (half-match trap).
- Paragraph Z: "ہر روز ایک ہی کام کر کے میں اکتا گیا ہوں اور کوئی نئی نوکری ڈھونڈ رہا ہوں۔" →
  bored and looking for a new job. **Yes.**

Only Z gives evidence for the whole sentence; Y contains a negative but about the wrong thing.

## Worked mini-task

**Paragraphs** (written for this page; three young people on learning a new skill):

- **A (ثنا):** میں نے پچھلے سال گٹار سیکھنا شروع کیا۔ شروع میں انگلیوں میں درد ہوتا تھا، مگر اب میں
  روز ایک گھنٹہ مشق کرتی ہوں۔ اگلے مہینے میں اسکول کے پروگرام میں پہلی بار سب کے سامنے بجاؤں گی۔
  *(I started learning the guitar last year. At first my fingers hurt, but now I practise for an hour
  every day. Next month I will play in front of everyone for the first time at the school event.)*
- **B (حمزہ):** میرے بڑے بھائی کہتے ہیں کہ تیراکی سب سے اچھی ورزش ہے، لیکن مجھے پانی سے ڈر لگتا ہے۔
  اس لیے میں نے کراٹے کی کلاس میں داخلہ لیا۔ مجھے اس سے بہت اعتماد ملا ہے۔ *(My elder brother says
  swimming is the best exercise, but I am afraid of water. So I joined a karate class. It has given me a
  lot of confidence.)*
- **C (زینب):** میں کھانا پکانا سیکھنا چاہتی تھی، مگر وقت ہی نہیں ملا۔ امتحانات کے بعد میرا ارادہ ہے کہ
  دادی سے بریانی بنانا سیکھوں۔ *(I wanted to learn to cook, but I never had the time. After my exams I
  intend to learn to make biryani from my grandmother.)*

**Sentences**

| Sentence | Answer | Evidence and trap |
|---|---|---|
| 1. یہ شخص جلد ہی لوگوں کے سامنے اپنا ہنر دکھائے گا/گی۔ (This person will soon show their skill in front of people.) | **A** | اگلے مہینے ... سب کے سامنے بجاؤں گی. C also looks to the future, but to learning, not performing (wrong time / wanted versus did). |
| 2. اس شخص نے کسی اور کی رائے پر عمل نہیں کیا۔ (This person did not act on someone else's advice.) | **B** | The brother recommends swimming; Hamza chose karate (whose opinion). |
| 3. اس شخص نے ابھی تک نیا ہنر سیکھنا شروع نہیں کیا۔ (This person has not yet started learning the new skill.) | **C** | چاہتی تھی ... وقت ہی نہیں ملا. A shares the words سیکھنا شروع کیا but says the opposite (shared word). |
| 4. نیا ہنر سیکھنے سے اس شخص کا خود پر بھروسہ بڑھا ہے۔ (Learning the skill has increased this person's self-belief.) | **B** | اعتماد ملا ہے is paraphrased as خود پر بھروسہ بڑھا. |

B answers two sentences, which is why the rubric matters: if each letter may be used more than once,
do not cross it off.

## More synonym and paraphrase pairs

| Paragraph says | Sentence may say | English |
|---|---|---|
| اعتماد | خود پر بھروسہ | confidence, self-belief |
| ڈر لگتا ہے | خوف | fear |
| مشق | تیاری / محنت | practice / effort |
| وقت ہی نہیں ملا | مصروفیت | busyness, no time |
| ارادہ ہے | منصوبہ | plan |
| مدد کی | ساتھ دیا | helped, supported |
| خوشی ہوئی | مسرت | happiness |
| بہت پیسے لگتے ہیں | مہنگا | it costs a lot / expensive |

## Step-by-step: checking a doubtful match

1. Split the sentence into its parts (who, what, when, whose view).
2. Find the words in the paragraph that prove **each** part.
3. Check the tense: past event, present habit or future plan.
4. Check whose view it is: the writer's own, or someone they mention.
5. If one part has no evidence, reject the paragraph, however many words it shares.

## More exam traps

- Treating a planned event (بجاؤں گی) and a wished-for one (چاہتی تھی) as the same.
- Missing a negative that reverses a sentence (شروع نہیں کیا).
- Matching on a name or place mentioned in both, rather than on meaning.

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Paraphrase | the same meaning in different words |
| Evidence | the exact words in the paragraph that justify a match |
| Distractor | a paragraph that shares words but not meaning |
| Two-part sentence | a statement with two conditions, both of which must be true |
| Elimination | ruling out paragraphs that cannot fit, then checking the remaining one |

## Self-test

1. How many marks is Exercise 2 worth, and which reading objectives does it test?
2. Why is the paragraph that repeats a word from the sentence often the wrong answer?
3. A paragraph says "میرے والد چاہتے ہیں کہ میں انجینئر بنوں، مگر مجھے آرٹ پسند ہے". Does it match
   "یہ شخص انجینئر بننا چاہتا/چاہتی ہے"? Why?
4. What does the marker پہلے… مگر اب… usually signal?
5. Give an Urdu synonym for مشکل and one for شروع.
6. Should you cross off a letter once you have used it?
7. In the worked mini-task, why is A wrong for sentence 3?
8. Which words in paragraph B are paraphrased by "خود پر بھروسہ بڑھا"?
9. Give a synonym for اعتماد and one for منصوبہ.
10. A sentence has two parts and the paragraph proves only one. What do you do?
11. Why is C wrong for sentence 1, even though it mentions a future plan?

**Answers:** 1. 9 marks; R1, R2 and R3. 2. Because the task setter rewords the correct paragraph and
places the shared word in a different paragraph as a distractor; matching on meaning avoids it. 3. No.
The father wants it; the writer prefers art. The sentence is about the writer's own wish, so this is
the "whose opinion" trap. 4. A change over time: a habit or situation that has altered. 5. دشوار for
مشکل; آغاز for شروع. 6. Only if the rubric says each letter is used once; otherwise a paragraph can
answer more than one sentence. 7. A has started (شروع کیا), so it is the opposite of "has not yet
started"; it only shares the words. 8. مجھے اس سے بہت اعتماد ملا ہے. 9. خود پر بھروسہ; ارادہ.
10. Reject that paragraph and look for one that proves both parts. 11. Zainab's plan is to learn a
skill, not to show it in front of people, so the sentence's key idea has no evidence in C.
