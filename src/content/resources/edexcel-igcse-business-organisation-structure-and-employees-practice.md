## Questions

**1.** Define the term "chain of command". **[2]**

**2.** Stansfell Printers has a director and two managers (production and sales). The production manager has 3 supervisors, and each supervisor has 6 operatives. The sales manager has 5 sales representatives.

**(a)** State the production manager's span of control. **[1]**
**(b)** State the span of control of each supervisor. **[1]**
**(c)** Calculate the total number of employees. **[2]**
**(d)** State the number of levels in the structure. **[1]**

**3.** Hollin Print has 18 employees and a flat structure. Outline one benefit of this structure for Hollin Print. **[2]**

**4.** Explain one disadvantage of a hierarchical structure. **[3]**

**5.** Tilmarsh Bakeries has six shops. Head office chooses the recipes and orders ingredients for every shop. Analyse one benefit to Tilmarsh Bakeries of this centralised approach. **[4]**

**6.** Explain why a manager who delegates a task is still accountable for the result. **[3]**

**7.** Give the functional area that is responsible for each activity.

**(a)** Setting the selling price of a new product. **[1]**
**(b)** Running an induction session for new staff. **[1]**
**(c)** Preparing the budget for next year. **[1]**
**(d)** Checking that finished goods meet the required standard. **[1]**
**(e)** Arranging packaging and distribution. **[1]**

**8.** A carpenter at Faresham Joinery works a basic week of 38 hours at 15.50 an hour. In one week the carpenter also works 5 hours of overtime paid at time and a half.

**(a)** Calculate the basic pay. **[2]**
**(b)** Calculate the overtime pay and the gross pay for the week. **[3]**
**(c)** Explain why the finance function must include wages in the cash-flow forecast. **[3]**

**9.** Explain the difference between dismissal and redundancy. **[3]**

**10.** Winterbeck Outdoor Supplies is a small business with 45 employees. The owner is choosing between two structures.

| | Option A: hierarchical | Option B: flat |
|---|---|---|
| Posts | Owner, 2 managers, 6 supervisors, 36 staff | Owner, 4 team leaders, 40 staff |
| Annual salary of each management post | Manager 38,000; supervisor 27,000 | Team leader 29,000 |

The owner wants quick responses to customers and lower costs. Justify which option the owner should choose. **[8]**

**11.** Orlanthe Appliances is a large manufacturer selling in five countries, with more than 250 employees. Head office approves every price change. This takes 12 days. Competitors in the main markets change prices within 2 days. The board proposes that regional managers set prices within a range agreed by head office. Evaluate this proposal. **[8]**

**12.** Outline one difference between a disciplinary procedure and a grievance procedure. **[2]**

## Answers

**1.** The line of authority from the top of the business to the bottom [1], along which instructions pass down and reports pass up [1]. **[2]**
*Examiner insight:* A definition question usually rewards a precise statement plus one further feature. Just writing "who is in charge" is too vague to show you know it is a line through several levels.

**2. (a)** The production manager has 3 supervisors directly below, so the span of control is **3** [1].
**(b)** Each supervisor has 6 operatives, so the span is **6** [1].
**(c)** 1 + 2 + 3 + (3 × 6) + 5 [1] = **29 employees** [1].
**(d)** Director, managers, supervisors and sales representatives, operatives: **4 levels** [1].
*Examiner insight:* Count only direct reports for span of control, and remember to include the top post and every group in a total. Showing the addition lets a small slip be seen as such.

**3.** Communication is faster [1] because there are few levels, so owner's instructions reach all 18 employees quickly and customers' needs are dealt with sooner [1]. **[2]**
*Examiner insight:* An outline gives two linked points. State the benefit, then say what it leads to for this business. A benefit with no consequence is only half of the answer.

**4.** Communication is slower [1] because messages pass through many levels [1], so a decision may reach staff late or be changed along the way, and the business may respond slowly to customers [1]. **[3]**
*Examiner insight:* Explain questions need a point followed by expansion. Naming "it is expensive" without saying why (more managers on salaries) leaves the expansion missing.

**5.** Head office can order ingredients for all six shops together [1], so orders are larger [1]. Suppliers may then charge less per unit [1], which lowers costs and increases the profit on each loaf sold [1]. **[4]**
*Examiner insight:* For analyse, build a chain of reasons from the benefit to its effect on the business. Listing two separate benefits in one line usually gives less than developing one.

**6.** Delegation passes authority to do the task, not the answerability for the outcome [1]. The manager gave the instruction and chose the person [1], so the manager must explain a poor result to their own senior manager and deal with the consequences [1]. **[3]**
*Examiner insight:* Separate the two words clearly. Answers that say the subordinate "takes all the blame" mix up doing the task with being answerable for it.

**7. (a)** Marketing [1]. **(b)** Human resources [1]. **(c)** Finance [1]. **(d)** Production [1]. **(e)** Marketing [1].
*Examiner insight:* Learn the specification's lists by area. Pricing and packaging and distribution are both under marketing, which is where answers often slip.

**8. (a)** 38 × 15.50 [1] = **589.00** [1].
**(b)** Overtime = 5 × 15.50 × 1.5 [1] = **116.25** [1]. Gross pay = 589.00 + 116.25 = **705.25** [1].
**(c)** Wages are cash going out of the business [1]. If they are left out, the forecast closing balance would be too high [1], and the business might not have enough cash on pay day [1]. **[3]**
*Examiner insight:* Show each stage of a wage calculation and give the unit. In part (c), say what happens to the forecast, not just that wages are "important".

**9.** Dismissal is when the employer ends the contract because of the employee's conduct or performance [1]. Redundancy is when the post itself disappears [1], for example because a department closes, so the employee has not done anything wrong [1]. **[3]**
*Examiner insight:* Give the reason behind each term. Saying "dismissal means losing your job" fits both words and shows no difference.

**10.** Option A cost: 2 × 38,000 + 6 × 27,000 = 238,000 [1]. Option B cost: 4 × 29,000 = 116,000 [1]. Option B is 122,000 a year cheaper [1]. Under Option B each team leader has 40 ÷ 4 = 10 staff, a span of 10, while A's supervisors each have 36 ÷ 6 = 6 [1]. Option B has a shorter chain of command (staff, team leader, owner), so customer matters can be decided and passed on faster [1]. A weakness of B is that a span of 10 means less close supervision and more pressure on each team leader [1]. Option A has more promotion steps but costs more and is slower [1]. Judgement: **Option B** should be chosen because it saves 122,000 and suits the aim of quick responses, provided team leaders are trained [1]. **[8]**
*Examiner insight:* Justify means recommending one option using the information given. Use the figures, weigh the other option, and end with a clear choice and a reason tied to the owner's aims.

**11.** The proposal would cut the delay: 12 − 2 = 10 days slower than competitors [1], so regional managers could match rivals and avoid losing sales [1]. Regional managers know their own markets, so prices may fit local demand better [1], and trusted managers may be more motivated [1]. However, prices may differ between countries, which can confuse customers and damage the brand [1]. Managers might set prices too low and reduce profit, or lack training to judge [1]. Judgement: the proposal is worthwhile, because a ten-day delay is costly in competitive markets [1], as long as head office sets the range and checks results regularly [1]. **[8]**
*Examiner insight:* An evaluate answer needs both sides and a supported conclusion. A balanced list that stops without a decision is usually not enough.

**12.** A disciplinary procedure is used by the employer to deal with an employee who has broken the rules [1], whereas a grievance procedure is used by an employee to make a formal complaint, for example about treatment by a manager [1]. **[2]**
*Examiner insight:* An outline of a difference needs both sides stated. Say who starts each procedure and why.

## Where marks are usually lost

- Giving the total number of staff when asked for one manager's span of control.
- Forgetting to count the top post when adding up employees.
- Describing a benefit without saying how it helps the named business.
- Writing "it is slower" with no reason such as the number of levels.
- Saying accountability passes to the person who is given the task.
- Putting pricing or packaging in the wrong functional area.
- Leaving out working or units in wage calculations.
- Stopping an evaluate answer after the list of points, with no judgement.
- Ignoring the case figures in a justify question, such as the salaries.
- Treating redundancy and dismissal as the same.
