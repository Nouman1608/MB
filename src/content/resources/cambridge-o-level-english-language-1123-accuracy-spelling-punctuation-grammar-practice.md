> **These are original questions written for Marlbridge**, for practice on the writing skill
> "demonstrate accuracy in spelling, punctuation and grammar", assessed as W5 in 1123 Paper 2. W5 is
> not marked separately in the examination; it is one of the objectives used to mark directed writing
> and the composition. The marks below are practice marks, one for each error correctly fixed unless
> stated otherwise. All passages are original.

## Section A

**1.** Correct each comma splice in two different ways: (a) "The shop was closed, we went home." (b) "My brother is older than me, he is shorter." **[4]**

**2.** Rewrite this fragment as part of a complete sentence: "Although the museum was free." **[1]**

**3.** Add apostrophes where needed: "The boys bikes were left outside the childrens library, and its lock was broken." (There are several boys.) **[3]**

**4.** Choose the correct word in each: (a) The coach praised (there / their / they're) effort. (b) The storm had little (affect / effect) on the match. (c) She needs to (practice / practise) the piano. **[3]**

**5.** Correct the verb in each: (a) "The bag of oranges were heavy." (b) "Everyone in the class have finished." **[2]**

**6.** Correct the grammar: "Me and Hamza could of won if there was less people in the race." **[3]**

## Section B

**7.** Explain why the comma in this sentence is wrong, and correct it: "The students who missed the trip, will receive a refund." **[2]**

**8.** Rewrite this sentence to remove the unclear pronoun: "When Sara spoke to her mother, she was upset." **[1]**

**9.** Rewrite this sentence to correct the misplaced opening phrase: "Running for the bus, my phone fell out of my pocket." **[1]**

**10.** Punctuate the dialogue correctly: wait for me shouted Amir I'm coming **[2]**

**11.** The paragraph below (original) contains six errors of punctuation, grammar or spelling. Rewrite it correctly.

> Every summer my grandparents village holds a fair. Stalls sell food, toys and clothes, there is a
> competition for the best vegetables. Last year my grandfather won first prize, he was delighted. The
> judges was impressed by his tomato's, which were the biggest anyone had ever saw. **[6]**

**12.** The passage below (original) contains eight errors. Identify and correct each one.

> The council have decided to close the libary on Sundays. Its a decision that will effect many
> families, especially those who's children study there at weekends. Less people may use the library
> as a result, the council says it will save money. But is a small saving worth the lose of a quiet
> place to work. **[8]**

**13.** Each sentence uses a colon or semicolon incorrectly. Explain the error and correct it: (a) "We needed: bread, milk and eggs." (b) "Because it was raining; the match was cancelled." **[4]**

## Answers

**1.** Any two correct methods for each sentence, one mark each:
(a) "The shop was closed. We went home." **[1]** / "The shop was closed, so we went home." **[1]**
(b) "My brother is older than me; he is shorter." **[1]** / "My brother is older than me, but he is shorter." **[1]**
Also creditable: making one clause subordinate, for example "Because the shop was closed, we went home" or "Although my brother is older than me, he is shorter."

**2.** For example: "Although the museum was free, few people visited it." **[1]**

**3.** boys' bikes, because there are several boys **[1]**; children's library, because "children" is a plural that does not end in s **[1]**; "its" stays without an apostrophe, because it is possessive (the lock belonging to the library), not "it is" **[1]**.

**4.** (a) their **[1]** (b) effect **[1]** (c) practise **[1]**

**5.** (a) "The bag of oranges was heavy." **[1]** (b) "Everyone in the class has finished." **[1]**

**6.** "Hamza and I" **[1]**; "could have" **[1]**; "fewer people" **[1]**. (Also acceptable: "if there had been fewer people".)

**7.** A comma must not separate a subject ("The students who missed the trip") from its verb ("will receive") **[1]**. Correct: "The students who missed the trip will receive a refund." **[1]**

**8.** For example: "Sara was upset when she spoke to her mother." **[1]**

**9.** For example: "As I ran for the bus, my phone fell out of my pocket." **[1]**

**10.** "Wait for me!" shouted Amir. "I'm coming!" **[2]** One mark for correct speech marks around both spoken parts, one for correct capitals and end punctuation.

**11.** Corrected:

> Every summer my grandparents' village holds a fair. Stalls sell food, toys and clothes, and there is
> a competition for the best vegetables. Last year my grandfather won first prize, and he was
> delighted. The judges were impressed by his tomatoes, which were the biggest anyone had ever seen.

One mark per correction:
- grandparents' (plural possessive) **[1]**;
- comma splice after "clothes" fixed **[1]**;
- comma splice after "first prize" fixed **[1]**;
- "were" for "was" (agreement with "judges") **[1]**;
- "tomatoes" (plain plural: no apostrophe, and spelled *-oes*) **[1]**;
- "had ever seen" (past participle after "had") **[1]**.

**12.** One mark per correction:
- "library" (spelling) **[1]**;
- "It's" (it is) **[1]**;
- "affect" (verb) **[1]**;
- "whose" (possessive, not "who is") **[1]**;
- "Fewer people" (countable) **[1]**;
- comma splice after "as a result" fixed, for example with a full stop or "and" **[1]**;
- "loss" (noun, not the verb "lose") **[1]**;
- question mark at the end of the final sentence **[1]**.
("The council have decided" is acceptable in British English, where a collective noun can take a plural verb; "The council has decided" is also correct.)

**13.** (a) The words before a colon should form a complete sentence, and "We needed" cannot stand alone **[1]**; correct: "We needed bread, milk and eggs." or "We needed three things: bread, milk and eggs." **[1]**
(b) A semicolon must join two main clauses, but "Because it was raining" is a subordinate clause **[1]**; correct: "Because it was raining, the match was cancelled." **[1]**

## Where marks are usually lost

- **Question 3:** adding an apostrophe to "its".
- **Question 6:** correcting "could of" but not "me and Hamza".
- **Question 11:** fixing the apostrophes but missing the comma splices.
- **Question 12:** changing "The council have" and missing the question mark.
