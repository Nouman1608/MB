Section 1.4 of Cambridge IGCSE ICT 0417 covers the characteristics and uses of desktop computers and of mobile computers (laptops, smartphones, tablets and phablets), and the advantages and disadvantages of each type, including portability and expandability.

Condensed for revision. For full explanations and a worked comparison, use the [types of computer study guide](/resources/cambridge-igcse-ict-0417-types-of-computer/), then try the [types of computer practice questions](/resources/cambridge-igcse-ict-0417-types-of-computer-practice/).

## The trade-off to remember

**Portability up, expandability down.** As computers get smaller and lighter, there is less room inside, so components are fixed in place and hard to upgrade.

Desktop → laptop → tablet → phablet → smartphone: more portable, less expandable, smaller screen, more reliance on touch input and battery power.

## Desktop

- **Characteristics**: fixed location; separate base unit, monitor, keyboard and mouse (or an all-in-one); mains powered; room for upgrades and cooling.
- **Uses**: office work, school computer rooms, high-performance gaming, video and music editing.
- **For**: expandable; more performance for the money; ergonomic full-size keyboard and large monitor; harder to steal or drop; no battery limit.
- **Against**: not portable; takes desk space; cables; stops in a power cut.

## Laptop

- **Characteristics**: all-in-one folding case; keyboard, touchpad, screen, camera, microphone and speakers built in; battery or mains.
- **Uses**: working and studying in different places, meetings and presentations, travel.
- **For**: portable; all parts in one unit; works without mains power; small footprint.
- **Against**: limited expandability; dearer than a desktop of similar power; smaller keyboard and screen; touchpad less comfortable; easier to lose or damage; battery wears.

## Smartphone

- **Characteristics**: phone with computer functions; touch screen; apps; mobile network and wi-fi; camera, GPS and sensors.
- **Uses**: communication, internet, navigation, photography, payments, learning and gaming apps, remote control of devices.
- **For**: most portable; connects in most places; many functions in one device.
- **Against**: small screen; slow on-screen typing; less processing power and storage; battery life; easily lost, risking personal data.

## Tablet

- **Characteristics**: flat, touch-screen computer larger than a phone; wi-fi, sometimes mobile network.
- **Uses**: reading, video, classroom apps, restaurant orders, drawing with a stylus.
- **For**: bigger screen than a phone; lighter than a laptop; long battery life; quick to start; easy touch interface.
- **Against**: on-screen keyboard slow for long work; less power than a laptop; few ports; screen can crack.

## Phablet

- **Characteristics**: a large-screen smartphone, between a phone and a small tablet in size.
- **For**: larger screen than a phone; one device instead of two.
- **Against**: hard to use one-handed; may not fit a pocket; smaller than a tablet; screen uses more battery.

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Portability | How easily a computer can be carried and used in different places |
| Expandability | How easily hardware can be added or upgraded |
| Base unit | The case of a desktop containing the motherboard, CPU and drives |
| All-in-one | A desktop with the base unit built into the monitor |
| Touchpad | Built-in pointing device on a laptop |
| Phablet | Smartphone with a screen large enough to approach tablet size |
| Remotely controlled device | A device operated from a distance, for example by an app on a smartphone |

## Exam traps

- "Laptops are more powerful than desktops" is the wrong way round for a given cost.
- Expandability is not storage capacity; it is the ability to add or upgrade hardware.
- A tablet's main input is the touch screen, not a keyboard.
- "Portable" scores only when linked to the scenario: "the nurse can carry it between wards".
- Small devices are easily lost, so stored personal data is at risk: a useful disadvantage that is often forgotten.
- Give both sides in a compare: "a desktop is expandable whereas a laptop's components are hard to upgrade".

## Self-test

1. Define *expandability*.
2. Give **two** characteristics of a desktop computer.
3. Name **two** uses of a desktop computer from the syllabus categories.
4. Why are laptops usually less expandable than desktops?
5. Give **two** advantages of a laptop compared with a desktop.
6. What is a phablet?
7. Give **one** disadvantage of a phablet compared with a smartphone.
8. Give **two** disadvantages of a smartphone for writing a long report.
9. Describe how a smartphone can be used to control a device remotely.
10. Give **one** advantage of a tablet over a laptop for a waiter taking orders.
11. Why might a desktop be healthier to use for long periods than a laptop?
12. Give **one** security risk that is greater for mobile computers.

## Self-test answers

1. How easily a computer can be upgraded or have extra hardware added.
2. Any two: fixed location; separate monitor, keyboard and mouse; mains powered; room for extra components.
3. Any two: office and business management; education; gaming; entertainment.
4. Components are packed into a small case and often fixed in place, so they are hard to replace or add to.
5. Portable; battery powered so usable without mains; all components in one unit; takes less space.
6. A smartphone with a large screen, between a phone and a small tablet in size.
7. Harder to use with one hand, or may not fit in a pocket, or uses more battery power for the screen.
8. Small screen makes the document hard to read; on-screen keyboard is slow and error-prone.
9. An app sends commands over wi-fi, Bluetooth or the internet to the device, such as heating or a drone, to change its settings.
10. Lighter and easier to hold while standing; touch screen quick to use; long battery life.
11. The keyboard and monitor can be positioned separately at the correct heights, reducing back and neck strain.
12. Mobile computers are more easily lost or stolen, so personal data stored on them may be accessed.
