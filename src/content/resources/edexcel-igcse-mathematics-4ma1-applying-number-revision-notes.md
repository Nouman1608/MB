These notes condense **section 1.10, Applying number**, of the Pearson Edexcel International GCSE Mathematics A (4MA1) specification, Issue 2. It is Foundation Tier content that the Higher Tier also assumes, so it applies to both tiers. For worked examples, use the [section 1.10 study guide](/resources/edexcel-igcse-mathematics-4ma1-applying-number/).

## Condensed recall: metric units (1.10 B)

The specification says metric units only.

| Quantity | Facts |
|---|---|
| Length | 10 mm = 1 cm; 100 cm = 1 m; 1000 m = 1 km |
| Mass | 1000 g = 1 kg; 1000 kg = 1 tonne |
| Capacity | 1000 ml = 1 litre; 100 cl = 1 litre |
| Area | 1 cm² = 100 mm²; 1 m² = 10 000 cm² |
| Volume | 1 cm³ = 1000 mm³; 1 m³ = 1 000 000 cm³ |
| Volume ↔ capacity | 1 cm³ = 1 ml; 1000 cm³ = 1 litre; 1 m³ = 1000 litres |

- Smaller unit: multiply. Larger unit: divide.
- For area or volume, convert the lengths first, then calculate.

## Condensed recall: time (1.10 C)

- 24-hour times use four digits: 7:05 pm = 19:05; 12:30 am = 00:30.
- Durations: count on to the next hour, then whole hours, then minutes.
- Decimal hours: minutes ÷ 60 → hours; decimal part × 60 → minutes. 2 h 18 min = 2.3 h; 3.45 h = 3 h 27 min.
- Working backwards: subtract the hours, then the minutes.
- Timetables: a row is one stop; a column is one journey.
- Days in months: 30 in April, June, September, November; 28 or 29 in February; 31 in the rest.

## Condensed recall: money (1.10 C)

- Money to 2 decimal places: £7.5 → £7.50.
- Pence to pounds: ÷ 100 before combining with pounds.
- Overtime: time and a half = 1.5 × rate; double time = 2 × rate.
- Bills: fixed charge + usage × rate; add a percentage with a multiplier.
- Shared costs: round up if everyone must cover the total.
- Currency at £1 = r units: pounds → foreign, × r; foreign → pounds, ÷ r.
- Two rates (selling and buying back): use the correct rate for each direction.
- Compare prices in two currencies only after converting to one currency.

## Condensed recall: rounding in context

| Situation | Round | Example |
|---|---|---|
| You must have enough | Up | 5.94 litres of paint, 2.5 litre tins → 3 tins |
| Only whole items that fit | Down | 500 cm ÷ 35 cm → 14 pieces |
| Money owed by each person | Up to the next penny | £34.1733 → £34.18 |
| Money as a general amount | Nearest penny | £115.702 → £115.70 |

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Metric units | Units based on powers of 10: mm, cm, m, km, g, kg, ml, litre |
| Capacity | How much a container holds, in ml or litres |
| Exchange rate | How much of one currency equals one unit of another |
| Commission | A fee charged for changing money, often a percentage |
| Overtime | Hours worked beyond the basic hours, often at a higher rate |
| Time and a half | 1.5 times the basic hourly rate |
| Standing charge | A fixed daily charge on a utility bill |
| Timetable | A table of departure and arrival times |

## Exam traps

- **Area factors:** 1 m² is 10 000 cm², not 100 cm².
- **Volume to litres:** cm³ ÷ 1000; m³ × 1000.
- **Minutes are not decimals:** 1.5 hours is 1 h 30 min, not 1 h 50 min.
- **Clock arithmetic across midnight:** 23:40 + 55 min = 00:35.
- **Wrong direction in currency:** check that the answer should be larger or smaller.
- **Missed step:** in painting problems, remember the number of coats and the area that is not painted.
- **Units in the answer:** litres, kg, £, minutes.

## Self-test

1. Change 3.25 kg into grams.
2. Change 85 cl into litres.
3. Change 4500 cm³ into litres.
4. Change 0.35 m² into cm².
5. Write 2 hours 18 minutes in hours as a decimal.
6. Write 3.45 hours in hours and minutes.
7. A film starts at 19:50 and lasts 2 hours 35 minutes. When does it finish? Give the time in the 12-hour clock.
8. A 5 m length of ribbon is cut into 35 cm pieces. How many pieces can be cut, and how much is left over?
9. The rate is £1 = 165 yen. Change 28 000 yen into pounds.
10. How many nights are there from 27 June to 4 July?
11. Basic pay is £12 per hour. How much is 6 hours of overtime at time and a half?
12. A meal costs £64.90 and is shared equally between 4 people. How much should each pay so the bill is covered?

**Answers**

1. 3250 g.
2. 0.85 litres.
3. 4.5 litres.
4. 3500 cm².
5. 18 ÷ 60 = 0.3, so 2.3 hours.
6. 0.45 × 60 = 27, so 3 h 27 min.
7. 22:25, which is 10:25 pm.
8. 500 ÷ 35 = 14 remainder 10: 14 pieces, with 10 cm left over.
9. 28 000 ÷ 165 = 169.696…, so £169.70.
10. June has 30 days: 27 June to 30 June is 3 nights, then 4 more to 4 July: 7 nights.
11. 6 × 18 = £108.
12. 64.90 ÷ 4 = 16.225, so £16.23 each.
