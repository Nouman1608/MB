# Appendix 1 at a glance

In Question 1 (8 marks) three passages are set and you answer on two. The syllabus names three groups. Learn passages from every group, because the three set passages may come from any of them.

Related: [Major Themes of the Qur'an revision notes](/resources/cambridge-o-level-islamiyat-major-themes-of-the-quran-revision-notes/).

## Self-test (cover the right-hand column)

1. How many passages are set, and how many do you answer on? *Three set, two answered.*
2. Which group contains Sura 112? *Allah in himself.*
3. Which group contains Sura 1? *Allah's relationship with the created world.*
4. Which group contains Sura 93? *Allah's Messengers.*
5. What two things must each passage answer cover? *The major theme or themes, and their importance in the lives of Muslims today.*
6. What must candidates show knowledge of, besides the translation? *The original Arabic.*
