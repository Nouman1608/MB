Condensed notes for the final weeks. For the full explanation and a worked report, use the
[Exercise 6 extended writing study guide](/resources/cambridge-igcse-urdu-language-exercise-6-extended-writing-exercise/).

## The task in one box

```
Exercise 6 – Extended writing exercise        Paper 1, 16 marks
Length     approximately 200 words of continuous prose
Stimulus   short prompts printed in the question paper
Specified  purpose, format and audience
Assesses   W1-W5     Time to give it: about 30 minutes
```

16 marks is more than a quarter of Paper 1 and twice Exercise 5 (about 120 words, 8 marks). The extra
marks reward **development and organisation**, not only correct sentences.

## The five writing objectives

- **W1** communicate ideas and opinions clearly, accurately and effectively
- **W2** coherent paragraphs with a range of linking devices
- **W3** a range of grammatical structures and vocabulary, used accurately
- **W4** control of punctuation and spelling
- **W5** register and style/format right for the purpose and audience

## Key terms

| English | Urdu | Note |
|---|---|---|
| purpose | مقصد | what the writing must do |
| format | طرزِ تحریر | article, blog, report, review, letter |
| audience / readers | مخاطب / قارئین | decides the register |
| headline | سرخی | expected on an article |
| introduction | تمہید | first paragraph |
| conclusion | اختتامیہ | last paragraph |
| recommendations | سفارشات | expected in a report |
| review | تبصرہ | describe, evaluate, recommend |
| opinion | رائے | give it with a reason |

## Format checklist

- **Article (مضمون):** headline, an opening that hooks the reader, a closing thought.
- **Blog (بلاگ):** first person, lively, may speak to readers directly.
- **Report (رپورٹ):** title, purpose in the first sentence, factual paragraphs, سفارشات.
- **Review (تبصرہ):** name what is reviewed, its good and weak points, recommend or not.
- **Letter (خط):** greeting and closing that suit the reader.

## The 200-word plan

```
1  Introduction          ~30 words    subject + format + purpose
2  Main point 1          ~60-70       point, reason, example
3  Main point 2 / other side  ~60-70  point, reason, example
4  Conclusion            ~30-40       opinion / recommendation
```

Plan 3 minutes, write 22, check 5.

## Linking-device bank

- **Adding:** اس کے علاوہ، مزید یہ کہ، نیز
- **Contrast:** لیکن، تاہم، دوسری طرف، اس کے باوجود
- **Cause and result:** کیونکہ، اس لیے، اس کی وجہ سے، چنانچہ
- **Order:** سب سے پہلے، پھر، آخر میں
- **Concluding:** مختصر یہ کہ

| passive | میلے کا اہتمام اسکول کے میدان میں کیا گیا۔ |
| suggestion | بہتر ہوگا کہ ہر پروگرام مختصر رکھا جائے۔ |

## Register in one line

Formal reader: **آپ**, formal verbs (مدعو کرنا، شرکت کرنا، اہتمام کرنا). Friendly reader: warmer, but still
correct written Urdu. Never switch between the two in one piece.

## Error watchlist

- نے + perfective verb agrees with the **object**: میں نے کہانی لکھی، میں نے خط لکھا۔
- Feminine nouns that catch learners out: محنت، عمارت، تقریب، کتاب، رائے
- Endings: ے and ی (لڑکے / لڑکی) change the meaning.
- Spelling: ضروری، ذریعے، تعلیم، پہلے، پھر
- Punctuation: **۔** at the end of each sentence, **،** inside it, **؟** for questions.

## Worked example: an article

**Task (written for this page):** Your school held a cleanliness week. Write an **article** for the
**school magazine** (audience: students and teachers) saying what happened, what students learned and
what you suggest for next year. Write about 200 words.

**Purpose:** inform and suggest. **Format:** article, so a headline (سرخی). **Register:** formal but
readable; no تم.

> **صفائی کا ہفتہ: چھوٹے قدم، بڑی تبدیلی**
>
> پچھلے مہینے ہمارے اسکول میں پہلی بار صفائی کا ہفتہ منایا گیا۔ اس کا مقصد یہ تھا کہ طلبہ اپنے اسکول
> اور محلے کو صاف رکھنے کی ذمہ داری محسوس کریں۔
>
> سب سے پہلے ہر جماعت نے اپنا کمرہ صاف کیا اور دیواروں پر صفائی کے بارے میں پوسٹر لگائے۔ اس کے بعد
> بڑی جماعتوں کے طلبہ نے اسکول کے قریب والے پارک سے کچرا اٹھایا۔ میں نے دیکھا کہ صرف دو گھنٹوں میں
> پچاس سے زیادہ تھیلے بھر گئے۔ یہ منظر دیکھ کر ہم سب حیران رہ گئے۔
>
> اس ہفتے سے ہم نے کئی باتیں سیکھیں۔ مثال کے طور پر، اگر ہر شخص اپنا کچرا کوڑے دان میں ڈالے تو صفائی
> کرنے والوں کا کام آدھا رہ جائے گا۔ اس کے علاوہ، مل کر کام کرنے سے جماعتوں کے درمیان دوستی بھی
> بڑھی۔
>
> تاہم کچھ مشکلات بھی پیش آئیں۔ دستانوں کی کمی تھی، اس لیے کئی طلبہ کو انتظار کرنا پڑا۔ میری تجویز
> ہے کہ اگلے سال والدین کو بھی مدعو کیا جائے اور سامان پہلے سے خرید لیا جائے۔
>
> مختصر یہ کہ صفائی کا ہفتہ بہت کامیاب رہا۔ امید ہے کہ یہ روایت ہر سال جاری رہے گی۔

**English gloss (by paragraph):** *Headline:* "Cleanliness week: small steps, a big change." (1) Last
month our school held a cleanliness week for the first time, so that students would feel responsible
for keeping their school and neighbourhood clean. (2) First each class cleaned its room and put up
posters about cleanliness; then older students collected litter from the park near the school. In only
two hours more than fifty bags were filled, which surprised everyone. (3) We learned several things:
for example, if everyone put their rubbish in a bin, the cleaners' work would be halved; working
together also strengthened friendships between classes. (4) However, there were difficulties: there
were not enough gloves, so many students had to wait. I suggest inviting parents next year and buying
equipment in advance. (5) In short, the week was a success, and I hope the tradition continues every
year.

The model is about 200 words including the headline.

| Objective | Where the model shows it |
|---|---|
| W1 ideas and opinions | every part of the task is covered; the conclusion gives a judgement |
| W2 paragraphs and linking | five paragraphs; سب سے پہلے، اس کے بعد، اس کے علاوہ، تاہم، مختصر یہ کہ |
| W3 range and accuracy | passive (منایا گیا، مدعو کیا جائے)، condition (اگر ... تو)، نے agreement (ہم نے کئی باتیں سیکھیں) |
| W4 punctuation and spelling | ۔ at every sentence end, ، inside sentences and after مثال کے طور پر |
| W5 register and format | headline, impersonal suggestion (میری تجویز ہے کہ ... مدعو کیا جائے), no تم |

## Step-by-step method

1. **Underline** purpose, format and audience in the rubric, and tick each prompt you must cover.
2. **Plan** four or five paragraph headings, one idea each, with one reason or example noted beside it.
3. **Choose** two structures you will show on purpose (for example a passive and a condition) and two
   linking devices you have not used yet.
4. **Write** paragraph by paragraph, keeping to the plan's word guide.
5. **Check** in three sweeps: agreement and نے; spelling of common words; punctuation ۔ ، ؟.

## Error-correction drill

| Wrong | Right | Why |
|---|---|---|
| ہم نے کئی باتیں سیکھا۔ | ہم نے کئی باتیں سیکھیں۔ | with نے the verb agrees with باتیں (feminine plural) |
| طلبہ نے پوسٹر لگایا۔ (several posters) | طلبہ نے پوسٹر لگائے۔ | plural object, plural verb |
| دوستی بڑھا۔ | دوستی بڑھی۔ | دوستی is feminine; no نے with بڑھنا |
| ہم سب حیران رہ گیا۔ | ہم سب حیران رہ گئے۔ | ہم takes a plural verb |
| اگلے سال والدین کو مدعو کرو۔ | اگلے سال والدین کو مدعو کیا جائے۔ | formal, impersonal register |

## Exam traps

- Writing a list of prompts instead of developed paragraphs.
- Copying a prompt sentence word for word.
- No headline on an article, no recommendations in a report.
- Mixing آپ and تم.
- Spending so long writing that no checking time is left.
- Answering only two of three prompts because the first one took most of the words.
- Ending without a conclusion, so the piece stops rather than finishes.

## Self-test

1. What three things must you underline in the rubric before planning?
2. How many marks is Exercise 6 worth, and roughly how long should you give it?
3. Name two features a report should show that an article need not.
4. Correct the error: طلبہ نے بہت محنت کیا۔
5. Give one contrast linking device and one cause-and-result linking device.
6. Rewrite in a formal register: "اگلی بار امی ابو کو بھی بلا لینا۔"
7. Why does a piece far shorter than 200 words usually score lower?
8. Correct the error: ہم نے کئی باتیں سیکھا۔
9. In the model article, find one passive structure and one condition.
10. Why does the model write "مدعو کیا جائے" rather than "مدعو کرو"?
11. Which linking device in the model moves from what went well to the difficulties?
12. Name two structures you could show on purpose to demonstrate range (W3).

**Answers:** 1. Purpose, format and audience. 2. 16 marks; about 30 minutes, at Paper 1's rate of roughly
two minutes a mark. 3. A statement of purpose at the start and a section of recommendations (سفارشات),
in a formal, often impersonal style. 4. طلبہ نے بہت محنت کی۔ (with نے the verb agrees with محنت, which
is feminine). 5. For example تاہم or دوسری طرف; اس لیے or اس کی وجہ سے. 6. For example: اگلے سال
والدین کو بھی مدعو کیا جائے۔ 7. Because it usually means points have been stated but not developed with
reasons and examples, which limits W1 and W2. 8. ہم نے کئی باتیں سیکھیں۔ 9. Passive: منایا گیا or مدعو کیا جائے;
condition: اگر ہر شخص ... ڈالے تو ... رہ جائے گا. 10. The impersonal passive suits a formal article for
students and teachers; مدعو کرو is an informal command (W5). 11. تاہم. 12. Any two of: a condition, a
relative clause, a passive, a suggestion with بہتر ہوگا کہ, a comparison, a contrast with اگرچہ ...
لیکن.
