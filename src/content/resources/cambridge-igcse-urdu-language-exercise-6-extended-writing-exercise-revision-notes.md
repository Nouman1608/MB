Condensed notes for the final weeks. For the full explanation and a worked report, use the
[Exercise 6 extended writing study guide](/resources/cambridge-igcse-urdu-language-exercise-6-extended-writing-exercise/).

## The task in one box

```
Exercise 6 – Extended writing exercise        Paper 1, 16 marks
Length     approximately 200 words of continuous prose
Stimulus   short prompts printed in the question paper
Specified  purpose, format and audience
Assesses   W1-W5     Time to give it: about 30 minutes
```

16 marks is more than a quarter of Paper 1 and twice Exercise 5 (about 120 words, 8 marks). The extra
marks reward **development and organisation**, not only correct sentences.

## The five writing objectives

- **W1** communicate ideas and opinions clearly, accurately and effectively
- **W2** coherent paragraphs with a range of linking devices
- **W3** a range of grammatical structures and vocabulary, used accurately
- **W4** control of punctuation and spelling
- **W5** register and style/format right for the purpose and audience

## Key terms

| English | Urdu | Note |
|---|---|---|
| purpose | مقصد | what the writing must do |
| format | طرزِ تحریر | article, blog, report, review, letter |
| audience / readers | مخاطب / قارئین | decides the register |
| headline | سرخی | expected on an article |
| introduction | تمہید | first paragraph |
| conclusion | اختتامیہ | last paragraph |
| recommendations | سفارشات | expected in a report |
| review | تبصرہ | describe, evaluate, recommend |
| opinion | رائے | give it with a reason |

## Format checklist

- **Article (مضمون):** headline, an opening that hooks the reader, a closing thought.
- **Blog (بلاگ):** first person, lively, may speak to readers directly.
- **Report (رپورٹ):** title, purpose in the first sentence, factual paragraphs, سفارشات.
- **Review (تبصرہ):** name what is reviewed, its good and weak points, recommend or not.
- **Letter (خط):** greeting and closing that suit the reader.

## The 200-word plan

```
1  Introduction          ~30 words    subject + format + purpose
2  Main point 1          ~60-70       point, reason, example
3  Main point 2 / other side  ~60-70  point, reason, example
4  Conclusion            ~30-40       opinion / recommendation
```

Plan 3 minutes, write 22, check 5.

## Linking-device bank

- **Adding:** اس کے علاوہ، مزید یہ کہ، نیز
- **Contrast:** لیکن، تاہم، دوسری طرف، اس کے باوجود
- **Cause and result:** کیونکہ، اس لیے، اس کی وجہ سے، چنانچہ
- **Order:** سب سے پہلے، پھر، آخر میں
- **Concluding:** مختصر یہ کہ

## Register in one line

Formal reader: **آپ**, formal verbs (مدعو کرنا، شرکت کرنا، اہتمام کرنا). Friendly reader: warmer, but still
correct written Urdu. Never switch between the two in one piece.

## Error watchlist

- نے + perfective verb agrees with the **object**: میں نے کہانی لکھی، میں نے خط لکھا۔
- Feminine nouns that catch learners out: محنت، عمارت، تقریب، کتاب، رائے
- Endings: ے and ی (لڑکے / لڑکی) change the meaning.
- Spelling: ضروری، ذریعے، تعلیم، پہلے، پھر
- Punctuation: **۔** at the end of each sentence, **،** inside it, **؟** for questions.

## Exam traps

- Writing a list of prompts instead of developed paragraphs.
- Copying a prompt sentence word for word.
- No headline on an article, no recommendations in a report.
- Mixing آپ and تم.
- Spending so long writing that no checking time is left.

## Self-test

1. What three things must you underline in the rubric before planning?
2. How many marks is Exercise 6 worth, and roughly how long should you give it?
3. Name two features a report should show that an article need not.
4. Correct the error: طلبہ نے بہت محنت کیا۔
5. Give one contrast linking device and one cause-and-result linking device.
6. Rewrite in a formal register: "اگلی بار امی ابو کو بھی بلا لینا۔"
7. Why does a piece far shorter than 200 words usually score lower?

**Answers:** 1. Purpose, format and audience. 2. 16 marks; about 30 minutes, at Paper 1's rate of roughly
two minutes a mark. 3. A statement of purpose at the start and a section of recommendations (سفارشات),
in a formal, often impersonal style. 4. طلبہ نے بہت محنت کی۔ (with نے the verb agrees with محنت, which
is feminine). 5. For example تاہم or دوسری طرف; اس لیے or اس کی وجہ سے. 6. For example: اگلے سال
والدین کو بھی مدعو کیا جائے۔ 7. Because it usually means points have been stated but not developed with
reasons and examples, which limits W1 and W2.
