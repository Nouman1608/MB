## Definition (Core)

> Aerobic respiration is the **chemical reactions in cells** that **use oxygen** to **break down nutrient molecules** to **release energy**.

Five ideas, all needed: chemical reactions · in cells · use oxygen · break down nutrient molecules · release energy.

## Word equation (Core)

```
glucose + oxygen → carbon dioxide + water
```

## Balanced equation (Extended only)

```
C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂ → 6CO₂ + 6H₂O
```

**Atom check:** C 6 = 6; H 12 = 12; O 6 + 12 = 18 = 12 + 6.

**Ratio:** 1 glucose : 6 oxygen : 6 carbon dioxide : 6 water.

## Ratio calculations (Extended only)

### Method in steps

1. Write the ratio from the equation.
2. Find how many "glucose units" the question gives you.
3. Multiply (forwards) or divide (backwards).
4. State the answer as a number of molecules.

| Given | Find | Working | Answer |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 glucose | CO₂ | 3 × 6 | 18 |
| 10 glucose | O₂ | 10 × 6 | 60 |
| 30 O₂ | glucose | 30 ÷ 6 | 5 |
| 48 H₂O | glucose | 48 ÷ 6 | 8 |
| 24 CO₂ | O₂ | 24 ÷ 6 × 6 | 24 |

Shortcut: oxygen used, carbon dioxide made and water made are always equal in number.

## Where substances come from and go (human)

| Substance | Route |
|---|---|
| Glucose | small intestine → plasma → cells |
| Oxygen | lungs → haemoglobin in red blood cells → cells |
| Carbon dioxide | cells → plasma → lungs → air |
| Water | joins body water; excess excreted |

## Respiration vs breathing vs gas exchange

| Process | What happens | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Breathing | air moved into and out of the lungs | lungs and chest |
| Gas exchange | oxygen diffuses into blood, carbon dioxide out | alveoli |
| Aerobic respiration | glucose broken down using oxygen to release energy | every living cell |

## Respiration vs photosynthesis

- Respiration: glucose + oxygen → carbon dioxide + water; releases energy; **all cells, all the time**.
- Photosynthesis: carbon dioxide + water → glucose + oxygen; needs light; cells with chloroplasts.
- Plants do both. Respiration does not stop in the light.

## Worked reminders

**Applying the equation to exercise.** More muscle contraction → more aerobic respiration → more oxygen used and more carbon dioxide made → faster, deeper breathing and faster heart rate to deliver oxygen and remove carbon dioxide. Use both sides of the equation in your answer.

**Applying the equation to a plant.** In bright light, photosynthesis uses carbon dioxide faster than respiration releases it, so the leaf takes in carbon dioxide overall. In darkness only respiration happens, so the leaf gives out carbon dioxide. Respiration has not changed; the balance has.

**Checking any balanced equation (Extended).**

1. Count C, H and O on the left.
2. Count C, H and O on the right, multiplying by the big number in front of each formula.
3. If any count differs, the equation is not balanced.

**Where marks go in "describe aerobic respiration".** Usually one mark for "chemical reactions in cells", one for "using oxygen", one for "break down nutrient molecules / glucose to release energy".

## Command words

- **Describe**: the full definition.
- **State**: the equation exactly, with arrows and plus signs.
- **Calculate** (Extended): ratio first, then working, then the number of molecules.
- **Explain**: link more or less respiration to more or less oxygen used and carbon dioxide released.

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Aerobic | Using oxygen |
| Nutrient molecule | Molecule from food, such as glucose, broken down in respiration |
| Reactant | Substance used up in a reaction (glucose, oxygen) |
| Product | Substance formed (carbon dioxide, water) |
| Balanced equation | Equation with equal numbers of each atom on both sides |
| Release (energy) | Energy already in glucose is made available; it is not created |

## Exam traps

- "Produces energy": write **releases** energy.
- "Respiration happens in the lungs": it happens in **cells**.
- Products and reactants swapped (that is photosynthesis).
- Energy written as a chemical product.
- (Extended) O instead of O₂; missing 6s; 6H₂O written as H₆O.
- (Extended) Multiplying when you should divide in a backwards calculation.
- "Plants respire only at night."

## Self-test

1. Write the definition of aerobic respiration.
2. Write the word equation.
3. Where in an organism does aerobic respiration take place?
4. Name the two products of aerobic respiration.
5. Which blood component carries oxygen to respiring cells?
6. A runner breathes faster. Give two reasons linked to aerobic respiration.
7. Do plants respire in the light? Explain.
8. (Extended) Write the balanced chemical equation.
9. (Extended) How many oxygen atoms are on each side of the balanced equation?
10. (Extended) How many carbon dioxide molecules are released from 9 glucose molecules?
11. (Extended) A cell releases 54 water molecules. How many glucose molecules were respired?
12. (Extended) Correct this equation: C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O → 6CO₂ + 6H₂O.

### Answers

1. The chemical reactions in cells that use oxygen to break down nutrient molecules to release energy.
2. Glucose + oxygen → carbon dioxide + water.
3. In (all living) cells.
4. Carbon dioxide and water.
5. Red blood cells (haemoglobin).
6. To take in more oxygen for more respiration; to remove the extra carbon dioxide produced.
7. Yes; respiration happens in all living cells all the time, alongside photosynthesis.
8. C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂ → 6CO₂ + 6H₂O.
9. 18.
10. 9 × 6 = 54.
11. 54 ÷ 6 = 9.
12. C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂ → 6CO₂ + 6H₂O (oxygen is O₂).
