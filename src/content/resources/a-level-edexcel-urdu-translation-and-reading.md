This guide covers **Paper 1**, the first of three papers in Pearson
Edexcel GCE A-Level Urdu (9UR0), for teaching from 2018. This is the
UK domestic GCE A-Level, not an International A-Level (IAL) -- Edexcel
does not offer Urdu as an IAL subject, unlike most other subjects in
the Marlbridge Edexcel matrix.

## Where this fits in 9UR0

Paper 1 (9UR0/01) combines three distinct skills in one paper:
translation from Urdu into English, reading comprehension in Urdu, and
an extended writing response to an independent research question. It
sits alongside Paper 2 (translation into Urdu and a written response
to works studied) and Paper 3 (listening, reading and writing).

## Syllabus coverage

**PEARSON EDEXCEL GCE A-LEVEL URDU (9UR0) — PAPER 1 TRANSLATION,
READING COMPREHENSION AND WRITING**

- Translation into English — translating a passage from Urdu into
accurate, fluent English
- Reading comprehension in Urdu — answering questions in Urdu on an
Urdu source text, testing detailed understanding
- Writing (research question) — an extended written response in Urdu
based on independent research into a topic relevant to Urdu-speaking
society or culture

## How to approach it

Because Paper 1 blends three distinct skills, revise them as separate
strands rather than assuming strength in one transfers to another --
translation rewards precision and register, reading comprehension
rewards close, detailed understanding of the source text, and the
research-based writing task rewards depth of independent knowledge
combined with structured argument in Urdu. The research question in
particular benefits from preparing in advance: build a well-researched
bank of knowledge on your chosen topic area well before the exam,
since this cannot be improvised on the day the way comprehension
questions can. Regular translation practice in both directions (even
though this paper only tests Urdu-to-English) builds the vocabulary
range and grammatical accuracy this paper rewards.
