## Section B

**6.** Compare a sperm cell with an egg cell in terms of size, numbers and motility. **[3]**

**8.** Explain how the egg cell is adapted to its function in terms of its energy stores and jelly coat. **[3]**

**9.** The diagram shows the lining of the uterus during a 28-day menstrual cycle. The thickness was 1 mm at day 5, 5 mm at day 14 and 9 mm at day 26.

**(a)** Name the hormone that causes the lining to thicken between days 5 and 14, and state which organ produces it. **[2]**

**(b)** Calculate the percentage increase in the thickness of the lining from day 5 to day 14. **[2]**

**(c)** State what happens to an egg at about day 14. **[1]**

**(d)** Name the hormone that keeps the lining thick after ovulation, and explain what happens to the lining if the egg is not fertilised. **[3]**

**10.** Describe the roles of FSH and LH in the menstrual cycle. **[4]**

**11.** Describe the early development of the zygote until it is implanted. **[3]**

**12.** Describe the exchange of substances at the placenta between the mother and the fetus. **[4]**

**13.** A pregnant woman is infected with a virus. Explain how this could affect the fetus. **[2]**

**14.** State **two** changes caused by testosterone at puberty and **two** caused by oestrogen. **[4]**

**15.** Explain why it is an advantage that the jelly coat of the egg changes after a sperm has entered. **[2]**

## Answers

**1.** B [1]. **[1]**

**2.** B [1]. **[1]**

**3.** The acrosome contains enzymes [1] that digest the jelly coat and membrane of the egg so the sperm nucleus can enter [1]. **[2]**

**4.** In the oviduct [1]. **[1]**

**5.** It protects the fetus from knocks (acts as a shock absorber) [1] and supports it [1]. **[2]**

**6.** Size: the sperm is very small; the egg is much larger [1]. Numbers: millions of sperm are made but usually one egg is released each month [1]. Motility: sperm can swim, whereas the egg cannot move itself [1]. **[3]**

**7.** The flagellum allows the sperm to swim to the egg [1]. The mitochondria release energy by respiration for the movement of the flagellum [1]. The acrosome contains enzymes that digest the egg's coat so that the sperm can enter [1]. **[3]**

**8.** The egg contains energy stores [1] that nourish the early embryo before it implants [1]. The jelly coat changes after a sperm has entered, which stops other sperm from entering [1]. **[3]**

**9.**

**(a)** Oestrogen [1], produced by the ovaries [1].

**(b)** (5 - 1) divided by 1 x 100 [1] = 400 per cent [1].

**(c)** The egg is released from the ovary (ovulation) [1].

**(d)** Progesterone [1]. If the egg is not fertilised, the level of progesterone falls [1] and the lining breaks down and is lost in menstruation [1]. **[8]**

**10.** FSH is released by the pituitary gland [1] and stimulates an egg to develop in a follicle in the ovary [1]. LH is released by the pituitary gland [1] and stimulates ovulation (release of the egg) [1]. **[4]**

**11.** The zygote divides by mitosis [1] to form a ball of cells (embryo) as it moves down the oviduct [1]. The embryo then becomes implanted in the lining of the uterus [1]. **[3]**

**12.** Oxygen and dissolved nutrients (such as glucose and amino acids) pass from the mother's blood to the fetus [1, 1]. Carbon dioxide and urea pass from the fetus's blood to the mother's blood [1, 1]. The bloods do not mix. **[4]**

**13.** Some viruses can pass across the placenta [1] and affect the development of the fetus [1]. **[2]**

**14.** Testosterone, any two of: deepening of the voice; facial and body hair; muscle growth [1, 1]. Oestrogen, any two of: breast development; widening of the hips; pubic hair; start of the menstrual cycle [1, 1]. **[4]**

**15.** The change stops other sperm from entering the egg [1], so that only one sperm nucleus fuses with the egg nucleus and the zygote has the correct diploid number of chromosomes [1]. **[2]**

## Where marks are usually lost

- Mixing up the roles of FSH and LH.
- Saying that the placenta lets the blood of mother and fetus mix.
- Naming the prostate gland as the place where sperm are made.
- Saying that the egg is released at the start of the cycle.
- Leaving out the function of the mitochondria in a sperm cell.
