## Section A

**1.** For the data 8, 3, 11, 5, 8, 14, 7, 4, find

**(a)** the median, **[1]**

**(b)** the mean, **[1]**

**(c)** the mode, **[1]**

**(d)** the range. **[1]**

**2.** The mean of six numbers is 12. Five of the numbers are 10, 15, 9, 14 and 11. Work out the sixth number. **[2]**

**3.** 12 boys took a mean time of 15 minutes to finish a task. 18 girls took a mean time of 20 minutes. Work out the mean time for all 30 students. **[3]**

**4.** The weekly pocket money, in dollars, of seven friends is 5, 6, 6, 7, 8, 8 and 50. Explain which average, the mean or the median, better represents this data. **[2]**

## Section B

**5.** The table shows the number of pets owned by 30 families.

| Number of pets | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Frequency | 7 | 10 | 9 | 3 | 1 |

**(a)** Write down the mode. **[1]**

**(b)** Find the median. **[1]**

**(c)** Calculate the mean. **[2]**

**6.** Five positive whole numbers have a mode of 4, a median of 5 and a mean of 6. Find a possible set of five numbers. **[3]**

**7.** The table shows the heights, h cm, of 40 students.

| Height h (cm) | 140 < h ≤ 150 | 150 < h ≤ 160 | 160 < h ≤ 170 | 170 < h ≤ 180 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Frequency | 5 | 12 | 15 | 8 |

**(a)** Write down the modal class. **[1]**

**(b)** Calculate an estimate for the mean height. **[3]**

**(c)** Explain why your answer to (b) is an estimate. **[1]**

**8.** In a game, the scores 1, 2, 3 and 4 have frequencies 4, k, 6 and 2. The mean score is 2.4. Find the value of k. **[3]**

**9.** Here are 19 values, in order:

11, 13, 14, 16, 17, 18, 20, 21, 23, 24, 25, 27, 28, 30, 31, 33, 35, 38, 42

**(a)** Find the median. **[1]**

**(b)** **(Higher tier only)** Find the interquartile range. **[2]**

**10.** **(Higher tier only)** The cumulative frequency table gives the times, t seconds, of 80 runners.

| Time t (s) | ≤ 20 | ≤ 40 | ≤ 60 | ≤ 80 | ≤ 100 | ≤ 120 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cumulative frequency | 5 | 17 | 40 | 64 | 76 | 80 |

The points are joined with straight lines.

**(a)** Estimate the median. **[1]**

**(b)** Estimate the interquartile range. **[3]**

**11.** **(Higher tier only)** In a reaction-time test, group A had a median of 24 s and an interquartile range of 14 s. Group B had a median of 29 s and an interquartile range of 8 s. Make two comparisons between the groups. **[2]**

**12.** **(Higher tier only)** Explain why the interquartile range can be a better measure of spread than the range. **[1]**

## Answers

**1. (a)** Ordered: 3, 4, 5, 7, 8, 8, 11, 14; median = (7 + 8) ÷ 2 = **7.5** [1].

**(b)** 60 ÷ 8 = **7.5** [1].

**(c)** **8** [1].

**(d)** 14 − 3 = **11** [1].

**2.** Total = 6 × 12 = 72 and the five numbers total 59 [1]; sixth number = **13** [1].

**3.** Boys' total = 12 × 15 = 180 and girls' total = 18 × 20 = 360 [1]; combined total = 540 [1]; mean = 540 ÷ 30 = **18 minutes** [1].

**4.** The median ($7) is better [1], because the extreme value of $50 makes the mean ($90 ÷ 7 ≈ $12.86) larger than six of the seven values [1].

**5. (a)** **1** [1].

**(b)** The 15th and 16th values: running totals 7, 17, so both are 1; median = **1** [1].

**(c)** Σfx = 0 + 10 + 18 + 9 + 4 = 41 [1]; 41 ÷ 30 = **1.37** (3 s.f.) [1].

**6.** Total = 5 × 6 = 30 [1]. In order, the numbers are 4, 4, 5, a, b with a + b = 17 [1], where a and b are different and both greater than 5, for example **4, 4, 5, 6, 11** (or 4, 4, 5, 7, 10 or 4, 4, 5, 8, 9) [1].

**7. (a)** **160 < h ≤ 170** [1].

**(b)** Midpoints 145, 155, 165, 175; fx = 725, 1860, 2475, 1400 [1]; Σfx = 6460 [1]; 6460 ÷ 40 = **161.5 cm** [1].

**(c)** The midpoints are used in place of the actual heights, which are not known [1].

**8.** Σf = 12 + k and Σfx = 30 + 2k [1]; 30 + 2k = 2.4(12 + k) [1]; 1.2 = 0.4k, so **k = 3** [1].

**9. (a)** (19 + 1) ÷ 2 = 10th value = **24** [1].

**(b)** Lower quartile = 5th value = 17 and upper quartile = 15th value = 31 [1]; IQR = **14** [1].

**10. (a)** Median at 40: the cumulative frequency at 60 s is exactly 40, so median ≈ **60 s** [1].

**(b)** Lower quartile at 20: 40 + 20 × 3/23 ≈ 42.6 s [1]. Upper quartile at 60: 60 + 20 × 20/24 ≈ 76.7 s [1]. IQR ≈ **34 s** (accept 32 to 36 from a curve) [1].

**11.** Group B had a higher median, so on average group B were slower [1]. Group A had a larger IQR, so group A's times were more varied (less consistent) [1].

**12.** The IQR uses the middle half of the data, so it is not affected by extreme values, whereas the range depends only on the largest and smallest values [1].

## Where marks are usually lost

- Question 1(a): not ordering the data first.
- Question 3: averaging 15 and 20 to get 17.5, which ignores the different group sizes.
- Question 5(b): giving the middle frequency instead of the middle value.
- Question 6: choosing a = b, which creates a second mode.
- Question 7(b): dividing by 4 (the number of classes) instead of 40.
- Question 11: comparing without context, such as "B is bigger".

## Frequently asked questions

**How do you find the median from a frequency table?**
Find the position of the middle value, ((n + 1)/2), then use running totals of the frequencies to see which value is in that position.

**How do you compare two distributions?**
Compare an average (such as the median) and a measure of spread (such as the IQR), and say what each comparison means in the context of the question.
