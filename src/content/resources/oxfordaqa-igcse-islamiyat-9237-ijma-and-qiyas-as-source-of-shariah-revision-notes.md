**These notes condense content area 3.2.2.6 of 9237: "The meaning, role and importance of Ijma (consensus) and Qiyas (analogy) in forming religious rulings."** The full explanation is in the [study guide](/resources/oxfordaqa-igcse-islamiyat-9237-ijma-and-qiyas-as-source-of-shariah/).

## Order of the sources

1. Qur'an
2. Sunnah
3. Ijma
4. Qiyas

Ijma and Qiyas are tested by the first two and must never contradict them. The Hadith of Mu'adh ibn Jabal (RA) (Abu Dawud) supports the order: the Book, the Sunnah, then striving to form an opinion.

## Ijma in brief

| Point | Notes |
|---|---|
| Meaning | Agreement of qualified scholars (mujtahidun) of a period on a religious ruling, after the Prophet (pbuh) |
| Who counts | Trained scholars, not ordinary people |
| Scope | Matters not settled by a clear text |
| Types | Explicit (all state agreement); silent (some state, others do not object) |
| Strongest form | Agreement of the companions |
| Schools | Maliki stress the agreement of the people of Madinah; Ja'fari accept it where it includes or reveals the Imam's view |

**Examples:** choosing Abu Bakr (RA) as first caliph; collecting the Qur'an into one book; praying Taraweeh behind one imam under Umar (RA); the grandmother's one-sixth share.

**Evidence:** Qur'an 4:115 ("the way of the believers"); Qur'an 4:59 ("those in authority among you"); the Hadith that the community will not agree on error.

## Qiyas in brief

| Part | Term | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Original case | Asl | Has a ruling in the Qur'an or Sunnah |
| New case | Far' | No ruling in the text |
| Ruling | Hukm | The ruling of the original case |
| Cause | 'Illa | The reason the two cases share |

**Steps:** find the original case; identify its reason; check that the new case shares it; give the new case the same ruling.

**Worked examples**

| Original case | New case | Shared reason | Ruling |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wine (Qur'an 5:90) | A modern drug | It clouds the mind and harms the person | Forbidden |
| Trade at the Friday call (62:9) | Other deals that distract from prayer | Distraction from prayer | Avoided at that time |
| "Uff" to parents (17:23) | Shouting at or hitting parents | Hurting parents | Forbidden even more strongly |

**Conditions:** no clear text on the new case; a ruling in the text for the original; a clear and truly shared reason; no conflict with a text; done by a qualified scholar.

## Schools and Qiyas

| School | Attitude |
|---|---|
| Hanafi | Wide use of reasoning, qiyas and istihsan |
| Maliki | Uses it with Madinah's practice and public interest |
| Shafi'i | Accepts it under strict rules |
| Hanbali | Uses it only where necessary |
| Ja'fari | Uses reason ('aql) instead of Sunni qiyas |

## Why they matter

- **Ijma:** certainty on questions agreed by scholars; unity of the community; protection from isolated error.
- **Qiyas:** answers new questions; keeps reasoning linked to a text; prevents arbitrary personal opinion.
- **Together:** they allow Shariah to stay relevant without new revelation.

## Ijma and Qiyas compared

| | Ijma | Qiyas |
|---|---|---|
| Method | Agreement | Analogy |
| Done by | Scholars together | A scholar applying a reason |
| Basis | The way of the believers | A text and a shared cause |
| Example | The Qur'an collected in one book | Modern drugs and wine |

## The prescribed passage: Qur'an 4:115

| Phrase | Point |
|---|---|
| "opposes the Messenger after guidance has become clear" | Do not oppose clear guidance; Ijma and Qiyas cannot be used against the Sunnah |
| "follows other than the way of the believers" | Used by many scholars as evidence for Ijma |
| "We will give him what he has taken" | Personal responsibility |
| "evil it is as a destination" | A strong warning |

**Wording to use:** "many scholars use this verse as evidence for Ijma".

## Model answer: a 4-mark question

**Question:** Explain **two** differences between Ijma and Qiyas. **[4]**

> Ijma is the agreement of qualified scholars on a ruling [1], while Qiyas is reasoning from a case in the text to a new case with the same cause [1]. Ijma is a community decision, such as the agreement to collect the Qur'an in one book [1], whereas Qiyas is applied case by case, such as treating modern drugs like wine [1].

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Ijma | Consensus of qualified scholars |
| Qiyas | Analogy |
| Mujtahid | A scholar qualified to give independent rulings |
| Asl | The original case in a Qiyas |
| Far' | The new case in a Qiyas |
| 'Illa | The effective cause shared by both cases |
| Hukm | A ruling |
| Istihsan | Preferring a fairer ruling |
| 'Aql | Reason |

## Exam traps

- Do not mix up the two: agreement versus analogy.
- Say qualified scholars for Ijma, not "everyone".
- Name the shared reason ('illa) in an example of Qiyas.
- Do not put Ijma or Qiyas above the Qur'an or Sunnah.
- Do not say 4:115 states the rule; many scholars derive it from the verse.

## Self-test

1. What does Ijma mean?
2. What does Qiyas mean?
3. What is the correct order of the four sources?
4. What is the 'illa in Qiyas?
5. Give one example of Ijma.
6. Give one example of Qiyas.
7. Which school gives special weight to the agreement of the people of Madinah?
8. Which phrase in 4:115 is used as evidence for Ijma?
9. Why can Qiyas not be used where there is a clear text?
10. Which school uses reason ('aql) rather than Sunni qiyas?

**Answers:** 1. Agreement of qualified scholars on a religious ruling. 2. Reasoning by analogy from a case in the text to a new case. 3. Qur'an, Sunnah, Ijma, Qiyas. 4. The shared reason or cause that links the two cases. 5. Any one: the choice of Abu Bakr (RA); the collection of the Qur'an; the grandmother's one-sixth share. 6. Any one: modern drugs are forbidden like wine; shouting at parents is forbidden like saying "uff". 7. The Maliki school. 8. "Follows other than the way of the believers." 9. The text decides the matter and a ruling cannot contradict it. 10. The Ja'fari school.
