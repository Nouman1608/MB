Subtopic 12.1 of Cambridge International AS & A Level Physics 9702 (2025–2027) asks you to define the radian, express angular displacement in radians, use angular speed, and recall and use ω = 2π/T and v = rω. These notes are condensed for recall. The [Kinematics of uniform circular motion study guide](/resources/cambridge-a-level-physics-9702-a-kinematics-of-uniform-circular-motion/) has full worked examples, and the [Kinematics of uniform circular motion practice questions](/resources/cambridge-a-level-physics-9702-a-kinematics-of-uniform-circular-motion-practice/) let you test yourself. Centripetal acceleration (12.2) is in the [Motion in a Circle revision notes](/resources/a-physics-circular-motion-revision-notes/).

## The radian

> One radian is the angle subtended at the centre of a circle by an arc equal in length to the radius.

```
θ = s / r          s = rθ   (θ in radians)
2π rad = 360°      π rad = 180°      1 rad ≈ 57.3°
```

| Degrees | Radians (exact) | Radians (decimal) |
|---|---|---|
| 30° | π/6 | 0.524 |
| 45° | π/4 | 0.785 |
| 60° | π/3 | 1.05 |
| 90° | π/2 | 1.57 |
| 120° | 2π/3 | 2.09 |
| 180° | π | 3.14 |
| 360° | 2π | 6.28 |

## Angular displacement

- Angle turned through by the radius to the object, measured at the centre, in radians.
- Can exceed 2π: n revolutions = 2πn rad.

## Angular speed

```
ω = Δθ / Δt        unit rad s⁻¹
ω = 2π / T = 2πf
```

- **Definition:** rate of change of angular displacement.
- **Uniform circular motion:** constant speed, so constant ω.
- **rpm → rad s⁻¹:** divide by 60, multiply by 2π. Example: 3000 rpm = 50 rev s⁻¹ = 314 rad s⁻¹.
- **Period from ω:** T = 2π/ω.

## Linear speed

```
v = rω        v = 2πr / T
```

- Derivation: s = rθ, divide by t.
- Direction: along the **tangent** at each point.
- Speed constant, **velocity changing** (direction changes) — leads to centripetal acceleration in 12.2.

## Same ω or same v?

| Situation | Shared quantity | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Points on one rigid rotating object | ω | v ∝ r |
| Two pulleys joined by a belt (no slip) | v at the rims | r₁ω₁ = r₂ω₂; smaller pulley spins faster |
| Two meshing gears | v at the teeth | r₁ω₁ = r₂ω₂ |
| Wheel rolling without slipping | v of vehicle = rim speed relative to axle | v = rω |

## Condensed worked example

A drill bit of radius 4.0 mm turns at 1200 rpm.

```
f = 1200 / 60 = 20 rev s⁻¹
ω = 2πf = 126 rad s⁻¹
T = 1 / f = 0.050 s
v = rω = 0.0040 × 126 = 0.50 m s⁻¹
```

Order of working: rpm → rev s⁻¹ → rad s⁻¹ → v. Convert millimetres to metres before multiplying.

## Reference values

- Earth's rotation (period taken as 24 h): ω = 7.27 × 10⁻⁵ rad s⁻¹.
- Second hand of a clock: ω = 2π/60 = 0.105 rad s⁻¹.
- Minute hand: ω = 2π/3600 = 1.75 × 10⁻³ rad s⁻¹.
- Hour hand: ω = 2π/43 200 = 1.45 × 10⁻⁴ rad s⁻¹.

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Radian | Angle subtended by an arc equal in length to the radius |
| Angular displacement | Angle turned through, measured at the centre |
| Angular speed ω | Rate of change of angular displacement |
| Period T | Time for one complete revolution |
| Frequency f | Revolutions per second, f = 1/T |
| Linear (tangential) speed v | Speed along the circular path, v = rω |
| Uniform circular motion | Motion in a circle at constant speed |

## Exam traps

- Calculating with degrees in s = rθ or ω = θ/t.
- Defining the radian as "57.3 degrees" or "the angle of a sector".
- Not converting minutes, hours or days to seconds.
- Using f where ω is needed (ω = 2πf, not f).
- Equal ω for belt-linked pulleys (it is equal rim speed).
- Different ω for points on the same disc.
- "Constant velocity" in uniform circular motion.

## Self-test

1. Define the radian.
2. Convert 120° to radians.
3. Convert 1.2 rad to degrees.
4. Define angular speed and give its unit.
5. A wheel makes 5.0 revolutions. What is its angular displacement?
6. A motor turns at 3000 rpm. Find ω.
7. A point on a disc is 0.40 m from the axis; ω = 2.5 rad s⁻¹. Find v.
8. A point 0.25 m from the axis moves at 2π m s⁻¹. Find its period of rotation.
9. Two points on a rotating disc are 0.10 m and 0.30 m from the axis. Compare their ω and v.
10. A belt joins a 3.0 cm pulley to a 9.0 cm pulley. The small one turns at 60 rad s⁻¹. Find ω for the large one.
11. Show that v = 2πr/T is consistent with v = rω.
12. The Moon orbits Earth in 27.3 days at a radius of 3.84 × 10⁸ m. Find its orbital speed.

**Answers:** 1. The angle subtended at the centre of a circle by an arc of length equal to the radius. 2. 2π/3 = 2.09 rad. 3. 1.2 × 180/π = 68.8°. 4. Rate of change of angular displacement; rad s⁻¹. 5. 5.0 × 2π = 31.4 rad. 6. (3000/60) × 2π = 314 rad s⁻¹. 7. v = 0.40 × 2.5 = 1.0 m s⁻¹. 8. ω = v/r = 8π rad s⁻¹, so T = 2π/ω = 0.25 s. 9. Same ω; the outer point has three times the linear speed. 10. Rim speed = 0.030 × 60 = 1.8 m s⁻¹; ω = 1.8 ÷ 0.090 = 20 rad s⁻¹. 11. ω = 2π/T, so rω = 2πr/T. 12. ω = 2π ÷ (27.3 × 86 400) = 2.66 × 10⁻⁶ rad s⁻¹; v = rω = 1.02 × 10³ m s⁻¹.
