## Section A

**1.** Five seedlings have heights of 12, 15, 14, 16 and 13 mm. Calculate the mean, the range and the standard deviation. The standard deviation is s = √[Σ(x − x̄)² ÷ (n − 1)]. Give s to 2 significant figures. **[4]**

**2.** The mass of a potato cylinder falls from 4.20 g to 3.78 g in a salt solution. Calculate the percentage change in mass. **[2]**

**3.** A student divides a mass of 6.84 g by a volume of 2.1 cm³ to find a density. Give the answer to an appropriate number of significant figures and explain your choice. **[2]**

**4.** Name the statistical test that should be used to analyse each investigation.

**(a)** The numbers of offspring with each of four phenotypes are compared with the numbers expected from a 9 : 3 : 3 : 1 ratio. **[1]**

**(b)** The mean heights of plants in two fields are compared. **[1]**

**(c)** Six pairs of measurements of soil moisture and the number of earthworms are tested for a link between them. **[1]**

**5.** A dihybrid cross is expected to give a 9 : 3 : 3 : 1 ratio. Of 152 offspring, 88, 28, 26 and 10 show the four phenotypes. The expected numbers are 85.5, 28.5, 28.5 and 9.5.

**(a)** Calculate χ² using the formula χ² = Σ (O − E)² ÷ E. **[2]**

**(b)** The critical value at p = 0.05 for 3 degrees of freedom is 7.81. State the conclusion. **[2]**

## Section B

**6.** A student investigates how the concentration of a coloured solution affects its absorbance in a colorimeter. State which variable goes on each axis of a graph of the results, and give **two** features of the axes that make the graph acceptable. **[3]**

**7.** On a graph of the volume of gas against time, the line of best fit passes through the points (20 s, 6.0 cm³) and (60 s, 18.0 cm³). Calculate the gradient, and give its unit and its meaning. **[3]**

**8.** A curve shows the volume of product formed against time for an enzyme reaction. A tangent drawn at time zero passes through (0 s, 0 cm³) and (40 s, 10 cm³). Calculate the initial rate of reaction and explain why a tangent is used. **[3]**

**9.** Six sites are sampled. The distance from the sea, in metres, and the number of plant species found are:

| Distance / m | 3 | 5 | 8 | 10 | 12 | 15 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Species | 6 | 9 | 10 | 13 | 15 | 14 |

**(a)** Rank both variables from lowest to highest. **[1]**

**(b)** Calculate the difference in ranks, d, for each site and Σd². **[1]**

**(c)** Calculate Spearman's rank correlation coefficient using rₛ = 1 − 6Σd² ÷ n(n² − 1). **[1]**

**(d)** The critical value for n = 6 at p = 0.05 is 0.886. State the conclusion. **[2]**

**10.** A t-test compares the mean mass of six plants grown with fertiliser and six without. The calculated value of t is 2.9. The critical value at p = 0.05 for 10 degrees of freedom is 2.23. Explain what this shows. **[3]**

**11.** A student measures the mean rate of an enzyme-catalysed reaction at four temperatures: 4.2 cm³ min⁻¹ at 20 °C, 8.2 at 30 °C, 16.2 at 40 °C and 10.7 at 50 °C. Write a conclusion that is supported by these data. **[4]**

**12.** A scatter graph shows a significant positive correlation between the nitrate concentration in a stream and the mass of algae. A student concludes that nitrate causes the algae to grow. Evaluate this conclusion. **[2]**

**13.** Two groups of students have mean reaction times of 15.0 s and 15.2 s. The standard deviation of the first group is 0.4 s and of the second group is 3.1 s. Explain what the standard deviations show about the two sets of data. **[2]**

## Answers

**How these answers work.** Marking points are shown in brackets. The mark total for each question is the sum of the points. Alternative wording that carries the same meaning is credited.

**1.** Mean = (12 + 15 + 14 + 16 + 13) ÷ 5 = **14.0 mm** [1]. Range = 16 − 12 = **4 mm** [1]. Deviations −2, 1, 0, 2, −1, squares 4, 1, 0, 4, 1, sum 10 [1]. s = √(10 ÷ 4) = √2.5 = **1.6 mm** [1]. **[4]**

**2.** Change = 3.78 − 4.20 = −0.42 g [1]. Percentage change = −0.42 ÷ 4.20 × 100 = **−10 %** (a decrease of 10.0 %) [1]. **[2]**

**3.** 6.84 ÷ 2.1 = 3.257... so **3.3 g cm⁻³** [1]. The volume has only two significant figures, so the answer is given to two significant figures [1]. **[2]**

**4. (a)** Chi-squared test [1]. **(b)** Student's t-test [1]. **(c)** Spearman's rank correlation coefficient [1]. **[3]**

**5. (a)** (88 − 85.5)² ÷ 85.5 = 0.073; (28 − 28.5)² ÷ 28.5 = 0.009; (26 − 28.5)² ÷ 28.5 = 0.219; (10 − 9.5)² ÷ 9.5 = 0.026 [1]. χ² = **0.33** [1].

**(b)** 0.33 is less than 7.81 [1], so the difference between the observed and expected numbers is not significant and could be due to chance; the results fit a 9 : 3 : 3 : 1 ratio [1]. **[4]**

**6.** Concentration (the independent variable) on the x-axis [1] and absorbance on the y-axis [1]. Any one feature [1]: each axis labelled with the quantity and its unit (concentration / mol dm⁻³; absorbance has no unit); an even linear scale that uses more than half of the grid. **[3]**

**7.** Gradient = (18.0 − 6.0) ÷ (60 − 20) [1] = 12.0 ÷ 40 = **0.30** [1]. Unit **cm³ s⁻¹**, and it is the rate of gas production [1]. **[3]**

**8.** Initial rate = 10 ÷ 40 = **0.25 cm³ s⁻¹** [1]. The reaction slows as the substrate is used up, so the graph is a curve and the rate changes [1]. The gradient of a tangent at t = 0 gives the rate at that instant, before the substrate falls and products build up [1]. **[3]**

**9. (a)** Distance ranks 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6; species ranks 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 5 [1].

**(b)** d = 0, 0, 0, 0, −1, 1; d² = 0, 0, 0, 0, 1, 1; Σd² = 2 [1].

**(c)** rₛ = 1 − (6 × 2) ÷ (6 × 35) = 1 − 12 ÷ 210 = **0.94** [1].

**(d)** 0.94 is greater than the critical value of 0.886 [1], so there is a significant positive correlation: the number of plant species tends to rise with distance from the sea. The null hypothesis is rejected at p = 0.05. Correlation alone does not show the cause [1]. **[5]**

**10.** The calculated t of 2.9 is greater than the critical value of 2.23 [1]. The difference between the means is significant at p = 0.05, so the null hypothesis is rejected [1]. There is less than a 5 % probability that the difference arose by chance [1]. **[3]**

**11.** Any four [1 each]: from 20 to 40 °C the mean rate increased, from 4.2 to 16.2 cm³ min⁻¹ [1]; the rate roughly doubled for each 10 °C rise between 20 and 40 °C (4.2, 8.2, 16.2) [1]; at 50 °C the rate fell to 10.7 cm³ min⁻¹ [1]; the optimum lies close to 40 °C, with the data supporting this although other temperatures between 30 and 50 °C were not tested [1]; the fall is explained by denaturation of the enzyme at the higher temperature [1]. **[4]**

**12.** A correlation shows that the two variables change together but does not show cause [1]. Another factor, such as temperature, light or phosphate, could affect both, or algae could affect nitrate; further experiments in which nitrate alone is changed would be needed [1]. **[2]**

**13.** The means are similar [1], but the second group has a much larger standard deviation, so its reaction times are more widely spread about the mean, whereas the first group's values are close together [1]. **[2]**

## Where marks are usually lost

- Question 1: dividing by n instead of n − 1 in the standard deviation.
- Question 3: giving every digit on the calculator display.
- Question 5(b): saying that the result "proves" the ratio, instead of saying that the difference is not significant.
- Question 7: omitting the unit of the gradient, or using two data points that are not on the line.
- Question 8: not explaining why a tangent, rather than a straight line between two points, is needed.
- Question 9: ranking the values in opposite directions for the two variables.
- Question 10: stating only that t is larger than the critical value without saying what that means.
- Question 12: saying that correlation proves cause.

## Frequently asked questions

**Do I have to remember critical values?**
No. Critical-value tables, or extracts from them, are provided in the assessment. You must work out which row and column to use.

**What does p = 0.05 mean?**
It means that if the null hypothesis were true there would be a 5 % probability of a result at least this extreme by chance alone.
