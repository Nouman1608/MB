## Constructions (G2)

| Construction | Key steps |
|---|---|
| Perpendicular bisector of AB | Arcs from A and B (same radius, more than half AB), join the crossings |
| Angle bisector | Arc cutting both arms; arcs from those points cross; join to the vertex |
| Perpendicular from a point to a line | Arc from the point cutting the line twice; bisect between the cutting points |
| Perpendicular at a point on a line | Equal marks either side; bisect between them |
| 60° angle | Equilateral triangle by two arcs of equal radius |

Keep all construction arcs.

| Locus | Shape |
|---|---|
| Fixed distance from a point | Circle |
| Equidistant from two points | Perpendicular bisector |
| Equidistant from two lines | Angle bisector |
| Fixed distance from a line | Parallel lines with rounded ends |

The shortest distance from a point to a line is **perpendicular**.

## Angle facts (G3)

| Fact | Value |
|---|---|
| Angles at a point | 360° |
| Angles on a straight line | 180° |
| Triangle | 180° |
| Quadrilateral | 360° |
| Interior angles of n-gon | (n − 2) × 180° |
| Exterior angles of any polygon | 360° |
| Regular polygon: exterior angle | 360° ÷ n |
| Regular polygon: interior angle | 180° − exterior |

Parallel lines: alternate equal, corresponding equal, co-interior add to 180°.

## Quadrilaterals (G4)

| Shape | Notes |
|---|---|
| Square | All sides and angles equal; diagonals equal, bisect at 90° |
| Rectangle | Opposite sides equal; diagonals equal |
| Parallelogram | Opposite sides parallel; diagonals bisect each other |
| Rhombus | Four equal sides; diagonals bisect at 90° |
| Kite | Two pairs of adjacent equal sides; diagonals cross at 90° |
| Trapezium | One pair of parallel sides |

## Congruence and proof (G5, G6)

SSS, SAS (included angle), ASA, RHS. Not SSA, not AAA. Proofs: fact, reason, result.

## Transformations (G7, G8)

- Enlargement by scale factor k: lengths × k, from a centre. A scale factor between 0 and 1 reduces the shape.
- Describe fully: **rotation** (angle, direction, centre), **reflection** (line), **translation** (vector), **enlargement** (scale factor, centre).
- **Higher tier only:** negative scale factor puts the image on the opposite side of the centre, inverted. Two reflections in perpendicular axes give a 180° rotation about the intersection.

## Circles (G9, G10)

Terms: radius, diameter, circumference, chord, tangent, arc, sector, segment.

**Higher tier only: theorems**

1. Angle at centre = 2 × angle at circumference.
2. Angle in a semicircle = 90°.
3. Angles in the same segment are equal.
4. Opposite angles in a cyclic quadrilateral add to 180°.
5. Tangent ⟂ radius at the point of contact.
6. Tangents from an outside point are equal.
7. Perpendicular from the centre bisects a chord.
8. Alternate segment theorem.

Always name the theorem in your reasons.

## Coordinates and 3D (G11 to G13)

- Fourth vertex of parallelogram ABCD: D = A + C − B.
- Diagonal length from coordinates: Pythagoras on the differences.
- Euler: faces + vertices − edges = 2.
- Plan = from above; elevations = from front and side.

## Common errors

- "Z" and "F" angles instead of correct terms.
- Using the wrong number of sides in (n − 2) × 180°.
- Rubbing out arcs.
- Using SSA as proof.
- Missing details in a transformation description.
- Circle theorems quoted with no named reason.

## Quick self-test

1. What is the sum of the interior angles of a decagon?
2. A regular polygon has an exterior angle of 24°. How many sides?
3. Name the locus of points equidistant from two fixed points.
4. Which congruence condition applies if two sides and the angle between them are equal?
5. A triangle (2, 2), (6, 2), (6, 4) is enlarged by scale factor 3/2 from the origin. Give the image of (6, 4).
6. (Higher tier only) The triangle (1, 1), (3, 1), (3, 2) is enlarged by scale factor −3, centre the origin. Give the image of (3, 2).
7. (Higher tier only) A and B are on a circle centre O, and angle AOB = 110°. C is on the major arc. Find angle ACB.
8. (Higher tier only) State the single transformation equal to a reflection in the y-axis followed by a reflection in the x-axis.
9. A, B, C are (0, 0), (4, 1), (5, 4). Find D to make ABCD a parallelogram.
10. A prism has 8 faces and 12 vertices. Use Euler's formula to find the number of edges.

### Answers

1. (10 − 2) × 180° = **1440°**.
2. 360 ÷ 24 = **15** sides.
3. The perpendicular bisector of the line joining them.
4. SAS.
5. (6 × 3/2, 4 × 3/2) = **(9, 6)**.
6. (3 × −3, 2 × −3) = **(−9, −6)**.
7. Angle at the circumference = 110° ÷ 2 = **55°** (angle at the centre is twice the angle at the circumference).
8. Rotation of 180° about the origin.
9. D = A + C − B = (0 + 5 − 4, 0 + 4 − 1) = **(1, 3)**.
10. F + V − E = 2, so 8 + 12 − E = 2 and E = **18**.
