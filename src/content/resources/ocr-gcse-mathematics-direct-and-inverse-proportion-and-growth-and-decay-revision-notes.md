## Direct proportion (5.02a)

**Spot it:** both quantities rise together, and zero of one means zero of the other. Doubling x doubles y.

**Method in steps**
1. Write y = kx.
2. Put in the known pair, then find k.
3. Write the equation with k as a number.
4. Substitute the new value.

**Reminder.** y is proportional to x. When x = 5, y = 12. Find y when x = 8.

```
12 = 5k, so k = 2.4
y = 2.4 × 8 = 19.2
```

**Currency.** Foreign currency from pounds: multiply by the rate. Pounds from foreign currency: divide by the rate. Check that the answer is sensible for the currency with the larger numbers.

**Unitary method.** Find the value of one unit first, then multiply. It is the same idea as finding k.

### Higher tier only: powers and roots

Use y = kxⁿ with the same four steps. Apply the power or root to x before using the value.

| Statement | Equation | If x doubles |
|---|---|---|
| y ∝ x² | y = kx² | y × 4 |
| y ∝ x³ | y = kx³ | y × 8 |
| y ∝ √x | y = k√x | y × √2 |

**Reminder (Higher tier only).** y is proportional to x². When x = 4, y = 48. Find x when y = 75.

```
48 = 16k, so k = 3
75 = 3x², so x² = 25 and x = 5
```

(Take the positive root when x is a length or a time.)

## Inverse proportion (5.02b)

**Spot it:** one quantity rises as the other falls, with a fixed total job, distance or amount to share. Doubling x halves y.

**Method in steps**
1. Write y = k/x, or use xy = k.
2. Put in the known pair and find k by multiplying.
3. Write y = k/x with k as a number.
4. Substitute the new value.

**Speed and time.** Distance is fixed, so speed × time is constant. Doubling the speed halves the time.

**Workers.** 8 painters take 6 days. The job is 8 × 6 = 48 painter-days. With 12 painters it takes 48 ÷ 12 = 4 days. This assumes everyone works at the same rate.

**Reminder.** y is inversely proportional to x. When x = 5, y = 12. Find y when x = 8.

```
k = 5 × 12 = 60
y = 60/8 = 7.5
```

Compare this with the direct reminder: the same pair of values gives 19.2 (direct) and 7.5 (inverse). When x goes up, y goes up in one case and down in the other.

### Higher tier only: powers and roots

| Statement | Equation | If x doubles |
|---|---|---|
| y ∝ 1/x² | y = k/x² | y ÷ 4 |
| y ∝ 1/√x | y = k/√x | y ÷ √2 |

**Reminder (Higher tier only).** y is inversely proportional to √x. When x = 9, y = 4. Find x when y = 3.

```
4 = k/3, so k = 12
3 = 12/√x, so √x = 4 and x = 16
```

## Direct or inverse: decide before you calculate

| Question to ask | Direct | Inverse |
|---|---|---|
| What happens to y when x increases? | y increases | y decreases |
| What stays constant? | y ÷ x | x × y |
| Straight line through the origin on a graph? | Yes (for y = kx) | No, it is a curve |
| Typical context | Cost and quantity, currency | Speed and time, workers and days |

## Growth and decay (5.03a)

### Simple interest

```
Interest = P × r × n / 100
```

The same amount is added every year. Total = P + interest. To find the rate or time, rearrange the same formula.

**Reminder.** £1,800 at 4% simple for 3 years: 1800 × 4 × 3 / 100 = £216.

### Compound interest and depreciation

Interest is added to the balance, so the base grows each year. Depreciation is a repeated percentage fall, so the base shrinks each year.

**Method in steps**
1. Convert each percentage to a multiplier.
2. Multiply the start value by one multiplier for each period.
3. Round at the end only.
4. Give the answer with units, and as money to the nearest penny.

**Reminder.** £6,000 at 3% compound for 2 years: 6000 × 1.03² = £6,365.40.

**Different rates each year.** Multiply different multipliers. For falls of 10% then 20%, the multiplier is 0.9 × 0.8 = 0.72. That is a 28% fall overall, not 30%.

**Reversing a change.** Divide by the multiplier. If a price after a 20% rise is £360, the original is 360 ÷ 1.2 = £300.

### Higher tier only: exponential formulas

```
A = A₀ × rⁿ
```

| Part | Meaning |
|---|---|
| A₀ | Starting amount |
| r | Multiplier per period. Above 1 is growth, between 0 and 1 is decay |
| n | Number of periods |

**Reading a formula.** For V = 900 × 0.9ⁿ the start is 900 and the value falls by 10% each period. After 3 periods V = 900 × 0.9³ = 656.1.

**Solving "how long until" questions.** Use trial. Calculate A for n = 1, 2, 3 and so on until the target is passed. State the whole number of periods that answers the question, and say what that means in context.

## Must-know distinctions

- **Simple and compound.** Simple adds the same amount each year. Compound multiplies, so the amounts get bigger each year.
- **Percentage and multiplier.** A 12% fall is a multiplier of 0.88, not 0.12.
- **Proportional and equal.** y ∝ x does not mean y = x. It means y = kx for a constant k.
- **Power on x only.** In y = kx², the square applies to x and not to k.
- **Interest rate and growth rate.** Both can be read from r. For 1.035 the rate is 3.5%.

## Quick self-test

Do these without notes. Questions 8 to 10 are Higher tier only.

1. 7 identical pens cost £9.45. Find the cost of 12 pens.
2. y is proportional to x. When x = 4.5, y = 18. Find y when x = 7.
3. y is inversely proportional to x. When x = 12, y = 5. Find x when y = 8.
4. 8 painters take 6 days. How long do 12 painters take?
5. Find the simple interest on £1,800 at 4% per year for 3 years.
6. Write the multiplier for (a) a 7% decrease and (b) a 2.5% increase.
7. Find the value of £6,000 after 2 years at 3% compound interest per year.
8. (Higher tier only) y is proportional to x². When x = 2, y = 20. Find y when x = 6.
9. (Higher tier only) y is inversely proportional to √x. When x = 9, y = 4. Find x when y = 3.
10. (Higher tier only) V = 900 × 0.9ⁿ. State the starting value and the percentage fall per period, then find V when n = 3.

### Answers

1. 9.45 ÷ 7 = £1.35 each, so 12 pens cost **£16.20**.
2. k = 18 ÷ 4.5 = 4, so y = 4 × 7 = **28**.
3. k = 12 × 5 = 60, so x = 60 ÷ 8 = **7.5**.
4. Total = 8 × 6 = 48 painter-days, so 48 ÷ 12 = **4 days**.
5. 1800 × 4 × 3 / 100 = **£216**.
6. (a) **0.93** (b) **1.025**.
7. 6000 × 1.03² = 6000 × 1.0609 = **£6,365.40**.
8. k = 20 ÷ 4 = 5, so y = 5 × 36 = **180**.
9. k = 4 × 3 = 12, so 3 = 12/√x, √x = 4 and x = **16**.
10. Start **900**, fall **10%** per period, and V = 900 × 0.729 = **656.1**.

## Where marks are usually lost

- Choosing direct proportion for an inverse context, or the reverse.
- Not showing the step where k is found, so a wrong final number has nothing to support it.
- Writing y = kx when the question says proportional to x², or applying the power to k.
- Multiplying by the exchange rate when the rate should divide, then not noticing the answer is the wrong size.
- Adding percentages across several years instead of multiplying multipliers.
- Using 0.12 for a 12% decrease, or 1.5 for a 5% increase.
- Stopping a trial-and-improvement answer at the first value above the target without checking the one before it.
- Leaving money to many decimal places, or giving 12622.5 without the pound sign and second decimal place.
- Not saying what the answer means when the question asks you to interpret it.
