## Answers

### Section A

**1.** **B** [1] **[1]**
*Examiner insight:* A describes covalent bonding, C metallic bonding and D a simple molecular substance.

**2.**

**(a)** 1+ (Rb⁺) [1]

**(b)** 2− (Se²⁻) [1] **[2]**

**3.**

**(a)** Li₃N [1]

**(b)** Ba(OH)₂ [1]

**(c)** (NH₄)₃PO₄ [1] **[3]**

**4.**

**(a)** Sodium carbonate [1]

**(b)** Iron(II) nitrate [1] **[2]**

**5.** Al³⁺ and S²⁻ ions [1]; two Al³⁺ (total +6) balance three S²⁻ (total −6) [1] **[2]**

**6.** An oxygen atom (2s² 2p⁴) gains two electrons to reach the configuration of neon, 1s² 2s² 2p⁶ [1] **[1]**

**7.** Three ions (one Ca²⁺ and two NO₃⁻) [1]; nine atoms (1 Ca, 2 N, 6 O) [1] **[2]**
*Examiner insight:* Each nitrate ion counts as one ion even though it contains four atoms.

### Section B

**8.**

**(a)** Each lithium atom transfers one electron to an oxygen atom [1]; two lithium atoms are needed for each oxygen atom, forming two Li⁺ ions and one O²⁻ ion [1]

**(b)** Li₂O [1]

**(c)** Two separate Li⁺ ions and one O²⁻ ion, each in square brackets with the charge outside [1]; the oxide ion shows eight outer electrons, two of them with a different symbol to show they came from lithium [1] **[5]**

**9.**

**(a)** K₂CrO₄ [1]

**(b)** Ag₂CrO₄ [1]

**(c)** NaHSO₄ [1]

**(d)** Ca(HCO₃)₂ [1] **[4]**

**10.**

**(a)** Mn⁴⁺ (two O²⁻ give −4) [1]

**(b)** Sc³⁺ (three SO₄²⁻ give −6, shared by two Sc) [1]

**(c)** Cr³⁺ (three OH⁻ give −3) [1] **[3]**

**11.**

**(a)** Each Na⁺ ion is surrounded by six Cl⁻ ions [1]; in a regular, repeating three-dimensional cubic arrangement [1]

**(b)** There are no discrete molecules; the formula only gives the 1 : 1 ratio of ions in the giant lattice [1]

**(c)** Each ion is attracted electrostatically to all the oppositely charged ions around it, in all directions [1] **[4]**

**12.** Indicative content; one mark per valid point, up to 6 [6] **[6]**

- Each calcium atom loses its two outer (4s) electrons to form a Ca²⁺ ion.
- Each nitrogen atom gains three electrons to form an N³⁻ ion.
- Both ions have noble gas configurations (Ca²⁺ like argon, N³⁻ like neon).
- Three Ca²⁺ ions (total +6) balance two N³⁻ ions (total −6).
- The formula is Ca₃N₂.
- The solid is held together by strong electrostatic attraction between the oppositely charged ions in a giant lattice.

*Examiner insight:* Six electrons are transferred from three calcium atoms to two nitrogen atoms; stating the totals shows the ratio is understood, not guessed.

## Where marks are usually lost

- Describing ionic bonding as electron sharing, or as electron transfer only.
- Missing brackets around compound ions.
- Writing charges into the formula of a compound.
- Confusing nitrate with nitride and sulfate with sulfide.
