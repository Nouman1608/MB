This set covers **section 1.6, Percentages**, of the Edexcel International GCSE Mathematics A (4MA1) specification, Issue 2. **Tiers:** questions marked **(Higher tier only)** use repeated percentage change and compound interest problems, which the specification lists for the Higher Tier only. Everything else is for both tiers. Give money answers to the nearest penny.

Related: [section 1.6 study guide](/resources/edexcel-igcse-mathematics-4ma1-percentages-edexcel-igcse-maths/) and [section 1.6 revision notes](/resources/edexcel-igcse-mathematics-4ma1-percentages-edexcel-igcse-maths-revision-notes/).

## Section A

**1.**

**(a)** Write 6.5% as a decimal. **[1]**

**(b)** Write 6.5% as a fraction in its simplest form. **[1]**

**2.** Work out 28% of 350. **[2]**

**3.** Write 54 as a percentage of 72. **[2]**

**4.** Write 750 m as a percentage of 3 km. **[2]**

**5.** Increase £68 by 35%. **[2]**

**6.** Decrease 2.4 kg by 15%. **[2]**

## Section B

**7.** A shopkeeper buys a lamp for £36 and sells it for £45.

**(a)** Work out the percentage profit. **[2]**

**(b)** In a sale, the selling price of £45 is reduced by 20%. What percentage profit does the shopkeeper make on a lamp sold in the sale? **[2]**

**8.**

**(a)** A restaurant bill is £153.60 after a 20% service charge is added. Work out the bill before the service charge. **[3]**

**(b)** A plant grows by 15% in a month. Its height is now 41.4 cm. Work out its height at the start of the month. **[3]**

**9.** Zara invests £2400 for 5 years at 2.8% per year compound interest.

**(a)** Work out the value of her investment after 5 years. **[2]**

**(b)** Work out the total interest she earns. **[1]**

**(c)** Another account pays 3% per year simple interest. Would Zara earn more or less interest in this account over 5 years, and by how much? **[2]**

**10.** A laptop costs £1150 when new. Its value falls by 30% in the first year and then by 18% in each year after that. Work out its value after 3 years. **[3]**

**11.** **(Higher tier only)** The price of a coat is increased by 12%. The new price is then decreased by 12%. Work out the overall percentage change in the price of the coat. **[3]**

**12.** **(Higher tier only)**

**(a)** The population of a town is 45 000. It decreases by 3% each year. After how many whole years will the population first be below 40 000? Show your working. **[3]**

**(b)** £7200 is invested at a fixed rate of compound interest. After 2 years it is worth £7938. Work out the rate of interest. **[3]**

**13.** A price of £54 includes VAT at 20%. Ella says, "The VAT is 20% of £54, which is £10.80."

**(a)** Explain why Ella is wrong. **[1]**

**(b)** Work out the VAT included in the price. **[2]**

**14.** **(Higher tier only)** A boat loses 15% of its value each year. Work out the overall percentage decrease in its value after 3 years. **[2]**

## Answers

**1. (a)** **0.065** [1]

**(b)** 6.5/100 = 65/1000 = **13/200** [1]

**2.** 0.28 × 350 [1] = **98** [1]

**3.** 54/72 × 100 [1] = **75%** [1]

**4.** 3 km = 3000 m, so 750/3000 × 100 [1] = **25%** [1]

**5.** 68 × 1.35 [1] = **£91.80** [1]

**6.** 2.4 × 0.85 [1] = **2.04 kg** [1]

**7. (a)** Profit = £9 [1]; 9/36 × 100 = **25%** [1]

**(b)** Sale price = 45 × 0.8 = £36 [1]; this equals the cost, so **0% profit** (he breaks even) [1]

**8. (a)** Multiplier 1.2 [1]; 153.60 ÷ 1.2 [1] = **£128** [1]

*Examiner insight:* Check a reverse percentage by working forwards: £128 × 1.2 = £153.60, so the answer is confirmed.

**(b)** Multiplier 1.15 [1]; 41.4 ÷ 1.15 [1] = **36 cm** [1]

**9. (a)** 2400 × 1.028⁵ [1] = **£2755.35** [1]

**(b)** 2755.35 − 2400 = **£355.35** [1]

**(c)** Simple interest = 5 × 0.03 × 2400 = £360 [1]; she would earn **more**, by **£4.65** [1]

**10.** 1150 × 0.7 × 0.82² [1] [1] = **£541.28** [1]

*Examiner insight:* The first mark is for the 0.7 multiplier for year 1, the second for 0.82² for years 2 and 3.

**11.** Multipliers 1.12 and 0.88 [1]; 1.12 × 0.88 = 0.9856 [1]; overall **decrease of 1.44%** [1]

**12. (a)** 45 000 × 0.97³ = 41 070 (still above 40 000) [1]; 45 000 × 0.97⁴ = 39 838 (below) [1]; **after 4 years** [1]

**(b)** 7938 ÷ 7200 = 1.1025 [1]; √1.1025 = 1.05 [1]; **5% per year** [1]

**13. (a)** The 20% is a percentage of the price **before** VAT, not of the £54 that already includes it [1]

**(b)** Price before VAT = 54 ÷ 1.2 = £45 [1]; VAT = 54 − 45 = **£9** [1]

**14.** 0.85³ = 0.614125 [1]; 1 − 0.614125 = 0.385875, a decrease of **38.6%** (3 s.f.) [1]

*Examiner insight:* 3 × 15% = 45% is the most common wrong answer: each year's loss is taken from a smaller value.

## Where marks are usually lost

- Writing 6.5% as 0.65 in question 1.
- Leaving question 4 as 750/3 by not converting km to m.
- Taking 20% of £153.60 and subtracting it in question 8(a), which gives £122.88.
- Using 0.7³ in question 10, ignoring that the rate changes after the first year.
- Assuming the changes cancel in question 11.
- Stating "3 years" in question 12(a) without checking the value after 4 years.
