This practice set covers section 2.9 of IB Diploma Programme Mathematics: Analysis and Approaches: exponential and logarithmic functions, their graphs, their inverse relationship and their use in models. It is for SL and HL. Questions are calculator-free unless labelled; give exact answers on calculator-free questions and 3 significant figures otherwise.

## Section A

**1.** For y = eˣ + 3, write down the domain, the range and the equation of the asymptote. **[3]**

**2.** **(a)** Write 6ˣ in the form e^(kx). **[1]**
**(b)** Write e^(3x) in the form aˣ. **[1]**

**3.** Find the exact value of:

**(a)** log₂ 2⁻⁴ **[1]**
**(b)** 7^(log₇ 11) **[1]**
**(c)** ln √e **[1]**

**4.** Let f(x) = ln(3x + 6). Write down the domain of f and the equation of the vertical asymptote, and find the x-intercept. **[3]**

**5.** Let f(x) = 5ˣ.

**(a)** Write down f⁻¹(x). **[1]**
**(b)** Find f⁻¹(125). **[1]**
**(c)** State the domain of f⁻¹. **[1]**

**6.** State whether each function shows growth or decay.

**(a)** y = 2e^(−0.3x) **[1]**
**(b)** y = 4(1.2)ˣ **[1]**

## Section B

**7.** Let f(x) = 6 − 2e^(3x).

**(a)** Write down the equation of the horizontal asymptote. **[1]**
**(b)** Find the y-intercept. **[1]**
**(c)** Find the exact x-intercept. **[2]**
**(d)** Write down the range of f. **[1]**
**(e)** Find f⁻¹(x), stating its domain. **[3]**

**8.** Let g(x) = ln(x + 3) − 1.

**(a)** Write down the domain of g and the equation of its vertical asymptote. **[2]**
**(b)** Find the exact x-intercept. **[2]**
**(c)** Find g⁻¹(x). **[2]**
**(d)** Write down the equation of the horizontal asymptote of the graph of g⁻¹. **[1]**

**9.** (calculator allowed) In an invented model, the mass of a radioactive sample after t days is M(t) = 120e^(−0.04t) grams.

**(a)** Write down the initial mass. **[1]**
**(b)** Find the mass after 15 days. **[1]**
**(c)** Find the half-life of the sample. **[2]**
**(d)** Find the time after which less than 15 g remains. **[2]**

**10.** (calculator allowed) Two invented savings models give the value of an account after t years as A(t) = 1000(1.04)ᵗ and B(t) = 1000e^(0.04t).

**(a)** Write B(t) in the form 1000bᵗ and hence explain which account grows faster. **[2]**
**(b)** Find the difference between the two values after 10 years. **[2]**

**11.** The graph of f(x) = aˣ passes through (2, 9/4).

**(a)** Find a. **[2]**
**(b)** Write down f⁻¹(x) and find f⁻¹(27/8). **[2]**
**(c)** Write down the line in which the graph of f is reflected to give the graph of f⁻¹. **[1]**

**12.** **(a)** Given that eˣ > x for all real x, and that x > ln x for all x > 0, explain why the graphs of y = eˣ and y = ln x never meet. **[2]**
**(b)** The point (0, 1) lies on y = eˣ. Write down the corresponding point on y = ln x. **[1]**

## Answers

**1.** Domain **x ∈ ℝ** [1]; range **y > 3** [1]; asymptote **y = 3** [1]

**2. (a)** **e^(x ln 6)** [1]
**(b)** **(e³)ˣ** [1]

**3. (a)** **−4** [1]
**(b)** **11** [1]
**(c)** ln e^(1/2) = **1/2** [1]

**4.** Domain **x > −2** [1]; asymptote **x = −2** [1]; 3x + 6 = 1 gives **x = −5/3** [1]

**5. (a)** **log₅ x** [1]
**(b)** **3** [1]
**(c)** **x > 0** [1]

**6. (a)** **Decay**, since the exponent −0.3x has a negative coefficient [1]
**(b)** **Growth**, since 1.2 > 1 [1]

**7. (a)** **y = 6** [1]
**(b)** **(0, 4)** [1]
**(c)** e^(3x) = 3 [1], so **x = (1/3) ln 3** [1]
**(d)** **y < 6** [1]
**(e)** e^(3x) = (6 − y)/2 [1]; 3x = ln((6 − y)/2), so **f⁻¹(x) = (1/3) ln((6 − x)/2)** [1], domain **x < 6** [1]
*Examiner insight:* The domain of f⁻¹ is the range of f found in (d); the asymptote y = 6 of f becomes the asymptote x = 6 of f⁻¹, which is a quick check on the answer.

**8. (a)** **x > −3** [1]; **x = −3** [1]
**(b)** ln(x + 3) = 1 [1], so **x = e − 3** [1]
**(c)** y + 1 = ln(x + 3), so x + 3 = e^(y + 1) [1]; **g⁻¹(x) = e^(x + 1) − 3** [1]
**(d)** **y = −3** [1]

**9. (a)** **120 g** [1]
**(b)** 120e^(−0.6) = **65.9 g** [1]
**(c)** e^(−0.04t) = 1/2 [1], so t = ln 2/0.04 = **17.3 days** [1]
**(d)** e^(−0.04t) = 15/120 = 1/8 [1], so t = ln 8/0.04 = **52.0 days** [1]
*Examiner insight:* 15 g is one-eighth of 120 g, three half-lives, so 3 × 17.3 = 52.0 days confirms the answer without a second calculation.

**10. (a)** B(t) = 1000(e^0.04)ᵗ = 1000(1.0408)ᵗ [1]; 1.0408 > 1.04, so **B grows faster** [1]
**(b)** A(10) = 1480.24 and B(10) = 1491.82 [1]; difference **$11.58** [1]

**11. (a)** a² = 9/4 [1]; a > 0, so **a = 3/2** [1]
**(b)** **f⁻¹(x) = log₁.₅ x** [1]; (3/2)³ = 27/8, so **f⁻¹(27/8) = 3** [1]
**(c)** **y = x** [1]

**12. (a)** For x > 0, eˣ > x > ln x, so eˣ > ln x [1]; for x ≤ 0, ln x is not defined. So no x gives eˣ = ln x and the graphs never meet [1]
**(b)** **(1, 0)** [1]

## Where marks are usually lost

- Ranges given with ≥ instead of >, as in questions 1 and 7.
- Logarithm domains stated with the wrong inequality, as in questions 4 and 8.
- Decimals on calculator-free questions where ln 3 or e − 3 is expected.
- Inverse asymptotes not swapped.
- Rounding the growth factor too early in question 10.
