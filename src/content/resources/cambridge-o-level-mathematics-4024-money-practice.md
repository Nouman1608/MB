These questions cover subtopic 1.16 Money of Cambridge O Level Mathematics (Syllabus D) 4024, 2025 to 2027. Questions marked *Calculator* are for Paper 2. The exchange rates are invented for practice. Give money answers correct to the nearest cent unless told otherwise. Show your working.

## Section A

**1.** A shopper buys 4 notebooks at $2.35 each, 2.5 kg of apples at $3.60 per kg and 6 pens at $0.45 each.

**(a)** Work out the total cost. **[3]**

**(b)** She pays with a $50 note. How much change does she get? **[1]**

**2.** The exchange rate is $1 = €0.92.

**(a)** Change €460 into dollars. **[2]**

**(b)** Change $500 into euros. **[1]**

**3.** The exchange rate is $1 = £0.79. Change $500 into pounds. **[1]**

**4.** A 1.5 litre bottle of juice costs $2.64. A 2 litre bottle of the same juice costs $3.40. Which bottle is better value? Show your working. **[3]**

**5.** Cloth costs $7.50 per metre and fuel costs $1.38 per litre.

**(a)** Find the cost of 2.4 m of cloth. **[1]**

**(b)** Find the cost of 35 litres of fuel. **[2]**

## Section B

**6.** *Calculator.* A bill of $184.60 is shared equally between 8 people. How much does each person pay? **[2]**

**7.** *Calculator.* A worker earns $14.20 per hour and works 40 hours. 12% is deducted from his pay. Work out his pay after deductions. **[3]**

**8.** *Calculator.* A sofa has a cash price of $620. Hire purchase terms are a deposit of $240 and 12 monthly payments of $38.50.

**(a)** Work out the total paid under hire purchase. **[2]**

**(b)** Work out the extra cost as a percentage of the cash price, correct to 3 significant figures. **[2]**

**9.** A phone plan costs $12 per month plus $0.15 per minute of calls. Work out the cost for a month in which 140 minutes of calls are made. **[2]**

**10.** *Calculator.* A traveller exchanges $800 into euros at $1 = €0.92. The bureau then takes a commission of 2% of the euros. How many euros does she receive? **[3]**

**11.** *Calculator.* Bureau A offers $1 = €0.92 and takes a fixed fee of $4. Bureau B offers $1 = €0.90 and charges no fee. Which bureau gives more euros for $300, and by how much? **[4]**

**12.** *Calculator.* A tourist changes £400 into dollars at £1 = $1.25. She spends $380. She changes the rest of her dollars back into pounds at $1 = £0.78. How many pounds does she receive? **[4]**

**13.** A shop gives a 15% discount on a $240 item. The customer then pays $12 for delivery. Work out the total cost. **[3]**

**14.** A trader buys 12 items at $18.50 each and sells all of them at $24.90 each. Work out the total profit. **[3]**

## Answers

**1. (a)** notebooks 4 × 2.35 = $9.40 **[1]**; apples 2.5 × 3.60 = $9.00 and pens 6 × 0.45 = $2.70 **[1]**; total **$21.10** **[1]**

**(b)** 50 - 21.10 = **$28.90** **[1]** **[4]**

**2. (a)** 460 ÷ 0.92 **[1]** = **$500** **[1]**

**(b)** 500 × 0.92 = **€460** **[1]** **[3]**

*Examiner insight:* Euros are worth more than dollars at this rate, so there are fewer euros than dollars. The answer in (a) must be bigger than 460.

**3.** 500 × 0.79 = **£395** **[1]**

**4.** 2.64 ÷ 1.5 = $1.76 per litre **[1]**; 3.40 ÷ 2 = $1.70 per litre **[1]**; the **2 litre bottle** is better value **[1]** **[3]**

**5. (a)** 2.4 × 7.50 = **$18.00** **[1]**

**(b)** 35 × 1.38 **[1]** = **$48.30** **[1]** **[3]**

**6.** 184.60 ÷ 8 = 23.075 **[1]**; **$23.08** **[1]** **[2]**

**7.** gross pay 14.20 × 40 = $568 **[1]**; deduction 0.12 × 568 = $68.16 **[1]**; net pay **$499.84** **[1]** **[3]**

**8. (a)** 12 × 38.50 = 462 **[1]**; 240 + 462 = **$702** **[1]**

**(b)** extra = 702 - 620 = $82 **[1]**; 82 ÷ 620 × 100 = **13.2%** **[1]** **[4]**

*Examiner insight:* Include the deposit in the total. Percent is of the cash price, not of the hire purchase price.

**9.** 0.15 × 140 = 21 **[1]**; 12 + 21 = **$33** **[1]** **[2]**

**10.** 800 × 0.92 = €736 **[1]**; commission 0.02 × 736 = €14.72 **[1]**; received 736 - 14.72 = **€721.28** **[1]** **[3]**

**11.** A: (300 - 4) × 0.92 **[1]** = €272.32 **[1]**; B: 300 × 0.90 = €270 **[1]**; **Bureau A** gives **€2.32** more **[1]** **[4]**

**12.** 400 × 1.25 = $500 **[1]**; 500 - 380 = $120 **[1]**; 120 × 0.78 **[1]** = **£93.60** **[1]** **[4]**

**13.** discount: 240 × 0.85 = $204 **[1]**; add delivery 204 + 12 **[1]** = **$216** **[1]** **[3]**

**14.** cost 12 × 18.50 = $222 **[1]**; income 12 × 24.90 = $298.80 **[1]**; profit **$76.80** **[1]** **[3]**

## Where marks are usually lost

- Multiplying when the question needs division in Question 2(a).
- Writing $23.075 instead of $23.08 in Question 6.
- Forgetting to include the deposit in Question 8(a).
- Using the hire purchase price as the base for the percentage in Question 8(b).
- Applying the fixed fee after the exchange in Question 11.
- Using the wrong exchange direction in Question 12.
- Taking 15% off the delivery cost as well in Question 13.

## Frequently asked questions

**How do I decide whether to multiply or divide by the exchange rate?**
Check which currency you are converting to. If one dollar buys 0.92 euros, dollars to euros means multiplying by 0.92, and euros to dollars means dividing by 0.92.

**Should I round money in the middle of a calculation?**
No. Keep the full value on the calculator and round only the final answer to the nearest cent.
