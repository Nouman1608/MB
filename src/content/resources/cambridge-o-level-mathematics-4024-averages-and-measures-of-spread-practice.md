These questions cover section 9.3, Averages and measures of spread, of the Cambridge O Level Mathematics (Syllabus D) 4024 syllabus for 2025, 2026 and 2027. All data is invented. Give non-exact answers to 3 significant figures. The methods are in the [averages and measures of spread study guide](/resources/cambridge-o-level-mathematics-4024-averages-and-measures-of-spread/), and the formulas are collected in the [averages and measures of spread revision notes](/resources/cambridge-o-level-mathematics-4024-averages-and-measures-of-spread-revision-notes/).

## Section A

**1.** Find the mean, median, mode and range of 14, 9, 11, 9, 17, 12, 8. **[4]**

**2.** Find the median and the range of 4.2, 3.8, 5.1, 4.6. **[2]**

**3.** The mean of eight numbers is 15. A ninth number is added and the mean becomes 16. Find the ninth number. **[3]**

**4.** State which average is most suitable in each case, with a reason.

**(a)** The most popular school subject in a survey.

**(b)** A typical salary in a small company where the director earns far more than everyone else. **[2]**

**5.** The amounts below have a mean of 7. Find x.

5, 9, x, 6, 8 **[2]**

## Section B

**6.** The shoe sizes of 30 students are shown.

| Size | 5 | 6 | 7 | 8 | 9 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Frequency | 3 | 8 | 12 | 5 | 2 |

Find the mean, median, mode and range. **[5]**

**7.** The heights of 40 plants are shown.

| Height h (cm) | 140 ≤ h < 150 | 150 ≤ h < 160 | 160 ≤ h < 170 | 170 ≤ h < 180 | 180 ≤ h < 190 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Frequency | 4 | 9 | 15 | 8 | 4 |

**(a)** Calculate an estimate of the mean height.

**(b)** Write down the modal class.

**(c)** Explain why your answer to (a) is an estimate. **[6]**

**8.** The numbers of texts sent by 30 people in a day are grouped below. Calculate an estimate of the mean.

| Texts | 0–4 | 5–9 | 10–14 | 15–19 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Frequency | 6 | 10 | 8 | 6 |

**[4]**

**9.** 12 people in a morning session had a mean waiting time of 34 minutes. 18 people in an afternoon session had a mean waiting time of 29 minutes. Find the mean waiting time of all 30 people. **[3]**

**10.** The table shows the scores on a spinner game. The mean score is 2.3. Find x.

| Score | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Frequency | 5 | x | 6 | 4 |

**[4]**

**11.** The ages of seven members of a club are 21, 23, 24, 24, 26, 28 and 95.

**(a)** Find the mean and the median age.

**(b)** Which better describes a typical member's age? Explain. **[4]**

**12.** Four whole numbers have a mode of 6, a median of 7 and a range of 7. Find the four numbers and their mean. **[4]**

**13.** Hamza estimates the mean of the data in question 7 using the upper boundary of each class (150, 160, 170, 180, 190). Explain the effect of this on his estimate. **[2]**

## Answers

**1.** Ordered: 8, 9, 9, 11, 12, 14, 17. Mean = 80 ÷ 7 = **11.4** [1]. Median = **11** [1]. Mode = **9** [1]. Range = 17 − 8 = **9** [1].

**2.** Ordered: 3.8, 4.2, 4.6, 5.1. Median = (4.2 + 4.6) ÷ 2 = **4.4** [1]. Range = 5.1 − 3.8 = **1.3** [1].

**3.** Total of eight = 8 × 15 = 120 [1]. Total of nine = 9 × 16 = 144 [1]. Ninth number = **24** [1].

**4. (a)** **Mode**, because subjects are categorical data [1].

**(b)** **Median**, because the director's salary would pull the mean up [1].

**5.** Total = 5 × 7 = 35, and 5 + 9 + 6 + 8 = 28 [1], so **x = 7** [1].

**6.** Σfx = 15 + 48 + 84 + 40 + 18 = 205 [1], so mean = 205 ÷ 30 = **6.83** [1]. Median: positions 15 and 16; running totals 3, 11, 23, so both are size 7: **median 7** [1]. **Mode 7** [1]. **Range 9 − 5 = 4** [1].

*Examiner insight:* The mode is the size 7, not its frequency 12.

**7. (a)** Midpoints 145, 155, 165, 175, 185 [1]. Σ(f × midpoint) = 580 + 1395 + 2475 + 1400 + 740 = 6590 [1]. Estimated mean = 6590 ÷ 40 = 164.75, so **165 cm** [1].

**(b)** **160 ≤ h < 170** [1].

**(c)** The exact heights are not known [1], so each class is represented by its midpoint [1].

**8.** Midpoints 2, 7, 12, 17 [1]. Products 12, 70, 96, 102 [1], total 280 [1]. Estimated mean = 280 ÷ 30 = **9.33** [1].

*Examiner insight:* For the discrete class 5–9 the midpoint is (5 + 9) ÷ 2 = 7.

**9.** Totals: 12 × 34 = 408 and 18 × 29 = 522 [1]. Combined total 930 [1]. Mean = 930 ÷ 30 = **31 minutes** [1].

*Examiner insight:* (34 + 29) ÷ 2 = 31.5 is wrong, because the afternoon group is larger.

**10.** Σf = 15 + x and Σfx = 5 + 2x + 18 + 16 = 39 + 2x [1]. 39 + 2x = 2.3(15 + x) [1], so 39 + 2x = 34.5 + 2.3x [1], giving 4.5 = 0.3x and **x = 15** [1].

*Examiner insight:* Check: Σf = 30 and Σfx = 69, and 69 ÷ 30 = 2.3.

**11. (a)** Mean = 241 ÷ 7 = **34.4** years [1]. Median = **24** years [1].

**(b)** The **median** [1]: the age 95 pulls the mean above six of the seven ages [1].

**12.** The mode 6 needs at least two 6s; if the middle two were 6 and 6 the median would be 6, so the two smallest are 6 and 6 [1]. Median 7: (6 + c) ÷ 2 = 7, so c = 8 [1]. Range 7: largest = 6 + 7 = 13 [1]. Numbers **6, 6, 8, 13**, mean = 33 ÷ 4 = **8.25** [1].

**13.** Each upper boundary is 5 cm more than the midpoint [1], so every value is overstated and the estimate is **5 cm too high** (169.75 cm instead of 164.75 cm) [1].

## Where marks are usually lost

- Not ordering data before finding the median.
- Giving the frequency instead of the value as the mode.
- Dividing Σfx by the number of columns instead of Σf.
- Midpoints of discrete classes worked out wrongly.
- Averaging two group means without weighting by group size.
- Missing the word "estimate" or the reason for it.
