These notes condense subtopic **1.13 Percentages** of the Cambridge IGCSE Mathematics 0580 syllabus for 2025, 2026 and 2027. Sections marked **(Extended)** are for Extended candidates only: repeated percentage change and reverse percentages. Worked examples are in the [percentages study guide](/resources/cambridge-igcse-mathematics-0580-percentages-cambridge-igcse-maths/), and exam-style questions are in the [percentages practice questions](/resources/cambridge-igcse-mathematics-0580-percentages-cambridge-igcse-maths-practice/).

## What the syllabus lists

- Calculate a given percentage of a quantity.
- Express one quantity as a percentage of another.
- Calculate percentage increase or decrease.
- Calculate with simple and compound interest. **Formulas are not given.**
- (Extended) Problems may include repeated percentage change.
- (Extended) Calculate using reverse percentages.
- Contexts: deposit, discount, profit and loss (as an amount or a percentage), earnings, percentages over 100%.

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Multiplier | The decimal you multiply by to make a percentage change in one step |
| Cost price | What a trader paid; the base for percentage profit or loss |
| Selling price | What the item is sold for |
| Discount | An amount or percentage taken off a price |
| Deposit | A first payment, often a percentage of the full price |
| Principal | The amount invested or borrowed |
| Simple interest | Interest on the principal only, the same every year |
| Compound interest | Interest on the running total, so it grows each year |
| Reverse percentage | Finding the original amount from the amount after a change |

## Core formulas in words

```
r% of Q                     = Q × r/100
A as a percentage of B      = A ÷ B × 100          (same units first)
percentage change           = change ÷ ORIGINAL × 100
new amount after r% rise    = original × (1 + r/100)
new amount after r% fall    = original × (1 - r/100)
simple interest             = principal × rate × years   (rate as a decimal)
compound total after n yrs  = principal × (1 + r/100)^n
```

## Multiplier table

| Change | Multiplier |
|---|---|
| +4% | 1.04 |
| +12.5% | 1.125 |
| −6% | 0.94 |
| −30% | 0.7 |
| +150% | 2.5 |

A percentage over 100%: 125% of a value is the value × 1.25. An increase of 150% multiplies by 2.5, not 1.5.

## Simple and compound interest side by side

| | Simple | Compound |
|---|---|---|
| Interest each year | the same | grows |
| Calculated on | original principal | previous year's total |
| One-line method | P × r × n (interest only) | P × (multiplier)ⁿ (total) |
| To get interest from the total | -- | total − principal |

## (Extended) Repeated percentage change

- Multiply the multipliers: +20% then −10% gives 1.2 × 0.9 = 1.08, an overall **8% increase**.
- The same percentage up then down always gives an overall decrease.
- For n equal changes, raise the multiplier to the power n.

## (Extended) Reverse percentages

```
new amount = original × multiplier
original   = new amount ÷ multiplier
```

- After a 30% discount the price is 70% of the original: divide by 0.7.
- After a 28% profit the selling price is 128% of cost: divide by 1.28.
- Check by applying the change to your answer.

## Exam traps

- Percentage change divided by the new value.
- Percentage profit worked out on the selling price.
- Using 0.12 instead of 1.12 for a 12% increase.
- Simple interest added year on year to the growing total (that is compound).
- Interest and total value mixed up.
- Units not matched before writing one quantity as a percentage of another, such as minutes and hours.
- (Extended) Adding successive percentage changes.
- (Extended) Taking the percentage of the new amount instead of dividing by the multiplier.
- Money not given to 2 decimal places.

## Self-test

1. Find 18% of $650.
2. Express 27 as a percentage of 60.
3. Increase 240 by 12.5%.
4. A price falls from $80 to $62. Find the percentage decrease.
5. A trader buys a vase for $40 and sells it for $52. Find the percentage profit.
6. Find the simple interest on $2400 for 5 years at 3.5% per year.
7. Find the value of $1500 after 2 years at 4% per year compound interest.
8. Express 85 as a percentage of 68.
9. A 15% deposit is paid on a sofa costing $1240. How much is the deposit?
10. (Extended) A value is increased by 20% and then decreased by 10%. Find the overall percentage change.
11. (Extended) After a 6% pay rise, a salary is $31 270. Find the salary before the rise.
12. (Extended) A coat costs $43.40 after a 30% discount. Find the original price.

### Answers

1. 0.18 × 650 = **$117**
2. 27 ÷ 60 × 100 = **45%**
3. 240 × 1.125 = **270**
4. 18 ÷ 80 × 100 = **22.5%**
5. 12 ÷ 40 × 100 = **30%**
6. 2400 × 0.035 × 5 = **$420**
7. 1500 × 1.04² = **$1622.40**
8. 85 ÷ 68 × 100 = **125%**
9. 0.15 × 1240 = **$186**
10. 1.2 × 0.9 = 1.08: **8% increase**
11. 31 270 ÷ 1.06 = **$29 500**
12. 43.40 ÷ 0.7 = **$62**
