## Questions

### Section A: short questions

**1.** Which structure carries both semen and urine out of the male body? **[1]**

A: sperm duct

B: ureter

C: urethra

D: prostate gland

**2.** Name the part of the male reproductive system that:

**(a)** adds fluid to sperm **[1]**

**(b)** carries sperm from the testes to the urethra. **[1]**

**3.** Where does fertilisation normally take place in humans? **[1]**

A: ovary

B: oviduct

C: uterus

D: vagina

**4.** State **two** functions of the vagina. **[2]**

**5.** Describe what happens to a human zygote between fertilisation and implantation. **[2]**

**6.** Explain why an egg cell contains energy stores. **[1]**

### Section B: structured questions

**7.** Identify each structure from its description.

**(a)** a narrow tube that leads from near an ovary to the uterus **[1]**

**(b)** a ring of muscle at the neck of the uterus **[1]**

**(c)** a sac of skin that holds the testes outside the body **[1]**

**(d)** a muscular organ with a thick lining in which the fetus develops **[1]**

**8.** Sperm and egg cells are both gametes, but they are very different.

**(a)** Explain why sperm have a flagellum and many mitochondria, but egg cells do not. **[3]**

**(b)** Suggest why a man produces very large numbers of sperm, while a woman usually releases one egg cell in each menstrual cycle. **[2]**

**9.** A man has a blockage in both of his sperm ducts. His testes produce sperm normally.

**(a)** Explain why he cannot make a woman pregnant through sexual intercourse. **[2]**

**(b)** Suggest whether he would still produce some fluid during ejaculation. Give a reason. **[1]**

**10.** A sperm reaches an egg cell in the oviduct.

**(a)** Describe the role of the enzymes in the acrosome. **[1]**

**(b)** After one sperm enters, the jelly coat of the egg changes. Explain why this change is important. **[2]**

**11.** *(Extended)* The table shows the concentrations of some substances in the fetus's blood in the umbilical cord, flowing towards the placenta and flowing away from it. The units are arbitrary.

| Substance | Blood flowing towards placenta | Blood flowing away from placenta |
|---|---|---|
| Oxygen | 4 | 11 |
| Carbon dioxide | 12 | 7 |
| Glucose | 3.5 | 4.8 |
| Urea | 6 | 4 |

**(a)** Describe and explain the change in oxygen concentration. **[2]**

**(b)** Explain the change in urea concentration. **[2]**

**(c)** Name the process by which most of these substances cross the placenta. **[1]**

**12.** *(Extended)* A pregnant woman becomes infected with the rubella virus.

**(a)** State why the fetus is at risk even though the mother's blood and the fetus's blood do not mix. **[1]**

**(b)** Suggest why pregnant women are advised not to drink alcohol. **[2]**

**13.** During pregnancy the fetus is surrounded by fluid inside a membrane.

**(a)** Name the membrane that holds this fluid. **[1]**

**(b)** State the function of the fluid. **[1]**

**(c)** State the function of the umbilical cord. **[1]**

## Answers

**1.** **C** [1]

*Examiner insight:* B, the ureter, carries urine from a kidney to the bladder and is not part of the reproductive system.

**2. (a)** **Prostate gland** [1]

**(b)** **Sperm duct** [1]

**3.** **B** [1]

**4.** Receives the **penis / semen** during sexual intercourse [1]; acts as the **birth canal** [1].

**5.** The zygote **divides** repeatedly to form an **embryo / ball of cells** [1], which moves along the **oviduct to the uterus** [1].

**6.** To provide **energy for the early cell divisions** of the zygote / embryo before it implants [1].

**7. (a)** **Oviduct** [1]

**(b)** **Cervix** [1]

**(c)** **Scrotum** [1]

**(d)** **Uterus** [1]

**8. (a)** Sperm must **swim to the egg**, and the **flagellum** moves them [1]. **Mitochondria** carry out **aerobic respiration**, releasing the **energy** the flagellum needs [1]. The egg cell does **not swim / is not motile**; it is moved along the oviduct, so it does not need these features [1].

**(b)** Most sperm **do not reach the egg** / many die on the way [1], so very large numbers make it more likely that **one sperm fertilises** the egg [1].

*Examiner insight:* "Mitochondria give energy" loses the mark in (a); say they release energy by respiration.

**9. (a)** Sperm **cannot travel from the testes to the urethra** [1], so **no sperm are in the semen** and no egg can be fertilised [1].

**(b)** **Yes**: the **prostate gland** still adds fluid to the urethra, beyond the blockage [1].

**10. (a)** They **digest a way through the jelly coat** so the sperm can enter the egg [1].

**(b)** It **stops more sperm from entering** the egg [1], so only **one sperm nucleus fuses** with the egg nucleus and the zygote has the correct amount of genetic information [1].

**11. (a)** Oxygen **rises from 4 to 11** [1]. Oxygen **diffuses from the mother's blood into the fetus's blood** in the placenta [1].

**(b)** Urea falls because it is an **excretory product of the fetus** [1] that **diffuses from the fetus's blood into the mother's blood**, and the mother excretes it [1].

**(c)** **Diffusion** [1]

*Examiner insight:* In (a) use the figures from the table; "it goes up" without numbers or the direction of diffusion is incomplete.

**12. (a)** Some **pathogens, including the rubella virus, can cross the placenta** into the fetus's blood [1].

**(b)** Alcohol is a **toxin** that can **cross the placenta** [1] and **affect / damage the development** of the fetus [1].

**13. (a)** **Amniotic sac** [1]

**(b)** It **cushions and protects** the fetus [1].

**(c)** It **carries the fetus's blood to and from the placenta** / connects the fetus to the placenta [1].

## Where marks are usually lost

- Confusing urethra with ureter, or oviduct with uterus.
- Saying sperm are made in the sperm ducts.
- Placing fertilisation in the uterus.
- Writing that mitochondria "make energy".
- (Extended) Describing a change in the data without saying which way substances diffuse.
- (Extended) Saying the bloods of mother and fetus mix.
