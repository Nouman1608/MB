These questions cover subtopic E7.4, Vector geometry, in the Cambridge IGCSE Mathematics 0580 syllabus for examination in 2025, 2026 and 2027. The subtopic is Extended only, and every question can be done without a calculator. Vectors such as OA are written without the arrow; O is the origin. Give answers in terms of the given vectors, in their simplest form. Learn the methods first with the [vector geometry study guide](/resources/cambridge-igcse-mathematics-0580-vector-geometry/) and the [vector geometry revision notes](/resources/cambridge-igcse-mathematics-0580-vector-geometry-revision-notes/).

## Questions

### Section A: short questions

**1.** OA = **a** and OB = **b**. Write down, in terms of **a** and **b**,

**(a)** AB, **[1]**

**(b)** BA. **[1]**

**2.** OA = **a** and OB = **b**. M is the midpoint of AB. Find AM in terms of **a** and **b**. **[1]**

**3.** OA = **a** and OB = **b**. P lies on AB with AP : PB = 2 : 3.

**(a)** Find AP. **[1]**

**(b)** Find OP, in its simplest form. **[1]**

**4.** A, B and C have position vectors (−1, 2), (3, 4) and (11, 8). Show that A, B and C are collinear. **[2]**

**5. (a)** Show that 6**b** − 3**a** is parallel to **a** − 2**b**. **[1]**

**(b)** Write down the ratio of their lengths. **[1]**

**6.** OABC is a parallelogram with OA = **a** and OC = **c**. Find, in terms of **a** and **c**,

**(a)** OB, **[1]**

**(b)** AC. **[1]**

### Section B: longer questions

**7.** In triangle OAB, OA = **a** and OB = **b**. P lies on OA with OP : PA = 2 : 1 and Q lies on OB with OQ : QB = 2 : 1.

**(a)** Find PQ in terms of **a** and **b**. **[2]**

**(b)** Show that PQ is parallel to AB and write down the ratio PQ : AB. **[2]**

**(c)** Write down the ratio of the area of triangle OPQ to the area of triangle OAB. **[1]**

**8.** In triangle OAB, OA = **a** and OB = **b**. M is the midpoint of OB. N lies on AB with AN : NB = 1 : 2. X is the point with OX = 2**a**.

**(a)** Find ON. **[2]**

**(b)** Find MN. **[2]**

**(c)** Show that M, N and X are collinear. **[2]**

**(d)** Write down the ratio MN : NX. **[1]**

**9.** OABC is a trapezium with OA = 3**a**, OC = **c** and CB = 2**a**.

**(a)** Find OB in terms of **a** and **c**. **[1]**

**(b)** Find AB in terms of **a** and **c**. **[2]**

**(c)** The diagonals OB and AC meet at X. Explain why triangles OXA and BXC are similar, and hence find OX in terms of **a** and **c**. **[3]**

**10.** OP = 2**a** − **b** and OR = 8**a** + m**b**. O, P and R are collinear.

**(a)** Find m. **[2]**

**(b)** Find PR in terms of **a** and **b**. **[1]**

**(c)** Write down the ratio OP : PR. **[1]**

**11.** ABCDEF is a regular hexagon with centre O. OA = **a** and OB = **b**.

**(a)** Explain why OC = **b** − **a**. **[1]**

**(b)** Find AC in terms of **a** and **b**. **[2]**

**(c)** Show that AC is parallel to FD. **[2]**

**12.** OA = (4, 2) and OB = (1, 5). P lies on AB with AP : PB = 1 : 2.

**(a)** Find AB as a column vector. **[1]**

**(b)** Find the position vector of P. **[2]**

**(c)** Q is the point (9, 9). Show that O, P and Q are collinear and find OP : PQ. **[2]**

## Answers

**1. (a)** **b − a** [1]

**(b)** **a − b** [1]

**2.** AM = ½AB = **½(b − a)** [1]

**3. (a)** AP = **⅖(b − a)** [1]

**(b)** OP = **a** + ⅖(**b** − **a**) = **⅗a + ⅖b** [1]

*Examiner insight:* AP : PB = 2 : 3 splits AB into 5 equal parts, so AP is 2 of them. Using ⅔ is the most common error.

**4.** AB = (4, 2) and AC = (12, 6) [1]; AC = 3AB, so AB and AC are parallel and share point A, hence A, B and C are collinear [1]

**5. (a)** 6**b** − 3**a** = −3(**a** − 2**b**), a scalar multiple, so the vectors are parallel [1]

**(b)** **3 : 1** [1]

**6. (a)** **a + c** [1]

**(b)** **c − a** [1]

**7. (a)** OP = ⅔**a** and OQ = ⅔**b** [1]; PQ = **⅔(b − a)** [1]

**(b)** AB = **b** − **a** and PQ = ⅔AB, so PQ is parallel to AB [1]; **PQ : AB = 2 : 3** [1]

**(c)** Area ratio = 2² : 3² = **4 : 9** [1]

**8. (a)** ON = **a** + ⅓(**b** − **a**) [1] = **⅔a + ⅓b** [1]

**(b)** OM = ½**b**, so MN = ON − OM [1] = **⅔a − ⅙b** [1]

**(c)** MX = OX − OM = 2**a** − ½**b** [1]; MX = 3(⅔**a** − ⅙**b**) = 3MN, so MN and MX are parallel and share the point M, hence M, N and X are collinear [1]

**(d)** MX = 3MN, so NX = 2MN and **MN : NX = 1 : 2** [1]

*Examiner insight:* the conclusion must mention the common point M. "MN is parallel to MX" on its own does not prove collinearity.

**9. (a)** OB = OC + CB = **c + 2a** [1]

**(b)** AB = AO + OB [1] = −3**a** + **c** + 2**a** = **c − a** [1]

**(c)** CB is parallel to OA, so angle XBC = angle XOA and angle XCB = angle XAO (alternate angles), making the triangles similar [1]; the scale factor is CB : OA = 2 : 3, so OX : XB = 3 : 2 [1]; OX = ⅗OB = **⅗(2a + c)** [1]

*Examiner insight:* the parallel sides of a trapezium always create a pair of similar triangles where the diagonals cross. Their ratio is the ratio of the parallel sides.

**10. (a)** OR must be a multiple of OP: 8**a** = 4 × 2**a**, so the multiplier is 4 [1]; m = 4 × (−1) = **−4** [1]

**(b)** PR = OR − OP = (8**a** − 4**b**) − (2**a** − **b**) = **6a − 3b** [1]

**(c)** PR = 3OP, so **OP : PR = 1 : 3** [1]

**11. (a)** OABC is a rhombus (all sides equal to the radius of the hexagon), so OC = AB = **b** − **a** [1]

**(b)** AC = OC − OA [1] = **b** − **a** − **a** = **b − 2a** [1]

**(c)** OD = −**a** and OF = −OC = **a** − **b**, so FD = OD − OF = −**a** − **a** + **b** = **b** − 2**a** [1]; FD = AC, so they are parallel (and equal in length) [1]

**12. (a)** **(−3, 3)** [1]

**(b)** OP = OA + ⅓AB [1] = (4, 2) + (−1, 1) = **(3, 3)** [1]

**(c)** OQ = (9, 9) = 3 × (3, 3) = 3OP, and both start at O, so O, P and Q are collinear [1]; **OP : PQ = 1 : 2** [1]

## Where marks are usually lost

- Reversing a vector: writing **a** − **b** for AB.
- Converting a ratio into the wrong fraction.
- Leaving answers unsimplified or with letters such as AB still in them.
- Proving collinearity without naming a shared point.
- Using the length ratio when an area ratio is asked for.
