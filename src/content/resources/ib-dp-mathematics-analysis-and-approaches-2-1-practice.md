## Section A

**1.** Write down the gradient and the y-intercept of the line y = 7 − 3x/4. **[2]**

**2.** For the line 5x − 2y + 8 = 0:

**(a)** find the gradient **[1]**
**(b)** find the x-intercept. **[1]**

**3.** Find the equation of the line through (4, −1) and (−2, 8), giving your answer in the form ax + by + d = 0 where a, b, d ∈ ℤ. **[3]**

**4.** Show that the points A(−1, 5), B(2, −1) and C(5, −7) are collinear. **[2]**

**5.** Find the equation of the line through (3, −2) that is perpendicular to 4x − 3y = 7. Give your answer in the form ax + by + d = 0. **[3]**

**6.** (calculator allowed) In this invented context, a wheelchair ramp rises 0.5 m over a horizontal distance of 8 m.

**(a)** Find the gradient of the ramp. **[1]**
**(b)** A guideline requires a gradient of at most 1/12. Determine whether the ramp meets it. **[1]**

## Section B

**7.** A is the point (2, −3) and B is the point (6, 5).

**(a)** Find the equation of the perpendicular bisector of AB in the form ax + by + d = 0. **[4]**
**(b)** Find the point where the perpendicular bisector meets the y-axis. **[1]**

**8.** Lines L₁ and L₂ have equations 2x − y = 4 and x + 3y = 9.

**(a)** Find the coordinates of their point of intersection, P. **[3]**
**(b)** Determine whether L₁ and L₂ are perpendicular. **[1]**
**(c)** Find the equation of the line L₃ through P that is perpendicular to L₂, in the form y = mx + c. **[2]**

**9.** Lines L₁: (k − 1)x + 4y = 3 and L₂: 2x + (k + 1)y = 5 are given, where k is a constant and k ≠ −1.

**(a)** Find the values of k for which L₁ and L₂ are parallel. **[3]**
**(b)** Find the value of k for which L₁ and L₂ are perpendicular. **[3]**

**10.** (calculator allowed) In this invented context, a tank holds 1200 litres of water and is drained so that the volume after t minutes is V = 1200 − 45t.

**(a)** Interpret the numbers 1200 and −45 in this model. **[2]**
**(b)** Find the time taken for the tank to empty. **[2]**
**(c)** A second tank holds 300 litres and is filled at 30 litres per minute, starting at the same time. Find the time at which the two tanks hold the same volume, and that volume. **[3]**

**11.** Triangle ABC has vertices A(1, 1), B(7, 3) and C(−1, 7).

**(a)** Show that angle BAC is a right angle. **[2]**
**(b)** Find the equation of the line through C parallel to AB, in the form ax + by + d = 0. **[2]**
**(c)** Find the area of triangle ABC. **[2]**

**12.** The line L passes through (2, −1) and (−4, 3).

**(a)** Find the equation of L in the form ax + by + d = 0 where a, b, d ∈ ℤ. **[3]**
**(b)** The point (p, p + 2) lies on L. Find p. **[2]**

## Answers

**1.** Gradient **−3/4** [1]; y-intercept **(0, 7)** [1]

**2. (a)** 2y = 5x + 8, gradient **5/2** [1]
**(b)** y = 0 gives 5x + 8 = 0, so x = **−8/5** [1]

**3.** m = (8 − (−1))/(−2 − 4) = 9/(−6) = −3/2 [1]. y + 1 = −(3/2)(x − 4) [1], so 2y + 2 = −3x + 12 and **3x + 2y − 10 = 0** [1]

**4.** Gradient AB = (−1 − 5)/(2 + 1) = −2 and gradient BC = (−7 + 1)/(5 − 2) = −2 [1]. Equal gradients with the common point B, so A, B and C are collinear [1]
*Examiner insight:* Equal gradients alone describe parallel lines; the shared point B is what makes them one line.

**5.** Gradient of 4x − 3y = 7 is 4/3, so the perpendicular gradient is −3/4 [1]. y + 2 = −(3/4)(x − 3) [1], so 4y + 8 = −3x + 9 and **3x + 4y − 1 = 0** [1]

**6. (a)** 0.5/8 = **0.0625** [1]
**(b)** 1/12 ≈ 0.0833 and 0.0625 < 0.0833, so **the ramp meets the guideline** [1]

**7. (a)** Midpoint (4, 1) [1]. Gradient of AB = 8/4 = 2 [1], so the perpendicular gradient is −1/2 [1]. y − 1 = −(1/2)(x − 4) gives **x + 2y − 6 = 0** [1]
**(b)** x = 0 gives y = 3: **(0, 3)** [1]

**8. (a)** From L₁, y = 2x − 4 [1]. Then x + 3(2x − 4) = 9, so 7x = 21 [1] and **P = (3, 2)** [1]
**(b)** Gradients 2 and −1/3; product −2/3 ≠ −1, so **not perpendicular** [1]
**(c)** Gradient of L₃ is 3 [1]; y − 2 = 3(x − 3), so **y = 3x − 7** [1]

**9. (a)** Gradients −(k − 1)/4 and −2/(k + 1) [1]. Parallel: (k − 1)/4 = 2/(k + 1), so k² − 1 = 8 [1] and **k = ±3** [1]
**(b)** [−(k − 1)/4] × [−2/(k + 1)] = −1 [1], so 2(k − 1) = −4(k + 1) [1], giving 6k = −2 and **k = −1/3** [1]
*Examiner insight:* In (a) check that the lines do not coincide: for k = 3 they are 2x + 4y = 3 and 2x + 4y = 5, which are distinct, and for k = −3 they are x − y = −3/4 and x − y = 5/2, also distinct.

**10. (a)** 1200 is the initial volume in litres [1]; −45 means the volume falls by 45 litres each minute [1]
**(b)** 1200 − 45t = 0 [1], so t = 26.7 minutes (3 s.f.) [1]
**(c)** 1200 − 45t = 300 + 30t [1], so 75t = 900 and **t = 12 minutes** [1]; volume = 1200 − 540 = **660 litres** [1]

**11. (a)** Gradient AB = 2/6 = 1/3 and gradient AC = 6/(−2) = −3 [1]. (1/3)(−3) = −1, so AB is perpendicular to AC and angle BAC = 90° [1]
**(b)** Gradient 1/3 through (−1, 7): y − 7 = (1/3)(x + 1) [1], so **x − 3y + 22 = 0** [1]
**(c)** AB = √(6² + 2²) = √40 and AC = √(2² + 6²) = √40 [1]. Area = (1/2)(√40)(√40) = **20** [1]

**12. (a)** m = (3 − (−1))/(−4 − 2) = −2/3 [1]. So L is 2x + 3y + d = 0, and (2, −1) gives 4 − 3 + d = 0 [1], so **2x + 3y − 1 = 0** [1]
**(b)** 2p + 3(p + 2) − 1 = 0 [1], so 5p = −5 and **p = −1** [1]

## Where marks are usually lost

- The gradient of ax + by + d = 0 taken as a or −b/a.
- The perpendicular gradient given as −m instead of −1/m.
- Answers left with fractions where integer coefficients were required.
- Equal gradients quoted as proof of collinearity without a shared point.
- Units or meaning missing when interpreting a gradient in context.
