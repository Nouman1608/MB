These notes condense subtopic **1.14 Using a calculator** of the Cambridge IGCSE Mathematics 0580 syllabus for 2025, 2026 and 2027. Core and Extended statements are identical, and the syllabus excludes this subtopic from the non-calculator Papers 1 and 2, so it is tested on Papers 3 and 4. Worked keying examples are in the [using a calculator study guide](/resources/cambridge-igcse-mathematics-0580-using-a-calculator-cambridge-igcse-maths/), and exam-style questions are in the [using a calculator practice questions](/resources/cambridge-igcse-mathematics-0580-using-a-calculator-cambridge-igcse-maths-practice/).

## The three statements

1. Use a calculator efficiently: do not round within a calculation; round only the final answer.
2. Enter values appropriately: 2 hours 30 minutes is 2.5 hours or 2° 30′ 0″.
3. Interpret the display appropriately: in money 4.8 means $4.80; in time 3.25 means 3 hours 15 minutes.

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Ans key | Recalls the last answer, with every digit |
| Memory (STO / RCL) | Stores a value to use later without retyping |
| Fraction template | A key that sets up a numerator over a denominator |
| ×10ˣ (EXP) key | Enters a power of 10 as part of one number |
| DMS key | Degrees-minutes-seconds; also converts hours to hours, minutes, seconds |
| Display | The number the calculator shows, before you interpret it |

## Keying rules

| Situation | Rule | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Fraction bar | brackets round numerator and denominator, or use the fraction template | (8.6 + 2.35) ÷ (4.1 × 0.72) |
| Square root of a sum | brackets under the root | √(5.8² + 7.3²) |
| Negative number to a power | brackets round the number | (−1.8)⁴ = 10.4976 |
| Standard form | ×10ˣ key | 3.6 ×10ˣ 5 ÷ 4.8 ×10ˣ −2 |
| Reciprocals | 1 ÷ (...) with brackets | 1 ÷ (1 ÷ 2.5 + 1 ÷ 7.5) |
| Multi-step problem | use Ans or memory; never retype a rounded value | area, then cost |
| π | use the π key (or 3.142 if told) | π × 3.47² |

**Check:** a quick 1 s.f. estimate catches a missing bracket or a misplaced decimal point.

## Entering time

```
minutes -> hours:   ÷ 60          1 h 50 min = 1 + 50 ÷ 60 = 1.8333... h
seconds -> minutes: ÷ 60          4 min 30 s = 4.5 min
DMS key:            1° 50′ 0″  is the same as 1 h 50 min
```

Type the conversion as part of the calculation (1 + 50 ÷ 60), so nothing is rounded.

## Reading the display

**Time (decimal hours)**

```
hours = whole-number part
minutes = decimal part × 60
seconds = any decimal part of the minutes × 60
2.85 h      -> 0.85 × 60 = 51           -> 2 h 51 min
4.583333 h  -> 0.583333 × 60 = 35       -> 4 h 35 min
```

**Money:** always 2 decimal places. A display of 7.1 is **$7.10**; a display of 3 is **$3.00**.

**Standard form:** a display such as 1.72E−05 is written **1.72 × 10⁻⁵**.

**Recurring decimals:** 0.333 333 3 from 1 ÷ 3 is exactly 1/3. Give 0.333 to 3 s.f. or the fraction if an exact answer is wanted.

**Context:** a display of 6.2 buses needed means **7 buses**; a display of 6.8 full boxes filled means **6 boxes**.

## Accuracy rules on Papers 3 and 4

- Non-exact answers: 3 significant figures unless the question says otherwise.
- Angles in degrees: 1 decimal place.
- Exact answers: give them exactly, such as 1.875.
- "Write down all the figures on your calculator display": copy every digit.
- A later part that uses an earlier answer: use the unrounded value.

## Exam traps

- Missing brackets round a numerator, denominator or the contents of a root.
- −1.8⁴ typed when (−1.8)⁴ was meant: one gives −10.4976 and the other 10.4976.
- A rounded area reused for a cost: $236.84 instead of $236.80.
- 1 h 50 min typed as 1.50, or 4 min 30 s typed as 4.30.
- 2.85 hours read as 2 hours 85 minutes or 2 hours 50 minutes.
- Money written to 1 or 3 decimal places.
- E notation copied as the answer.
- Rounding in the "write down all the figures" part.

## Self-test

1. Work out (8.6 + 2.35) ÷ (4.1 × 0.72). Give your answer correct to 3 significant figures.
2. Work out √(5.8² + 7.3²). Write down all the figures on your display, then round to 3 significant figures.
3. Work out (−1.8)⁴.
4. Work out (3.6 × 10⁵) ÷ (4.8 × 10⁻²). Give your answer in standard form.
5. How should you enter 1 hour 50 minutes as a number of hours?
6. A calculation gives a time of 2.85 hours. Write this in hours and minutes.
7. A display shows 7.1 for an amount in dollars. Write the amount correctly.
8. A calculation gives a time of 4.583 333 hours. Write this in hours and minutes.
9. 2.7 kg of rice costs $8.10. Find the cost per kilogram.
10. Work out 1 ÷ (1/2.5 + 1/7.5).
11. A square floor has side 4.37 m. Flooring costs $12.40 per m². Find the cost of flooring the whole floor.
12. The display shows 1.72E−05. Write this value in standard form.

### Answers

1. 10.95 ÷ 2.952 = 3.709 349... = **3.71**
2. **9.323625904**, then **9.32**
3. **10.4976** (exact)
4. **7.5 × 10⁶**
5. **1 + 50 ÷ 60** (= 1.8333... h), or 1° 50′ 0″ with the DMS key
6. 0.85 × 60 = 51: **2 hours 51 minutes**
7. **$7.10**
8. 0.583 333 × 60 = 35: **4 hours 35 minutes**
9. 8.10 ÷ 2.7 = 3: **$3.00**
10. **1.875** (exact)
11. 4.37² × 12.40 = 236.801 56: **$236.80** (rounding the area to 19.1 first gives $236.84, which is wrong)
12. **1.72 × 10⁻⁵**
