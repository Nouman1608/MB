This page is a guide to AQA A-Level Business (7132) past papers for the June 2024, June 2025 and June 2026 series. For each series you get the grade thresholds, copied from AQA's grade-boundary documents, and, where AQA has published its report on the examination, a short account of what the examiners saw, written by us. We do not host any papers. Every paper, mark scheme and report is on AQA's own site, linked below.

## Where to get the official papers

- [AQA A-Level Business (7132) subject page](https://www.aqa.org.uk/subjects/business/a-level/business-7132)
- [AQA A-Level Business (7132) specification](https://www.aqa.org.uk/subjects/business/a-level/business-7132/specification)
- [AQA A-Level Business (7132) assessment resources](https://www.aqa.org.uk/subjects/business/a-level/business-7132/assessment-resources)
- [AQA A-Level Business (7138) assessment resources](https://www.aqa.org.uk/subjects/business/a-level/business-7138/assessment-resources), for the replacement specification
- [AQA past paper finder](https://www.aqa.org.uk/find-past-papers-and-mark-schemes)
- [AQA grade boundaries](https://www.aqa.org.uk/exams-administration/results-days/grade-boundaries)

AQA publishes question papers, inserts, mark schemes and reports on the examination on the assessment resources page for the subject.

Use the three documents together. Sit the paper first, under timed conditions. Mark it strictly with the mark scheme, which tells you what each mark needs. Then read the report on the examination for that paper: it explains why answers that looked reasonable still lost marks. Our notes for each series below are a quick way into that report. If a paper exposes a weak topic, go back to the matching study guide, for example [financial management](/resources/aqa-a-level-business-financial-management/), [human resource management](/resources/aqa-a-level-business-human-resource-management/) or [choosing strategic direction](/resources/aqa-a-level-business-choosing-strategic-direction/), and see the [exam preparation guide](/resources/aqa-a-level-business-exam-preparation/) for technique.

Check which specification a paper was set on. A-Level Business 7132 is a linear qualification, not tiered. AQA's subject page describes it as an outgoing specification, to be used for cohorts taking exams up to 2027, and a replacement, 7138, has been taught since September 2026. Check the specification version on the front cover of any older paper. 7138 has its own papers (Paper 1 on what is business and managing marketing and finance, Paper 2 on managing people and operations, and Paper 3 on business and society, the external environment and strategy), each 2 hours and 90 marks, and AQA lists sample assessment materials for it rather than live past papers, so none of the series below apply to 7138. Do not mix the two specifications when you practise.

The three 7132 papers each last 2 hours, carry 100 marks and are worth one third of the A-level (300 marks in total). Each draws on all ten content sections. Paper 1 has 15 multiple-choice questions (15 marks), short-answer questions (35 marks) and two essays, one from a choice of two in each of two sections (25 marks each). Paper 2 has three compulsory data-response questions of about 33 marks each. Paper 3 has one compulsory case study followed by about six questions.

## June 2025

The June 2025 reports describe a paper set that was accessible, with very few unanswered questions and strong use of numbers and context. On Paper 1, the multiple-choice mean rose by about a mark on the year before. On Paper 2, calculations were often weaker than the written answers and analysis was the weakest skill, with too much assertion in chains of argument. On Paper 3 the case study had less financial data than in earlier years, and the best answers used market data and developed a few issues in depth. Examiners also said that candidates often knew the vocabulary of a model without understanding how it works.

**Where marks were lost**

- Paper 7132/1: a reverse return on capital employed calculation, and a current ratio worked out from net current assets instead of adding the current assets; a question on labour turnover and retrenchment was the hardest multiple-choice item.
- Paper 7132/1: confusion of fiscal policy with monetary policy or with legislation, and little on how taxes affect a market; an HR answer on innovation that discussed the effect on employees rather than the role of the HR function.
- Paper 7132/1: essays that described types of market research without linking them to market share, essays on quality control that muddled it with quality assurance, and uncertainty about what investment means.
- Paper 7132/2: calculations laid out without a formula, labelled workings or the unit; inventory turnover explained as falling sales with no wider context; decision tree answers with the initial cost not subtracted or no qualitative factors.
- Paper 7132/2: threat and opportunity answers that went through the appendix factor by factor, described GDP as falling when it was rising more slowly, and read rising unemployment as fewer people being available for work.
- Paper 7132/2: Herzberg mixed up with Maslow or Taylor, training treated as a hygiene factor, a retailer treated as a manufacturer in a just-in-time answer, and non-current assets confused with current assets.
- Paper 7132/3: Lewin's force field model recalled by its terms without the mechanics, internal weaknesses labelled as threats, one question answered about operations the question did not ask about, and generic counterpoints (such as "forecasts may be wrong") that were not tied to the context.

## June 2024

The June 2024 reports say Paper 1 multiple choice averaged the same as in 2023, with a wider spread across questions. Paper 2 was accessible with few unanswered questions and little sign of timing problems, but weaker answers lacked knowledge of key models and terms or did not develop their analysis, and evaluation was often rushed and one-sided. On Paper 3, candidates handled the case study well in places but struggled to bring different parts of it together and to evaluate with balance.

**Where marks were lost**

- Paper 7132/1: break-even given as a sum of money rather than units of output; NPV calculations that left out the initial cost or subtracted in the wrong direction; exchange rate and elasticity questions among the hardest multiple-choice items.
- Paper 7132/1: arguing for both a bank loan and share capital instead of recommending one; role culture understood less well than power culture; exchange rate effects argued with flawed logic.
- Paper 7132/1: an essay on measuring the success of a non-profit organisation that gave generic points on ratios, and an essay on motivation that wandered into sales, profit and growth.
- Paper 7132/2: the wrong figure used under the line in a payables days formula, gross profit and gross profit margin treated as the same, and an assumption that lower labour productivity always means less output or lower quality.
- Paper 7132/2: Bowman's strategic clock not known, trade unions confused with trading blocs, a cash flow answer that confused profit with revenue and ignored the extra outflows of expansion, and ethics confused with legislation.
- Paper 7132/2: a campaign evaluation that argued for and against without answering whether it was a good idea, and data taken at face value without noting its age or sample size.
- Paper 7132/3: capacity utilisation defined but its advantages near full capacity not recognised, franchising misunderstood, diversification written about where differentiation was asked, and quantitative data used only descriptively.

## Frequently asked questions

**Where can I find AQA A-Level Business 7132 past papers?**
AQA publishes papers, inserts, mark schemes and reports on the examination on the assessment resources page for 7132, and offers a past paper finder on its website.

**Which 7132 papers should I sit when practising a series?**
Sit all three papers for the series. Each is 2 hours and 100 marks. Mark them with the official mark scheme.

**Do the 7132 papers match the new 7138 specification?**
No. 7138 has different papers and no live papers yet; AQA lists sample assessment materials for it. The series on this page were set on 7132, which AQA describes as outgoing, for cohorts taking exams up to 2027.

**Are the paper-level thresholds the ones that decide my grade?**
No. The A-level is linear, so the subject-level boundaries out of 300 decide the grade. AQA gives the paper-level figures as notional boundaries, for illustration only.

**Can grade thresholds tell me what mark I need next time?**
No. Thresholds change every series and should only be used as a rough guide for the series they belong to, never to predict future ones.
