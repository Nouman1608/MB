## Section A

**1.** ξ = {x: x is an integer, 1 ⩽ x ⩽ 15}, A = {x: x is a prime number} and B = {x: x is an odd number}.

**(a)** List the elements of A ∩ B. **[2]**

**(b)** Find n(A ∪ B). **[2]**

**(c)** List the elements of (A ∪ B)'. **[1]**

**2.** Write each statement using a symbol.

**(a)** 6 is not an element of set P. **[1]**

**(b)** Q is a subset of R. **[1]**

**(c)** The set with no elements. **[1]**

**3.** D = {x: 3 < x ⩽ 8, x is an integer}. List the elements of D and write down n(D). **[2]**

**4.** ξ = {1, 2, 3, ..., 10}, P = {2, 4, 6, 8, 10} and Q = {1, 2, 3, 5, 7}.

**(a)** List the elements of P ∩ Q'. **[2]**

**(b)** List the elements of P' ∩ Q. **[2]**

**5.** A ⊆ B, n(A) = 4 and n(B) = 7. Write down:

**(a)** n(A ∪ B) **[1]**

**(b)** n(A ∩ B) **[1]**

**6.** E = {x: x is a multiple of 5, 20 ⩽ x ⩽ 45}.

**(a)** List the elements of E. **[2]**

**(b)** F is the set of square numbers. Find E ∩ F. **[1]**

**7.** A = {2, 4, 6}, B = {x: x is an even integer, 0 < x ⩽ 10} and C = {1, 2, 9}. Copy each statement and put ⊆ or ⊈ in the gap.

**(a)** A ... B **[1]**

**(b)** C ... B **[1]**

**(c)** B ... A **[1]**

## Section B

**8.** There are 45 children in a class. 28 have a cat and 20 have a dog. Six have neither.

**(a)** How many have both a cat and a dog? **[3]**

**(b)** How many have a cat but no dog? **[1]**

**9.** n(ξ) = 30, n(A) = 15, n(B) = 12 and n(A ∩ B) = 5. Find:

**(a)** n(A ∪ B) **[2]**

**(b)** n((A ∪ B)') **[1]**

**10.** ξ = {a, b, c, d, e, f, g, h}, A = {a, b, c, d} and B = {c, d, e, f}.

**(a)** List A', B' and A' ∩ B'. **[3]**

**(b)** List (A ∪ B)'. **[1]**

**(c)** What do you notice about your answers to (a) and (b)? **[1]**

**11.** The set M = {(x, y): y = 2x + 1}.

**(a)** Is (3, 7) ∈ M? Give a reason. **[1]**

**(b)** Is (2, 6) ∈ M? Give a reason. **[1]**

**12.** Sixty students were asked which of football (F), hockey (H) and swimming (S) they play. The results are:

- 30 play football, 26 play hockey and 22 swim.
- 10 play football and hockey, 8 play hockey and swimming, 7 play football and swimming.
- 4 play all three.

**(a)** How many play football and hockey but not swimming? **[1]**

**(b)** How many play exactly one of the three activities? **[3]**

**(c)** How many play none of the three? **[2]**

**13.** In a group of 40 people, n(A) = 18 and n(B) = 22. The number in both A and B is x. The number in neither set is 2x - 5.

**(a)** Write an expression for n(A ∪ B) in terms of x. **[1]**

**(b)** Form an equation and solve it to find x. **[3]**

**14.** Explain why n(A ∩ B) = n(A) when A ⊆ B, and use this to find n(A ∪ B) if n(A) = 9 and n(B) = 20. **[3]**

## Answers

**1. (a)** A = {2, 3, 5, 7, 11, 13} and B = {1, 3, 5, ..., 15} **[1]**; A ∩ B = **{3, 5, 7, 11, 13}** **[1]**

**(b)** B has 8 elements and 2 is the only extra element in A **[1]**; n(A ∪ B) = **9** **[1]**

**(c)** **{4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 14}** **[1]** **[5]**

**2. (a)** 6 ∉ P **[1]**

**(b)** Q ⊆ R **[1]**

**(c)** ∅ **[1]** **[3]**

**3.** **{4, 5, 6, 7, 8}** **[1]**; n(D) = **5** **[1]** **[2]**

**4. (a)** P ∩ Q' means in P but not in Q **[1]**; **{4, 6, 8, 10}** **[1]**

**(b)** P' ∩ Q means in Q but not in P: **{1, 3, 5, 7}** **[2]** **[4]**

**5. (a)** A ∪ B = B, so **7** **[1]**

**(b)** A ∩ B = A, so **4** **[1]** **[2]**

**6. (a)** **{20, 25, 30, 35, 40, 45}** **[2]** (one mark if one element is missing or extra)

**(b)** The only square number in E is 25, so E ∩ F = **{25}** **[1]** **[3]**

**7. (a)** B = {2, 4, 6, 8, 10}, so A **⊆** B **[1]**

**(b)** 1 and 9 are not in B, so C **⊈** B **[1]**

**(c)** 8 and 10 are in B but not A, so B **⊈** A **[1]** **[3]**

**8. (a)** At least one pet: 45 - 6 = 39 **[1]**; 28 + 20 - 39 **[1]** = **9** **[1]**

**(b)** 28 - 9 = **19** **[1]** **[4]**

*Check:* 19 + 9 + 11 + 6 = 45.

**9. (a)** 15 + 12 - 5 **[1]** = **22** **[1]**

**(b)** 30 - 22 = **8** **[1]** **[3]**

**10. (a)** A' = **{e, f, g, h}** **[1]**; B' = **{a, b, g, h}** **[1]**; A' ∩ B' = **{g, h}** **[1]**

**(b)** A ∪ B = {a, b, c, d, e, f}, so (A ∪ B)' = **{g, h}** **[1]**

**(c)** They are the same set: A' ∩ B' = (A ∪ B)' **[1]** **[5]**

**11. (a)** Yes, because 2 × 3 + 1 = 7 **[1]**

**(b)** No, because 2 × 2 + 1 = 5, not 6 **[1]** **[2]**

**12. (a)** 10 - 4 = **6** **[1]**

**(b)** Hockey and swimming only: 8 - 4 = 4; football and swimming only: 7 - 4 = 3. Football only = 30 - 6 - 3 - 4 = 17 **[1]**; hockey only = 26 - 6 - 4 - 4 = 12 **[1]**; swimming only = 22 - 4 - 3 - 4 = 11, and 17 + 12 + 11 = **40** **[1]**

**(c)** Inside the circles: 4 + 6 + 4 + 3 + 17 + 12 + 11 = 57, or 30 + 26 + 22 - 10 - 8 - 7 + 4 = 57 **[1]**; none = 60 - 57 = **3** **[1]** **[6]**

**13. (a)** n(A ∪ B) = 18 + 22 - x = **40 - x** **[1]**

**(b)** Neither = 40 - (40 - x) = x, so 2x - 5 = x **[1]**; x = 5 **[1]**; check: both 5, neither 5, union 35 = 18 + 22 - 5 **[1]** **[4]**

*Examiner insight:* A common slip is to write 40 = 18 + 22 + x + (2x - 5), forgetting that the overlap is counted twice when the circle totals are added.

**14.** If A ⊆ B, every element of A is also in B **[1]**, so the elements common to both are exactly the elements of A and n(A ∩ B) = n(A) **[1]**; n(A ∪ B) = 9 + 20 - 9 = **20** **[1]** **[3]**

## Where marks are usually lost

- Forgetting that 2 is prime in Question 1.
- Listing P ∩ Q instead of P ∩ Q' in Question 4(a).
- Adding 28 and 20 without removing the overlap in Question 8.
- Forgetting the "neither" set in Question 9.
- Subtracting the pairwise overlap from a circle total without removing the centre in Question 12.
- Forming the equation 40 = 18 + 22 + x + (2x - 5) in Question 13.

## Frequently asked questions

**What is the difference between ∈ and ⊆?**
Use ∈ between an element and a set, such as 3 ∈ A. Use ⊆ between two sets, such as {3} ⊆ A.
