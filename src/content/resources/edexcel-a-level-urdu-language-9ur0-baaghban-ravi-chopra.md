This guide is for students writing a Paper 2 Section C essay on **باغبان** (*Baaghban*), listed in Appendix 1 of the Pearson Edexcel A Level Urdu (9UR0) specification with the name **روی چوپڑہ** (Ravi Chopra) and the year 2003. The [Baaghban revision notes](/resources/edexcel-a-level-urdu-language-baaghban-revision-notes/) outline the story of Raj and Pooja Malhotra, their four sons and Alok, the meaning of the title and the main themes. This guide concentrates on the skill that most separates strong from average answers on this film: **evaluating** a work whose message is very clear, rather than simply agreeing with it. Practise with the [Baaghban practice questions](/resources/edexcel-a-level-urdu-language-9ur0-baaghban-ravi-chopra-practice/).

Prescribed works can change between series, so check the current specification.

## The rules for a film essay

- Section C; allowed only alongside one literary-text essay, never with a second film.
- One question from a choice of two, set in Urdu, answered in Urdu, with no notes or dictionary.
- About 320–370 words suggested; the whole essay is marked.
- Three grids: critical and analytical response (AO4, 20), range of grammatical structures and vocabulary including cinematic terminology (AO3, 15), accuracy (AO3, 10).

The AO4 grid asks for "convincing interpretations and points of view, consistently justified with appropriately selected evidence" and arguments with linked conclusions. With *Baaghban*, the risk is an essay that only repeats the film's moral. Interpretation means asking **how** the film persuades, and **whether** its picture is fair.

## Why evaluation matters with this film

*Baaghban* is a family melodrama with a strong moral voice. Its sympathies are clear: the parents are wronged, the sons are ungrateful, Alok is loyal. A middle-band essay says this. A top-band essay asks:

- **How** does the film make us feel this way? (Casting, music, contrast, structure, the final speech.)
- **What** does it leave out or simplify? (The sons' point of view, pressures of modern urban life, the daughters-in-law as individuals.)
- **Is** the message strengthened or weakened by the method?

You do not have to dislike the film to evaluate it. You can admire its emotional power and still note that it presents characters in black and white.

## The five features for Baaghban

| Feature | Questions to prepare |
|---|---|
| Form and technique | How do songs, music, contrasting scenes, casting and the final speech guide our sympathy? |
| Themes, concepts and issues | duty to parents; old age and dignity; marriage as companionship; gratitude and ingratitude; money and independence |
| Characterisation | Raj and Pooja as an ideal couple; the sons as a group or as individuals; Alok as a foil |
| Structure of the plot | a happy beginning, separation in the middle, and a public reckoning at the end |
| Social and cultural setting | an Indian middle-class family; the joint-family ideal under pressure from urban, individual lifestyles |

## Technique: four tools the film uses

**1. Songs.** Songs mark the emotional stages of the story. Early celebration songs, such as the Holi song, show the family together and the couple's joy; later, a song of separation, *Main Yahan Tu Wahan*, expresses the parents' longing when they live apart. Ask what each song does that dialogue could not.

**2. Contrast and parallel editing.** Scenes of the parents' loneliness are set against scenes of the sons' comfort. This **متوازی تدوین** (cross-cutting between parallel scenes) steers the audience's judgement.

**3. Casting.** Amitabh Bachchan and Hema Malini were among the most famous stars of Hindi cinema; their presence gives the parents dignity and draws sympathy before the story begins. Alok is played by Salman Khan, a popular star in a supporting role, which raises the importance of the loyal "adopted" son.

**4. The final speech.** The film ends with Raj speaking publicly about children's duty to parents. A direct statement of the message is typical of melodrama. You can argue either way: it gives the film a powerful climax, or it tells the audience what to think instead of letting them decide.

## Characterisation: using a foil

A **foil** (**تقابلی کردار**, a character who highlights another by contrast) is a useful concept here. Alok, a young man the couple cared for as a boy, treats them with the devotion their sons lack. The contrast suggests that loyalty is shown by actions, not only by blood. For a top-band answer, push further: does using Alok as a perfect contrast make the sons less believable? Does the film allow the sons any reasons of their own?

## Worked example: an evaluative paragraph

Question (original): **«فلم "باغبان" اپنا پیغام مؤثر انداز میں پہنچاتی ہے، مگر اس کے کردار حقیقی کم اور علامتی زیادہ ہیں۔» اس بیان کا جائزہ لیجیے۔**

> اس میں کوئی شک نہیں کہ "باغبان" کا پیغام ناظرین کے دلوں تک پہنچتا ہے۔ جب راج اور پوجا الگ الگ گھروں میں ایک دوسرے کو یاد کرتے ہیں اور پس منظر میں جدائی کا گانا چلتا ہے تو ناظرین ان کی تنہائی کو اپنے اندر محسوس کرتے ہیں۔ لیکن اسی جذباتی شدت کی ایک قیمت بھی ہے۔ چاروں بیٹے تقریباً ایک ہی سانچے میں ڈھلے ہوئے نظر آتے ہیں: خود غرض، بے حس اور احسان فراموش۔ فلم ہمیں یہ جاننے کا موقع کم دیتی ہے کہ شہر کی مہنگی زندگی یا ان کی اپنی مجبوریاں ان کے رویے کی وجہ ہو سکتی ہیں۔ یوں یہ کردار جیتے جاگتے انسانوں سے زیادہ ایک اخلاقی سبق کی علامت بن جاتے ہیں۔

(There is no doubt that the message of *Baaghban* reaches the audience's hearts. When Raj and Pooja miss each other in separate homes and a song of separation plays in the background, the audience feels their loneliness within themselves. But this very emotional intensity has a price. The four sons appear almost cast in a single mould: selfish, unfeeling and ungrateful. The film gives us little chance to learn whether expensive city life or their own constraints might explain their behaviour. In this way these characters become less living human beings than symbols of a moral lesson.)

**Why it works:** it concedes the film's strength with evidence (song, separation), then turns with **لیکن ... ایک قیمت بھی ہے**; the metaphor **ایک ہی سانچے میں ڈھلے ہوئے** shows range; the final sentence answers the question's contrast between **حقیقی** and **علامتی**. Check the details against your own viewing and add a specific scene for each son you mention.

## Common mistakes

- Agreeing with the film's message without analysing how it is delivered.
- Writing about the sons as a group without naming any scene.
- Calling Ravi Chopra the director on the strength of the specification list alone; the list gives a name and year only.
- Treating the film as a documentary about all families.
- Writing a second film essay.

## Exam technique

- Decide in your plan where you will **concede** (the film's power) and where you will **question** (simplification, the final speech).
- In each paragraph, pair a technique with its effect and a judgement.
- Link to Theme 1, family, only to deepen your point about the film, not as a separate essay on care of the elderly.
- Vary structures: **اس میں کوئی شک نہیں کہ ... لیکن**, **جتنا ... اتنا**, passives and conditionals.
