Cambridge IGCSE English Literature (0475) is not tiered, and every candidate takes Paper 1, Poetry and Prose (1 hour 30 minutes, 50 marks, 50%), plus exactly one of three routes, each worth the remaining 50%. Route A is Paper 2, Drama (1 hour 30 minutes, 50 marks, 50%). Route B is Paper 3, Drama (Open Text) (45 minutes, 25 marks, 25%) together with Paper 4, Unseen (1 hour 15 minutes, 25 marks, 25%). Route C is Paper 3 together with Component 5, Coursework (25 marks, 25%). The 2026 syllabus is a single-year edition, so check it carefully. Detailed pages sit alongside this one for [Paper 1 Poetry and Prose](/resources/igcse-english-literature-paper-1-poetry-and-prose/), [Paper 4 Unseen](/resources/igcse-english-literature-paper-4-unseen/) and [Component 5 Coursework](/resources/cambridge-igcse-english-literature-component-5-coursework-study-guide/).

## Choosing your route

Your centre decides which route you sit, so confirm it early. The routes are not three different qualifications: they share Paper 1, and Routes B and C share the same physical Paper 3. What changes is how the second half of the marks is earned: a single drama paper, an open-text drama paper plus an unseen paper, or an open-text drama paper plus a coursework portfolio. Prepare for the route you have been entered for rather than trying to cover all three.

## Paper 1: Poetry and Prose (50 marks, 50%)

You have 1 hour 30 minutes to write two questions on two texts. With 50 marks over 90 minutes, that is 1.8 minutes per mark, so roughly 45 minutes per answer including planning. Revise your set poems and prose text so that you can select short, precise quotations and comment on how the writer shapes meaning, not just what happens. See the [Paper 1 revision notes](/resources/igcse-english-literature-paper-1-poetry-and-prose-revision-notes/) for support.

## Route A: Paper 2, Drama (50 marks, 50%)

Paper 2 mirrors Paper 1 in size: 1 hour 30 minutes, 50 marks, two questions on two texts. The pace is therefore the same, 1.8 minutes per mark. Track how each play builds character and tension through dialogue and staging, and be ready to treat a play as written for performance. The [Paper 2 Drama page](/resources/igcse-english-literature-paper-2-drama/) covers this paper.

## Routes B and C: Paper 3, Drama (Open Text) (25 marks, 25%)

Paper 3 lasts 45 minutes, carries 25 marks and asks one question on one text. That is 1.8 minutes per mark again, so spend about 5 minutes planning and the rest writing a sustained, focused response. The text is an open text, which means you can bring detailed knowledge of the play you have studied.

## Route B: Paper 4, Unseen (25 marks, 25%)

Paper 4 lasts 1 hour 15 minutes for 25 marks, a slower pace of 75 / 25 = 3 minutes per mark. The one question requires critical commentary on a passage you have not seen before, so the extra time is for close reading: annotate first, decide what the passage does, then write. Practise with unfamiliar short texts of different kinds.

## Route C: Component 5, Coursework (25 marks, 25%)

Coursework is a portfolio of two assignments, each on a different text, internally assessed and externally moderated. There is no timed paper, so the skills are planning, drafting and polishing within the deadlines your centre sets. Keep advice from your teacher and follow the specification on length and conditions, which this page does not set out.

## Timing and marks-per-minute

- Paper 1: 90 / 50 = 1.8 minutes per mark.
- Paper 2 (Route A): 90 / 50 = 1.8 minutes per mark.
- Paper 3 (Routes B and C): 45 / 25 = 1.8 minutes per mark.
- Paper 4 (Route B): 75 / 25 = 3 minutes per mark.
- Written exam time by route: Route A is 90 + 90 = 180 minutes, Route B is 90 + 45 + 75 = 210 minutes, and Route C is 90 + 45 = 135 minutes plus coursework.

Each route totals 100 marks, since 50 + 50, 50 + 25 + 25 and 50 + 25 + 25 are all 100.

## Command words and question styles

Literature questions are often phrased as "How does the writer...", "Explore the ways in which..." or "To what extent...". Each wants analysis of language, structure and form with evidence, not retelling. "Explore" invites more than one interpretation; "how" points you to writer's methods; "to what extent" needs a judgement.

## Worked practice scenario

An original Paper 4 style practice, using four invented lines.

> The last bus leaves; the shelter hums with rain.
> A coat still holds the shape of someone gone.
> The timetable repeats what we both know,
> and neither of us says the thing we came for.

Question: "Write a critical commentary on how the poet presents silence between two people."

Plan: (1) Overall effect: parting that is unspoken. (2) Setting: "last bus", "rain" suggest an ending. (3) Image of the coat: absence made physical. (4) Final line: restraint and the unsaid.

Model paragraph: "The poet makes silence feel heavy by filling the setting with small sounds while the speakers say nothing. The shelter 'hums with rain', so the only voice in the scene belongs to the weather, and the verb 'hums' gives that noise a steady, almost comforting quality that contrasts with the strain between the people. The phrase 'the thing we came for' is deliberately vague; by withholding what is missing, the poet lets the reader share the speakers' reluctance, and the neat, final line stops without resolution, mirroring the conversation that never starts."

It names features, quotes briefly, and explains the effect on the reader.

## Before/during exam checklist

- Confirm your route and the texts you have studied for it.
- Learn short quotations you can adapt to different questions.
- Plan for a few minutes before each essay; do not start writing blind.
- Time each question by the minutes-per-mark figures above.
- On Paper 4, annotate the passage before choosing an angle.
- Write about methods and effects, not plot summary.
- Finish with a short conclusion that answers the question.
- Leave time to check quotations are copied accurately.

## Frequently asked questions

**How many papers must I take for Cambridge IGCSE English Literature 0475?**
You take Paper 1, Poetry and Prose, plus exactly one of three routes. Each route is worth the remaining 50%, so every route totals 100% with Paper 1.

**What are the three routes?**
Route A is Paper 2, Drama. Route B is Paper 3, Drama (Open Text) plus Paper 4, Unseen. Route C is Paper 3 plus Component 5, Coursework.

**How long is Paper 3?**
Paper 3 lasts 45 minutes, is worth 25 marks and 25%, and asks one question on one text. It is the same paper whether you take Route B or Route C.

**How is Component 5 assessed?**
It is a portfolio of two assignments, each on a different text, worth 25 marks and 25%. It is internally assessed and externally moderated, with no fixed exam duration.

**Which grades are available?**
The record states grades A* to G are available. The 2026 syllabus is a single-year edition.
