This guide is for students writing a Paper 2 Section C essay on **بن روئے** (*Bin Roye*), listed in Appendix 1 of the Pearson Edexcel A Level Urdu (9UR0) specification with the name **مومنہ دُرید** (Momina Duraid) and the year 2015. The [Bin Roye revision notes](/resources/edexcel-a-level-urdu-language-bin-roye-revision-notes/) outline the story of Saba and Irtaza, the meaning of the title and the main themes. This guide concentrates on **writing about a film as a film**: the techniques examiners expect you to analyse, how to prepare evidence from a screen work, and how to plan a 45-mark essay. Practise with the [Bin Roye practice questions](/resources/edexcel-a-level-urdu-language-9ur0-bin-roye-momina-duraid-practice/).

Prescribed works can change between series, so check the current specification.

## The rules for a film essay

- A film essay goes in **Section C**. You may write it only if your other essay is on a literary text; two film essays are not allowed.
- You choose one of two questions on the film, set in Urdu, and answer in Urdu, with no notes and no dictionary.
- The specification suggests about 320–370 words; the whole essay is marked regardless of length.
- Three grids: critical and analytical response (AO4, 20), range of grammatical structures and vocabulary (AO3, 15), accuracy (AO3, 10).

The range grid explicitly rewards **terminology appropriate for literary and cinematic analysis**. The specification's own examples of cinematic terms include "camera technique", "hand-held camera" and "use of black and white". A film essay with no film vocabulary cannot reach the top of that grid.

## The five features for a film

| Feature | For *Bin Roye*, ask ... |
|---|---|
| Form and technique | How do camera, music, editing, colour, costume and setting shape what we feel? |
| Themes, concepts and issues | Unspoken love, family expectations, grief, a young woman's choices |
| Characterisation | How are Saba and Irtaza presented, by performance as well as by dialogue? Who changes, and how is the change shown? |
| Structure of the plot | Where are the turning points? How does the film move between celebration and loss? |
| Social and cultural setting | Family homes, weddings and gatherings; expectations about marriage and emotional expression |

## Film techniques to analyse

Learn these terms in Urdu and practise using each in a sentence about the film:

| Urdu | Meaning | What to look for |
|---|---|---|
| عکس بندی | cinematography | how scenes are framed and lit |
| کلوز اپ | close-up | a face held on screen to show feeling the character does not say |
| کیمرے کا زاویہ | camera angle | looking up or down at a character to suggest power or vulnerability |
| روشنی | lighting | bright, warm scenes of celebration against darker scenes of grief |
| تدوین | editing | how quickly shots change; placing joy and sorrow side by side |
| پس منظر موسیقی | background score | music that tells us how to feel before anyone speaks |
| گانا | song | songs that express what characters cannot say, or mark a celebration |
| ملبوسات | costume | colours and clothing that change as a character's situation changes |
| منظر کی ترتیب | arrangement of a shot (setting, objects, positions) | who stands close, who stands apart |
| ماضی کی جھلک (فلیش بیک) | flashback | memory interrupting the present |

The point of naming a technique is to explain its **effect**: **کلوز اپ کے ذریعے ناظرین صبا کے وہ جذبات دیکھ لیتے ہیں جو وہ زبان سے ادا نہیں کر پاتی** (through the close-up the audience sees the feelings Saba cannot put into words). This fits a film whose central tension is silence.

## Preparing evidence: a scene log

You cannot take notes into the exam, so you need a small set of scenes you can describe precisely. While watching, keep a log in Urdu for six to eight key scenes:

| Column | What to write |
|---|---|
| منظر | a short label you will remember |
| کیا ہوتا ہے | one sentence |
| تکنیک | one or two techniques you noticed |
| اثر | the effect on the audience |
| موضوع | which theme it supports |

Choose scenes across the whole film: an early scene that sets up the relationships, the main turning point, a scene of celebration, a scene of grief, the climax and the ending. Rewatch these scenes rather than the whole film when revising.

## Worked example: unpacking a question

Question (original): **«فلم "بن روئے" میں موسیقی اور مناظر کا تضاد کہانی کے جذباتی اثر کو بڑھاتا ہے۔» اس بیان کا جائزہ لیجیے۔**

Translation: "In *Bin Roye*, the contrast between music and scenes intensifies the emotional impact of the story." Evaluate this statement.

**Key words:** **تضاد** (contrast) and **جذباتی اثر** (emotional impact). The question is about **technique**, so the essay must analyse how specific sequences are put together, not just what happens.

**Plan:**

1. **Introduction**: the film, its genre (romantic family drama), position on the statement.
2. **Celebration**: a wedding or family occasion; songs, colour and lighting create joy, which raises the emotional stakes.
3. **Juxtaposition**: a sequence where joy and grief are placed close together; analyse editing and music.
4. **Silence**: a moment where the absence of music or words carries the emotion, which lets you qualify the statement (not only music creates impact).
5. **Conclusion**: a judgement on how far contrast is the film's main emotional technique.

## Worked example: an annotated body paragraph

> فلم میں خوشی کے مناظر محض سجاوٹ کے لیے نہیں رکھے گئے۔ شادی کی تقریبات میں شوخ رنگ، تیز روشنی اور گانوں کی رونق ناظرین کو ایک بے فکر دنیا میں لے جاتی ہے، اسی لیے جب کہانی اچانک دکھ کی طرف مڑتی ہے تو یہ صدمہ اور بھی گہرا محسوس ہوتا ہے۔ ہدایت کاری کا یہ انداز تضاد پر مبنی ہے: جتنی بلندی پر خوشی دکھائی جاتی ہے، اتنی ہی گہرائی میں غم اترتا ہے۔ تاہم میرے خیال میں فلم کا سب سے مؤثر لمحہ وہ ہے جب موسیقی رک جاتی ہے اور صرف صبا کا خاموش چہرہ کلوز اپ میں نظر آتا ہے۔

(The scenes of happiness in the film are not placed there merely as decoration. At the wedding celebrations, bright colours, strong lighting and the liveliness of the songs take the audience into a carefree world, which is why, when the story suddenly turns towards sorrow, the shock feels all the deeper. This approach rests on contrast: the higher the joy is shown, the deeper the grief descends. However, in my view the film's most effective moment is when the music stops and only Saba's silent face is seen in close-up.)

**Why it works:** a claim about purpose (**محض سجاوٹ کے لیے نہیں**); named techniques (colour, lighting, songs, close-up); effect explained (**صدمہ اور بھی گہرا محسوس ہوتا ہے**); the correlative **جتنی ... اتنی ہی** as complex language; and **تاہم** to qualify. In your own essay, name the specific scene from your scene log.

## Common mistakes

- Writing only about the love story, with no film technique.
- Using film terms as labels without explaining effect.
- Calling the name in the specification's list the "director" without checking; the list gives a name and year only. The revision notes record that Momina Duraid produced the film.
- Writing two film essays.
- Describing scenes so vaguely ("in one scene ...") that they cannot count as evidence.

## Exam technique

- Choose the question whose key word you can support from at least three scenes in your log.
- In each paragraph: scene, technique, effect, link to the question.
- Use present tense for what happens in the film (**صبا خاموش رہتی ہے**), the usual convention for discussing a work.
- Show range with passives (**دکھایا گیا ہے**), causatives, correlatives (**جتنا ... اتنا**), and concessive clauses.
