Condensed notes for sub-topic 3.1, International trade, from benefits and challenges to balances, trading blocs and restrictions.

## Four facts to start with

1. **Exports** are sold abroad; **imports** are bought from abroad.
2. **Balance of Trade** = exports of goods − imports of goods.
3. **Balance of Payments** records all money flows, including services and investment.
4. Tariffs, quotas and embargoes **restrict** trade.

## Key terms

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Export | Good or service sold to another country |
| Import | Good or service bought from another country |
| Visible trade | Trade in goods |
| Invisible trade | Trade in services such as tourism, banking and insurance |
| Balance of Trade | Value of exports of goods minus value of imports of goods |
| Balance of Payments | Record of all money flowing into and out of a country |
| Surplus | More money in than out |
| Deficit | More money out than in |
| Free trade | Trade without barriers |
| Trading bloc | Group of countries with special trade terms |
| Tariff | Tax on imports |
| Quota | Limit on the quantity of imports |
| Embargo | Ban on trade |

## Benefits to a country

Wider choice of goods; higher standard of living; lower cost of imports; more investment; creation of jobs.

## Challenges to exporters and importers

Distance; language; methods of payment; documentation; transport; customs duties; non-payment; currencies; foreign exchange rates; competition; different legal systems.

## Balance calculations

| Step | Rule |
|---|---|
| Balance of Trade | Exports of goods − imports of goods |
| Balance on services | Exports of services − imports of services |
| Balance of Payments | Add Balance of Trade, balance on services and other flows |
| Positive answer | Surplus |
| Negative answer | Deficit |

Example ($ million): goods exports 340, goods imports 395, service exports 210, service imports 150, income inflow 20. Balance of Trade = −55; services = +60; Balance of Payments = −55 + 60 + 20 = +25.

## Trading blocs

| Advantages | Disadvantages |
|---|---|
| Bigger market with no or lower tariffs | Must follow bloc rules |
| Cheaper imports from members | More competition for local firms |
| More foreign investment | Possible loss of trade outside the bloc |

## Trade restrictions

| Restriction | Meaning | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Tariff | Tax on imports | Higher prices, government revenue |
| Quota | Limit on quantity | Less supply, higher prices |
| Embargo | Ban | No trade |

Tariff sum: price $400, tariff 20% means 0.20 × 400 = $80, new price $480.

## Support for exporters

International trade fairs; cheaper bank loans; insurance guarantees.

## Worked example: exchange rate

Pay 12,000 foreign units. At 2.0 units per pound: 6,000 pounds. At 1.5 units per pound: 8,000 pounds. The pound has weakened, so imports cost more.

## Model answers to learn

**Explain one benefit of international trade to a country.** International trade gives consumers a wider choice of goods, because goods that cannot be made or grown at home can be imported. This raises the standard of living.

**Explain one effect of a tariff on a retailer.** A tariff raises the price the retailer pays for imported goods. The retailer must either raise selling prices, which may reduce sales, or accept a lower profit.

## Which balance do commercial activities affect?

| Activity | Balance of Trade | Balance of Payments |
|---|---|---|
| Factory wins export orders | Improves | Improves |
| Hotels attract foreign tourists | No effect (service) | Improves |
| Shop imports more foreign goods | Worsens | Worsens |
| Bank sells services abroad | No effect (service) | Improves |

## Exam traps

- **BoT includes services.** It does not.
- **Subtraction reversed.**
- **Tariff and quota confused.**
- **No interpretation** of surplus or deficit.
- **Challenges listed, not explained.**
- **Embargo as a tax.** It is a ban.

## Self-test

1. Define an import.
2. Give three benefits of international trade to a country.
3. Give three challenges for an exporter.
4. How is the Balance of Trade calculated?
5. Exports of goods $60m, imports $75m. What is the Balance of Trade?
6. What is invisible trade?
7. What is a tariff?
8. What is the difference between a quota and an embargo?
9. Give two advantages of joining a trading bloc.
10. Name two sources of support for exporters.

**Answers**

1. A good or service bought from another country.
2. Any three: wider choice, higher standard of living, lower cost of imports, more investment, job creation.
3. Any three: distance, language, payment methods, documentation, transport, customs duties, non-payment, currencies, exchange rates, competition, different legal systems.
4. Exports of goods minus imports of goods.
5. 60 − 75 = −$15m, a deficit.
6. Trade in services such as tourism, banking and insurance.
7. A tax on imported goods.
8. A quota limits the quantity of imports; an embargo bans trade altogether.
9. Any two: bigger market, cheaper imports, more investment, easier trade.
10. Any two: international trade fairs, cheaper bank loans, insurance guarantees.
