These questions cover Cambridge IGCSE ICT 0417 Topic 14: creating, editing and applying styles (font, alignment, enhancement, spacing and bullets) and the purpose and uses of a corporate house style. Styles are tested practically in Papers 2 and 3, so the page includes practical-style tasks with an examiner-style checklist as well as theory questions.

## Section B: structured questions

**5.** A report contains 40 headings, each formatted by hand.

**(a)** Explain two advantages of using a heading style instead. **[2]**
**(b)** The manager decides all headings should be green rather than blue. Describe how a style makes this change quick. **[2]**

**6.** A style called *Body* must be 11 point, serif, fully justified, with 6 points of spacing after each paragraph and single line spacing.

**(a)** Identify which of these settings are *spacing* settings. **[2]**
**(b)** Explain the difference between spacing after a paragraph and line spacing. **[2]**

**7.** A bank uses a corporate house style for its letters, leaflets and website.

**(a)** Describe two features that the house style is likely to specify. **[2]**
**(b)** Explain two benefits to the bank of using a house style. **[4]**

**8.** A student formats one subheading by hand as 14 point, bold, underlined and left aligned. She wants every other subheading to match.

**(a)** Describe how she can update the *Subheading* style to match this example. **[2]**
**(b)** Give two text enhancements and two text alignments named in the 0417 syllabus. **[2]**
**(c)** Explain why a sans-serif font is often chosen for headings in a presentation shown on screen. **[1]**

## Section C: practical-style tasks

Use a word processor with any three paragraphs of text and a short list. Take screenshots of each style's settings as evidence.

**9.** Create a style called *MB-Title*: sans-serif font, 20 point, bold, centred, dark red. Apply it to the first line. **[5]**

**10.** Create a style called *MB-Bullet*: square bullets, indented 1.5 cm, left aligned, 1.5 line spacing. Apply it to the list. **[4]**

**11.** Modify *MB-Title* so that it is also italic, and show that the change has been applied to the title. **[2]**

## Worked answers

**1.** Serif: for example Times New Roman, Georgia, Garamond (1). Sans-serif: for example Arial, Calibri, Helvetica, Verdana (1). **[2]**

**2.** Text that is aligned to both the left and the right margins (spaces between words are adjusted). **[1]**

**3.** Any two of: bullet shape; alignment; line spacing; indent. **[2]**

**4.** A set of rules for the appearance of all of an organisation's documents, such as fonts, colours, logo and layout, so they look consistent. **[1]**

**5. (a)** Any two: all headings are formatted consistently (1); formatting is applied in one step, saving time (1); fewer errors because no heading is missed or formatted differently (1); headings can be used for navigation or a contents list (1). **[2]**
**(b)** Modify the heading style's colour once (1); every heading that uses the style changes to green automatically, so 40 headings do not have to be changed by hand (1). **[2]**

**6. (a)** 6 points of spacing after each paragraph (1); single line spacing (1). **[2]**
**(b)** Spacing after a paragraph is the gap left below the paragraph before the next one begins (1); line spacing is the space between the lines inside the paragraph (1). **[2]**

**7. (a)** Any two: the fonts and sizes to use; the colour scheme; position and size of the logo; layout/margins; styles for headings and body text. **[2]**
**(b)** Two benefits explained, 2 marks each:
- Customers immediately recognise the bank's documents (1), which builds a consistent brand image and trust (1).
- Staff can use templates and styles that already exist (1), saving time and making documents from different departments look the same (1).
- Documents look professional (1), which gives a good impression of the bank (1). **[4]**

**8. (a)** Select the formatted subheading (1) and choose to update the *Subheading* style to match the selection, so every paragraph using that style takes the new formatting (1). **[2]**
**(b)** Enhancements: any two of bold, underline, italic (1). Alignments: any two of left, right, centre, fully justified (1). **[2]**
**(c)** Sans-serif letters have no small strokes, so they are often clearer and easier to read on a screen or from a distance (1). **[1]**

**9.** 1 mark each:
- Style named *MB-Title* exactly.
- Sans-serif font.
- 20 point and bold.
- Centred.
- Dark red, and the style is applied to the first line. **[5]**

**10.** 1 mark each:
- Square bullet shape.
- Indent 1.5 cm.
- Left aligned with 1.5 line spacing.
- Style applied to every item in the list (no items formatted by hand). **[4]**

**11.** The *MB-Title* style definition is changed to include italic (1); the title updates automatically to bold italic without being reformatted by hand (1). **[2]**

## Frequently asked questions

**Does the examiner check the style or just the appearance?**
Tasks often ask for evidence of the style settings, so take a screenshot of the style definition as well as the formatted text. Text that only looks right may not show that a style was created and applied.

**What is the quickest way to change every heading at once?**
Modify the heading style. Every paragraph that uses that style updates automatically.
