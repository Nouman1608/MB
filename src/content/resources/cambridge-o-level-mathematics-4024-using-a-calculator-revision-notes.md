Condensed notes for subtopic **1.14 Using a calculator** of Cambridge O Level Mathematics (Syllabus D) 4024, 2025 to 2027. The [Using a calculator study guide](/resources/cambridge-o-level-mathematics-4024-using-a-calculator/) explains each skill with worked examples; the [Using a calculator practice questions](/resources/cambridge-o-level-mathematics-4024-using-a-calculator-practice/) give exam-style questions with mark schemes.

## Syllabus outcomes

1. Use a calculator efficiently (do not round within a calculation; round the final answer only).
2. Enter values appropriately (for example, 2 hours 30 minutes as 2.5 hours or 2° 30′ 0″).
3. Interpret the calculator display appropriately (4.8 in money is $4.80; 3.25 in time is 3 hours 15 minutes).

This subtopic is assessed on Paper 2 only. Paper 1 is a non-calculator paper.

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Scientific calculator | calculator required for Paper 2 (no algebraic or graphical calculators) |
| Display | the number shown after a calculation |
| Premature rounding | rounding a value before the end of a calculation |
| Memory / answer key | stores a full value to reuse |
| Significant figures | the accuracy required for non-exact answers (3 s.f.) |
| DMS | degrees, minutes and seconds |

## Paper 2 accuracy rules

| Situation | Rule |
|---|---|
| Non-exact numerical answer | 3 significant figures |
| Angle in degrees | 1 decimal place |
| Value used in a later part | use the unrounded value |
| Value of π | calculator π or 3.142 |
| Money | nearest cent (2 d.p.) |
| Accuracy marks | lost if rounding is done too early |

## Keying in: brackets and order

| Calculation | Key in | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 12.8 ÷ (3.6 - 1.15) | 12.8 ÷ (3.6 - 1.15) | 5.2244... |
| 12.8 ÷ 3.6 - 1.15 | as written | 2.4055... |
| 85.6 ÷ (1.2 + 0.35) | brackets on the denominator | 55.2258... |
| √(6.4² + 3.7²) | brackets under the root | 7.3925... |
| 5 + 4 × 3² ÷ 2 | as written | 23 |
| 1500 × 1.04⁶ | power key | 1897.9785... |

A fraction bar means brackets round the top and bottom.

## Negative numbers, powers and roots

| Expression | Value | Note |
|---|---|---|
| (-3.2)² | 10.24 | brackets round the negative |
| -3.2² | -10.24 | square applies to 3.2 only |
| 7.2^(1/3) | 1.93... | brackets on the fractional index |
| 9.5³ | 857.375 | power key or x³ |

Use the negative (-) key for a negative sign and the subtraction key for taking away.

## Entering time

| Time | Decimal hours | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| 2 h 30 min | 2.5 | 30 ÷ 60 |
| 1 h 45 min | 1.75 | 45 ÷ 60 |
| 2 h 15 min | 2.25 | 15 ÷ 60 |
| 3 h 20 min | 3.333... | 20 ÷ 60 |
| 45 min | 0.75 | 45 ÷ 60 |

Never type 2 h 30 min as 2.30.

| Display (hours) | Convert | Time |

| 0.7 | 0.7 × 60 = 42 | 42 min |
| 1.2 | 0.2 × 60 = 12 | 1 h 12 min |

## Angles: degrees, minutes, seconds

| Conversion | Working |
|---|---|
| 2° 30′ 0″ → decimal | 2 + 30/60 = 2.5° |
| 37.65° → DMS | 0.65 × 60 = 39′, so 37° 39′ |
| 12° 15′ → decimal | 12 + 15/60 = 12.25° |

## Early rounding: example

```
2.34 x 5.67 x 1.5 = 19.9017 → 19.9 (3 s.f.)
round 2.34 x 5.67 = 13.2678 to 13.3 first: 13.3 x 1.5 = 19.95 → 20.0
```

The answers differ, so the early rounding is wrong.

|---|---|

| 2 × 3.142 × 6.3 | 39.589... = 39.6 (3 s.f.) |

## Exam traps

- Do not round until the end; keep the unrounded value in your working.
- Put brackets round every denominator and fractional power.
- 2 h 30 min is 2.5 hours.
- 3.25 hours is 3 h 15 min.
- A money display of 4.8 is $4.80.
- Write standard form properly: not 1.47E4.
- Check the answer against an estimate.
- Use a scientific calculator only; graphical and algebraic calculators are not allowed.

## Self-test

1. Work out 64.8 ÷ (2.7 + 1.35) to 3 s.f.
2. Convert 3 hours 40 minutes to hours as a decimal (3 s.f.).
3. A calculator shows 5.6 for an amount of money in dollars. Write it correctly.
4. A time display shows 2.4 hours. Write it in hours and minutes.
5. Write 6.5 × 10⁻⁴ as an ordinary number.
6. Work out (-4.5)².
7. Convert 18° 45′ to degrees as a decimal.
8. Work out √(9.8² - 4.2²) to 3 s.f.

**Answers**

1. 64.8 ÷ 4.05 = **16**.
2. 3 + 40/60 = **3.67 hours**.
3. **$5.60**.
4. 0.4 × 60 = 24, so **2 hours 24 minutes**.
5. **0.00065**.
6. **20.25**.
7. 18 + 45/60 = **18.75°**.
8. √(96.04 - 17.64) = √78.4 = 8.854..., so **8.85**.
