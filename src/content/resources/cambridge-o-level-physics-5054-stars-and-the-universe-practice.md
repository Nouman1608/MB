Take the speed of light in a vacuum as 3.0 × 10⁸ m/s and one year as 3.15 × 10⁷ s.

---

## Section A

**1.** State **three** facts about the Sun as a star, covering its size, its composition and the radiation it emits. **[3]**

**2.** Name the process that releases energy in a stable star and state what it converts into what. **[2]**

**3.** Define a light-year. **[2]**

**4.** State the approximate diameter of the Milky Way, in light-years. **[1]**

**5.** A student writes: "The Milky Way is the Universe." Correct the statement. **[2]**

**6.** Define redshift. **[2]**

## Section B

**7.** **(a)** Show that one light-year is about 9.5 × 10¹⁵ m. **[2]**
**(b)** A star is 8.6 light-years from the Earth. Calculate its distance in metres. **[1]**
**(c)** The Sun is 1.5 × 10¹¹ m from the Earth. Use your answer to (b) to explain why the syllabus describes other stars in the Milky Way as "much further away" than the Sun. **[2]**

**8.** A star forms from an interstellar cloud.

**(a)** State **two** things that the cloud contains. **[2]**
**(b)** Explain why the cloud collapses and why its temperature rises. **[2]**
**(c)** Explain why a stable star stays the same size for a long time. **[3]**

**9.** Describe what happens to a star of similar mass to the Sun after most of the hydrogen in its centre has been converted to helium. **[3]**

**10.** A star much more massive than the Sun reaches the end of its stable stage.

**(a)** Name the type of star it expands to form. **[1]**
**(b)** Describe what happens to it next, and what is left at its centre. **[3]**
**(c)** Explain why planets that form from its remains can contain heavier elements than the original interstellar cloud. **[2]**

**11.** In a laboratory, a line in the spectrum of hydrogen has a wavelength of 434 nm. The same line is observed at 441 nm in the light from galaxy A and at 470 nm in the light from galaxy B.

**(a)** State what the observations show about the motion of both galaxies. **[1]**
**(b)** Compare the two galaxies, giving **two** conclusions about B relative to A. **[3]**

**12.** Describe, qualitatively, how redshift provides evidence for the Big Bang theory. **[4]**

**13.** Light from a distant galaxy takes 2.5 million years to reach the Earth. State its distance in light-years and calculate it in metres. **[2]**

---

## Answers

**1.** It is a medium-sized star [1]; it consists mostly of hydrogen and helium [1]; it radiates most of its energy in the infrared, visible and ultraviolet regions of the electromagnetic spectrum [1].

**2.** Nuclear fusion [1]; hydrogen is fused into helium [1].

**3.** The distance [1] travelled in a vacuum by light in one year [1].

**4.** About 100 000 light-years [1].

**5.** The Milky Way is one galaxy (the one containing the Sun) [1]; it is one of many billions of galaxies that make up the Universe [1].

**6.** An increase in the observed wavelength of electromagnetic radiation [1] emitted from receding (moving away) stars and galaxies [1].

**7. (a)** distance = speed × time [1] = 3.0 × 10⁸ × 3.15 × 10⁷ = 9.45 × 10¹⁵ m ≈ 9.5 × 10¹⁵ m [1].
**(b)** 8.6 × 9.5 × 10¹⁵ = **8.2 × 10¹⁶ m** [1].
**(c)** 8.2 × 10¹⁶ ÷ 1.5 × 10¹¹ ≈ 5.5 × 10⁵ [1], so the star is over half a million times further away than the Sun, which is why it is described as much further away [1].

**8. (a)** Gas (which contains hydrogen; accept hydrogen) [1]; dust [1].
**(b)** Its own internal gravitational attraction pulls the material inwards, so it collapses [1]; as it collapses its temperature increases, forming a protostar [1].
**(c)** The inward force of gravitational attraction [1] is balanced by an outward force due to the high temperature in the centre [1]; with balanced forces the star neither collapses nor expands, and fusion of hydrogen into helium keeps the centre hot [1].

**9.** It expands to form a red giant [1]; the red giant forms a planetary nebula [1] with a white dwarf at its centre [1].

**10. (a)** A red supergiant [1].
**(b)** It explodes as a supernova [1], forming a nebula containing hydrogen and new heavier elements [1], leaving a neutron star or a black hole at the centre [1].
**(c)** The supernova nebula contains new heavier elements made by the star [1]; new stars with orbiting planets may form from this nebula, so the planets include those heavier elements [1].

**11. (a)** Both wavelengths are longer than 434 nm, so both galaxies show redshift and are moving away from the Earth (receding) [1].
**(b)** The increase for B (36 nm) is greater than for A (7 nm), so B has the greater redshift [1]; B is therefore moving away from the Earth faster than A [1]; and B is further away than A [1].

**12.** Light from distant galaxies is redshifted, so the galaxies are moving away from the Earth [1]. The further away the galaxy, the greater its redshift and the faster it recedes [1]. This shows that the Universe is expanding [1]. Tracing the expansion back in time suggests that everything started together in a very small region, as the Big Bang theory proposes [1].

**13.** 2.5 million (2.5 × 10⁶) light-years [1]; 2.5 × 10⁶ × 9.5 × 10¹⁵ = **2.4 × 10²² m** [1].

## Where marks are usually lost

- Writing that a light-year is "the time light takes to travel".
- Giving "red giant" for a massive star, or "white dwarf" after a supernova.
- Leaving "new heavier elements" out of the supernova nebula.
- Defining redshift without the word "receding" or without "observed wavelength".
- Concluding "the Big Bang" from redshift in one step, without "the Universe is expanding".
- Saying the Sun is powered by fission or by burning.
