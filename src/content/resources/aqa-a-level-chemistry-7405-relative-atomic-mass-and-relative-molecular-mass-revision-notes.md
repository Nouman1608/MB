## What you must do

- **Define Aᵣ** in terms of carbon-12.
- **Define Mᵣ** in terms of carbon-12.
- Use **relative formula mass** for ionic compounds.

## Key terms

| Term | Definition or meaning |
|---|---|
| Relative atomic mass, Aᵣ | Average mass of an atom of an element compared with 1/12 of the mass of an atom of carbon-12 |
| Relative molecular mass, Mᵣ | Average mass of a molecule compared with 1/12 of the mass of an atom of carbon-12 |
| Relative formula mass | The same quantity for an ionic compound, found from its formula |
| Relative isotopic mass | Mass of an atom of one isotope compared with 1/12 of the mass of an atom of carbon-12 |
| Carbon-12 standard | Carbon-12 atom given a mass of exactly 12, so 1/12 of it is exactly 1 |
| Water of crystallisation | Water bonded within the crystal of a hydrated salt, written after a dot |

## The definitions as ratios

```
Aᵣ = average mass of 1 atom of the element ÷ (1/12 × mass of 1 atom of ¹²C)
Mᵣ = average mass of 1 molecule            ÷ (1/12 × mass of 1 atom of ¹²C)
```

Three must-have parts: **average**, **the particle**, **1/12 of the mass of an atom of carbon-12**. A ratio of two masses has **no units**.

## Which term?

| If the substance is... | Say... | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Made of simple molecules | Relative molecular mass | O₂, NH₃, C₂H₅OH |
| Ionic | Relative formula mass | KBr, Na₂CO₃, Fe₂O₃ |
| A hydrated ionic salt | Relative formula mass | Na₂CO₃·10H₂O |

Same arithmetic; only the name differs.

## Formula masses in steps

1. Expand brackets: the outside subscript multiplies **every** atom inside.
2. A hydrate's ·xH₂O is **added**, x lots of 18.0.
3. Multiply each count by Aᵣ and add.
4. Use the Periodic Table values to one decimal place.

| Formula | Working | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Ca(NO₃)₂ | 40.1 + 2 × (14.0 + 3 × 16.0) | 164.1 |
| (NH₄)₂SO₄ | 2 × (14.0 + 4 × 1.0) + 32.1 + 4 × 16.0 | 132.1 |
| Fe₂O₃ | 2 × 55.8 + 3 × 16.0 | 159.6 |
| Ca₃(PO₄)₂ | 3 × 40.1 + 2 × (31.0 + 4 × 16.0) | 310.3 |
| Na₂CO₃·10H₂O | (2 × 23.0 + 12.0 + 3 × 16.0) + 10 × 18.0 | 286.0 |

## Percentage by mass

```
% of element = (number of atoms × Aᵣ) ÷ formula mass × 100
```

- % N in (NH₄)₂SO₄ = 28.0 ÷ 132.1 × 100 = **21.2%**
- % Fe in Fe₂O₃ = 111.6 ÷ 159.6 × 100 = **69.9%**
- % water in Na₂CO₃·10H₂O = 180.0 ÷ 286.0 × 100 = **62.9%**

## Reverse problems

- **Unknown element:** subtract the known part, then match the Aᵣ to the Periodic Table. Example: XBr₂ has a formula mass of 199.9; 199.9 − 2 × 79.9 = 40.1, so X is calcium.
- **Unknown x in a hydrate:** (formula mass − anhydrous part) ÷ 18.0.
- **Unknown subscript:** NaₓPO₄ with formula mass 164.0: (164.0 − 95.0) ÷ 23.0 = 3, so Na₃PO₄.

## Must-know distinctions

- **Aᵣ vs relative isotopic mass:** Aᵣ is a weighted average across isotopes; relative isotopic mass is for one isotope.
- **Aᵣ vs mass number:** mass number is a count of protons and neutrons in one atom; Aᵣ is a measured average and rarely whole.
- **Mᵣ vs molar mass:** same number; Mᵣ has no units, molar mass is in g mol⁻¹.

## Exam traps

- "Mass of an atom compared with carbon": missing *average*, *1/12* and *carbon-12*.
- Units on Aᵣ or Mᵣ.
- "Relative molecular mass of magnesium oxide": it is ionic, so relative formula mass.
- Al₂(SO₄)₃ with 4 oxygens instead of 12.
- CuSO₄·5H₂O worked out as 159.6 × 90.0.
- Using Cl = 35 or Cu = 64 instead of the values supplied.
- Answers given to more decimal places than the data.

## Self-test

1. Define relative atomic mass.
2. Why does relative molecular mass have no units?
3. State the correct term for the relative mass of potassium bromide, and why.
4. Calculate the Mᵣ of glucose, C₆H₁₂O₆.
5. Calculate the relative formula mass of Mg(OH)₂.
6. Calculate the relative formula mass of Ba(OH)₂·8H₂O (Ba = 137.3).
7. Calculate the percentage by mass of nitrogen in urea, CO(NH₂)₂.
8. MCl₂ has a relative formula mass of 95.3. Identify M.
9. FeSO₄·xH₂O has a relative formula mass of 277.9. Find x.
10. Explain why the Aᵣ of copper is 63.5 although no copper atom has that mass.
11. What is the difference between Aᵣ and relative isotopic mass?
12. Calculate the relative formula mass of KAl(SO₄)₂·12H₂O.

### Answers

1. The average mass of an atom of an element compared with one twelfth of the mass of an atom of carbon-12.
2. It is a ratio of two masses, so the units cancel.
3. Relative formula mass, because KBr is ionic and has no molecules.
4. 6 × 12.0 + 12 × 1.0 + 6 × 16.0 = **180.0**.
5. 24.3 + 2 × 17.0 = **58.3**.
6. 137.3 + 34.0 + 144.0 = **315.3**.
7. 28.0 ÷ 60.0 × 100 = **46.7%**.
8. 95.3 − 71.0 = 24.3: **magnesium**.
9. FeSO₄ = 55.8 + 32.1 + 64.0 = 151.9; (277.9 − 151.9) ÷ 18.0 = **7**.
10. Copper is a mixture of isotopes (about 63 and 65); Aᵣ is their weighted mean.
11. Aᵣ is an average over all isotopes in a natural sample; relative isotopic mass is the mass of one isotope.
12. 39.1 + 27.0 + 2 × 96.1 + 12 × 18.0 = **474.3**.
