# Responding to questions, general criteria and moderation

The endorsement has no marks and is reported separately (Not Classified, Pass, Merit or Distinction); it does not change your written grade. Check your centre's exact arrangements with your teacher.

Companion to the reviewed [Spoken Language Endorsement revision notes](/resources/edexcel-igcse-english-language-spoken-language-endorsement-revision-notes/) and [practice questions](/resources/edexcel-igcse-english-language-spoken-language-endorsement-practice/).

## Listening and responding (AO6 strand)

AO6 includes: listen and respond appropriately to spoken language, including to questions and feedback to presentations.

| Grade | Responding |
|---|---|
| Pass | Responds to questions or feedback appropriately in a straightforward manner |
| Merit | Responds formally and in some detail |
| Distinction | Listens and responds perceptively and, if appropriate, elaborates with further ideas and information |

## General criteria for any grade

You must be audible and use spoken Standard English, meaning being intelligible and generally using language appropriate to the formal setting.

## Moderation facts

- Centres record a sample of presentations for each series, not every student.
- 30 or fewer students: all recorded. More than 30: a sample of 30, including at least 10 at each grade (Distinction, Merit, Pass) where available. Not Classified students are not included.
- A record sheet (Appendix 3 of the specification) is completed for every student.
- All work is submitted digitally.

## Answer pattern for questions

Listen to the whole question; pause; answer directly; give a reason or example; for Distinction, add a further idea.

## Traps

- One-word answers.
- Reading from notes in the answers.
- Forgetting to be audible.

## Self-test

1. Name the two general criteria. 2. What does Distinction add to Merit in responding? 3. How many recordings for a centre of 50 students?

**Answers:** 1. Audible; spoken Standard English. 2. Perceptive listening and elaboration with further ideas. 3. A sample of 30, with at least 10 at each grade where available.
