## Questions

### Section A: short questions

**1.** Explain why the model of the plasma membrane is described as a fluid mosaic. **[2]**

**2.** State the effect of cholesterol on the molecules in a plasma membrane. **[1]**

**3.** Which statement about active transport is correct?

A: It moves substances down a concentration gradient.

B: It needs no carrier proteins.

C: It can move substances against a concentration gradient.

D: It is the same as facilitated diffusion. **[1]**

**4.** A cell has a water potential of -500 kPa. It is placed in a solution with a water potential of -300 kPa. State the direction of net water movement and name the process. **[2]**

**5.** Explain why glucose needs a membrane protein to enter a cell, whereas oxygen does not. **[2]**

### Section B: longer questions

**6.** A student placed cylinders of potato in sucrose solutions of different concentrations. The cylinders were blotted and weighed before and after 40 minutes.

| Sucrose / mol dm-3 | Initial mass / g | Final mass / g |
|---|---|---|
| 0.1 | 3.20 | 3.52 |
| 0.3 | 3.10 | 3.19 |
| 0.5 | 3.00 | 2.91 |
| 0.7 | 3.30 | 3.05 |
| 0.9 | 3.10 | 2.77 |

**(a)** Calculate the percentage change in mass of the cylinder at each concentration. **[3]**

**(b)** Use your values to estimate the sucrose concentration that has the same water potential as the potato tissue. **[2]**

**(c)** Explain why percentage change in mass was calculated rather than the change in mass. **[1]**

**(d)** Explain the change in mass of the cylinder in 0.9 mol dm-3 sucrose. **[3]**

**7.** The cell-surface membrane of an epithelial cell in the small intestine is covered with microvilli. A section of the cell has a flat surface of 10 µm x 10 µm. The surface has 300 microvilli, each a cylinder 0.1 µm in diameter and 0.8 µm long. Ignore the area of the tips.

**(a)** Calculate the total surface area of the section, including the microvilli. **[3]**

**(b)** Explain how microvilli increase the rate of absorption. **[2]**

**8.** Two exchange surfaces are compared. Surface P has an area of 15 units, a concentration difference of 8 units and a thickness of 3 units. Surface Q has an area of 15 units, a concentration difference of 4 units and a thickness of 1.5 units. Rate is proportional to (area x concentration difference) / thickness. Compare the rates of diffusion across the two surfaces. **[3]**

**9.** The uptake of nitrate ions by root hair cells was measured in aerated water and in water to which a respiratory inhibitor had been added. Uptake was much lower with the inhibitor. Explain this result. **[4]**

**10.** Compare facilitated diffusion with active transport. **[4]**

**11.** Explain why a plant cell does not burst when placed in pure water, but an animal cell may. **[3]**

## Answers

**1.** Mosaic: proteins are scattered among the phospholipids like tiles in a mosaic [1]. Fluid: the phospholipids and many proteins can move sideways within the layer [1]. **[2]**

**2.** It restricts the movement of the other molecules in the membrane [1]. **[1]**

**3.** **C** [1]. **[1]**

**4.** Water moves into the cell [1] because the solution has a higher water potential than the cell; the process is osmosis [1]. **[2]**
*Examiner insight:* -300 kPa is higher (less negative) than -500 kPa. Check the signs before you decide on direction.

**5.** Glucose is a polar molecule and cannot pass through the hydrophobic centre of the phospholipid bilayer [1], so it needs a carrier or channel protein [1]. **[2]**
*Examiner insight:* Oxygen is small and non-polar, so it dissolves in the bilayer and needs no protein.

**6. (a)** Percentage change = (final - initial) / initial x 100 [1]. At 0.1: +10.0 per cent; 0.3: +2.9; 0.5: -3.0; 0.7: -7.6; 0.9: -10.6 [2 for all five correct; 1 for three or four correct]. **[3]**

**(b)** The percentage change is zero between 0.3 and 0.5 [1]. Interpolating gives 0.3 + 0.2 x (2.9 / 5.9) = 0.40 mol dm-3 (accept 0.38 to 0.42) [1]. **[2]**

**(c)** The starting masses were different, so the percentage change allows a fair comparison [1]. **[1]**

**(d)** The mass fell by 10.6 per cent [1]. The solution had a lower water potential than the tissue, so water left the cells by osmosis [1] through the partially permeable membrane [1]. **[3]**

**7. (a)** Side area of one microvillus = pi x 0.1 x 0.8 = 0.251 µm2 [1]. Added area = 300 x 0.251 = 75.4 µm2 [1]. Total = 100 + 75.4 = 175 µm2 [1]. **[3]**
*Examiner insight:* The area is 1.75 times the flat area. If the question asks for how many times larger, divide your total by 100.

**(b)** The larger surface area of membrane holds more carrier and channel proteins [1], so more molecules are absorbed each second [1]. **[2]**

**8.** P: 15 x 8 / 3 = 40 [1]. Q: 15 x 4 / 1.5 = 40 [1]. The rates are the same, because the lower concentration difference at Q is compensated by the thinner surface [1]. **[3]**

**9.** The inhibitor stops respiration, so less ATP is made [1]. Active transport needs ATP [1]. Nitrate ions are moved against a concentration gradient by carrier proteins in the membrane [1]. With less ATP, the carriers cannot move the ions, so uptake falls [1]. **[4]**

**10.** Any four from: both use membrane proteins [1]; both are specific to certain substances [1]; both can reach a maximum rate when all proteins are in use [1]; facilitated diffusion moves substances down a concentration gradient whereas active transport moves substances against it [1]; active transport needs ATP whereas facilitated diffusion does not [1]; facilitated diffusion uses channels or carriers whereas active transport uses only carrier proteins [1]. **[4]**

**11.** Water enters both cells by osmosis because the pure water has a higher water potential [1]. The plant cell has a cellulose cell wall that resists expansion, so the cell becomes turgid and stops taking in water [1]. The animal cell has no cell wall, so it may keep taking in water until the membrane bursts [1]. **[3]**

## Where marks are usually lost

- Getting the direction of osmosis wrong by misreading negative values.
- Leaving out the unit in a surface area answer.
- Describing active transport without naming ATP and carrier proteins.
- Giving a comparison where only one process is described.
- Using raw mass change instead of percentage change.
