## Questions

**1.** A crate is pulled along horizontal ground at a constant speed of 1.2 m s⁻¹ by a rope. The tension in the rope is 50 N and the rope makes an angle of θ° with the horizontal. The power of the tension is 54 W.
**(a)** Find θ.
**(b)** Find the work done by the tension in 30 seconds. **[4]**

**2.** Particles P and Q, of masses 0.4 kg and 0.6 kg, move towards each other along a straight line on a smooth horizontal surface. P moves at 5 m s⁻¹ and Q moves at 2 m s⁻¹. After they collide, P is at rest. Find the speed of Q after the collision and the kinetic energy lost in the collision. **[4]**

**3.** A train starts from rest at station P. It accelerates uniformly for 40 s until its speed is 16 m s⁻¹, travels at this constant speed, and then decelerates uniformly at 0.5 m s⁻² until it stops at station Q. The whole journey takes 200 s. Find the distance PQ. **[4]**

**4.** Particle A, of mass 2 kg, is on a rough horizontal table. It is attached by a light inextensible string, which passes over a small smooth pulley at the edge of the table, to particle B, of mass 3 kg, which hangs freely. The system is released from rest and B accelerates downwards at 4 m s⁻². Find the tension in the string and the coefficient of friction between A and the table. **[4]**

**5.** A particle P moves in a straight line. Its velocity t seconds after leaving a point O is `v = 6t − t² + 7` m s⁻¹, for 0 ≤ t ≤ 5.
**(a)** Find the maximum velocity of P.
**(b)** Find the distance of P from O when it reaches this maximum velocity. **[4]**

**6.** A particle of mass 5 kg rests on a rough plane inclined at 30° to the horizontal. The coefficient of friction is 0.2. A force P N acting up the plane, parallel to a line of greatest slope, keeps the particle from sliding down. Find the least possible value of P. **[4]**

**7.** A particle of mass 4 kg slides down a line of greatest slope of a rough plane inclined at an angle α to the horizontal, where sin α = 0.28 and cos α = 0.96. It passes a point A with speed 2 m s⁻¹ and a point B, 10 m further down the plane, with speed 6 m s⁻¹. Using an energy method:
**(a)** find the work done against friction as the particle moves from A to B
**(b)** find the coefficient of friction between the particle and the plane. **[6]**

## Answers

**1.** **(a)** Power = (component of the tension along the ground) × speed, so 50 cos θ × 1.2 = 54 [1]. cos θ = 54 ÷ 60 = 0.9, so **θ = 25.8** [1].
**(b)** In 30 s the crate moves 1.2 × 30 = 36 m [1]. Work done = 50 × 0.9 × 36 = **1620 J** (the same as 54 W × 30 s) [1].

*Tip:* only the component of the force in the direction of motion does work, and the power is that component multiplied by the speed.

*Try the real question next:* Cambridge International AS & A Level Mathematics 9709, June 2025, Paper 42, Question 1.

**2.** Take P's direction of motion as positive. Momentum: 0.4 × 5 + 0.6 × (−2) = 0.4 × 0 + 0.6v [1], so 0.8 = 0.6v and Q moves at **4/3 m s⁻¹ (1.33 m s⁻¹)** in P's original direction [1]. KE before = ½(0.4)(25) + ½(0.6)(4) = 6.2 J; KE after = ½(0.6)(4/3)² = 0.533 J [1]. KE lost = **5.67 J** (exactly 17/3 J) [1].

*Tip:* decide one positive direction first. Here the particles move towards each other, so Q's velocity before the collision is negative.

*Try the real question next:* Cambridge International AS & A Level Mathematics 9709, June 2025, Paper 42, Question 2.

**3.** Time decelerating = 16 ÷ 0.5 = 32 s [1]. Distance accelerating = ½ × 40 × 16 = 320 m and distance decelerating = ½ × 32 × 16 = 256 m [1]. The train travels at constant speed for 200 − 40 − 32 = 128 s, covering 128 × 16 = 2048 m [1]. PQ = **2624 m** [1].

*Tip:* sketch the velocity–time graph first; the distance is the area under it.

*Try the real question next:* Cambridge International AS & A Level Mathematics 9709, June 2025, Paper 42, Question 4.

**4.** For B: 30 − T = 3 × 4, so **T = 18 N** [2]. For A: 18 − F = 2 × 4, so F = 10 N [1]. R = 20 N, so μ = 10 ÷ 20 = **0.5** [1].

*Tip:* write Newton's second law for each particle separately, with the tension acting on both.

*Try the real question next:* Cambridge International AS & A Level Mathematics 9709, June 2025, Paper 42, Question 5.

**5.** **(a)** dv/dt = 6 − 2t = 0 when t = 3 [1]. v = 18 − 9 + 7 = **16 m s⁻¹** [1].
**(b)** s = ∫₀³ (6t − t² + 7) dt = [3t² − t³/3 + 7t]₀³ [1] = 27 − 9 + 21 = **39 m** [1].

*Tip:* for maximum velocity, differentiate v and set the acceleration to zero.

*Try the real question next:* Cambridge International AS & A Level Mathematics 9709, June 2025, Paper 42, Question 6.

**6.** R = 50 cos 30° = 43.3 N [1]. The particle is about to slide down, so friction acts up the plane: F = 0.2 × 43.3 = 8.66 N [1]. Resolving along the plane: P + 8.66 = 50 sin 30° = 25 [1], so **P = 16.3 N** [1].

*Tip:* friction always opposes the motion that would happen, so decide which way the particle would slide.

*Try the real question next:* Cambridge International AS & A Level Mathematics 9709, June 2025, Paper 42, Question 5(b).

**7.** **(a)** PE lost from A to B = 4 × 10 × 10 × 0.28 = 112 J [1]. KE gained = ½ × 4 × (6² − 2²) = 64 J [1]. Work done against friction = 112 − 64 = **48 J** [1].
**(b)** The friction force is constant, so F = 48 ÷ 10 = 4.8 N [1]. R = 4 × 10 × 0.96 = 38.4 N [1]. μ = F ÷ R = 4.8 ÷ 38.4 = **0.125** [1].

*Tip:* any potential energy lost that does not appear as kinetic energy has been used doing work against friction.

*Try the real question next:* Cambridge International AS & A Level Mathematics 9709, June 2025, Paper 42, Question 7.

## Where marks are usually lost

- The full force, rather than its component, used for work done.
- Velocities given the wrong sign in momentum questions.
- Friction drawn the wrong way on an inclined plane.
- Maximum velocity found by setting v = 0 instead of dv/dt = 0.

## Frequently asked questions

**What topics come up on Cambridge 9709 Paper 4 Mechanics?**
These practice questions cover work and power, momentum and collisions, kinematics with constant and variable acceleration, connected particles over a pulley, friction on an inclined plane, and energy methods.

**What value of g should you use in 9709 Mechanics?**
These questions take g = 10 m s⁻², which is the value used in Cambridge 9709 Mechanics questions unless a question states otherwise.

**How do you find the work done by a force at an angle?**
Only the component of the force in the direction of motion does work, so multiply that component (for example F cos θ) by the distance moved, not the full force.

**How do you find the maximum velocity when v is given as a function of t?**
Differentiate v to get the acceleration and set dv/dt = 0, then substitute that time back into v. Setting v = 0 instead is a common mistake.

**Which way does friction act on a particle on a slope?**
Friction opposes the motion that would happen, so first decide which way the particle would slide. If it is about to slide down, friction acts up the plane.
