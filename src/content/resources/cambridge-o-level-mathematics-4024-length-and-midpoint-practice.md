**(a)** Find the length of CD. **[3]**

## Answers

**1. (a)** differences 6 and 8 **[1]**; 36 + 64 = 100 **[1]**; AB = **10** **[1]**

**(b)** (1 + 7)/2 = 4 and (2 + 10)/2 = 6 **[1]**; **(4, 6)** **[1]** **[5]**

**2. (a)** differences 6 and -8 **[1]**; 36 + 64 = 100 **[1]**; CD = **10** **[1]**

**(b)** (-4 + 2)/2 = -1 and (3 + (-5))/2 = -1 **[1]**; **(-1, -1)** **[1]** **[5]**

*Examiner insight:* (-8)² is 64. Squaring removes the negative sign.

**3. (a)** differences 6 and 6; 36 + 36 = 72 **[1]**; √72 = √36 × √2 **[1]**; **6√2** **[1]**

**(b)** (-2 + 4)/2 = 1 and (-1 + 5)/2 = 2, so **(1, 2)** **[2]**

**(c)** **8.49** **[1]** **[6]**

**4.** (2 + (-6))/2 = -2 **[1]**; (-7 + 1)/2 = -3; **(-2, -3)** **[1]** **[2]**

**5.** (-1 + x)/2 = 3 so x = 7 **[1]**; (4 + y)/2 = 1 so y = -2; **B(7, -2)** **[1]** **[2]**

**6.** AC² = 2² + 4² = 20 **[1]**; BC² = (-2)² + 4² = 20 **[1]**; AC = BC = √20, so two sides are equal and the triangle is **isosceles** **[1]** **[3]**

**7.** PQ² = 3² + 4² = 25 **[1]**; QR² = 4² + 3² = 25 **[1]**; PR² = 7² + 1² = 50 **[1]**; PQ² + QR² = 50 = PR², so angle PQR is a **right angle** **[1]** **[4]**

*Examiner insight:* Compare squares, so there is no need to take square roots. The converse of Pythagoras finishes the argument.

**8.** centre = ((-2 + 6)/2, (3 + (-3))/2) = **(2, 0)** **[1]**; diameter² = 8² + 6² = 100, diameter = 10 **[1]**; radius = **5** **[1]** **[3]**

**9.** midpoint of AC = ((1 + 7)/2, (2 + 5)/2) = (4, 3.5) **[1]**; midpoint of BD = ((5 + 3)/2, (2 + 5)/2) = (4, 3.5) **[1]**; the midpoints are the same, so the diagonals bisect each other **[1]** **[3]**

**10.** (k - 1)² + 4² = 25 **[1]**; (k - 1)² = 9 **[1]**; **k = 4** or **k = -2** **[1]** **[3]**

**11.** (a + 5)/2 = 2, so **a = -1** **[1]**; (3 + b)/2 = 4, so **b = 5** **[1]**; both found correctly **[1]** **[3]**

**12.** sides along the axes: 6 and 8 **[1]**; hypotenuse √(36 + 64) = 10 **[1]**; perimeter = **24** **[1]** **[3]**

**13.** 3² + 3² = 18 **[1]**; √18 = **3√2** **[1]** **[2]**

**14.** P = (3, y), so 3² + y² = 25 **[1]**; y² = 16 **[1]**; **P = (3, 4)** or **(3, -4)** **[1]** **[3]**

## Where marks are usually lost

- Stopping at 100 instead of taking the square root in Questions 1 and 2.
- Writing (-8)² as -64 in Question 2.
- Subtracting the coordinates for the midpoint in Question 4.
- Dividing by 2 for only one coordinate in Questions 1(b) and 2(b).
- Giving only one value of k in Question 10 or one point in Question 14.
- Not stating a conclusion in the "show that" questions 6, 7 and 9.

## Frequently asked questions

**Does the order of the points matter in the length formula?**
No. The differences are squared, so (x₂ - x₁)² equals (x₁ - x₂)².

**Why is the midpoint the mean of the coordinates?**
Halfway between two numbers is their average. The midpoint is halfway across and halfway up, so you find the average of the x-values and the average of the y-values.
