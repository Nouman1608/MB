## Section A

**1.** **(a)** Write 5 400 seconds in hours and minutes. **[1]**

**(b)** Work out the number of seconds in 3 days. **[2]**

**2.** **(a)** Write 9.40 p.m. in the 24-hour clock. **[1]**

**(b)** Write 03 15 in the 12-hour clock. **[1]**

**3.** Work out the time from 09 35 to 22 50. **[2]**

**4.** A night train leaves at 21 40 and arrives at 03 15 the next day. How long does the journey take? **[2]**

**5.** A coach leaves at 08 45. The journey takes 3 hours 40 minutes. At what time does it arrive? **[2]**

**6.** A train leaves at 11 45 and arrives at 13 10. Work out the journey time. **[2]**

## Section B

**7.** A flight leaves at 22 40 local time and takes 9 hours 35 minutes. The destination is 5 hours ahead of the departure place. Work out the local time on arrival. Say which day it is. **[3]**

**8.** Work out the number of hours in a year of 365 days. **[2]**

**9.** A film starts at 7.45 p.m. and lasts 2 hours 18 minutes. Write the time that it ends, in the 12-hour clock. **[2]**

**10.** A job takes 3 hours 50 minutes. It is done on each of 4 days. Work out the total time. **[2]**

**11.** Write 2.4 hours in hours and minutes. **[1]**

**12.** Work out the number of seconds in 2 hours 15 minutes. **[2]**

**13.** A plane takes off at 23 50 on Monday and lands at 06 15 on Tuesday. There is no time difference between the two places. How long was the flight? **[2]**

**14.** City A is 3 hours behind City B. A meeting starts at 14 30 in City B. **(a)** What time is it in City A when the meeting starts? **[1]** **(b)** The meeting lasts 1 hour 45 minutes. What time is it in City A when the meeting ends? **[2]**

## Answers

**1. (a)** 5 400 ÷ 60 = 90 minutes = **1 hour 30 minutes** **[1]**

**(b)** 3 × 24 × 60 × 60 **[1]** = **259 200 seconds** **[1]** **[3]**

**2. (a)** **21 40** **[1]**

**(b)** **3.15 a.m.** **[1]** **[2]**

*Examiner insight:* For p.m. times add 12 to the hour: 9 + 12 = 21.

**3.** 09 35 to 10 00 is 25 minutes, 10 00 to 22 00 is 12 hours, 22 00 to 22 50 is 50 minutes **[1]**; **13 hours 15 minutes** **[1]** **[2]**

**4.** 21 40 to midnight is 2 h 20 min **[1]**; midnight to 03 15 is 3 h 15 min, total **5 h 35 min** **[1]** **[2]**

*Examiner insight:* Subtracting 21 40 from 03 15 directly gives a nonsense answer. Go through midnight.

**5.** 08 45 + 3 h = 11 45 **[1]**; + 40 min = **12 25** **[1]** **[2]**

**6.** 11 45 to 12 00 is 15 min, 12 00 to 13 00 is 1 h, 13 00 to 13 10 is 10 min **[1]**; **1 hour 25 minutes** **[1]** **[2]**

**7.** 22 40 + 9 h 35 min = 32 15, which is 08 15 the next day **[1]**; add 5 hours: 13 15 **[1]**; **13 15 local time the next day** **[1]** **[3]**

**8.** 365 × 24 **[1]** = **8 760 hours** **[1]** **[2]**

**9.** 7.45 p.m. + 2 h = 9.45 p.m. **[1]**; + 18 min = **10.03 p.m.** **[1]** **[2]**

**10.** 4 × 3 h 50 min = 12 h + 200 min **[1]**; 200 min = 3 h 20 min, so **15 h 20 min** **[1]** **[2]**

**11.** 0.4 × 60 = 24, so **2 hours 24 minutes** **[1]**

**12.** 2 h 15 min = 135 min **[1]**; 135 × 60 = **8 100 seconds** **[1]** **[2]**

**13.** 23 50 to midnight is 10 minutes **[1]**; midnight to 06 15 is 6 h 15 min, total **6 h 25 min** **[1]** **[2]**

**14. (a)** 14 30 - 3 h = **11 30** **[1]**

**(b)** The meeting ends at 14 30 + 1 h 45 min = 16 15 in City B **[1]**; in City A this is **13 15** **[1]** **[3]**

## Where marks are usually lost

- Using 100 minutes in an hour in Questions 3, 5 and 10.
- Subtracting directly across midnight in Question 4.
- Adding the time difference the wrong way in Question 7.
- Writing 9.40 p.m. as 09 40 in Question 2(a).
- Giving 2.4 hours as 2 hours 40 minutes in Question 11.
- Forgetting to state the next day in Question 7.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is 12.00 a.m. noon or midnight?**
Midnight, which is 00 00 in the 24-hour clock. Noon is 12.00 p.m., which is 12 00.

**How do I know whether to add or subtract a time difference?**
A place that is ahead has a later time on the clock, so add the difference. A place that is behind has an earlier time, so subtract it.
