**These notes condense content area 3.2.2.1 of 9237: "The significance of the Qur'an as the final revelation, including the way in which it was revealed, preserved, and compiled."** The full explanation is in the [study guide](/resources/oxfordaqa-igcse-islamiyat-9237-revelation-and-compilation-of-the-quran/).

## Three stages

1. **Revealed:** through Jibril (AS) to the Prophet (pbuh) over about 23 years.
2. **Preserved:** memorised, written and reviewed in his lifetime.
3. **Compiled:** collected under Abu Bakr (RA); copied to a standard text under Uthman (RA).

## Why the Qur'an is the final revelation

| Point | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Word of Allah in Arabic | Revealed through Jibril (AS) |
| Confirms earlier scripture | Qur'an 5:48 |
| For all people | "guidance for the people" (2:185) |
| No prophet after Muhammad (pbuh) | "seal of the prophets" (33:40) |
| Religion completed | Qur'an 5:3 |
| Protected by Allah | "We will be its guardian" (15:9) |

## Revelation

- **First revelation:** Cave of Hira, Ramadhan; Surah al-Alaq 96:1-5; remembered as Laylat al-Qadr.
- **Duration:** about 23 years, in Makkah and Madinah.
- **Why gradual:**
  - to strengthen the Prophet's (pbuh) heart (25:32)
  - to answer questions and events as they arose
  - to make it easy to memorise
  - to introduce laws step by step (for example alcohol)
- **Order:** the book is arranged as the Prophet (pbuh) directed, not in order of revelation.

## Preservation in his lifetime

| Method | Detail |
|---|---|
| Memory | Companions memorised; huffaz know it by heart |
| Writing | Scribes such as Zayd ibn Thabit (RA), Ali (RA), Uthman (RA) and Ubayy ibn Ka'b (RA) wrote on leather, bones, palm stalks and stones |
| Review | Yearly with Jibril (AS); twice in the last year |
| Use | Recited in daily prayers and taught in circles |

## First compilation: Abu Bakr (RA)

- **Cause:** many huffaz martyred at Yamama.
- **Idea from:** Umar (RA), who feared parts might be lost.
- **Compiler:** Zayd ibn Thabit (RA).
- **Method:** accepted a verse only if written in the Prophet's (pbuh) presence and confirmed by witnesses.
- **Keepers of the sheets:** Abu Bakr (RA), then Umar (RA), then Hafsa (RA).

## Standard copies: Uthman (RA)

- **Cause:** differences in recitation across newly conquered regions.
- **Warning from:** Hudhayfa ibn al-Yaman (RA).
- **Team:** Zayd ibn Thabit (RA) and three members of the Quraysh.
- **Rule:** if they differed, write in the dialect of the Quraysh.
- **Result:** copies sent to the main regions; other materials burned to prevent confusion; Hafsa's (RA) sheets returned.

## The prescribed passage: Sahih al-Bukhari 4987

| Part of the Hadith | Use it to show |
|---|---|
| Hudhayfa warns about differences "as the Jews and the Christians did before" | Fear that earlier scriptures were disputed; desire to protect the Qur'an |
| "Save this nation before they differ about the Book" | Unity of the Ummah depends on one text |
| Uthman (RA) borrows Hafsa's (RA) sheets | Continuity with Abu Bakr's (RA) compilation |
| Writing in the Quraysh dialect | The Qur'an was revealed in that dialect |
| Other copies burned | The need to remove causes of confusion |
| A copy sent to every region | Standard text for all Muslims |

## Compare the two compilations

| | Abu Bakr (RA) | Uthman (RA) |
|---|---|---|
| Reason | Loss of huffaz | Differences in recitation |
| Aim | Gather the text in one place | Standardise copies |
| Compiler | Zayd (RA) | Zayd (RA) with three Quraysh |
| Outcome | One set of sheets | Copies for each region |

## Timeline to remember

1. **Ramadhan, about 610 CE:** first revelation in the Cave of Hira.
2. **Makkah and Madinah, about 23 years:** revelation in parts; scribes write; companions memorise.
3. **Final Ramadhan of his life:** the Qur'an is reviewed with Jibril (AS) twice.
4. **11 AH:** the Prophet (pbuh) dies; the Qur'an exists in memory and in writing, not in one volume.
5. **Soon after, under Abu Bakr (RA):** Yamama; the Qur'an is collected by Zayd (RA).
6. **Under Uthman (RA), about 25 AH or soon after:** standard copies are made and sent to the regions.

Dates after the Prophet's (pbuh) death are traditional approximations; the order of events is what matters.

## Model answer: a 4-mark question

**Question:** Explain **two** ways in which the Qur'an was preserved in the Prophet's (pbuh) lifetime. **[4]**

> It was memorised [1]: the Prophet (pbuh) recited it and the companions learnt it by heart, so the text lived in many minds [1]. It was also written down [1]: scribes such as Zayd ibn Thabit (RA) recorded the revelation on leather, bones and palm stalks as it came [1].

## What a top answer on a 12-mark question includes

- A clear line of argument about which stage (revelation, preservation or compilation) matters most.
- At least two named people and what each did.
- Direct use of the Hadith: Hudhayfa's warning, Uthman's (RA) response and the dialect rule.
- A judgement that links the stages: each depended on the one before.

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Wahy | Revelation |
| Jibril (AS) | The angel who brought revelation |
| Hafiz (plural huffaz) | A person who has memorised the whole Qur'an |
| Suhuf | The sheets on which the Qur'an was gathered |
| Mushaf | A bound copy of the Qur'an |
| Laylat al-Qadr | The Night of Power, when revelation began |
| Yamama | The battle in which many huffaz were martyred |

## Exam traps

- Do not say Uthman (RA) changed the text; he standardised copies.
- Keep the two compilations separate.
- Remember that preservation started in the Prophet's (pbuh) lifetime.
- When asked "why", give the cause (Yamama; differences in recitation).
- Use the Hadith by quotation or close summary in a 12-mark answer.

## Self-test

1. Who brought the revelation to the Prophet (pbuh)?
2. Which surah contains the first revelation?
3. Give two reasons why the Qur'an was revealed gradually.
4. What is a hafiz?
5. Why did Abu Bakr (RA) arrange for the Qur'an to be collected?
6. Who collected the Qur'an for Abu Bakr (RA)?
7. Who kept the sheets after Umar (RA)?
8. Who warned Uthman (RA) about differences in recitation?
9. Which dialect was to be used if there was disagreement?
10. What did Uthman (RA) do with the other written materials?

**Answers:** 1. Jibril (AS). 2. Surah al-Alaq (96:1-5). 3. Any two: to strengthen the Prophet's (pbuh) heart; to answer events and questions; to help memorisation; to introduce laws gradually. 4. Someone who has memorised the whole Qur'an. 5. Many huffaz were martyred at Yamama, and there was a fear that parts might be lost. 6. Zayd ibn Thabit (RA). 7. Hafsa (RA). 8. Hudhayfa ibn al-Yaman (RA). 9. The dialect of the Quraysh. 10. He ordered them to be burned to prevent confusion.
