**These notes condense content area 3.1.1.7 of 9237: the life, role and importance of Prophets Adam (AS), Ibraheem (AS), Musa (AS) and Isa (AS); the nature of the Tawrat, Zabur, Injil and the Suhuf of Ibraheem (AS); and belief in Hazrat Muhammad (pbuh) as the Seal of Prophethood.** The full explanation is in the [Prophethood and holy books study guide](/resources/oxfordaqa-igcse-islamiyat-9237-prophethood-and-holy-books/).

## Prophets: the basics

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Nabi | Prophet: a human chosen to receive revelation |
| Rasul | Messenger: a prophet who brings a message or book |
| Mu'jizah | Miracle given by Allah's permission |
| Wahy | Revelation |

- Prophets were **human**, **truthful**, **trustworthy** and taught the **same core message** (Tawhid).
- Allah sent a messenger to every people (16:36).
- Muslims honour all of them: "no distinction" (2:285).
- Twenty-five prophets are named in the Qur'an.

## The four prophets

### Adam (AS)

- Created from clay; taught the names; angels bowed; Iblis refused.
- Ate from the forbidden tree; repented: "Our Lord, we have wronged ourselves..." (7:23); Allah forgave (2:37).
- First human and prophet; steward (*khalifah*) of earth (2:30).
- **Lessons:** repentance, human dignity, Satan as enemy; no inherited sin.

### Ibraheem (AS)

- Rejected idol worship; broke the idols (21:57-63).
- Thrown into fire: "O fire, be coolness and safety" (21:69).
- Left Hajar and Isma'il in Makkah: Zamzam.
- Dream of sacrificing his son; replaced by a ram (37:102-107).
- Built the Ka'aba with Isma'il (2:127). Received Suhuf (87:18-19).
- **Lessons:** submission, Tawhid. Linked to Eid ul-Adha and Hajj.

### Musa (AS)

- Born in Egypt; saved from Pharaoh; raised in his household.
- Fled to Madyan; burning bush; Allah spoke to him (*Kalimullah*, 4:164).
- Confronted Pharaoh; led the Exodus; sea parted (26:63).
- Received the Tawrat.
- **Lessons:** courage, patience, justice against tyranny.

### Isa (AS)

- Miraculous birth to Maryam (3:47; 19:20-21); spoke in the cradle (19:30).
- Miracles by Allah's permission (3:49); received the Injil.
- Muslims: a mighty prophet, not divine or God's son (5:72-75); not crucified, raised by Allah (4:157-158); to return before the Last Day (Hadith).
- **Lessons:** humility, mercy; a link to Christians and Jews.

## The holy books

| Book | Prophet | Key facts |
|---|---|---|
| Suhuf | Ibraheem (AS) | Scrolls; 87:18-19 |
| Tawrat | Musa (AS) | "guidance and light" (5:44) |
| Zabur | Dawud (AS) | Psalms of praise; 4:163 |
| Injil | Isa (AS) | "guidance and light" (5:46) |
| Qur'an | Muhammad (pbuh) | Final and preserved; 15:9 |

- Earlier books came from Allah; Muslims believe parts of the texts were changed or lost (2:79; 5:13).
- The Qur'an confirms and is a criterion over them (5:48).
- Muslims respect Jews and Christians as "People of the Book".

## The Seal of Prophethood

- Qur'an 33:40: "the Messenger of Allah and last of the prophets."
- Hadith: the Prophet (pbuh) is the last brick of a completed house (Bukhari and Muslim); "there will be no prophet after me" (Bukhari).
- **Consequences:** the Qur'an is final; the message is universal (21:107); the Sunnah is the model; religion is complete (5:3).

## The passage: Qur'an 9:128-129

| Phrase | Shows |
|---|---|
| "from among yourselves" | Human messenger, a model to follow |
| "Grievous to him is what you suffer" | Compassion |
| "concerned over you" | Desire for guidance |
| "kind and merciful" | Mercy towards believers |
| "Sufficient for me is Allah" | Trust in Allah even if rejected |

Use it for Muhammad's (pbuh) character, for the continuing link between Risalah and mercy, and for trust in Allah.

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Khatam an-Nabiyyin | Seal of the Prophets |
| Kalimullah | One whom Allah spoke to (Musa) |
| Khalilullah | Friend of Allah (Ibraheem) |
| Khalifah | Steward |
| Tawrat, Zabur, Injil | Books of Musa, Dawud, Isa |
| Suhuf | Scrolls |
| Tahrif | Changing or altering a text |
| Ahl al-Kitab | People of the Book |

## Exam traps

- Do not call prophets divine or say Muslims worship them.
- Do not say Muslims reject the earlier books; they believe in them as originally revealed.
- Do not mix Suhuf (Ibraheem) with Zabur (Dawud).
- Do not retell a story with no importance; add the lesson.
- Do not say Isa (AS) was crucified; the Qur'an says Allah raised him.

## Model answer shapes

- **2 marks "Give two books revealed before the Qur'an":** the Tawrat; the Injil.
- **4 marks "Explain why Musa (AS) is important":** He confronted the tyrant Pharaoh and led his people to freedom, showing that Allah helps the oppressed [1 and 1]. He received the Tawrat, which Muslims believe was guidance and light for his people [1 and 1].
- **6 marks:** two prophets or reasons developed, plus a source (for example 33:40 or 21:69).
- **12 marks:** discuss whether belief in all prophets or belief in the Seal is more significant, use three phrases from 9:128-129, and give a judgement.

## A model 6-mark paragraph

**Question:** Explain **two** reasons why belief in the Seal of Prophethood is important. Apply **one** teaching. **[6]**

Belief in the Seal of Prophethood is important because it means the Qur'an is the final revelation [1]. Since no prophet comes after Muhammad (pbuh), Muslims rely on the Qur'an as the preserved guidance for all time and do not wait for another book [1]. It is also important because it makes his message universal [1], so Islam applies to every people and every age, and his Sunnah remains the model for daily life [1]. The Qur'an says that he is "the Messenger of Allah and last of the prophets" (33:40) [1], which gives the belief a direct basis in revelation [1].

## One chain, one message

- **Adam (AS):** the first prophet; taught Tawhid and repentance.
- **Ibraheem (AS):** the model of submission; built the Ka'aba.
- **Musa (AS):** confronted tyranny; received the Tawrat.
- **Isa (AS):** healed by Allah's permission; received the Injil.
- **Muhammad (pbuh):** the Seal; received the Qur'an.

Each prophet is described as calling to worship Allah alone, which is why the Qur'an says they are all to be honoured equally (2:285). The details of law differ; the core message of Tawhid does not.

## Self-test

1. Who received the Zabur?
2. Which verse says Muhammad (pbuh) is the last of the prophets?
3. What happened when Ibraheem (AS) was thrown into the fire?
4. How did Allah speak to Musa (AS)?
5. What do Muslims believe about the crucifixion of Isa (AS)?
6. What does *tahrif* mean?
7. Name the five books or scrolls in the specification.
8. What does "from among yourselves" in 9:128 show?
9. What lesson comes from Adam's repentance?
10. Why does the Seal of Prophethood make the Qur'an important?

**Answers:** 1. Dawud (AS). 2. Qur'an 33:40. 3. Allah made the fire cool and safe. 4. At the burning bush, directly; he is called Kalimullah. 5. He was not killed or crucified; Allah raised him. 6. Alteration of a text. 7. Suhuf, Tawrat, Zabur, Injil, Qur'an. 8. The Prophet (pbuh) was human, a model people could follow. 9. Anyone who repents sincerely can be forgiven. 10. As no prophet follows, the Qur'an is the final, preserved guidance for all time.
