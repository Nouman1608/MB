**These notes condense AQA GCSE History 8145 Paper 1 Section B, the wider world depth study.** Find your school's *Conflict and tension* option -- the First World War, the inter-war years, East and West, Asia, or the Gulf and Afghanistan -- and learn its timeline, then use the technique sheet for the four questions (40 marks + 4 SPaG). For full explanations see the [wider world depth studies study guide](/resources/gcse-history-wider-world-depth-studies-study-guide/); for practice see the [wider world depth studies practice questions](/resources/gcse-history-wider-world-depth-studies-practice/).

## Syllabus coverage

**AQA GCSE HISTORY (8145) -- 3.2.2 SECTION B: WIDER WORLD DEPTH STUDIES** -- BA the First World War, 1894-1918; BB the inter-war years, 1918-1939; BC East and West, 1945-1972; BD Asia, 1950-1975; BE the Gulf and Afghanistan, 1990-2009. Each has three parts; all three are tested every series.

## Technique sheet: questions 1-4

| Q | Marks | Do this | Avoid this |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Source analysis | 4 | Use the source's content *and* its provenance to show how it supports the view | Describing the picture only |
| 2 How useful | 12 | For each source: content, provenance, context -- what it is useful *for* | "It's biased so it's useless" |
| 3 Write an account | 8 | A sequence of events linked by cause and consequence ("this led to...") | A list of unlinked facts |
| 4 How far do you agree | 16 + 4 SPaG | Both sides, developed with evidence, then a clear judgement | Sitting on the fence; ignoring spelling and grammar |

## Worked example: question 3 account

*Write an account of how the Berlin Blockade increased tension between East and West.* **[8]**

Chain: Western zones merge and introduce a new currency (1948) -> Stalin blocks road and rail routes to West Berlin -> the Allies fly in supplies for almost a year -> Stalin lifts the blockade (May 1949) -> NATO is formed, and Germany splits into two states. Each arrow is a "this led to" link -- that is what earns the higher level.

## Worked example: question 4 plan

*"The main reason for the outbreak of war in 1939 was the policy of appeasement." How far do you agree?* **[16 + 4 SPaG]**

1. **Agree:** appeasement let Hitler rearm, remilitarise the Rhineland (1936) and take Austria and the Sudetenland (1938) unopposed, convincing him Britain and France would not fight.
2. **Disagree:** Hitler's own aims (overturning Versailles, *Lebensraum*) drove events; the Nazi–Soviet Pact (August 1939) freed him to invade Poland; the League's failures over Manchuria and Abyssinia had already weakened collective security.
3. **Judgement:** say which mattered most and why, for example that Hitler's aims were the main cause and appeasement allowed them to be achieved without war until 1939.

Write in full sentences with accurate spelling, punctuation and grammar: the four SPaG marks are awarded on this question.

## Exam traps

- Question 1: forgetting provenance.
- Question 2: treating usefulness as reliability only.
- Question 3: no consequence links.
- Question 4: weak judgement; careless SPaG.
- BB students: confusing the League's 1920s successes with its 1930s failures.
- BD students: mixing Korea (UN war) with Vietnam (no UN force).

## Frequently asked questions

**What is a narrative account?** A connected story that explains how events caused or led to an outcome.

**Are SPaG marks only on question 4?** In Section B, yes -- 4 marks on the essay.

**Do sources always include a cartoon?** Not always, but visual sources are common, so practise cartoons and posters.

## Self-test

1. Which crisis in 1908-09 involved Bosnia? *The Bosnian crisis.*
2. Name a League success in the 1920s. *The Aaland Islands (1921).*
3. What year was SALT 1 signed? *1972.*
4. When did Chinese troops enter the Korean War? *October 1950.*
5. When did Iraq invade Kuwait? *August 1990.*
