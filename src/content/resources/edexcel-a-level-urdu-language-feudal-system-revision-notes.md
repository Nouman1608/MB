Condensed recall for **sub-theme 4.2** of Theme 4 in Pearson Edexcel A Level Urdu (9UR0): the feudal system (جاگیرداری نظام). For fuller explanation and a model paragraph, read the [9UR0 subject guide](/resources/edexcel-a-level-urdu-language-9ur0-subject-guide/) and the [Paper 1 study guide](/resources/a-level-edexcel-urdu-translation-and-reading/).

## Key idea

**جاگیرداری نظام** is the feudal (landholding) system, in which large areas of land are held by a few landowners and worked by tenants and labourers.

## Vocabulary

| Urdu | Meaning |
|---|---|
| جاگیردار | large landholder |
| زمیندار | landowner |
| کسان | farmer, peasant |
| زمین | land |
| غربت | poverty |
| استحصال | exploitation |
| زرعی اصلاحات | land (agrarian) reforms |

## Sentence to learn

زمین چند لوگوں کے پاس ہوتی ہے۔ (Land is held by a few people.) زمین is feminine, so the verb is ہوتی.

## Writing a balanced response

Give the criticisms (for example poverty and exploitation) and the arguments for reform, each with reasons and sourced evidence, then say which you find more convincing. Avoid unsupported statistics and sweeping claims. Link ideas with connectives such as **اس کے برعکس** and **نتیجتاً**.

## Exam traps

- Quoting statistics you cannot trace to a source.
- One-sided answers that only criticise or only defend the system.
- Wrong agreement: check the gender of the noun before choosing the verb ending.

## Self-test

1. What does جاگیرداری نظام mean?
2. Give the Urdu for 'land reforms' and 'exploitation'.
3. Translate: 'land is held by a few people'.
4. Why is the verb ہوتی in that sentence?
5. What makes a response on this topic balanced?

**Answers:** 1. The feudal (landholding) system. 2. زرعی اصلاحات and استحصال. 3. زمین چند لوگوں کے پاس ہوتی ہے۔ 4. Because زمین is feminine. 5. Criticisms and arguments for reform, each with reasons and sourced evidence, and a judgement.
