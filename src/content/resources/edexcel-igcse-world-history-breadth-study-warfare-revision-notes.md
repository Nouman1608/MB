## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Blitzkrieg | Fast attack combining tanks, aircraft and radio |
| Strategic bombing | Bombing industry and cities to break the enemy's ability to fight |
| Amphibious | Landing troops from the sea |
| MAD | Mutually Assured Destruction |
| Proliferation | Spread of nuclear weapons to more states |
| Asymmetric warfare | Conflict between unequal forces, often guerrillas v a superpower |
| Surgical strike | Precise attack on a specific target |
| Drone / UAV | Unmanned aerial vehicle |

## Worked example: explain two ways different

*Explain two ways in which air warfare in 1940 was different from air warfare in 1991.* (6 marks)

1. **Accuracy:** in 1940 bombing was inaccurate, so cities were hit widely in the Blitz; in 1991 laser- and
   satellite-guided weapons hit specific buildings.
2. **Technology:** in 1940 piston-engined fighters fought within sight of each other, guided by early radar;
   in 1991 stealth aircraft and cruise missiles struck from long range, often unseen.

## Essay plan in outline

*"Air power was the most important change in warfare in the years 1919–2011." How far do you agree?*

- **For:** Blitzkrieg relied on dive-bombers; the Battle of Britain; carriers at Midway; atomic bombs;
  1967 air strikes; Gulf War air campaign; drones.
- **Against:** nuclear weapons as a separate revolution; continuing importance of ground forces (D-Day,
  1973, 1991 ground war); guerrilla wars where air power failed (Vietnam, Afghanistan).
- **Judgement:** air power changed most, but it rarely won wars without troops on the ground.

## Turning points to argue about

- **1939–40:** Blitzkrieg restores movement to land warfare.
- **1942:** Midway shows the carrier is now the decisive warship.
- **1945:** atomic bombs create a weapon that could end civilisation.
- **1991:** the Gulf War shows the power of precision-guided weapons.
- **2001:** 9/11 makes terrorism and counter-insurgency central to Western strategy.

## Common mistakes and exam traps

- **Assuming technology always wins.** Guerrillas defeated superpowers in Vietnam and Afghanistan.
- **Confusing V-1 and V-2.** V-1 = flying bomb; V-2 = ballistic rocket.
- **Treating MAD as a weapon.** It is a theory of deterrence.
- **Leaving out sea warfare.** It is a named theme.

## Frequently asked questions

**Why did Blitzkrieg work in 1939–40?**
Speed, concentration of tanks, air support and radio communication
against slower, defensive opponents.

**Why were carriers decisive in the Pacific?**
Aircraft could strike far beyond the range of battleship
guns.

**What made drones important?**
Strikes without risk to the operator's life, at long range.
