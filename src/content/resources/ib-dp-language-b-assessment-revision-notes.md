Condensed for quick recall of how the course is assessed. For the full subject overview, use the
[IB DP Language B subject overview](/resources/ib-dp-language-b-subject-guide/).

## Same weightings at SL and HL — the difference is complexity, not structure

| Component | Weight |
|---|---|
| Paper 1 (productive writing, choice of tasks) | 25% |
| Paper 2 (listening + reading comprehension) | 50% (25% each) |
| Individual oral (recorded by your own teacher) | 25% |

## SL vs HL — what actually differs

- **HL only:** study of **two literary works** originally written in the target language.
- HL students are expected to extend the **range and complexity** of language used/understood.
- **HL individual oral:** built around an extract from one of the two literary works, connected to a broader theme.
- **SL individual oral:** based on a photograph or short stimulus connected to one of the **five
  prescribed themes** (identities, experiences, human ingenuity, social organization, sharing the planet).

## Exam traps

- Preparing for the oral using the wrong format for your level (photograph/stimulus at SL, literary extract at HL).
- Underpreparing Paper 2, since it's worth 50% — half the total grade — split evenly between listening and reading.
- HL students not building familiarity with both required literary works before the oral.

## Self-test

1. What is Paper 2 worth, and how is it split?
2. What's required only at HL, not SL?
3. What is the SL individual oral based on?
4. Who conducts and records the individual oral?

**Answers:** 1. 50%, split evenly between listening comprehension (25%) and reading comprehension (25%). 2. Study of two literary works originally written in the target language. 3. A photograph or short stimulus connected to one of the five prescribed themes. 4. The student's own teacher.
