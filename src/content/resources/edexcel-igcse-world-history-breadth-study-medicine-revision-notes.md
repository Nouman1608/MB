|---|---|

| Germ theory | Microbes cause disease and decay |

## Worked example: explain two ways different

*Explain two ways in which surgery in 1850 was different from surgery in 1900.* (6 marks)

1. **Infection:** in 1850 surgeons operated in ordinary clothes with unwashed instruments, so infection
   killed many; by 1900 aseptic methods (sterilised instruments, gowns, gloves) kept germs out.
2. **Understanding:** in 1850 surgeons did not know germs caused infection; by 1900 germ theory, from
   Pasteur and Koch, explained it and guided practice.

## Essay plan in outline

*"War was the main reason for change in medicine in the years c1848–c1948." How far do you agree?*

- **For war:** Crimea brought Nightingale to prominence; the First World War spread mobile X-rays,
  blood storage and plastic surgery and widened women's roles; the Second World War funded mass
  production of penicillin, advanced skin grafting and led to the NHS.
- **Against:** the key discoveries came in peacetime — chloroform (1847), germ theory (1861), Lister
  (1865), Koch, blood groups (1901), Fleming (1928); public health acts and Liberal reforms were
  peacetime government action.
- **Judgement:** war accelerated and spread changes; science and government usually began them.

## Common mistakes and exam traps

- **Confusing antiseptic and aseptic.**
- **Saying Fleming developed penicillin.** He noticed it; Florey and Chain made it usable.
- **Mixing up the two Public Health Acts.** 1848 voluntary; 1875 compulsory.
- **Treating war as only positive.** It sped up some changes but also diverted resources.
- **Narrating one discovery.** (c) answers must cover change across the period.

## Frequently asked questions

**Why was there little progress before 1860?** No knowledge of germs, belief in miasma, and limited
government action.

**What was the significance of germ theory?** It explained disease and led to antiseptics, vaccines and
targeted cures.

**Why was the NHS created in 1948?** Wartime experience, the Beveridge Report and Labour's election victory.
