These questions cover **section 5.11 of IB DP Mathematics: Analysis and Approaches: definite integrals, areas where f(x) can be negative, and areas between curves**. It is SL content, so the set suits SL and HL students. Questions are labelled "(calculator-free)" or "(calculator allowed)". Give calculator-free answers exactly, and calculator answers to 3 significant figures.

Learn the methods first in the [section 5.11 study guide](/resources/ib-ib-dp-mathematics-analysis-and-approaches-dp-mathematics-analysis-and-approaches-ib-dp-mathematics-analysis-and-approaches-5-11/) and the [section 5.11 revision notes](/resources/ib-ib-dp-mathematics-analysis-and-approaches-dp-mathematics-analysis-and-approaches-ib-dp-mathematics-analysis-and-approaches-5-11-revision-notes/).

## Section A

**1.** (calculator-free) Find ∫₁⁹ 2/√x dx. **[2]**

**2.** (calculator-free) Find ∫₀^(π/2) (3 sin x + 2 cos 2x) dx. **[3]**

**3.** (calculator-free) Find ∫₁³ 4/(2x − 1) dx, giving your answer in the form ln k. **[3]**

**4.** (calculator-free) Find the exact value of ∫₀¹ 3x² e^(x³) dx. **[3]**

**5.** (calculator-free) A function g satisfies ∫₀³ g(x) dx = 5 and ∫₃⁷ g(x) dx = −2.

**(a)** Write down ∫₀⁷ g(x) dx. **[1]**
**(b)** Find ∫₇⁰ (g(x) + 1) dx. **[3]**

**6.** (calculator-free) Given that ∫₀ᵃ e^(x/2) dx = 2, where a > 0, find the exact value of a. **[4]**

**7.** (calculator-free) Find the exact value of ∫₁^e (ln x)/x dx. **[3]**

## Section B

**8.** (calculator-free) Let f(x) = x(x − 2)(x − 3).

**(a)** Show that ∫₀² f(x) dx = 8/3. **[3]**
**(b)** Find ∫₂³ f(x) dx. **[2]**
**(c)** Hence find the total area enclosed by the graph of f and the x-axis for 0 ≤ x ≤ 3. **[2]**

**9.** (calculator-free) Find the area of the region enclosed by y = x² − 4 and y = 2 − x. **[6]**

**10.** (calculator-free) Consider y = sin 2x and y = sin x, for 0 ≤ x ≤ π.

**(a)** Show that the curves meet when x = 0, x = π/3 and x = π. **[3]**
**(b)** Find the total area enclosed between the curves for 0 ≤ x ≤ π. **[6]**

**11.** (calculator-free) The region R is bounded by y = eˣ, y = e^(−x) and the line x = ln 2. Show that the area of R is 1/2. **[5]**

**12.** (calculator allowed) The curves y = 3 sin x and y = x²/2 meet at the origin and at the point where x = p, p > 0.

**(a)** Find the value of p. **[2]**
**(b)** Find the area of the region enclosed by the two curves. **[3]**

**13.** (calculator-free) Let f(x) = 9 − x².

**(a)** Find the area of the region enclosed by the graph of f and the x-axis. **[3]**
**(b)** Find the area of the region enclosed by the graph of f and the line y = 5. **[4]**

## Answers

**1.** [4√x]₁⁹ [1] = 12 − 4 = **8** [1] **[2]**

**2.** [−3 cos x + sin 2x]₀^(π/2) [1] [1] = (0 + 0) − (−3 + 0) = **3** [1] **[3]**

**3.** [2 ln(2x − 1)]₁³ [1] = 2 ln 5 − 2 ln 1 [1] = **ln 25** [1] **[3]**

**4.** [e^(x³)]₀¹ [1] = e¹ − e⁰ [1] = **e − 1** [1] **[3]**

**5. (a)** 5 + (−2) = **3** [1]
**(b)** ∫₇⁰ (g + 1) dx = −∫₀⁷ (g + 1) dx [1] = −(3 + [x]₀⁷) [1] = −(3 + 7) = **−10** [1] **[4]**

**6.** [2e^(x/2)]₀ᵃ = 2e^(a/2) − 2 [1] [1]; 2e^(a/2) − 2 = 2 gives e^(a/2) = 2 [1]; **a = 2 ln 2 (= ln 4)** [1] **[4]**

**7.** g = ln x, g′ = 1/x: anti-derivative (1/2)(ln x)² [1]; (1/2)(ln e)² − (1/2)(ln 1)² [1] = **1/2** [1] **[3]**

**8. (a)** f(x) = x³ − 5x² + 6x [1]; [x⁴/4 − 5x³/3 + 3x²]₀² [1] = 4 − 40/3 + 12 = 8/3 [1]
**(b)** [x⁴/4 − 5x³/3 + 3x²]₂³ = (81/4 − 45 + 27) − 8/3 [1] = 9/4 − 8/3 = **−5/12** [1]
**(c)** The curve is below the axis on (2, 3), so area = 8/3 + 5/12 [1] = **37/12** [1] **[7]**
*Examiner insight:* ∫₀³ f(x) dx = 9/4 is the signed value; giving it as the area in (c) shows the regions have not been separated.

**9.** x² − 4 = 2 − x gives x² + x − 6 = 0 [1], so x = −3 or x = 2 [1]; 2 − x is on top (at x = 0: 2 > −4) [1]; area = ∫₋₃² (6 − x − x²) dx [1] = [6x − x²/2 − x³/3]₋₃² = 22/3 − (−27/2) [1] = **125/6** [1] **[6]**

**10. (a)** 2 sin x cos x = sin x [1], so sin x(2 cos x − 1) = 0 [1]; sin x = 0 gives x = 0, π, and cos x = 1/2 gives x = π/3 [1]
**(b)** On (0, π/3) sin 2x is on top; on (π/3, π) sin x is on top [1]; ∫₀^(π/3) (sin 2x − sin x) dx = [−(1/2)cos 2x + cos x]₀^(π/3) [1] = 3/4 − 1/2 = 1/4 [1]; ∫ from π/3 to π of (sin x − sin 2x) dx = [−cos x + (1/2)cos 2x] from π/3 to π [1] = 3/2 − (−3/4) = 9/4 [1]; total **5/2** [1] **[9]**

**11.** The curves meet at x = 0 (both equal 1) [1]; for 0 < x < ln 2, eˣ > e^(−x) [1]; area = ∫₀^(ln 2) (eˣ − e^(−x)) dx = [eˣ + e^(−x)]₀^(ln 2) [1] = (2 + 1/2) − (1 + 1) [1] = 1/2 [1] **[5]**

**12. (a)** Using the GDC to solve 3 sin x = x²/2 [1]: **p = 2.20** (2.2013…) [1]
**(b)** Area = ∫₀^p (3 sin x − x²/2) dx [1] [1] = **2.99** (2.9908…) [1] **[5]**

**13. (a)** Roots x = ±3 [1]; ∫₋₃³ (9 − x²) dx = [9x − x³/3]₋₃³ [1] = 18 − (−18) = **36** [1]
**(b)** 9 − x² = 5 at x = ±2 [1]; area = ∫₋₂² (9 − x² − 5) dx [1] = [4x − x³/3]₋₂² [1] = 16/3 − (−16/3) = **32/3** [1] **[7]**
