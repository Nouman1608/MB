## Section A

**1.** **(Higher tier only)** **a** = (3, −2) and **b** = (−1, 4).

**(a)** Find 2**a** + **b** as a column vector. **[2]**

**(b)** Find |**a** − 2**b**|, giving your answer to 3 significant figures. **[2]**

**2.** **(Higher tier only)** Find the magnitude of the vector (−9, 40). **[2]**

**3.** **(Higher tier only)** The vector (k, 8) has magnitude 17. Find the two possible values of k. **[2]**

**4.** **(Higher tier only)** A is the point (−3, 2) and B is the point (5, −4). Find →AB as a column vector and find |→AB|. **[2]**

**5.** **(Higher tier only)** Find the values of p and q such that p(2, 3) + q(1, −1) = (8, 7). **[3]**

**6.** **(Higher tier only)** Show that 4**a** − 6**b** is parallel to 6**a** − 9**b**, and find the ratio of their lengths. **[2]**

## Section B

**7.** A(2, 1), B(6, 2) and C(7, 5) are three vertices of parallelogram ABCD.

**(a)** Find the coordinates of D. **[2]**

**(b)** Find the length of the diagonal AC. **[2]**

**8.** **(Higher tier only)** In a quadrilateral OPQR, →OP = **p**, →PQ = 2**q** and →QR = **p** − **q**.

**(a)** Find →OR in terms of **p** and **q**. **[1]**

**(b)** Find →RP in terms of **p** and **q**. **[2]**

**9.** **(Higher tier only)** In triangle OAB, →OA = **a** and →OB = **b**. M is the midpoint of AB. N is the point on OB with ON : NB = 2 : 1.

**(a)** Find →AB. **[1]**

**(b)** Find →OM. **[2]**

**(c)** Find →MN, simplifying your answer. **[2]**

**10.** **(Higher tier only)** In triangle OAB, →OA = 6**a** and →OB = 6**b**. P is on OA with OP : PA = 1 : 2, and Q is on OB with OQ : QB = 1 : 2.

Show that PQ is parallel to AB and that AB is three times as long as PQ. **[4]**

**11.** **(Higher tier only)** Using the triangle in question 10, R is the midpoint of AB and X is the midpoint of PQ.

Prove that O, X and R lie on a straight line. **[4]**

**12.** **(Higher tier only)** OABC is a quadrilateral with →OA = 4**a**, →OC = **c** and →CB = 2**a**.

**(a)** Find →AB in terms of **a** and **c**. **[1]**

**(b)** Explain why OABC is a trapezium. **[2]**

## Answers

**1. (a)** 2**a** = (6, −4) [1]; 2**a** + **b** = **(5, 0)** [1].

**(b)** **a** − 2**b** = (3 + 2, −2 − 8) = (5, −10) [1]; √(25 + 100) = √125 = **11.2** [1].

**2.** √((−9)² + 40²) = √(81 + 1600) = √1681 [1] = **41** [1].

**3.** k² + 64 = 289, so k² = 225 [1]; k = **15 or −15** [1].

**4.** →AB = (5 − (−3), −4 − 2) = **(8, −6)** [1]; |→AB| = √(64 + 36) = **10** [1].

**5.** 2p + q = 8 and 3p − q = 7 [1]. Adding: 5p = 15, so **p = 3** [1]; **q = 2** [1].

**6.** 4**a** − 6**b** = 2(2**a** − 3**b**) and 6**a** − 9**b** = 3(2**a** − 3**b**), so both are multiples of 2**a** − 3**b** and are parallel [1]. Lengths in the ratio **2 : 3** [1].

**7. (a)** →BC = (1, 3) [1]; D = A + →BC = **(3, 4)** [1].

**(b)** →AC = (5, 4) [1]; |→AC| = √41 = **6.40** units [1].

**8. (a)** →OR = **p** + 2**q** + **p** − **q** = **2p + q** [1].

**(b)** →RP = →RQ + →QP = −(**p** − **q**) − 2**q** [1] = **−p − q** [1].

**9. (a)** →AB = **b − a** [1].

**(b)** →OM = **a** + ½(**b** − **a**) [1] = **½a + ½b** [1].

**(c)** →MN = →MO + →ON = −½**a** − ½**b** + ⅔**b** [1] = **−½a + (1/6)b** [1].

**10.** →OP = 2**a** and →OQ = 2**b** [1]. →PQ = −2**a** + 2**b** = 2(**b** − **a**) [1]. →AB = −6**a** + 6**b** = 6(**b** − **a**) = 3→PQ [1]. →AB is a scalar multiple of →PQ, so PQ is parallel to AB, and the multiple 3 shows AB is three times as long [1].

**11.** →OR = 6**a** + ½ × 6(**b** − **a**) = 3**a** + 3**b** [1]. →OX = 2**a** + ½ × 2(**b** − **a**) = **a** + **b** [1]. →OR = 3→OX, so OX and OR are parallel [1]. They share the point O, so O, X and R lie on a straight line [1].

**12. (a)** →AB = −4**a** + **c** + 2**a** = **c − 2a** [1].

**(b)** →OA = 4**a** = 2→CB, so OA is parallel to CB [1]. →AB = **c** − 2**a** is not a multiple of →OC = **c**, so the other pair of sides is not parallel; exactly one pair of parallel sides makes OABC a trapezium [1].

## Where marks are usually lost

- Question 3: giving only k = 15.
- Question 4: working out →AB as A − B, which gives (−8, 6).
- Question 9(c): taking ON as ½**b** because N is "on OB"; the ratio 2 : 1 makes it ⅔**b**.
- Question 10: showing →AB = 3→PQ but not writing the word "parallel".
- Question 11: stopping at "parallel" without naming the common point O.

## Frequently asked questions

**How do you prove three points are collinear with vectors?**
Show that the vector joining the first two points is a multiple of the vector joining the first and third (or second and third), and state that they share a point. Both facts are needed.

**How do you find a fraction of a line from a ratio?**
For a ratio m : n, the point is m/(m + n) of the way from the first end. OP : PA = 1 : 2 means OP is one third of OA.
