These notes cover **Exercise 1, Short-answer questions**, of 3248 Paper 1: "candidates read a short text printed in the question paper and answer a series of questions that test their skimming and gist-reading skills and require short answers of a single word or phrase". For the whole paper see the [O Level Urdu Paper 1 study guide](/resources/o-level-cambridge-urdu-first-and-second-language/); for worked examples see the [Exercise 1 Short-answer questions study guide](/resources/cambridge-o-level-urdu-language-3248-exercise-1-short-answer-questions/).

## Task facts

| Fact | Detail |
|---|---|
| Marks | 8 |
| Objectives | R1, R2, R3, R4 |
| Text | short; the shortest on the paper |
| Text types | advertisement, brochure, leaflet, guide, report, manual, instructions, newspaper or magazine article |
| Task | questions needing a single word or phrase |
| Language | questions and answers in Urdu |
| Dictionaries | not allowed |
| Paper | 1 hour 45 minutes, 50 marks, five compulsory exercises |

## Skills tested

- **Skimming:** quick reading to see the topic and layout.
- **Gist:** the main idea or purpose of the text.
- **R1:** find and select the relevant detail.
- **R2:** understand ideas, opinions and attitudes, such as advice.
- **R3:** connect ideas, such as a reason and its result.
- **R4:** understand what is implied but not stated.

## Five-step routine

1. Title and headings.
2. First sentence of each paragraph.
3. Read the questions; underline the question word and key noun.
4. Go back to the matching part of the text.
5. Write the shortest complete answer.

## Question words

| Word | English | Answer type |
|---|---|---|
| کیا | what | thing, action, idea |
| کب | when | time, date, day |
| کہاں | where | place |
| کون / کس | who / which | person or group |
| کتنا / کتنے | how much / how many | number with unit |
| کیوں | why | reason |
| کیسے | how | method |

Signals for reasons: *کیونکہ*، *اس لیے*، *تاکہ*، *کی وجہ سے*. Signals for advice: *مشورہ*، *چاہیے*، *بہتر ہے*. Signals for conditions: *اگر*، *صرف*، *شرط*، *بشرطیکہ*.

## Answer length

| Do | Do not |
|---|---|
| Give a word or phrase | Copy a whole sentence |
| Keep the condition (*وردی میں طلبہ*) | Give half the idea (*طلبہ*) |
| Give two items if two are asked | Give an extra answer that may contradict |
| Give the unit with a number (*تین دن*) | Write a bare number if the unit matters |

## Counting and numbers

- From the 12th to the 14th is **three** days, because both ends count.
- Check whether the number belongs to the right season, person or place.
- Opening time and closing time are different questions. Read the question word and the verb: *کھلتا ہے* or *بند ہوتا ہے*.

## Gist and purpose questions

- Ask: "Why was this written?" The usual purposes are to inform, to invite, to advise, to warn or to persuade.
- Use the title, the first sentence and the last sentence.
- Answer in a short phrase: *لوگوں کو میلے میں آنے کی دعوت دینا*.

## Typical slips

| Slip | Fix |
|---|---|
| Opening time given for a closing-time question | Underline the verb in the question |
| Days counted wrongly | Count both the first and last day |
| Whole sentence copied | Cut to the part that answers |
| Condition left out | Add the short condition phrase |
| Noun given for a *کیوں* question | Give the reason |
| Number from the wrong season | Link every number to its subject |

## Reading different text types

| Text type | What to expect | Where answers hide |
|---|---|---|
| Advertisement or leaflet | dates, times, prices, places | headings and the first paragraph |
| Brochure or guide | features, rules, who can join | lists and short rules |
| Instructions or manual | steps, numbers, warnings | sentences with *کیونکہ* or *اگر* |
| Newspaper or magazine article | who, what, where, a quoted opinion | quoted speech and the last paragraph |
| Report | figures, causes, results | sentences with *کی وجہ سے* or *نتیجے میں* |

A short plan for the last minute of the exercise: check every number and unit, check that each answer matches its question word, and check that no answer is a whole copied sentence.

## Exam traps

- Reading the whole text word by word. Skim first.
- Writing sentences. Use phrases.
- Forgetting that some answers are only implied.
- Spending too long. This exercise is worth 8 of 50 marks.
- Answering *کیوں* with a noun.

## Self-test

1. How many marks is Exercise 1 worth?
2. Name **three** text types that can appear.
3. What does *کتنے* ask for?
4. What does *کیوں* ask for?
5. Give **two** signal words for a reason.
6. A leaflet says a course runs from 3 to 7 March. How many days is that?
7. Why is *طلبہ* a weak answer to "کن کے لیے داخلہ مفت ہے؟" if the text says "وردی میں آنے والے طلبہ"?
8. What is meant by gist?
9. Which reading objective covers an implied meaning?
10. Should you write a full sentence for each answer?

**Answers**

1. Eight marks.
2. Any three: advertisement, brochure, leaflet, guide, report, manual, instructions, newspaper or magazine article.
3. A number or amount, with its unit.
4. A reason.
5. Any two: *کیونکہ*، *اس لیے*، *تاکہ*، *کی وجہ سے*.
6. Five days (3, 4, 5, 6, 7).
7. It leaves out the condition. Only students in school uniform have free entry.
8. The main idea or overall purpose of the text.
9. R4.
10. No. The syllabus asks for a single word or phrase.
