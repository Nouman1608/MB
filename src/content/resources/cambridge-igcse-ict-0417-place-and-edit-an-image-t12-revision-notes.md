These notes condense the "Place and edit an image" skills in Topic 12 of Cambridge IGCSE ICT 0417. For explanations and worked examples, read the [place and edit an image study guide](/resources/cambridge-igcse-ict-0417-place-and-edit-an-image-t12/), then try the [place and edit an image practice questions](/resources/cambridge-igcse-ict-0417-place-and-edit-an-image-t12-practice/).

## Syllabus coverage

| Syllabus wording | What you must do |
|---|---|
| Place an image with precision | Use position and alignment settings, not guesswork |
| Resize as specified to maintain or adjust the aspect ratio | Lock the ratio to keep the shape; unlock it when both dimensions are given |
| Crop an image | Remove unwanted edges |
| Rotate an image | Turn it through a stated angle |
| Reflect (flip) horizontally or vertically | Make a mirror image |
| Adjust the brightness | Make the whole image lighter or darker |
| Adjust the contrast | Change the difference between light and dark areas |
| Group and layer images | Group, ungroup, bring to front, send to back |

## Precise placement checklist

- Set **wrapping** first (square or tight to position freely; in line to treat as a character).
- Use the **position dialog**: horizontal and vertical values relative to page, margin or paragraph.
- Use **alignment**: left, centre, right; top, middle, bottom.
- Remember the **anchor**: the image moves with its paragraph.
- Check in **print preview**: inside margins, not covering text.

## Aspect ratio in three lines

- **Maintain**: lock on, enter one dimension, the other follows (or drag a corner handle).
- **Adjust**: lock off, enter both dimensions given.
- **Calculate**: new other side = old other side x (new side / old side).

*Example:* 18 cm by 12 cm, new width 6 cm: scale 6 / 18 = 1/3; new height 12 / 3 = 4 cm.

## Crop, rotate, flip

| Edit | What changes | Watch for |
|---|---|---|
| Crop | Edges removed; remaining part keeps its scale | Crop first, then resize to the final size |
| Rotate 90° | Image turns; width and height swap | Check margins afterwards |
| Rotate by other angles | Image tilts | Enter the exact angle |
| Flip horizontally | Left and right swap (vertical mirror line) | Text becomes mirror writing |
| Flip vertically | Top and bottom swap (horizontal mirror line) | Left and right stay in place |

**Rotate 180° = flip horizontally + flip vertically.**

## Brightness and contrast

| Setting | Increase | Decrease |
|---|---|---|
| Brightness | Whole image lighter; can look washed out | Whole image darker |
| Contrast | Lights lighter, darks darker; more vivid | Image looks flat and grey |

Enter the percentage given; do not pick a similar-looking preset.

## Layers and groups

| Command | Result |
|---|---|
| Bring to front | Object on top of everything |
| Send to back | Object behind everything |
| Bring forward / send backward | Up or down one layer |
| Group | Objects act as one: move, resize and copy together |
| Ungroup | Objects separate so one can be edited |

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Aspect ratio | The relationship between an image's width and height |
| Lock aspect ratio | Setting that keeps width and height in proportion when resizing |
| Crop | Remove unwanted outer parts of an image |
| Rotate | Turn an image through an angle about its centre |
| Reflect (flip) | Produce a mirror image horizontally or vertically |
| Brightness | How light or dark the whole image is |
| Contrast | The difference between the lightest and darkest areas |
| Layer | The front-to-back position of overlapping objects |
| Group | Combine objects so they behave as one |
| Anchor | The paragraph an image is attached to |

## Exam traps

- A **side handle** changes one dimension only: distortion.
- "Adjust the aspect ratio" is correct **only** when the task gives both dimensions.
- Horizontal flip and vertical flip are **different**; name the mirror line if unsure.
- A flip is **not** a rotation: text reads as mirror writing after a flip.
- After rotating 90°, the image may **overflow** the margin.
- Group objects **before** moving a multi-part logo.
- Layering changes **visibility**, not size or position.

## Self-test

1. What does "place an image with precision" require you to use?
2. A 24 cm by 16 cm image is resized to 8 cm wide, maintaining the ratio. What is its new height?
3. When should the aspect ratio lock be switched off?
4. A 10 cm wide image has 1.5 cm cropped from each side. What width remains?
5. A 9 cm by 6 cm image is rotated 90°. What are its new width and height?
6. What does a horizontal flip do?
7. Which single rotation equals a horizontal flip followed by a vertical flip?
8. What is the effect of reducing the contrast?
9. Give one reason for increasing the brightness of a photograph.
10. A shape hides part of a photo placed earlier. Give two commands that would make the photo visible.
11. State one reason to group objects.
12. Why might you need to ungroup objects?

## Self-test answers

1. Position and alignment settings (exact measurements relative to the page, margin or paragraph).
2. 16 x 8 / 24 = 5.33 cm (to 2 d.p.).
3. When the task specifies both the width and the height and the shape is meant to change.
4. 10 - 1.5 - 1.5 = 7 cm.
5. 6 cm wide and 9 cm high.
6. Swaps the left and right sides to give a mirror image.
7. A rotation of 180°.
8. The difference between light and dark areas decreases, so the image looks flatter and greyer.
9. It was taken in poor light and is too dark to see detail.
10. Bring the photo to the front, or send the shape to the back (or send it backward).
11. So the objects move, resize and copy together and keep their positions.
12. To edit or move one part of the group on its own.
