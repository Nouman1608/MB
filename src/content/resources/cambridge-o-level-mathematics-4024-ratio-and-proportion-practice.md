## Section A

**1.** Write the ratio 36 : 48 : 60 in its simplest form. **[2]**

**2.** Write the ratio 1.2 m : 40 cm in its simplest form. **[2]**

**3.** Write the ratio 3/4 : 5/6 in its simplest form. **[2]**

**4.** Share $360 in the ratio 5 : 3 : 4. **[3]**

**5.** The ratio of boys to girls in a school is 7 : 5. There are 35 girls. Find the total number of students. **[3]**

**6.** A rope of length 7.2 m is cut into two pieces in the ratio 5 : 4. Find the length of the shorter piece. **[2]**

**7.** The angles of a triangle are in the ratio 2 : 3 : 7. Find the largest angle. **[3]**

## Section B

**8.** Two friends have money in the ratio 5 : 3. The first has $24 more than the second. How much does the first friend have? **[3]**

**9.** A recipe for 4 people uses 250 g of flour. How much flour is needed for 10 people? **[2]**

**10.** A map has scale 1 : 25 000.

**(a)** Two villages are 8.4 cm apart on the map. Find the real distance in kilometres. **[2]**

**(b)** The real distance from a hotel to a beach is 3.5 km. How far apart are they on the map? **[2]**

**11.** Shop A sells 750 g of rice for $2.85. Shop B sells 1.2 kg for $4.32. Shop C sells 500 g for $1.95. Which shop gives the best value? Show your working. **[3]**

**12.** a : b = 2 : 3 and b : c = 4 : 5. Find a : b : c. **[3]**

**13.** A bag contains red and blue counters in the ratio 3 : 5. There are 40 counters in total. Eight more red counters are added. Find the new ratio of red to blue counters, in simplest form. **[3]**

**14.** Paint is made by mixing red and yellow in the ratio 3 : 7. How much red paint is needed to make 4.5 litres of the mixture? **[3]**

## Answers

**1.** Divide by 12 **[1]**; **3 : 4 : 5** **[1]** **[2]**

**2.** 1.2 m = 120 cm **[1]**; 120 : 40 = **3 : 1** **[1]** **[2]**

*Examiner insight:* Convert to the same unit first. The answer 1.2 : 40 is not simplified.

**3.** Multiply both parts by 12 **[1]**; 9 : 10 **[1]** **[2]**

**4.** 5 + 3 + 4 = 12 parts **[1]**; 360 ÷ 12 = 30 **[1]**; **$150, $90, $120** **[1]** **[3]**

**5.** 5 parts = 35, so 1 part = 7 **[1]**; boys = 7 × 7 = 49 **[1]**; total = 49 + 35 = **84** **[1]** **[3]**

**6.** 9 parts, 7.2 ÷ 9 = 0.8 **[1]**; shorter piece = 4 × 0.8 = **3.2 m** **[1]** **[2]**

**7.** 12 parts = 180° so 1 part = 15° **[1]**; 7 × 15 **[1]** = **105°** **[1]** **[3]**

**8.** Difference of 2 parts = $24 **[1]**; 1 part = $12 **[1]**; first friend has 5 × 12 = **$60** **[1]** **[3]**

*Examiner insight:* A difference of 24 matches the difference in parts, not the total.

**9.** 250 ÷ 4 = 62.5 g per person **[1]**; 62.5 × 10 = **625 g** **[1]** **[2]**

**10. (a)** 8.4 × 25 000 = 210 000 cm **[1]** = **2.1 km** **[1]**

**(b)** 3.5 km = 350 000 cm **[1]**; 350 000 ÷ 25 000 = **14 cm** **[1]** **[4]**

**11.** Cost per 100 g: A $0.38, B $0.36, C $0.39 **[2]** (one mark if two correct); **Shop B** has the lowest cost per 100 g **[1]** **[3]**

*Check:* 2.85 ÷ 7.5 = 0.38, 4.32 ÷ 12 = 0.36 and 1.95 ÷ 5 = 0.39.

**12.** Make b the same: 12 in both **[1]**; a : b = 8 : 12 and b : c = 12 : 15 **[1]**; **8 : 12 : 15** **[1]** **[3]**

**13.** 8 parts = 40, so 1 part = 5: red 15, blue 25 **[1]**; red now 23 **[1]**; ratio **23 : 25** **[1]** **[3]**

*Examiner insight:* Work out the actual numbers before changing the quantity. The ratio 23 : 25 cannot be simplified.

**14.** 3 + 7 = 10 parts **[1]**; 4.5 ÷ 10 = 0.45 litres per part **[1]**; 3 × 0.45 = **1.35 litres** **[1]** **[3]**

## Where marks are usually lost

- Not changing units before simplifying in Question 2.
- Dividing $360 by 5 or by 3 instead of by 12 in Question 4.
- Giving the number of boys instead of the total in Question 5.
- Choosing the longer piece in Question 6.
- Using 24 as the total in Question 8.
- Comparing prices per pack rather than per unit in Question 11.
- Adding 8 to the number of parts rather than to the number of red counters in Question 13.

## Frequently asked questions

**Does a ratio need units?**
No, but both quantities must be in the same unit when you form the ratio.

**How do I check a ratio sharing question?**
Add the shares. They must equal the original quantity. Then check that the shares are in the right ratio.
