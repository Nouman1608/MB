**These notes condense content area 3.2.1.6 of 9237: "The events of Isra wal Mir'aj and their importance."** The full account is in the [ascension study guide](/resources/oxfordaqa-igcse-islamiyat-9237-the-ascension-of-hazrat-muhammad/).

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Isra | The night journey from Makkah to Jerusalem |
| Mi'raj | The ascent through the heavens |
| Buraq | The white animal that carried the Prophet (pbuh) |
| Al-Masjid al-Haram | The Sacred Mosque in Makkah |
| Al-Masjid al-Aqsa | The Farthest Mosque in Jerusalem |
| Sidrat al-Muntaha | The Lote Tree of the Furthest Boundary |
| Bayt al-Ma'mur | The house in the seventh heaven visited by angels |
| As-Siddiq | "The one who affirms the truth": title of Abu Bakr (RA) |
| Salah | The five daily prayers |

## Timeline of the night

1. Jibril (AS) comes; the Prophet's (pbuh) heart is washed with Zamzam water.
2. **Buraq** carries him from al-Masjid al-Haram to **al-Masjid al-Aqsa**.
3. He leads the prophets in prayer at al-Aqsa.
4. Ascent through the seven heavens, meeting prophets.
5. **Sidrat al-Muntaha**: Allah speaks to him.
6. He is shown Paradise and the Fire.
7. Five daily prayers are commanded (after the number is reduced from fifty).
8. Return to Makkah the same night.

## Prophets met in the heavens (traditional account)

| Heaven | Prophet |
|---|---|
| 1st | Adam (AS) |
| 2nd | Isa (AS) and Yahya (AS) |
| 3rd | Yusuf (AS) |
| 4th | Idris (AS) |
| 5th | Harun (AS) |
| 6th | Musa (AS) |
| 7th | Ibrahim (AS) |

## The five daily prayers

- Fifty were commanded at first.
- Musa (AS) advised the Prophet (pbuh) to ask for a reduction because the Ummah could not bear it.
- He returned several times until it became **five**.
- The reward of **fifty** remains.
- Importance: a direct gift from Allah; mercy and ease; the central act of worship.

## Response of the Quraysh

- Some mocked: a month's journey in one night.
- They asked him to describe Jerusalem; he did so accurately.
- Some wavered in faith.
- **Abu Bakr (RA):** "If he said it, then it is true"; title **as-Siddiq**.

## Why it matters

| Reason | Detail |
|---|---|
| Comfort after the Year of Sorrow and Ta'if | Allah honoured him when he was most in need |
| Five daily prayers | The Pillar given in the heavens |
| Leadership of the prophets | He led them in prayer at al-Aqsa |
| Unity of the message | He met earlier prophets who brought Tawhid |
| Status of Jerusalem | Al-Aqsa, the first qiblah, blessed land |
| Test of faith | Shows who believes without proof |
| Mercy | Fifty reduced to five |

## Using the prescribed passage, Qur'an 17:1-2

| Words | Use for |
|---|---|
| "Exalted is He" | Allah's power; the journey is a miracle |
| "took His Servant by night" | The Isra; honour of being called "servant" |
| "from al-Masjid al-Haram to al-Masjid al-Aqsa" | The two mosques; the link of the holy places |
| "whose surroundings We have blessed" | Blessed status of the land |
| "to show him of Our signs" | Purpose: to show the Prophet (pbuh) Allah's signs |
| "Indeed, He is the Hearing, the Seeing" | Allah knew his sorrow and his needs |
| "We gave Moses the Scripture" | Link to the earlier prophets |
| "that you not take other than Me as Disposer of affairs" | Tawhid; reliance on Allah alone |

## Lessons for Muslims

- **Hope after hardship:** the journey came after the Year of Sorrow and Ta'if; difficulty is followed by Allah's support.
- **Prayer is central:** the one duty given directly in the heavens.
- **Belief in the unseen:** Muslims accept what Allah and His Prophet (pbuh) report, as Abu Bakr (RA) did.
- **Respect for Jerusalem:** al-Aqsa is a blessed place and was the first qiblah.
- **Ease in religion:** the reduction from fifty to five shows that Islam does not place a burden beyond ability (2:286).

## How to build a 6-mark answer

1. State the first reason and explain it with a detail from the night (AO1).
2. State the second reason and explain it (AO1).
3. Add one quotation, such as 17:1 or 4:103, and say how it supports your point (AO2).

**Mini example (five prayers):** "The prayers were commanded directly by Allah during the Mi'raj. This shows how important Salah is, because it is the one act of worship given in the heavens rather than through Jibril on earth."

## Different views

- Majority of scholars: the journey was in **body and spirit**.
- Some scholars: a vision or spiritual journey.
- Both views agree it was a miracle and a sign from Allah.

## Exam traps

- **Isra** = Makkah to Jerusalem; **Mi'raj** = ascent.
- The five prayers were first **fifty**, reduced to **five**.
- The prayers were commanded by **Allah**, not by Jibril (AS) in Makkah.
- Abu Bakr (RA) gained the title **as-Siddiq**.
- Al-Aqsa is in **Jerusalem**.
- Give the **context**: after the Year of Sorrow and Ta'if.

## Self-test

1. What does Isra mean?
2. What does Mi'raj mean?
3. Name the animal that carried the Prophet (pbuh).
4. From which mosque to which mosque did the Isra go?
5. Which prophet advised him to ask for a reduction in the prayers?
6. How many prayers were commanded at first?
7. What did the Prophet (pbuh) do at al-Aqsa?
8. What title was given to Abu Bakr (RA) after this event?
9. Why was the timing of the journey significant?
10. Which verses are the prescribed passage?

**Answers:** 1. The night journey from Makkah to Jerusalem. 2. The ascent through the heavens. 3. Buraq. 4. From al-Masjid al-Haram in Makkah to al-Masjid al-Aqsa in Jerusalem. 5. Musa (AS). 6. Fifty. 7. He led the prophets in prayer. 8. As-Siddiq. 9. It came after the Year of Sorrow and Ta'if, as a comfort and honour. 10. Qur'an 17:1-2.
