These notes condense section 5.02, Direct and inverse proportion, of OCR GCSE (9-1) Mathematics J560, Version 2.1 (August 2026): 5.02a direct proportion and 5.02b inverse proportion. Simple word problems, currency conversion, y = kx and y = k/x are for **both tiers**. Proportion to a power or root of another quantity is **Higher tier only**.

The [5.02 study guide](/resources/ocr-gcse-mathematics-j560-direct-and-inverse-proportion/) explains each method with worked examples; the [5.02 practice questions](/resources/ocr-gcse-mathematics-j560-direct-and-inverse-proportion-practice/) give exam-style questions with mark schemes. For 5.02 with growth and decay, see the [direct and inverse proportion and growth and decay revision notes](/resources/ocr-gcse-mathematics-direct-and-inverse-proportion-and-growth-and-decay-revision-notes/).

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Directly proportional (y ∝ x) | y = kx: the ratio y/x is constant | Cost of petrol and litres bought |
| Inversely proportional (y ∝ 1/x) | y = k/x: the product xy is constant | Speed and time for a fixed journey |
| Constant of proportionality, k | The fixed number linking the two quantities | k = 3.5 in y = 3.5x |
| Unitary method | Find the value for one unit, then scale | 1 notebook costs £1.48 |
| Exchange rate | A direct proportion between two currencies | £1 = $1.30 |
| Best buy | The option with the lowest cost per unit (or most per £1) | 15 eggs at 22p each |

## Which proportion?

| Clue | Type |
|---|---|
| "Twice as many cost twice as much" | Direct |
| More workers, pumps or speed means less time | Inverse |
| Ratio y/x the same for every pair | Direct |
| Product xy the same for every pair | Inverse |
| Neither constant | Not proportional |

## Methods

**Simple direct proportion.**

```
unitary:  5 notebooks cost £7.40 → 1 costs £1.48 → 8 cost £11.84
scaling:  y₁/x₁ = y₂/x₂
```

**Best buy.** Compare the same measure for each option: 6 eggs for £1.38 is 23p per egg; 15 eggs for £3.30 is 22p per egg, so the pack of 15 is better value.

**Currency.** Multiply by the rate to go "forward"; divide to go back. With £1 = $1.30: £85 → $110.50; $65 → £50.

**Simple inverse proportion.**

```
find the fixed total, then share it
3 printers take 40 minutes → 120 printer-minutes → 5 printers take 24 minutes
50 mph for 3 hours → 150 miles → at 60 mph, 2.5 hours
```

**Formal proportion (both tiers).**

```
1. Write y = kx or y = k/x.
2. Substitute the given pair to find k.
3. Rewrite the equation with k as a number.
4. Substitute to find the unknown.
```

**Higher tier only: power and root proportion.**

| Statement | Equation |
|---|---|
| y ∝ x² | y = kx² |
| y ∝ x³ | y = kx³ |
| y ∝ √x | y = k√x |
| y ∝ 1/x² | y = k/x² |
| y ∝ 1/√x | y = k/√x |

Same four steps; when finding x, undo the power or root at the end.

## Scaling effects (Higher tier only)

```
y ∝ xⁿ:     x × m  →  y × mⁿ
y ∝ 1/xⁿ:   x × m  →  y ÷ mⁿ
y ∝ x³, x halved:  y × (1/2)³ = y × 1/8
```

## Exam traps

- **Wrong type:** check whether the answer should go up or down before calculating.
- **k not written into the equation** before it is used.
- **Mixed comparison measures** in a best buy.
- **Currency direction:** dividing when you should multiply, or the other way round.
- **Rounding money** before the final step in a two-step conversion.
- **Constant difference mistaken for proportion:** y = x + 5 is not proportional.
- **(Higher tier only) Power forgotten** when scaling: in y ∝ x², doubling x multiplies y by 4.
- **(Higher tier only) Root forgotten** when solving: if 135 = 5x³, then x³ = 27 and x = 3. The cube root is the last step.

## Quick self-test

1. Is this direct proportion, inverse proportion or neither? x: 3, 6, 9; y: 8, 4, 8/3.
2. 5 notebooks cost £7.40. Find the cost of 8 notebooks.
3. Which is better value: 6 eggs for £1.38 or 15 eggs for £3.30?
4. £1 = $1.30. Convert £85 to dollars and $65 to pounds.
5. y ∝ x and y = 21 when x = 6. Find y when x = 10.
6. y ∝ x and y = 9 when x = 12. Find x when y = 15.
7. 3 identical printers take 40 minutes to print a job. How long would 5 printers take?
8. A journey at 50 mph takes 3 hours. How long does it take at 60 mph?
9. y ∝ 1/x and y = 6 when x = 5. Find y when x = 12.
10. (Higher tier only) y ∝ x² and y = 20 when x = 2. Find y when x = 5.
11. (Higher tier only) y ∝ 1/√x and y = 4 when x = 9. Find y when x = 36.
12. (Higher tier only) y ∝ x³. What happens to y when x is halved?

### Answers

1. xy = 24 each time, so **inverse proportion**.
2. £7.40 ÷ 5 = £1.48; × 8 = **£11.84**.
3. 23p per egg against 22p per egg: **15 eggs for £3.30**.
4. 85 × 1.30 = **$110.50**; 65 ÷ 1.30 = **£50**.
5. k = 3.5, so y = 3.5 × 10 = **35**.
6. k = 0.75, so x = 15 ÷ 0.75 = **20**.
7. 120 printer-minutes ÷ 5 = **24 minutes**.
8. 150 miles ÷ 60 = **2.5 hours** (2 hours 30 minutes).
9. k = 30, so y = 30/12 = **2.5**.
10. 20 = 4k, so k = 5; y = 5 × 25 = **125**.
11. 4 = k/3, so k = 12; y = 12/6 = **2**.
12. y is **multiplied by 1/8** (divided by 8).
