## Questions

### Section A: short questions

**1.** Which ratio uses average inventory?

A: acid test (liquid) ratio

B: rate of inventory turnover

C: current (working capital) ratio

D: gross profit margin

**[1]**

**2.** Revenue is 50,000 and gross profit is 20,000. What is the mark-up?

A: 40.00%

B: 66.67%

C: 60.00%

D: 150.00%

**[1]**

**3.** State the formula for return on capital employed, including how capital
employed is calculated. **[2]**

**4.** Current assets are 28,600, including inventory of 11,400. Current liabilities
are 13,000. Calculate the current ratio and the acid test ratio. **[2]**

**5.** Credit sales were 219,000 and trade receivables at the year end were 27,000.
Calculate trade receivables turnover in days. **[2]**

**6.** Explain why a business's profit margin is always lower than its gross profit
margin when it has expenses. **[2]**

### Section B: structured questions

**7.** Corbridge Traders Ltd sells only on credit and buys only on credit. For the
year ended 31 December 2028: revenue 360,000; opening inventory 30,000; purchases
225,000; closing inventory 36,000; expenses 88,400; debenture interest 3,000.

At 31 December 2028: inventory 36,000; trade receivables 44,000; bank 5,000; trade
payables 27,000; other payables 4,000; ordinary shares 250,000; retained earnings
34,000; 6% debentures 50,000.

**(a)** Calculate the gross profit margin, mark-up and profit margin. **[3]**

**(b)** Calculate the return on capital employed. **[2]**

**(c)** Calculate the current ratio and the acid test ratio. **[2]**

**(d)** Calculate the rate of inventory turnover and inventory turnover in days. **[2]**

**(e)** Calculate trade receivables turnover and trade payables turnover in days. **[2]**

**8.** A business has a current ratio of 1.8 : 1, an acid test ratio of 0.9 : 1 and
current liabilities of 25,000. Calculate its current assets and its inventory. **[3]**

**9.** A trader's credit purchases were 182,500 and trade payables turnover was 36
days.

**(a)** Calculate trade payables. **[2]**

**(b)** State what happens to trade payables turnover if the trader starts paying
suppliers sooner. **[1]**

**10.** Cost of sales was 117,000 and the rate of inventory turnover was 9 times.
Opening inventory was 12,200. Calculate closing inventory and inventory turnover in
days. **[4]**

**11.** A business has a gross profit margin of 40% and revenue of 95,000. Calculate
gross profit, cost of sales and mark-up. **[3]**

**12.** Explain why debenture interest is added back to profit for the year when
calculating return on capital employed. **[2]**

## Answers

**1.** B [1]. Inventory turnover in days also uses it; the acid test uses closing
inventory. **[1]**

**2.** B [1]. Cost of sales 30,000; 20,000 ÷ 30,000 × 100 = 66.67%. **[1]**

**3.** ROCE = profit for the year before interest ÷ capital employed × 100 [1], where
capital employed = issued shares + reserves + non-current liabilities [1]. **[2]**

**4.** Current ratio 28,600 : 13,000 = **2.20 : 1** [1]. Acid test 17,200 : 13,000
= **1.32 : 1** [1]. **[2]**

**5.** 27,000 ÷ 219,000 × 365 [1] = **45.00 days** [1]. **[2]**

**6.** Gross profit margin uses gross profit, before expenses [1]; profit margin uses
profit for the year, which is gross profit less expenses (plus any other income),
so it is a smaller figure divided by the same revenue [1]. **[2]**

**7.** Workings: cost of sales = 30,000 + 225,000 − 36,000 = 219,000; gross profit
= 141,000; profit for the year = 141,000 − 88,400 − 3,000 = 49,600.

**(a)** Gross profit margin 141,000 ÷ 360,000 × 100 = **39.17%** [1]. Mark-up
141,000 ÷ 219,000 × 100 = **64.38%** [1]. Profit margin 49,600 ÷ 360,000 × 100
= **13.78%** [1].

**(b)** Capital employed = 250,000 + 34,000 + 50,000 = 334,000 [1]. ROCE = 52,600
÷ 334,000 × 100 = **15.75%** [1].

**(c)** Current assets 85,000; current liabilities 31,000. Current ratio
**2.74 : 1** [1]; acid test 49,000 : 31,000 = **1.58 : 1** [1].

**(d)** Average inventory 33,000. Rate of turnover 219,000 ÷ 33,000 = **6.64 times** [1];
inventory turnover 33,000 ÷ 219,000 × 365 = **55.00 days** [1].

**(e)** Receivables 44,000 ÷ 360,000 × 365 = **44.61 days** [1]. Payables 27,000 × 365
÷ 225,000 = **43.80 days** [1]. **[11]**

**8.** Current assets = 1.8 × 25,000 = **45,000** [1]. Liquid assets = 0.9 × 25,000
= 22,500 [1]. Inventory = 45,000 − 22,500 = **22,500** [1]. **[3]**

**9. (a)** 182,500 × 36 ÷ 365 [1] = **18,000** [1].

**(b)** It falls (fewer days) [1]. **[3]**

**10.** Average inventory = 117,000 ÷ 9 = 13,000 [1]. Closing inventory = (13,000 × 2)
− 12,200 [1] = **13,800** [1]. Inventory turnover = 13,000 ÷ 117,000 × 365
= **40.56 days** [1]. **[4]**

**11.** Gross profit = 95,000 × 40% = **38,000** [1]. Cost of sales = **57,000** [1].
Mark-up = 38,000 ÷ 57,000 × 100 = **66.67%** [1]. **[3]**

**12.** Capital employed includes non-current liabilities such as debentures [1], so
the profit must be measured before the return paid to those lenders, otherwise the
profit and the capital would not match [1]. **[2]**

## Where marks are usually lost

- Using closing inventory instead of average inventory.
- Forgetting to add back interest in ROCE.
- Including other payables in trade payables turnover.
- Giving a ratio as a percentage or without ": 1".
- Rounding before the final step.
