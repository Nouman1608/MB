This guide is for students who choose **measures to address environmental issues in Pakistan (پاکستان میں ماحولیاتی مسائل سے متعلّق اقدامات)**, the research subject of Theme 4, in Pearson Edexcel A Level Urdu (9UR0). The specification sets three aspects, and you must research all of them:

- **موسمیاتی تبدیلیوں اور آلودگی کے مسائل کے سدِباب کے لئے حکومت کی کارروائیاں**: government action to tackle climate change and pollution
- **قدرتی آفات کے سدِباب کے لئے حکومت کے اقدام**: government measures against natural disasters
- **پاکستان میں غیر ملکی امدادی ادارے**: foreign aid agencies in Pakistan

Research must be based on **Pakistan only**. The [environmental issues revision notes](/resources/edexcel-a-level-urdu-language-environmental-issues-in-pakistan-revision-notes/) describe the main problems and several measures, such as tree planting and anti-smog action. This guide organises the subject around **who acts** (the government and foreign agencies), because two aspects name the government and one names foreign agencies, and shows how to prepare evidence for Paper 1 Section C. A full Section C practice question on this subject, with an unseen text and mark scheme, is on the [translation and reading practice page](/resources/edexcel-a-level-urdu-translation-practice/).

## The rules in brief

- Research all three aspects, independently, from at least two authentic Urdu sources; not your Paper 2 works.
- In the exam: no notes, no dictionary; one unseen Urdu text and a statement; about 320–370 words in Urdu.
- Three grids: research knowledge and analysis (AO4, 20), response to the text (AO2, 10), language (AO3, 10).
- Every aspect is about **action**: **کارروائیاں**, **اقدام**, and the work of agencies. Describing problems without measures will not answer the question.

The key word **سدِباب** means "prevention, remedy, stopping something at its source"; learn it, because a statement will very likely use it.

## Aspect 1: government action on climate change and pollution

Build your research around **laws, institutions and programmes**, with at least one example of each:

**Laws and policies.** The Pakistan Environmental Protection Act of 1997 is the main framework law on pollution; national climate change policy documents and later climate legislation set goals for adaptation and emissions. Find which body is responsible for each and what your sources say about enforcement.

**Institutions.** A federal ministry for climate change, federal and provincial environmental protection agencies (**تحفظِ ماحول کے ادارے**), and courts, which have at times ordered action on air quality and pollution.

**Programmes.** Tree planting, restrictions on plastic bags, anti-smog measures in Punjab, and support for solar power. For each, note the stated aim, one piece of evidence about results, and one criticism.

**Analytical angle:** distinguish **mitigation** (**تخفیف**, reducing emissions and pollution) from **adaptation** (**موافقت**, coping with effects such as heat and floods). Because Pakistan's share of global emissions is small and its exposure large, many argue that adaptation should be the priority. This distinction shows understanding that a list of measures does not.

## Aspect 2: government measures against natural disasters

Pakistan faces floods, earthquakes, heatwaves, droughts and glacial lake outburst floods in the northern mountains. Organise measures by **stage of the disaster cycle**:

| Stage | Urdu | Examples to research |
|---|---|---|
| Preparedness | پیشگی تیاری | early-warning systems, flood forecasting, heatwave alerts, building codes |
| Response | فوری امداد | rescue, relief camps, food and medicine, the role of the army |
| Recovery | بحالی | cash grants, rebuilding homes and infrastructure |
| Risk reduction | خطرات میں کمی | relocating settlements, protecting riverbanks, planting to reduce erosion |

**Institutions.** The National Disaster Management Authority (NDMA) coordinates at national level, with Provincial Disaster Management Authorities (PDMAs) in the provinces. After the earthquake of October 2005 in Kashmir and northern Pakistan, a dedicated authority was set up for reconstruction. The floods of 2010 and 2022 tested the system on an enormous scale.

**Analytical angle:** critics often argue that more is spent after disasters than before them, and that rebuilding in the same flood-prone places repeats the risk. Your research should test this claim with at least one example.

## Aspect 3: foreign aid agencies in Pakistan

**غیر ملکی امدادی ادارے** covers several kinds of organisation. Know the categories and one example of each:

- **United Nations agencies**, such as UNICEF, the World Food Programme and UNDP, which work on emergency relief, child health, food security and climate projects.
- **International financial institutions**, such as the World Bank and the Asian Development Bank, which provide loans and grants for reconstruction and infrastructure.
- **Bilateral donors**: other governments' development agencies.
- **International non-governmental organisations**, which deliver relief and development projects, often with local partners.

**Questions to research:**

1. **What do they contribute?** Funding, technical expertise, logistics in emergencies, and data.
2. **Grants or loans?** Loans add to debt; this distinction matters when evaluating "help".
3. **Transparency and reach.** Does aid reach affected communities, and is its use transparent? Critics raise these questions; find a source that addresses them.
4. **Dependence or partnership?** Does foreign aid strengthen Pakistani institutions or replace them?
5. **Climate justice.** After the 2022 floods, Pakistan argued strongly at the 2022 UN climate conference for a fund to compensate vulnerable countries for **loss and damage** (**نقصان اور تباہی**), and such a fund was agreed there. This connects foreign assistance to the question of who should pay for climate change.

|---|---|---|---|

| آفات کے سدِباب کے اقدام | امداد تیز، تیاری کمزور | [named source]: سیلاب کے بعد امدادی کیمپ قائم کیے گئے | تعمیرِ نو انھی خطرناک جگہوں پر |
| غیر ملکی امدادی ادارے | مالی اور تکنیکی مدد | [named source]: ایک بین الاقوامی بینک نے تعمیرِ نو کے لیے قرض دیا | قرض کا بوجھ، شفافیت |

Fill each bracket with a real report you have read and can name in the exam.

## Worked example: a paragraph on foreign agencies

> دیے گئے متن میں غیر ملکی امداد کو ایک ضرورت کے طور پر پیش کیا گیا ہے، اور میری تحقیق بھی اس کی تصدیق کرتی ہے کہ بڑی آفات کے بعد اقوامِ متحدہ کے اداروں نے خوراک اور صحت کی فوری سہولیات فراہم کیں۔ تاہم یہ پہلو بھی قابلِ غور ہے کہ امداد کا ایک بڑا حصہ قرضوں کی شکل میں ہوتا ہے، جن کی واپسی آخرکار عوام ہی کو کرنی پڑتی ہے۔ میرے خیال میں غیر ملکی ادارے اسی وقت حقیقی مددگار ثابت ہو سکتے ہیں جب وہ مقامی اداروں کو مضبوط کریں، نہ کہ ان کی جگہ لیں۔

(The given text presents foreign aid as a necessity, and my research also confirms that after major disasters UN agencies provided immediate food and health services. However, it is also worth considering that a large share of aid comes in the form of loans, which in the end the public have to repay. In my view foreign agencies can prove genuinely helpful only when they strengthen local institutions rather than take their place.)

**Why it works:** text and research are both signalled; the grant-versus-loan distinction adds analysis; the conclusion uses **نہ کہ** ("rather than") and an obligation structure (**واپسی ... کرنی پڑتی ہے**, feminine agreement with واپسی). In your own answer, replace "my research" with a named source.

## Common mistakes

- Describing floods and smog in detail without saying what the government or agencies did.
- Ignoring the third aspect; foreign aid agencies are named explicitly in the specification.
- Treating all foreign help as the same: distinguish grants from loans, and relief from long-term projects.
- Quoting amounts of aid or numbers of trees planted without a source.
- Writing **اقدامات** with feminine agreement; it is masculine plural (**اقدامات کیے گئے**).

## Exam technique

- Expect a statement that questions effectiveness, for example that measures exist only on paper. Plan one paragraph per aspect, each with a measure, evidence and a judgement.
- Use the disaster-cycle stages to show structured knowledge in a few words.
- Make the conclusion answer the statement directly, and suggest what would make measures work better.
