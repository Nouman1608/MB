**These notes condense content area 3.2.1.8 of 9237: "The support given to Hazrat Muhammad (pbuh) and the Muhajirun by the Ansaar in Madinah and the early Sahabah (companions), including Hazrat Ayub Ansari (RA), Hazrat Hamza (RA), Hazrat Talha (RA) and Hazrat Zubair (RA)."** The full account is in the [supporters study guide](/resources/oxfordaqa-igcse-islamiyat-9237-supporters-of-hazrat-muhammad/).

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Ansaar | The helpers: Muslims of Madinah |
| Muhajirun | The emigrants from Makkah |
| Sahabah | The companions of the Prophet (pbuh) |
| Ithar | Preferring others over yourself |
| Mu'akhat | The brotherhood between Muhajirun and Ansaar |
| Sayyid al-Shuhada | "Master of the Martyrs": title of Hamza (RA) |
| Hawari | A faithful helper: title of Zubair (RA) |
| Shahid | Martyr |

## How the Ansaar helped

- Shared homes and food.
- Paired with Muhajirun as brothers; offered a share of property and orchards.
- Kept the promise of Aqaba to protect the Prophet (pbuh).
- Example: Sa'd ibn al-Rabi' (RA) offered half his wealth to Abdur Rahman ibn Awf (RA), who asked to be shown the market.
- **Qur'an 59:9:** they "love those who emigrated to them ... but give them preference over themselves".

**Effects:** no hunger or homelessness for the migrants; loyalty in faith above tribe; a base for the community; a model of generosity.

## Four companions at a glance

| | Abu Ayyub al-Ansari (RA) | Hamza (RA) | Talha (RA) | Zubair (RA) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Background | Banu Najjar of Madinah | Uncle and foster brother of the Prophet (pbuh) | Merchant of the Quraysh | Cousin of the Prophet (pbuh); son of Safiyyah (RA) |
| How he joined | Welcomed him in Madinah | Accepted Islam in about the sixth year after Abu Jahl insulted the Prophet (pbuh) | Early convert through Abu Bakr (RA) | Early convert as a youth |
| Main deed | Hosted the Prophet (pbuh) for about seven months | Strengthened Islam by his conversion; fought at Badr | Shielded the Prophet (pbuh) at Uhud | First to draw a sword for Islam (tradition); fought in many battles |
| End | Died at Constantinople, about 52 AH | Martyred at Uhud, 3 AH | One of the ten promised Paradise | One of the ten promised Paradise |
| Quality | Hospitality | Courage | Sacrifice | Readiness to serve |
| Title | | Lion of Allah; Master of the Martyrs | Talha (RA) the Generous | The Prophet's (pbuh) hawari |

## Why each matters

- **Abu Ayyub (RA):** first host in Madinah; shows the welcome of the Ansaar.
- **Hamza (RA):** gave early Muslims strength and protection; shows courage.
- **Talha (RA):** saved the Prophet (pbuh) in battle; shows sacrifice.
- **Zubair (RA):** an example of a young Muslim who served from the start.

## The prescribed passage: Jami at-Tirmidhi 3675

| Words | Use for |
|---|---|
| "ordered us to give charity" | The Sahabah answered the Prophet's (pbuh) call |
| "Today I will outdo Abu Bakr (RA)" | Competing in good deeds |
| "half of my wealth" | Umar's (RA) generosity |
| "everything he had" | Abu Bakr's (RA) complete sacrifice |
| "Allah and His Messenger" | Trust in Allah; faith as the first priority |
| "I will never do better than Abu Bakr (RA)" | Humility; recognising excellence |

**Link:** the hadith matches the spirit of the Ansaar (ithar) and of Talha (RA) (sacrifice).

## Qualities of the Sahabah

| Quality | Example |
|---|---|
| Generosity | Ansaar sharing wealth; Abu Bakr's (RA) charity |
| Courage | Hamza (RA) at Badr; Talha (RA) at Uhud |
| Loyalty | Abu Ayyub's service; Zubair's readiness |
| Sacrifice | Leaving Makkah; spending wealth |
| Humility | Umar's (RA) words to Abu Bakr (RA) |
| Trust in Allah | "Allah and His Messenger" |

## Lessons Muslims take from the supporters

- **Share what you have:** the Ansaar gave homes and wealth to people they had never met.
- **Put others first:** ithar is praised in Qur'an 59:9.
- **Stand by the truth in danger:** Hamza (RA) and Talha (RA) risked their lives.
- **Serve in every stage of life:** Abu Ayyub (RA) hosted the Prophet (pbuh) and still joined an army in old age.
- **Do good in competition:** Umar (RA) and Abu Bakr (RA) each tried to give more.

## How to build a 4-mark "explain" answer

1. Name the person or group and what they did.
2. Say why it helped the Prophet (pbuh) or the community.
3. Name a second contribution.
4. Say what it shows about faith.

**Mini example (Talha (RA)):** "Talha (RA) shielded the Prophet (pbuh) at Uhud and was wounded [1]. This protected the Prophet's (pbuh) life at a dangerous moment and shows his loyalty and sacrifice [1]."

## Exam traps

- **Ansaar** = helpers of Madinah; **Muhajirun** = migrants from Makkah.
- Hamza (RA) died at **Uhud**, not Badr.
- Abu Ayyub (RA) hosted the Prophet (pbuh) while the mosque was built.
- Talha (RA) protected the Prophet (pbuh) at **Uhud**.
- Zubair (RA) was the Prophet's (pbuh) **cousin**.
- For the hadith, include **Abu Bakr's (RA) reply** "Allah and His Messenger".

## Self-test

1. What does Ansaar mean?
2. What does Muhajirun mean?
3. Which companion hosted the Prophet (pbuh) in Madinah?
4. Why was Hamza (RA) called the Master of the Martyrs?
5. At which battle did Talha (RA) shield the Prophet (pbuh)?
6. How was Zubair (RA) related to the Prophet (pbuh)?
7. What did Abdur Rahman ibn Awf (RA) ask for when offered half of Sa'd's wealth?
8. Which verse praises the Ansaar for preferring others?
9. What did Abu Bakr (RA) say he had left for his family?
10. What was Umar's (RA) reaction to Abu Bakr's (RA) gift?

**Answers:** 1. Helpers. 2. Emigrants (migrants). 3. Abu Ayyub al-Ansari (RA). 4. Because he was martyred at Uhud and was the Prophet's (pbuh) beloved uncle. 5. Uhud. 6. He was his cousin. 7. To be shown the market. 8. Qur'an 59:9. 9. "Allah and His Messenger." 10. He said that he would never do better than Abu Bakr (RA).
