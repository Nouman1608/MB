Condensed notes for **Part 2: Topic conversation** of the optional Component 5 Speaking test of Cambridge IGCSE Urdu as a Second Language (0539). For fuller explanations and a worked conversation, use the [Part 2 Topic conversation study guide](/resources/cambridge-igcse-urdu-language-0539-part-2-topic-conversation/).

## Part 2 in a box

```
Part 2  Topic conversation
Time        3 to 4 minutes
Marks       20 (of 60 for Speaking)
Content     spontaneous questions about your presentation
Objectives  S1, S2, S3, S4, S5
Language    Urdu throughout; recorded; no dictionaries
```

- Same teacher or examiner as Part 1.
- The examiner should give you the chance to **explain and justify** opinions.
- You are expected to give **natural replies**.

## Two criteria, 10 marks each

| Criterion | Looks for |
|---|---|
| Comprehension and responsiveness | understands questions; natural, spontaneous replies, even to unexpected questions; keeps the conversation going without frequent prompting |
| Quality of language | vocabulary, pronunciation and intonation, structures |

| Band | In short (comprehension and responsiveness) |
|---|---|
| 9 to 10 | very good understanding; natural replies to the unexpected; sustained contribution |
| 7 to 8 | few problems; thoughtful replies; relevant and at length |
| 5 to 6 | understands familiar ideas; may sound prepared; needs prompting to develop topics |
| 3 to 4 | difficulty understanding; limited, brief replies |
| 1 to 2 | significant difficulty; very brief, little communicated |

## ARE: Answer, Reason, Example

| Step | Urdu starters |
|---|---|
| Answer | جی ہاں، ... / میرے خیال میں ... |
| Reason | کیونکہ ... / اس لیے ... / ایک وجہ یہ ہے کہ ... |
| Example | مثال کے طور پر ... / پچھلے سال ... / میرے گھر میں ... |
| Extra (if time) | لیکن ... / اگر ... تو ... / مجھے امید ہے کہ ... |

Aim for 20 to 40 seconds for an opinion question and 10 to 15 seconds for a simple factual one.

## Question types

| Type | Example | Starter |
|---|---|---|
| Reason | آپ نے یہ موضوع کیوں چنا؟ | اس لیے کہ ... |
| Opinion | آپ کا کیا خیال ہے؟ | میرے خیال میں ... |
| Description | یہ کیسے ہوتا ہے؟ | سب سے پہلے ... |
| Hypothetical | اگر آپ ... ہوتے تو؟ | اگر میں ... ہوتا تو ... |
| Future | کیا یہ قائم رہے گا؟ | مجھے امید ہے ... |
| Agree or disagree | کیا آپ متفق ہیں؟ | کچھ حد تک ... لیکن ... |
| Unexpected | (anything you did not plan for) | اچھا سوال ہے ... |

## Repair phrases

| Need | Urdu |
|---|---|
| Repeat | کیا آپ دوبارہ کہہ سکتے ہیں؟ |
| Simplify | ذرا آسان الفاظ میں پوچھیے |
| Check meaning | کیا آپ کا مطلب یہ ہے کہ ... ؟ |
| Time to think | مجھے ذرا سوچنے دیجیے |
| Unsure | مجھے پکا نہیں معلوم، لیکن میرا خیال ہے کہ ... |

Use them once or twice, then answer fully.

## Moving the conversation forward (S5)

- Add a second point: *اس کے علاوہ ...*
- Refer back: *جیسا کہ میں نے پہلے بتایا ...*
- Return a short question when natural: *آپ کا کیا خیال ہے؟*

## Tenses to show

| Time | Example |
|---|---|
| Past | پچھلے سال میں نے ایک مشاعرے میں شرکت کی تھی |
| Present | میں ہر ہفتے شعر پڑھتا ہوں |
| Future | میں اگلے سال بھی جاؤں گا |
| Conditional | اگر مجھے موقع ملا تو میں شعر سناؤں گا |

## Faults and fixes

| Fault | Fix |
|---|---|
| One-word answers | use ARE |
| Reciting the presentation | answer the question asked |
| Silence | use a repair phrase |
| Only present tense | add a past or future idea |
| Reading the card | put the card away after Part 1 |
| Rehearsed answers | practise with a partner who changes the order |

## Exam traps

- Short answers lead to prompting, which lowers the responsiveness mark.
- Memorised replies do not suit unexpected questions.
- Do not speak only about the title; answer the actual question.
- Do not worry about a small slip: keep going.

## Self-test

1. How long is Part 2?
2. How many marks is it worth?
3. Are the questions known in advance?
4. Name the two criteria.
5. What does ARE stand for?
6. Give **one** Urdu phrase for asking the examiner to repeat.
7. Which band describes answers that are brief and need prompting?
8. What does S5 ask you to do?
9. Why add a past or future idea?
10. Give **two** Urdu words that introduce a reason.

**Answers**

1. Three to four minutes.
2. Twenty marks.
3. No. The questions are spontaneous and about the presentation.
4. Comprehension and responsiveness; quality of language.
5. Answer, Reason, Example.
6. For example *کیا آپ دوبارہ کہہ سکتے ہیں؟*
7. The weak band (3 to 4 marks), and more generally the lower bands.
8. Take part in a conversation and contribute to help move it forward.
9. It shows a range of tenses and structures, and makes the answer fuller.
10. Any two: کیونکہ، اس لیے، ایک وجہ یہ ہے کہ.
