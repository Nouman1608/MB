Commerce (0715, 2028): advertising and sales promotion, banking and digital banking, insurance, and
sources of finance with the six commercial calculations. Scenario questions follow the Paper 2 style of
applying knowledge to a named business.

## Section A - multiple choice

**1.** An advert by a national tea board saying "Tea - the nation's favourite drink" is an example of: **[1]**
A competitive advertising   B collective advertising   C informative advertising   D sales promotion

**2.** A gym collects a different monthly fee from each member's bank account depending on classes
attended. This payment method is a: **[1]**
A standing order   B direct debit   C credit transfer   D cheque

## Section B - structured questions

**4.** *GlowSkin is launching a new sunscreen for teenagers in three countries.*
(a) Identify **two** suitable methods of digital advertising media and justify each. **[4]**
(b) Explain **two** methods of appeal GlowSkin could use. **[4]**
(c) Explain **two** cultural or legal considerations that could affect its advertising. **[4]**

**5.** Explain **two** sales promotion methods a new café could use in its first month. **[4]**

**6.** *Amara, a market trader, wants to stop carrying large amounts of cash.*
(a) Explain **two** banking services that would help her. **[4]**
(b) Discuss the impact of digital banking on small traders like Amara. **[6]**

**7.** Explain how an exporter could use documentary credit (a letter of credit) to reduce the risk of
non-payment. **[3]**

**8.** *Harbour Hardware insures its premises worth $250 000. The insurer charges $0.40 per $100 insured.*
(a) Calculate the annual premium. **[2]**
(b) Explain how pooling of risk allows the insurer to pay claims. **[3]**
(c) Identify **two** other types of insurance risk Harbour Hardware should cover and explain why. **[4]**

**9.** State the role of an insurance broker. **[2]**

**10.** The table shows Lotus Bags' figures for one year.

| Item | $ |
|---|---|

| Cost of sales | 60 000 |
| Expenses | 22 000 |

Calculate: (a) revenue; (b) gross profit; (c) gross profit margin; (d) profit for the year; (e) working

**11.** Lotus Bags buys each bag for $15. Calculate the mark-up percentage. **[2]**

**12.** *A young entrepreneur needs $40 000 to open a food truck. She has $5 000 savings.*

## A note on exam technique for this topic

In calculations, write the formula, substitute the numbers and give the unit ($ or %). In "evaluate"
questions, weigh both sources or strategies against the scenario and finish with a justified choice.

## Frequently asked questions

**Which formulas must I learn for 0715 Topic 5?** Revenue, gross profit, gross profit margin, profit for the
year, working capital and mark-up percentage.

**What is the difference between a cash discount and a trade discount?** A cash discount rewards prompt
payment. A trade discount is given to traders who buy goods for resale.

**Is crowdfunding short-term or long-term finance?** The 0715 syllabus lists crowdfunding as a source of
long-term finance, with shares, loans, debentures and venture capital.
