## Answers

**1. (a)** **13** **[1]**

**(b)** **5** **[1]**

**(c)** **144** **[1]** **[3]**

**2.** ∛64 = 4 and √81 = 9 **[1]**; 3 × 4 - 9 = **3** **[1]** **[2]**

*Examiner insight:* Multiply before you subtract. Writing 3 × 4 - 9 as 3 × (4 - 9) would give -15.

**3.** (-3)³ = -27 **[1]**; -27 + 25 = **-2** **[1]** **[2]**

**4.** -2⁴ = **-16** **[1]**; (-2)⁴ = **16** **[1]** **[2]**

*Examiner insight:* In -2⁴ the power applies to the 2 only. The brackets in (-2)⁴ put the minus sign inside the power.

**5.** √25 = 5 and √144 = 12 **[1]**; **5/12** **[1]** **[2]**

**6.** 0.07 × 0.07 = 0.0049, so **0.07** **[1]** **[1]**

**7.** 9² = 81 and 10² = 100 **[1]**; so √90 lies between **9 and 10** **[1]** **[2]**

**8.** 14² = 196 (too small) **[1]**; 15² = 225, so **n = 15** **[1]** **[2]**

**9.** 2¹⁰ = 1024 **[1]**; 1024 - 1000 = **24** **[1]** **[2]**

**10. (a)** ∛343 = **7 cm** **[1]**

**(b)** One face = 7 × 7 = 49 cm² **[1]**; six faces: **294 cm²** **[1]** **[3]**

**11. (a)** **16 807** **[1]**

**(b)** **6** **[1]**

**(c)** 1.628894... **[1]**; **1.63** **[1]** **[4]**

*Examiner insight:* Show the unrounded value 1.628894... before the rounded answer. Never round in the middle of a calculation.

**12.** Side = √20.25 = 4.5 cm **[2]**; perimeter = 4 × 4.5 = **18 cm** **[1]** **[3]**

*Examiner insight:* Take the square root first. Dividing the area by 4 to get the side is a common error.

**13. (a)** **x = 12** **[1]** or **x = -12** **[1]**

**(b)** **x = -3** **[1]**

**(c)** 64, as 8² = 64 and 4³ = 64 **[1]** **[4]**

**14. (a)** **3/10** **[1]**

**(b)** 3⁴ = 81 and 2³ = 8 **[1]**; 81 × 8 = **648** **[1]** **[3]**

**15.** √(64 + 36) = √100 = 10 **[1]**; √64 + √36 = 8 + 6 = 14, which is different **[1]** **[2]**

*Examiner insight:* A root of a sum is not the sum of the roots. Work out the bracket first.

**16. (a)** √169 = **13** **[1]**

**(b)** 15² = 225 **[1]**; 225 - 169 = **56** more tiles **[1]** **[3]**

## Where marks are usually lost

- Treating -2⁴ as 16 in Question 4.
- Not simplifying 5/12 or giving a decimal approximation in Question 5.
- Choosing 14 in Question 8 because 14² is close to 200, when the question asks for a square greater than 200.
- Giving only x = 12 in Question 13(a).
- Splitting a root over a sum in Question 15.
- Rounding the value of 1.05 to the power 10 early, or giving it to 2 significant figures, in Question 11(c).

## Frequently asked questions

**Why does x² = 144 have two answers but x³ = -27 only one?**
Both 12 and -12 square to 144. The only real number that cubes to -27 is -3.

**Does the symbol √ ever give a negative answer?**
No. The symbol means the positive root. A negative answer comes only from solving an equation, where you write ±.
