Condensed notes for subtopic **1.16 Money** of Cambridge O Level Mathematics (Syllabus D) 4024, 2025 to 2027. The [Money study guide](/resources/cambridge-o-level-mathematics-4024-money/) explains each method with worked examples; the [Money practice questions](/resources/cambridge-o-level-mathematics-4024-money-practice/) give exam-style questions with mark schemes. All exchange rates in these notes are invented for practice.

## Syllabus outcomes

1. Calculate with money.
2. Convert from one currency to another.

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Cash price | price paid in full at once |
| Deposit | first payment in an instalment plan |
| Hire purchase | paying by deposit and instalments |
| Gross pay | pay before deductions |
| Net pay | pay after deductions |
| Exchange rate | value of one currency in another |
| Commission | fee as a percentage of an amount |
| Unit cost | cost of one item or one unit |

## Writing money

| Display | Write as |
|---|---|
| 4.8 | $4.80 |
| 12.5 | $12.50 |
| 23.075 | $23.08 |
| 33.9285... | $33.93 |

Round to the nearest cent at the end. Include the currency sign.

## Core calculations

| Task | Method | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Cost of a quantity | unit cost × quantity | 2.4 m at $7.50 = $18.00 |
| Total bill | add each line | 9.40 + 9.00 + 2.70 = $21.10 |
| Change | amount paid - total | 50 - 21.10 = $28.90 |
| Share a cost | divide by number of people | 184.60 ÷ 8 = $23.08 (rounded) |
| Wages | rate × hours | $14.20 × 40 = $568 |
| Net pay | gross × (1 - deduction) | 568 × 0.88 = $499.84 |
| Profit | income - cost | 298.80 - 222.00 = $76.80 |
| Savings | amount × number of months + extras | 85 × 18 + 120 = $1 650 |
| Phone plan | fixed charge + rate × units | 12 + 0.15 × 140 = $33 |

## Best buy

Find the cost per unit or the amount per dollar.

| Offer | Unit cost |
|---|---|
| 1.5 L for $2.64 | $1.76 per litre |
| 2 L for $3.40 | $1.70 per litre |

The lower unit cost is better value. Check that the units are the same.

## Hire purchase

total paid = deposit + (number of payments × payment)

| Item | Working |
|---|---|
| Deposit | $240 |
| 12 payments of $38.50 | $462 |
| Total paid | $702 |
| Cash price | $620 |
| Extra cost | $82 |
| Extra as % of cash price | 82 ÷ 620 × 100 = 13.2% |

## Currency conversion

If $1 = €0.92:

| Direction | Operation | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Dollars to euros | multiply by 0.92 | $500 → €460 |
| Euros to dollars | divide by 0.92 | €460 → $500 |

Sense check: if each unit of the first currency is worth more than a unit of the second, you get a bigger number of the second currency.

### Two-step conversion

If $1 = €0.92 and $1 = £0.79:

```
€460 → $500 (÷ 0.92) → £395 (× 0.79)
```

### Fees

| Type | How to apply | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Percentage commission | multiply the amount by (1 - rate) | €736 less 2% = 736 × 0.98 = €721.28 |
| Fixed fee | subtract before or after exchange, as stated | ($300 - $4) × 0.92 = €272.32 |

Compare two bureaux by working out what you receive for the same sum:

| Bureau | Rate | Fee | Receives for $300 |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | $1 = €0.92 | $4 | €272.32 |
| B | $1 = €0.90 | none | €270.00 |

A is better by €2.32.

### Changing back

£400 at £1 = $1.25 gives $500. Spending $380 leaves $120. Changing back at $1 = £0.78 gives £93.60. The tourist ends with less than the starting amount because of the loss on the exchanges and the spending.

## Presenting answers

- Currency sign and two decimal places.
- State rates in words: "$1 = €0.92".
- Label each stage of a multi-step problem.

## Exam traps

- Multiply or divide? Check by sense: a stronger currency gives fewer units.
- Do not forget the deposit in a hire purchase total.
- Percentage of the original, not the final amount.
- Compare unit costs, not total prices.
- Round money to the nearest cent only at the end.
- Write $4.80, not $4.8.
- A fee may be taken before or after the exchange; read the wording.
- Check that net pay is smaller than gross pay.

## Self-test

1. Six tickets cost $7.85 each. Find the total.
2. A shopper pays with $20 for goods costing $13.65. Find the change.
3. $1 = €0.92. Change $250 into euros.
4. $1 = €0.92. Change €368 into dollars.
5. A deposit of $150 and 10 payments of $28 are paid for a bike with cash price $400. Find the extra cost.
6. A 750 g jar costs $2.40 and a 1.2 kg jar costs $3.60. Which is cheaper per 100 g?
7. A worker earns $11.50 per hour for 36 hours. Find the pay.
8. $1 = £0.79. Find the cost in dollars of an item that costs £63.20.

**Answers**

1. 6 × 7.85 = **$47.10**.
2. 20 - 13.65 = **$6.35**.
3. 250 × 0.92 = **€230**.
4. 368 ÷ 0.92 = **$400**.
5. 150 + 280 = $430, so the extra cost is **$30**.
6. $0.32 per 100 g for the 750 g jar and $0.30 per 100 g for the 1.2 kg jar, so the **1.2 kg jar** is cheaper.
7. 11.50 × 36 = **$414**.
8. 63.20 ÷ 0.79 = **$80**.
