These questions cover section 7.3, Magnitude of a vector, of the Cambridge O Level Mathematics (Syllabus D) 4024 syllabus for 2025, 2026 and 2027. Column vectors are typed as (x, y), with x on top. Give non-exact answers to 3 significant figures unless told otherwise. The method is explained in the [magnitude of a vector study guide](/resources/cambridge-o-level-mathematics-4024-magnitude-of-a-vector/), and the [magnitude of a vector revision notes](/resources/cambridge-o-level-mathematics-4024-magnitude-of-a-vector-revision-notes/) list the key values. Show your working.

## Section A

**1.** Find the magnitudes of (−5, 12) and (9, −40). **[2]**

**2.** Find |(3, −6)|, giving your answer as a simplified surd. **[2]**

**3.** P is (−2, 7) and Q is (4, −1). Find |PQ|. **[2]**

**4.** a = (6, −2). Find |a| and |−5a|, each to 3 significant figures. **[2]**

**5.** The vector (k, 8) has magnitude 17 and k is positive. Find k. **[2]**

**6.** Which of these vectors has the greatest magnitude? Show your working.

(7, −7), (−9, 4), (2, 10) **[2]**

## Section B

**7.** a = (4, −3) and b = (−1, 7).

**(a)** Find |a| and |b|, giving |b| as a simplified surd.

**(b)** Find |a + b|.

**(c)** Find |2a − b|, giving your answer to 3 significant figures. **[6]**

**8.** The vector (t, t − 7) has magnitude 13. Find the two possible values of t. **[5]**

**9.** O is the origin, OA = (6, 8) and OB = (−6, 8).

**(a)** Show that triangle OAB is isosceles.

**(b)** Find the perimeter of triangle OAB. **[4]**

**10.** Each hour, a boat's position changes by the vector (9, 12), measured in kilometres east and north. The figures are invented.

**(a)** Find the distance the boat travels in one hour, and hence its speed.

**(b)** Find the distance between the boat's starting point and its position after 2.5 hours. **[4]**

**11.** u = (2, k) and v = (−4, 2). Given that |u| = |v|, find the possible values of k. **[3]**

**12.** a = (1, 2) and b = (3, 1).

**(a)** Find |a + b|.

**(b)** Find |a| + |b| to 3 significant figures, and comment on your answers to (a) and (b).

**(c)** State the condition under which |a + b| = |a| + |b| for non-zero vectors. **[4]**

## Answers

**1.** √(25 + 144) = **13** [1]. √(81 + 1600) = √1681 = **41** [1].

**2.** √(9 + 36) = √45 [1] = √9 × √5 = **3√5** [1].

**3.** PQ = (6, −8) [1], |PQ| = √100 = **10** [1].

*Examiner insight:* |PQ| is the distance from P to Q, so this is also how to find the length of a line segment from coordinates.

**4.** |a| = √40 = **6.32** [1]. |−5a| = 5 × √40 = **31.6** [1].

*Examiner insight:* Multiply the unrounded √40 by 5. Using 6.32 × 5 = 31.6 happens to agree here, but early rounding can change the third figure.

**5.** k² + 64 = 289 [1], so k² = 225 and **k = 15** [1].

**6.** √98 ≈ 9.90, √97 ≈ 9.85 and √104 ≈ 10.2 [1], so **(2, 10)** has the greatest magnitude [1].

*Examiner insight:* You can compare x² + y² (98, 97, 104) without taking roots, because a larger square means a larger length.

**7. (a)** |a| = √25 = **5** [1]. |b| = √50 = **5√2** [1].

**(b)** a + b = (3, 4) [1], so |a + b| = **5** [1].

**(c)** 2a − b = (8 − (−1), −6 − 7) = (9, −13) [1], so |2a − b| = √250 ≈ **15.8** [1].

*Examiner insight:* In (b), |a + b| = 5 is far less than |a| + |b| ≈ 12.1; combine first, then find the length.

**8.** t² + (t − 7)² = 169 [1]. t² + t² − 14t + 49 = 169 [1], so 2t² − 14t − 120 = 0 and t² − 7t − 60 = 0 [1]. (t − 12)(t + 5) = 0 [1], so **t = 12 or t = −5** [1].

*Examiner insight:* Check: (12, 5) and (−5, −12) both have magnitude 13. A missing middle term (−14t) from expanding (t − 7)² is the usual error.

**9. (a)** |OA| = √(36 + 64) = 10 [1] and |OB| = √(36 + 64) = 10 [1]. Two equal sides, so the triangle is isosceles [1].

**(b)** AB = OB − OA = (−12, 0), so |AB| = 12 and the perimeter is 10 + 10 + 12 = **32** [1].

**10. (a)** √(81 + 144) = 15 [1], so the speed is **15 km/h** [1].

**(b)** 2.5 × (9, 12) = (22.5, 30) [1], and √(22.5² + 30²) = **37.5 km** [1]. (Or 2.5 × 15 = 37.5.)

**11.** |v|² = 16 + 4 = 20 [1]. 4 + k² = 20, so k² = 16 [1], **k = 4 or k = −4** [1].

*Examiner insight:* Setting the squared magnitudes equal avoids square roots altogether.

**12. (a)** a + b = (4, 3), so |a + b| = **5** [1].

**(b)** √5 + √10 ≈ **5.40** [1]. |a + b| is smaller than |a| + |b| [1].

**(c)** a and b point in the **same direction** (one is a positive multiple of the other) [1].

## Where marks are usually lost

- Adding components before squaring.
- Negative components squared without brackets on the calculator.
- Forgetting the square root, or leaving √45 unsimplified when a surd is asked for.
- Adding magnitudes instead of finding the magnitude of the combined vector.
- Losing the second solution when solving for an unknown component.
- Expanding (t − 7)² as t² + 49.
