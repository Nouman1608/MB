## The core idea

**Scarcity**: resources are finite, wants are unlimited. Therefore choices must be made, and every choice has an **opportunity cost** — the *next best* alternative given up.

Applies to all three agents: consumers, producers and government.

## Factors of production

| Factor | Meaning | Reward |
|---|---|---|
| Land | All natural resources | Rent |
| Labour | Human physical and mental effort | Wages |
| Capital | Man-made aids to production | Interest |
| Enterprise | Risk-taking, organising the others | Profit |

**Capital means equipment, not money.** Money is a medium of exchange.

## Needs, wants and goods

- **Need** — essential for survival: food, shelter, clothing, water.
- **Want** — desirable but not essential.
- **Free good** — no opportunity cost, unlimited supply (sunlight).
- **Economic good** — scarce, has an opportunity cost, therefore has a price.

## Production possibility curve

| Position | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **On** the curve | All resources used efficiently |
| **Inside** | Resources unemployed or inefficiently used |
| **Outside** | Unattainable with current resources and technology |
| Movement **along** | Opportunity cost — more of one means less of the other |
| Shift **outward** | Economic growth: more/better resources, new technology, education |
| Shift **inward** | Loss of resources: war, natural disaster, emigration |

## The three questions and the three systems

Every economy must answer **what** to produce, **how**, and **for whom**.

- **Market economy** — the price mechanism decides. Efficient, responsive; but inequality and market failure.
- **Planned economy** — the state decides. Can pursue equality; but slow and often inefficient.
- **Mixed economy** — both. What nearly every real economy is.

## Exam traps

- Opportunity cost is the **next best** alternative, not everything given up.
- Capital is not money.
- A point inside the PPC is **inefficient**, not impossible.
- The curve shifts outward from increased *capacity*, not from simply producing more.
- Scarcity affects rich countries too — it is not the same as poverty.
- Free goods are not "goods that are free of charge" — they have zero opportunity cost.

## Self-test

1. Define opportunity cost precisely.
2. A farmer can grow wheat or barley on the same field. What is the opportunity cost of wheat?
3. What does a point inside the PPC indicate?
4. Name the four factors of production and their rewards.
5. Give one advantage and one disadvantage of a market economy.

**Answers:** 1. The value of the next best alternative forgone when a choice is made. 2. The barley that could have been grown instead. 3. Resources are unemployed or being used inefficiently. 4. Land–rent, labour–wages, capital–interest, enterprise–profit. 5. Advantage: responds efficiently to consumer demand through the price mechanism. Disadvantage: produces inequality and fails to supply public goods.
