OCR GCSE (9-1) Business (J204) introduces how businesses start, grow and operate: what
they do, how they market their products, how they manage people, how they run their
operations and finances, and how outside forces affect them. The course has two
components, each examined by its own written paper. Both papers last 1 hour 30
minutes, carry 80 marks and are worth 50% of the GCSE, and students must take both. The
specification was first assessed in 2019. This page explains how the two components
divide the content and how they work together.

## How the course is organised

The content is split evenly between two components, and each component matches one
paper.

**Component 01: Business activity, marketing and people.** Three sections:

- *Business activity*: the role of business enterprise and what businesses are set up to
  do.
- *Marketing*: how businesses identify and meet customer needs.
- *People*: how businesses organise and manage the people who work for them.

**Component 02: Operations, finance and influences on business.** Four sections:

- *Operations*: how businesses produce goods and provide services.
- *Finance*: the financial information and decisions that keep a business running.
- *Influences on business*: the outside factors a business has to respond to.
- *The interdependent nature of business*: how decisions in one area of a business
  affect every other area.

The final section of Component 02 is the clearest statement of the course's main idea:
the functions of a business cannot be understood in isolation.

## How it's assessed

| Paper | Title | Time | Marks | Weighting |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| J204/01 | Business Activity, Marketing and People | 1 hour 30 minutes | 80 | 50% |
| J204/02 | Operations, Finance and Influences on Business | 1 hour 30 minutes | 80 | 50% |

J204 is not tiered, so every student sits the same two papers, and it is linear, with
both papers taken at the end of the course. All of the assessment is through these two
exams.

Component 02 includes synoptic assessment. That means the second paper can ask you to
draw on Component 01 content, for example the effect of a finance decision on
marketing or staffing, as well as its own topics. Component 01 is examined on its own
content.

## How the parts fit together

Component 01 deals with the parts of a business that face outward to customers and
inward to staff: why the business exists, how it sells, and who does the work.
Component 02 adds how the business produces, how it pays for everything, and what is
happening in the world around it. The section on the interdependent nature of business
then ties the two together.

Several skills run across both papers:

- **Applying knowledge** to the business described in a question, rather than writing
  generally.
- **Using numbers**, such as interpreting financial information and carrying out simple
  calculations.
- **Analysing** the likely effects of a decision or change.
- **Evaluating**, which means weighing up options and reaching a justified conclusion.

Because the second paper is synoptic, Component 01 topics need to stay fresh right up to
the end of the course. A useful check when revising any Component 02 section is to ask
how it would change a decision about marketing or people: how a new production method
might affect staffing, for instance, or how a change in costs might affect pricing. If
you can answer that kind of question confidently, you are preparing for the synoptic
marks as well as for the section itself.

## Planning your study

Most schools teach Component 01 first and Component 02 second, which follows the logic
of the course: you need to know what a business does, who its customers are and how its
people are organised before you can analyse its operations, finances and external
pressures.

A workable plan across the course is:

- **First stage:** business activity, marketing and people, with regular short
  application questions based on real or case-study businesses.
- **Second stage:** operations, finance and influences on business, finishing with the
  interdependent nature of business, and looking back at Component 01 throughout.
- **Final months:** revision across both components and full timed papers.

It helps to follow a few real businesses in the news through the course and ask how
each new topic applies to them. Start exam-style questions as soon as each section is
complete, and use the exam preparation page for paper-by-paper technique and advice on
the synoptic marks.

## Frequently asked questions

**How many papers are there in OCR GCSE Business?**
Two. Paper 1 covers Business Activity, Marketing and People, and Paper 2 covers Operations, Finance and Influences on Business. Each lasts 1 hour 30 minutes, carries 80 marks and is worth 50%.

**Is OCR GCSE Business J204 tiered?**
No. All students sit the same two papers.

**Which J204 paper is synoptic?**
Component 02, Operations, Finance and Influences on Business, includes synoptic assessment, so it can draw on content from Component 01 as well as its own.

**Do you have to take both J204 components?**
Yes. Students must complete both components to receive the GCSE.

**What order should I study J204 in?**
Most students study Component 01 first, then Component 02, because the second component builds on the first and its paper is synoptic.
