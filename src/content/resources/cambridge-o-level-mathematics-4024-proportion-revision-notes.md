Condensed notes for subtopic **2.8 Proportion** of Cambridge O Level Mathematics (Syllabus D) 4024, 2025–2027. The [proportion study guide](/resources/cambridge-o-level-mathematics-4024-proportion/) has full worked examples; the [proportion practice questions](/resources/cambridge-o-level-mathematics-4024-proportion-practice/) give exam-style questions with mark schemes.

## Syllabus outcome

Express direct and inverse proportion in algebraic terms and use this form to find unknown quantities. Includes linear, square, square root, cube and cube root proportion. The symbol ∝ must be known.

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Directly proportional | y = k × (power or root of x); y increases as x increases (k > 0) |
| Inversely proportional | y = k ÷ (power or root of x); y decreases as x increases (k > 0) |
| ∝ | "is proportional to" |
| Constant of proportionality, k | The fixed number linking the two quantities |
| Equation connecting y and x | The proportion equation with k replaced by its value |

## Forms to write instantly

```
y ∝ x      → y = kx           y ∝ 1/x    → y = k/x
y ∝ x²     → y = kx²          y ∝ 1/x²   → y = k/x²
y ∝ √x     → y = k√x          y ∝ 1/√x   → y = k/√x
y ∝ x³     → y = kx³          y ∝ 1/x³   → y = k/x³
y ∝ ∛x     → y = k∛x
```

## Method

1. Write the equation with k.
2. Substitute the known pair; solve for k.
3. Rewrite with k.
4. Substitute to find y, or rearrange to find x.

## Quick examples

| Given | k | Use |
|---|---|---|
| y ∝ √x; y = 12 when x = 9 | 4 | x = 25 → y = 20; y = 10 → x = 6.25 |
| F ∝ 1/d²; F = 20 when d = 3 | 180 | d = 6 → F = 5; F = 0.8 → d = 15 |
| m ∝ r³; m = 54 when r = 3 | 2 | r = 5 → m = 250; m = 16 → r = 2 |
| T ∝ ∛V; T = 6 when V = 27 | 2 | V = 125 → T = 10 |
| t ∝ 1/w; t = 15 when w = 6 | 90 | w = 9 → t = 10 |
| y ∝ 1/√x; y = 6 when x = 4 | 12 | x = 9 → y = 4; y = 1.5 → x = 64 |

## Working backwards to x

Undo the operations in reverse:

```
y = 4√x, y = 10:     √x = 10/4 = 2.5      → x = 2.5² = 6.25
y = 12/√x, y = 1.5:  √x = 12/1.5 = 8      → x = 64
m = 2r³, m = 16:     r³ = 8               → r = ∛8 = 2
```

## Effect of a change (no k needed)

Multiply x by c → multiply y by c raised to the same power (or its reciprocal for inverse).

```
y ∝ x²,   x × 3     → y × 9
y ∝ x³,   x halved  → y × 1/8
y ∝ √x,   x × 9     → y × 3
y ∝ 1/x², x doubled → y × 1/4
y ∝ x²,   x + 10%   → y × 1.21, so +21%
y ∝ 1/x,  x + 25%   → y × 1/1.25 = 0.8, so −20%
```

## Identify from a table

Check which of y/x, y/x², y/x³, y/√x, xy, x²y is constant.

```
x:  1    2    3    4
y:  0.5  4    13.5 32      y/x³ = 0.5 each time → y = 0.5x³
```

## Exam traps

- "y is proportional to the square of x" is y = kx², not y² = kx.
- Square or cube the given x before dividing to find k.
- Keep k exact: k = 5/3, not 1.67.
- Inverse means divide by the power of x; direct means multiply.
- Percentage change with powers: square or cube the multiplier, then convert.
- In context, check that the answer is sensible: more workers should mean less time.
- A length found from a square must be positive.

## Self-test

1. Write "p is inversely proportional to the cube of q" as an equation.
2. y ∝ x and y = 21 when x = 3. Find y when x = 8.
3. y ∝ x² and y = 50 when x = 5. Find y when x = 3.
4. y ∝ √x and y = 15 when x = 25. Find y when x = 16.
5. y ∝ 1/x and y = 8 when x = 3. Find x when y = 6.
6. A ∝ r² and A = 12 when r = 2. Find r when A = 75.
7. P ∝ 1/V². P = 4 when V = 5. Find P when V = 10.
8. y ∝ x³. x is doubled. What happens to y?
9. y ∝ 1/√x. x is multiplied by 4. What happens to y?
10. y ∝ x². x increases by 10%. Find the percentage increase in y.
11. 8 pumps empty a tank in 6 hours. How long do 12 pumps take, assuming inverse proportion?
12. In a table, x = 1, 2, 4 and y = 12, 6, 3. What is the relationship?

**Answers**

1. **p = k/q³**.
2. k = 7; y = 7 × 8 = **56**.
3. k = 2; y = 2 × 9 = **18**.
4. k = 3; y = 3 × 4 = **12**.
5. k = 24; 6 = 24/x, **x = 4**.
6. k = 3; r² = 25, **r = 5**.
7. k = 100; P = 100/100 = **1**.
8. y is multiplied by **8**.
9. y is multiplied by 1/√4 = **½** (halved).
10. 1.1² = 1.21, so **21%**.
11. k = 48 pump-hours; 48/12 = **4 hours**.
12. xy = 12 each time, so **y = 12/x** (inverse proportion).
