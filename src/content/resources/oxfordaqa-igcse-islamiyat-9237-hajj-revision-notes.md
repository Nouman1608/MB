**These notes condense content area 3.1.2.6 of 9237: "The role and importance of Hajj, including its origins and the promoting of the spirit of global brotherhood and unity (Ummah)", "The importance of Hajj rituals and how Hajj is performed, including Ihram, Meeqat and the actions pilgrims perform at sites including the Ka'aba, Mina, Arafat and Muzdalifah" and "The role and importance of Umrah, including how Umrah is performed."** The fuller account is in the [Hajj study guide](/resources/oxfordaqa-igcse-islamiyat-9237-hajj/).

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Hajj | The pilgrimage to Makkah in Dhul Hijjah; the fifth Pillar |
| Umrah | The lesser pilgrimage, any time of year |
| Ihram | The state of purity entered at the Meeqat; also the white clothing for men |
| Meeqat | The fixed boundary points around Makkah where Ihram must begin |
| Niyyah | Intention |
| Talbiyah | "Here I am, O Allah, here I am": the pilgrim's call |
| Tawaf | Seven circuits around the Ka'aba |
| Sa'i | Seven journeys between Safa and Marwa |
| Wuquf | The standing at Arafat |
| Jamarat | The three pillars where pilgrims throw stones at Mina |
| Ummah | The worldwide Muslim community |
| Zamzam | The spring that appeared for Hajar and Ismail |

## Who must go

Every adult Muslim of sound mind who is **physically and financially able**, once in a lifetime (3:97). The journey must also be safe, and the family's needs must be met while away.

## Origins

- **Ibrahim (AS) and Ismail (AS)** built the Ka'aba for the worship of one God (2:127).
- **Hajar** ran between Safa and Marwa seeking water; **Zamzam** appeared.
- **The sacrifice**: Ibrahim's obedience; Allah replaced it with a ram.
- **The Prophet (pbuh)** cleansed the Ka'aba of idols after the conquest of Makkah and led the **farewell Hajj**, showing the correct rites.

## Unity and brotherhood

- Same white clothing: no rank, wealth or race.
- Same rites, same words, same place.
- Millions from every country gather: a picture of the **Ummah**.
- Reminds Muslims of the Day of Judgement, when all stand equal before Allah.

## Ihram and Meeqat

| Point | Detail |
|---|---|
| Meeqat | Boundary points; a pilgrim must not pass them without Ihram |
| Steps | Wash; put on Ihram; make the intention; say the Talbiyah |
| Men | Two white unsewn sheets |
| Women | Modest clothing, hair covered, face open |
| Forbidden | Cutting hair or nails, perfume, hunting, sexual relations, arguing, wrongdoing |
| Meaning | Equality, humility, full focus on Allah |

## The rites in order

1. **Makkah:** Ihram; Tawaf; Sa'i.
2. **8th: Mina** (prayer and worship in tents).
3. **9th: Arafat** (stand from noon to sunset).
4. **9th night: Muzdalifah** (Maghrib and Isha together; night in the open; gather pebbles).
5. **10th: Mina and Makkah** (stone the large pillar; sacrifice; shave or trim hair; Tawaf al-Ifadah).
6. **11th to 13th: Mina** (stone the three pillars).
7. **Farewell Tawaf** before leaving.

## What each site means

| Site | Action | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Ka'aba | Tawaf, seven circuits, anticlockwise | Allah is the centre of life; unity of worship |
| Safa and Marwa | Sa'i, seven times | Hajar's trust in Allah |
| Mina | Tents; stoning; sacrifice | Rejecting temptation; Ibrahim's obedience; sharing meat |
| Arafat | Standing, prayer, forgiveness | The heart of Hajj; the Day of Judgement; "Hajj is Arafat" |
| Muzdalifah | Night prayer; gather pebbles | Remembering Allah at al-Mash'ar al-Haram (2:198) |

## Umrah

| Feature | Umrah |
|---|---|
| Time | Any time of year |
| Rites | Ihram at the Meeqat; Tawaf; Sa'i; shave or trim hair |
| Not included | Mina, Arafat, Muzdalifah, the stoning, the sacrifice |
| Status | Highly recommended; scholars differ on whether it is obligatory |
| Value | A pilgrimage of reward that renews faith |

## Using the prescribed passage, Qur'an 2:197-199

| Words | Use for |
|---|---|
| "Hajj is [during] well-known months" | Fixed time for Hajj |
| "no sexual relations and no disobedience and no disputing" | Discipline of Ihram; self-control |
| "whatever good you do - Allah knows it" | Sincerity; deeds count |
| "the best provision is fear of Allah" | Taqwa matters more than money or supplies |
| "when you depart from Arafat, remember Allah at al-Mash'ar al-Haram" | Muzdalifah; remembrance |
| "depart from the place from where [all] the people depart" | Equality: all pilgrims move together |
| "ask forgiveness of Allah. Indeed, Allah is Forgiving and Merciful" | Forgiveness as the aim of Hajj |

## Exam traps

- Hajj is obligatory **only for those who are able**.
- Arafat is on the **9th**; it is the part of Hajj that cannot be missed.
- Umrah has **no** Arafat and **no** fixed season.
- Do not just list rites; say what each one means.
- Tawaf is **anticlockwise**; seven circuits; Sa'i is seven journeys.

## Self-test

1. In which month does Hajj take place?
2. Name the fixed points at which a pilgrim must enter Ihram.
3. What do men wear in Ihram?
4. Give two actions forbidden in Ihram.
5. How many times do pilgrims circle the Ka'aba?
6. Whose search for water does Sa'i remember?
7. On which day do pilgrims stand at Arafat?
8. Where do pilgrims spend the night after Arafat?
9. Give one reason for stoning the pillars.
10. Give two differences between Hajj and Umrah.

**Answers:** 1. Dhul Hijjah. 2. The Meeqat. 3. Two plain white unsewn sheets. 4. Any two: cutting hair or nails; using perfume; hunting; sexual relations; arguing. 5. Seven. 6. Hajar's. 7. The 9th of Dhul Hijjah. 8. Muzdalifah. 9. It shows rejecting temptation and Satan, as Ibrahim (AS) did. 10. Any two: Hajj is on set days, Umrah at any time; Hajj includes Mina, Arafat and Muzdalifah, Umrah does not; Hajj is a Pillar, Umrah is highly recommended.
