**These notes condense content area 3.2.2.9 of 9237: "The rights of neighbours including the importance of Amanat. The prohibition of backbiting, arrogance and jealousy."** The full explanation is in the [study guide](/resources/oxfordaqa-igcse-islamiyat-9237-social-impact-sources-of-authority/).

## Neighbours

| Source | Teaching |
|---|---|
| Qur'an 4:36 | Do good to the near neighbour and the neighbour farther away, alongside worship of Allah and kindness to parents |
| Hadith (Bukhari and Muslim) | Jibril kept advising about the neighbour until the Prophet (pbuh) thought he would be made an heir |
| Hadith (Bukhari) | Whoever believes in Allah and the Last Day should honour his neighbour |
| Hadith (Muslim) | Add water to the stew and share it with neighbours |
| Bukhari 6016 | He does not believe whose neighbour does not feel safe from his evil |

**Rights:** safety; privacy; consideration (noise, parking, rubbish, light); kindness (greeting, gifts, food); help in need; polite handling of disputes.

**Non-Muslim neighbours:** also owed these rights.

**Social impact:** safe, caring neighbourhoods; trust between families; a good example of Islam.

## Amanat

- **Meaning:** a trust: property, secrets, positions or duties placed in a person's care.
- **Qur'an 4:58:** render trusts to those to whom they are due.
- **Qur'an 23:8:** believers are attentive to their trusts and promises.
- **Qur'an 8:27:** do not betray trusts.
- **Hadith:** three signs of a hypocrite: lies when speaking, breaks promises, betrays a trust.
- **Example:** al-Amin; Hazrat Ali (RA) returned the deposits at the time of the Hijrah.
- **In neighbourhoods:** spare keys, children, parcels, absent neighbours' homes, secrets.

## Backbiting (ghibah)

| Point | Detail |
|---|---|
| Meaning | Mentioning something about a person in his absence that he would dislike |
| True or false? | Backbiting is true; slander (buhtan) is false (Muslim) |
| Qur'an 49:12 | Would you eat the flesh of your dead brother? |
| Harm | Breaks trust, damages reputation, causes quarrels |
| Permitted | Seeking justice, warning of danger, asking for a ruling |
| Remedy | Stay silent; defend the absent; seek forgiveness; ask pardon |

## Arrogance (kibr)

- **Meaning:** thinking oneself better and rejecting truth or looking down on people.
- **Hadith (Muslim):** no one with an atom's weight of arrogance enters Paradise.
- **Qur'an 31:18:** do not turn your cheek in contempt; do not walk exultantly.
- **Iblis (7:12):** the first arrogance.
- **Opposite:** humility (tawadu').

## Jealousy (hasad)

- **Meaning:** wishing that another lose a blessing.
- **Not the same as ghibtah:** wanting a similar good without wishing loss to the other.
- **Qur'an 113:5:** refuge from the evil of an envier when he envies.
- **Qur'an 4:32:** do not wish for what Allah has given others.
- **Hadith (Bukhari and Muslim):** do not envy, hate or turn away from one another.
- **Example:** the sons of Adam (5:27-30).
- **Remedy:** gratitude and praying for the other person.

## The prescribed passage: Sahih al-Bukhari 6016

"By Allah, he does not believe! ... One whose neighbour does not feel safe from his evil."

| Part | Point |
|---|---|
| The oath, "he does not believe" | Great seriousness; faith and behaviour are linked |
| "neighbour does not feel safe" | Safety and trust are the test |
| "from his evil" | Includes harm by hand, tongue, noise or neglect |

## Model answer: a 4-mark question

**Question:** Explain **two** reasons why backbiting is forbidden. **[4]**

> Backbiting breaks trust and damages a person's reputation when he cannot defend himself [1]; the Qur'an compares it to eating the flesh of a dead brother (49:12) [1]. It also causes quarrels and spreads suspicion in a community [1], which goes against the Prophet's (pbuh) teaching that believers are like one body [1].

## Neighbours: a checklist to remember

1. **Safety:** never harm them.
2. **Privacy:** do not pry or spread their secrets.
3. **Consideration:** noise, parking, rubbish, light.
4. **Kindness:** greet them, share food, give gifts.
5. **Help:** visit the sick, support the elderly, assist in hardship.
6. **Trust:** keep what they entrust to you (Amanat).
7. **Peace:** settle disputes politely.

## Four wrongs and their opposites

| Wrong | Meaning | Opposite quality |
|---|---|---|
| Backbiting | Speaking of an absent person in a way he dislikes | Defending the absent; silence |
| Arrogance | Looking down on others and rejecting truth | Humility |
| Jealousy | Wishing loss to another | Gratitude and good wishes |
| Betrayal of trust | Failing to protect what is entrusted | Amanat |

## Linking the Hadith to the community

The Prophet (pbuh) said that believers in their mutual kindness are like one body (Bukhari and Muslim). This image supports every point above: when one neighbour suffers from gossip, envy, pride or betrayal, the whole community is harmed.

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Jar | Neighbour |
| Amanat | Trust |
| Ghibah | Backbiting |
| Buhtan | Slander |
| Kibr | Arrogance |
| Hasad | Jealousy or envy |
| Ghibtah | Admiration without wishing loss |
| Tawadu' | Humility |

## Exam traps

- Backbiting is true; slander is false.
- Neighbours' rights are wider than gifts.
- Jealousy wishes loss to another; admiration does not.
- Include Amanat as the specification does.
- Use the prescribed Hadith with its exact message.

## Self-test

1. Which verse links neighbours with worship of Allah and kindness to parents?
2. What did Jibril keep advising the Prophet (pbuh) about?
3. What does Bukhari 6016 say about a person whose neighbour is not safe from him?
4. What is Amanat?
5. Give one sign of a hypocrite from the Hadith.
6. What is backbiting?
7. What is the difference between backbiting and slander?
8. Which verse compares backbiting to eating a dead brother's flesh?
9. What is arrogance, according to the Hadith?
10. Give one remedy for jealousy.

**Answers:** 1. Qur'an 4:36. 2. The neighbour. 3. He does not believe. 4. A trust: something placed in a person's care that must be protected and returned. 5. Any one: lies when he speaks; breaks promises; betrays a trust. 6. Mentioning something about a person in his absence that he would dislike. 7. Backbiting is true; slander is false. 8. Qur'an 49:12. 9. Rejecting the truth and looking down on people. 10. Any one: gratitude; praying for the other person; remembering that Allah provides as He wills.
