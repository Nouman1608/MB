Cambridge International AS & A Level Business (9609) studies how
organisations are set up, how they manage people, markets, operations
and money, and how they make decisions in a changing environment. The
syllabus covered here is for examination in 2026, 2027 and 2028. The
course is assessed through four externally assessed written papers. Papers
1 and 2 make up the AS Level and count for 20% and 30% of the full A
Level; Papers 3 and 4 complete the A Level and count for 30% and 20%.

## How the course is organised

9609 is built around five functional areas of business, and each area
appears twice: once at AS Level and again, in more strategic form, at A
Level.

| Area | AS Level topic | A Level topic |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Business and its environment | Topic 1: enterprise, business structure, size of business, business objectives, stakeholders | Topic 6: external influences on business activity, business strategy |
| Human resource management | Topic 2: HRM, motivation, management | Topic 7: organisational structure, business communication, leadership, HRM strategy |
| Marketing | Topic 3: the nature of marketing, market research, the marketing mix | Topic 8: marketing analysis, marketing strategy |
| Operations management | Topic 4: the nature of operations, inventory management, capacity utilisation and outsourcing | Topic 9: location and scale, quality management, operations strategy |
| Finance and accounting | Topic 5: business finance, sources of finance, cash flows, costs, budgets | Topic 10: financial statements, analysis of published accounts, investment appraisal, finance and accounting strategy |

The AS topics (1 to 5) focus on understanding how each function works.
The A Level topics (6 to 10) ask how each function contributes to a
business's overall strategy, and most end with a strategy subtopic of
their own.

## How it's assessed

| Paper | Title | Stage | Duration | Marks | Weighting (full A Level) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Paper 1 | Business Concepts 1 | AS | 75 minutes | 40 | 20% |
| Paper 2 | Business Concepts 2 | AS | 90 minutes | 60 | 30% |
| Paper 3 | Business Decision-Making | A Level | 105 minutes | 60 | 30% |
| Paper 4 | Business Strategy | A Level | 75 minutes | 40 | 20% |

All four papers are externally assessed written examinations. The course
is staged. Papers 1 and 2 can be taken as a standalone Cambridge
International AS Level Business, in which case they count for 40% and 60%
of the AS Level. They can also form the first half of the full A Level,
completed by adding Papers 3 and 4 in a later series. Papers 3 and 4
count only towards the full A Level. Confirm your route with your school,
as it decides how the AS papers are weighted for you.

## How the parts fit together

The paired structure is the key to the course. Each A Level topic picks
up where its AS partner stopped: motivation and management lead into
leadership and HRM strategy; the marketing mix leads into marketing
analysis and strategy; costs and budgets lead into financial statements,
analysis of published accounts and investment appraisal. A student who
has a secure grasp of the AS functional knowledge finds the A Level
strategy topics far more manageable.

The functions also connect sideways. A decision about location and scale
in operations has financial consequences, affects the workforce and may
change the marketing position, and external influences from Topic 6 can
alter all of these at once. The paper titles reflect this progression:
the AS papers focus on business concepts, while the A Level papers focus
on decision-making and strategy, where you are expected to weigh several
functional areas together. Numerical skills from the finance topics,
such as working with cash-flow forecasts, budgets and accounts, are used
across the course rather than in one place only.

## Planning your study

A natural order follows the syllabus numbering within each stage:

- **AS stage:** Topic 1 first, since enterprise, business structure,
  objectives and stakeholders frame everything else. Then Topics 2 to 5,
  keeping finance work regular rather than leaving it to the end.
- **A Level stage:** Topic 6, external influences and business strategy,
  sets the strategic frame. Then Topics 7 to 10, revisiting the matching
  AS topic before each one.

Throughout the course, follow business news and collect short real-world
examples for each function, since applying ideas to a particular
business is central to the subject. Begin past-paper questions on each
topic once it is finished. Save full timed papers and paper-specific
technique for revision, when the exam preparation page is the right
place to turn.

## Frequently asked questions

**How many papers does Cambridge A Level Business 9609 have?**
There are four papers. Papers 1 and 2 (Business Concepts 1 and 2) form the AS Level, and Papers 3 and 4 (Business Decision-Making and Business Strategy) complete the full A Level.

**Can I take AS Level Business on its own?**
Yes. Papers 1 and 2 can be a standalone Cambridge International AS Level, weighted 40% and 60% of the AS Level. They can also be the first half of the full A Level, completed with Papers 3 and 4 in a later series.

**What are the five areas of 9609 Business?**
Business and its environment, human resource management, marketing, operations management, and finance and accounting. Each appears at AS Level and again at A Level.

**How long is Paper 3 Business Decision-Making?**
Paper 3 lasts 105 minutes, carries 60 marks and is worth 30% of the full A Level.
