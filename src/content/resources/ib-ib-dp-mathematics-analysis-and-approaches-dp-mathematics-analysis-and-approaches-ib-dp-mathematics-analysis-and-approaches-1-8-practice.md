## Section A

**1.** (calculator-free) Find the sum to infinity of 64 + 48 + 36 + …. **[2]**

**2.** (calculator-free) Explain why the series 3 − 6 + 12 − 24 + … has no sum to infinity. **[1]**

**3.** (calculator-free) Find the value of Σ (n = 2 to ∞) 18(2/3)ⁿ. **[3]**

**4.** (calculator-free) Write the recurring decimal 0.727272… as a fraction in its lowest terms. **[2]**

**5.** (calculator-free) A geometric series has first term 15 and sum to infinity 25. Find the common ratio and the third term. **[3]**

## Section B

**6.** (calculator-free) The first three terms of a geometric sequence are 12, k and 3.

**(a)** Find the possible values of k. **[2]**
**(b)** For each value of k, find the sum to infinity of the series. **[3]**

**7.** (calculator-free) Consider the geometric series 3 + 3(1 − 2x) + 3(1 − 2x)² + ….

**(a)** Find the values of x for which the series has a sum to infinity. **[3]**
**(b)** Find an expression for the sum to infinity in terms of x, simplifying your answer. **[2]**
**(c)** Given that the sum to infinity is 4, find x. **[2]**

**8.** (calculator-free) A geometric series has first term 2 and common ratio 4/x, where x ≠ 0.

**(a)** Find the values of x for which the series converges. **[2]**
**(b)** The sum to infinity is 3. Find x. **[3]**

**9.** (calculator-free) The second term of a geometric series is 4 and the sum to infinity is 18. Find the two possible values of the common ratio and the corresponding first terms. **[5]**

**10.** (calculator allowed) A geometric series has first term 50 and common ratio 0.8.

**(a)** Find S∞. **[1]**
**(b)** Find the least number of terms for which S∞ − Sₙ < 1. **[3]**

**11.** (calculator allowed) A pendulum swings 40 cm on its first swing. Each later swing is 90% of the length of the previous one.

**(a)** Find the length of the fifth swing. **[2]**
**(b)** Find the total distance the pendulum would travel if it swung indefinitely. **[2]**
**(c)** Find the least number of swings after which the total distance travelled exceeds 350 cm. **[3]**

**12.** (calculator-free) A ball is dropped from a height of 3 m. After each bounce it rises to 2/3 of the height from which it last fell. Assuming it keeps bouncing indefinitely, find the total vertical distance it travels. **[3]**

## Answers

**1.** r = 48/64 = 0.75, and |r| < 1 [1]. S∞ = 64/0.25 = **256** [1]

**2.** r = −2, so |r| = 2 > 1 and the series diverges [1]

**3.** First term (n = 2): 18 × 4/9 = 8 [1]; r = 2/3 [1]. Sum = 8/(1/3) = **24** [1]
*Examiner insight:* Taking the first term as 18 or 12 is the usual slip. Substitute the lower limit.

**4.** 0.72 + 0.0072 + …: u₁ = 0.72, r = 0.01 [1]. S∞ = 0.72/0.99 = 72/99 = **8/11** [1]

**5.** 15/(1 − r) = 25, so 1 − r = 3/5 and **r = 2/5** [1]. |r| < 1 as required. u₃ = 15 × (2/5)² [1] = **12/5** [1]

**6. (a)** k/12 = 3/k [1], so k² = 36 and **k = 6 or k = −6** [1]
**(b)** k = 6: r = 1/2, S∞ = 12/(1/2) = **24** [1]. k = −6: r = −1/2 [1], S∞ = 12/(3/2) = **8** [1]
*Examiner insight:* Both ratios have |r| < 1, so both series converge. Discarding k = −6 loses two marks.

**7. (a)** r = 1 − 2x [1]. |1 − 2x| < 1 gives −1 < 1 − 2x < 1 [1], so **0 < x < 1** [1]
**(b)** S∞ = 3/(1 − (1 − 2x)) [1] = **3/(2x)** [1]
**(c)** 3/(2x) = 4, so **x = 3/8** [1]. 0 < 3/8 < 1, so the series converges for this value [1]

**8. (a)** |4/x| < 1 [1], so |x| > 4: **x < −4 or x > 4** [1]
**(b)** 2/(1 − 4/x) = 3 [1], so 2x/(x − 4) = 3 and 2x = 3x − 12 [1]. **x = 12**, which satisfies x > 4 [1]

**9.** u₁r = 4 and u₁/(1 − r) = 18 [1]. Substituting u₁ = 18(1 − r): 18r(1 − r) = 4 [1], so 9r² − 9r + 2 = 0 [1]. (3r − 1)(3r − 2) = 0, so **r = 1/3 or r = 2/3**, both with |r| < 1 [1]. The first terms are **u₁ = 12** and **u₁ = 6** respectively [1]

**10. (a)** 50/0.2 = **250** [1]
**(b)** S∞ − Sₙ = 50(0.8)ⁿ/0.2 = 250(0.8)ⁿ [1]. 250(0.8)ⁿ < 1 gives 0.8ⁿ < 0.004, so n > ln 0.004/ln 0.8 = 24.7… [1]. Least **n = 25** [1]
*Examiner insight:* ln 0.8 is negative, so the inequality reverses. A GDC table (n = 24 gives 1.18, n = 25 gives 0.944) is an equally valid method.

**11. (a)** u₅ = 40 × 0.9⁴ [1] = **26.2 cm** (3 s.f.) [1]
**(b)** S∞ = 40/(1 − 0.9) [1] = **400 cm** [1]
**(c)** Sₙ = 40(1 − 0.9ⁿ)/0.1 = 400(1 − 0.9ⁿ) > 350 [1], so 0.9ⁿ < 0.125 and n > 19.7… [1]. The total first exceeds 350 cm after **20 swings** [1]

**12.** Rebound heights: 2 + 4/3 + …, with u₁ = 2 and r = 2/3 [1]. Sum of rebounds = 2/(1/3) = 6 [1]. Total = 3 + 2 × 6 = **15 m** [1]
*Examiner insight:* Each rebound height is travelled up and then down, so it counts twice; the original 3 m drop counts once.

## Where marks are usually lost

- No statement that |r| < 1 before applying S∞.
- Non-strict inequalities, or only half of a two-interval answer, in convergence questions.
- Rejecting a valid negative ratio.
- First term of a sigma series taken from the coefficient instead of the lower limit.
- Inequality not reversed after dividing by a negative logarithm.
