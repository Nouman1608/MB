Condensed recall for **7.3 Technology and sustainability**. The syllabus points: digital applications for all or part of the accounting records; the need to store accounting data safely and sustainably; the risks of not doing so; the four storage types (manual, data storage devices, cloud services, other digital services) and their advantages and disadvantages. No specific software is required. Figures below are invented; amounts in dollars.

## Digital applications

**All or part.** A business may keep every record digitally or mix digital and manual books, for example a spreadsheet sales journal with a handwritten petty cash book.

| Benefits | Limitations |
|---|---|
| Both sides of an entry posted from one input | Cost of equipment, programs and training |
| No arithmetic errors in totals and balances | Input errors repeated in every report; omission and principle errors not detected |
| Trial balance and statements produced quickly | Depends on power, devices and often internet |
| Balances available at any time (e.g. for credit control) | Hacking, viruses and unauthorised access |
| Easy to back up and share | Depends on trained staff |

## Safe and sustainable storage

- **Why keep data:** to prepare statements; to collect debts and pay suppliers; for decisions; as evidence for tax authorities and disputes; to check records.
- **Safe** = complete, accurate, available, protected from loss and unauthorised access. Links to **confidentiality** (7.2).
- **Sustainable** = limits environmental harm and waste: less paper and printing, sensible energy use, responsible disposal of old devices.

**Quick figures.** A business prints 3 copies of 60 invoices a week for 50 weeks: 3 × 60 × 50 = **9,000 sheets**. Printing 1 copy and emailing the rest: **3,000 sheets**, a saving of 6,000 (two-thirds).

## Risks of not storing data safely and sustainably

| Cause | Result |
|---|---|
| Fire, flood, theft | Records destroyed; cannot prepare statements |
| Device failure, accidental deletion | Recent entries lost |
| Hacking, viruses, staff without permission | Data exposed or altered; fraud hidden; confidentiality breached |
| Missing records | Penalties from tax authorities; disputes cannot be settled |
| Obsolete formats | Records cannot be opened |
| Excess printing; dumped devices | Higher cost; environmental damage; harm to reputation |

**Protection:** regular back-ups kept at another place; passwords and limited access; security software; fire-resistant storage; readable formats; print only what is needed; wipe and recycle old devices.

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Digital application | A program or online service used to record or process accounting data |
| Manual storage | Paper books, files and documents |
| Data storage device | The business's own hard drives, external drives, USB flash drives, memory cards |
| Cloud service | Storage on a provider's remote servers reached through the internet |
| Other digital services | Records kept by services the business uses, e.g. online banking, email, digital invoicing |
| Back-up | A copy of data kept so it can be restored if the original is lost |
| Sustainable storage | Storage that limits environmental harm and waste |

## The four storage types compared

| | Manual | Devices | Cloud | Other digital services |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Key advantage | No power or internet needed; cannot be hacked online | Fast; business controls the data; no continuing fee | Access anywhere; off-site safety; provider backs up | Records created automatically |
| Key disadvantage | Slow; uses paper and space; easily destroyed | Lost, stolen or fails; e-waste | Needs internet; continuing fee; depends on provider | Data scattered; may be kept for a limited time |

## Exam traps

- **Brand names.** Describe what the application does, not a product.
- **Safely ≠ sustainably.** Safety protects the data; sustainability protects the environment and resources.
- **One-sided answers.** Discuss means advantages and disadvantages.
- **"Computers make no mistakes."** They do not make arithmetic errors, but wrong inputs give wrong outputs.
- **Vague risk.** Name the cause, what happens to the data, and the effect on the business.
- **Advice needs a decision.** A combination, such as cloud plus an external back-up, is often the best recommendation.

## Self-test

2. Give two benefits of keeping ledgers digitally.
3. Give two limitations of keeping ledgers digitally.
4. Give three reasons a business needs to keep its accounting data.
5. Distinguish between storing data safely and storing data sustainably.
6. Name the four types of storage system in the syllabus.
7. Give one advantage and one disadvantage of manual storage.
8. Why are USB flash drives a risky way to keep the only copy of records?
9. Give two disadvantages of cloud services.
10. Give an example of "other digital services" and one disadvantage of relying on it.
11. A business posts 250 statements a month at $0.70 postage each. How much a year would emailing save on postage?
12. Link safe storage to one fundamental ethical principle.
