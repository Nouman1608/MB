**These notes condense content area 3.1.2.1 of 9237: "the role and importance of the Five Pillars" and "the meaning and importance of Amr bil maroof (commanding good) and Nahi annil munkar (forbidding evil)".** The full explanation is in the [Key practices in Islam study guide](/resources/oxfordaqa-igcse-islamiyat-9237-key-practices-in-islam/).

## The Five Pillars in one table

| # | Pillar | Act | Role | Quick proof |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Shahadah | Declaration of faith | Entry into Islam; Tawhid and prophethood | Hadith of Ibn Umar |
| 2 | Salah | Five daily prayers | Remembrance, discipline, humility | 4:103; 29:45 |
| 3 | Sawm | Fasting in Ramadhan | Self-control; taqwa | 2:183 |
| 4 | Zakah | Giving a share of wealth | Purifies wealth; helps the poor | 9:60 |
| 5 | Hajj | Pilgrimage to Makkah | Unity; equality; submission | 3:97 |

**Order:** this table follows the order of the specification (3.1.2.2 to 3.1.2.6). The Hadith of Ibn Umar lists them as Shahadah, prayer, Zakah, Hajj, fasting. Any order is fine in an answer if all five are correct.

## Why the pillars matter (six headings)

1. **Foundation:** "Islam is built upon five".
2. **Identity and unity:** shared acts across the world.
3. **Obedience:** performed because Allah commands.
4. **Spiritual growth:** taqwa.
5. **Equality:** same rows, same fast, same Ihram.
6. **Social care:** Zakah and the fast build concern for the poor.

**Ability conditions:** Zakah (reaching *nisab*), Hajj (health and means, "for whoever is able", 3:97), Sawm (not ill, not travelling, etc.), Salah (mature and sane).

## Shi'a note

- Twelver Shi'a Muslims often speak of ten practices (*Furu' ad-Din*): prayer, fasting, Hajj, Zakah, Khums, Jihad, enjoining good, forbidding evil, love for the Prophet's family (Tawalla) and disassociation from their enemies (Tabarra).
- Keep this brief and neutral.

## Amr bil maroof and Nahi annil munkar

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *Amr bil maroof* | Commanding or encouraging what is good |
| *Nahi annil munkar* | Forbidding or discouraging what is wrong |
| *Ma'ruf* | What is recognised as good |
| *Munkar* | What is recognised as wrong |

**Sources:** 3:104 (a nation inviting to good); 3:110 (best nation); 9:71 (believers are allies); Hadith in Sahih Muslim on hand, tongue and heart.

**Three levels (Sahih Muslim):**

| Level | Example | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Hand | Stopping a bully | Only with right and ability; respect the law |
| Tongue | Advising a friend | Wisely and kindly (16:125) |
| Heart | Disliking the wrong | The weakest level of faith |

**Conditions:** knowledge; gentleness; start with oneself (2:44); avoid causing greater harm; use lawful means.

**Hadith of the ship (Bukhari):** if the people on the lower deck pierce the hull and others do not stop them, all drown. It shows that the whole community is responsible.

**Importance:** protects society; shows care; supports the Five Pillars; makes faith active.

**Common examples:** encouraging honesty in business, speaking against bullying, reminding a friend to pray, refusing to join in gossip, telling a shopkeeper he gave too much change.

## The passage: the Hadith of Jibril (Bukhari 50)

| Detail | Use for |
|---|---|
| "What is Islam?" "To worship Allah alone ... offer prayers perfectly ... pay the compulsory charity (Zakah) ... observe fasts during the month of Ramadhan" | Islam is defined by actions |
| "What is Ihsan?" "To worship Allah as if you see Him" | Sincerity behind the pillars |
| "What is faith?" | Belief comes first, practice follows |
| "That was Jibril who came to teach the people their religion" | These three levels summarise the religion |

**Note:** this report names four practices; other reports and the Hadith of Ibn Umar give all five. Do not say the Hadith "forgets" the Shahadah; say the Shahadah is expressed through "worship Allah alone".

## Exam traps

- State the pillar, then its role and importance.
- Do not say all Muslims must do Hajj. It is for those able to go.
- "Forbidding evil" does not mean taking the law into your own hands.
- Do not confuse *iman* (belief) with *Islam* (practices).
- Add a source: a verse, a Hadith or the passage.

## A model 6-mark paragraph

**Question:** Explain **two** ways in which the Five Pillars help Muslims. Apply **one** teaching. **[6]**

The Five Pillars give Muslims discipline, because prayer five times a day and fasting in Ramadhan require them to organise their time and control their desires [1 and 1]. They also build unity and care for others, since Zakah supports the poor and Hajj brings together people from every nation in the same dress [1 and 1]. The Prophet (pbuh) taught that "Islam is built upon five", which presents them as the supports that hold up the whole structure of a Muslim's life [1 and 1].

## Each pillar: an "explain" building block

Use this pattern, *pillar + what it asks + what it achieves*, in a 4-mark answer.

| Pillar | What it asks | What it achieves |
|---|---|---|
| Shahadah | Sincere declaration of Tawhid and the prophethood of Muhammad (pbuh) | Makes a person a Muslim; the base of belief |
| Salah | Five set prayers, with purity and facing the Qiblah | Daily remembrance of Allah; discipline; humility |
| Sawm | No food, drink or sexual relations from dawn to sunset in Ramadhan | Self-control; empathy with the hungry; gratitude |
| Zakah | Paying 2.5 per cent of qualifying savings each year to those entitled | Purifies wealth; reduces poverty; builds community |
| Hajj | A journey to Makkah once in a lifetime, if able | Unity; equality; submission to Allah |

**Tip:** if you give a figure, such as 2.5 per cent for Zakah on savings, make sure the question is about that pillar. Zakah is covered in detail under 3.1.2.5.

## Self-test

1. Name the Five Pillars.
2. Which pillar is the declaration of faith?
3. What does *Amr bil maroof* mean?
4. What are the three levels of changing a wrong?
5. Which verse describes a nation inviting to good?
6. Why is Hajj not required of everyone?
7. In the Hadith of Jibril, what is Ihsan?
8. What does the Hadith of the ship teach?
9. What are the Shi'a ten practices called?
10. Give one condition for forbidding wrong well.

**Answers:** 1. Shahadah, Salah, Zakah, Sawm, Hajj. 2. The Shahadah. 3. Commanding what is good. 4. Hand, tongue, heart. 5. Qur'an 3:104. 6. It is only for those who are able in health and means (3:97). 7. To worship Allah as if you see Him, and if not, He sees you. 8. The whole community is responsible; if wrong is ignored, everyone suffers. 9. Furu' ad-Din. 10. Any one: knowledge; gentleness; start with oneself; avoid greater harm.
