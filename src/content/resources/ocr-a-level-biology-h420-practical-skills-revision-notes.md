These notes condense learning outcomes 1.2.1(a) to (j) of the OCR A Level Biology A specification (H420, Version 4.1, April 2026, first assessment 2025), together with the Practical Endorsement arrangements in Section 5f. For explanations and worked examples, read the [practical skills study guide](/resources/ocr-a-level-biology-h420-practical-skills/), then test yourself with the [practical skills practice questions](/resources/ocr-a-level-biology-h420-practical-skills-practice/). The apparatus and techniques are in the [use of apparatus and techniques revision notes](/resources/ocr-a-level-biology-h420-use-of-apparatus-and-techniques-revision-notes/).

## Key definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Practical Endorsement | Teacher-assessed practical component, reported separately from the A Level grade |
| CPAC | Common Practical Assessment Criteria: the five competencies assessed |
| PAG | Practical Activity Group: one of the 12 groups of practical activities |
| Hazard | Something that could cause harm |
| Risk | The likelihood and seriousness of harm from a hazard |
| Control measure | An action that reduces a risk |
| Contemporaneous record | A record made at the time of the work |
| Primary source | An original report of research or data |
| Citation | A reference that credits a source |

## The skills in 1.2.1

| Group | Outcomes |
|---|---|
| Independent thinking | (a) apply investigative approaches and methods |
| Scientific methods and practices | (b) use equipment safely and correctly; (c) follow written instructions; (d) make and record observations; (e) keep records; (f) present data scientifically; (g) use software and tools |
| Research and referencing | (h) online and offline research; (i) cite sources |
| Instruments and equipment | (j) use a wide range of instruments, equipment and techniques |

## How the Practical Endorsement works

- A **minimum of 12** practical activities, from PAG1 to PAG12.
- Together they must cover all the skills in 1.2.1 and all the apparatus and techniques in 1.2.2.
- Assessed by the teacher against **CPAC**; the result is a **Pass** or not.
- Reported separately on the certificate.
- You may work in groups, but each learner must show independent evidence.
- Keep a record of every practical.

## The five CPAC competencies

| No. | Competency | Key evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Follows written procedures | Follows instructions correctly |
| 2 | Applies investigative approaches when using instruments and equipment | Uses apparatus correctly with minimal prompting; works methodically; controls variables; chooses suitable measurement strategies |
| 3 | Safely uses a range of equipment and materials | Identifies hazards, assesses risks, uses safety equipment |
| 4 | Makes and records observations | Accurate, precise and sufficient data; units and conventions |
| 5 | Researches, references and reports | Uses software; cites sources |

## Safe working: 1.2.1(b)

| Part | Question |
|---|---|
| Hazard | What could cause harm? |
| Risk | How likely and how serious? |
| Control | What will reduce the risk? |

| Hazard | Control |
|---|---|
| Scalpel | Cut onto a tile, away from the body |
| Hot water | Small volumes, stable position, eye protection |
| Hydrogen peroxide | Eye protection, rinse splashes |
| Bacteria | Aseptic technique, disinfect, wash hands |
| Living organisms | Handle gently; follow ethical guidelines |

## Records: 1.2.1(c) to (g)

A good record has:

1. date and title,
2. aim,
3. apparatus,
4. method and any changes,
5. raw data in a table (units in headings),
6. observations,
7. calculations and graphs,
8. conclusion,
9. evaluation,
10. references.

Record data **at the time**, not afterwards.

**Software:** a spreadsheet for means and rates (`=AVERAGE(A2:C2)`), graph software, a data logger, a computer model.

## Research and referencing: 1.2.1(h) and (i)

- Use several sources: textbooks, journals, reputable websites.
- Judge reliability: author, date, evidence, whether primary.
- Cite every source, with author, year, title and publisher or journal.
- In text, use a short form such as (Author, year).
- Do not copy text without credit.

## Link to the written papers

Questions can ask you to:

- describe how to use apparatus,
- explain why a step is included,
- plan an investigation,
- process data,
- identify hazards and controls,
- evaluate a method.

## Writing good answers

- Use the terms hazard, risk and control measure.
- Be specific: "wear eye protection when handling hydrogen peroxide".
- Link a skill to its purpose.
- Quote units and resolution.

## Exam traps

- A hazard with no control.
- "Be careful" as a safety measure.
- Records written from memory.
- Using group data as personal evidence.
- No citation for a source.
- Confusing PAG numbers.
- Assuming the Endorsement contributes to the A Level grade; it is reported separately.

2. What does CPAC stand for?
3. How many CPAC competencies are there?
4. Which PAG covers microscopy?
5. Which PAG covers research skills?
6. Define a hazard and a risk.
7. Give a control measure for using a scalpel.
8. Why should data be recorded at the time?
9. Give two things a citation should include.
10. Is the Practical Endorsement included in the A Level grade?

### Answers

1. 12.
2. Common Practical Assessment Criteria.
3. Five.
4. PAG1.
5. PAG12.
6. A hazard is something that could cause harm; a risk is the likelihood and seriousness of that harm.
7. Cut onto a tile, away from the body.
8. So that the record is accurate and complete and shows independent work.
9. Any two: author, year, title, publisher or journal.
10. No. It is reported separately.

## Frequently asked questions

**Does the Practical Endorsement affect my A Level grade?**
No. It is reported separately, alongside the grade, as a Pass or not.

**Do I have to do every practical on my own?**
You may work in groups, but you need to show independent evidence of your competence.

**Are practical skills tested in the exams?**
Yes. The written papers include questions on practical skills from section 1.1, and these may draw on practical work done during the course.
