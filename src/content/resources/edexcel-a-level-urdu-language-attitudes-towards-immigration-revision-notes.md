Condensed recall for **sub-theme 3.2** of Theme 3 in Pearson Edexcel A Level Urdu (9UR0): attitudes towards immigration (ہجرت (ترکِ وطن) کی جانب رجحانات و طرزِ فکر). For fuller explanation and a model paragraph, read the [Theme 3 study guide](/resources/edexcel-a-level-urdu-language-theme-3-study-guide/).

## Key ideas

- **Welcoming attitudes**: migrants seen as contributors and neighbours.
- **Worries and objections**: jobs, housing, public services, change in local identity.
- **Prejudice and discrimination**: how stereotypes and unfair treatment affect lives, and what law, schools and communities can do.
- **Integration and identity**: whether newcomers keep their customs while taking part in wider society; how second and third generations see themselves.
- **Media and politicians** shape public attitudes.

## How to discuss attitudes

State what people on each side say, give the reasons they offer, then say which you find more convincing and why. You do not need to claim one view is the only reasonable one.

## Connectives to reuse

| Urdu | Use |

| ایک طرف ... دوسری طرف | on one hand ... on the other |
| آخر میں | finally |

## Exam traps

- Confusing emigration (leaving) with immigration (arriving).
- Unsupported claims about whole groups.
- Disrespectful tone: write respectfully about different groups.

## Self-test

1. Give the Urdu for 'prejudice' and 'integration'.
2. Name two worries people sometimes express about immigration.
3. What is the three-step way to discuss attitudes?
4. What does شناخت mean?
5. Which two groups can shape public attitudes, according to the guide?

**Answers:** 1. تعصب and انضمام. 2. For example, jobs and housing. 3. State each side's view, give their reasons, then say which you find more convincing and why. 4. Identity. 5. The media and politicians.
