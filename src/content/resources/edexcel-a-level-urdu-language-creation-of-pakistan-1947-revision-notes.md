Condensed recall for **sub-theme 4.1** of Theme 4 in Pearson Edexcel A Level Urdu (9UR0): the creation of Pakistan in 1947 (قیامِ پاکستان (1947)). For fuller explanation and a model paragraph, read the [9UR0 subject guide](/resources/edexcel-a-level-urdu-language-9ur0-subject-guide/) and the [Paper 1 study guide](/resources/a-level-edexcel-urdu-translation-and-reading/).

## Context

Theme 4 is پاکستانی سیاست کے رُخ (aspects of Pakistani politics). Its three sub-themes are the creation of Pakistan in 1947, the feudal system, and environmental issues (the research subject). Research for Theme 4 must be based on Pakistan.

## Key terms

| Urdu | Meaning |
|---|---|
| قیام | creation, founding |
| آزادی | independence |
| تقسیم | partition |
| ہجرت | migration |
| مہاجر | migrant |
| مسلم لیگ | the Muslim League |

## Key facts

- Pakistan came into being in **1947**, with the partition of British India into two independent states, India and Pakistan.
- **قائدِ اعظم** is the title of Muhammad Ali Jinnah (محمد علی جناح), founder of Pakistan and its first Governor-General.
- **دو قومی نظریہ** (two-nation theory): the idea that Muslims and Hindus of the subcontinent were two separate nations; the basis of the demand for Pakistan.
- **قراردادِ پاکستان**: the Pakistan (Lahore) Resolution, passed in Lahore in March 1940, calling for Muslim-majority areas to form independent states. Check dates and wording against your sources before using them.

## Writing about 1947

Give causes, events and consequences in order, name the terms in Urdu, and keep to facts you have checked in a source. Where you give an opinion, support it with a reason and use a connective such as **نتیجتاً** or **اس کے علاوہ**.

## Exam traps

- Quoting dates, figures or wording from memory without checking.
- Telling a narrative with no analysis.
- Confusing ہجرت (migration) with تقسیم (partition).

## Self-test

1. What does قیامِ پاکستان mean?
2. Who is the قائدِ اعظم?
3. What is the دو قومی نظریہ?
4. When and where was the Lahore Resolution passed?
5. Which country must Theme 4 research be based on?

**Answers:** 1. The creation (founding) of Pakistan. 2. Muhammad Ali Jinnah, founder of Pakistan and its first Governor-General. 3. The two-nation theory: Muslims and Hindus of the subcontinent were two separate nations. 4. In Lahore, in March 1940. 5. Pakistan.
