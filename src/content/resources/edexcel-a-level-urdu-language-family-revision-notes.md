Condensed recall for **sub-theme 1.1** of Theme 1 in Pearson Edexcel A Level Urdu (9UR0): family (خاندان). For fuller explanation and a model paragraph, read the [Theme 1 study guide](/resources/edexcel-a-level-urdu-language-theme-1-study-guide/).

## Key ideas

- **مشترکہ خاندانی نظام** = the joint family system (several generations or related households living and sharing responsibilities).
- **الگ گھرانا / چھوٹا خاندان** = a separate or smaller household.
- Also be ready to discuss: the role of elders and who cares for them, changing roles of parents and children, marriage customs and expectations, and the effect of migration from villages to cities and of work abroad.

## Vocabulary

| Urdu | Meaning |
|---|---|
| خاندان | family |
| بزرگ | elder |
| رشتے دار | relative |
| شادی | marriage |
| ذمہ داری | responsibility |
| روایت | tradition |
| نسل | generation |
| دیکھ بھال | care |

## Building a balanced argument

| Joint family | Smaller household |
|---|---|
| Support and shared costs | Privacy and independence |
| Easier care of elders | Couples make their own decisions |

The strongest position is usually balanced: society is changing, and neither form is simply right or wrong. Give a benefit, a cost and a reasoned opinion.

## Grammar to control

- Perfective sentences with **نے**: the verb agrees with the object, not the subject (جوڑوں نے الگ گھر بنا لیے ہیں).
- Contrast frame: پہلے ... لیکن اب.
- Feminine agreement: مشکل ہو گئی ہے agrees with دیکھ بھال.

## Connectives to reuse

| Urdu | Use |
|---|---|
| اس کے برعکس | in contrast |
| اس کے علاوہ | in addition |
| نتیجتاً | as a result |
| میرے خیال میں | in my opinion |
| ایک طرف ... دوسری طرف | on one hand ... on the other |
| آخر میں | finally |

## Exam traps

- Dropping نے or making the verb agree with the wrong noun.
- English word order instead of verb-final Urdu.
- A one-sided argument with no reasons.

## Self-test

1. What does مشترکہ خاندانی نظام mean?
2. Give the Urdu for 'elder' and 'generation'.
3. Why is the verb plural in جوڑوں نے الگ گھر بنا لیے ہیں?
4. Name one benefit and one cost of smaller households.
5. Which connective introduces a contrast between past and present?

**Answers:** 1. The joint family system, in which several generations or related households live and share responsibilities together. 2. بزرگ and نسل. 3. With نے the verb agrees with the object گھر, masculine plural here. 4. For example, privacy and independence (benefit); caring for elders becomes harder (cost). 5. پہلے ... لیکن اب.
