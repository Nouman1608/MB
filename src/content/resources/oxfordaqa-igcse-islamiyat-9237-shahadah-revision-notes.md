**These notes condense content area 3.1.2.2 of 9237: "the meaning, importance, and use of the Shahadah in a Muslim's life".** The full explanation is in the [Shahadah study guide](/resources/oxfordaqa-igcse-islamiyat-9237-shahadah/).

## The words

*Ashhadu an la ilaha illa Allah, wa ashhadu anna Muhammadan rasul Allah.*
"I bear witness that there is no god but Allah, and I bear witness that Muhammad is the Messenger of Allah."

| Part | Teaches | Linked to |
|---|---|---|
| "There is no god but Allah" | **Tawhid** (oneness) | Key belief 1; 3.1.1.1 |
| "Muhammad is the Messenger of Allah" | **Risalah** (messengership) | Key belief 4; 3.1.1.7 |

- Also called the **Kalimah** or **Kalimah Tayyibah**.
- *La ilaha* (rejects false gods) and *illa Allah* (affirms Allah): negation then affirmation.
- **Shahadah** = witness, testimony.

## The meaning behind the words

- To "bear witness" is to state what you know to be true.
- Both halves are needed: Tawhid tells who to worship, Risalah tells how.
- The conditions scholars give (seven): **knowledge, certainty, acceptance, submission, truthfulness, sincerity, love**.

**Memory aid:** **K**ind **C**hildren **A**lways **S**hare **T**heir **S**weets **L**ovingly (knowledge, certainty, acceptance, submission, truthfulness, sincerity, love).

## Why it matters

1. First pillar; doorway to Islam.
2. Summary of the faith: Tawhid and Risalah.
3. Foundation of the other four pillars.
4. Gives identity and unity.
5. "The highest branch of faith" (Sahih Muslim).
6. Last words bring hope (Abu Dawud).

## Where it is used

| Moment | Use |
|---|---|
| Conversion | Said sincerely, ideally with witnesses; no priest or special ceremony required |
| Birth | The call to prayer whispered into a newborn's ear |
| Azaan and Iqamah | Both halves are called out |
| Each prayer | In the Tashahhud, the sitting recitation |
| Friday khutbah | The sermon normally begins with it |
| Hardship | Repeated as trust in Allah |
| Dying | Encouraged as the last words |

## Shi'a note

Shi'a Muslims often add a statement about Ali (RA) as the friend (*wali*) of Allah in the Azaan or prayer. Many Shi'a scholars say this addition is not part of the Shahadah itself. Sunni Muslims do not add it. Both groups agree on the two core statements.

## The passage: Qur'an 3:18-19

| Phrase | Meaning | Use for |
|---|---|---|
| "Allah witnesses that there is no deity except Him" | Allah Himself gives the testimony | Certainty of Tawhid |
| "and [so do] the angels and those of knowledge" | Angels and scholars join in | Shared witness |
| "maintaining [creation] in justice" | Tawhid and justice go together | Order of the world |
| "the Exalted in Might, the Wise" | Names of Allah | Power and wisdom |
| "the religion in the sight of Allah is Islam" | Submission is the true religion | Result of the Shahadah |
| "did not differ except after knowledge had come to them" | Differences came from jealousy | Warning |
| "swift in [taking] account" | Responsibility | Consequence |

## Exam traps

- Give **both** halves of the Shahadah.
- Do not write "Muslims worship Muhammad". They follow him as messenger and worship Allah alone.
- Do not say a Muslim must say it only once. It recurs in prayer.
- A convert needs sincerity, not a priest.
- Use "use" in the specification wording: give at least two examples (conversion, prayer, birth, death).

## A model 6-mark paragraph

**Question:** Explain **two** reasons why the Shahadah is important. Apply **one** teaching. **[6]**

The Shahadah is important because it is the way a person enters Islam [1]. A person who sincerely declares belief in Allah and in Muhammad (pbuh) as His messenger is regarded as a Muslim, and the other pillars are built on this [1]. It is also important because it states Tawhid, the most important belief, so Muslims renew their commitment each time they say it in prayer [1 and 1]. The Prophet (pbuh) said that the highest branch of faith is the statement "La ilaha illa Allah" [1], which shows its place above all other acts of faith [1].

## Living the Shahadah: quick examples

- **Honesty at work:** believing Allah alone is Lord and sees every action.
- **No fear of people:** no one else has authority in matters of right and wrong.
- **Following the Sunnah:** kindness to neighbours, visiting the sick, gentle speech.
- **Gratitude:** thanking Allah after success.

Use one of these as the "explain" part of a 4-mark answer on how the Shahadah affects a Muslim's life.

## Model answer shapes

- **1 mark "What is the Shahadah?":** The declaration of faith.
- **2 marks "Give two times when Muslims say the Shahadah":** In the Azaan; in prayer; at conversion; at death.
- **4 marks "Explain the meaning of the second part of the Shahadah":** It means accepting Muhammad (pbuh) as Allah's final messenger [1 and 1], so Muslims follow the Qur'an he brought and his Sunnah [1 and 1].
- **12 marks:** examine whether the Shahadah is the most important pillar. Support: first pillar, basis of the rest, in the Azaan and prayer. Challenge: the others complete the faith; the Hadith of Jibril separates faith and practice. Use "Allah witnesses that there is no deity except Him" and "the religion in the sight of Allah is Islam". Judge.

## Self-test

1. Write the Shahadah in English.
2. What does *la ilaha* mean?
3. Name the two parts of the Shahadah.
4. Name two conditions of the Shahadah.
5. Who bears witness in Qur'an 3:18?
6. Give two occasions when a Muslim uses the Shahadah.
7. What did the Prophet (pbuh) say about the last words of a person?
8. What does *Kalimah* mean?
9. Which part of prayer includes the Shahadah?
10. Why does the Shahadah need both halves?

**Answers:** 1. "I bear witness that there is no god but Allah, and I bear witness that Muhammad is the Messenger of Allah." 2. "No god". 3. Tawhid and Risalah. 4. Any two from knowledge, certainty, acceptance, submission, truthfulness, sincerity, love. 5. Allah, the angels and those of knowledge. 6. Any two: becoming Muslim; in the Azaan; in each prayer; newborn; at death. 7. Whoever's last words are "La ilaha illa Allah" will enter Paradise (Abu Dawud). 8. The word (of faith). 9. The Tashahhud. 10. Tawhid shows whom to worship and Risalah shows how, through the messenger.
