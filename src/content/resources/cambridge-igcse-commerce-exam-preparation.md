Cambridge IGCSE Commerce 0715 has two compulsory, externally assessed papers: Paper 1 Multiple Choice (1 hour, 40 marks, 30%) and Paper 2 Written (2 hours, 80 marks, 70%), which contains structured questions of varying length. The syllabus is not tiered, grades run A* to G, and this edition is for examination in 2028 only. The six content areas run from commerce and production to sustainability and ethics, and the site has study material for several of them, including [Commerce and Production](/resources/igcse-cambridge-commerce-commerce-and-production/), [Globalisation of Trade](/resources/igcse-cambridge-commerce-globalisation-of-trade/) and [Logistics in Commerce](/resources/cambridge-igcse-commerce-logistics-in-commerce-study-guide/).

## Paper 1: Multiple Choice

Paper 1 is 60 minutes for 40 marks, so there are 60 / 40 = 1.5 minutes, or 90 seconds, per mark. It is compulsory and can involve simple calculations. It counts for 30% of the grade, so it is worth taking seriously even though Paper 2 is larger. **Preparation priority**: because the questions can draw on any of the six content areas, spread your practice across them, and use wrong answers in practice as a map of what to revise. In the exam, do the questions you can answer quickly first, then return to the ones that need a calculation or a second reading, and give every question an answer.

## Paper 2: Written

Paper 2 is 120 minutes for 80 marks and 70% of the grade, so 120 / 80 = 1.5 minutes per mark, the same rate as Paper 1. It consists of compulsory structured questions of varying length, and these too may involve simple calculations. Because it carries more than twice the weight of Paper 1 (70% against 30%), most of your revision effort should aim at writing clear, developed answers. **Preparation priority**: learn each topic well enough to explain it in your own words, and practise linking it to a stated business or trading situation.

## Timing arithmetic for both papers

| Paper | Duration | Marks | Weighting | Minutes per mark |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Paper 1 | 60 min | 40 | 30% | 1.5 |
| Paper 2 | 120 min | 80 | 70% | 1.5 |

At 1.5 minutes per mark, a 2-mark part merits about 3 minutes, a 4-mark part about 6 minutes and an 8-mark part about 12 minutes. The two papers together total 120 marks over 180 minutes, so 3 hours of examination in all. Weighted contribution per raw mark is not the same: 30% over 40 marks is 0.75% per mark on Paper 1, whereas 70% over 80 marks is 0.875% per mark on Paper 2.

## Answering structured questions

Look at the mark allocation first. Parts worth one or two marks need a short, precise point. Longer parts usually need a point, a reason and a link to the situation described. Where a question names a particular business, make sure at least part of your answer refers to it, not to commerce in general. Because the syllabus topics (for example logistics, aids to trade and sustainability) overlap in real trade, a good answer can use a concept from one area to support a point in another.

## Command words

State and identify ask for a fact. Explain asks for a fact with a reason. Describe asks for features. Compare needs both sides discussed. Calculate wants working shown. **Preparation priority**: underline the command word before planning your answer, and check afterwards that your response does what it asks.

## Worked practice scenario: a trade discount and mark-up calculation

Question (original): "A retailer is offered a washing machine with a list price of $400 and a trade discount of 15%. The retailer adds a mark-up of 30% on the price it pays. (a) Calculate the price the retailer pays. (b) Calculate the retailer's selling price. (c) Explain one reason why the manufacturer offers trade discounts."

```
(a) Trade discount = 15% of $400 = 0.15 x 400 = $60
    Price paid = 400 - 60 = $340

(b) Mark-up = 30% of $340 = 0.30 x 340 = $102
    Selling price = 340 + 102 = $442

(c) A trade discount encourages retailers to buy in larger
    quantities or to buy regularly from that manufacturer,
    which helps the manufacturer plan production and increase sales.
```

Notice that the mark-up is calculated on the price the retailer pays, as the question states, not on the list price. Reading that wording carefully is where the marks are.

## Before/during exam checklist

- Know the paper structure: Paper 1 is 1 hour, Paper 2 is 2 hours.
- Practise percentages, discounts and profit calculations until they are quick.
- Revise all six content areas, as both papers are compulsory and cover the subject broadly.
- On Paper 1, answer every question; do not leave blanks.
- On Paper 2, read the mark allocation before you start writing.
- Underline the command word and the name of the business in each question.
- Show working for every calculation.
- Use spare time to check arithmetic and the wording of the question.

## Self-test

3. Which of the two papers is more heavily weighted, and by how much?
4. In the worked scenario, what price does the retailer pay?

## Frequently asked questions

**What papers make up Cambridge IGCSE Commerce 0715?**
Paper 1 Multiple Choice (1 hour, 40 marks, 30%) and Paper 2 Written (2 hours, 80 marks, 70%). Both are compulsory and externally assessed.

**Are there calculations in the exam?**
Yes. Both papers may involve simple calculations, so practise routine percentage and money calculations.

**Is the syllabus tiered?**
No. All candidates take the same papers and grades run A* to G.

**How much time per mark do I have?**
On both papers it is 1.5 minutes per mark: 60 minutes for 40 marks and 120 minutes for 80 marks.

**Which examination year does this edition cover?**
This syllabus edition is for examination in 2028 only, and it had not yet had its first sitting at the verification date.
