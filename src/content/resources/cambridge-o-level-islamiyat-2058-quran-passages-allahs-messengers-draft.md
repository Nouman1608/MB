# Allah's Messengers: passages 11 to 15 of Appendix 1

This is the third of the three groups in Appendix 1. Related pages: [Major Themes of the Qur'an study guide](/resources/cambridge-o-level-islamiyat-major-themes-of-the-quran/) and [revision notes](/resources/cambridge-o-level-islamiyat-major-themes-of-the-quran-revision-notes/).

## How Question 1 is set (applies to every passage below)

The syllabus (Paper 1, Question 1) says three passages are set from Appendix 1 and candidates may choose any two, for 8 marks. Section 5.1 says candidates explore (1) the major theme or themes in each passage and (2) the importance of those themes in the lives of Muslims today, and must show knowledge of the original Arabic as well as the translation. The site's own record of the 2058/11 June 2024 paper (decision log E697) gives the wording "(a) themes [4], (b) importance in a Muslim's life today [4]". The 4 and 4 split is **not re-verified against the paper in this draft**.

**Answer pattern for each passage you choose:** (1) name the theme in the passage's own terms; (2) point to the verse number or Arabic word that shows it; (3) for part (b), give one concrete way a Muslim acts or thinks differently today because of it. Do not narrate the passage line by line.

## 11. Sura 2.30-37

**Syllabus group:** Allah's Messengers (Appendix 1, passage 11)

**Themes it addresses:** Adam as vicegerent on earth; the angels' question; Adam taught the names; Iblis's refusal; the tree and the slip; Adam learning words from his Lord and being forgiven.

**Link to the group theme:** The syllabus lists the story of Adam in the Messengers group. How far the course presents Adam as a prophet is for the teacher to confirm.

**Key Arabic terms to learn (transliteration; teacher to check):** khalifa, Iblis, Shaytan, tawba.

**How the exam asks about it:** As one of three passages printed in Arabic and English, you may choose it. Part (a) asks for its main theme or themes; part (b) asks why those themes matter to Muslims today. It is the longest set passage: choose the two or three strongest themes (knowledge, temptation and free will, repentance and mercy).

**How to answer:** Make each stage a theme and cite verse numbers (30, 31-33, 34, 35-36, 37). In (b), link repentance and responsibility as khalifa.

**Teacher to confirm:** themes above, terms, and any example of practice named in (b).

## 12. Sura 6.75-79

**Syllabus group:** Allah's Messengers (Appendix 1, passage 12)

**Themes it addresses:** Abraham shown God's power and laws in the heavens and earth; his reasoning about star, moon and sun, each of which sets; his turning to the Creator and declaring freedom from giving partners to God.

**Link to the group theme:** Abraham models how a prophet reaches tawhid by reasoning and then declares it publicly.

**Key Arabic terms to learn (transliteration; teacher to check):** Ibrahim, afala (set), muqin (certainty), shirk.

**Arabic (copied from the site's existing Paper 1 practice page, recorded there as Tanzil text checked against the syllabus; not re-checked here):**

**How the exam asks about it:** The decision log (E697) records this passage as set in the 2058/11 June 2024 paper; that is a site record, not checked against the paper for this draft. As one of three passages printed in Arabic and English, you may choose it. Part (a) asks for its main theme or themes; part (b) asks why those themes matter to Muslims today.

**How to answer:** Name the themes (tawhid, reason and certainty, rejecting shirk). Cite verses 76-78 for the three stages and 79 for the conclusion. In (b), link to using reason and holding to one God today.

**Teacher to confirm:** themes above, terms, and any example of practice named in (b).

## 13. Sura 5.110

**Syllabus group:** Allah's Messengers (Appendix 1, passage 13)

**Themes it addresses:** God reminding Jesus, son of Mary, of His favours: strengthening with the holy spirit, teaching the Book, Wisdom, Law and Gospel, miracles done by God's leave, and protection from those who rejected the signs.

**Link to the group theme:** A messenger's abilities come from God's permission; the passage presents Jesus as a prophet sent with signs.

**Key Arabic terms to learn (transliteration; teacher to check):** 'Isa, ruh al-qudus, bi-idhni (by My leave).

**How the exam asks about it:** As one of three passages printed in Arabic and English, you may choose it. Part (a) asks for its main theme or themes; part (b) asks why those themes matter to Muslims today.

**How to answer:** Name the themes (support and teaching from God; miracles by God's leave) and cite the verse's clauses. In (b), link to Muslim respect for Jesus as a prophet.

**Teacher to confirm:** themes above, terms, and any example of practice named in (b).

## 14. Sura 93

**Syllabus group:** Allah's Messengers (Appendix 1, passage 14)

**Themes it addresses:** God's assurance to the Prophet (pbuh) that He has not forsaken him; the Hereafter better than the present; God's past care (orphan, wandering, in need); the instruction to treat the orphan and beggar well and to speak of God's bounty.

**Link to the group theme:** Addressed to the Prophet (pbuh): comfort, remembered favours, and commands that also guide all Muslims.

**Key Arabic terms to learn (transliteration; teacher to check):** yatim, sa'il, ni'ma.

**How the exam asks about it:** As one of three passages printed in Arabic and English, you may choose it. Part (a) asks for its main theme or themes; part (b) asks why those themes matter to Muslims today.

**How to answer:** Two themes: reassurance, and gratitude shown through kindness to the vulnerable. Cite verses 3-5, 6-8 and 9-11. In (b), care for orphans and the needy.

**Teacher to confirm:** themes above, terms, and any example of practice named in (b).

## 15. Sura 108

**Syllabus group:** Allah's Messengers (Appendix 1, passage 15)

**Themes it addresses:** God has given the Prophet (pbuh) abundance; he should pray and sacrifice to his Lord; the one who hates him will be cut off.

**Link to the group theme:** The shortest passage: God's favour and the Prophet's response of worship.

**Key Arabic terms to learn (transliteration; teacher to check):** al-Kawthar, salli, wanhar, abtar.

**How the exam asks about it:** As one of three passages printed in Arabic and English, you may choose it. Part (a) asks for its main theme or themes; part (b) asks why those themes matter to Muslims today.

**How to answer:** Give two themes with verse numbers (1; 2; 3). In (b), link gratitude shown in prayer and sacrifice.

**Teacher to confirm:** themes above, terms, and any example of practice named in (b).
