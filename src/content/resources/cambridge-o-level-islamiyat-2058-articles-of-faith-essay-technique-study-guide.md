# Writing a Topic 4 essay

**Paper 2 Topic 4, The Articles of Faith and the Pillars of Islam, is examined by one or two questions of 14 marks, usually in more than one part, requiring essay-length answers.** Part (a) is marked for knowledge (AO1) with a maximum of 10 marks; part (b), for understanding (AO2), carries the remaining 4 marks on the syllabus levels. Candidates answer Question 1, Question 2 and two others from the five on the paper, so Topic 4 is one of the choices for the final two.

Related pages: [Articles of Faith and Pillars of Islam study guide](/resources/cambridge-o-level-islamiyat-articles-of-faith-and-pillars-of-islam/) and [revision notes](/resources/cambridge-o-level-islamiyat-articles-of-faith-and-pillars-of-islam-revision-notes/).

## What the syllabus lists

- **The six Articles of Faith:** God, including what Muslims believe about Him; angels, their nature and duties; books, their contents and purpose; prophets, their character and function; God's predestination and decree, its meaning and significance; Resurrection and the last day, the main events and its significance.
- **Jihad** in its range of meanings: physical, mental and spiritual.
- **The five Pillars:** shahada; salat (preparations, performance, importance, congregational prayers on Fridays and festivals, times of prayer, place of prayer, private prayer, delayed prayer); zakat; sawm (the way it is observed, its significance and those exempted); hajj (main observances and their significance).

Candidates should know each belief and observance and also understand its significance for individual Muslims and communities.

## How the part (a) and part (b) split works

| Part | Marks | What earns the top level |
|---|---|---|
| (a) | 10 | Accurate, detailed, well-developed points; Level 4 is 8 to 10 marks and is likely to quote Qur'an verses and Hadiths |
| (b) | 4 | Clear understanding of the question, your own reasoning shown (Level 2 is 3 to 4 marks) |

A typical pairing is "(a) Describe ... (b) Explain the importance of ... for Muslims today". Write (a) as facts in order; write (b) as consequences.

## Outline 1: Fasting (sawm)

*(a) Facts:* fasting in Ramadan from dawn to sunset, abstaining from food, drink and marital relations and from bad words and deeds; those who are exempt: the sick, travellers, the elderly, children, pregnant and nursing women and menstruating women, who make up days later or pay fidya where they cannot. *(b) Significance:* self-control, gratitude, empathy for the poor and increased worship, especially on Laylat al-Qadr. (All from the site's reviewed page.)

## Outline 2: Belief in angels

*(a) Facts:* created from light, do not eat or sleep, obey Allah without free will; Jibril delivered revelation, Mika'il oversees provision and rain, Israfil will sound the trumpet, the Angel of Death takes souls, the Kiraman Katibin record deeds, Munkar and Nakir question the dead. *(b) Significance:* Muslims know they are never unobserved, which encourages honesty even in private.

## Outline 3: Predestination (al-qadr)

*(a) Facts:* Allah knows everything before it happens, has recorded it, wills it and creates it, and humans still make real choices and are judged on them. *(b) Significance:* patience in hardship, humility in success and continued effort ("tie your camel and trust in Allah").

## Common mistakes

- Listing the Pillars with no detail on how each is performed.
- Writing about jihad as only physical struggle: the syllabus lists physical, mental and spiritual meanings.
- A part (b) that describes the practice again instead of explaining its significance.
- Covering only the five Pillars and ignoring the six Articles.
