These notes condense subtopic **1.17 Exponential growth and decay** of the Cambridge IGCSE Mathematics 0580 syllabus for 2025, 2026 and 2027. It is **Extended only**, so it is examined on Papers 2 and 4. The syllabus statement is "Use exponential growth and decay", with depreciation and population change as examples, and it notes that knowledge of e is not required. Worked examples are in the [exponential growth and decay study guide](/resources/cambridge-igcse-mathematics-0580-exponential-growth-and-decay/), and exam-style questions are in the [exponential growth and decay practice questions](/resources/cambridge-igcse-mathematics-0580-exponential-growth-and-decay-practice/).

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Exponential growth | Repeated multiplication by the same factor greater than 1 |
| Exponential decay | Repeated multiplication by the same factor between 0 and 1 |
| Multiplier | The factor for one period, such as 1.06 or 0.88 |
| Period | The time over which one change happens: a year, a month, 20 minutes |
| Depreciation | A fall in value over time, usually by a percentage each year |
| Doubling or halving time | The length of one period when the multiplier is 2 or 0.5 |
| Linear change | The same amount added or subtracted each period |

## The model

```
V = A × m^n
A = starting value (n = 0)
m = multiplier for ONE period
n = number of periods
```

| Wording | m |
|---|---|
| grows by r% per period | 1 + r/100 |
| falls by r% per period | 1 − r/100 |
| doubles each period | 2 |
| triples each period | 3 |
| halves each period | 0.5 |
| loses a fifth each period | 0.8 |

## Counting periods

- n counts **changes**, not points in time. After 3 years of yearly change, n = 3.
- If the change happens every 20 minutes, 2 hours is 6 periods.
- If the value halves every 3 days, 12 days is 4 periods.

## Reading a given formula

- V = 3500 × 1.04ⁿ: starts at 3500, increases by 4% per period.
- V = 12 000 × 0.85ⁿ: starts at 12 000, decreases by 15% per period.
- m > 1 means growth; 0 < m < 1 means decay.

## Overall change over several periods

```
overall multiplier = m^n
overall % change   = (m^n - 1) × 100     (negative for a decrease)
```

Two 15% increases: 1.15² = 1.3225, an overall increase of **32.25%**, not 30%.

## "When does it pass?" by trial

1. Substitute whole numbers for n.
2. Find the values just before and just after the target.
3. Write both in your answer.
4. Answer the question asked ("after how many years", "in which year").

Example: 1800 × 1.12ⁿ passes 3000 between n = 4 (2832) and n = 5 (3172), so **5 years**.

## Graph features

- The graph of V = A × mⁿ crosses the vertical axis at the starting value A.
- Growth (m > 1): the curve gets steeper as n increases.
- Decay (0 < m < 1): the curve gets less steep and approaches zero but never reaches it.

## Linear or exponential?

| Linear | Exponential |
|---|---|
| same **amount** each period | same **percentage** or factor each period |
| "loses $500 per year" | "loses 8% of its value each year" |
| straight-line graph | curved graph |
| V = A + kn | V = A × mⁿ |

## Exam traps

- An 18% decrease entered as × 0.18 instead of × 0.82.
- A 12% increase entered as × 1.2 instead of × 1.12.
- Simple (linear) change used: A × r × n.
- Hours used as n when the change happens every 20 minutes.
- Each year's value rounded before the next year is calculated.
- Overall percentage found by adding the yearly percentages.
- A trial table showing only one side of the target.
- n = 0 treated as the first year.

## Self-test

1. Write down the multiplier for an increase of 4.5% per year.
2. Write down the multiplier for a decrease of 18% per year.
3. $5000 is invested and grows by 4.5% each year. Find its value after 3 years.
4. A machine loses 18% of its value each year. What percentage of its original value is it worth after 5 years?
5. A substance of mass 80 g halves every 3 days. Find its mass after 12 days.
6. A sample of 320 cells triples every hour. How many cells are there after 4 hours?
7. A population of 1800 grows by 12% each year. After how many complete years does it first exceed 3000?
8. V = 3500 × 1.04ⁿ. Write down the starting value and the percentage change per period.
9. V = 12 000 × 0.85ⁿ. Write down the percentage change per period.
10. A laptop loses $150 of value each year. Is this exponential decay? Explain.
11. A price rises by 15% and then by another 15%. Find the overall percentage increase.
12. A quantity is multiplied by 0.97 each month. Describe the change in words.

### Answers

1. **1.045**
2. **0.82**
3. 5000 × 1.045³ = 5705.83...: **$5705.83**
4. 0.82⁵ = 0.3707...: **37.1%** (3 s.f.)
5. 4 halvings: 80 × 0.5⁴ = **5 g**
6. 320 × 3⁴ = **25 920**
7. n = 4: 2832; n = 5: 3172. **5 years**
8. Starting value **3500**; **4% increase** per period
9. **15% decrease** per period
10. **No.** The same amount is lost each year, so it is linear decay. Exponential decay loses the same percentage of the current value.
11. 1.15² = 1.3225: **32.25%**
12. It **decreases by 3% each month**.
