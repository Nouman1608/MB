These notes condense subtopic **1.15 Time** of the Cambridge IGCSE Mathematics 0580 syllabus for 2025, 2026 and 2027. Core and Extended candidates learn identical content, and it can appear on any paper. Worked examples are in the [time study guide](/resources/cambridge-igcse-mathematics-0580-time/), and exam-style questions are in the [time practice questions](/resources/cambridge-igcse-mathematics-0580-time-practice/).

## The three statements

1. Calculate with time: seconds, minutes, hours, days, weeks, months, years, and the relationships between them (1 year = 365 days).
2. Calculate times in the 24-hour and 12-hour clock (3.15 a.m. = 03 15; 3.15 p.m. = 15 15).
3. Read clocks and timetables, including time zones, local times and time differences.

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 24-hour clock | Times from 00 00 to 23 59, always four digits |
| 12-hour clock | Times from 12.00 to 11.59 with a.m. (after midnight) or p.m. (after noon) |
| Midnight | 00 00 in the 24-hour clock; 12.00 a.m. |
| Noon | 12 00 in the 24-hour clock; 12.00 p.m. |
| Duration | The length of time between two times |
| Local time | The time in a particular place |
| Time difference | How many hours one place is ahead of or behind another |

## Conversion facts

```
1 min = 60 s       1 h = 60 min = 3600 s       1 day = 24 h = 1440 min = 86 400 s
1 week = 7 days = 168 h        1 year = 12 months = 365 days
```

**Decimal to mixed units:** multiply the decimal part by the next conversion factor.

```
3.75 h   -> 0.75 × 60 = 45     -> 3 h 45 min
0.3 days -> 0.3 × 24 = 7.2 h   -> 0.2 × 60 = 12  -> 7 h 12 min
```

**Mixed units to one unit:** multiply and add. 4 h 18 min = 4 × 60 + 18 = 258 min.

**Large numbers of seconds:** divide by 86 400, then 3600, then 60, keeping the remainder each time.

## 12-hour and 24-hour clock

| 12-hour | 24-hour |
|---|---|
| 12.45 a.m. | 00 45 |
| 6.05 a.m. | 06 05 |
| 12.20 p.m. | 12 20 |
| 8.25 p.m. | 20 25 |
| 11.59 p.m. | 23 59 |

- p.m. times from 1.00 p.m. onwards: add 12 to the hour.
- 24-hour times from 13 00 onwards: subtract 12 and write p.m.
- After 23 59 comes 00 00, never 24 00 plus minutes.

## Durations: count on

```
08 47 -> 09 00    13 min
09 00 -> 14 00     5 h
14 00 -> 14 12    12 min
total              5 h 25 min
```

Across midnight: count to 00 00, then on from 00 00. For more than one day, take whole days of 24 hours first.

## Timetables

- One column = one journey. Read down it.
- A blank or dash means that service does not stop there.
- "Latest bus to arrive by ..." → work backwards from the deadline.
- "Next bus after ..." → find the first time in the row that is at or after the given time.
- Include any changeover time or walking time the question gives.

## Time zones

```
destination AHEAD by n hours   -> add n hours to the departure-zone time
destination BEHIND by n hours  -> subtract n hours
```

Method for a flight:

1. Add the flight time to the departure time (departure-zone time).
2. Convert to the arrival zone.
3. Check the date: did you cross midnight?

To find a flight time from two local times, convert both into one zone first.

## Exam traps

- 2.4 hours written as 2 h 40 min (it is 2 h 24 min).
- Times subtracted as decimals: 14.12 − 8.47 = 5.65.
- 12.45 a.m. written as 12 45 (it is 00 45).
- 24 15 written for a time after midnight (it is 00 15).
- Ignoring a day change in a time-zone question.
- Using a bus that arrives after the train leaves, because the changeover time was ignored.
- 1 year treated as 52 weeks exactly (365 days is 52 weeks and 1 day).

## Self-test

1. Write 3.75 hours in hours and minutes.
2. Write 4 hours 18 minutes in minutes.
3. Write 0.3 days in hours and minutes.
4. Write 5400 seconds in hours.
5. Write 8.25 p.m. in the 24-hour clock.
6. Write 00 45 in the 12-hour clock.
7. Find the time from 08 47 to 14 12.
8. A night shift runs from 22 15 to 06 40. How long is it?
9. A coach leaves at 22 35 and the journey takes 3 hours 50 minutes. When does it arrive?
10. How many hours are there in 2 weeks?
11. City A is 4 hours ahead of City B. It is 18 30 in City B. What time is it in City A?
12. A flight leaves City B at 23 20 local time and takes 6 hours 55 minutes. City A is 4 hours ahead of City B. Find the local time in City A when it lands.

### Answers

1. 0.75 × 60 = 45: **3 h 45 min**
2. 240 + 18 = **258 minutes**
3. 0.3 × 24 = 7.2 h; 0.2 × 60 = 12: **7 h 12 min**
4. 5400 ÷ 3600 = **1.5 hours**
5. **20 25**
6. **12.45 a.m.**
7. 13 min + 5 h + 12 min = **5 h 25 min**
8. 22 15 → 00 00 is 1 h 45 min; 00 00 → 06 40 is 6 h 40 min: **8 h 25 min**
9. 22 35 + 3 h 50 min = **02 25** the next day
10. 14 × 24 = **336 hours**
11. 18 30 + 4 h = **22 30**
12. City B time on landing: 23 20 + 6 h 55 min = 06 15 the next day; City A: 06 15 + 4 h = **10 15 the next day**
