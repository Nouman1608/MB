This guide covers **Topic 1 Business and its environment**, an AS
Level topic for Cambridge International AS & A Level Business 9609,
2026–2028 series (Version 2, no significant changes affecting teaching
from the prior version). AS Level candidates study topics 1.1–5.5; A
Level candidates study all topics 1.1–10.4.

## Where this fits in 9609

Business and its environment is the first of five AS Level topics, and
it sets up the vocabulary and concepts — what enterprise is, how
businesses are structured and measured, what objectives they pursue,
and who their stakeholders are — that every later AS and A Level topic
assumes. Questions on marketing, operations or finance later in the
syllabus routinely expect answers framed around a business's stated
objectives or its stakeholders' competing interests.

## Syllabus coverage

**CAMBRIDGE AS & A LEVEL BUSINESS 9609 — TOPIC 1 BUSINESS AND ITS
ENVIRONMENT**

- 1.1 Enterprise — the role of the entrepreneur and the risks and
rewards of enterprise
- 1.2 Business structure — the main forms of business organisation and
their legal and practical implications
- 1.3 Size of business — how business size is measured, and the
opportunities and problems associated with growth
- 1.4 Business objectives — the range of objectives a business may
pursue, and how and why these change over time
- 1.5 Stakeholders in a business — the internal and external groups
with an interest in a business, and how their objectives can conflict

## How to approach it

This topic is conceptual rather than numerical, so the main way marks
are lost is through vague, generic answers — examiners consistently
reward responses that apply a concept to the specific business context
given in the question rather than reciting a definition in the
abstract. Stakeholder analysis (1.5) deserves particular attention:
practise identifying where two stakeholder groups' objectives conflict
(shareholders wanting higher dividends versus employees wanting higher
wages, for instance) and evaluating how a business might balance that
tension, since "conflicting stakeholder objectives" is a recurring
essay-style question. For business structure and size (1.2–1.3), be
ready to recommend and justify a suitable structure or growth strategy
for a given scenario, since AS Level Business consistently tests
application and judgement, not just recall of definitions. Because
this topic recurs conceptually across the whole qualification, treat
it as a foundation to revisit rather than a box to tick early and
forget — a shaky grasp of objectives and stakeholders here tends to
weaken essay answers on marketing and finance topics much later in
the course.
