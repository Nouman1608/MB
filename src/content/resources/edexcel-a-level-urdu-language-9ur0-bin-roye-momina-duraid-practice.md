> **These are original questions written for Marlbridge** for practice on the prescribed film بن روئے
> (*Bin Roye*) for Paper 2 Section C. They are not past-paper questions. Questions 1–10 use practice
> marks; question 11 follows the specification's essay format (45 marks on three grids). Your
> evidence must come from your own viewing of the film.

Related: [Bin Roye study guide](/resources/edexcel-a-level-urdu-language-9ur0-bin-roye-momina-duraid/) and

## Section A

**1.** State the name and year the specification gives for بن روئے, and the section of Paper 2 in which it is answered. **[3]**

**2.** Give the English meaning of: (a) عکس بندی (b) تدوین (c) پس منظر موسیقی (d) ملبوسات (e) کیمرے کا زاویہ **[5]**

**3.** What does the title **بن روئے** mean, and why is it an apt title for a film about unspoken feelings? **[3]**

**4.** Correct the error in each sentence.
(a) فلم میں صبا کی خاموشی کو بہت مؤثر انداز میں دکھایا گئی ہے۔
(b) شادی کے مناظر خوشی کا ماحول پیدا کرتی ہیں۔
(c) ہدایت کار نے کلوز اپ کا استعمال کی ہے۔ **[3]**

**5.** Which grid rewards the use of cinematic terminology, and how many marks does it carry? **[2]**

**6.** A candidate plans to write essays on بن روئے and باغبان. Explain why this is not allowed and what they should do instead. **[2]**

## Section B

**7.** Translate into Urdu:

> The film-makers place scenes of celebration next to moments of grief. As a result, the audience feels the loss more deeply.

**[7]**

**8.** Complete a scene-log entry in Urdu for **one** key scene of your choice, with the headings منظر، کیا ہوتا ہے، تکنیک، اثر، موضوع. **[5]**

**9.** Write **one** sentence in Urdu that names a film technique, links it to a character, and explains its effect on the audience. **[3]**

**10.** This sentence from an answer describes but does not analyse: «اس منظر میں صبا روتی ہے اور گانا چلتا ہے۔» Rewrite it as analysis and explain what you have added. **[4]**

**11.** Answer in Urdu, in about 320–370 words.

**«فلم "بن روئے" یہ پیغام دیتی ہے کہ جذبات کو چھپانا انسان کو اندر سے توڑ دیتا ہے۔» اس بیان کا جائزہ لیجیے، اور اپنے جواب میں فلم کی تکنیک کا بھی ذکر کیجیے۔**

**[45]**

## Answers

**1.** مومنہ دُرید (Momina Duraid) [1]; 2015 [1]; Section C, the film section [1].

**2.** (a) cinematography [1] (b) editing [1] (c) background score [1] (d) costume [1] (e) camera angle [1]

**3.** It means "without crying" [1]. The film is about feelings that are held back [1], so the title draws attention to who expresses grief and love and who suppresses them, and to the cost of that silence [1].

**4.** (a) دکھایا گیا ہے: with کو marking the object, the passive takes default masculine singular [1] (b) پیدا کرتے ہیں: مناظر is masculine plural [1] (c) استعمال کیا ہے: with نے the verb agrees with استعمال, masculine [1]

**5.** Range of grammatical structures and vocabulary (AO3) [1], 15 marks [1].

**6.** Two film essays are never allowed: a candidate writes two literary-text essays, or one literary text and one film [1]. They should replace one film with a literary text, for example by answering on a prescribed novel, play or essays in Section B [1].

**7.** Model: **فلم سازوں نے جشن کے مناظر کو غم کے لمحات کے ساتھ ساتھ رکھا ہے۔ اس کے نتیجے میں ناظرین نقصان کو زیادہ گہرائی سے محسوس کرتے ہیں۔**

Marking: فلم سازوں نے [1]؛ جشن کے مناظر کو (accept خوشی کی تقریبات کے مناظر) [1]؛ غم کے لمحات کے ساتھ ساتھ [1]؛ رکھا ہے, default masculine with کو [1]؛ اس کے نتیجے میں [1]؛ ناظرین نقصان کو [1]؛ زیادہ گہرائی سے محسوس کرتے ہیں [1].

**8.** One mark for each heading completed accurately and specifically: a recognisable label [1]; one sentence on what happens [1]; at least one named technique [1]; an effect on the audience [1]; a relevant theme [1]. Vague entries such as "a sad scene" earn no mark for the first two headings.

**9.** A named technique [1]; a character correctly linked to it [1]; a clearly explained effect, in accurate Urdu [1]. Example: **کلوز اپ میں صبا کے چہرے پر ٹھہری ہوئی خاموشی ناظرین کو اس کے اندر کی کشمکش کا احساس دلاتی ہے۔**

**10.** Rewritten, for example: **اس منظر میں پس منظر میں چلتا ہوا گانا صبا کے آنسوؤں کو ایک ایسی آواز دیتا ہے جو وہ خود اپنے الفاظ میں نہیں دے پاتی** [1], with the technique named precisely (پس منظر میں گانا) [1], its effect explained [1] and a link to the film's theme of unspoken feeling [1].

**11.** Marked on three levels-based grids: critical and analytical response (AO4) [20]; range of grammatical structures and vocabulary, including cinematic terminology (AO3) [15]; accuracy of language (AO3) [10]. Total 45.

**Indicative content.** Credit any well-argued response supported by evidence from the film. Likely points:

- **Support for the statement:** Saba's unspoken love leads to misunderstanding and suffering; the title draws attention to grief that is held in; her silence becomes the source of the story's conflict.
- **Technique:** close-ups and performance show feeling that is not spoken; songs and background music voice emotion; the contrast between celebrations and loss intensifies the cost of silence; changes in setting or costume mark the heroine's changing situation.
- **Qualification:** the film also shows growth through grief, so hiding feelings breaks but does not finally destroy; other characters' choices and family pressure share responsibility; candidates may argue that the film presents expression of feeling as healing.
- **Social and cultural setting:** expectations within the family, especially on young women, make it hard to speak about love and marriage; link to Theme 1.
- **Conclusion:** a judgement on how far the film delivers the message in the statement.

At the top of the AO4 range, each point is supported by a specific scene; at the top of the range grid, cinematic terms are used frequently and accurately.

**Model-answer outline (in Urdu):** opening that states a qualified position on the statement; paragraph 1 on how Saba's silence creates the conflict, with a close-up or performance moment from your scene log; paragraph 2 on songs and the contrast between celebration and loss as the film's way of voicing hidden feeling; paragraph 3 on growth through grief, which qualifies the word **توڑ دیتا ہے**; conclusion: **فلم دکھاتی ہے کہ چھپائے ہوئے جذبات دکھ دیتے ہیں، مگر انسان اسی دکھ سے مضبوط بھی ہو سکتا ہے**.
