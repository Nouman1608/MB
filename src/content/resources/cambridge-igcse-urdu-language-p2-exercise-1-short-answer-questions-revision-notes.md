Condensed notes for the final weeks. For the full explanation and a worked announcement, use the
[Exercise 1 short answer questions study guide](/resources/cambridge-igcse-urdu-language-p2-exercise-1-short-answer-questions/).

## The exercise in one box

```
Paper 2, Exercise 1 – Short answer questions     8 marks, 8 questions
Hear       a series of short spoken texts, e.g. travel announcement,
           recorded phone message, brief dialogue
Write      short answers (a word or phrase), in Urdu
Assesses   L1 select information, L2 ideas, opinions and attitudes
Heard      twice, with pauses to read and write
```

## Key terms

| Urdu | English |
|---|---|
| اعلان | announcement |
| پیغام | message |
| مکالمہ | dialogue |
| روانگی / آمد | departure / arrival |
| کے بجائے | instead of |
| تاخیر | delay |
| گزارش ہے کہ | (formal) you are requested to |
| رابطہ کیجیے | please contact |

## Where the details come in each text type

- **Announcement (اعلان):** who it is for → the change (time, platform, gate) → the reason → an
  instruction (گزارش ہے کہ ...).
- **Phone message (پیغام):** who is calling and from where → who it is for → the new arrangement → what to
  bring or do → when to call back.
- **Brief dialogue (مکالمہ):** a question → an answer, often with a change (پہلے ... لیکن اب) → who will
  do or bring what.
- **Weather or travel report:** places → conditions → warnings and advice → temperatures.

Answers follow the order of the text, so if you miss one, move straight on to the next question.

## Question word → answer form

- **کب؟ / کس دن؟** a day or date
- **کتنے بجے؟** a clock time
- **کہاں؟** a place (keep the postposition: پلیٹ فارم دو پر)
- **کتنے؟ / کتنی؟** a number or amount (with its unit)
- **کون؟** a person or group
- **کیوں؟** a reason, often after کیونکہ or کی وجہ سے

## Numbers that sound alike

- سترہ 17 / ستر 70
- اٹھارہ 18 / اسی 80
- اٹھائیس 28 / اڑتیس 38 / اڑتالیس 48
- انتیس 29 / انتالیس 39 / انچاس 49 (ان- = one less than the next ten)
- پچیس 25 / پچاس 50 / پچپن 55

Figures are fine in your answer if they are quicker and certain.

## Times and days

| Heard | Means |
|---|---|
| سوا تین | 3:15 |
| ساڑھے تین | 3:30 |
| پونے تین | 2:45 |
| ڈیڑھ | 1:30 |
| ڈھائی | 2:30 |

- **جمعرات** Thursday, **جمعہ** Friday: do not mix them.
- **ہفتہ** Saturday, and also "week".
- **پیر** = **سوموار** = Monday.
- Parts of the day: صبح، دوپہر، سہ پہر، شام، رات.

## Signals of a change

- **کے بجائے**: the word *before* it is the old detail.
- **اب**: the new arrangement follows.
- **پہلے ... لیکن اب ...**: the detail after لیکن is current.
- **معاف کیجیے / میرا مطلب ہے**: a self-correction; the corrected detail counts.

## The two-hearing routine

1. **Pause:** read every question, circle the question word, predict the form.
2. **First hearing:** notes only, key words and figures.
3. **Second hearing:** confirm, fill gaps, check changes.
4. **After:** short, complete answers; no blanks.

## Exam traps

- Giving the old time or place after کے بجائے.
- Reading پونے as "quarter past" instead of "quarter to".
- A number with no unit, or a place without the platform or gate word.
- Full sentences where a phrase will do.

## Self-test

1. What does سوا پانچ بجے mean, and what does پونے پانچ بجے mean?
2. Which is 38: اٹھائیس, اڑتیس or اڑتالیس?
3. In "ملاقات منگل کے بجائے بدھ کو ہوگی", which day is the meeting now?
4. Which two assessment objectives does Exercise 1 test?
5. Which day is جمعرات?
6. Why predict the form of the answer before the recording starts?

**Answers:** 1. 5:15 and 4:45. 2. اڑتیس. 3. بدھ (Wednesday); منگل is the old day, because it comes before
کے بجائے. 4. L1 (identify and select relevant information) and L2 (understand ideas, opinions and
attitudes). 5. Thursday (جمعہ is Friday). 6. Knowing you are listening for a time, place or number tells
you which word in the text to catch, so you are not trying to understand every word at once.
