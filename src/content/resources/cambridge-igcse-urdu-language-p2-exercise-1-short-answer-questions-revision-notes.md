## The exercise in one box

```
Paper 2, Exercise 1 – Short answer questions     8 marks, 8 questions
Hear       a series of short spoken texts, e.g. travel announcement,
           recorded phone message, brief dialogue
Write      short answers (a word or phrase), in Urdu
Assesses   L1 select information, L2 ideas, opinions and attitudes
Heard      twice, with pauses to read and write
```

## Key terms

| Urdu | English |
|---|---|
| اعلان | announcement |
| پیغام | message |
| مکالمہ | dialogue |
| روانگی / آمد | departure / arrival |
| کے بجائے | instead of |
| تاخیر | delay |
| گزارش ہے کہ | (formal) you are requested to |
| رابطہ کیجیے | please contact |

## Where the details come in each text type

- **Announcement (اعلان):** who it is for → the change (time, platform, gate) → the reason → an
  instruction (گزارش ہے کہ ...).
- **Phone message (پیغام):** who is calling and from where → who it is for → the new arrangement → what to
  bring or do → when to call back.
- **Brief dialogue (مکالمہ):** a question → an answer, often with a change (پہلے ... لیکن اب) → who will
  do or bring what.
- **Weather or travel report:** places → conditions → warnings and advice → temperatures.

Answers follow the order of the text, so if you miss one, move straight on to the next question.

## Question word → answer form

- **کب؟ / کس دن؟** a day or date
- **کتنے بجے؟** a clock time
- **کہاں؟** a place (keep the postposition: پلیٹ فارم دو پر)
- **کتنے؟ / کتنی؟** a number or amount (with its unit)
- **کون؟** a person or group
- **کیوں؟** a reason, often after کیونکہ or کی وجہ سے

## Numbers that sound alike

- سترہ 17 / ستر 70
- اٹھارہ 18 / اسی 80
- اٹھائیس 28 / اڑتیس 38 / اڑتالیس 48
- انتیس 29 / انتالیس 39 / انچاس 49 (ان- = one less than the next ten)
- پچیس 25 / پچاس 50 / پچپن 55

Figures are fine in your answer if they are quicker and certain.

## Times and days

| Heard | Means |
|---|---|
| سوا تین | 3:15 |
| ساڑھے تین | 3:30 |
| پونے تین | 2:45 |
| ڈیڑھ | 1:30 |
| ڈھائی | 2:30 |

- **جمعرات** Thursday, **جمعہ** Friday: do not mix them.
- **ہفتہ** Saturday, and also "week".
- **پیر** = **سوموار** = Monday.
- Parts of the day: صبح، دوپہر، سہ پہر، شام، رات.

## Signals of a change

- **کے بجائے**: the word *before* it is the old detail.
- **اب**: the new arrangement follows.
- **پہلے ... لیکن اب ...**: the detail after لیکن is current.
- **معاف کیجیے / میرا مطلب ہے**: a self-correction; the corrected detail counts.

## The two-hearing routine

1. **Pause:** read every question, circle the question word, predict the form.
2. **First hearing:** notes only, key words and figures.
3. **Second hearing:** confirm, fill gaps, check changes.
4. **After:** short, complete answers; no blanks.

## Exam traps

- Giving the old time or place after کے بجائے.
- Reading پونے as "quarter past" instead of "quarter to".
- A number with no unit, or a place without the platform or gate word.
- Full sentences where a phrase will do.

## What earns the mark

There are 8 questions for 8 marks, so each answer is a single detail. The examiner needs to see that
you caught **the right detail**, not that you can write a long sentence. Three things lose the mark
most often: the old detail after a change, a detail that belongs to a different question, and an
answer missing the word that makes it precise (بجے، پلیٹ فارم، روپے).

## Worked example: a recorded phone message

The message below is written for this page; in the exam you would hear it twice.

> السلام علیکم، میں ڈاکٹر عمران کے کلینک سے فوزیہ بول رہی ہوں۔ یہ پیغام احمد صاحب کے لیے ہے۔ آپ کی
> ملاقات جمعرات کے بجائے جمعہ کو ساڑھے دس بجے ہوگی۔ براہِ کرم اپنی پرانی رپورٹیں ساتھ لائیے۔ اگر یہ
> وقت مناسب نہ ہو تو شام پانچ بجے سے پہلے ہمیں فون کیجیے۔ شکریہ۔

**English gloss:** "Hello, this is Fauzia speaking from Dr Imran's clinic. This message is for Mr
Ahmed. Your appointment will be on Friday instead of Thursday, at half past ten. Please bring your old
reports with you. If this time is not suitable, please phone us before five in the evening. Thank you."

| Question | Predicted form | Answer | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| ملاقات اب کس دن ہوگی؟ | a day | جمعہ | جمعرات comes before کے بجائے, so it is the old day |
| ملاقات کتنے بجے ہوگی؟ | a clock time | ساڑھے دس بجے (10:30) | ساڑھے = half past |
| احمد صاحب کو کیا ساتھ لانا ہے؟ | a thing | پرانی رپورٹیں | keep پرانی: it makes the answer precise |
| کس وقت سے پہلے فون کرنا ہے؟ | a clock time | شام پانچ بجے | سے پہلے is in the question, so do not repeat it |

The message follows the pattern in the table above: who is calling and from where, who it is for, the
new arrangement, what to bring, when to call back. Knowing that order lets you be ready for each detail
before it comes.

## Step-by-step method

1. **Read the context line** for each text (for example "a message from a clinic") and picture who is
   speaking.
2. **Circle the question word** and write the form you expect in the margin: day, time, place, number,
   person, reason.
3. **Underline change words already in the question** (اب، نئی، بدلی ہوئی): they warn you that the
   recording will give an old detail too.
4. **First hearing:** jot figures and key words only; numbers can go down as digits.
5. **Second hearing:** check each answer against its question word, and listen again for کے بجائے,
   لیکن اب and self-corrections.
6. **Write the final answer** in a word or short phrase, in Urdu script, with its unit.

## Vocabulary for messages and announcements

| Urdu | English |
|---|---|
| بول رہا / رہی ہوں | (on the phone) this is ... speaking |
| براہِ کرم | please |
| ملاقات | appointment, meeting |
| منسوخ | cancelled |
| ملتوی | postponed |
| روانہ ہونا | to depart |
| پہنچنا | to arrive, reach |
| مناسب | suitable |
| ساتھ لائیے | please bring with you |
| واپس فون کیجیے | please call back |

## Connectives that steer the answer

| Urdu | English | What it tells you |
|---|---|---|
| کے بجائے | instead of | the old detail is before it |
| لیکن اب | but now | the new detail follows |
| اگر ... تو | if ... then | a condition: the answer may apply only in one case |
| کیونکہ / کی وجہ سے | because / because of | a reason |
| اس کے بعد | after that | the next step in a sequence |
| سے پہلے / کے بعد | before / after | a time limit |

## More exam traps

- Writing ساڑھے alone without the hour, or the hour without ساڑھے.
- Answering a کون question with a place, or a کہاں question with a person.
- Repeating the question's own words (سے پہلے، کو) inside the answer, which wastes time and can confuse
  the answer.
- Mixing up the caller and the person the message is for.

## Self-test

1. What does سوا پانچ بجے mean, and what does پونے پانچ بجے mean?
2. Which is 38: اٹھائیس, اڑتیس or اڑتالیس?
3. In "ملاقات منگل کے بجائے بدھ کو ہوگی", which day is the meeting now?
4. Which two assessment objectives does Exercise 1 test?
5. Which day is جمعرات?
6. Why predict the form of the answer before the recording starts?
7. In the worked message, who is the message for, and who is calling?
8. What does ڈیڑھ بجے mean?
9. Heard: "پرواز منسوخ نہیں ہوئی، صرف ملتوی ہوئی ہے۔" Is the flight cancelled?
10. Which word in a question tells you to expect a clock time?
11. Give the Urdu for "please" and for "suitable".
12. What should you do if you miss the answer to one question during the first hearing?

**Answers:** 1. 5:15 and 4:45. 2. اڑتیس. 3. بدھ (Wednesday); منگل is the old day, because it comes before
کے بجائے. 4. L1 (identify and select relevant information) and L2 (understand ideas, opinions and
attitudes). 5. Thursday (جمعہ is Friday). 6. Knowing you are listening for a time, place or number tells
you which word in the text to catch, so you are not trying to understand every word at once. 7. It is for احمد صاحب; فوزیہ is calling from
Dr Imran's clinic. 8. Half past one (1:30). 9. No: it is postponed (ملتوی), not cancelled (منسوخ).
10. کتنے بجے. 11. براہِ کرم; مناسب. 12. Move straight on to the next question, because the answers follow
the order of the text, and listen for the missed detail on the second hearing.
