These notes condense subtopic **1.11 Ratio and proportion** of the Cambridge IGCSE Mathematics 0580 syllabus for 2025, 2026 and 2027. Core and Extended candidates learn identical content. Worked examples are in the [ratio and proportion study guide](/resources/cambridge-igcse-mathematics-0580-ratio-and-proportion-cambridge-igcse-maths/), and exam-style questions with mark schemes are in the [ratio and proportion practice questions](/resources/cambridge-igcse-mathematics-0580-ratio-and-proportion-cambridge-igcse-maths-practice/).

## The three skills in 1.11

1. Give ratios in their simplest form (20 : 30 : 40 = 2 : 3 : 4).
2. Divide a quantity in a given ratio.
3. Use proportional reasoning and ratios in context: recipes, map scales, best value.

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Ratio | A comparison of quantities, written a : b or a : b : c |
| Simplest form | Whole-number parts with no common factor except 1 |
| Part | One unit of the ratio; a : b has a + b parts in total |
| Form 1 : n | Every part divided by the first part, so the first part is 1 |
| Direct proportion | Two quantities that change by the same multiplier |
| Unitary method | Find the value of one item, then multiply |
| Scale (map) | 1 : n means 1 unit on the map is n of the same units in real life |
| Best value | The option with the lower cost per unit, or the more units per unit of money |

## Simplest form checklist

- **Decimals:** multiply all parts by a power of 10 first. 0.25 : 1.5 → 25 : 150 → 1 : 6.
- **Fractions:** multiply all parts by the lowest common denominator. 2/5 : 1/2 → (× 10) 4 : 5.
- **Units:** make them the same. 40 min : 2 h → 40 : 120 → 1 : 3.
- **Divide by the HCF**, not just by a common factor, or keep cancelling until nothing divides every part.
- **Form 1 : n:** 5 : 8 → 1 : 1.6. Here n may be a decimal.

## Sharing: find one part

| You are given | Divide it by | Example (ratio 4 : 9) |
|---|---|---|
| The total | total number of parts | total 91 → 91 ÷ 13 = 7 per part |
| One share | that share's parts | the 9-share is 63 → 63 ÷ 9 = 7 |
| The difference | difference in parts | difference 35 → 35 ÷ 5 = 7 |

Then multiply one part by each share's number of parts. Check that the shares add to the total.

**Changing ratios:** if one quantity stays fixed, write the shares as multiples of an unknown (3k and 4k), form an equation from the new ratio, and solve for k.

## Proportion in context

**Recipes**

```
scale factor = new number of people ÷ original number of people
multiply EVERY ingredient by the scale factor
```

Limiting ingredient: for each ingredient, find how many people it could serve. The smallest answer is the limit.

**Map scales**

```
1 m = 100 cm        1 km = 1000 m = 100 000 cm
map -> real:   multiply by the scale number, then convert units
real -> map:   convert to cm, then divide by the scale number
```

With 1 : 50 000, 1 cm on the map is 50 000 cm = 0.5 km.

**Best value**

- Compare the **same measure** for every option: cost per item, cost per 100 g, or grams per dollar.
- Lower cost per unit is better; more units per dollar is better.
- Finish with a sentence naming the better option.

## Exam traps

- Ratio written in reverse order.
- Mixed units left in a ratio.
- A ratio "simplified" to fractions or decimals, such as 1.5 : 1 instead of 3 : 2, when simplest form was asked.
- A difference divided by the total parts.
- One ingredient scaled, the others forgotten.
- Dividing by 1000 instead of 100 000 to change cm to km.
- Best-value answer with numbers but no conclusion, or comparisons that use different units.

## Self-test

1. Write 36 : 84 in its simplest form.
2. Write 0.25 : 1.5 in its simplest form.
3. Write 2/5 : 1/2 in its simplest form.
4. Write 40 minutes : 2 hours in its simplest form.
5. Write 5 : 8 in the form 1 : n.
6. Share 96 in the ratio 5 : 3.
7. Sunita and Ali share money in the ratio 4 : 9. Ali receives $35 more than Sunita. How much does each receive?
8. The ratio of cats to dogs at a rescue centre is 2 : 3. There are 18 dogs. How many cats are there?
9. Five pens cost $3.40. Find the cost of eight pens.
10. A map has scale 1 : 50 000. Two towns are 6 cm apart on the map. Find the real distance in km.
11. Pack A has 6 rolls for $4.50 and pack B has 10 rolls for $7.20. Which is better value?
12. Concrete is mixed using cement, sand and gravel in the ratio 1 : 2 : 4. How much sand is needed with 28 kg of gravel?

### Answers

1. ÷ 12: **3 : 7**
2. 25 : 150 → **1 : 6**
3. × 10: **4 : 5**
4. 40 : 120 → **1 : 3**
5. 8 ÷ 5 = 1.6, so **1 : 1.6**
6. 96 ÷ 8 = 12; **60 and 36**
7. 9 − 4 = 5 parts = $35, so one part is $7. Sunita **$28**, Ali **$63**
8. 3 parts = 18, so one part = 6; cats = 2 × 6 = **12**
9. One pen costs $0.68; 8 × 0.68 = **$5.44**
10. 6 × 50 000 = 300 000 cm = **3 km**
11. A: $0.75 per roll; B: $0.72 per roll. **Pack B** is better value
12. 4 parts = 28 kg, so one part = 7 kg; sand = 2 × 7 = **14 kg**
