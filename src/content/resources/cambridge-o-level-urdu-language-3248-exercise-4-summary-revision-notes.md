These notes cover **Exercise 4, Summary**, of 3248 Paper 1: "candidates write a paragraph-length summary (of no more than 100 words) about an aspect or aspects of the text in Exercise 3. Candidates should use their own words as far as possible." For the whole paper see the [O Level Urdu Paper 1 study guide](/resources/o-level-cambridge-urdu-first-and-second-language/); for a worked example see the [Exercise 4 Summary study guide](/resources/cambridge-o-level-urdu-language-3248-exercise-4-summary/).

## Task facts

| Fact | Detail |
|---|---|
| Marks | 10 |
| Objectives | R2, R3, W1, W2, W3, W4, W5 |
| Text | the same text as Exercise 3 |
| Task | one paragraph, no more than 100 words, about the aspect named |
| Language | question and answer in Urdu; dictionaries not allowed |
| Wording | own words as far as possible |
| Paper | 1 hour 45 minutes, 50 marks, five compulsory exercises |
| Not printed in the syllabus | the split of the 10 marks: use past-paper mark schemes |

## The eight-step method

1. Read the question and underline the **aspect**.
2. Find the points (use Exercise 3 notes).
3. Choose the **main** points; drop examples and background.
4. Put the points in a **logical order**.
5. Rewrite each in **own words**.
6. **Link** with connectives.
7. **Count** the words; cut to 100 or fewer.
8. **Check** grammar, punctuation, spelling.

## Aspect words

| Urdu | English | What to include |
|---|---|---|
| مشکلات / مسائل | difficulties | each problem, briefly |
| فوائد | benefits | each advantage |
| اقدامات / کوششیں | actions | what was done |
| وجوہات / اسباب | causes | reasons |
| نتائج | results | outcomes |
| مشورے | advice | recommendations |

If the question has two aspects (for example problems and actions), cover both in a balanced way.

## Own-words toolkit

| Technique | Example |
|---|---|
| Use a synonym | *ہمت نہیں ہاری* becomes *ہار نہیں مانی* |
| Change the verb | *ذمہ داری دی* becomes *ذمہ داری سونپی* |
| Change the structure | *بارش میں کتابیں گیلی ہو گئیں* becomes *بارش سے کتابیں خراب ہو گئیں* |
| Combine two sentences | join with *اور* or *جس سے* |
| Turn a list into a general word | *گاڑی، بس، رکشہ* becomes *ٹرانسپورٹ* |

Keep names, numbers and technical terms as they are.

## Linking words

| Purpose | Words |
|---|---|
| Add | اور، نیز، اس کے علاوہ |
| Contrast | لیکن، مگر، تاہم |
| Reason | اس لیے، کیونکہ |
| Result | چنانچہ، نتیجتاً، جس سے |
| Order | پہلے، پھر، اس کے بعد، آخر میں |

## What to leave out

- Examples and anecdotes (a mother's story).
- Quotations.
- Repeated points.
- Background not asked for.
- Your opinion.
- Anything not in the text.

## Writing checks

- One paragraph, complete sentences.
- Third person, neutral register.
- Every sentence ends with *۔*; commas are *،*.
- Verbs agree with their subjects, in gender and number.
- Spelling is checked for common words such as *انھوں*، *مشکلات*، *ذمہ داری*.

## Typical faults

| Fault | Fix |
|---|---|
| Copying | Change verbs and structure |
| Wrong aspect | Re-read the question |
| Too long | Cut examples and repeated ideas |
| A list of notes | Write linked sentences |
| Opinion | Remove it |
| No link between problem and solution | Add *تاہم* or *اس کے حل کے لیے* |

## Planning frame

Use a three-line plan in the margin before you write.

| Line | Content |
|---|---|
| Opening | one sentence that names the topic and the aspect: *گلی کی لائبریری کو شروع میں کئی مشکلات پیش آئیں۔* |
| Middle | the points in order, each in one clause, joined by connectives |
| Close | the last point or result; no conclusion of your own |

## Mini example

Text points: *کچرا سڑک پر پھیلتا تھا؛ بلدیہ نے بڑے ڈبے رکھے؛ لوگوں نے ڈبے استعمال کیے؛ محلہ صاف ہو گیا۔*

Summary frame: *محلے میں کچرا سڑک پر پھیلتا تھا۔ بلدیہ نے بڑے ڈبے رکھوائے، جنھیں لوگوں نے استعمال کیا، اس لیے محلہ صاف ہو گیا۔*

Notice that two points are joined with *جنھیں* and the result is introduced with *اس لیے*. The summary has no opinion and no extra detail.

## Final check list

- Does the first sentence name the aspect?
- Does every sentence give a point from the text?
- Are two or more points joined with a linking word?
- Is any sentence a copy of the text? Change its verb or structure.
- Is the count 100 or fewer?
- Does each sentence end with *۔*?

## Exam traps

- Starting with a long introduction.
- Giving benefits when the question asks about difficulties.
- Treating the summary as a retelling of the whole article.
- Forgetting to leave time to count the words.
- Copying a whole sentence because it seems correct.

## Self-test

1. How many marks is Exercise 4 worth?
2. What is the word limit?
3. From which text is the summary written?
4. Name **two** objectives tested besides R2 and R3.
5. Should a summary be a list or a paragraph?
6. Should you give your own opinion?
7. Change *ہمت نہیں ہاری* using your own words.
8. Give **two** linking words for contrast.
9. What should you do if the question names two aspects?
10. Why read the Exercise 4 question before finishing Exercise 3?

**Answers**

1. Ten.
2. No more than 100 words.
3. The same text as Exercise 3.
4. Any two of W1, W2, W3, W4, W5.
5. A paragraph of continuous prose.
6. No. Only points from the text.
7. For example, *ہار نہیں مانی*.
8. Any two: لیکن، مگر، تاہم.
9. Cover both aspects, in a balanced way.
10. If the aspect is named, you can check that your notes for it are complete.
