Condensed recall for section 4.6 (Circle properties) of the 4MA1 specification. The vocabulary and the tangent and chord facts are Foundation Tier content for both tiers; intersecting chords, cyclic quadrilaterals and the five angle theorems are **Higher tier only**. Worked examples are in the [Circle properties study guide](/resources/edexcel-igcse-mathematics-4ma1-circle-properties/), and exam-style questions in the [Circle properties practice questions](/resources/edexcel-igcse-mathematics-4ma1-circle-properties-practice/).

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Centre | The fixed point every point on the circle is equidistant from |
| Radius | Centre to circumference |
| Diameter | Chord through the centre = 2 × radius |
| Chord | Segment joining two points on the circumference |
| Circumference | The distance around the circle |
| Tangent | Line touching the circle at one point only |
| Arc | Part of the circumference (minor or major) |
| Sector | Region between two radii and an arc |
| Segment | Region between a chord and an arc |
| Cyclic quadrilateral | Quadrilateral with all four vertices on a circle (Higher) |
| Subtended | The angle an arc or chord makes at a point |

## Both tiers: three facts plus radii

| Fact | Gives you |
|---|---|
| Tangent ⟂ radius at the point of contact | A 90° angle → Pythagoras or trigonometry |
| Two tangents from a point are equal | An isosceles triangle; with radii, a kite |
| Perpendicular from centre to chord bisects the chord (and converse) | A 90° angle and two equal halves |
| Radii are equal | An isosceles triangle |

**Kite from two tangents:** angle at centre + angle at external point = 180° (the other two angles are 90°). Angle at P 50° → angle at O 130°.

**Chord distance:** d² + (half-chord)² = r². Radius 10, chord 12 → half-chord 6 → d = 8.

## Higher tier only: intersecting chords

```
inside:   AX × XB = CX × XD       (parts of each chord)
outside:  PA × PB = PC × PD       (distances from P to near and far points)
```

PB is the full distance from P, not the chord AB.

## Higher tier only: the five theorems

| Theorem (wording to write as a reason) | Picture to look for |
|---|---|
| The angle at the centre is twice the angle at the circumference | Arrowhead from the ends of an arc to O and to a point on the circle |
| The angle in a semicircle is a right angle | Triangle standing on a diameter |
| Angles in the same segment are equal | Bow-tie: two angles on the same chord, same side |
| Opposite angles of a cyclic quadrilateral add up to 180° | Four points on the circle joined in order |
| Alternate segment theorem: the angle between a tangent and a chord equals the angle in the alternate segment | Tangent meeting a chord at the point of contact |

Formal proofs are not required, but these reasons are.

## Quick chains

- Angle at circumference 35° → centre 70°.
- Centre angle 150° → circumference on major arc 75°, on minor arc 105°.
- Diameter AB, angle CAB 40° → angle ABC 50°.
- Tangent–chord angle 58° → angle in alternate segment 58° → centre angle 116° → base angles of the isosceles triangle 32° each (= 90° − 58°).

## Exam traps

- Sector ↔ segment mix-up.
- Using "same segment" for angles on opposite sides of a chord (they sum to 180°).
- Treating a quadrilateral with a vertex at O as cyclic.
- Forgetting the reflex angle at the centre when the circumference point is on the minor arc.
- Writing PA × AB in the external chord rule.
- Reasons like "circle rule" or "Z angle" score nothing.
- Measuring angles on a diagram marked "not accurately drawn".

## Self-test

1. What is the name of a line that touches a circle at exactly one point?
2. What angle does a tangent make with the radius at the point of contact?
3. Two tangents from P touch a circle at A and B. PA = 7 cm. Write down PB.
4. Radius 5 cm, chord 8 cm. How far is the chord from the centre?
5. Tangent from P, radius 9 cm, OP = 15 cm. Find the tangent length.
6. (Higher) The angle at the circumference is 47°. Find the angle at the centre on the same arc.
7. (Higher) A cyclic quadrilateral has one angle of 118°. Find the opposite angle.
8. (Higher) Chords meet inside a circle: AX = 3, XB = 8, CX = 4. Find XD.
9. (Higher) Outside point: PA = 2, PB = 9, PC = 3. Find PD.
10. (Higher) The angle between a tangent and a chord is 66°. Find the angle in the alternate segment.
11. (Higher) AB is a diameter, C on the circle, angle ABC = 24°. Find angle BAC.
12. Chord AB, centre O, angle OAB = 28°. Find angle AOB.

### Answers

1. A tangent.
2. 90°.
3. 7 cm (tangents from a point are equal).
4. √(25 − 16) = 3 cm.
5. √(225 − 81) = 12 cm.
6. 94°.
7. 62°.
8. 3 × 8 = 4 × XD, so XD = 6.
9. 2 × 9 = 3 × PD, so PD = 6.
10. 66°.
11. 90° − 24° = 66°.
12. 180° − 2 × 28° = 124°.

## Frequently asked questions

**Which circle properties are on the Foundation tier of 4MA1?**
The vocabulary, the fact that a tangent is perpendicular to the radius, that two tangents from a point are equal, and that the perpendicular from the centre bisects a chord. The circle theorems and intersecting chords are Higher tier only.

**Do I need to prove the circle theorems in 4MA1?**
No. The specification says formal proof is not required, but you must use the theorems and quote them as reasons.
