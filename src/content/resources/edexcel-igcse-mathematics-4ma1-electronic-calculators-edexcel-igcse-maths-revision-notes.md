Condensed notes for section 1.11 A of Pearson Edexcel International GCSE Mathematics A (4MA1): "use a scientific electronic calculator to determine numerical results". It is Foundation Tier content that the Higher Tier also assumes, so it applies to both tiers. For full explanations, read the [electronic calculators study guide](/resources/edexcel-igcse-mathematics-4ma1-electronic-calculators-edexcel-igcse-maths/); for exam-style questions, use the [electronic calculators practice questions](/resources/edexcel-igcse-mathematics-4ma1-electronic-calculators-edexcel-igcse-maths-practice/).

## The minimum calculator

- Both tiers: +, −, ×, ÷, x², √x, memory, brackets, x^y, x^(1/y), x̄, Σx, Σfx, sin, cos, tan and their inverses.
- Higher Tier adds: standard form.
- Banned in every paper: databanks, retrieval of text or formulae, QWERTY keyboards, symbolic algebra, symbolic differentiation or integration.

## Safe key sequences

```
Fraction line         ( numerator ) ÷ ( denominator )   or the fraction template
Root of an expression √( 52.3 − 4.7² )                  root covers the whole expression
Fractional power      32 ^ ( 2 ÷ 5 ) = 4                bracket the power
Negative power        2.5 ^ (−) 3 = 0.064
Negative base         ( (−) 1.4 ) ^ 4 = 3.8416           bracket the negative
Standard form         8.1 ×10^x 6 ÷ 2.7 ×10^x (−) 2       = 3 × 10⁸
Inverse trig          tan⁻¹( 3.4 ÷ 5.1 ) = 33.7°        bracket the ratio
Mean from a table     Σfx ÷ Σf
```

## What goes wrong, and what the wrong entry gives

| Intended | Wrong entry | Wrong result | Correct result |
|---|---|---|---|
| (4.6² − 1.8) / (2.3 + √7.29) | no brackets | 23.077... | 3.872 |
| 32^(2/5) | 32 ^ 2 ÷ 5 | 204.8 | 4 |
| (−1.4)⁴ | −1.4⁴ | −3.8416 | 3.8416 |
| (8.1 × 10⁶) ÷ (2.7 × 10⁻²) | 8.1 × 10 ^ 6 ÷ 2.7 × 10 ^ −2 | 30 000 | 3 × 10⁸ |
| sin 30° | radian mode | −0.988... | 0.5 |

Learn the right-hand column as "sanity values": if your calculator gives the wrong-column answer, you know which habit failed.

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Order of operations | Brackets, then powers and roots, then × and ÷, then + and − |
| Fraction template | A key that sets up separate boxes for numerator and denominator |
| Negative key (−) | Makes a number negative; different from the subtraction key |
| Ans | The result of the last calculation, reusable in the next one |
| Memory | A stored value that stays until it is replaced |
| ×10^x key | Enters the power of 10 of a number in standard form as part of one number |
| Degree mode | The angle setting needed for 4MA1; check sin 30 = 0.5 |
| Σf, Σfx | Total frequency; total of frequency × value |
| x̄ | The mean, Σfx ÷ Σf for a frequency table |
| Natural display | A display that shows exact fractions, surds or multiples of π |

## Display reading

- A fraction answer such as 17/10 can be toggled to 1.7. Give a decimal only when asked.
- 1 ÷ 7 shows 0.1428571429; the last digit is rounded, so the display is not exact.
- A surd answer such as 2√5 must be changed to a decimal (4.472135955) if the question wants all figures on the display.
- Interpret the display in context: money to 2 decimal places, whole items rounded up or down by sense, decimal hours converted with × 60.

## Exam traps

- **All figures, then rounded.** "Write down all the figures" means every digit shown; a rounded answer loses that mark even if the rounded answer to the next part is right.
- **Early rounding.** √(7.3² + 4.1²) × 3.6 is 30.14 to 4 s.f.; rounding the root to 8.37 first gives 30.13.
- **Odd and even powers.** (−a)³ and −a³ agree; (−a)² and −a² do not. Bracket every time so you never have to decide.
- **Mean from a table.** Divide Σfx by Σf, not by the number of rows.
- **Standard form answers.** 230 400 000 000 is correct but not "in standard form"; write 2.304 × 10¹¹.
- **Mode.** Statistics mode left on can change what the keys do. Clear or return to normal calculation mode after using it.

## Method to show

1. Write the numerator and denominator values separately.
2. Write the full display.
3. Round as instructed.
4. Check with a 1 significant figure estimate.

## Self-test

1. Work out (4.6² − 1.8) / (2.3 + √7.29).
2. What does a calculator give for 32 ^ 2 ÷ 5, and what is 32^(2/5)?
3. Work out 2.5⁻³.
4. Work out (−1.4)⁴ and −1.4⁴.
5. Work out (8.1 × 10⁶) ÷ (2.7 × 10⁻²) in standard form.
6. Your calculator gives sin 30 = −0.988. What is wrong?
7. Find tan⁻¹(3.4 ÷ 5.1) to 1 decimal place.
8. Values 1, 2, 3, 4 have frequencies 5, 8, 4, 3. Find Σfx and the mean.
9. Write √50 × 4 correct to 3 significant figures, then explain why rounding √50 to 7.1 first gives a wrong answer.
10. Write 1 ¾ + 2 ½, divided by 3 ⅔ − 1 ⅙, as a decimal.
11. Estimate (48.7 × 0.312) / (9.86 − 2.03), then calculate it to 3 s.f.
12. Which calculator facility is on the Higher Tier list but not the Foundation list?

### Answers

1. 19.36 ÷ 5 = **3.872**.
2. 32² ÷ 5 = **204.8**; 32^(2/5) = **4**.
3. **0.064**.
4. **3.8416** and **−3.8416**.
5. **3 × 10⁸**.
6. It is in **radian mode**; switch to degree mode, then sin 30 = 0.5.
7. **33.7°**.
8. Σfx = 5 + 16 + 12 + 12 = **45**; mean = 45 ÷ 20 = **2.25**.
9. √50 × 4 = 28.28427... = **28.3**; 7.1 × 4 = 28.4, so the early rounding changes the third significant figure.
10. (17/4) ÷ (5/2) = 17/10 = **1.7**.
11. Estimate: 50 × 0.3 / (10 − 2) = 15/8, about **2**; calculator: 15.1944 ÷ 7.83 = 1.940536... = **1.94**.
12. **Standard form**.
