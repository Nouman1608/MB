**These notes condense Paper 1 Topic 4 of Cambridge IGCSE Islamiyat 0493, The First Islamic
Community.** The topic is a who's who of the people around the Prophet (pbuh). Questions name people
and ask you to write about their lives (part (a), 10 marks) and the importance of their actions
(part (b), 4 marks). Learn each person as a short card: who they were, two or three events, and one
lesson.

## Syllabus coverage

**0493 Paper 1, Topic 4 (2026–2027 syllabus):** the Prophet's wives; his descendants, including
children, grandchildren and the line of Shi'a Imams; his leading Companions: the Ten Blessed, the
Scribes, the major characters of his ministry, the Emigrants and Helpers, and the four Rightly Guided
Caliphs during his lifetime (their rule after 632 is Paper 2).

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Ummahat al-Mu'minin | Mothers of the Believers: the Prophet's wives (Sura 33.6) |
| Ahl al-Bayt | People of the House: the Prophet's family |
| Sahaba | Companions: Muslims who met the Prophet (pbuh) |
| Al-Ashara al-Mubashshara | The Ten given good news of paradise |
| Muhajirun | Emigrants from Makka to Madina |
| Ansar | Helpers: Muslims of Madina, from Aws and Khazraj |
| Imam (Shi'a usage) | Divinely appointed leader from the Prophet's family |
| Ghayba | Occultation of the twelfth Imam |
| Dhu al-Nurayn | "Possessor of two lights": Uthman |
| Al-Siddiq / al-Faruq | Titles of Abu Bakr / Umar |

## Family links to remember

| Person | Link to the Prophet (pbuh) |
|---|---|
| Abu Bakr | Father of A'isha |
| Umar | Father of Hafsa |
| Uthman | Married his daughters Ruqayya, then Umm Kulthum |
| Ali | Cousin; married his daughter Fatima |
| Abu Sufyan | Father of Umm Habiba |
| Hamza, Abu Talib, Abu Lahab, al-Abbas | His uncles |
| Ja'far ibn Abi Talib | Cousin, Ali's brother |
| Zaynab bint Jahsh | His cousin, later his wife |

## Wives: one line each

- **Khadija:** first believer; supported him with wealth and comfort; died 619.
- **Sawda:** early Muslim widow; married after Khadija's death.
- **A'isha:** scholar; narrated many Hadiths; taught after his death.
- **Hafsa:** Umar's daughter; kept the Qur'an sheets.
- **Zaynab bint Khuzayma:** "mother of the poor"; died soon after marriage.
- **Umm Salama:** advised him at Hudaybiyya.
- **Zaynab bint Jahsh:** marriage referred to in Sura 33.37; generous to the poor.
- **Juwayriya:** her marriage led to the freeing of many of her tribe.
- **Umm Habiba:** stayed firm in Islam in Abyssinia.
- **Safiyya:** from Banu Nadir; married after Khaybar.
- **Maymuna:** last marriage, 7 AH.

## Descendants

- **Children of Khadija:** al-Qasim, Abdullah (died young); Zaynab, Ruqayya, Umm Kulthum, Fatima.
- **Son of Mariya:** Ibrahim, died as an infant.
- **Grandchildren:** Hasan and Husayn (Fatima and Ali); Umama (Zaynab).
- **Fatima:** the only child to outlive him; died a few months after him.

**The twelve Imams (Twelver Shi'a):** Ali; Hasan; Husayn; Ali Zayn al-Abidin; Muhammad al-Baqir;
Ja'far al-Sadiq; Musa al-Kazim; Ali al-Rida; Muhammad al-Jawad; Ali al-Hadi; Hasan al-Askari;
Muhammad al-Mahdi.

## The Ten Blessed

Abu Bakr · Umar · Uthman · Ali · Talha · al-Zubayr · Abd al-Rahman ibn Awf · Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas ·
Sa'id ibn Zayd · Abu Ubayda ibn al-Jarrah.

Memory hook: the four caliphs, then six more; four of those six (Talha, al-Zubayr, Abd al-Rahman
and Sa'd) were among the early converts invited to Islam by Abu Bakr.

## Scribes

Zayd ibn Thabit (chief scribe, later compiler), Ubayy ibn Ka'b, Ali, Uthman, Mu'awiya.

## Major characters: quick cards

| Person | Two facts | Lesson |
|---|---|---|
| Bilal | Tortured, saying "One, One"; first mu'adhdhin | Firmness of faith; equality regardless of origin |
| Hamza | Defended the Prophet (pbuh) in Makka; martyred at Uhud | Courage in defending the weak |
| Ja'far | Spoke before the Negus; martyred at Mu'ta | Explaining Islam clearly and calmly |
| Zayd ibn Haritha | Freed slave, loved as a son; martyred at Mu'ta | Loyalty; rank does not depend on birth |
| Salman al-Farsi | Searched for truth across lands; idea of the trench | Seeking truth; skills of every people are valued |
| Mus'ab ibn Umayr | Gave up luxury; first teacher in Madina | Sacrifice for faith; teaching wins hearts |
| Abd al-Rahman ibn Awf | "Show me the market"; generous trader | Self-reliance and generosity |

## Emigrants and Helpers

- Emigrants left wealth and family for faith.
- Helpers gave shelter and shared property; praised in Sura 59.9.
- Brotherhood pairs, for example Abd al-Rahman ibn Awf and Sa'd ibn al-Rabi'.
- Lesson: welcome those in need; those helped should work and not depend on charity.

## Caliphs before 632: one key act each

- **Abu Bakr:** companion in the cave of Thawr.
- **Umar:** his conversion brought open prayer at the Ka'ba.
- **Uthman:** bought the well of Ruma; equipped the Tabuk army.
- **Ali:** slept in the Prophet's bed at the Hijra.

## Exam traps

- **Wrong period:** caliphs' rule after 632 earns nothing in a Paper 1 answer.
- **Name-swapping:** two Zaynabs among the wives; Zaynab the daughter is a third.
- **Lists without facts:** the Ten Blessed need facts, not only names.
- **Part (b) as narrative:** give a lesson and the reason for it.

## Self-test

1. Who was the only one of the Prophet's (pbuh) children to outlive him?
2. Which wife was known as "mother of the poor"?
3. Which wife kept the written sheets of the Qur'an?
4. Why is Uthman called Dhu al-Nurayn?
5. Name the third Shi'a Imam and where he was martyred.
6. Name the twelfth Imam and what Twelver Shi'a believe about him.
7. Name the six members of the Ten Blessed who were not caliphs.
8. Who was the chief scribe of revelation?
9. Which Companion is named in the Qur'an?
10. What did Abd al-Rahman ibn Awf say when offered half of Sa'd's wealth?
11. Which verse praises the Helpers, and for what?
12. How did Ali risk his life on the night of the Hijra?

**Answers:** 1. Fatima. 2. Zaynab bint Khuzayma. 3. Hafsa. 4. He married two of the Prophet's
daughters, Ruqayya and then Umm Kulthum. 5. Husayn, at Karbala. 6. Muhammad al-Mahdi; he is in
occultation and will return. 7. Talha, al-Zubayr, Abd al-Rahman ibn Awf, Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas, Sa'id ibn
Zayd, Abu Ubayda ibn al-Jarrah. 8. Zayd ibn Thabit. 9. Zayd ibn Haritha (Sura 33.37). 10. He asked to
be shown the market so he could trade. 11. Sura 59.9; they preferred the Emigrants above themselves
even when in need. 12. He slept in the Prophet's bed while the assassins waited outside.
