| Topic | Key points | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Rulings (hukm) | Binding decisions by a judge (qadi); a fatwa from a mufti guides but does not bind | Qur'an 4:58 judge with justice |
| Equal justice | Same rule for strong and weak; even his own daughter | Theft case; Qur'an 4:135 |
| Judge's limits | A judgment cannot make a wrong right | Bukhari: a piece of Fire |
| Judge's reward | Two rewards if correct, one if mistaken after striving | Bukhari |
| Purposes of law | Protect faith, life, mind, family, property | Scholars of Shariah |
| Testimony (shahadah) | Truthful evidence, even against oneself | Qur'an 4:135 |

| Strict standards | Four witnesses for adultery; punishment for false accusation | Qur'an 24:4 |

## Interest (riba)

- **Meaning:** an increase fixed in advance on a loan, in return for time.
- **Qur'an 2:275:** Allah has permitted trade and forbidden interest. Qur'an 2:276: Allah destroys interest and gives increase to charity. Qur'an 3:130: do not consume interest doubled and multiplied.
- **Hadith (Muslim):** the one who takes, pays, records and witnesses interest are all cursed.
- **Final Sermon:** riba cancelled, beginning with that owed to Abbas.
- **Reasons:** exploitation of borrowers; debt traps; unfairness in making money from money alone; wealth moving from poor to rich; obedience to Allah.
- **Trade is allowed:** profit linked to goods, work and shared risk.

| Alternative | How it works |
|---|---|
| Mudarabah | One partner gives money, the other work; shared profit |
| Musharakah | Partners invest together and share profit and loss |
| Murabaha | The bank buys and sells at an agreed profit by instalments |
| Ijarah | Leasing |
| Qard hasan | Interest-free benevolent loan |

## Islamic wills (wasiyyah)

- **Qur'an:** a bequest for parents and relatives (2:180); fixed shares (4:11-12).
- **Hadith:** do not let two nights pass without a written will (Bukhari). Sa'd (RA): one-third, and one-third is much (Bukhari).
- **Limit:** up to one-third, for people or causes who are not already heirs.
- **No bequest to an heir** who already has a fixed share.

**Order of payment:** (1) funeral costs and debts, (2) the will (up to one-third), (3) fixed shares (faraid).

**Worked example.** Estate £144,000 after debts; wife, son and daughter; one-third to charity.

| Step | Calculation | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Will | £144,000 ÷ 3 | £48,000 |
| Remainder | £144,000 − £48,000 | £96,000 |
| Wife (one-eighth) | £96,000 ÷ 8 | £12,000 |
| Children | £96,000 − £12,000 | £84,000 |
| Son (two parts) | £84,000 ÷ 3 × 2 | £56,000 |
| Daughter (one part) | £84,000 ÷ 3 | £28,000 |
| Check | £48,000 + £12,000 + £56,000 + £28,000 | £144,000 |

## Key characteristics of an Islamic state

| Characteristic | Evidence or example |
|---|---|
| Sovereignty of Allah; law based on Qur'an and Sunnah | Rulers carry out, not invent, the law |
| Justice and equality before the law | Qur'an 4:58; the theft case |
| Consultation (shura) | Qur'an 42:38; 3:159 |
| Trustworthy leadership (amanah) | Qur'an 4:58 |
| Protection of life, property, faith and minorities | Charter of Madinah; Qur'an 2:256 |
| Social welfare | Zakah; care for the poor, orphan, traveller |
| Defence and security | Protecting the people |
| Accountability | Abu Bakr (RA): obey me as long as I obey Allah |

Model: the Prophet's (pbuh) rule in Madinah and the first four caliphs. Muslims have different views on modern forms of government: state principles and early examples.

## Model answer: a 4-mark question

**Question:** Explain **two** reasons why honest testimony is important in Islam. **[4]**

> Honest testimony makes justice possible [1], because judges rely on witnesses, so a false witness can cause an innocent person to be punished [1]. It is also a trust before Allah [1]: the Qur'an says that whoever conceals testimony has a sinful heart, and false testimony is among the gravest sins [1].

## Exam traps

- Interest is forbidden; trade and profit are permitted.
- A will is limited to one-third for non-heirs.
- Debts and funeral costs are paid first.
- False testimony is a major sin; concealing testimony is also wrong.
- State the principles of an Islamic state, not one political system.

## Self-test

1. What is the difference between a qadi and a mufti?
2. Which verse commands judging with justice?
3. What does Qur'an 2:283 say about concealing testimony?
4. Define riba.
5. What does Qur'an 2:275 say about trade and interest?
6. Give two reasons why interest is forbidden.
7. What is the maximum share of an estate that can be given by will to non-heirs?
8. In what order is an estate dealt with?
9. Give two key characteristics of an Islamic state.
10. Which verses support shura?

**Answers:** 1. A qadi gives a binding judgment; a mufti gives a non-binding opinion. 2. Qur'an 4:58. 3. That whoever conceals it has a sinful heart. 4. An increase fixed in advance on a loan in return for time. 5. Allah has permitted trade and forbidden interest. 6. Any two: exploitation; debt; unfairness; concentration of wealth; command of Allah. 7. One-third. 8. Funeral costs and debts, then the will, then the fixed shares. 9. Any two: law based on Qur'an and Sunnah; justice; consultation; trustworthy leaders; protection of minorities; welfare; defence; accountability. 10. Qur'an 42:38 and 3:159.
