Causes and effects of wars (750-1500) is world history topic 2 for Paper 2. Revise at least two wars from different regions under three headings: causes, practices of war and their impact on the outcome, and effects.

Related: [Medieval wars study guide](/resources/ib-ib-dp-world-history-dp-history-ib-dp-world-history-world-history-topics-2/) and [Medieval wars practice questions](/resources/ib-ib-dp-world-history-dp-history-ib-dp-world-history-world-history-topics-2-practice/).

## War profiles

**The First Crusade (1095-1099)**
- *Causes*: Byzantine appeal against the Seljuks; Urban II's preaching at Clermont (1095); religious enthusiasm and the promise of remission of sins; land, wealth and adventure; papal leadership.
- *Practices*: armies of knights and foot soldiers led by great nobles; long sieges (Nicaea 1097, Antioch 1098, Jerusalem 1099); victory at Dorylaeum (1097); Muslim states divided between Seljuk rivals and the Fatimids.
- *Effects*: four crusader states; capture of Jerusalem (July 1099) with massacre of its inhabitants; military orders such as the Templars and Hospitallers; later Muslim counter-crusade under Zengi, Nur al-Din and Saladin.

**The Hundred Years' War (1337-1453)**
- *Causes*: Gascony and vassalage; Edward III's claim to the French throne (1328 succession); Flanders and the wool trade; the Franco-Scottish alliance; confiscation of Gascony (1337).
- *Practices*: longbow and dismounted men-at-arms (Crécy 1346, Poitiers 1356, Agincourt 1415); *chevauchées*; truces and treaties (Brétigny 1360, Troyes 1420); French recovery under Charles VII; artillery (Formigny 1450, Castillon 1453).
- *Effects*: English expelled except Calais; stronger French monarchy (permanent taxes, companies of ordinance); national identity; English political instability feeding the Wars of the Roses.

**The Mongol invasions of Japan (1274, 1281)**
- *Causes*: Kublai Khan's demand for tribute; Japanese refusal; Mongol imperial ideology of universal rule; the war against the Song.
- *Practices*: amphibious landings at Hakata Bay; Mongol, Chinese and Korean forces; explosive projectiles; Japanese defensive wall; typhoons destroyed the fleets.
- *Effects*: no conquest; Kamakura shogunate weakened by costs and unrewarded warriors, falling in 1333; belief in divine protection.

**The Ottoman conquest of Constantinople (1453)**
- *Causes*: Ottoman expansion and Mehmed II's ambition; the city's strategic position; Byzantine weakness.
- *Practices*: blockade by land and sea; giant cannon; ships hauled overland into the Golden Horn.
- *Effects*: end of Byzantium; new Ottoman capital; Ottoman control of the straits; Greek scholars moved west.

## Types of medieval war

| Type | Definition | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Crusade | War sanctioned by the papacy, with spiritual rewards for participants | First Crusade |
| Dynastic war | War over rights of succession or inheritance | Hundred Years' War |
| Civil war | War between factions within one kingdom | Wars of the Roses |
| War of conquest | War to annex territory or exact tribute | Mongol invasions; Ottoman conquest |
| Holy war (*jihad*) | War presented as religious duty in Islamic terms | Saladin's campaigns against the crusader states |

## Causes: ranking framework

| Cause | Ask yourself |
|---|---|
| Religious | Was religion a motive, a justification, or both? |
| Dynastic | Whose claim was at stake, and why was it disputed then? |
| Territorial | Which lands were contested, and what were they worth? |
| Economic | Who gained from trade, plunder or ransom? |
| Political | Did a ruler need war to secure authority at home? |
| Trigger | What turned tension into fighting in that year? |

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Vassal | A lord who held land from a superior lord in return for loyalty and service |
| *Chevauchée* | Destructive mounted raid through enemy territory |
| Longbow | Powerful bow with a high rate of fire, used en masse by English armies |
| Men-at-arms | Fully armoured soldiers, mounted or on foot |
| Companies of ordinance | France's permanent royal cavalry companies from 1445 |
| Crusader states | The county of Edessa, principality of Antioch, county of Tripoli and kingdom of Jerusalem |
| Military orders | Religious orders of knights, such as the Templars and Hospitallers |
| *Kamikaze* | "Divine wind": the storms credited with saving Japan in 1274 and 1281 |
| Siege train | Artillery and equipment moved with an army for sieges |

## Exam traps

- **Narrative.** Telling the story of a war earns little; organise by cause or effect.
- **Effects beyond 1500.** Stay inside the topic's dates.
- **Religion as the only crusade motive.** Show how religious and material motives combined.
- **Technology as the only explanation.** The longbow did not prevent England's defeat; leadership, money and alliances mattered too.
- **Same-region pairs.** The Hundred Years' War and the Wars of the Roses are both European.
- **"Short-term" without a date.** Name the trigger and its year.

## Self-test

1. Who appealed to the West for help in the 1090s, and against whom?
   *The Byzantine emperor Alexios I, against the Seljuk Turks.*
2. Where and when did Urban II preach the First Crusade?
   *At Clermont, in November 1095.*
3. Name the four crusader states.
   *Edessa, Antioch, Tripoli and Jerusalem.*
4. On what grounds did Edward III claim the French throne?
   *Through his mother, Isabella, sister of the last Capetian king, Charles IV.*
5. Which French king was captured at Poitiers in 1356?
   *John II.*
6. Which battle of 1453 ended the Hundred Years' War, and what weapon is it associated with?
   *Castillon, field artillery.*
7. What were the companies of ordinance?
   *France's permanent royal cavalry companies, created in 1445.*
8. Why did Kublai Khan invade Japan?
   *Japan refused his demand to submit as a tributary.*
9. What defensive measure did the Japanese take after 1274?
   *They built a wall along Hakata Bay.*
10. Why did the Mongol invasions weaken the Kamakura shogunate?
    *Defence was costly and, with no land conquered, it could not reward the warriors who fought.*
11. What technology helped Mehmed II breach the walls of Constantinople?
    *Great cannon, including guns cast by Orban.*
12. Give one long-term effect of the First Crusade on the Muslim world.
    *It provoked a counter-crusade that united Syria and Egypt under leaders such as Nur al-Din and Saladin.*
