Quick-recall notes for the 3.1.4.1 Data handling statement in OxfordAQA International GCSE Psychology
(9218): "Recognise and use expressions in decimal form: use ratios, fractions and percentages, estimate
results, find arithmetic means and use an appropriate number of significant figures." These skills are
tested on both papers. For explanations and worked examples, use the
[maths skills study guide](/resources/oxfordaqa-igcse-psychology-9218-maths-skills-data-handling/);
for averages, graphs and distributions, see the
[Research Methods (Paper 2) revision notes](/resources/oxfordaqa-igcse-psychology-9218-research-methods-paper2-revision-notes/).
All figures below are invented for practice.

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Decimal form** | A number written with a decimal point, such as 0.75 |
| **Fraction** | A part of a whole written as top ÷ bottom, such as 3/4 |
| **Percentage** | A value out of 100 |
| **Ratio** | A comparison of the sizes of parts, such as 2 : 5 |
| **Simplest form** | A fraction or ratio divided down by its highest common factor |
| **Arithmetic mean** | Total of the values divided by the number of values |
| **Estimate** | A rough answer from rounded numbers, used to check or predict |
| **Significant figures** | Digits counted from the first non-zero digit, showing precision |
| **Decimal places** | Digits after the decimal point |
| **Percentage change** | (new − original) ÷ original × 100 |

## Conversions

| To get... | From a fraction | From a decimal | From a percentage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fraction | — | Write over 10, 100...; simplify (0.75 = 75/100 = 3/4) | Write over 100; simplify (40% = 2/5) |
| Decimal | Top ÷ bottom (3/8 = 0.375) | — | ÷ 100 (62% = 0.62) |
| Percentage | Top ÷ bottom × 100 (3/8 = 37.5%) | × 100 (0.2 = 20%) | — |

**Learn by heart:** 1/2 = 0.5 = 50%; 1/4 = 0.25 = 25%; 3/4 = 0.75 = 75%; 1/5 = 0.2 = 20%;
1/8 = 0.125 = 12.5%; 1/10 = 0.1 = 10%; 1/3 ≈ 0.333 ≈ 33.3%.

## Percentages

- **Of a total:** part ÷ total × 100. 12 helped, 30 did not → 12 ÷ 42 × 100 = 28.6% (3 s.f.).
- **Change:** (new − original) ÷ original × 100. 40 → 52 = 30% increase.
- **Of an amount:** percentage ÷ 100 × amount. 35% of 60 = 0.35 × 60 = 21.

## Ratios

- Simplify by dividing all parts by the same number: 12 : 30 = 2 : 5.
- To find a fraction of the whole, add the parts: 2 : 5 → 2/7 and 5/7.
- Use ratios to compare groups of different sizes: 9 : 27 and 6 : 18 are both 1 : 3.

## Mean

Total ÷ number of values; keep the unit. Reaction times 0.42, 0.38, 0.51, 0.47, 0.40 s → 2.18 ÷ 5 =
0.436 s → **0.44 s** (2 s.f.).

## Estimating

Round each number to 1 s.f. and calculate. 312 × 0.48 ≈ 300 × 0.5 = 150 (exact 149.76). Use the
estimate to spot calculator slips, such as a misplaced decimal point.

## Significant figures

1. Count from the **first non-zero** digit.
2. Look at the next digit: **5 or more** rounds up.
3. Keep the **size** of the number (fill with zeros).

| Number | 2 s.f. | 3 s.f. |
|---|---|---|
| 28.5714 | 29 | 28.6 |
| 0.004615 | 0.0046 | 0.00462 |
| 12,846 | 13,000 | 12,800 |

**Appropriate number:** match the least precise data, or follow the question exactly.

**Significant figures versus decimal places**

| Number | Decimal places | Significant figures |
|---|---|---|
| 0.0046 | 4 | 2 |
| 12.50 | 2 | 4 |
| 340 | 0 | 2 (or 3 if the zero was measured) |

## Worked mini-example

Eight participants recalled 12, 15, 9, 14, 10, 13, 11 and 16 middle words.

- Mean = 100 ÷ 8 = 12.5 words; to 2 s.f., 13 words.
- Three recalled more than 13 words: 3/8 = 0.375 = 37.5%.
- Ratio of more than 13 : 13 or fewer = 3 : 5.
- Estimate check: values cluster around 12-13, so a mean of 12.5 is sensible.

## Checklist for any calculation

- Is the **total** right (for example, participants × trials)?
- Have I kept full values until the **final** step?
- Have I given the **unit**?
- Have I used the **number of significant figures** asked for?
- Does my answer match a quick **estimate**?

## Exam traps

- Dividing by one group instead of the **total**.
- Rounding in the middle of a calculation.
- Counting **leading zeros** as significant.
- Writing 12,846 to 2 s.f. as "13".
- Confusing **percentage points** (52 − 40 = 12) with **percentage change** (30%).
- Leaving a ratio or fraction unsimplified when asked for simplest form.

## Self-test

1. Convert 3/5 to a decimal and a percentage.
2. Convert 0.125 to a fraction in its simplest form.
3. 27 of 36 participants conformed at least once. Give this as a fraction in its simplest form and a
   percentage.
4. Simplify the ratio 14 : 42.
5. In a ratio of 3 : 5, what fraction of the total is the first part?
6. Calculate 15% of 80.
7. A score rose from 25 to 30. Calculate the percentage increase.
8. Find the mean of 4, 7, 9, 6 and 9.
9. Estimate 198 × 0.31.
10. Give 0.04827 to 2 significant figures.
11. Give 3,456 to 1 significant figure.
12. A mean is calculated as 6.666... words. Give it to 3 significant figures.

**Answers:**
1. 0.6 and 60%.
2. 1/8.
3. 3/4 and 75%.
4. 1 : 3.
5. 3/8.
6. 0.15 × 80 = 12.
7. (30 − 25) ÷ 25 × 100 = 20%.
8. 35 ÷ 5 = 7.
9. About 200 × 0.3 = 60 (exact 61.38).
10. 0.048.
11. 3,000.
12. 6.67 words.
