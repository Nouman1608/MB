## Section A

**1.** Which row correctly shows the reactants and products of aerobic respiration?

A: carbon dioxide and water, giving glucose and oxygen

B: glucose and carbon dioxide, giving oxygen and water

C: glucose and oxygen, giving carbon dioxide and water

D: glucose and oxygen, giving lactic acid

**[1]**

**2.** In which part of a cell does most aerobic respiration take place?

A: chloroplast

B: mitochondrion

C: nucleus

D: ribosome

**[1]**

**3.** Define aerobic respiration. **[3]**

**4.** State the word equation for aerobic respiration. **[2]**

**5.** Explain why aerobic respiration is described as releasing a relatively large amount of energy. **[2]**

## Section B

**6.** The balanced chemical equation for aerobic respiration is:

C6H12O6 + 6O2 → 6CO2 + 6H2O

**(a)** State the number of oxygen molecules needed to break down **one** glucose molecule. **[1]**

**(b)** Show that the equation is balanced by counting the oxygen atoms on each side. **[2]**

**(c)** State **one** way in which the equation for photosynthesis is related to this equation. **[1]**

**(d)** State **one** place in the body where the carbon dioxide is removed. **[1]**

**7.** A student set up a respirometer containing 15 g of germinating peas. A chemical absorbed the carbon dioxide produced. The coloured liquid in the capillary tube moved 2.4 cm cubed in 20 minutes.

**(a)** Calculate the rate of oxygen uptake in cm cubed per minute. **[1]**

**(b)** Calculate the rate in cm cubed per gram per minute. **[2]**

**(c)** Explain why the liquid moved towards the peas. **[3]**

**(d)** The student repeated the experiment with 15 g of peas that had been boiled. Predict the result and explain it. **[2]**

**8.** Name **two** products of aerobic respiration, and describe how **each** can be shown in an experiment. **[4]**

**9.** Muscle cells contain more mitochondria than skin cells. Explain why. **[3]**

**10.** Compare photosynthesis and aerobic respiration, giving **three** differences. **[3]**

**11.** Balance the equation: C6H12O6 + O2 → CO2 + H2O. Show your working. **[3]**

**12.** Suggest why a person who runs a long distance breathes more deeply for several minutes afterwards. **[2]**

**13.** Name the two raw materials needed for aerobic respiration and state where each comes from in a human. **[2]**

## Answers

**1.** C [1]. **[1]**

**2.** B [1]. **[1]**

**3.** The release of a relatively large amount of energy [1] by the breakdown of glucose [1] in the presence of oxygen [1]. **[3]**

**4.** Glucose + oxygen [1] gives carbon dioxide + water [1]. **[2]**

**5.** The glucose is broken down completely to carbon dioxide and water [1], so much more of the stored chemical energy is released than in anaerobic respiration, where the breakdown is only partial [1]. **[2]**

**6.**

**(a)** Six [1].

**(b)** Left side: 6 (glucose) + 12 (6 x O2) = 18 [1]. Right side: 12 (6 x CO2) + 6 (6 x H2O) = 18 [1].

**(c)** The photosynthesis equation is the reverse of the aerobic respiration equation [1].

**(d)** The lungs [1]. **[5]**

**7.**

**(a)** 2.4 divided by 20 = 0.12 cm cubed per minute [1].

**(b)** 0.12 divided by 15 [1] = 0.008 cm cubed per gram per minute [1].

**(c)** The peas take in oxygen for respiration [1] and the carbon dioxide produced is absorbed by the chemical [1], so the gas volume falls and the liquid moves towards the peas [1].

**(d)** There would be no movement (or very little) [1], because boiled peas are dead and do not respire [1]. **[8]**

**8.** Carbon dioxide [1]: bubble the gas through limewater, which goes cloudy (or use hydrogencarbonate indicator, which turns yellow) [1]. Heat [1]: germinating seeds in a vacuum flask raise the temperature compared with boiled seeds [1]. (Accept water, shown by cobalt chloride paper turning pink.) **[4]**

**9.** Muscle cells contract and use a lot of energy [1]. Mitochondria are the site of aerobic respiration, which releases energy [1]. More mitochondria means more aerobic respiration, so more energy is released to meet the demand [1]. **[3]**

**10.** Any three of: photosynthesis takes in carbon dioxide and water but respiration takes in glucose and oxygen [1]; photosynthesis produces glucose and oxygen but respiration produces carbon dioxide and water [1]; photosynthesis stores energy but respiration releases energy [1]; photosynthesis happens only in cells with chlorophyll and in light but respiration occurs in all living cells all the time [1]; photosynthesis occurs in chloroplasts and respiration in mitochondria [1]. **[3]**

**11.** Six carbon atoms on the left need 6CO2 [1]. Twelve hydrogen atoms need 6H2O [1]. The oxygen atoms on the right total 18, and 6 come from glucose, so 6O2 are needed: C6H12O6 + 6O2 → 6CO2 + 6H2O [1]. **[3]**

**12.** The muscles have used a lot of oxygen and the level of carbon dioxide in the blood is higher [1]. Deep breathing brings in more oxygen for aerobic respiration and removes the extra carbon dioxide [1]. **[2]**

**13.** Glucose, from the digestion of carbohydrates in food [1]. Oxygen, from the air breathed into the lungs and carried in the blood [1]. **[2]**

## Where marks are usually lost

- Writing "energy is made" or "produced".
- Putting the numbers in the wrong places in the chemical equation.
- Forgetting that the equation for respiration is not the same as that for photosynthesis.
- Not mentioning that boiled peas are dead in the control.
- Giving "in the lungs" when asked where respiration happens; it happens in cells.
