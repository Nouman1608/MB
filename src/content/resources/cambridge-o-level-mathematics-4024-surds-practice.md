## Section A

**1.** Write √72 in the form a√b, where a and b are integers and b is as small as possible. **[2]**

**2.** Simplify √45 + √20. **[2]**

**3.** Simplify √75 + √12 - √27. **[3]**

**4.** Work out (3√2) × (4√5). **[2]**

**5.** Expand and simplify (2 + √5)². **[2]**

**6.** Expand and simplify (√3 + 2)(√3 - 2). **[2]**

**7.** Rationalise the denominator of 6/√3. **[2]**

## Section B

**8.** Expand and simplify (3 + √2)(1 + √2). **[3]**

**9.** Write 5/(2√5) in the form k√5, where k is a fraction. **[2]**

**10.** Rationalise the denominator of 3/(2 + √5) and simplify. **[3]**

**11.** Show that 4/(3 - √5) = 3 + √5. **[3]**

**12.** A rectangle has sides of length √12 cm and √27 cm. Find, in exact form:

**(a)** its area **[2]**

**(b)** its perimeter **[2]**

**13.** Show that (2 + √3)/(2 - √3) can be written as 7 + 4√3. **[3]**

**14.** Show that √50 ÷ √2 is an integer, and state its value. **[2]**

**15.** Simplify √200 - √50. **[2]**

**16.** A right-angled triangle has two shorter sides of length √7 cm and 3 cm. Find the exact length of the hypotenuse. **[2]**

**17.** Write (√5 + 1)/(√5 - 1) in the form a + b√5, where a and b are fractions. **[3]**

## Answers

**1.** 72 = 36 × 2 **[1]**; √72 = **6√2** **[1]** **[2]**

*Examiner insight:* Use the largest square factor, 36. Starting with 4 gives 2√18, which is not fully simplified.

**2.** √45 = 3√5 and √20 = 2√5 **[1]**; **5√5** **[1]** **[2]**

**3.** √75 = 5√3, √12 = 2√3, √27 = 3√3 **[2]**; 5√3 + 2√3 - 3√3 = **4√3** **[1]** **[3]**

**4.** 3 × 4 = 12 and √2 × √5 = √10 **[1]**; **12√10** **[1]** **[2]**

**5.** 4 + 2√5 + 2√5 + 5 **[1]**; **9 + 4√5** **[1]** **[2]**

*Examiner insight:* (2 + √5)² is not 4 + 5. There is a middle term of 2 × 2 × √5 = 4√5.

**6.** 3 - 2√3 + 2√3 - 4 **[1]**; **-1** **[1]** **[2]**

**7.** multiply top and bottom by √3: 6√3/3 **[1]**; **2√3** **[1]** **[2]**

**8.** 3 + 3√2 + √2 + (√2 × √2) **[1]**; = 3 + 3√2 + √2 + 2 **[1]**; **5 + 4√2** **[1]** **[3]**

**9.** multiply top and bottom by √5: 5√5/10 **[1]**; **√5/2** (which is (1/2)√5) **[1]** **[2]**

**10.** multiply top and bottom by (2 - √5) **[1]**; numerator 6 - 3√5 and denominator 4 - 5 = -1 **[1]**; **3√5 - 6** **[1]** **[3]**

*Check:* 3 ÷ (2 + 2.236) = 0.708 and 3 × 2.236 - 6 = 0.708.

**11.** multiply top and bottom by (3 + √5) **[1]**; denominator 9 - 5 = 4 **[1]**; 4(3 + √5)/4 = **3 + √5** **[1]** **[3]**

**12. (a)** √12 = 2√3 and √27 = 3√3 **[1]**; area = √12 × √27 = √324 = **18 cm²** **[1]**

**(b)** perimeter = 2(2√3 + 3√3) **[1]** = **10√3 cm** **[1]** **[4]**

**13.** multiply top and bottom by (2 + √3) **[1]**; numerator (2 + √3)² = 4 + 4√3 + 3 = 7 + 4√3 **[1]**; denominator 4 - 3 = 1, so the result is **7 + 4√3** **[1]** **[3]**

**14.** √50 ÷ √2 = √(50 ÷ 2) **[1]** = √25 = **5** **[1]** **[2]**

*Examiner insight:* Dividing the roots is quicker than simplifying each one.

**15.** √200 = 10√2 and √50 = 5√2 **[1]**; **5√2** **[1]** **[2]**

**16.** h² = (√7)² + 3² = 7 + 9 = 16 **[1]**; h = **4 cm** **[1]** **[2]**

*Examiner insight:* Squaring √7 gives 7. The answer is a whole number, so the hypotenuse is rational even though one side is a surd.

**17.** multiply top and bottom by (√5 + 1) **[1]**; numerator 6 + 2√5 and denominator 4 **[1]**; result **3/2 + (1/2)√5** **[1]** **[3]**

## Where marks are usually lost

- Using a square factor that is not the largest in Question 1, and stopping at 2√18.
- Treating √45 + √20 as √65 in Question 2.
- Forgetting the middle term when expanding in Question 5.
- Multiplying only the numerator when rationalising in Questions 7, 9 and 10.
- Using the wrong sign for the conjugate in Questions 10 and 11.
- Leaving the denominator -1 unresolved in Question 10.
- Not cancelling the final fraction in Question 17.
- Forgetting that √7 squared is 7 in Question 16.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I use a calculator to rationalise a denominator?**
You should show the algebra. A calculator decimal can be used to check the result but does not replace the exact working.

**What is a conjugate?**
For a + √b the conjugate is a - √b. Multiplying them gives a² - b, which has no surd.
