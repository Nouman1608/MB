This guide goes deeper on one topic: **Theme 3, Topic 3 Education Post-16** in OxfordAQA
International GCSE Urdu (9264), for International GCSE exams May/June 2026 onwards. The
[Theme 3 study guide](/resources/igcse-oxfordaqa-urdu-study-employment/) shows how the theme moves
from present studies to future work; this page covers the step in between: what you will study
after your current exams, where, and why.

## What this topic covers

The specification (section 3.1.3) lists Education post-16 as the third topic of Theme 3. It deals
with study options after the current stage of schooling:

- **Routes** -- staying on at school, going to a college, vocational training, and later
  university.
- **Choices** -- which subjects to continue, and how to decide.
- **Practicalities** -- admission, applications, fees, scholarships, living away from home.
- **Weighing options** -- the advantages and disadvantages of each route, including studying
  abroad.

Post-16 routes differ from country to country, so the vocabulary here is general. Use the names of
real courses only where you are sure of them, and describe the route in plain words otherwise
(for example, "two more years of study at school"). Jobs and careers belong to Topic 4,
[Jobs, career choices and ambitions](/resources/oxfordaqa-igcse-urdu-language-9264-jobs-career-choices/);
this topic is about study.

## Where this fits in 9264

Education post-16 has no separate paper. It can be tested in Paper 1 (Reading, Grammar and
Meaning), which includes translation from English into Urdu, and in Paper 2 (Writing). Each paper
lasts 1 hour 45 minutes and is worth 70 marks (50% of the qualification).

## Structures that carry this topic

### 1. The future tense

The future ends in گا for a male speaker and گی for a female one:

- امتحانات کے بعد میں کالج جاؤں گا۔ (After my exams I will go to college.)
- میری بہن یونیورسٹی میں داخلہ لے گی۔ (My sister will enrol at university.)

### 2. Intentions: ارادہ ہے کہ and چاہنا

- میرا ارادہ ہے کہ میں سائنس کے مضامین پڑھوں۔ (I intend to study science subjects.) After
  ارادہ ہے کہ the subjunctive (پڑھوں) is the natural choice.
- میں اعلیٰ تعلیم حاصل کرنا چاہتا ہوں۔ (I want to go on to higher education.) A girl says
  چاہتی ہوں.
- میں سوچ رہی ہوں کہ ... (I am thinking that ...) shows a decision not yet made.

### 3. Conditions about results

- اگر میرے نمبر اچھے آئے تو میں وظیفے کے لیے درخواست دوں گا۔ (If I get good marks, I will apply
  for a scholarship.)
- اگر مجھے وظیفہ مل گیا تو فیصلہ آسان ہو جائے گا۔ (If I get a scholarship, the decision will
  become easy.)

### 4. Weighing options

- ایک طرف یونیورسٹی کی تعلیم مہنگی ہے، دوسری طرف اس سے زیادہ مواقع ملتے ہیں۔ (On the one hand
  university is expensive; on the other, it brings more opportunities.)
- پیشہ ورانہ تربیت کا فائدہ یہ ہے کہ ... (The advantage of vocational training is that ...)
- اس کالج کا نقصان یہ ہے کہ وہ گھر سے دور ہے۔ (The disadvantage of this college is that it is far
  from home.)

### 5. Reporting what others want

میرے والدین چاہتے ہیں کہ میں اسی اسکول میں رہوں۔ (My parents want me to stay at the same school.)
Again, the verb after کہ is subjunctive.

## Worked example 1: writing about your plans

Task (original): *Write about your plans after your exams. Say what you intend to study and where,
mention one other option, and give your reasons.*

Model paragraph:

امتحانات کے بعد میرا ارادہ ہے کہ میں کالج میں داخلہ لوں اور سائنس کے مضامین پڑھوں۔ اگر میرے
نمبر اچھے آئے تو مجھے وظیفہ بھی مل سکتا ہے۔ میرے بڑے بھائی نے پیشہ ورانہ تربیت حاصل کی اور اب وہ
بجلی کا کام کرتے ہیں، لیکن مجھے پڑھائی زیادہ پسند ہے۔ ایک طرف یونیورسٹی کی تعلیم مہنگی ہے، دوسری
طرف اس سے مستقبل میں زیادہ مواقع ملتے ہیں۔

Gloss: "After my exams I intend to enrol at college and study science subjects. If I get good marks,
I may also get a scholarship. My older brother did vocational training and now works as an
electrician, but I prefer studying. On the one hand university education is expensive; on the other,
it brings more opportunities in the future."

Why it works:

```
intention (subjunctive)   میرا ارادہ ہے کہ میں ... داخلہ لوں ... پڑھوں
condition                 اگر ... تو ... مل سکتا ہے
other option (past)       میرے بڑے بھائی نے ... تربیت حاصل کی
preference                مجھے پڑھائی زیادہ پسند ہے
balanced judgement        ایک طرف ...، دوسری طرف ...
```

The paragraph does more than state a plan: it compares two routes and weighs cost against
opportunity, which is what makes a post-16 answer persuasive.

## Worked example 2: translating into Urdu

Task (original): *Translate into Urdu: "After my exams I will go to college. I would like to study at
a university abroad, but living there is expensive."*

Answer: امتحانات کے بعد میں کالج جاؤں گا۔ میں بیرونِ ملک کسی یونیورسٹی میں پڑھنا چاہتا ہوں،
لیکن وہاں رہنے کے اخراجات بہت زیادہ ہیں۔

Decisions to make:

1. "Will go" is جاؤں گا (or جاؤں گی for a girl).
2. "Would like to" is ... چاہتا ہوں; Urdu uses the present of چاہنا where English uses "would
   like".
3. "Living there is expensive" reads naturally as "the expenses of living there are very high"
   (وہاں رہنے کے اخراجات بہت زیادہ ہیں). Translating the sense keeps the Urdu natural.

## Worked example 3: a short reading question

Text (original): میرا بھائی یونیورسٹی جانا چاہتا تھا، لیکن والد صاحب کی بیماری کی وجہ سے اس نے
پیشہ ورانہ تربیت کا انتخاب کیا تاکہ وہ جلدی کمانے لگے۔

Question: *Why did the brother choose vocational training?* **[2]**

Answer: Because of his father's illness **[1]**, so that he could start earning sooner **[1]**.

How to get there:

1. Do not confuse the wish with the outcome. چاہتا تھا (he wanted) tells you what he hoped for,
   university, but the question asks what he actually chose and why.
2. کی وجہ سے (because of) gives the cause, and تاکہ (so that) gives the purpose. Two marks, two
   connectives, two points.
3. An answer of "he wanted to go to university" is accurate about the text but answers a different
   question, so it scores nothing.

## Common mistakes

- **Future gender.** A girl writing میں کالج جاؤں گا has the wrong ending; it should be جاؤں گی.
- **داخلہ لینا and داخلہ دینا.** A student takes admission (داخلہ لیتا ہے); the college gives it
  (داخلہ دیتا ہے).
- **One option only.** Stating a plan without weighing any alternative leaves out comparison and
  judgement.
- **Drifting into Topic 4.** "I want to be a doctor" is a career ambition; in this topic, explain
  the study route that leads there.
- **Invented course names.** Describe a route in plain Urdu if you are unsure of its official name.
- **Plural of موقع.** The plural is مواقع, as in زیادہ مواقع.

## Exam technique

- **Track who wants what.** Reading texts on this topic often contrast the writer's wish with a
  parent's or friend's view; questions may ask for either.
- **Spot the condition.** Sentences with اگر ... تو ... often carry the answer to "what would make
  the decision easier" or "what does the plan depend on".
- **Cover every bullet point** of a writing task, and give at least one advantage and one
  disadvantage.
- **Budget by marks.** At about 1.5 minutes per mark, leave time to check every future verb for
  گا / گی.
