## Questions

### Section A: short questions

**1.** Which gland secretes testosterone?

A: adrenal gland

B: ovary

C: pancreas

D: testis

**[1]**

**2.** State how hormones are transported around the body. **[1]**

**3.** Name the hormone secreted by the adrenal glands. **[1]**

**4.** State **two** effects of adrenaline on the body. **[2]**

**5.** *(Extended)* Name the gland that secretes glucagon. **[1]**

**6.** State which type of control, nervous or hormonal, has the longer-lasting effect. **[1]**

### Section B: structured questions

**7.** Hormones are produced by glands and act on target organs.

**(a)** Describe what is meant by a hormone. **[3]**

**(b)** Adrenaline is carried to every organ in the body, but only some organs respond. Suggest why. **[1]**

**8.** Complete the table.

| Gland | Hormone |
|---|---|
| Pancreas | ................ |
| ................ | oestrogen |
| ................ | adrenaline |

**[3]**

**9.** A deer hears a wolf and runs away.

**(a)** State the name given to this kind of situation in which adrenaline is secreted. **[1]**

**(b)** Explain how **two** of the Core effects of adrenaline help the deer to escape. **[4]**

**(c)** *(Extended)* Explain how adrenaline's effect on blood glucose concentration helps the deer's leg muscles. **[2]**

**10.** *(Extended)* The table shows a person's heart rate and blood glucose concentration after an injection of adrenaline.

| Time after injection / minutes | 0 | 3 | 6 | 12 | 24 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Heart rate / beats per minute | 64 | 88 | 96 | 90 | 70 |
| Blood glucose / arbitrary units | 4.8 | 5.3 | 6.0 | 6.6 | 5.6 |

**(a)** Calculate the percentage increase in heart rate between 0 and 6 minutes. **[2]**

**(b)** Describe the changes in blood glucose concentration. **[2]**

**(c)** Name the **two** effects of adrenaline on metabolic activity shown by this data. **[2]**

**11.** A footballer sees a ball flying towards his face and ducks within a fraction of a second. Five minutes later his heart is still beating faster than normal.

**(a)** State which type of control caused him to duck, and give a reason. **[2]**

**(b)** Explain why his heart is still beating faster five minutes later. **[2]**

**12.** Compare nervous control and hormonal control. **[2]**

**13.** A student writes: "Adrenaline is an electrical impulse made in the kidneys. It travels along nerves and decreases the heart rate."

Identify and correct **three** errors in the statement. **[3]**

**14.** *(Extended)* Three hormones affect blood glucose concentration: insulin, glucagon and adrenaline.

**(a)** Name the gland that secretes both insulin and glucagon. **[1]**

**(b)** State which of the three hormones decreases blood glucose concentration. **[1]**

**(c)** Suggest why it is useful for adrenaline to increase blood glucose concentration during a fight or flight situation. **[2]**

## Answers

**1.** **D** [1].

**2.** In the blood (plasma) [1].

**3.** Adrenaline [1].

**4.** Any two of: increased breathing rate; increased heart rate; increased pupil diameter [1] [1].

*Examiner insight:* "Changes pupil size" does not score; say the diameter increases.

**5.** Pancreas [1].

**6.** Hormonal [1].

**7. (a)** A chemical substance produced by a gland [1]; carried by the blood [1]; that alters the activity of one or more specific target organs [1].

**(b)** Only target organs respond to adrenaline [1].

**8.** Pancreas: insulin (or glucagon) [1]; ovaries [1]; adrenal glands [1].

**9. (a)** Fight or flight [1].

**(b)** Any two, each with its explanation:

- increased breathing rate [1], so more oxygen is taken in for respiration in the leg muscles [1]
- increased heart rate [1], so blood carrying oxygen and glucose reaches the muscles faster [1]
- increased pupil diameter [1], so more light enters the eyes and the deer can see better [1]

**(c)** Adrenaline increases the blood glucose concentration [1], so more glucose is available for respiration in the muscle cells, releasing more energy for muscle contraction [1].

*Examiner insight:* In (b), the effect earns one mark and the link to escaping earns the other. Listing three effects with no explanation scores only the effect marks.

**10. (a)** (96 − 64) ÷ 64 × 100 [1] = **50%** [1].

**(b)** Blood glucose increased from 4.8 to a peak of 6.6 arbitrary units at 12 minutes [1]; it then fell to 5.6 by 24 minutes [1].

**(c)** Increasing heart rate [1]; increasing blood glucose concentration [1].

**11. (a)** Nervous control [1]; the response was very fast [1].

**(b)** Adrenaline was secreted (by the adrenal glands) [1]; hormonal effects last longer, so adrenaline is still increasing his heart rate [1].

**12.** Nervous control acts faster than hormonal control [1]; the effects of nervous control are shorter-lived, whereas hormonal effects last longer [1].

*Examiner insight:* The syllabus limits the comparison to speed and duration. Each sentence should mention both types of control.

**13.** Any three corrections: adrenaline is a chemical (hormone), not an electrical impulse [1]; it is made in the adrenal glands, not the kidneys [1]; it travels in the blood, not along nerves [1]; it increases, not decreases, the heart rate [1].

*Examiner insight:* There are four errors, so any three correct corrections score full marks. Each mark needs the correct version, not just the word "wrong".

**14. (a)** Pancreas [1].

**(b)** Insulin [1].

**(c)** More glucose is available to the muscles [1] for respiration, releasing more energy for muscle contraction in fighting or running away [1].

## Where marks are usually lost

- Saying hormones travel along nerves.
- Writing "the whole body" instead of target organs.
- Mixing up adrenal glands and kidneys in body diagrams.
- Listing adrenaline's effects without explaining how they help.
- (Extended) Saying adrenaline decreases blood glucose.
- One-sided comparisons.
