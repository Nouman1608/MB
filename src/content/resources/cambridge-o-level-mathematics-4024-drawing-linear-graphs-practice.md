## Answers

**1.** y at x = -1: -5 **[1]**; at x = 0: -2 and at x = 1: 1 **[1]**; at x = 3: 7 **[1]** **[3]**

*Examiner insight:* y goes up by 3 each time x goes up by 1, which gives a quick check.

**2.** For (4, 10): 3(4) - 2 = 10, so it is on the line **[1]**; for (-1, -4): 3(-1) - 2 = -5, not -4, so it is not on the line **[1]** **[2]**

**3.** y values: **7, 4, 1, -2, -5, -8** **[3]** (two marks for five correct, one for three correct)

**4. (a)** x = 0 gives 3y = 12, so **(0, 4)** **[1]**

**(b)** y = 0 gives 2x = 12, so **(6, 0)** **[1]**

**(c)** 2(3) + 3(2) = 6 + 6 = 12 **[1]** **[3]**

**5.** -2y = -x + 6 **[1]**; **y = x/2 - 3** **[1]** **[2]**

*Examiner insight:* Dividing by -2 changes every sign, so -x becomes x/2 and 6 becomes -3.

**6. (a)** **y = -2** **[1]**

**(b)** **x = -3** **[1]** **[2]**

**7.** table of values: (-2, -8), (-1, -5), (0, -2), (1, 1), (2, 4), (3, 7) **[1]**; points plotted correctly **[1]**; a single straight line drawn with a ruler through all the points **[1]**; the line crosses the y-axis at (0, -2) and slopes upward **[1]** **[4]**

**8.** plot **(0, 4)** and **(6, 0)** **[2]**; the check point (3, 2) lies on the line **[1]** **[3]**

**9.** x = 0 gives y = 5, so **(0, 5)** **[1]**; y = 0 gives 2x = 5, so x = 2.5 **[1]**; **(2.5, 0)** **[1]** **[3]**

**10. (a)** **(1, 2)** **[1]**

**(b)** y = 3(1) - 2 = 1, so **(1, 1)** **[1]** **[2]**

**11.** y = 5 - 2x **[1]**; y values **7, 5, 1, -3** **[1]** **[2]**

**12.** the lines cross at **(2, 3)** **[1]**; y = x + 1: 2 + 1 = 3 ✓ **[1]**; y = 5 - x: 5 - 2 = 3 ✓ **[1]** **[3]**

**13.** The coefficient of x is negative **[1]**; as x increases by 1, y falls by 3 (for example from 4 at x = 0 to 1 at x = 1) **[1]** **[2]**

**14.** They cross at **(-2, 3)** **[1]**; x = -2 is parallel to the y-axis **[1]**; y = 3 is parallel to the x-axis **[1]** **[3]**

## Where marks are usually lost

- Wrong signs with negative x-values in Questions 1 and 3.
- Mixing up the x- and y-intercepts in Question 4.
- Not changing every sign when dividing by -2 in Question 5.
- Confusing horizontal and vertical lines in Question 6.
- Joining points with a freehand line or a zigzag in Question 7.
- Forgetting to extend the line beyond the plotted points.
- Plotting (x, y) the wrong way round.

## Frequently asked questions

**How many points do I need to draw a straight line?**
Two points fix the line, but use three so that you can check your work. If the three points are not in a line, there is an error.

**Does the order of terms matter in y = 4 - 3x?**
No. It is the same as y = -3x + 4. Write it in whichever order helps you substitute.
