**These notes condense key question 1.3, "How was Germany unified?", for Cambridge O Level History 2147.** Learn the
sequence of 1848–50 and of Bismarck's three wars, and be ready to weigh Bismarck against other factors. For full
explanation, use the [Germany study guide](/resources/cambridge-o-level-world-history-2147-unification-germany/); for
exam practice, use the [Germany practice questions](/resources/cambridge-o-level-world-history-2147-unification-germany-practice/).

## Condensed recall by focus point

**Why not unified in 1848–50?**
- The Frankfurt Parliament had no army, no taxes and no way to make princes obey.
- It wasted months arguing over "greater" versus "smaller" Germany.
- Frederick William IV refused its crown (April 1849); the rump was dispersed (June 1849).
- In Prussia the army regained Berlin and the king imposed his own constitution (December 1848).
- Austria recovered, and at Olmütz (1850) forced Prussia to drop the Erfurt Union.

**How did Bismarck bring about Austria's defeat in 1866?**
- Used the Schleswig-Holstein question to fight Denmark alongside Austria (1864).
- Gastein Convention (1865) left the duchies divided, a ready source of quarrels.
- Secured Russian goodwill, French neutrality and an Italian alliance; Britain stayed out.
- Provoked war; Moltke's army won at Königgrätz (3 July 1866).

**How did Bismarck bring about France's defeat in 1870?**
- Refused Napoleon III compensation after 1866; Luxembourg crisis (1867) humiliated France.
- Hohenzollern candidature for the Spanish throne alarmed France.
- Edited Ems telegram provoked France into declaring war (19 July 1870).
- Southern states joined Prussia; Sedan (September 1870) captured Napoleon III.

**How far was Bismarck responsible?**
- For: diplomacy, timing of wars, lenient peace of 1866, Ems.
- Against: Prussian industry and the Zollverein, Roon's army reforms, Moltke's generalship, the mistakes of Austria
  and France, nationalism in the south, non-intervention of Russia and Britain.

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| German Confederation | Loose league of states set up in 1815, chaired by Austria |
| Zollverein | Prussian-led customs union of German states, from 1834 |
| Greater Germany (Grossdeutsch) | A united Germany including Austria's German lands |
| Smaller Germany (Kleindeutsch) | A united Germany under Prussia, excluding Austria |
| Olmütz | 1850 agreement forcing Prussia to abandon its union plan |
| Realpolitik | Politics based on practical power rather than ideals |
| Needle gun | Prussian breech-loading rifle, faster to reload than Austrian muzzle-loaders |
| Indemnity Bill | 1866 law approving Bismarck's earlier spending, which split the liberals |
| North German Confederation | Prussian-led union of northern states from 1867 |
| Ems telegram | Report of the king's meeting with the French ambassador, edited by Bismarck |

## Exam traps

- **Mixing up the wars and their results.** 1864: duchies taken from Denmark. 1866: Austria excluded. 1870–71: south
  joins, Empire created, Alsace-Lorraine taken.
- **Assuming the Frankfurt Parliament was revolutionary.** Most members were moderate liberals who wanted a monarch.
- **Claiming Austria joined the Zollverein.** It was deliberately kept out.
- **Assuming the southern states were eager to join Prussia.** Catholic Bavaria was suspicious; war with France changed
  opinion.
- **In a (c) answer, praising Bismarck without testing the claim** against Moltke, industry and the mistakes of his
  opponents.

## Self-test

1. Who chaired the German Confederation?
2. Why did the Zollverein help Prussia's position in Germany?
3. How did the Schleswig-Holstein crisis of 1848 expose the Frankfurt Parliament's weakness?
4. Why did Frederick William IV refuse the crown in 1849?
5. What did Prussia agree at Olmütz?
6. Why was Bismarck appointed in 1862?
7. What did the Gastein Convention decide?
8. Which country did Prussia ally with in April 1866, and what was it promised?
9. Name **two** consequences of the Treaty of Prague.
10. Why did France object to the Hohenzollern candidature?
11. What did Bismarck do with the Ems telegram?
12. Where and when was the German Empire proclaimed?

**Answers:** 1. Austria. 2. It tied most German states' trade and economies to Prussia while excluding Austria.
3. Prussia made the Malmö armistice with Denmark without consulting it, showing it had no power of its own. 4. He would
not accept a crown from an elected assembly, and feared Austria's reaction. 5. To abandon the Erfurt Union and accept
the restored Confederation under Austria. 6. To break the deadlock with parliament over paying for army reforms. 7.
Prussia would administer Schleswig and Austria Holstein. 8. Italy, promised Venetia. 9. Any two: Austria excluded from
German affairs; Confederation dissolved; Prussia annexed Hanover, Schleswig-Holstein and other states; North German
Confederation formed. 10. It feared being surrounded by Hohenzollern rulers in Prussia and Spain. 11. Edited and
published it so that it seemed both sides had been insulted, provoking France. 12. In the Hall of Mirrors at
Versailles, 18 January 1871.
