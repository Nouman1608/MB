This guide covers **Theme 2, Art and culture in the Urdu-speaking world (اردو بولنے والی دُنیا میں تمدن اور فنونِ لطیفہ)**, in Pearson Edexcel GCE A-Level Urdu (9UR0), for teaching from 2018. Its three sub-themes are folklore and tradition (لوک رسوم و روایت), popular culture (مقبول کلچر) and the media (میڈیا), the last being the research subject. The guide gives the ideas, the vocabulary and a model of controlled written Urdu.

## Where this fits in 9UR0

Theme 2 is one of the content themes of the qualification. As the [Paper 1 study guide](/resources/a-level-edexcel-urdu-translation-and-reading/) describes, research for Theme 2 may be based on Pakistan or on one area of India where Urdu is an official language, but on only one country or area. That is different from Themes 1, 3 and 4, so check which place you chose before you start collecting material. Examples drawn from sources, listening and reading in the [Paper 3 study guide](/resources/a-level-edexcel-urdu-listening-reading-writing/) often draw on cultural subjects, so the vocabulary below is useful beyond the research task.

## Sub-theme 2.1: folklore and tradition (لوک رسوم و روایت)

Ideas to discuss:

- **Oral tradition**: stories, songs and sayings passed on by speaking and performance from one generation to the next.
- **Folk tales and poetry**: romances such as Heer and Ranjha, retold in the Punjabi poem attributed to Waris Shah, composed in the eighteenth century, and Sohni and Mahiwal. These are told in several regional languages and are part of the shared culture of the region.
- **Festivals and customs**: Eid celebrations, melas (fairs), wedding customs such as the mehndi, and seasonal festivals.
- **Performance traditions**: qawwali, folk music and storytelling, and the mushaira (poetry gathering) in Urdu literary culture.
- **Preservation and change**: how traditions are recorded, taught, adapted and sometimes lost.

## Sub-theme 2.2: popular culture (مقبول کلچر)

Ideas to discuss:

- **Television drama**, film and music, and how audiences relate to them.
- **Sport**, especially cricket, as a shared passion, with its fans, heroes and public occasions.
- **Fashion and food**, and the role of festivals and markets.
- **Youth culture and social media**: how young people follow trends, create content and share them.
- **Local and global influences**: the way regional entertainment and international styles mix, and the debate about whether this enriches or dilutes local culture.

## Sub-theme 2.3: the media (میڈیا)

This is the research subject. Your research must cover the aspects set by the specification, concern one country or area allowed for this theme, and be presented in Urdu. Areas to explore:

- Print media (newspapers and magazines) and broadcast media (radio and television), and the growth of private television channels.
- **Online and social media**, and how people get news and entertainment.
- The **influence** of the media on opinion, language and behaviour.
- **Freedom and responsibility**: regulation, censorship, accuracy and the spread of rumours.
- The use of Urdu in the media and how its register changes between news, drama and advertising.

Build a research file with named sources, dates and figures you have checked yourself, and never quote a figure you cannot trace. Compare at least two viewpoints on each point so that you can discuss both benefits and risks.

## Worked example: a model paragraph, with annotation

Task: write a short opinion on the importance of folk traditions.

لوک گیت اور کہانیاں نسل در نسل زبانی طور پر منتقل ہوتی رہی ہیں۔ ہیر رانجھا جیسی داستانیں آج بھی ڈراموں اور گانوں میں پیش کی جاتی ہیں۔ میری رائے میں اگر نوجوان ان روایات سے کٹ گئے تو ہماری ثقافتی شناخت کمزور ہو جائے گی۔ اس لیے اسکولوں اور میڈیا کو چاہیے کہ وہ ان روایات کو زندہ رکھیں۔

What makes it work:

- **Structure**: a general statement, an example, an opinion with a condition (اگر ... تو), and a recommendation.
- **Agreement**: the plural subject لوک گیت اور کہانیاں takes the plural feminine verb form ہوتی رہی ہیں.
- **Passive voice**: پیش کی جاتی ہیں is a passive, agreeing with the feminine plural داستانیں.
- **Connectives**: اس لیے, اگر ... تو and میری رائے میں give the argument its direction.

## Useful connectives and phrases

Learn these as ready-made building blocks and practise using each in a sentence of your own.

| Urdu | Use |
|---|---|
| اس کے برعکس | in contrast |
| اس کے علاوہ | in addition |
| نتیجتاً | as a result |
| میرے خیال میں | in my opinion |
| ایک طرف ... دوسری طرف | on one hand ... on the other |
| آخر میں | finally |

Build each paragraph around one of these links so that your argument can be followed from the first sentence to the last.

## Common mistakes

- Treating Theme 2 as only literature. It also covers customs, popular entertainment and media.
- Writing a list of cultural items without explaining their importance.
- Dropping gender agreement in passives and compound verbs.
- Research on the media based on several countries instead of one.
- Vocabulary taken from English with no check that an Urdu equivalent is more suitable.

## Exam technique

Choose one clear viewpoint and support it with specific examples. Use a short plan, one idea per paragraph, and a conclusion that answers the statement. Where a source text is given, use it as well as your own research and make clear which is which. Allow time to check script, agreement and spelling.

## Check your understanding

1. What does لوک رسوم و روایت refer to?
   *Answer: Folk customs and tradition.*
2. Give the Urdu for "poetry gathering".
   *Answer: مشاعرہ.*
3. For Theme 2 research, how many countries or areas may you use?
   *Answer: One: Pakistan or one area of India where Urdu is an official language.*
4. Why does the verb in داستانیں پیش کی جاتی ہیں end in ہیں?
   *Answer: The subject is plural, and the feminine plural passive agrees with it.*
5. Name two kinds of media you could compare in research.
   *Answer: For example, newspapers and television, or print and social media.*
6. Translate into Urdu: "the media influences young people".
   *Answer: میڈیا نوجوانوں پر اثر ڈالتا ہے۔*
