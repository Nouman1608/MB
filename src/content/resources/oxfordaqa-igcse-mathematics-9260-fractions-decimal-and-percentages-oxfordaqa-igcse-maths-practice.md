## Section A: Short questions (Core and Extension)

**1.** Write 9/40 as **(a)** a decimal and **(b)** a percentage. **[2]**

**2.** Write 0.075 as a fraction in its simplest form. **[2]**

**3.** Write these numbers in order of size, smallest first. **[2]**

0.42, 3/7, 41.5%, 5/12

**4.** Work out 2/3 of 3/4 of 840. **[2]**

**5.** Write 1 hour 15 minutes as a percentage of 50 minutes. **[2]**

**6.** Write 84/126 in its simplest form. **[1]**

## Section B: Structured questions

**7.** A club raises $2,400. It gives 3/8 of the money to charity A and 0.25 of the money to charity B. It gives 30% of the remainder to charity C and keeps the rest.

**(a)** Work out how much charity C receives. **[3]**

**(b)** What fraction of the $2,400 does the club keep? Give your answer in its simplest form. **[2]**

**8.** Without using a calculator, show that 35% of 160 is greater than 2/7 of 189. **[3]**

**9.** Amir scored 51 out of 60 in Test 1 and 69 out of 80 in Test 2. In which test did he do better? Show how you decide. **[3]**

**10.** The population of Town A rose from 18,000 to 22,500. The population of Town B rose from 40,000 to 48,000.

**(a)** Write the new population of Town A as a percentage of its old population. **[2]**

**(b)** Which town's population increased by the greater percentage? Show your working. **[3]**

**11.** A jacket costs $80. In a sale its price is reduced by 1/4. A further 10% is then taken off the sale price.

**(a)** Work out the final price of the jacket. **[2]**

**(b)** Write the total reduction as a percentage of the original price. **[2]**

**(c)** Explain why the total reduction is not 35%. **[1]**

**12.** *(Extension only)*

**(a)** Write 0.636363... as a fraction in its simplest form. **[3]**

**(b)** Show that 0.1333... = 2/15. **[3]**

**13.** *(Extension only)* Use fractions to show that 0.444... + 0.555... = 1. **[2]**

**14.** *(Extension only)* A shop sells a phone for $240. This selling price is 160% of the price the shop paid for it. Work out the price the shop paid. **[2]**

## Answers

**1. (a)** 9 ÷ 40 = **0.225** [1].

**(b)** 0.225 × 100 = **22.5%** [1]. **[2]**

**2.** 0.075 = 75/1000 [1] = **3/40** (dividing by the HCF, 25) [1]. **[2]**

**3.** Decimals: 0.4200, 0.4286, 0.4150, 0.4167 [1]. Order: **41.5%, 5/12, 0.42, 3/7** [1]. **[2]**

*Examiner insight:* write the converted decimals to four places so that the comparison is clear, then give the answer in the original forms.

**4.** 3/4 of 840 = 630 [1]; 2/3 of 630 = **420** [1]. (Or 2/3 × 3/4 = 1/2, and 1/2 of 840 = 420.) **[2]**

**5.** 1 hour 15 minutes = 75 minutes [1]; 75 ÷ 50 × 100 = **150%** [1]. **[2]**

**6.** **2/3** (dividing by the HCF, 42) [1]. **[1]**

**7. (a)** A: 3/8 × 2,400 = 900, and B: 0.25 × 2,400 = 600 [1]. Remainder = 2,400 − 900 − 600 = 900 [1]. C: 0.3 × 900 = **$270** [1].

**(b)** Kept = 900 − 270 = 630 [1]; 630/2,400 = **21/80** [1]. **[5]**

*Examiner insight:* "30% of the remainder" acts on $900, not on $2,400. Finding the remainder first earns a method mark even if a later step goes wrong.

**8.** 35% of 160: 10% = 16, so 30% = 48 and 5% = 8, giving 56 [1]. 2/7 of 189: 189 ÷ 7 = 27, and 27 × 2 = 54 [1]. 56 > 54, so 35% of 160 is greater [1]. **[3]**

**9.** Test 1: 51 ÷ 60 × 100 = 85% [1]. Test 2: 69 ÷ 80 × 100 = 86.25% [1]. He did better in **Test 2** [1]. **[3]**

**10. (a)** 22,500 ÷ 18,000 × 100 [1] = **125%** [1].

**(b)** Town A: increase of 4,500, and 4,500 ÷ 18,000 × 100 = 25% [1]. Town B: increase of 8,000, and 8,000 ÷ 40,000 × 100 = 20% [1]. **Town A** had the greater percentage increase [1]. **[5]**

*Examiner insight:* Town B gained more people (8,000 against 4,500) but a smaller percentage. Percentage change always divides by the original value.

**11. (a)** Single multiplier: 3/4 × 0.9 = 0.675 [1]. Final price = 0.675 × 80 = **$54** [1].

**(b)** Reduction = 80 − 54 = $26 [1]; 26 ÷ 80 × 100 = **32.5%** [1].

**(c)** The 10% is taken off the sale price of $60, not off $80, so it is only $6, and 1/4 then 10% combine by multiplying the multipliers (0.75 × 0.9), not by adding the percentages [1]. **[5]**

*Examiner insight:* successive reductions are operators applied one after another (N13). Multiply them; never add them.

**12. (a)** x = 0.6363..., so 100x = 63.6363... [1]. 99x = 63 [1]. x = 63/99 = **7/11** [1].

**(b)** x = 0.1333..., 10x = 1.333..., 100x = 13.333... [1]. 100x − 10x: 90x = 12 [1]. x = 12/90 = 2/15 [1]. **[6]**

**13.** 0.444... = 4/9 and 0.555... = 5/9 [1]. 4/9 + 5/9 = 9/9 = 1 [1]. **[2]**

**14.** 240 ÷ 1.6 [1] = **$150** [1]. **[2]**

*Examiner insight:* 160% means the multiplier is 1.6, so divide by it. Finding 60% of 240 and subtracting gives $96, which is wrong.

## Where marks are usually lost

- Ordering by number of digits instead of by value, or giving the order as converted decimals.
- Applying a percentage to the whole amount when the question says "of the remainder".
- Leaving fractions unsimplified when the simplest form is asked for.
- Dividing a percentage change by the new value.
- Writing only the final line in a "show that" question.
- In reverse percentages, working out a percentage of the given value instead of dividing by the multiplier.
