## Section A

**1.** (non-calculator) Write an expression for

**(a)** 5 less than 3 times m, **[1]**

**(b)** the total cost, in cents, of x apples at 40 cents each and y bananas at 25 cents each. **[1]**

**2.** (non-calculator) p = 6 and q = −4. Find the value of

**(a)** 3p + 2q, **[1]**

**(b)** p² − q². **[1]**

**3.** (non-calculator) a = −5. Find the value of

**(a)** a² − 3a, **[1]**

**(b)** 2a³. **[1]**

**4.** (non-calculator) P = 2(l + w). Find P when l = 8.5 and w = 3.25. **[2]**

**5.** (non-calculator) n is an integer.

**(a)** 2n is an even number. Write an expression for the next even number after 2n. **[1]**

**(b)** Show that the sum of the two consecutive odd numbers 2n + 1 and 2n + 3 is always a multiple of 4. **[2]**

**6.** (non-calculator) A plumber charges a call-out fee of $40 plus $35 for each hour worked. Write a formula for the total charge, C dollars, for a job lasting h hours. **[2]**

## Section B

**7.** (non-calculator) x = ½ and y = −3. Find the value of

**(a)** 4x − y, **[1]**

**(b)** xy², **[2]**

**(c)** (x − y) ÷ (2x). **[2]**

**8.** (calculator) The time, T seconds, for one swing of a pendulum of length l metres is given by T = 2π√(l ÷ g), where g = 9.8.

**(a)** Find T when l = 1.6. Give your answer correct to 3 significant figures. **[2]**

**(b)** Without using your calculator, explain what happens to T when l is multiplied by 4. **[1]**

**9.** (calculator) The volume of a cylinder is V = πr²h.

**(a)** Find V when r = 3.5 and h = 8. Give your answer correct to 3 significant figures. **[2]**

**(b)** Find h when V = 500 and r = 4. Give your answer correct to 3 significant figures. **[2]**

**10.** (non-calculator) Rosa is r years old. Her brother is 3 years older than Rosa. Her mother's age is twice the sum of Rosa's age and her brother's age.

**(a)** Write an expression for her brother's age. **[1]**

**(b)** Write an expression for her mother's age, in its simplest form. **[1]**

**(c)** Write an expression for the total of all three ages, in its simplest form. **[2]**

**(d)** Find the total of their ages when Rosa is 12. **[1]**

**11.** (calculator) An object moves with initial speed u m/s and acceleration a m/s².

**(a)** Use s = ut + ½at² to find s when u = 8, t = 2.5 and a = −3.2. **[2]**

**(b)** Use v² = u² + 2as to find v when u = 8, a = −3.2 and s takes your value from part (a). **[2]**

**12.** (non-calculator) n, n + 1 and n + 2 are three consecutive integers.

**(a)** Write an expression for their sum, in its simplest form. **[2]**

**(b)** Explain why the sum of any three consecutive integers is a multiple of 3. **[1]**

**(c)** Sam says that the sum of any three consecutive integers is a multiple of 6. Give an example to show that Sam is wrong. **[1]**

## Answers

**1. (a)** **3m − 5** [1]

**(b)** **40x + 25y** [1] **[2]**

**2. (a)** 18 + (−8) = **10** [1]

**(b)** 36 − 16 = **20** [1] **[2]**

*Examiner insight:* (−4)² = 16, so p² − q² = 36 − 16. Writing −4² = −16 gives 36 + 16 = 52.

**3. (a)** 25 − (−15) = **40** [1]

**(b)** 2 × (−125) = **−250** [1] **[2]**

**4.** 2 × (8.5 + 3.25) = 2 × 11.75 [1] = **23.5** [1] **[2]**

**5. (a)** **2n + 2** [1]

**(b)** (2n + 1) + (2n + 3) = 4n + 4 [1]; = 4(n + 1), which is 4 times an integer, so it is a multiple of 4 [1] **[2]**

*Examiner insight:* testing one pair, such as 5 + 7 = 12, is not enough. The algebra shows the result for every integer n.

**6.** 40 + 35h seen [1]; **C = 40 + 35h** [1] **[2]**

*Examiner insight:* the second mark needs "C =". Without it, 40 + 35h is an expression, not a formula.

**7. (a)** 4 × ½ − (−3) = 2 + 3 = **5** [1]

**(b)** ½ × (−3)² = ½ × 9 [1] = **4.5** [1]

**(c)** (½ − (−3)) ÷ (2 × ½) = 3.5 ÷ 1 [1] = **3.5** [1] **[5]**

*Examiner insight:* in (b) only y is squared. (xy)² = (−1.5)² = 2.25 is a different expression.

**8. (a)** 2π√(1.6 ÷ 9.8) [1] = 2.5387... = **2.54 s** [1]

**(b)** √(4l ÷ g) = 2√(l ÷ g), so **T doubles** [1] **[3]**

**9. (a)** π × 3.5² × 8 [1] = 307.87... = **308** [1]

**(b)** 500 = π × 16 × h, so h = 500 ÷ (16π) [1] = 9.947... = **9.95** [1] **[4]**

**10. (a)** **r + 3** [1]

**(b)** 2(r + r + 3) = **4r + 6** [1]

**(c)** r + (r + 3) + (4r + 6) [1] = **6r + 9** [1]

**(d)** 6 × 12 + 9 = **81** [1] **[5]**

**11. (a)** 8 × 2.5 + ½ × (−3.2) × 2.5² = 20 + (−10) [1] = **10** [1]

**(b)** v² = 8² + 2 × (−3.2) × 10 = 64 − 64 [1] = 0, so **v = 0** [1] **[4]**

*Examiner insight:* in (a), square 2.5 before multiplying by −3.2 and ½. Squaring (½ × −3.2 × 2.5) instead gives a wrong positive value.

**12. (a)** n + n + 1 + n + 2 [1] = **3n + 3** [1]

**(b)** 3n + 3 = 3(n + 1), which is 3 times an integer [1]

**(c)** For example 2 + 3 + 4 = 9, which is **not** a multiple of 6 [1] **[4]**

*Examiner insight:* in (c) one counterexample is enough to show a general statement is false, but the example must be checked: 1 + 2 + 3 = 6 does not disprove Sam's claim.

## Where marks are usually lost

- Squaring a negative value without brackets, as in Questions 2(b) and 3.
- Squaring the whole product xy instead of y alone, as in Question 7(b).
- Testing numbers instead of using algebra in "show that" questions about all integers.
- Leaving out "C =" when a formula is asked for.
- Writing the mother's age as 2r + 3 instead of 2(2r + 3) in Question 10.
- Rounding part-way through a long substitution on the calculator paper.

## Frequently asked questions

**What is the difference between an expression and a formula?**
An expression, such as 40 + 35h, has no equals sign. A formula has a subject and an equals sign, such as C = 40 + 35h.

**Do I need to learn formulas like s = ut + ½at² for 0580?**
Formulas like these are given in the question when they are needed. Your job is to substitute accurately.

**Why should I put negative values in brackets?**
So that a power or a subtraction applies to the whole number, including its sign, as in (−4)² = 16.
