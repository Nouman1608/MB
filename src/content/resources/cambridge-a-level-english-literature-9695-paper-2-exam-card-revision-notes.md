# Paper 2 exam card

## Structure

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Paper | Prose and Unseen, written, 2 hours |
| Total | 50 marks (Section A 25, Section B 25) |
| Section A: Prose | One question on a prose set text; (a) essay or (b) passage-based |
| Section B: Unseen | One question from a choice of two, each on a previously unseen text |
| Allowed | No set texts; no dictionaries |
| Weight | 50 per cent of AS Level; 25 per cent of A Level |

## Unseen rules from the syllabus

- Passages cover two of the three categories: prose, poetry and drama.
- All passages are from works originally written in English.
- Authors and dates are not given.
- Literary or historical background is not expected.
- You are tested on reading critically and on how a writer's choices of language, form and structure express meaning, and on knowledge of the text type.

## Assessment objectives (each 25 per cent)

AO1 knowledge and understanding; AO2 analysis of language, form and structure; AO3 personal response; AO4 communication. AO5 is not assessed on Paper 2.

## Paragraph check

Point, short evidence, named choice, effect, link to the question or to your overall argument.

## Self-test

1. Which two of prose, poetry and drama appear in the unseen section? *Two of the three, as set on the paper.*
2. Is background knowledge expected for the unseen? *No.*
3. How many unseen questions do you answer? *One of two.*
4. What are Section A's two question types? *(a) an essay; (b) a question based on a printed passage.*
5. What is the maximum for each section? *25 marks.*

*Sources: Cambridge International, AS & A Level Literature in English 9695 syllabus for 2024, 2025 and 2026 (sections 3 and 4).*
