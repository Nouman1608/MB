## Questions

**1.** Convert 3.2 m² to cm². **[1]**

**2.** *(no calculator)* A sector of a circle has radius 9 cm and arc length 5π cm. Find the angle of the sector and its area, giving the area in terms of π. **[3]**

**3.** *(no calculator)* A circle has area 36π cm². Find its circumference in terms of π. **[2]**

**4.** *(no calculator)* A cylinder has radius 5 cm and height 8 cm. Find its volume in terms of π. **[2]**

**5.** A solid is made by joining a cone to a hemisphere. Both have diameter 12 cm. The total height of the solid is 20 cm. Find the volume of the solid. **[4]**

**6.** A trapezium has parallel sides of 7 cm and 11 cm, and a perpendicular height of 5 cm. Find its area. **[2]**

---

## Answers

**1.** 1 m² = 100 × 100 = 10 000 cm², so 3.2 m² = **32 000 cm²** [1].

*Tip:* square the length conversion: there are 100² square centimetres in a square metre.

*Try the real question next:* Cambridge O Level Mathematics 4024, June 2025, Paper 22, Question 5.

**2.** 5π = (θ/360) × 2π × 9, so θ = **100°** [1]. Area = (100/360) × π × 9² [1] = **22.5π cm²** [1].

*Tip:* find the angle from the arc length first, then use it for the area.

*Try the real question next:* Cambridge O Level Mathematics 4024, June 2025, Paper 12, Question 23.

**3.** πr² = 36π, so r = 6 cm [1]. Circumference = 2π × 6 = **12π cm** [1].

*Tip:* work back to the radius before using any other formula.

*Try the real question next:* Cambridge O Level Mathematics 4024, June 2025, Paper 12, Question 23.

**4.** V = π × 5² × 8 [1] = **200π cm³** [1].

*Tip:* square the radius, not the diameter.

*Try the real question next:* Cambridge O Level Mathematics 4024, June 2025, Paper 22, Question 21.

**5.** Radius = 6 cm, so the height of the cone = 20 − 6 = 14 cm [1]. Hemisphere = ⅔π × 6³ = 144π [1]. Cone = ⅓π × 6² × 14 = 168π [1]. Total = 312π = **980 cm³** (3 s.f.) [1].

*Tip:* the hemisphere's height is its radius; subtract it from the total height to get the cone's height.

*Try the real question next:* Cambridge O Level Mathematics 4024, June 2025, Paper 22, Question 21.

**6.** ½ × (7 + 11) × 5 [1] = **45 cm²** [1].

*Tip:* the height must be perpendicular to the parallel sides.

## Where marks are usually lost

- Converting m² to cm² by multiplying by 100 instead of 10 000.
- Using the diameter as the radius.
- Using the total height of a composite solid as the cone's height.
