**These notes condense content area 3.1.1.1 of 9237: "the different types of Tawhid and Shirk, and their meaning and importance".** The full explanation is in the [Oneness of God (Tawhid) study guide](/resources/oxfordaqa-igcse-islamiyat-9237-oneness-of-god-tawhid/). Use this page for recall: read a section, cover it, say it back.

## One sentence to remember

**Tawhid** is the belief that Allah is one and unique: in His lordship, in His right to be worshipped, and in His names and qualities. **Shirk** is giving any part of that to someone or something else.

## The three types of Tawhid

| Type | Arabic | Meaning | Key phrase | Proof |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lordship | Tawhid ar-Rububiyyah | Allah alone creates, owns and sustains | "Is there a creator other than Allah?" | 35:3 |
| Worship | Tawhid al-Uluhiyyah | Allah alone is worshipped and asked for help | "You alone we worship" | 1:5 |
| Names and qualities | Tawhid al-Asma wa as-Sifat | Allah's names are unique; nothing is like Him | "There is nothing like unto Him" | 42:11 |

**Memory aid:** **R**uler, **W**orship, **N**ames, or "Creates, Worshipped, Unlike".

- Some books add Tawhid al-Hakimiyyah (Allah's authority to legislate). Mention it only as an extra.
- The people of Makkah accepted Rububiyyah but mixed in idols, which is why the Prophet (pbuh) stressed Uluhiyyah.

## Surah al-Ikhlas (Qur'an 112:1-4), line by line

| Words | Shows |
|---|---|
| "He is Allah, [who is] One" | Allah is single and unique (*Ahad*) |
| "the Eternal Refuge" | All depend on Him; He depends on none (*As-Samad*) |
| "He neither begets nor is born" | No children, no parents, no beginning |
| "nor is there to Him any equivalent" | No partner or equal of any kind |

Why it is the prescribed passage: it is the shortest complete statement of Tawhid and is recited in prayer.

## Shirk

**Definition:** associating partners with Allah. Qur'an 4:48: Allah does not forgive association with Him if a person dies still in it, but forgives lesser sins for whom He wills. Qur'an 31:13 calls it "great injustice".

| | Major shirk (*akbar*) | Minor shirk (*asghar*) |
|---|---|---|
| What | Worshipping others, or giving others Allah's powers | Show-off and weakened sincerity |
| Examples | Idols; praying to the dead as if they could answer on their own; saying Allah has a child | **Riya'**; trusting a lucky charm |
| Status | Outside Islam unless repented | A grave sin; reduces reward |
| Remedy | Repent and return to Tawhid | Repent and renew sincerity |

**Hidden shirk (*khafi*):** a name for subtle show-off in the heart.

## Why Tawhid is important

1. **Basis of the Shahadah** and so of Islam.
2. **Equality:** all are servants of one Lord, so no one is above another by birth.
3. **Freedom from fear:** only Allah brings benefit or harm.
4. **Sincerity:** prayer, sacrifice, life and death are "for Allah" (6:162).
5. **Unity:** one God, one Qiblah, one Ummah.
6. **Trust (tawakkul):** effort, then reliance on Allah.

**Hadith link:** the Prophet (pbuh) told Mu'adh that Allah's right over His servants is that they worship Him and associate nothing with Him (Bukhari and Muslim).

## Link to other topics

- **Shahadah (3.1.2.2):** the first half of the Shahadah is Tawhid in a sentence.
- **Salah (3.1.2.3):** the Azaan and every prayer repeat Tawhid; "Allahu Akbar" means Allah is greater than anything.
- **Key beliefs (3.1.1.2):** Tawhid heads the list.
- **99 names (3.1.1.3):** the names explain Allah's qualities, the third type of Tawhid.
- **Makkah (3.2.1.4 and 3.2.1.5):** the call to Tawhid and the rejection of idols caused the opposition.

## Exam traps

- Do not stop at "one God": say **types**, **meaning**, **importance**.
- Do not say Muslims "worship the Ka'aba" or "worship Muhammad (pbuh)". They worship Allah alone.
- Minor shirk does not mean leaving Islam.
- A "give" question wants a name or example, not a paragraph.
- Do not turn the passage into a quotation dump: say what each phrase shows.

## A model line for each tariff

- **2 marks, "Give two types of Tawhid":** Rububiyyah (oneness of lordship); Uluhiyyah (oneness of worship).
- **4 marks, "Explain two ways Shirk can be committed":** Worshipping an idol or other created thing, which gives it the worship that belongs to Allah alone [2]. Doing good deeds for praise, which spoils sincerity because the deed is meant for people not Allah [2].
- **12 marks:** plan three points (foundation, equality, freedom from fear), tie each to a phrase of Qur'an 112:1-4, add a counter-view (other beliefs also shape behaviour), then reach a judgement.

## A model 6-mark paragraph

**Question:** Explain **two** reasons why Tawhid is important. Apply **one** teaching. **[6]**

Tawhid is the foundation of the Shahadah, so every other belief and practice rests on it [1 and 1]. It also gives equality and freedom from fear, because if Allah alone is worshipped no human ruler or class has a claim over another [1 and 1]. Surah al-Ikhlas says "nor is there to Him any equivalent", so nothing and nobody can be treated as Allah's equal, and all people stand equal as His servants [2 for a specific, applied use of the source].

**Why it scores:** two reasons, each explained, plus a source used to prove one of them.

## Self-test

1. Name the three types of Tawhid.
2. What does *As-Samad* mean?
3. Which verse says Allah does not forgive association with Him?
4. Give one example of major shirk.
5. Give one example of minor shirk.
6. What is riya'?
7. Why did the people of Makkah accept Rububiyyah but still need the message of Uluhiyyah?
8. Give two ways Tawhid affects a Muslim's behaviour.
9. What does "He neither begets nor is born" teach?
10. How is Tawhid linked to the Shahadah?

**Answers:** 1. Rububiyyah, Uluhiyyah and Asma wa as-Sifat. 2. The Eternal Refuge: everyone needs Him and He needs no one. 3. Qur'an 4:48. 4. Worshipping an idol (or praying to a created being as if it had Allah's power). 5. Showing off in worship (riya'). 6. Doing a good deed so that people will praise you instead of seeking Allah's approval. 7. They believed Allah created the world but also worshipped idols as intermediaries, so they did not give worship to Allah alone. 8. Any two: sincerity in prayer; equality; trust in Allah; courage against superstition; gratitude. 9. Allah has no children and no parents, and was not created, so nothing is part of Him or equal to Him. 10. The first half of the Shahadah declares that there is no deity except Allah, which is the statement of Tawhid.
