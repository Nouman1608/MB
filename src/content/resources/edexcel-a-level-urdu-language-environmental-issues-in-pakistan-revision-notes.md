Condensed recall for **sub-theme 4.3** of Theme 4 in Pearson Edexcel A Level Urdu (9UR0): measures to address environmental issues in Pakistan (پاکستان میں ماحولیاتی مسائل سے متعلّق اقدامات), which is the research subject for this theme. For fuller explanation and a model paragraph, read the [9UR0 subject guide](/resources/edexcel-a-level-urdu-language-9ur0-subject-guide/) and the [Paper 1 study guide](/resources/a-level-edexcel-urdu-translation-and-reading/).

## Research rules

- Theme 4 research must be **based on Pakistan** and presented in Urdu.
- Choose the responses to environmental issues that you can support with named, dated, checked sources.
- Keep a research file of named sources, dates and figures you have checked yourself; never quote a statistic you cannot trace. Compare at least two viewpoints on each point.

## Sentence to learn

ماحولیاتی آلودگی ایک بڑا مسئلہ ہے۔ (Environmental pollution is a big problem.)

## Building a Section C answer

Use both your research and the unseen Urdu text, and make clear which is which. Plan first, give each paragraph one main idea, and link paragraphs with connectives such as **اس کے علاوہ** and **نتیجتاً**. See the [Paper 1 study guide](/resources/a-level-edexcel-urdu-translation-and-reading/).

## Exam traps

- Research not about Pakistan.
- Describing problems without discussing the measures taken.
- Untraceable statistics.

## Self-test

1. Give the Urdu for 'climate change' and 'flood'.
2. Translate: 'environmental pollution is a big problem'.
3. Which country must the research be based on?
4. What should you keep in your research file?
5. How do you show examiners which material is research and which is from the text?

**Answers:** 1. موسمیاتی تبدیلی and سیلاب. 2. ماحولیاتی آلودگی ایک بڑا مسئلہ ہے۔ 3. Pakistan. 4. Named sources, dates and figures you have checked yourself. 5. Make clear in the answer which is which.
