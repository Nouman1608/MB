Revision notes for **sub-theme 4.3** of Theme 4 in Pearson Edexcel A Level Urdu (9UR0): measures to address environmental issues in Pakistan (پاکستان میں ماحولیاتی مسائل سے متعلّق اقدامات), which is the research subject for this theme. For the wider theme, read the [9UR0 subject guide](/resources/edexcel-a-level-urdu-language-9ur0-subject-guide/) and the [Paper 1 study guide](/resources/a-level-edexcel-urdu-translation-and-reading/).

## Research rules

- Theme 4 research must be **based on Pakistan** and presented in Urdu.
- Choose the responses to environmental issues that you can support with named, dated, checked sources.
- Keep a research file of named sources, dates and figures you have checked yourself; never quote a statistic you cannot trace. Compare at least two viewpoints on each point.

## Issues and perspectives

The wording of the sub-theme is about **اقدامات** (measures), so the problems are only the starting point. For each issue, be ready to say what has been done, by whom, and how well it has worked.

**The main problems to know.**

- **Floods (سیلاب)**: Pakistan has suffered severe floods, notably in 2010 and 2022, which damaged homes, crops and infrastructure across large areas.
- **Heat and water**: heatwaves (**گرمی کی لہر**), drought (**خشک سالی**) and water shortage (**پانی کی قلت**) affect farming and cities alike. Glaciers (**گلیشیئر**) in the northern mountains feed the rivers, so their melting raises concern about both floods and long-term supply.
- **Air pollution and smog**: in winter, cities in Punjab such as Lahore are often covered by smog (**اسموگ**, also spelled **سموگ**), linked to vehicle exhaust, industry, brick kilns and crop-residue burning.
- **Waste and plastic**: rubbish (**کچرا**) and plastic bags block drains and pollute rivers and the sea.
- **Deforestation** (**جنگلات کی کٹائی**): loss of forest cover increases soil erosion and flood risk.

**Measures you can research.**

- **Tree planting (شجرکاری)**: large campaigns such as the Billion Tree Tsunami in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa and the later national Ten Billion Tree Tsunami programme. Supporters praise their scale; critics ask how many saplings survive and how results are checked.
- **Bans on plastic bags** in some cities and provinces, and how far they are enforced.
- **Anti-smog measures**: restrictions on burning crop residue, controls on brick kilns and vehicles, and school closures or warnings on the worst days.
- **Flood management**: early warning, rescue and relief, and the debate about rebuilding in safer places.
- **Clean energy**: growth of solar power (**شمسی توانائی**) on homes and farms, and policy on renewable energy (**قابلِ تجدید توانائی**).
- **Awareness (آگاہی)**: campaigns in schools and the media, and the work of non-governmental organisations.

**Evaluating the measures.** A recurring argument is that Pakistan contributes relatively little to global emissions but suffers heavily from climate change, so some argue that richer countries should help pay for adaptation. Another is that laws exist but **عمل درآمد** (enforcement) is weak. A balanced answer gives credit for what has been done, identifies a weakness, and suggests what would make the measure work better.

## Sentence to learn

ماحولیاتی آلودگی ایک بڑا مسئلہ ہے۔ (Environmental pollution is a big problem.)

## Useful sentences with grammar notes

1. **حکومت نے پلاسٹک کے تھیلوں پر پابندی لگائی ہے۔** (The government has banned plastic bags.) With **نے**, the verb agrees with the object پابندی (feminine): لگائی ہے.
2. **شجرکاری مہم کے تحت لاکھوں پودے لگائے گئے۔** (Under the tree-planting campaign, a great many saplings were planted.) Passive لگائے گئے agrees with پودے (masculine plural). لاکھوں means 'hundreds of thousands' loosely; use an exact figure only from a source.
3. **سردیوں میں اسموگ کی وجہ سے لوگوں کو سانس لینے میں دشواری ہوتی ہے۔** (In winter, people have difficulty breathing because of smog.) Dative construction: لوگوں کو ... دشواری ہوتی ہے, with the verb agreeing with دشواری (feminine).
4. **اگر ان اقدامات پر عمل نہ کیا گیا تو آنے والی نسلوں کو نقصان ہو گا۔** (If these measures are not acted on, future generations will suffer.) Note نہ (not نہیں) inside the condition, and نقصان ہو گا agreeing with the masculine نقصان.
5. **ماہرین کے مطابق صرف قانون بنانا کافی نہیں، اس پر عمل درآمد بھی ضروری ہے۔** (According to experts, making a law is not enough; enforcing it is also necessary.) **کے مطابق** is the key phrase for attributing a view to a source.

## Building a Section C answer

Use both your research and the unseen Urdu text, and make clear which is which. Plan first, give each paragraph one main idea, and link paragraphs with connectives such as **اس کے علاوہ** and **نتیجتاً**. See the [Paper 1 study guide](/resources/a-level-edexcel-urdu-translation-and-reading/).

Useful signposts: **میری تحقیق کے مطابق** (according to my research) for your own material, and **دیے گئے متن میں** (in the given text) for the source.

## Model paragraph

پاکستان کو موسمیاتی تبدیلی کے سنگین اثرات کا سامنا ہے، جن میں سیلاب، گرمی کی لہریں اور پانی کی قلت شامل ہیں۔ میری تحقیق کے مطابق حکومت نے ان مسائل سے نمٹنے کے لیے کئی اقدامات کیے ہیں، مثلاً بڑے پیمانے پر شجرکاری کی مہمات اور بعض شہروں میں پلاسٹک کے تھیلوں پر پابندی۔ دیے گئے متن میں بھی اس بات پر زور دیا گیا ہے کہ عوامی آگاہی کے بغیر کوئی منصوبہ کامیاب نہیں ہو سکتا۔ میرے خیال میں یہ اقدامات درست سمت میں ایک قدم ہیں، لیکن جب تک قوانین پر سختی سے عمل درآمد نہیں ہو گا، نتائج محدود رہیں گے۔

**Translation:** Pakistan faces serious effects of climate change, including floods, heatwaves and water shortage. According to my research, the government has taken several measures to tackle these problems, for example large-scale tree-planting campaigns and a ban on plastic bags in some cities. The given text also stresses that no plan can succeed without public awareness. In my opinion these measures are a step in the right direction, but as long as laws are not strictly enforced, the results will remain limited.

**Why it works:** research and source text are clearly separated; the measures, not just the problems, are the focus; and the judgement is qualified with a reason (جب تک ... نہیں).

## Discussion and essay prompts

- «پاکستان میں ماحولیاتی قوانین کی کمی نہیں، ان پر عمل درآمد کی کمی ہے۔» (Pakistan does not lack environmental laws; it lacks their enforcement.) Discuss with reference to your research.
- How effective have tree-planting campaigns been? What evidence would you need to judge them?
- «ماحول کی حفاظت صرف حکومت کی ذمہ داری نہیں۔» (Protecting the environment is not the government's responsibility alone.) What can citizens do?
- Should richer countries help Pakistan to adapt to climate change? Give reasons.

## Exam traps

- Research not about Pakistan.
- Describing problems without discussing the measures taken.
- Untraceable statistics.
- Writing about climate change in general terms with no named Pakistani measure, place or organisation.
- Agreement slips with feminine nouns such as آلودگی, پابندی and آگاہی, and with masculine plural اقدامات (اقدامات کیے گئے).
- Using نہیں instead of نہ inside an اگر clause.
