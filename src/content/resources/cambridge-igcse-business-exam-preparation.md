Cambridge IGCSE Business has changed its code. The syllabus coded 0450 is examined for the last time in 2026, and its replacement, Cambridge IGCSE Business 0264, is examined in 2027, 2028 and 2029, with March 2027 in India as the first series. Both versions have the same paper structure, not tiered, with grades A* to G: Paper 1 Short Answer and Data Response (1 hour 30 minutes, 80 marks, 50%, four questions) and Paper 2 Case Study (1 hour 30 minutes, 80 marks, 50%, four questions based on a case study insert). All questions are compulsory and both papers are externally assessed. ## Which code applies to you

A learner starting now follows 0264. The 0450 syllabus, for 2026 only, is a legacy teach-out version. If you are using older past papers, they are labelled 0450; they remain useful practice because the paper titles, durations, marks and weightings above are the same, but always check the 0264 syllabus for content changes before relying on them.

## Paper 1: Short Answer and Data Response (1 hour 30 minutes, 80 marks, 50%)

Paper 1 has four compulsory questions that mix short answers with structured data responses. At 90 minutes for 80 marks, you have 90 / 80 = 1.125 minutes per mark. Because the four questions are compulsory, average time is 90 / 4 = 22.5 minutes per question on average, although the marks printed on the paper should guide your split. Practise using the data given: quote a figure, then say what it means for the business. The syllabus topics are understanding business activity, people in business, marketing, operations management, financial information and decisions, and external influences on business activity, so the paper can range across all of them.

## Paper 2: Case Study (1 hour 30 minutes, 80 marks, 50%)

Paper 2 has four questions based on a case study provided as an insert with the paper. The rate is again 90 / 80 = 1.125 minutes per mark. Read the insert actively at the start: underline the type of business, its location, its objectives and any figures. Your answers should use these details, because an answer that could be about any business gains less than one tied to the case. Practise recommendations, since business questions often end with a judgement that must be justified.

## Timing and marks per minute

- Paper 1: 90 minutes / 80 marks = 1.125 minutes per mark.
- Paper 2: 90 minutes / 80 marks = 1.125 minutes per mark.
- Whole qualification: 90 + 90 = 180 minutes for 80 + 80 = 160 marks.
- A 6-mark question therefore takes about 6 x 1.125 = 6.75 minutes, so aim for around 7.

Spend the first five minutes of Paper 2 on the insert, then plan the remaining time using the marks printed against each question.

## Command words and question styles

"State" and "identify" want a brief point. "Define" wants a precise meaning. "Explain" wants a reason or consequence. "Calculate" wants working and units. "Analyse" wants a chain of reasoning from a cause to an effect. "Justify" and "recommend" want a decision supported by evidence and a reason for rejecting the alternative. In business, the most common loss of marks is a correct statement that is not linked to the business in the question. Check the syllabus for the exact command-word definitions.

## Worked practice scenario

Original question: "A bakery has revenue of 240 000, cost of sales of 150 000 and other expenses of 62 000. Calculate the gross profit, the net profit and the net profit margin, and recommend one way to improve the margin."

```
Gross profit = revenue - cost of sales = 240 000 - 150 000 = 90 000
Gross profit margin = 90 000 / 240 000 x 100 = 37.5%

Net profit = gross profit - expenses = 90 000 - 62 000 = 28 000
Net profit margin = 28 000 / 240 000 x 100 = 11.7% (to 1 decimal place)
```

Recommendation: "The bakery could negotiate lower prices from flour suppliers or change to a cheaper supplier, which would reduce cost of sales and raise both margins. The risk is lower quality, which could reduce sales, so the owner should test samples first." The structure is figure, action, benefit and risk, which shows evaluation.

## Before/during exam checklist

- Check you are using the 0264 syllabus if you start now.
- Practise both papers in full 90-minute sessions.
- Learn definitions and formulas for profit, margins and costs.
- Annotate the case study insert before answering.
- Always link points to the business in the question.
- Show formulas and units on calculations.
- Finish "recommend" questions with a clear decision and a reason.
- Use the marks printed against each question to decide how long to spend on it.

## Frequently asked questions

**How many papers are there in Cambridge IGCSE Business?**
Two compulsory papers: Paper 1 Short Answer and Data Response and Paper 2 Case Study. The qualification is not tiered.

**How long is each paper?**
Each is 1 hour 30 minutes and 80 marks, and each is 50% of the qualification.

**What is the difference between 0450 and 0264?**
0264 replaces 0450 with a new name and code. 0450 was examined for the last time in 2026, and 0264 is examined in 2027, 2028 and 2029.

**What is the Case Study paper?**
Paper 2 has four questions based on a case study provided as an insert with the paper.

**Are all questions compulsory?**
Yes. All questions on both papers are compulsory.
