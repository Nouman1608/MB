These notes condense section 10.01, Units and measurement, of OCR GCSE (9-1) Mathematics J560, Version 2.1 (August 2026). The section has three statements: 10.01a Units of measurement, 10.01b Compound units and 10.01c Maps and scale drawings. All three are content for **both tiers**, and the specification names two formulae you must know: speed = distance ÷ time and density = mass ÷ volume.

Worked examples are in the [10.01 study guide](/resources/ocr-gcse-mathematics-j560-units-and-measurement-ocr-gcse-maths/) and exam-style questions in the [10.01 practice questions](/resources/ocr-gcse-mathematics-j560-units-and-measurement-ocr-gcse-maths-practice/). For 10.01 with perimeter and arc length, use the [units, compound measures, maps, bearings and perimeter revision notes](/resources/ocr-gcse-mathematics-units-compound-measures-maps-bearings-perimeter-revision-notes/).

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning | Quick example |
|---|---|---|
| Capacity | How much a container holds, usually in ml or litres | 1 litre = 1000 cm³ |
| Compound unit | A unit made of two others, showing one quantity per unit of another | km/h, g/cm³, £ per hour |
| Speed | Distance travelled per unit of time | 150 km in 2 h is 75 km/h |
| Average speed | Total distance ÷ total time for the whole journey | Not the mean of the speeds |
| Density | Mass per unit volume | 540 g in 200 cm³ is 2.7 g/cm³ |
| Pressure | Force per unit area | 300 N on 2 m² is 150 N/m² |
| Unit price | Cost of one unit of quantity | 180p for 600 g is 0.3 p per g |
| Rate of pay | Money earned per unit of time | £12.50 per hour |
| Map scale | The ratio of map length to real length | 1 : 50 000 |
| Bearing | Angle measured clockwise from north, written with three figures | 072°, 305° |
| Back bearing | The bearing for the return direction | 072° becomes 252° |
| Scale drawing | An accurate drawing in which every length is reduced by the same factor | 1 cm to 2 km |

## Conversion factors to know

```
length  1 km = 1000 m    1 m = 100 cm     1 cm = 10 mm
area    1 m² = 10 000 cm²               1 cm² = 100 mm²
volume  1 m³ = 1 000 000 cm³            1 cm³ = 1000 mm³
capacity 1 litre = 1000 ml = 1000 cm³   1 m³ = 1000 litres
mass    1 tonne = 1000 kg   1 kg = 1000 g
time    1 h = 60 min   1 min = 60 s   1 day = 24 h
```

- Smaller unit: multiply. Larger unit: divide.
- Area factor = (length factor)². Volume factor = (length factor)³.
- Time: hours to minutes × 60; minutes to hours ÷ 60. 0.1 h = 6 min.
- Money: convert both prices to one currency before comparing. If £1 = €1.15, multiply pounds by 1.15 to get euros, and divide euros by 1.15 to get pounds.

## Compound units: the checklist

**The three forms.**

```
speed = distance ÷ time      distance = speed × time      time = distance ÷ speed
density = mass ÷ volume      mass = density × volume      volume = mass ÷ density
```

Pressure is force ÷ area. The specification names pressure as an example of a compound unit, not as a formula to recall, so use the relationship and units the question gives.

**Before you calculate.**

1. Convert so the units match the answer's unit (hours, not minutes, for km/h).
2. Write the answer's unit from the units you divided: grams ÷ cm³ gives g/cm³.

**Changing a compound unit.** Convert the top and the bottom separately.

| Change | Factor | Why |
|---|---|---|
| m/s to km/h | × 3.6 | × 3600 s in an hour, ÷ 1000 m in a km |
| km/h to m/s | ÷ 3.6 | the reverse |
| g/cm³ to kg/m³ | × 1000 | 1 m³ = 1 000 000 cm³ and 1000 g = 1 kg |
| N/cm² to N/m² | × 10 000 | 1 m² = 10 000 cm² |

**Algebraic contexts.** Keep the conversion with the letter. A jug holding p litres fills 1000p ÷ 200 = 5p cups of 200 ml. A machine making n items per minute makes 60n items per hour.

## Maps, bearings and scale drawings

**Scales.**

- 1 : 50 000 means 1 cm on the map is 50 000 cm = 500 m in real life.
- Map to real: multiply by the scale number, then convert to sensible units.
- Real to map: convert to cm, then divide by the scale number.
- Words to ratio: put both parts in cm, then simplify to 1 : n.

**Bearings.**

- Start at the point after the word "from". Draw a north line there.
- Measure clockwise. Give three figures: 008°, 064°, 271°.
- Back bearing: add 180° if the bearing is less than 180°, otherwise subtract 180°.
- North lines are parallel, so co-interior angles add to 180° and alternate angles are equal. Use these to "show" a bearing.

**Scale drawings.** Draw each length and angle accurately, then convert measured lengths back to real distances. To locate a point from two bearings, draw both lines and mark where they cross.

## Exam traps

- **Area and volume factors:** 1 m² is 10 000 cm², not 100. 1 m³ is 1 000 000 cm³, not 1000.
- **Decimal time:** 3.25 hours is 3 h 15 min, not 3 h 25 min.
- **Average speed:** never average the speeds. Total distance ÷ total time.
- **Time in minutes:** a speed in km/h needs the time in hours. 45 minutes is 0.75 h.
- **Mixed area units in pressure:** an area in cm² gives N/cm², not N/m².
- **Best buys:** compare cost per same unit (per ml, per g), and state the better value in words.
- **Bearings from the wrong point:** "the bearing of A from B" starts at B.
- **Two-figure bearings:** write 045°, not 45°.
- **Missing units:** the final answer needs its unit, especially when the question says "give your answer in".

## Quick self-test

1. Convert 3.6 m³ to litres, and 0.75 m² to cm².
2. A tank holds k m³ of water. Write its capacity in litres in terms of k.
3. Write 2.7 hours in hours and minutes.
4. A train leaves at 22:47 and arrives at 01:15 the next day. How long is the journey?
5. Convert 72 km/h to m/s.
6. A cyclist rides 24 km at 16 km/h, then 12 km at 24 km/h. Find the average speed for the whole ride.
7. Gold has density 19.3 g/cm³. Find the mass of 12 cm³ of gold.
8. An object of mass 3.2 kg has volume 400 cm³. Find its density in g/cm³ and in kg/m³.
9. A force of 360 N acts on an area of 0.06 m². Find the pressure in N/m² and in N/cm².
10. On a map with scale 1 : 50 000, two villages are 4.6 cm apart. Find the real distance in km.
11. Write the scale "2 cm represents 5 km" as a ratio in the form 1 : n.
12. The bearing of X from Y is 236°. Find the bearing of Y from X.

### Answers

1. 3.6 × 1000 = **3600 litres**; 0.75 × 10 000 = **7500 cm²**.
2. 1 m³ = 1000 litres, so **1000k litres**.
3. 0.7 × 60 = 42, so **2 h 42 min**.
4. 22:47 to 23:00 is 13 min; 23:00 to 01:15 is 2 h 15 min. Total **2 h 28 min**.
5. 72 ÷ 3.6 = **20 m/s**. (72 000 m ÷ 3600 s = 20.)
6. Times: 24 ÷ 16 = 1.5 h and 12 ÷ 24 = 0.5 h. Average = 36 ÷ 2 = **18 km/h** (not the mean of 16 and 24, which is 20).
7. 19.3 × 12 = **231.6 g**.
8. 3.2 kg = 3200 g; 3200 ÷ 400 = **8 g/cm³** = **8000 kg/m³**.
9. 360 ÷ 0.06 = **6000 N/m²**; 0.06 m² = 600 cm², so 360 ÷ 600 = **0.6 N/cm²**.
10. 4.6 × 50 000 = 230 000 cm = 2300 m = **2.3 km**.
11. 5 km = 500 000 cm, so 2 : 500 000 = **1 : 250 000**.
12. 236° is more than 180°, so 236° − 180° = **056°**.
