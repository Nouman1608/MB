## Section A - short questions

**1.** A bar chart of monthly sales shown at a meeting is an example of which type of communication? **[1]**
A oral   B written   C visual   D electronic only

**2.** Which method allows people in different countries to see and hear each other in a live meeting? **[1]**
A email   B videoconferencing   C letter   D poster

**3.** Which factor is most important when a business sends employees' salary details? **[1]**
A distance   B urgency   C security   D cost

**4.** Define oral communication. **[2]**

**5.** State **two** disadvantages of using posters to communicate with customers. **[2]**

**6.** Identify **two** factors, other than security, that affect the choice of method of communication. **[2]**

## Section B - structured questions

**7.** Distinguish between written and electronic communication, giving an example of each. **[4]**

**8.** *QuickParts supplies car parts to garages. A garage has a customer's car stuck on its lift and needs a part within two hours.*
Recommend the most appropriate method for the garage to contact QuickParts and justify your choice. **[4]**

**9.** *ShopRight, an online retailer, plans to replace most of its telephone customer service staff with a web-based chatbot.*
Evaluate this decision. **[6]**

**10.** Explain **one** advantage and **one** disadvantage of each of the following for a business:
(a) email (2 marks)
(b) social media (2 marks)
(c) face-to-face communication (2 marks) **[6]**

**11.** *A farm cooperative in a remote area with poor internet coverage needs to send its members details of a new price agreement that they must sign and return.*
Explain which method of communication is most suitable, referring to **two** factors. **[4]**

**12.** *Global Textiles has offices in four countries. Its directors must agree a confidential plan to close one factory.*
(a) Explain **two** reasons why the directors might choose videoconferencing. (4 marks)
(b) Explain **two** reasons why they might prefer to meet face to face. (4 marks) **[8]**

## Answers

**1.** C. A chart conveys information through images. **[1]**

**2.** B. Videoconferencing links people by live sound and video. **[1]**

**3.** C. Salary details are confidential. **[1]**

**4.** Communication using the spoken word [1], either in person or at a distance, for example by telephone [1]. **[2]**

**5.** Any two: one-way, so customers cannot ask questions [1]; limited space for information [1]; only seen by people passing that place [1]; can be damaged by weather or removed [1]. **[max 2]**

**6.** Any two: urgency [1]; cost [1]; distance [1]; availability of networks [1]; importance of the message [1]. **[max 2]**

**7.** Written communication sets the message down in words that can be kept as a record [1], for example a letter or a printed report [1]. Electronic communication is sent through digital devices and networks [1], for example videoconferencing or web-based chat [1]. (Accept email as an example of either, if explained as overlapping both types.) **[4]**

**8.** Method: telephone [1]. Urgency: the part is needed within two hours, and a call gets an immediate answer about whether the part is in stock and when it can arrive [1]. Two-way conversation lets the garage give the exact car model and check details instantly, avoiding mistakes [1]. An email might not be read for some time, so it is less suitable [1]. (Accept web-based chat if justified by speed and two-way contact.) **[4]**

**9.** Up to 2 for benefits, up to 2 for drawbacks, up to 2 for a justified judgement.
Benefits: chatbots answer common questions, such as order tracking, instantly and 24 hours a day [1]; they handle many customers at once, so there are no queues [1]; wage costs fall [1].
Drawbacks: chatbots cannot solve complex or unusual problems, such as damaged goods or refunds [1]; customers may be frustrated and shop elsewhere [1]; fewer staff may mean job losses and lower morale [1]; set-up and maintenance cost [1].
Judgement: for example, a good decision for simple queries if ShopRight keeps enough human staff, reachable from the chat, for complex problems [2]. **[6]**

**10.** (a) Email: advantage, fast, cheap and provides a written record with attachments [1]; disadvantage, may be ignored, lost, hacked or sent to the wrong person [1]. (2 marks)
(b) Social media: advantage, reaches a very large audience cheaply, and customers can reply and share [1]; disadvantage, messages are public and negative comments spread quickly [1]. (2 marks)
(c) Face to face: advantage, body language and tone aid understanding and build trust [1]; disadvantage, travel time and cost, and no record unless notes are taken [1]. (2 marks) **[6]**

**11.** Letter by post (recorded delivery) [1]. Availability of networks: poor internet means emails or online forms may not reach members or be returned [1]. Importance of the message: the agreement must be signed and kept as a record, which a letter allows [1]. Recorded post also proves it was received, which a telephone call cannot [1]. **[4]**

**12.** (a) Any two, 2 marks each: distance, because directors in four countries can meet without long flights [2]; cost, because travel and hotel costs are saved [2]; urgency, because the meeting can be arranged quickly [2]; documents and plans can be shared on screen during the discussion [2]. (max 4 marks)
(b) Any two, 2 marks each: security, because a confidential plan discussed in one room is less likely to be overheard or hacked than over a network [2]; importance of the message, because closing a factory is a serious decision that benefits from full discussion and reading each other's reactions [2]; trust and agreement are easier to build in person [2]; no risk of dropped connections during a crucial discussion [2]. (max 4 marks) **[8]**

## Where marks are usually lost

- Naming a type when the question asks for a method, or the reverse.
- Recommending a method without using the scenario's factors.
- Ignoring security when the information is confidential.
- Recommending email or videoconferencing where the scenario says networks are poor.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is email written or electronic communication?** Both: it is written in words and sent electronically. Choose the type that fits the point you are making.

**Which factors does 0715 list for choosing a method?** Urgency, cost, security, distance, availability of networks and importance of the message.
