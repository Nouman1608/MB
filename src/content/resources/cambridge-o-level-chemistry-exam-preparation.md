Cambridge O Level Chemistry 5070 is not tiered. Every candidate sits Paper 1 Multiple Choice (1 hour, 40 marks, 30%) and Paper 2 Theory (1 hour 45 minutes, 80 marks, 50%), plus one practical component: either Paper 3 Practical Test (1 hour 30 minutes, 40 marks, 20%) or Paper 4 Alternative to Practical (1 hour, 40 marks, 20%). All are externally assessed. Content knowledge comes from topics such as [Atoms, Elements and Compounds](/resources/cambridge-o-level-chemistry-elements-compounds-and-mixtures-atomic-structure/), [Acids, Bases and Salts](/resources/acids-bases-and-salts/) and [Alloys and Extraction of Metals](/resources/alloys-and-extraction-of-metals/), and the series window stated for this syllabus is 2026-2028, with June and November sittings.

## Paper 1: Multiple Choice

This paper is 60 minutes for 40 marks, or 60 / 40 = 1.5 minutes per mark. That gives you time to work a short calculation or read a diagram properly. **Preparation priority**: spread practice over all twelve content areas, from states of matter through to experimental techniques and chemical analysis, because multiple-choice questions can come from any of them. Underline key words, and test each option instead of choosing the first plausible one.

## Paper 2: Theory

Paper 2 is the largest component at 80 marks and 50%. With 105 minutes available, the allowance is 105 / 80 = 1.3125 minutes per mark, about 79 seconds. **Preparation priority**: aim to write one clear chemical point per mark, using the correct terms (for example, name the type of bonding or reaction rather than describing it loosely). Practise equations, including state symbols where required, and the standard calculations. Because the paper accounts for half of the grade, reserve the largest block of revision time for structured, written practice.

## The practical component: Paper 3 or Paper 4

Every candidate takes one practical component. Paper 3 Practical Test is hands-on, lasting 90 minutes for 40 marks, a generous 90 / 40 = 2.25 minutes per mark. Paper 4 Alternative to Practical is a written paper of 60 minutes for 40 marks, or 60 / 40 = 1.5 minutes per mark. Each is 20% of the grade and only the one you sit counts. **Preparation priority**: confirm which you are entered for. For Paper 3, practise with real apparatus whenever you can: reading burettes and thermometers, recording results in tables, and following written instructions precisely. For Paper 4, practise describing methods, interpreting results, drawing graphs and naming apparatus from diagrams. In both, learn the common qualitative tests and how to report observations in words.

## Total time and marks

Sitting Paper 3, a candidate has 60 + 105 + 90 = 255 minutes of examination. Sitting Paper 4, it is 60 + 105 + 60 = 225 minutes. Either way the total is 40 + 80 + 40 = 160 marks, with weightings of 30% + 50% + 20% = 100%.

## Question styles and command words

Expect state, name, describe, explain, calculate, suggest and deduce. Calculation questions reward shown working and units. "Suggest" invites you to apply a known principle to a new situation. **Preparation priority**: for every topic, work at least one question in which the context is unfamiliar, so that you practise reasoning from principles rather than reproducing notes.

## Worked practice scenario: percentage yield

Question (original): "A student burns 2.40 g of magnesium in air and collects 3.80 g of magnesium oxide. 2Mg + O2 -> 2MgO. (Ar: Mg = 24, O = 16.) Calculate the percentage yield."

```
Step 1  Moles of Mg = 2.40 / 24 = 0.100 mol
Step 2  Mole ratio Mg : MgO = 2 : 2 = 1 : 1, so moles MgO = 0.100 mol
Step 3  Mr of MgO = 24 + 16 = 40
        Theoretical mass = 0.100 x 40 = 4.00 g
Step 4  Percentage yield = (3.80 / 4.00) x 100 = 95.0%
```

A possible reason for the loss: some magnesium oxide escapes as smoke or is left on the crucible lid. A comment of this kind can earn a mark when the question asks for a reason.

## Before/during exam checklist

- Confirm whether you sit Paper 3 or Paper 4.
- Do timed multiple-choice sets at 1.5 minutes per question.
- Learn equations with correct state symbols and practise balancing.
- Revise the qualitative tests and what each result shows.
- On Paper 2, check the marks available and match the number of points.
- Show every step of a calculation with units.
- For practical work, record all readings in a table with units and consistent decimal places.
- Use spare minutes for checking calculations and the sense of your answers.

## Frequently asked questions

**How many papers are there in Cambridge O Level Chemistry 5070?**
Three: Paper 1 Multiple Choice, Paper 2 Theory, and one practical paper chosen from Paper 3 Practical Test or Paper 4 Alternative to Practical.

**What are the weightings?**
Paper 1 is 30%, Paper 2 is 50% and the practical paper is 20%.

**Are there tiers?**
No. The syllabus is not tiered.

**How long is each practical option?**
Paper 3 is 1 hour 30 minutes and Paper 4 is 1 hour, each worth 40 marks.

**Which series are available?**
The syllabus states an examination window of 2026-2028, with June and November series.
