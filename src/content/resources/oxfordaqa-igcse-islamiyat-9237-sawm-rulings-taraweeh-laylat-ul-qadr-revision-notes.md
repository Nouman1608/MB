## The prescribed passage: Qur'an 2:183-185

| Phrase | Use it for |
|---|---|
| "decreed upon you ... as it was decreed upon those before you" | Fasting is an obligation with a long history |
| "that you may become righteous" | The spiritual purpose: taqwa |
| "whoever among you is ill or on a journey ... an equal number of other days" | Exemptions and qada |
| "a ransom ... of feeding a poor person" | Fidya |
| "the month of Ramadhan ... in which was revealed the Qur'an" | Link to Laylat ul-Qadr and Taraweeh recitation |
| "Allah intends for you ease and does not intend for you hardship" | Mercy and balance |
| "perhaps you will be grateful" | Gratitude as an aim of fasting |

## A valid fast

- **Niyyah** before dawn.
- **Suhoor** before Fajr (Sunnah, blessed); **iftar** promptly at Maghrib.
- **Dawn to sunset**: "until the white thread ... from the black thread" (2:187).
- Abstain from food, drink and sexual relations; avoid lying, backbiting and anger, which ruin the reward.

## Invalidated by

Deliberate eating or drinking · deliberate sexual relations (qada **plus** kaffarah) · deliberate vomiting · start of menstruation or post-natal bleeding · smoking.
**Not** invalidated by: forgetful eating or drinking ("Allah fed him and gave him drink", Bukhari).

## Exemptions: who owes what

| Group | Qada (make up) | Fidya (feed the poor) |
|---|---|---|
| Temporary illness | Yes | No |
| Travel | Yes | No |
| Menstruation / post-natal bleeding | Yes | No |
| Pregnancy or breastfeeding | Yes | In some schools, as well |
| Old age or permanent illness | No | Yes, one poor person per day |
| Children, the insane | No | No |

**Kaffarah** for deliberately breaking a fast by sexual relations: sixty consecutive days of fasting or, if unable, feeding sixty poor people.

| Spiritual | Moral | Social |
|---|---|---|

## Taraweeh

- Voluntary night prayer after Isha in Ramadhan.
- The Prophet (pbuh) led it for a few nights, then stopped in case it became obligatory (Bukhari).
- Hazrat Umar (RA) gathered the people behind one reciter.
- Sunni practice: 8 or 20 rak'ahs; many complete the Qur'an in the month.
- Shi'a Muslims generally do not pray it in congregation, but offer extra night prayers.

## Itikaaf

- Seclusion in the mosque, usually the **last ten days** of Ramadhan.
- The Prophet (pbuh) observed it every year (Bukhari); mentioned in 2:187.
- Leave only for necessities; time spent on prayer, Qur'an and dhikr.
- Purpose: undivided worship and being ready for Laylat ul-Qadr.

## Laylat ul-Qadr

- **Surah 97:** Qur'an sent down; "better than a thousand months"; angels and the Spirit descend; "Peace it is until the emergence of dawn".
- "A blessed night" (44:3).
- Seek it in the **odd nights of the last ten** (Bukhari); the 27th is a common view, not a certainty.
- Du'a taught to Aisha (RA): "O Allah, You are pardoning and love to pardon, so pardon me" (Tirmidhi).
- Standing in prayer on it with faith and hope of reward forgives past sins (Bukhari).

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Sawm | Fasting |
| Suhoor | Pre-dawn meal |
| Iftar | Meal that breaks the fast at sunset |
| Taqwa | God-consciousness, the purpose of fasting |
| Qada | Making up missed fasts |
| Fidya | Feeding a poor person for each fast that cannot be made up |
| Kaffarah | Expiation for deliberately breaking a fast |
| Taraweeh | Voluntary night prayer of Ramadhan |
| Itikaaf | Seclusion in the mosque for worship |
| Laylat ul-Qadr | The Night of Power or Decree |
| Zakat-ul-Fitr | Charity paid before the Eid prayer at the end of Ramadhan |

## Exam traps

- Forgetful eating does **not** break the fast.
- Qada ≠ fidya ≠ kaffarah: learn which applies to whom.
- Taraweeh is voluntary, not a pillar.
- Laylat ul-Qadr's date is not fixed; say "sought in the odd nights of the last ten".
- Benefits need a category and an explanation, not a list of words.

## Self-test

1. What does Qur'an 2:183 give as the purpose of fasting?
2. What is suhoor?
3. Does eating by mistake break the fast?
4. What must someone with a temporary illness do about missed fasts?
5. What is fidya, and who pays it?
6. What is the kaffarah for deliberately breaking a fast with sexual relations?
7. Why did the Prophet (pbuh) stop leading Taraweeh in congregation?
8. Which caliph gathered the people for Taraweeh behind one reciter?
9. In which part of Ramadhan is Itikaaf usually observed?
10. According to Surah 97, Laylat ul-Qadr is better than how long?
11. When did the Prophet (pbuh) tell Muslims to seek Laylat ul-Qadr?
12. Give one social benefit of fasting.

**Answers:** 1. "That you may become righteous", that is, taqwa. 2. The meal eaten before dawn. 3. No; Allah fed him and gave him drink. 4. Make up the same number of days later (qada). 5. Feeding a poor person for each missed day, paid by those who can never fast, such as the very old or permanently ill. 6. Sixty consecutive days of fasting, or if unable, feeding sixty poor people. 7. He feared it would be made obligatory. 8. Hazrat Umar (RA). 9. The last ten days. 10. A thousand months. 11. In the odd nights of the last ten nights of Ramadhan. 12. Any one: empathy with the poor; more charity; shared iftar; unity of the Ummah; equality of rich and poor.
