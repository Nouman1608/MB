Condensed recall for **13.3 Local and global resilience** (Unit 6: Global risks and resilience).

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Resilience | Capacity of a person, community or system to anticipate, absorb, adapt to and recover from shocks |
| Adaptation | Adjusting to expected change |
| Mitigation | Reducing the cause of the risk |

## Responses by scale

| Scale | Examples |
|---|---|
| Local | Community early-warning systems, flood defences and building standards, diversified livelihoods, savings groups, local knowledge and social networks |
| National | Planning and building regulation, social protection, emergency services, investment in infrastructure and institutions |
| Global | Agreements and frameworks such as the Paris Agreement on climate change and the Sendai Framework for Disaster Risk Reduction (both adopted in 2015); international organisations and financial institutions |

## Resilience is unevenly available

Wealth, governance, information and social cohesion all affect it.

## Evaluating resilience measures

Weigh:

- **Who pays** for resilience.
- Whether measures reduce the **underlying cause** or only the symptoms.
- Whether **short-term protection** can create long-term vulnerability.
- **Top-down** measures: often well funded but poorly matched to local needs.
- **Community-based** measures: fit local needs well but may lack resources.

## Exam traps

- Using resilience, adaptation and mitigation as synonyms.
- Describing only one scale of response.
- Assuming a well-funded measure works well locally.
- Judging a measure by its cost alone, without asking whether it fits local needs.

## Self-test

1. Define resilience.
2. Distinguish adaptation from mitigation.
3. Give one local and one global response.
4. When were the Paris Agreement and Sendai Framework adopted?
5. Why is resilience unevenly available?

**Answers:** 1. The capacity to anticipate, absorb, adapt to and recover from shocks. 2. Adaptation
adjusts to expected change; mitigation reduces the cause. 3. For example community early warning
(local) and the Paris Agreement (global). 4. Both in 2015. 5. Wealth, governance, information and
social cohesion differ between places.
