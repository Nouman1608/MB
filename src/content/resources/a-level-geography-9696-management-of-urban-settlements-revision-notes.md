Condensed recall for **6.4 The management of urban settlements**, including the required case studies. For full explanations, read the [Core Human Geography study guide](/resources/a-level-cambridge-geography-core-human-geography/).

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Shanty town (squatter / informal settlement) | Housing built without legal tenure, often lacking services |
| Self-help scheme | Residents build or improve housing with support |
| Site and service | Plots with basic services provided for residents to build on |
| Upgrading | Improving existing informal housing in place |
| Infrastructure | Basic systems such as power, transport, water and sanitation |
| Sustainable city | Meets present needs without harming future generations |

## Required case studies

The syllabus requires **both**:

1. **A shanty town in an LIC or MIC:** growth and causes, living conditions and problems, and attempts to improve them (upgrading, self-help, site and service, or clearance) with evaluation.
2. **Provision of infrastructure (power or transport) for a city:** need, scheme, costs and benefits, who gained and lost, and sustainability.

## Strategies for informal settlements

| Strategy | Advantage | Weakness |
|---|---|---|
| Clearance and relocation | Removes unsafe housing | Destroys communities and livelihoods |
| Upgrading in place | Keeps community; cheaper | Slow; tenure and funding needed |
| Self-help | Low cost, empowers residents | Needs materials and security of tenure |
| Site and service | Planned layout | Sites may be far from jobs |

## Infrastructure and transport

Aims: reduce congestion and pollution, improve access to jobs. Tools: public transport such as buses, metro or tram; road management and charging; walking and cycling; park-and-ride. Power: generation and distribution to meet demand with lower emissions. Consider cost, funding, displacement and environmental effects.

## Evaluation points

Who benefits, cost against benefit, short against long term, and sustainability. Mixed approaches usually work better than one measure.

## Exam traps

- Cover both required case studies; they are separate.
- Use real, checked facts for the chosen places.
- Evaluate success: improvement for some groups can disadvantage others.

## Self-test

1. Define a shanty town.
2. What is site and service?
3. Give one weakness of clearance.
4. Name two ways of easing urban congestion.
5. What does sustainable mean for a city?
6. What two case studies does 6.4 require?

**Answers:** 1. Housing built without legal tenure, often lacking basic services. 2. Providing plots with basic services for residents to build on. 3. It destroys communities and livelihoods. 4. Public transport investment and congestion charging. 5. Meeting current needs without harming future generations. 6. A shanty town in an LIC or MIC, and infrastructure (power or transport) for a city.
