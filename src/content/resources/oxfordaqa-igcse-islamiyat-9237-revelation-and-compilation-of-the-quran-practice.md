## Section A: short-answer questions

**1.** Name the angel who brought the Qur'an to the Prophet (pbuh). **[1]**

**2.** What is the term for a person who has memorised the whole Qur'an? **[1]**

**3.** Give **two** ways in which the Qur'an was preserved during the Prophet's (pbuh) lifetime. **[2]**

**4.** The Qur'an was revealed gradually over about twenty-three years.

**(a)** State **one** place where it was revealed. **[1]**

**(b)** Explain **one** reason why it was revealed gradually. **[3]**

**5.** Explain **two** reasons why Hazrat Abu Bakr (RA) arranged for the Qur'an to be collected. **[4]**

**6.** Explain **two** steps that Hazrat Uthman (RA) took to standardise the text of the Qur'an. **[4]**

Apply **one** teaching from a source of authority in your answer. **[6]**

> Hudhaifa was afraid of their (the people of Sham and Iraq) differences in the recitation of the

'The work of Hazrat Uthman (RA) was more important than the work of Hazrat Abu Bakr (RA) in protecting the Qur'an.'

Examine why this statement is significant for Muslims.

In your answer you should:

## Answers

**How the parts are marked.** One mark for each correct answer in the 1- and 2-mark questions. In the 4-mark questions, one mark for each point identified and one for explaining it. The 6-mark questions give four AO1 marks on that pattern plus up to two AO2 marks: **one** for a general, accurate use of a source of authority, **two** for a specific use applied to the question. The 12-mark question is marked by levels:

| Level | Marks | What the answer does |
|---|---|---|
| 4 | 10–12 | Thorough examination of a range of points; logical reasoning with detailed evidence; excellent, direct use of the passage |
| 3 | 7–9 | Clear examination of more than one point; reasoning with some detail; good use of the passage |
| 2 | 4–6 | Some accurate examination; reasoning not always supported; general use of the passage. **Maximum Level 2 if only one point is made** |
| 1 | 1–3 | Limited examination with little reasoning. **Maximum Level 1 if the passage is not used** |
| 0 | 0 | Nothing worthy of credit |

**1.** Jibril (AS) [1].

**2.** A hafiz (plural huffaz) [1].

**3.** Any two, [1] each: memorisation by the Prophet (pbuh) and the companions; writing by scribes of the revelation; yearly review with Jibril (AS); recitation in the daily prayers.

**4. (a)** Makkah or Madinah [1]. Accept the Cave of Hira.

**(b)** One reason identified [1] and explained [2].

- **To strengthen his heart** [1]: the Prophet (pbuh) faced hardship and opposition [1], and each new part of revelation brought comfort and direction (Qur'an 25:32) [1].
- **To answer events and questions** [1]: verses came when situations arose, such as battles or questions from believers [1], so the guidance was clear and relevant [1].
- **To ease memorisation** [1]: the community was mostly oral [1], so learning in parts helped people remember it accurately [1].
- **To introduce laws step by step** [1]: for example the prohibition of alcohol came in stages [1], which helped people to change their habits [1].

**5.** For each of two reasons: reason identified [1] and explained [1].

- **Deaths at Yamama** [1]: many huffaz were martyred in battle [1].
- **Fear of loss** [1]: Umar (RA) worried that parts of the Qur'an would disappear if more huffaz died [1].
- **A single checked copy** [1]: until then it was held in memory and on separate materials [1].
- **Protection of the text** [1]: Muslims believe it is a trust that must be guarded (Qur'an 15:9) [1].

**6.** For each of two steps: step identified [1] and explained [1].

- **Borrowed the sheets from Hafsa (RA)** [1]: they were Abu Bakr's checked compilation, so the new copies rested on a trusted base [1].
- **Appointed a team** [1]: Zayd ibn Thabit (RA) and three members of the Quraysh copied them into volumes [1].
- **Used the dialect of the Quraysh where they differed** [1]: the Qur'an had been revealed in that dialect [1].
- **Sent a copy to each main region** [1]: so that Muslims everywhere had the same text [1].
- **Ordered other materials burned** [1]: to remove the cause of confusion over recitation [1].

**7.** AO1 **[4]**: for each of two reasons, reason identified [1] and explained [1]. AO2 **[2]**: one source of authority used generally [1] or specifically and applied [2].

Reasons (AO1):

- **The last prophet** [1]: Muhammad (pbuh) is the seal of the prophets, so no later scripture is expected [1].
- **Confirms and completes earlier scripture** [1]: it confirms the truth of the Tawrat and Injil and corrects what people changed [1].
- **For all people** [1]: it addresses all of humankind in every age [1].
- **Protected** [1]: Allah promised to guard it, so it remains unchanged [1].

Applications (AO2), for example:

- "Seal of the prophets" (Qur'an 33:40) [2].
- "Indeed, it is We who sent down the Qur'an and indeed, We will be its guardian" (Qur'an 15:9) [2].
- Qur'an 5:3: "This day I have perfected for you your religion" [2].

**8.** AO1 **[4]**: as in question 7. AO2 **[2]**: as in question 7.

Ways (AO1):

- **Two methods checking each other** [1]: memory and writing were compared [1].
- **Strict standards** [1]: Zayd (RA) accepted a verse only if written in the Prophet's (pbuh) presence and confirmed by witnesses [1].
- **Agreement of the companions** [1]: the standard text was accepted by those who had learned it from the Prophet (pbuh) [1].
- **One text for all** [1]: Muslims everywhere read the same Qur'an [1].

Applications (AO2), for example:

- Bukhari 4987: Hudhayfa's warning to "save this nation before they differ about the Book" and Uthman's (RA) action [2].
- Qur'an 15:9: Allah's promise to guard the Qur'an [2].

**9.** Marked by the levels above. Possible content:

*Points of significance*

- **For the statement:** Uthman (RA) produced a standard text that all Muslims could follow; he prevented the disputes that Hudhayfa (RA) feared; the passage shows him acting before the community divided; copies in every region still guide the text today.
- **Against the statement:** Abu Bakr (RA) made the first complete written collection, which Uthman (RA) used as his base; without it there would have been nothing to copy; Abu Bakr (RA) faced the danger of loss after Yamama; both actions were needed.
- **Balanced view:** the work of the two caliphs was a single process; one gathered, the other standardised.

*Use of the passage*

- "Differences in the recitation of the Qur'an" gives the cause of Uthman's (RA) action.
- "Save this nation before they differ" shows the aim: unity of the Ummah.
- "As the Jews and the Christians did before" shows the concern to avoid disagreement over the Book.

Level 4 answers use the passage directly, argue both sides with named people and events, and reach a reasoned judgement.
