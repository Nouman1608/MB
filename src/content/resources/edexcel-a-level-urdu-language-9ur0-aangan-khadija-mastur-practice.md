## Section A

**1.** State the author, the form, and the date the specification gives for آنگن, and explain why that date should not be described as the date the novel was written. **[4]**

**2.** List the five features of a work that the specification says responses should relate to. **[5]**

**3.** Give the English meaning of: (a) نقطۂ نظر (b) بیانیہ (c) ستم ظریفی (d) نفسیاتی کشمکش (e) موڑ **[5]**

**4.** Correct the error in each sentence.
(a) مصنفہ نے آنگن کو ایک علامت کے طور پر پیش کیا ہیں۔
(b) ناول میں سیاست گھر کے اندر داخل ہو جاتا ہے۔
(c) عالیہ کی کشمکش آخر تک ختم نہیں ہوتا۔ **[3]**

**5.** Name the three grids used to mark each essay and the marks for each. **[3]**

**6.** A candidate supports an argument with a scene they remember from a television version of the novel. Explain why this earns no credit. **[2]**

## Answers

**1.** خدیجہ مستور (Khadija Mastur) [1]; a novel [1]; 1999 [1]; 1999 is the year printed in the specification's list, but the novel was first published in 1962, so it is not a novel written in the 1990s [1].

**2.** Form and technique of presentation [1]; key themes, concepts and issues [1]; characterisation [1]; structure of the plot [1]; social and cultural setting [1].

**3.** (a) point of view, perspective [1] (b) narrative [1] (c) irony [1] (d) psychological conflict [1] (e) turning point [1]

**4.** (a) پیش کیا ہے: with نے the verb agrees with the object (آنگن marked by کو, so default masculine singular) [1] (b) داخل ہو جاتی ہے: سیاست is feminine [1] (c) ختم نہیں ہوتی: کشمکش is feminine [1]

**5.** Critical and analytical response, AO4, 20 marks [1]; range of grammatical structures and vocabulary, AO3, 15 marks [1]; accuracy of language, AO3, 10 marks [1].

**6.** The specification gives no credit for essays on literary texts based on screen versions adapted from them [1]; evidence must be taken from the original novel [1].

**7.** Model: **ناول نگار قومی سیاست کو ایک ہی گھرانے پر اس کے اثرات کے ذریعے دکھاتی ہیں۔ ایک ہی خاندان کے افراد مخالف فریقوں کا ساتھ دیتے ہیں، اور ان کے جھگڑے آنگن تک پہنچ جاتے ہیں۔**

Marking: ناول نگار ... دکھاتی ہیں, respectful feminine plural [1]؛ قومی سیاست کو [1]؛ ایک ہی گھرانے پر اس کے اثرات کے ذریعے [1]؛ ایک ہی خاندان کے افراد [1]؛ مخالف فریقوں کا ساتھ دیتے ہیں [1]؛ اور ان کے جھگڑے [1]؛ آنگن تک [1]؛ پہنچ جاتے ہیں, masculine plural agreeing with جھگڑے [1]. Accept "even" rendered with بھی (آنگن تک بھی).

**8.** One mark for each: a clear position on how women's lives are presented [1]; a reference to a technique or the novel's design, for example the courtyard or Aliya's perspective [1]; accurate Urdu with correct agreement [1]. Example: **"آنگن" میں عورتوں کی زندگی کو آنگن کی چار دیواری میں قید دکھایا گیا ہے، اور عالیہ کی نظر سے مصنفہ یہ سوال اٹھاتی ہیں کہ کیا عورت اپنی تقدیر کا فیصلہ خود کر سکتی ہے۔**

**9.** Weaknesses, any two: it narrates events in order instead of arguing [1]; no link to a theme or the question [1]; no analysis of the writer's technique; vague evidence (کچھ لوگ) rather than specific characters or scenes. Rewritten first sentence, for example: **مصنفہ عالیہ کو ایک ایسے گھر میں دکھاتی ہیں جہاں سیاست رشتوں کو توڑنے لگتی ہے** [1], which makes a claim that the paragraph can then support [1].

**10.** One mark each, up to four: a clear position on what the ending makes the reader think; reference to the ending's setting after the creation of Pakistan; a comparison between the characters' hopes and what they find; how the ending changes our judgement of earlier events or characters; one technique (structure, irony, the courtyard symbol); a conclusion that answers the question.

**11.** Marked on three levels-based grids: critical and analytical response (AO4) [20]; range of grammatical structures and vocabulary, including literary terminology (AO3) [15]; accuracy of language (AO3) [10]. Total 45.

**Indicative content.** Credit any well-argued response supported by evidence from the novel. Likely points:

- **Agreement:** national politics enters the household; the division between supporters of the Congress and the Muslim League splits members of one family; Aliya observes how political commitment affects relationships, marriages and futures; the ending after the creation of Pakistan shows migration and the gap between hope and reality.
- **Technique:** the courtyard as a symbol of the household as a small society; events seen through Aliya's perspective, so history is experienced from inside the home; the structure moving towards Partition.
- **Qualification:** the novel is also about forces that are not historical events, such as women's limited choices, class and pride within the family, which shape lives as much as politics.
- **Conclusion:** a judgement that answers **تنقیدی جائزہ لیجیے**, for example that the novel shows history changing lives, but through the pressures that already existed inside the house.

At the top of the AO4 range, each point is justified with specific, appropriately selected references and the argument is consistently linked to the statement. At the top of the range grid, the essay uses complex structures and literary terminology throughout.
