This guide goes deeper on one topic: **Theme 3, Topic 4 Jobs, Career Choices and Ambitions** in
OxfordAQA International GCSE Urdu (9264), for International GCSE exams May/June 2026 onwards. The
[Theme 3 study guide](/resources/igcse-oxfordaqa-urdu-study-employment/) sets out the whole theme;
this page focuses on its final topic, where you talk about work now and the career you hope for.

## What this topic covers

The specification (section 3.1.3) lists Jobs, career choices and ambitions as the fourth topic of
Theme 3. It brings together:

- **Part-time work and work experience** -- what you do or did, where, and what you learned.
- **Professions** -- naming jobs and saying what they involve.
- **Career choices** -- what matters to you in a job (salary, interest, helping others), and the
  qualities a job needs.
- **Ambitions** -- what you hope to become, why, and what your plan depends on.

The study route to a career belongs to Topic 3,
[Education post-16](/resources/oxfordaqa-igcse-urdu-language-9264-education-post-16/). Here the
focus is on the work itself.

## Where this fits in 9264

This topic has no separate paper. It can be tested in Paper 1 (Reading, Grammar and Meaning), which
includes translation from English into Urdu, and in Paper 2 (Writing). Each paper lasts 1 hour
45 minutes and is worth 70 marks (50% of the qualification).

## Structures that carry this topic

### 1. Ambition with بننا چاہنا

- میں ڈاکٹر بننا چاہتا ہوں۔ (I want to become a doctor.)
- میں صحافی بننا چاہتی ہوں۔ (I want to become a journalist; a girl speaking.)

بننا means "to become"; بنانا means "to make". Mixing them changes the meaning entirely.

### 2. Ambition plus reason

A career ambition needs a justification: میں نرس بننا چاہتی ہوں کیونکہ میں بیمار لوگوں کی مدد کرنا
چاہتی ہوں۔ (I want to become a nurse because I want to help sick people.)

### 3. Part-time work: present and past

- ہفتے کے آخر میں میں اپنے چچا کی دکان پر کام کرتا ہوں۔ (At weekends I work in my uncle's shop.)
- پچھلی گرمیوں میں میں نے ایک دفتر میں کام کا تجربہ حاصل کیا۔ (Last summer I did work experience
  in an office.)
- وہاں میں فون سنتی تھی۔ (I used to answer the phone there.) The habitual past describes repeated
  tasks and takes no نے.

### 4. What matters in a job

- میرے لیے اچھی تنخواہ سے زیادہ اہم یہ ہے کہ مجھے اپنا کام پسند ہو۔ (For me, enjoying my work is
  more important than a good salary.) The verb after کہ is subjunctive (ہو).
- اس کام کے لیے صبر اور محنت کی ضرورت ہوتی ہے۔ (This work needs patience and hard work.)

### 5. Conditions and hopes

- اگر میں ڈاکٹر بن گیا تو اپنے گاؤں میں ایک کلینک کھولوں گا۔ (If I become a doctor, I will open a
  clinic in my village.)
- مجھے امید ہے کہ میں ایک دن اپنا کاروبار شروع کروں گی۔ (I hope I will start my own business one
  day.)

## Worked example 1: part-time work and an ambition

Task (original): *Write about work. Describe a part-time job or work experience, say what you
learned, and explain the career you would like and why.*

Model paragraph:

ہفتے کے آخر میں میں اپنے چچا کی دکان پر کام کرتا ہوں، جہاں میں گاہکوں کی مدد کرتا ہوں اور سامان
ترتیب سے رکھتا ہوں۔ اس کام سے میں نے سیکھا ہے کہ وقت کی پابندی کتنی ضروری ہے۔ مستقبل میں میں
صحافی بننا چاہتا ہوں کیونکہ مجھے لکھنا اور لوگوں سے بات کرنا پسند ہے۔ میرے لیے اچھی تنخواہ سے زیادہ
اہم یہ ہے کہ مجھے اپنا کام دلچسپ لگے۔

Gloss: "At weekends I work in my uncle's shop, where I help customers and arrange the stock. From
this work I have learned how important punctuality is. In the future I want to become a journalist
because I like writing and talking to people. For me, finding my work interesting is more important
than a good salary."

Why it works:

```
present routine           ... دکان پر کام کرتا ہوں، جہاں ...
what was learned          اس کام سے میں نے سیکھا ہے کہ ...
ambition + reason         ... صحافی بننا چاہتا ہوں کیونکہ ...
priority (comparison)     ... سے زیادہ اہم یہ ہے کہ ... لگے
```

The ambition links back to the part-time job (talking to people), and the closing comparison shows
a considered view of what makes a good career.

## Worked example 2: translating into Urdu

Task (original): *Translate into Urdu: "I would like to become a nurse because I want to help sick
people. Last summer I did work experience in a hospital."* (A girl is writing.)

Answer: میں نرس بننا چاہتی ہوں کیونکہ میں بیمار لوگوں کی مدد کرنا چاہتی ہوں۔ پچھلی گرمیوں میں
میں نے ایک ہسپتال میں کام کا تجربہ حاصل کیا۔

Decisions to make:

1. "Would like to become" is بننا چاہتی ہوں: present of چاہنا, feminine for a girl.
2. "Help sick people" is بیمار لوگوں کی مدد کرنا; مدد takes کی before it.
3. "Did work experience" is a transitive past, so میں نے, and حاصل کیا agrees with the masculine
   تجربہ.

## Worked example 3: a short reading question

Text (original): میری خالہ ایک بینک میں کام کرتی ہیں اور ان کی تنخواہ اچھی ہے، لیکن وہ کہتی ہیں
کہ انہیں اپنا کام اکتا دینے والا لگتا ہے۔ اس لیے وہ اب شام کو کھانا پکانے کی کلاسیں لیتی ہیں اور
ایک دن اپنا ریستوران کھولنا چاہتی ہیں۔

Question: *Give one advantage and one disadvantage of the aunt's job, and state her ambition.*
**[3]**

Answer: Advantage: the pay is good **[1]**. Disadvantage: she finds the work boring **[1]**.
Ambition: to open her own restaurant one day **[1]**.

How to get there:

1. Sort the text into layers: a fact (she works in a bank), an opinion (the work is boring) and an
   aspiration (a restaurant).
2. لیکن (but) separates the advantage from the disadvantage; اس لیے (so, therefore) introduces the
   consequence, the cooking classes.
3. The cooking classes are what she does now, not her ambition. An answer that gives "cooking
   classes" as the ambition misreads the time frame and loses the mark.

## Common mistakes

- **بنانا for بننا.** میں ڈاکٹر بنانا چاہتا ہوں means "I want to make a doctor".
- **Gender of چاہنا.** A girl writes چاہتی ہوں, not چاہتا ہوں.
- **Bare ambition.** "I want to be an engineer" with no reason is a list, not an answer.
- **نے with the habitual past.** میں نے فون سنتی تھی is wrong; the habitual past takes no نے.
- **Drifting into Topic 3.** A careers task wants the job and the reasons; a brief mention of the
  study route is fine, but it should not take over.
- **ملازمت and نوکری register.** Both mean "job"; ملازمت is slightly more formal and suits
  formal writing.

## Exam technique

- **Separate fact from aspiration.** In reading texts, distinguish what someone does now (a
  part-time job) from what they hope to do (an ambition); questions often target one or the other.
- **Look for the condition.** Plans in texts often depend on something (اگر مجھے موقع ملا تو ...);
  a question may ask what.
- **Cover every bullet point** of a writing task, and justify every ambition.
- **Budget by marks.** At about 1.5 minutes per mark, leave time to check every چاہتا / چاہتی and
  every past verb for نے.
