Cambridge runs two main exam series every year, May/June and October/November, and O Level
subjects can be sat in either. In Pakistan, schools often split a student's subjects across
the two, and private candidates can choose. The choice affects how much preparation time is
left, when results arrive and how soon a resit is possible.

## The two series

| | May/June | October/November |
|---|---|---|
| Exam period | Late April to early June | Late September to mid-November |
| Results | August | January |
| Next chance to resit | October/November | May/June |

For the October/November 2026 series, Cambridge releases O Level and IGCSE results on
14 January 2027. The [exam calendar](/exam-calendar/) lists entry deadlines and results days
for the coming series, from Cambridge's own published dates.

A third series, February/March, is offered only in certain countries, such as India; check
with your school before relying on it.

## Choosing a series

**May/June** suits a student who needs the most teaching time. It is the main series, and
results in August still leave October/November for a resit before most students move on to
A Level.

**October/November** suits:

- subjects that are ready early, so they are out of the way before the main series;
- a resit of a subject sat in May/June;
- students who want fewer papers in a single series.

The trade-off is time. Preparation for October/November runs through the summer holidays, and
the exams start soon after the new school year begins.

## Spreading subjects across both series

Splitting subjects can make each series lighter, but plan it subject by subject:

1. **Put the subjects that build on each other together.** A student taking Physics and
   Mathematics usually benefits from finishing the Mathematics needed for Physics first.
2. **Leave the hardest subject the most time.** That usually means May/June, with
   October/November as the resit option.
3. **Check A Level requirements.** Sixth-form colleges set their own entry conditions; make sure
   every subject a college asks for will have a result in time.
