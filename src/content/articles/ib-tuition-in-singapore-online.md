Many international schools in Singapore teach the International Baccalaureate: the Middle
Years Programme (MYP) in the early secondary years and the Diploma Programme (DP) in the last
two. Students usually look for help in one or two subjects, most often Mathematics, a science or
Economics, and the right tutor is one who teaches from the IB's own subject guide and
assessment criteria.

## Which IB subjects are taught

All IB tuition at Marlbridge is **one-to-one**, in 1-hour classes, live online.

**IB Diploma Programme:**
[Mathematics: Analysis and Approaches](/boards/ib/ib-dp/mathematics-analysis-and-approaches/),

[Physics](/boards/ib/ib-dp/physics/), [Chemistry](/boards/ib/ib-dp/chemistry/),
[Biology](/boards/ib/ib-dp/biology/), [Computer Science](/boards/ib/ib-dp/computer-science/),
[Economics](/boards/ib/ib-dp/economics/), [Business](/boards/ib/ib-dp/business/),
[World History](/boards/ib/ib-dp/world-history/),

[Language A: Literature](/boards/ib/ib-dp/language-a-literature/) and

Each subject page sets out the course structure and how it is assessed, with free study
material.

## What one-to-one IB tuition is good for

- **Higher Level content.** HL topics, such as calculus in Mathematics: Analysis and Approaches
  HL, often need more time than the school timetable gives them.
- **Paper technique.** IB markschemes reward method and clear reasoning. Working through past
  questions with a teacher shows where marks are lost.
- **Understanding the criteria.** For internal assessments and MYP tasks, a teacher can explain
  what each assessment criterion asks for and how a piece of work is judged.

**What tuition cannot do:** internal assessments, the Extended Essay and MYP projects must be
the student's own work, under the IB's academic integrity rules. A tutor can explain the
criteria and teach the subject; the writing has to be the student's, and the school's
supervisor stays responsible for guidance on the piece itself.

## Class times in Singapore time

Singapore is three hours ahead of Lahore all year, so a 5:00 pm class in Singapore is 2:00 pm in
Lahore. Because IB classes are one-to-one, the time is arranged around your child's week:
after school, in the evening or at the weekend.

Every class is taught live online by a named subject teacher from our academy in Lahore.
Marlbridge has no office, branch or teacher in Singapore.

## Fees

IB classes are charged per 1-hour class. The [Singapore page](/singapore/) shows the fee in
Singapore dollars; it is an indicative conversion of the Pakistan fee, and the exact fee is
confirmed in writing before any payment.

**Not taught:** the Singapore national curriculum and its examinations, including the PSLE and
the Singapore-Cambridge GCE and SEC examinations. Families at schools teaching Cambridge IGCSE or
A Level can see those courses on the Singapore page too.

## Before the trial class

1. Tell us the programme (DP or MYP), the subject and, for the Diploma, whether it is Higher or
   Standard Level.
2. Bring a recent test or a marked piece of work, so the teacher can see where marks are being
   lost.
3. Book the [free trial class](/trial/). Our guide to
   [group classes or one-to-one tuition](/articles/group-classes-or-one-to-one-tuition/)
   explains how one-to-one classes work.
