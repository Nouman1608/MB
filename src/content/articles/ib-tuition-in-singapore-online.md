Some international schools in Singapore are IB World Schools, and the IB's own school directory
lists which programmes each one offers. The International Baccalaureate has the Middle Years
Programme (MYP) in the early secondary years and the Diploma Programme (DP) in the last two.
Our own site analytics give a small picture of what IB families in Singapore look for: between
July and early October 2026, the visits from Singapore that stayed longer than five seconds
(about 21 of them, with automated traffic removed) landed mostly on IB Diploma pages (Computer
Science, Mathematics: Analysis and Approaches, Economics, Environmental Systems and Societies),
on IB MYP pages, and on A Level resources. Students usually look for help in one or two subjects, most often Mathematics, a science or
Economics, and the right tutor is one who teaches from the IB's own subject guide and
assessment criteria.

## Languages: lessons are in English only

Marlbridge teaches in English only. For [DP Language B](/boards/ib/ib-dp/language-b/) and
[MYP Language Acquisition](/boards/ib/ib-myp/myp-language-acquisition/), no French, German,
Spanish or other language is offered. Any language named in a free resource there is an
example, not a language we teach; the lessons themselves are in English.

For Language B, the assessment parts a student prepares for are Paper 1 (one writing task, 30
marks), Paper 2 (listening, 25 marks, and reading, 40 marks) and the Individual Oral (30
marks), which the teacher marks and the IB moderates.

## What one-to-one IB tuition is good for

- **Higher Level content.** HL topics, such as calculus in Mathematics: Analysis and Approaches
  HL, often need more time than the school timetable gives them.
- **Paper technique.** IB markschemes reward method and clear reasoning. Working through past
  questions with a teacher shows where marks are lost.
- **Understanding the criteria.** For internal assessments and MYP tasks, a teacher can explain
  what each assessment criterion asks for and how a piece of work is judged.

**What tuition cannot do:** internal assessments, the Extended Essay and MYP projects must be
the student's own work, under the IB's academic integrity rules. A tutor can explain the
criteria and teach the subject; the writing has to be the student's, and the school's
supervisor stays responsible for guidance on the piece itself.

## Class times in Singapore time

Singapore is on UTC+8 and Pakistan on UTC+5, and neither changes its clocks, so Singapore is
three hours ahead of Lahore all year:

| In Singapore | In Lahore |
| --- | --- |
| 4:00 pm, after school | 1:00 pm |
| 7:00 pm | 4:00 pm |
| 2:00 pm Saturday | 11:00 am Saturday |

Because IB classes are one-to-one, the time is arranged around your child's timetable,
co-curricular activities and weekend.

Messages: WhatsApp replies come within an hour between 8am and 11pm Pakistan time, which is 11am
to 2am in Singapore. Emails are answered the same day.

Our teachers are in Lahore, and the named subject teacher delivers each class live online.
Marlbridge has no Singapore office, branch or staff.

For families whose school also teaches Cambridge IGCSE or A Level: Cambridge places Singapore
in administrative zone 5, and its May/June 2027 timetables for zones 1 to 5 run from 26 April
to 11 June 2027. Cambridge had not yet published entry deadlines or results dates for that
series when we last checked (23 November 2026); see our [exam calendar](/exam-calendar/).

## Fees for IB classes in Singapore dollars

IB classes are charged per 1-hour class. The [Singapore page](/singapore/) shows the fee in
Singapore dollars; it is an indicative conversion of the Pakistan fee, and the exact fee is
confirmed in writing before any payment.

**Not taught:** the Singapore national curriculum and its examinations, including the PSLE and
the Singapore-Cambridge GCE and SEC examinations. Families at schools teaching Cambridge IGCSE or
A Level can see those courses on the Singapore page too.

## What to prepare for the trial

1. Tell us the programme (DP or MYP), the subject and, for the Diploma, whether it is Higher or
   Standard Level.
2. Bring a recent test or a marked piece of work, so the teacher can see where marks were
   dropped.
3. Book the [free trial class](/trial/). Our guide to
   [group classes or one-to-one tuition](/articles/group-classes-or-one-to-one-tuition/)
   explains how one-to-one classes work.
