Families searching for Cambridge exam dates for 2027 will find some dates and not others.
Cambridge has published the exam timetables for the May/June 2027 series, but at the time of
writing it had not published the key-dates document that gives that series' entry deadlines
and results dates. This article separates what is confirmed from what is not, and says
where each date comes from.

## What is published for May/June 2027

Cambridge allocates every country to one of six administrative zones, and its final June 2027
exam timetables are issued zone by zone. Our [exam calendar](/exam-calendar/) records them
from Cambridge's Version 1 timetables, dated November 2026:

- **Zones 1 to 5:** the timetabled exam period runs from 26 April to 11 June 2027.
- **Zone 6:** the timetabled exam period runs from 26 April to 12 June 2027.
- **Zone 5:** the first week of the timetable is 26 April to 1 May 2027.

These are the outer dates of the exam window. The date and time of a particular paper depend on
the subject, the paper and your zone, so a student needs the zone timetable itself, not the
window above. Cambridge publishes one PDF timetable for each of the six zones. They are linked
from the [exam calendar](/exam-calendar/), and Cambridge's administrative zone lookup shows
which zone a country is in.

## What is not yet published

At the time of writing, Cambridge's key-dates page showed no key-dates document for the
June 2027 series, so the following are unconfirmed:

- the final and late entry deadlines;
- the results release dates for IGCSE, O Level and AS and A Level;
- the deadline for enquiries about results and for access to scripts.

We have not estimated these from earlier years. Our records of Cambridge's pages for this
series were last read on 23 November 2026. We will check Cambridge's key-dates page weekly
from 4 January 2027 and update this article and the [exam calendar](/exam-calendar/) when the
document appears. Until then, ask your school's exams officer, or the centre where you enter,
to confirm the deadlines that apply to you.

## What a key-dates document contains

For the October/November 2026 series, Cambridge's International key-dates document (number
746006) lists a final entries deadline of 16 August 2026, a late entries deadline of
21 September 2026, results release dates of 7 January 2027 for Cambridge International
AS & A Level and 14 January 2027 for Cambridge IGCSE and Cambridge O Level, an enquiries
about results deadline of 26 February 2027 and an access to scripts deadline of
12 March 2027. The same document type will give the equivalent dates for June 2027 when it is
published. It is written for international centres; UK centres have a separate document.

## Other 2027 series with published dates

- **October/November 2026 results:** 7 January 2027 for AS & A Level and 14 January 2027
  for IGCSE and O Level, from the key-dates document above.
- **February/March 2027:** Cambridge's March series monthly calendar gives an exam period of
  3 February to 4 March 2027 and results available to schools on 13 May 2027. The March series is
  offered only in certain countries, such as India, so check your syllabus and
  your centre.

## Which series is which

Cambridge runs May/June and October/November as its two main global series, and a
February/March series in certain countries only. For how the two main series differ for
O Level candidates, see [O Level in May/June or October/November](/articles/o-level-june-or-november-exam-series/).
For the difference between the two qualifications, see
[IGCSE or O Level: which should my child take?](/articles/igcse-or-o-level-which-should-my-child-take/).
