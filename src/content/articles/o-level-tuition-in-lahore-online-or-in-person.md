Most O Level students in Pakistan sit Cambridge O Level in the June or November series, often
in several subjects at once, and many families look for extra help in one or two of them. In Lahore the
first question is often practical: should the classes be at an academy, or online at home?
At Marlbridge the answer can be either, with the same teachers.

## The two options

**In person, in Lahore.** Classes are taught at the Learners Academy campus, Commercial 17/18,
Block B, Al-Kabir Town, Lahore. Marlbridge is the international branch of Learners Academy.

**Live online, anywhere in Pakistan.** Students in Karachi, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad
or elsewhere join the same teachers live online. Online classes are live, not recorded, and
are priced the same as classes at the academy.

Class times for both are agreed with you before the first class.

## How they compare

| | At the academy | Live online |
|---|---|---|
| Teachers | Same subject teachers | Same subject teachers |
| Fees | Same fee | Same fee |
| Travel | Needed, within Lahore | None |
| Works for | Students who focus better away from home | Students outside Lahore, or with long journeys |

Neither format changes what is taught: every class follows the official Cambridge syllabus for
the subject, and the course pages on this site list every topic.

## Group or one-to-one

Both formats come as group classes (charged per subject per month) or one-to-one classes
(charged per class). The [Pakistan page](/pakistan/) shows both fees in rupees, and the
discounts for three or more subjects and for siblings. Our guide to
[group classes or one-to-one tuition](/articles/group-classes-or-one-to-one-tuition/) explains
which suits which student.

## How to decide

1. **Distance.** If the academy is a long journey, online saves that time every week for the
   same teacher and fee.
2. **Focus.** Some students work better in a classroom away from home; others are fine at a
   desk with a laptop. You know your child.
3. **Subjects.** If your child needs help in a subject the school teaches differently, ask for
   that subject's teacher in the trial, whichever format you choose.
4. **Try it.** The [free trial class](/trial/) runs in the format you are considering. If you
   are unsure between O Level and IGCSE for a subject, our guide
   [IGCSE or O Level](/articles/igcse-or-o-level-which-should-my-child-take/) may help first.

You can message us on WhatsApp or use the trial form; tell us whether you prefer the academy
or online, and we will confirm a teacher and a time.
