Many families in the UAE look for a tutor for one subject, usually IGCSE or A Level
Mathematics, a science or English, a term or two before exams. Online tuition from outside the
country can work well, but it helps to know exactly how it works before booking. This guide
explains how Marlbridge does it. The [UAE page](/uae/) has the current fees and the full list
of what is taught.

## Who teaches, and from where

Every class is taught live online by a named subject teacher from our academy in Lahore,
Pakistan. Marlbridge has no office, branch or teacher based in the UAE, and does not claim to.
Each teacher has a profile on the [tutoring page](/tutoring/) listing the subjects and boards
they teach.

## Which boards and qualifications

UAE schools that follow a British curriculum mostly use Pearson Edexcel or Cambridge, often
both in the same school. Marlbridge teaches:

- **Pearson Edexcel** International GCSE and International A Level, for example
  [Edexcel IGCSE Mathematics](/boards/edexcel/igcse/mathematics/).
- **Cambridge** IGCSE and AS & A Level.
- **OxfordAQA** and **AQA** courses.
- **IB** Diploma and MYP, one-to-one only.

Each course page names the exact specification code it is taught against, so you can check it
matches your child's school. If you are not sure which board your child's school uses, the
school's options booklet or the front page of a past paper will say.

**Not taught:** the UAE Ministry of Education curriculum, Indian CBSE or ICSE, the American
curriculum or Advanced Placement (AP), and the French Baccalauréat.

## Class times

The UAE is one hour behind Lahore all year, because neither country changes its clocks. A
5:00 pm class in Dubai is 6:00 pm in Lahore, so after-school times in the UAE fall in the
early evening for the teacher. Group class times are agreed with the families in each group
before the trial; one-to-one classes can be arranged at a time that suits you.

## Group or one-to-one

- **Group classes** are small, with a published maximum group size, and are charged per
  subject per month. They run three times a week for each subject.
- **One-to-one classes** are charged per class, and you choose how many to take.
- **IB** is one-to-one only.

The UAE group fee is a set price in dirhams. One-to-one and IB figures in dirhams are
indicative conversions of the Pakistan fee, and the exact fee is confirmed in writing before
any payment. The [pricing page](/pricing/) and the [UAE page](/uae/) show the current numbers
and discounts. Our guide to [group classes or one-to-one tuition](/articles/group-classes-or-one-to-one-tuition/)
explains which suits which student.

## Booking the free trial

1. Fill in the short [trial form](/trial/) with the subject, board if you know it, and your
   time zone. You can also message us on WhatsApp.
2. We reply, match a specialist teacher for that subject and level, and agree a time in UAE
   time.
3. Your child attends the first class free, in the format you are considering: a real group
   class, or an individual class.
4. You decide whether to continue. There is no registration fee, and fees are billed monthly
   only after the trial.

## Before the first class

- Have the board, the syllabus code and the exam series ready (for example, Edexcel
  International GCSE Mathematics A, sitting in summer).
- Bring recent marked work or a mock paper: it shows the teacher where marks are being lost.
- Look at the course's [printable checklist](/checklists/) together and tick what feels secure.
  The topics left unticked are where the first classes should go.

times on their country pages.
