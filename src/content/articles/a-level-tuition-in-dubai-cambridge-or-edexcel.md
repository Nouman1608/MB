British-curriculum schools in Dubai and Abu Dhabi mostly teach one of two international
A Levels: Cambridge International AS & A Level or Pearson Edexcel International A Level (IAL).
Both are accepted by universities, but they are built differently, and a tutor needs to know
which one your child sits, and which papers are already done.

## How the two are structured

**Pearson Edexcel International A Level is unit-based.** Each subject is split into units that
can be sat in different exam series. In Mathematics (YMA01), for example, the full A Level is
six units of 1 hour 30 minutes each: four Pure units (P1 to P4) and one pair of applied units,
such as Mechanics 1 with Statistics 1. Science IALs have six units, three for the International
AS and three more for the full A Level. Units can be resat to raise the overall grade.

**Cambridge International AS & A Level is staged.** The AS papers can be taken at the end of
the first year and carried forward, or every paper can be sat together at the end of the course.
In Physics (9702), for example, Papers 1 to 3 make up the AS Level and Papers 4 and 5 complete
the A Level.

## Why it matters for tuition

- **Edexcel IAL:** tuition is planned unit by unit. A student about to sit P3 needs P3, not a
  general revision of everything, and a weak unit grade can be targeted for a resit.
- **Cambridge:** if AS papers have already been sat, the focus moves to the A Level papers; if
  every paper will be taken together, the whole course needs revising before the final series.
- **Either way,** the teacher should work from that specification's own past papers and mark
  schemes. An Edexcel P1 question and a Cambridge Pure Mathematics 1 question test similar
  content in different ways.

## Class times and fees from the UAE

The UAE is one hour behind Lahore all year, so a 5:00 pm class in Dubai is 6:00 pm in Lahore.
Group class times are agreed with the families in each group before the trial; one-to-one
classes can be arranged at a time that suits you. Every class is taught live online by a named
subject teacher from our academy in Lahore. Marlbridge has no office, branch or teacher in the
UAE.

Group classes have a set UAE fee in dirhams, per subject per month; one-to-one classes are
charged per class. The [UAE page](/uae/) shows the current fees. Our guide to
[how online tuition works from the UAE](/articles/how-online-igcse-and-a-level-tuition-works-from-the-uae/)
covers the rest.

## Before the trial class

1. Check the specification code on a past paper or school report, and note which units or
   papers have already been sat, with the grades.
2. Bring a recent test, so the teacher can see where marks are being lost.
3. Book the [free trial class](/trial/) in the format you are considering, group or one-to-one.
