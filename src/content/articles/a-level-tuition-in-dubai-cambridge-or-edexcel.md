Students at British-curriculum schools in Dubai and Abu Dhabi may sit one of two international
A Levels: Cambridge International AS & A Level or Pearson Edexcel International A Level (IAL).
Both are accepted by universities, but they are built differently, and a tutor needs to know
which one your child sits, and which papers are already done.

## How the two are structured

**Pearson Edexcel International A Level is unit-based.** Each subject is split into units that
can be sat in different exam series. In Mathematics (YMA01), for example, the full A Level is
six units of 1 hour 30 minutes each: four Pure units (P1 to P4, coded WMA11 to WMA14) and one pair of applied units,
such as Mechanics 1 (WME01) with Statistics 1 (WST01). Science IALs have six units, three for the International
AS and three more for the full A Level. Units can be resat to raise the overall grade.

**Cambridge International AS & A Level is staged.** The AS papers can be taken at the end of
the first year and carried forward, or every paper can be sat together at the end of the course.
In Physics (9702), for example, Papers 1 to 3 make up the AS Level and Papers 4 and 5 complete
the A Level.

## Why it matters for tuition

- **Edexcel IAL:** tuition is planned unit by unit. A student about to sit P3 needs P3, not a
  general revision of everything, and a weak unit grade can be targeted for a resit.
- **Cambridge:** if AS papers have already been sat, the focus moves to the A Level papers; if
  every paper will be taken together, the whole course needs revising before the final series.
- **Either way,** the teacher should work from that specification's own past papers and mark
  schemes. An Edexcel P1 question and a Cambridge Pure Mathematics 1 question test similar
  content in different ways.

## Time zone, regulators and exam dates for UAE students

**Clocks.** Dubai runs on UTC+4 and Lahore on UTC+5, with no seasonal change in either country;
the gap is one hour in every month, so 5:00 pm in Dubai is 6:00 pm in Lahore. In a group,
the class time is agreed among its families and confirmed before the trial; a one-to-one class
is set around your child's own timetable. The teacher joins live from our Lahore academy; we
have nobody based in the UAE.

**Regulators.** Private schools in Dubai answer to the Knowledge and Human Development
Authority (KHDA), which the UAE government portal describes as responsible for the growth and quality of private education in Dubai. In Abu Dhabi
the education sector regulator is the Department of Education and Knowledge (ADEK). Ask your
child's school which A Level it enters candidates for; the answer decides which of the two
sections above applies.

**Cambridge in 2027.** Dubai sits in Cambridge administrative zone 4, which decides the
timetable a school receives. Cambridge's zone timetables for zones 1 to 5 schedule the
May/June 2027 exams between 26 April and 11 June. Entry and results dates were still
unpublished on 23 November 2026; your school's exams officer will have them once released, and
the [exam calendar](/exam-calendar/) here is updated at the same time.

**Fees.** Group classes have a set UAE fee in dirhams, per subject per month; one-to-one
classes are charged per class. The [UAE page](/uae/) shows the current fees. Our guide to
[how online tuition works from the UAE](/articles/how-online-igcse-and-a-level-tuition-works-from-the-uae/)
covers the rest.

## Preparing for the trial

1. Check the specification code on a past paper or school report, and note which units or
   papers have already been sat, with the grades.
2. Bring a recent test paper or marked homework; the teacher reads it for the questions that cost marks.
3. Book the [free trial class](/trial/) in the format you are considering, group or one-to-one.
