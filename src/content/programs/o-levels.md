Marlbridge teaches **Cambridge O Level** to students typically aged 14–16, across 17
subjects including the sciences, mathematics, business, Islamiyat, Pakistan Studies and
Urdu.

## How lessons run

Group classes run **45 to 50 minutes, three times a week, per subject**, with a maximum of
15 students. One-to-one classes run for **an hour**, as often as you want them. Teaching is
in person at the academy in Pakistan and live online everywhere else, with lesson times
agreed in your own time zone.

## What lessons are taught against

Classes follow the current Cambridge syllabus for the subject, by syllabus code. The topic
lists, assessment structure and paper weightings for every O Level subject Marlbridge
teaches are published on this site — including which syllabuses are being taught out and
which have replaced them — so a family can check the course matches the entry the school
has made. The printable syllabus checklists cover the same ground and are free to use.

## Choosing a teacher

Every O Level class is taught by a named subject specialist whose profile lists the subjects
they teach and cites where those details were verified from. You will know who is teaching
before the first class, not after.
