Marlbridge teaches International GCSE to students typically aged 14–16, for **Cambridge,
OxfordAQA and Pearson Edexcel** — 22 subjects across the sciences, mathematics, business,
humanities and languages.

## How lessons run

Group classes run **45 to 50 minutes, three times a week, per subject**, with a maximum of
15 students. One-to-one classes run for **an hour**, as often as you want them. Teaching is
in person at the academy in Pakistan and live online everywhere else, with lesson times
agreed in your own time zone.

## What lessons are taught against

Each class is taught against the published syllabus for the board the student is actually
entered for — not a generic IGCSE course. The topic list, assessment structure and paper
weightings for every board and subject combination Marlbridge teaches are published on this
site, so you can see before enrolling exactly what a course covers and how it is examined.
The printable syllabus checklists cover the same ground and are free to use whether or not
you study with us.

## Choosing a teacher

Every IGCSE class is taught by a named subject specialist whose profile lists the subjects
they teach and cites where those details were verified from. You will know who is teaching
before the first class, not after.
