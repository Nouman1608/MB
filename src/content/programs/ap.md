Marlbridge teaches eleven AP® courses one-to-one, live online, with in-person classes also available in Lahore:

- **Sciences:** [AP Chemistry](/advanced-course-resources/chemistry/), [AP Biology](/advanced-course-resources/biology/), [AP Physics 1](/advanced-course-resources/physics-1/), [AP Physics 2](/advanced-course-resources/physics-2/), [AP Physics C: Mechanics](/advanced-course-resources/physics-c-mechanics/) and [AP Physics C: Electricity and Magnetism](/advanced-course-resources/physics-c-electricity-and-magnetism/)
- **Mathematics:** [AP Calculus AB](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-ab/), [AP Calculus BC](/advanced-course-resources/calculus-bc/) and [AP Statistics](/advanced-course-resources/statistics/)
- **Economics:** [AP Microeconomics](/advanced-course-resources/microeconomics/) and [AP Macroeconomics](/advanced-course-resources/macroeconomics/)

Teaching follows the College Board course frameworks for the 2026–27 school year and the May 2027 exams. Each course page in the free [Marlbridge AP study library](/advanced-course-resources/) sets out the official units with their exam weightings, the exam format, and the study guides, practice questions and checklists available for each topic.

Marlbridge is an independent tuition provider, not an AP school or an exam centre. Students take the AP Exams through their school or an authorized test centre. For the AP science courses, tuition supports the hands-on laboratory work the courses require in school; it does not replace it.

AP tuition is **one-to-one only**: Rs 6,000 per class in Pakistan, with indicative conversions for other currencies on the [pricing page](/pricing/#one-to-one-only). The multi-subject and sibling discounts do not apply. The first class is a free trial. AP courses are taught by the same Marlbridge subject teachers who teach Chemistry, Biology, Physics, Mathematics, Statistics and Economics at other levels.
