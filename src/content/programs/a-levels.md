Marlbridge teaches AS and A Level to students typically aged 16–18, for **AQA, Cambridge,
OCR, OxfordAQA and Pearson Edexcel** — 18 subjects, including the sciences, mathematics,
economics, business, law and the humanities.

## How lessons run

Group classes run **45 to 50 minutes, three times a week, per subject**, with a maximum of
15 students. One-to-one classes run for **an hour**, as often as you want them. Teaching is
in person at the academy in Pakistan and live online everywhere else, with lesson times
agreed in your own time zone.

## AS and A2

AS and full A Level are taught as the same programme, tracked by stage: material published
on this site is tagged AS or A2 so a student sitting AS this year can tell which topics are
in their series and which belong to the second year.

## What lessons are taught against

Each class is taught against the published specification for the board the student is
entered for. The topic lists, assessment structure and paper weightings for every board and
subject combination Marlbridge teaches are published here, so you can see what a course
covers and how it is examined before enrolling.

## Choosing a teacher

Every A Level class is taught by a named subject specialist whose profile lists the subjects
they teach and cites where those details were verified from. You will know who is teaching
before the first class, not after.
